## Statement-fidelity findings (all 16 cards compared)

Substantive:

1. **E-SS4.PR-1 (p. 131)** — printed growth condition: `|f (z)| ≤ A e^{a|z|^q}` where
   `1/p + 1/q = 1`; the card writes `\abs{f(z)} \leq A e^{C\abs{z}^q}` "for some $A,C>0$",
   replacing the printed constant `a` by `C`.
2. **E-SS4.PR-2 (p. 132–133)** — the card omits the printed final sentence of part (c):
   "Note that the solution u depends on the choice c."
3. **E-SS4.EX-5(a) (p. 128)** — printed "if α1 , . . . , αk are the roots of **R** in the upper
   half-plane" (R = P/Q has no roots; the zeros meant are those of Q). The card writes "roots of
   $Q$" — a deliberate correction of the book's slip, not a reproduction.

OCR-ambiguous (the card cannot be confirmed against the text layer):

4. **E-SS4.EX-12 (p. 131)** — printed OCR: "(b) Apply the Phragmén–Lindelöf principle to the
   function F (z) = g(z)e^{γz} where γ = iπ sin π/(2β) and the sector 0 ≤ θ ≤ π/β < π, and let
   **β → π** to deduce that e^{πz} g(z) is bounded in the closed upper half-plane." The card has
   `γ = iπ e^{-i\pi/(2\beta)}/\sin \pi/(2\beta)` and "let $\beta\downarrow 1$". The card's `β↓1` is
   the reading under which the sector `0 ≤ θ ≤ π/β` tends to the half-plane `0 ≤ θ ≤ π`; the OCR
   text layer cannot settle it, and it also loses the exponent in `|g(z)| ≤ c e^{πR sin (θ/2)}`
   (card: `\sin^2(\theta/2)`). Check the page image before changing the card.
5. **E-SS4.PR-3** and **E-SS4.PR-4** are starred (∗) in print; the cards do not record the star.
6. **E-SS4.PR-3 (p. 133)** — the card adds the imperative "Prove that" in (a) and (b) where the
   printed problem states the assertions; content unchanged. Its hint matches the printed hint.
