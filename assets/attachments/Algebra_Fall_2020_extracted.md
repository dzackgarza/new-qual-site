# Algebra Qualifying Exam, Fall 2020

<a id="instructions"></a>
## Instructions

Complete all 8 problems. Each problem is worth 10 points. In multi-part problems, you may assume the result of any part (even if you have not been able to do it) in working on subsequent parts.

<a id="problems"></a>
## Problems

1. Using Sylow theory show that every group of order $2p$  where $p$  is a prime is not simple.
2. Classify all groups of order $2p$  and justify your answer. For the non-abelian group(s), give a presentation by generators and relations.

1. Let $G$  be a group of order $60$  whose Sylow- $3$  subgroup is normal.

1. Prove that $G$  is solvable.
2. Prove that the Sylow- $5$  subgroup is also normal.

1. Define what it means for a finite extension field $E$  over $F$  to be a Galois extension.
2. Determine the Galois group of $f(x)=x^{3}-7$  over $\mathbb{Q}$ . [Justify your answer carefully.]
3. Find all subfields of the splitting field of $f(x)=x^{3}-7$  over $\mathbb{Q}$ .

2. Let $K$  be a Galois extension of the field $F$ , and let $F\subset E\subset K$  be an inclusion of fields. Let $G$  be the Galois group of $K$  over $F$ , and $H$  that of $K$  over $E$ . Suppose that $H$  contains $N_{G}(P)$ , where $P$  is a $p$  Sylow subgroup of $G$  ( $p$  is a prime). Prove that $[E:F]\equiv 1 \hspace{0.25em} mod \hspace{0.25em} p$ .
3. Consider the following $3\times 3$ -matrix.

$$
B=\left(\begin{matrix}1&3&3\\2&2&3\\-1&-2&-2\end{matrix}\right)
$$

1. Find the minimal polynomial of $B$ .
2. Find a $3\times 3$  matrix $J$  in Jordan canonical form such that $B=PJP^{-1}$  where $P$  is an invertible matrix.

4. Let $R$  be a ring with $1$  and $M$  a left $R$ -module. If $I$  is a left ideal of $R$ , define

$$
IM=\left\{\sum_{finite}^{​}a_{i}m_{i}\mid a_{i}\in I, \hspace{0.25em} m_{i}\in M\right\}
$$

- to be the collection of all finite sums of elements of the form $am$ , where $a\in I$  and $m\in M$

1. Prove that $IM$  is a submodule of $M$ .
2. Let $M$  and $N$  be left $R$ -modules, $I$  a nilpotent left ideal of $R$  and $f:M\rightarrow N$  an $R$ -module homomorphism. Prove that if the induced homomorphism $‾:M/IM\rightarrow N/IN$  is surjective then so is $f$ .

5. Let $A$  be an $n\times n$  matrix over the real numbers $\mathbb{R}$ . One can make $\mathbb{R}^{n}$  into a $\mathbb{R[}x]$ -module by letting $f(x)\cdot v=f(A)(v)$  for $f(x\mathbb{)\in R[}x]$  and $v\in \mathbb{R}^{n}$ . Assume that the module $\mathbb{R}^{n}$  has the following direct sum decomposition:

$$
\mathbb{R}^{n}\cong \frac{\mathbb{R[}x]}{\left⟨(x-1)^{3}\right⟩}\oplus \frac{\mathbb{R[}x]}{\left⟨(x^{2}+1)^{2}\right⟩}\oplus \frac{\mathbb{R[}x]}{\left⟨(x-1)(x^{2}-1)(x^{2}+1)^{4}\right⟩}\oplus \frac{\mathbb{R[}x]}{\left⟨(x+2)(x^{2}+1)^{2}\right⟩}
$$

1. Determine the elementary divisors and invariant factors of $A$ .
2. Determine the minimal polynomial of $A$ .
3. Determine the characteristic polynomial of $A$ .

6. Let $A$  be an $n\times n$  matrix over $\mathbb{C}$  such that the group generated by $A$  under multiplication is finite. Prove that $Tr(A^{-1})=\overline{Tr(A)}$ , where $\overline{Tr(A)}$  denotes the complex conjugate of the trace of $A$ .
