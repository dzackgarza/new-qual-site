## Problems

1. Let $p$  and $q$  be distinct primes.

1. Let $‾\in Z_{p}$  denote the class of $q$  modulo $p$  and let $k$  denote the order of $‾$  as an element of $Z_{p}^{*}$ . Prove that no group of order $pq^{k}$  is simple.
2. Let $G$  be a group of order $pq$ . Prove $G$  is not simple.

2. Let $G$  be a group of order 30.

1. Show that $G$  has a subgroup of order 15.
2. Show that every group of order 15 is cyclic.
3. Show that $G$  is isomorphic to some semidirect product $Z_{15}\rtimes Z_{2}$ .
4. Exhibit three nonisomorphic groups of order 30, and prove that they are not isomorphic. You are not required to use your answer to (c).

1. Define prime ideal, and give an example of a nontrivial ideal in the ring $\mathbb{Z}$ , that is not prime, and show it is not prime.
2. Define maximal ideal and give an example of a nontrivial maximal ideal in the ring $\mathbb{Z}$ , and show it is maximal.

3. Let $R$  be a commutative ring with unit element $1\ne 0$ . Recall that $x\in R$  is called nilpotent if $x^{n}=0$  for some positive integer $n$ .

1. Show that the collection of all nilpotent elements of $R$  forms an ideal.
2. Show that if $x$  is nilpotent, then $x$  is contained in every prime ideal of $R$ .
3. Suppose that $x\in R$  is not nilpotent, and let $S=\{x^{n}:n\in N\}$ . There is at least one ideal of $R$  disjoint from $S$ , namely $(0)$ . By Zorn's lemma, the set of ideals disjoint from $S$  has an element that is maximal with respect to inclusion, say $I$ . In other words, $I$  is disjoint from $S$ , and if $J$  is any ideal disjoint from $S$  with $I\subseteq J\subseteq R$ , then $J=I$  or $J=R$ . Show that $I$  is a prime ideal.
4. Deduce from (a) and (b) that the set of nilpotent elements of $R$  is the intersection of all of the prime ideals of $R$ .

4. For this problem, we let $L/K$  denote a finite extension of fields.

1. Define what it means for $L/K$  to be separable.
2. Show that if $K$  is a finite field, then $L/K$  is always separable.
3. Give an example of a finite extension $L/K$  that is not separable.

5. Let $K$  be the splitting field of $x^{4}-2$  over $Q$ , and let $G=Gal(K/Q)$ .

1. Show that $K/Q$  contains both $Q(\sqrt{-1})$  and $Q(\sqrt[4]{2})$  and has degree 8 over $Q$ .
2. Let $N=Gal(K/Q(\sqrt{-1}))$  and $H=Gal(K/Q(\sqrt[4]{2}))$ . Show that $N$  is normal in $G$  and that $NH=G$ . Hint: What field is left fixed by $NH$ ?
3. Show that $Gal(K/Q)$  is generated by elements $\sigma$  and $\tau$ , of orders 4 and 2 respectively, with $\tau \sigma \tau ^{-1}=\sigma ^{-1}$ .

- [This is equivalent to saying that $Gal(K/Q)$  is the dihedral group of order 8.]

4. How many distinct quartic subfields of $K$  are there? Justify your answer.

2. Suppose that $\alpha \in ‾$  satisfies $\alpha ^{17}=1$  and $\alpha \ne 1$ . Show that $F(\alpha )/F$  has degree 8.

1. What does it mean for a finite group $G$  to be solvable?

1. Suppose that $T$  is an $n\times n$  matrix over a field $F$  such that $T^{2}=I$ . Show that if the characteristic of $F$  is not equal to 2, then $T$  may be diagonalized, and enumerate the possibilities for the diagonal form of $T$ .
