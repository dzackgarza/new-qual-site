## Directions

Work all the problems. They are weighted evenly.

## Problems

1. Suppose $X$  is a compact metric space and $\mathscr{U}$  is an open covering of $X$ . Prove that there is a number $\delta >0$  so that for every $x\in X$ , the ball of radius $\delta$  centered at $x$  is contained in some element of $\mathscr{U}$ .
2. Prove that a connected normal space with more than a single point must be uncountable.
3. Suppose $f:X\rightarrow Y$  is a function, and let $G=\{(x,f(x)):x\in X\}\subset X\times Y$ .

1. Prove that if $Y$  is Hausdorff and $f$  is continuous, then $G$  is closed.
2. Prove that if $Y$  is compact and $G$  is closed, then $f$  is continuous. (Hint: Start by proving that the projection $p:X\times Y\rightarrow X$  is a closed map.)

4. Let $X$  be the topological space formed by filling in two disks in the torus, as shown. Calculate $H_{*}(X\mathbb{,Z)}$  by any method you wish.
5. Let $X$  be the topological space formed by identifying the edges of a pentagon, as shown.

- Suppose $Y$  is a 3-fold covering space of $X$ . What surfaces could $Y$  be? Justify your answer, but you need not exhibit the covering maps explicitly.

6. Let $n\geq 2$ . Prove that the covering map $\pi :S^{n}\mathbb{\rightarrow R}\mathbb{P}^{n}$  is not nullhomotopic.
7. Define a linear map $\mathbb{R}^{2}\rightarrow \mathbb{R}^{2}$  by the matrix

$$
A=\left[\begin{matrix}1&-1\\1&2\end{matrix}\right].
$$

- Let $T^{2}=\mathbb{R}^{2}/\mathbb{Z}^{2}$  be endowed with the quotient topology.

1. Prove that $A$  induces a well-defined, continuous map $f:T^{2}\rightarrow T^{2}$ .
2. Suppose $g:T^{2}\rightarrow T^{2}$  is a continuous map that is homotopic to $f$ . Prove or disprove: $g$  has a fixed point.

8. Do one of the following.

1. Give (with justification) a contractible subset $X\subset \mathbb{R}^{2}$  that is not a retract of $\mathbb{R}^{2}$ .
2. Give (with justification) two topological spaces that have the same homology groups but that are not homotopy equivalent.
