## Problems

1. What does it mean to say that $p:Y\rightarrow X$  is a covering map?
2. Let $p:Y\rightarrow X$  be a covering map. Prove that, for any $y\in Y$ , the homomorphism

$$
p_{*}:\pi _{1}(Y,y)\rightarrow \pi _{1}(X,p(y))
$$

- is injective.

3. Let $p:Y\rightarrow X$  be a covering map with $Y$  and $X$  path-connected. Suppose that $p_{*}$  is an isomorphism. Prove that $p$  is a homeomorphism.

1. Let $X$  be the topological space obtained by identifying three distinct points on the torus $S^{1}\times S^{1}$ . Calculate the fundamental group of $X$ .
2. Let $X$  be a topological space obtained by attaching a 2-cell to $\mathbb{R}P^{2}$  via some map $f:S^{1}\mathbb{\rightarrow R}P^{2}$ . What are the possibilities for the homology groups $H_{*}(X\mathbb{;Z)}$ ?
3. Show that $\mathbb{R}P^{2}\vee S^{1}$  is not homotopy equivalent to a compact surface (possibly with boundary).

1. State the Lefschetz Fixed Point Theorem for a finite simplicial complex $X$ .
2. Use degree theory to prove this Theorem in the case that $X=S^{n}$ .

4. Show that a compact subset of a Hausdorff space is closed.
5. A topological space is totally disconnected if its only connected subsets are one-point sets. Is it true that if $X$  has the discrete topology, it is totally disconnected? Is the converse true? Justify your answers.
6. Recall that a topological space $X$  is said to be regular if for every point $p\in X$  and closed subset $F\subset X$  not containing $p$ , there exists disjoint open sets $U,V$  with $p\in U$  and $F\subset V$ . Let $X$  be a regular space that has a countable basis for its topology, and let $U$  be an open subset of $X$ .

1. Show that $U$  is a countable union of closed subsets of $X$ .
2. Show that there is a continuous function $f:X\rightarrow [0,1]$  such that $f(x)>0$  for $x\in U$  and $f(x)=0$  for $x\notin U$ .
