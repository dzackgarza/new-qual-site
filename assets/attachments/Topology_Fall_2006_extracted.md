# Topology Qualifying Exam (August 15, 2006)

<a id="topology-qualifying-exam"></a>
## Instructions

In problems 1, 2, and 3, give complete arguments starting from the definitions.

<a id="problems"></a>
## Problems

1. A topological space $X$  is sequentially compact if every infinite sequence in $X$  has a convergent subsequence. Prove that every compact metric space is sequentially compact.

1. Prove that if the space $X$  is connected and locally path connected then $X$  is path connected.
2. Is the converse true? Give a proof or a counterexample.

2. If $f$  is a function from $X$  to $Y$ , consider the graph $G=\{(x,y)\in X\times Y\mid f(x)=y\}$ .

1. Prove that if $f$  is continuous and $Y$  is Hausdorff, then $G$  is a closed subset of $X\times Y$ .
2. Prove that if $G$  is closed and $Y$  is compact, then $f$  is continuous.

1. State van Kampen's theorem.
2. What are the fundamental groups of the torus $T^{2}$  and the real projective plane $\mathbb{R}P^{2}$ ? (Do not prove your answers.)
3. Use van Kampen's theorem and part (b) to compute the fundamental group of the one-point union $X=T^{2}\mathbb{\vee R}P^{2}$ .

1. Give the definitions of covering space and deck transformation (or covering transformation).
2. Describe the universal cover of the Klein bottle and its group of deck transformations.

3. Prove that there does not exist a continuous map $f:S^{2}\rightarrow S^{2}$  from the unit sphere in $\mathbb{R}^{3}$  to itself such that $f(x)⟂x$  (as vectors in $\mathbb{R}^{3}$ ) for all $x\in S^{2}$ .

1. What is the degree of the antipodal map $a_{n}:S^{n}\rightarrow S^{n}$ ? (Do not prove your answer.)
2. Define the structure of a CW complex on real projective $n$ -space $\mathbb{R}P^{n}$ .
3. Use parts (a) and (b) to compute the cellular homology of $\mathbb{R}P^{n}$ . Show your work.

1. State the Mayer-Vietoris theorem.
2. Use it to compute the homology of the space $X$  obtained by gluing two solid tori along their boundary as follows. Let $D^{2}$  be the unit disk and let $S^{1}$  be the unit circle in the complex plane $\mathbb{C}$ . Let $A=S^{1}\times D^{2}$  and $B=S^{1}\times D^{2}$ . Then $X$  is the quotient space of the disjoint union $A\sqcup B$  obtained by identifying $(z,w)\in A$  with $(zw^{3},w)\in B$  for all $(z,w)\in S^{1}\times S^{1}$ .
