## Problems

1. Does there exist a conformal mapping of the region $\Omega _{1}=\{z\mathbb{\in C:|}z-1|<1/2\}$  onto the region $\Omega _{2}=\{z\mathbb{\in C:|}z+1|>1\}$ ?
2. Find all holomorphic functions: $f\mathbb{:C\smallsetminus \{}0\mathbb{\}\rightarrow C}$  such that

$$
|f(z)|<\left(|z|^{1/2}+|z|^{-1/2}\right) \quad for all \quad z\ne 0
$$

3. Suppose that $f(z)$  is an entire function and that $lim_{z\rightarrow \infty }f(z)=\infty$ . Prove that $f$  must be a polynomial.
4. Show that

$$
\int_{0}^{\infty }\frac{logx}{1+x^{4}} \, dx=-\frac{\pi ^{2}}{8\sqrt{2}}
$$

5. Let $\mathbb{D}$  denote the unit open disk in the complex plane. Does there exist an analytic function $f\mathbb{:D\rightarrow D}$  such that

1. $f(1/2)=3/4$
2. $f^{'}(1/2)=2/3$

6. Let $\lambda$  be a fixed complex number with $Re(\lambda )>1$ . How many solutions the equation

$$
e^{z}=z+\lambda
$$

- has in the left half-plane $\{z\mathbb{\in C:}Re(z)<0\}$ ?

7. Suppose that $f$  is complex analytic in a punctured neighborhood $U$  of $z_{0}$  except for poles at all points of a sequence $\{z_{n}\}\rightarrow z_{0}$ . Show that $f(U)$  is dense in the complex plane (note: $z_{0}$  is not an isolated singularity of $f$ ).
