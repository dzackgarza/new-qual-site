# Algebra Qualifying Exam, January 2017

Problems 2 and 8 are worth 15 points each, and all others are worth 10 points (90 points total).

<a id="problems"></a>
## Problems

1. Let $G$  be a finite group and let $\pi :G\rightarrow Sym(G)$  be the Cayley representation. (Recall that this means that for an element $x$  in $G$ , $\pi (x)$  acts by left translation on $G$ .) Prove that $\pi (x)$  is an odd permutation if and only if the order $|\pi (x)|$  of $\pi (x)$  is even and $|G|/|\pi (x)|$  is odd.

1. How many isomorphism classes of abelian groups of order 56 are there? Give a representative for one of each class.
2. Prove that if $G$  is a group of order 56, then either the Sylow-2 subgroup or the Sylow-7 subgroup is normal.
3. Give two non-isomorphic groups of order 56 where the Sylow-7 is normal, and the Sylow-2 subgroup is not normal. Justify that your two groups are not isomorphic.

2. Let $R$  be a commutative ring with $1$ . Suppose that $M$  is a free $R$ -module with a finite basis $X$ .

1. Let $I$  be a proper ideal of $R$ . Prove that $M/IM$  is a free $R/I$ -module with basis $X^{'}$ , where $X^{'}$  is the image of $X$  under the canonical map $M\rightarrow M/IM$ .
2. Prove that any two bases of $M$  have the same number of elements. You may assume that the result is true when $R$  is a field.

1. Let $R$  be an integral domain with quotient field $F$ . Suppose that $p(x)$ , $a(x)$ , and $b(x)$ , are monic polynomials in $F[x]$  with $p(x)=a(x)b(x)$ , and with $p(x)$  in $R[x]$ , $a(x)$  not in $R[x]$ , and both $a(x)$  and $b(x)$  not constant. Prove that $R$  is not a UFD. You may assume Gauss' lemma.
2. Prove that $\mathbb{Z[}2\sqrt{2}]$  is not a UFD. Hint: let $p(x)=x^{2}-2$ .

1. Let $R$  be an integral domain, and let $M$  be a nonzero torsion $R$ -module.
2. Prove that if $M$  is finitely generated then the annihilator in $R$  of $M$  is nonzero.
3. Give an example of a non-finitely generated torsion $R$ -module whose annihilator is $(0)$ . Justify your answer.

3. Let $A$  be an $n\times n$  matrix with all entries equal to $0$  except for the $n-1$  entries just above the diagonal being equal to $2$ .

1. What is the Jordan Canonical Form of $A$ , viewed as a matrix in $M_{n}\mathbb{(C)}$ ?
2. Find a non-zero matrix $P\in M_{n}\mathbb{(C)}$  such that $P^{-1}AP$  is in Jordan Canonical Form.

4. Let $F$  be a field and let $f(x)\in F[x]$ .

1. Define what is a splitting field of $f(x)$  over $F$ .
2. Let $F$  now be a finite field with $q$  elements. Let $E/F$  be a finite extension of degree $n>0$ . Exhibit an explicit polynomial $g(x)\in F[x]$  such that $E/F$  is a splitting field of $g(x)$  over $F$ . Fully justify your answer.
3. Show that the extension $E/F$  in (b) is a Galois extension.

1. Let $K$  denote the splitting field of $x^{5}-2$  over $\mathbb{Q}$ . Show that the Galois group of $K\mathbb{/Q}$  is isomorphic to the group of invertible matrices $\left(\begin{matrix}a&b\\0&1\end{matrix}\right)$  where $a\in \mathbb{F}_{5}^{*}$  and $b\in \mathbb{F}_{5}$ .
2. Determine all intermediate fields between the splitting field $K$  of $x^{5}-2$  and $\mathbb{Q}$  which are Galois over $\mathbb{Q}$ .
