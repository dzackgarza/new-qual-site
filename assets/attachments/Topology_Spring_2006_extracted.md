# Qualifying Exam in Topology — January 2006

<a id="qualifying-exam-in-topology"></a>
## Problems

1. Suppose $(X,d)$  is a metric space. State criteria for continuity of a function $f:X\rightarrow X$  in terms of

1. open sets,
2. $\epsilon$ 's and $\delta$ 's, and
3. convergent sequences.

- Then prove that (iii) implies (i).

2. Prove that every compact, Hausdorff topological space is normal.
3. Write $Y$  for the interval $[0,\infty )$ , equipped with the usual topology. Find, with proof, all subspaces $Z$  of $Y$  which are retractions of $Y$ .
4. State, without proof, a criterion in terms of the fundamental group for a covering map $p:\tilde{X}\rightarrow X$  to be regular. Give an example of a covering map that is not regular, and show explicitly how it fails to satisfy this criterion.
5. Start with the unit disk $\mathbb{D}^{2}$  and identify points on the boundary if their angles, thought of in polar coordinates, differ by a multiple of $\frac{\pi }{2}$ . Let $X$  be the resulting space. Use van Kampen's theorem to compute $\pi _{1}(X,*)$ .
6. Let $S^{2}\mathbb{\rightarrow R}\mathbb{P}^{2}$  be the universal covering map. Is this map null-homotopic? Give a proof of your answer.
7. Compute the homology of the space $X$  obtained by attaching a Mobius band to $\mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^{2}$  via a homeomorphism of its boundary circle to the standard $\mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^{1}$  in $\mathbb{R}\mathbb{P}^{2}$ .
8. Let $X=S^{2}/\{p_{1}=\cdots =p_{k}\}$  be the topological space obtained from the 2-sphere by identifying $k$  distinct points on it $(k\geq 2)$ .

1. Find the fundamental group of $X$ .
2. Find the Euler characteristic of $X$ .
3. Find the homology groups of $X$ .

9. Let $M$  and $N$  be finite CW-complexes.

1. Describe the cellular structure of $M\times N$  in terms of the cellular structure of $M$  and $N$ .
2. Show that the Euler characteristic of $M\times N$  is the product of the Euler characteristics of $M$  and $N$ .
