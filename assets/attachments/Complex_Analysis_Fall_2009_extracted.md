## Problems

1. Let $f$  be analytic on the unit disk. Is it possible that $f​\left(\frac{1}{n}\right)$  takes the following values for $n=2,3,4,\ldots$ ? Why or why not?

1. $(-1)^{n}$
2. $e^{-n}$  for $n$  even, and $0$  for $n$  odd
3. $\frac{1}{\lfloor \sqrt{n}\rfloor }$  (here $\lfloor x\rfloor$  is the greatest integer not greater than $x$ )
4. $\frac{n-2}{n-1}$

2. Use complex analysis to prove the Fundamental Theorem of Algebra: if $p(z)$  is a nonconstant polynomial with complex coefficients, then there exists a complex number $z_{0}$  such that $p(z_{0})=0$ .
3. Compute the integral

$$
\int_{0}^{\infty }\frac{cosx}{\left(1+x^{2}\right)^{2}} \, dx
$$

4. Give the Laurent series for the following functions, and characterize their singularities at zero (essential, removable or pole):

1. $\frac{sin^{2}z}{z}$
2. $ze^{-1/z^{2}}$
3. $\frac{1}{z(4-z)}$

5. Let $D$  be the domain in the complex plane consisting of the interior of the unit disk, with the subdisk of radius $\frac{1}{2}$  and center $\frac{1}{2}$  removed. Construct an analytic function that maps $D$  conformally onto the first quadrant $\mathfrak{R}z>0\mathfrak{,I}z>0$ . (See figure.)

6. Give Laplace's equation in polar coordinates. In other words, if $x=rcos\theta ,y=rsin\theta$ , write down a relation equivalent to

$$
\frac{\partial ^{2}f}{\partial x^{2}}+\frac{\partial ^{2}f}{\partial y^{2}}=0
$$

- in terms $r,\theta$ , and the partial derivatives of $f$  with respect to $r,\theta$ .
