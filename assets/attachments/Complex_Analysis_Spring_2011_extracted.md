## Problems

1. Include brief justification for your answers to Parts b) and c) of this problem.

1. Complete the definition: $f:\mathbb{R}^{n}\rightarrow \mathbb{R}^{m}$  is (real) differentiable at a point $a\in \mathbb{R}^{n}$  if there is a linear transformation ... .
2. Give an example of a function $g:\mathbb{R}^{2}\mathbb{\rightarrow R}$  whose first order partial derivatives exist everywhere, such that $g$  is not differentiable at $(0,0)$ .
3. Give an example of a function $h:\mathbb{R}^{2}\rightarrow \mathbb{R}^{2}$  which is differentiable everywhere, but not complex differentiable anywhere.

2. Find all meromorphic functions that have a simple pole at the point 2 and a double pole at $\infty$ , but are analytic elsewhere. Prove that your list is complete.
3. Write $\mathbb{D}$  for the open unit disc and set $G=\{z\mathbb{\in C:}Re(z)>0$  and $|z-1|>1\}$ . Find all conformal one-to-one maps of $G$  onto $\mathbb{D}$ . You may express the maps as compositions, but should explain why your list is complete.
4. Use methods of complex variables to evaluate the integral

$$
\int_{0}^{\infty }\frac{cosx-cos4x}{x^{2}} \, dx.
$$

5. Suppose $f$  is entire and has Taylor series $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty }a_{n}z^{n}$  about $0$ .

1. Express (no proof required) $a_{n}$  as a contour integral along the circle of radius $R$  centered at $0$ .
2. Apply Part a) to verify that the power series $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty }a_{n}z^{n}$  converges uniformly on each bounded subset of $\mathbb{C}$ .
3. Determine, with proof, those functions $f$  for which the power series $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty }a_{n}z^{n}$  converges uniformly on all of $\mathbb{C}$ .

6. Set $H_{+}=\{z\mathbb{\in C:}Imz>0\}$ . Suppose $f:H_{+}\mathbb{\cup R\rightarrow C}$  satisfies the following:

1. $f(i)=i$ ,
2. $f$  is continuous,
3. $f$  is analytic on $H_{+}$ ,
4. $f(z)$  is real if and only if $z$  is real.

- Show that $f\left(H_{+}\right)$  is a dense subset of $H_{+}$ .
