Instructions: Do as many problems as you can, as completely as you can. (b) (3 pts) Let A be a proper subring of $\mathbb { Z } [ i ]$ which contains Z. Suppose the field of fractions of A is Q(i). Show that A is NOT a unique factorization domain (UFD).

(5) (10 pts) Prove that an abelian group A is injective as a Z-module if and only if it is divisible.

(6) (a) (7 pts) Let $F \subseteq E$ be a field extension, and $f ( x ) \in F [ x ]$ . Prove that
