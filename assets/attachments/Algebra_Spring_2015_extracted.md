# 2015 Winter Algebra Qual

## Problems

1. For a prime $p$ , let $G$  be a finite $p$ -group, and let $N$  be a normal subgroup of $G$  of order $p$ . Prove that $N$  is contained in the center of $G$ .
2. Let $\mathbb{F}$  be a finite field.

1. Give, with proof, the decomposition of the additive group $\mathbb{(F,+)}$  into a direct sum of cyclic groups.
2. The exponent of a finite group is the least common multiple of the orders of its elements. Prove that a finite abelian group has an element of order equal to its exponent.
3. Prove that the multiplicative group $(\mathbb{F}^{\times },\cdot )$  is cyclic.

3. Let $F$  be a field, let $V$  be a finite dimensional $F$ -vector space, and let $A,B:V\rightarrow V$  be commuting $F$ -linear maps. Suppose there is a basis $\mathscr{B}_{1}$  with respect to which $A$  is diagonalizable and a basis $\mathscr{B}_{2}$  with respect to which $B$  is diagonalizable. Prove that there is a basis $\mathscr{B}_{3}$  with respect to which $A$  and $B$  are both diagonalizable.
4. Let $N$  be a positive integer, and let $G$  be a finite group of order $N$ .

1. Let $SymG$  be the set of all bijections from $G$  to $G$ , viewed as a group under composition. Note that $SymG\cong S_{N}$ . Prove that the Cayley map $C:G\rightarrow SymG$  given by $g\mapsto (x\mapsto gx)$  is an injective homomorphism.
2. Let $\Phi :SymG\rightarrow S_{N}$  be an isomorphism. For $a\in G$ , define $\epsilon (a)\in \{\pm 1\}$  to be the sign of the permutation $\Phi (C(a))$ . Suppose that a has order $d$ . Prove that $\epsilon (a)=-1$  if and only if $d$  is even and $N/d$  is odd.
3. Suppose $N>2$  and $N\equiv 2 \hspace{0.25em} (mod \hspace{0.25em} 4)$ . Prove that $G$  is not simple. (Hint: Use part b).)

5. Let $f(x)=x^{4}-5\in \mathbb{Q}[x]$ .

1. Compute the Galois group of $f$  over $\mathbb{Q}$ .
2. Compute the Galois group of $f$  over $\mathbb{Q(}\sqrt{5})$ .

6. Let $F$  be a field, and let $n$  be a positive integer. Consider

$$
A=\left[\begin{matrix}1&\ldots &1\\\vdots &\ddots &\vdots \\1&\ldots &1\end{matrix}\right]\in M_{n}(F)
$$

- Show that $A$  has a Jordan normal form over $F$ , and find it.
- (Suggestion: treat the cases $n\cdot 1\ne 0$  in $F$  and $n\cdot 1=0$  in $F$  separately.)

7. Let $R$  be a commutative ring. Let $S$  be a subset of $R$  which is nonempty, does not contain $0$ , and for all $x,y\in S$  we have $xy\in S$ . Let $\mathscr{I}$  be the set of all ideals $I$  of $R$  such that $I\cap S=⌀$ . Show that for every ideal $I\mathscr{\in I}$ , there is an ideal $J\mathscr{\in I}$  such that $I\subset J$  and $J$  is not properly contained in any other ideal in $\mathscr{I}$ . Prove that every such ideal $J$  is prime.
8. Let $R$  be a principal ideal domain, and let $M$  be a finitely generated $R$ -module.

1. Prove that there are $R$ -submodules $0=M_{0}\subset M_{1}\subset \ldots \subset M_{n}=M$  of $M$  such that for all $0\leq i\leq n-1$ , $M_{i+1}/M_{i}$  is cyclic (i.e., generated by a single element).
2. Is the integer $n$  of part a) uniquely determined by $M$ ? (Prove your answer.)
