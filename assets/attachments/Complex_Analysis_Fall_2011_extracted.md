# Qualifying Examination in Complex Analysis

August 12, 2011, 9–11 a.m.

$\mathbb{D}$  denotes the (open) unit disk, $\overline{\mathbb{D}}$  the closed unit disk, and $\mathbb{T}=\partial \mathbb{D}$  the unit circle. Provide justifications as appropriate.

## Problems

1. (15 points) Use methods of complex analysis to evaluate

$$
\int_{0}^{\infty }\frac{\sqrt{x}}{(x+1)^{2}} \, dx
$$

- Be sure to provide complete justifications.

2. (15 points) Let $c$  be a complex number such that $|c|<1/3$ . Show that on the open set $Re(z)<1$  the function $f(z)=ce^{z}$  has exactly one fixed point, i.e., a point $z_{0}$  such that $f(z_{0})=z_{0}$ .
3. (20 points) Let $‾(a,r)$  denote the closed disk of radius $r>0$  about a point $a\mathbb{\in C}$ . Let $f$  be a holomorphic function on an open set containing $‾(a,r)$ , and let $M=sup_{z\in ‾(a,r)}|f(z)|$ . Prove that for $z\in ‾(a,r/2)$ , $z\ne a$ , we have

$$
\frac{|f(z)-f(a)|}{|z-a|}\leq \frac{2M}{r}.
$$

4. (15 points) Suppose $\Omega \subset \mathbb{C}$  is a region containing $\mathbb{D}$  and $f$  is holomorphic on $\Omega$ . Suppose that on $\mathbb{D}$  we have $f(z)=\sum a_{n}z^{n}$  and the series has radius of convergence equal to 1.

1. Give an example of such an $f$  so that the series converges at every point of $\mathbb{T}$ .
2. Give an example of such an $f$  that is analytic at $z_{0}\mathbb{\in T}$  and for which $\sum a_{n}z_{0}^{n}$  diverges.
3. Prove that $f$  cannot be analytic at every point of $\mathbb{T}$ .

5. (20 points) Consider $f(x,y)=x^{2}-2y+y^{3}$ . Let $P=(1,1)$ .

1. Let $X=\{(x,y)\in \mathbb{R}^{2}:f(x,y)=0\}$ . Prove that there is a neighborhood $U$  of $P$  so that $U\cap X$  is given by $y=\phi (x)$  for some $\mathscr{C}^{1}$  function $\phi$ . Give $\phi ^{'}(x)$ .
2. Now consider the same equation in $\mathbb{C}^{2}$  (i.e., $Y=\{(x,y)\in \mathbb{C}^{2}:f(x,y)=0\}$ . Prove that the analogous statement holds. What is $\phi ^{'}(x)$  as an $\mathbb{R}$ -linear map from $\mathbb{C}$  to $\mathbb{C}$ ? Why is $\phi$  holomorphic? (Hint: If you consider $g\mathbb{:C\rightarrow C}$  as a map $\tilde{g}:\mathbb{R}^{2}\rightarrow \mathbb{R}^{2}$ , how is holomorphicity of $g$  characterized by $D\tilde{g}$ ?)

6. (15 points) Do either a or b.

1. Suppose $f$  is meromorphic on $\mathbb{D}$ , continuous on $\overline{\mathbb{D}}$  except at finitely many points of $\mathbb{D}$ , and real on $\mathbb{T}$ . Prove that $f$  is a rational function.
2. Let $\Omega \subset \mathbb{C}$  be the region inside the unit circle $|z|=1$  and outside the circle $\left|z-\frac{1}{4}\right|=\frac{1}{4}$ . Find a one-to-one conformal map from $\Omega$  onto an annulus $r<|z|<1$  for the appropriate value of $r$ .
