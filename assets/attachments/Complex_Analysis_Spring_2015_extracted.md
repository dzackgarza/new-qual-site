## Problems

1. Prove that the function $f(z)=-\frac{1}{2}\left(z+\frac{1}{z}\right)$  provides a conformal equivalence from the open half disc $D^{+}:=\{z=x+iy:|z|<1,y>0\}$  to the upper half plane $H:=\{z=x+iy:y>0\}$ .
2. Suppose $n$  is an integer and $a$  is a real number satisfying $0<a<n$ . Compute the integral

$$
\int_{0}^{\infty }\frac{x^{a-1}}{1+x^{n}} \, dx.
$$

3. Let $0<r<1$ . Show that for all sufficiently large $n$ , the polynomials $P_{n}(z)=1+2z+3z^{2}+\cdots +nz^{n-1}$  have no zeros in $|z|<r$ .
4. Let $f$  be analytic in $\Omega :0<|z-a|<r$  except at a sequence of poles $a_{n}\in \Omega$  with $lim_{n\rightarrow \infty }a_{n}=a$ . Show that for any $w\mathbb{\in C}$ , there exists a sequence $z_{n}\in \Omega$  such that $lim_{n\rightarrow \infty }f(z_{n})=w$ .
5. Suppose $f$  is entire and maps a circle $|z-a|=r$  into $\mathbb{R}$ . Prove that $f$  is constant.
6. Let $\psi _{\alpha }(z)=\frac{\alpha -z}{1-‾z}$  with $|\alpha |<1$  and $\mathbb{D=\{}z:|z|<1\}$ .

- Prove that

$$
\frac{1}{\pi }\iint_{\mathbb{D}}^{​}\left|\psi _{\alpha }^{'}\right| \, dx \, dy=\frac{1-|\alpha |^{2}}{|\alpha |^{2}}log\frac{1}{1-|\alpha |^{2}}.
$$

- Hint: polar coordinates may come in handy.
