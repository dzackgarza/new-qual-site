# Algebra Qualifying Examination, Spring 2021

<a id="algebra-qualifying-examination"></a>
## Instructions

Justify all the calculations and state the theorems you use in your answers. In a multi-part problem, you are free to use earlier parts in doing later parts, even if you didn’t solve them.

<a id="problems"></a>
## Problems

1. [10 pts] Let

$$
A=\left[\begin{matrix}4&1&-1\\-6&-1&2\\2&1&1\end{matrix}\right]\in M_{3}(C).
$$

1. [4 pts] Find the Jordan canonical form $J$  of $A$ .
2. [5 pts] Find an invertible matrix $P$  such that $P^{-1}AP=J$ . (You should not need to compute $P^{-1}$ .
3. [1 pt] Write down the minimal polynomial of $A$ .

2. [10 pts] Let $H$  be a normal subgroup of a finite group $G$ , where the order of $H$  is the smallest prime $p$  dividing $|G|$ . Prove that $H$  is contained in the center of $G$ .
3. [15 pts]

1. [5 pts] Show that every group of order $p^{2}$ , with $p$  prime, is abelian.
2. [2 pts] State the three Sylow Theorems.
3. [6 pts] Show that any group of order $4225$  $(=5^{2}\cdot 13^{2})$  is abelian.
4. [2 pts] Write down exactly one representative from each isomorphism class of (abelian) groups of order $4225$ .

4. [10 pts] Set $f(x)=x^{4}+4x^{2}+64\in Q[x]$ .

1. [4 pts] Find the splitting field $K$  of $f(x)$  over $Q$ .
2. [3 pts] Find the Galois group $G$  of $f(x)$ .
3. [3 pts] Exhibit explicitly the correspondence between subgroups of $G$  and intermediate fields between $Q$  and $K$ .

5. [10 pts] Suppose that $f(x)\in (Z/nZ)[x]$  is a zero divisor. Show that there is a nonzero $a\in Z/nZ$  with $af(x)=0$ .
6. [10 pts]

1. [2 pts] Carefully state the definition of Noetherian for a commutative ring $R$ .
2. [2 pts] Let $R$  be the subset of $Z[x]$  consisting of all polynomials

$$
f(x)=a_{0}+a_{1}x+a_{2}x^{2}+\cdots +a_{n}x^{n}
$$

- such that $a_{k}$  is even for $1\leq k\leq n$ . Show that $R$  is a subring of $Z[x]$ .

3. [6 pts] Show that $R$  is not Noetherian.

- Hint: Consider the ideal generated by $\{2x^{k}:1\leq k\in Z\}$ .

7. [10 pts] Let $p$  be a prime number, and let $F$  be a field of characteristic $p$ . Show that if $a\in F$  is not a $p$ th power in $F$ , then $x^{p}-a\in F[x]$  is irreducible.
