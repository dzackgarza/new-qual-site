## Problems

Notation: $\mathbb{R}$  is the set of real numbers; $m$  and $m^{*}$  are Lebesgue measure and Lebesgue outer measure, respectively.

1. Let $f$  be a polynomial with real coefficients. Show that there exists $x_{0}\mathbb{\in R}$  such that $|f(x_{0})|\leq |f(x)|$  for all $x\mathbb{\in R}$ .
2. Prove that there exists $\epsilon >0$  such that

$$
cos(sinx)>\frac{1}{2}(1+cos^{2}x)
$$

- whenever $0<|x|<\epsilon$ .

3. Let $f(x)=x^{3}$  and $E\mathbb{\subset R}$ . Show that $m^{*}(E)=0$  if and only if $m^{*}(f(E))=0$ .
4. Let $f(x)$  be Lebesgue measurable on $\mathbb{R}$ . Suppose that $f(x)$  and $xf(x)$  are both integrable over the whole of $\mathbb{R}$ . Define

$$
F(t):=\int_{\mathbb{R}}^{​}f(x)sin(tx) \, dx
$$

- Prove that $F(t)$  is differentiable at every $t$ , and find a formula for $F^{'}(t)$ .

5. Let $f\in L^{1}\mathbb{(R)}$ , and for $r>0$  put

$$
f_{r}(x):=\frac{1}{2r}\int_{x-r}^{x+r}f \, dm
$$

1. Prove that $\left\|f_{r}\right\|_{1}\leq \left\|f\right\|_{1}$  for all $r>0$ . (Hint: use Fubini’s theorem.)
2. Prove that $f_{r}\rightarrow f$  in $L^{1}$  as $r\downarrow 0$ .

6. Let $f:[0,1\mathbb{]\rightarrow R}$  be a bounded Lebesgue measurable function. Prove that

$$
\lim_{p\to \infty }\left\|f\right\|_{p}=\left\|f\right\|_{\infty }
$$
