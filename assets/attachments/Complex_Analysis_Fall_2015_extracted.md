## Instructions

The problems count equally. Give clear reasoning and state clearly which theorems you are using. $\mathbb{D}$  denotes the open unit disk in the complex plane $\mathbb{C}$ .

<a id="problems"></a>
## Problems

1. Evaluate the integral

$$
\int_{0}^{\infty }\frac{cosx}{1+x^{4}} \, dx.
$$

1. Let $\{f_{n}\}$  be a sequence of analytic functions on a region $\Omega$  in $\mathbb{C}$  and assume that for some function $g$  on $\Omega$ , $f_{n}\rightarrow g$  uniformly on compact subsets of $\Omega$ . Show that $f_{n}^{'}\rightarrow g^{'}$  uniformly on compact subsets of $\Omega$  and that $g$  is analytic on $\Omega$ .
2. Explain what part (a) has to do with differentiation of power series.

2. Let $U$  be the upper half plane $\{z\mathbb{\in C\mid}Im(z)>0\}$  with the slit $\{iy\mid 0<y\leq 1\}$  removed. Find a conformal equivalence from $U$  to $\mathbb{D}$ .
3. Expand the following functions into Laurent series in the indicated regions:

1. $f(z)=\frac{z^{2}-1}{(z+2)(z+3)}$ , $2<|z|<3$ .
2. $g(z)=sin\left(\frac{z}{1-z}\right)$ , $0<|z-1|<\infty$ .

4. Let $f$  be entire and suppose that $lim_{z\rightarrow \infty }f(z)=\infty$ . Show that $f$  is a polynomial.
5. Find the number of roots of $z^{4}-6z+3=0$  in $|z|<1$ , $1<|z|<2$ , and $|z|>2$  respectively.
6. Let $f(z)$  be an analytic on $\mathbb{D}$ , with $Re(f(z))>0$ , $f(0)=a>0$ . Show that

$$
\left|\frac{f(z)-a}{f(z)+a}\right|\leq |z|, \quad \quad \left|f^{'}(0)\right|\leq 2a.
$$
