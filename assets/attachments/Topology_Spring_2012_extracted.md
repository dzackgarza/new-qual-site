## Instructions

The following nine questions have equal weight (11 points each) - answer all of them.

## Problems

1. For any integer $n\geq 2$  let $X_{n}$  denote the space formed by attaching a 2-cell to the circle $S^{1}$  via the attaching map $a_{n}:S^{1}\rightarrow S^{1}$  defined by $a_{n}(e^{i\theta })=e^{in\theta }$ . (In other words, $X_{n}$  is the quotient of the disjoint union of the disk $D^{2}$  and the circle $S^{1}$  by the relation which identifies a point $e^{i\theta }$  on the boundary of $D^{2}$  with $e^{in\theta }\in S^{1}$ .)

1. Compute the fundamental group and the homology of $X_{n}$ . (6 points)
2. Exactly one of the $X_{n}$  (for $n\geq 2$ ) is homeomorphic to a surface. Identify, with proof, both this value of $n$  and the surface that $X_{n}$  is homeomorphic to (including a description of the homeomorphism). (5 points)

1. State what it means for a covering space to be normal (sometimes this condition is instead called regular). (4 points)
2. Let $\Theta$  be the topological space formed as the union of a circle and a diameter of the circle (so this space looks exactly like the letter $\Theta$  ). Give an example of a covering space of $\Theta$  which is not normal. (7 points)

2. Give a self-contained proof that the $0$ th singular homology $H_{0}(X)$  is isomorphic to $\mathbb{Z}$  for every path-connected space $X$ .

1. Prove that for every continuous map $f:S^{2}\rightarrow S^{2}$  there is some $x$  such that either $f(x)=x$  or $f(x)=-x$ . (Hint: Where $A:S^{2}\rightarrow S^{2}$  is the antipodal map, you are being asked to prove that either $f$  or $A\circ f$  has a fixed point.) (7 points)
2. Exhibit a continuous map $f:S^{3}\rightarrow S^{3}$  such that for every $x\in S^{3}$ , $f(x)$  is equal to neither $x$  nor $-x$ . (Hint: It might help to first think about how you could do this for a map from $S^{1}$  to $S^{1}$ .) (4 points)

3. Suppose that $U$  and $V$  are open subsets of a space $X$ , with $X=U\cup V$ . Find, with proof, a general formula relating the Euler characteristics of $X$ , $U$ , $V$ , and $U\cap V$ .
4. Prove that any finite tree is contractible, where a tree is a connected graph that contains no closed edge paths.
5. Let $X$  be a topological space.

1. State what it means for $X$  to be Hausdorff. (3 points)
2. Prove that every compact subset of a Hausdorff space is closed. (8 points)

6. Let $X$  be a topological space.

1. State what it means for $X$  to be compact (3 points).
2. Let

$$
X=\{0\}\cup \left\{1/n\mid n\in \mathbb{Z}_{+}\right\}.
$$

- Is $X$  compact? (4 points)

3. Let

$$
X=(0,1].
$$

- Is $X$  compact? (4 points)

7. Let $X$  be a path connected topological space.

1. Prove that $X$  is connected. (5 points)
2. Is the converse true? Prove or give a counter example. (6 points)
