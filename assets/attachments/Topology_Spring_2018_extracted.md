# Topology Qualifying Exam Jan. 2018

Each problem is worth 10 points.

1. Given two spaces $X$  and $Y$ , let $\pi _{X}:X\times Y\rightarrow X$  and $\pi _{Y}:X\times Y\rightarrow Y$  be the two standard projection maps.

1. Show that, for any other space $Z$ , a function $f:Z\rightarrow X\times Y$  is continuous if and only if $\pi _{X}\circ f$  and $\pi _{Y}\circ f$  are continuous.
2. Use this to show that the cartesian product of two path-connected topological spaces is path-connected.

2. Let $U\subset \mathbb{R}^{n}$  be an open set which is bounded in the standard Euclidean metric. Prove that the quotient space $\mathbb{R}^{n}/U$  is not Hausdorff.
3. Suppose that $X$  is a Hausdorff topological space and that $A$  is a subset of $X$ . Prove that if $A$  is compact in the subspace topology then $A$  is closed as a subset of $X$ .

1. Write down (without proof) a presentation for $\pi _{1}(\Sigma _{2},p)$ , where $\Sigma _{2}$  is a closed, connected, orientable genus 2 surface and $p$  is any point in $\Sigma _{2}$ .
2. Show that $\pi _{1}(\Sigma _{2},p)$  is not abelian by showing that it surjects onto a free group of rank 2.
3. Show that there is no covering space map from $\Sigma _{2}$  to $S^{1}\times S^{1}$ . You may use the fact that $\pi _{1}(S^{1}\times S^{1})\cong \mathbb{Z}^{2}$  together with the result in part (b) above.

4. It is a fact that if $X$  is a single point then $H_{1}(X)=0$ . One of the following is the correct justification of this fact in terms of the singular chain complex. Which one is correct and why is it correct?

1. $C_{1}(X)=0$ .
2. $C_{1}(X)\ne 0$  but $ker\partial _{1}=0$ , with $\partial _{1}:C_{1}(X)\rightarrow C_{0}(X)$ .
3. $ker\partial _{1}\ne 0$  but $ker\partial _{1}=image \hspace{0.25em} \partial _{2}$ , with $\partial _{2}:C_{2}(X)\rightarrow C_{1}(X)$ .

5. For topological spaces $X,Y$ , the mapping cone $C(f)$  of a map $f:X\rightarrow Y$  is defined to be the quotient space $(X\times [0,1])\sqcup Y/\sim$ , where $(x,0)\sim (x^{'},0)$  for all $x,x^{'}\in X$  and $(x,1)\sim f(x)$  for all $x\in X$ . Let $\phi _{k}:S^{n}\rightarrow S^{n}$  be a degree $k$  map for some integer $k$ . Find $H_{i}(C(\phi _{k}))$  for all $i$ .
6. Prove or disprove: Every continuous map from $S^{2}$  to $S^{2}$  has a fixed point.
7. Let $D$  be a closed disk embedded in the torus $T=S^{1}\times S^{1}$ , and let $X$  be the result of removing the interior of $D$  from $T$ . Let $B$  be the boundary of $X$ , i.e. the circle boundary of the original closed disk $D$ . Compute $H_{i}(T,B)$  for all $i$ .
