# Topology Qualifying Exam — August 2004

<a id="topology-qualifying-exam"></a>
## Problems

1. Let $X$  be a non-compact locally compact Hausdorff space, with topology $\mathscr{T}$ . Let $\hat{X}:=X\cup \{\infty \}$  ( $X$  with one point adjoined), and consider the family $\mathscr{B}$  of subsets of $\hat{X}$  defined by

$$
\mathscr{B}:=\mathscr{T\cup \{}S\cup \{\infty \}:S\subset X, \hspace{0.25em} X-S compact\}.
$$

1. Prove that $\mathscr{B}$  is a topology on $\hat{X}$ , that the resulting space is compact, and that $X$  is dense in $\hat{X}$ .
2. Prove that if $Y\supset X$  is a compact space such that $X$  is dense in $Y$  and $Y-X$  is a singleton, then $Y$  is homeomorphic to $\hat{X}$ . (The space $\hat{X}$  is called the one-point compactification of $X$ .)
3. Find the one-point compactifications of

1. $X=(0,1)$
2. $X=\mathbb{R}^{2}$

2. Let $X$  be a compact Hausdorff space and suppose $R\subset X\times X$  is a closed equivalence relation. Show that the quotient space $X/R$  is Hausdorff.
3. Let $X$  be a topological space.

1. Prove that $X$  is connected if and only if there is no continuous nonconstant map to the discrete two-point space $\{0,1\}$ .
2. Suppose in addition that $X$  is compact and that $Y$  is a connected Hausdorff space. Suppose further that there is a surjective continuous map $f:X\rightarrow Y$  with the property that every pre-image $f^{-1}(y)$ , $y\in Y$ , is a connected subspace of $X$ . Show that $X$  is connected.
3. Give an example showing that the conclusion of b) may be false if $X$  is not compact.

4. Let $X$  be the space formed by identifying the boundary of a Möbius band $M$  with a meridian of the torus $T^{2}$ . Compute $\pi _{1}(X)$  and $H_{*}(X)$ .
5. Describe the 3-fold connected covering spaces of $S^{1}\vee S^{1}$ .
6. Let $M_{g}^{2}$  be the compact oriented surface of genus $g$ . Show that there exists a continuous map $f:M_{g}^{2}\rightarrow S^{2}$  that is not homotopic to a constant map.
7. Let $f,g:S^{n}\rightarrow S^{n}$ , where $f(x)\ne g(x)$  for all $x$ . Show that $f$  is homotopic to $a\circ g$ , where $a(x)=-x$  is the antipodal map.
8. Let $f:S^{2n}\rightarrow S^{2n}$ . Show that there exists at least one point $x$  such that $f(x)=\pm x$ .
