:::{.problem title="?"}
(June 2009 #4a) Let $\{f_n\}$ be a sequence of real-valued continuous functions such that $f_n\to f$
uniformly on $[0,1]$, and let $\{x_n\}\subset[0,1]$ be a sequence which converges to $x$. Show that
$$\lim_{n\to\infty}f_n(x_n)=f(x).$$
:::
