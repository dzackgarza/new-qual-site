:::{.problem title="?"}
Let $p : (\widetilde{X},y_0) \to (X,x_0)$ be a covering space projection, $x_0 \in A \subseteq X$, and
$\iota : A \to X$ the inclusion map. Show that
$$q = p|_{p^{-1}(A)} : (p^{-1}(A),y_0) \to (A,x_0)$$
is also a covering space, and
$$\ker(\iota_*) \subseteq \operatorname{im}(q_*) \subseteq \pi_1(A,x_0).$$
:::
