::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $D=\{z\in\mathbb{C}:|z|<1\}$ and $f:D\to D$ be a holomorphic function.
Prove $$\frac{|f(0)|-|z|}{1+|f(0)||z|}\le|f(z)|\le\frac{|f(0)|+|z|}{1-|f(0)||z|},\qquad\forall z\in D.$$
:::
