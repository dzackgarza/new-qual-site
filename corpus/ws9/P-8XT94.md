::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $(X,\mathcal{M},\mu)$ be a measure space with $\mu(X)<\infty$.
Let $\mathcal{N}\subseteq\mathcal{M}$ be a $\sigma$-algebra.
If $f\ge 0$ is $\mathcal{M}$-measurable and $\mu$-integrable then prove that there exists an $\mathcal{N}$-measurable and $\mu$-integrable function $g\ge 0$ so that $$\int_E g\,d\mu=\int_E f\,d\mu\qquad\text{for all }E\in\mathcal{N}.$$
:::
