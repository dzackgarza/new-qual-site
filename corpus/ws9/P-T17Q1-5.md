:::{.problem title="?"}
Let $K$ be a graph with five vertices $a_0,a_1,a_2,b_1,b_2$ and six edges $e_{ij}$ for $i\in\{0,1,2\}$ and
$j\in\{1,2\}$, where $e_{ij}$ has endpoints $a_i$ and $b_j$. Let $X$ be the CW complex obtained from $K$
by attaching a $2$-cell along each loop formed by a cycle of four distinct edges in $K$. Find
$\pi_1(X)$ and show that $\pi_1(X)$ is isomorphic to a familiar group.
:::
