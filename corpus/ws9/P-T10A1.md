:::{.problem title="?"}
Let $X$ be a topological space and let $f,g:X\to\mathbb R$ be continuous functions.

(a) Show that the set $L=\{p\in X:f(p)\le g(p)\}$ is a closed subset of $X$.

(b) Show that the function $h:X\to\mathbb R$ given by $h(p)=\min\{f(p),g(p)\}$ is continuous.
:::
