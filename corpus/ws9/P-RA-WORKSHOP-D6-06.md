::: {.problem title="?"}
(June 2005 #1b) Use the definition of the Riemann integral to prove that if $f$ is bounded on $[a,b]$ and is continuous everywhere except for finitely many points in $(a,b)$, then $f\in\mathcal R$ on $[a,b]$.
:::
