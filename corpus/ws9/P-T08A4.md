:::{.problem title="?"}
Let $Y=(0,1)\times(0,1)$ and let $X=Y\cup\{*\}$. Let
$\mathcal T_1:=\{U\mid U\text{ is a Euclidean open subset of }Y\}$,
$\mathcal T_2:=\{X-C\mid C\text{ is a compact subset of the Euclidean space }Y\}$, and let
$\mathcal T:=\mathcal T_1\cup\mathcal T_2$. Then $(X,\mathcal T)$ is a topological space called the
one-point compactification of $(0,1)\times(0,1)$ (you do not need to prove this). Show that the space
$(X,\mathcal T)$ is compact.
:::
