::: problem
Let $K$ be the splitting field of $x^5 - 5$ over $\mathbb{Q}$.

a. What is the dimension of $K$ over $\mathbb{Q}$?

b. What is the Galois group of $K$ over $\mathbb{Q}$? (Identify the isomorphism type of the Galois group, and also indicate how its elements act as permutations of the roots of $f$.)

c. What are all the subfields of $K$? Be sure to explain how you know you have included all of them.

d. Which of these subfields are Galois over $\mathbb{Q}$?
:::
