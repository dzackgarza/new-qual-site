::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $(\Omega,\mathcal{A},\mu)$ be a measure space.
Prove directly from the definition of convergence almost everywhere that if for all $n$, $$\mu\{x\in\Omega:|f_n(x)|>1/n\}<n^{-3/2},$$ then $f_n\to 0$ a.e. $(\mu)$.
:::
