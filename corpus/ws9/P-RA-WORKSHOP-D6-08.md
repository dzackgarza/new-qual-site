::: {.problem title="?"}
(January 2009 #4b) Let $f$ be a continuous real-valued function on $[0,1]$.
Prove that there exists at least one point $\xi\in[0,1]$ such that $$\int_0^1x^4f(x)\,dx=\frac15f(\xi).$$
:::
