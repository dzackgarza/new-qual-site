:::{.problem title="?"}
(a) Describe how to construct a cell structure on the 2-sphere $S^2$ consisting of one 0-cell, one
1-cell and two 2-cells, and explain how to use this cell structure to calculate the simplicial homology
groups of $S^2$.

(b) Explain how a long exact sequence may be used to calculate all of the (singular) homology groups
$H_i(S^n)$ of the $n$-sphere $S^n$ (and calculate these groups for all $i$ and $n$).
:::
