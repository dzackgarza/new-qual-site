::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $R$ be a PID with field of fractions $F$.
Let $V$ be a vector space of dimension $n$ over $F$.

a. Show that every finitely generated $R$-module in $V$ is free of rank $\leq n$.

b. Let $M$ and $N$ be free $R$-modules of rank $n$ in $V$.
Show that there exists a nonzero $\alpha \in R$ such that $\alpha M \subseteq N$.
Use this to show that there exists a basis $\{e_1,\dots,e_n\}$ of $M$ over $R$ and nonzero elements $\beta_1,\dots,\beta_n$ in $F$ such that $\{\beta_1 e_1,\dots,\beta_n e_n\}$ is a basis of $N$ over $R$.
:::
