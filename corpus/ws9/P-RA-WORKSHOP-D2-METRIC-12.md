:::{.problem title="?"}
Prove Proposition 1.4: Every open set $G\subset\mathbb R$ can be written as a finite or countable union of disjoint
open intervals $(a_j,b_j)$ with at most one $a_j=-\infty$ and at most one $b_j=\infty$.
:::
