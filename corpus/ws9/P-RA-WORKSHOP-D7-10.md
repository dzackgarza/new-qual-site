:::{.problem title="?"}
(June 2005 #5) Suppose that $f\in C([0,1])$ and that
$$\int_0^1f(x)x^n\,dx=0$$
for all $n=99,100,101,\ldots$. Show that $f\equiv0$.

Note: Many variations on this problem exist. See June 2012 #6b and others.
:::
