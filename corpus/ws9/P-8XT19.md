::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $u$ be a subharmonic function defined on $\mathbb{C}$.
Let $M(r):=\max_{|z|=r}u(z)$.
Prove that $$u(z)\le\frac{\log r_2-\log|z|}{\log r_2-\log r_1}M(r_1)+\frac{\log|z|-\log r_1}{\log r_2-\log r_1}M(r_2)$$ for $0<r_1\le|z|\le r_2$.
:::
