::: {.problem title="?"}
Assume that $f:\mathbb{C}\to\mathbb{C}$ is an entire function, not identically equal to $0$, and let $Z=\{z\in\mathbb{C}:f(z)=0\}$.
Prove that if $Z$ is unbounded, then $f$ has an essential singularity at $\infty$.
:::
