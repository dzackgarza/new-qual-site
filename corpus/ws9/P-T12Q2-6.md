:::{.problem title="?"}
Suppose $G$ is a finite group acting on a Hausdorff space $X$ and assume the action is fixed point free
(i.e., if $g\in G$ is not the identity element, then $g\cdot x\ne x$ for all $x\in X$).

(a) Prove that the action is necessarily a covering space action. (Note: Recall that this means every
point $x\in X$ has an open neighborhood $U$ such that $U\cap g\cdot U=\varnothing$ for all non-identity
elements $g\in G$.)

(b) Assume $X$ is simply connected and let $X/G$ be the quotient space. Prove every map from $X/G$ to
$S^1$ is contractible.
:::
