:::{.problem title="?"}
A space $(X,\mathcal{T})$ is called *locally compact* if, for every $x \in X$ and $x \in U \in \mathcal{T}$,
there is a compact set $C \subseteq U$ containing an open neighborhood of $x$. Show that the Cartesian
product of two locally compact spaces is locally compact.
:::
