:::{.problem title="?"}
a) Define what it means for vectors $v_1, \ldots, v_n$ in a vector space $V$ to be linearly independent.

b) Suppose that vectors $v_1, \ldots, v_n$ in a vector space $V$ span $V$ and that no proper subset of $\{v_1, \ldots, v_n\}$ spans $V$. Prove that $v_1, \ldots, v_n$ are linearly independent.
:::
