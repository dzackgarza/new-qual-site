:::{.problem title="?"}
Let $\{a_k\}_{k=1}^{\infty}$ be a bounded sequence of real numbers and let
$$E:=\left\{s\in\mathbb R:\text{ the set }\{k\in\mathbb N:a_k\ge s\}\text{ has at most finitely many elements}\right\}.$$
Prove that
$$\limsup_{k\to\infty}a_k=\inf E.$$
:::
