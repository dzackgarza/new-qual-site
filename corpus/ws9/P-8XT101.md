::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $f,g\in L^1([0,1])$.
Suppose $$\int_0^1 x^n f(x)\,dx=\int_0^1 x^n g(x)\,dx$$ for all integers $n\ge 0$.
Prove that $f(x)=g(x)$ a.e.
:::
