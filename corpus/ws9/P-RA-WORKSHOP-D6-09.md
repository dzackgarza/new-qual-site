:::{.problem title="?"}
(June 2009 #5b) Let $\phi$ be a real-valued function defined on $[0,1]$ such that $\phi$, $\phi'$, and
$\phi''$ are continuous on $[0,1]$. Prove that
$$
\int_0^1\frac{x\phi'(x)-\phi(x)+\phi(0)}{x^2}\cos x\,dx
<\frac32\|\phi''\|_\infty,
$$
where $\|\phi''\|_\infty=\sup_{[0,1]}|\phi''(x)|$. Note that $3/2$ may not be the smallest possible
constant.
:::
