:::{.problem title="?"}
Define $f,\alpha\in\mathcal B([-2,2])$ by
$$
f(x):=\begin{cases}
-1,&x\in[-2,0),\\
3,&x\in[0,2],
\end{cases}
\qquad
\alpha(x):=\begin{cases}
-2,&x\in[-2,0],\\
1,&x\in(0,2].
\end{cases}
$$
Determine whether $f$ is Riemann--Stieltjes integrable with respect to $\alpha$ over $[-1,1]$. If it is,
evaluate
$$\int_{-1}^{1}f(x)\,d\alpha(x).$$
:::
