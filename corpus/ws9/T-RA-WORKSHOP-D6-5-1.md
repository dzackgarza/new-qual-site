::: {.theorem title="Theorem 5.1 (Riemann’s condition)"}
$f\in\mathcal R(\alpha)$ on $[a,b]$ if and only if for every $\epsilon>0$ there exists a partition $P\in\mathcal P_{[a,b]}$ such that $$U(P,f,\alpha)-L(P,f,\alpha)<\epsilon.$$
:::
