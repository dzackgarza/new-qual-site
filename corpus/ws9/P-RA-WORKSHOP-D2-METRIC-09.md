:::{.problem title="?"}
(January 2011 #3a) Let $(X,d)$ be a metric space, $K\subset X$ be compact, and $F\subset X$ be closed. If
$K\cap F=\varnothing$, prove that there exists an $\epsilon>0$ so that $d(k,f)\ge\epsilon$ for all $k\in K$ and
$f\in F$.
:::
