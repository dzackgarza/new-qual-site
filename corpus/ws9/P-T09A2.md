:::{.problem title="?"}
For a topological space $X$ and $y\in X$, the path component $P_y$ of $X$ containing $y$ is the largest
path-connected subset with $y\in P_y\subseteq X.

(a) Show that this concept is well-defined (that is, show that every point $y$ is contained in a largest
path-connected subset).

(b) Give an example of a space and point $y\in X$ so that $P_y$ is neither an open nor a closed subset
of $X$.
:::
