::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $\Delta_2$ and $\Delta'_2$ be distinct $2$-simplices, and let $X$ be the quotient space obtained by identifying the six vertices of $\Delta_2\sqcup\Delta'_2$ to a single point.
Identify a $\Delta$-complex structure for $X$ and compute the simplicial homology groups $H^\Delta_n(X)$ for all $n$.
:::
