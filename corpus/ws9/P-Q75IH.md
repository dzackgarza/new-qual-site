:::{.problem title="?"}
Prove the following variant of the Lebesgue differentiation theorem: Let $\mu$ be a finite Borel measure on $\mathbb{R}$, singular with respect to Lebesgue measure. Then for Lebesgue almost every $x\in\mathbb{R}$,
$$\lim_{\epsilon\to 0} \frac{\mu([x-\epsilon,x+\epsilon])}{2\epsilon} = 0.$$
:::
