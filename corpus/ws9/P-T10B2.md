:::{.problem title="?"}
Attach a 2-disc $D^2$ to a torus $S^1\times S^1$ by the attaching map
$e^{2\pi it}\mapsto(e^{2\pi it},e^{2\pi it})$, thinking of the boundary $\partial D^2$ and each $S^1$
factor as the unit circle in the complex plane, and let $X$ be the resulting space. Compute a
presentation for the fundamental group of $X$.
:::
