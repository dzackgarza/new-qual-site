:::{.problem title="?"}
Let $X=[0,1]\times[0,1]$ denote the rectangle in $\mathbb R^2$. Let $\sim$ be the equivalence relation generated by $(0,p)\sim(1,1-p)$ where $0\le p\le1$. The quotient space $X/\sim$ is called the Möbius band. Show that $S^1$ is a retract of the Möbius band.
:::
