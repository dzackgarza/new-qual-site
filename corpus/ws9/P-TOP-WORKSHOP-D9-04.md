(Michigan Sept ’08) Compute the homology of the space formed as the union of the unit sphere $$\{(x,y,z)\mid x^2+y^2+z^2=1\}$$ and the closed interval along the $z$-axis from $(0,0,-1)$ to $(0,0,1)$.
