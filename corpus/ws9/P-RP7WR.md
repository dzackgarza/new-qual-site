:::{.problem title="?"}
a. What finite groups $G$ can occur as collections of automorphisms of some field $F$? More precisely, for which $G$ does there exist a field $F$ such that $G$ is a subgroup of the automorphism group of $F$? Explain.

b. What finite groups $G$ can occur as collections of automorphisms of some finite field $F$? Explain.
