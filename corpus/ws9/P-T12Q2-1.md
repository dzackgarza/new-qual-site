:::{.problem title="?"}
Assume that $(X,\tau)$ is a topological space with the property that for every open set $G\subseteq X$,
the closure of $G$, $\overline G$, is open. Such topological spaces are called *extremally disconnected*.
Prove the following.

(a) If $F\subseteq X$ is a closed set, then the interior of $F$, $F^\circ$, is closed.

(b) If $G\subseteq X$ is an open set, then $G$ equipped with the subspace topology is extremally
disconnected.

(c) If $X$ is Hausdorff and $f:\mathbb R\to X$ is continuous, prove that $f$ is a constant function.
:::
