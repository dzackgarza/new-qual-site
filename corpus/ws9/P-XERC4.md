::: {.problem title="?"}
In each item, a commutative ring $R$ and an ideal $I \subseteq R$ are given.
Determine whether $I$ is prime, maximal, both, or neither.

a. $R = \mathbb{C}[x]$, $I = (x^2+1)$.

b. $R = \mathbb{Z}[x]$, $I = (6, x)$.

c. $R = \mathbb{C}[x,y]$, $I = (y^2 - x^3)$.
:::
