::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $Y$ be a $\Delta$-complex.

(a) Prove that if $H_4(Y)\ne 0$, then $Y$ must have a simplex of dimension 4.

(b) Prove that if $H_4(Y)=\mathbb Z/7\mathbb Z$, then $Y$ also must have a simplex of dimension 5.
:::
