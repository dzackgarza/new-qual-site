:::{.problem title="?"}
$X$ is the space obtained by gluing a disk to a sphere as follows: Let $\alpha:S^1\to S^2$ be the map
that “wraps $S^1$ around the equator of $S^2$ three times”—more explicitly, $\alpha$ is the composition
of the map $S^1\to S^1$ sending $z$ to $z^3$ (where $S^1$ is identified with complex numbers of norm
one) and the canonical inclusion $S^1\subseteq S^2$. We glue $D^2$ onto $S^2$ by attaching its boundary
$\partial D^2=S^1$ according to $\alpha$:
$$X=(S^2\sqcup D^2)/\sim,$$
where $\sim$ is the equivalence relation generated by $\alpha(z)\sim z$ for all $z\in\partial D^2=S^1$.
Compute the singular homology of $X$.
:::
