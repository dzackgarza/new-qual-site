:::{.problem title="?"}
Let $Y$ be the standard 3-simplex $\Delta^3$ with a total ordering on its four vertices. Let $X$ be the
$\Delta$-complex obtained from $Y$ by identifying, for each $k\le 3$, all of its $k$-dimensional faces
such that the identifications respect the vertex ordering. Thus $X$ has a single $k$-simplex for each
$k\le 3$. Compute the simplicial homology groups of the $\Delta$-complex $X$.
:::
