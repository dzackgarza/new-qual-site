:::{.problem title="?"}
Define the term identification map in the category of topological spaces. Let $\pi:X\to Y$ be a surjective, continuous map of topological spaces. Suppose that $\pi$ maps closed sets to closed sets. Show that $\pi$ is an identification map. What happens if we replace closed sets by open sets? Justify your answers.
:::
