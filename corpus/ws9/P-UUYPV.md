:::{.problem title="?"}
Let $F(z)$ be a non-constant meromorphic function on the complex plane $\mathbb{C}$ such that $F(z+1)=F(z)=F(z+i)$ for all $z$. Let $Q$ be a square with vertices $z, z+1, z+i,$ and $z+1+i$ such that $F$ has no zeros and no poles on $\partial Q$. Prove that inside $Q$ the function $F$ has the same number of zeros as poles (counting multiplicities).
:::
