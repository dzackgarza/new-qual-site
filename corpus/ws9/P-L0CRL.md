::: problem
Let $R$ be a commutative ring with $1$.
We say $R$ is a local ring if $R$ has exactly one maximal ideal, $M$.
Prove that, in a local ring $R$, any $r \in R$ is either a unit or an element of the maximal ideal $M$.
:::
