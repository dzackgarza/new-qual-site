:::{.problem title="?"}
(June 2008 #4b) Assume $\beta>0$, $a_n>0$, $n=1,2,\ldots$, and the series $\sum a_n$ is divergent.
Show that
$$
\sum\frac{a_n}{\beta+a_n}
$$
is also divergent.
:::
