::: {.problem title="?"}
Assume $f,f_j\in L^2(\mathbb{R}^n)$ for $j=1,2,\ldots$, $f_j\to f$ a.e. and $\int f_j^2\,dx\to\int f^2\,dx$.
Prove $\int |f_j-f|^2\,dx\to 0$.
:::
