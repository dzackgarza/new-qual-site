(January 2007, 6a) Let $f$ be a function of bounded variation on $[a,b]$. Furthermore, assume that for some $c>0$,
$|f(x)|\ge c$ on $[a,b]$. Show that $g(x)=1/f(x)$ is of bounded variation on $[a,b]$.
