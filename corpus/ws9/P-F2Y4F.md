::: problem
a. Prove that $K = (\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z})[y]/(y^4 + y^3 + y^2 + y + 1)$ is a field.

b. Prove that $x^4 + x + 1$ and $x^2 + x + 1$ factor completely over the field $K$ above.

c. Prove that $x^3 + x + 1$ is irreducible over the field $K$ above.
:::
