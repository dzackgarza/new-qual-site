::: {.problem title="?"}
(June 2010 #6a) Let $f:[0,1]\to\mathbb R$ be continuous with $f(0)\ne f(1)$ and define $f_n(x)=f(x^n)$.
Prove that $f_n$ does not converge uniformly on $[0,1]$.
:::
