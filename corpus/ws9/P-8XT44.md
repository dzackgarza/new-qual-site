::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $U\subset\mathbb{C}$ be an open set, $D=\{z\in\mathbb{C}:|z|<1\}$ and $\mathcal{F}$ be the set of all holomorphic functions $f:U\to D$.
Given $z_0\in U$, show that there exists $f_0\in\mathcal{F}$ such that $$|f_0''(z_0)|=\sup_{f\in\mathcal{F}}|f''(z_0)|.$$
:::
