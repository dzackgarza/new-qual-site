:::{.problem title="?"}
Let $X$ be a set with two elements $\{a,b\}$. Give $X$ the indiscrete topology. Give $X\times\mathbb R$
the product topology. Let $A\subset X\times\mathbb R$ be
$(\{a\}\times[0,1])\cup(\{b\}\times(0,1))$. Prove that $A$ is compact.
:::
