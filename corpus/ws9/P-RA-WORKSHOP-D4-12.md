:::{.problem title="?"}
(January 2012 #1b) Let $y\in\mathbb R$ and $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ be given. Suppose that for every
sequence $\{x_n\}$ we have
$$
\liminf_{n\to\infty}|f(x_n)-f(y)|\le
\liminf_{n\to\infty}|x_n-y|.
$$
Prove that $f$ is continuous at $y$.
:::
