::: problem
Let $p$ be prime, $G$ be a group of order $p^n > 1$, and $X$ be a finite $G$-set whose size is not divisible by $p$. Prove that $G$ has a fixed point in $X$: i.e., an $x \in X$ such that $gx = x$ for all $g \in G$.
:::
