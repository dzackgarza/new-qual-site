:::{.problem title="?"}
Give a complete factorization of each of the following over the indicated rings. Explain why the factors you obtain are irreducible.
a. $x^5-1$ over $\mathbf{C}$.
b. $x^6-1$ over $\mathbf{Q}$.
c. $x^7+3x^5-6x^3+9x-15$ over $\mathbf{Q}$.
d. $x^{11}-x$ over $\mathbf{F}_{11}$.
e. $x^{13}-x-1$ over $\mathbf{F}_{13}$.
:::
