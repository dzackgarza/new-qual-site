::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $X$ be the space obtained from the sphere $S^2$ by joining the North and South poles together with a straight line segment.

(i) Describe a structure of a CW complex on $X$.

(ii) Compute the homology of $X$ using cellular homology, with decomposition from (i).
