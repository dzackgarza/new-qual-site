::: {.problem title="?"}
Suppose that $d,d'$ are metrics on the set $X$ with $d(x,y)\le d'(x,y)$ for every $x,y\in X$.
Show that the metric topology $(X,d)$ is coarser than the metric topology $(X,d')$.
:::
