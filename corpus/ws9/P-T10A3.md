:::{.problem title="?"}
A space $(X,\mathcal T)$ is called locally path-connected if for every $p\in X$, every open
neighborhood of $p$ contains a path-connected open neighborhood of $p$. Show that the product of two
locally path-connected spaces is locally path-connected.
:::
