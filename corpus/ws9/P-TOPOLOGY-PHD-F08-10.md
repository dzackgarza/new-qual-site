:::{.problem title="?"}
Construct a topological space $X$ by starting with $\mathbb R$ with the usual topology and defining $x$ to be equivalent to $y$ if $x-y$ is rational. Show that the resulting quotient or identification space $X$ has the indiscrete topology, that is, the only open sets are $\varnothing$ and $X$.
:::
