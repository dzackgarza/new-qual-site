:::{.problem title="?"}
Does there exist a nonempty measurable set $E \subset \mathbb{R}$ satisfying the following two properties:

a. given $x,y \in E$, there exists $z \notin E$ that lies between $x$ and $y$;
b. $E$ has no isolated points?
:::
