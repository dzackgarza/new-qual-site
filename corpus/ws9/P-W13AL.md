::: problem
a. Suppose $K/F$ is a field extension. Prove that the elements of $K$ that are algebraic over $F$ form a subfield of $K$.

b. Find the minimal polynomial of $2\sqrt{2} - \sqrt{3}$ over $\mathbb{Q}$.
:::
