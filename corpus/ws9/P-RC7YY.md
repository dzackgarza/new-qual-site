:::{.problem title="?"}
a. Define *unitary operator* on a complex Hilbert space.

b. Let $S$ be a unitary operator on a complex Hilbert space. Prove that for every complex number $|\lambda|<1$ the operator $S-\lambda I$ is invertible.

c. For a fixed vector $v$ in the Hilbert space and all $|\lambda|<1$, define
$$h(\lambda) = \langle (S+\lambda I)(S-\lambda I)^{-1}v, v\rangle.$$
Show $\text{Re}(h)$ is a positive harmonic function (you may not use the spectral theorem).
:::
