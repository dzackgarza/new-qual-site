::: {.problem title="?"}
A. Construct a Lebesgue measurable subset $A$ of $\mathbb{R}$ so that for all reals $a<b$, $0<m(A\cap[a,b])<b-a$, where $m$ is Lebesgue measure on $\mathbb{R}$.

B. Suppose $A\subseteq\mathbb{R}$ is a Lebesgue measurable set and assume that $$m(A\cap(a,b))\le\frac{b-a}{2}$$ for any $a,b\in\mathbb{R}$, $a<b$.
Prove that $m(A)=0$.
:::
