:::{.problem title="?"}
(January 2008 #5a) Let
$$f_n(x)=\frac{x}{1+nx^2}$$
for $n\in\mathbb N$. Let $\mathcal F=\{f_n:n=1,2,3,\ldots\}$ and let $[a,b]$ be any compact subset of
$\mathbb R$. Is $\mathcal F$ equicontinuous? Justify your answer.
:::
