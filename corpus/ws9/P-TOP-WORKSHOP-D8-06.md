:::{.problem title="?"}
(Purdue Jan ’08) Let $p:E\to B$ be a covering map. Suppose that points are closed in $B$. Let $A\subset E$
be compact. Prove that for every $b\in B$, the set $A\cap p^{-1}(b)$ is finite.
:::
