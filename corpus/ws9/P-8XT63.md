::: {.problem title="?"}
Suppose that $f:\mathbb{D}\to\mathbb{C}$ is holomorphic and injective in some annulus $\{z:r<|z|<1\}$, where $\mathbb{D}$ is the open unit disc.
Show that $f$ is injective in $\mathbb{D}$.
:::
