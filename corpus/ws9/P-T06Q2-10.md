::: {.problem title="?"}
The suspension $SX$ of a topological space $X$ is the space $(X\times I)/\sim$ where $\sim$ is the equivalence relation generated by $(0,x)\sim(0,x')$ and $(1,x)\sim(1,x')$ for all $x,x'\in X$.
Compute the homology groups of $SX$ in terms of those of $X$.
:::
