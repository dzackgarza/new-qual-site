:::{.problem title="?"}
A set is said to have the countable-closed topology if the closed sets are the countable sets together with the empty set. If $X$ is a topological space with an uncountably infinite number of points is the diagonal $\Delta:=\{(x,x)\mid x\in X\}$ closed in the finite complement topology? Justify your answer carefully.
:::

::: remark
The source first names the countable-closed topology and then asks about the finite-complement topology; both terms are retained as printed.
:::
