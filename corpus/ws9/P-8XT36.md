::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $U\subset\mathbb{C}$ be an open set.
Assume $f,g:U\to\mathbb{C}$ are holomorphic functions such that $\overline{f}g$ is holomorphic.
Prove either $f$ is constant or $g$ is identically equal to $0$.
:::
