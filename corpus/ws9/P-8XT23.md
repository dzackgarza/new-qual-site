::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $f$ be a holomorphic function on the unit disc $\{z:|z|<1\}$ satisfying $f(0)=0$ and $\operatorname{Re}f(z)\le A$ for some positive number $A>0$.
Prove: $$|f(z)|\le\frac{2A|z|}{1-|z|}.$$
:::
