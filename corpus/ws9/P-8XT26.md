::: {.problem title="?"}
If the sequence of Lebesgue measurable functions $\{f_n\}_{n=1}^\infty$ on $\mathbb{R}^d$ satisfies $$\lim_{n\to\infty}\int_{\mathbb{R}^d}|f_n(x)|^2\,dx=0,$$ show that there exists a subsequence of functions $\{f_{n_j}\}_{j=1}^\infty$ such that $$f_{n_j}(x)\to 0\text{ a.e. }x.$$
:::
