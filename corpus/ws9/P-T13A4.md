:::{.problem title="?"}
A space is locally connected if for each point $x\in X$ and every neighborhood $U$ of $x$, there is a
connected neighborhood $V$ of $x$ contained in $U$.

(a) Prove that $X$ is locally connected if and only if for every open set $U$ of $X$, each connected
component of $U$ is open in $X$.

(b) Prove that if $p:X\to Y$ is a quotient map and $X$ is locally connected, then $Y$ is locally
connected.
:::
