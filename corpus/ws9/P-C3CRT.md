::: problem
Suppose that $R$ is a commutative ring and $I$ and $J$ are ideals of $R$. Suppose that $I + J = R$.

a. Prove that $IJ = I \cap J$.

b. Prove that $R/(IJ)$ is isomorphic to $R/I \oplus R/J$. (This is the Chinese Remainder Theorem).
:::
