:::{.problem title="?"}
For $f:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$ in $L^1(\mathbb{R})$, let $Mf$ be the (centered) Hardy-Littlewood maximal function. Prove there is a constant $A$ such that for any $\lambda>0$,
$$m\{x\in\mathbb{R}: Mf(x)>\lambda\} \le \frac{A}{\lambda}||f||_{L^1}$$
where $m$ is Lebesgue measure. If you use a covering lemma, you should prove it.
:::
