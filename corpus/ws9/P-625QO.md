:::{.problem title="?"}
Let $T$ be a linear transformation on $C_c(\mathbb{R})$ (continuous functions with compact support) that has the following two properties:
$$||Tf||_{L^\infty} \le ||f||_{L^\infty} \quad \text{and} \quad m\{x\in\mathbb{R}: |Tf(x)|>\lambda\} \le \frac{||f||_{L^1}}{\lambda}$$
where $m$ denotes Lebesgue measure. Prove that
$$\int |Tf(x)|^2\,dx \le C\int |f(x)|^2\,dx$$
for all $f\in C_c(\mathbb{R})$ and some fixed number $C$.
:::
