::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $f:[0,1]\to\mathbb{R}$ be continuous with $$\min_{0\le x\le 1}f(x)=0.$$ Assume that for all $0\le a<b\le 1$ we have $$\int_a^b\Bigl(f(x)-\min_{a\le y\le b}f(y)\Bigr)\,dx\le\frac12|b-a|.$$

(a) Prove that for all $\lambda\ge 0$: $$\bigl|\{x:f(x)>\lambda+1\}\bigr|\le\frac12\bigl|\{x:f(x)>\lambda\}\bigr|,$$ where $|S|$ denotes Lebesgue measure of the set $S$.

(b) Prove that for all $1\le c<2$, $$\int_0^1 c^{f(x)}\,dx\le\frac{100}{2-c}.$$ (The constant $100$ is not optimal, but is certainly large enough.)
:::
