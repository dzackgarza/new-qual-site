:::{.problem title="?"}
(January 2005 #3b) Suppose $f_n:[0,1]\to\mathbb R$ are continuous functions converging uniformly to
$f:[0,1]\to\mathbb R$. Either prove that
$$\lim_{n\to\infty}\int_{1/n}^1f_n(x)\,dx=\int_0^1f(x)\,dx$$
or give a counterexample.
:::
