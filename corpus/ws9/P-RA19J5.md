:::{.problem title="?"}
Use the Riemann condition to show that $f\in\mathcal R_\alpha[0,4]$ where $f(x)=e^{2x}$ and
$$\alpha(x)=\begin{cases}
x+1,&0\le x\le2,\\
3x-2,&2<x\le4.
\end{cases}$$
Compute the value of the Riemann--Stieltjes integral
$$\int_0^4 f(x)\,d\alpha.$$
:::
