::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $K$ be the splitting field of $x^4 - 6$ over $\mathbb{Q}$.

a. What is the dimension of $K$ over $\mathbb{Q}$?

b. What is the Galois group of $K$ over $\mathbb{Q}$?
You must not only identify the group, but describe how it acts on the roots of $x^4 - 6$.

c. How many subfields does $K$ have of each possible dimension over $\mathbb{Q}$?

d. How many of those subfields in each dimension are Galois over $\mathbb{Q}$?
:::
