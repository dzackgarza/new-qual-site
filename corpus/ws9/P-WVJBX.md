:::{.problem title="?"}
Suppose $\{f_n\}_{n=1}^\infty \subset L^2(\mathbb{R})$ is a sequence that converges to $0$ in the $L^2$ norm; in other words,
$$\|f_n\|_{L^2(\mathbb{R})} = \left(\int_{-\infty}^\infty |f_n|^2 dx\right)^{1/2} \to 0.$$
Prove that there exists a subsequence $\{f_{n_k}\}$ such that $f_{n_k} \to 0$ almost everywhere.
:::
