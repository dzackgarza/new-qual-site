::: {.problem title="?"}
Suppose $X$ and $Y$ are topological spaces and $f:X\to Y$ is a continuous function.

(a) Prove there exists a topological space $Z$, a quotient map $q:X\to Z$ and a one-to-one continuous map $h:Z\to Y$ such that $f=h\circ q$.

(b) (This part is a continuation of part (a).) If $Z_1$ is a topological space, $q_1:X\to Z_1$ is a quotient map, $h_1:Z_1\to Y$ is one-to-one and continuous, and $f=h_1\circ q_1$, prove that $Z$ and $Z_1$ are homeomorphic.
:::
