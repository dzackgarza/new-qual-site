::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $E$ be a measurable subset of $[0,1]$.
Suppose there exists $\alpha\in(0,1)$ such that $$m(E\cap J)\ge\alpha\cdot m(J)$$ for all subintervals $J$ of $[0,1]$.
Prove that $m(E)=1$.
:::
