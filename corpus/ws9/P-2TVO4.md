:::{.problem title="?"}
Let $p < q$ be two distinct odd primes such that $p \nmid q - 1$. Let $n = pq$ and $\zeta_n$ be a primitive $n$-th root of unity in $\mathbb{C}$.

a. Show that the splitting field $E$ of $(x^p - 2)(x^q - 2)$ over $\mathbb{Q}$ is $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_n, \sqrt[n]{2})$. (Note: $\gcd(p,q) = 1$; so there are integers $a$ and $b$ such that $pa + qb = 1$.)

b. Determine $[E : \mathbb{Q}]$.

c. Find two subgroups of $\mathrm{Gal}(E/\mathbb{Q})$ of order $(p-1)(q-1)$.

d. Show that the Sylow $p$-subgroup of $\mathrm{Gal}(E/\mathbb{Q})$ is normal and determine its fixed field.
:::
