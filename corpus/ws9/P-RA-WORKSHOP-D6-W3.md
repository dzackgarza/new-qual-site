::: {.problem title="?"}
(January 2007 #1) Let $$f(x)=\int_1^x\frac1t\,dt$$ for $x>0$.
(a) Use an $\epsilon$-$\delta$ proof to show that $f$ is continuous on $(0,\infty)$.
(b) Use an $\epsilon$-$\delta$ proof to show that $f$ is differentiable on $(0,\infty)$.
:::
