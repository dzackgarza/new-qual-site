::: problem
Let $K$ be the splitting field of $x^3 - 7$ over $\mathbb{Q}$.

a. Find the Galois group of $K$ over $\mathbb{Q}$.

b. Find all of the subfields of $K$.

c. Which of the subfields you found above are Galois over $\mathbb{Q}$?
:::
