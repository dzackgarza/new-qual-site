:::{.problem title="?"}
The free group $F(a,b)$ on two generators is the fundamental group of the space $Z=S^1\vee S^1$,
using the wedge point as basepoint, with $a$ and $b$ corresponding to each of the loops $S^1$.
Construct the covering space of $Z$ corresponding to the subgroup
$H=\langle a^2b,aba,ab^2,b^2a\rangle$, determine the index of $H$ in $F(a,b)$, and decide if $H$ is a
normal subgroup of $F(a,b)$.
:::
