::: problem
Find the Galois groups, over $\mathbb{Q}$, of the following polynomials.

a. $x^4 + 4$.

b. $x^3 + x^2 - 2x - 1$. Hint: show that if $r$ is a root, then $r^2 - 2$ also is a root.
:::
