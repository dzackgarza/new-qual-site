:::{.problem title="?"}
(January 2010 #5) Suppose that $f:[a,b]\to\mathbb R$ is continuous, $f\ge0$ on $[a,b]$, and put
$$M=\sup\{f(x):x\in[a,b]\}.$$
Prove that
$$\lim_{p\to\infty}\left(\int_a^b f(x)^p\,dx\right)^{1/p}=M.$$
:::
