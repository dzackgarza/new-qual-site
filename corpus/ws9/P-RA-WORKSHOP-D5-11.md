:::{.problem title="?"}
Assume that $f:[0,1]\to\mathbb R$ is continuous on $[0,1]$ and differentiable on $(0,1)$ with $f(0)=f(1)=0$
and $f(c)=1$ for some $c\in(0,1)$. Prove that there exists some $s\in(0,1)$ such that $|f'(s)|>2$.
:::
