:::{.problem title="?"}
A space $X$ is called *unicoherent* if whenever $X=U\cup V$ with $U,V$ nonempty, open, and
path-connected, then $U\cap V$ is path-connected. Use the Mayer--Vietoris sequence to show that, for
$n\ge 2$, $S^n$ is unicoherent.
:::
