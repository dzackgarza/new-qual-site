:::{.problem title="?"}
Find the (simplicial) homology groups of the space $X$ obtained from the 3-simplex
$\Delta^3=[v_0,v_1,v_2,v_3]$ by gluing the face $[v_0,v_1,v_2]$ to the face $[v_0,v_2,v_3]$ and the face
$[v_0,v_1,v_3]$ to the face $[v_1,v_2,v_3]$ (respecting the ordering of vertices as written, i.e., the
first gluing map sends $v_1$ to $v_2$, and so on).
:::
