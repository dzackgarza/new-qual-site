::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $\mathcal B=\{[a,b):a,b\in\mathbb R\}$; let $\mathbb R_\ell$ denote $\mathbb R$ equipped with the topology generated by the basis $\mathcal B$ (you do not need to prove $\mathcal B$ is a basis).
Decide (with proof) if the following sets are compact in $\mathbb R_\ell$:

(a) $A=\{1/n:n\in\mathbb N\}\cup\{0\}$.

(b) $B=\{-1/n:n\in\mathbb N\}\cup\{0\}$.
:::
