:::{.problem title="?"}
Let $k$ and $n$ be natural numbers greater than $1$. Let $G$ be a subgroup of index $k$ of the free group
$F_n$ on $n$ generators. Then $G$ is isomorphic to the free group $F_m$ for some natural number $m$; find
$m$ as a function of $k$ and $n$.
:::
