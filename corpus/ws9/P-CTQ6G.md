::: {.problem title="?"}
(1) $Y$ is metric space.
Prove $y\in Y$ is isolated iff the complement $\{y\}^c$ is not dense in $Y$

(2) Let $X$ be a countable nonempty complete metric space.
Prove that the set of isolated points is dense in $X$.
:::
