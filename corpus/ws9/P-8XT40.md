::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $f:[0,1]\to[0,1]$ be a continuous function and $\varphi:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$ be a $C^1$ function with $\varphi(0)=0$.
Prove $$\int_0^1 \varphi\circ f\,dx=\int m(\{x\in[0,1]:f(x)>t\})\varphi'(t)\,dt.$$
:::
