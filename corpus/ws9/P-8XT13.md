::: {.problem title="?"}
Suppose $A$ is a Lebesgue measurable set in $\mathbb{R}$ with $m(A)>0$.
Does there exist a sequence $\{x_n\}_{n=1}^\infty$ such that the complement of $\bigcup_{n=1}^\infty(A+x_n)$ in $\mathbb{R}$ has measure $0$?
Justify your answer.
(We define $A+x_n=\{a+x_n\in\mathbb{R}:a\in A\}$.)
:::
