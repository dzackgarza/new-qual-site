:::{.problem title="?"}
(January 2010 #4) Suppose that $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ is differentiable at $a\in\mathbb R$. If
$\{x_n\}$ is an increasing sequence of real numbers converging to $a$ and $\{y_n\}$ is a decreasing sequence
of real numbers converging to $a$, prove that
$$\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{f(y_n)-f(x_n)}{y_n-x_n}=f'(a).$$
:::
