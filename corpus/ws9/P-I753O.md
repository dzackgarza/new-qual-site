:::{.problem title="?"}
Let $T$ be a linear transformation on $C_0(\mathbb{R})$, the space of continuous functions of compact support, that has the following two properties:
$$\|Tf\|_{L^\infty} \le \|f\|_{L^\infty}, \text{ and } \left|\{x \in \mathbb{R} : |Tf(x)| > \lambda\}\right| \le \frac{\|f\|_{L^1}}{\lambda}.$$
(Here $|A|$ denotes the Lebesgue measure of the set $A$.) Prove that
$$\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} |Tf(x)|^2 dx \le C\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} |f(x)|^2 dx$$
for all $f \in C_0(\mathbb{R})$ and some fixed number $C$.
:::
