::: {.problem title="?"}
(a) Define absolute continuity of a function $f:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$ and of a function $f:[a,b]\to\mathbb{R}$.

(b) Show that if $f$ and $g$ are absolutely continuous on $[a,b]$, $a,b\in\mathbb{R}$, $a<b$, then $f\cdot g$ is absolutely continuous on $[a,b]$.

(c) Give an example to show that (b) is false if $[a,b]$ is replaced by $\mathbb{R}$.
:::
