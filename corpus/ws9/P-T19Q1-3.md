::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $X=\mathbb R\times\mathbb R$ with the product topology $\mathcal T$, giving $\mathbb R$ the usual metric topology, and let $p:(X,\mathcal T)\to(\mathbb R,\text{usual})$ be the projection on the first coordinate, $p(x,y)=x$.
Let $A=\{(x,y)\in X:x\ge0\text{ or }y=0\}$, with the subspace topology that it inherits from $X$.
Show that $f=p|_A:A\to\mathbb R$ is a quotient map, but that $f$ is neither an open map nor a closed map.
:::
