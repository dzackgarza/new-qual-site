:::{.problem title="?"}
Suppose that $A,B\subseteq X$ are closed subsets of the topological space $X$, and both $A\cup B$ and
$A\cap B$ are connected subsets of $X$. Show that both $A$ and $B$ are connected subsets of $X$.
:::
