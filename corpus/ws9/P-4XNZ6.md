:::{.problem title="?"}
Prove that the Hardy-Littlewood maximal function $f^*$ for an integrable function $f$ satisfies
$$m(\{x \in \mathbb{R}^d : f^*(x) > \alpha\}) \le \frac{3^d}{\alpha}\|f\|_{L^1(\mathbb{R}^d)}$$
where $\alpha > 0$. Recall that
$$f^*(x) = \sup_{x\in B} \frac{1}{m(B)}\int_B |f(y)|dy, \quad x \in \mathbb{R}^d$$
where the supremum is taken over all balls containing the point $x$. You may assume the Vitali 3-times Covering Lemma. State it clearly if you use it.
:::
