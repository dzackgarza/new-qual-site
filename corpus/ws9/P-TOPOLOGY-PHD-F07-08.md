::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $X=\prod_{\mu\in M}X_\mu$ and $Y=\prod_{\mu\in M}Y_\mu$ be the Cartesian products of the topological spaces $(X_\mu)_{\mu\in M}$ and $(Y_\mu)_{\mu\in M}$ and let $X$ and $Y$ have the product topologies, respectively.
Prove that if for each $\mu\in M$ the maps $f_\mu:X_\mu\to Y_\mu$ are continuous then $f:X\to Y$ defined by $f(x)_\mu=f_\mu(x_\mu)$ is continuous.
:::
