::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $Q$ be the unit square in $\mathbb{R}^2$.
Consider functions $f_n\in L^1(Q)$ such that $f_n\to f$ almost everywhere in $Q$ and $\int_Q |f_n|\to\int_Q |f|<\infty$.

(a) Prove that $\int_A |f_n|\to\int_A |f|$ for every measurable subset $A$ of $Q$.

(b) Prove that $f_n\to f$ in $L^1$.
:::
