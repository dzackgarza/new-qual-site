:::{.problem title="?"}
Let $C$ be a circle in the $xy$-plane, oriented counterclockwise, and not passing through the origin. Prove that
$$\oint_C \frac{-y}{x^2+y^2}\,dx + \frac{x}{x^2+y^2}\,dy$$
equals $0$ if the origin is outside the circle, and $2\pi$ if the origin is inside the circle.
:::
