::: {.problem title="?"}
Recall that the Fibonacci numbers are defined by $F_0 = 1, F_1 = 1$, and then $$F_n = F_{n-1} + F_{n-2} \quad \text{for integers } n \geq 2.$$

Prove that any two successive Fibonacci numbers $F_n, F_{n+1}$ are relatively prime.
:::
