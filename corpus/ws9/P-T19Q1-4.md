:::{.problem title="?"}
Show that if $f,g:(X,\mathcal T)\to(Y,\mathcal T')$ and $h,k:(Y,\mathcal T')\to(Z,\mathcal T'')$ are continuous and
$f\simeq g$ and $h\simeq k$, then $h\circ f\simeq k\circ g$. Show, however, that the converse
($h\circ f\simeq k\circ g$ implies $f\simeq g$ and $h\simeq k$) need not be true.
:::
