::: {.problem title="?"}
(a) State a version of the Stone--Weierstrass Theorem.

(b) Let $A$ be a uniformly dense subspace of $C[0,1]$ and let $$B=\Bigl\{F(x):F(x)=\int_0^x f(t)\,dt,\ 0\le x\le 1,\ f\in A\Bigr\}.$$ Prove that $B$ is uniformly dense in $$C_0[0,1]:=\{g\in C[0,1]:g(0)=0\}.$$

(c) Prove that the span of $\{\sin(nx):n\in\mathbb{N}\}$ is dense in $C_0[0,1]$.
:::
