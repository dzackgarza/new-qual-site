::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $U\subset\mathbb{C}$ be an open set, $f:U\to\mathbb{C}$ be a holomorphic function and $z_0\in U$.
Prove that if $f'(z_0)=0$, then $f$ is not one-to-one in any neighborhood of $z_0$.
:::
