:::{.problem title="?"}
Let $f:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$ be a function and let $a$ be a real number. Let $S$ be the statement "If $f$ has a local minimum at $a$, then $f'(a)=0$ or $f$ is not differentiable at $a$".

a) Write the contrapositive of $S$.
b) Write the converse of $S$.
c) Which of the statements in (a) and (b) are true? If either statement is false, give a counterexample.
:::
