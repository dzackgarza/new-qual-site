:::{.problem title="?"}
(June 2013 #5b) Let $(X,d)$ be a complete metric space, $A\subset X$ be a bounded set, and
$F:X\to X$. Assume there exists some $k>0$ such that
$$d(F(a),F(b))\le kd(a,b)\qquad\text{for all }a,b\in X$$
and that $A\subset F(A)$. Provide a description of $A$ if $k<1$. Can anything be said about $A$ if $k\ge1$?
:::
