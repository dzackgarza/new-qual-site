::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $f$ be an entire non-constant function that satisfies the functional equation $$f(1-z) = 1-f(z)$$ for all $z\in\mathbb{C}$.
Show that $f(\mathbb{C})=\mathbb{C}$.
:::
