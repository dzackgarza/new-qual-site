::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $f:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$ be a Lebesgue integrable function such that $$\int_a^b f(x)\,dx=0$$ for every $a<b$.
Show that $f(x)=0$ for almost every $x\in\mathbb{R}$.
:::
