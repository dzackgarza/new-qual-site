([KRD10, Exercise 6.2.B]) If $f:(a,b)\to\mathbb R$ is continuously differentiable and $f'(x_0)\ne0$
for some $x_0\in(a,b)$, then $f$ is injective on some interval $(c,d)$ containing $x_0$.
