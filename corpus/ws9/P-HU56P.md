::: {.problem title="?"}
(Frattini) Let $K$ be a normal subgroup of a finite group $G$, and let $P$ be a Sylow $p$-subgroup of $K$.
Recall $N_G(P)$ denotes the normalizer of $P$ in $G$.
Show that $G = KN_G(P)$.
:::
