:::{.problem title="?"}
Let $S^1$ and $D^2$ be the unit circle and closed unit disk in the (Euclidean) space $\mathbb R^2$.
Let $\sim$ be the smallest equivalence relation on the product space $S^1\times D^2$ satisfying
$(x,y)\sim(x,(0,0))$ for all $x\in S^1$ and $y\in D^2$. Let
$Z:=(S^1\times D^2)/\sim$ be the corresponding quotient space. Prove that $Z$ is homeomorphic to $S^1$.
:::
