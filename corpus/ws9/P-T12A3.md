:::{.problem title="?"}
Let $X=S^3$ (i.e., the subspace of $\mathbb R^3$ consisting of points $(x,y,z)$ such that
$x^2+y^2+z^2=1$). Define an equivalence relation on $X$ by $(x,y,z)\sim(x',y',z')$ if and only if
$z=z'$. Let $Y=X/\sim$ equipped with the quotient topology. Prove $Y$ is homeomorphic to the closed
interval $[-1,1]$.
:::
