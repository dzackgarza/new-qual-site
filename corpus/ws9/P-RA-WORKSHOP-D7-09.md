:::{.problem title="?"}
(January 2020 #4a) Let $M<\infty$ and $\mathcal F\subset C[a,b]$. Assume that each $f\in\mathcal F$ is
differentiable on $(a,b)$ and satisfies $|f(a)|\le M$ and $|f'(x)|\le M$ for all $x\in(a,b)$. Prove that
$\mathcal F$ is equicontinuous on $[a,b]$.
:::
