:::{.problem title="?"}
For a Radon measure $\mu$, with $\int_{\mathbb{R}^n} d\mu = C$. Prove that for all $\epsilon > 0$, there exists a set $E_\epsilon \subset \mathbb{R}^n$ s.t. $\mathcal{M}^1(E_\epsilon) := \inf_{E_\epsilon \subset \cup B_i}\{\sum_i \operatorname{diam} B_i\} < 10\epsilon$ and for any $x \notin E_\epsilon$, $r > 0$
$$\int_{B_r(x)} d\mu \le \frac{Cr}{\epsilon}.$$
(Hint: use Vitali covering lemma.)
:::
