::: {.theorem}
Let $K\subset\mathbb R^n$ be compact.
A collection of functions $\mathcal F\subset C(K,\mathbb R^m)$ is compact if and only if $\mathcal F$ is closed, bounded, and (pointwise) equicontinuous.
:::
