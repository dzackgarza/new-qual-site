::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $D(0,r)=\{z\in\mathbb{C}:|z|<r\}$.
Assume $r>1$ and $f:\overline{D(0,r)}\setminus D(0,1)\to\mathbb{C}$ is a continuous function, holomorphic on $D(0,r)\setminus\overline{D(0,1)}$ that satisfies $$\max_{\partial D(0,1)}|f(z)|=1\qquad\text{and}\qquad\max_{\partial D(0,r)}|f(z)|=R.$$ Prove $\log|f(z)|\le\frac{\log R}{\log r}\log|z|$.
:::
