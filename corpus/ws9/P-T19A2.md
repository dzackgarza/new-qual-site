:::{.problem title="?"}
Show that a topological space $(X,\mathcal T)$ is *Hausdorff* if and only if the diagonal
$\Delta=\{(x,x):x\in X\}$ is a closed subset of $X\times X$, where $X\times X$ is equipped with the
product topology.
:::
