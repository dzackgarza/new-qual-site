:::{.problem title="?"}
If $H:X\times[0,1]\to X$ is a homotopy with $H_0=H_1$ equal to the identity map, show that the map
$\gamma:I\to X$ given by $\gamma(t)=H(x_0,t)$ is a loop in $X$ representing an element
$g=[\gamma]\in\pi_1(X,x_0)$ which lies in the center of $\pi_1(X,x_0)$, i.e., $gh=hg$ for all
$h\in\pi_1(X,x_0)$.
:::
