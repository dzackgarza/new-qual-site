:::{.problem title="?"}
Let $U \subset \mathbb{C}$ be an open set containing the closure $\overline{D}$ of a unit disk. If a sequence $\{f_n : U \to \mathbb{C}\}$ of holomorphic functions converges uniformly to $f$ on compact subsets of $U$, then show that there exists an integer $N$ such that $f$ and $f_n$ have the same number of zeros in $D$ for $n \ge N$.
:::
