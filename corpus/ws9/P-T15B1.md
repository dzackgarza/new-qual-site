:::{.problem title="?"}
Suppose $X,X_1,X_2$ are path-connected spaces, $p:X_1\to X$ and $q:X_2\to X$ are (surjective)
covering maps, and $f:X_1\to X_2$ is a continuous map with $q\circ f=p$. Show that $f$ is also a
covering map.
:::
