:::{.problem title="?"}
Suppose that $f:[0,1]\to\mathbb R$ is differentiable and $f(0)=0$. Assume that there is a $k>0$ such that
$$|f'(x)|\le k|f(x)|$$
for all $x\in[0,1]$. Prove that $f(x)=0$ for all $x\in[0,1]$.
:::
