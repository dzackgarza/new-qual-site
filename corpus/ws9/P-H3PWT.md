::: problem
a. Let $F$ be a field, and let $K, L$ be finite dimensional extensions of $F$. Suppose $[K : F]$ and $[L : F]$ are relatively prime. Prove that $[KL : F] = [K : F][L : F]$. Here $KL$ is the compositum of $K$ and $L$.

b. Give an example of fields $F, K, L$ such that $K \cap L = F$ and $[KL : F] < [K : F][L : F]$.
:::
