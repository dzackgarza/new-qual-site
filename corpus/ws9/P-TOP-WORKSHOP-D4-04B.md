:::{.problem title="?"}
(June ’04 # A2) Let $X$ be the unit sphere in $\mathbb R^3$ and define an equivalence relation on $X$ by
$$ (x,y,z)\sim(x',y',z')\Longleftrightarrow z=z'.$$
Let $Z=X/\sim$ be the quotient space under this equivalence relation, with the quotient topology. Show
that $Z$ is homeomorphic to the interval $[-1,1]$.
:::
