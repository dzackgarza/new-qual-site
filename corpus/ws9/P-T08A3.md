:::{.problem title="?"}
(a) Prove that a map $f:X\to Y$ between topological spaces is continuous if and only if
$$f(\overline A)\subseteq\overline{f(A)}$$
for all $A\subseteq X$.

(b) Prove that if $f$ is continuous and $f(A)$ is closed for some $A\subseteq X$ then
$$f(\overline A)=f(A).$$
:::
