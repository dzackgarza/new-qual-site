::: {.problem title="?"}
Assume that $X$ is a reflexive Banach space and $\phi$ is a continuous linear functional on $X$.
Prove that there is a norm one vector $x$ such that $\phi(x)=\|\phi\|$.
Give an counterexample in the case $X=l_1$.
:::
