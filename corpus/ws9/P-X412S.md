::: problem
Let $K$ be the splitting field of $X^4 - 12$ over $\mathbb{Q}$.

a. Give an explicit description of $K$ and compute its degree over $\mathbb{Q}$.

b. What is the Galois group of $K$ over $\mathbb{Q}$? You should identify the group as well as its action on the roots of $X^4 - 12$.
:::
