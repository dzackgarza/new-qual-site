:::{.problem title="?"}
(June 2007 #3a) Suppose that $f,g:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ are differentiable, that $f(x)\le g(x)$ for all
$x\in\mathbb R$, and that $f(x_0)=g(x_0)$ for some $x_0$. Prove that $f'(x_0)=g'(x_0)$.
:::
