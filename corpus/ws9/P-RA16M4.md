:::{.problem title="?"}
Suppose that $f:[0,2]\to\mathbb R$ is continuous on $[0,2]$, differentiable on $(0,2)$, and such that
$f(0)=f(2)=0$ and $f(c)=1$ for some $c\in(0,2)$. Prove that there is an $x\in(0,2)$ such that
$|f'(x)|>1$.
:::
