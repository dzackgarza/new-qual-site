:::{.problem title="?"}
Show that if $X=\mathbb R$ is the set of real numbers with the infinite ray topology
$\mathcal T_X=\{(a,\infty)\mid a\in\mathbb R\}\cup\{\varnothing,\mathbb R\}$, then there are compact subsets
$C,D$ of $X$ such that $C\cap D$ is not compact.
:::
