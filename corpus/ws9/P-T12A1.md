:::{.problem title="?"}
Recall that a continuous function $f:X\to Y$ between two topological spaces is called a *closed
mapping* if for every closed subset $C$ of $X$, $f(C)$ is a closed subset of $Y$. Prove that if
$f:X\to Y$ is continuous, $X$ is compact, and $Y$ is Hausdorff, then $f$ is a closed mapping.
:::
