::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $\Omega\subset\mathbb{C}$ be a bounded region and $\{f_n\}$ be a sequence of continuous functions on $\overline{\Omega}$ which are holomorphic in $\Omega$.
If $\{f_n\}$ converges uniformly on the boundary of $\Omega$, then prove that $f_n$ converges uniformly on $\Omega$.
:::
