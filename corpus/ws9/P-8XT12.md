::: {.problem title="?"}
Assume $f,f_j\in L^2([0,1])$ for $j=1,2,\ldots$ and $\|f_j-f\|_{L^2}\to 0$.
Prove there exists a subsequence $\{f_{j'}\}\subset\{f_j\}$ such that $f_{j'}\to f$ a.e.
