::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $\{E_k\}$ be a sequence of Lebesgue measurable sets in $\mathbb{R}^k$ such that $$\sum_{k=1}^\infty m(E_k)<\infty.$$ Prove that almost every $x\in\mathbb{R}^k$ lies in at most finitely many sets $E_k$.
:::
