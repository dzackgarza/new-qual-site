::: {.theorem}
If $f\in\mathcal R(\alpha)$ on $[a,b]$ then $\alpha\in\mathcal R(f)$ on $[a,b]$ and
$$
\int_a^b f\,d\alpha=f(b)\alpha(b)-f(a)\alpha(a)-\int_a^b\alpha\,df.
$$
:::
