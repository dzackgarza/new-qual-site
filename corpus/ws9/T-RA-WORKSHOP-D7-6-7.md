::: {.theorem title="Theorem 6.7 (Stone–Weierstrass)"}
If $f$ is a continuous function on $[a,b]$ then there exists a sequence of polynomials $\{P_n\}$ which converges uniformly to $f$.
More generally: If $\mathcal A$ is a self-adjoint algebra of continuous functions on a compact set $K$ which separates points of $K$ and vanishes at no point of $K$, then given any $f\in C(K)$ there exists a sequence $f_n\in\mathcal A$ such that $f_n\to f$ uniformly on $K$.
:::
