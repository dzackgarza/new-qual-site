::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $X$ be the space obtained by attaching a torus $T=S^1\times S^1$ to a cylinder $C=S^1\times I$ via a homeomorphism of the circle $S^1\times\{(1,0)\}$ of $T$ with the circle $S^1\times\{0\}$ of $C$.
Compute the homology groups of $X$.
:::
