:::{.problem title="?"}
(January 2017, 2a) Define $f:[0,1]\to[-1,1]$ by
$$
f(x):=
\begin{cases}
x\sin\left(\frac1x\right),&0<x\le1,\\
0,&x=0.
\end{cases}
$$
Determine, with justification, whether $f$ is of bounded variation on the interval $[0,1]$.
:::
