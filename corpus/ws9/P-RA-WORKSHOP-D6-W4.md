:::{.problem title="?"}
([Apo74, 7.2]) If $f\in\mathcal R(\alpha)$ on $[a,b]$ and
$\int_a^b f\,d\alpha=0$ for every $f$ which is monotonic on $[a,b]$, prove that $\alpha$ must be constant
on $[a,b]$.
