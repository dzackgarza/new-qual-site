:::{.problem title="?"}
It is known that if $p:\widetilde X\to X$ is a covering space and $x_0\in X$ then the cardinality of $p^{-1}(x_0)$ is the index of $p_*\pi_1(\widetilde X,y_0)$ in $\pi_1(X,x_0)$ where $p(y_0)=x_0$. Use this fact to deduce that there is no $n$-sheeted covering of the circle $S^1$ for any finite $n$.
:::
