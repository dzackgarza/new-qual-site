::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $\{\varphi_k\}_{k=1}^\infty$ be an orthonormal basis for $L^2(\mathbb{R}^d)$ and define $\varphi_{k,j}(x,y)=\varphi_k(x)\varphi_j(y)$.
Prove that $\{\varphi_{k,j}\}_{k,j=1}^\infty$ is an orthonormal basis of $L^2(\mathbb{R}^d\times\mathbb{R}^d)$.
:::
