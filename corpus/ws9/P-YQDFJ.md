::: {.problem title="?"}
Suppose $f,g:[0,1]\to\mathbb{R}$ are both continuous and of bounded variation.
Show that the set $\{(f(t),g(t))\in\mathbb{R}^2 : t\in[0,1]\}$ cannot cover the entire unit square $[0,1]\times[0,1]$.
:::
