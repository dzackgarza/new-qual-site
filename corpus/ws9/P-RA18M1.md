Determine whether the following sequences converge and carefully justify your claims:
$$(i)\quad x_n=\frac{2n\cdot n!}{n^n};\qquad
(ii)\quad y_n=\sum_{k=1}^{n}\frac{\cos(k!)}{k(k+1)}.$$
