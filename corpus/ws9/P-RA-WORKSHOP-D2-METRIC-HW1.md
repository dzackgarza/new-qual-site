::: {.problem title="?"}
Throughout, $X$ is a metric space.
State the definition of what it means for a set $K\subset X$ to be compact, including the definition of open cover.
:::

:::: {.solution}
> **AI-Generated Solution**

<1>1. Definitions.
<2>1. An open cover of $K \subseteq X$ is a collection $\{U_\alpha\}_{\alpha \in A}$ of open subsets of $X$ such that $K \subseteq \bigcup_{\alpha \in A} U_\alpha$.
<2>2. A finite subcover of an open cover $\{U_\alpha\}$ is a finite subcollection $\{U_{\alpha_1}, \ldots, U_{\alpha_m}\}$ with $K \subseteq \bigcup_{j=1}^m U_{\alpha_j}$.
<2>3. $K$ is compact if every open cover of $K$ has a finite subcover.
<1>2. Q.E.D.
:::
