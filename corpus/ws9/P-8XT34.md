::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $\varphi:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$ be a non-negative $C^\infty$ function with compact support such that $\int_{\mathbb{R}}\varphi(x)\,dx=1$.
Define $$\varphi_\sigma(x)=\sigma^{-1}\varphi(x/\sigma)\qquad\text{and}\qquad u_\sigma(x)=\int\varphi_\sigma(x-y)u(y)\,dy.$$ For $u\in L^2(\mathbb{R})$, prove $$\int_{\mathbb{R}}|u_\sigma(x)|^2\,dx\le\int_{\mathbb{R}}|u(x)|^2\,dx.$$
:::
