::: {.problem title="?"}
(January 2006 #7a) Let $f$ be continuous on $[0,1]$ and $f(0)=f(1)=0$.
Show that there is a sequence of polynomials $\{P_n\}$ such that $x(1-x)P_n(x)$ converges to $f$ uniformly.
:::
