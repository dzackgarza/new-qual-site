::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $X$ be a Hausdorff space, $p\in X$, and $A\subseteq X$ a compact subset of $X$ disjoint from $p$.
Show that there exist disjoint open sets $U,V\subseteq X$ with $p\in U$ and $A\subseteq V$.
:::
