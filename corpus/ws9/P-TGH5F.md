::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $v_1, \dots, v_k$ be vectors in $\mathbb{R}^n$.
Define the span $V = \langle v_1, \dots, v_k \rangle$ of the vectors.
Prove that there is a subset of the vectors $v_1, \dots, v_k$ that forms a basis for $V$.
:::
