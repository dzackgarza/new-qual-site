:::{.problem title="?"}
(January 2020, 6a) Let $\{a_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}\subseteq\mathbb R$ and a strictly increasing sequence
$\{x_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}\subseteq(0,1)$ be given. Assume that $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n$ is absolutely convergent,
and define $\alpha:[0,1]\to\mathbb R$ by
$$
\alpha(x):=
\begin{cases}
a_n,&x=x_n,\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
$$
Prove or disprove: $\alpha$ has bounded variation on $[0,1]$.
:::
