::: {.theorem title="Theorem 5.5 (Mean value theorem)"}
If $f:[a,b]\to\mathbb R$ is continuous then there exists some $c\in(a,b)$ such that $$\frac1{b-a}\int_a^b f(x)\,dx=f(c).$$
:::
