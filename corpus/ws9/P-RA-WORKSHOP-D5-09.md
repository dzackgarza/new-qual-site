:::{.problem title="?"}
(June 2012 #1a) Suppose that $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ satisfies $f(0)=0$. Prove that $f$ is
differentiable at $x=0$ if and only if there is a function $g:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ which is continuous at
$x=0$ and satisfies $f(x)=xg(x)$ for all $x\in\mathbb R$.
:::
