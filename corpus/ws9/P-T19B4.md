:::{.problem title="?"}
For a topological space $X$, the cone on $X$, $cX$, is the quotient space of $X\times[0,1]$ under
the equivalence relation $(x,s)\sim(y,t)$ iff either $(x,s)=(y,t)$ or $s=t=1$. The suspension of $X$,
$\Sigma X$, is the union of two copies of $cX$ along $X\times\{0\}$, realized as the quotient space of
$X\times[0,1]$ under the equivalence relation $(x,s)\sim(y,t)$ iff either $(x,s)=(y,t)$ or $s=t=1$ or
$s=t=0$.

(a) Show that the cone on $X$ is contractible.

(b) Use a Mayer–Vietoris sequence to show that for every $n\ge 1$ we have
$$\widetilde H_n(\Sigma X)\cong\widetilde H_{n-1}(X).$$
:::
