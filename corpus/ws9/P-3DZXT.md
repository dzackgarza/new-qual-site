::: {.problem title="?"}
a. State the binomial theorem.
b. Prove that $(x+y)^5 \equiv x^5 + y^5 \pmod 5$.
c. Prove by mathematical induction that $(x+y)^{5^n} \equiv x^{5^n} + y^{5^n} \pmod 5$ for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$.
:::
