:::{.problem title="?"}
(a) State the definition of what it means for a topological space to be regular. Since there is some
ambiguity in the literature about the definition of the term “regular”, include the Hausdorff property
in the definition (some use “regular Hausdorff” or “$T_3$” for the same thing).

(b) Prove that a subspace of a regular space is also regular.

(c) Prove that a product of two regular spaces (equipped with the product topology) is also regular.
:::
