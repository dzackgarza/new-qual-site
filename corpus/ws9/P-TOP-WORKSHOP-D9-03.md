::: {.problem title="?"}
(Michigan May ’09) Let $$S^1=\{(x,y,0,0)\in\mathbb R^4\mid x^2+y^2=1\}$$ be the unit circle and consider $M=\mathbb R^4\setminus S^1$.
Compute the fundamental group $\pi_1(M)$ and the homology groups $H_*(M)$ of $M$.
:::
