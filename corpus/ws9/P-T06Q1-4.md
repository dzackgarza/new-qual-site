:::{.problem title="?"}
Let $\mathcal T,\mathcal T'$ be two topologies on $X$. Show that if $(X,\mathcal T)$ is compact and
Hausdorff, $\mathcal T\subseteq\mathcal T'$, and $\mathcal T\ne\mathcal T'$, then $(X,\mathcal T')$ is
Hausdorff but not compact.
:::
