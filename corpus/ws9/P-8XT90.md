::: {.problem title="?"}
Prove or disprove that there exists a sequence $(P_n)$ of polynomials such that $(P_n(t))$ converges to one for every $t\in[0,1]$ but $\int_0^1 P_n(t)\,dt$ converges to two as $n\to\infty$.
:::
