:::{.problem title="?"}
(Jan ’04 # A3) Let $(X,\tau)$ be a Hausdorff space and let
$$\tau'=\{U\subseteq X\mid X\setminus U\text{ is compact}\}\cup\{\varnothing\}.$$
Show that $\tau'$ is a topology on $X$ and is coarser than $\tau$. Show that, in general, they need not be
equal.
:::
