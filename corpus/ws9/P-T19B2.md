:::{.problem title="?"}
Let $p:\widetilde X\to X$ and $q:\widetilde Y\to Y$ be covering maps such that both $\widetilde X$
and $\widetilde Y$ are connected and simply-connected. Show that for every map $f:X\to Y$ there is a
map $\widetilde f:\widetilde X\to\widetilde Y$ such that
$$f\circ p=q\circ\widetilde f.$$
:::
