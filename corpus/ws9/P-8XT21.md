::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $f(z)$ be an entire function such that $$\max_{|z|=R}|f(z)|\le AR^k+B,$$ for positive constants $A,B$ and all $R>1000$.
Show that $f$ is a polynomial of degree at most $k$.
:::
