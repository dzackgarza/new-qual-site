:::{.problem title="?"}
Let $X$ be a path-connected Hausdorff space. The suspension $SX$ is the quotient of the space
$X\times I$ by the smallest equivalence relation such that $(p,0)\sim(q,0)$ and
$(p,1)\sim(q,1)$ for all $p,q\in X$. Prove that the homology groups satisfy
$$H_i(SX)=H_{i-1}(X)$$
for all $i>1$.
:::
