::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $G$ be the free group generated by two elements, $G=\langle x,y\rangle$.
Use covering space theory to prove that the commutator subgroup $[G,G]$ of $G$ (the normal subgroup generated by $xyx^{-1}y^{-1}$) is not finitely generated.
:::
