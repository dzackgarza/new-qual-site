:::{.problem title="?"}
Suppose $R$ is a commutative ring (with 1), $I$ is a proper ideal in $R$, and $a \in R$. Suppose $(a) + I = R$. Prove that $a+I$ is a unit (i.e., invertible) in the quotient ring $R/I$.

(For half credit: Prove that if $a$ and $n$ are relatively prime integers, then $a+n\mathbb{Z}$ is a unit in $\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$.)
:::
