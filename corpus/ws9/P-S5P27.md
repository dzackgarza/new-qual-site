::: problem
a. Let $F$ be a field and let $K$ be a field containing $F$. Suppose that $\alpha, \beta \in K$ are both algebraic over $F$. Prove that $\alpha + \beta$ is algebraic over $F$.

b. What is the minimal polynomial of $\sqrt{5} + 2\sqrt{7}$ over $\mathbb{Q}$?
:::
