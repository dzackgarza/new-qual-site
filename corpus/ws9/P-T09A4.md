:::{.problem title="?"}
Let $(X,\mathcal T)$ and $(X,\mathcal T')$ be topological spaces with $\mathcal T\subseteq\mathcal T'$.

(a) If $(X,\mathcal T')$ is normal, must $(X,\mathcal T)$ also be normal?

(b) If $(X,\mathcal T')$ is compact, must $(X,\mathcal T)$ also be compact?

Prove your answers in both parts.
:::
