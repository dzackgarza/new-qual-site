:::{.problem title="?"}
(June 2017, 2) Prove: $f\in\mathcal R(\alpha)$ on $[a,b]$ if and only if for any $a<c<b$, $f\in\mathcal R(\alpha)$ on
$[a,c]$ and on $[c,b]$. In addition, if either condition holds, then we have that
$$
\int_a^c f\,d\alpha+\int_c^b f\,d\alpha=\int_a^b f\,d\alpha.
$$
:::
