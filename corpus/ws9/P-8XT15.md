::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $f_n$ be a sequence of continuous functions on $\mathbb{R}$ satisfying $0\le f_n\le f_{n+1}\le 1$ for all $x\in\mathbb{R}$ and $n\in\mathbb{Z}^+$.
Let $f(x)=\lim_{n\to\infty}f_n(x)$.
Show that if $f$ is continuous at $x$, then for any $\varepsilon>0$ there exist $\delta$ and $N$ such that $|f_n(y)-f_n(x)|<\varepsilon$ whenever $|y-x|<\delta$ and $n>N$.
:::
