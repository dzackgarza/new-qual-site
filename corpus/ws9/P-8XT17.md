::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $f(z)$ be a holomorphic function on $D:=\{z\in\mathbb{C}:|z|<1\}$, $|f(z)|<1$, $f(\alpha)=0$ for some $|\alpha|<1$.
Show that for $z\in D$ $$|f(z)|\le\left|\frac{z-\alpha}{1-\overline{\alpha}z}\right|.$$
:::
