:::{.problem title="?"}
(Purdue Jan ’09) Let $p:E\to B$ be a covering map with $B$ connected. Suppose that
$p^{-1}(b_0)$ is finite for some $b_0\in B$. Prove that, for every $b\in B$, $p^{-1}(b)$ has the same number
of elements as $p^{-1}(b_0)$.
:::
