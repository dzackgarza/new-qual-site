::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $f_i:[0,1]\to\mathbb{R}$, $i=1,2,\ldots$, be an increasing sequence of continuous functions that is uniformly bounded, i.e., for all $x\in[0,1]$ and $i\ge 1$, $f_i(x)\le f_{i+1}(x)\le M<\infty$ for some fixed $M$.
Show that $\lim_{i\to\infty}f_i(x)=g(x)$ is continuous if and only if the $f_i$ converge uniformly to $g$.
:::
