:::{.problem title="?"}
(January 2006, 4a) Let
$$
f_n(x):=
\begin{cases}
\dfrac1n,&x\in\left(\dfrac{1}{2^{n+1}},\dfrac{1}{2^n}\right],\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
$$
Show that $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}f_n$ does not satisfy the Weierstrass M-test but that it nevertheless converges uniformly on $\mathbb R$.
:::
