::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $f\in L^1(0,\infty)$.
For $x>0$, define $$g(x)=\int_0^\infty f(t)e^{-tx}\,dt.$$ Prove that $g(x)$ is differentiable for $x>0$ with derivative $$g'(x)=\int_0^\infty -tf(t)e^{-tx}\,dt.$$
:::
