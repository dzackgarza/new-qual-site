::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $V = \{(x,y,z) \in \mathbb{R}^3 \mid 3x+4y+5z=0\}$.

a. Show that $V$ is a linear subspace of $\mathbb{R}^3$.
b. Prove or disprove: there is a linear transformation $S: \mathbb{R}^3 \to \mathbb{R}^3$ with kernel equal to $V$.
c. Prove or disprove: there is a linear transformation $T: \mathbb{R}^3 \to \mathbb{R}^3$ with image equal to $V$.
d. Prove or disprove: there is a linear transformation $U: \mathbb{R}^3 \to \mathbb{R}^3$ with kernel and image equal to $V$.
:::
