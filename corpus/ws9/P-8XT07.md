::: {.problem title="?"}
Assume that $f$ and $g$ are entire functions and that $g$ never vanishes.
If $|f(z)|\le|g(z)|$ for all $z\in\mathbb{C}$, then prove that there is a constant $C$ such that $f(z)=Cg(z)$.
:::
