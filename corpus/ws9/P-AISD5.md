:::{.problem title="?"}
Let $X$ be a compact space, and let
$$A_1 \supseteq A_2 \supseteq \cdots \supseteq A_n \supseteq \cdots$$
be a descending chain of non-empty closed subsets of $X$. Show that their intersection
$$\bigcap_{n=1}^{\infty} A_n$$
is non-empty.
:::
