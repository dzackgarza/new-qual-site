:::{.problem title="?"}
Let $X=\mathbb{RP}^2\vee\mathbb{RP}^2$, i.e., the result of gluing two copies of the real projective
plane together along specified base points. Recall that $\mathbb{RP}^2$ is the quotient of $S^2$ by the
antipodal map.

(a) Find $\pi_1(X)$.

(b) Find the homology of $X$.
:::
