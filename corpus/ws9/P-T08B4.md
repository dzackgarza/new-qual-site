:::{.problem title="?"}
Let $X$ be a hexagon in $\mathbb R^2$. Define an equivalence relation on $X$ corresponding to labeling
the 6 edges in the boundary of $X$ in a counterclockwise fashion in order by: counterclockwise $a$,
counterclockwise $b$, counterclockwise $a$, counterclockwise $b$, counterclockwise $a$,
counterclockwise $b$. Let $M$ be the corresponding quotient space. Compute $H_n(M)$ for all $n\ge 0$.
:::
