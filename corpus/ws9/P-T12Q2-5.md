::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $X$ be the space obtained from the torus $T=S^1\times S^1$ by gluing in a disk $D^2$ along its boundary $\partial D^2=S^1$ using the map $\alpha:S^1\to T$ given by $z\mapsto(z,(1,0))$ for $z\in S^1$.

(a) Find $\pi_1(X)$.

(b) Give a CW complex for $X$.
:::
