::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $p_i:(\widetilde X_i,\widetilde x_i)\to(X_i,x_i)$ be covering spaces for $i=1,2$.

(a) Show that the product space $\widetilde X_1\times\widetilde X_2$ together with the map $p:\widetilde X_1\times\widetilde X_2\to X_1\times X_2$ defined by $p(y,z):=(p_1(y),p_2(z))$ is also a covering space.

(b) Find the universal covering of the space $S^1\times D^2\times S^1$.
:::
