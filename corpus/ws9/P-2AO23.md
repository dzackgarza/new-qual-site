::: {.problem title="?"}
Determine all entire functions $f$ (i.e., $f(z)$ is holomorphic on the whole $z$-plane) that satisfy the inequality $$|f(z)| \le |z|^2|\operatorname{Im} z|^2$$ for $|z|$ sufficiently large.
:::

::: {.solution}
> **AI-Generated Solution**

**Goal:** Determine all entire $f$ with $|f(z)| \le |z|^2|\operatorname{Im} z|^2$ for $|z|$ sufficiently large.

<1>1. On the real axis, $f$ vanishes for all large $x$.
Proof: for real $z = x$ with $|x|$ sufficiently large, $\operatorname{Im} z = 0$, so the hypothesis gives $|f(x)| \le |x|^2 \cdot 0 = 0$, i.e. $f(x) = 0$ for $|x| \ge R$.

<1>2. $f \equiv 0$ on $\CC$.
Proof: $f$ is entire and vanishes on the set $\{x \in \RR : |x| \ge R\}$, which has an accumulation point; by the identity theorem, $f \equiv 0$.

<1>3. Conversely, $f \equiv 0$ satisfies the inequality.
Proof: both sides are $0$.

<1>4. Q.E.D. Proof: <1>1–<1>3 show the only such function is the zero function.
:::
