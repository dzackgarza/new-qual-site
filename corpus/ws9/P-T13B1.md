:::{.problem title="?"}
Let $X$ be the space obtained from the 2-sphere $S^2$ by identifying the north and south poles (i.e., by
identifying two diametrically opposite points).

(a) Show that $X$ is homotopy equivalent to $S^1\vee S^2$.

(b) Describe all connected covering spaces of $X$.
:::
