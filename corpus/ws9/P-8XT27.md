::: {.problem title="?"}
Prove the following statement without using Egoroff's Theorem: Suppose $\{f_k\}_{k=1}^\infty$ is a sequence of measurable functions defined on a measurable set $E$, $f_k\to f$ a.e. on $E$ and there exists $g\in L^1(E)$ such that $|f_k|\le g$ for all $k$.
Given $\varepsilon>0$, there exists a closed set $A_\varepsilon$ such that $m(E\setminus A_\varepsilon)<\varepsilon$ and $f_k\to f$ uniformly on $A_\varepsilon$.
:::
