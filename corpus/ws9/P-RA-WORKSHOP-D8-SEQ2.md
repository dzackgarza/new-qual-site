:::{.problem title="?"}
Show that the infinite series
$$
\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}x^n2^{-nx}
$$
converges uniformly on $[0,B]$ for any $B>0$. Does this series converge uniformly on $[0,\infty)$?
:::
