:::{.problem title="?"}
Let $K$ be the splitting field of $x^3 + 2$ over $\mathbb{Q}$.

a. What is the dimension of $K$ over $\mathbb{Q}$?

b. What is the Galois group of $K$ over $\mathbb{Q}$? (Identify the isomorphism type of the group, and also indicate how its elements act as permutations of the roots of $x^3 + 2$.)

c. Show that $K$ contains a primitive third root of unity $\zeta_3$. What is the Galois group of $K$ over $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_3)$? What is the Galois group of $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_3)$ over $\mathbb{Q}$? Again identify these groups as permutation groups and indicate their isomorphism type.

d. How are the three groups discussed in this problem related?
:::
