:::{.problem title="?"}
Suppose that $f:[a,b]\to\mathbb R$ is continuous and define $M:[a,b]\to\mathbb R$ by
$$M(x)=\sup\{f(y):a\le y\le x\}.$$
Show that $M$ is continuous on $[a,b]$.
:::
