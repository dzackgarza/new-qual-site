::: {.problem title="?"}
(a) State the Riesz Representation Theorem for the dual $L_p^*(\mu)$ of $L_p(\mu)$, $1\le p<\infty$.

(b) Let $\mu$ be a finite measure on the measurable space $(\Omega,\Sigma)$.
Prove the following part of the proof of the above Theorem: If $F\in L_p^*(\mu)$, then there exists an $h\in L^1(\mu)$ so that $$F(\chi_A)=\int_A h\,d\mu\qquad\text{for all }A\in\Sigma.$$
:::
