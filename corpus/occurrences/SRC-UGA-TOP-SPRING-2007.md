::: remark
UGA topology Spring 2007. The paper is the current Quals posting `Topology [Spring 2007].docx` under `https://www.math.uga.edu/sites/default/files/Quals/`. The list is the sitting order on that paper. Exam #1 (the product of two compact spaces is compact) has no matching P-card. Exam #3 (two disks glued by a homeomorphism of the boundary circles are homeomorphic to $S^2$) has no matching P-card. Exam #5 (van Kampen's theorem and $\pi_1$ of the Klein bottle) has no matching P-card; `P-LZDYV` merges several sittings' van Kampen computations. Exam #6 (definition of a regular covering, and a non-regular 3-sheeted cover of the figure-eight) has no matching P-card; `P-T5QRW` is a non-regular cover of the theta graph.
:::
