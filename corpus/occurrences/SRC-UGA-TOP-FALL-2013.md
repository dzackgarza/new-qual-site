::: remark
UGA topology Fall 2013. The paper is the current Quals posting `Topology [Fall 2013].docx` under `https://www.math.uga.edu/sites/default/files/Quals/`. The list is the sitting order on that paper. Exam #1 (the product of two compact spaces is compact) has no matching P-card. Exam #4 (definition of a covering space, and a simply connected covering is a homeomorphism) has no matching P-card. Exam #5 (every free group is a subgroup of $F_2$, via covering spaces) has no matching P-card. Exam #6 is listed as `P-FQNYQ`. Exam #7 (Mayer–Vietoris homology of a named space on the paper) has no matching P-card. Exam #8 (cellular homology of a named CW quotient) has no matching P-card. Exam #9 (possible genera of an oriented surface admitting a fixed-point-free map homotopic to the identity) has no matching P-card.
