Roy Smith, *Algebra Course Notes* (843-1 through 845-3). Course notes with no publication year, so the date is recorded as unknown. Cited in the authored wiki as `[@Smi]`. Provenance href is the Fall 2006 Math 8000 homework/tests packet `8000e.pdf` (12 pages).

Listed so far from that packet: abelian groups 1, 2, 6; Euclidean domains 1, 5; Noetherian rings 1, 7; normality Exercise 1; Sylow 2; cycles 1.

Remaining in `8000e.pdf` (same sheet numbering):
- Generators / free abelian groups: items 0 (reading), 3, 4, 5
- Euclidean domains: problems 2–4, 6–9
- Finitely generated abelian groups and $k[X]$ modules: all of #1–#4 (entire block)
- Jordan forms: Exercises 1–4 (entire block)
- Noetherian rings: problems 2–6, 8–10
- Normality / localization: Exercises 2–8
- Sylow: problems 1, 3–10
- Cycles in $S(n)$ and commutators: problems 2–10
- Galois groups: the five listed prelim pointers at the end of the packet

Also remaining: the separate 843–845 course notes cited on the syllabus (not this PDF). Problem cards about Smith normal form are a different Smith.
