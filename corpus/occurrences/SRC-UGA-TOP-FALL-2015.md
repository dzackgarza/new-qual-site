::: remark
UGA topology Fall 2015. The paper is the current Quals posting `Topology [Fall 2015].docx` under `https://www.math.uga.edu/sites/default/files/Quals/`. The list is the sitting order on that paper. Exam #1 (if $A$ and $B$ are compact then so is $A\times B$) has no matching P-card; `P-K5XUM` and `P-AZ46Z` state the biconditional. Exam #2 (a simple chain of open sets joining any two points of a connected space) has no matching P-card. Exam #3 (a cell structure on $T^2$ making a cylindrical self-map cellular) has no matching P-card. Exam #4 (the quotient of $T^2$ identifying $(z,1)$ with a power of $z$: $\pi_1$, and whether it is a surface) has no matching P-card. Exam #5 (the nerve of a given collection of sets, and its homology) has no matching P-card. Exam #6 (a continuous vector field on the disk with prescribed boundary data has a zero) has no matching P-card. Exam #9 (deck transformations of $\mathbb{R}\times[-1,1]$ with quotient a Möbius band) has no matching P-card; that statement is one part of the larger card `P-SDQTV`.
:::
