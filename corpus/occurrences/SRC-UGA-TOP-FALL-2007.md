UGA topology Fall 2007. The paper is the current Quals posting `Topology [Fall 2007].docx` under `https://www.math.uga.edu/sites/default/files/Quals/`. The list is the sitting order on that paper. Exam #3 (all connected 2-sheeted covers of $S^1\vee S^1$, and the index-2 subgroups of $F_2$) has no matching P-card. Exam #5 (van Kampen's theorem, and $\pi_1$ of two tori glued along a circle) has no matching P-card; `P-LZDYV` merges that with several other sittings' van Kampen computations. Exam #8 (a map homotopic to the identity on a closed orientable surface with no fixed points: the genus) has no matching P-card.
