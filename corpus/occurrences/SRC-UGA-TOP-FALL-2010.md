::: remark
UGA topology Fall 2010. The paper is the current Quals posting `Topology [Fall 2010].docx` under `https://www.math.uga.edu/sites/default/files/Quals/`. The list is the sitting order on that paper. Exam #1 (definition of compactness, and $X\times Y$ compact iff both factors are) has no matching P-card; `P-K5XUM` and `P-AZ46Z` state only the product biconditional. Exam #2 (definitions of connected and path-connected, path-connected implies connected, and the converse) has no matching P-card. Exam #3 ($\pi_1$ of the surface obtained from a cylinder by identifying $(z,0)$ with $(z^n,1)$) has no matching P-card. Exam #4 (homology of that surface) has no matching P-card. Exam #5 (degree of a map $S^n\to S^n$, and the degree of a fixed-point-free map) has no matching P-card. Exam #7 (classification of compact connected surfaces, and that a compact connected surface with nonempty boundary is homotopy equivalent to a wedge of circles) has no matching P-card; `P-M62FC` states only the wedge-of-circles half.
:::
