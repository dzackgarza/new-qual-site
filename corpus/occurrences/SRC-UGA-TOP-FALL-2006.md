::: remark
UGA topology Fall 2006. The paper is the current Quals posting `Topology [Fall 2006].docx` under `https://www.math.uga.edu/sites/default/files/Quals/`. The list is the sitting order on that paper. Exam #4 (van Kampen's theorem, $\pi_1$ of $T^2$ and of $\RP^2$, and $\pi_1$ of their wedge) has no matching P-card; `P-LZDYV` merges several sittings' van Kampen computations. Exam #5 (definitions of covering space and deck transformation, and the universal cover of the Klein bottle) has no matching P-card; that statement is part of the larger card `P-SDQTV`. Exam #6 (no map $S^2\to S^2$ orthogonal to the radius, the degree of the antipodal map, a CW structure on $\RP^n$, and its cellular homology) has no matching P-card; `P-M2NVE` and `P-UEGAX` each state only some of those parts.
:::
