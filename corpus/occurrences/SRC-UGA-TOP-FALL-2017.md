UGA topology Fall 2017. The paper is the current Quals posting `Topology [Fall 2017].docx` under `https://www.math.uga.edu/sites/default/files/Quals/`. The list is the sitting order on that paper. Exam #2 (a sequence of connected sets with consecutive nonempty intersections has connected union) has no matching P-card. Exam #4 (every continuous map $\RP^2\to S^1$ is null-homotopic, and the covering $S^2\to\RP^2$ is not) has no matching P-card; `P-OMOPR` and `P-6XN3Z` each state only one of those two parts. Exam #9 ($\RR^n\not\cong\RR^m$ for $n\neq m$) has no matching P-card; `P-NSD6Y` is only the $n=2$ case.
