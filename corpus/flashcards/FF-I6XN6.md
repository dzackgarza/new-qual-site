::: {.fact title="Groups of Order 12"}
2 Abelian, 3 Nonabelian

1. $\ZZ/4\ZZ \times \ZZ/3\ZZ$

2. $(\ZZ/2\ZZ)^2 \times \ZZ/3\ZZ$

3. $A_4$

4. $D_6$

5. The dicyclic group of order 12, $\gens{a, b \suchthat a^6, \, b^2 a^{-3}, \, bab\inv a}$
:::
