::: {.definition title="Null Set"}
A set $A$ is *null* iff for every $\varepsilon>0$ there exists a cover $\theset{U_j}\covers A$ such that $\sum \mu(U_j) < \varepsilon$, i.e. $\mu(A) = 0$.
:::
