::: {.fact}
$ \chi(\Sigma_g) = 2-2g $ if $ \Sigma_g $ is orientable, $ \chi(M_g) = 2-g $ if $ M_g $ is nonorientable.

Puncturing reduces $ \chi $ by 1.
:::
