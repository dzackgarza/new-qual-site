::: {.definition title="Definition: Diagonalizable."}
For a matrix $A$, similar to a diagonal matrix.
I.e. there exists a diagonal $D$ and some $P\in \mathrm{GL}(n, R)$ such that $A = PDP^{-1}$
:::
