::: {.definition title="Class Equation"}
$$
\abs{G} = \abs{Z(G)} + \sum_{i} [G: C_G(x_i)]
$$
where $C_G(x) \da \ts{ g\in G \st gxg\inv = x }$ is the centralizer of $x$.
:::
