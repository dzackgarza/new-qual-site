::: {.definition title="Retract"}
Retract: A subspace $A \subset X$ is a *retract* of $X$ iff there exists a continuous map $f: X\to A$ such that $f\mid_{A} = \id_A$.

Equivalently it is a *left* inverse to the inclusion.
:::
