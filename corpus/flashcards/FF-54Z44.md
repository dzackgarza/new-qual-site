::: {.fact title="Bessel's Inequality"}
For $x\in H$ a Hilbert space and $\theset{e_k}$ an orthonormal sequence,
$$
\sum \abs{\inner{x}{e_k}}^2 \leq \norm{x}^2
$$

Proof idea:
$$
\left\|x-\sum_{n=1}^{N}\left\langle x, u_{n}\right\rangle u_{n}\right\|^{2}=\cdots
.$$
:::
