::: {.theorem}
If $f, g$ are holomorphic on $\bar{D}(z_0)$ and $\abs{f - g} < \abs{f} + \abs{g}$ on $\bd D$, then $f,g$ are nonvanishing on $\bd D$ and have the same number of zeros.
:::
