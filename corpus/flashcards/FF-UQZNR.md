::: {.fact title="Inverting series: ${1\over \sin(z) } = \cdots$"}
$$
{1\over \sin(z)} = \frac{1}{z}+\frac{1}{3 !} z+\frac{7}{360} z^{3}+\mathrm{O}\left(z^{5}\right)
.$$
:::
