::: {.strategy title="Replacing a sequence of sets by a sequence of disjoint sets"}
Given $\theset{E_j}_{j\in \NN}$, set $F_j\definedas E_j \setminus \qty{\union_{k<j} E_k}$, then $\union E_j = \disjoint F_j$.
:::
