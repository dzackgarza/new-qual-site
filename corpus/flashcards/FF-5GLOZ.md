::: {.fact title="Series expansion for $\csch(z) = {1\over \sinh(z)}$"}
$$
\operatorname{csch} x=x^{-1}-\frac{x}{6}+\frac{7 x^{3}}{360}-\frac{31 x^{5}}{15120}+\cdots
.$$
:::
