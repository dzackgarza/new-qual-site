::: {.fact title="What is the Borel-Cantelli lemma?"}
$ \sum P(E_n) < \infty \implies P(\limsup_n E_n) == 0 $

If $ E_n $ are independent events and $ \sum P(E_n) = \infty $< then $ P(\limsup_n E_n) = 1 $.
:::
