::: {.fact title="For matrices, does $ A^n=B^n\implies A=B $?"}
No:

$$A=\left[\begin{array}{cc} 0 & 1 \\ -1 & 0 \end{array}\right] \Longrightarrow A^2=\left[\begin{array}{ll}1 & 0 \\0 & 1\end{array}\right]$$
:::
