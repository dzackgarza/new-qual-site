::: {.definition title="Definition: prime ideal."}
An ideal $\mathfrak{p}\normal R$ is prime iff it is proper and $ab\in \mathfrak{p} \implies a\in \mathfrak{p}$ or $b\in \mathfrak{p}$.
:::
