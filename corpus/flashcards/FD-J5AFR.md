::: {.definition title="Definition: Infinity Norm"}
The least upper bound that holds almost everywhere:
$$
\norm{f}_\infty \definedas \inf_{\alpha \geq 0} \theset{\alpha \suchthat m\theset{\abs{f} \geq \alpha} = 0}
.$$
:::
