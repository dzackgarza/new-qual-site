::: {.fact title="Where is ${1\over x^p}$ integrable in $\RR$? (Depending on $p$)"}
- $p < 1 \iff {1\over x^p} \in L^1((0, 1))$

- $p > 1 \iff {1\over x^p} \in L^1((1, \infty))$
:::
