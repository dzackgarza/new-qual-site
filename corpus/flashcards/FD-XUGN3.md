::: {.definition title="Exponential definitions of $\cosh$ and $\sinh$"}
$$
\cosh(z) = {e^{iz} + e^{-iz}\over 2} \\
\sinh(z) = {e^{iz} - e^{-iz}\over 2} \\
.$$
:::
