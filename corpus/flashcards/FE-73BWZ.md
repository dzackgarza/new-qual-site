::: {.example title="Example of a sequence of differentiable functions whose derivatives do not converge pointwise."}
$$
f_n(x) = {\sin(nx) \over n} \to 0 \quad\text{pointwise but}\quad f_n'(\pi) = (-1)^n
$$
:::
