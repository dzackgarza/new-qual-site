::: {.fact title="The tor complex between $ C_n $ and $ A $."}
$$\operatorname{Tor}^{\mathbf{Z}}_*(C_n, A) = (A/nA)\cdot t^0 \oplus A[n]\cdot t^1 \oplus \cdots$$
:::
