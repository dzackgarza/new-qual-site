::: {.example title="Example of a sequence of bounded functions whose pointwise limit is unbounded."}
$$f_n(x) = {1 \over x + {1\over n}} \to {1\over x}$$ on $(0, 1)$, noting that $\abs{f_n(x)} \leq n$.
:::
