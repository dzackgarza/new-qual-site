::: {.theorem title="Continuity of measure from above/below"}
- Below: $E_i \nearrow E \implies \mu(E_i) \to \mu(E)$

- Above: $E_i\searrow E, ~\mu(E_1) < \infty \implies \mu(E_i) \to \mu(E)$
:::
