::: {.theorem title="Rouche"}
If $f$ and $g$ are holomorphic on $\abs{z-z_0} \leq R$ with $f,g\neq 0$ on $\abs{z-z_0} = R$ and
$$
\abs{f-g} < \abs{f} + \abs{g}
,$$
then $f$ and $g$ have the same number of zeros in this region.
:::
