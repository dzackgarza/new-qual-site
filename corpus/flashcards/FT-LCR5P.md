::: {.theorem title="Dominated Convergence Theorem"}
If $\theset{f_n} \subset L^1$ and $f_n\to f$ a.e. with $\abs{f_n} \leq g \in L^1$ for every $n$, then
$$
\lim \int f_n = \int \lim f_n
$$
:::
