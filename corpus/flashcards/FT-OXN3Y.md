::: {.theorem title="Gauss' Lemma"}
Let $R$ be a UFD with fraction field $F$ and $p \in R[x]$.
If $p$ is reducible in $F[x]$, then $p$ is reducible in $R[x]$.

> Implies that $R$ a UFD implies $R[\theset{x_i}]$ is again a UFD.
:::
