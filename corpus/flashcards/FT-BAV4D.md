::: {.theorem title="Euler's Theorem"}
If $\gcd(a, n) = 1$ then $a^{\phi(n)} \equiv 1 \mod n$.

The coprimality hypothesis is needed: $2^{\phi(4)} = 4 \equiv 0 \mod 4$.
:::
