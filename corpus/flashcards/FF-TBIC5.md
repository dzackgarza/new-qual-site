::: {.fact title="Exponential expansions of $\sin(z)$"}
$$\begin{align*}
\sin(\theta) &= \frac{e^{i\theta} - e^{-i\theta}}{2i} = \frac{z - z\inv}{2i} \\
d\theta &= \frac{dz}{iz}
\end{align*}$$
:::
