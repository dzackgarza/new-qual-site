::: {.definition}
A **composition series** of $G$ is a sequence of subgroups
$$
1=N_{0} \leq N_{1} \leq N_{2} \leq \cdots \leq N_{k-1} \leq N_{k}=G
$$
such that $N_i \normal N_{i+1}$ and the *composition factors* $N_{i+1}/N_i$ are simple.
:::
