::: {.definition title="Definition: A pole $a$ of order $m$"}
The smallest $m$ such that 
$$
\lim_{z\to a}(z-a)^{m+1}f(z) < \infty \text{ but } \lim_{z\to a}(z-a)^{k\leq m} f(z) = \infty
.$$
:::
