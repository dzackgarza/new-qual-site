::: {.fact title="What is Krull's Hauptidealsatz?"}
Let $ R $ be a Noetherian ring and $ a $ an element of $ R $ which is neither a zero divisor nor a unit.
Then every minimal prime ideal $ P $ containing $ a $ has height 1.
:::
