::: {.theorem title="Cauchy Integral Formula for $f(z)$"}
For $f$ holomorphic in $U\supseteq \bar D$, then for any $z\in D$,
$$
f(z) = {1 \over 2\pi i} \int _{\bd D} {f(\xi) \over \xi - z} \,d\xi
.$$
:::
