::: {.fact title="Bernoulli's Inequality"}
$$
(1+x)^n \geq 1 + nx
$$
for all $n\in \RR$ and $x\geq -1$.
If $n\in 2\ZZ$, then this is valid for all $x\in \RR$.
Prove by induction.
:::
