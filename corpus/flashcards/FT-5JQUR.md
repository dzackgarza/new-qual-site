::: {.theorem title="Cauchy-Goursat Theorem"}
If $f$ is holomorphic on a simply connected region $\Omega$ containing a contour $\gamma$, then $$\int_\gamma f = 0.$$ Moreover, this holds for any contour $\gamma \subset \Omega$.
:::
