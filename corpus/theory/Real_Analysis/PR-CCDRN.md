::: {.proposition}
If $f\in L^1(\RR^d)\cap L^2(\RR^d)$, then its [[D-5LZQ4|Fourier transform]] $\widehat f$ lies in $L^2(\RR^d)$ and
$$
\norm{f}_{L^2}^2 = \int_{\RR^d} \abs{f}^2 = \int_{\RR^d} \abs{\widehat f}^2 = \norm{\widehat{f}}_{L^2}^2 .
$$
:::
