::: {.definition}
A \dfn{Hilbert space} is an inner product space $(H,\inner{\cdot}{\cdot})$ over $\RR$ or $\CC$ that is a [[D-BG455|Banach space]] under the norm $\norm{x}\coloneqq\sqrt{\inner{x}{x}}$.
:::
