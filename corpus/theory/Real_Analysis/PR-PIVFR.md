::: {.proposition}
Let $E\subseteq\RR^n$ be [[D-MDJII|Lebesgue measurable]].
Then $E = H \cup N$ with $H\cap N=\emptyset$, where $H$ is an [[D-RPOGQ|$F_\sigma$ set]] and $N$ has Lebesgue measure $0$.
:::
