::: {.definition}
For $f\in L^1(\mathbb R^n)$, its Fourier transform is
\[
\widehat f(\xi)
:=\int_{\mathbb R^n} f(x)e^{-2\pi i x\cdot\xi}\,dx,
\qquad \xi\in\mathbb R^n.
\]
:::
