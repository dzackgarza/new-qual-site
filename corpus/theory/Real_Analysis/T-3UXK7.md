::: {.theorem}
Let $f,g\in L^1(\RR^n)$ be essentially bounded.
Then the [[D-TS42Y|convolution]] $f*g$ is defined at every $x\in\RR^n$ and
$$
\lim_{\abs{x}\to\infty}(f*g)(x)=0 .
$$
:::
