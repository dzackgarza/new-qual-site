::: {.theorem}
Let $X$ and $Y$ be [[D-BG455|Banach spaces]] and let $T\colon X\to Y$ be a continuous linear surjection.
Then $T$ is an open map: $T(V)$ is open in $Y$ for every open $V\subseteq X$.
:::
