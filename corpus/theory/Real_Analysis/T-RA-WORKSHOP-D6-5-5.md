::: {.theorem}
Let $a<b$ and let $f\colon[a,b]\to\RR$ be continuous.
Then there exists $c\in(a,b)$ such that
$$
\frac{1}{b-a}\int_a^b f(x)\,dx=f(c).
$$
[@Apo74].
:::
