::: {.definition}
Let $(X,\mcm)$ be a measurable space.
A \dfn{simple function} on $X$ is a function $s\colon X\to\CC$ of the form
$$
s(x) = \sum_{j=1}^n c_j \chi_{E_j}(x),
$$
where $n\geq 1$, $c_1,\ldots,c_n\in\CC$, $E_1,\ldots,E_n\in\mcm$, and $\chi_E$ is the indicator function of $E$.
:::
