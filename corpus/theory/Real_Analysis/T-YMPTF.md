:::{.theorem}
Let $\{E_{k}\}$ be a countable collection of measurable sets.
Then
$$
\sum_{k} m(E_{k}) < \infty \implies \text{ almost every } x\in \RR \text{ is in at most finitely many } E_{k}
.$$
:::
