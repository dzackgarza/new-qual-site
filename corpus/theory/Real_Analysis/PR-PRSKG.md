::: {.proposition}
Let $1\leq p\leq\infty$, $f\in L^p(\RR^n)$, and $g\in L^1(\RR^n)$.
Then the [[D-TS42Y|convolution]] $f*g$ is defined almost everywhere, $f*g\in L^p(\RR^n)$, and
$$
\norm{f \ast g}_p \leq \norm{f}_p \norm{g}_1 .
$$
This is the case $q=1$, $r=p$ of Young's convolution inequality [[PR-2C3SZ]].
:::
