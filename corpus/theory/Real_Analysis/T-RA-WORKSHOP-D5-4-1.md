::: {.theorem}
Let $a<b$, let $f\colon[a,b]\to\RR$, and let $c\in(a,b)$.
If $f$ has a local maximum or a local minimum at $c$ and $f$ is differentiable at $c$, then $f'(c)=0$.
:::
