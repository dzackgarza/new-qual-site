::: {.definition}
Let $\phi\colon\RR^n\to\CC$ be a function and let $t>0$.
The \dfn{dilation} of $\phi$ by $t$ is the function $\phi_t\colon\RR^n\to\CC$ given by
$$
\phi_{t}(x) \coloneqq t^{-n} \phi\qty{t^{-1} x}.
$$
:::
