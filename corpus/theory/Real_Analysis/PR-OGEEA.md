::: {.proposition}
Every singleton $\theset{x}\subseteq\RR$ is closed, and $\QQ=\bigcup_{q\in\QQ}\theset{q}$ is a countable union of closed sets, hence an [[D-RPOGQ|$F_\sigma$ set]] in $\RR$.
:::
