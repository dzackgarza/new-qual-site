::: {.definition}
Let $(X,\mcm,\mu)$ be a [[D-QYLPH|measure]] space.
A [[D-DHFN4|measurable]] function $f\colon X\to\CC$ is \dfn{integrable} if
$$
\norm{f}_1 \coloneqq \int_X \abs{f} \dmu < \infty.
$$
:::
