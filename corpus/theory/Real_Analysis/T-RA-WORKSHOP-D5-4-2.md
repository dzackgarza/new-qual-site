::: {.theorem}
Let $a<b$ and let $f\colon[a,b]\to\RR$ be continuous on $[a,b]$ and differentiable on $(a,b)$.
Then there exists $\xi\in(a,b)$ such that
$$
\frac{f(b)-f(a)}{b-a}=f'(\xi).
$$
:::
