::: {.theorem}
Let $X$ and $Y$ be [[D-BG455|Banach spaces]] and let $T\colon X\to Y$ be a continuous linear bijection.
Then the linear map $T\inv\colon Y\to X$ is continuous, so $T$ is a homeomorphism.
:::
