::: {.fact}
A topological space $X$ is a [[FD-WHWSQ|Baire space]] if and only if for every countable family $(U_n)_{n\geq 1}$ of open [[FD-BA2WU|dense]] subsets of $X$, the intersection $\bigcap_{n\geq 1} U_n$ is dense in $X$.
:::
