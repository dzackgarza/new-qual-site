::: {.theorem}
Let $f\in L^1(\RR^n)$.
For every $\varepsilon>0$ there exists $R>0$ such that
$$
\int_{\theset{x\in\RR^n\suchthat\abs{x}\geq R}} \abs{f} < \varepsilon .
$$
:::
