::: {.theorem}
Let $X$ be a topological space, and let $f_n\colon X\to\CC$ for $n\geq1$ and $f\colon X\to\CC$ be functions.
If each $f_n$ is [[D-AEAAD|continuous]] and $f_n\to f$ [[D-YZC3C|uniformly]] on $X$, then $f$ is continuous.
:::
