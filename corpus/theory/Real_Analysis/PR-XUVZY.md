::: {.proposition}
Let $(X,\mcm,\mu)$ be a [[D-QYLPH|measure space]], let $1\leq p<\infty$, and let $f,g\in L^p(\mu)$.
Then $f+g\in L^p(\mu)$ and
$$
\norm{f+g}_p \leq \norm{f}_p+ \norm{g}_p .
$$
:::
