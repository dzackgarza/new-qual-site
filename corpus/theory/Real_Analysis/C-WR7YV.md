::: {.corollary}
$$
\sum_{k=1}^\infty \frac{1}{2^k} = 1.
$$
:::

::: {.proof}
The partial sums are $\sum_{k=1}^N 2^{-k} = 1-2^{-N}$, which converge to $1$ as $N\to\infty$.
:::
