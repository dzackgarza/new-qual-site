::: {.proposition}
Let $n\geq0$ be an integer and $x\in\RR$.
If $x\geq-1$, or if $n$ is even, then
$$
(1 + x)^n \geq 1 +nx .
$$
:::

::: {.corollary}
For all $x\in\RR$,
$$
1-x \leq e^{-x} .
$$
:::
