::: {.proposition}
Let $(X,\mcm,\mu)$ be a [[D-QYLPH|measure space]] and let $f_n\in$ [[D-BF5L2|$L^+$]] for $n\geq1$.
Then
$$
\int_X \sum_{n=1}^\infty f_n\dmu = \sum_{n=1}^\infty\int_X f_n\dmu
$$
in $[0,\infty]$ [@Fol13].
:::
