::: {.proposition}
Let $f,g\in L^1(\RR^n)$.
If their [[D-5LZQ4|Fourier transforms]] satisfy $\widehat f(\xi) = \widehat g(\xi)$ for all $\xi\in\RR^n$, then $f=g$ almost everywhere.
:::
