::: {.theorem}
Let $E$ be a set, let $f_n\colon E\to\RR$ for $n\geq1$, and let $(M_n)_{n\geq1}$ be a sequence of real numbers such that $\abs{f_n(x)}\leq M_n$ for all $n\geq1$ and all $x\in E$.
If $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}M_n$ converges, then $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}f_n$ converges [[D-RA-WORKSHOP-D7-CONVERGENCE|uniformly]] on $E$.
:::
