::: {.proposition}
Let $x\in\CC$.
The series $\sum_{k=0}^\infty x^k$ converges if and only if $\abs{x} < 1$, and in that case
$$
\sum_{k=0}^\infty x^k = \frac 1 {1-x} .
$$
:::
