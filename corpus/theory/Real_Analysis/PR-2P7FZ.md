::: {.proposition}
Let $1\le p,q,r\le\infty$ satisfy $\frac 1 p + \frac 1 q = \frac 1 r + 1$.
If $f\in L^p(\RR^n)$ and $g\in L^q(\RR^n)$, then the [[D-TS42Y|convolution]] $f\ast g$ lies in $L^r(\RR^n)$ and
$$
\norm{f \ast g}_{r} \leq \norm{f}_{p}\norm{g}_{q} .
$$
:::
