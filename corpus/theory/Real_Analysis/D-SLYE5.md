::: {.definition}
Let $H$ be a [[D-7QQUO|Hilbert space]].
A family $(u_{n})_{n\geq 1}$ of vectors in $H$ is \dfn{complete} if the only $x\in H$ with $\inner{x}{u_{n}} = 0$ for all $n$ is $x = 0$.
:::
