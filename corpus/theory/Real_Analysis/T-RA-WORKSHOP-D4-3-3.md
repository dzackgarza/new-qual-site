::: {.theorem}
Let $K\subseteq\RR^n$ be [[D-EILKJ|compact]] and let $f\colon K\to\RR^m$ be continuous.
Then $f$ is [[D-WGYSB|uniformly continuous]] on $K$.
[@Rud76].
:::
