::: {.proposition}
Let $(X,\mcm,\mu)$ be a [[D-QYLPH|measure space]] and let $f\colon X\to\CC$ be measurable with $\norm{f}_\infty<\infty$, that is, $f$ is [[D-3PVRB|essentially bounded]]. Then there is a bounded measurable function $g\colon X\to\CC$ with $\sup_{x\in X}\abs{g(x)}\leq\norm{f}_\infty$ and $f=g$ $\mu$-almost everywhere.
:::
