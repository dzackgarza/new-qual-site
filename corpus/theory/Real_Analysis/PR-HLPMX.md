::: {.proposition}
Let $(Y,\mcm,\mu)$ be a [[D-QYLPH|measure space]], $f\in$ [[D-BF5L2|$L^+$]], and $X,A,B\in\mcm$.
If $X\subseteq A\cup B$, then
$$
\int_X f\dmu \leq \int_A f\dmu + \int_B f\dmu,
$$
with equality when $X=A\cup B$ and $A\cap B=\emptyset$.
:::
