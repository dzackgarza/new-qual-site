::: {.definition}
A \dfn{Banach space} is a normed vector space $(V,\norm{\cdot})$ over $\RR$ or $\CC$ that is [[D-G5N6I|complete]] as a metric space with the metric $d(x,y)\coloneqq\norm{x-y}$.
:::
