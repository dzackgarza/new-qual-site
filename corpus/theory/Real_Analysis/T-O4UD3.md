::: {.theorem}
Let $X$ and $Y$ be metric spaces with $X$ [[D-EILKJ|compact]].
Every continuous function $f\colon X\to Y$ is [[D-WGYSB|uniformly continuous]] [@Mun00]; [@Rud76].
:::

::: {.corollary}
Let $U\subseteq\RR^n$ and let $f\colon U\to \RR$ be continuous.
For every compact $K \subseteq U$, the restriction $\restrictionof{f}{K}$ is uniformly continuous.
:::
