::: {.proposition}
If $f, g \in L^1(\RR^n)$, then the [[D-TS42Y|convolution]] $f\ast g$ is defined almost everywhere, $f\ast g\in L^1(\RR^n)$, and $\norm{f\ast g}_1\le\norm{f}_1\norm{g}_1$.
:::
