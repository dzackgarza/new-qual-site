::: {.definition}
A [[D-QYLPH|measure]] space $(X, \mcm, \mu)$ is \dfn{$\sigma$-finite} if there exist sets $E_1,E_2,\ldots\in\mcm$ with $X=\bigcup_{k=1}^\infty E_k$ and $\mu(E_k)<\infty$ for every $k$.
:::
