::: {.proposition}
Let $X$ be a topological space.

(a) A subset of a [[D-5NODS|meager]] set is meager.

(b) A countable union of meager sets is meager.

(c) A countable intersection of [[D-GG91S|residual]] sets is residual.

(d) A countable intersection of dense open sets is residual.

[@Fol13, sect. 5.3]
:::

::: {.corollary}
If $X$ is a nonempty complete metric space, then every residual subset of $X$ is dense, and in particular nonempty.
A residual set contains a countable intersection of dense open sets, which is dense by the [[FT-6WPJI|Baire category theorem]].
:::
