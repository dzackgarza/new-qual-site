::: {.proposition}
Let $(X,\mcm,m)$ be a [[D-QYLPH|measure]] space and let $A,B,C\in\mcm$.
If $m(A) = m(B) + m(C)$ and $m(C) < \infty$, then
$$
m(A) - m(C) = m(B).
$$
:::
