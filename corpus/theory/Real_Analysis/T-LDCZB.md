::: {.theorem}
Let $H$ be a [[D-7QQUO|Hilbert space]] and let $\Lambda\in H\dual$ be a continuous [[D-EPSKF|linear functional]] on $H$.
Then there exists a unique $y \in H$ such that
$$
\Lambda(x) = \inner{x}{y} \quad\text{for all } x\in H .
$$
:::
