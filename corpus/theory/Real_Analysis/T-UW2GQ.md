::: {.theorem}
Let $a<b$, and let $f\colon[a,b]\to\RR$ be [[D-HHVPT|continuous]] on $[a,b]$ and differentiable on $(a,b)$.
Then there exists $\xi\in(a,b)$ such that
$$
f(b)-f(a)=f'(\xi)(b-a).
$$
:::
