::: {.proposition}
Let $m$ be Lebesgue measure on $\RR^n$, let $E\subseteq\RR^n$ be [[D-MDJII|Lebesgue measurable]], let $h\in\RR^n$, and let $\delta>0$.
Then $E+h\coloneqq\theset{x+h\suchthat x\in E}$ and $\delta E\coloneqq\theset{\delta x\suchthat x\in E}$ are Lebesgue measurable, and
$$
m(E+h)=m(E), \qquad m(\delta E)=\delta^n\,m(E).
$$
:::
