::: {.definition}
Let $H$ be a [[D-7QQUO|Hilbert space]] over $\CC$.
The \dfn{dual} of $H$ is the set $H\dual$ of [[D-EPSKF|linear functionals]] $L\colon H\to \CC$ that are continuous with respect to the norm of $H$.
:::
