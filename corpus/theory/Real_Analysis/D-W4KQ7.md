::: {.definition}
Let $H$ be a [[D-7QQUO|Hilbert space]].
A bounded operator $U\in B(H)$ is a \dfn{partial isometry} if $\norm{Ux}=\norm{x}$ for every $x\in(\ker U)^\perp$.
:::
