::: {.definition}
Let $X$ be a topological space.
A subset $S\subseteq X$ is \dfn{meager}, or \dfn{of first category}, in $X$ if it is a countable union of [[D-2MJRE|nowhere dense]] subsets of $X$.
A subset is \dfn{of second category} in $X$ if it is not of first category in $X$.
:::
