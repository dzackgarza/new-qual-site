::: {.proposition}
Let $(X,\mcm,\mu)$ be a [[D-QYLPH|measure space]] and $f\in$ [[D-BF5L2|$L^+$]].
Then
$$
\int_X f\dmu = 0 \quad\iff\quad f = 0 \ \mu\text{-almost everywhere}.
$$
:::
