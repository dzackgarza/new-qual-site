::: {.proposition}
Let $f\in L^1(\RR^n)$ and $h\in\RR^n$.
Then $x\mapsto f(x+h)$ lies in $L^1(\RR^n)$ and
$$
\int_{\RR^n} f(x+h)\dx = \int_{\RR^n} f(x)\dx .
$$
:::
