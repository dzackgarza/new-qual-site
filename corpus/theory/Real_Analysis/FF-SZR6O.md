::: {.fact}
Let $X$ be a topological space and $A\subseteq X$.
Then $A$ is [[FD-QEQIY|meagre]] in $X$ if and only if $A$ is a countable union of [[D-2MJRE|nowhere dense]] subsets of $X$.
:::
