::: {.fact}
Let $H$ be a [[D-7QQUO|Hilbert space]], let $(e_k)_{k\geq 1}$ be an [[D-4IXAO|orthonormal]] sequence in $H$, and let $x\in H$.
Then
$$
\sum_{k=1}^\infty \abs{\inner{x}{e_k}}^2 \leq \norm{x}^2.
$$
:::

::: {.proof}
Fix $N\geq 1$ and put $s_N\coloneqq\sum_{k=1}^{N}\inner{x}{e_k}e_k$.
By orthonormality, $\inner{x}{s_N} = \inner{s_N}{s_N} = \sum_{k=1}^{N}\abs{\inner{x}{e_k}}^2$, so
$$
0\leq\norm{x - s_N}^2 = \norm{x}^2 - 2\operatorname{Re}\inner{x}{s_N} + \norm{s_N}^2 = \norm{x}^2 - \sum_{k=1}^{N}\abs{\inner{x}{e_k}}^2.
$$
Letting $N\to\infty$ gives the inequality.
:::
