::: {.definition}
A topological space $X$ is a \dfn{Baire space} if whenever $(U_n)_{n\geq 1}$ is a countable family of open [[FD-BA2WU|dense]] subsets of $X$, the intersection $\bigcap_{n\geq 1} U_n$ is dense in $X$.
:::
