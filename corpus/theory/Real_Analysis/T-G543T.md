::: {.theorem}
For $h\in\RR^n$ and $f\in L^1(\RR^n)$ let $(\tau_hf)(x)\coloneqq f(x-h)$.
For every $f\in L^1(\RR^n)$,
$$
\lim_{h\to 0}\norm{\tau_h f - f}_1 = 0 .
$$
:::
