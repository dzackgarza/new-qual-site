::: {.proposition}
Let $m$ be Lebesgue measure on $\RR^n$ and let $f\colon\RR^n\to[-\infty,\infty]$ be [[D-DHFN4|measurable]] with $\int_{\RR^n}\abs{f}\,dm<\infty$.
Then $m\qty{\theset{x\in\RR^n\suchthat \abs{f(x)} = \infty}} = 0$.
:::
