::: {.proposition}
Let $S$ be a set and let $f_n\colon S\to\CC$ for $n\geq1$.
If the series $\sum_{n\geq1}f_n$ [[D-YZC3C|converges uniformly]] on $S$, then
$$
\sup_{x\in S}\abs{f_n(x)}\convergesto{n\to\infty}0 .
$$
:::
