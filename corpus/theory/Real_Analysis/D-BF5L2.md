::: {.definition}
Let $(X,\mcm)$ be a measurable space.
The class \dfn{$L^+$}, or $L^+(X,\mcm)$, is the set of [[D-DHFN4|measurable]] functions $f\colon X\to[0,\infty]$.
:::
