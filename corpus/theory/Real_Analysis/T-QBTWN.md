::: {.theorem}
Let $H\neq\theset{0}$ be a [[D-7QQUO|Hilbert space]] over $\CC$, and let $\dualof{H}$ be its [[D-PQIQO|dual]]. Then the [[D-T4LOC|operator norm]] $\norm{L}_{\mathrm{op}}\coloneqq\sup_{\norm{x}=1}\abs{L(x)}$ is a norm on the vector space $\dualof{H}$.
:::
