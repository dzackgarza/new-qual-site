::: {.theorem}
Let $F\colon\RR\to\RR$ be increasing and right continuous, and let $\mu$ be the complete Lebesgue--Stieltjes measure associated to $F$, with $\sigma$-algebra $\mathcal M_\mu$.
If $E\in\mathcal M_\mu$, then
$$
\mu(E) = \inf\theset{\mu(U) \st U\supseteq E,\ U\text{ open}} = \sup\theset{\mu(K) \st K\subseteq E,\ K\text{ compact}}.
$$
For $E\subseteq\RR$ the following are equivalent:

(a) $E\in\mathcal M_\mu$;

(b) $E = V\setminus N_1$ with $V$ a [[D-RPOGQ|$G_\delta$ set]] and $\mu(N_1)=0$;

(c) $E = H\cup N_2$ with $H$ an [[D-RPOGQ|$F_\sigma$ set]] and $\mu(N_2)=0$.

:::
