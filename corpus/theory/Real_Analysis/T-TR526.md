::: {.theorem}
Let $a<b$, let $A\coloneqq[a,b]$, and let $f_n\colon A\to\RR$ for $n\geq1$ be differentiable on $A$.
Suppose that $\sum_{n=1}^\infty f_n'$ converges [[D-RA-WORKSHOP-D7-CONVERGENCE|uniformly]] on $A$ to a function $g\colon A\to\RR$, and that $\sum_{n=1}^\infty f_n(x_0)$ converges for some $x_0\in A$.
Then $\sum_{n=1}^\infty f_n$ converges uniformly on $A$ to a function $f\colon A\to\RR$, $f$ is differentiable on $A$, and $f'=g$ on $A$ [@Abb16].
:::
