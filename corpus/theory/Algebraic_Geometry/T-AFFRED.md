::: {.theorem}
Let $X$ be a Noetherian scheme.
Then $X$ is affine if and only if its reduction $X_{\mathrm{red}}$ is affine.
:::

Write $\mathcal{N} \subseteq \OO_X$ for the sheaf of nilpotent sections, the ideal sheaf of the closed immersion $X_{\mathrm{red}} \to X$ ([[D-SCHRED]]).

<1>1. If $X = \Spec A$, then $X_{\mathrm{red}} = \Spec A/N$ for the nilradical $N$ of $A$.

::: {.proof}
This is the construction of the reduced induced structure on an affine scheme.
:::

<1>2. $\mathcal{N}^n = 0$ for some $n \geq 1$.

::: {.proof}
Cover $X$ by finitely many affine opens $\Spec A_j$ with $A_j$ Noetherian; then $\mathcal{N}|_{\Spec A_j}$ is the sheaf of the nilradical $N_j$ of $A_j$.
$N_j$ is generated by finitely many nilpotents $x_1, \ldots, x_s$, say with $x_i^m = 0$ for all $i$, and a product of $s(m-1) + 1$ generators contains some $x_i$ at least $m$ times, so $N_j^{s(m-1)+1} = 0$.
Take $n$ to be the largest of these exponents over the finitely many $j$.
:::

<1>3. If $X_{\mathrm{red}}$ is affine, then $H^1(X, \mci) = 0$ for every coherent sheaf of ideals $\mci$ on $X$.

::: {.proof}
For $0 \leq i < n$, the sheaf $\mci \mathcal{N}^i / \mci \mathcal{N}^{i+1}$ is coherent and annihilated by $\mathcal{N}$, so it is the pushforward of a coherent $\OO_{X_{\mathrm{red}}}$-module along the closed immersion $X_{\mathrm{red}} \to X$, which is a homeomorphism.
Its cohomology on $X$ is its cohomology on the affine scheme $X_{\mathrm{red}}$, so $H^1(X, \mci \mathcal{N}^i / \mci \mathcal{N}^{i+1}) = 0$.
The exact sequences
$$0 \to \mci \mathcal{N}^{i+1} \to \mci \mathcal{N}^{i} \to \mci \mathcal{N}^i / \mci \mathcal{N}^{i+1} \to 0$$
give $H^1(X, \mci \mathcal{N}^{i+1}) = 0 \Rightarrow H^1(X, \mci \mathcal{N}^i) = 0$.
Since $\mci \mathcal{N}^n = 0$ by step <1>2, descending induction on $i$ gives $H^1(X, \mci) = H^1(X, \mci \mathcal{N}^0) = 0$.
:::

<1>4. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>1 gives one direction.
Conversely, if $X_{\mathrm{red}}$ is affine, step <1>3 and Serre's criterion [[T-5IOUR]] show that $X$ is affine.
:::
