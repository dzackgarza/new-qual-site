::: {.definition title="tensor algebra"}
Let $M$ be an $A$-module.
The $n$-th tensor power is $T^n M \da M^{\tensor n}$, with $T^0M = A$, and these assemble into a graded ring $T^\bullet M$ with multiplication by concatenation of simple tensors.
This algebra is not commutative.
Its universal property is that of a free object: any $A$-linear map $M \to B$ into an $A$-algebra extends uniquely to $T^\bullet M \to B$.
:::

::: {.definition title="symmetric algebra"}
$\Sym^n M$ is the quotient of $T^nM$ by the ideal generated by the elements $(m_1 \tensor \cdots \tensor m_n) - (m_{\sigma(1)}\tensor\cdots\tensor m_{\sigma(n)})$ for $\sigma \in S_n$, and $\Sym^\bullet M$ is the resulting graded ring.
Universal property: an $A$-linear map $M \to B$ to a commutative $A$-algebra extends uniquely to $\Sym^\bullet M \to B$.
:::

::: {.definition title="exterior algebra"}
$\Wedge^n M$ is the quotient of $T^n M$ by the ideal generated by the elements $(m_1 \tensor \cdots \tensor m_n)$ with $m_i = m_j$ for some $i \neq j$.
This forces $m_1 \wedge m_2 = -(m_2 \wedge m_1)$ and more generally $m_1 \wedge \cdots \wedge m_n = \sgn(\sigma)\, m_{\sigma(1)}\wedge \cdots \wedge m_{\sigma(n)}$.
Universal property: an $A$-linear map $\phi: M \to B$ with $\phi(m)^2 = 0$ for all $m$ extends uniquely to $\Wedge^\bullet M \to B$.
:::

::: {.remark}
For $M \cong A^{\oplus m}$ free of rank $m$: $T^n(A^m) \cong A^{m^n}$, with basis the words $e_{i_1}\tensor \cdots \tensor e_{i_n}$; $\Wedge^n(A^m) \cong A^{\binom{m}{n}}$, with basis the increasing wedges $e_{i_1}\wedge \cdots \wedge e_{i_n}$, $i_1 < \cdots < i_n$; and $\Sym^n(A^m) \cong A^{\binom{m+n-1}{n}}$, with basis the degree-$n$ monomials.

All three constructions sheafify: for quasicoherent $\mcf$ one sets $\Wedge^n\mcf|_{\Spec A} = \Wedge^n M$ where $\mcf|_{\Spec A} \cong \widetilde M$, and checks these glue.
For $\mcf$ locally free of rank $m$, the top wedge $\Wedge^m \mcf$ has rank $1$; this is the **determinant** line bundle, and it is the construction behind the canonical sheaf $\omega_X = \Wedge^{\dim X}\Omega_X$.
$\Sym^\bullet$ is likewise what feeds the relative $\Proj$: $\PP(\mce) = \mathcal{P}roj\, \Sym^\bullet \mce$.
:::
