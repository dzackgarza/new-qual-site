::: {.definition title="Star"}
Let $\tau \in \Sigma$ be a cone.
Put $N_\tau \definedas \ZZ\generators{\tau \intersect N}$, the sublattice generated by the lattice points of $\tau$, and
$$
N(\tau)_\RR \definedas N_\RR / (N_\tau)_\RR .
$$
For $\sigma \geq \tau$ write $\bar\sigma$ for the image of $\sigma$ under the quotient map.
The \dfn{star} of $\tau$ is
$$
\Star(\tau) \definedas \theset{ \bar\sigma \subseteq N(\tau)_\RR \st \tau \leq \sigma \in \Sigma } .
$$
:::

::: {.proposition}
$\Star(\tau)$ is a fan in $N(\tau)_\RR$, and the orbit closure of $\tau$ is its toric variety:
$$
V(\tau) \definedas \cl \Orb(\tau) = X_{\Star(\tau)} , \qquad \dim V(\tau) = n - \dim \tau .
$$
:::

::: {.remark title="Orbit closures"}
By the orbit-cone correspondence, the torus orbits of $X_\Sigma$ are the $\Orb(\tau)$ for $\tau\in\Sigma$.
Every torus-invariant irreducible closed subvariety of $X_\Sigma$ is $V(\tau)$ for a unique $\tau\in\Sigma$, hence is the toric variety of the fan $\Star(\tau)$, of dimension $n-\dim\tau$.

A ray $\rho$ gives $V(\rho) = D_\rho$, the boundary divisor, and $\Star(\rho)$ is the image of the fan of cones containing $\rho$ in $N_\RR/\RR\rho$.
For a complete toric surface, $\Star(\rho)$ is the complete fan in $\RR$, so $D_\rho \cong \PP^1$.
For the star subdivision of a smooth $n$-dimensional cone along the sum of its ray generators, the new ray $\rho$ has $\Star(\rho)$ the fan of $\PP^{n-1}$, so the exceptional divisor is $D_\rho \cong \PP^{n-1}$.
A maximal cone gives a point.

The boundary $X_\Sigma \setminus T$ is the union of the divisors $D_\rho$.
For rays $\rho_1,\ldots,\rho_k$, the intersection $D_{\rho_1}\cap\cdots\cap D_{\rho_k}$ is $V(\tau)$ if $\rho_1,\ldots,\rho_k$ span a cone $\tau\in\Sigma$, and is empty otherwise; when $\Sigma$ is smooth, the boundary is a simple normal crossings divisor.
:::
