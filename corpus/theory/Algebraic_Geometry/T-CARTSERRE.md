::: {.theorem title="Cartan--Serre"}
Let $X$ be a compact complex manifold, or more generally a compact complex space, and let $\mathcal{F}$ be a coherent analytic sheaf on $X$.
Then $H^i(X, \mathcal{F})$ is a finite-dimensional complex vector space for every $i$.
:::

::: {.theorem title="Serre's algebraic counterpart"}
Let $X$ be a projective scheme over a Noetherian ring $A$ and $\mathcal{F}$ a coherent sheaf on $X$.
Then each $H^i(X, \mathcal{F})$ is a finitely generated $A$-module.
With $\OO_X(1)$ very ample, $\mathcal{F}(m)$ is generated by global sections for $m \gg 0$, and $H^i(X, \mathcal{F}(m)) = 0$ for $i > 0$ and $m \gg 0$.
:::

::: {.remark}
On a compact complex manifold the theorem gives finite-dimensionality of $H^0(X, \Omega^p)$ and of the Dolbeault groups $H^q(X, \Omega^p)$, and so the Hodge numbers $h^{p,q}$ are defined.
The analytic statements for Stein manifolds are Cartan's Theorems A and B: every coherent analytic sheaf is generated by global sections at each stalk, and has vanishing cohomology in positive degrees.
:::
