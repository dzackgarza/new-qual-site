::: {.definition title="Kähler differentials"}
Let $A\to B$ be a ring map.
The module of \dfn{Kähler differentials} $\Omega_{B/A}$ is the $B$-module generated by symbols $db$ for $b\in B$, subject to additivity, $da=0$ for $a$ in the image of $A$, and the Leibniz rule $d(bb')=b\,db'+b'\,db$.
For every $B$-module $M$, composition with $d$ gives a natural bijection
$$
\Hom_B(\Omega_{B/A},M)\cong\Der_A(B,M).
$$
Here $\Der_A(B,M)$ denotes the additive maps $D:B\to M$ annihilating $A$ and satisfying $D(bb')=bD(b')+b'D(b)$ [@Har10a, Chapter II, §8].
:::

::: {.definition title="Sheaf of differentials"}
For a morphism of schemes $f:X\to Y$, the \dfn{sheaf of relative differentials} $\Omega_{X/Y}$ is obtained by gluing the sheaves $\widetilde{\Omega_{B/A}}$ on affine opens $\Spec B\subseteq X$ mapping into affine opens $\Spec A\subseteq Y$ [@Har10a, Chapter II, §8].
:::

::: {.proposition}
$\Omega_{X/Y}$ is [[D-QNTZY|quasi-coherent]], and it is coherent when $Y$ is noetherian and $f$ is of finite type.
Differentials commute with localization: $\Omega_{B_s/A}\cong(\Omega_{B/A})_s$.
For the affine diagonal with ideal $J=\ker(B\otimes_A B\to B)$, there is a canonical isomorphism $\Omega_{B/A}\cong J/J^2$, sending $db$ to $1\otimes b-b\otimes1$.
Thus differentials are also the conormal sheaf of the diagonal; this description is computed on affine neighborhoods, and for a separated morphism the diagonal itself is a closed immersion [@Har10a, Chapter II, §8].
:::

::: {.proposition title="Differential exact sequences"}
For morphisms $X\xrightarrow{f}Y\to S$, there is a natural exact sequence
$$
f^*\Omega_{Y/S} \to \Omega_{X/S} \to \Omega_{X/Y} \to 0
.
$$
For an $S$-scheme $X$ and a closed immersion $i:Z\hookrightarrow X$ with ideal sheaf $\mci$, there is a natural exact sequence
$$
\mci/\mci^2\longrightarrow i^*\Omega_{X/S}\longrightarrow\Omega_{Z/S}\longrightarrow0.
$$
Its first map sends the class of a local section $g$ of $\mci$ to $dg\otimes1$.
Affinely, for $B\to A$ and an ideal $I\subseteq A$, it is the map $I/I^2\to\Omega_{A/B}\otimes_A A/I$ [@Har10a, Propositions II.8.4A, II.8.11 and II.8.12].
:::

::: {.theorem title="Regularity and differential rank"}
Let $X$ be an irreducible scheme of finite type over a perfect field $k$, of dimension $n$.
For every point $x\in X$, the local ring $\OO_{X,x}$ is regular if and only if $(\Omega_{X/k})_x$ is free of rank $n$, as proved in [[P-AGH281DIFFNONCLOSED]], part (c).
Consequently $X$ is smooth over $k$ if and only if $\Omega_{X/k}$ is locally free of rank $n$.
Here regularity and [geometric regularity](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/038S) agree over a perfect field, and geometric regularity is equivalent to smoothness for schemes locally of finite type over a field.
:::

::: {.example title="Zero differentials without finite type"}
Let $K$ be an algebraic closure of $\QQ$.
The scheme $\Spec K$ has dimension zero and $\Omega_{K/\QQ}=0$: for $\alpha\in K$, differentiation of its separable minimal polynomial gives $p'(\alpha)d\alpha=0$ with $p'(\alpha)\ne0$, so $d\alpha=0$.
Nevertheless $\Spec K\to\Spec\QQ$ is not smooth, because it is not of finite type.
A finite list of algebraic generators would generate a finite extension of $\QQ$, whereas $K$ contains elements of arbitrarily large degree, for example roots of the Eisenstein polynomials $T^m-2$.
:::
