::: {.example title="The fan"}
Take $N = \ZZ^2$ with rays
\[
u_1 = (1,0), \quad u_2 = (0,1), \quad u_3 = (-1,a), \quad u_4 = (0,-1)
\]
and the four cones spanned by consecutive pairs.
Each consecutive pair has determinant $\pm 1$:
\[
\det(u_1, u_2) = 1, \quad \det(u_2, u_3) = 1, \quad \det(u_3, u_4) = 1, \quad \det(u_4, u_1) = 1 ,
\]
so $\FF_a$ is smooth, and the support is all of $N_\RR$, so it is complete.
:::

::: {.example title="Intersection numbers"}
Read the relations $u_{i-1} + u_{i+1} = a_i u_i$ off the coordinates:
\[
u_1 + u_3 = (0,a) = a \cdot u_2 , \qquad u_3 + u_1 = (0,a) = -a \cdot u_4 , \qquad u_4 + u_2 = (0,0) = 0 \cdot u_1 .
\]
Hence
\[
D_2^2 = -a, \qquad D_4^2 = a, \qquad D_1^2 = D_3^2 = 0 .
\]
$D_2$ is the negative section, $D_4$ a positive section, $D_1$ and $D_3$ the two invariant fibres of $\FF_a \to \PP^1$, which is the toric morphism induced by projection $N \to \ZZ$ onto the first coordinate.
:::

::: {.example title="Class group"}
$\Cl(\FF_a) = \ZZ^4 / M \cong \ZZ^2$ of rank $4 - 2 = 2$, generated by a fibre $F = D_1$ and the section $S = D_2$, with $F^2 = 0$, $F \cdot S = 1$, $S^2 = -a$.
The fan is smooth so $\Pic = \Cl$ and $\rank \Pic(\FF_a) = 2$.
The remaining classes are $D_3 \sim F$ and $D_4 \sim S + aF$.
Also $-K = \sum D_i \sim 2S + (a+2)F$.
:::

::: {.remark}
$\FF_0$ has rays $(\pm 1, 0), (0, \pm 1)$, a product fan, so $\FF_0 = \PP^1 \times \PP^1$, the smooth quadric in $\PP^3$.
$\FF_1$ is $\PP^2$ blown up at a point, with $D_2$ the exceptional curve of self-intersection $-1$.
For $a \geq 2$ the surface is not Fano: $-K \cdot D_2 = 2 - a \leq 0$.
:::
