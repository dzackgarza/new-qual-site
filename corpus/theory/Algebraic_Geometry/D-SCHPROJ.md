::: {.definition title="The homogeneous spectrum"}
Let $S = \bigoplus_{d \geq 0} S_d$ be a graded ring and $S_+ \da \bigoplus_{d \geq 1} S_d$ the \dfn{irrelevant ideal}. Then $\Proj S$ is the set of homogeneous primes $\mfp$ with $\mfp \not\supseteq S_+$, topologised by the closed sets $V(\mfa) = \ts{\mfp \st \mfp \supseteq \mfa}$ for $\mfa$ homogeneous.
Its structure sheaf sends $U$ to the functions $s: U \to \coprod_{\mfp \in U} S_{(\mfp)}$ with $s(\mfp) \in S_{(\mfp)}$ and locally of the form $a/f$ with $a, f$ homogeneous of the *same* degree and $f \notin \mfp$, where $S_{(\mfp)}$ is the degree-zero part of the homogeneous localisation.
:::

::: {.remark}
Let $k$ be an algebraically closed field and $S=k[x_0,\ldots,x_n]$.
The closed points of $\Proj S$ are the homogeneous primes generated by the linear forms vanishing on a line through the origin of $k^{n+1}$, so they correspond to the points of $\PP^n(k)$.
For homogeneous $f$ of degree $d$ and $v\in k^{n+1}\sm\ts{0}$, $f(\lambda v)=\lambda^df(v)$, so whether $f$ vanishes on the line $kv$ does not depend on $v$.
For $a,f$ homogeneous of the same degree $d$ and $f(v)\ne0$, $a(\lambda v)/f(\lambda v)=a(v)/f(v)$, so $a/f$ has a well-defined value at the point $[v]$.
The irrelevant ideal $S_+$ is the ideal of the origin of $k^{n+1}$ and the only homogeneous prime containing $S_+$; it contains the ideal of every line through the origin and corresponds to no point of $\PP^n(k)$.
:::
