::: {.example title="Dualising a plane cone"}
In the plane the dual of $\sigma = \Cone(v_1, v_2)$ is found by rotating each generator a quarter turn and orienting the result inwards.
Rotation by $\pi/2$ is
$$
e_1 \mapsto e_2, \quad e_2 \mapsto -e_1, \qquad R = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & -1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{bmatrix} .
$$
For $v_1 = (2,1)$ and $v_2 = (1,4)$ this gives $R v_1 = (-1,2)$ and $R v_2 = (-4,1)$.
The second normal points out of the cone, so replacing it by $(4,-1)$ gives
$$
\dualof{\sigma} = \Cone\big( (4,-1),\ (-1,2) \big) .
$$
The pairings $\inner{(4,-1)}{(1,4)} = 4 - 4 = 0$ and $\inner{(-1,2)}{(2,1)} = -2 + 2 = 0$ show that these vectors are the inward normals to the two walls.
:::

::: {.example title="The semigroup and its equations"}
The Hilbert basis of $S_\sigma = \dualof{\sigma} \intersect M$ is
$$
(4,-1), \quad (1,0), \quad (0,1), \quad (-1,2) .
$$
Write $u = x^4 \inverseof{y}$, $v = x$, $w = y$, $t = \inverseof{x} y^2$ for the corresponding characters.

The exponent vectors satisfy
$$
(4,-1) + (0,1) = (4,0) , \qquad (1,0) + (-1,2) = (0,2) , \qquad (4,-1) + (-1,2) = (3,1) ,
$$
so $uw = v^4$, $vt = w^2$, and $ut = v^3 w$.
Hence
$$
X_\sigma = \Spec k[x^4 \inverseof{y}, x, y, \inverseof{x} y^2] = \Spec \frac{k[u,v,w,t]}{(uw - v^4,\ vt - w^2,\ ut - v^3 w)} ,
$$
a surface in $\AA^4$.
The three equations are the $2 \times 2$ minors of
$$
\begin{bmatrix} v & w & u \\ w & t & v^3 \end{bmatrix} ,
$$
so this is a determinantal presentation of the singularity.
:::

::: {.remark}
Let $N'$ be the sublattice generated by $v_1$ and $v_2$. Since
$$
\abs{\det \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 4 \end{bmatrix}} = 7,
$$
the quotient $N/N'$ has order $7$. Over an algebraically closed field of characteristic different from $7$, $X_\sigma$ is the quotient of $\AA^2$ by the diagonalizable group
$$
\Hom(N/N',\GG_m)\cong\mu_7,
$$
so $X_\sigma \cong \AA^2/\mu_7$ and $\Cl(X_\sigma)\cong\ZZ/7$.
:::
