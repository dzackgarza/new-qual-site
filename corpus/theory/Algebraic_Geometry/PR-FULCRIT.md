::: {.proposition title="Fan criteria"}
Let $X_\Sigma$ be the toric variety of a fan $\Sigma$ in $N_\RR \cong \RR^n$.

| $X_\Sigma$ has the property | exactly when $\Sigma$ satisfies |
| --- | --- |
| normal | always: each $S_\sigma$ is saturated |
| smooth | the primitive ray generators of every cone extend to a $\ZZ$-basis of $N$ |
| simplicial, equivalently $\QQ$-factorial | every cone is generated by linearly independent rays |
| complete | $\abs{\Sigma} = N_\RR$ |
| projective | $\Sigma$ is the normal fan of a full-dimensional lattice polytope, equivalently $\Sigma$ is complete and admits a strictly upper convex integral support function |

If $X_\Sigma$ is smooth and complete, then it is Fano in the convention of [[D-VARFANO]] exactly when the anticanonical support function is strictly upper convex. Equivalently,
$$
P_{-K_{X_\Sigma}}
=\theset{m\in M_\RR\st\inner{m}{u_\rho}\geq-1\text{ for every }\rho\in\Sigma(1)}
$$
is a reflexive lattice polytope whose normal fan is $\Sigma$.

If $\sigma$ is full-dimensional with primitive ray generators $v_1,\ldots,v_n$, then smoothness of $\sigma$ is equivalent to
$$
\abs{\det(v_1,\ldots,v_n)}=1.
$$
A simplicial affine chart is the quotient of a smooth affine toric variety by the finite diagonalizable group described in [[PR-TORSMAFF]].
:::

::: {.proposition title="Divisor positivity"}
Let $\Sigma$ be complete, and let $D=\sum_\rho a_\rho D_\rho$ be a torus-invariant Cartier divisor with support function $\varphi_D$ and Cartier data $m_\sigma$ on maximal cones.

| $D$ has the property | exactly when |
| --- | --- |
| base point free | $\varphi_D$ is upper convex |
| ample | $\varphi_D$ is strictly upper convex |
| very ample | $D$ is ample and $S_\sigma=\NN(P_D\intersect M-m_\sigma)$ for every maximal cone $\sigma$ |
:::

::: {.remark title="Relations among the criteria"}
Every smooth cone is simplicial. Every projective toric variety is complete, but a complete fan need not admit a strictly upper convex integral support function and therefore need not be projective.
For a smooth complete toric variety, every ample Cartier divisor is very ample. On a complete toric surface the same conclusion holds for ample Cartier divisors because every lattice polygon is very ample. Thus an ample Cartier divisor that is not very ample can occur only in dimension at least three, and not on a smooth complete toric variety.

A reflexive polytope may define a singular Gorenstein toric Fano variety under the broader convention allowing singular Fano varieties. The term Fano on this site follows [[D-VARFANO]] and includes smoothness, so reflexivity alone is not the criterion used in the table above.
:::

::: {.example title="Positivity on $\PP^n$ and $\FF_m$"}
On $\PP^n$ with $D = \sum a_i D_i$, base point free means $\sum a_i \geq 0$ and ample means $\sum a_i > 0$.

On $\FF_m$ with rays labelled as $D_1, \ldots, D_4$ in cyclic order, $\Pic(\FF_m) = \generators{D_1, D_4}$, and $D = a D_1 + b D_4$ is ample exactly when $a > 0$ and $b > 0$.
:::
