::: {.proposition title="Fan criteria"}
Let $X_\Sigma$ be the toric variety of a fan $\Sigma$ in $N_\RR \cong \RR^n$.

| $X_\Sigma$ has the property | exactly when $\Sigma$ satisfies |
| --- | --- |
| normal | always: each $S_\sigma$ is saturated [Cox--Little--Schenck, *Toric Varieties*, Theorem 3.1.5] |
| smooth | the primitive ray generators of every cone extend to a $\ZZ$-basis of $N$ [*ibid.*, Definition 1.2.16 and Theorem 1.3.12, with Theorem 3.1.19(a)] |
| simplicial, equivalently $\QQ$-factorial | every cone is generated by linearly independent rays [*ibid.*, Proposition 4.2.7] |
| complete | $\abs{\Sigma} = N_\RR$ [*ibid.*, Theorem 3.4.6, (b)$\Leftrightarrow$(d)] |
| projective | $\Sigma$ is the normal fan of a full-dimensional lattice polytope [*ibid.*, Proposition 3.1.6], equivalently $\Sigma$ is complete and admits a strictly upper convex integral support function [*ibid.*, Theorem 6.1.14] |

If $X_\Sigma$ is smooth and complete, then it is Fano in the convention of [[D-VARFANO]] exactly when the anticanonical support function is strictly upper convex [*ibid.*, Theorem 6.1.14, applied to $D=-K_{X_\Sigma}=\sum_\rho D_\rho$]. Equivalently,
$$
P_{-K_{X_\Sigma}}
=\theset{m\in M_\RR\st\inner{m}{u_\rho}\geq-1\text{ for every }\rho\in\Sigma(1)}
$$
is a reflexive lattice polytope whose normal fan is $\Sigma$: the polytope of a Cartier divisor is computed from the same inequalities in [*ibid.*, (6.1.1)], its facets lie on $\inner{m}{u_\rho}=-1$ with $u_\rho$ primitive, and $0$ is interior because the right side is $-1$; reflexivity is the correspondence with Gorenstein Fano toric varieties of [*ibid.*, Definition 8.3.1 and Theorem 8.3.4], where the smoothness of the present statement is not assumed.

If $\sigma$ is full-dimensional with primitive ray generators $v_1,\ldots,v_n$, then smoothness of $\sigma$ is equivalent to
$$
\abs{\det(v_1,\ldots,v_n)}=1,
$$
which is [*ibid.*, Definition 1.2.16] in the case of a cone spanning $N_\RR$.
A simplicial affine chart is the quotient of a smooth affine toric variety by the finite diagonalizable group described in [[PR-TORSMAFF]] [*ibid.*, §1.3, following Theorem 1.3.12, and Theorem 3.1.19(b)].
:::

::: {.proposition title="Divisor positivity"}
Let $\Sigma$ be complete, and let $D=\sum_\rho a_\rho D_\rho$ be a torus-invariant Cartier divisor with support function $\varphi_D$ and Cartier data $m_\sigma$ on maximal cones.

| $D$ has the property | exactly when |
| --- | --- |
| base point free | $\varphi_D$ is upper convex [*ibid.*, Theorem 6.1.7, (a)$\Leftrightarrow$(d)] |
| ample | $\varphi_D$ is strictly upper convex [*ibid.*, Theorem 6.1.14, with Definition 6.1.12] |
| very ample | $D$ is ample and $S_\sigma=\NN(P_D\intersect M-m_\sigma)$ for every maximal cone $\sigma$ [*ibid.*, Proposition 6.1.10(c) and Definition 2.2.17] |
:::

::: {.remark title="Relations among the criteria"}
Every smooth cone is simplicial. Every projective toric variety is complete, but a complete fan need not admit a strictly upper convex integral support function and therefore need not be projective: [*ibid.*, Example 4.2.13] is a fan that is the normal fan of no lattice polytope.
For a smooth complete toric variety, every ample Cartier divisor is very ample [*ibid.*, Theorem 6.1.15]. On a complete toric surface the same conclusion holds for ample Cartier divisors because every lattice polygon is normal [*ibid.*, Corollary 2.2.13], a normal lattice polytope is very ample [*ibid.*, Proposition 2.2.18], and very ampleness of $D_P$ is the semigroup condition of Proposition 6.1.10(c). Thus an ample Cartier divisor that is not very ample can occur only in dimension at least three, and not on a smooth complete toric variety.

A reflexive polytope may define a singular Gorenstein toric Fano variety under the broader convention allowing singular Fano varieties [*ibid.*, Definition 8.3.1 and Theorem 8.3.4]. The term Fano on this site follows [[D-VARFANO]] and includes smoothness, so reflexivity alone is not the criterion used in the table above.
:::

::: {.example title="Positivity on $\PP^n$ and $\FF_m$"}
On $\PP^n$ with $D = \sum a_i D_i$, base point free means $\sum a_i \geq 0$ and ample means $\sum a_i > 0$.

On $\FF_m$ with $m \ge 0$, label the rays as in [[FE-TORHIRZ]]: $u_1=(1,0)$, $u_2=(0,1)$, $u_3=(-1,m)$, $u_4=(0,-1)$. Then $D_1=F$ is a fibre, $S=D_2$ is the negative section, and $D_4\sim S+mF$. Since $F$ and $S$ form a basis of $\Pic(\FF_m)$, so do $F$ and $S+mF$, and $\Pic(\FF_m) = \generators{D_1, D_4}$.
For $D = a D_1 + b D_4 = (a+mb)F+bS$, one has $D\cdot F=b$ and $D\cdot S=a$. The curves $F$ and $S$ span the cone of curves, so $D = a D_1 + b D_4$ is ample exactly when $a > 0$ and $b > 0$, by the numerical criterion [@Har77, Theorem V.1.10] together with $D^2 = 2ab + mb^2 > 0$.
:::
