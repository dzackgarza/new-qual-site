::: {.definition title="Globally generated"}
$\mcf$ is **generated by global sections** if there are $\ts{s_i}_{i \in I} \subseteq \Gamma(X, \mcf)$ whose images in $\mcf_x$ generate that stalk as an $\OO_{X,x}$-module for every $x$.
Equivalently, there is a surjection $\bigoplus_{i \in I} \OO_X \surjects \mcf$.
:::

::: {.remark}
The quotient formulation is the usable one: global generation says $\mcf$ has a presentation by a *free* sheaf with *globally defined* generators, which is a global condition where quasicoherence is a local one.

Every quasicoherent sheaf on an affine is globally generated, by any generating set of the corresponding module; this is why the condition carries no information affine-locally and all of its content is projective.
$\OO_{\PP^n}(d)$ is globally generated exactly for $d \geq 0$, the degree-$d$ forms having no common zero, and fails for $d < 0$ because there are no sections at all.

For an invertible $\mcl$, global generation is exactly what produces a morphism $X \to \PP^n$ from a choice of generating sections, with $\mcl = f^*\OO(1)$; the morphism is an immersion only under the stronger condition of very ampleness.
:::
