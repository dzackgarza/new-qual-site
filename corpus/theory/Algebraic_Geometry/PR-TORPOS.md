For a complete toric variety, convex support means base-point free, strict convexity means ample, and vertex semigroups decide very ampleness.

::: {.definition title="Support function"}
For a Cartier divisor $D = \sum a_\rho D_\rho$ the \dfn{support function} $\varphi_D : \abs{\Sigma} \to \RR$ is the unique function that is linear on each cone with $\varphi_D(u_\rho) = -a_\rho$.
On a maximal cone $\sigma$ it is $\varphi_D = \inner{m_\sigma}{\wait}$ for the Cartier datum $m_\sigma \in M$.
It is \dfn{integral} if $\varphi_D(\abs{\Sigma} \intersect N) \subseteq \ZZ$, and \dfn{upper convex} if
$$
\varphi_D(t u + (1-t) v) \geq t \varphi_D(u) + (1-t)\varphi_D(v) ,
$$
for $0\leq t\leq1$. It is \dfn{strictly upper convex} if it is upper convex and $m_\sigma \neq m_{\sigma'}$ for distinct maximal cones $\sigma,\sigma'$.
:::

::: {.proposition title="Positivity"}
Let $X_\Sigma$ be complete.

- $D$ is base point free, equivalently $\OO(D)$ is globally generated, iff $\varphi_D$ is upper convex, iff $m_\sigma \in P_D$ for every $\sigma \in \Sigma(n)$, iff $P_D = \operatorname{Conv}\theset{m_\sigma \st \sigma \in \Sigma(n)}$.

- $D$ is ample iff $\varphi_D$ is strictly upper convex, iff $P_D$ is $n$-dimensional and its normal fan is $\Sigma$.

- $D$ is very ample iff it is ample and, for every maximal cone $\sigma$,
$$
S_\sigma
=\NN\bigl(P_D\intersect M-m_\sigma\bigr),
$$
where the right side is the subsemigroup of $M$ generated by the differences $m-m_\sigma$ with $m\in P_D\intersect M$.
:::

::: {.remark}
For an ample divisor, the cone generated by $P_D-m_\sigma$ is $\sigma^\vee$, whose lattice semigroup is $S_\sigma$. Thus the very-ampleness condition says that the lattice points of $P_D$ generate the full local semigroup at every vertex.
On a smooth complete toric variety every ample Cartier divisor is very ample.

With the sign convention $\varphi_D(u_\rho)=-a_\rho$, upper convexity uses the displayed $\geq$ inequality. Strict upper convexity means that the linear part changes across every wall between maximal cones.
:::
