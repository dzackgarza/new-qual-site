::: {.example}
Let $k$ be a field and let $X=\Spec k[x,y,z]/(xy-z^2)$, the affine quadric cone in $\AA^3_k$, with vertex $O$.
Let $Y\subseteq X$ be the ruling $y=z=0$.
Then $\Cl(X)\cong\ZZ/2\ZZ$, generated by the class of $Y$ [@Har77, Example II.6.5.2].
The divisor $Y$ is not a Cartier divisor: its prime ideal is not principal in the local ring $\OO_{X,O}$, so $Y$ has no local equation at the vertex [@Har77, Example II.6.11.3].
The ring $\OO_X(X)=k[x,y,z]/(xy-z^2)$ is local with maximal ideal $(x,y,z)$, so every invertible $\OO_X$-module is free and $\Pic(X)=0$; hence $\CaCl(X)=0$ as well, by [[PR-DIVLB]], and $\CaCl(X)\into\Cl(X)$ is not surjective.
:::
