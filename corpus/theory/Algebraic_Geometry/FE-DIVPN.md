::: {.example title="Projective and affine spaces"}
Let $k$ be a field and let $n\ge1$.
Then $\Cl(\PP_k^n)\cong\ZZ$, generated by the hyperplane $H=V(x_0)$, with the isomorphism given by degree.
For a prime divisor $D=V(f)$ defined by an irreducible homogeneous polynomial of degree $d$, the rational function $g=f/x_0^d$ satisfies $D-dH=\operatorname{div}(g)$.
Every divisor is therefore equivalent to a multiple of $H$, and the degree of a principal divisor is zero, so that multiple is unique [@Har10a, Proposition II.6.4].
For $n=0$, projective space is $\Spec k$ and its class group is zero.
For every $n\ge0$, $\Cl(\AA_k^n)=0$, because $k[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$ is a unique factorization domain [@Har10a, Proposition II.6.2].
:::

::: {.example title="A hypersurface complement"}
Let $k$ be a field, let $Y\subseteq\PP_k^2$ be an integral curve of degree $d\ge1$, and put $X=\PP_k^2\setminus Y$.
The divisor restriction sequence is
$$
\ZZ\xrightarrow{1\mapsto[Y]}\Cl(\PP_k^2)\longrightarrow\Cl(X)\longrightarrow0.
$$
Under the degree isomorphism $\Cl(\PP_k^2)\cong\ZZ$, its first map is multiplication by $d$.
Hence $\Cl(X)\cong\ZZ/d\ZZ$ [@Har10a, Example II.6.5.1].
In particular, the complement of an integral conic has class group $\ZZ/2\ZZ$.
:::

::: {.remark}
Let $Z$ be a noetherian integral separated scheme regular in codimension one, and let $W\subsetneq Z$ be closed.
Restriction $\Cl(Z)\to\Cl(Z\setminus W)$ is surjective, with kernel generated by the prime divisors contained in $W$.
It is an isomorphism if $W$ has codimension at least two [@Har10a, Proposition II.6.5].
For a noetherian normal domain $A$, the criterion $\Cl(\Spec A)=0$ is equivalent to $A$ being a unique factorization domain [@Har10a, Proposition II.6.2].
:::
