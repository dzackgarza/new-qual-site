::: {.example title="The fan"}
For weights $q_0, \ldots, q_n$ with $\gcd = 1$, choose $v_0, \ldots, v_n \in N \cong \ZZ^n$ generating $N$ and satisfying the single relation
\[
q_0 v_0 + q_1 v_1 + \cdots + q_n v_n = 0 .
\]
The fan of $\PP(q_0, \ldots, q_n)$ has these as rays, with maximal cones spanned by every $n$ of them.
Taking all $q_i = 1$ and $v_i = e_i$, $v_0 = -\sum e_i$ recovers $\PP^n$.
:::

::: {.example title="P(1,1,2)"}
Solve $1 \cdot v_0 + 1 \cdot v_1 + 2 \cdot v_2 = 0$ in $\ZZ^2$ with
\[
v_2 = (1,0), \quad v_1 = (0,1), \quad v_0 = (-2,-1)
\]
— check $(-2,-1) + (0,1) + 2(1,0) = (0,0)$, and $v_1, v_2$ generate $N$.
The three maximal cones have determinants
\[
\det(v_1, v_2) = -1, \qquad \det(v_2, v_0) = -1, \qquad \det(v_0, v_1) = -2 ,
\]
so exactly one torus-fixed point is singular, an $A_1$ point of index $2$.
Its cone is $\Cone\big( (-2,-1), (0,1) \big)$, which is $\Cone\big( (0,1), (2,-1) \big)$ after a change of basis: the quadric cone $V(xy - z^2)$.
:::

::: {.remark}
$\Cl\big(\PP(1,1,2)\big) = \ZZ^3 / M \cong \ZZ$, and the classes of $D_0, D_1, D_2$ are the weights $1, 1, 2$.
So $\Cl$ is generated by $\OO(1)$, which is a Weil divisor class that is not Cartier at the singular point; $\Pic$ is the index-two subgroup generated by $\OO(2)$.
This is the smallest example where $\Pic \subsetneq \Cl$ with both torsion free, and it is the cone over a conic in disguise.
:::

::: {.example title="P(1,1,2) from a polygon"}
Let $P = \operatorname{Conv}(0, 2e_1, e_2) \subseteq M_\RR = \RR^2$, with lattice points $0, e_1, 2e_1, e_2$.
The characters of these points give $(\CC^*)^2 \to \PP^3$, $(s,t) \mapsto [1 : s : s^2 : t]$, and $X_P$ is the closure of the image,
\[
X_P = V(y_0 y_2 - y_1^2) \subseteq \PP^3 ,
\]
the projective cone over a conic.
The inward facet normals of $P$ are $(0,1)$, $(1,0)$ and $(-1,-2)$, with the relation $1 \cdot (1,0) + 2 \cdot (0,1) + 1 \cdot (-1,-2) = 0$, so the normal fan is the fan of $\PP(1,1,2)$ above.
:::
