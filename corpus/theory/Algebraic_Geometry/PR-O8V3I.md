::: {.proposition}
For a fan $\Sigma$ in $N_\RR$ with $\dim N = n$, there is an inclusion-reversing bijection
$$
\theset{\text{cones } \sigma \in \Sigma} \longleftrightarrow \theset{T\text{-orbits in } X_\Sigma} , \qquad \sigma \mapsto O(\sigma) = T \cdot x_\sigma ,
$$
under which $\dim O(\sigma) = n - \dim \sigma$, and $\overline{O(\sigma)} = \bigcup_{\tau \supseteq \sigma} O(\tau)$.
:::

::: {.remark}
The distinguished point $x_\sigma\in U_\sigma(k)$ corresponds to the semigroup homomorphism
$$
S_\sigma\longrightarrow k,
\qquad
m\longmapsto
\begin{cases}
1,&m\in\sigma^\perp,\\
0,&m\notin\sigma^\perp.
\end{cases}
$$
If $u\in\operatorname{relint}(\sigma)\cap N$, then $x_\sigma$ is the limit at $0$ of the one-parameter subgroup $\lambda^u\colon\GG_m\to T$ inside $U_\sigma$.

The zero cone corresponds to the dense torus orbit. A full-dimensional cone corresponds to a torus-fixed point. A ray $\rho\in\Sigma(1)$ corresponds to the invariant prime divisor $D_\rho$, and the toric boundary is
$$
X_\Sigma\sm T=\bigcup_{\rho\in\Sigma(1)}D_\rho.
$$

The principal-divisor map on characters is
$$
M\longrightarrow\ZZ^{\Sigma(1)},
\qquad
m\longmapsto\sum_{\rho\in\Sigma(1)}\inner{m}{u_\rho}D_\rho,
$$
and there is an exact sequence
$$
M\longrightarrow\ZZ^{\Sigma(1)}\longrightarrow\Cl(X_\Sigma)\longrightarrow0.
$$
If the ray generators span $N_\RR$, equivalently if $X_\Sigma$ has no torus factor, the first map is injective. For the cone generated by $(0,1)$ and $(d,-1)$ this cokernel is $\ZZ/d\ZZ$.
:::
