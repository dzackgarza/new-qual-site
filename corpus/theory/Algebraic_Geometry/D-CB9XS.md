::: {.definition title="Twisting sheaves"}
$\OO_{\PP^n}(-1)$ is the \dfn{tautological} bundle: its fibre over $p \in \PP^n$ is the line in $\AA^{n+1}$ that $p$ is.
Its dual is $\OO(1)$. For $n\geq0$, $\OO(n)$ is the $n$-fold tensor product of $\OO(1)$ with itself over $\OO_{\PP^n}$, and $\OO(-n)=\dualof{\OO(n)}$; on $\Proj S$ these are the sheaves $\widetilde{S(n)}$ associated to the shifted graded modules.
:::

::: {.proposition}
$\Pic(\PP^n_k) = \ZZ$, generated by $\OO(1)$, and
$$
H^0(\PP^n, \OO(d)) = S_d = \theset{\text{homogeneous polynomials of degree } d} ,
$$
of dimension $\binom{n+d}{n}$ for $d \geq 0$ and zero for $d < 0$ [@Har77, Theorem III.5.1].

The two halves come from different places, and neither is a formal consequence of the other.
The class group of $\PP^n_k$ is $\ZZ$ on the class of a hyperplane, the isomorphism being the degree homomorphism, part (c) of [@Har77, Proposition II.6.4] with $D\sim\deg(D)H$ its part (a); $\PP^n_k$ is smooth, hence locally factorial, so its Cartier and Weil class groups agree [@Har77, Corollary II.6.16], and $X$ is integral, so $\Pic(X)\cong\CaCl(X)$ [@Har77, Proposition II.6.15] ([[PR-DIVLB]]).
The section computation is the explicit one of [@Har77, Theorem III.5.1], where the higher cohomology of every $\OO(d)$ is computed at the same time.
:::

::: {.remark}
For $n \geq 1$, $\OO(-1)$ has no nonzero global sections, whereas the global sections of $\OO(1)$ are the linear forms.

By Serre's theorem, every coherent sheaf on $\PP^n_k$ is a quotient of a finite sum of twists $\OO(-d_i)$.
On a smooth projective curve, line bundles correspond to [[D-5PQ5W|divisors]] modulo linear equivalence; their degree need not determine their isomorphism class.
:::
