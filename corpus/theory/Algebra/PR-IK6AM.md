::: {.proposition}
A field $k$ is [[FD-YYLYR|perfect]], that is, every irreducible polynomial in $k[x]$ is separable, if and only if either

- $\ch k = 0$, or

- $\ch k = p > 0$ and $k^p = k$.
:::
