::: {.definition}
Let $(X,\leq)$ be a partially ordered set.
A \dfn{chain} in $X$ is a subset $C\subseteq X$ that is totally ordered by $\leq$: for all $c,c'\in C$, $c\leq c'$ or $c'\leq c$.
:::

::: {.definition}
Let $(X,\leq)$ be a partially ordered set and $S\subseteq X$.
An \dfn{upper bound} of $S$ is an element $x\in X$ such that $s\leq x$ for all $s\in S$.
:::
