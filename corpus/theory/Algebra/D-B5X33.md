::: {.definition}
Let $R$ be a commutative ring and let $M$ and $N$ be $R$-modules.
The \dfn{$R$-module structure} on $M\tensor_R N$ is the scalar multiplication determined on pure tensors by
$$
r\cdot (m\tensor n) \coloneqq (rm)\tensor n, \qquad r\in R,\ m\in M,\ n\in N.
$$
:::

::: {.remark}
The scalar multiplication is well defined: for fixed $r\in R$, the map $M\times N\to M\tensor_R N$, $(m,n)\mapsto (rm)\tensor n$, is $R$-bilinear, so it induces an additive map $M\tensor_R N\to M\tensor_R N$.
Since $(rm)\tensor n = m\tensor (rn)$, the same structure is determined by $r\cdot(m\tensor n) = m\tensor(rn)$.
:::
