::: {.definition}
Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $A$ an $n\times n$ matrix over $R$.
$A$ is \dfn{diagonalizable} if it is similar to a diagonal matrix: there exist a diagonal $n\times n$ matrix $D$ over $R$ and $P\in \GL_n(R)$ such that $A = PDP^{-1}$.
:::
