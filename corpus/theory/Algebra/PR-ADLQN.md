::: {.proposition}
Let $F$ be a finite field.
Then $\ch F = p$ for a prime $p$, the [[D-EOCCU|prime subfield]] of $F$ is isomorphic to $\FF_p$, and $F \cong \FF_{p^n}$ for the unique integer $n = [F:\FF_p] \geq 1$, so that $\abs{F} = p^n$.
:::
