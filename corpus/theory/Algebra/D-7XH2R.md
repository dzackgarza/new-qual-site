::: {.definition}
Let $R$ be a ring.
A proper [[D-GOFWL|ideal]] $\mfm \normal R$ is \dfn{maximal} if for every ideal $I \normal R$ with $\mfm \subsetneq I$, we have $I = R$.
:::
