::: {.corollary}
Let $G$ be a finite group, let $H\leq G$ be a [[D-IQ4OX|subgroup]], and let $G/H$ be the set of left cosets of $H$ in $G$, so that $[G:H] = \size (G/H)$ is the [[D-VJGH5|index]] of $H$.
Then
$$
\size G = [G:H]\, \size H,
\qquad\text{equivalently}\qquad
[G:H] = \frac{\size G}{\size H}.
$$
:::
