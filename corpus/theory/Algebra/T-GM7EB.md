::: {.theorem}
Let $G$ be a finite group.
Then $G$ is [[D-53JVH|nilpotent]] if and only if, for every prime $p$ dividing $\abs{G}$, the [[D-7TQ2M|Sylow $p$-subgroup]] of $G$ is [[D-EKE4Q|normal]].
:::
