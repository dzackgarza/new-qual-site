::: {.theorem}
Let $k$ be a field and $A\in\Mat_n(k)$, with [[D-QFYAC|characteristic polynomial]] $\chi_A$ and [[D-GK5SF|minimal polynomial]] $\min_A$.
Then $\chi_A(A) = 0$; equivalently, $\min_A$ divides $\chi_A$.
:::
