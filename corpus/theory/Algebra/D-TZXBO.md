::: {.definition}
A commutative [[D-GURUB|ring]] $R$ is \dfn{Noetherian} if it satisfies the ascending chain condition on [[D-GOFWL|ideals]]: for every chain of ideals
$$
I_1\subseteq I_2\subseteq\cdots
$$
there exists $N\geq1$ such that $I_N=I_{N+1}=\cdots$.
:::
