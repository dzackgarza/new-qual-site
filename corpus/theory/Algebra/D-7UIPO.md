::: {.definition}
Let $n\geq 1$.
A subgroup $H\leq S_n$ is \dfn{transitive} if its action on $\theset{1, \ldots, n}$ is [[D-KGGWK|transitive]], that is, if for all $i, j\in\theset{1,\ldots,n}$ there exists $\sigma\in H$ with $\sigma(i) = j$.
:::
