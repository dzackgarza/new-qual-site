::: {.definition}
Let $n\geq 1$.
A matrix $A\in \Mat(n\times n; \CC)$ is \dfn{normal} if $A^{\dagger} A = AA^{\dagger}$, where $A^{\dagger} = \bar A^t$ is the conjugate transpose of $A$.
:::
