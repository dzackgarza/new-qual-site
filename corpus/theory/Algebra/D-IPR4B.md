::: {.definition}
Let $k$ be a field, let $n\geq 1$, and let $\SF(x^n-1)$ be a splitting field of $x^n-1$ over $k$.
A subfield of $\SF(x^n-1)$ is a \dfn{cyclotomic field}.
:::
