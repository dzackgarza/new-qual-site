::: {.definition}
Let $R$ be a commutative ring.
The \dfn{max spectrum} of $R$ is the set of [[D-7XH2R|maximal ideals]] of $R$:
$$
\mspec(R) \coloneqq \theset{\mfm \subseteq R \suchthat \mfm \text{ is a maximal ideal}}.
$$
:::
