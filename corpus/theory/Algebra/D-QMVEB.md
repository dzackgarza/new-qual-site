::: {.definition}
Let $G$ be a group and $H\leq G$ a subgroup.
The \dfn{normal core} of $H$ in $G$ is
$$
H_G \coloneqq \Intersect_{g\in G} gHg^{-1}.
$$
:::

::: {.proposition}
Let $H\leq G$, and let $\psi\colon G \to \Sym(G/H)$, $g \mapsto (xH\mapsto gxH)$, be the action of $G$ on the left cosets of $H$.
Then $H_G$ is the largest [[D-EKE4Q|normal subgroup]] of $G$ contained in $H$, and
$$
H_G = \gens{ N \st N \normal G,\ N \leq H} = \ker \psi.
$$
:::

::: {.proof}
For $g\in G$, $\psi(g)=\id$ if and only if $gxH=xH$ for all $x\in G$, that is, $x^{-1}gx\in H$ for all $x$, that is, $g\in\Intersect_{x\in G}xHx^{-1}=H_G$.
Hence $H_G=\ker\psi$ is a normal subgroup of $G$, and $H_G\subseteq 1\cdot H\cdot 1=H$.
If $N\normal G$ and $N\leq H$, then $N=gNg^{-1}\subseteq gHg^{-1}$ for every $g$, so $N\subseteq H_G$.
Therefore the subgroup generated by all such $N$ lies in $H_G$, and it contains $H_G$, which is one of them.
:::
