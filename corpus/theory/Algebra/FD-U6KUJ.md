::: {.definition}
Let $R$ be an [[D-QJ3QL|integral domain]] and $M$ an $R$-module.
$M$ is \dfn{torsion-free} if its [[FD-BPUNZ|torsion submodule]] is zero: $\tor(M) = \theset{0}$.
:::
