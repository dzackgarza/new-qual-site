::: {.definition}
Let $R$ be a commutative ring.
A proper [[D-GOFWL|ideal]] $\mfp \normal R$ is \dfn{prime} if for all $a, b\in R$,
$$
ab\in \mfp \implies a\in \mfp \text{ or } b\in \mfp.
$$
:::
