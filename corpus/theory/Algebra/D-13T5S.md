::: {.definition}
Let $G$ be a group and let $k$ be a field.
A \dfn{representation} of $G$ over $k$ is a $k$-vector space $V$ together with a group homomorphism $\rho\colon G\to\GL_k(V)$.
Equivalently, it is a left module over the [[D-FHUV5|group ring]] $k[G]$, where $g\in G$ acts on $V$ by $\rho(g)$.
:::
