::: {.definition}
Let $R$ be a commutative ring and let $S\subseteq R$ be a subset.
The \dfn{ideal generated by} $S$ is
$$
\gens{S} \coloneqq \theset{ \sum_{i=1}^n r_i s_i \st n\geq 0,\ r_i\in R,\ s_i\in S }.
$$
:::

::: {.example}
For $a, b\in R$,
$$
\gens{a, b} = Ra + Rb = \theset{ r_1 a + r_2 b \st r_1, r_2 \in R }.
$$
:::
