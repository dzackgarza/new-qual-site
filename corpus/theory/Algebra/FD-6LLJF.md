::: {.definition}
Let $R$ be a commutative [[D-GURUB|ring]].
An element $r\in R$ is \dfn{irreducible} if $r$ is nonzero, $r$ is not a [[D-QQIQZ|unit]], and whenever $r=ab$ with $a,b\in R$, $a$ or $b$ is a unit.
:::
