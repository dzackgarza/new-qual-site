::: {.definition}
A [[D-TZXBO|Noetherian]] local ring $R$ is \dfn{Gorenstein} if $R$, as a module over itself, has finite injective dimension.
A commutative Noetherian ring $R$ is \dfn{Gorenstein} if its localization $R_{\mathfrak p}$ is a Gorenstein local ring for every prime ideal $\mathfrak p\subseteq R$ [@Eis95, Chapter 21].
:::

::: {.remark}
A commutative Noetherian ring of finite injective dimension over itself is Gorenstein; conversely, a Gorenstein ring of finite Krull dimension has finite injective dimension over itself [@Eis95, Chapter 21].
:::
