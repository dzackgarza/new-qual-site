::: {.proposition}
Let $k$ be a field, $f \in k[x]$ irreducible, $\SF(f)$ a splitting field of $f$ over $k$, and $\alpha_1, \ldots, \alpha_m$ the distinct roots of $f$ in $\SF(f)$.
Then $\Gal(\SF(f)/k)$ acts faithfully and transitively on $\theset{\alpha_1, \ldots, \alpha_m}$, so it is isomorphic to a [[D-7UIPO|transitive subgroup]] of $S_m$.
If $f$ is separable of degree $n$, then $m = n$.
:::
