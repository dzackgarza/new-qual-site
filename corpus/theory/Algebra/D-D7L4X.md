::: {.definition}
Let $G$ be a group.
Set $G^0 \coloneqq G$ and $G^{i+1} \coloneqq [G, G^i]$ for $i\geq 0$, where $[G, G^i]$ is the subgroup generated by the [[D-BQ4BQ|commutators]] $ghg^{-1}h^{-1}$ with $g\in G$ and $h\in G^i$.
The \dfn{lower central series} of $G$ is the chain $G^0 \geq G^1 \geq \cdots$.
:::
