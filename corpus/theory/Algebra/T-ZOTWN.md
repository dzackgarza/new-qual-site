::: {.theorem}
Let $R$ be a nonzero unital ring that is [[D-CYAJI|semisimple]] as a left module over itself.
Then there are integers $m\geq 1$ and $n_1,\ldots,n_m\geq 1$ and [[D-7O2CH|division rings]] $D_1,\ldots,D_m$ such that
$$
R \cong \bigoplus_{i=1}^m \Mat_{n_i}(D_i),
$$
a finite direct sum of the rings of $n_i\times n_i$ matrices over $D_i$.
:::

::: {.slogan}
Semisimple rings are finite direct sums of matrix rings over division rings.
:::
