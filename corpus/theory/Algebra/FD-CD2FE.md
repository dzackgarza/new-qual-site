::: {.definition}
Let $R$ be a [[D-GURUB|ring]].
A proper [[D-GOFWL|ideal]] $I\subsetneq R$ is \dfn{maximal} if for every ideal $J$ of $R$ with $I\subsetneq J$, $J=R$.
:::
