::: {.definition}
Let $R$ be a commutative [[D-GURUB|ring]].
An element $r\in R$ is \dfn{prime} if $r$ is nonzero, $r$ is not a [[D-QQIQZ|unit]], and for all $a,b\in R$, $r\divides ab$ implies $r\divides a$ or $r\divides b$.
:::
