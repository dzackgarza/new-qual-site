::: {.definition}
Let $F$ be a field, $K\subseteq F$ a subfield, and $\alpha\in F$.
The \dfn{subfield generated by $K$ and $\alpha$}, written $K(\alpha)$, is the smallest subfield of $F$ containing $K$ and $\alpha$, that is, the intersection of all subfields of $F$ that contain $K\cup\theset{\alpha}$.
:::

::: {.remark}
The generated subring satisfies
$$
K[\alpha]\subseteq K(\alpha).
$$
If $\alpha$ is algebraic over $K$, then $K[\alpha]$ is already a field and hence equals $K(\alpha)$. If $\alpha$ is transcendental, then $K[\alpha]\cong K[x]$ and $K(\alpha)\cong K(x)$, so the inclusion is strict.
:::
