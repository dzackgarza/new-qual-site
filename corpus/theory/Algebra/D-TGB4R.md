::: {.definition}
A commutative ring $R$ is \dfn{local} if it has a unique [[D-7XH2R|maximal ideal]] $\mfm$, that is, $\mspec R = \theset{ \mfm }$.
The \dfn{residue field} of the local ring $(R,\mfm)$ is $\kappa \coloneqq R/\mfm$.
:::
