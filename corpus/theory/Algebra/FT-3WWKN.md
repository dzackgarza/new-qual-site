::: {.theorem}
Let $K/F$ be a finite field extension.
The following are equivalent:

1. $K$ is a splitting field over $F$ of a [[D-ZT46D|separable]] polynomial in $F[x]$.

2. The [[D-5FG7E|fixed field]] of $\Aut(K/F)$ is $F$.

3. $[K:F]=\abs{\Aut(K/F)}$.

4. $K/F$ is [[D-LZTAK|normal]] and [[D-JGYLA|separable]].

A finite extension satisfying these conditions is [[D-5JYEI|Galois]].
:::
