::: {.definition}
Let $G$ be a group.
Set $\gamma_1(G) \coloneqq G$ and $\gamma_{i+1}(G) \coloneqq [\gamma_i(G), G]$ for $i\geq 1$, where $[\gamma_i(G), G]$ is the subgroup generated by the [[D-BQ4BQ|commutators]] $hgh^{-1}g^{-1}$ with $h\in \gamma_i(G)$ and $g\in G$.
The \dfn{lower central series} of $G$ is the chain $\gamma_1(G) \geq \gamma_2(G) \geq \cdots$.
:::

::: {.remark}
The series is descending, while the [[D-XEDSI|upper central series]] is ascending.
:::
