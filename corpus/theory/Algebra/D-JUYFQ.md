::: {.definition}
Let $R$ be a ring and $M,N$ be [[D-NQZUY|$R$-modules]].
A map $f\colon M\to N$ is a \dfn{morphism of modules}, or $R$-linear map, if
$$
f(rm + m') = rf(m) + f(m')\qquad\text{for all } r\in R \text{ and } m,m'\in M.
$$
:::
