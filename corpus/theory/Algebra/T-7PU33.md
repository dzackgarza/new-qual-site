::: {.theorem}
Let $G$ be a group with [[D-XEDSI|upper central series]] $Z_0\leq Z_1\leq\cdots$.
Then $G$ is [[D-53JVH|nilpotent]] if and only if $Z_n=G$ for some $n\geq 0$.
:::
