::: {.definition}
Let $R$ and $S$ be [[D-GURUB|rings]].
A \dfn{ring morphism} is a map $f\colon R\to S$ such that for all $a, b\in R$:

- $f(1_R) = 1_S$;

- $f(a+b) = f(a) + f(b)$;

- $f(ab) = f(a)f(b)$.
:::
