::: {.definition}
Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $I\subseteq R$ an [[D-GOFWL|ideal]].
The \dfn{radical} of $I$ is
$$
\rad{I} \coloneqq \theset{r\in R\suchthat r^n\in I\text{ for some } n\geq 0}.
$$
The ideal $I$ is \dfn{radical} if $\rad{I} = I$.
:::
