::: {.definition}
Let $R$ be a [[D-GURUB|ring]].
An element $a\in R$ is \dfn{unipotent} if $a-1$ is [[D-GIGM2|nilpotent]].
:::

::: {.example}
Let $k$ be a field and let $A\in\Mat_{n\times n}(k)$ be upper triangular with every diagonal entry equal to $1$.
Then $A - I$ is strictly upper triangular, so $(A-I)^n = 0$ and $A$ is unipotent.
:::
