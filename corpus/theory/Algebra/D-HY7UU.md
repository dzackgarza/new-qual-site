::: {.definition}
Let $R$ be a ring.
An [[D-NQZUY|$R$-module]] $M$ is \dfn{cyclic} if there exists $m\in M$ such that
$$
M = Rm \coloneqq \theset{ rm \st r\in R }.
$$
:::
