:::{.proposition}
If $G\actson X$ transitively, then for any choice of $x\in X$ there is an isomorphism of sets given by
\[
\Phi: G/G_{x} &\mapsvia{\sim} X \\
gG_{x} &\mapsto g\actson x
.\]
:::
