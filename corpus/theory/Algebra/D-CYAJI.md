::: {.definition}
Let $R$ be a ring.
An $R$-module $M$ is \dfn{semisimple} if there is a family $(M_j)_{j\in J}$ of [[D-4KM4P|simple]] submodules of $M$ with
$$
M = \bigoplus_{j\in J} M_j.
$$
:::
