::: {.definition}
\dfn{Euler's totient function} $\phi\colon\ZZ_{\geq 1}\to\ZZ_{\geq 1}$ is
$$
\phi(n) \coloneqq \size \theset{ k\in\ZZ \st 1\leq k\leq n,\ \gcd(k ,n ) = 1}.
$$
:::
