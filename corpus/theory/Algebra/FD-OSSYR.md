::: {.definition}
Let $R$ be a commutative [[D-GURUB|ring]].
An [[D-GOFWL|ideal]] $\mfp\subseteq R$ is \dfn{prime} if $\mfp\neq R$ and, for all $a,b\in R$, $ab\in \mfp$ implies $a\in \mfp$ or $b\in \mfp$.
:::
