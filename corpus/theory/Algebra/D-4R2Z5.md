::: {.definition}
Let $n\geq 1$.
The \dfn{dihedral group} of order $2n$ is the group with [[D-V6UJ7|presentation]]
$$
D_n \coloneqq \gens{r, s \st r^n, s^2, srs^{-1}r } .
$$
:::

::: {.proposition}
For $n\geq 1$,
$$
\gens{r, s \st r^n, s^2, srs^{-1}r } \cong \gens{r, s \st r^n, s^2, (rs)^2 },
$$
by an isomorphism sending $r\mapsto r$ and $s\mapsto s$.
:::

::: {.remark}
For $n\geq 3$, $D_n$ is the group of symmetries of a regular $n$-gon in the plane, with $r$ the rotation by $2\pi/n$ and $s$ a reflection.
Labeling the vertices $1, \ldots, n$ in cyclic order identifies $D_n$ with a subgroup of $S_n$, in which $r$ is an $n$-cycle.
:::

::: {.example}
Label the vertices of a square $1,2,3,4$ in cyclic order.
Then $D_4 = \gens{(1,2,3,4), (1,3)} \leq S_4$, where $(1,2,3,4)$ is the rotation by $2\pi/4$ and $(1,3)$ is the reflection across the diagonal through the vertices $2$ and $4$.
:::

::: {.example}
Label the vertices of a regular pentagon $1,\ldots,5$ in cyclic order.
Then $D_5 = \gens{(1,2,3,4,5), (1,5)(2,4)} \leq S_5$, where $(1,2,3,4,5)$ is the rotation by $2\pi/5$ and $(1,5)(2,4)$ is the reflection across the line through vertex $3$ and the center.
:::
