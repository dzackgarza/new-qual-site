::: {.theorem}
Let $F$ be a field.
Its [[D-EOCCU|prime subfield]] is isomorphic to $\QQ$ if $\ch F=0$, and to $\FF_p$ if $\ch F=p>0$, in which case $p$ is prime.
:::
