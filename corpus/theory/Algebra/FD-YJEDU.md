::: {.definition}
Let $L/K$ be a field extension and let $\alpha\in L$ be algebraic over $K$, with minimal polynomial $m_\alpha\in K[x]$, the monic irreducible polynomial in $K[x]$ having $\alpha$ as a root.
A \dfn{conjugate} of $\alpha$ over $K$ is a root of $m_\alpha$, in a splitting field of $m_\alpha$ over $L$, other than $\alpha$.
:::
