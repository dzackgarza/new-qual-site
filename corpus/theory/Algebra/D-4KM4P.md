::: {.definition}
Let $R$ be a ring.
An $R$-module $M$ is \dfn{simple} if every submodule $M' \leq M$ is either $0$ or $M$.
A commutative ring $R$ is \dfn{simple} if it is simple as an $R$-module, that is, if its only ideals are $0$ and $R$.
:::
