::: {.proposition}
Let $R$ be a [[D-GURUB|ring]] and $I\subsetneq R$ a proper [[D-GOFWL|ideal]].
Then $I$ is contained in a [[D-7XH2R|maximal ideal]] of $R$.
In particular, applying this to $I=0$, every nonzero ring has a maximal ideal [@AM18, Theorem 1.3 and Corollary 1.4].
:::
