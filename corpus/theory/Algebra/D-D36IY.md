::: {.definition}
Let $G$ be a group and let $H\subseteq G$ be a subset.
The \dfn{subgroup generated by} $H$ is the intersection of all subgroups of $G$ containing $H$:
$$
\gens{H} \coloneqq \bigcap \theset{M \suchthat H\subseteq M \leq G}.
$$
:::

::: {.proposition}
Let $G$ be a group and let $H\subseteq G$ be a subset.
Then $\gens{H}$ is the smallest subgroup of $G$ containing $H$, and
$$
\gens{H} = \theset{ h_1^{\varepsilon_1} \cdots h_n^{\varepsilon_n} \suchthat n\geq 0,\ h_i \in H,\ \varepsilon_i\in\theset{1,-1}}.
$$
:::
