::: {.definition}
Let $L/k$ be a field extension and let $H \leq \Aut_{\Fieldsover{k}}(L)$ be a subgroup of the automorphisms of $L$ fixing $k$.
The \dfn{fixed field} of $H$ is
$$
L^H \coloneqq \theset{ \ell \in L \st \sigma(\ell) = \ell \text{ for all } \sigma\in H }.
$$
:::
