::: {.definition}
An [[D-QJ3QL|integral domain]] $R$ with [[D-OXIVT|field of fractions]] $K$ is a \dfn{valuation ring} if for every nonzero $x\in K$, $x\in R$ or $x^{-1} \in R$.
:::
