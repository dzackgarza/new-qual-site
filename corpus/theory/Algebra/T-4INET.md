::: {.theorem}
Let $G$ be a group with identity $e$ and [[D-BGNME|lower central series]] $\gamma_1(G)\geq\gamma_2(G)\geq\cdots$.
Then $G$ is [[D-53JVH|nilpotent]] if and only if $\gamma_n(G)=\theset{e}$ for some $n\geq1$.
:::
