::: {.theorem}
Let $G$ be a group with identity $e$ and [[D-BGNME|lower central series]] $G^0\geq G^1\geq\cdots$.
Then $G$ is [[D-53JVH|nilpotent]] if and only if $G^n=\theset{e}$ for some $n\geq0$.
:::
