::: {.definition}
Let $G$ be a group.
A \dfn{normal series} of $G$ is a chain of [[D-IQ4OX|subgroups]]
$$
G = G^0 \supseteq G^1 \supseteq G^2 \supseteq \cdots
$$
such that $G^{i+1}$ is a [[D-EKE4Q|normal subgroup]] of $G^i$ for every $i$.
:::
