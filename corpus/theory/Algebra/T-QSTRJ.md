::: {.theorem}
Let $R$ be a nonzero [[D-GURUB|ring]].
Then every proper [[D-GOFWL|ideal]] of $R$ is contained in a proper [[D-7XH2R|maximal ideal]]; in particular, $R$ has a proper maximal ideal.
:::
