::: {.definition}
Let $n\geq 1$.
The \dfn{special unitary group} is
$$
\SU_n(\CC) \coloneqq \theset{ A \in \U_n(\CC) \st \det A = 1  },
$$
where $\U_n(\CC)$ is the [[D-QZ2LQ|unitary group]].
:::
