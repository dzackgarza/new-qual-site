::: {.definition}
Let $R$ be a commutative ring and let $x_1,\ldots,x_n$ be indeterminates over $R$.
For $k\geq1$ the \dfn{power sum} is $p_k\coloneqq\sum_{i=1}^n x_i^k$, and $p_0\coloneqq1$.
For a [[D-YTABL|partition]] $\lambda$ one sets $p_\lambda\coloneqq\prod_i p_{\lambda_i}$.
:::
