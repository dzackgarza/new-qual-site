::: {.theorem}
Let $K/F$ be a finite [[D-5JYEI|Galois]] extension with Galois group $G=\Gal(K/F)$.
The maps
$$
\begin{aligned}
\theset{E\suchthat F\subseteq E\subseteq K\text{ a field}}&\longleftrightarrow\theset{H\suchthat H\le G}\\
E&\mapsto\Gal(K/E)=\theset{\tau\in G\suchthat\tau|_E=\id_E}\\
K^H&\mapsfrom H
\end{aligned}
$$
are mutually inverse bijections, where $K^H$ is the [[D-5FG7E|fixed field]] of $H$.
If $E$ corresponds to $H$, and $E_1,E_2$ correspond to $H_1,H_2$, then:

1. $E_1\subseteq E_2$ if and only if $H_2\le H_1$.

2. $\abs H=[K:E]$ and $[E:F]=[G:H]$.

3. $K/E$ is Galois with $\Gal(K/E)=H$.

4. $E/F$ is Galois if and only if $H$ is [[D-EKE4Q|normal]] in $G$, in which case $\Gal(E/F)\cong G/H$.

5. The intersection $E_1\cap E_2$ corresponds to the subgroup $\gens{H_1,H_2}$ generated by $H_1$ and $H_2$, and the compositum $E_1E_2$ corresponds to $H_1\cap H_2$.
:::
