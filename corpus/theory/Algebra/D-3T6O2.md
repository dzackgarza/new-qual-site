::: {.proposition}
Let $G$ be a group, let $X$ be a set, and let $\Aut(X)$ be the group of bijections $X\to X$ under composition.
[[D-WYC7C|Actions]] of $G$ on $X$ correspond bijectively to group homomorphisms
$$
\phi\colon G\longrightarrow\Aut(X).
$$
An action corresponds to $\phi(g)(x)\coloneqq g\cdot x$, and a homomorphism $\phi$ corresponds to the action $g\cdot x\coloneqq\phi(g)(x)$.
:::
