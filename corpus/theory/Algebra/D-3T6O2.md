::: {.definition}
Let $G$ be a group with identity $e$, and let $X$ be a set.
An \dfn{action} of $G$ on $X$ is a map
$$
G \times X \to X, \qquad (g,x) \mapsto g\cdot x,
$$
such that for all $g,h\in G$ and $x\in X$:

1. $e\cdot x = x$;

2. $g\cdot (h\cdot x) = (gh)\cdot x$.
:::

::: {.proposition}
Let $G$ be a group, let $X$ be a set, and let $\Aut(X)$ be the group of bijections $X\to X$ under composition.
Actions of $G$ on $X$ correspond bijectively to group homomorphisms $\phi\colon G\to\Aut(X)$: an action corresponds to $\phi(g)(x) \coloneqq g\cdot x$, and a homomorphism $\phi$ corresponds to the action $g\cdot x \coloneqq \phi(g)(x)$.
:::
