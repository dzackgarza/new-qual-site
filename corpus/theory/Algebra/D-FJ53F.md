::: {.definition}
Let $R$ be a commutative ring.
An [[D-GOFWL|ideal]] $I\normal R$ is \dfn{irreducible} if for all ideals $J_1, J_2 \normal R$ with $I = J_1 \cap J_2$, either $J_1 = I$ or $J_2 = I$.
:::
