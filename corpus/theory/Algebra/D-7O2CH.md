::: {.definition}
A \dfn{division ring}, or \dfn{skew field}, is a [[D-GURUB|ring]] $R$, not necessarily commutative, with $1\neq 0$ in which every nonzero element is a [[D-QQIQZ|unit]], that is, $R\setminus\theset 0 \subseteq R\units$.
:::
