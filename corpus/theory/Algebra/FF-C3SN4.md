::: {.fact}
Up to isomorphism, the groups of order $9$ are $\ZZ/9\ZZ$ and $(\ZZ/3\ZZ)^2$.
Both are abelian.
:::

::: {.proof}
This is [[PR-IGLFV|the classification of groups of order $p^2$]] with $p=3$.
:::
