::: {.theorem}
Let $G$ be a finite group acting linearly on a finite-dimensional vector space $V$ over a field $k$ in which $\size G!$ is invertible.
Then the invariant ring $k[V]^G$ is generated as a $k$-algebra by its elements of degree at most $\size G$.

This is Noether's theorem: Cox--Little--O'Shea, *Ideals, Varieties, and Algorithms*, Chapter 7, §3, Theorem 5 states that $k[x_1,\ldots,x_n]^G$ is generated by the Reynolds images $R_G(x^\beta)$ with $\abs\beta\leq\size G$ over a field of characteristic zero; the proof divides by $\size G$ in the Reynolds operator and applies Newton's identities in degrees at most $\size G$, so it uses only that $\size G!$ is invertible.
:::
