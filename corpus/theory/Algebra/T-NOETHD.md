::: {.theorem}
Let $G$ be a finite group acting linearly on a finite-dimensional vector space $V$ over a field $k$ in which $\size G!$ is invertible.
Then the invariant ring $k[V]^G$ is generated as a $k$-algebra by its elements of degree at most $\size G$.
:::
