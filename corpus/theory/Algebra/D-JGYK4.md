::: {.definition}
Let $R$ be a commutative ring.
A proper [[D-GOFWL|ideal]] $I\subsetneq R$ is \dfn{primary} if for all $p,q\in R$ with $pq\in I$, either $p\in I$ or there exists $n\geq 1$ such that $q^n\in I$.
:::
