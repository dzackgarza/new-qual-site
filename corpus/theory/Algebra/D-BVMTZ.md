::: {.definition}
Let $k$ be a field and let $f\in k[x]$ be nonconstant.
The polynomial $f$ is \dfn{reducible} if there exist $g, h\in k[x]$ with $\deg g, \deg h \geq 1$ and $f = gh$.
The polynomial $f$ is \dfn{irreducible} if it is not reducible.
:::
