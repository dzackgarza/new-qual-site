::: {.definition}
Let $G$ be a group.
\dfn{Normalizers grow} in $G$ if for every proper subgroup $H < G$, the subgroup $H$ is a proper subgroup of its [[D-OZ2RR|normalizer]] $N_G(H)$.
:::
