::: {.definition}
Let $F$ be a field and $n\geq 1$.
The \dfn{orthogonal group} is
$$
\Orth_n(F) \coloneqq \theset{ A\in\Mat_{n\times n}(F) \st A^tA = AA^t = I }.
$$
:::

::: {.remark}
$\Orth_n(\CC)$ is a complex Lie group of dimension $n(n-1)/2$.
:::
