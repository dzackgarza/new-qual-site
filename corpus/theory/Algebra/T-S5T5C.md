::: {.theorem}
Let $G$ be a finite group and $p$ a prime, write $\abs G = p^a m$ with $p\nmid m$, let $n_p$ be the number of [[D-7TQ2M|Sylow $p$-subgroups]] of $G$, and let $S_p$ be one of them.
Then:

1. $n_p \divides m$, and in particular $n_p \leq m$.

2. $n_p \equiv 1 \pmod p$.

3. $n_p = [G : N_G(S_p)]$, where $N_G(S_p)$ is the [[D-OZ2RR|normalizer]] of $S_p$ in $G$.
:::
