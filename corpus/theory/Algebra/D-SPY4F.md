::: {.definition}
A group $G$ is \dfn{cyclic} if $G=\generators g$ for some $g\in G$, that is, if every element of $G$ is a power of the single element $g$; such an element $g$ is a \dfn{generator} of $G$.
:::

::: {.remark}
A generator of a cyclic group need not be unique, and a cyclic group is abelian because two powers $g^m$ and $g^n$ of one element commute.
:::
