:::{.definition}
Writing a cycle as a product of transpositions,
the map defined by
\[
\sgn: S_n &\to (\ZZ/2, +) \\
\prod_{i\leq 2k} (a_i b_i) &\mapsto 0 \\
\prod_{i\leq 2k+1} (a_i b_i) &\mapsto 1
.\]
:::
