::: {.fact}
A commutative ring $R$ is [[D-PQHHJ|reduced]] if and only if it has no nonzero [[D-GIGM2|nilpotent]] elements, that is, its [[D-C2IM4|nilradical]] $\nilrad{R}$ is $0$.
:::
