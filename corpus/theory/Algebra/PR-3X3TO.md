::: {.proposition}
Let $k$ be a field of [[D-JNCUB|characteristic]] $p > 0$.
For all $a, b \in k$,
$$
(a+b)^p = a^p + b^p \quad\text{and}\quad (ab)^p = a^p b^p.
$$
:::
