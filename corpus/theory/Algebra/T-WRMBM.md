::: {.theorem}
Let $G$ be a finite group and $p$ a prime, and write $\abs G=p^a m$ with $p\nmid m$.
Then $G$ has a subgroup of order $p^n$ for every $0\leq n\leq a$; in particular, $G$ has a [[D-7TQ2M|Sylow $p$-subgroup]], of order $p^a$.
Moreover, every $p$-subgroup of $G$ is contained in a Sylow $p$-subgroup [@DF04].
:::

::: {.remark}
For $a\geq1$ this strengthens [[T-3KCD6|Cauchy's theorem]], which gives a subgroup of order $p$.
:::
