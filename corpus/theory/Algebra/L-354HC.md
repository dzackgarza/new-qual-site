::: {.lemma}
Let $p$ be a prime, let $G$ be a finite [[D-FIB7S|$p$-group]], and let $G$ [[D-WYC7C|act]] on a finite set $X$.
Let $\Fix(G)\coloneqq\theset{x\in X\suchthat gx=x\text{ for all }g\in G}$.
Then
$$
\abs X\equiv\abs{\Fix(G)}\pmod p.
$$
:::

::: {.proof}
The orbits of $G$ partition $X$.
A point of $\Fix(G)$ is an orbit of size $1$.
Every other orbit $Gx$ has size $[G:G_x]>1$ by the orbit-stabilizer theorem, and this index divides $\abs G$, so it is a positive power of $p$.
Reducing $\abs X=\abs{\Fix(G)}+\sum\abs{Gx}$, the sum over the orbits of size greater than $1$, modulo $p$ gives the congruence.
:::
