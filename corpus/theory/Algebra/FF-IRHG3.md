::: {.fact}
Up to isomorphism there are fourteen groups of order $16$.
The five abelian ones are
$$
\ZZ/16\ZZ,\quad(\ZZ/4\ZZ)^2,\quad\ZZ/8\ZZ\times\ZZ/2\ZZ,\quad\ZZ/4\ZZ\times(\ZZ/2\ZZ)^2,\quad(\ZZ/2\ZZ)^4,
$$
and the nine nonabelian ones are

1. $(\ZZ/4\ZZ\times\ZZ/2\ZZ)\rtimes\ZZ/2\ZZ=\gens{a,b,c\suchthat a^4=b^2=c^2=1,\ ab=ba,\ cac^{-1}=ab,\ cbc^{-1}=b}$,

2. $\ZZ/4\ZZ\rtimes\ZZ/4\ZZ$, with a generator of the second factor acting by inversion,

3. the modular group $\ZZ/8\ZZ\rtimes\ZZ/2\ZZ=\gens{r,s\suchthat r^8=s^2=1,\ srs^{-1}=r^5}$,

4. the [[D-4R2Z5|dihedral group]] $D_8$ of order $16$,

5. $D_4\times\ZZ/2\ZZ$, with $D_4$ the dihedral group of order $8$,

6. $Q_8\times\ZZ/2\ZZ$, with $Q_8$ the [[D-KRKV7|quaternion group]],

7. the generalized quaternion group $Q_{16}=\gens{a,b,c\suchthat a^4=b^2=c^2=abc}$,

8. the semidihedral group $QD_{16}=\gens{r,s\suchthat r^8=s^2=1,\ srs^{-1}=r^3}$,

9. the Pauli group $(\ZZ/4\ZZ\times\ZZ/2\ZZ)\rtimes\ZZ/2\ZZ=\gens{a,b,c\suchthat a^4=b^2=c^2=1,\ ab=ba,\ cac^{-1}=a,\ cbc^{-1}=a^2b}$, generated by the Pauli matrices.
:::

::: {.remark}
The groups in items 1 and 9 are both semidirect products $(\ZZ/4\ZZ\times\ZZ/2\ZZ)\rtimes\ZZ/2\ZZ$ and are not isomorphic: the center of the group in item 1 is $\gens{a^2,b}\cong(\ZZ/2\ZZ)^2$, and the center of the Pauli group is $\gens{a}\cong\ZZ/4\ZZ$.
:::
