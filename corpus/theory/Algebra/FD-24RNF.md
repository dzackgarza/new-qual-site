::: {.definition}
Let $K$ be a [[D-UI6CU|field]].
The \dfn{field of rational functions} in the variable $x$ over $K$ is the field of fractions of the polynomial ring $K[x]$:
$$
K(x)\coloneqq\theset{\frac{P(x)}{Q(x)} \st P,Q \in K[x],\ Q\neq 0}.
$$
:::
