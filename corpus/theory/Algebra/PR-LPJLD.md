::: {.proposition}
Let $k$ be a field and $V, W$ finite-dimensional $k$-vector spaces.
The $k$-linear map
$$
\begin{aligned}
V\dual \tensor_k W &\to \Hom_k(V, W) \\
\tilde v \tensor w &\mapsto \big(x \mapsto \tilde v(x)\, w\big)
\end{aligned}
$$
is an isomorphism.
:::
