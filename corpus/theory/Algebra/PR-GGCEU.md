::: {.proposition}
Let $R$ be a ring, $S \subseteq R$ a subring, and $I \subseteq R$ a two-sided ideal.
Then $S + I$ is a subring of $R$, $S \intersect I$ is a two-sided ideal of $S$, and there is a ring isomorphism
$$
\frac{S+I}{I} \cong \frac{S}{S \intersect I}.
$$
:::
