::: {.proposition}
Let $p$ be a prime and let $m\divides n$.
Then the extension $\FF_{p^n}/\FF_{p^m}$ is [[D-5JYEI|Galois]], and $\Gal(\FF_{p^n}/\FF_{p^m})$ is cyclic of order $n/m$, generated by the automorphism $x\mapsto x^{p^m}$ [@DF04, §14.3].
:::

::: {.remark}
Every finite extension $L/F$ of a finite field $F$ has this form: if $\abs F=p^m$ and $[L:F]=d$, then $\abs L=p^{md}$.
In particular $\Gal(\FF_{p^n}/\FF_p)$ is cyclic of order $n$, generated by the Frobenius automorphism $x\mapsto x^p$.
:::
