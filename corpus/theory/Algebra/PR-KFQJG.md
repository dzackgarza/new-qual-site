::: {.proposition}
Let $k$ be a field and $E, F$ algebraic extensions of $k$ contained in a common field, with compositum $EF$.
Then $E/k$ and $F/k$ are [[D-JGYLA|separable]] if and only if $EF/k$ is separable.
:::
