::: {.proposition}
Let $R$ be a ring and $M, N$ left $R$-modules.
A map of sets $\phi\colon M \to N$ is an $R$-module homomorphism if and only if
$$
\phi(r x + y) = r\,\phi(x) + \phi(y) \quad \text{for all } r \in R \text{ and } x, y \in M.
$$
:::
