::: {.definition}
Let $G$ be a group and $S\subseteq G$ a subset.
The \dfn{centralizer} of $S$ in $G$ is
$$
Z_G(S) \coloneqq \theset{g\in G \st gsg^{-1} = s \text{ for every } s\in S}.
$$
:::
