::: {.theorem}
Let $G$ be a finite group and $p$ a prime.
Any two [[D-7TQ2M|Sylow $p$-subgroups]] of $G$ are conjugate: if $P,Q\in\Syl_p(G)$, then there exists $g\in G$ with $gPg^{-1}=Q$.
:::
