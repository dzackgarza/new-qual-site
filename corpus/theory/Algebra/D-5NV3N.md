::: {.definition}
Let $G$ be a group with identity $e$.
A \dfn{central series} of $G$ is a finite chain of subgroups
$$
G = G^0 \geq G^1 \geq \cdots \geq G^n = \theset{e}
$$
with each $G^i \normal G$ and $[G, G^i] \leq G^{i+1}$ for $0\leq i < n$, where $[G, G^i]$ is the subgroup generated by the [[D-BQ4BQ|commutators]] $ghg^{-1}h^{-1}$ with $g\in G$ and $h\in G^i$.
:::

::: {.remark}
For subgroups $G^{i+1}\leq G^i$ normal in $G$, the condition $[G, G^i] \leq G^{i+1}$ holds if and only if $G^i/G^{i+1}$ is contained in the [[D-NK7G7|center]] of $G/G^{i+1}$.
:::
