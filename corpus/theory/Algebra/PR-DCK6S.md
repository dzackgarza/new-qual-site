::: {.proposition}
Let $m \geq 1$ and let $\zeta_m$ be a [[FD-HM4P2|primitive $m$th root of unity]] in $\CC$.
The splitting field of $x^m - 1$ over $\QQ$ is the [[D-IPR4B|cyclotomic field]] $\QQ(\zeta_m)$, and
$$
\Gal(\QQ(\zeta_m)/\QQ) \cong (\ZZ/m\ZZ)\units.
$$
:::
