::: {.definition}
Let $R$ be a commutative [[D-GURUB|ring]].
An element $p\in R$ is \dfn{irreducible} if $p$ is nonzero, $p\notin R^\times$, and whenever $p=ab$ with $a,b\in R$, $a \in R^\times$ or $b\in R^\times$.
:::
