::: {.definition}
Let $R$ be a commutative ring.
The \dfn{nilradical} of $R$ is the set of [[D-GIGM2|nilpotent]] elements of $R$:
$$
\nilrad{R} \coloneqq \theset{x\in R \suchthat x \text{ is nilpotent}}.
$$
:::
