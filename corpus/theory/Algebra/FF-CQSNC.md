::: {.proposition}
Let $(R,\mfm)$ be a Noetherian [[D-TGB4R|local ring]] with residue field $k=R/\mfm$.
The minimal number of generators of $\mfm$ is
$$
\dim_k\mfm/\mfm^2.
$$
Consequently, by [[D-DEFREGLR]], $R$ is regular if and only if
$$
\dim_k\mfm/\mfm^2=\dim R.
$$
:::

::: {.proof}
By [[FF-45SK3|Nakayama's lemma for a local ring]], a family in $\mfm$ generates $\mfm$ minimally exactly when its image is a basis of the $k$-vector space $\mfm/\mfm^2$.
:::

::: {.remark}
By Krull's height theorem, the extension of [[PR-VARHT|Krull's principal ideal theorem]] to ideals generated by $r$ elements, $\dim R\le\dim_k\mfm/\mfm^2$ for every Noetherian local ring $(R,\mfm)$; regularity is the case of equality.
:::
