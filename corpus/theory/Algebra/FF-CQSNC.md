::: {.fact}
A Noetherian [[D-TGB4R|local ring]] $(R,\mfm)$ with residue field $k=R/\mfm$ is \dfn{regular} if $\mfm$ can be generated by $\dim R$ elements, where $\dim R$ is the Krull dimension of $R$.
By [[FF-45SK3|Nakayama's lemma for a local ring]], the minimal number of generators of $\mfm$ is $\dim_k\mfm/\mfm^2$, so $R$ is regular if and only if $\dim_k\mfm/\mfm^2=\dim R$.

A commutative Noetherian ring $R$ is \dfn{regular} if the localization $R_{\mfp}$ is a regular local ring for every prime ideal $\mfp\subseteq R$.
:::

::: {.remark}
By Krull's height theorem, the extension of [[FF-ESIOA|Krull's Hauptidealsatz]] to ideals generated by $r$ elements, $\dim R\le\dim_k\mfm/\mfm^2$ for every Noetherian local ring $(R,\mfm)$; regularity is the case of equality.
:::
