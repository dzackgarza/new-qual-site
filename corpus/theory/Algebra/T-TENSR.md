::: {.theorem}
Let $m, n\geq 1$.
Then
$$
\ZZ/m\ZZ\tensor_\ZZ\ZZ/n\ZZ\cong\ZZ/\gcd(m,n)\ZZ.
$$
In particular $\ZZ/m\ZZ\tensor_\ZZ\ZZ/n\ZZ=0$ when $\gcd(m,n)=1$.
:::

::: {.proof}
Tensoring the exact sequence $\ZZ\mapsvia{n}\ZZ\to\ZZ/n\ZZ\to0$ with $\ZZ/m\ZZ$ gives, by right exactness of $-\tensor_\ZZ\ZZ/m\ZZ$, the exact sequence
$$
\ZZ/m\ZZ\mapsvia{n}\ZZ/m\ZZ\to\ZZ/n\ZZ\tensor_\ZZ\ZZ/m\ZZ\to0,
$$
so $\ZZ/n\ZZ\tensor_\ZZ\ZZ/m\ZZ\cong(\ZZ/m\ZZ)/n(\ZZ/m\ZZ)$.
The image of multiplication by $n$ on $\ZZ/m\ZZ$ is generated by the class of $\gcd(m,n)$, hence is $\gcd(m,n)\ZZ/m\ZZ$, and the quotient is $\ZZ/\gcd(m,n)\ZZ$.
:::
