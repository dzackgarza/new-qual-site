::: {.definition}
Let $F$ be a field, $K\subseteq F$ a subfield, and $\alpha\in F$.
The \dfn{subring generated by $K$ and $\alpha$}, written $K[\alpha]$, is the smallest subring of $F$ containing $K$ and $\alpha$, that is, the intersection of all subrings of $F$ that contain $K\cup\theset{\alpha}$.
:::

::: {.remark}
$K[\alpha]=\theset{g(\alpha) \st g\in K[x]}$, the image of the evaluation homomorphism $K[x]\to F$, $g\mapsto g(\alpha)$: this image is a subring containing $K$ and $\alpha$, and every such subring contains each $g(\alpha)$.
:::
