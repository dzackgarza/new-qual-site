::: {.definition}
Let $R$ be a commutative [[D-GURUB|ring]].
A nonzero element $r\in R$ that is not a [[D-QQIQZ|unit]] is \dfn{irreducible} if for all $a,b\in R$,
$$
r=ab \implies a \in R^{\times} \text{ or }  b\in R^{\times}.
$$
:::
