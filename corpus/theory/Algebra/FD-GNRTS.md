::: {.definition}
Let $R$ be a [[D-GURUB|ring]].
An $R$-module $M$ is \dfn{Noetherian} if there is no infinite strictly increasing sequence $M_1 \subsetneq M_2 \subsetneq \cdots$ of submodules of $M$.
:::
