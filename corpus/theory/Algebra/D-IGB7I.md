::: {.definition}
Let $R$ be a nonzero commutative ring and let $M$ be a [[D-I7D56|free]] $R$-module.
The \dfn{free rank} of $M$ is the cardinality of a [[D-I7D56|basis]] of $M$.
:::

::: {.remark}
The free rank is well defined: over a nonzero commutative ring, any two bases of a free module have the same cardinality.
For a maximal ideal $\mathfrak m\subset R$, a basis $B$ of $M$ maps bijectively onto a basis of the $R/\mathfrak m$-vector space $M/\mathfrak mM$, so $\abs{B}=\dim_{R/\mathfrak m}M/\mathfrak mM$.
:::
