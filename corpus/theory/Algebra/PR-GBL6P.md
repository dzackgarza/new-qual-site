::: {.proposition}
Let $k$ be a field and $A \in \Mat_{n\times n}(k)$ with $n \geq 1$, and let $\min_A(x)$ be the [[D-GK5SF|minimal polynomial]] of $A$.
Then $\min_A(x) = \det(xI - A)$ if and only if $A$ admits a cyclic vector: some $v \in k^n$ such that $v, Av, A^2v, \ldots$ span $k^n$.
:::
