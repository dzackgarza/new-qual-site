::: {.definition}
A commutative ring $R$ is \dfn{regular} if $R$ is Noetherian and for every prime ideal $\mfp\in\Spec R$ the localization $R_{\mfp}$ is a [[D-DEFREGLR|regular local ring]]: its maximal ideal $\mfp R_{\mfp}$ is generated by $\dim R_{\mfp}$ elements, where $\dim$ denotes Krull dimension.
:::
