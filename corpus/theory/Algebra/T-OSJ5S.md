::: {.theorem}
Let $G$ be a group, and let
$$
G=G_0 \trianglerighteq G_1 \trianglerighteq \cdots \trianglerighteq G_r=\theset{e}
\quad\text{and}\quad
G=H_0 \trianglerighteq H_1 \trianglerighteq \cdots \trianglerighteq H_s=\theset{e}
$$
be two [[D-FYX4I|composition series]] of $G$.
Then $r=s$, and there is a permutation $\pi$ of $\theset{1,\ldots,r}$ such that $G_{i-1}/G_i\cong H_{\pi(i)-1}/H_{\pi(i)}$ for every $i$.
:::
