::: {.proposition}
Let $k$ be a field and $V, W$ finite-dimensional $k$-vector spaces.
The map
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Hom_k(V, W) &\to \Hom_k(W, V)\dual \\
T &\mapsto \big(S \mapsto \Tr(T \circ S)\big)
\end{aligned}
$$
is an isomorphism of $k$-vector spaces, where $T \circ S\colon W \to W$.
:::
