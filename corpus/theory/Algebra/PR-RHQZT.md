::: {.proposition}
Let $R$ be a [[D-GURUB|ring]] and $I\subseteq R$ an [[D-GOFWL|ideal]].
Then $I\neq R$ if and only if $I$ contains no [[D-QQIQZ|unit]] of $R$.
:::
