::: {.definition}
Let $G$ be a group.
Set $G^{(0)} \coloneqq G$ and $G^{(i+1)} \coloneqq [G^{(i)}, G^{(i)}]$, the [[D-BQ4BQ|commutator subgroup]] of $G^{(i)}$, for $i\geq 0$.
The \dfn{derived series} of $G$ is the chain $G^{(0)} \geq G^{(1)} \geq G^{(2)} \geq \cdots$.
:::
