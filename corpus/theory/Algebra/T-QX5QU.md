::: {.theorem}
Let $K\subseteq\CC$ be a finite [[D-5JYEI|Galois extension]] of $\QQ$ with $\Gal(K/\QQ)$ abelian.
Then there is an integer $m\geq 1$ such that $K \subseteq \QQ(\zeta_m)$, where $\zeta_m\coloneqq e^{2\pi i/m}$.
:::
