::: {.definition}
Let $R$ be a commutative ring.
The \dfn{prime spectrum}, or \dfn{spectrum}, of $R$ is the set of [[D-5BM46|prime ideals]] of $R$:
$$
\Spec(R) \coloneqq \theset{\mfp \normal R \suchthat \mfp \text{ is prime}}.
$$
:::
