::: {.definition}
Let $n\geq 1$, and for $A\in\Mat_{n\times n}(\CC)$ let $A^\dagger$ be its conjugate transpose.
The \dfn{unitary group} is
$$
\U_n(\CC) \coloneqq \theset{ A\in\Mat_{n\times n}(\CC) \st A^\dagger A = AA^\dagger = I_n }.
$$
:::
