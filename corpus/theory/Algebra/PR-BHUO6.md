::: {.proposition}
Let $k$ be a field and let $V, W$ be $k$-vector spaces, at least one of which is finite-dimensional.
The $k$-linear map
$$
\begin{aligned}
V\dual \tensor_k W\dual &\to (V \tensor_k W)\dual \\
v \tensor w &\mapsto \big(x \tensor y \mapsto v(x)\, w(y)\big)
\end{aligned}
$$
is an isomorphism.
:::
