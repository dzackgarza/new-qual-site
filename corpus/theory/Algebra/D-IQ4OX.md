::: {.definition}
Let $G$ be a group with identity $e$.
A subset $H\subseteq G$ is a \dfn{subgroup} of $G$ if

1. $gh\in H$ for all $g,h\in H$;

2. $e\in H$;

3. $g^{-1} \in H$ for all $g\in H$.
:::
