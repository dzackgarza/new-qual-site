::: {.theorem}
Let $k$ be a field.

- If $k$ is perfect, then every finite [[D-LZTAK|normal]] extension of $k$ is [[D-5JYEI|Galois]].

- If $k$ is perfect, then the splitting field over $k$ of every nonconstant polynomial $f\in k[x]$ is Galois over $k$.
:::
