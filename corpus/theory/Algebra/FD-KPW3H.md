::: {.definition}
Let $K$ be a [[D-UI6CU|field]].
A polynomial $p\in K[x]$ is \dfn{irreducible} if $p$ is nonconstant and there are no $q,r\in K[x]$ with $p=qr$, $\deg q<\deg p$, and $\deg r<\deg p$.
:::
