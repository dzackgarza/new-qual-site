::: {.theorem}
Let $R$ be a [[D-TZXBO|Noetherian]] ring and $I\subsetneq R$ a proper ideal.
Then $I$ is a finite intersection of [[D-JGYK4|primary ideals]] [@DF04, Theorem 21, §15.2].
:::
