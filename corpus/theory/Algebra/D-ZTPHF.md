::: {.definition}
Let $G$ be a group with identity $e$ and $g\in G$.
If $g^n=e$ for some integer $n\geq 1$, the \dfn{order} of $g$, written $o(g)$, is the smallest such $n$.
If no such $n$ exists, $g$ has \dfn{infinite order}.
:::
