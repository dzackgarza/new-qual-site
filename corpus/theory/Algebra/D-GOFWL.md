::: {.definition}
Let $R$ be a commutative ring.
An \dfn{ideal} $I\normal R$ is a subset $I\subseteq R$ such that $(I, +)$ is a subgroup of $(R, +)$ and $ri \in I$ for all $r\in R$ and $i\in I$.
:::

::: {.proposition}
Let $R$ be a commutative ring.
A nonempty subset $I\subseteq R$ is an ideal if and only if $I + I \subseteq I$ and $RI \subseteq I$.
:::
