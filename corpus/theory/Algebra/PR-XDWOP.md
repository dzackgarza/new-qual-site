::: {.proposition}
Let $f\in\ZZ[x]$ be monic of degree $n$ with nonzero [[D-W3DSO|discriminant]] $\Delta$, and let $G$ be the Galois group of $f$ over $\QQ$, regarded as a subgroup of $S_n$ through its action on the roots of $f$.
Let $p$ be a prime with $p\notdivides\Delta$, and let $f\equiv f_1f_2\cdots f_m\pmod p$ be the factorization into monic irreducibles in $\FF_p[x]$.
Then $G$ contains a permutation of cycle type $(\deg f_1, \deg f_2, \ldots, \deg f_m)$.
Equivalently, the Galois group of $f\bmod p$ over $\FF_p$, which is cyclic and generated by the Frobenius automorphism, is isomorphic to a subgroup of $G$ through a bijection between the roots of $f\bmod p$ and the roots of $f$.
:::

::: {.remark}
The Chebotarev density theorem strengthens this: for each cycle type occurring in $G$, the set of primes $p\notdivides\Delta$ for which $f\bmod p$ factors with that cycle type has natural density equal to the proportion of elements of $G$ of that cycle type.
:::
