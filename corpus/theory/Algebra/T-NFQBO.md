::: {.theorem}
Let $G$ be a group, $S \leq G$ a subgroup, and $N \normal G$ a [[D-EKE4Q|normal subgroup]].
Then
$$
\frac{SN}{N} \cong \frac{S}{S\intersect N}.
$$
If $S$ and $N$ are finite, then
$$
\abs{SN} = \frac{\abs S \abs N}{\abs{S\intersect N}}.
$$
