::: {.definition}
Let $G$ be a group and $H\leq G$ a subgroup.
The \dfn{normal closure} of $H$ in $G$ is the [[D-D36IY|subgroup generated by]] the conjugates of $H$:
$$
H^G \coloneqq \gens{ \Union_{g\in G} gHg^{-1} }.
$$
:::

::: {.proposition}
Let $G$ be a group and $H\leq G$.
Then $H^G$ is the smallest [[D-EKE4Q|normal subgroup]] of $G$ containing $H$:
$$
H^G = \bigcap \theset{N \st H \leq N \normal G }.
$$
:::
