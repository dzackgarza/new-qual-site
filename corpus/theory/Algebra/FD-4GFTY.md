::: {.definition}
Let $K/F$ be a finite field extension, and let $\Aut(K/F)$ be the group of [[D-MN47W|field automorphisms]] of $K$ that restrict to the identity on $F$.
$K/F$ is \dfn{Galois} if $\abs{\Aut(K/F)} = [K: F]$.
:::
