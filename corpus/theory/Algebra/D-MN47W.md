::: {.definition}
Let $L/k$ be a field extension.
The \dfn{automorphism group} of $L/k$ is the group, under composition,
$$
\Aut(L/k) \coloneqq \theset{\sigma\colon L \to L \text{ a field automorphism} \suchthat \restrictionof{\sigma}{k} = \id_k}.
$$
:::
