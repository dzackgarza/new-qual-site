::: {.definition}
Let $G$ be a group.
For $a, b\in G$, the \dfn{commutator} of $a$ and $b$ is $[a,b] \coloneqq aba^{-1}b^{-1}$.
The \dfn{commutator subgroup} of $G$ is the subgroup generated by all commutators:
$$
[G, G] \coloneqq \gens{ aba^{-1} b^{-1} \st a, b\in G }.
$$
:::

::: {.proposition}
Let $G$ be a group.
Then $[G,G]$ is a [[D-EKE4Q|normal subgroup]] of $G$ and $G/[G,G]$ is abelian.
If $N\normal G$ and $G/N$ is abelian, then $[G, G]\leq N$.
:::

::: {.remark}
An element of $[G, G]$ is a product of commutators, and need not itself be a commutator.
:::
