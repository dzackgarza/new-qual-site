::: {.definition}
Let $F$ be a [[D-UI6CU|field]] and $n\geq 1$ an integer.
An element $\zeta\in F$ is a \dfn{primitive $n$th root of unity} if $\zeta^n = 1$ and $\zeta^m\neq 1$ for $1\leq m<n$, that is, $\zeta$ has [[D-ZTPHF|order]] $n$ in $F^\times$.
:::
