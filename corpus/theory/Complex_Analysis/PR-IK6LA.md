::: {.proposition}
The map
$$
\begin{aligned}
F\colon \theset{z\in\CC : \abs{z}>1} &\to \CC\sm[-2, 2], \\
z &\mapsto z+ \inverseof{z}
\end{aligned}
$$
is a [[D-TM4TE|biholomorphism]].
