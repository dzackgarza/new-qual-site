::: {.definition}
For $z,w\in\CC$ with $\operatorname{Re}z>0$ and $\operatorname{Re}w>0$, the \dfn{beta function} is
$$
B(z,w)\coloneqq\int_0^1 t^{z-1}(1-t)^{w-1}\dt,
$$
where $t^{s}\coloneqq e^{s\log t}$ for $t>0$, with $\log$ the real natural logarithm.
:::
