::: {.theorem}
The group $\Aut_\CC(\DD)$ of [[D-TM4TE|biholomorphisms]] $\DD\to\DD$ is
$$
\Aut_\CC(\DD)=\Bigl\{z\mapsto e^{i\theta}\,\frac{\alpha-z}{1-\overline{\alpha}z} : \theta\in\RR,\ \alpha\in\DD\Bigr\}.
$$
:::
