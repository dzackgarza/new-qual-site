::: {.proposition}
Let $f$ be [[D-E7A5W|holomorphic]] on a punctured disc about $z_0$ with a simple [[D-AUD6K|pole]] at $z_0$.
Then its [[D-C3JIU|residue]] at $z_0$ is
$$
\Res_{z=z_0}f = \lim_{z\to z_0} (z-z_0) f(z).
$$
:::
