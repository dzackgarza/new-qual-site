::: {.theorem}
Let $\Omega\subseteq\CC$ be a bounded open set whose boundary $\bd\Omega$ is a finite union of piecewise smooth closed curves, oriented positively (with $\Omega$ on the left), and let $f,g\in C^1(\overline\Omega)$ be real-valued.
Then
$$
\int_{\bd\Omega}f\dx+g\dy=\iint_\Omega\Big(\frac{\partial g}{\partial x}-\frac{\partial f}{\partial y}\Big)\dA.
$$
In vector form, for the vector field $F=(f,g)$ with scalar curl $\curl F=g_x-f_y$,
$$
\int_{\bd\Omega}F\cdot\dr=\iint_\Omega\curl F\dA.
$$
:::

::: {.example}
Taking $(f,g)=(0,x)$, $(-y,0)$, and $\big(-\frac y2,\frac x2\big)$, each with $g_x-f_y=1$, gives the area of $\Omega$:
$$
\mu(\Omega)=\oint_{\bd\Omega}x\dy=-\oint_{\bd\Omega}y\dx=\frac12\oint_{\bd\Omega}(x\dy-y\dx).
$$
:::

::: {.remark}
For $f$ [[D-E7A5W|holomorphic]] and injective on a neighborhood of $\overline\Omega$, the real Jacobian determinant of $f$ is $\abs{f'(z)}^2$, so the area of the image is $\mu(f(\Omega))=\iint_\Omega\abs{f'(z)}^2\dA$.
:::
