::: {.theorem}
Let $S$ be a connected Riemann surface with universal cover $\widetilde S\to S$.
Then $\widetilde S$ is a simply connected Riemann surface, and its group $G$ of [[D-4VGAW|deck transformations]] acts on $\widetilde S$ freely and properly discontinuously by biholomorphisms, with $S$ biholomorphic to $\widetilde S/G$.
Moreover, $\widetilde S$ is biholomorphic to exactly one of $\CP^1$, $\CC$, and $\DD$.

The classification is Theorem 2.1 of §2 of C. McMullen, *Advanced Complex Analysis* (Harvard Math 213a course notes, 2017; `assets/attachments/McMullen_-_Advanced_Complex_Analysis.pdf`): every simply connected Riemann surface is isomorphic to $\HH$, $\CC$ or $\CP^1$. The covering-space statements above are the standard theory of the universal cover, and the three models are pairwise non-isomorphic — $\CP^1$ is compact, and a biholomorphism $\CC\to\DD$ would be a bounded entire function.
:::
