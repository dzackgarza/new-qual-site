::: {.theorem}
Let $S$ be a connected Riemann surface with universal cover $\widetilde S\to S$.
Then $\widetilde S$ is a simply connected Riemann surface, and its group $G$ of [[D-4VGAW|deck transformations]] acts on $\widetilde S$ freely and properly discontinuously by biholomorphisms, with $S$ biholomorphic to $\widetilde S/G$.
Moreover, $\widetilde S$ is biholomorphic to exactly one of $\CP^1$, $\CC$, and $\DD$.

The classification is Theorem 2.1 of §2 of C. McMullen, *Advanced Complex Analysis*, which adds that an arbitrary Riemann surface satisfies $X\cong\CP^1$, $X=\CC/\Gamma$ or $X=\widehat\CC/\Gamma$ for a group $\Gamma$ of automorphisms. The quotient and deck-transformation statements are the standard theory of the universal cover [@Hat02, §1.3]. The three models are pairwise non-isomorphic: $\CP^1$ is compact, and a biholomorphism $\CC\to\DD$ would be a bounded entire function.
:::
