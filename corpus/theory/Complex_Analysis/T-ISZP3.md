::: {.theorem}
Let $\Omega\subseteq\CC$ be open, let $z_0\in\Omega$, and let $f$ be [[D-E7A5W|holomorphic]] on $\Omega\setminus\{z_0\}$ and bounded on $\Omega\setminus\{z_0\}$.
Then $z_0$ is a [[D-BQLJV|removable singularity]] of $f$: $f$ extends to a holomorphic function on $\Omega$.
:::
