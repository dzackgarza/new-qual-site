::: {.theorem ref="CauchyTheorem"}
Let $\Omega\subseteq\CC$ be a simply connected region and let $f$ be [[D-E7A5W|holomorphic]] on $\Omega$.
Then for every closed piecewise $C^1$ curve $\gamma$ in $\Omega$,
$$
\int_\gamma f(z)\dz=0.
$$
:::
