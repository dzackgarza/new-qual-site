::: {.proposition}
For $0<\alpha\le2\pi$, let $S_\alpha\coloneqq\theset{ z \st 0 < \arg(z) < \alpha }$, with $\arg z\in(0,\alpha)$ as in [[PR-AQFRA]].
Then
$$
\begin{aligned}
F\colon S_{\alpha} &\to \DD, \\
z &\mapsto {z^{\pi/\alpha} - i \over z^{\pi/\alpha} + i}
\end{aligned}
$$
is a [[D-TM4TE|biholomorphism]].
