::: {.theorem}
The fractional linear transformation
\[
G(w)=i\frac{1-w}{1+w}
\]
maps the unit disk $\DD$ biholomorphically onto the upper half-plane $\HH$.
Its inverse is
\[
F(z)=\frac{i-z}{i+z},
\]
which maps $\HH$ biholomorphically onto $\DD$.
:::
