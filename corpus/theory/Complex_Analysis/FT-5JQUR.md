::: {.theorem}
Let $\Omega\subseteq\CC$ be a simply connected open set, let $f$ be [[D-E7A5W|holomorphic]] on $\Omega$, and let $\gamma$ be a closed piecewise smooth curve in $\Omega$.
Then
$$
\int_\gamma f(z)\dz = 0.
$$
:::
