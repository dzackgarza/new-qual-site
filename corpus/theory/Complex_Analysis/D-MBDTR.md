::: {.definition}
Let $\Omega\subseteq\CC$ be open and let $\mathcal F$ be a family of [[D-E7A5W|holomorphic]] functions on $\Omega$.
The family $\mathcal F$ is \dfn{uniformly bounded on compact subsets} of $\Omega$ if for every [[D-EILKJ|compact]] set $K\subseteq\Omega$ there exists $M>0$ such that
$$
\abs{f(z)}\le M\qquad\text{for all } z\in K \text{ and all } f\in\mathcal F.
$$
:::
