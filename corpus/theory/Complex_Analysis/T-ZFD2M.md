::: {.theorem}
Let $\SL_2(\ZZ)$ act on $\HH$ by $z\mapsto(az+b)/(cz+d)$, and let
$$
F\coloneqq\theset{z\in\HH : \abs z\geq1,\ \abs{\Re z}\leq\tfrac12}.
$$
Every orbit of $\SL_2(\ZZ)$ meets $F$, and two distinct points of $F$ are congruent under $\SL_2(\ZZ)$ exactly when they are related by $z\mapsto z+1$ across the sides $\Re z=\pm\frac12$, or by $z\mapsto-1/z$ across the arc $\abs z=1$.

This is [@SS03, ch. 10, Problem 2(a)--(b)], where $F$ is called the fundamental domain of the group generated by $\tau\mapsto\tau+1$ and $\tau\mapsto-1/\tau$; part (c) of the same problem is that these two generators generate $\SL_2(\ZZ)$ modulo $\theset{\pm I}$.
:::
