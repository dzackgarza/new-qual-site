::: {.definition}
Let $\Omega\subseteq\CC$ be open.
A function $f\colon\Omega\to\CC$ is \dfn{univalent} if $f$ is [[D-E7A5W|holomorphic]] and injective.
:::

::: {.remark}
A univalent function has nonvanishing derivative and is a biholomorphism onto its open image ([[P-Z776N]]); conversely, every biholomorphism onto an open subset of $\CC$ is univalent.
:::
