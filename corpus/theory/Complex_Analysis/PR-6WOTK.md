::: {.proposition}
Let $D\subseteq\CC$ be a connected open set, let $u\colon D\to\RR$ be [[D-CFBSA|harmonic]], and let $M\in\RR$ satisfy $u(z) \leq M$ for all $z \in D$.
If $u(z_0)=M$ for some $z_0 \in D$, then $u(z)=M$ for all $z \in D$.
:::
