::: {.corollary}
Let $g$ and $h$ be [[D-E7A5W|holomorphic]] near $z_0$, with $h(z_0)=0$ and $h'(z_0)\neq0$.
Then
$$
\Res_{z=z_0}\frac{g(z)}{h(z)}=\frac{g(z_0)}{h'(z_0)}.
$$
In particular, $\Res_{z=z_0}\frac{1}{h(z)}=\frac{1}{h'(z_0)}$.
:::
