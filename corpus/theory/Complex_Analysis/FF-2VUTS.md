::: {.fact}
$\arctan\qty{\frac{\sqrt3}{3}}=\frac{\pi}{6}$, because $\tan\frac{\pi}{6}=\frac{1/2}{\sqrt3/2}=\frac{1}{\sqrt3}=\frac{\sqrt3}{3}$ and $\frac{\pi}{6}\in\qty{-\frac{\pi}{2},\frac{\pi}{2}}$.
:::
