::: {.definition}
For $z\in\CC$ with $\operatorname{Re}z>0$, the \dfn{gamma function} is
$$
\Gamma(z)\coloneqq\int_0^\infty t^{z-1}e^{-t}\dt,
$$
where $t^{z-1}\coloneqq e^{(z-1)\log t}$ for $t>0$, with $\log$ the real natural logarithm.
:::
