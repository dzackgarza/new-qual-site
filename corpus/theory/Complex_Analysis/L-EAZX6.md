:::{.lemma}
Given two sequences of real numbers \( \ts{ a_k } , \ts{ b_k } \) which satisfy

1. The sequence of partial sums \( \ts{ A_n } \) is bounded,
2. $b_k \searrow 0$.

then 
\[
\sum_{k\geq 1} a_k b_k < \infty
.\]
:::
