::: {.theorem}
Let $\Omega\subseteq\CC$ be a connected open set and let $f\colon \Omega \to \CC$ be [[D-E7A5W|holomorphic]] and nonconstant.
Then $\abs{f}$ attains no local maximum in $\Omega$.
:::
