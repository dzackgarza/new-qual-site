::: {.definition}
Let $\Omega\subseteq\CC$ be open.
A family $\mathcal F=\{f_j\}_{j\in J}$ of [[D-E7A5W|holomorphic]] functions on $\Omega$ is \dfn{normal} if every sequence in $\mathcal F$ has a subsequence that [[D-AIQG3|converges locally uniformly]] on $\Omega$.
:::
