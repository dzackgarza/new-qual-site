::: {.fact}
$\arctan(\sqrt 3)=\frac{\pi}{3}$, because $\tan\frac{\pi}{3}=\frac{\sqrt3/2}{1/2}=\sqrt3$ and $\frac{\pi}{3}\in\qty{-\frac{\pi}{2},\frac{\pi}{2}}$.
:::
