::: {.theorem}
Let $\Omega\subseteq\CC$ be a connected open set and let $(f_k)_{k\geq1}$ be [[D-OHFRH|univalent]] functions on $\Omega$ converging [[D-AIQG3|locally uniformly]] on $\Omega$ to $f$.
Then $f$ is either univalent or constant.
:::
