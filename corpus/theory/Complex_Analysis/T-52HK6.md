::: {.theorem}
Let $\Omega\subseteq\CC$ be open, let $f$ be [[D-7DFVJ|meromorphic]] on $\Omega$, and let $\gamma\colon[a,b]\to\Omega$ be a closed piecewise $C^1$ curve that meets no zero and no pole of $f$.
Then the integral of the [[D-WHYOA|logarithmic derivative]] of $f$ along $\gamma$ is the [[D-PJ7JM|winding number]] of the closed curve $f\circ\gamma$ about $0$:
$$
\frac{1}{2\pi i}\int_\gamma\frac{f'(z)}{f(z)}\dz=\Index_{w=0}(f\circ\gamma)=\frac{1}{2\pi i}\int_{f\circ\gamma}\frac{dw}{w}.
$$
:::

::: {.proof}
The curve $f\circ\gamma$ is closed, piecewise $C^1$, and avoids $0$. Substituting $w=f(\gamma(t))$, $dw=f'(\gamma(t))\gamma'(t)\dt$,
$$
\int_\gamma\frac{f'(z)}{f(z)}\dz=\int_a^b\frac{f'(\gamma(t))\gamma'(t)}{f(\gamma(t))}\dt=\int_{f\circ\gamma}\frac{dw}{w}.
$$
:::
