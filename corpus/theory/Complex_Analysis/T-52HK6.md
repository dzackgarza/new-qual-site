::: {.theorem}
Let $\Omega\subseteq\CC$ be open, let $f$ be [[D-7DFVJ|meromorphic]] on $\Omega$, and let $\gamma\colon[a,b]\to\Omega$ be a closed piecewise $C^1$ curve that meets no zero and no pole of $f$.
Then the integral of the [[D-IA5FS|logarithmic derivative]] of $f$ along $\gamma$ is the [[D-PJ7JM|winding number]] of the closed curve $f\circ\gamma$ about $0$:
$$
\frac{1}{2\pi i}\int_\gamma\frac{f'(z)}{f(z)}\dz=n(f\circ\gamma,0)=\frac{1}{2\pi i}\int_{f\circ\gamma}\frac{dw}{w}.
$$

In the form that counts zeros and poles, this is [@SS03, ch. 3, Theorem 4.1 and Corollary 4.2].
:::
