::: {.theorem ref="Liouville"}
Let $f$ be an [[D-E7A5W|entire]] function.
If there is $M>0$ with $\abs{f(z)}\leq M$ for all $z\in\CC$, then $f$ is constant [@SS03, ch. 2, Corollary 4.5].
:::
