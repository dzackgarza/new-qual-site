::: {.proposition}
The [[D-Q3MYK|Gamma function]], defined by $\Gamma(s)=\int_0^\infty t^{s-1}e^{-t}\dt$ for $\Re s>0$, extends to a [[D-7DFVJ|meromorphic]] function on $\CC$ whose only singularities are simple [[D-AUD6K|poles]] at $s=0,-1,-2,\ldots$.
:::
