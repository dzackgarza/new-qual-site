::: {.theorem}
Let $n\geq1$ and $K\subseteq\RR^n$.
Then $K$ is closed and bounded if and only if every sequence in $K$ has a subsequence converging to a point of $K$.
In particular, every bounded sequence in $\RR^n$ has a convergent subsequence.

This is [@Rud76, ch. 2, Theorem 2.41] together with [@Rud76, ch. 3, Theorem 3.6(a)]: 2.41 identifies closed and bounded with compactness, and 3.6(a) turns compactness into the subsequence property. The last sentence is [@Rud76, ch. 3, Theorem 3.6(b)].
:::
