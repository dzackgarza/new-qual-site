::: {.theorem}
Let $D\subseteq\CC$ be an open disc and let $f\colon D\to\CC$ be continuous.
If $\int_{\bd T} f(z)\dz = 0$ for every closed triangle $T\subset D$, then $f$ is [[D-E7A5W|holomorphic]] on $D$.
:::
