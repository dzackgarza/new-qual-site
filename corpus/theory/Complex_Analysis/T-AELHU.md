::: {.theorem}
If $f\colon\CC\to\CC$ is [[D-E7A5W|entire]] and nonconstant, then its image $f(\CC)$ is either $\CC$ or $\CC\sm\ts{z_0}$ for some $z_0\in\CC$.
:::
