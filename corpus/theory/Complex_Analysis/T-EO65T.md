::: {.theorem ref="Casorati"}
Let $\Omega\subseteq\CC$ be open, let $z_0\in\Omega$, and let $f$ be [[D-E7A5W|holomorphic]] on $\Omega\setminus\{z_0\}$ with an [[D-VKP6N|essential singularity]] at $z_0$.
Then for every $\delta>0$ with $\{z : 0<\abs{z-z_0}<\delta\}\subseteq\Omega$, the image $f(\{z : 0<\abs{z-z_0}<\delta\})$ is dense in $\CC$.
:::
