::: {.definition}
Let $\Omega\subseteq\CC$ be open and let $\mathcal F\coloneqq\{f_j\}_{j\in J}$ be a family of functions $\Omega\to\CC$.
The family $\mathcal F$ is \dfn{normal} if every sequence $(f_k)_{k\ge1}$ in $\mathcal F$ has a subsequence $(f_{k_i})_{i\ge1}$ that [[D-AIQG3|converges locally uniformly]] on $\Omega$, that is, uniformly on every compact subset of $\Omega$.
:::
