::: {.theorem}
The [[D-Q3MYK|Gamma function]] satisfies the reflection formula
$$
\Gamma(z)\Gamma(1-z)=\frac{\pi}{\sin(\pi z)},
$$
and $1/\Gamma$ is an entire function whose zeros are exactly the simple zeros at $z=0,-1,-2,\ldots$.

This is [@SS03, ch. 6, Theorem 1.4] for the reflection formula and [@SS03, ch. 6, Theorem 1.6] for the reciprocal, which is entire with simple zeros at the nonpositive integers and vanishes nowhere else.
:::
