::: {.theorem}
Let $X$ be a compact connected complex manifold.
Then every holomorphic function $f\colon X\to\CC$ is constant.

This is the [[T-BYNL5|maximum modulus principle]] read in a chart: $\abs f$ attains a maximum on the compact $X$, and the set on which the maximum is attained is both open and closed in $X$.
:::
