::: {.definition}
A \dfn{conic section} is the zero set in $\RR^2$ of a polynomial $Q(x,y)=ax^2+bxy+cy^2+dx+ey+f$ with $a,b,c$ not all zero.
For a nondegenerate real conic with at least one real point, the sign of the discriminant $\Delta\coloneqq b^2-4ac$ of its quadratic part decides the type: $\Delta<0$ for an ellipse, $\Delta=0$ for a parabola, and $\Delta>0$ for a hyperbola; the ellipse is a circle exactly when moreover $a=c$ and $b=0$.

The ellipse and the hyperbola appear in [@Ahl73, ch. 3, §4.2, A Survey of Elementary Mappings] as (16) and (17), the images of the circle $\abs w=\rho$ and of a ray under $z=\tfrac12(w+1/w)$.
:::
