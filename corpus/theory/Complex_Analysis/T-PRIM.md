::: {.theorem}
Let $\Omega\subseteq\CC$ be [[D-GFM35|simply connected]] and let $f$ be [[D-E7A5W|holomorphic]] on $\Omega$.
Then $f$ has a [[D-6RZQN|primitive]] on $\Omega$.
Explicitly, for a fixed $z_0\in\Omega$ and any curve in $\Omega$ from $z_0$ to $z$, the function $F(z)=\int_{z_0}^{z}f$ is a primitive of $f$.

This is [@SS03, ch. 3, Theorem 5.2].
:::
