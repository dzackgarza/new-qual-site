- How are $\sin(2t)$ and $\cos(2t)$ written in terms of $\tan(t)$?

::: {.fact}
For every $t\in\RR$ with $\cos t\neq0$,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\sin(2t) &= {2\tan(t) \over 1+\tan^2(t)}, \\
\cos(2t) &= {1-\tan^2(t) \over 1+\tan^2(t)}.
\end{aligned}
$$
:::
