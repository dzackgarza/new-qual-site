::: {.theorem ref="Morera"}
Let $\Omega\subseteq\CC$ be open and let $f\colon\Omega\to\CC$ be continuous.
If $\int_{\partial T}f(z)\dz=0$ for every closed triangle $T\subseteq\Omega$, then $f$ is [[D-E7A5W|holomorphic]] on $\Omega$.
:::
