::: {.theorem}
Let $z_0\in\CC$, let $\delta>0$, and let $f$ be [[D-7DFVJ|meromorphic]] on the punctured disc $\{z : 0<\abs{z-z_0}<\delta\}$.
If there are three distinct points of $\CC\cup\{\infty\}$ that $f$ does not take on this punctured disc, then $f$ extends to a meromorphic function on the disc $\{z : \abs{z-z_0}<\delta\}$.

This is Picard's great theorem in its value-omission form; [@SS03, ch. 3, §3], in the paragraph following Theorem 3.3, records the value-distribution form — near an essential singularity $f$ takes every complex value infinitely often with at most one exception — and states that no proof is given there, and [@McM17, Theorem 4.35] states that form and proves it by the rescaling argument of Theorem 4.38 and Lemma 4.39 of that section.
:::
