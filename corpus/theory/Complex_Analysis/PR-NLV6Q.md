::: {.proposition}
For $\Re s>0$ the integral
$$
\Gamma(s)=\int_0^\infty e^{-t}t^{s-1}\dt
$$
converges absolutely, and it defines a [[D-E7A5W|holomorphic]] function on the right half-plane $\theset{s\in\CC\st\Re s>0}$.
:::
