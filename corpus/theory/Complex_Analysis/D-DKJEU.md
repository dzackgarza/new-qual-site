:::{.definition}
A map of the following form is a *linear fractional transformation*:
\[
T(z) = {az + b \over cz + d}
,\]
where the denominator is assumed to not be a multiple of the numerator.
These have inverses given by
\[
T^{-1}(w) = {dw-b \over -cw + a}
.\]
:::
