::: {.corollary}
Let $\Omega\subseteq\CC$ be a connected open set and let $f,g$ be [[D-E7A5W|holomorphic]] on $\Omega$.
If $f=g$ on a subset of $\Omega$ that has a [[D-TFSPT|limit point]] in $\Omega$, then $f=g$ on $\Omega$.
:::
