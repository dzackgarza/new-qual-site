::: {.proposition}
For $s\in\CC$ with $\Re(s)>0$, the [[D-Q3MYK|Gamma function]] satisfies
$$
\Gamma(s+1) = s\,\Gamma(s).
$$
Consequently $\Gamma(n+1)=n!$ for every integer $n\ge0$.
:::
