::: {.lemma}
Let $(c_k)_{k\ge0}$ be a nonincreasing sequence of nonnegative real numbers with $c_k\to0$, and let $f(z)\coloneqq\sum_{k\ge0}c_kz^k$.
Then the series $f(z)$ converges for every $z$ with $\abs{z}=1$ and $z\neq1$.
:::
