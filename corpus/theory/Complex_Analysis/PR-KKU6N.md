::: {.proposition}
Let $S\coloneqq\theset{z\in\CC : -\pi/2<\Re z<\pi/2,\ \Im z>0}$.
Then $z\mapsto\sin(z)$ is a [[D-TM4TE|biholomorphism]] $S\to\HH$, so its inverse $\arcsin\colon\HH\to S$ is a biholomorphism from the upper half-plane onto the vertical half-strip.
:::

::: {.proof}
This is [[PR-3LBLV]] together with the holomorphy of the inverse of a bijective holomorphic map.
:::
