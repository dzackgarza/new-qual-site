::: {.theorem}
Let $A_1,\ldots,A_n\subseteq\RR^n$ be compact.
Then there is an affine hyperplane $P$ that divides each $A_i$ into two pieces of equal $n$-dimensional measure [@Hat02, Corollary 2B.7], the case of three compact sets in $\RR^3$ being [@Hat02, §1.1, Exercise 9].
:::
