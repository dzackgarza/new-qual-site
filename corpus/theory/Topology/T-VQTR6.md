::: {.theorem}
For $n\geq 1$, the sphere $S^n$ has a continuous field of nonzero tangent vectors if and only if $n$ is odd.
In particular, every continuous tangent vector field on $S^{2k}$ vanishes somewhere [@Hat02].
:::
