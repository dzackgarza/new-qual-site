::: {.fact}
The following manifolds are not [[D-K5MLW|orientable]]: the real projective spaces $\RP^n$ for $n\geq 2$ even [@Hat02, §3.3, p. 231], the Klein bottle and more generally the closed nonorientable surfaces $N_k$ [@Hat02, Example 2.37 and Theorem 3.26, pp. 141, 236], and the open Möbius band.
:::
