::: {.proposition}
Let $X$ be a path-connected space.
Then $X$ is [[D-GFM35|simply connected]] if and only if $\pi_1(X, x_0) = 1$ for some, equivalently every, basepoint $x_0\in X$ [@Hat02, §1.1, p. 28].
:::
