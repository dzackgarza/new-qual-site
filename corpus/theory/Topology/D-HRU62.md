::: {.definition}
Let $X_0$ and $X_1$ be topological spaces, $A\subseteq X_1$ a subspace, and $f\colon A\to X_0$ a continuous map.
The space obtained by \dfn{attaching $X_1$ to $X_0$ along $f$} is the quotient space
$$
X_0\sqcup_f X_1\coloneqq\qty{X_0\sqcup X_1}/\qty{a\sim f(a) \text{ for } a\in A},
$$
where $\sim$ is the equivalence relation generated by $a\sim f(a)$.
:::

::: {.example}
Attaching an $n$-cell to $X_0$ along $\varphi\colon S^{n-1}\to X_0$ is the case $X_1 = D^n$, $A = S^{n-1}$, $f = \varphi$.
For a continuous map $g\colon X\to Y$, the [[D-RMQ7W|mapping cylinder]] of $g$ is the case $X_0 = Y$, $X_1 = X\times[0,1]$, $A = X\times\ts{1}$, $f(x, 1) = g(x)$.
:::
