::: {.definition}
Let $(X, d)$ be a metric space and $A\subseteq X$ a nonempty subset.
The \dfn{diameter} of $A$ is $\diam(A)\coloneqq\sup_{p, q\in A} d(p, q)\in[0,\infty]$.
:::
