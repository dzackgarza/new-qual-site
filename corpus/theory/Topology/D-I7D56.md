::: {.definition}
Let $R$ be a ring and $M$ a left [[D-NQZUY|$R$-module]].
A subset $B\subseteq M$ is a \dfn{basis} of $M$ if $B$ is [[D-5RWYR|linearly independent]] and a [[D-DUCA5|generating set]] for $M$.
:::

::: {.remark}
An $R$-module is [[D-LIEMF|free]] exactly when it has a basis.
:::
