::: {.definition}
Let $p\colon \tilde X \to X$ be a [[D-ANO2D|covering space]] and $x_0 \in X$.
For $\tilde x\in p\inv(x_0)$ and a loop $\gamma$ in $X$ based at $x_0$, let $\tilde\gamma$ be the unique lift of $\gamma$ with $\tilde\gamma(0) = \tilde x$.
The \dfn{monodromy action} is the right action of $\pi_1(X, x_0)$ on $p\inv(x_0)$ given by
$$
\tilde x \cdot [\gamma] \coloneqq \tilde\gamma(1)
.$$
:::

::: {.remark}
The endpoint $\tilde\gamma(1)$ depends only on the homotopy class $[\gamma]$, by the homotopy lifting property of covering spaces.
The stabilizer of $\tilde x$ is $p_*\pi_1(\tilde X, \tilde x)$.
If $X$ is path-connected, the action is transitive if and only if $\tilde X$ is path-connected, and then $\tilde x\cdot[\gamma]\mapsto p_*\pi_1(\tilde X, \tilde x)\,[\gamma]$ is a bijection from $p\inv(x_0)$ onto the set of right cosets of $p_*\pi_1(\tilde X, \tilde x)$ in $\pi_1(X, x_0)$.
:::
