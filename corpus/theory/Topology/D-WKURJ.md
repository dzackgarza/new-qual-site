::: {.definition}
Let $X$ be a set.
A collection $\mcb$ of subsets of $X$ is a \dfn{basis} for a topology on $X$ if

- every $x\in X$ lies in some $B\in\mcb$, and

- whenever $x \in B_1 \intersect B_2$ with $B_1, B_2\in\mcb$, there exists $B_3\in\mcb$ with $x\in B_3 \subseteq B_1 \intersect B_2$.

The \dfn{topology generated by} $\mcb$ is the collection of all $U\subseteq X$ such that for each $x\in U$ there exists $B\in\mcb$ with $x\in B \subseteq U$.
:::

::: {.proposition}
Let $\mcb$ be a basis for a topology on a set $X$.
The topology generated by $\mcb$ is a [[D-2TZAI|topology]] on $X$, and its open sets are exactly the unions of elements of $\mcb$.
:::
