::: {.definition}
Let $h_*$ be a sequence of functors $h_n$ from pairs of topological spaces to abelian groups.
The functors $h_*$ satisfy \dfn{excision} if for every pair $(X, A)$ and every subspace $Z\subseteq A$ with $\cl_X(Z) \subseteq A^\circ$, the inclusion $(X\sm Z, A\sm Z)\injects (X, A)$ induces isomorphisms
$$
h_n(X\sm Z, A\sm Z) \mapsvia{\sim} h_n(X, A) \quad\text{for all } n
.$$
:::

::: {.remark}
Excision is one of the Eilenberg--Steenrod axioms for a homology theory.
Singular homology satisfies it [@Hat02, Theorem 2.20, p. 119]; relative homotopy groups do not.
:::
