::: {.definition}
Let $M_1$ and $M_2$ be connected $n$-manifolds, and let $B_i\subseteq M_i$ be a closed $n$-ball embedded in the interior of a larger ball.
The \dfn{connected sum} $M_1\# M_2$ is the quotient of the disjoint union $(M_1\sm B_1)\disjoint(M_2\sm B_2)$ obtained by identifying the boundary spheres $\partial B_1$ and $\partial B_2$ by a homeomorphism [@Hat02, §3.3 Exercise 6].
:::
