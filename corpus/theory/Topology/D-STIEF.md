::: {.definition}
For $1\leq n\leq k$, the \dfn{Stiefel manifold} $V_n(\RR^k)$ is the space of ordered orthonormal $n$-frames in $\RR^k$, topologized as a subspace of the product of $n$ copies of $S^{k-1}$.
$V_k(\RR^k)=O(k)$, a $k$-frame in $\RR^k$ being an orthogonal $k\times k$ matrix, and $V_1(\RR^k)=S^{k-1}$ [@Hat02, Example 4.53].
:::
