::: {.proposition}
Every $n$-manifold $M$ has an orientable two-sheeted [[D-ANO2D|covering space]] $\tilde M\to M$, the [[D-WX7JH|orientation double cover]] [@Hat02].
If $M$ is connected, then $M$ is [[D-K5MLW|orientable]] if and only if $\tilde M$ has two components; in particular, $M$ is orientable if $\pi_1(M)$ has no subgroup of index two [@Hat02].
:::
