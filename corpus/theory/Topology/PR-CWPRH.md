::: {.proposition}
For abelian groups $G_1,G_2,\ldots$ there is a connected CW complex $X$ with $\tilde H_n(X)\cong G_n$ for all $n\geq1$, namely the wedge $\bigvee_n M(G_n,n)$ of the [[D-KC4BS|Moore spaces]] of [[PR-B6BB2|additive reduced homology]] [@Hat02, Example 2.41].
:::
