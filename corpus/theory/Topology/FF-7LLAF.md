::: {.fact}
The integral homology groups of $\RP^4$ are
$$
H_k(\RP^4;\ZZ) \cong \begin{cases}
\ZZ & k = 0,\\
\ZZ/2 & k = 1, 3,\\
0 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$
[@Hat02, Example 2.42, p. 144].
:::
