::: {.definition}
Let $X$ be a topological space.
A subset $Q\subseteq X$ is \dfn{dense} in $X$ if every [[D-JMRPA|neighborhood]] of every point $x\in X$ intersects $Q$.
:::

::: {.proposition}
A subset $Q\subseteq X$ is dense in $X$ if and only if its [[D-ASXW6|closure]] is $\cl_X(Q) = X$.
:::
