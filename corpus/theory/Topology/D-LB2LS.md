::: {.definition}
Let $(X, \tau)$ be a topological space and $A\subseteq X$ a subset.
The \dfn{subspace topology} on $A$ is
$$
\tau_A\coloneqq\ts{A\cap U \st U\in\tau}.
$$
:::

See [@Mun00, §16].
