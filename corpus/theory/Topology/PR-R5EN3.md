::: {.proposition}
Let $p\colon(\tilde X, \tilde x_0)\to(X, x_0)$ be a [[D-ANO2D|covering space]].
The induced map $p_*\colon \pi_1(\tilde X, \tilde x_0) \to \pi_1(X, x_0)$ is injective, and its image consists of the classes of loops at $x_0$ whose lifts to $\tilde X$ starting at $\tilde x_0$ are loops [@Hat02].
:::
