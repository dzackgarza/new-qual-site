::: {.definition}
Let $X$ and $Y$ be [[D-ZOU5G|CW complexes]] with $n$-skeleta $X^n$ and $Y^n$.
A [[D-AEAAD|continuous map]] $f\colon X\to Y$ is \dfn{cellular} if $f(X^n) \subseteq Y^n$ for every $n\geq 0$.
:::
