::: {.definition}
Let $X$ be a topological space.
A \dfn{universal cover} of $X$ is a [[D-ANO2D|covering space]] $p\colon \tilde X\to X$ with $\tilde X$ [[D-GFM35|simply connected]] [@Hat02].
:::

::: {.remark}
[[PR-UBJ6P|The existence and uniqueness theorem]] says when a universal cover exists, that it is unique, and that its deck group is $\pi_1(X)$.
:::
