::: {.corollary}
Let $X$, $Y$ and $Z$ be topological spaces with $Z$ [[D-K43GA|contractible]], and let $\tilde f\colon X\to Z$ and $p\colon Z\to Y$ be continuous maps.
Then $f \coloneqq p\circ \tilde f\colon X\to Y$ is [[D-MGRZP|nullhomotopic]].
:::
