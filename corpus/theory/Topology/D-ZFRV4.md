::: {.definition}
A topological space $X$ is \dfn{Hausdorff}, or $T_2$, if for all $p, q \in X$ with $p\neq q$ there exist disjoint open sets $U, V\subseteq X$ with $p\in U$ and $q\in V$.
:::
