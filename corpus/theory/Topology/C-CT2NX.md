::: {.corollary}
Let $A$ and $B$ be closed surfaces, and let $A \# B$ be their connected sum.
Then the [[D-QK5BM|Euler characteristics]] satisfy
$$
\chi(A \# B) = \chi(A) + \chi(B) - 2
.$$
:::
