::: {.theorem}
The suspension map
$$
\pi_i(S^n) \to \pi_{i+1}(S^{n+1})
$$
is an isomorphism for $i < 2n-1$ and a surjection for $i = 2n-1$.
More generally, the suspension map $\pi_i(X)\to\pi_{i+1}(SX)$ has the same behaviour whenever $X$ is an $(n-1)$-connected CW complex [@Hat02, Corollary 4.24, p. 360].
:::

::: {.remark}
In particular, $\pi_{n+k}(S^n)$ is independent of $n$ for $n > k+1$; this common value is the $k$th stable homotopy group of spheres.
:::
