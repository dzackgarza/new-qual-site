::: {.fact}
The integral homology groups of $\CP^2$ are
$$
H_k(\CP^2;\ZZ) \cong \begin{cases}
\ZZ & k = 0, 2, 4,\\
0 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$
[@Hat02, §2.2, p. 140].
:::
