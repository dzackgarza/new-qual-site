::: {.definition}
Let $X$ be a topological space and $I = [0,1]$.
The space $X$ is \dfn{path connected} if for every pair of points $x\neq y$ in $X$ there exists a [[D-J6XOC|path]] $f\colon I \to X$ with $f(0) = x$ and $f(1) = y$.
:::
