::: {.definition}
For $x_0 \in X$ and $n\geq 1$,
\[
\pi_n(X, x_0) \da \ts{ f: (I^n, \del I^n) \to (X, x_0) } / \homotopic
,\]
homotopy classes rel $\del I^n$, equivalently $[(S^n, s_0), (X, x_0)]_*$.
The product concatenates in the first coordinate, exactly as for $\pi_1$.
$\pi_1$ is the fundamental group, and $\pi_n$ is abelian for $n\geq 2$.
:::

::: {.concept}
See Hatcher, §4.1, p. 340.
:::
