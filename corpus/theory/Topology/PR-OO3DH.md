::: {.proposition}
Let $X$ and $Y$ be path-connected spaces.
Then the projections induce an isomorphism
$$
\pi_1 (X \cross Y) \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ } \pi_1(X) \cross \pi_1(Y)
$$
[@Hat02].
:::
