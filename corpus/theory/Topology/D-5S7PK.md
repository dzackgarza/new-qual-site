::: {.definition}
Let $f\colon Z\to X$ and $g\colon Z\to Y$ be continuous maps.
The \dfn{pushout} of $X \xleftarrow{f} Z \mapsvia{g} Y$ is the [[D-ITBUT|quotient space]]
$$
X \disjoint_Z Y \coloneqq \qty{X \disjoint Y}/\sim
,$$
where $\sim$ is the equivalence relation generated by $f(z)\sim g(z)$ for $z\in Z$, together with the maps $j_X\colon X\to X\disjoint_Z Y$ and $j_Y\colon Y\to X\disjoint_Z Y$ induced by the inclusions.
:::

::: {.remark}
The maps satisfy $j_X\circ f = j_Y\circ g$, and $(X\disjoint_Z Y, j_X, j_Y)$ is the [[D-5MX7E|colimit]] of $X \xleftarrow{f} Z \mapsvia{g} Y$ in the category of topological spaces: for continuous maps $a\colon X\to W$ and $b\colon Y\to W$ with $a\circ f = b\circ g$ there is a unique continuous $\varphi\colon X\disjoint_Z Y\to W$ with $\varphi\circ j_X = a$ and $\varphi\circ j_Y = b$.
When $g$ is the inclusion of a subspace $Z\subseteq Y$, the pushout is the space obtained by [[D-HRU62|attaching $Y$ to $X$ along $f$]].
The dual construction is the [[D-VWYRN|pullback]].
If $X = U\union V$ with $U, V$ open and path-connected, $U\intersect V$ path-connected and $x_0\in U\intersect V$, then $X$ is the pushout of $U \hookleftarrow U\intersect V \injects V$, and the Seifert--van Kampen theorem gives the corresponding pushout of groups, the [[D-WSFYS|amalgamated free product]] $\pi_1(X, x_0)\cong \pi_1(U, x_0) \ast_{\pi_1(U\intersect V, x_0)} \pi_1(V, x_0)$ [@Hat02, Theorem 1.20, p. 43].
:::
