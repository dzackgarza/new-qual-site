::: {.definition}
Let $X$ and $Y$ be topological spaces.
A [[D-AEAAD|continuous map]] $f\colon X\to Y$ is \dfn{proper} if for every [[D-EILKJ|compact]] subset $K \subseteq Y$, the preimage $f\inv(K) \subseteq X$ is compact.
:::
