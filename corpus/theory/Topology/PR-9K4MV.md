::: {.proposition}
Let $A\subseteq X$ be a [[D-6UHU7|deformation retract]] of $X$, with inclusion $\iota\colon A\injects X$, and let $a\in A$.
Then $\iota_*\colon\pi_1(A,a)\to\pi_1(X,a)$ is an isomorphism [@Hat02, Proposition 1.17].
:::
