::: {.proposition}
For $n\geq 1$ and all $i$, the reduced cohomology groups of spheres satisfy
$$
\tilde H^i(S^n) \cong \tilde H^{i-1}(S^{n-1})
$$
[@Hat02, §3.2, p. 219].
:::

::: {.remark}
The statement fails for unreduced cohomology: for $n\geq 2$, $H^1(S^n) = 0$ while $H^0(S^{n-1})\cong\ZZ$.
:::
