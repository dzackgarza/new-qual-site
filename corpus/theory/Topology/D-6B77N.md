::: {.definition}
Let $R$ be a ring and let $T$ be an additive covariant functor from $R$-modules to abelian groups.
For an $R$-module $A$, choose a [[D-PKIY7|projective resolution]] $\cdots\to P_1\to P_0\to A\to 0$ and let $P_\bullet$ be the complex $\cdots \to P_1\to P_0\to 0$ with $A$ deleted.
The \dfn{left derived functors} of $T$ are
$$
L_n T(A) \coloneqq H_n\qty{T(P_\bullet)}, \qquad n\geq 0
.$$
:::

::: {.remark}
Any two projective resolutions of $A$ are chain homotopy equivalent, so $L_nT(A)$ is well defined up to natural isomorphism, and a morphism $A\to A'$ induces $L_nT(A)\to L_nT(A')$.
If $T$ is right exact, then $L_0T\cong T$.
:::

:::
