::: {.definition}
A topological space $X$ is \dfn{normal} if for all disjoint [[D-UI7ZL|closed]] subsets $A, B\subseteq X$ there exist disjoint open sets $U, V\subseteq X$ with $A\subseteq U$ and $B\subseteq V$.
:::
