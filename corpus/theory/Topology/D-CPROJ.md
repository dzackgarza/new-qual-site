::: {.definition}
For $n\geq 0$, \dfn{complex projective $n$-space} $\CP^n$ is the space of complex lines through the origin in $\CC^{n+1}$: the quotient of $\CC^{n+1}\sm\theset{0}$ by the equivalence relation $x\sim\lambda x$ for $\lambda\neq0$, equivalently the quotient of the unit sphere $S^{2n+1}\subseteq\CC^{n+1}$ by $x\sim\lambda x$ for $\abs{\lambda}=1$ [@Hat02, Example 0.6].
:::
