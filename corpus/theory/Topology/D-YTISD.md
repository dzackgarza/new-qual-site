::: {.definition}
Let $p\colon\tilde X\to X$ be a [[D-ANO2D|covering space]].
The covering $p$ is \dfn{normal}, also called \dfn{regular}, if for every $x\in X$ and all $\tilde x_1, \tilde x_2\in p\inv(x)$ there is a [[D-4VGAW|deck transformation]] $f\colon\tilde X\to\tilde X$ with $f(\tilde x_1) = \tilde x_2$.
:::
