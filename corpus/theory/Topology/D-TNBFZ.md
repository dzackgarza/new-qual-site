::: {.definition}
Let $X$ and $Y$ be topological spaces.
A map $q\colon X\to Y$ is a \dfn{quotient map} if

1. $q$ is surjective, and

2. for every $U\subseteq Y$, $U$ is open in $Y$ if and only if $q\inv(U)$ is open in $X$

[@Mun00, sec. 22].
:::
