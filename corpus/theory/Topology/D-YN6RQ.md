::: {.definition}
Let $X$ be a topological space.
For $x, y\in X$, put $x\sim y$ if there exists a [[D-X73EB|path-connected]] subspace $U\subseteq X$ with $x, y\in U$.
The equivalence classes of $\sim$ are the \dfn{path components} of $X$.
:::

::: {.proposition}
The relation $\sim$ is an equivalence relation on $X$.
:::
