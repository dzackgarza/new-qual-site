::: {.fact}
The integral homology groups of $\RP^3$ are
$$
H_k(\RP^3;\ZZ) \cong \begin{cases}
\ZZ & k = 0, 3,\\
\ZZ/2 & k = 1,\\
0 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$
[@Hat02].
:::
