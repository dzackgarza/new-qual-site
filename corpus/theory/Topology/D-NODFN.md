::: {.definition}
Let $(I,\leq)$ be a directed set.
A \dfn{directed system} of abelian groups indexed by $I$ consists of abelian groups $A_\alpha$ for $\alpha\in I$ and homomorphisms $f_{\alpha\beta}\colon A_\alpha\to A_\beta$ for $\alpha\leq\beta$, with $f_{\alpha\alpha}=\id_{A_\alpha}$ and $f_{\beta\gamma}\circ f_{\alpha\beta}=f_{\alpha\gamma}$ for $\alpha\leq\beta\leq\gamma$.
Its \dfn{direct limit} is the set
$$
\varinjlim_\alpha A_\alpha\coloneqq\qty{\Disjoint_{\alpha\in I}A_\alpha}/\sim,
$$
where $\sim$ is the equivalence relation generated by $a\sim f_{\alpha\beta}(a)$ for $a\in A_\alpha$ and $\alpha\leq\beta$, with the group operation $[a]+[b]\coloneqq[f_{\alpha\gamma}(a)+f_{\beta\gamma}(b)]$ for $a\in A_\alpha$, $b\in A_\beta$, and any $\gamma\geq\alpha,\beta$ [@Hat02].
For each $\alpha$, the map $\iota_\alpha\colon A_\alpha\to\varinjlim A_\alpha$, $a\mapsto[a]$, is a homomorphism with $\iota_\beta\circ f_{\alpha\beta}=\iota_\alpha$.
:::

::: {.remark}
Because $I$ is directed, $a\in A_\alpha$ and $b\in A_\beta$ satisfy $a\sim b$ if and only if $f_{\alpha\gamma}(a)=f_{\beta\gamma}(b)$ for some $\gamma\geq\alpha,\beta$; this makes the group operation well defined.
With the maps $\iota_\alpha$, the direct limit is the [[D-5MX7E|colimit]] of the system in the category of abelian groups.
:::

::: {.proposition}
Direct limits over a directed set are exact: if $A_\alpha\mapsvia{u_\alpha}B_\alpha\mapsvia{v_\alpha}C_\alpha$ is an exact sequence for each $\alpha\in I$, and the $u_\alpha$ and $v_\alpha$ commute with the maps of directed systems $(A_\alpha)$, $(B_\alpha)$, $(C_\alpha)$, then the induced sequence $\varinjlim A_\alpha\mapsvia{u}\varinjlim B_\alpha\mapsvia{v}\varinjlim C_\alpha$ is exact.
:::

::: {.proposition}
If a CW complex $X$ is the union of a family of subcomplexes $X_\alpha$ directed by inclusion, then the inclusions induce an isomorphism $\varinjlim_\alpha H_n(X_\alpha;G)\cong H_n(X;G)$ for every $n$ and every abelian group $G$ [@Hat02].
:::
