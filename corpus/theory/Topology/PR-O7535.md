::: {.proposition}
Let $A\subseteq X$ be a retract, with retraction $r\colon X\to A$.
If $X$ is [[D-ZFRV4|Hausdorff]], then $A = \theset{x\in X \st r(x) = x}$ is closed in $X$.
If $X$ is connected or compact, then so is $A = r(X)$ [@Mun00, Theorems 23.5 and 26.5].
:::
