::: {.definition}
Let $n\geq0$.
An \dfn{$n$-manifold} is a [[D-ZFRV4|Hausdorff space]] $M$ in which every point has an open neighborhood [[D-9KQZT|homeomorphic]] to $\RR^n$ [@Hat02].
:::

::: {.remark}
The definition of a topological manifold in [@Lee13] also requires $M$ to be [[D-23NTI|second-countable]].
The open long line is a Hausdorff space in which every point has an open neighborhood homeomorphic to $\RR$, and it is not second-countable.
:::
