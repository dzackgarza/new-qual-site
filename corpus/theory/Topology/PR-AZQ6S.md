::: {.proposition}
Let $M$ be a compact orientable $n$-manifold with boundary $\del M$.
Then for all $k$
$$
H^{n-k}(M, \del M; \ZZ) \cong H_k(M; \ZZ), \qquad H^{n-k}(M; \ZZ) \cong H_k(M, \del M; \ZZ)
$$
[@Hat02, Theorem 3.43, p. 254].
:::
