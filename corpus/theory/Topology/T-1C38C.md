::: {.theorem}
Let $X$ be path-connected, locally path-connected, and semilocally simply connected.
Sending a path-connected covering space $p\colon(\tilde X,\tilde x_0)\to(X,x_0)$ to the subgroup $p_*\pi_1(\tilde X,\tilde x_0)\leq\pi_1(X,x_0)$ gives a bijection between basepoint-preserving isomorphism classes of path-connected covering spaces of $(X,x_0)$ and subgroups of $\pi_1(X,x_0)$, and a bijection between isomorphism classes of path-connected covering spaces $\tilde X\to X$ and conjugacy classes of subgroups of $\pi_1(X,x_0)$ [@Hat02, Theorem 1.38].
:::
