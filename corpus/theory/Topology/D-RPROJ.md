::: {.definition}
For $n\geq 0$, \dfn{real projective $n$-space} $\RP^n$ is the space of lines through the origin in $\RR^{n+1}$: the quotient of $\RR^{n+1}\sm\theset{0}$ by the equivalence relation $x\sim\lambda x$ for $\lambda\neq0$, equivalently the quotient $S^n/(x\sim -x)$ of the unit sphere by the antipodal identification [@Hat02, Example 0.4].
:::
