::: {.definition}
For $n\geq 0$, the \dfn{$n$-sphere} is
$$
S^n \coloneqq \theset{x\in\RR^{n+1} \st \norm{x} = 1},
$$
and the \dfn{$n$-disc} is
$$
D^n \coloneqq \theset{x\in\RR^n \st \norm{x} \leq 1}.
$$
The boundary of $D^{n+1}$ is $S^n$, and collapsing the boundary to a point gives a homeomorphism $S^n\cong D^n/\bd D^n$ [@Hat02, Example 0.3].
:::
