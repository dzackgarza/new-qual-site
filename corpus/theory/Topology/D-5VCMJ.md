::: {.definition}
Let $X$ be a topological space and $A\subseteq X$.
A point $p\in A$ is an \dfn{interior point} of $A$ if there exists a [[D-JMRPA|neighborhood]] $U$ of $p$ with $U\subseteq A$.
The \dfn{interior} $A^\circ$ of $A$ is the set of interior points of $A$.
:::
