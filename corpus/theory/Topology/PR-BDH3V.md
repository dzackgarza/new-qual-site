::: {.proposition}
There is a homeomorphism
$$
\RP^2 \# \KK \cong \RP^2 \# \TT^2
,$$
and both surfaces are homeomorphic to $\RP^2\#\RP^2\#\RP^2$ [@Mun00].
:::
