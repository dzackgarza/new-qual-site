::: {.proposition}
Let $X$ be a topological space in which every one-point set is closed, that is, a $T_1$ space.
Then every finite subset of $X$ is closed.
Every [[D-ZFRV4|Hausdorff space]] is $T_1$, so in a Hausdorff space every finite subset, in particular every singleton, is closed [@Mun00].
:::
