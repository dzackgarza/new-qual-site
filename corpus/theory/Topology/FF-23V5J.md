::: {.fact}
Let $\Sigma$ be a connected [[FD-QPIIL|closed surface]] with [[D-QK5BM|Euler characteristic]] $\chi(\Sigma) = 2$.
Then $\Sigma$ is orientable of genus $0$, and $\Sigma \cong S^2$ [@Mun00]; [@Hat02, Examples 2.36--2.37, p. 141].
:::
