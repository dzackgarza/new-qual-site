::: {.fact}
Let $\Sigma$ be a connected [[FD-QPIIL|closed surface]].
If its [[D-QK5BM|Euler characteristic]] is $\chi(\Sigma) = 2$, then $\Sigma \cong S^2$.
:::

[@Mun00, §§77--78].
