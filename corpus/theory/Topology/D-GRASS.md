::: {.definition}
For $1\leq n\leq k$, the \dfn{Grassmann manifold} $G_n(\RR^k)$ is the space of $n$-dimensional linear subspaces of $\RR^k$, topologized as the quotient of the [[D-STIEF|Stiefel manifold]] $V_n(\RR^k)$ by the map sending an orthonormal $n$-frame to the subspace it spans.
The complex and quaternionic Grassmann manifolds $G_n(\CC^k)$ and $G_n(\HH^k)$ are defined in the same way from the Hermitian inner products on $\CC^k$ and $\HH^k$ [@Hat02, Example 4.53].
:::
