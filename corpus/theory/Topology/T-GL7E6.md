::: {.theorem}
Let $n\geq 2$ and let $X$ be an $(n-1)$-connected space.
Then $\tilde H_i(X) = 0$ for $i < n$, and the Hurewicz map $\pi_n(X)\to H_n(X)$ is an isomorphism [@Hat02, Theorem 4.32, p. 366].
For $n = 1$ and $X$ path-connected, the Hurewicz map $\pi_1(X)\to H_1(X)$ induces an isomorphism from the abelianization of $\pi_1(X)$ onto $H_1(X)$ [@Hat02, Theorem 2A.1, p. 166].
:::
