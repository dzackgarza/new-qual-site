::: {.fact}
Let $A$ and $B$ be connected [[FD-QPIIL|closed surfaces]].
The [[D-QK5BM|Euler characteristic]] of their connected sum is
$$
\chi(A\# B) = \chi(A) + \chi(B) - 2
$$
[@Hat02].
:::
