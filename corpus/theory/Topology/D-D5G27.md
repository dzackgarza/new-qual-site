::: {.definition}
Let $(X, d)$ be a metric space, $S\subseteq X$ a nonempty subset, and $x\in X$.
The \dfn{distance from $x$ to $S$} is
$$
\dist(x, S)\coloneqq\inf_{s\in S} d(x, s).
$$
:::
