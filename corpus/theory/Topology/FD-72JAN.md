::: {.definition}
Let $X$ be a topological space and $A\subseteq X$.
A point $x\in X$ is a \dfn{limit point} of $A$ if every open set $U\subseteq X$ with $x\in U$ contains a point $y\in A\sm\ts{x}$.
:::
