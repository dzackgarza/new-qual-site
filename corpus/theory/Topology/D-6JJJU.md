::: {.definition}
Let $X$ be a topological space.
A collection $\mcs$ of subsets of $X$ is \dfn{locally finite} if every $x\in X$ has a [[D-JMRPA|neighborhood]] $U$ such that $U\intersect S\neq\emptyset$ for only finitely many $S\in\mcs$.
:::
