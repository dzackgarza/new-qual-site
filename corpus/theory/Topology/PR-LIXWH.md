::: {.proposition}
The Klein bottle is a connected sum of two projective planes,
$$
\KK \cong \RP^2 \# \RP^2
.$$
Equivalently, $\KK$ is the union of two Möbius bands glued along their boundary circles [@Hat02].
:::
