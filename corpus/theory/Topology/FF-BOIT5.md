::: {.fact}
Let $\Sigma$ be a connected [[FD-QPIIL|closed surface]] with [[D-QK5BM|Euler characteristic]] $\chi(\Sigma) = -2$.
Then $\Sigma$ is homeomorphic to the orientable surface $\Sigma_2$ of genus $2$ or to the nonorientable surface $N_4 = \#_{i=1}^4\RP^2$, and orientability decides which [@Mun00, Theorem 77.5]; [@Hat02, Examples 2.36--2.37, p. 141].
:::
