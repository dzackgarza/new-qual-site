::: {.definition}
For $n\geq 1$, the \dfn{$n$-torus} is the product
$$
T^n \coloneqq (S^1)^n
$$
of $n$ circles, with the product topology; $T^2 = S^1\cross S^1$ is the \dfn{torus} [@Hat02, Example 1B.5].
:::
