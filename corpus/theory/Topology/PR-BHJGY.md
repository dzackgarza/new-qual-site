::: {.proposition}
Let $p\colon(\tilde X, \tilde x_0)\to(X, x_0)$ be a [[D-2PNEG|covering space]] with $X$ and $\tilde X$ path-connected.
The number of sheets of $p$, that is, the cardinality of $p^{-1}(x_0)$, equals the index
$$
[\pi_1(X, x_0) : p_*(\pi_1(\tilde X, \tilde x_0))]
$$
[@Hat02, Proposition 1.32, p. 61].
:::
