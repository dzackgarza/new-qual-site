::: {.definition}
Let $X$ be a topological space and $p\in X$.
A \dfn{neighborhood basis} at $p$ is a collection $\mathcal B_p$ of [[D-JMRPA|neighborhoods]] of $p$ such that every neighborhood $N$ of $p$ contains some $B\in\mathcal B_p$.
:::
