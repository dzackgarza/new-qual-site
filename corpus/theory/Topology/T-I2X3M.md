::: {.theorem}
Every map $f\colon X\to Y$ of CW complexes is homotopic to a cellular map.
If $f$ is already cellular on a subcomplex $A\subseteq X$, the homotopy may be taken to be stationary on $A$ [@Hat02].
:::
