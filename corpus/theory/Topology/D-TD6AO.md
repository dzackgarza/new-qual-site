::: {.definition}
Let $\mathcal C$ be a category and $(I,\leq)$ a directed set.
A \dfn{directed system} in $\mathcal C$ consists of objects $X_i$ for $i\in I$ and morphisms $f_{ij}\colon X_i\to X_j$ for $i\leq j$, with $f_{ii}=\id_{X_i}$ and $f_{jk}\circ f_{ij}=f_{ik}$ for $i\leq j\leq k$.
A \dfn{colimit} of the system is an object $X$ with morphisms $\iota_i\colon X_i\to X$ satisfying $\iota_j\circ f_{ij}=\iota_i$ for $i\leq j$, such that for every object $Y$ and morphisms $\psi_i\colon X_i\to Y$ with $\psi_j\circ f_{ij}=\psi_i$ for $i\leq j$ there is a unique morphism $h\colon X\to Y$ with $h\circ\iota_i=\psi_i$ for all $i$.
:::

::: {.remark}
The morphisms $\iota_i$ go from the system to $X$; reversing every morphism gives the [[D-QXER7|limit]], whose structure morphisms go from the limit to the system.
This is the special case of the [[D-5MX7E|colimit of a diagram]] in which the indexing category is the directed set $I$.
:::

::: {.example}
In sets, and for abelian groups with the group structure of the [[D-NODFN|direct limit]], the colimit is
$$
\varinjlim_iX_i=\qty{\Disjoint_{i\in I}X_i}/\sim,
$$
where $\sim$ is the equivalence relation generated by $x\sim f_{ij}(x)$ for $x\in X_i$ and $i\leq j$, with $\iota_i$ sending $x$ to its class [@Hat02, p. 243].
:::
