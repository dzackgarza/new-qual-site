::: {.proposition}
Let $(X,A)$ be a good pair: $A$ is a nonempty closed subspace of $X$ and a deformation retract of some neighborhood of $A$ in $X$.
Then the quotient map $q\colon(X,A)\to(X/A,A/A)$ induces isomorphisms $q_*\colon H_n(X,A)\to H_n(X/A,A/A)\cong\tilde H_n(X/A)$ for all $n$ [@Hat02, Proposition 2.22].
:::
