::: {.definition}
A topological space $X$ is \dfn{locally connected} if for every $x\in X$ and every [[D-JMRPA|neighborhood]] $U$ of $x$ there is a [[D-YO6NZ|connected]] open set $V$ with $x\in V\subseteq U$.
:::
