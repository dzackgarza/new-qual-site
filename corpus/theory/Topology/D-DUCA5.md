::: {.definition}
Let $R$ be a ring and $M$ a left [[D-NQZUY|$R$-module]].
A subset $S\subseteq M$ is a \dfn{generating set} for $M$ if every $x\in M$ is a finite $R$-linear combination of elements of $S$: there exist $n\geq 0$, $s_1,\ldots,s_n\in S$, and $r_1,\ldots,r_n\in R$ with $x = \sum_{i=1}^n r_i s_i$.
:::
