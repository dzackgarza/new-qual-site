::: {.proposition}
Let $K\subseteq S^3$ be a tame knot, for example a smooth or polygonal one.
Then
$$
H_i(S^3 \sm K;\ZZ) \cong \begin{cases}
\ZZ & i = 0, 1,\\
0 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$
[@Hat02, Corollary 3.45, p. 255].
:::
