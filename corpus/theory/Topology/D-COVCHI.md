::: {.proposition}
Let $M_g$ and $M_h$ be closed orientable surfaces, and let $M_g\to M_h$ be an $n$-sheeted covering.
Then
$$
\chi(M_g) = n\,\chi(M_h),
$$
equivalently $g = n(h-1)+1$ [@Hat02, §2.2, Exercise 23].
:::
