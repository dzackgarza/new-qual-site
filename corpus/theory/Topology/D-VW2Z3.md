::: {.definition}
A topological space $X$, not necessarily Hausdorff, is \dfn{quasicompact} if every open [[D-AOJG3|cover]] of $X$ has a finite subcover.
A quasicompact space is \dfn{compact} if it is also [[D-6FMP3|Hausdorff]].
:::
