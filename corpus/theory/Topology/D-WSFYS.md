::: {.definition}
Let $A$, $B$, and $C$ be groups, and let $i\colon C\to A$ and $j\colon C\to B$ be homomorphisms.
The \dfn{free product with amalgamation} of $A$ and $B$ over $C$ is
$$
A \ast_C B \coloneqq \qty{A \ast B} / N,
$$
where $A\ast B$ is the [[D-JDDCP|free product]] and $N$ is the normal subgroup of $A\ast B$ generated by $\theset{ i(c) \inverseof{j(c)} \st c\in C }$ [@Hat02].
:::

::: {.proposition}
Together with the homomorphisms $A\to A\ast_C B$ and $B\to A\ast_C B$ induced by the inclusions of $A$ and $B$ into $A\ast B$, the group $A\ast_C B$ is the [[D-5S7PK|pushout]] of $A\xleftarrow{i} C\xrightarrow{j} B$ in $\Grp$.
:::

::: {.remark}
The [[T-BTPU4|Seifert--van Kampen theorem]] computes the fundamental group of a union of two open sets as an amalgamated product.
:::
