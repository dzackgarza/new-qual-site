::: {.proposition}
Let $(X, d_X)$ be a compact metric space and $(Y, d_Y)$ a metric space.
Every continuous map $f\colon X\to Y$ is uniformly continuous [@Mun00, Theorem 27.6]; [@Rud76].
:::
