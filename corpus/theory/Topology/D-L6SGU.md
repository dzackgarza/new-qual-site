::: {.definition}
A topological space $(X, \tau)$ is \dfn{compact} if $X$ is [[D-ZFRV4|Hausdorff]] and [[D-MQSFD|quasicompact]], that is, every open [[D-AOJG3|cover]] of $X$ has a finite subcover: for every family $(U_j)_{j\in J}$ in $\tau$ with $X = \bigcup_{j\in J} U_j$, there is a finite subset $J'\subseteq J$ with $X = \bigcup_{j\in J'} U_j$.
:::

::: {.remark}
This is the Bourbaki convention; the definition in [[D-EILKJ]] omits the Hausdorff condition.
:::
