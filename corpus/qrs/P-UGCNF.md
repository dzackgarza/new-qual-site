Prove that $\displaystyle{f(z)=-\frac{1}{2}\left(z+\frac{1}{z}\right)}$ is a conformal map from half disc $\{z=x+iy:\ |z|<1,\ y>0\}$ to upper

half plane ${\mathbb H}=\{z=x+iy:\ y>0\}$.
