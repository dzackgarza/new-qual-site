Suppose $A, B \subseteq \RR^n$ are disjoint and compact.
Prove that there exist $a\in A, b\in B$ such that
\[  
\norm{a - b} = \inf\theset{\norm{x-y} \suchthat x\in A,\, y\in B}
.\]
