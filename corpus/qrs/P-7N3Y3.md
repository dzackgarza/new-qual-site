Suppose $f$ is entire and there exist $A, R >0$ and natural number
$N$ such that $|f(z)| \leq A |z|^N$ for $|z| \geq R$. Show that (i)
$f$ is a polynomial and (ii) the degree of $f$ is at most $N$.
