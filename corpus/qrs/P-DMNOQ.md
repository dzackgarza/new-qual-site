a. Prove that if $c>0$,
\[
\abs{w_1} = c\abs{w_2} \implies \abs{w_1 - c^2 w_2} = c\abs{w_1 - w_2}
.\]

b. Prove that if $c>0$ and $c\neq 1$, with $z_1\neq z_2$, then the following equation represents a circle:
\[
\abs{z-z_1 \over z-z_2} = c
.\]
Find its center and radius.

> Hint: use part (a)
