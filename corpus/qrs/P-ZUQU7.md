Define
\[  
G \definedas \theset{z\in \CC\suchthat \Re(z) > 0, \, \abs{z-1} > 1}
.\]

Find all of the injective conformal maps $G\to \DD$.
These may be expressed as compositions of maps, but explain why this list is complete.
