Let $\Omega$ be the region inside the unit circle $\abs{z} = 1$ and outside the circle $\abs{z-{1\over 4}} = {1\over 4}$.

Find an injective conformal map from $\Omega$ onto some annulus $\theset{r < \abs{z} < 1}$ for constant $r$.
