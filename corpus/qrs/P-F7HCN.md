Prove the following:

a. $\sum_{n} nz^n$ does not converge at any point of $S^1$

b. $\sum_n {z^n \over n^2}$ converges at every point of $S^1$.

c. $\sum_n {z^n \over n}$ converges at every point of $S^1$ except $z=1$.
