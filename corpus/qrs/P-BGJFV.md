::: problem
Suppose $f$ is analytic in an open set containing the unit disc $\mathbb D$ and $|f(z)| =1$ when $|z|$=1. Show that either $f(z) = e^{i \theta}$ for some $\theta \in \mathbb R$ or there are finite number of $z_k \in \mathbb D$, $k \leq n$ and $\theta \in \mathbb R$ such that $\displaystyle f(z) = e^{i\theta} \prod_{k=1}^n \frac{z-z_k}{1 - \bar{z}_k z } \, .$

> Also cf.
> Stein et al, 1.4.7, 3.8.17
:::
