Let $f$ be non-constant, analytic in $\abs{z} > 0$, where $f(z_n) = 0$
for infinitely many points $z_n$ with $\lim_{n\to\infty} z_n = 0$.

Show that $z=0$ is an essential singularity for $f$.

> Example: $f(z) = \sin(1/z)$.
