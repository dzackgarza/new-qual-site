::: exercise
Show that if $X$ is second countable and Hausdorff, or a metric space, then TFAE:

- $X$ is compact

- Every infinite subset $A\subseteq X$ has a limit point in $X$.

- Every sequence in $X$ has a convergent subsequence in $X$.
:::
