::: problem
Let $x_0 = a, x_1 = b$, and set
\[  
x_n \definedas {x_{n-1} + x_{n-2} \over 2} \quad n\geq 2
.\]

Show that $\theset{x_n}$ is a Cauchy sequence and find its limit in terms of $a$ and $b$.
:::
