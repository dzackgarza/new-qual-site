Suppose $f$ is entire and there exist $A, R >0$ and natural number
$N$ such that $$|f(z)| \geq A |z|^N\ \text{for}\ |z| \geq R.$$ Show
that

1. $f$ is a polynomial and
2. the degree of $f$ is at least $N$.
