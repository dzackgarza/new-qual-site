::: problem
Let $F$ be a field.
Let $f(x)$ be an irreducible polynomial in $F[x]$ of degree $n$ and let $g(x)$ be any polynomial in $F[x]$.
Let $p(x)$ be an irreducible factor (of degree $m$) of the polynomial $f(g(x))$.

Prove that $n$ divides $m$.
Use this to prove that if $r$ is an integer which is not a perfect square, and $n$ is a positive integer then every irreducible factor of $x^{2n} - r$ over $\QQ[x]$ has even degree.
:::
