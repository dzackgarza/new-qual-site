Show that if $f: D(0, R) \rightarrow \mathbb{C}$ is holomorphic, with $|f(z)| \leq M$ for some $M>0$, then
\[
\left|\frac{f(z)-f(0)}{M^{2}-\overline{f(0)} f(z)}\right| \leq \frac{|z|}{M R} .
\]
