::: problem
For $k=1,2,\cdots, n$, suppose $\abs{a_k} < 1$ and
\[
f(z) \definedas \qty{z - a_1 \over 1 - \bar a_q z} \qty{z-a_2 \over 1 - \bar a_2 z} \cdots \qty{z - a_n \over 1 - \bar a_n z}
.\]
Show that $f(z) = b$ has $n$ solutions in $\abs{z} < 1$.
:::
