::: problem
Apply Rouché's Theorem to prove the Fundamental Theorem of Algebra: If $$P_n(z) = a_0 + a_1z + \cdots + a_{n-1}z^{n-1} + a_nz^n\quad  (a_n \neq 0)$$ is a polynomial of degree n, then it has n zeros in $\mathbb C$.
:::
