Define $f \colon [0,1] \to [-1,1]$ by
$$f(x):= \begin{cases} x\sin\big({\frac{1}{x}}\big) & 0 < x \leq 1 \\ 0 & x = 0 \end{cases}$$
Determine, with justification, whether $f$ is if bounded variation
on the interval $[0,1]$.
