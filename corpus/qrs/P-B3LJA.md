Prove that the sequence $\left(1+\frac{z}{n}\right)^{n}$ converges uniformly to $e^{z}$ on compact subsets of $\mathbb{C}$. 

> Hint: $e^{\pi \log w_{n}}=w_{n}^{n}$ and $e^{x}$ is uniform continuous on compact subsets of $\mathbb{C}$.
