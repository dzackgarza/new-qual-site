Let $f_n, f$ be analytic functions on the unit disk ${\mathbb D}$.
Show that the following are equivalent.

(i) $f_n(z)$ converges to $f(z)$ uniformly on compact subsets in
$\mathbb D$.

(ii) $\int_{|z|= r} |f_n(z) - f(z)| \, |dz|$ converges to $0$ if
$0< r<1$.
