Suppose $f_n:[0,1]\to\mathbb{R}$ are continuous
functions converging uniformly to $f:[0,1]\to\mathbb{R}$. Either
prove that
$\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}\int_{1/n}^1 f_n(x)\,dx=\int_0^1 f(x)\,dx$
or give a counterexample.
