::: problem
Suppose $f: \HH\union \RR\to \CC$ satisfies the following:

- $f(i) = i$

- $f$ is continuous

- $f$ is analytic on $\HH$

- $f(z) \in \RR \iff z\in \RR$.

Show that $f(\HH)$ is a dense subset of $\HH$.
:::
