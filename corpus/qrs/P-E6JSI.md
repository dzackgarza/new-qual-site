::: problem
Let $S\definedas \theset{z\in \DD\suchthat \Im(z) \geq 0}$.
Suppose $f:S\to \CC$ is continuous on $S$, real on $S\intersect \RR$, and holomorphic on $S^\circ$.

Prove that $f$ is the restriction of a holomorphic function on $\DD$.
:::
