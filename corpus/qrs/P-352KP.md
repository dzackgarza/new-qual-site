::: problem
Assume $f$ is continuous in the region: $x \geq x_0, \; 0 \leq y \leq b$ and the limit $$\displaystyle \lim_{x \rightarrow + \infty} f(x + iy) = A$$ exists uniformly with respect to $y$ (independent of $y$). Show that $$\lim_{x \rightarrow + \infty} \int_{\gamma_x} f(z) dz  = iA b \; , \; \;$$ where $\gamma_x : = \{ z \; | \; z = x + it, \; 0 \leq  t \leq b\}.$
:::

::: {.solution}
> **AI-Generated Solution**

**Goal:** If $f$ is continuous on $\{x \ge x_0, 0 \le y \le b\}$ and $\lim_{x \to +\infty} f(x + iy) = A$ uniformly in $y \in [0,b]$, show $\lim_{x \to +\infty} \int_{\gamma_x} f(z)\,dz = iAb$, where $\gamma_x$ is the vertical segment from $x$ to $x + ib$.

<1>1. Parametrize the segment: $\gamma_x: z(t) = x + it$, $t \in [0, b]$; then $\int_{\gamma_x} f = i\int_0^b f(x + it)\,dt$.
Proof: $\int_{\gamma_x} f(z)\,dz = \int_0^b f(x + it) \cdot i\,dt = i\int_0^b f(x + it)\,dt$.

<1>2. By uniform convergence, $\lim_{x \to +\infty} f(x + it) = A$ uniformly for $t \in [0, b]$.
Proof: Given $\eps > 0$, uniform convergence gives $X$ with $\abs{f(x + it) - A} < \eps$ for all $x > X$ and all $t \in [0,b]$.

<1>3. $\lim_{x \to +\infty} \int_0^b f(x + it)\,dt = \int_0^b A\,dt = Ab$.
Proof: For $x > X$ (from <1>2), $\abs{\int_0^b f(x+it)\,dt - Ab} = \abs{\int_0^b (f(x+it) - A)\,dt} \le \int_0^b \abs{f(x+it) - A}\,dt < b\eps$.

<1>4. Q.E.D. Proof: <1>1 and <1>3: $\int_{\gamma_x} f = i\int_0^b f(x + it)\,dt \to i\cdot Ab = iAb$.
:::
