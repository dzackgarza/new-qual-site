::: problem
Let $f$ and $g$ be non-zero analytic functions on a region $\Omega$.
Assume $|f(z)| = |g(z)|$ for all $z$ in $\Omega$.
Show that $f(z) = e^{i \theta} g(z)$ in $\Omega$ for some $0 \leq \theta < 2 \pi$.
:::
