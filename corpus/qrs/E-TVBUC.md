::: exercise
- Show that if $E\subseteq \RR^n$ is measurable with $\mu(E) < \infty$ and $f\in L^p(X)$ then $$\norm{f}_{L^p(X)} \converges{p\to\infty}\to \norm{f}_\infty.$$
:::
