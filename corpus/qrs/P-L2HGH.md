::: problem
Let $f\in L^1(\RR)$.
Show that
\[
\forall\varepsilon > 0 \exists \delta > 0 \text{ such that } \qquad 
m(E) < \delta 
\implies 
\int _{E} |f(x)| \, dx < \varepsilon
\]
:::
