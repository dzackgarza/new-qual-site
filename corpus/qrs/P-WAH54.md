::: problem
- Show that if $\theset{\vector v_i}$ is a basis for $V$ where $\dim(V) = n$ and $T(\vector v_i) = \vector v_{i+1 \mod n}$ then $T$ is diagonalizable with minimal polynomial $x^n-1$.
:::
