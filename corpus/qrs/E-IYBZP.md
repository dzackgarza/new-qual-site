::: exercise
If $f$ is holomorphic on a region $\Omega$ and for each $z_0\in\Omega$ at least one coefficient in the power series $f(z)=\sum_{n=0}^\infty c_n(z-z_0)^n$ is zero, show that $f$ is a polynomial.
