:::{.exercise title="Sinc Function"}
Show
\[
\int_{(0, \infty)} {\sin(x) \over x }\dx = {\pi \over 2}
.\]
:::

:::{.solution}
Take an indented semicircle.
Let $I$ be the original integral, then 
\[
I = {1\over 2i} \int_\RR {e^{iz} - 1 \over z } \dz
.\]

:::
