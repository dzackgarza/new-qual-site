Suppose that $f\in C([0,1])$ and that
$\displaystyle \int_0^1 f(x)x^n\,dx=0$ for all
$n=99,100,101,\ldots$. Show that $f\equiv 0$.\

> Note: Many variations on this problem exist. See June 2012 \#6b and
others.
