- $f\in L^1$ and $g$ smooth and compactly supported (and in fact $f\ast g$ is smooth)

- Show that if $f\in L^1$ and $g'$ exists with $\dd{g}{x_i}$ all bounded, then $$\dd{}{x_i}(f\ast g) = f \ast \dd{g}{x_i}$$
