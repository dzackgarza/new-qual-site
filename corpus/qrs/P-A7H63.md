::: problem
Find all entire functions satisfying
\[
\abs{f(z)} \leq \abs{z}^{1\over 2} \quad\text{ for } \abs{z} > 10
.\]
:::

::: {.solution}
> **AI-Generated Solution**

**Goal:** Find all entire functions with $\abs{f(z)} \leq \abs{z}^{1/2}$ for $\abs z > 10$.

<1>1. Write the Taylor expansion $f(z) = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} a_n z^n$.
    Proof: $f$ is entire, hence has a power series about $0$ converging on all of $\CC$.

<1>2. Cauchy's estimate: $\abs{a_n} \leq \frac{M(R)}{R^n}$ where $M(R) := \max_{\abs z = R} \abs{f(z)}$.
    Proof: Standard Cauchy estimate for the $n$-th Taylor coefficient on the circle of radius $R$.

<1>3. For $R > 10$ and $n \geq 1$, $\abs{a_n} \leq \frac{R^{1/2}}{R^n} = R^{1/2 - n}$.
    Proof: <1>2 with $M(R) \leq R^{1/2}$ by hypothesis.

<1>4. $a_n = 0$ for all $n \geq 1$.
    Proof: <1>3 holds for arbitrarily large $R$, and $R^{1/2 - n} \to 0$ as $R \to \infty$ when $n \geq 1$; hence $\abs{a_n} = 0$.

<1>5. $f$ is constant: $f \equiv a_0$.
    Proof: <1>1 and <1>4 leave only the constant term.

<1>6. $a_0 = 0$.
    Proof: $\abs{a_0} = \abs{f(z)} \leq \abs{z}^{1/2}$ for all $\abs z > 10$ (e.g. along $z = R > 10$, letting $R \to \infty$), forcing $a_0 = 0$.

<1>7. Q.E.D.
    Proof: <1>5 and <1>6 show the only such function is $f \equiv 0$; conversely $f \equiv 0$ clearly satisfies the bound.

:::
