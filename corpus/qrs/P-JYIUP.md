Show that

\[
\int_{0}^{\infty} \frac{\sin x}{x} d x=\frac{\pi}{2}
.\]

> Hint: use the fact that this integral equals $\frac{1}{2 i} \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \frac{e^{i x}-1}{x} d x$, and integrate around an indented semicircle.
