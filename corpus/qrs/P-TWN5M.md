::: problem
Geometrically describe the following subsets of $\CC$:

a. $\abs{z-1} = 1$
b. $\abs{z-1} = 2\abs{z-2}$
c. $1/z = \bar z$
d. $\Re(z) = 3$
e. $\Im(z) = a$ with $a\in \RR$.
f. $\Re(z) > a$ with $a\in \RR$.
g. $\abs{z-1} < 2\abs{z-2}$
:::
