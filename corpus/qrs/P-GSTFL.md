Suppose $\theset{g_n}$ is a uniformly convergent sequence of functions from $\RR$ to $\RR$ and $f:\RR\to \RR$ is uniformly continuous.
Prove that the sequence $\theset{f\circ g_n}$ is uniformly convergent.
