Suppose $f:\DD\to \DD$ is analytic.
Prove that 
\[  
\forall a\in \DD, \qquad {\abs{f'(a)} \over 1 - \abs{f(a)}^2 } \leq {1 \over 1 - \abs{a}^2}
.\]
