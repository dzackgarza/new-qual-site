::: problem
- Show that if $M^\text{orientable} \mapsvia{\pi_k} M^\text{non-orientable}$ is a $k\dash$fold cover, then $k$ is even or $\infty$.

- Show that $M$ is orientable if $\pi_1(M)$ has no subgroup of index 2.
:::
