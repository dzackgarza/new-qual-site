Let $E$ be an extension field of $F$ and $\alpha\in E$ be algebraic of odd degree over $F$.

a.
Show that $F(\alpha) = F(\alpha^2)$.

b.
Prove that $\alpha^{2020}$ is algebraic of odd degree over $F$.
