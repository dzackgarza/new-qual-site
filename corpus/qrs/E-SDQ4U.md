::: exercise
- Prove the following inclusions of $L^p$ spaces for $m(X) < \infty$:
\[
L^\infty(X) &\subset L^2(X) \subset L^1(X) \\
\ell^2(\ZZ) &\subset \ell^1(\ZZ) \subset \ell^\infty(\ZZ)
.\]
:::
