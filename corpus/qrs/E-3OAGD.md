::: exercise
- $\star$: Show that if $E\subseteq \RR^n$ is measurable then $m(E) = \sup \theset{ m(K) \suchthat K\subset E\text{ compact}}$ iff for all $\eps> 0$ there exists a compact $K\subseteq E$ such that $m(K) \geq m(E) - \eps$.
:::
