Prove that the distinct complex numbers $z_1, z_2, z_3$ are the vertices of an equilateral triangle if and only if
\[
z_{1}^{2}+z_{2}^{2}+z_{3}^{2}=z_{1} z_{2}+z_{2} z_{3}+z_{3} z_{1}
.\]
