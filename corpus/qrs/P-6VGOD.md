-   What happens if we simply assume that $f$ is analytic for
    $|z| < 1$ and $|f(z)| < 1$ for $|z| < 1$? Suppose that $f(z)
    \not\equiv  z$. Can f have more than one fixed point in
    $|z| < 1$?

> Hint: The map
$\displaystyle{\psi_{\alpha}(z)=\frac{\alpha-z}{1-\bar{\alpha}z}}$
> may be useful.
