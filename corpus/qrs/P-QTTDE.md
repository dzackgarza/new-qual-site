::: {.problem title="Hungerford 1.9.3"}
Let $F$ be a free group and for a fixed integer $n$, let $H_n$ be the subgroup generated by the set $\{ x^n \mid x \in F \}$.
Show that $H_n \trianglelefteq F$.
:::
