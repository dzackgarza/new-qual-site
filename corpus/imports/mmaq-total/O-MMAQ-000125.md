::: problem
Let $f$ be a meromorphic function in the plane such that
`\begin{align*}
\lim_{|z|\to\infty} |f(z)| = \infty
\end{align*}`{=tex}

1.  Show that $f$ has only finitely many poles.

2.  Show that $f$ is a rational function.
:::
