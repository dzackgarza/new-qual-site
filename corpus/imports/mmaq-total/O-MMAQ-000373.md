::: problem
Define a family $\mct$ of subsets of $\RR$ by saying that $A \in T$ is $\iff A = \emptyset$ or $\RR \setminus A$ is a finite set.

Prove that $\mct$ is a topology on $\RR$, and that $\RR$ is compact with respect to this topology.
:::
