::: problem
Let $(f_n)$ be a sequence of holomorphic functions in a domain $D$.
Suppose that $f_n \to f$ uniformly on each compact subset of $D$.
Show that

-   $f$ is holomorphic on $D$.

-   $f_n' \to f'$ uniformly on each compact subset of $D$.
:::
