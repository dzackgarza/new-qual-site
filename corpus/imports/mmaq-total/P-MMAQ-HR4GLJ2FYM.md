::: problem
Let $\theset{a_n}$ be a sequence of real numbers such that
$$
\theset{b_n} \in \ell^2(\NN) \implies \sum a_n b_n < \infty.
$$
Show that $\sum a_n^2 < \infty$.

> Note: Assume $a_n, b_n$ are all non-negative.
:::
