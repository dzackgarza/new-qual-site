::: problem
1.  Give an example of a continuous $f\in L^1(\RR)$ such that $f(x) \not\to 0$ as$\abs x \to \infty$.

2.  Show that if $f$ is *uniformly* continuous, then
    $$
    \lim_{\abs{x} \to \infty} f(x) = 0.
    $$
:::
