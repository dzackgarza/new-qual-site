::: problem
Let $\phi\in L^\infty(\RR)$. Show that the following limit exists and satisfies the equality
$$
\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty}\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}} \frac{|\phi(x)|^{n}}{1+x^{2}} d x\right)^{\frac{1}{n}} = \norm{\phi}_\infty.
$$
:::
