::: problem
Let $1 \leq p,q \leq \infty$ be conjugate exponents, and show that
$$
f \in L^p(\RR^n) \implies \|f\|_{p}=\sup _{\|g\|_{q}=1}\left|\int f(x) g(x) d x\right|
$$
:::
