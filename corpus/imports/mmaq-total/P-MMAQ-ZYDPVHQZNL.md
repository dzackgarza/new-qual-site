::: problem
Let
$$
X = \theset{(0, y) \mid - 1 \leq y \leq 1} \cup \theset{\qty{x, s = \sin\qty{1 \over x}} \mid 0 < x \leq 1}
.$$

Prove that $X$ is connected but not path connected.
:::
