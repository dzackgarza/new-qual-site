::: problem
Let $X$ be a complete metric space and define a norm
$$
\|f\|:=\max \{|f(x)|: x \in X\}.
$$

Show that $(C^0(\RR), \norm{\wait} )$ (the space of continuous functions $f: X\to \RR$) is complete.
:::
