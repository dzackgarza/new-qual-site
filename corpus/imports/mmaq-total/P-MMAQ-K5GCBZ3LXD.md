::: problem
Use $n$-th roots of unity (i.e. solutions of $z^n - 1 =0$) to show
that
$$2^{n-1} \sin\frac{\pi}{n} \sin\frac{2\pi}{n} \cdots \sin\frac{(n-1)\pi}{n}
= n
\; .$$

> Hint: $1 - \cos 2 \theta = 2 \sin^2 \theta,\; \sin 2 \theta = 2 \sin \theta \cos \theta$.
:::
