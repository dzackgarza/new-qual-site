::: problem
Let $X$ be a topological space.

i.  State what it means for $X$ to be compact.

ii.
Let $X = \theset{0} \cup \theset{{1\over n} \mid n \in \ZZ^+ }$.
Is $X$ compact?

iii.
Let $X = (0, 1]$.
Is $X$ compact?
:::
