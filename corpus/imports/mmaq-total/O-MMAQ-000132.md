::: problem
Prove that
$$
\left|\frac{d^{n}}{d x^{n}} \frac{\sin x}{x}\right| \leq \frac{1}{n}
$$

for all $x \neq 0$ and positive integers $n$.

> Hint: Consider $\displaystyle\int_0^1 \cos(tx) dt$
:::
