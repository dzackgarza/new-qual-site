::: problem
Prove that if $(X, d)$ is a compact metric space, $f : X \to X$ is a
continuous map, and $C$ is a constant with $0 < C < 1$ such that
$$
d(f (x), f (y)) \leq C \cdot d(x, y) \quad \forall x, y
,$$
then $f$ has a fixed point.
:::
