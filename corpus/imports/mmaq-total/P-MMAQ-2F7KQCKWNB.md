::: problem
Let $f(x, y)$ on $[-1, 1]^2$ be defined by
$$
f(x, y) = \begin{cases}
\frac{x y}{\left(x^{2}+y^{2}\right)^{2}} & (x, y) \neq (0, 0) \\
0 & (x, y) = (0, 0)
\end{cases}
$$
Determine if $f$ is integrable.
:::
