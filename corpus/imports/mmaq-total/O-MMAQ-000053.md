::: problem
Assume $f$ is continuous in the region: $x \geq x_0, \; 0 \leq y \leq b$ and the limit $$\displaystyle \lim_{x \rightarrow + \infty} f(x + iy) = A$$ exists uniformly with respect to $y$ (independent of $y$).

Show that $$\lim_{x \rightarrow + \infty} \int_{\gamma_x} f(z) dz  = iA b \; , \; \;$$ where $\gamma_x : = \{ z \; | \; z = x + it, \; 0 \leq  t \leq b\}.$
:::
