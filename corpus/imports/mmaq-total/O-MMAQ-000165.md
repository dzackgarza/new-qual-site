::: problem
Define
$$
f(x) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^{x}}.
$$

Show that $f$ converges to a differentiable function on $(1, \infty)$ and that
$$
f'(x)  =\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left(\frac{1}{n^{x}}\right)^{\prime}.
$$

> Hint:
> $$
> \left(\frac{1}{n^{x}}\right)^{\prime}=-\frac{1}{n^{x}} \ln n
> $$
:::
