::: problem
Let
$$
f_{n}(x)=n x(1-x)^{n}, \quad n \in \mathbb{N}.
$$

1.  Show that $f_n \to 0$ pointwise but not uniformly on $[0, 1]$.

    > Hint: Consider the maximum of $f_n$.

2.  $$
    \lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} \int_{0}^{1} n(1-x)^{n} \sin x d x=0
    $$
:::
