::: problem
Let `\begin{align*} X=\left\{(x, y) \in \mathbb{R}^{2} | x>0, y \geq 0, \text { and } \frac{y}{x} \text { is rational }\right\} \end{align*}`{=tex} and equip $X$ with the subspace topology induced by the usual topology on $\RR^2$.

Prove or disprove that $X$ is connected.
:::
