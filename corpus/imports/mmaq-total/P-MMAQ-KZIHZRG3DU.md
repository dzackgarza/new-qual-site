::: problem
Let $G$ be a finite group with $n$ distinct conjugacy classes.
Let $g_1 \cdots g_n$ be representatives of the conjugacy classes of $G$.

Prove that if $g_i g_j = g_j g_i$ for all $i, j$ then $G$ is abelian.
:::
