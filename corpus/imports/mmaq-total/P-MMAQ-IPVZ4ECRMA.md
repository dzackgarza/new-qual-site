::: problem
Is there a covering map from
$$
X_3 = \theset{x^2 + y^2 = 1} \cup \theset{(x - 2)^2 + y^2 = 1} \cup \theset{(x + 2)^2 + y^2 = 1} \subset \RR^2
$$
to the wedge of two $S^1$'s? If there is, give an example; if not, give a proof.
:::
