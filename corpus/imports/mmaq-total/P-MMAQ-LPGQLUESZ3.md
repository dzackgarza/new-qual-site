::: problem
Let $f: [0, 1] \to \RR$ be continuous.
Show that
$$
\sup \left\{\|f g\|_{1} \suchthat g \in L^{1}[0,1],~~ \|g\|_{1} \leq 1\right\}=\|f\|_{\infty}
$$
:::
