::: problem
Let $f(x) = x^2$ and $E \subset [0, \infty) \definedas \RR^+$.

1.  Show that
    $$
    m^*(E) = 0 \iff m^*(f(E)) = 0.
    $$

2.  Deduce that the map

`\begin{align*}
\phi: \mathcal{L}(\RR^+) &\to \mathcal{L}(\RR^+) \\
E &\mapsto f(E)
\end{align*}`{=tex}
is a bijection from the class of Lebesgue measurable sets of $[0, \infty)$ to itself.
:::
