::: problem
Let $C$ and $C'$ be two circles and let $z_1 \in C$, $z_2 \notin C$,
$z'_1 \in C'$, $z'_2 \notin C'$. Show that there is a unique
fractional linear transformation $f$ with $f(C) = C'$ and
$f(z_1) = z'_1$, $f(z_2) = z'_2$.
:::
