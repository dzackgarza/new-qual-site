::: problem
Show that the space $C^1([a, b])$ is a Banach space when equipped with the norm
$$
\|f\|:=\sup _{x \in[a, b]}|f(x)|+\sup _{x \in[a, b]}\left|f^{\prime}(x)\right|.
$$
:::
