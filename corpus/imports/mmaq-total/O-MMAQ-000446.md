::: problem
For any $n \geq 1$ let $S^n = \theset{(x_0 , \cdots , x_n )\mid \sum x_i^2 =1}$ denote the $n$ dimensional unit sphere and let $$E = \theset{(x_0 , . . . , x_n )\mid x_n = 0}$$ denote the "equator".

Find, for all $k$, the relative homology $H_k (S^n , E)$.
:::
