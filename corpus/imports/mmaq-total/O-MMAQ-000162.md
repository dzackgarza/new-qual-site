::: problem
Let $E \subset \RR$ be measurable with $m(E) < \infty$.
Define
$$
f(x)=m(E \cap(E+x)).
$$

Show that

1.  $f\in L^1(\RR)$.
2.  $f$ is uniformly continuous.
3.  $\lim _{|x| \rightarrow \infty} f(x)=0$

> Hint:
> $$
> \chi_{E \cap(E+x)}(y)=\chi_{E}(y) \chi_{E}(y-x)
> $$
:::
