::: problem
Let $g\in L^\infty([0, 1])$
Prove that
$$
\int_{[0,1]} f(x) g(x) d x=0 \quad\text{for all continuous } f:[0, 1] \to \RR \implies g(x) = 0 \text{ almost everywhere. }
$$
:::
