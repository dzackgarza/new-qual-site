::: problem
Let $p$ be a prime number.
Let $A$ be a $p \times p$ matrix over a field $F$ with 1 in all entries except 0 on the main diagonal.

Determine the Jordan canonical form (JCF) of $A$

(a) When $F = \QQ$,

(b) When $F = \FF_p$.

> Hint: In both cases, all eigenvalues lie in the ground field.
> In each case find a matrix $P$ such that $P\inv AP$ is in JCF.
:::
