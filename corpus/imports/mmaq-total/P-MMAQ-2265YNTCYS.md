::: problem
Let $X = S_1 \cup S_2 \subset \RR^3$ be the union of two spheres of radius 2, one about $(1, 0, 0)$ and the other about $(-1, 0, 0)$, i.e.  `\begin{align*} S_1 &= \theset{(x, y,z) \mid (x-1)^2 + y^2 +z^2 = 4} \\ S_2 &= \theset{(x, y, z) \mid (x + 1)^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 4} .\end{align*}`{=tex}

a.  Give a description of $X$ as a CW complex.

b.  Write out the cellular chain complex of $X$.

c.  Calculate $H_* (X; Z)$.
:::
