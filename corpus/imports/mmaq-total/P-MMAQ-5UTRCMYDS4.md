::: problem
Let $(X, \mathcal M, \mu)$ be a measure space and suppose $f$ is a measurable function on $X$.
Show that
$$
\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} \int_{X} f^{n} ~d \mu =
\begin{cases}
\infty & or \\
\mu(f\inv(1)),
\end{cases}
$$
and characterize the collection of functions of each type.
:::
