::: problem
(1) Let $f(z) \in H({\mathbb D})$, $\text{Re}(f(z)) >0$, $f(0)= a>0$.
Show that $$\abs{ \frac{f(z)-a}{f(z)+a}} \leq |z|, \; \; \;
|f'(0)| \leq 2a.$$

(2) Show that the above is still true if $\text{Re}(f(z)) >0$ is replaced with $\text{Re}(f(z)) \geq 0$.
:::
