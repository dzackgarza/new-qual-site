::: problem
If $f$ is a nonnegative measurable function on $\mathbb{R}$ and $p > 0$, show that
$$\int f^p ~dx = \int_0^{\infty} p t^{p-1} \abs{\{x : f(x) > t\}} ~dt$$
where $\abs{\{x : f(x) > t\}}$ is the Lebesgue measure of the set $\{x : f(x) > t\}$.
:::
