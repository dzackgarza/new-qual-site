::: problem
Suppose $f$ is entire and there exist $A, R >0$ and natural number $N$ such that $$|f(z)| \geq A |z|^N\ \text{for}\ |z| \geq R.$$ Show that

(i) $f$ is a polynomial and

(ii) the degree of $f$ is at least $N$.
:::
