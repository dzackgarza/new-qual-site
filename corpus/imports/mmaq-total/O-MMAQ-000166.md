::: problem
Let $f, g: [a, b] \to \RR$ be measurable with
$$
\int_{a}^{b} f(x) ~d x=\int_{a}^{b} g(x) ~d x.
$$

Show that either

1. $f(x) = g(x)$ almost everywhere, or

2. There exists a measurable set $E \subset [a, b]$ such that
   $$
   \int_{E} f(x) ~d x>\int_{E} g(x) ~d x
   $$
:::
