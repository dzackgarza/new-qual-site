::: problem
Let $\zeta = e^{2\pi i/8}$.

(a) What is the degree of $\QQ(\zeta)/\QQ$?

(b) How many quadratic subfields of $\QQ(\zeta)$ are there?

(c) What is the degree of $\QQ(\zeta, \sqrt[4] 2)$ over $\QQ$?
:::
