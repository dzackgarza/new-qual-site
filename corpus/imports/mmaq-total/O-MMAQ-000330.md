::: problem
Show that the order of the group $\text{GL}_n(\mathbb F_q)$ of invertible $n\times n$ matrices over the field $\mathbb F_q$ of $q$ elements is given by $(q^n-1)(q^n-q)\dots(q^n-q^{n-1})$.
:::
