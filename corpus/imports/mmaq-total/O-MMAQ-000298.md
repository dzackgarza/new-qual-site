::: problem
Let $\overline{\mathbb F_p}$ denote the algebraic closure of $\mathbb F_p$. Show that
the Galois group $\Gal(\overline{\mathbb F_p}/\mathbb F_p)$
has no non-trivial finite subgroups.
:::
