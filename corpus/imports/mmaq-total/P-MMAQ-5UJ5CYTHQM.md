::: problem
Prove that there does not exist a continuous map $f : S^2 \to S^2$ from the unit sphere in $\RR^3$ to itself such that $f (\vector x) \perp \vector x$
(as vectors in $\RR^3$ for all $\vector x \in S^2$).
:::
