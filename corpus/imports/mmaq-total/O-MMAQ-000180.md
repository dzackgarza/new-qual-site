::: problem
Let $f: [1, \infty) \to \RR$ such that $f(1) = 1$ and
$$
f^{\prime}(x)= \frac{1} {x^{2}+f(x)^{2}}
$$

Show that the following limit exists and satisfies the equality
$$
\lim _{x \rightarrow \infty} f(x) \leq 1 + \frac \pi 4
$$
:::
