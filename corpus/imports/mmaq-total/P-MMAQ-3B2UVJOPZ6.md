::: problem
Let $f\in L^1(\RR)$. Show that
$$
\forall\varepsilon > 0 ~~\exists \delta > 0 \text{ such that } m(E) < \delta \implies \int_{E}|f(x)| d x<\varepsilon
$$
:::
