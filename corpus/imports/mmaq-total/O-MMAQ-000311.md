::: problem
Let
`\begin{align*}
R=\mathbb Z[x]/(x^2+x+1).
\end{align*}`{=tex}

- Answer the following questions with suitable justification.
  - Is $R$ a Noetherian ring?
  - Is $R$ an Artinian ring?
- Prove that $R$ is an integrally closed domain.
:::
