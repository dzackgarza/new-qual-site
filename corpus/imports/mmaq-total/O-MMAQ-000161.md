::: problem
Let $f$ be Lebesgue measurable on $\RR$ and $E \subset \RR$ be measurable such that
$$
0<A=\int_{E} f(x) d x<\infty.
$$

Show that for every $0 < t < 1$, there exists a measurable set $E_t \subset E$ such that
$$
\int_{E_{t}} f(x) d x=t A.
$$
:::
