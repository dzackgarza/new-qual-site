For any positive integer $n$, let $G_n$ be the group generated by
$a$ and $b$ subject to the following three relations:
\begin{align*}
a^2=1, \quad b^2=1, \quad \text{and} \quad (ab)^n=1.
.\end{align*}

- Find the order of the group $G_n$
:::
