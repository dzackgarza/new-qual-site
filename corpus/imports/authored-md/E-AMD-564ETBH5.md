\envlist
\[
a + \nilrad{R} \text{ nilpotent } &\implies (a+ \nilrad{R})^n \definedas a^n + \nilrad{R}= \nilrad{R} \\
&\implies a^n \in \nilrad{R} \\
&\implies \exists \ell \text{ such that } (a^n)^\ell = 0 \\
&\implies a\in \nilrad{R}
.
\]
:::
