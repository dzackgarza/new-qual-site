::: {.problem}
Given: $H \subseteq G, P \in \text{Syl}_p(G)$

Show: $\exists g \in G \ni gPg^{-1} \in Syl_p(H)$

Given: $H \normal G$

Show: $P\cap H \in \text{Syl}_p(H)$

Given: $P  \normal G$

Show: $P \cap H \in \text{Syl}_p(H)$ and $|\text{Syl}_p(H)| = 1$
:::
