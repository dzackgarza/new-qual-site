::: {.problem}
Given: $G \actson X, \quad |G| < \infty , \quad 1 < |X| < \infty$

- Show: $\exists g\in G$ such that $\forall x\in X, g\actson x \neq x$

- Show: This holds if $|G| = \infty$, but not if $|X| = \infty$ as well.
:::
