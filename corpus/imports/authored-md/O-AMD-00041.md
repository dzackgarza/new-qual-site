::: {.problem}
Given: $G \actson X$ transitively, $H \normal G$

- Show: $H \actson X$, but possibly not transitively

- Show: $G$ acts transitively on $\theset{ \mathcal{O}_{h} : h\ \in H}$

- Show: $\forall i, j, |\mathcal{O}_{h_i}| = |\mathcal{O}_{h_j}|$

- Given: $x\in \mathcal{O}_h$ Show: $|\mathcal{O}_h| = |H : H \cap G_x|$

- Show: $|\{\mathcal{O}_h\}_{h\in H}| = [G : HG_x]$
:::
