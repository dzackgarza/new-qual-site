::: {.problem}
Show: TFAE; $\text{clos}(H)$ satisfies:

- It is the smallest normal subgroup of $G$ containing $H$.

- It is the subgroup generated by all conjugates of $H$.

- $\text{clos}(H) = \intersect_{H \leq N \normal G} N$

- If $\phi: G \rightarrow X$ and $\phi(H) = e$, then $\phi$ factors through $G/\text{clos}(H)$
:::
