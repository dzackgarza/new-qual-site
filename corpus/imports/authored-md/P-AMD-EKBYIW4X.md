::: {.problem}
$|G| < \infty$
- Given: $G$ is characteristically simple
   - Show: $\exists H~(\text{simple})~ \ni G \cong H^n$.
   - Show: Whether or not the converse holds
- Given: $N \normal G$ minimal
   - Show: $N$ is characteristically simple, $N \cong H^n$
:::
