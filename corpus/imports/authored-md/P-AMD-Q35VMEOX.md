::: {.problem}
Let $G$ be a topological group.
Show that $H_*(G)$ is an algebra.
Show that $G\actson H_*(G)$, which factors through the homomorphism $G \into \pi_0(G)$ yielding a trivial action if $G$ is path-connected.
:::
