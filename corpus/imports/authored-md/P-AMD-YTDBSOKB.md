::: {.problem}
For each of these actions of $\mathbb{Z}_2$ on $S^n$, compute $\pi_1(S^n/\mathbb{Z}_2)$

1. $S^1, z\mapsto -z$

2. $S^2, (x,y,z) \mapsto (-x,-y,z)$

3. $S^3, (z,w) \mapsto (-z, -w)$
:::
