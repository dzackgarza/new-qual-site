::: {.problem}
Compute a presentation of $\pi_1(T)$ and prove it is isomorphic to $\mathbb{Z}^2$.
:::

::: {.remark}
The source prints $\mathbb{Z}_2$. The torus has $\pi_1(T) = \gens{a, b \suchthat aba\inv b\inv} \cong \ZZ\cross\ZZ$, and $\ZZ/2 \not\cong \ZZ^2$; Hatcher records the torus as a $K(\ZZ\cross\ZZ, 1)$ in §2.2.
That $T$ is the torus rather than $\RP^2$, whose fundamental group really is $\ZZ/2$, is fixed by the next item of the same source list, which asks the reader to show $T \setminus D^1 \simeq S^1 \vee S^1$.
:::
