::: {.exercise}
Analyze groups of order $pq$ with $q<p$.

> Hint: consider the cases when $p$ does or does not divide $q-1$.

- Show that if $q$ does not divide $p-1$, then $G$ is cyclic.

- Show that $G$ is never simple.
:::
