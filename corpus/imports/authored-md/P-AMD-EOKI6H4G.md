::: {.problem}
Given: $H \leq G$.
Show: $\text{core}(H)$ is

- The largest $N \normal G, N \subseteq H$

- Generated by all normal subgroups contained in $H$

- Given by $\intersect_{g\in G} gHg^{-1}$

- The kernel of $G \actson \frac{G}{H} \ni x \actson gH = (xg)H$
:::
