**Goal:** Apply the Seifert-van Kampen theorem to compute the fundamental group $\pi_1(X, x_0)$ of a space $X$ given as the union of path-connected open subsets $U, V$ with path-connected intersection $U \cap V$.

<1>1. Statement of the Seifert-van Kampen Theorem.
<2>1. Let $X$ be a topological space covered by two open subsets $U$ and $V$ such that $X = U \cup V$.
<2>2. Suppose $U, V$, and their intersection $U \cap V$ are path-connected, and let $x_0 \in U \cap V$ be a common basepoint.
<2>3. Let $i \colon U \cap V \hookrightarrow U$ and $j \colon U \cap V \hookrightarrow V$ be the inclusion maps.
<2>4. The Seifert-van Kampen theorem asserts that the natural homomorphism induced by the inclusions $k \colon U \hookrightarrow X$ and $l \colon V \hookrightarrow X$, $$\Phi \colon \pi_1(U, x_0) *_{\pi_1(U \cap V, x_0)} \pi_1(V, x_0) \xrightarrow{\cong} \pi_1(X, x_0),$$ is an isomorphism from the amalgamated free product to $\pi_1(X, x_0)$.
<2>5. Proof: Standard Seifert-van Kampen theorem (e.g. Hatcher Theorem 1.20). Q.E.D.

<1>2. Presentation formulation.
<2>1. If $\pi_1(U, x_0) \cong \langle S_U \mid R_U \rangle$ and $\pi_1(V, x_0) \cong \langle S_V \mid R_V \rangle$ are presentations, and $\pi_1(U \cap V, x_0)$ is generated by a set $S_{UV}$, then: $$\pi_1(X, x_0) \cong \langle S_U \sqcup S_V \mid R_U \sqcup R_V \sqcup \{ i_*(g) j_*(g)^{-1} : g \in S_{UV} \} \rangle.$$ <2>2. Proof: Definition of amalgamated free product of groups.
Q.E.D.

<1>3. Q.E.D. <2>1. Proof: By <1>1 and <1>2.
