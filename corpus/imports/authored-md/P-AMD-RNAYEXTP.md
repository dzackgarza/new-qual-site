::: {.problem}
For each of the following $f: S^1 \rightarrow S^1$, identify the corresponding $f_*: \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{Z}$:

1. $z\mapsto z^n$

2. $\bar{x} \mapsto -\bar{x}$

3. $e^{i\theta} \mapsto e^{2\pi i\sin\theta}$
:::
