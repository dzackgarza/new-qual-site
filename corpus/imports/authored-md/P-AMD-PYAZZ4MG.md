**REVISIT** Let $f,g : S^1 \rightarrow X$, $P = X \cup_f B^2 \cong X \coprod B^2 / \sim$, where $x \sim f(x)$, $Q = X \cup_g B^2$.
Show that $f\simeq g \implies P\simeq Q$.
