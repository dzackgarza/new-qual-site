::: {.problem}
Let $\Lambda_*$ be the exterior algebra generated by the symbols $\theset{dx_i}^n$ over a field $k$.
Show that letting $d  = \cdot \vee dx_1$ yields a chain complex $0 \into \Lambda^0 \into \Lambda^1 \into \cdots \into \Lambda^n \into 0$ with trivial homology.
Compute what happens when $dx_1$ is replaced with an arbitrary non-zero element in $\Lambda^1$.
:::
