::: {.problem}
Suppose $X = \union_i^n A_i$ such that for any $1\leq k \leq n,~ \intersect_i^k A_i$ is either empty or contractible, show $i\geq n-1 \implies \tilde H_i(X) = 0$ and that this bound is sharp.
:::
