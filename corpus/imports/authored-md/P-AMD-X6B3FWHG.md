Given: $|G| < \infty$
- Show $\Phi(G)~\text{char}~ G$
- Show $\Phi(G)$ is nilpotent
- Given: $|P| = p^e$
   - Show: $P / \Phi(P)$ is an elementary abelian p-group
   - Show:$N \normal P, P/N$ is elementary abelian $\implies \Phi(P) \subseteq N$
:::
