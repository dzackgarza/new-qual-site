::: {.exercise}
Let $p$ be a prime and $\abs{G} = p^3$. 
Prove that $G$ has a normal subgroup $N$ of order $p^2$.
- Suppose $N = \gens{h}$ is cyclic and classify all possibilities for $G$ if:
  - $\abs h = p^3$
  - $\abs h = p$.

  > Hint: Sylow and semidirect products.
:::
