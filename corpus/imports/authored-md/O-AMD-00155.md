::: {.problem}
Let $R = \ZZ[x,y]$, and $M = R/(x-y), N = R/(x,y)$.
Construct free resolutions of $M,N$ to compute:

- $\ext_R^*(M, M)$

- $\ext_R^*(M, N)$

- $\ext_R^*(N, M)$

- $\ext_R^*(N, N)$
:::
