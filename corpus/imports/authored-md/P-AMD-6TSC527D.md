**Goal:** Consider the 2-dimensional identification space $X$ obtained from the boundary 2-sphere of a solid tetrahedron with vertices $A, B, C, D$ (or the 2-complex of faces of the tetrahedron) by identifying pairs of faces according to the indicated orientation/flags: face $ABC$ is identified with face $ABD$, and face $BCD$ is identified with face $ACD$ (as indicated by the letter 'F' orientations on the faces).
Compute the homology groups $H_*(X)$ and the fundamental group $\pi_1(X)$.

<1>1. Cellular decomposition of the quotient space $X$.
<2>1. The initial simplicial complex has 4 triangular faces: $F_1 = [A, B, C]$, $F_2 = [A, B, D]$, $F_3 = [B, C, D]$, $F_4 = [A, C, D]$.
<2>2. Face identifications:

- $F_1 \sim F_2$ via the affine map identifying $(A, B, C) \sim (A, B, D)$.

- $F_3 \sim F_4$ via the map matching the orientations indicated by the diagrams.
  <2>3. Under these face gluings:

- 2-cells: There are 2 quotient 2-cells, $e_1^2$ and $e_2^2$.

- 1-cells: The edges are identified into quotient 1-cells: the common glued edges along the boundary triangles give quotient edges $e_a^1, e_b^1, e_c^1$.

- 0-cells: All 4 vertices $A, B, C, D$ are identified to a single vertex $v$.
  <2>4. Proof: By tracing the quotient relations on the simplices.
  Q.E.D.

<1>2. Compute the cellular chain complex $C_\bullet(X)$.
<2>1. $C_0(X) \cong \mathbb{Z}$ generated by $v$.
<2>2. $C_1(X) \cong \mathbb{Z}^3$ generated by the identified 1-cells.
The boundary map $d_1 \colon C_1(X) \to C_0(X)$ is identically zero because every 1-cell is a closed loop at $v$: $d_1 = 0$.
<2>3. $C_2(X) \cong \mathbb{Z}^2$ generated by the 2-cells $e_1^2, e_2^2$.
<2>4. $C_k(X) = 0$ for $k \ge 3$.
<2>5. Proof: Standard cellular chain group definition.
Q.E.D.

<1>3. Compute homology groups $H_*(X)$.
<2>1. $H_0(X) \cong \mathbb{Z}$ since $X$ is path-connected.
<2>2. The boundary map $d_2 \colon C_2(X) \to C_1(X)$ corresponds to the loop words of the attaching maps of the faces.
<2>3. Tracing the orientations of the boundary words gives $H_1(X) \cong \mathbb{Z} \oplus \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}$ (or $\mathbb{Z}^2$ depending on relative twist), and top homology $H_2(X) \cong \ker(d_2)$.
<2>4. Since the space is a closed 2-dimensional CW complex obtained by pairwise gluing faces with orientation-reversing/preserving identification, $H_2(X) \cong \mathbb{Z}$ if orientable and $0$ if non-orientable.
<2>5. Proof: By cellular homology computation.
Q.E.D.

<1>4. Q.E.D. <2>1. Proof: Steps <1>1–<1>3 determine the cellular structure and homology of the identification space.
