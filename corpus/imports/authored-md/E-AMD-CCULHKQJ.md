::: {.exercise}
Show that if $K/E/F$ with $K/F$ Galois then $K/E$ is always Galois with $g(K/E) \leq g(K/F)$.

- Show additionally $E/F$ is Galois $\iff g(K/E) \normal g(K/F)$.

- Show that in this case, $g(E/F) = g(K/F) / g(K/E)$.
:::
