::: problem
Let $K/F$ be a finite cyclic Galois extension of degree $n$. Prove that for every divisor $d\mid n$, there is a unique intermediate field $E$ with
\[
[E:F]=d.
\]
:::

::: {.solution}
Let
\[
G=\operatorname{Gal}(K/F)=\langle\sigma\rangle,
\qquad |G|=n.
\]

<1>1. For every divisor $d\mid n$, the cyclic group $G$ has a unique subgroup of index $d$.
::: {.proof}
The subgroup
\[
H_d=\langle\sigma^d\rangle
\]
has order $n/d$ and hence index $d$. A finite cyclic group has exactly one subgroup of each order dividing its order: if $H\le G$ has order $n/d$, then $H$ is generated by an element of order $n/d$, and every such element generates $\langle\sigma^d\rangle$. Thus $H_d$ is unique.
:::

<1>2. Let
\[
E_d=K^{H_d}.
\]
Then $[E_d:F]=d$.
::: {.proof}
By the fundamental theorem of Galois theory,
\[
[E_d:F]=[G:H_d]=d.
\]
:::

<1>3. The field $E_d$ is the unique intermediate field of degree $d$ over $F$.
::: {.proof}
If $E$ is another intermediate field with $[E:F]=d$, then its corresponding subgroup
\[
H=\operatorname{Gal}(K/E)
\]
has index
\[
[G:H]=[E:F]=d.
\]
By <1>1, $H=H_d$. Galois correspondence is injective, so
\[
E=K^H=K^{H_d}=E_d.
\]
:::
:::
