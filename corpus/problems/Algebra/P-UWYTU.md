::: problem
What are the cyclic extensions of prime degree $p$?
:::

::: solution
Let $K/F$ be cyclic of prime degree $p$.

If $\operatorname{char}F\ne p$ and $F$ contains a primitive $p$th root of unity $\zeta_p$, Kummer theory gives
\[
K=F(\alpha),\qquad \alpha^p=a\in F^\times,
\]
with $a\notin F^{\times p}$. A generator of the Galois group acts by
\[
\sigma(\alpha)=\zeta_p\alpha.
\]
The extension depends only on the one-dimensional $\mathbb F_p$-subspace generated by the class of $a$ in
\[
F^\times/F^{\times p}.
\]
If $\mu_p\not\subset F$, the corresponding classification is obtained after adjoining $\zeta_p$ together with the usual descent condition; there is no equally simple description by adjoining a $p$th root of an element of $F$ itself.

If $\operatorname{char}F=p$, Artin--Schreier theory gives
\[
K=F(\alpha),\qquad \alpha^p-\alpha=a\in F,
\]
where $a\notin\wp(F)$ and
\[
\wp(x)=x^p-x.
\]
A generator acts by
\[
\sigma(\alpha)=\alpha+1.
\]
The extension depends on the one-dimensional $\mathbb F_p$-subspace generated by the class of $a$ in
\[
F/\wp(F).
\]
These are the Kummer and Artin--Schreier classifications of cyclic extensions of prime degree.
:::
