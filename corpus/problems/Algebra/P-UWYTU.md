::: {.problem}
What are the cyclic extensions of prime degree $p$?
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $K/F$ be cyclic of prime degree $p$.

If $\characteristic F\ne p$ and $F$ contains a primitive $p$th root of unity $\zeta_p$, Kummer theory gives
$$
K=F(\alpha),\qquad \alpha^p=a\in F^\times,
$$
with $a\notin F^{\times p}$. A generator of the Galois group acts by
$$
\sigma(\alpha)=\zeta_p\alpha.
$$
The extension depends only on the one-dimensional $\FF_p$-subspace generated by the class of $a$ in
$$
F^\times/F^{\times p}.
$$
If $\mu_p\not\subset F$, let $F'=F(\zeta_p)$, a cyclic extension of degree dividing $p-1$, and let $\omega:\Gal(F'/F)\to(\ZZ/p)^\times$ be given by $\tau(\zeta_p)=\zeta_p^{\omega(\tau)}$. Then $K'=KF'$ is cyclic of degree $p$ over $F'$, so $K'=F'(\alpha)$ with $\alpha^p=a\in F'^\times$, and the class of $a$ in $F'^\times/F'^{\times p}$ satisfies $\tau(a)\equiv a^{\omega(\tau)}$ for every $\tau\in\Gal(F'/F)$. Conversely, each such line determines a cyclic extension of $F$ of degree $p$, namely the fixed field in $K'$ of the unique subgroup of $\Gal(K'/F)$ of order $[F':F]$. In general $K$ is not obtained by adjoining a $p$th root of an element of $F$.

If $\characteristic F=p$, Artin--Schreier theory gives
$$
K=F(\alpha),\qquad \alpha^p-\alpha=a\in F,
$$
where $a\notin\wp(F)$ and
$$
\wp(x)=x^p-x.
$$
A generator acts by
$$
\sigma(\alpha)=\alpha+1.
$$
The extension depends on the one-dimensional $\FF_p$-subspace generated by the class of $a$ in
$$
F/\wp(F).
$$
:::
