::: {.problem}
What are the cyclic extensions of prime degree $p$?
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $K/F$ be cyclic of prime degree $p$, and let $\sigma$ generate $\Gal(K/F)$.

::: pf

::: pf-step
If $\characteristic F\ne p$ and $F$ contains a primitive $p$th root of unity $\zeta_p$, then
$$
K=F(\alpha),\qquad \alpha^p=a\in F^\times,
$$
with $a\notin F^{\times p}$, and $\sigma(\alpha)=\zeta_p\alpha$ for a suitable generator $\sigma$.

::: pf-proof
This is Kummer theory.
The extension depends only on the one-dimensional $\FF_p$-subspace generated by the class of $a$ in $F^\times/F^{\times p}$.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
If $\characteristic F\ne p$ and $\mu_p\not\subset F$, then $K$ corresponds to a line in $F'^\times/F'^{\times p}$, where $F'=F(\zeta_p)$, on which $\Gal(F'/F)$ acts through the cyclotomic character.

::: pf-proof
The field $F'=F(\zeta_p)$ is cyclic over $F$ of degree dividing $p-1$.
Let $\omega:\Gal(F'/F)\to(\ZZ/p)^\times$ be given by $\tau(\zeta_p)=\zeta_p^{\omega(\tau)}$.
Then $K'=KF'$ is cyclic of degree $p$ over $F'$, so $K'=F'(\alpha)$ with $\alpha^p=a\in F'^\times$, and the class of $a$ in $F'^\times/F'^{\times p}$ satisfies $\tau(a)\equiv a^{\omega(\tau)}$ for every $\tau\in\Gal(F'/F)$.
Conversely, each such line determines a cyclic extension of $F$ of degree $p$, namely the fixed field in $K'$ of the unique subgroup of $\Gal(K'/F)$ of order $[F':F]$.
In general $K$ is not obtained by adjoining a $p$th root of an element of $F$.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
If $\characteristic F=p$, then
$$
K=F(\alpha),\qquad \alpha^p-\alpha=a\in F,
$$
with $a\notin\wp(F)$ for $\wp(x)=x^p-x$, and $\sigma(\alpha)=\alpha+1$ for a suitable generator $\sigma$.

::: pf-proof
This is Artin--Schreier theory.
The extension depends only on the one-dimensional $\FF_p$-subspace generated by the class of $a$ in $F/\wp(F)$.
:::
