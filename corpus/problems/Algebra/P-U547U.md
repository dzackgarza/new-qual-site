::: {.problem}
State and prove the singular value decomposition.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $A\in M_{m\times n}(\CC)$. The singular value decomposition says that there exist unitary matrices
\[
U\in U(m),\qquad V\in U(n)
\]
and an $m\times n$ diagonal matrix
\[
\Sigma=\operatorname{diag}(\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_r,0,\ldots),
\qquad \sigma_i\ge0,
\]
such that
\[
A=U\Sigma V^*.
\]
The numbers $\sigma_i$ are the square roots of the nonzero eigenvalues of $A^*A$.

Since $A^*A$ is Hermitian positive semidefinite, the spectral theorem gives an orthonormal basis
\[
v_1,\ldots,v_n
\]
of eigenvectors with
\[
A^*Av_i=\sigma_i^2v_i,
\qquad \sigma_i\ge0.
\]
For each $\sigma_i>0$, define
\[
u_i=\frac{Av_i}{\sigma_i}.
\]
Then
\[
\langle u_i,u_j\rangle
=\frac1{\sigma_i\sigma_j}\langle v_i,A^*Av_j\rangle
=\delta_{ij}.
\]
Thus the $u_i$ form an orthonormal set. Extend them to an orthonormal basis of $\CC^m$.

Let $V$ be the unitary matrix with columns $v_i$ and $U$ the unitary matrix with columns $u_i$. Then
\[
Av_i=\sigma_i u_i
\]
for the nonzero singular values, while $Av_i=0$ when $\sigma_i=0$. Therefore
\[
AV=U\Sigma,
\]
so
\[
A=U\Sigma V^*.
\]

Over $\RR$, the same proof gives orthogonal $U,V$.
:::
