::: {.problem}
State and prove the singular value decomposition.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $A\in M_{m\times n}(\CC)$. The singular value decomposition states that there exist unitary matrices $U\in U(m)$ and $V\in U(n)$ and an $m\times n$ matrix $\Sigma$ whose only nonzero entries are $\Sigma_{ii}=\sigma_i>0$ for $i\le r$, such that $A=U\Sigma V^*$; the $\sigma_i$ are the square roots of the nonzero eigenvalues of $A^*A$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #eigenbasis}
$\CC^n$ has an orthonormal basis $v_1,\ldots,v_n$ with $A^*Av_i=\sigma_i^2v_i$, where $\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_r>0$ and $\sigma_i=0$ for $i>r$.

::: pf-proof
$A^*A$ is Hermitian and positive semidefinite, since $\inner{A^*Av}{v}=\norm{Av}^2\ge0$.
The spectral theorem gives an orthonormal eigenbasis with nonnegative eigenvalues; order it so that the positive eigenvalues come first.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #orthonormal}
The vectors $u_i\coloneqq Av_i/\sigma_i$ for $i\le r$ are orthonormal, and $Av_i=0$ for $i>r$.

::: pf-proof
For $i,j\le r$,
$$
\inner{u_i}{u_j}=\frac{1}{\sigma_i\sigma_j}\inner{v_i}{A^*Av_j}=\frac{\sigma_j^2}{\sigma_i\sigma_j}\inner{v_i}{v_j}=\delta_{ij}.
$$
For $i>r$, $\norm{Av_i}^2=\inner{A^*Av_i}{v_i}=0$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #assemble}
$A=U\Sigma V^*$.

::: pf-proof
Extend $u_1,\ldots,u_r$ to an orthonormal basis $u_1,\ldots,u_m$ of $\CC^m$, and let $U$ and $V$ be the unitary matrices with columns $u_i$ and $v_i$.
By step [](#orthonormal){.pf-ref}, $Av_i=\sigma_iu_i$ for $i\le r$ and $Av_i=0$ for $i>r$, which says $AV=U\Sigma$.
:::

:::

:::

:::

::: {.remark}
Over $\RR$, the same proof gives orthogonal $U$ and $V$.
:::
