::: {.problem}
Let
$$
L=\QQ(\sqrt[3]2,\sqrt3,\zeta_3),
$$
where $\zeta_3$ is a primitive cube root of unity. Compute $\Gal(L/\QQ)$ and describe the intermediate fields, at least up to conjugacy.
:::

::: {.solution}
Write $a=\sqrt[3]2$, $b=\sqrt3$ and $\omega=\zeta_3$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #degree}
$[L:\QQ]=12$.

::: pf-proof
$\QQ(a,\omega)$ is the splitting field of $x^3-2$, of degree $6$ with Galois group $S_3$, whose only quadratic subfield is $\QQ(\omega)=\QQ(\sqrt{-3})$.
Since $\QQ(\sqrt3)\ne\QQ(\sqrt{-3})$, $b\notin\QQ(a,\omega)$, and $L=\QQ(a,\omega)(b)$ has degree $2$ over it.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #group}
$\Gal(L/\QQ)\cong S_3\times C_2\cong\boxed{D_6}$, the dihedral group of order $12$, generated by
$\sigma:(a,\omega,b)\mapsto(\omega a,\omega,b)$, $\tau:(a,\omega,b)\mapsto(a,\omega^2,b)$ and $\gamma:(a,\omega,b)\mapsto(a,\omega,-b)$.

::: pf-proof
$L$ is the compositum of the Galois extensions $\QQ(a,\omega)$ and $\QQ(b)$, so it is Galois, and by step [](#degree){.pf-ref} the intersection of these two fields is $\QQ$.
Hence $\Gal(L/\QQ)\cong\Gal(\QQ(a,\omega)/\QQ)\times\Gal(\QQ(b)/\QQ)\cong S_3\times C_2$, with $\sigma,\tau$ generating the first factor ($\sigma^3=\tau^2=1$, $\tau\sigma\tau=\sigma^{-1}$) and $\gamma$ the second.
$S_3\times C_2\cong D_6$ because $\sigma\gamma$ has order $6$ and $\tau$ inverts it.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
Up to conjugacy, the proper nontrivial subgroups and their fixed fields are as follows, where $i=(\omega-\omega^2)/b$:
$$
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
H & L^H & [L^H:\QQ]\\ \hline
\langle\sigma\rangle & \QQ(\omega,b) & 4\\
\langle\tau\rangle & \QQ(a,b) & 6\\
\langle\gamma\rangle & \QQ(a,\omega) & 6\\
\langle\tau\gamma\rangle & \QQ(a,i) & 6\\
\langle\tau,\gamma\rangle & \QQ(a) & 3\\
\langle\sigma,\tau\rangle & \QQ(b) & 2\\
\langle\sigma,\gamma\rangle & \QQ(\omega) & 2\\
\langle\sigma,\tau\gamma\rangle & \QQ(i) & 2
\end{array}
$$
The conjugates of $\langle\tau\rangle$, $\langle\tau\gamma\rangle$ and $\langle\tau,\gamma\rangle$ give the further fields $\QQ(\omega^ja,b)$, $\QQ(\omega^ja,i)$ and $\QQ(\omega^ja)$ for $j=1,2$; the other subgroups listed are normal.

::: pf-proof
In $D_6$ the subgroups up to conjugacy are: the trivial group; the central $\langle\gamma\rangle$; two classes of non-central involutions, represented by $\tau$ and $\tau\gamma$; $\langle\sigma\rangle$; the Klein four-groups, conjugate to $\langle\tau,\gamma\rangle$; the three subgroups of order $6$; and the whole group.
Each listed field is fixed by the listed generators, since $\omega-\omega^2=\sqrt{-3}$ makes $i=\sqrt{-3}/\sqrt3$ fixed by $\sigma$ and by $\tau\gamma$, and its degree is $[G:H]$, so by the Galois correspondence it is the whole fixed field.
Conjugating by $\sigma^j$ replaces $a$ by $\omega^ja$.
:::
