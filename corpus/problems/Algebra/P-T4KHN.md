::: {.problem}
Show that the nilradical is $\sqrt{(0)}$.
:::

::: {.solution}
By definition,
\[
\sqrt{(0)}=\{x\in R:x^n=0\text{ for some }n\ge1\},
\]
which is exactly the set of nilpotent elements, i.e. the nilradical.
:::
