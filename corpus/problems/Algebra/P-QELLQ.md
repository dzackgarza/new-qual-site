::: {.problem}
State and prove the **Hilbert Basis Theorem**: if $R$ is a Noetherian ring, then the polynomial ring $R[x]$ is also Noetherian.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $R$ be Noetherian and let $I\subseteq R[x]$ be an ideal. For $d\ge0$, let $L_d\subseteq R$ consist of $0$ and the leading coefficients of the elements of $I$ of degree $d$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #chain}
Each $L_d$ is an ideal of $R$, $L_0\subseteq L_1\subseteq\cdots$, and there is $N$ with $L_d=L_N$ for all $d\ge N$.

::: pf-proof
If $f,g\in I$ have degree $d$ and leading coefficients $a,b$, and $r\in R$, then $f+g$ and $rf$ lie in $I$ and have degree-$d$ coefficients $a+b$ and $ra$; each is either the leading coefficient of a degree-$d$ element or $0$. Multiplying by $x$ preserves the leading coefficient and raises the degree by one, so $L_d\subseteq L_{d+1}$. An ascending chain of ideals in the Noetherian ring $R$ stabilizes.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #generators}
There are finitely many $f_{di}\in I$, for $0\le d\le N$ and $1\le i\le r_d$, such that $f_{di}$ has degree $d$ and the leading coefficients $a_{di}$ of $f_{d1},\ldots,f_{dr_d}$ generate $L_d$.

::: pf-proof
Each $L_d$ is finitely generated because $R$ is Noetherian; choose generators $a_{di}\ne0$ and, by definition of $L_d$, elements $f_{di}\in I$ of degree $d$ with leading coefficient $a_{di}$.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
$I$ equals the ideal $J$ generated by the $f_{di}$, so $\boxed{R[x]\text{ is Noetherian}}$.

::: pf-proof
$J\subseteq I$ since each $f_{di}\in I$. For the reverse inclusion, use induction on the degree $m$ of $g\in I$; the case $g=0$ is clear. Let $c$ be the leading coefficient of $g$, and let $d=\min(m,N)$. By steps [](#chain){.pf-ref} and [](#generators){.pf-ref}, $c\in L_m=L_d$, so $c=\sum_i r_ia_{di}$ with $r_i\in R$. Then $h=\sum_ir_ix^{m-d}f_{di}\in J$ has degree at most $m$ and coefficient $c$ in degree $m$, so $g-h\in I$ has degree less than $m$, and lies in $J$ by induction. Hence $g\in J$. Thus every ideal of $R[x]$ is finitely generated.
:::
