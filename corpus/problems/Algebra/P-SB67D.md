::: {.problem}
Let $p$ be prime and let $\zeta_p$ be a primitive $p$th root of unity. Is the homomorphism
$$
\ZZ[t]/(t^p-1)\longrightarrow \ZZ[\zeta_p],
\qquad t\longmapsto \zeta_p,
$$
an isomorphism?
:::

::: {.solution}

Let $\Phi_p(t)=1+t+\cdots+t^{p-1}$ and let $\operatorname{ev}\colon\ZZ[t]\to\ZZ[\zeta_p]$ be evaluation at $\zeta_p$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #kernel}

$\operatorname{ev}$ is surjective with kernel $(\Phi_p(t))$.

::: pf-proof

Surjectivity holds because $\ZZ[\zeta_p]$ is generated by $\zeta_p$. The minimal polynomial of $\zeta_p$ over $\QQ$ is the monic polynomial $\Phi_p\in\ZZ[t]$. If $f\in\ZZ[t]$ has $f(\zeta_p)=0$, division by the monic $\Phi_p$ in $\ZZ[t]$ gives $f=q\Phi_p+r$ with $q,r\in\ZZ[t]$ and $\deg r<p-1$; then $r(\zeta_p)=0$ forces $r=0$.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

The map is $\boxed{\text{not an isomorphism}}$: it is surjective but not injective. Since $t^p-1=(t-1)\Phi_p(t)$, $(t^p-1)\subseteq(\Phi_p(t))$, so $\operatorname{ev}$ induces the given map $\ZZ[t]/(t^p-1)\to\ZZ[\zeta_p]$, which is surjective with kernel $(\Phi_p(t))/(t^p-1)$ by step [](#kernel){.pf-ref}. This kernel is nonzero: $\Phi_p(t)\notin(t^p-1)$ because $\deg\Phi_p=p-1<p$. In fact $\ZZ[\zeta_p]\cong\ZZ[t]/(\Phi_p(t))$.

:::
