::: {.problem}
Let
\[
\ZZ_n[m]=\{x\in \ZZ/n\ZZ : mx=0\}
\]
be the subgroup of $m$-torsion elements of $\ZZ/n\ZZ$. Prove that
\[
\ZZ_n[m]\cong \ZZ/\gcd(m,n)\ZZ.
\]
:::

::: {.solution}
Set $d=\gcd(m,n)$.

<1>1. The subgroup $\ZZ_n[m]$ is generated by the class of $n/d$.
::: {.proof}
First,
\[
m\frac nd=\frac md\,n,
\]
so $m[n/d]_n=0$ in $\ZZ/n\ZZ$. Thus
\[
\langle[n/d]_n\rangle\subseteq \ZZ_n[m].
\]

Conversely, suppose $[x]_n\in\ZZ_n[m]$. Then $n\mid mx$. Dividing by $d$ gives
\[
\frac nd\mid \frac md x.
\]
Since
\[
\gcd\!\left(\frac nd,\frac md\right)=1,
\]
Euclid's lemma yields $n/d\mid x$. Hence $[x]_n$ lies in the subgroup generated by $[n/d]_n$.
:::

<1>2. The element $[n/d]_n$ has order $d$.
::: {.proof}
Its order is the least positive integer $r$ such that
\[
n\mid r\frac nd.
\]
Cancelling the positive integer $n/d$ gives $d\mid r$. Hence the least such $r$ is $d$.
:::

<1>3. Therefore
\[
\ZZ_n[m]\cong \ZZ/d\ZZ=\ZZ/\gcd(m,n)\ZZ.
\]
::: {.proof}
By <1>1, the subgroup is cyclic generated by $[n/d]_n$, and by <1>2 that generator has order $d$. A cyclic group of order $d$ is isomorphic to $\ZZ/d\ZZ$.
:::
:::
