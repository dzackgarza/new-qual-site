::: {.problem}
Show that if $G$ is finite and solvable, then every composition factor of $G$ has prime order.
:::

::: {.solution}

Let $1=G_0\normal G_1\normal\cdots\normal G_r=G$ be a composition series, so each factor $G_i/G_{i-1}$ is a nontrivial simple group.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #solvable-factor}

Each $G_i/G_{i-1}$ is solvable.

::: pf-proof

Subgroups and quotients of solvable groups are solvable, and $G_i/G_{i-1}$ is a quotient of the subgroup $G_i$ of $G$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #abelian}

A nontrivial simple solvable group $H$ is abelian.

::: pf-proof

Since $H$ is solvable and nontrivial, its derived subgroup $H'=[H,H]$ is a proper normal subgroup; otherwise the derived series would be constant at $H$. Simplicity forces $H'=1$, so $H$ is abelian.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #prime}

A finite simple abelian group $A$ is cyclic of prime order.

::: pf-proof

For $1\neq a\in A$, the subgroup $\langle a\rangle$ is normal because $A$ is abelian, so simplicity gives $A=\langle a\rangle$. A finite cyclic group of composite order $mk$ with $m,k>1$ has a proper nontrivial subgroup of order $m$, so $\abs{A}$ is prime.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

By steps [](#solvable-factor){.pf-ref}, [](#abelian){.pf-ref}, and [](#prime){.pf-ref}, every composition factor of $G$ has prime order.

:::
