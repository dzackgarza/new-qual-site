::: {.problem}
Show that $S_n$ is generated by transpositions.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #cycle}

Every cycle is a product of transpositions.

::: pf-proof

For a $k$-cycle,
$$
(a_1a_2\cdots a_k)=(a_1a_k)(a_1a_{k-1})\cdots(a_1a_2),
$$
as one checks by following each $a_i$ through the product from right to left.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

$S_n$ is generated by transpositions.

::: pf-proof

Every permutation is a product of disjoint cycles, and each cycle is a product of transpositions by step [](#cycle){.pf-ref}. The identity is the empty product.

:::

:::

:::

:::

::: {.remark}
The adjacent transpositions already generate: $S_n=\langle(12),(23),\ldots,(n-1\ n)\rangle$, since for $i<j-1$,
$$
(i\ j)=(j-1\ j)(i\ j-1)(j-1\ j),
$$
and induction on $j-i$ expresses every transposition through adjacent ones.
:::
