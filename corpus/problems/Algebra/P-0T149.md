::: {.problem}
Let $u$ be algebraic over a field $K$, let $f\in K[x]$ be the minimal polynomial of $u$, and let $L$ be the normal closure of $K(u)/K$. Let $F$ be the splitting field of $f$ over $K$. Prove that $L=F$.
:::


::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #f-normal-contains-ku}
The splitting field $F$ is a normal extension of $K$ containing $K(u)$.

::: pf-proof
By definition, $u$ is a root of $f$, so $u\in F$ and therefore $K(u)\subseteq F$. A splitting field of a polynomial over $K$ is normal over $K$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #l-subset-f}
Hence $L\subseteq F$.

::: pf-proof
The normal closure $L$ is the smallest normal extension of $K$ containing $K(u)$. By step [](#f-normal-contains-ku){.pf-ref}, $F/K$ is normal and contains $K(u)$. Minimality of $L$ therefore gives
\[
L\subseteq F.
\]
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #f-roots-in-l}
Every root of $f$ lies in $L$.

::: pf-proof
The field $L/K$ is normal and contains the root $u$ of the irreducible polynomial $f\in K[x]$. For a normal extension, every irreducible polynomial over the base that has one root in the extension splits completely there. Hence all roots of $f$ lie in $L$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #f-subset-l}
Hence $F\subseteq L$.

::: pf-proof
The splitting field $F$ is generated over $K$ by all roots of $f$. By step [](#f-roots-in-l){.pf-ref}, all these roots lie in $L$, so
\[
F\subseteq L.
\]
:::

:::

::: pf-step
Therefore $L=F$.

::: pf-proof
Combine steps [](#l-subset-f){.pf-ref} and [](#f-subset-l){.pf-ref}.
:::
