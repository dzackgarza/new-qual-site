::: {.exercise}
Let $G$ be a group and let $g_i\in G$.
Prove that the size of a conjugacy class of $g_i$ is the index of its centralizer, $[G: Z(g_i)] \definedas [G: C_G(g_i)]$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Write $x=g_i$, let $G$ act on itself by conjugation, $h\cdot y=hyh^{-1}$, and write $\operatorname{Cl}_G(x)=\{hxh^{-1}:h\in G\}$ for the conjugacy class of $x$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #stabilizer}
The orbit of $x$ is $\operatorname{Cl}_G(x)$, and its stabilizer is $C_G(x)$.

::: pf-proof
The orbit is $\{h\cdot x:h\in G\}=\operatorname{Cl}_G(x)$, and $h\cdot x=x$ exactly when $hxh^{-1}=x$, that is, $h\in C_G(x)$.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
$\boxed{\abs{\operatorname{Cl}_G(x)}=[G:C_G(x)]}$; for finite $G$ this is $\abs G/\abs{C_G(x)}$.

::: pf-proof
The map $G/C_G(x)\to\operatorname{Cl}_G(x)$, $hC_G(x)\mapsto hxh^{-1}$, is well defined and injective because
$$
hxh^{-1}=kxk^{-1}
\iff k^{-1}h\in C_G(x)
\iff hC_G(x)=kC_G(x),
$$
using step [](#stabilizer){.pf-ref}, and it is surjective by definition of $\operatorname{Cl}_G(x)$.
:::

:::

:::

:::

::: {.remark title="Variations"}
For finite $G$, the size of every conjugacy class divides $\abs G$: by the last step, $\abs{\operatorname{Cl}_G(x)}=[G:C_G(x)]$, and Lagrange's theorem gives $\abs G=[G:C_G(x)]\,\abs{C_G(x)}$.
:::
