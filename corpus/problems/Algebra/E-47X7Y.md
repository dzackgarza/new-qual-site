::: {.exercise}
Prove that the size of a conjugacy class of $g_i$ is the index of its centralizer, $[G: Z(g_i)] \da [G: C_G(g_i)]$.
:::

::: solution
Let $G$ act on itself by conjugation:
\[
h\cdot x=hxh^{-1}.
\]
The orbit of $x$ is its conjugacy class
\[
\operatorname{Cl}_G(x)=\{hxh^{-1}:h\in G\},
\]
and its stabilizer is
\[
\operatorname{Stab}_G(x)
=\{h:hxh^{-1}=x\}
=C_G(x).
\]

The orbit-stabilizer bijection is explicitly
\[
G/C_G(x)\longrightarrow \operatorname{Cl}_G(x),
\qquad
hC_G(x)\longmapsto hxh^{-1}.
\]
Indeed,
\[
hxh^{-1}=kxk^{-1}
\iff k^{-1}h\in C_G(x)
\iff hC_G(x)=kC_G(x).
\]
Thus
\[
\boxed{|\operatorname{Cl}_G(x)|=[G:C_G(x)]}.
\]
For finite $G$, this is $|G|/|C_G(x)|$.
:::
