::: {.problem}
Compute all intermediate fields of
$$L=\QQ(2^{1/4},\zeta_8)/\QQ.$$
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $\alpha=2^{1/4}\in\RR$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #field}
$L=\QQ(\alpha,i)$ is the splitting field of $x^4-2$, and $[L:\QQ]=8$.

::: pf-proof
Since $\zeta_8=(1+i)/\sqrt2$ and $\sqrt2=\alpha^2$, $L=\QQ(\alpha,i)$, which contains the roots $\pm\alpha,\pm i\alpha$ and is generated by them. The polynomial $x^4-2$ is Eisenstein at $2$, so $[\QQ(\alpha):\QQ]=4$; and $i\notin\QQ(\alpha)\subseteq\RR$, so $[L:\QQ(\alpha)]=2$.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
$G=\Gal(L/\QQ)\cong D_4=\langle r,s\mid r^4=s^2=1,\ srs=r^{-1}\rangle$, where $r(\alpha)=i\alpha$, $r(i)=i$, $s(\alpha)=\alpha$, $s(i)=-i$.

::: pf-proof
By step [](#field){.pf-ref}, $x^4-2$ is irreducible over $\QQ(i)$ (degree count $[L:\QQ(i)]=4$) and $x^2+1$ is irreducible over $\QQ(\alpha)$, so $r$ and $s$ exist. Checking on $\alpha$ and $i$ gives $r^4=s^2=1$ and $srs=r^{-1}$, and $\langle r,s\rangle$ has order $8=\abs{G}$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #quadratic}
The intermediate fields of degree $2$ over $\QQ$ are $\QQ(i)$, $\QQ(\sqrt2)$, $\QQ(\sqrt{-2})$, fixed by $\langle r\rangle$, $\langle r^2,s\rangle$, $\langle r^2,rs\rangle$ respectively.

::: pf-proof
These are the three subgroups of order $4$ of $D_4$. Each listed generator of the field is fixed by the generators of its subgroup: for instance $rs(\alpha)=i\alpha$ and $rs(i)=-i$, so $rs(\sqrt{-2})=rs(i\alpha^2)=(-i)(i\alpha)^2=i\alpha^2$. Each field has degree $2$, which equals the index of the subgroup.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #quartic}
The intermediate fields of degree $4$ over $\QQ$ are
$$\QQ(\zeta_8)=\QQ(\sqrt2,i),\quad\QQ(\alpha),\quad\QQ(i\alpha),\quad\QQ((1+i)\alpha),\quad\QQ((1-i)\alpha),$$
fixed by $\langle r^2\rangle$, $\langle s\rangle$, $\langle r^2s\rangle$, $\langle rs\rangle$, $\langle r^3s\rangle$ respectively.

::: pf-proof
These are the five subgroups of order $2$ of $D_4$. The generators are fixed: $r^2$ fixes $\sqrt2=\alpha^2$ and $i$; $r^2s$ sends $i\alpha\mapsto(-i)(-\alpha)$; $rs$ sends $(1+i)\alpha\mapsto(1-i)i\alpha=(1+i)\alpha$; $r^3s$ sends $(1-i)\alpha\mapsto(1+i)(-i\alpha)=(1-i)\alpha$. Each generator has $4$ distinct conjugates under $G$, so each field has degree $4$, which equals the index of the subgroup.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
The intermediate fields of $L/\QQ$ are $\boxed{\QQ,\ L,\ \text{the three fields of degree } 2,\ \text{and the five fields of degree } 4}$ of steps [](#quadratic){.pf-ref} and [](#quartic){.pf-ref}.

::: pf-proof
$D_4$ has exactly ten subgroups: the trivial group, five of order $2$, three of order $4$, and $D_4$. By the Galois correspondence and steps [](#quadratic){.pf-ref} and [](#quartic){.pf-ref}, these correspond to $L$, the quartic fields, the quadratic fields, and $\QQ$.
:::
