::: problem
Let $E/F$ be a finite field extension. Prove that $E/F$ is algebraic and that there is a polynomial $g\in F[x]$ whose splitting field $L/F$ is finite and contains $E$.
:::


::: {.solution}
<1>1. Every element of $E$ is algebraic over $F$.
::: {.proof}
Let $\alpha\in E$. Since $E$ is finite-dimensional over $F$, the vectors
\[
1,\alpha,\alpha^2,\ldots,\alpha^{[E:F]}
\]
are linearly dependent over $F$. Thus some nonzero polynomial in $F[x]$ vanishes at $\alpha$, so $\alpha$ is algebraic over $F$.
:::

<1>2. The extension $E/F$ is generated by finitely many algebraic elements.
::: {.proof}
Choose an $F$-basis $e_1,\ldots,e_n$ of $E$. Then
\[
E=F(e_1,\ldots,e_n),
\]
and by <1>1 every $e_i$ is algebraic over $F$.
:::

<1>3. Let $f_i\in F[x]$ be the minimal polynomial of $e_i$ and set
\[
g=f_1\cdots f_n\in F[x].
\]
If $L$ is the splitting field of $g$ over $F$, then $E\subseteq L$.
::: {.proof}
Since $e_i$ is a root of $f_i$, every $e_i$ lies in the splitting field $L$ of $g$. Hence
\[
E=F(e_1,\ldots,e_n)\subseteq L.
\]
:::

<1>4. The splitting field $L/F$ is finite.
::: {.proof}
The polynomial $g$ has finite degree, so it has finitely many roots in an algebraic closure. The splitting field $L$ is generated over $F$ by those finitely many roots, each algebraic over $F$. A field extension generated by finitely many algebraic elements is finite.
:::

<1>5. Therefore every finite extension is algebraic and is contained in a finite splitting field.
::: {.proof}
Algebraicity is <1>1. The field $L$ constructed in <1>3 is a splitting field over $F$, contains $E$, and is finite over $F$ by <1>4.
:::
:::
