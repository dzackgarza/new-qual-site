::: problem
Show that the order of the group $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{F}_q)$ of invertible $n\times n$ matrices over the field $\mathbb{F}_q$ of $q$ elements is $(q^n-1)(q^n-q)\cdots(q^n-q^{n-1})$.
:::
