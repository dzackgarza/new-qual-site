::: problem
Let $f\in K[x]$ be a separable polynomial of degree $5$, and let $G$ be the Galois group of its splitting field over $K$.

1. Give a necessary and sufficient permutation-theoretic condition for $3\mid |G|$.
2. If $f$ is irreducible, show that
   \[
   3\mid |G|
   \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
   G\cong A_5\ \text{or}\ S_5.
   \]
3. Give an irreducible quintic over $\QQ$ for which $3\nmid |G|$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $\Omega$ be the set of five distinct roots of $f$ in a splitting field. Then $G$ acts faithfully on $\Omega$, so we regard $G$ as a subgroup of $S_5$.

<1>1. We have $3\mid |G|$ if and only if $G$ contains a $3$-cycle.
::: {.proof}
If $3\mid |G|$, Cauchy's theorem gives an element of order $3$ in $G$. An element of order $3$ in $S_5$ must have cycle type $(3)(1)(1)$, hence is a $3$-cycle.

Conversely, a $3$-cycle has order $3$, so if $G$ contains one then Lagrange's theorem gives $3\mid |G|$.
:::

<1>2. Assume now that $f$ is irreducible. Then $3\mid |G|$ if and only if $A_5\leq G\leq S_5$.
::: {.proof}
Irreducibility and separability imply that $G$ acts transitively on the five roots, hence $5\mid |G|$. By Cauchy's theorem, $G$ contains an element of order $5$, which in $S_5$ is a $5$-cycle.

Suppose $3\mid |G|$. By <1>1, $G$ also contains a $3$-cycle. Both a $5$-cycle and a $3$-cycle are even, so the subgroup
\[
H:=G\cap A_5
\]
contains elements of orders $5$ and $3$. Therefore $15\mid |H|$. Since $H\leq A_5$ and $|A_5|=60$, the only possible orders are $15$, $30$, or $60$.

The case $|H|=30$ is impossible because an index-$2$ subgroup of $A_5$ would be normal, whereas $A_5$ is simple. The case $|H|=15$ is also impossible: Sylow's theorems would force the Sylow $5$- and Sylow $3$-subgroups of $H$ both to be normal, making $H\cong C_{15}$; but $A_5$ has no element of order $15$. Hence $|H|=60$, so $H=A_5$. Thus
\[
A_5\leq G\leq S_5,
\]
and consequently $G=A_5$ or $G=S_5$.

The converse is immediate, since both $|A_5|=60$ and $|S_5|=120$ are divisible by $3$.
:::

<1>3. The polynomial $x^5-2\in\QQ[x]$ is an irreducible example with $3\nmid |G|$.
::: {.proof}
Eisenstein's criterion at $2$ shows that $x^5-2$ is irreducible. Let
\[
\alpha=2^{1/5},
\qquad
\zeta=\zeta_5.
\]
Its splitting field is
\[
L=\QQ(\alpha,\zeta).
\]
We have
\[
[\QQ(\alpha):\QQ]=5,
\qquad
[\QQ(\zeta):\QQ]=\varphi(5)=4.
\]
The intersection $\QQ(\alpha)\cap\QQ(\zeta)$ has degree over $\QQ$ dividing both $5$ and $4$, hence is $\QQ$. Therefore
\[
[L:\QQ]=5\cdot4=20.
\]
Since $L/\QQ$ is the splitting field of a separable polynomial, it is Galois, so
\[
|G|=[L:\QQ]=20.
\]
Thus $3\nmid |G|$.
:::
