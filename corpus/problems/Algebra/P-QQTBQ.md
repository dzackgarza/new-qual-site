::: problem
Prove that every finitely generated torsion-free abelian group is free abelian.
:::

::: solution
**Goal:** Prove that if $G$ is a finitely generated abelian group and $\operatorname{Tor}(G) = \{0\}$, then $G \cong \mathbb{Z}^r$ for some non-negative integer $r \ge 0$.

<1>1. Structure Theorem Approach:
    *Proof:*
    <2>1. By the Fundamental Theorem of Finitely Generated Abelian Groups (or classification of finitely generated modules over the PID $\mathbb{Z}$):
        $$G \cong \mathbb{Z}^r \oplus \mathbb{Z}/d_1\mathbb{Z} \oplus \cdots \oplus \mathbb{Z}/d_k\mathbb{Z}$$
        for non-negative integer $r \ge 0$ (the rank) and invariant factors $d_1 \mid d_2 \mid \cdots \mid d_k \ge 2$.
    <2>2. The torsion subgroup is $\operatorname{Tor}(G) \cong \mathbb{Z}/d_1\mathbb{Z} \oplus \cdots \oplus \mathbb{Z}/d_k\mathbb{Z}$.
    <2>3. Because $G$ is torsion-free, $\operatorname{Tor}(G) = \{0\}$, which forces $k = 0$ (no torsion summands).
    <2>4. Therefore $G \cong \mathbb{Z}^r$, which is free abelian of rank $r$.

<1>2. Self-contained / Direct Proof (via Vector Space Embedding):
    *Proof:*
    <2>1. Let $G$ be generated by $S = \{x_1, \dots, x_n\}$, and suppose $G$ is torsion-free.
    <2>2. Consider the tensor product $V = G \otimes_\mathbb{Z} \mathbb{Q}$, which is a finite-dimensional $\mathbb{Q}$-vector space of dimension $r \le n$.
    <2>3. **The canonical map $\iota: G \to G \otimes_\mathbb{Z} \mathbb{Q}$ ($g \mapsto g \otimes 1$) is injective:**
        - An element $g \in \ker\iota \iff g \otimes 1 = 0 \in G \otimes_\mathbb{Z} \mathbb{Q}$.
        - In $\mathbb{Q}$-localization of modules, $g \otimes 1 = 0 \iff \exists m \in \mathbb{Z}_{>0}$ such that $m g = 0$ in $G$.
        - Since $G$ is torsion-free, $mg = 0 \implies g = 0$.
        - Thus $\iota$ embeds $G$ as a subgroup of $V \cong \mathbb{Q}^r$.
    <2>4. **$G$ is contained in a free $\mathbb{Z}$-submodule of $V$:**
        - Let $\{v_1, \dots, v_r\}$ be a basis of $V$ over $\mathbb{Q}$.
        - Each generator $x_i \in S$ can be written as $x_i = \sum_{j=1}^r \frac{a_{ij}}{b_i} v_j$ with $a_{ij}, b_i \in \mathbb{Z}$.
        - Let $M = \prod_{i=1}^n b_i$. Then $M \cdot x_i \in \operatorname{span}_\mathbb{Z}\{v_1, \dots, v_r\}$ for all $i$.
        - Thus $G \subseteq \frac{1}{M} \bigoplus_{j=1}^r \mathbb{Z} v_j \cong \mathbb{Z}^r$.
    <2>5. **Subgroup of a free abelian group is free abelian:**
        - $G$ is isomorphic to a subgroup of the free abelian group $\mathbb{Z}^r$.
        - Every subgroup of a free abelian group of rank $r$ is free abelian of rank $k \le r$.
        - Since $\mathbb{Q} \otimes G \cong \mathbb{Q}^r$, the rank is precisely $r$.
        - Hence $G \cong \mathbb{Z}^r$.

<1>3. Conclusion:
    Every finitely generated torsion-free abelian group is free abelian. Q.E.D.
:::
