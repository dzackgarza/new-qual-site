::: {.problem}
Let $P\in \Syl_p(G)$ and let $H\normal G$. Show that $P\intersect H \in \Syl_p(H)$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let
$$
\cardinality{P}=p^a,
\qquad
\cardinality{H}=p^b m,
\qquad p\nmid m.
$$
Since $P$ is Sylow in $G$, $p^a$ is the largest power of $p$ dividing $\cardinality{G}$.

::: pf

::: pf-step

The product $PH$ is a subgroup of $G$.

::: pf-proof

Because $H\trianglelefteq G$, the product of the subgroup $P$ with the normal subgroup $H$ is a subgroup.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

Write $\cardinality{P\cap H}=p^c$. Then $c\ge b$.

::: pf-proof

By the product formula,
$$
\cardinality{PH}=\frac{\cardinality{P}\cardinality{H}}{\cardinality{P\cap H}}
=p^{a+b-c}m.
$$
Since $PH\le G$, the $p$-part of $\cardinality{PH}$ cannot exceed the $p$-part $p^a$ of $\cardinality{G}$. Therefore
$$
a+b-c\le a,
$$
so $c\ge b$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

One also has $c\le b$.

::: pf-proof

The intersection $P\cap H$ is a $p$-subgroup of $H$. Since $p^b$ is the largest power of $p$ dividing $\cardinality{H}$, Lagrange's theorem gives
$$
\cardinality{P\cap H}=p^c\le p^b.
$$
Thus $c\le b$.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Hence $P\cap H$ is a Sylow $p$-subgroup of $H$.

::: pf-proof

By steps [](#s2){.pf-ref} and [](#s3){.pf-ref}, $c=b$. Therefore
$$
\cardinality{P\cap H}=p^b,
$$
which is the full $p$-part of $\cardinality{H}$. Hence $P\cap H\in\Syl_p(H)$.

:::
