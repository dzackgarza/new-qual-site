::: {.problem}
Show that a matrix $A \in M_n(K)$ is similar to a diagonal matrix over $K$ if and only if all the elementary divisors of $A$ are linear (degree 1 polynomials over $K$).
:::

::: {.solution}
View $V=K^n$ as a $K[x]$-module by
\[
x\cdot v=Av.
\]
The elementary-divisor decomposition has the form
\[
V\cong\bigoplus_j K[x]/(p_j^{e_j}),
\]
where each $p_j$ is monic irreducible.

If $A$ is diagonalizable over $K$, choose an eigenbasis with eigenvalues $\lambda_1,\dots,\lambda_n$. Then
\[
V\cong\bigoplus_{i=1}^n K[x]/(x-\lambda_i),
\]
so every elementary divisor has degree $1$.

Conversely, if every elementary divisor has degree $1$, then each is necessarily
\[
x-\lambda
\]
for some $\lambda\in K$; in particular no higher power $(x-\lambda)^e$ with $e>1$ occurs. Hence
\[
V\cong\bigoplus_i K[x]/(x-\lambda_i),
\]
a direct sum of one-dimensional eigenspaces. Therefore $V$ has an eigenbasis and $A$ is diagonalizable.

Thus
\[
\boxed{A\text{ is diagonalizable over }K
\iff\text{ all elementary divisors have degree }1}.
\]
:::
