::: problem
Show that the transitive subgroups of the symmetric group $S_3$ (acting naturally on $\{1, 2, 3\}$) are precisely $S_3$ and $A_3$.
:::

::: solution
Let $H\le S_3$ act transitively on $\{1,2,3\}$. By orbit--stabilizer,
\[
|H|=3\,|H_1|,
\]
so $3\mid |H|$. Since $|H|\mid6$, we have $|H|=3$ or $6$.

If $|H|=6$, then $H=S_3$. If $|H|=3$, then $H$ is generated by a $3$-cycle. The two $3$-cycles generate the same subgroup
\[
A_3=\{1,(123),(132)\},
\]
which is transitive. Hence the transitive subgroups are exactly $A_3$ and $S_3$.
:::
