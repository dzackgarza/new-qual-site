::: problem
How many elements in the automorphism group $\operatorname{Aut}(\mathbb{Z}/20\mathbb{Z})$ have order 4?
:::

::: solution
**Goal:** Compute $|\{x \in \operatorname{Aut}(\mathbb{Z}/20\mathbb{Z}) \mid \operatorname{ord}(x) = 4\}|$.

<1>1. Identification of the Automorphism Group:
    *Proof:*
    <2>1. The automorphism group of a cyclic group $\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$ is isomorphic to the group of units modulo $n$:
        $$\operatorname{Aut}(\mathbb{Z}/20\mathbb{Z}) \cong (\mathbb{Z}/20\mathbb{Z})^\times.$$
    <2>2. The order of this group is given by Euler's totient function:
        $$\varphi(20) = \varphi(4 \cdot 5) = \varphi(2^2) \cdot \varphi(5) = (2^2 - 2^1) \cdot (5 - 1) = 2 \cdot 4 = 8.$$

<1>2. Group Structure via Chinese Remainder Theorem:
    *Proof:*
    <2>1. By the **Chinese Remainder Theorem** for rings:
        $$\mathbb{Z}/20\mathbb{Z} \cong \mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}/5\mathbb{Z}.$$
    <2>2. Taking the group of units:
        $$(\mathbb{Z}/20\mathbb{Z})^\times \cong (\mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z})^\times \times (\mathbb{Z}/5\mathbb{Z})^\times.$$
    <2>3. We determine the component groups:
        - $(\mathbb{Z}/4\mathbb{Z})^\times = \{1, 3\} \cong \mathbb{Z}_2$.
        - $(\mathbb{Z}/5\mathbb{Z})^\times = \{1, 2, 3, 4\} \cong \mathbb{Z}_4$ (generated by 2, since $2^1=2, 2^2=4, 2^3=3, 2^4=1$).
    <2>4. Therefore:
        $$\operatorname{Aut}(\mathbb{Z}/20\mathbb{Z}) \cong \mathbb{Z}_2 \times \mathbb{Z}_4.$$

<1>3. Counting Elements of Order 4 in $\mathbb{Z}_2 \times \mathbb{Z}_4$:
    *Proof:*
    <2>1. An element $(a, b) \in \mathbb{Z}_2 \times \mathbb{Z}_4$ has order:
        $$\operatorname{ord}(a, b) = \operatorname{lcm}(\operatorname{ord}(a), \operatorname{ord}(b)).$$
    <2>2. The orders of elements in $\mathbb{Z}_2$ are:
        - Order 1: $1$ element ($0$).
        - Order 2: $1$ element ($1$).
    <2>3. The orders of elements in $\mathbb{Z}_4$ are:
        - Order 1: $1$ element ($0$).
        - Order 2: $1$ element ($2$).
        - Order 4: $2$ elements ($1, 3$).
    <2>4. For $\operatorname{lcm}(\operatorname{ord}(a), \operatorname{ord}(b)) = 4$, since $\operatorname{ord}(a) \in \{1, 2\}$, we must have $\operatorname{ord}(b) = 4$.
    <2>5. The choice of $a \in \mathbb{Z}_2$ is completely arbitrary ($2$ choices: order 1 or order 2).
    <2>6. The choice of $b \in \mathbb{Z}_4$ must have order 4 ($2$ choices: $1$ or $3$).
    <2>7. Total number of pairs $(a, b)$ of order 4 is:
        $$|\mathbb{Z}_2| \times |\{b \in \mathbb{Z}_4 \mid \operatorname{ord}(b) = 4\}| = 2 \times 2 = 4.$$

<1>4. Explicit Listing of Order 4 Elements in $(\mathbb{Z}/20\mathbb{Z})^\times$:
    *Proof:*
    <2>1. The units are $\{1, 3, 7, 9, 11, 13, 17, 19\} \subset \mathbb{Z}/20\mathbb{Z}$.
    <2>2. Testing their orders modulo 20:
        - $3^2 = 9, 3^4 = 81 \equiv 1 \implies \operatorname{ord}(3) = 4$.
        - $7^2 = 49 \equiv 9, 7^4 \equiv 9^2 \equiv 1 \implies \operatorname{ord}(7) = 4$.
        - $13^2 = 169 \equiv 9, 13^4 \equiv 1 \implies \operatorname{ord}(13) = 4$.
        - $17^2 = 289 \equiv 9, 17^4 \equiv 1 \implies \operatorname{ord}(17) = 4$.
        - ($1, 9, 11, 19$ have order 1 or 2).
    <2>3. The 4 elements of order 4 are $\{3, 7, 13, 17\}$.

<1>5. Conclusion:
    There are exactly 4 elements of order 4 in $\operatorname{Aut}(\mathbb{Z}/20\mathbb{Z})$. Q.E.D.
:::
