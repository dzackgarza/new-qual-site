::: problem
Let $G$ be a finite group and let $g \in G$. Prove that the order of $g$, denoted $\operatorname{ord}(g)$, divides the order of the group $|G|$.
:::

::: solution
The cyclic subgroup generated by $g$ has order
\[
|\langle g\rangle|=\operatorname{ord}(g).
\]
Since $\langle g\rangle\le G$, Lagrange's theorem gives
\[
|G|=[G:\langle g\rangle]\,|\langle g\rangle|.
\]
Therefore
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{ord}(g)\mid |G|}.
\]
In particular, writing $|G|=m\operatorname{ord}(g)$ gives
\[
g^{|G|}=(g^{\operatorname{ord}(g)})^m=e.
\]
:::
