::: problem
Can you get a long exact sequence from a short exact sequence of abelian groups together with another abelian group?
:::


::: {.solution}
Let
\[
0\longrightarrow A'\longrightarrow A\longrightarrow A''\longrightarrow 0
\]
be a short exact sequence of abelian groups, and let $B$ be another abelian group.

<1>1. Applying the left-exact functor $\Hom(B,-)$ and its right derived functors gives a long exact sequence
\[
0\to\Hom(B,A')\to\Hom(B,A)\to\Hom(B,A'')\to\Ext^1(B,A')\to\Ext^1(B,A)\to\Ext^1(B,A'')\to\Ext^2(B,A')\to\cdots.
\]
::: {.proof}
In the category of abelian groups, $\Hom(B,-)$ is left exact. Its right derived functors are $\Ext^i(B,-)$. The general long-exact-sequence theorem for right derived functors applied to the displayed short exact sequence gives the stated sequence.
:::

<1>2. Applying the contravariant left-exact functor $\Hom(-,B)$ gives
\[
0\to\Hom(A'',B)\to\Hom(A,B)\to\Hom(A',B)\to\Ext^1(A'',B)\to\Ext^1(A,B)\to\Ext^1(A',B)\to\cdots.
\]
::: {.proof}
The functor $\Hom(-,B)$ is contravariant and left exact. Its right derived functors in the first variable are $\Ext^i(-,B)$, and the variance reverses the arrows among the $\Hom$ and $\Ext$ terms as displayed.
:::

<1>3. Applying the right-exact functor $-\otimes B$ and its left derived functors gives the Tor long exact sequence
\[
\cdots\to\Tor_2(A'',B)\to\Tor_1(A',B)\to\Tor_1(A,B)\to\Tor_1(A'',B)\to A'\otimes B\to A\otimes B\to A''\otimes B\to0.
\]
::: {.proof}
The functor $-\otimes B$ is right exact, and its left derived functors are $\Tor_i(-,B)$. The general long-exact-sequence theorem for left derived functors yields the displayed exact sequence.
:::

Thus a short exact sequence of abelian groups, together with a third abelian group, naturally produces the standard long exact $\Ext$ and $\Tor$ sequences.
:::
