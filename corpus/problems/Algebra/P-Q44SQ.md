::: {.problem}
Show that every subgroup of order $5$ in $S_5$ acts transitively on $\{1,2,3,4,5\}$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $H\le S_5$ have order $5$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #five-cycle}
$H=\langle\sigma\rangle$ for a $5$-cycle $\sigma$.

::: pf-proof
A group of prime order is cyclic, generated by any $\sigma\ne e$, which has order $5$. The order of a permutation is the least common multiple of its cycle lengths, each at most $5$, so some cycle has length $5$, and $\sigma$ is a $5$-cycle.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
$H$ acts transitively on $\{1,2,3,4,5\}$.

::: pf-proof
By step [](#five-cycle){.pf-ref}, the orbit of any point under $\langle\sigma\rangle$ is the support of the $5$-cycle $\sigma$, which is the whole set.
:::
