::: {.problem}
- Is $\QQ(2^{1\over 3})/\QQ$ normal?
:::

::: {.solution}
No. Let $\alpha=2^{1/3}$. Its minimal polynomial over $\mathbb Q$ is
\[
x^3-2,
\]
which is irreducible by Eisenstein at $2$. Its three roots are
\[
\alpha,\quad \omega\alpha,\quad \omega^2\alpha,
\]
where $\omega$ is a primitive cube root of unity. Since $\mathbb Q(\alpha)\subset\mathbb R$, it does not contain the two nonreal roots $\omega\alpha$ and $\omega^2\alpha$. Hence the minimal polynomial of $\alpha$ does not split over $\mathbb Q(\alpha)$, so
\[
\mathbb Q(2^{1/3})/\mathbb Q
\]
is not normal.
:::
