::: {.problem}
Describe the subgroups of $\ZZ^3$ and the possible structures of the quotient by such a subgroup.
:::

::: {.solution}
::: pf

::: {.pf-step #stacked-basis}
For every subgroup $H\le\ZZ^3$ there are an integer $r\in\{0,1,2,3\}$, a basis $e_1,e_2,e_3$ of $\ZZ^3$, and positive integers $d_1\mid d_2\mid\cdots\mid d_r$ with
$$
H=d_1\ZZ e_1\oplus\cdots\oplus d_r\ZZ e_r .
$$
In particular $H$ is free abelian of rank $r\le 3$.

::: pf-proof
$\ZZ$ is a PID, so a submodule of the free module $\ZZ^3$ is free of rank $r\le3$. Write a basis of $H$ as the columns of an integer $3\times r$ matrix $A$. Its Smith normal form $UAV=\operatorname{diag}(d_1,\ldots,d_r)$, with $U\in\GL_3(\ZZ)$ and $V\in\GL_r(\ZZ)$, gives the basis $e_i$ as the columns of $U^{-1}$ and gives the divisibility chain.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #quotient}
$\ZZ^3/H\cong\ZZ^{3-r}\oplus\ZZ/d_1\ZZ\oplus\cdots\oplus\ZZ/d_r\ZZ$.

::: pf-proof
In the basis of step [](#stacked-basis){.pf-ref}, the quotient is the direct sum of $\ZZ e_i/d_i\ZZ e_i\cong\ZZ/d_i\ZZ$ for $i\le r$ and of $\ZZ e_i$ for $i>r$.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
The possible quotients are exactly the groups $\boxed{\ZZ^{3-r}\oplus\ZZ/d_1\ZZ\oplus\cdots\oplus\ZZ/d_r\ZZ}$ with $0\le r\le3$ and positive integers $d_1\mid\cdots\mid d_r$; equivalently, the abelian groups generated by at most $3$ elements.

::: pf-proof
Step [](#quotient){.pf-ref} shows that every quotient has this form. Conversely, for given $r$ and $d_1\mid\cdots\mid d_r$, the subgroup $d_1\ZZ\oplus\cdots\oplus d_r\ZZ\oplus 0^{3-r}$ of $\ZZ^3$ has this quotient. A quotient of $\ZZ^3$ is generated by $3$ elements, and an abelian group generated by at most $3$ elements is a quotient of $\ZZ^3$.
:::
