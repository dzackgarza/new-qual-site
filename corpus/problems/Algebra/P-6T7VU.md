::: problem
Let $R$ be a Principal Ideal Domain (PID), and let $r, s \in R \setminus \{0\}$ be non-zero elements.
Determine the **invariant factors** of the $R$-module:
$$M = R/(r) \oplus R/(s).$$
:::

::: solution
The module
\[
M=R/(r)\oplus R/(s)
\]
has presentation matrix
\[
A=\begin{pmatrix}r&0\\0&s\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Over a PID, the Smith normal form has invariant factors $d_1\mid d_2$, determined up to associates by the determinantal divisors
\[
\Delta_1=d_1,
\qquad
\Delta_2=d_1d_2.
\]
Here
\[
\Delta_1\sim\gcd(r,s)
\]
because $\Delta_1$ is a generator of the ideal generated by all entries of $A$, and
\[
\Delta_2\sim rs
\]
because the only $2\times2$ minor is $rs$.
Therefore
\[
d_1\sim\gcd(r,s),
\qquad
d_2\sim\frac{rs}{\gcd(r,s)}\sim\operatorname{lcm}(r,s).
\]
Thus, up to multiplication by units, the invariant factors are
\[
\boxed{\gcd(r,s),\ \operatorname{lcm}(r,s)}.
\]
Equivalently,
\[
M\cong R/(\gcd(r,s))\oplus R/(\operatorname{lcm}(r,s)).
\]
:::
