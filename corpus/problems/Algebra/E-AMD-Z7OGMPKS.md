::: {.exercise}
Show that
\[
[S_n,S_n]=A_n
\]
for all $n\ge1$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Since
\[
S_n/A_n\cong C_2
\]
is abelian, the commutator subgroup satisfies
\[
[S_n,S_n]\le A_n.
\]

For $n=1,2$, both sides are trivial. Assume $n\ge3$. The group $A_n$ is generated by $3$-cycles. For distinct $a,b,c$,
\[
[(bc),(ab)]=(abc)
\]
with the convention $[x,y]=xyx^{-1}y^{-1}$. Hence every $3$-cycle lies in $[S_n,S_n]$, so
\[
A_n\le[S_n,S_n].
\]
Therefore $[S_n,S_n]=A_n$.
:::
