::: {.exercise}
Show that
$$
[S_n,S_n]=A_n
$$
for all $n\ge1$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Write $[x,y]=xyx^{-1}y^{-1}$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #upper}
$[S_n,S_n]\le A_n$.

::: pf-proof
$A_n$ is the kernel of the sign homomorphism, so $S_n/A_n$ embeds in the abelian group $\{\pm1\}$, and every commutator lies in $A_n$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #lower}
$A_n\le[S_n,S_n]$.

::: pf-proof
For $n\le2$, $A_n$ is trivial. For $n\ge3$, $A_n$ is generated by $3$-cycles, and for distinct $a,b,c$,
$$
[(bc),(ab)]=(abc).
$$
Hence every $3$-cycle lies in $[S_n,S_n]$.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
$[S_n,S_n]=A_n$.

::: pf-proof
Combine steps [](#upper){.pf-ref} and [](#lower){.pf-ref}.
:::
