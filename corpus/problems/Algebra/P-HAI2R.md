::: {.problem}
Let $f(x) = x^3 - 7$.

1. Let $K$ be the splitting field for $f$ over $\mathbb Q$.
   Describe the Galois group of $K / \mathbb Q$ and the intermediate fields between $\mathbb Q$ and $K$.
   Which intermediate fields are not Galois over $\mathbb Q$?

2. Let $L$ be the splitting field for $f$ over $\mathbb R$.
   What is the Galois group $L/ \mathbb R$?

3. Let $M$ be the splitting field for $f$ over $\mathbb F_{13}$, the field with 13 elements.
   What is the Galois group of $M / \mathbb F_{13}$?
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $\alpha=\sqrt[3]{7}$ and $\omega=e^{2\pi i/3}$, so the roots of $f$ in $\CC$ are $\alpha_1=\alpha$, $\alpha_2=\omega\alpha$, $\alpha_3=\omega^2\alpha$.

::: pf

::: pf-step

$K=\QQ(\alpha,\omega)$ has degree $6$ over $\QQ$, and $\Gal(K/\QQ)\cong S_3$, acting as all permutations of $\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3$.

::: pf-proof

$f$ is Eisenstein at $7$, so $[\QQ(\alpha):\QQ]=3$.
$K$ contains $\alpha$ and $\omega=\alpha_2/\alpha_1$, and $\QQ(\alpha,\omega)$ contains all roots, so $K=\QQ(\alpha,\omega)$.
Since $\QQ(\alpha)\subset\RR$ and $\omega\notin\RR$, $[K:\QQ(\alpha)]=2$, so $[K:\QQ]=6$.
$\Gal(K/\QQ)$ embeds in the permutation group $S_3$ of the roots and has order $6$.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

The intermediate fields are $\QQ$, $\QQ(\omega)=\QQ(\sqrt{-3})$, $\QQ(\alpha_1)$, $\QQ(\alpha_2)$, $\QQ(\alpha_3)$ and $K$; the three cubic fields $\QQ(\alpha_i)$ are exactly the ones not Galois over $\QQ$.

::: pf-proof

$S_3$ has subgroups $1$, $A_3$, the three subgroups of order $2$, and $S_3$.
$A_3$ fixes $\omega=\alpha_2/\alpha_1$, and $[\QQ(\omega):\QQ]=2=[S_3:A_3]$, so its fixed field is $\QQ(\omega)$.
The subgroup of order $2$ fixing the root $\alpha_i$ has fixed field $\QQ(\alpha_i)$, of degree $3$.
An intermediate field is Galois over $\QQ$ exactly when its subgroup is normal; $1$, $A_3$ and $S_3$ are normal, and the subgroups of order $2$ are not.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

$L=\CC$ and $\Gal(L/\RR)\cong\ZZ/2\ZZ$, generated by complex conjugation.

::: pf-proof

$\alpha\in\RR$ and $\omega\alpha\notin\RR$, so $L=\RR(\omega)=\CC$.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

$M=\FF_{13}(\beta)\cong\FF_{13^3}$ for a root $\beta$ of $f$, and $\Gal(M/\FF_{13})\cong\ZZ/3\ZZ$, generated by $x\mapsto x^{13}$.

::: pf-proof

Since $3\mid 13-1$, an element $a\in\FF_{13}^\times$ is a cube if and only if $a^4=1$.
Here $7^2=49\equiv10$ and $7^4\equiv100\equiv9\not\equiv1\pmod{13}$, so $f$ has no root in $\FF_{13}$ and, being cubic, is irreducible.
Moreover $3^3=27\equiv1\pmod{13}$, so $3$ is a primitive cube root of unity in $\FF_{13}$, and the roots of $f$ are $\beta,3\beta,9\beta\in\FF_{13}(\beta)$.
Hence $M=\FF_{13}(\beta)$ has degree $3$, and the Galois group of a degree-$3$ extension of finite fields is cyclic of order $3$, generated by Frobenius.

:::
