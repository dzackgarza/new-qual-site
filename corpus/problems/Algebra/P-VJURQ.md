::: {.problem}
Construct, for every $n\ge2$, a polynomial over $\QQ$ whose Galois group is $S_n$. Give an explicit example for $n=7$.
:::

::: {.solution}
A standard uniform family is
\[
f_n(x)=x^n-x-1.
\]
A theorem of Nart--Vila, later proved independently by Osada, states that for every $n\ge2$ the splitting field of $f_n$ over $\QQ$ has Galois group
\[
\operatorname{Gal}(f_n/\QQ)\cong S_n.
\]
Selmer had earlier proved that $x^n-x-1$ is irreducible for every $n\ge2$.

Thus one may take
\[
\boxed{f_n(x)=x^n-x-1.}
\]

In particular, for $n=7$,
\[
\boxed{x^7-x-1}
\]
has Galois group $S_7$ over $\QQ$.

One route to the Galois-group theorem is: irreducibility makes the Galois group transitive; the ramification analysis shows that the group is generated by transpositions; and a transitive subgroup of $S_n$ generated by transpositions is all of $S_n$.
:::
