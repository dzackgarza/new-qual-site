::: {.problem}
Show that every nonzero ring has a maximal ideal.
:::

::: {.solution}
If $R\ne0$, then $(0)$ is a proper ideal. By Zorn's lemma, as in [[P-USAQT]], it lies in a maximal ideal.
:::
