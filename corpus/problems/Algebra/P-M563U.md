::: problem
Let $G$ act on a set $X$ and let $x\in X$. Show that the stabilizer
\[
G_x=\{g\in G:g\cdot x=x\}
\]
is a subgroup of $G$.
:::

::: {.solution}
The identity fixes $x$, so $e\in G_x$.

If $g,h\in G_x$, then
\[
(gh^{-1})\cdot x
=g\cdot(h^{-1}\cdot x).
\]
Since $h\cdot x=x$, applying $h^{-1}$ gives $h^{-1}\cdot x=x$. Therefore
\[
(gh^{-1})\cdot x=g\cdot x=x,
\]
so $gh^{-1}\in G_x$.

By the subgroup criterion, $G_x\le G$.
:::
