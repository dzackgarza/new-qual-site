::: {.problem}
Let $G$ act on a set $X$ and let $x\in X$. Show that the stabilizer
$$
G_x=\{g\in G:g\cdot x=x\}
$$
is a subgroup of $G$.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #identity}
$e\in G_x$.

::: pf-proof
$e\cdot x=x$ by the action axioms.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #closed}
If $g,h\in G_x$, then $gh^{-1}\in G_x$.

::: pf-proof
Applying $h^{-1}$ to $h\cdot x=x$ gives $h^{-1}\cdot x=x$, so $(gh^{-1})\cdot x=g\cdot(h^{-1}\cdot x)=g\cdot x=x$.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
$G_x\le G$.

::: pf-proof
This is the subgroup criterion, by steps [](#identity){.pf-ref} and [](#closed){.pf-ref}.
:::
