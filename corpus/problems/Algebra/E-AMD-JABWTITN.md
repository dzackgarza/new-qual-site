::: {.exercise}
Show that if $K/F$ is the splitting field of a separable polynomial, then $K/F$ is Galois.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $K$ be the splitting field over $F$ of a separable polynomial $f\in F[x]$.

<1>1. The extension $K/F$ is normal.
::: {.proof}
Every splitting field over $F$ is normal.
:::

<1>2. The extension $K/F$ is separable.
::: {.proof}
The field $K$ is generated over $F$ by the roots of $f$. Since $f$ is separable, each of these roots is separable over $F$, and an algebraic extension generated by separable elements is separable.
:::

Therefore $K/F$ is normal and separable, hence Galois.
:::
