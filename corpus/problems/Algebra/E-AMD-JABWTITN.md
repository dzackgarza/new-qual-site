::: {.exercise}
Show that if $K/F$ is the splitting field of a separable polynomial then it is Galois.
:::

::: {.solution}
**Goal.** Show the splitting field of a separable polynomial is Galois.

<1>1. $K/F$ is a splitting field, so it is normal.
<2>1. $K$ is generated over $F$ by the roots of a polynomial $f \in F[x]$.
::: {.proof}
definition of splitting field.
:::
<2>2. Hence $K/F$ is normal (every irreducible polynomial in $F[x]$ with a root in $K$ splits in $K$).
::: {.proof}
a splitting field is a normal extension.
:::

<1>2. $K/F$ is separable.
<2>1. $K$ is generated by the roots of the separable polynomial $f$.
::: {.proof}
$f$ is separable by hypothesis.
:::
<2>2. Hence $K/F$ is separable (generated by separable elements).
::: {.proof}
an extension generated by separable elements is separable.
:::

<1>3. $K/F$ is normal and separable, hence Galois.
::: {.proof}
a Galois extension is by definition a normal and separable algebraic extension.
:::

<1>4. Q.E.D.
::: {.proof}
<1>3 is the claim.
:::

:::
