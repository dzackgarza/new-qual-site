::: {.problem}
What is the Galois group of $x^7 - 1$ over the rationals?
:::

::: {.solution}

Let $\zeta_7$ be a primitive seventh root of unity.

::: pf

::: pf-step

The splitting field of $x^7-1$ over $\QQ$ is $K=\QQ(\zeta_7)$, and $[K:\QQ]=6$.

::: pf-proof

The roots of $x^7-1$ are the powers of $\zeta_7$, so they generate $\QQ(\zeta_7)$. The minimal polynomial of $\zeta_7$ is the cyclotomic polynomial
$$
\Phi_7(x)=x^6+x^5+\cdots+x+1,
$$
which is irreducible over $\QQ$, so $[K:\QQ]=6$.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

$\Gal(K/\QQ)\cong(\ZZ/7\ZZ)^\times$.

::: pf-proof

A $\QQ$-automorphism of $K$ sends $\zeta_7$ to a root of $\Phi_7$, that is, to $\zeta_7^a$ with $a\in(\ZZ/7\ZZ)^\times$, and it is determined by this image. The map $\sigma\mapsto a$ is an injective homomorphism, since $\zeta_7^a\mapsto(\zeta_7^b)^a$ under composition. Both groups have order $6$, so it is an isomorphism.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

The group $(\ZZ/7\ZZ)^\times$ is cyclic of order $6$, generated by $3$, so the Galois group of $x^7-1$ over $\QQ$ is $\boxed{C_6}$.

:::
