::: {.problem}
Prove the alternating group on $n$ letters is generated by the 3-cycles for $n \geq 3$.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #three-cycles-even}
Every $3$-cycle lies in $A_n$.

::: pf-proof
For distinct letters $a,b,c$ we have $(abc)=(ac)(ab)$, a product of two transpositions.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #pairs}
Every product of two transpositions is a product of $3$-cycles.

::: pf-proof
If the transpositions are equal, their product is the identity, the empty product. If they share exactly one letter, then $(ab)(bc)=(abc)$. If they are disjoint, then $(ab)(cd)=(abc)(bcd)$.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
The $3$-cycles generate $A_n$.

::: pf-proof
Every element of $A_n$ is a product of an even number of transpositions, so it is a product of pairs of transpositions, hence of $3$-cycles by step [](#pairs){.pf-ref}. Combined with step [](#three-cycles-even){.pf-ref}, the subgroup generated by the $3$-cycles is exactly $A_n$.
:::
