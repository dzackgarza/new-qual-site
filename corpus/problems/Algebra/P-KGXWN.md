::: {.problem}
Prove the alternating group on $n$ letters is generated by the 3-cycles for $n \geq 3$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Every element of $A_n$ is a product of an even number of transpositions, so it suffices to express a product of two transpositions as a product of $3$-cycles.

If the transpositions share one letter, then
\[
(ab)(bc)=(abc).
\]
If they are disjoint, then
\[
(ab)(cd)=(abc)(bcd).
\]
If they are equal, their product is the identity. Hence every even permutation is a product of $3$-cycles.

Conversely, every $3$-cycle is even because
\[
(abc)=(ac)(ab).
\]
Therefore the subgroup generated by all $3$-cycles is exactly $A_n$ for $n\ge3$.
:::
