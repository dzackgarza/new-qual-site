::: {.problem}
(a) State the fundamental theorem of Galois theory for a finite Galois extension $L/K$.

(b) Describe $\Gal(\QQ(\zeta_{43})/\QQ)$.

(c) How many proper subfields does $\QQ(\zeta_{43})$ have?
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: pf-step
(a) Let $L/K$ be finite Galois with group $G=\Gal(L/K)$.
The assignments
$$
E\longmapsto \Gal(L/E),
\qquad
H\longmapsto L^H
$$
are inclusion-reversing, mutually inverse bijections between intermediate fields $K\subseteq E\subseteq L$ and subgroups $H\le G$.
Under this correspondence $[E:K]=[G:H]$, and $E/K$ is Galois exactly when $H\normal G$, in which case $\Gal(E/K)\cong G/H$.

::: pf-proof
This is the statement of the fundamental theorem of Galois theory.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #galois-group}
(b) $\Gal(\QQ(\zeta_{43})/\QQ)\cong(\ZZ/43\ZZ)^\times$, which is cyclic of order $42$.

::: pf-proof
Since $43$ is prime, the $43$rd cyclotomic polynomial is irreducible of degree $\varphi(43)=42$, so $[\QQ(\zeta_{43}):\QQ]=42$.
An automorphism is determined by $\zeta_{43}\mapsto\zeta_{43}^a$ with $a\in(\ZZ/43\ZZ)^\times$, and every such $a$ occurs, so $a\mapsto(\zeta_{43}\mapsto\zeta_{43}^a)$ is an isomorphism from $(\ZZ/43\ZZ)^\times$.
The multiplicative group of a finite field is cyclic, so this group is cyclic of order $42$.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
(c) $\QQ(\zeta_{43})$ has $\boxed{7}$ proper subfields, $6$ of which properly contain $\QQ$.

::: pf-proof
A cyclic group of order $n$ has exactly one subgroup of order $d$ for each divisor $d$ of $n$.
Since $42=2\cdot3\cdot7$ has $(1+1)^3=8$ divisors, the group of step [](#galois-group){.pf-ref} has $8$ subgroups, and by (a) $\QQ(\zeta_{43})$ has $8$ subfields.
Excluding $\QQ(\zeta_{43})$ itself leaves $7$ proper subfields; excluding $\QQ$ as well leaves $6$.
:::
