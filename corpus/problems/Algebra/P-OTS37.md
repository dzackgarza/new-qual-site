::: problem
Let $V$ be an $n$-dimensional vector space over a field $K$, and identify $M_n(K)$ with $\End_K(V)$.
Describe the left, right, and two-sided ideals of $M_n(K)$, and identify the simple left and right ideals geometrically.
:::

::: {.solution}
Write multiplication in $\End_K(V)$ as composition.

<1>1. Left ideals are determined by subspaces on which every map vanishes.
::: {.proof}
For a subspace $U\le V$, set
\[
L_U=\{f\in\End_K(V):U\subseteq\ker f\}.
\]
This is a left ideal because if $f$ vanishes on $U$, then so does $g\circ f$ for every $g$.

Conversely, every left ideal of $M_n(K)$ is of this form. In matrix language, left ideals are exactly the sets of matrices whose rows lie in a fixed subspace of $K^n$; equivalently, after choosing coordinates they are generated by a collection of primitive idempotents.
:::

<1>2. Right ideals are determined by allowed image subspaces.
::: {.proof}
For a subspace $W\le V$, set
\[
R_W=\{f\in\End_K(V):\operatorname{im}f\subseteq W\}.
\]
This is a right ideal because $f\circ g$ still has image contained in $W$.
Conversely every right ideal is of this form. In matrix language, its matrices have all columns in a fixed subspace.
:::

<1>3. The only two-sided ideals are $0$ and $M_n(K)$.
::: {.proof}
Let $I\ne0$ be a two-sided ideal and choose $0\ne A=(a_{ij})\in I$. For some $i,j$, $a_{ij}\ne0$. Multiplying on the left and right by matrix units gives
\[
E_{ki}AE_{j\ell}=a_{ij}E_{k\ell}\in I.
\]
Since $a_{ij}$ is invertible in $K$, every matrix unit $E_{k\ell}$ belongs to $I$. Hence $I=M_n(K)$.
:::

<1>4. The simple one-sided ideals correspond to one-dimensional quotients or subspaces.
::: {.proof}
A nonzero left ideal $L_U$ is simple exactly when $U$ is a hyperplane, so $V/U$ is one-dimensional. Equivalently, a simple left ideal consists of maps factoring through one fixed one-dimensional quotient of $V$.

A nonzero right ideal $R_W$ is simple exactly when $W$ is one-dimensional. Thus simple right ideals consist of maps whose image lies in one fixed line of $V$.
:::
:::
