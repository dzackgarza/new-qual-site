::: {.exercise}
7. If $F$ is some (not necessarily Galois) field extension over $K$ such that $[F: K]=6$ and Aut $_{K} F \simeq S_{3}$, then $F$ is the splitting field of an irreducible cubic over $K[x]$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let
\[
G=\operatorname{Aut}_K(F)\cong S_3.
\]

::: pf

::: pf-step
The extension $F/K$ is Galois and $G=\Gal(F/K)$.

::: pf-proof
For every finite extension,
\[
|\operatorname{Aut}_K(F)|\le [F:K],
\]
with equality if and only if the extension is Galois. Here
\[
|\operatorname{Aut}_K(F)|=|S_3|=6=[F:K].
\]
Hence $F/K$ is Galois.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
Let $H\le G$ be a subgroup of order $2$, and put $E=F^H$. Then $[E:K]=3$.

::: pf-proof
By the Galois correspondence,
\[
[E:K]=[G:H]=6/2=3.
\]
Since $F/K$ is finite Galois, the intermediate extension $E/K$ is finite separable.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #alpha-generates-e-cubic}
There exists $\alpha\in E$ such that $E=K(\alpha)$, and its minimal polynomial $f\in K[x]$ is irreducible of degree $3$.

::: pf-proof
By the primitive element theorem, the finite separable extension $E/K$ is simple, say $E=K(\alpha)$. Then
\[
\deg f=[K(\alpha):K]=[E:K]=3.
\]
Thus $f$ is an irreducible cubic.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #f-splits-over-f}
The polynomial $f$ splits over $F$.

::: pf-proof
Because $F/K$ is normal, every $K$-embedding of $E=K(\alpha)$ into an algebraic closure extends to an element of $G$. Hence every $K$-conjugate of $\alpha$ is of the form $\sigma(\alpha)$ for some $\sigma\in G$, and therefore lies in $F$. Thus every root of the separable polynomial $f$ lies in $F$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #splitting-field-is-f}
The splitting field of $f$ inside $F$ is all of $F$.

::: pf-proof
Let $L$ be the field generated over $K$ by all roots of $f$. Since those roots are the $G$-orbit of $\alpha$,
\[
L=K(\sigma(\alpha):\sigma\in G).
\]
An element $\tau\in G$ fixes $L$ pointwise exactly when it fixes every $\sigma(\alpha)$. Since
\[
\operatorname{Stab}_G(\alpha)=\Gal(F/K(\alpha))=\Gal(F/E)=H,
\]
this pointwise stabilizer is
\[
\bigcap_{\sigma\in G}\sigma H\sigma^{-1},
\]
the core of $H$ in $G$.

Under $G\cong S_3$, a subgroup of order $2$ is generated by a transposition. Its three conjugates are the three distinct order-$2$ subgroups, whose intersection is trivial. Hence
\[
\Gal(F/L)=1.
\]
By the Galois correspondence, $L=F$.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
Therefore $F$ is the splitting field over $K$ of the irreducible cubic $f$.

::: pf-proof
By step [](#alpha-generates-e-cubic){.pf-ref}, $f$ is irreducible cubic, and by steps [](#f-splits-over-f){.pf-ref} and [](#splitting-field-is-f){.pf-ref} its splitting field is exactly $F$.
:::
