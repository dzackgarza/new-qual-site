::: {.problem}
(a) State the structure theorem for finitely generated modules over a PID.

(b) Find the decomposition of the $\ZZ$-module $M$ generated by $w,x,y,z$ satisfying the relations
$$\begin{aligned}
3w + 12y + 3x + 6z &=0 \\
6y &= 0 \\
-3w -3x + 6y &= 0.
\end{aligned}$$
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: pf-step

(a) If $R$ is a PID and $M$ is a finitely generated $R$-module, then
$$
M\cong R^r\oplus R/(d_1)\oplus\cdots\oplus R/(d_t),
\qquad
d_1\mid d_2\mid\cdots\mid d_t,
$$
where the $d_i$ are nonzero nonunits, and $r$ and the ideals $(d_i)$ are uniquely determined by $M$.

::: pf-proof

This is the invariant factor form of the structure theorem; it follows from the Smith normal form of a presentation matrix of $M$.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

(b) The module $M$ is the cokernel of the relation matrix
$$
A=
\begin{pmatrix}
3&3&12&6\\
0&0&6&0\\
-3&-3&6&0
\end{pmatrix}
$$
acting on $\ZZ^4$ with respect to the ordered generators $(w,x,y,z)$.

::: pf-proof

The rows of $A$ are the coefficient vectors of the three displayed relations.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

The Smith normal form of $A$ is the $3\times 4$ matrix with diagonal entries $3,6,6$ and all other entries $0$.

::: pf-proof

The determinantal divisors, the gcds of the $k\times k$ minors, are
$$
\Delta_1=3,
\qquad
\Delta_2=18,
\qquad
\Delta_3=108.
$$
Thus the invariant factors are
$$
3,\quad 18/3=6,\quad108/18=6.
$$

:::

:::

::: pf-step

$$
M\cong\boxed{\ZZ\oplus\ZZ/3\ZZ\oplus\ZZ/6\ZZ\oplus\ZZ/6\ZZ}.
$$

::: pf-proof

Passing to Smith normal form preserves the cokernel. The matrix has rank $3$ with invariant factors $3,6,6$, and $\ZZ^4$ has four generators, so the cokernel is $\ZZ/3\ZZ\oplus\ZZ/6\ZZ\oplus\ZZ/6\ZZ$ together with one free summand.

:::
