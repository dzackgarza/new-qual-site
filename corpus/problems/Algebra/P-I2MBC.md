::: {.problem}
Now we'll take the tensor product of two abelian groups, that is, $\ZZ\dash$modules.
Take $\ZZ/p\ZZ$ and $\ZZ/q\ZZ$, where $p$ and $q$ are distinct primes.
What is their tensor product?
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #generator}

The tensor product $\ZZ/p\ZZ\otimes_{\ZZ}\ZZ/q\ZZ$ is generated by $1\otimes1$.

::: pf-proof

Every simple tensor is $a\otimes b=ab(1\otimes1)$ for integers $a,b$, and simple tensors generate the tensor product.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

$\ZZ/p\ZZ\otimes_{\ZZ}\ZZ/q\ZZ=\boxed{0}$.

::: pf-proof

We have $p(1\otimes1)=p\otimes1=0\otimes1=0$ and $q(1\otimes1)=1\otimes q=1\otimes0=0$.
Since $p$ and $q$ are distinct primes, $up+vq=1$ for some integers $u,v$, so $1\otimes1=u\,p(1\otimes1)+v\,q(1\otimes1)=0$.
By step [](#generator){.pf-ref}, the tensor product is $0$.

:::
