::: {.exercise}
Show that $A_n$ is generated by $3$-cycles for $n \ge 3$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Every element of $A_n$ is a product of an even number of transpositions, hence a product of pairs of transpositions.

Each pair is a product of $3$-cycles. If the transpositions share a point, then
\[
(a\ b)(b\ c)=(a\ b\ c).
\]
If they are disjoint, then
\[
(a\ b)(c\ d)=(a\ b\ c)(b\ c\ d).
\]
If they are equal, their product is the identity.

Thus every element of $A_n$ is a product of $3$-cycles. Conversely, every $3$-cycle is even because
\[
(a\ b\ c)=(a\ c)(a\ b).
\]
Therefore the $3$-cycles generate $A_n$ for $n\ge3$.
:::
