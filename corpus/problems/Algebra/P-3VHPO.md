::: {.problem}
Show that
$$
\QQ(2^{1/3})
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\QQ(\zeta_3 2^{1/3})
$$
are isomorphic fields over $\QQ$, but are not identical as subfields of $\CC$.
:::

::: {.solution}
The collection's provenance is the problem list alone, so no retained solution set covers this exercise; the argument is the card's own.

Write $\alpha=2^{1/3}$ and $\beta=\zeta_3\alpha$, where $\zeta_3=e^{2\pi i/3}\ne1$ satisfies $\zeta_3^3=1$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #minpoly}
$x^3-2$ is irreducible over $\QQ$ and is the minimal polynomial of both $\alpha$ and $\beta$.

::: pf-proof
$x^3-2$ is Eisenstein at the prime $2$ ($2$ divides $-2$ but $2^2$ does not), hence irreducible over $\QQ$; it is monic, so it is the minimal polynomial of any of its roots. Both $\alpha^3=2$ and $\beta^3=\zeta_3^3\alpha^3=2$ hold, so both are roots.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #iso}
$\QQ(\alpha)\cong\QQ[x]/(x^3-2)\cong\QQ(\beta)$ as fields over $\QQ$.

::: pf-proof
For a root $\gamma$ of an irreducible monic $m\in\QQ[x]$, evaluation at $\gamma$ induces a $\QQ$-isomorphism $\QQ[x]/(m)\to\QQ(\gamma)$. Applying this with $m=x^3-2$ and $\gamma=\alpha$, then with $\gamma=\beta$ (step [](#minpoly){.pf-ref}), gives the two isomorphisms, and the composite sends $\alpha$ to $\beta$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #real}
$\QQ(\alpha)\subseteq\RR$.

::: pf-proof
$\alpha$ is real and $\QQ\subseteq\RR$; a field contained in $\RR$ together with a real element generates a subfield of $\RR$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #nonreal}
$\beta\notin\QQ(\alpha)$.

::: pf-proof
$\zeta_3=\cos\frac{2\pi}{3}+i\sin\frac{2\pi}{3}=-\frac12+\frac{\sqrt3}{2}i$ is nonreal and $\alpha=\sqrt[3]2\ne0$ is real nonzero, so $\beta=\zeta_3\alpha$ has nonzero imaginary part and is nonreal. By step [](#real){.pf-ref} $\QQ(\alpha)$ consists of real numbers, so $\beta\notin\QQ(\alpha)$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step}
The two fields are isomorphic over $\QQ$ but distinct as subfields of $\CC$.

::: pf-proof
Step [](#iso){.pf-ref} gives the isomorphism. For distinctness: $\beta\in\QQ(\beta)$ but $\beta\notin\QQ(\alpha)$ by step [](#nonreal){.pf-ref}, so the two subsets of $\CC$ differ.
:::
