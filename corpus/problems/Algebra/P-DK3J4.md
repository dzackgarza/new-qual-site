::: {.problem}
Describe the Galois theory of $f(x) = x^{2n} - 2$ over $\QQ$: irreducibility, splitting field, and Galois group structure.
:::

::: {.solution}
Put $m=2n$ and $\alpha=2^{1/m}$. By Eisenstein at $2$,
$$
x^m-2
$$
is irreducible over $\QQ$, so $[\QQ(\alpha):\QQ]=m$.
Its roots are
$$
\alpha\zeta_m^j,\qquad j\in\ZZ/m\ZZ,
$$
where $\zeta_m$ is a primitive $m$th root of unity. Hence the splitting field is
$$
K=\QQ(\alpha,\zeta_m).
$$

Let
$$
E=\QQ(\alpha),\qquad F=\QQ(\zeta_m).
$$
Then
$$
[E:\QQ]=m,\qquad [F:\QQ]=\varphi(m),
$$
and therefore
$$
[K:\QQ]
=\frac{m\varphi(m)}{[E\cap F:\QQ]}.
$$

Every $\sigma\in\Gal(K/\QQ)$ is determined by
$$
\sigma(\alpha)=\alpha\zeta_m^b,\qquad
\sigma(\zeta_m)=\zeta_m^a,
$$
with $b\in\ZZ/m\ZZ$ and $a\in(\ZZ/m\ZZ)^\times$. Thus the Galois group embeds in the affine group
$$
\ZZ/m\ZZ\rtimes(\ZZ/m\ZZ)^\times,
$$
where $(a,b)$ acts on the root index $j$ by $j\mapsto aj+b$. The affine group has order $m\varphi(m)$, so the image has index $[E\cap F:\QQ]$ in it.

Over $F$, the extension $K/F$ is a cyclic Kummer extension of degree dividing $m$, generated by adjoining an $m$th root of $2$. Hence $\Gal(K/\QQ)$ is solvable: its normal subgroup $\Gal(K/F)$ is cyclic, with quotient the abelian group
$$
\Gal(F/\QQ)\cong(\ZZ/m\ZZ)^\times.
$$

For example, $m=2$ gives $K=\QQ(\sqrt2)$ with Galois group $C_2$, while $m=4$ gives $K=\QQ(\sqrt[4]2,i)$ with Galois group $D_4$ of order $8$.
:::
