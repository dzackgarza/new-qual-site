::: {.problem}
Describe the Galois theory of $f(x) = x^{2n} - 2$ over $\mathbb{Q}$: irreducibility, splitting field, and Galois group structure.
:::

::: {.solution}
Put $m=2n$ and $\alpha=2^{1/m}$. By Eisenstein at $2$,
\[
x^m-2
\]
is irreducible over $\mathbb Q$, so $[\mathbb Q(\alpha):\mathbb Q]=m$.
Its roots are
\[
\alpha\zeta_m^j,\qquad j\in\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z,
\]
where $\zeta_m$ is a primitive $m$th root of unity. Hence the splitting field is
\[
K=\mathbb Q(\alpha,\zeta_m).
\]

Let
\[
E=\mathbb Q(\alpha),\qquad F=\mathbb Q(\zeta_m).
\]
Then
\[
[E:\mathbb Q]=m,\qquad [F:\mathbb Q]=\varphi(m),
\]
and therefore
\[
[K:\mathbb Q]
=\frac{m\varphi(m)}{[E\cap F:\mathbb Q]}.
\]
No further simplification is valid without determining the intersection $E\cap F$ for the particular $m$.

Every $\sigma\in\operatorname{Gal}(K/\mathbb Q)$ is determined by
\[
\sigma(\alpha)=\alpha\zeta_m^b,\qquad
\sigma(\zeta_m)=\zeta_m^a,
\]
with $b\in\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z$ and $a\in(\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z)^\times$. Thus the Galois group embeds in the affine group
\[
\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z\rtimes(\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z)^\times,
\]
where $(a,b)$ acts on the root index $j$ by $j\mapsto aj+b$. The image consists precisely of those pairs compatible with the relations coming from the intersection $E\cap F$.

Over $F$, the extension $K/F$ is a cyclic Kummer extension of degree dividing $m$, generated by adjoining an $m$th root of $2$. Hence $\operatorname{Gal}(K/\mathbb Q)$ is solvable: it is an extension of the cyclic group $\operatorname{Gal}(K/F)$ by a subgroup of the abelian group
\[
\operatorname{Gal}(F/\mathbb Q)\cong(\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z)^\times.
\]

For example, $m=2$ gives $K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt2)$ with Galois group $C_2$, while $m=4$ gives $K=\mathbb Q(\sqrt[4]2,i)$ with Galois group $D_4$ of order $8$.
:::
