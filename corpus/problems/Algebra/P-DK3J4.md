::: problem
Describe the Galois theory of $f(x) = x^{2n} - 2$ over $\mathbb{Q}$: irreducibility, splitting field, and Galois group structure.
:::

::: solution
**Goal:** Determine the splitting field and Galois group $\operatorname{Gal}(K/\mathbb{Q})$ for $f(x) = x^{2n} - 2$.

<1>1. Irreducibility of $f(x) = x^{2n} - 2$ over $\mathbb{Q}$:
    *Proof:*
    <2>1. Consider the polynomial $f(x) = x^{2n} - 2 \in \mathbb{Z}[x]$.
    <2>2. The prime $p = 2$ divides the constant term $-2$ and all intermediate coefficients $0$.
    <2>3. $p = 2$ does not divide the leading coefficient $1$, and $p^2 = 4 \nmid -2$.
    <2>4. By Eisenstein's Criterion at $p = 2$, $f(x)$ is irreducible over $\mathbb{Q}$.
    <2>5. Thus, adjoining any single root $\alpha = \sqrt[2n]{2}$ yields a field extension $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt[2n]{2})$ of degree $2n$ over $\mathbb{Q}$.

<1>2. The Splitting Field:
    *Proof:*
    <2>1. The roots of $x^{2n} - 2$ in $\mathbb{C}$ are given by:
        $$\alpha_k = \sqrt[2n]{2} \, \zeta_{2n}^k, \qquad k = 0, 1, \dots, 2n-1,$$
        where $\zeta_{2n} = e^{2\pi i / (2n)}$ is a primitive $(2n)$-th root of unity.
    <2>2. The splitting field is $K = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt[2n]{2}, \zeta_{2n})$.
    <2>3. Note that $\zeta_{2n} = \frac{\alpha_1}{\alpha_0}$, so $K$ is generated by any two distinct roots whose quotient is a primitive root.
    <2>4. The field $K$ contains two key subfields:
        - The radical extension $E = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt[2n]{2})$ with $[E : \mathbb{Q}] = 2n$.
        - The cyclotomic extension $F = \mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{2n})$ with $[F : \mathbb{Q}] = \varphi(2n)$.

<1>3. The Galois Group as a Semidirect Product / Subgroup of Affine Group:
    *Proof:*
    <2>1. Since $K = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt[2n]{2}, \zeta_{2n})$, any automorphism $\sigma \in \operatorname{Gal}(K/\mathbb{Q})$ is determined by its action on $\sqrt[2n]{2}$ and $\zeta_{2n}$:
        $$\sigma(\sqrt[2n]{2}) = \sqrt[2n]{2} \, \zeta_{2n}^b \quad (b \in \mathbb{Z}/2n\mathbb{Z}), \qquad \sigma(\zeta_{2n}) = \zeta_{2n}^a \quad (a \in (\mathbb{Z}/2n\mathbb{Z})^\times).$$
    <2>2. The cyclotomic extension $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{2n})/\mathbb{Q}$ is abelian with Galois group $(\mathbb{Z}/2n\mathbb{Z})^\times$.
    <2>3. The extension $K/\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{2n})$ is a Kummer extension: since $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{2n})$ contains the $(2n)$-th roots of unity, $K/\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{2n})$ is cyclic of degree dividing $2n$:
        $$\operatorname{Gal}(K/\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{2n})) \cong \mathbb{Z}/d\mathbb{Z} \quad \text{where } d = [K : \mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{2n})].$$
    <2>4. The Galois group $\operatorname{Gal}(K/\mathbb{Q})$ embeds into the **affine group** (Frobenius group) $\operatorname{Aff}(\mathbb{Z}/2n\mathbb{Z}) = \mathbb{Z}/2n\mathbb{Z} \rtimes (\mathbb{Z}/2n\mathbb{Z})^\times$ under the action $x \mapsto ax + b$.
    <2>5. Commutation relation: for $\tau: \sqrt[2n]{2} \mapsto \sqrt[2n]{2}\zeta_{2n}$ and $\sigma_a: \zeta_{2n} \mapsto \zeta_{2n}^a$:
        $$\sigma_a \tau \sigma_a^{-1}(\sqrt[2n]{2}) = \sigma_a(\tau(\sqrt[2n]{2})) = \sigma_a(\sqrt[2n]{2}\zeta_{2n}) = \sqrt[2n]{2}\zeta_{2n}^a = \tau^a(\sqrt[2n]{2}).$$
        Thus $\sigma_a \tau \sigma_a^{-1} = \tau^a$.

<1>4. Degree $[K : \mathbb{Q}]$:
    *Proof:*
    <2>1. If $n = 1$ ($x^2 - 2$): $K = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2})$, $[K : \mathbb{Q}] = 2$, $\operatorname{Gal} \cong \mathbb{Z}_2$.
    <2>2. If $n = 2$ ($x^4 - 2$): $K = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt[4]{2}, i)$, $[K : \mathbb{Q}] = 8$, $\operatorname{Gal} \cong D_4$.
    <2>3. In general, $[K : \mathbb{Q}] = 2n \cdot \frac{\varphi(2n)}{[\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt[2n]{2}) \cap \mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{2n}) : \mathbb{Q}]}$. Since $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt[2n]{2}) \cap \mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{2n}) = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2})$ (when $8 \mid 2n$), the degree is $2n \varphi(2n)$ (or $n \varphi(2n)$ if $\sqrt{2} \in \mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{2n})$).

<1>5. Conclusion:
    $x^{2n} - 2$ is irreducible with splitting field $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt[2n]{2}, \zeta_{2n})$ and solvable Galois group isomorphic to a subgroup of $\mathbb{Z}/2n\mathbb{Z} \rtimes (\mathbb{Z}/2n\mathbb{Z})^\times$. Q.E.D.
:::
