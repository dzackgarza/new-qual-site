::: {.problem}
Describe subgroups of the free group $F_2=\langle a,b\rangle$. Give examples having ranks $3$, $4$, and countably infinite rank. For rank $4$, give both normal and nonnormal examples.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #description}

Every subgroup of $F_2$ is free, of rank at most countable, and a subgroup of finite index $d$ has rank $d+1$.

::: pf-proof

By the Nielsen--Schreier theorem every subgroup of a free group is free, and a subgroup of the countable group $F_2$ has a countable basis. If $H\le F_r$ has finite index $d$, the Schreier index formula gives $\operatorname{rank}(H)=1+d(r-1)$, which is $d+1$ for $r=2$.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Rank $3$: $H_2=\ker(F_2\twoheadrightarrow C_2)$, with $a\mapsto 1$ and $b\mapsto 0$, is a normal subgroup of rank $3$.

::: pf-proof

It is normal as a kernel, and it has index $2$, so step [](#description){.pf-ref} gives rank $3$.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Rank $4$, normal: $H_3=\ker(F_2\twoheadrightarrow C_3)$, with $a\mapsto 1$ and $b\mapsto 0$, is a normal subgroup of rank $4$.

::: pf-proof

It is normal as a kernel, and it has index $3$, so step [](#description){.pf-ref} gives rank $4$.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Rank $4$, nonnormal: let $\varphi\colon F_2\twoheadrightarrow S_3$ send $a\mapsto(1\,2)$ and $b\mapsto(1\,2\,3)$. Then $K=\varphi^{-1}(\langle(1\,2)\rangle)$ is a nonnormal subgroup of rank $4$.

::: pf-proof

The map $\varphi$ is surjective since $(1\,2)$ and $(1\,2\,3)$ generate $S_3$. Since $\langle(1\,2)\rangle$ has index $3$ in $S_3$, $K$ has index $3$ in $F_2$, hence rank $4$ by step [](#description){.pf-ref}. If $K$ were normal, then $\langle(1\,2)\rangle=\varphi(K)$ would be normal in $S_3=\varphi(F_2)$, which it is not.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Countably infinite rank: the commutator subgroup $[F_2,F_2]=\ker(F_2\twoheadrightarrow\ZZ^2)$ is normal of infinite index, since $\ZZ^2$ is infinite. It is free and countably generated by step [](#description){.pf-ref}. It is not finitely generated, because a nontrivial finitely generated normal subgroup of a finitely generated free group has finite index. Therefore $[F_2,F_2]$ is free of countably infinite rank.

:::
