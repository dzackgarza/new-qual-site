::: problem
Let $K\subseteq\mathbb{R}$ be a field of characteristic $0$, and let $f(x) \in K[x]$ be an irreducible polynomial of degree $n \ge 3$ such that $f(x)$ has exactly one real root $u \in \mathbb{R}$ and $n - 1$ non-real complex roots.
(1) Prove that the extension degree $[K(u) : K] = n$.
(2) Prove that $K(u)/K$ is not a Galois extension.
(3) Prove that the automorphism group $\operatorname{Aut}(K(u)/K)$ is trivial ($\operatorname{Aut}(K(u)/K) = \{\operatorname{id}\}$).
:::

::: solution
Because $f$ is irreducible and $f(u)=0$, it is the minimal polynomial of $u$ over $K$. Hence
\[
[K(u):K]=\deg f=n.
\]

Since $K\subseteq\RR$ and $u\in\RR$, we have
\[
K(u)\subseteq\RR.
\]
All roots of $f$ other than $u$ are nonreal, so none lies in $K(u)$. Thus $f$ does not split over $K(u)$. In characteristic $0$ the extension is separable, but it is not normal; consequently
\[
K(u)/K\quad\text{is not Galois}.
\]

Finally let
\[
\sigma\in\Aut_K(K(u)).
\]
Since $\sigma$ fixes the coefficients of $f$,
\[
0=\sigma(f(u))=f(\sigma(u)).
\]
Thus $\sigma(u)$ is a root of $f$ lying in $K(u)$. The only such root is $u$, so $\sigma(u)=u$. Since $K(u)$ is generated by $K$ and $u$, this forces $\sigma=\mathrm{id}$.

Therefore
\[
\boxed{[K(u):K]=n,\qquad K(u)/K\text{ is not Galois},\qquad \Aut_K(K(u))=1.}
\]
:::
