::: problem
Do you know an example of a local ring?
Another one?
What about completions?
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. A field is a local ring.
::: {.proof}
its unique maximal ideal is $(0)$.
:::

<1>2. A discrete valuation ring is a local ring.
::: {.proof}
a DVR has a unique nonzero maximal ideal (the ideal generated by a uniformizer); e.g. $\ZZ_{(p)}$ (the localization of $\ZZ$ at a prime $p$) or $k[[t]]$.
:::

<1>3. The ring of formal power series $k[[x_1, \ldots, x_n]]$ over a field $k$ is a local ring.
::: {.proof}
its unique maximal ideal is $(x_1, \ldots, x_n)$, the ideal of power series with zero constant term.
:::

<1>4. The localization $R_{\mathfrak m}$ of a ring $R$ at a maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$ is a local ring.
::: {.proof}
its unique maximal ideal is $\mathfrak m R_{\mathfrak m}$.
:::

<1>5. Completions of local rings are again local.
::: {.proof}
the completion $\widehat{R}$ of a local ring $(R, \mathfrak m)$ is local with maximal ideal $\widehat{\mathfrak m}$; e.g. the $p$-adic integers $\ZZ_p = \varprojlim \ZZ/p^n\ZZ$ is the completion of $\ZZ_{(p)}$ and is local with maximal ideal $(p)$.
:::

<1>6. Q.E.D.
::: {.proof}
<1>1–<1>5 give examples of local rings and their completions.
:::
:::
