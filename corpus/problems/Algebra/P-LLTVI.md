::: {.problem}
Describe the subgroups of $\ZZ^2$.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #free}
Every subgroup $H\le\ZZ^2$ is free abelian of rank $0$, $1$ or $2$.

::: pf-proof
$\ZZ$ is a PID, so every submodule of the free module $\ZZ^2$ is free of rank at most $2$.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
A subgroup of rank $0$ is $0$, and a subgroup of rank $1$ is $\ZZ(a,b)$ for a nonzero $(a,b)\in\ZZ^2$.

::: pf-proof
This restates step [](#free){.pf-ref}: a free abelian group of rank $1$ is generated by one nonzero element.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
For every subgroup $H$ of rank $2$ there are a basis $u_1,u_2$ of $\ZZ^2$ and integers $d_1,d_2>0$ with $d_1\mid d_2$ such that $H=\ZZ d_1u_1\oplus\ZZ d_2u_2$; in particular $[\ZZ^2:H]=d_1d_2<\infty$.

::: pf-proof
Let $A$ be the $2\times2$ integer matrix whose columns form a basis of $H$. Smith normal form gives $U,V\in\GL_2(\ZZ)$ with $UAV=\diag(d_1,d_2)$, $d_1,d_2>0$, $d_1\mid d_2$; the entries are nonzero because $A$ has rank $2$. The columns of $AV$ are again a basis of $H$, and they equal $U^{-1}\diag(d_1,d_2)$, so with $u_1,u_2$ the columns of $U^{-1}$ we get $H=\ZZ d_1u_1\oplus\ZZ d_2u_2$. Then $\ZZ^2/H\cong\ZZ/d_1\ZZ\oplus\ZZ/d_2\ZZ$.
:::
