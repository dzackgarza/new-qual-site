::: {.exercise}
Let $R$ be a PID, $n>0$, and $A\in M_n(R)$. Put
$$
M=R^n/\im A.
$$

(a) Give a necessary and sufficient condition for $M$ to be torsion.

(b) For $R=F[x]$ with $F$ a field, give a square matrix $A$ whose cokernel is $R\times F$.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: pf-step

(a) The cokernel is torsion exactly when $\det A\ne0$.

::: pf-proof

By Smith normal form there are invertible $P,Q$ such that
$$
PAQ=\diag(d_1,\ldots,d_r,0,\ldots,0),
$$
with each $d_i\ne0$. Hence
$$
M\cong\bigoplus_{i=1}^rR/(d_i)\oplus R^{n-r}.
$$
This module is torsion exactly when $r=n$, i.e. exactly when $A$ has full rank over the fraction field of $R$, equivalently when $\det A\ne0$.

Alternatively, if $d=\det A\ne0$, the adjugate identity
$$
A\operatorname{adj}(A)=dI
$$
shows that $d$ annihilates the entire cokernel.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

(b) Take $R=F[x]$, $n=2$, and
$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}0&0\\0&x\end{pmatrix}.
$$

::: pf-proof

Then
$$
\im A=0\times xR,
$$
so
$$
\coker A
\cong R\times R/(x)
\cong R\times F.
$$

:::
