::: {.exercise}
Let $F/K$ be a finite Galois extension and let $A,B$ be intermediate fields. Prove
$$
\Aut_{AB}(F)=\Aut_A(F)\cap\Aut_B(F)
$$
and
$$
\Aut_{A\cap B}(F)=\Aut_A(F)\vee\Aut_B(F),
$$
where $\vee$ denotes the subgroup generated by the two subgroups.
:::

::: {.solution}
Write $H_A=\Aut_A(F)$ and $H_B=\Aut_B(F)$. Since $F/K$ is finite Galois, so is $F/E$ for every intermediate field $E$, and $\Aut_E(F)=\Gal(F/E)$.

::: pf

::: pf-step
$\Aut_{AB}(F)=H_A\cap H_B$.

::: pf-proof
The compositum $AB$ is the smallest subfield of $F$ containing $A$ and $B$, and the fixed field of an automorphism is a subfield. Hence an automorphism fixes $AB$ exactly when it fixes both $A$ and $B$.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
$\Aut_{A\cap B}(F)=H_A\vee H_B$.

::: pf-proof
Let $H=H_A\vee H_B$. An element is fixed by $H$ exactly when it is fixed by both generating subgroups, so
$$
F^H=F^{H_A}\cap F^{H_B}=A\cap B,
$$
where $F^{H_A}=A$ and $F^{H_B}=B$ by the Galois correspondence. By the Galois correspondence again, $H=\Gal(F/F^H)=\Aut_{A\cap B}(F)$.
:::
