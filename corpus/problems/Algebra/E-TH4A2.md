::: {.exercise}
Let $F/K$ be a finite Galois extension and let $A,B$ be intermediate fields. Prove
\[
\operatorname{Aut}_{AB}(F)=\operatorname{Aut}_A(F)\cap\operatorname{Aut}_B(F)
\]
and
\[
\operatorname{Aut}_{A\cap B}(F)=\operatorname{Aut}_A(F)\vee\operatorname{Aut}_B(F),
\]
where $\vee$ denotes the subgroup generated by the two subgroups.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let
\[
G=\operatorname{Gal}(F/K),\qquad H_A=\operatorname{Gal}(F/A),\qquad H_B=\operatorname{Gal}(F/B).
\]

An automorphism fixes the compositum $AB$ exactly when it fixes both $A$ and $B$. Hence
\[
\operatorname{Gal}(F/AB)=H_A\cap H_B.
\]

For the second identity, let
\[
H=\langle H_A,H_B\rangle=H_A\vee H_B.
\]
Its fixed field is
\[
F^H=F^{H_A}\cap F^{H_B}=A\cap B,
\]
because an element is fixed by the generated subgroup exactly when it is fixed by both generating subgroups. By the finite Galois correspondence,
\[
H=\operatorname{Gal}(F/A\cap B).
\]
Thus
\[
\operatorname{Aut}_{A\cap B}(F)=\operatorname{Aut}_A(F)\vee\operatorname{Aut}_B(F).
\]
:::
