::: exercise
8. Recall the definition of the join of two subgroups $H \vee G$ (or $H+G$ ). For $F$ a finite dimensional Galois extension over $K$ and let $A$ and $B$ be intermediate extensions. Prove that
a. $\operatorname{Aut}_{A B} F=\mathrm{Aut}_{A} F \cap \mathrm{Aut}_{B} F$
b. Aut $_{A \cap B} F=\mathrm{Aut}_{A} F \vee \mathrm{Aut}_{B} F$
:::

::: {.solution}
**Part (a).**

<1>1. $\operatorname{Aut}_{AB} F = \operatorname{Aut}_A F \cap \operatorname{Aut}_B F$.
<2>1. $\sigma \in \operatorname{Aut}_{AB} F$ iff $\sigma$ fixes every element of $AB$.
Proof: definition of $\operatorname{Aut}_{AB} F$.
<2>2. $\sigma$ fixes every element of $AB$ iff $\sigma$ fixes every element of $A$ and every element of $B$.
Proof: $AB$ is generated (as a field) by $A \cup B$, so fixing $A \cup B$ is equivalent to fixing $AB$.
<2>3. Hence $\sigma \in \operatorname{Aut}_{AB} F$ iff $\sigma \in \operatorname{Aut}_A F$ and $\sigma \in \operatorname{Aut}_B F$.
Proof: <2>1 and <2>2.

<1>2. Q.E.D. (part (a)).
Proof: <1>1.

**Part (b).**

<1>1. By the fundamental theorem of Galois theory, the correspondence $E \mapsto \operatorname{Aut}_E F$ is an order-reversing bijection between intermediate fields and subgroups of $\operatorname{Gal}(F/K)$.
Proof: fundamental theorem of Galois theory.

<1>2. Under this correspondence, $AB$ corresponds to $\operatorname{Aut}_A F \cap \operatorname{Aut}_B F$ and $A \cap B$ corresponds to $\operatorname{Aut}_A F \vee \operatorname{Aut}_B F$.
Proof: the join (compositum) of fields corresponds to the intersection of subgroups, and the intersection of fields corresponds to the join (subgroup generated by the union) of subgroups.

<1>3. Hence $\operatorname{Aut}_{A \cap B} F = \operatorname{Aut}_A F \vee \operatorname{Aut}_B F$.
Proof: <1>2.

<1>4. Q.E.D. (part (b)).
Proof: <1>3.
:::
