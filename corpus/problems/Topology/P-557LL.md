::: problem
Describe the CW complex structure of the real projective plane $\mathbb{RP}^2$, compute its cellular boundary maps, and compute its simplicial/cellular homology and cohomology groups with coefficients in $\mathbb{Z}$ and $\mathbb{Z}_2$.
:::

::: solution
**Goal:** Compute the cellular homology $H_*(\mathbb{RP}^2; \mathbb{Z})$, $H_*(\mathbb{RP}^2; \mathbb{Z}_2)$, and cohomology groups $H^*(\mathbb{RP}^2; \mathbb{Z})$.

<1>1. CW Complex Structure of $\mathbb{RP}^2$:
    *Proof:*
    <2>1. $\mathbb{RP}^2$ is obtained by identifying antipodal points on the boundary $S^1$ of the closed 2-disk $D^2$.
    <2>2. The standard CW structure on $\mathbb{RP}^2$ consists of one cell in each dimension $0, 1, 2$:
        - $0$-cell: $e^0$ (a single vertex $v$).
        - $1$-cell: $e^1$ (an edge attached to $v$ at both ends, forming the 1-skeleton $\mathbb{RP}^1 \cong S^1$).
        - $2$-cell: $e^2$ (attached along the boundary $\partial D^2 = S^1$ by the degree-2 covering map $z \mapsto z^2$, traveling around the 1-cell twice: $a^2$).
    <2>3. The cellular chain complex $C_*^{\text{cell}}(\mathbb{RP}^2; \mathbb{Z})$ is:
        $$0 \longrightarrow C_2 \xrightarrow{d_2} C_1 \xrightarrow{d_1} C_0 \longrightarrow 0$$
        where $C_2 = \mathbb{Z}\langle e^2 \rangle \cong \mathbb{Z}$, $C_1 = \mathbb{Z}\langle e^1 \rangle \cong \mathbb{Z}$, and $C_0 = \mathbb{Z}\langle e^0 \rangle \cong \mathbb{Z}$.

<1>2. Cellular Boundary Operators:
    *Proof:*
    <2>1. **$d_1: C_1 \to C_0$:**
        The 1-cell starts and ends at $e^0$, so:
        $$d_1(e^1) = e^0 - e^0 = 0.$$
    <2>2. **$d_2: C_2 \to C_1$:**
        The attaching map $\phi_2: \partial D^2 \to \mathbb{RP}^1$ wraps $\partial D^2$ twice around $e^1$ in the same orientation (degree $+1 + 1 = 2$).
        Therefore:
        $$d_2(e^2) = 2 e^1.$$
    <2>3. The cellular chain complex is explicitly:
        $$0 \longrightarrow \mathbb{Z} \xrightarrow{\times 2} \mathbb{Z} \xrightarrow{\times 0} \mathbb{Z} \longrightarrow 0.$$

<1>3. Computation of Homology Groups $H_*(\mathbb{RP}^2; \mathbb{Z})$:
    *Proof:*
    <2>1. **$H_0(\mathbb{RP}^2; \mathbb{Z})$:**
        $$H_0 = \ker(d_0)/\operatorname{im}(d_1) = \mathbb{Z}/(0) \cong \mathbb{Z}.$$
    <2>2. **$H_1(\mathbb{RP}^2; \mathbb{Z})$:**
        $$H_1 = \ker(d_1)/\operatorname{im}(d_2) = \mathbb{Z}/(2\mathbb{Z}) \cong \mathbb{Z}_2.$$
    <2>3. **$H_2(\mathbb{RP}^2; \mathbb{Z})$:**
        $$H_2 = \ker(d_2)/\operatorname{im}(d_3) = (0)/(0) \cong 0.$$
    <2>4. For $k \ge 3$, $H_k(\mathbb{RP}^2; \mathbb{Z}) = 0$.

<1>4. Homology with $\mathbb{Z}_2$ Coefficients:
    *Proof:*
    <2>1. Tensoring with $\mathbb{Z}_2$, the boundary map $d_2 \otimes \operatorname{id}_{\mathbb{Z}_2} = \times 2 = \times 0$:
        $$0 \longrightarrow \mathbb{Z}_2 \xrightarrow{0} \mathbb{Z}_2 \xrightarrow{0} \mathbb{Z}_2 \longrightarrow 0.$$
    <2>2. Therefore:
        $$H_0(\mathbb{RP}^2; \mathbb{Z}_2) \cong \mathbb{Z}_2, \quad H_1(\mathbb{RP}^2; \mathbb{Z}_2) \cong \mathbb{Z}_2, \quad H_2(\mathbb{RP}^2; \mathbb{Z}_2) \cong \mathbb{Z}_2.$$

<1>5. Cohomology Groups $H^*(\mathbb{RP}^2; \mathbb{Z})$:
    *Proof:*
    <2>1. By the Universal Coefficient Theorem for Cohomology:
        $$H^0(\mathbb{RP}^2) \cong \mathbb{Z}, \quad H^1(\mathbb{RP}^2) \cong 0, \quad H^2(\mathbb{RP}^2) \cong \mathbb{Z}_2.$$

<1>6. Conclusion:
    The homology of $\mathbb{RP}^2$ is $H_* = (\mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{Z}_2, 0, 0, \dots)$, with $H_*(\mathbb{RP}^2; \mathbb{Z}_2) = (\mathbb{Z}_2, \mathbb{Z}_2, \mathbb{Z}_2, 0, \dots)$. Q.E.D.
