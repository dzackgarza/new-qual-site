::: problem
Describe the CW complex structure of the real projective plane $\mathbb{RP}^2$, compute its cellular boundary maps, and compute its simplicial/cellular homology and cohomology groups with coefficients in $\mathbb{Z}$ and $\mathbb{Z}_2$.
:::

::: solution
<1>1. The standard CW structure on $\mathbb{RP}^2$ has one cell $e^k$ in each dimension $k=0,1,2$.
<2>1. The $1$-skeleton is $\mathbb{RP}^1\cong S^1$.
<2>2. The $2$-cell is attached by the degree-$2$ map $S^1\to S^1$.

<1>2. With integer coefficients, the cellular chain complex is
\[
0\longrightarrow\mathbb Z
\xrightarrow{\,2\,}\mathbb Z
\xrightarrow{\,0\,}\mathbb Z
\longrightarrow0.
\]
Hence
\[
H_k(\mathbb{RP}^2;\mathbb Z)\cong
\begin{cases}
\mathbb Z,&k=0,\\
\mathbb Z/2,&k=1,\\
0,&k\ge2.
\end{cases}
\]

<1>3. With $\mathbb Z_2$ coefficients, multiplication by $2$ becomes zero, so the cellular chain complex is
\[
0\longrightarrow\mathbb Z_2
\xrightarrow{0}\mathbb Z_2
\xrightarrow{0}\mathbb Z_2
\longrightarrow0.
\]
Therefore
\[
H_k(\mathbb{RP}^2;\mathbb Z_2)\cong
\begin{cases}
\mathbb Z_2,&k=0,1,2,\\
0,&k>2.
\end{cases}
\]

<1>4. The integral cellular cochain complex is
\[
0\longleftarrow\mathbb Z
\xleftarrow{0}\mathbb Z
\xleftarrow{2}\mathbb Z
\longleftarrow0,
\]
so
\[
H^k(\mathbb{RP}^2;\mathbb Z)\cong
\begin{cases}
\mathbb Z,&k=0,\\
\mathbb Z/2,&k=2,\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\]

<1>5. With $\mathbb Z_2$ coefficients all cellular coboundaries vanish, hence
\[
H^k(\mathbb{RP}^2;\mathbb Z_2)\cong
\begin{cases}
\mathbb Z_2,&k=0,1,2,\\
0,&k>2.
\end{cases}
\]
These cellular groups agree with singular homology and cohomology; any triangulation gives the same groups simplicially.
:::
