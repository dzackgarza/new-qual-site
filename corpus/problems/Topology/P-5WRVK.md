::: {.problem}
Let $M = ([0, 1] \times [0, 1]) / \sim$ be the standard Möbius strip, where $(0, y) \sim (1, 1 - y)$.

(a) Prove that $M$ deformation-retracts onto its central circle $C = \{[(x, 1/2)] \mid x \in [0, 1]\} \cong S^1$.

(b) Compute the fundamental group $\pi_1(M, x_0) \cong \ZZ$ and identify its generator $[\alpha]$.

(c) Describe the boundary curve $\partial M$ and prove that the inclusion $\iota: \partial M \hookrightarrow M$ induces the map $\iota_*: \pi_1(\partial M) \to \pi_1(M)$ given by multiplication by $2$ ($[\partial M] = \alpha^2 \in \pi_1(M)$).
:::

::: {.solution}

Let $q:[0,1]\times[0,1]\to M$ be the quotient map.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #retraction}

(a) $H_s(q(x,y))=q\bigl(x,(1-s)y+s/2\bigr)$ is a strong deformation retraction of $M$ onto $C$.

::: pf-proof

$H$ is well defined because $(0,y)\sim(1,1-y)$ implies
$$\bigl(0,(1-s)y+s/2\bigr)\sim\bigl(1,1-((1-s)y+s/2)\bigr)=\bigl(1,(1-s)(1-y)+s/2\bigr).$$
It is continuous on $M\times I$ because $q\times\mathrm{id}_I$ is a quotient map ($I$ is locally compact). It fixes $C=\{q(x,1/2)\}$ pointwise, and $H_1$ maps $M$ onto $C$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #generator}

(b) $\pi_1(M,x_0)\cong\ZZ$ for $x_0=q(0,1/2)$, generated by $[\alpha]$ with $\alpha(t)=q(t,1/2)$.

::: pf-proof

By step [](#retraction){.pf-ref}, the inclusion $C\into M$ is a homotopy equivalence, and $\alpha$ traverses $C\cong S^1$ once, so $[\alpha]$ generates $\pi_1(C,x_0)\cong\ZZ$.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

(c) $\partial M$ is one circle, traversed once by $\gamma_0*\gamma_1$ with $\gamma_0(t)=q(t,0)$ and $\gamma_1(t)=q(t,1)$, and $[\partial M]=[\alpha]^2$, so $\iota_*$ is multiplication by $2$.

::: pf-proof

The edges $y=0$ and $y=1$ map onto $\partial M$, and $q(1,0)=q(0,1)$, $q(1,1)=q(0,0)$, so $\gamma_0*\gamma_1$ is a loop that traverses $\partial M$ once. The homotopy of step [](#retraction){.pf-ref} carries both $\gamma_0$ and $\gamma_1$ to $\alpha$, so $\gamma_0*\gamma_1$ is freely homotopic to $\alpha*\alpha$; after the change of basepoint along $s\mapsto q(0,s/2)$, $[\partial M]=[\alpha]^2$. Identifying $\pi_1(\partial M)\cong\ZZ$ by $[\gamma_0*\gamma_1]$ and $\pi_1(M)\cong\ZZ$ by $[\alpha]$ via step [](#generator){.pf-ref}, $\iota_*$ is multiplication by $\boxed{2}$.

:::
