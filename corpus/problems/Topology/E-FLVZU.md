::: {.exercise}
- What does it mean for a topological space to be **compact**?
:::

::: {.solution}
A topological space $X$ is compact if every open cover of $X$ has a finite subcover: whenever $X=\bigcup_{i\in I}U_i$ with each $U_i$ open, there are $i_1,\ldots,i_r\in I$ with $X=U_{i_1}\cup\cdots\cup U_{i_r}$.
:::
