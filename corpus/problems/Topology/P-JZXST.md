::: {.problem}
Show that the torus $T$ is a $2$-sheeted covering space of the Klein bottle $K$.
:::

::: {.hint}
Draw the square for $T$ and cut it down the center to see two copies of the square for $K$.
:::

::: {.solution}

Let $a,b:\RR^2\to\RR^2$ be $a(x,y)=(x+1,y)$ and $b(x,y)=(-x,y+1)$, and let $\Gamma$ be the group of homeomorphisms of $\RR^2$ they generate.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #k}

$\RR^2/\Gamma\cong K$, and $\Gamma$ acts freely and properly discontinuously.

::: pf-proof

Every element of $\Gamma$ has the form $(x,y)\mapsto(\pm x+m,y+n)$ with $m,n\in\ZZ$, the sign being $(-1)^n$. A nonidentity element with $n\ne0$ moves $y$ by $n$; one with $n=0$ is a nonzero horizontal translation. So for a point $p$, every nonidentity $g\in\Gamma$ moves $p$ by distance at least $1$ unless $n=0$ and $m=0$; hence the action is free, and the ball of radius $1/2$ about $p$ is disjoint from its translates. The square $[0,1]\times[0,1]$ is a fundamental domain: $a$ glues $(0,y)$ to $(1,y)$ and $ab$ glues $(x,0)$ to $(1-x,1)$, which is the Klein bottle identification.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #t}

The subgroup $\Gamma'=\langle a,b^2\rangle$ consists of the translations by $\ZZ\times2\ZZ$, it has index $2$ in $\Gamma$, and $\RR^2/\Gamma'\cong T$.

::: pf-proof

$b^2(x,y)=(x,y+2)$, so $\Gamma'$ is the translation lattice $\ZZ\times2\ZZ$, and $\RR^2/(\ZZ\times2\ZZ)$ is a torus. By the description in step [](#k){.pf-ref}, $\Gamma'$ is the set of elements with $n$ even, which is a subgroup of index $2$.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

The induced map $T=\RR^2/\Gamma'\to\RR^2/\Gamma=K$ is a $\boxed{2\text{-sheeted covering}}$.

::: pf-proof

The quotient maps $\RR^2\to\RR^2/\Gamma'$ and $\RR^2\to\RR^2/\Gamma$ are covering maps because both actions are free and properly discontinuous (step [](#k){.pf-ref}). For such an action, the map $\RR^2/\Gamma'\to\RR^2/\Gamma$ is the quotient of $T$ by the free action of $\Gamma/\Gamma'\cong\ZZ/2$, generated by the involution induced by $b$, so it is a covering whose number of sheets is $[\Gamma:\Gamma']=2$ (step [](#t){.pf-ref}).

:::
