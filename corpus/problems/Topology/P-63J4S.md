::: problem
Let $L = \mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z} = \langle (1,0), (0,1) \rangle \subset \mathbb{R}^2$ be the standard integer lattice, and let $H = \langle (1,0), (0, p) \rangle$ for a positive integer $p \ge 1$.
(1) Prove that $H$ is a subgroup of $L$ of index $[L : H] = p$.
(2) Describe the quotient group $L/H$ and the corresponding $p$-sheeted covering space of the 2-torus $T^2 = \mathbb{R}^2 / L$.
:::

::: solution
Since
\[
H=\langle(1,0),(0,p)\rangle=\mathbb Z\times p\mathbb Z,
\]
it is visibly a subgroup of $L=\mathbb Z^2$.

<1>1. Define
\[
\phi:L\longrightarrow\mathbb Z/p,
\qquad
\phi(m,n)=n\pmod p.
\]
This is surjective and
\[
\ker\phi=\mathbb Z\times p\mathbb Z=H.
\]
Hence the first isomorphism theorem gives
\[
L/H\cong\mathbb Z/p.
\]
Therefore
\[
[L:H]=p.
\]
A set of coset representatives is
\[
(0,0),(0,1),\dots,(0,p-1).
\]

<1>2. The corresponding connected covering of
\[
T^2=\mathbb R^2/L
\]
is
\[
\mathbb R^2/H\longrightarrow\mathbb R^2/L,
\qquad
[(x,y)]_H\longmapsto[(x,y)]_L.
\]
Because $[L:H]=p$, this covering has $p$ sheets.

<1>3. Since
\[
\mathbb R^2/H\cong(\mathbb R/\mathbb Z)\times(\mathbb R/p\mathbb Z),
\]
the covering space is itself a torus. After rescaling the second circle to $\mathbb R/\mathbb Z$, the covering is equivalent to
\[
S^1\times S^1\longrightarrow S^1\times S^1,
\qquad
(z,w)\longmapsto(z,w^p).
\]
:::
