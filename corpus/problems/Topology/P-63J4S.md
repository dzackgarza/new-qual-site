::: {.problem}
Let $L = \ZZ \times \ZZ = \langle (1,0), (0,1) \rangle \subset \RR^2$ be the standard integer lattice, and let $H = \langle (1,0), (0, p) \rangle$ for a positive integer $p \ge 1$.

(a) Prove that $H$ is a subgroup of $L$ of index $[L : H] = p$.

(b) Describe the quotient group $L/H$ and the corresponding $p$-sheeted covering space of the 2-torus $T^2 = \RR^2 / L$.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #index}

(a) $H=\ZZ\times p\ZZ$ is a subgroup of $L$ with $L/H\cong\ZZ/p\ZZ$, so $[L:H]=\boxed{p}$.

::: pf-proof

$H$ is generated by elements of $L$, so it is a subgroup, and it equals $\{(m,pn)\}=\ZZ\times p\ZZ$. The homomorphism $\phi:L\to\ZZ/p\ZZ$, $\phi(m,n)=n\bmod p$, is surjective with kernel $\ZZ\times p\ZZ=H$, so the first isomorphism theorem gives $L/H\cong\ZZ/p\ZZ$. The cosets of $(0,0),(0,1),\ldots,(0,p-1)$ are the $p$ cosets.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

(b) $L/H\cong\boxed{\ZZ/p\ZZ}$, and the covering of $T^2$ corresponding to $H$ is $\RR^2/H\to\RR^2/L$, $[(x,y)]_H\mapsto[(x,y)]_L$, a $p$-sheeted covering by a torus, equivalent to $S^1\times S^1\to S^1\times S^1$, $(z,w)\mapsto(z,w^p)$.

::: pf-proof

$L$ acts on $\RR^2$ by translations freely and properly discontinuously, so $\RR^2\to\RR^2/H\to\RR^2/L$ are coverings and the fiber of the second is $L/H$, of size $p$ by step [](#index){.pf-ref}. Further, $\RR^2/H\cong(\RR/\ZZ)\times(\RR/p\ZZ)$ is a torus. Identifying $\RR/\ZZ$ and $\RR/p\ZZ$ with $S^1$ by $x\mapsto e^{2\pi ix}$ and $y\mapsto e^{2\pi iy/p}$, the covering becomes $(z,w)\mapsto(z,w^p)$.

:::
