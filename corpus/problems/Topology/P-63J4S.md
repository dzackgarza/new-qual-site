::: problem
Let $L = \mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z} = \langle (1,0), (0,1) \rangle \subset \mathbb{R}^2$ be the standard integer lattice, and let $H = \langle (1,0), (0, p) \rangle$ for a positive integer $p \ge 1$.
(1) Prove that $H$ is a subgroup of $L$ of index $[L : H] = p$.
(2) Describe the quotient group $L/H$ and the corresponding $p$-sheeted covering space of the 2-torus $T^2 = \mathbb{R}^2 / L$.
:::

::: solution
**Goal:** Prove $[L : H] = p$ for $H = \langle (1,0), (0,p) \rangle \le L = \mathbb{Z}^2$, compute $L/H \cong \mathbb{Z}_p$, and interpret geometrically as a $p$-sheeted covering of the torus $T^2$.

<1>1. Algebraic Computation of the Index $[L : H]$:
    *Proof:*
    <2>1. The parent group is the free abelian group $L = \mathbb{Z}^2 = \mathbb{Z}(1, 0) \oplus \mathbb{Z}(0, 1)$.
    <2>2. The subgroup is $H = \mathbb{Z}(1, 0) \oplus \mathbb{Z}(0, p) = \mathbb{Z} \times p\mathbb{Z}$.
    <2>3. Using the direct sum decomposition of abelian groups:
        $$L / H = (\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}) / (\mathbb{Z} \times p\mathbb{Z}) \cong (\mathbb{Z}/\mathbb{Z}) \times (\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}) \cong \{0\} \times \mathbb{Z}_p \cong \mathbb{Z}_p.$$
    <2>4. The index of $H$ in $L$ is the order of the quotient group:
        $$[L : H] = |L / H| = |\mathbb{Z}_p| = p.$$
    <2>5. A complete set of coset representatives for $L/H$ is:
        $$\{(0, k) + H \mid k = 0, 1, 2, \dots, p-1\}.$$

<1>2. Determinant / Volume of Fundamental Domain Interpretation:
    *Proof:*
    <2>1. In terms of lattice basis matrices in $\mathbb{R}^2$, let $B_L = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$ and $B_H = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & p \end{pmatrix}$.
    <2>2. The index is the ratio of the areas of fundamental parallelograms:
        $$[L : H] = \frac{\operatorname{vol}(\mathbb{R}^2 / H)}{\operatorname{vol}(\mathbb{R}^2 / L)} = \frac{|\det B_H|}{|\det B_L|} = \frac{p}{1} = p.$$

<1>3. Covering Space of the 2-Torus $T^2 = \mathbb{R}^2 / L$:
    *Proof:*
    <2>1. The fundamental group of the 2-torus is $\pi_1(T^2) \cong \mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z} = L$.
    <2>2. By the Galois correspondence for covering spaces, the subgroup $H \le \pi_1(T^2)$ corresponds to a connected covering space:
        $$\tilde{T}_H = \mathbb{R}^2 / H = \mathbb{R}^2 / (\mathbb{Z} \times p\mathbb{Z}) \cong (\mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Z}) \times (\mathbb{R}/p\mathbb{Z}) \cong S^1 \times S^1 = T^2.$$
    <2>3. The covering projection $q: \mathbb{R}^2/H \to \mathbb{R}^2/L$ given by $(x, y) \pmod H \mapsto (x, y) \pmod L$ is a **$p$-sheeted unbranched covering map** (unwrapping the second circle of the torus by a factor of $p$).

<1>4. Conclusion:
    $H = \mathbb{Z} \times p\mathbb{Z}$ has index $p$ in $L = \mathbb{Z}^2$, quotient $L/H \cong \mathbb{Z}_p$, and corresponds to a $p$-fold torus covering. Q.E.D.
:::
