::: {.problem}
5. $T\union_f D^2$, where $f$ attaches inside the torus
:::

::: {.solution}
The statement is incomplete as a standalone problem: the attaching map $f:S^1\to T$ is not specified beyond the phrase “attaches inside the torus.” Different choices give non-homotopy-equivalent spaces. For example, attaching along a null-homotopic loop gives $T^2\vee S^2$, while attaching along a primitive essential loop kills one generator of $\pi_1(T^2)$ and yields a different homotopy type. The missing attaching curve is therefore essential source data and no unique homology computation is determined by this card.
:::
