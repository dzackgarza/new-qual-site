::: {.problem}
The question is unrecovered. The source retains only the item "$T\cup_f D^2$, where $f$ attaches inside the torus" in a list of spaces under the heading Simplicial Homology. It gives no instruction and does not specify the attaching curve.
:::

::: {.solution}
The homotopy class of the attaching map $f:S^1\to T$ is not specified by the phrase “attaches inside the torus.” Different choices give non-homotopy-equivalent spaces. For example, attaching along a null-homotopic loop gives $T^2\vee S^2$, while attaching along a primitive essential loop kills one generator of $\pi_1(T^2)$ and yields a different homotopy type. Thus the homology depends on the attaching curve.
:::
