::: problem
a. Give the definitions of **covering space** and **deck transformation** (or covering transformation).

b. Describe the universal cover of the Klein bottle and its group of deck transformations.

c. Explicitly give a collection of deck transformations on $\{(x, y) \mid -1 \leq x \leq 1, -\infty < y < \infty\}$ such that the quotient is a Möbius band.

d. Find the universal cover of $\mathbb{RP}^2 \times S^1$ and explicitly describe its group of deck transformations.
:::

::: solution
**Goal:** Define covering spaces and deck transformations, and compute universal covers and deck transformation groups for the Klein bottle, Möbius strip, and $\mathbb{RP}^2 \times S^1$.

<1>1. Part (a): Definitions:
    *Proof:*
    <2>1. **Covering space:** A continuous surjective map $p: \widetilde{X} \to X$ is a *covering map* (and $\widetilde{X}$ is a *covering space* of $X$) if every point $x \in X$ has an open neighborhood $U \subseteq X$ that is **evenly covered**: $p^{-1}(U) = \bigsqcup_{\alpha} V_\alpha$ is a disjoint union of open sets $V_\alpha \subseteq \widetilde{X}$ such that $p|_{V_\alpha}: V_\alpha \to U$ is a homeomorphism for each $\alpha$.
    <2>2. **Deck transformation:** A *deck transformation* (or covering automorphism) of $p: \widetilde{X} \to X$ is a homeomorphism $f: \widetilde{X} \to \widetilde{X}$ such that $p \circ f = p$. The set of all deck transformations forms a group $\operatorname{Deck}(\widetilde{X}/X)$ under composition.

<1>2. Part (b): Universal cover of the Klein bottle $K$:
    *Proof:*
    <2>1. The universal cover is the Euclidean plane $\widetilde{K} = \mathbb{R}^2$, with projection $p: \mathbb{R}^2 \to K$.
    <2>2. The group of deck transformations is $\pi_1(K)$, generated by the two isometries:
        $$T(x, y) = (x, y + 1), \qquad R(x, y) = (x + 1, -y).$$
    <2>3. These satisfy the relation $R T R^{-1} = T^{-1}$, giving the presentation $\operatorname{Deck}(\mathbb{R}^2/K) \cong \langle R, T \mid R T R^{-1} = T^{-1} \rangle$.
    <2>4. The action is free, properly discontinuous, and isometric.

<1>3. Part (c): Möbius band deck transformations on $[-1, 1] \times \mathbb{R}$:
    *Proof:*
    <2>1. Let $M = [-1, 1] \times \mathbb{R}$.
    <2>2. Define the transformation $\tau: M \to M$ by:
        $$\tau(x, y) = (-x, y + 1).$$
    <2>3. The group of deck transformations is the infinite cyclic group $G = \langle \tau \rangle \cong \mathbb{Z}$, where $\tau^n(x, y) = ((-1)^n x, y + n)$.
    <2>4. The quotient space $M / G = ([-1, 1] \times \mathbb{R}) / \mathbb{Z}$ is homeomorphic to the standard Möbius band $[-1, 1] \times [0, 1] / \sim$ where $(x, 0) \sim (-x, 1)$.

<1>4. Part (d): Universal cover of $\mathbb{RP}^2 \times S^1$:
    *Proof:*
    <2>1. The universal cover of $\mathbb{RP}^2$ is $S^2 = \{v \in \mathbb{R}^3 \mid \|v\| = 1\}$ with deck transformation group generated by the antipodal map $a: v \mapsto -v$ ($\operatorname{Deck}(S^2/\mathbb{RP}^2) = \{I, a\} \cong \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}$).
    <2>2. The universal cover of $S^1 \cong \mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Z}$ is $\mathbb{R}$, with deck transformation group generated by translation $t: \theta \mapsto \theta + 1$ ($\operatorname{Deck}(\mathbb{R}/S^1) \cong \mathbb{Z}$).
    <2>3. By the product property of universal covers, the universal cover of $\mathbb{RP}^2 \times S^1$ is:
        $$\widetilde{X} = S^2 \times \mathbb{R}.$$
    <2>4. The deck transformation group is the direct product:
        $$\operatorname{Deck}((S^2 \times \mathbb{R}) / (\mathbb{RP}^2 \times S^1)) \cong \operatorname{Deck}(S^2/\mathbb{RP}^2) \times \operatorname{Deck}(\mathbb{R}/S^1) \cong \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}.$$
    <2>5. The action of $(k, m) \in \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}$ on $(v, \theta) \in S^2 \times \mathbb{R}$ is given explicitly by:
        $$(k, m) \cdot (v, \theta) = ((-1)^k v, \, \theta + m).$$

<1>5. Conclusion:
    All universal covers and explicit deck transformation groups have been specified. Q.E.D.
:::
