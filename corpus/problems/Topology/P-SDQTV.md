::: {.problem}
(a) Give the definitions of **covering space** and **deck transformation** (or covering transformation).

(b) Describe the universal cover of the Klein bottle and its group of deck transformations.

(c) Explicitly give a collection of deck transformations on $\{(x, y) \mid -1 \leq x \leq 1, -\infty < y < \infty\}$ such that the quotient is a Möbius band.

(d) Find the universal cover of $\RP^2 \times S^1$ and explicitly describe its group of deck transformations.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: pf-step
(a) A covering map is a surjective continuous map $p\colon\widetilde X\to X$ such that every $x\in X$ has an open neighborhood $U$ for which $p^{-1}(U)=\bigsqcup_{\alpha}U_\alpha$ is a disjoint union of open sets, each restriction $p|_{U_\alpha}\colon U_\alpha\to U$ being a homeomorphism; $\widetilde X$ is then a covering space of $X$.
A deck transformation of $p$ is a homeomorphism $h\colon\widetilde X\to\widetilde X$ satisfying $p\circ h=p$.
:::

::: pf-step
(b) The universal cover of the Klein bottle $K$ is $\RR^2$, with deck group $\Gamma = \langle R, T \mid RTR^{-1}=T^{-1}\rangle \cong \pi_1(K)$ generated by
$$
T(x,y)=(x,y+1),\qquad R(x,y)=(x+1,-y).
$$

::: pf-proof

::: pf-step
$RTR^{-1}=T^{-1}$, and every element of $\Gamma$ has the form $R^m T^n(x,y) = (x+m, (-1)^m(y+n))$.

::: pf-proof
We have $R^{-1}(x,y) = (x-1,-y)$, so $RTR^{-1}(x,y) = RT(x-1,-y) = R(x-1,-y+1) = (x, y-1) = T^{-1}(x,y)$.
The relation lets every word be rewritten with all powers of $R$ to the left, and $R^mT^n(x,y) = (x+m,(-1)^m(y+n))$ by induction on $m$.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
$\Gamma$ acts freely and properly discontinuously on $\RR^2$, and $\RR^2/\Gamma \cong K$.

::: pf-proof
If $R^mT^n$ fixes a point then $m = 0$ from the first coordinate, and then $n = 0$; so the action is free.
For the open unit disc $D$ about any point, $R^mT^n(D) \cap D \neq \emptyset$ forces $\abs{m} \leq 1$, and then, for each of these $m$, the second coordinate leaves only finitely many $n$; so only finitely many translates meet $D$.
The square $[0,1]^2$ meets every orbit, and the only identifications on its boundary are $(x,0)\sim(x,1)$ by $T$ and $(0,y)\sim(1,1-y)$ by $RT^{-1}$, after using $T$; these are the edge identifications of the Klein bottle.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
$\RR^2 \to K$ is the universal cover and $\Gamma$ is its deck group, isomorphic to $\pi_1(K)$.

::: pf-proof
The quotient map by a free, properly discontinuous action is a normal covering whose deck group is the acting group, and $\RR^2$ is simply connected.
The presentation of $\Gamma$ holds because the normal forms $R^mT^n$ of the first step act distinctly.
:::

::: pf-step
(c) The group $\langle\tau\rangle\cong\ZZ$ generated by $\tau(x,y)=(-x,y+1)$ acts on $[-1,1]\times\RR$ with quotient the Möbius band.

::: pf-proof
We have $\tau^m(x,y)=((-1)^m x,y+m)$, which has no fixed point for $m \neq 0$ and moves the second coordinate by $m$; so the action is free and properly discontinuous.
The strip $[-1,1]\times[0,1]$ meets every orbit, and its only boundary identification is $(x,0)\sim(-x,1)$, which is the Möbius band.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
(d) The universal cover of $\RP^2\times S^1$ is
$$
S^2\times\RR\longrightarrow\RP^2\times S^1,
\qquad
(v,t)\longmapsto([v],e^{2\pi i t}),
$$
with deck group $\{(v,t)\mapsto((-1)^\varepsilon v,t+m) : (\varepsilon,m)\in\ZZ/2\times\ZZ\} \cong \boxed{\ZZ/2\times\ZZ}$.

::: pf-proof
The map is the product of the covering maps $S^2 \to \RP^2$ and $\RR \to S^1$, so it is a covering map, and $S^2 \times \RR$ is simply connected.
The listed maps are deck transformations, they act freely and transitively on each fibre $\{\pm v\} \times (t + \ZZ)$, and the deck group of a universal cover acts simply transitively on fibres; so they are all of them.
:::
