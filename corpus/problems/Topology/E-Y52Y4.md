::: exercise
Show that in a *compact* Hausdorff space, $A$ is closed iff $A$ is compact.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. ($\Rightarrow$) If $A$ is closed, then $A$ is compact (a closed subset of a compact space is compact).
Proof: closed subsets of compact spaces are compact.

<1>2. ($\Leftarrow$) If $A$ is compact, then $A$ is closed (a compact subset of a Hausdorff space is closed).
Proof: compact subsets of Hausdorff spaces are closed.

<1>3. Hence $A$ is closed iff $A$ is compact.
Proof: <1>1 and <1>2.

<1>4. Q.E.D.
Proof: <1>3.
:::
