::: {.exercise}
Show that in a *compact* Hausdorff space, $A$ is closed iff $A$ is compact.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

($\Rightarrow$) If $A$ is closed, then $A$ is compact (a closed subset of a compact space is compact).

::: pf-proof

closed subsets of compact spaces are compact.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

($\Leftarrow$) If $A$ is compact, then $A$ is closed (a compact subset of a Hausdorff space is closed).

::: pf-proof

compact subsets of Hausdorff spaces are closed.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

Hence $A$ is closed iff $A$ is compact.

::: pf-proof

:::
