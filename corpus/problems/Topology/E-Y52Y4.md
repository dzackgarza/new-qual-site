::: {.exercise}
Show that in a *compact* Hausdorff space, $A$ is closed iff $A$ is compact.
:::

::: {.solution}

Let $X$ be compact Hausdorff and $A\subseteq X$.

::: pf

::: pf-step

If $A$ is closed, then $A$ is compact.

::: pf-proof

An open cover $\{U_\alpha\}$ of $A$ by open sets of $X$, together with $X\setminus A$, covers $X$. A finite subcover of $X$, with $X\setminus A$ removed, covers $A$.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

If $A$ is compact, then $A$ is closed.

::: pf-proof

A compact subset of a Hausdorff space is closed: for $x\notin A$, separate $x$ from each $a\in A$ by disjoint open sets, take a finite subcover of $A$ by the neighborhoods of the points $a$, and intersect the corresponding finitely many neighborhoods of $x$ to get a neighborhood of $x$ disjoint from $A$.

:::
