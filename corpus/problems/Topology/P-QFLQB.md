::: {.problem}
Compute the simplicial homology of the figure eight, triangulated as the $1$-dimensional simplicial complex $K$ with vertices $1, 2, 3, 4, 5$ and edges $[12], [13], [23], [34], [35], [45]$, as pictured.

![](../../assets/Topology/650_UCSD_Qual_Questions/Quals/assets/1513072050945.png)
:::

::: {.solution}
Orient each edge $[ij]$ with $i < j$, so that $\partial_1[ij] = [j] - [i]$.
The chain groups are $C_0 \cong \ZZ^5$, $C_1 \cong \ZZ^6$, and $C_k = 0$ for $k \geq 2$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #h0}
$H_0(K) \cong \ZZ$.

::: pf-proof
$H_0 = C_0 / \im \partial_1$.
The relations $[2] = [1]$, $[3] = [1]$, $[4] = [3]$, and $[5] = [3]$ hold in the quotient, so it is generated by $[1]$.
The augmentation $C_0 \to \ZZ$, $\sum n_i [i] \mapsto \sum n_i$, vanishes on $\im \partial_1$ and is surjective, so $[1]$ has infinite order.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #h1}
$H_1(K) = \ker \partial_1$ is free of rank $2$, generated by the cycles $z_1 = [12] + [23] - [13]$ and $z_2 = [34] + [45] - [35]$.

::: pf-proof
Since $C_2 = 0$, $H_1 = \ker \partial_1$, a subgroup of $\ZZ^6$ and hence free.
By step [](#h0){.pf-ref}, $\im \partial_1$ has rank $5 - 1 = 4$, so $\ker \partial_1$ has rank $6 - 4 = 2$.
Both $z_1$ and $z_2$ are cycles: $\partial_1 z_1 = ([2]-[1]) + ([3]-[2]) - ([3]-[1]) = 0$, and likewise for $z_2$.
A cycle $z = \sum n_{ij}[ij]$ minus $n_{12} z_1 + n_{34} z_2$ is a cycle with no $[12]$ or $[34]$ term; its coefficient of $[2]$ in $\partial_1$ is then the $[23]$ coefficient up to sign, and of $[4]$ the $[45]$ coefficient, so both vanish, and then the coefficients of $[1]$ and $[5]$ force the $[13]$ and $[35]$ terms to vanish.
So $z_1, z_2$ generate $\ker \partial_1$.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
$$
\boxed{H_k(K) \cong \begin{cases} \ZZ, & k = 0, \\ \ZZ^2, & k = 1, \\ 0, & k \geq 2. \end{cases}}
$$

::: pf-proof
Steps [](#h0){.pf-ref} and [](#h1){.pf-ref} give $H_0$ and $H_1$, and $H_k = 0$ for $k \geq 2$ because $C_k = 0$.
:::
