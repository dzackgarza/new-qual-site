::: {.problem}
Assume $f(z)$ is analytic on the unit disk $\DD = \{z \in \CC \mid \abs{z} < 1\}$, maps $\DD \to \DD$, satisfies $f(0) = 0$, and is not a rotation (i.e. $f(z) \ne e^{i\theta} z$).
Show that the series of iterates:
$$\sum_{n=1}^\infty f^n(z)$$
converges uniformly on compact subsets of $\DD$ to an analytic function, where $f^1(z) = f(z)$ and $f^{n+1}(z) = f(f^n(z))$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Define $g(z)=f(z)/z$ for $z\ne0$ and $g(0)=f'(0)$; $g$ is holomorphic on $\DD$ because $f(0)=0$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #g-small}
$\abs{g(z)}<1$ for all $z\in\DD$.

::: pf-proof
By the Schwarz lemma, $\abs{f(z)}\le\abs{z}$ on $\DD$ and $\abs{f'(0)}\le1$, so $\abs{g}\le1$ on $\DD$.
If $\abs{g(z_0)}=1$ at some $z_0\in\DD$, the maximum modulus principle makes $g$ a unimodular constant $e^{i\theta}$, and then $f(z)=e^{i\theta}z$ is a rotation, contrary to hypothesis.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #contraction}
Let $K\subset\DD$ be compact and choose $r<1$ with $K\subseteq\overline{D_r(0)}$. Then there is $c<1$ with $\abs{f^n(z)}\le rc^n$ for all $z\in K$ and $n\ge1$.

::: pf-proof
Let $c\coloneqq\max_{\abs z\le r}\abs{g(z)}$, which is less than $1$ by step [](#g-small){.pf-ref} and compactness of $\overline{D_r(0)}$.
Then $\abs{f(w)}\le c\abs{w}\le\abs{w}$ for $\abs w\le r$, so $f$ maps $\overline{D_r(0)}$ into itself, and induction on $n$ gives $\abs{f^n(z)}\le c^n\abs{z}\le rc^n$ for $\abs z\le r$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #uniform}
$\sum_{n\ge1} f^n$ converges uniformly on each compact $K\subset\DD$.

::: pf-proof
The geometric series $\sum_{n\ge1} rc^n$ converges, so the Weierstrass $M$-test and step [](#contraction){.pf-ref} give uniform convergence on $K$.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
The sum is analytic on $\DD$.

::: pf-proof
Each $f^n$ is holomorphic on $\DD$, and a locally uniform limit of holomorphic functions is holomorphic (Weierstrass); step [](#uniform){.pf-ref} supplies the locally uniform convergence of the partial sums.
:::
