::: problem
Assume $f(z)$ is analytic on the unit disk $\mathbb{D} = \{z \in \mathbb{C} \mid |z| < 1\}$, maps $\mathbb{D} \to \mathbb{D}$, satisfies $f(0) = 0$, and is not a rotation (i.e. $f(z) \ne e^{i\theta} z$).
Show that the series of iterates:
$$\sum_{n=1}^\infty f^n(z)$$
converges uniformly on compact subsets of $\mathbb{D}$ to an analytic function, where $f^1(z) = f(z)$ and $f^{n+1}(z) = f(f^n(z))$.
:::

::: solution
By Schwarz's lemma,
\[
|f(z)|\le|z|
\qquad(z\in\mathbb D).
\]
Since $f$ is not a rotation, equality cannot occur at any nonzero point, and
\[
|f'(0)|<1.
\]
Define
\[
g(z)=
\begin{cases}
f(z)/z,&z\ne0,\\
f'(0),&z=0.
\end{cases}
\]
Then $g$ is holomorphic on $\mathbb D$ and
\[
|g(z)|<1
\qquad(z\in\mathbb D).
\]

<1>1. Let $K\subset\mathbb D$ be compact. Choose $r<1$ with
\[
K\subseteq\overline{D_r}.
\]
By compactness,
\[
c:=\max_{|z|\le r}|g(z)|<1.
\]
Hence
\[
|f(z)|\le c|z|
\qquad(|z|\le r).
\]

<1>2. Since $f(\overline{D_r})\subseteq\overline{D_r}$, induction gives
\[
|f^n(z)|\le c^n|z|\le rc^n
\qquad(z\in K).
\]

<1>3. The geometric series
\[
\sum_{n=1}^\infty rc^n
\]
converges. The Weierstrass $M$-test therefore implies that
\[
\sum_{n=1}^\infty f^n(z)
\]
converges uniformly on $K$. Since $K$ was arbitrary, the convergence is locally uniform on $\mathbb D$.

<1>4. Every iterate $f^n$ is holomorphic, so the locally uniform limit theorem implies that the sum is holomorphic on $\mathbb D$.
:::
