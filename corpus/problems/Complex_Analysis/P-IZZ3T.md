::: problem
Suppose $f, g: [0, 1] \to \RR$ where $f$ is Riemann integrable and for $x, y\in [0, 1]$,
\[
\abs{g(x) - g(y)} \leq \abs{f(x) - f(y)}
.\]

Prove that $g$ is Riemann integrable.
:::
