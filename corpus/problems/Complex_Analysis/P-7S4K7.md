::: {.problem}
Show that any power series is continuous on its domain of convergence.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $\sum a_n z^n$ have radius of convergence $R>0$; its domain of convergence is the open disk $\abs z < R$.
Fix $z_0$ with $\abs{z_0} < R$ and choose $\rho$ with $\abs{z_0} < \rho < R$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #uniform}
The series converges uniformly on the closed disk $\abs z \leq \rho$.

::: pf-proof
A power series converges absolutely inside its radius of convergence, so $\sum_n \abs{a_n} \rho^n < \infty$.
For $\abs z\le\rho$, $\abs{a_n z^n} \leq \abs{a_n} \rho^n$, so the Weierstrass $M$-test applies.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #continuous-on-disk}
The sum of the series is continuous on $\abs z \leq \rho$.

::: pf-proof
The partial sums $\sum_{n=0}^{N} a_n z^n$ are polynomials, hence continuous, and by step [](#uniform){.pf-ref} they converge uniformly on $\abs z\le\rho$.
A uniform limit of continuous functions is continuous.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
The sum of the series is continuous at $z_0$, and hence on $\abs z<R$.

::: pf-proof
$z_0$ is an interior point of $\abs z\le\rho$, so step [](#continuous-on-disk){.pf-ref} gives continuity at $z_0$, and $z_0$ was an arbitrary point of the open disk.
:::
