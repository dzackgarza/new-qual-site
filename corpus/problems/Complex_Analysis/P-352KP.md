::: {.problem}
Assume $f$ is continuous in the region: $x \geq x_0, \; 0 \leq y \leq b$ and the limit $$\displaystyle \lim_{x \rightarrow + \infty} f(x + iy) = A$$ exists uniformly with respect to $y$ (independent of $y$). Show that $$\lim_{x \rightarrow + \infty} \int_{\gamma_x} f(z) dz  = iA b \; , \; \;$$ where $\gamma_x \coloneqq \{ z \; \st \; z = x + it, \; 0 \leq  t \leq b\}.$
:::

::: {.solution}

Orient $\gamma_x$ upward, by $z(t)=x+it$ for $t\in[0,b]$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #parametrize-segment}
$\int_{\gamma_x} f(z)\,dz = i\int_0^b f(x + it)\,dt$.

::: pf-proof
Substitute $z=x+it$, $dz=i\,dt$.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
$\int_{\gamma_x} f(z)\,dz\to iAb$ as $x\to+\infty$.

::: pf-proof
Let $\varepsilon>0$.
By the uniform convergence, there is $X\geq x_0$ with $\abs{f(x + it) - A} < \varepsilon$ for all $x > X$ and all $t \in [0,b]$.
For $x>X$, step [](#parametrize-segment){.pf-ref} gives
$$
\abs{\int_{\gamma_x} f(z)\,dz - iAb} = \abs{i\int_0^b (f(x+it) - A)\,dt} \le \int_0^b \abs{f(x+it) - A}\,dt \leq b\varepsilon.
$$
:::
