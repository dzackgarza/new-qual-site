::: {.problem}
Let $\Omega \subset \CC$ be an open and bounded domain and $\phi: \Omega \to \Omega$ a holomorphic map.
Prove that if there exists a point $z_0 \in \Omega$ such that $\phi(z_0) = z_0$ and $\phi'(z_0) = 1$, then $\phi(z) = z$ for all $z \in \Omega$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Translate so that $z_0=0$; thus $0\in\Omega$, $\phi(0)=0$ and $\phi'(0)=1$.
Let $\phi^{\circ n}$ denote the $n$-fold iterate of $\phi$.
Choose $M$ with $\abs{w}\le M$ for every $w\in\Omega$, and $r>0$ with $\overline{D_r(0)}\subset\Omega$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #taylor}
If $\phi$ is not the identity near $0$, then $\phi(z)=z+az^m+O(z^{m+1})$ for some $m\ge2$ and $a\ne0$.

::: pf-proof
$\phi(z)-z$ is holomorphic near $0$, not identically zero there, and vanishes to order at least $2$ at $0$ because $\phi(0)=0$ and $\phi'(0)=1$.
Let $m$ be its order of vanishing and $a$ its leading coefficient.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #iterate}
In the situation of step [](#taylor){.pf-ref}, $(\phi^{\circ n})^{(m)}(0)=m!\,na$ for every $n\ge1$.

::: pf-proof
By induction $\phi^{\circ n}(z)=z+na z^m+O(z^{m+1})$: composing gives
$$
\phi^{\circ (n+1)}(z) = \phi^{\circ n}(z) + a\bigl(\phi^{\circ n}(z)\bigr)^m + O(z^{m+1}) = z+(n+1)a z^m+O(z^{m+1}).
$$
Read off the coefficient of $z^m$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #local-identity}
$\phi(z)=z$ near $0$.

::: pf-proof
Every iterate maps $\Omega$ into $\Omega$, so $\abs{\phi^{\circ n}}\le M$ on $D_r(0)$.
Cauchy's estimate gives $\abs{(\phi^{\circ n})^{(m)}(0)}\le m!M/r^m$.
If $\phi$ were not the identity near $0$, step [](#iterate){.pf-ref} would give $n\abs{a}\le M/r^m$ for every $n$, which is impossible since $a\ne0$.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
$\phi(z)=z$ on $\Omega$.

::: pf-proof
By step [](#local-identity){.pf-ref} and the identity theorem on the connected domain $\Omega$.
:::
