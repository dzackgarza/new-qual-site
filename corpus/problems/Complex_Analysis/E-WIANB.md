::: {.exercise}
For $\Omega\subseteq\CC$, show that $A(\CC)\definedas \theset{f: \Omega \to \CC \st f\text{ is holomorphic, bounded}}$ is a Banach space.

> Hint: Apply Morera's Theorem and Cauchy's Theorem
:::
