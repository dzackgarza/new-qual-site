::: {.problem}
Suppose $\sum_{n=0}^\infty a_n z^n$ converges for some $z_0 \neq 0$.

a.  
Prove that the series converges absolutely for each $z$ with $\abs z < \abs z_0$.

b.  
Suppose $0 < r < \abs{z_0}$ and show that the series converges uniformly on $\abs{z} \leq r$.

:::

::: {.solution}
**Part (a).**

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #terms-bounded-a}
Since $\sum a_n z_0^n$ converges, $a_n z_0^n \to 0$, so $\{a_n z_0^n\}$ is bounded: $|a_n z_0^n| \le M$ for some $M$.

::: pf-proof
the terms of a convergent series tend to $0$, hence are bounded.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #term-ratio-bound-a}
For $|z| < |z_0|$, $|a_n z^n| = |a_n z_0^n| \left|\frac{z}{z_0}\right|^n \le M \left|\frac{z}{z_0}\right|^n$.

::: pf-proof
Step [](#terms-bounded-a){.pf-ref}.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #geometric-converges-a}
$\sum M \left|\frac{z}{z_0}\right|^n$ converges (geometric series with ratio $|z/z_0| < 1$).

::: pf-proof
geometric series.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #absolute-convergence-a}
Hence $\sum |a_n z^n|$ converges by the comparison test.

::: pf-proof
Steps [](#term-ratio-bound-a){.pf-ref} and [](#geometric-converges-a){.pf-ref}.
:::

:::

:::

**Part (b).**

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #term-bound-b}
For $|z| \le r < |z_0|$, $|a_n z^n| \le M \left(\frac{r}{|z_0|}\right)^n$.

::: pf-proof
Step [](#term-ratio-bound-a){.pf-ref} of part (a), with $|z| \le r$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #geometric-converges-b}
$\sum M \left(\frac{r}{|z_0|}\right)^n$ converges (geometric series with ratio $r/|z_0| < 1$).

::: pf-proof
geometric series.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #uniform-convergence-b}
Hence $\sum a_n z^n$ converges uniformly on $|z| \le r$ by the Weierstrass $M$-test.

::: pf-proof
Steps [](#term-bound-b){.pf-ref} and [](#geometric-converges-b){.pf-ref}.
:::

:::

::: pf-qed
Step [](#absolute-convergence-a){.pf-ref} (a) and step [](#uniform-convergence-b){.pf-ref} (b).
:::
