::: {.problem}
Suppose $\sum_{n=0}^\infty a_n z^n$ converges for some $z_0 \neq 0$.

(a) Prove that the series converges absolutely for each $z$ with $\abs z < \abs{z_0}$.

(b) Suppose $0 < r < \abs{z_0}$ and show that the series converges uniformly on $\abs{z} \leq r$.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #terms-bounded}
There is $M$ with $\abs{a_n z_0^n} \le M$ for all $n$.

::: pf-proof
Since $\sum a_n z_0^n$ converges, $a_n z_0^n \to 0$, and a convergent sequence is bounded.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #term-bound}
For every $z$, $\abs{a_n z^n} \le M \abs{z/z_0}^n$.

::: pf-proof
$\abs{a_n z^n} = \abs{a_n z_0^n}\,\abs{z/z_0}^n$; apply step [](#terms-bounded){.pf-ref}.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
(a) For $\abs z<\abs{z_0}$, $\sum_n \abs{a_n z^n}$ converges.

::: pf-proof
By step [](#term-bound){.pf-ref}, compare with the geometric series $\sum M\abs{z/z_0}^n$, whose ratio is less than $1$.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
(b) For $0<r<\abs{z_0}$, $\sum a_n z^n$ converges uniformly on $\abs z\le r$.

::: pf-proof
By step [](#term-bound){.pf-ref}, $\abs{a_n z^n}\le M(r/\abs{z_0})^n$ for $\abs z\le r$, and $\sum M(r/\abs{z_0})^n$ is a convergent geometric series.
The Weierstrass $M$-test gives uniform convergence.
:::
