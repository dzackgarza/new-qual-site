:::{.problem}
Suppose $\sum_{n=0}^\infty a_n z^n$ converges for some $z_0 \neq 0$.

a.  
Prove that the series converges absolutely for each $z$ with $\abs z < \abs z_0$.

b.  
Suppose $0 < r < \abs{z_0}$ and show that the series converges uniformly on $\abs{z} \leq r$.

:::

::: {.solution}
**Part (a).**

<1>1. Since $\sum a_n z_0^n$ converges, $a_n z_0^n \to 0$, so $\{a_n z_0^n\}$ is bounded: $|a_n z_0^n| \le M$ for some $M$.
::: {.proof}
the terms of a convergent series tend to $0$, hence are bounded.
:::

<1>2. For $|z| < |z_0|$, $|a_n z^n| = |a_n z_0^n| \left|\frac{z}{z_0}\right|^n \le M \left|\frac{z}{z_0}\right|^n$.
::: {.proof}
<1>1.
:::

<1>3. $\sum M \left|\frac{z}{z_0}\right|^n$ converges (geometric series with ratio $|z/z_0| < 1$).
::: {.proof}
geometric series.
:::

<1>4. Hence $\sum |a_n z^n|$ converges by the comparison test.
::: {.proof}
<1>2 and <1>3.
:::

**Part (b).**

<1>1. For $|z| \le r < |z_0|$, $|a_n z^n| \le M \left(\frac{r}{|z_0|}\right)^n$.
::: {.proof}
<1>2 of part (a), with $|z| \le r$.
:::

<1>2. $\sum M \left(\frac{r}{|z_0|}\right)^n$ converges (geometric series with ratio $r/|z_0| < 1$).
::: {.proof}
geometric series.
:::

<1>3. Hence $\sum a_n z^n$ converges uniformly on $|z| \le r$ by the Weierstrass $M$-test.
::: {.proof}
<1>1 and <1>2.
:::

<1>4. Q.E.D.
::: {.proof}
<1>4 (a) and <1>3 (b).
:::
:::
