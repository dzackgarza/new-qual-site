::: problem
Let $P, Q$ be polynomials with no common zeros.
Assume $a$ is a root of $Q$.
Find the principal part of $P/Q$ at $z=a$ in terms of $P$ and $Q$ if $a$ is (1) a simple root, and (2) a double root.
:::
