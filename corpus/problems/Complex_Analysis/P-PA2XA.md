::: problem
Suppose that $f$ is holomorphic on a punctured open set $\Omega\setminus\theset{w_0}$ and let $T\subset \Omega$ be a triangle containing $w_0$.
Prove that if $f$ is bounded near $w_0$, then $\int_T f(z) ~dz = 0$.
:::
