:::{.problem}
Show that $f'$ bounded implies $f$ is uniformly continuous.

:::

:::{.solution}
Apply the MVT:
\[
\abs{f(x) - f(y)} = \abs{f(\xi)} \abs{x-y} \convergesto{y\to x} 0
.\]

:::
