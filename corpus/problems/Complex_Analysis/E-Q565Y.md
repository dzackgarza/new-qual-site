::: {.exercise}
Show that if $f$ is holomorphic on a connected region $\Omega$ and $f'\equiv 0$ on $\Omega$, then $f$ is constant on $\Omega$.
:::

::: {.solution}

Fix $z_0\in\Omega$ and let $A\coloneqq\{z\in\Omega:f(z)=f(z_0)\}$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #open}
$A$ is open.

::: pf-proof
Let $z\in A$ and choose $r>0$ with $D(z,r)\subseteq\Omega$.
For $w\in D(z,r)$, integrating along the segment from $z$ to $w$ gives $f(w)-f(z)=\int_{[z,w]} f'(\zeta)\,d\zeta=0$, so $D(z,r)\subseteq A$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #closed}
$A$ is closed in $\Omega$.

::: pf-proof
$A$ is the preimage of the point $f(z_0)$ under the continuous map $f$.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
$f$ is constant on $\Omega$.

::: pf-proof
$A$ contains $z_0$ and is open and closed in the connected set $\Omega$ by steps [](#open){.pf-ref} and [](#closed){.pf-ref}, so $A=\Omega$.
:::
