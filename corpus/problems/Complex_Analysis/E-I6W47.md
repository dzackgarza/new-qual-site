::: {.exercise}
Show that if $u$ is harmonic on $\RR^2$ and bounded, then $u$ is constant.
:::

::: {.solution title="Using Liouville"}
Write $f=u+iv$ for $v$ a harmonic conjugate of $u$, then $f$ is holomorphic on $\CC$.
Now $e^f = e^{u+iv} = e^u e^{iv}$ and thus
\[
\abs{e^f}= \abs{e^u} \abs{e^{iv}} = e^u
\]
is bounded, so $e^f$ is a bounded entire function and thus constant by Liouville.
Then $0=(e^f)'=f'e^f$, and $e^f$ has no zeros, so $f'\equiv0$ and $f$ is constant. Hence $u=\Re f$ is constant.
:::
