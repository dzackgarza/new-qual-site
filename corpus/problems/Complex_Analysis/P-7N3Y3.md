::: {.problem}
Suppose $f$ is entire and there exist $A, R >0$ and natural number $N$ such that $|f(z)| \leq A |z|^N$ for $|z| \geq R$.
Show that (i) $f$ is a polynomial and (ii) the degree of $f$ is at most $N$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Write the Taylor series of $f$ at the origin as
\[
f(z)=\sum_{k=0}^\infty a_kz^k.
\]
For any $\rho\ge R$, Cauchy's estimate gives
\[
|a_k|=\frac{|f^{(k)}(0)|}{k!}
\le \frac{M(\rho)}{\rho^k},
\qquad
M(\rho):=\max_{|z|=\rho}|f(z)|.
\]
The hypothesis gives $M(\rho)\le A\rho^N$, hence
\[
|a_k|\le A\rho^{N-k}.
\]
If $k>N$, letting $\rho\to\infty$ yields $a_k=0$. Thus all Taylor
coefficients above degree $N$ vanish, so
\[
f(z)=a_0+a_1z+\cdots+a_Nz^N.
\]
Therefore $f$ is a polynomial and
\[
\boxed{\deg f\le N}
\]
unless $f\equiv0$, in which case the conclusion is immediate.
:::
