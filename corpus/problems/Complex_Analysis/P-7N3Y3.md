::: {.problem}
Suppose $f$ is entire and there exist $A, R >0$ and natural number $N$ such that $\abs{f(z)} \leq A \abs{z}^N$ for $\abs{z} \geq R$.
Show that (i) $f$ is a polynomial and (ii) the degree of $f$ is at most $N$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Write $f(z)=\sum_{k\ge0} a_kz^k$, the Taylor series of $f$ at $0$, which converges on $\CC$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #coefficient-bound}
For every $k\ge0$ and $\rho\ge R$, $\abs{a_k}\le A\rho^{N-k}$.

::: pf-proof
Cauchy's estimate on the circle $\abs{z}=\rho$ gives $\abs{a_k}=\frac{\abs{f^{(k)}(0)}}{k!}\le\rho^{-k}\max_{\abs{z}=\rho}\abs{f(z)}$, and the hypothesis bounds the maximum by $A\rho^N$.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
$f(z)=a_0+a_1z+\cdots+a_Nz^N$, so $f$ is a polynomial of degree at most $N$.

::: pf-proof
For $k>N$, letting $\rho\to\infty$ in step [](#coefficient-bound){.pf-ref} gives $a_k=0$.
:::
