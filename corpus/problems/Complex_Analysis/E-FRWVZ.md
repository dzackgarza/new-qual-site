::: {.exercise}
\[
\int_{0}^{\infty} \frac{\cos (x)}{x^{2}+b^{2}} d x=\frac{\pi \mathrm{e}^{-b}}{2 b} .
.\]
:::


::: {.solution}
The integrand $\frac{\cos x}{x^2+b^2}$ is even, so the integral is half of $\int_\RR\frac{\cos x}{x^2+b^2}\,dx$.
