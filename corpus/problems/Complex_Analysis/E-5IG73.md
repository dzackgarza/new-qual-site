::: {.problem}
Let $f(z) = \sum_{n=0}^\infty c_n z^n$ be analytic and one-to-one in $|z| < 1$. 
For $0<r<1$, let $D_r$ be the disk $|z|<r$. 
Show that the area of $f(D_r)$ is finite and is given by
$$
S = \pi \sum_{n=1}^\infty n |c_n|^2 r^{2n}
.$$ 

> Note that in
general the area of $f(D_1)$ is infinite.

:::

::: {.solution}
Write $f=u+iv$. By the Cauchy--Riemann equations the Jacobian determinant of $f$ is $u_xv_y-u_yv_x=u_x^2+v_x^2=\abs{f'}^2$. Since $f$ is injective on $\DD_r$, the change of variables formula gives, in polar coordinates $z=\rho e^{it}$ on $\DD_r$,
\[
\mu(f(\DD_r))
&= \int_{\DD_r} \abs{f'(z)}^2\,dA \\
&= \int_{\DD_r} \sum_{j, k\geq 1} jk c_j \bar{c_k} z^{j-1}\bar{z}^{k-1}\,dA \\
&= \int_0^r\int_0^{2\pi} \sum_{j, k\geq 1} 
jk c_j \bar{c_k} \rho^{j+k-1} e^{i(j-k)t} \,dt\,d\rho \\
&= \int_0^r 2\pi\sum_{k\geq 1} k^2 \abs{c_k}^2 \rho^{2k-1} \,d\rho \\
&= \pi \sum_{k\geq 1} k \abs{c_k}^2 r^{2k}
.\]
The double series converges uniformly on $\overline{\DD_r}$, which justifies integrating term by term, and only the terms $j=k$ survive the $t$-integration. The area is finite because $\abs{f'}^2$ is bounded on the compact set $\overline{\DD_r}$.
:::
