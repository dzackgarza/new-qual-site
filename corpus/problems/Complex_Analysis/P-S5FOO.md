::: problem
$\displaystyle \|f\|_{(\infty, s)} = \text{sup}_{z \in D_s(z_0)}|f(z)|$ and $\displaystyle \|f\|_{(1, r)} = \int_{D_r(z_0)} |f(z)|dx dy$.
:::
