::: {.exercise}
Let $f$ be entire with $\im(f) \intersect \DD_r(z_0)$ empty.
Show $f$ must be constant without using the Casorati-Weierstrass or Picard theorems.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $g(z) \coloneqq f(z) - z_0$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #bounded}
$1/g$ is entire and $\abs{1/g(z)}\le 1/r$ for all $z\in\CC$.

::: pf-proof
Since $f(z)\notin\DD_r(z_0)$, we have $\abs{g(z)}\ge r>0$ for every $z$.
So $g$ has no zeros, $1/g$ is entire, and $\abs{1/g}\le1/r$.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
$f$ is constant.

::: pf-proof
By Liouville's theorem and step [](#bounded){.pf-ref}, $1/g$ equals a constant $c$, and $c\ne0$ because $1/g$ has no zeros.
Hence $f = z_0 + 1/c$ is constant.
:::
