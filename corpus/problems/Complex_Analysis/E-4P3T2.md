::: {.exercise}
Show that $\sum_{k\geq 0}z^k/k!$ converges locally uniformly to $e^z$.

:::

::: {.solution}
Let $K$ be compact, and choose $M$ with $\abs{z} \leq M$ for $z\in K$. Then, with $\norm{\cdot}_\infty$ the supremum over $K$,
\[
\norm{e^z - \sum_{0\leq k \leq n} z^k/k!}_\infty 
&= \norm{\sum_{k\geq n+1}z^k/k! }_{\infty} \\
&\leq \sum_{k\geq n+1} M^k /k!
.\]
The right side is the tail of the convergent series $\sum_{k\geq0}M^k/k!=e^M$, so it tends to $0$ as $n\to\infty$. Hence the partial sums converge to $e^z$ uniformly on $K$ (this is the Weierstrass $M$-test with $M_k=M^k/k!$).

:::
