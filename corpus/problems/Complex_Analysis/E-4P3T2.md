::: {.exercise}
Show that $\sum_{k\geq 0}z^k/k!$ converges locally uniformly to $e^z$.

:::

::: {.solution}
Let $K\subset\CC$ be compact, choose $M$ with $\abs{z} \leq M$ for $z\in K$, and write $\norm{\cdot}_\infty$ for the supremum over $K$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #tail}
$\displaystyle\norm{e^z - \sum_{0\leq k \leq n} \frac{z^k}{k!}}_\infty\le\sum_{k\geq n+1} \frac{M^k}{k!}$.

::: pf-proof
The series converges to $e^z$ pointwise, so
$$
\begin{aligned}
\norm{e^z - \sum_{0\leq k \leq n} \frac{z^k}{k!}}_\infty
&= \norm{\sum_{k\geq n+1}\frac{z^k}{k!} }_{\infty}
\leq \sum_{k\geq n+1} \frac{M^k}{k!}.
\end{aligned}
$$
:::

:::

::: pf-step
The partial sums converge to $e^z$ uniformly on $K$.

::: pf-proof
The bound of step [](#tail){.pf-ref} is the tail of the convergent series $\sum_{k\geq0}M^k/k!=e^M$, so it tends to $0$ as $n\to\infty$.
This is the Weierstrass $M$-test with $M_k=M^k/k!$.
:::
