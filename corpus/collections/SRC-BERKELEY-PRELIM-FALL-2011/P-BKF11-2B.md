::: {.problem}
Let $k$ be one of the fields $\mathbb C$, $\mathbb R$, $\mathbb Q$, or $\mathbb F_{4044121}$, where $4044121=2011^2$ and $2011$ is prime.

For which of these choices of $k$ is
\[
k[x]/(x^4+6x-12)
\]
a field?
