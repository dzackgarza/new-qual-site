::: {.problem}
The Bessel function
\[
J_1(x)=a_0+a_1x+a_2x^2+\cdots
\]
satisfies
\[
x^2\frac{d^2J_1}{dx^2}+x\frac{dJ_1}{dx}+(x^2-1)J_1=0
\]
and has derivative $1$ at $0$. Find the coefficients $a_n$.
:::
