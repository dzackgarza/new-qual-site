::: {.problem}
Let $f(x)$ be an irreducible polynomial over $\mathbb Q$.
Let $a\in\mathbb C$ be a nonzero root such that $a^2$ is also a root.
Prove that for some $n$, the polynomial $f(x)$ divides $x^n-1$.
:::
