::: {.problem}
Suppose $a_n>0$, $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n=L<\infty$, and for every $n$,
\[
a_n\le \sum_{m=n+1}^{\infty}a_m.
\]
Show that for every $t$ with $0<t<L$ there is a subseries $\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}a_{n_i}$ whose sum is $t$.
:::
