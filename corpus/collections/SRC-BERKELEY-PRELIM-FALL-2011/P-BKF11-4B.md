::: {.problem}
The sequence $(u_n)$ is defined by
\[
u_0=0,\qquad u_1=1,\qquad u_n=3u_{n-1}+u_{n-2}.
\]
Calculate
\[
\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{u_n}{u_{n-1}}.
\]
:::
