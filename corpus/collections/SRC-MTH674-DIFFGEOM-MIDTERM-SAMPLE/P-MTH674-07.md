::: {.problem}
(a) Let
\[
\pi_P:S^2\longrightarrow\mathbb{RP}^2,
\qquad
\pi_P(x,y,z)=[x,y,z],
\]
be the natural projection.
Show that the open subsets of $\mathbb{RP}^2$ are precisely the images under $\pi_P$ of open subsets of $S^2$.

(b) Show that $\pi_P:S^2\to\mathbb{RP}^2$ is smooth and has rank $2$ everywhere.

(c) Let
\[
F:\mathbb R^2\longrightarrow\mathbb{RP}^2,
\qquad F(x,y)=[x,y,1],
\]
and let $X=x\partial_x-y\partial_y$ on $\mathbb R^2$.
Prove that there is a smooth vector field on $\mathbb{RP}^2$ that is $F$-related to $X$, and find its coordinate expressions in the standard charts on $\mathbb{RP}^2$.
:::
