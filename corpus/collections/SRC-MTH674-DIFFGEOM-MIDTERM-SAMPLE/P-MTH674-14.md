::: {.problem}
(a) Let $\varphi_3$ denote the standard coordinates on
\[
\mathcal U_3=\{[x,y,z]\in\mathbb{RP}^2:z\ne0\},
\qquad
(u^1,u^2)=\varphi_3([x,y,z])=(x/z,y/z).
\]
The coordinate tangent vector $v=\partial/\partial u^1$ is represented by a curve $\alpha$ in $\mathbb{RP}^2$, hence by a one-parameter family of lines in $\mathbb R^3$.
Describe this family for a curve $\alpha$ of your choice.

(b) Let $\varphi_{34}$ denote the coordinates on $\operatorname{Gr}(4,2)$ from Problem XII, with coordinate matrix
\[
\begin{pmatrix}u_1^1&u_1^2\\u_2^1&u_2^2\end{pmatrix}=\varphi_{34}(\mathcal P).
\]
The coordinate tangent vector $v=\partial/\partial u_2^2$ is represented by a curve $\alpha$ in $\operatorname{Gr}(4,2)$, hence by a one-parameter family of planes in $\mathbb R^4$.
Describe this family for a curve $\alpha$ of your choice.
