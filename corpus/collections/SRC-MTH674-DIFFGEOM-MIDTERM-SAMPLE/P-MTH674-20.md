(a) Show that the set of all lines in $\mathbb R^2$ can be identified with an open subset of $\mathbb{RP}^2$.

(b) Show that the set of all planes in $\mathbb R^3$ can be identified with an open subset of $\mathbb{RP}^3$.

(c) Show that the space of all oriented lines in $\mathbb R^3$ can be identified with $TS^2$.
