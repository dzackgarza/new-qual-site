::: {.problem}
Let $\mathcal U\subset\RR^2$ be open.
A proper coordinate patch is a one-to-one immersion
\[
x:\mathcal U\longrightarrow\RR^3
\]
such that
\[
x^{-1}:x(\mathcal U)\longrightarrow\mathcal U
\]
is continuous for the subspace topology on $x(\mathcal U)$.
A surface is a subset $S\subset\RR^3$ with the subspace topology such that each $p\in S$ lies in the image $x(\mathcal U)$ of a proper coordinate patch $x$ whose image $x(\mathcal U)$ is an open neighborhood of $p$ in $S$.
Show that a surface is a differentiable manifold.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

Each inverse map
$$
x^{-1}:x(U)\to U
$$
is a topological chart on $S$.


::: pf-proof

By definition of a proper coordinate patch, $x$ is one-to-one and its inverse on $x(U)$ is continuous. Since $x$ is smooth, it is continuous. Therefore
$$
x:U\to x(U)
$$
is a homeomorphism. By the definition of a surface, $x(U)$ is open in $S$, and $U$ is open in $\RR^2$. Thus $x^{-1}$ is a coordinate chart.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

Let
$$
x:U\to S,
\qquad
y:V\to S
$$
be two such patches with overlapping images. Their transition map
$$
y^{-1}\circ x
$$
is smooth.


::: pf-proof

Fix $u_0\in U$ with
$$
x(u_0)=y(v_0)
$$
for some $v_0\in V$. Since $y$ is an immersion, the $3\times2$ derivative matrix $Dy_{v_0}$ has rank $2$. Hence some $2\times2$ minor is nonzero. Let
$$
\pi:\RR^3\to\RR^2
$$
be projection onto the corresponding two ambient coordinates. Then
$$
D(\pi\circ y)_{v_0}
$$
is invertible.

By the inverse function theorem, after shrinking around $v_0$,
$$
\pi\circ y
$$
is a diffeomorphism onto an open subset of $\RR^2$. After also shrinking around $u_0$ so that the images lie in this common surface neighborhood,
$$
y^{-1}\circ x
=(\pi\circ y)^{-1}\circ\pi\circ x.
$$
The right-hand side is smooth. Since $u_0$ was arbitrary, the full transition map is smooth.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

The proper patches form a smooth atlas on $S$.


::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref}, their inverse maps are topological charts covering $S$. By step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref}, all transition maps are smooth.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

The surface $S$ is Hausdorff and second countable.


::: pf-proof

The topology on $S$ is the subspace topology inherited from $\RR^3$. Subspaces of Hausdorff spaces are Hausdorff, and subspaces of second-countable spaces are second countable. Since $\RR^3$ has both properties, so does $S$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-5}

The surface $S$ is a differentiable $2$-manifold.


::: pf-proof

Step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} supplies a smooth atlas with coordinate images open in $\RR^2$, and step [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} supplies the Hausdorff and second-countable axioms.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-5){.pf-ref} is the assertion that $S$ is a differentiable manifold.

:::
