::: {.problem}
Let $\mathcal U\subset\mathbb R^2$ be open.
A proper coordinate patch is a one-to-one immersion
\[
x:\mathcal U\longrightarrow\mathbb R^3
\]
such that
\[
x^{-1}:x(\mathcal U)\longrightarrow\mathcal U
\]
is continuous for the subspace topology on $x(\mathcal U)$.
A surface is a subset $S\subset\mathbb R^3$ with the subspace topology such that each $p\in S$ has a neighborhood contained in the image of a proper coordinate patch.
Show that a surface is a differentiable manifold.
:::
