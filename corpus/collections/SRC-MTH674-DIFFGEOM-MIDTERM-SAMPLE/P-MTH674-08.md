::: {.problem}
(a) Let
\[
X=x^2y\,\partial_y-z\,\partial_z,
\qquad
Y=xy\,\partial_x+y\,\partial_y-z^2\,\partial_z
\]
be vector fields on $\mathbb R^3$.
Compute $[X,Y]$.

(b) Let $(\mathcal U,\phi)$ and $(\mathcal U,\psi)$ be coordinate systems on a two-dimensional manifold with transition function
\[
(u^1,u^2)=\psi\circ\phi^{-1}(x^1,x^2)
=\bigl((x^1)^2-(x^2)^2,\,2x^1x^2\bigr).
\]
If $X=x^2\partial_{x^1}$ in the $\phi$-coordinates, find its coordinate expression in the $\psi$-coordinates.

(c) Express $X=y\partial_x$ in polar coordinates.
:::
