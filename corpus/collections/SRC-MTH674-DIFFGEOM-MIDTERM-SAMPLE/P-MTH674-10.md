::: {.problem}
The equations
\[
x=\rho\cos\theta\sin\phi,
\qquad
y=\rho\sin\theta\sin\phi,
\qquad z=\rho\cos\phi
\]
define spherical coordinates $(\rho,\theta,\phi)$ on $\mathbb R^3$.

(a) Let $V=\partial/\partial\phi$ and $W=\partial/\partial\rho$.
Express $V$ and $W$ in Cartesian coordinates $(x,y,z)$.

(b) Let
\[
\pi_P:\mathbb R^3\setminus\{0\}\longrightarrow\mathbb{RP}^2
\]
be the projection.
Find an expression for $(\pi_P)_*(V)$ in suitable coordinates on $\mathbb{RP}^2$.
:::
