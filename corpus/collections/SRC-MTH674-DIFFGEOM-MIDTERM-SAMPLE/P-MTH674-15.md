::: {.problem}
(a) For $A\in GL(m)$, let
\[
L_A:GL(m)\to GL(m),\qquad L_A(X)=AX.
\]
Describe
\[
(L_A)_*:T_I GL(m)\to T_A GL(m).
\]

(b) Let $\Psi:GL(m)\to GL(m)$ be $\Psi(X)=X^TX$.
Describe
\[
\Psi_*:T_I GL(m)\to T_I GL(m).
\]

(c) Let $\iota:GL(m)\to GL(m)$ be inversion, $\iota(X)=X^{-1}$.
Describe
\[
\iota_*:T_I GL(m)\to T_I GL(m).
\]

(d) Let
\[
m:GL(m)\times GL(m)\longrightarrow GL(m),\qquad m(A,B)=AB.
\]
Show that
\[
m_*:T_I GL(m)\oplus T_I GL(m)\to T_I GL(m)
\]
is given by $m_*(V,W)=V+W$.
:::

::: {.solution}

Identify
$$
T_XGL(m)\cong\Mat_m(\RR)
$$
for every $X\in GL(m)$, since $GL(m)$ is an open subset of the vector space $\Mat_m(\RR)$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

(a) For $V\in T_IGL(m)\cong\Mat_m(\RR)$,
$$
\boxed{(L_A)_{*I}(V)=AV}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Choose a smooth curve $X(t)$ in $GL(m)$ with
$$
X(0)=I,
\qquad
X'(0)=V.
$$
Then
$$
(L_A\circ X)(t)=AX(t),
$$
so
$$
(L_A)_{*I}(V)
=\left.\frac d{dt}\right|_{t=0}AX(t)
=AV.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

(b) For $V\in T_IGL(m)$,
$$
\boxed{\Psi_{*I}(V)=V^T+V}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Let $X(t)$ be as in step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref}. Then
$$
\Psi(X(t))=X(t)^TX(t).
$$
Differentiate using the product rule:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Psi_{*I}(V)
&=\left.\frac d{dt}\right|_{t=0}X(t)^TX(t)\\
&=X'(0)^TX(0)+X(0)^TX'(0)\\
&=V^T+V.
\end{aligned}
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

(c) For $V\in T_IGL(m)$,
$$
\boxed{\iota_{*I}(V)=-V}.
$$


::: pf-proof

For a curve $X(t)$ through $I$ with velocity $V$, the identity
$$
X(t)X(t)^{-1}=I
$$
holds for all $t$. Differentiating at $t=0$ gives
$$
V\,I+I\left.\frac d{dt}\right|_{t=0}X(t)^{-1}=0.
$$
Therefore
$$
\left.\frac d{dt}\right|_{t=0}X(t)^{-1}=-V,
$$
which is the asserted differential.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

(d) If
$$
(V,W)\in T_IGL(m)\oplus T_IGL(m),
$$
then
$$
\boxed{m_{*(I,I)}(V,W)=V+W}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Choose smooth curves $A(t),B(t)$ in $GL(m)$ such that
$$
A(0)=B(0)=I,
\qquad
A'(0)=V,
\qquad
B'(0)=W.
$$
Then
$$
m(A(t),B(t))=A(t)B(t).
$$
By the product rule,
$$
\begin{aligned}
m_{*(I,I)}(V,W)
&=\left.\frac d{dt}\right|_{t=0}A(t)B(t)\\
&=A'(0)B(0)+A(0)B'(0)\\
&=V+W.
\end{aligned}
$$

:::

:::

Steps [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} through [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} give the requested differentials for parts (a)--(d), respectively.
