::: {.problem}
Let $M$ be a differentiable manifold.

(a) State the $n$-submanifold property for a subset $N\subset M$.

(b) Let $F:M\to P$ be a smooth map of constant rank $k$.
Starting from the rank theorem, show that for any $p\in P$ the level set
\[
N_p=\{q\in M:F(q)=p\}
\]
satisfies the submanifold property, provided it is nonempty.

(c) Show that the three-dimensional unit sphere
\[
S^3=\{(x,y,z,u)\in\mathbb R^4:x^2+y^2+z^2+u^2=1\}
\]
is an embedded submanifold of $\mathbb R^4$.

(d) Show that the subgroup $SL(n)\subset GL(n)$ of matrices of determinant $1$ is an embedded submanifold of $GL(n)$.

(e) Show that the group $SO(n)\subset GL(n)$ of orthogonal matrices of determinant $1$ is an embedded submanifold of $GL(n)$.

(f) Let $M,N$ be differentiable manifolds and $b\in N$.
Show that
\[
i_b:M\longrightarrow M\times N,\qquad i_b(p)=(p,b),
\]
is an embedding.
:::
