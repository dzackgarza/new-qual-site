::: {.problem}
(a) Define
\[
F:\mathbb R^m\longrightarrow\mathbb{RP}^m,
\qquad
F(x^1,\ldots,x^m)=[x^1,\ldots,x^m,1].
\]
Show that $F$ is smooth onto a dense open subset of $\mathbb{RP}^m$.

(b) Define similarly
\[
G:\mathbb C^m\longrightarrow\mathbb{CP}^m,
\qquad
G(z^1,\ldots,z^m)=[z^1,\ldots,z^m,1].
\]
Show that $G$ is a diffeomorphism onto a dense open subset of $\mathbb{CP}^m$.

(c) Let $\widehat p(z)$ be a complex polynomial in one variable and let
\[
G:\mathbb C\longrightarrow\mathcal U\subset\mathbb{CP}^1
\]
be as in part (b), with image $\mathcal U$.
Define $p:\mathcal U\to\mathcal U$ by
\[
p\circ G=G\circ\widehat p.
\]
Show that $p$ extends to a smooth map on all of $\mathbb{CP}^1$.
:::
