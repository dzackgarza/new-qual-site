::: {.problem}
Let $\mathbb{CP}^n$ and $\operatorname{Gr}(n,2)$ denote the complex projective space and Grassmann manifold constructed in Problems XI and XII.

(a) Let $S^3$ be the unit sphere in $\mathbb C^2\cong\mathbb R^4$, and let the Hopf map
\[
\pi_H:S^3\longrightarrow S^2
\]
be the restriction of the projective projection to $S^3$.
Describe $\pi_H^{-1}(p)$ for $p\in S^2$.

(b) Show that
\[
\pi_{Gr}:\mathcal T\longrightarrow\operatorname{Gr}(n,2)
\]
is differentiable.

(c) Show that $\operatorname{Gr}(n,2)$ is compact.
:::
