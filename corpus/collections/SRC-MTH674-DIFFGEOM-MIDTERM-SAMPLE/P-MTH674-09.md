::: {.problem}
Let
\[
M=\{(x,y,z)\in\mathbb R^3:z=x^2+y^2\}
\]
with the subspace topology.

(a) Let $\varphi:M\to\mathbb R^2$ be given by $\varphi(x,y,z)=(x,y)$.
Show that $\varphi$ is a global coordinate map for $M$.

(b) Let
\[
F:M\longrightarrow S^2,
\qquad
F(x,y,z)=\frac{(-2x,-2y,1)}{\sqrt{1+4x^2+4y^2}}.
\]
Is $F$ smooth?

(c) Let
\[
v=\left.\frac{\partial}{\partial\varphi^1}\right|_{(0,1,1)}.
\]
Compute a coordinate expression for $F_*(v)$.

(d) Let $f:M\to\mathbb R$ be $f(x,y,z)=xyz^2$.
Compute
\[
\left.\frac{\partial}{\partial\varphi^1}\right|_{(1,-1,2)}f.
\]
:::
