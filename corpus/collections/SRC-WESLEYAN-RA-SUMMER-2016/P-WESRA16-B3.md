::: {.problem}
Let $(X,\mathcal M,\mu)$ be a measure space and let $f_n:X\to\mathbb R$ be measurable.

(a) Define $f_n\to0$ in $L^1$ and define $f_n\to0$ in measure.

(b) Prove that $L^1$ convergence to $0$ implies convergence in measure to $0$.
