::: {.problem}
Let $f\in L^1(\mathbb R)$ and $\beta\in(0,1)$.
Prove that
\[
\int_{\mathbb R}\frac{|f(x)|}{|x-a|^\beta}\,dx<\infty
\]
for Lebesgue-almost every $a\in\mathbb R$.
:::
