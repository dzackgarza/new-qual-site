::: problem
Let $f(x) = x^6 - 3$.
Let $K$ be the splitting field of $f(x)$ over $\mathbb{Q}$.
Show that $\mathrm{Gal}(K/\mathbb{Q})$ is isomorphic to a dihedral group.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. $f(x) = x^6 - 3$ is irreducible over $\QQ$.
Proof: Eisenstein's criterion at the prime $3$.

<1>2. The roots of $f$ are $\alpha \zeta_6^k$ for $k = 0, \ldots, 5$, where $\alpha = 3^{1/6}$ and $\zeta_6 = e^{2\pi i/6}$.
Proof: the sixth roots of $3$.

<1>3. $K = \QQ(\alpha, \zeta_6)$.
Proof: the splitting field is generated by one root $\alpha$ and the primitive sixth root of unity $\zeta_6$.

<1>4. $[\QQ(\alpha) : \QQ] = 6$ and $[\QQ(\zeta_6) : \QQ] = \varphi(6) = 2$.
Proof: <1>1 gives the first; $\zeta_6$ has minimal polynomial $x^2 - x + 1$.

<1>5. $\zeta_6 \notin \QQ(\alpha)$, so $[K : \QQ] = 12$.
Proof: $\QQ(\alpha) \subseteq \RR$ (since $\alpha$ is real), but $\zeta_6 = \frac{1}{2} + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}i$ is not real.

<1>6. $\operatorname{Gal}(K/\QQ)$ has order $12$.
Proof: $K/\QQ$ is Galois (splitting field of a separable polynomial) and $[K:\QQ] = 12$ by <1>5.

<1>7. $\operatorname{Gal}(K/\QQ) \cong D_6$ (the dihedral group of order $12$).
<2>1. An automorphism is determined by $\alpha \mapsto \alpha \zeta_6^a$ and $\zeta_6 \mapsto \zeta_6^b$ with $a \in \{0,\ldots,5\}$ and $b \in \{1, 5\}$.
Proof: $\alpha$ must map to a root of $f$, and $\zeta_6$ to a primitive sixth root of unity.
<2>2. These $12$ choices form a group generated by $\sigma: \alpha \mapsto \alpha\zeta_6$, $\zeta_6 \mapsto \zeta_6$ (order $6$) and $\tau: \alpha \mapsto \alpha$, $\zeta_6 \mapsto \zeta_6^{-1}$ (order $2$), with $\tau\sigma\tau = \sigma^{-1}$.
Proof: direct check of the relations.
<2>3. This is the presentation of the dihedral group $D_6$.
Proof: $D_6 = \langle r, s \mid r^6 = s^2 = 1,\ srs = r^{-1}\rangle$.

<1>8. Q.E.D.
Proof: <1>7.
:::
