::: {.problem}
Let $K/F$be a Galois extension with group$G$, and let $\alpha_1, \ldots, \alpha_n \in K$.
   Show that $p(x) = \prod_{i=1}^n (x - \alpha_i) \in F[x]$if and only if the set$\{\alpha_1, \ldots, \alpha_n\}$is a single orbit under the action of$G$on$K$.
:::
