::: {.problem}
Let $F$ be a field and let $p(x)$ be an irreducible polynomial in $F[x]$.
Let $K$ be the splitting field of $p(x)$.
Show that the Galois group $G = \operatorname{Gal}(K/F)$ acts transitively on the roots of $p(x)$.
(For full credit, do this without using the fundamental theorem of Galois theory.)
:::
