::: {.problem}
Let $X$ and $Y$ be two topological spaces and let $X\times Y$ be endowed with the product topology.

(a) Prove that if $X$ and $Y$ each have a countable dense subset, then so does $X\times Y$.

(b) Prove that if $X$ and $Y$ are both connected, then so is $X\times Y$.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. Let $D\subseteq X$ and $E\subseteq Y$ be countable dense subsets.
::: {.proof}
These exist by the hypothesis in part (a).
:::

<1>2. The set $D\times E$ is countable.
::: {.proof}
A finite Cartesian product of countable sets is countable.
:::

<1>3. The set $D\times E$ is dense in $X\times Y$.
::: {.proof}
It suffices to meet every nonempty basic open set. Let $U\times V$ be nonempty and open with $U\subseteq X$, $V\subseteq Y$. Since $D$ is dense, choose $d\in D\cap U$; since $E$ is dense, choose $e\in E\cap V$. Then
$$
(d,e)\in(D\times E)\cap(U\times V).
$$
Thus every nonempty basic open set meets $D\times E$, so $\overline{D\times E}=X\times Y$.
:::

<1>4. Hence if $X$ and $Y$ each have a countable dense subset, then so does $X\times Y$.
::: {.proof}
Combine <1>2 and <1>3.
:::

<1>5. Assume now that $X$ and $Y$ are connected. If either space is empty, then $X\times Y=\varnothing$ is connected; hence assume both are nonempty and choose $y_0\in Y$.
::: {.proof}
This isolates the degenerate case and supplies a base point for the connectedness argument.
:::

<1>6. The subspace $X\times\{y_0\}$ is connected.
::: {.proof}
It is homeomorphic to $X$, which is connected by hypothesis.
:::

<1>7. For each $x\in X$, the subspace
$$
C_x=(X\times\{y_0\})\cup(\{x\}\times Y)
$$
is connected.
::: {.proof}
The two pieces are connected because they are homeomorphic to $X$ and $Y$, respectively, and they intersect at the point $(x,y_0)$. The union of two connected subspaces with nonempty intersection is connected.
:::

<1>8. The union of the connected sets $C_x$ is all of $X\times Y$ and they have the common connected subset $X\times\{y_0\}$.
::: {.proof}
Every $(x,y)\in X\times Y$ lies in $\{x\}\times Y\subseteq C_x$. By construction, every $C_x$ contains $X\times\{y_0\}$.
:::

<1>9. Therefore $X\times Y$ is connected.
::: {.proof}
A union of connected subspaces having a common point, or more generally a common nonempty connected subspace, is connected. Apply this to the family $\{C_x:x\in X\}$ from <1>7 and <1>8.
:::
:::
