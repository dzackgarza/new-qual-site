::: {.problem}
Let $X=\RR^3-S^1$, the complement of a single circle in $\RR^3$.

(a) Describe a deformation retraction of $X$ onto $S^1\vee S^2$.

(b) Compute $\pi_1(X)$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $C=S^1\subset\RR^3$ be the standard unknotted circle, and choose a closed tubular neighborhood $N\cong S^1\times D^2$ of $C=S^1\times\{0\}$, formed by small normal discs along $C$.

<1>1. $X=\RR^3\setminus C$ deformation retracts onto $X_0=\RR^3\setminus\operatorname{int}(N)$.

::: {.proof}
Inside $N\setminus C\cong S^1\times(D^2\setminus\{0\})$, push each point radially outward in its normal disc onto $S^1\times\partial D^2$, and fix the points outside $N$.
:::

<1>2. In $S^3=\RR^3\cup\{\infty\}$, the set $E=S^3\setminus\operatorname{int}(N)$ is a solid torus, and $X_0=E\setminus\{\infty\}$.

::: {.proof}
A tubular neighborhood of the unknot and the closure of its complement form the genus-one Heegaard splitting of $S^3$ into two solid tori.
:::

<1>3. $X_0$ deformation retracts onto $E\setminus\operatorname{int}(B)$ for a closed ball $B\subset\operatorname{int}(E)$ centered at $\infty$.

::: {.proof}
Push the punctured ball $B\setminus\{\infty\}$ radially onto $\partial B$ and fix $E\setminus B$.
:::

<1>4. A solid torus with an open $3$-ball removed deformation retracts onto an embedded copy of $S^2\vee S^1$.

::: {.proof}
View the solid torus as a $3$-ball ($0$-handle) with one $1$-handle attached, and choose the removed ball inside the $0$-handle.
The punctured $0$-handle is a shell $S^2\times I$.
Collapse the shell onto its inner boundary sphere together with two arcs from one chosen point of that sphere to the two attaching discs of the $1$-handle, and collapse the $1$-handle $D^1\times D^2$ onto its core $D^1\times\{0\}$.
The two arcs and the core form one circle meeting the sphere only at the chosen point, so the resulting spine is $S^2\vee S^1$.
:::

<1>5. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
For (a), compose the deformation retractions of steps <1>1, <1>3, and <1>4, using step <1>2 to identify $E\setminus\operatorname{int}(B)$ with a solid torus minus an open ball; this deformation retracts $X$ onto $S^1\vee S^2$.
For (b), a deformation retraction induces an isomorphism on $\pi_1$, and by the Seifert--van Kampen theorem
$$\pi_1(X)\cong\pi_1(S^1\vee S^2)\cong\pi_1(S^1)*\pi_1(S^2)\cong\boxed{\ZZ}.$$
:::
