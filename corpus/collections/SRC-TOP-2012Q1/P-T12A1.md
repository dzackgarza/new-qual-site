::: {.problem}
Recall that a continuous function $f:X\to Y$ between two topological spaces is called a *closed mapping* if for every closed subset $C$ of $X$, $f(C)$ is a closed subset of $Y$.
Prove that if $f:X\to Y$ is continuous, $X$ is compact, and $Y$ is Hausdorff, then $f$ is a closed mapping.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. Let $C \subseteq X$ be closed.
::: {.proof}
take an arbitrary closed subset.
:::

<1>2. $C$ is compact.
::: {.proof}
a closed subset of a compact space is compact.
:::

<1>3. $f(C)$ is compact.
::: {.proof}
the continuous image of a compact set is compact.
:::

<1>4. $f(C)$ is closed.
::: {.proof}
a compact subset of a Hausdorff space is closed.
:::

<1>5. Hence $f$ is a closed mapping.
::: {.proof}
<1>1–<1>4 hold for every closed $C$.
:::

<1>6. Q.E.D.
::: {.proof}
<1>5.
:::
:::
