::: {.problem}
Recall that a continuous function $f:X\to Y$ between two topological spaces is called a *closed mapping* if for every closed subset $C$ of $X$, $f(C)$ is a closed subset of $Y$.
Prove that if $f:X\to Y$ is continuous, $X$ is compact, and $Y$ is Hausdorff, then $f$ is a closed mapping.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $C\subseteq X$ be closed.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #c-compact}
$C$ is compact.

::: pf-proof
A closed subset of a compact space is compact.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #image-compact}
$f(C)$ is compact.

::: pf-proof
By step [](#c-compact){.pf-ref} and continuity of $f$, since a continuous image of a compact set is compact.
:::

:::

::: pf-qed
By step [](#image-compact){.pf-ref}, $f(C)$ is a compact subset of the Hausdorff space $Y$, hence closed.
Since $C$ was an arbitrary closed subset of $X$, $f$ is a closed mapping.
:::

:::
