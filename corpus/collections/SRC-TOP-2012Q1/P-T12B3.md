::: {.problem}
Let $X$ be a space obtained by identifying the three vertices of a standard 2-simplex.

(a) Describe a structure of a $\Delta$-complex on $X$.

(b) Write down the chain complex corresponding to the $\Delta$-complex in (a). Be sure to include the differentials of the complex.

(c) Compute the homology of $X$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $v_0,v_1,v_2$ be the vertices of the standard $2$-simplex $\sigma=[v_0,v_1,v_2]$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #delta-structure}
(a) $X$ is a $\Delta$-complex with one $0$-simplex $v$, the common image of $v_0,v_1,v_2$; three $1$-simplices $e_0=[v_1,v_2]$, $e_1=[v_0,v_2]$, $e_2=[v_0,v_1]$, each a loop at $v$; and one $2$-simplex $\sigma$.

::: pf-proof
Identifying the three vertices leaves the open edges and the open face unchanged, so each characteristic map is injective on the interior of its simplex.
The faces of $\sigma$ are the three edges and the faces of each edge are $v$, so the face of each simplex is a simplex of the complex, with the vertex order inherited from $\sigma$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #chain-complex}
(b) The simplicial chain complex is
$$0\to\ZZ\xrightarrow{\partial_2}\ZZ^3\xrightarrow{\partial_1}\ZZ\to0,\qquad \partial_2(\sigma)=e_0-e_1+e_2,\qquad \partial_1(e_i)=v-v=0,$$
in the bases $\sigma$; $e_0,e_1,e_2$; $v$.

::: pf-proof
The boundary of $[v_0,v_1,v_2]$ is $[v_1,v_2]-[v_0,v_2]+[v_0,v_1]$, and each edge has both endpoints at $v$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #homology-groups}
(c) $H_0(X)\cong\ZZ$, $H_1(X)\cong\ZZ^2$, and $H_k(X)=0$ for $k\ge2$.

::: pf-proof
By step [](#chain-complex){.pf-ref}, $\partial_1=0$, so $H_0(X)=C_0\cong\ZZ$ and $\ker\partial_1=\ZZ^3$.
The image of $\partial_2$ is generated by $e_0-e_1+e_2$, a primitive vector of $\ZZ^3$, so $H_1(X)=\ZZ^3/\langle e_0-e_1+e_2\rangle\cong\ZZ^2$.
$\partial_2$ sends the generator $\sigma$ to a nonzero element, so $H_2(X)=\ker\partial_2=0$; there are no simplices of dimension above $2$.
:::

:::

::: pf-qed

:::
