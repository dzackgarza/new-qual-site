::: {.problem}
Let $X$ be a space obtained by identifying the three vertices of a standard 2-simplex.

(a) Describe a structure of a $\Delta$-complex on $X$.

(b) Write down the chain complex corresponding to the $\Delta$-complex in (a). Be sure to include the differentials of the complex.

(c) Compute the homology of $X$.
:::

::: {.solution}
**Part (a).**

<1>1. $X$ has one $0$-simplex $v$ (all three vertices identified), three $1$-simplices $e_1, e_2, e_3$ (the three edges, each with both endpoints at $v$), and one $2$-simplex $\sigma$ (the face).
::: {.proof}
the standard $2$-simplex has three vertices, three edges, and one face; identifying the three vertices gives one $0$-cell, and the three edges become loops at $v$, while the face remains a single $2$-cell.
:::

<1>2. This is a valid $\Delta$-complex structure.
::: {.proof}
each simplex is attached by a map that is injective on its interior, and the boundary of the $2$-simplex is glued to the three $1$-simplices in the standard way.
:::

**Part (b).**

<1>1. The chain complex is
$$0 \to \ZZ \xrightarrow{\partial_2} \ZZ^3 \xrightarrow{\partial_1} \ZZ \to 0,$$
with $\partial_2(\sigma) = e_1 - e_2 + e_3$ and $\partial_1(e_i) = v - v = 0$ for each $i$.
::: {.proof}
the boundary of the $2$-simplex is the alternating sum of its three edges; each edge has both endpoints at the single vertex $v$, so its boundary is $v - v = 0$.
:::

**Part (c).**

<1>1. $H_0(X) = \ZZ$.
::: {.proof}
$X$ is connected (a single $0$-cell with $\partial_1 = 0$).
:::

<1>2. $H_1(X) = \ZZ^2$.
<2>1. $\ker \partial_1 = \ZZ^3$.
::: {.proof}
$\partial_1 = 0$.
:::
<2>2. $\operatorname{im} \partial_2 = \langle e_1 - e_2 + e_3 \rangle \cong \ZZ$.
::: {.proof}
<1>1 of part (b).
:::
<2>3. Hence $H_1 = \ZZ^3 / \langle e_1 - e_2 + e_3 \rangle \cong \ZZ^2$.
::: {.proof}
<2>1 and <2>2.
:::

<1>3. $H_2(X) = 0$.
::: {.proof}
$\partial_2$ is injective (it sends the generator $\sigma$ to the nonzero element $e_1 - e_2 + e_3$), so $\ker \partial_2 = 0$.
:::

<1>4. Q.E.D.
::: {.proof}
$H_0 = \ZZ$, $H_1 = \ZZ^2$, $H_2 = 0$ (<1>1–<1>3).
:::
:::
