::: problem
For $i \geq 0$, calculate $\operatorname{Ext}^i_\mathbb{Z}(\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{Z})$ and $\operatorname{Ext}^i_\mathbb{Z}(\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{Q})$.
:::
