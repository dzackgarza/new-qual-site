::: {.problem}
Let $k$ and $n$ be natural numbers greater than $1$.
Let $G$ be a subgroup of index $k$ of the free group $F_n$ on $n$ generators.
Then $G$ is isomorphic to the free group $F_m$ for some natural number $m$; find $m$ as a function of $k$ and $n$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let
\[
R_n=S^1\vee\cdots\vee S^1
\]
be the rose with one vertex and $n$ loop edges, with basepoint $x_0$ its unique vertex.
Then
\[
\pi_1(R_n,x_0)\cong F_n.
\]

<1>1. The subgroup $G\le F_n$ of index $k$ is realized by a connected $k$-sheeted covering
\[
p:(\widetilde R,\widetilde x_0)\longrightarrow(R_n,x_0)
\]
such that
\[
p_*\pi_1(\widetilde R,\widetilde x_0)=G.
\]
::: {.proof}
The rose $R_n$ is connected, locally path-connected, and semilocally simply connected.
The classification theorem for connected covering spaces therefore assigns to the subgroup $G\le\pi_1(R_n,x_0)$ a connected based covering whose induced fundamental-group image is exactly $G$.
The number of sheets of this covering is the subgroup index
\[
[F_n:G]=k.
\]
:::

<1>2. The covering graph $\widetilde R$ has exactly $k$ vertices.
::: {.proof}
The base graph $R_n$ has one vertex $x_0$.
Since $p$ is $k$-sheeted, its fiber over $x_0$ has exactly $k$ points.
A covering of graphs lifts vertices to vertices, and every vertex of $\widetilde R$ lies over the unique vertex of $R_n$.
Hence
\[
|V(\widetilde R)|=k.
\]
:::

<1>3. The covering graph $\widetilde R$ has exactly $kn$ edges.
::: {.proof}
Each of the $n$ open edges of $R_n$ is evenly covered.
Over any one such edge, a $k$-sheeted covering has exactly $k$ disjoint open edge lifts, each mapped homeomorphically onto the base edge.
Thus each of the $n$ base edges has $k$ lifts, giving
\[
|E(\widetilde R)|=kn.
\]
:::

<1>4. A connected finite graph with $v$ vertices and $e$ edges has free fundamental group of rank
\[
e-v+1.
\]
::: {.proof}
Choose a maximal tree $T$ in the graph.
Because the graph is connected, $T$ contains every vertex and hence has exactly
\[
v-1
\]
edges.
Collapsing the contractible tree $T$ to a point leaves a wedge of one circle for every edge outside $T$.
The number of such edges is
\[
e-(v-1)=e-v+1.
\]
Therefore the graph has fundamental group free of that rank.
:::

<1>5. The group $G$ is free of rank
\[
\boxed{m=k(n-1)+1}.
\]
::: {.proof}
Apply <1>4 to the connected covering graph $\widetilde R$.
By <1>2--<1>3,
\[
v=k,
\qquad
e=kn.
\]
Hence
\[
\operatorname{rank}\pi_1(\widetilde R)
=kn-k+1
=k(n-1)+1.
\]
By <1>1, the injective homomorphism $p_*$ identifies this fundamental group with $G$.
Thus
\[
G\cong F_{k(n-1)+1}.
\]
:::
:::
