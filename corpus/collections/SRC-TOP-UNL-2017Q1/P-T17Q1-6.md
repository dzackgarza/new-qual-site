::: {.problem}
Let $X=T^2=(I\times I)/\sim$ (where $(x,0)\sim(x,1)$ and $(0,x)\sim(1,x)$ for all $x\in I$) be the torus with basepoint $x_0=[(0,0)]$.
Find two 2-sheeted covering spaces $p:(Y,y_0)\to(X,x_0)$ and $p':(Y',y'_0)\to(X,x_0)$ satisfying the property that $p_*(\pi_1(Y,y_0))\ne p'_*(\pi_1(Y',y'_0))$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Identify
\[
X=T^2=S^1\times S^1,
\qquad
x_0=(1,1),
\]
and use the standard identification
\[
\pi_1(T^2,x_0)\cong\mathbb Z^2
\]
in which $(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$ are the positively oriented loops in the first and second circle factors.

<1>1. The map
\[
p:S^1\times S^1\longrightarrow S^1\times S^1,
\qquad
p(z,w)=(z^2,w),
\]
with basepoint $y_0=(1,1)$, is a $2$-sheeted covering.
::: {.proof}
The squaring map
\[
s:S^1\to S^1,
\qquad s(z)=z^2,
\]
is the standard $2$-sheeted covering of the circle, while the identity of $S^1$ is a one-sheeted covering.
The product of covering maps is a covering map, so
\[
p=s\times\operatorname{id}_{S^1}
\]
is a covering.
For each $(u,v)\in T^2$, the first coordinate has exactly two square roots in $S^1$, and the second coordinate is forced to be $v$; hence every fiber has exactly two points.
Also $p(1,1)=(1,1)=x_0$.
:::

<1>2. Under $\pi_1(T^2)\cong\mathbb Z^2$, the induced homomorphism is
\[
p_*(m,n)=(2m,n),
\]
so
\[
p_*\pi_1(Y,y_0)=2\mathbb Z\times\mathbb Z.
\]
::: {.proof}
A loop representing $(m,n)$ winds $m$ times around the first factor and $n$ times around the second.
The squaring map has degree $2$, so it doubles the winding number in the first coordinate, while the identity leaves the second winding number unchanged.
Therefore
\[
p_*(m,n)=(2m,n).
\]
Its image consists exactly of the pairs whose first coordinate is even.
:::

<1>3. The map
\[
p':S^1\times S^1\longrightarrow S^1\times S^1,
\qquad
p'(z,w)=(z,w^2),
\]
with basepoint $y'_0=(1,1)$, is a $2$-sheeted covering and satisfies
\[
p'_*(m,n)=(m,2n).
\]
Hence
\[
p'_*\pi_1(Y',y'_0)=\mathbb Z\times2\mathbb Z.
\]
::: {.proof}
Now use
\[
p'=\operatorname{id}_{S^1}\times s.
\]
The same product-cover argument as in <1>1 shows that $p'$ is $2$-sheeted.
The identity preserves the first winding number and $s$ doubles the second, giving the displayed formula for $p'_*$ and its image.
:::

<1>4. The two induced subgroups of $\pi_1(X,x_0)$ are distinct.
::: {.proof}
The element
\[
(1,0)
\]
belongs to
\[
\mathbb Z\times2\mathbb Z
\]
but not to
\[
2\mathbb Z\times\mathbb Z.
\]
Thus
\[
p_*(\pi_1(Y,y_0))
\ne
p'_*(\pi_1(Y',y'_0)),
\]
as required.
:::
