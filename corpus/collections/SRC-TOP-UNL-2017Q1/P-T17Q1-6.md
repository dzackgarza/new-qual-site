::: {.problem}
Let $X=T^2=(I\times I)/\sim$ (where $(x,0)\sim(x,1)$ and $(0,x)\sim(1,x)$ for all $x\in I$) be the torus with basepoint $x_0=[(0,0)]$.
Find two 2-sheeted covering spaces $p:(Y,y_0)\to(X,x_0)$ and $p':(Y',y'_0)\to(X,x_0)$ satisfying the property that $p_*(\pi_1(Y,y_0))\ne p'_*(\pi_1(Y',y'_0))$.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. Identify the fundamental group of the torus $X = T^2 = S^1 \times S^1$.
<2>1. $X = S^1 \times S^1$, so $\pi_1(X, x_0) \cong \pi_1(S^1) \times \pi_1(S^1) \cong \mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}$.
Proof: the fundamental group of a product space is the product of the fundamental groups.
<2>2. Let $a = (1, 0)$ be the standard generator represented by the loop around the first circle, and $b = (0, 1)$ the generator represented by the loop around the second circle.
Proof: standard product basis of $\mathbb{Z}^2$.

<1>2. Construct the first 2-sheeted covering $p: (Y, y_0) \to (X, x_0)$.
<2>1. Let $Y = S^1 \times S^1$ (viewed as complex numbers of unit modulus $\{z \in \mathbb{C} : |z|=1\}^2$) and define $p(z_1, z_2) = (z_1^2, z_2)$, with basepoint $y_0 = (1, 1) \in Y$ mapping to $x_0 = (1, 1) \in X$.
Proof: definition of the degree-2 covering on the first factor and identity on the second factor.
<2>2. $p$ is a 2-to-1 covering map: for each $(w_1, w_2) \in X$, $p^{-1}(w_1, w_2) = \{(\sqrt{w_1}, w_2), (-\sqrt{w_1}, w_2)\}$, consisting of 2 points.
Proof: squaring map $z \mapsto z^2$ on $S^1$ is a 2-sheeted covering map.
<2>3. The induced map on fundamental groups is $p_*(m, n) = (2m, n)$.
Proof: a loop of degree $m$ in the first coordinate of $Y$ maps under $z_1 \mapsto z_1^2$ to a loop of degree $2m$ in $X$.
<2>4. Hence the image subgroup is $H_1 = p_*(\pi_1(Y, y_0)) = 2\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z} = \langle 2a, b \rangle$.
Proof: <2>3.

<1>3. Construct the second 2-sheeted covering $p': (Y', y'_0) \to (X, x_0)$.
<2>1. Let $Y' = S^1 \times S^1$ and define $p'(z_1, z_2) = (z_1, z_2^2)$, with basepoint $y'_0 = (1, 1)$.
Proof: definition of the degree-2 covering on the second factor and identity on the first factor.
<2>2. $p'$ is a 2-to-1 covering map.
Proof: squaring map on the second factor.
<2>3. The induced map on fundamental groups is $p'_*(m, n) = (m, 2n)$.
Proof: loop of degree $n$ in the second coordinate maps to degree $2n$.
<2>4. Hence the image subgroup is $H_2 = p'_*(\pi_1(Y', y'_0)) = \mathbb{Z} \times 2\mathbb{Z} = \langle a, 2b \rangle$.
Proof: <2>3.

<1>4. The two image subgroups are distinct: $H_1 \neq H_2$.
<2>1. The element $a = (1, 0)$ belongs to $H_2 = \mathbb{Z} \times 2\mathbb{Z}$, but $(1, 0) \notin H_1 = 2\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}$ since $1 \notin 2\mathbb{Z}$.
Proof: parity of $1$.
<2>2. Hence $p_*(\pi_1(Y, y_0)) \neq p'_*(\pi_1(Y', y'_0))$.
Proof: <2>1.

<1>5. Q.E.D. Proof: <1>2, <1>3, and <1>4.
