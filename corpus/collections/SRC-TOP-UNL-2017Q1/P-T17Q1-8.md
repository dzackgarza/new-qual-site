::: {.problem}
If $X$ is a $\Delta$-complex with at most one $k$-simplex for each $0\le k\le5$, show that each of the homology groups $H_k(X)$ must be cyclic for all $0\le k\le5$.
Can all of these groups be non-trivial?
Explain why or why not.
:::
