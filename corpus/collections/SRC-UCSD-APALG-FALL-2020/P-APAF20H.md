::: problem
Let $U\colon G\to\mathrm{U}(H)$ be a unitary representation of a compact group $G$ on a Hilbert space $H$.
Prove that, if $K$ is a closed subspace of $H$ invariant under the action of $G$, so is $K^\perp$.
:::
