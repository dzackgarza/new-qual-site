::: problem
Let $R\colon G\to\mathrm{GL}(H)$ be a linear representation of a compact group $G$ on a finite-dimensional Hilbert space $H$.
Prove that, for all $g\in G$, the eigenvalues of $R(g)$ have modulus $1$.
:::
