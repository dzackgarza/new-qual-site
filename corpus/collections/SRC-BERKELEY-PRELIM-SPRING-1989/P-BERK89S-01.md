::: {.problem}
Let $(a_n)$ be positive and suppose
\[
\sum_{n=1}^\infty a_n<\infty.
\]
Prove that there are positive numbers $c_n$ such that
\[
c_n\to\infty
\]
and
\[
\sum_{n=1}^\infty c_na_n<\infty.
\]
:::
