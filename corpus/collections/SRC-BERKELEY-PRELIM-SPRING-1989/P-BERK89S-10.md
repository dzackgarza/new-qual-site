::: {.problem}
Let
\[
X=\begin{pmatrix}A&B\\C&D\end{pmatrix}
\]
be a real $2n\times2n$ matrix, where $A,B,C,D$ are $n\times n$ matrices that commute pairwise. Prove that $X$ is invertible if and only if
\[
AD-BC
\]
is invertible.
:::
