:::{.problem}
Let $f:[0,1]\times[0,1]\to\mathbb R$ be continuous and define
\[
g(x)=\max\{f(x,y):y\in[0,1]\}.
\]
Prove that $g$ is continuous on $[0,1]$.
:::
