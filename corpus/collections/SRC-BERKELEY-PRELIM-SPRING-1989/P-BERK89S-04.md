:::{.problem}
Let $1<\lambda<\infty$. Prove that
\[
f_\lambda(z)=z+\lambda-e^z
\]
has exactly one zero in the half-plane
\[
\operatorname{Re}z<0,
\]
and that this zero is real.
:::
