:::{.problem}
Let $f$ be analytic from the open unit disk $D$ to itself. Suppose there are distinct $a,b\in D$ such that
\[
f(a)=a,
\qquad
f(b)=b.
\]
Prove that
\[
f(z)=z
\]
for all $z\in D$.
:::
