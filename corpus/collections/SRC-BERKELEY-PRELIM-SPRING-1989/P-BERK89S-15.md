::: {.problem}
Let $B=(b_{ij})_{i,j=1}^{20}$ be a real $20\times20$ matrix satisfying
\[
b_{ii}=0
\]
for every $i$, and
\[
b_{ij}\in\{1,-1\}
\]
for $i\ne j$. Prove that $B$ is nonsingular.
:::
