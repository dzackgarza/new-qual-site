:::{.problem}
Let $S$ be the subspace of $M_n(\mathbb R)$ generated by all matrices of the form
\[
AB-BA,
\qquad A,B\in M_n(\mathbb R).
\]
Prove that
\[
\dim S=n^2-1.
\]
:::
