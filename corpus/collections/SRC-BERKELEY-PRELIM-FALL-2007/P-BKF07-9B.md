::: {.problem}
Let \(f\) be holomorphic on a neighborhood of the closed unit disk
\[
\overline{B_1(0)}=\{z:|z|\le1\}.
\]
Suppose
\[
\max_{|z|=1}|f(z)|\le1.
\]
Prove that there exists \(z\) with \(|z|\le1\) such that
\[
f(z)=z.
\]
:::
