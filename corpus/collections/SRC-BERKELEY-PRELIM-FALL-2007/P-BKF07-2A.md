::: {.problem}
Let \(f,g\) be entire functions satisfying, for every \(z\in\mathbb C\),
\[
f'(z)=g(z),\qquad g'(z)=-f(z),\qquad f(2z)=2f(z)g(z).
\]
Find all possibilities for \(f\).
:::
