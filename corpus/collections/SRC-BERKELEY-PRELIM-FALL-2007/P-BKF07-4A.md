::: {.problem}
Let \((a_n)*{n\ge1}\) and \((b_n)*{n\ge1}\) be real sequences.
Suppose
\[
0\le a_{n+1}\le a_n+b_n
\]
for every \(n\ge1\), and suppose \(\sum_{n=1}^\infty b_n\) converges.
Prove that \(\lim_{n\to\infty}a_n\) exists and is finite.
:::
