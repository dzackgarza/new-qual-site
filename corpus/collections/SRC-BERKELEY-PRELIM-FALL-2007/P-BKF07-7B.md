::: {.problem}
Let \(f:[0,\infty)\to\mathbb R\) be continuous and suppose
\[
\lim_{x\to\infty}f(x)
\]
exists and is finite. Must \(f\) be uniformly continuous? Give a proof or a counterexample.
:::
