::: {.problem}
Given a positive integer \(n\), determine all possible triples
\[
(\operatorname{rank}A,\operatorname{rank}B,\operatorname{rank}C)
\]
as \(A,B,C\) range over real \(n\times n\) matrices satisfying
\[
A+B+C=0.
\]
:::
