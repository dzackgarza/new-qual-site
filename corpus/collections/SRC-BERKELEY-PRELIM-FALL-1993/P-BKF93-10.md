::: {.problem}
Let $f:[0,\infty)\to\mathbb R$ be continuous. Let $A$ be the set of all real numbers $a$ for which there exists a sequence $x_n\to\infty$ such that
\[
f(x_n)\to a.
\]
Prove that if $a,b\in A$, then every point of the interval with endpoints $a$ and $b$ also belongs to $A$.
:::
