::: {.problem}
Let $f:[0,1]\to\mathbb R$ be continuous and define
\[
h(z)=\int_0^1 f(t)\cos(zt)\,dt.
\]

1. Prove that $h$ is entire.
2. Prove that $h\equiv0$ only if $f\equiv0$.
:::
