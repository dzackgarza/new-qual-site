::: {.problem}
Let $x(t)$ solve
\[
x''-2b x'+cx=0
\]
for all real $t$, where $b,c\in\mathbb R$, and suppose
\[
x(0)=x(1)=0.
\]
Prove that
\[
x(n)=0
\]
for every integer $n$.
:::
