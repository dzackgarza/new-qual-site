::: {.problem}
Prove that
\[
\begin{pmatrix}
0&5&1&0\\
5&0&5&1\\
1&5&0&5\\
0&1&5&0
\end{pmatrix}
\]
has two positive and two negative eigenvalues, counted with multiplicity.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let
$$
A=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&5&1&0\\
5&0&5&1\\
1&5&0&5\\
0&1&5&0
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

The subspaces
$$
V_+\coloneqq
\operatorname{span}\{e_1+e_4,e_2+e_3\},
\qquad
V_-\coloneqq
\operatorname{span}\{e_1-e_4,e_2-e_3\}
$$
are $A$-invariant, and
$$
\RR^4=V_+\oplus V_-.
$$

::: pf-proof

Set
$$
u_1=e_1+e_4,
\qquad
u_2=e_2+e_3,
\qquad
v_1=e_1-e_4,
\qquad
v_2=e_2-e_3.
$$
Direct calculation gives
$$
Au_1=6u_2,
\qquad
Au_2=6u_1+5u_2,
$$
and
$$
Av_1=4v_2,
\qquad
Av_2=4v_1-5v_2.
$$
Thus both subspaces are invariant. The four displayed vectors are linearly
independent, so their two spans form a direct-sum decomposition of
$\RR^4$.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Relative to the ordered bases $(u_1,u_2)$ and $(v_1,v_2)$, the two
restrictions of $A$ are
$$
A_+=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&6\\
6&5
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
A_-=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&4\\
4&-5
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

::: pf-proof

This is exactly the coordinate form of the four identities in step [](#s1){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

Each of $A_+$ and $A_-$ has one positive and one negative eigenvalue.

::: pf-proof

Both matrices are real symmetric, so their eigenvalues are real. Moreover,
$$
\det A_+=-36<0,
\qquad
\det A_-=-16<0.
$$
For a real $2\times2$ matrix with real eigenvalues, a negative determinant
means that the product of the two eigenvalues is negative. Hence each
restriction has one positive and one negative eigenvalue.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s4}

The matrix $A$ has exactly two positive and two negative eigenvalues,
counted with multiplicity.

::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1){.pf-ref}, $A$ is the direct sum of its restrictions to $V_+$ and
$V_-$. Thus its multiset of eigenvalues is the union of the eigenvalue
multisets of $A_+$ and $A_-$. Step [](#s3){.pf-ref} contributes one positive and one
negative eigenvalue from each summand, giving two of each.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s4){.pf-ref} is the required signature statement.

:::
