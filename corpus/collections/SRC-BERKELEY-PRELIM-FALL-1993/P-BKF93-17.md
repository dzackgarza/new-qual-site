::: {.problem}
Let $w$ be positive and continuous on $[0,1]$, let $n$ be a positive integer, and equip the space $P_n$ of real polynomials of degree at most $n$ with
\[
\langle p,q\rangle=\int_0^1 p(t)q(t)w(t)\,dt.
\]

1. Prove that $P_n$ has an orthonormal basis $p_0,p_1,\ldots,p_n$ with $\deg p_k=k$.
2. Prove that
\[
\langle p_k,p_k'\rangle=0
\]
for every $k$.
:::
