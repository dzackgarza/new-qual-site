::: {.problem}
Identify $M_n(\mathbb R)$ with $\mathbb R^{n^2}$ and let
\[
F:M_n(\mathbb R)\to\mathbb R,
\qquad
F(X)=\det X.
\]
For $n\ge2$, find all critical points of $F$, i.e. all matrices $X$ such that
\[
DF(X)=0.
\]
:::
