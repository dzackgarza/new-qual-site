::: {.problem}
Let $I\triangleleft\mathbb C[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$ be an ideal such that $\sqrt I$ is maximal.
Prove that
\[
\mathbb C[x_1,\ldots,x_n]/I
\]
is finite-dimensional over $\mathbb C$.
:::
