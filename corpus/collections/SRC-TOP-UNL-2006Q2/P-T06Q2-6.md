::: {.problem}
Define the topological space $X=(I\times S^1)/\sim$ where $\sim$ is the equivalence relation generated by $(1,z)\sim(0,z^5)$ for all $z\in S^1$ (where we think of $S^1$ as the unit circle in $\mathbb C$). Give a presentation for the fundamental group of $X$, carefully justifying your calculation.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let
\[
f:S^1\longrightarrow S^1,
\qquad
f(z)=z^5.
\]
Then $X$ is the mapping torus of $f$.

<1>1. Give $S^1$ the CW structure with one vertex $v=1$ and one oriented $1$-cell $a$.
With this structure, $f$ fixes $v$ and sends the loop $a$ around itself five times.
::: {.proof}
The point $1\in S^1$ satisfies
\[
f(1)=1.
\]
If
\[
a(u)=e^{2\pi i u},
\qquad 0\le u\le1,
\]
parametrizes the oriented $1$-cell, then
\[
f(a(u))=e^{10\pi i u},
\]
which traverses the same circle five times in the positive direction.
Thus on the fundamental group of the fiber,
\[
f_*([a])=[a]^5.
\]
:::

<1>2. The quotient $X$ has a CW structure with one $0$-cell, two loop $1$-cells $a,t$, and one $2$-cell.
::: {.proof}
The copy
\[
\{0\}\times S^1
\]
descends to the fiber loop $a$.
The path
\[
s\longmapsto (s,1),
\qquad 0\le s\le1,
\]
descends to a loop $t$, because
\[
(1,1)\sim(0,1^5)=(0,1).
\]
Thus the quotient of
\[
(I\times\{1\})\cup(\{0\}\times S^1)
\]
is a wedge of the two loops $t$ and $a$ at one vertex.

The remaining open set
\[
(0,1)\times(S^1\setminus\{1\})
\]
is an open $2$-cell.
Equivalently, the characteristic square
\[
[0,1]\times[0,1]
\longrightarrow X,
\qquad
(s,u)\longmapsto [s,e^{2\pi i u}],
\]
has interior mapped homeomorphically onto this $2$-cell and boundary mapped into the two-loop $1$-skeleton.
:::

<1>3. With the orientations in <1>1--<1>2, the attaching word of the $2$-cell is
\[
t\,a^5\,t^{-1}a^{-1}.
\]
::: {.proof}
Traverse the boundary of the characteristic square counterclockwise, beginning at $(0,0)$.

Along the bottom edge $u=0$, the map is
\[
s\longmapsto [s,1],
\]
so this edge contributes $t$.

Along the right edge $s=1$, the quotient relation gives
\[
[1,e^{2\pi i u}]
=
[0,e^{10\pi i u}],
\]
so this edge traverses the fiber loop five times and contributes $a^5$.

Along the top edge the boundary orientation runs from $s=1$ to $s=0$, so it contributes $t^{-1}$.
Along the left edge the boundary orientation runs from $u=1$ to $u=0$, so it contributes $a^{-1}$.

Multiplying these four contributions in order gives
\[
t\,a^5\,t^{-1}a^{-1}.
\]
:::

<1>4. The fundamental group has presentation
\[
\pi_1(X)
\cong
\left\langle a,t\ \middle|\ t^{-1}at=a^5\right\rangle.
\]
::: {.proof}
By <1>2, the $1$-skeleton is a wedge of two circles, so its fundamental group is the free group on $a$ and $t$.
Attaching the single $2$-cell quotients this free group by the normal closure of its attaching word from <1>3.
Hence
\[
\pi_1(X)
\cong
\left\langle a,t\ \middle|\ t a^5t^{-1}a^{-1}=1\right\rangle.
\]
The relation is equivalent to
\[
t a^5t^{-1}=a,
\]
and therefore to
\[
t^{-1}at=a^5.
\]
This gives the stated presentation.
:::
