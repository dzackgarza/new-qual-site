::: {.problem}
Define the topological space $X=(I\times S^1)/\sim$ where $\sim$ is the equivalence relation generated by $(1,z)\sim(0,z^5)$ for all $z\in S^1$ (where we think of $S^1$ as the unit circle in $\CC$). Give a presentation for the fundamental group of $X$, carefully justifying your calculation.
:::

::: {.solution}
$X$ is the mapping torus of $f\colon S^1\to S^1$, $f(z)=z^5$.
Give $S^1$ the CW structure with vertex $1$ and one $1$-cell parametrized by $u\mapsto e^{2\pi iu}$, $0\le u\le1$; then $f(1)=1$ and $f(e^{2\pi iu})=e^{10\pi iu}$ runs five times around the circle.

::: pf

::: pf-step

$X$ is a CW complex with one $0$-cell $[0,1]$, two loops $a$ (the image of $\{0\}\times S^1$) and $t$ (the image of $s\mapsto(s,1)$), and one $2$-cell with characteristic map $\Phi\colon[0,1]^2\to X$, $\Phi(s,u)=[s,e^{2\pi iu}]$.

::: pf-proof

$t$ is a loop because $(1,1)\sim(0,1^5)=(0,1)$, so the image of $(I\times\{1\})\cup(\{0\}\times S^1)$ is a wedge of the two loops $a$ and $t$.
$\Phi$ maps the open square homeomorphically onto the image of $(0,1)\times(S^1\setminus\{1\})$, and its boundary into this wedge.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

The $2$-cell is attached along $t\,a^5\,t^{-1}a^{-1}$.

::: pf-proof

Traverse $\partial[0,1]^2$ counterclockwise from $(0,0)$.
The bottom edge $u=0$ maps by $s\mapsto[s,1]$ and gives $t$.
On the right edge, $[1,e^{2\pi iu}]=[0,e^{10\pi iu}]$ runs five times around $a$ and gives $a^5$.
The top edge, traversed from $s=1$ to $s=0$, gives $t^{-1}$, and the left edge, traversed from $u=1$ to $u=0$, gives $a^{-1}$.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

By the Seifert--van Kampen theorem, $\pi_1(X)$ is the free group on $a,t$ modulo the normal closure of the attaching word of step [](#s2){.pf-ref}, so
$$\pi_1(X)\cong\langle a,t\mid ta^5t^{-1}a^{-1}\rangle=\langle a,t\mid t^{-1}at=a^5\rangle,$$
since $ta^5t^{-1}=a$ is equivalent to $t^{-1}at=a^5$.

:::
