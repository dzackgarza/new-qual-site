::: {.problem title="?"}
(a) Define what it means for a topological space to be normal.

(b) Prove that a closed subspace of a normal space is normal.

(c) Suppose that $X$ is a normal topological space, that $C\subseteq X$ is closed, and that $C\subseteq O_1\cup O_2$, where $O_1,O_2$ are open.
Prove that there exist closed sets $C_1,C_2\subseteq X$ with $C=C_1\cup C_2$ and $C_i\subseteq O_i$, $i=1,2$.
:::
