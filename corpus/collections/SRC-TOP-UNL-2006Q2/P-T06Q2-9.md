::: {.problem}
Let $X$ be a topological space and let $U_1\subseteq U_2\subseteq U_3\subseteq\cdots$ be an increasing open cover of $X$.
Prove that for $n\ge 0$ the singular chain groups of $X$ satisfy $$C_n(X)=\bigcup_{j=1}^{\infty}C_n(U_j).$$ To be completely explicit, on the right we really mean $(i_j)_\#C_n(U_j)$ where $i_j:U_j\to X$ is the inclusion map.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

For every singular simplex $\sigma\colon\Delta^n\to X$ there is $J$ with $\sigma(\Delta^n)\subseteq U_J$.

::: pf-proof

$K=\sigma(\Delta^n)$ is compact, so finitely many $U_{j_1},\ldots,U_{j_r}$ cover it.
The cover is increasing, so $K\subseteq U_J$ for $J=\max\{j_1,\ldots,j_r\}$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

$C_n(X)\subseteq\bigcup_j(i_j)_\#C_n(U_j)$.

::: pf-proof

Let $c=\sum_{r=1}^Na_r\sigma_r\in C_n(X)$, a finite sum.
By step [](#s1){.pf-ref} each $\sigma_r$ has image in some $U_{j_r}$, and hence in $U_J$ for $J=\max_rj_r$.
Each $\sigma_r$ then factors as $i_J\circ\bar\sigma_r$ with $\bar\sigma_r\colon\Delta^n\to U_J$, and $\bar c=\sum a_r\bar\sigma_r\in C_n(U_J)$ satisfies $(i_J)_\#\bar c=c$.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Each $(i_j)_\#$ is a homomorphism $C_n(U_j)\to C_n(X)$, so $\bigcup_j(i_j)_\#C_n(U_j)\subseteq C_n(X)$; step [](#s2){.pf-ref} gives the reverse inclusion.

:::
