::: {.problem}
The suspension $SX$ of a topological space $X$ is the space $(X\times I)/\sim$ where $\sim$ is the equivalence relation generated by $(0,x)\sim(0,x')$ and $(1,x)\sim(1,x')$ for all $x,x'\in X$.
Compute the homology groups of $SX$ in terms of those of $X$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Write points of $X\times I$ as $(x,t)$, let $q\colon X\times I\to SX$ be the quotient map, and put $A=q(X\times[0,\tfrac23))$ and $B=q(X\times(\tfrac13,1])$.
If $X=\varnothing$, then $SX=\varnothing$ and all $H_n(SX)$ vanish; assume from now on that $X\neq\varnothing$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

$A$ and $B$ are open, contractible, and cover $SX$.

::: pf-proof

$X\times[0,\tfrac23)$ and $X\times(\tfrac13,1]$ are open and saturated, so their images are open, and together they cover $SX$.
$q(x,t)\mapsto q(x,(1-s)t)$, $0\le s\le1$, is well defined because all points with $t=0$ are identified, and it contracts $A$ to the point $q(X\times\{0\})$; likewise $q(x,t)\mapsto q(x,1-(1-s)(1-t))$ contracts $B$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

$A\cap B$ deformation retracts onto a copy of $X$.

::: pf-proof

No identifications occur for $0<t<1$, so $A\cap B\cong X\times(\tfrac13,\tfrac23)$, which deformation retracts onto $X\times\{\tfrac12\}$ by $(x,t)\mapsto(x,(1-s)t+s/2)$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

$\widetilde H_n(SX)\cong\widetilde H_{n-1}(X)$ for every $n\ge1$.

::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1){.pf-ref}, the reduced Mayer--Vietoris sequence of $SX=A\cup B$ has $\widetilde H_k(A)=\widetilde H_k(B)=0$, so it contains the exact segment $0\to\widetilde H_n(SX)\xrightarrow{\partial}\widetilde H_{n-1}(A\cap B)\to0$.
Step [](#s2){.pf-ref} identifies $\widetilde H_{n-1}(A\cap B)$ with $\widetilde H_{n-1}(X)$.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Each point $q(x,t)$ is joined to $q(x,0)$ by $s\mapsto q(x,(1-s)t)$, and all these endpoints coincide, so $SX$ is path-connected and $H_0(SX)\cong\ZZ$.
Reduced and ordinary homology agree in positive degrees, so step [](#s3){.pf-ref} gives, for nonempty $X$,
$$H_n(SX)\cong\begin{cases}\ZZ,&n=0,\\\widetilde H_0(X),&n=1,\\H_{n-1}(X),&n\ge2.\end{cases}$$

:::
