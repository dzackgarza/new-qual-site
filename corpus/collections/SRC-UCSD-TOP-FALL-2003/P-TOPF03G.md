::: problem
Consider the standard embedding $\mathbb{CP}^1 \hookrightarrow \mathbb{CP}^2$.
Show that any map $f : S^2 \to \mathbb{CP}^2$ whose image $f(S^2)$ is disjoint from $\mathbb{CP}^1$ must be null-homotopic.
:::
