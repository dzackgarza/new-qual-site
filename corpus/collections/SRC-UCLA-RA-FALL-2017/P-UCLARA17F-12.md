::: {.problem}
Problem 12. Let $f$, $g$, and $h$ be complex-valued functions defined on $\mathbb C$ with
\[
f=g\circ h.
\]
Show that if $h$ is continuous, and both $f$ and $g$ are non-constant and holomorphic, then $h$ is holomorphic as well.
:::
