::: {.problem}
Problem 7. Find a doubly infinite sequence $\{a_{n,m}:n,m\in\mathbb Z\}$ such that for every $m$,
\[
\sum_n a_{n,m}=0,
\]
and for every $n$,
\[
\sum_m a_{n,m}=0,
\]
with all these series converging absolutely, but such that
\[
\sum_n\sum_m |a_{n,m}|=\infty.
\]
:::
