::: {.problem}
Problem 6. Show that if $A\in M_{n,n}(\mathbb C)$ is normal, then
\[
A^*=P(A)
\]
for some polynomial $P(x)$ with complex coefficients.
Here $A^*$ is the conjugate transpose of $A$.
:::
