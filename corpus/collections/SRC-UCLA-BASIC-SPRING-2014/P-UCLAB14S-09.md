::: {.problem}
Problem 9. Find a function that minimizes
\[
\int_0^1 |f'(x)|^2\,dx
\]
among all $f\in C^1(\mathbb R)$ such that $f(0)=0$ and $f(1)=1$. Is the minimizing $C^1$ function unique on $[0,1]$?
