::: {.problem}
Let $S$ be the set of all sequences $(x_1,x_2,\ldots)$ such that
\[
x_n\in\{0,1\}
\]
for every $n$.
Prove that there is no bijection from $\mathbb N=\{1,2,\ldots\}$ onto $S$.
:::
