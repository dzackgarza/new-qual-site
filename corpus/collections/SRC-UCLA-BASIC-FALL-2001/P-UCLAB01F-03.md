::: {.problem}
Let $E\subseteq\mathbb R$, and let $(f_n)$ be a sequence of continuous real-valued functions on $E$.
Suppose $f_n\to f$ uniformly on $E$.
Prove that $f$ is continuous on $E$.
:::
