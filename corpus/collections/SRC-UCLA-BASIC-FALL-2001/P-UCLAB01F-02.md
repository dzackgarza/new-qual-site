::: {.problem}
Let $\mathbb N$ denote the positive integers and define
\[
a_n=\frac{(-1)^n}{n}.
\]
Let $\alpha\in\mathbb R$.
Prove that there is a bijection $\sigma:\mathbb N\to\mathbb N$ such that
\[
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_{\sigma(n)}=\alpha.
\]
:::
