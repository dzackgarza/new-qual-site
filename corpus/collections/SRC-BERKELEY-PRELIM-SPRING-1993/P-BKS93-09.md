::: {.problem}
Let $a\in\mathbb C$ and let $\varepsilon>0$. Define
\[
f(z)=\sin z+\frac1{z-a}.
\]
Prove that $f$ has infinitely many zeros in the source's strip
\[
|\,[\text{unrecovered coordinate of }z]\,|<\varepsilon.
\]
:::
