::: {.problem}
Let $f$ be analytic in the unit disk $\mathbb D$.

1. Prove that there is a sequence $(z_n)$ in $\mathbb D$ such that
   \[
   |z_n|\to1
   \]
   and the finite limit
   \[
   \lim_{n\to\infty}f(z_n)
   \]
   exists.
2. If $f$ is nonconstant, prove that there are sequences $(z_n)$ and $(w_n)$ in $\mathbb D$ such that
   \[
   |z_n|\to1,
   \qquad
   |w_n|\to1,
   \]
   both finite limits
   \[
   \lim_{n\to\infty}f(z_n),
   \qquad
   \lim_{n\to\infty}f(w_n)
   \]
   exist, and the two limits are different.
:::
