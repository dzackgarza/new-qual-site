::: {.problem}
Let $(a_n)$ and $(\varepsilon_n)$ be sequences of positive real numbers. Assume
\[
\varepsilon_n\to0
\]
and that there is a number $k\in(0,1)$ such that
\[
a_{n+1}\le k a_n+\varepsilon_n
\]
for every $n$. Prove that
\[
a_n\to0.
\]
:::
