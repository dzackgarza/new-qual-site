::: {.problem}
For every fixed $\zeta\in\mathbb C$, prove that
\[
\frac1{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}e^{2\zeta\cos\theta}\,d\theta
=
\sum_{n=0}^\infty\left(\frac{\zeta^n}{n!}\right)^2.
\]
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

For every real $\theta$,
$$
e^{2\zeta\cos\theta}
=
e^{\zeta e^{i\theta}}
e^{\zeta e^{-i\theta}}.
$$

::: pf-proof

Euler's identity gives
$$
e^{i\theta}+e^{-i\theta}=2\cos\theta.
$$
Therefore
$$
e^{\zeta e^{i\theta}}e^{\zeta e^{-i\theta}}
=
e^{\zeta(e^{i\theta}+e^{-i\theta})}
=
e^{2\zeta\cos\theta}.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

One has the absolutely and uniformly convergent expansion
$$
e^{2\zeta\cos\theta}
=
\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}
\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}
\frac{\zeta^{m+n}}{m!n!}
e^{i(m-n)\theta}
$$
for $0\le\theta\le2\pi$.

::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1){.pf-ref} and the exponential power series,
$$
e^{\zeta e^{i\theta}}
=
\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}\frac{\zeta^m e^{im\theta}}{m!},
\qquad
e^{\zeta e^{-i\theta}}
=
\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{\zeta^n e^{-in\theta}}{n!}.
$$
The absolute values of the terms in their Cauchy product have total sum
$$
\sum_{m,n\ge0}\frac{|\zeta|^{m+n}}{m!n!}
=
e^{2|\zeta|}<\infty,
$$
independently of $\theta$. Hence the double series converges absolutely and uniformly by the Weierstrass test, and multiplication gives the displayed expansion.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

For integers $m,n\ge0$,
$$
\frac1{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}e^{i(m-n)\theta}\,d\theta
=
\begin{cases}
1,&m=n,\\
0,&m\ne n.
\end{cases}
$$

::: pf-proof

If $m=n$, the integrand is $1$. If $m\ne n$, then
$$
\int_0^{2\pi}e^{i(m-n)\theta}\,d\theta
=
\left[
\frac{e^{i(m-n)\theta}}{i(m-n)}
\right]_0^{2\pi}
=0.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s4}

Therefore
$$
\boxed{
\frac1{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}e^{2\zeta\cos\theta}\,d\theta
=
\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}
\left(\frac{\zeta^n}{n!}\right)^2}.
$$

::: pf-proof

By uniform convergence in step [](#s2){.pf-ref}, integrate the double series term-by-term. Step [](#s3){.pf-ref} kills every term with $m\ne n$, while the terms with $m=n$ contribute
$$
\frac{\zeta^{2n}}{(n!)^2}
=
\left(\frac{\zeta^n}{n!}\right)^2.
$$
Summing over $n\ge0$ gives the formula.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s4){.pf-ref} is the required identity.

:::
