::: {.problem}
For every fixed $\zeta\in\mathbb C$, prove that
\[
\frac1{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}e^{2\zeta\cos\theta}\,d\theta
=
\sum_{n=0}^\infty\left(\frac{\zeta^n}{n!}\right)^2.
\]
:::
