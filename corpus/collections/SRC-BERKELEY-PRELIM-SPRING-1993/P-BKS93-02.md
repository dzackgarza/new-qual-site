::: {.problem}
Let $A=(a_{ij})$ be an $n\times n$ matrix such that
\[
\sum_{j=1}^n |a_{ij}|<1
\]
for every $i$.
Prove that $I-A$ is invertible.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

Suppose
$$
(I-A)x=0
$$
for some vector
$$
x=(x_1,\ldots,x_n)^T.
$$
Then
$$
x=Ax.
$$

::: pf-proof

Rearrange $(I-A)x=0$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

Choose an index $i$ such that
$$
\abs{x_i}=\max_{1\le j\le n}\abs{x_j}.
$$
Then
$$
\abs{x_i}
\le
\left(\sum_{j=1}^n\abs{a_{ij}}\right)\abs{x_i}.
$$

::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1){.pf-ref},
$$
x_i=\sum_{j=1}^n a_{ij}x_j.
$$
Hence
$$
\begin{aligned}
\abs{x_i}
&\le\sum_{j=1}^n\abs{a_{ij}}\abs{x_j}\\
&\le\sum_{j=1}^n\abs{a_{ij}}\abs{x_i},
\end{aligned}
$$
using maximality of $\abs{x_i}$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

One has
$$
x=0.
$$

::: pf-proof

By hypothesis,
$$
\sum_{j=1}^n\abs{a_{ij}}<1.
$$
If $\abs{x_i}>0$, step [](#s2){.pf-ref} would give
$$
1
\le
\sum_{j=1}^n\abs{a_{ij}}
<1,
$$
a contradiction. Thus $x_i=0$. Since $\abs{x_i}$ was the maximum of the coordinate moduli, every coordinate of $x$ is zero.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s4}

The matrix $I-A$ is invertible.

::: pf-proof

Step [](#s3){.pf-ref} shows
$$
\ker(I-A)=\{0\}.
$$
A linear endomorphism of a finite-dimensional vector space with trivial kernel is bijective. Therefore
$$
\boxed{I-A\text{ is invertible}}.
$$

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s4){.pf-ref} is the required conclusion.

:::
