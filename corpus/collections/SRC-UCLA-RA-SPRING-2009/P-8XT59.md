::: {.problem}
Let $X$ be a Banach space and let $X^*$ be its dual Banach space.
Prove that if $X^*$ is separable then $X$ is separable.
:::

::: {.solution}
If $X=\{0\}$, the conclusion is immediate.
Assume $X\ne\{0\}$.
Let
\[
S_{X^*}=\{\varphi\in X^*: \|\varphi\|=1\}.
\]
Since $X^*$ is a separable metric space, its subspace $S_{X^*}$ is separable.
Choose a sequence
\[
(\varphi_n)_{n\ge1}\subset S_{X^*}
\]
dense in $S_{X^*}$.
For every $n$, the definition of the dual norm allows us to choose $x_n\in X$ with
\[
\|x_n\|=1,
\qquad
|\varphi_n(x_n)|>\frac12.
\]
Let
\[
M=\overline{\operatorname{span}}\{x_1,x_2,\ldots\}.
\]

<1>1. The closed subspace $M$ is separable.
::: {.proof}
The set of all finite linear combinations of the $x_n$ with rational coefficients is countable in the real case; in the complex case use coefficients in $\mathbb Q+i\mathbb Q$.
This countable set is dense in $\operatorname{span}\{x_n:n\ge1\}$ and hence in its closure $M$.
:::

<1>2. One has $M=X$.
::: {.proof}
Suppose instead that $M\ne X$.
By the Hahn--Banach theorem, there is a nonzero functional $\varphi\in X^*$ such that
\[
\varphi|_M=0.
\]
After rescaling, assume $\|\varphi\|=1$, so $\varphi\in S_{X^*}$.
By density of $(\varphi_n)$, choose $n$ such that
\[
\|\varphi_n-\varphi\|<\frac12.
\]
Since $x_n\in M$, one has $\varphi(x_n)=0$.
Therefore
\[
|\varphi_n(x_n)|
=|(\varphi_n-\varphi)(x_n)|
\le \|\varphi_n-\varphi\|\,\|x_n\|
<\frac12,
\]
contradicting the choice $|\varphi_n(x_n)|>1/2$.
Thus $M=X$.
:::

By <1>1 and <1>2, $X$ is separable.
:::
