::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $I=I_{0,0}=[0,1]$ be the unit interval, and for $n=0,1,2,\ldots$ and $0\le j\le 2^n-1$ let $$I_{n,j}=[j2^{-n},(j+1)2^{-n}].$$ For $f\in L^1(I,dx)$ define $$E_n f(x)=\sum_{j=0}^{2^n-1}\Bigl(2^n\int_{I_{n,j}}f\,dt\Bigr)\chi_{I_{n,j}}.$$ Prove that if $f\in L^1(I,dx)$ then $\lim_{n\to\infty}E_n f(x)=f(x)$ almost everywhere on $I$.
:::
