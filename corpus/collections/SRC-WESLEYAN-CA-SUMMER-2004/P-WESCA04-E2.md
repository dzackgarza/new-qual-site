\[
f(z)=
\begin{cases}
e^{-1/z^4},&z\ne0,\\
0,&z=0.
\end{cases}
\]
The source states that $f$ satisfies the Cauchy–Riemann equations at every point of $\mathbb C$ but is not analytic.
Explain how this is possible.
