::: {.problem}
Let
\[
\beta(t)=te^{it},\qquad 0\le t\le4\pi,
\]
and let $\gamma$ be the closed curve obtained by following $\beta$ and then the line segment from $4\pi$ to $0$.
Evaluate
\[
\int_\gamma \frac{z^2+1}{(z+1)(z+4)}\,dz.
\]
:::
