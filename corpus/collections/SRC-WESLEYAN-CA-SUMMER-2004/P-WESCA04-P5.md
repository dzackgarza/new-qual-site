::: {.problem}
Let $D\subset\mathbb C$ be a bounded domain.
Suppose each $f_n$ is continuous on $\overline D$ and analytic on $D$.
If $(f_n)$ converges uniformly on $\partial D$, prove that it converges uniformly on $\overline D$.
:::
