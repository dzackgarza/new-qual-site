::: {.problem}
Let $f$ have a pole of order $m$ at $z_0$, and let $g$ have a pole of order $n$ at the same point.
Show that $f+g$ has either a removable singularity at $z_0$ or a pole of order at most
\[
\max\{m,n\}.
\]
:::
