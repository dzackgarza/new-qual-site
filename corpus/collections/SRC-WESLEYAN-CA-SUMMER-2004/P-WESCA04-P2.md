::: {.problem}
Let $D_1,D_2\subset\mathbb C$ be simply connected domains whose intersection is nonempty and connected.
Prove that both
\[
D_1\cap D_2
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
D_1\cup D_2
\]
are simply connected.
:::
