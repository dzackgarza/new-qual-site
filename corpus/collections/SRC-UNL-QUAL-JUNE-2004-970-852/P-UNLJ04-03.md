::: {.problem}
Show that if $X$ is a Hausdorff space and $A,B\subseteq X$ are disjoint finite subsets of $X$, then there are disjoint open sets $U,V$ in $X$ with $A\subseteq U$ and $B\subseteq V$.
:::
