::: {.problem}
Let $Y$ be the standard 3-simplex $\Delta^3$ with a total ordering on its four vertices.
Let $X$ be the $\Delta$-complex obtained from $Y$ by identifying, for each $k\le 3$, all of its $k$-dimensional faces such that the identifications respect the vertex ordering.
Thus $X$ has a single $k$-simplex for each $k\le 3$.
Compute the simplicial homology groups of the $\Delta$-complex $X$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $v_0<v_1<v_2<v_3$ be the ordered vertices of $Y$, and let $\sigma_k$ be the unique $k$-simplex of $X$, the common image of all $k$-faces of $Y$.
Then $C_k(X)=\ZZ\sigma_k$ for $0\le k\le3$ and $C_k(X)=0$ otherwise.

<1>1. $d_1=0$, $d_2(\sigma_2)=\sigma_1$, and $d_3=0$.

::: {.proof}
Every face of $Y$ of dimension $k$ maps to $\sigma_k$ preserving vertex order.
For an edge, $d_1[v_i,v_j]=v_j-v_i=\sigma_0-\sigma_0=0$.
For a $2$-face, $d_2[v_i,v_j,v_k]=[v_j,v_k]-[v_i,v_k]+[v_i,v_j]=\sigma_1-\sigma_1+\sigma_1=\sigma_1$.
For the $3$-simplex, $d_3[v_0,v_1,v_2,v_3]=[v_1,v_2,v_3]-[v_0,v_2,v_3]+[v_0,v_1,v_3]-[v_0,v_1,v_2]=\sigma_2-\sigma_2+\sigma_2-\sigma_2=0$.
:::

<1>2. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
By step <1>1:
$H_0(X)=C_0/\operatorname{im}d_1=\ZZ\sigma_0\cong\ZZ$;
$H_1(X)=\ker d_1/\operatorname{im}d_2=\ZZ\sigma_1/\ZZ\sigma_1=0$;
$H_2(X)=\ker d_2/\operatorname{im}d_3=0$, since $d_2$ is an isomorphism;
$H_3(X)=\ker d_3=\ZZ\sigma_3\cong\ZZ$, since $C_4(X)=0$;
and $H_k(X)=0$ for $k\ge4$.
Hence
$$H_k(X)\cong\begin{cases}\ZZ,&k=0,3,\\0,&\text{otherwise}.\end{cases}$$
:::
:::
