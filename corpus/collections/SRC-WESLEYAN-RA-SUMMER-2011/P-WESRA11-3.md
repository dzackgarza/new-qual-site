::: {.problem}
For $A\subset\mathbb R$, define the Lebesgue outer measure $m^*(A)$.
Prove that for every sequence $(A_i)$ of subsets of $\mathbb R$,
\[
m^*\!\left(\bigcup_{i=1}^\infty A_i\right)
\le \sum_{i=1}^\infty m^*(A_i).
\]
:::
