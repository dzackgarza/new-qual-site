::: problem
Let $\mu$ be a finite Borel measure on $\mathbb R^d$.
For $t>0$ set
\[
S_t=\{x\in\mathbb R^d:|x|=t\}.
\]
Prove that
\[
\{t>0:\mu(S_t)>0\}
\]
is at most countable.
:::
