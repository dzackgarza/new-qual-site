::: {.problem}
Let $a,b\ne0$ be complex and $f(z)=az+bz^{-1}$. Determine the image under $f$ of the unit circle $|z|=1$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Write
$$
a=\abs a\,e^{i\alpha},
\qquad
b=\abs b\,e^{i\beta},
$$
and put
$$
\phi\coloneqq\frac{\alpha+\beta}{2},
\qquad
\delta\coloneqq\frac{\alpha-\beta}{2}.
$$

<1>1. If $z=e^{it}$ lies on the unit circle and
$$
u\coloneqq t+\delta,
$$
then
$$
e^{-i\phi}f(z)
=
(\abs a+\abs b)\cos u
+
i(\abs a-\abs b)\sin u.
$$

::: {.proof}
Since $z^{-1}=e^{-it}$,
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(e^{it})
&=
\abs a\,e^{i(\alpha+t)}
+
\abs b\,e^{i(\beta-t)}\\
&=
e^{i\phi}
\left(
\abs a\,e^{i(t+\delta)}
+
\abs b\,e^{-i(t+\delta)}
\right).
\end{aligned}
$$
Using
$$
e^{iu}=\cos u+i\sin u,
\qquad
e^{-iu}=\cos u-i\sin u,
$$
gives the stated formula.
:::

<1>2. If $\abs a\ne\abs b$, the image is the ellipse centered at $0$
whose principal axes have directions
$$
e^{i\phi}
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
ie^{i\phi},
$$
with semiaxis lengths
$$
\abs a+\abs b
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\bigl|\abs a-\abs b\bigr|.
$$

::: {.proof}
Set
$$
w=e^{-i\phi}f(z)=X+iY.
$$
By step <1>1,
$$
X=(\abs a+\abs b)\cos u,
\qquad
Y=(\abs a-\abs b)\sin u.
$$
When $\abs a\ne\abs b$, eliminating $u$ gives
$$
\boxed{
\frac{X^2}{(\abs a+\abs b)^2}
+
\frac{Y^2}{(\abs a-\abs b)^2}
=1
}.
$$
As $t$ runs through a full period, so does $u$, so every point of this
ellipse occurs. Multiplication by $e^{i\phi}$ rotates its principal axes
to the stated directions.
:::

<1>3. If $\abs a=\abs b$, the image is the line segment
$$
\boxed{
\left\{
2\abs a\,e^{i\phi}s:
-1\le s\le1
\right\}.
}
$$

::: {.proof}
When $\abs a=\abs b$, the sine term in step <1>1 vanishes and
$$
f(e^{it})
=
2\abs a\,e^{i\phi}\cos u.
$$
As $u$ varies, $\cos u$ assumes every value in $[-1,1]$. Hence the image
is exactly the displayed segment.
:::

<1>4. Thus the image of the unit circle is a rotated ellipse, with the
equal-modulus case interpreted as its degenerate line-segment limit.

::: {.proof}
Steps <1>2 and <1>3 exhaust the cases
$\abs a\ne\abs b$ and $\abs a=\abs b$.
:::

<1>5. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>4 gives the complete image.
:::
