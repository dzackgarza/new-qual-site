::: problem
Let $R=\mathbb Z[x]$ and $I=(7,x-3)$.

(a) Show that for every $r\in R$ there is an integer $0\le\alpha\le6$ such that $r-\alpha\in I$.

(b) Find $\alpha$ for $r=x^{250}+15x^{14}+x^2+5$.
:::
