::: {.problem}
Let $A$ be a complex $n\times n$ matrix and $B=A^*$ its Hermitian transpose. Suppose $AB=BA$. Let $\alpha,\beta$ be the corresponding operators on $\mathbb C^n$. Prove that $\alpha$ and $\beta$ have the same image and the same kernel.
:::
