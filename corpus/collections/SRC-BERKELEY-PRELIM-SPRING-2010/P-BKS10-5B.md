::: {.problem}
Let \(f\) be continuous on \([0,1]\) and suppose
\[
\int_0^1 f(x)x^n\,dx=0
\]
for all sufficiently large \(n\). Show that \(f\equiv0\).

Hint: first treat the case in which the integral vanishes for every \(n\ge0\).
:::
