::: {.problem}
Let \(X\) be a compact metric space and let \(f:X\to X\) be an isometry. Show that \(f\) is surjective.

Hint: for a point \(x\), find \(m<n\) such that \(f^n(x)\) is close to \(f^m(x)\).
:::
