::: problem
Let $f:[0,1]\to\mathbb{R}$ be a bounded Lebesgue measurable function.
Prove that
\[
\lim_{p\to\infty}\|f\|_p=\|f\|_\infty.
\]
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. Let $M=\|f\|_\infty$.
::: {.proof}
definition.
:::

<1>2. $\|f\|_p\le M$ for all $p$, so $\limsup\|f\|_p\le M$.
::: {.proof}
$|f|\le M$ a.e.
:::

<1>3. For $\epsilon>0$, let $E_\epsilon=\{|f|>M-\epsilon\}$, $m(E_\epsilon)>0$ by definition of essential sup.
::: {.proof}
$M$ least essential bound.
:::

<1>4. $\|f\|_p\ge (M-\epsilon)m(E_\epsilon)^{1/p}\to M-\epsilon$.
::: {.proof}
$\|f\|_p^p\ge\int_{E_\epsilon}(M-\epsilon)^p=(M-\epsilon)^p m(E_\epsilon)$.
:::

<1>5. Hence $\liminf\|f\|_p\ge M$, combined gives limit $M$.
::: {.proof}
<1>2 and <1>4.
:::

<1>6. Q.E.D.
::: {.proof}
<1>5.
:::
:::
