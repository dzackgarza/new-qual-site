::: problem
Let $f:[0,1]\to\mathbb{R}$ be a bounded Lebesgue measurable function.
Prove that
\[
\lim_{p\to\infty}\|f\|_p=\|f\|_\infty.
\]
:::

::: solution
<1>1. Obtain the upper bound.
::: proof
Let
\[
M:=\|f\|_\infty.
\]
Since $[0,1]$ has measure $1$ and $|f|\le M$ almost everywhere,
\[
\|f\|_p^p=\int_0^1|f|^p\,dx\le M^p.
\]
Hence
\[
\|f\|_p\le M
\]
for every finite $p$, so
\[
\limsup_{p\to\infty}\|f\|_p\le M.
\]
:::

<1>2. Obtain the matching lower bound from the essential supremum.
::: proof
Fix $\varepsilon>0$. If $M=0$, the result is immediate, so assume $M>0$ and choose $0<\varepsilon<M$. By definition of essential supremum,
\[
E_\varepsilon:=\{x\in[0,1]:|f(x)|>M-\varepsilon\}
\]
has positive measure. Therefore
\[
\|f\|_p^p
\ge \int_{E_\varepsilon}|f|^p\,dx
\ge (M-\varepsilon)^p m(E_\varepsilon).
\]
Thus
\[
\|f\|_p
\ge (M-\varepsilon)m(E_\varepsilon)^{1/p}.
\]
Letting $p\to\infty$ gives
\[
\liminf_{p\to\infty}\|f\|_p\ge M-\varepsilon.
\]
Since $\varepsilon>0$ is arbitrary,
\[
\liminf_{p\to\infty}\|f\|_p\ge M.
\]
Combining with Step 1 yields
\[
\boxed{\lim_{p\to\infty}\|f\|_p=\|f\|_\infty.}
\]
:::
:::
