::: {.problem}
Suppose V is an n-dimensional vector space over the field F. Let $W \subset V$ be a subspace of dimension $r < n$ . Show that

$W = \bigcap  \{ U \mid U i s a n ( n - 1 )$ − dimensional subspace of V and $W \subset U \}$
:::
