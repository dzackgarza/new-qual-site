::: {.problem}
Let $F : M \to N$ be a smooth map of smooth manifolds so that $F _ { * } : T _ { p } \to T _ { F ( p ) }$ is the zero map for all $p .$ Show that $F$ must be constant if M is connected.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

Fix $p\in M$. There are coordinate neighborhoods
\[
(U,\phi)
\quad\text{of }p
\]
and
\[
(V,\psi)
\quad\text{of }F(p)
\]
such that
\[
F(U)\subseteq V
\]
and
\[
\phi(U)\subseteq\RR^m
\]
is connected.


::: pf-proof

Choose any chart $(V,\psi)$ around $F(p)$. Since $F$ is continuous,
\[
F^{-1}(V)
\]
is an open neighborhood of $p$. Choose a coordinate chart around $p$ whose
domain lies inside this preimage, and shrink its coordinate image to an open
ball if necessary. An open ball is connected.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

The coordinate representative
\[
\widetilde F
=
\psi\circ F\circ\phi^{-1}
:
\phi(U)\to\psi(V)
\subseteq\RR^n
\]
has derivative zero at every point of $\phi(U)$.


::: pf-proof

Let
\[
x=\phi(q),
\qquad
q\in U.
\]
By the chain rule,
\[
D\widetilde F_x
=
d\psi_{F(q)}
\circ
dF_q
\circ
d(\phi^{-1})_x.
\]
By hypothesis,
\[
dF_q=0
\]
for every $q\in M$. Hence
\[
D\widetilde F_x=0.
\]

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

The map $\widetilde F$ is constant on $\phi(U)$.


::: pf-proof

The set $\phi(U)$ is connected by step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} and
\[
D\widetilde F=0
\]
everywhere by step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref}. For any two points in the open ball $\phi(U)$, join
them by the line segment between them. Restricting $\widetilde F$ to that
segment gives a differentiable map of one real variable with derivative zero,
so it is constant along the segment. Hence $\widetilde F$ is constant on the
whole ball.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

The map $F$ is locally constant on $M$.


::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref},
\[
\psi\circ F\circ\phi^{-1}
\]
is constant on $\phi(U)$. Since $\phi$ and $\psi$ are bijections on their
chart domains, $F$ is constant on $U$. The point $p$ was arbitrary.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-5}

For any $y\in N$, the fiber
\[
F^{-1}(\{y\})
\]
is open in $M$.


::: pf-proof

If $p$ belongs to the fiber, step [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} gives an open neighborhood $U$ of
$p$ on which $F$ is constant. Since its value at $p$ is $y$, one has
\[
U\subseteq F^{-1}(\{y\}).
\]
Thus every point of the fiber is interior.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-6}

The map $F$ is constant on the connected manifold $M$.


::: pf-proof

Choose $p_0\in M$ and let
\[
y_0=F(p_0).
\]
The set
\[
A=F^{-1}(\{y_0\})
\]
is nonempty and open by step [](#s1-5){.pf-ref}. Its complement is the union of the other
fibers, each of which is open by the same step, so $A$ is also closed.
Connectedness of $M$ implies
\[
A=M.
\]
Therefore
\[
F(p)=y_0
\]
for every $p\in M$.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-6){.pf-ref} is the required conclusion.

:::
