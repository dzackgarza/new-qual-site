::: {.problem}
Compute $H _ { k , d R } ( S ^ { n } )$ for $k \in \NN .$
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

For $n=0$,
\[
\boxed{
H^k_{dR}(S^0)
\cong
\begin{cases}
\RR^2,&k=0,\\
0,&k>0.
\end{cases}}
\]


::: pf-proof

The zero-sphere
\[
S^0=\{-1,1\}
\]
has two connected components. By the degree-zero computation,
\[
H^0_{dR}(S^0)
\cong
\operatorname{Map}(\pi_0(S^0),\RR)
\cong
\RR^2.
\]
There are no nonzero differential forms of positive degree on a
zero-dimensional manifold.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

Assume henceforth that $n\ge1$. Let
\[
U
=
S^n\setminus\{\text{south pole}\},
\qquad
V
=
S^n\setminus\{\text{north pole}\}.
\]
Then
\[
U\simeq\{\mathrm{pt}\},
\qquad
V\simeq\{\mathrm{pt}\},
\qquad
U\cap V\simeq S^{n-1}.
\]


::: pf-proof

Stereographic projection identifies both $U$ and $V$ with
\[
\RR^n,
\]
so each is contractible.

Removing both poles from the sphere gives a space diffeomorphic to
\[
S^{n-1}\times\RR.
\]
Projection to the first factor is a deformation retraction, so
\[
U\cap V\simeq S^{n-1}.
\]

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

Consequently,
\[
H^k_{dR}(U)
\cong
H^k_{dR}(V)
\cong
\begin{cases}
\RR,&k=0,\\
0,&k>0,
\end{cases}
\]
and
\[
H^k_{dR}(U\cap V)
\cong
H^k_{dR}(S^{n-1}).
\]


::: pf-proof

Apply homotopy invariance of de Rham cohomology to step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} and use the
cohomology of a point.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

For every $k\ge2$, the Mayer--Vietoris connecting homomorphism gives
an isomorphism
\[
\boxed{
H^{k-1}_{dR}(S^{n-1})
\xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }
H^k_{dR}(S^n)}.
\]


::: pf-proof

The relevant part of the de Rham Mayer--Vietoris sequence is
\[
\begin{aligned}
H^{k-1}_{dR}(U)\oplus H^{k-1}_{dR}(V)
&\longrightarrow
H^{k-1}_{dR}(U\cap V)\\
&\xrightarrow{\delta}
H^k_{dR}(S^n)\\
&\longrightarrow
H^k_{dR}(U)\oplus H^k_{dR}(V).
\end{aligned}
\]
For $k\ge2$, all four end groups vanish by step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref}. Hence exactness makes
$\delta$ an isomorphism. Identify the intersection term using step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-5}

For every $n\ge1$,
\[
\boxed{H^0_{dR}(S^n)\cong\RR}.
\]


::: pf-proof

The sphere $S^n$ is connected for $n\ge1$. Degree-zero de Rham cohomology
consists of locally constant real-valued functions, hence of constant
functions.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-6}

If $n>1$, then
\[
\boxed{H^1_{dR}(S^n)=0}.
\]


::: pf-proof

For $n>1$, the intersection
\[
U\cap V\simeq S^{n-1}
\]
is connected. The beginning of the Mayer--Vietoris sequence is
\[
0
\longrightarrow
H^0(S^n)
\longrightarrow
H^0(U)\oplus H^0(V)
\xrightarrow{\rho}
H^0(U\cap V)
\longrightarrow
H^1(S^n)
\longrightarrow0,
\]
because
\[
H^1(U)=H^1(V)=0.
\]
By connectedness,
\[
H^0(S^n)\cong
H^0(U)\cong
H^0(V)\cong
H^0(U\cap V)\cong\RR.
\]
Under these identifications,
\[
\rho(a,b)=a-b,
\]
which is surjective. Exactness therefore gives
\[
H^1_{dR}(S^n)=0.
\]

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-7}

For $n=1$,
\[
\boxed{H^1_{dR}(S^1)\cong\RR}.
\]


::: pf-proof

Now $U$ and $V$ are open arcs, while
\[
U\cap V
\]
has two connected components. Hence
\[
H^0(U)\oplus H^0(V)\cong\RR^2,
\qquad
H^0(U\cap V)\cong\RR^2.
\]
The same exact sequence becomes
\[
0
\to
\RR
\to
\RR^2
\xrightarrow{\rho}
\RR^2
\to
H^1(S^1)
\to0.
\]
The map $\rho$ sends the pair of constants on $U$ and $V$ to their
difference on the two overlap components, so its image is the
one-dimensional diagonal subspace
\[
\{(c,c):c\in\RR\}
\]
up to the harmless choice of signs on the two components. Therefore
\[
\operatorname{coker}\rho\cong\RR,
\]
and exactness gives the result.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-8}

Assume inductively that for some $n>1$,
\[
H^j_{dR}(S^{n-1})
\cong
\begin{cases}
\RR,&j=0,n-1,\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\]
Then for $2\le k\le n$,
\[
H^k_{dR}(S^n)
\cong
\begin{cases}
\RR,&k=n,\\
0,&2\le k<n.
\end{cases}
\]


::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1-4){.pf-ref},
\[
H^k_{dR}(S^n)
\cong
H^{k-1}_{dR}(S^{n-1}).
\]
The induction hypothesis says that the right-hand side is nonzero in the
range $1\le k-1\le n-1$ exactly when
\[
k-1=n-1,
\]
that is, when $k=n$. In that case it is $\RR$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-9}

For $k>n$,
\[
\boxed{H^k_{dR}(S^n)=0}.
\]


::: pf-proof

The manifold $S^n$ has dimension $n$, so
\[
\Omega^k(S^n)=0
\qquad(k>n).
\]
Therefore its de Rham cohomology vanishes in those degrees.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-10}

Thus for every $n\ge1$,
\[
\boxed{
H^k_{dR}(S^n)
\cong
\begin{cases}
\RR,&k=0\text{ or }k=n,\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}}
\]


::: pf-proof

The base case $n=1$ is steps [](#s1-5){.pf-ref} and [](#s1-7){.pf-ref}. For $n>1$, step [](#s1-5){.pf-ref} computes
degree $0$, step [](#s1-6){.pf-ref} computes degree $1$, step [](#s1-8){.pf-ref} computes degrees
$2$ through $n$ inductively, and step [](#s1-9){.pf-ref} handles degrees above the
dimension.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} gives the $S^0$ case and step [](#s1-10){.pf-ref} gives the computation for all
positive-dimensional spheres.

:::
