::: {.problem}
Let $f:(-1,1)\to\mathbb R$ be differentiable and suppose
\[
\lim_{x\to0}\frac{f(x)}{x^2}
\]
exists and is finite. Must $f''(0)$ exist? Give a proof or a counterexample.
:::
