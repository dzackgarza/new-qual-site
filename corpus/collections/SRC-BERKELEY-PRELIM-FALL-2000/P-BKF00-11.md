::: {.problem}
Let $f_n:\mathbb R^k\to\mathbb R^m$ be continuous, let $K\subset\mathbb R^k$ be compact, and suppose $f_n\to f$ uniformly on $K$. Prove that
\[
S=f(K)\cup\bigcup_{n=1}^{\infty}f_n(K)
\]
is compact.
:::
