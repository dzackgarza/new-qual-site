::: {.problem}
Let
\[
f(z)=\frac{p(z)}{q(z)},
\]
where $p,q$ are nonzero complex polynomials, $\deg p<\deg q$, and $q$ has no zeros with nonnegative imaginary part. Prove that for every $z_0$ with $\operatorname{Im}z_0>0$,
\[
f(z_0)=\frac{1}{2\pi i}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{f(t)}{t-z_0}\,dt.
\]
:::
