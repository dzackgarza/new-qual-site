::: {.problem}
Let $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ be uniformly continuous and suppose $f(0)=0$. Prove that there exists $B>0$ such that
\[
|f(x)|\le1+B|x|
\]
for every $x\in\mathbb R$.
:::
