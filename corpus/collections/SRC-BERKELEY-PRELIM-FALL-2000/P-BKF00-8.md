::: {.problem}
Let $p$ be prime and $n$ a positive integer. Prove that there exists
\[
A\in GL_n(\mathbb F_p)
\]
which, as a permutation of the nonzero vectors of $\mathbb F_p^n$, is a single cycle of length
\[
p^n-1.
\]
:::
