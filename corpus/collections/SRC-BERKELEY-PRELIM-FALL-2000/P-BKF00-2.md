::: {.problem}
Let $A$ be a subset of a compact metric space $(X,d)$. Assume that for every continuous function
\[
f:X\to\mathbb R,
\]
the restriction $f|_A$ attains a maximum on $A$. Prove that $A$ is compact.
:::
