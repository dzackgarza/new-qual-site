::: {.problem}
For $t\in\mathbb R$, define
\[
Q_t(x_1,x_2,x_3)
=2x_1^2+x_2^2+3x_3^2+2t x_1x_2+2x_1x_3.
\]
Determine all real $t$ for which $Q_t$ is positive definite.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #matrix-of-Qt}
The symmetric matrix of $Q_t$ is
$$
M_t=
\begin{pmatrix}
2&t&1\\
t&1&0\\
1&0&3
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

::: pf-proof
For $x=(x_1,x_2,x_3)^T$, direct multiplication gives
$Q_t(x)=x^TM_tx$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #leading-principal-minors}
The leading principal minors of $M_t$ are
$$
\Delta_1=2,
\qquad
\Delta_2=2-t^2,
\qquad
\Delta_3=5-3t^2.
$$

::: pf-proof
The first two are immediate. For the determinant,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\det M_t
&=2\det\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&3\end{pmatrix}
-t\det\begin{pmatrix}t&0\\1&3\end{pmatrix}
+\det\begin{pmatrix}t&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}\\
&=6-3t^2-1\\
&=5-3t^2.
\end{aligned}
$$
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #positive-definite-range}
The form $Q_t$ is positive definite exactly when
$$
\boxed{-\sqrt{\frac53}<t<\sqrt{\frac53}}.
$$

::: pf-proof
By Sylvester's criterion, $Q_t$ is positive definite if and only if all
three leading principal minors in step [](#leading-principal-minors){.pf-ref} are positive. Since
$\Delta_1=2>0$, this is equivalent to
$$
t^2<2
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
t^2<\frac53.
$$
The second inequality is stronger, so the condition is precisely
$t^2<5/3$, which is the displayed interval.
:::

:::

::: pf-qed
Step [](#positive-definite-range){.pf-ref} gives all and only the parameters for which $Q_t$ is positive
definite.
:::
