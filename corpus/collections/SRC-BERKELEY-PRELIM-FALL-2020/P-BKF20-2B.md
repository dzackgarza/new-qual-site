::: problem
Prove or give a counterexample.

(a) If $\sum a_n$ and $\sum b_n$ converge, then $\sum(a_n+b_n)$ converges.

(b) If $\sum a_n$ and $\sum b_n$ converge, then $\sum a_nb_n$ converges.
