::: {.problem}
(a) For positive integer $n$, find all poles of
\[
\frac{\pi}{z^n\tan(\pi z)}
\]
and the residues of all simple poles.

(b) Prove that for positive even $n$,
\[
\frac{\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}m^{-n}}{\pi^n}
\]
is rational.
:::
