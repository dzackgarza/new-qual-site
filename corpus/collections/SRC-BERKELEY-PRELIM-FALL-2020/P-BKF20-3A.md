::: {.problem}
Let $X$ be a metric space and let $T_1\supseteq T_2\supseteq\cdots$ be nonempty closed subsets of $X$. If $T_1$ is compact, show that
\[
\bigcap_{n=1}^{\infty}T_n\ne\varnothing.
\]
:::
