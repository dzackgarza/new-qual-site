::: problem
Prove that there do not exist real polynomials $a,b,c,d$ such that
\[
a(x)<b(x)<c(x)<d(x)\quad(0<x<1)
\]
and
\[
b(x)<d(x)<a(x)<c(x)\quad(-1<x<0).
\]
:::
