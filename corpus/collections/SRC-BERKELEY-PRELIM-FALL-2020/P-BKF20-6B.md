::: problem
Let
\[
H_{ij}=\int_0^1t^it^j\,dt,\qquad0\le i,j\le n,
\]
and let $P_i(t)=\sum_{j=0}^ip_{ij}t^j$ be orthonormal on $[0,1]$. If $P=(p_{ij})$, show that
\[
H^{-1}=P^TP.
\]
:::
