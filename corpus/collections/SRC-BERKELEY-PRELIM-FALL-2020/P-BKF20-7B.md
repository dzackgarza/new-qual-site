::: {.problem}
Let $A$ be linear on a vector space $W$ over a field $k$, with $A^5=I$.

(a) If $\operatorname{char}k\ne5$, show that $W=U\oplus V$, where $U=\{u:Au=u\}$ and $AV=V$.

(b) Give an example showing this can fail in characteristic $5$.
:::
