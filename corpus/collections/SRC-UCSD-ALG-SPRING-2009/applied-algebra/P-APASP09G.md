::: problem
Let $f_1 = x^2 y^2 - xy$ and $f_2 = x^3 y - 1$.

(a) Calculate a Gröbner basis for $\langle f_1, f_2 \rangle \cap k[x]$ and for $\langle f_1, f_2 \rangle$, where $\langle f_1, f_2 \rangle$ is the ideal in $k[x, y]$ generated by $f_1$ and $f_2$.

(b) What is the variety $V(f_1, f_2) = \{(a, b) : f(a, b) = 0,\; f \in \langle f_1, f_2 \rangle\}$ for $k = \mathbb{C}$?
:::
