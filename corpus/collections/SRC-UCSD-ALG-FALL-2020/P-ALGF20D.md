::: problem
Let $F$ be an algebraically closed field.
Let $A$ be the $n \times n$ matrix over $F$ such that every entry of $A$ is $1$.
Find the Jordan canonical form of $A$.
(The answer may depend on the properties of the field $F$).
:::
