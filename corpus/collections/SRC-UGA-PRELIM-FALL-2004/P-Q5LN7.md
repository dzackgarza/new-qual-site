::: {.problem}
Let $\alpha$ be the complex number $-2+2\sqrt{3}i$.
Express $\alpha^3$ and the two square roots of $\alpha$ in the standard form $a+bi$.
:::

::: solution
The number
\[
\alpha=-2+2\sqrt3\,i
\]
has modulus $4$ and argument $2\pi/3$, so
\[
\alpha=4e^{2\pi i/3}.
\]
Hence
\[
\alpha^3=4^3e^{2\pi i}=64.
\]
Its square roots are
\[
\pm 2e^{\pi i/3}
=\pm(1+\sqrt3\,i).
\]
Thus the two square roots are
\[
1+\sqrt3\,i,\qquad -1-\sqrt3\,i.
\]
:::
