::: {.problem}
Evaluate the line integral $\oint_C P\,dx + Q\,dy$ where $P = y^2$, $Q = 2x - 3y$, and $C$ is the circle $x^2 + y^2 = 9$, oriented counterclockwise.
:::

::: {.solution}
Assume $C$ is oriented counterclockwise. Green's theorem gives
\[
\oint_C P\,dx+Q\,dy
=\iint_D\left(\frac{\partial Q}{\partial x}-\frac{\partial P}{\partial y}\right)dA,
\]
where $D$ is the disk $x^2+y^2\le9$. Here
\[
\frac{\partial Q}{\partial x}-\frac{\partial P}{\partial y}=2-2y.
\]
Therefore
\[
\oint_C P\,dx+Q\,dy
=2\iint_D dA-2\iint_D y\,dA.
\]
By symmetry $\iint_D y\,dA=0$, while $\operatorname{area}(D)=9\pi$. Hence
\[
\oint_C P\,dx+Q\,dy=18\pi.
\]
With clockwise orientation the value would be $-18\pi$.
:::
