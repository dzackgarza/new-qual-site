::: {.problem}
Let $T: V \to W$ be an injective linear transformation and suppose $v_1, \dots, v_n$ are linearly independent vectors in $V$.
Prove that the vectors $T(v_1), \dots, T(v_n)$ are linearly independent in $W$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Suppose
\[
a_1T(v_1)+\cdots+a_nT(v_n)=0.
\]
By linearity,
\[
T(a_1v_1+\cdots+a_nv_n)=0.
\]
Since $T$ is injective, its kernel is $\{0\}$, so
\[
a_1v_1+\cdots+a_nv_n=0.
\]
The vectors $v_1,\ldots,v_n$ are linearly independent, hence every $a_i=0$. Therefore $T(v_1),\ldots,T(v_n)$ are linearly independent.
:::
