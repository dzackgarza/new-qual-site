::: problem
Let $X$ be an $n$-dimensional manifold and $X_0$ its $R$-orientation sheaf.
Give $R$ the discrete topology.
If $X$ is $R$-orientable, prove $X_0$ is homeomorphic to $X \times R$.
:::
