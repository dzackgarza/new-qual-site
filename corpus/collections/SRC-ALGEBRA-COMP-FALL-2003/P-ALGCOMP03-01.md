::: {.problem}
(a) If $A$ and $B$ are subgroups of finite index in a group $G$, and $[G:A]$ and $[G:B]$ are relatively prime, prove that $G=AB$.

(b) If $H$ is a proper subgroup of a finite group $G$, prove that
\[
\bigcup_{x\in G}x^{-1}Hx\ne G.
\]
:::
