::: {.problem}
Set $f(x) = x^3 - 5 \in \QQ[x]$.

a. Find the splitting field $K$ of $f(x)$ over $\QQ$.

b. Find the Galois group $G$ of $K$ over $\QQ$.

c. Exhibit explicitly the correspondence between subgroups of $G$ and intermediate fields between $\QQ$ and $K$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let
$$
\alpha=\sqrt[3]{5}\in\RR
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\zeta=e^{2\pi i/3}.
$$
The roots of $x^3-5$ are
$$
\alpha,\quad \zeta\alpha,\quad \zeta^2\alpha.
$$

::: pf

::: pf-step
The splitting field is
$$
K=\QQ(\alpha,\zeta),
$$
and
$$
[K:\QQ]=6.
$$

::: pf-proof
The polynomial $x^3-5$ is Eisenstein at $5$, hence irreducible over $\QQ$. Therefore
$$
[\QQ(\alpha):\QQ]=3.
$$
Adjoining $\zeta$ gives all three roots, so $K=\QQ(\alpha,\zeta)$ is the splitting field.

The field $\QQ(\alpha)$ is contained in $\RR$, while $\zeta\notin\RR$, so $\zeta\notin\QQ(\alpha)$. Since $\zeta$ satisfies
$$
t^2+t+1=0,
$$
we have
$$
[K:\QQ(\alpha)]=2.
$$
Hence
$$
[K:\QQ]=2\cdot3=6.
$$
:::

:::

::: pf-step
Define automorphisms $\sigma,\tau\in\operatorname{Gal}(K/\QQ)$ by
$$
\sigma(\alpha)=\zeta\alpha,
\qquad
\sigma(\zeta)=\zeta,
$$
and
$$
\tau(\alpha)=\alpha,
\qquad
\tau(\zeta)=\zeta^2.
$$
Then
$$
\sigma^3=\tau^2=1,
\qquad
\tau\sigma\tau=\sigma^{-1}.
$$

::: pf-proof
The map $\sigma$ cyclically permutes the three roots of $x^3-5$ and fixes $\zeta$, so it extends to a $\QQ$-automorphism of $K$ of order $3$.
The map $\tau$ is complex conjugation on $K$, hence has order $2$.
Finally,
$$
(\tau\sigma\tau)(\alpha)
=\tau\sigma(\alpha)
=\tau(\zeta\alpha)
=\zeta^2\alpha
=\sigma^{-1}(\alpha),
$$
and both sides send $\zeta$ to $\zeta$, so $\tau\sigma\tau=\sigma^{-1}$.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
The Galois group is
$$
G=\operatorname{Gal}(K/\QQ)
=\langle\sigma,\tau\mid \sigma^3=\tau^2=1,\ \tau\sigma\tau=\sigma^{-1}\rangle
\cong S_3.
$$

::: pf-proof
The six elements
$$
1,\sigma,\sigma^2,\tau,\sigma\tau,\sigma^2\tau
$$
are distinct, so the subgroup generated by $\sigma$ and $\tau$ has order $6$.
Since $[K:\QQ]=6$, the full Galois group also has order $6$, so these automorphisms exhaust it.
The displayed presentation is the standard presentation of $S_3$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #sigma-fixed-field}
The subgroup $\langle\sigma\rangle$ of order $3$ fixes exactly
$$
\QQ(\zeta).
$$

::: pf-proof
The automorphism $\sigma$ fixes $\zeta$, so
$$
\QQ(\zeta)\subseteq K^{\langle\sigma\rangle}.
$$
By the fundamental theorem of Galois theory,
$$
[K^{\langle\sigma\rangle}:\QQ]
=\frac{|G|}{|\langle\sigma\rangle|}
=2.
$$
Also $[\QQ(\zeta):\QQ]=2$, so equality holds.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #order-two-fixed-fields}
The three subgroups of order $2$ have fixed fields
$$
K^{\langle\tau\rangle}=\QQ(\alpha),
$$
$$
K^{\langle\sigma\tau\rangle}=\QQ(\zeta^2\alpha),
$$
and
$$
K^{\langle\sigma^2\tau\rangle}=\QQ(\zeta\alpha).
$$

::: pf-proof
The first equality is immediate because $\tau$ fixes $\alpha$, and the fixed field of an order-$2$ subgroup has degree $3$ over $\QQ$.

For $\sigma\tau$, using composition from right to left,
$$
(\sigma\tau)(\zeta^2\alpha)
=\sigma(\zeta\alpha)
=\zeta^2\alpha,
$$
so $\QQ(\zeta^2\alpha)$ lies in the fixed field. Since $\zeta^2\alpha$ is a root of the irreducible polynomial $x^3-5$, this field has degree $3$, hence is the whole fixed field.

Similarly,
$$
(\sigma^2\tau)(\zeta\alpha)
=\sigma^2(\zeta^2\alpha)
=\zeta\alpha,
$$
so $\QQ(\zeta\alpha)$ is the fixed field of $\langle\sigma^2\tau\rangle$.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
The complete subgroup--field correspondence is
$$
\begin{array}{c|c}
H\le G & K^H\\ \hline
G & \QQ\\
\langle\sigma\rangle & \QQ(\zeta)\\
\langle\tau\rangle & \QQ(\alpha)\\
\langle\sigma\tau\rangle & \QQ(\zeta^2\alpha)\\
\langle\sigma^2\tau\rangle & \QQ(\zeta\alpha)\\
\{1\} & K
\end{array}.
$$

::: pf-proof
These are all subgroups of $S_3$: the whole group, its unique subgroup of order $3$, its three subgroups of order $2$, and the trivial subgroup. By step [](#sigma-fixed-field){.pf-ref} and step [](#order-two-fixed-fields){.pf-ref} their fixed fields are exactly those displayed, and the fundamental theorem of Galois theory reverses inclusions and gives all intermediate fields.
:::
