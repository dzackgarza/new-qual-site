::: {.problem}
Let $f$ be holomorphic on $\mathbb C$ and suppose that $f(0)=0$, $f(1)=1$, and $f(\mathbb D)\subseteq\mathbb D$, where $\mathbb D$ is the unit disc.
Show that

(a) $f'(1)\in\mathbb R$;

(b) $f'(1)\ge1$.
:::
