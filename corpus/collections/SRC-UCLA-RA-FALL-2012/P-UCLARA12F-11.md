::: {.problem}
Find all holomorphic functions $f:\mathbb C\to\mathbb C$ satisfying
\[
f(z+1)=f(z),\qquad f(z+i)=e^{2\pi}f(z)
\]
for every $z\in\mathbb C$.
:::
