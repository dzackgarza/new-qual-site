::: {.problem}
Define
\[
\pi=4\int_0^1\frac{dx}{1+x^2}.
\]
Prove that
\[
\pi=4\left(1-\frac13+\frac15-\frac17+\cdots\right).
\]
Be careful to give a complete proof.
:::
