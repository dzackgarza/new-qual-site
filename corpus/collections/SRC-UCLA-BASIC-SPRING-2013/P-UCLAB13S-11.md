::: {.problem}
Define the Fibonacci sequence by $F_0=0$, $F_1=1$, and $F_n=F_{n-1}+F_{n-2}$ for $n\ge2$.

(a) Show that the limit of $F_n/F_{n-1}$ as $n\to\infty$ exists and find its value.

(b) Prove that
\[
F_{2n+1}F_{2n-1}-F_{2n}^2=1
\]
for all $n\ge1$.
:::
