::: {.problem}
Denote by $h_n$ the $n$th harmonic number,
\[
h_n=1+\frac12+\frac13+\cdots+\frac1n.
\]
Prove that the limit
\[
\gamma=\lim_{n\to\infty}(h_n-\ln n)
\]
exists.
:::
