::: {.problem}
Consider the Hilbert space $\ell^2(\ZZ)$.
Show that the Borel $\sigma$-algebra associated to the norm topology agrees with the Borel $\sigma$-algebra associated to the weak topology.
:::

::: {.solution}

Let
$$
\mathcal B_{\mathrm w}
$$
denote the Borel $\sigma$-algebra for the weak topology and
$$
\mathcal B_{\norm{\cdot}}
$$
the Borel $\sigma$-algebra for the norm topology.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

One has
$$
\mathcal B_{\mathrm w}
\subset
\mathcal B_{\norm{\cdot}}.
$$


::: pf-proof

The weak topology is coarser than the norm topology because every continuous linear functional on a normed space is norm-continuous. Hence every weakly open set is norm-open. Therefore the $\sigma$-algebra generated by the weakly open sets is contained in the norm Borel $\sigma$-algebra.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

For every fixed $x\in\ell^2(\ZZ)$, the map
$$
y\longmapsto\norm{y-x}_2^2
$$
is $\mathcal B_{\mathrm w}$-measurable.


::: pf-proof

Let $(e_k)_{k\in\ZZ}$ be the standard orthonormal basis. For each $k$, the coordinate map
$$
y\longmapsto
\langle y-x,e_k\rangle
$$
is weakly continuous, hence weak-Borel measurable.

Choose an increasing sequence of finite sets
$$
F_1\subset F_2\subset\cdots\subset\ZZ,
\qquad
\bigcup_{n=1}^\infty F_n=\ZZ.
$$
Define
$$
S_n(y)
\coloneqq
\sum_{k\in F_n}
\abs{\langle y-x,e_k\rangle}^2.
$$
Each $S_n$ is $\mathcal B_{\mathrm w}$-measurable as a finite sum of measurable functions. By Parseval's identity,
$$
S_n(y)
\longrightarrow
\sum_{k\in\ZZ}
\abs{\langle y-x,e_k\rangle}^2
=
\norm{y-x}_2^2.
$$
A pointwise limit of measurable functions is measurable.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

Every norm-open ball in $\ell^2(\ZZ)$ belongs to $\mathcal B_{\mathrm w}$.


::: pf-proof

For $x\in\ell^2(\ZZ)$ and $r>0$,
$$
B(x,r)
=
\{y:\norm{y-x}_2^2<r^2\}.
$$
By step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref}, the squared-distance function is weak-Borel measurable, so
$$
B(x,r)
=
\bigl(\norm{\cdot-x}_2^2\bigr)^{-1}(( -\infty,r^2))
$$
belongs to $\mathcal B_{\mathrm w}$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

Every norm-open subset of $\ell^2(\ZZ)$ belongs to $\mathcal B_{\mathrm w}$.


::: pf-proof

The norm topology on $\ell^2(\ZZ)$ is separable. For example, finitely supported sequences with rational real and imaginary parts form a countable dense subset.

Every open subset of a separable metric space is a countable union of open balls: choose a countable base consisting of balls with centers in a fixed countable dense set and rational radii. By step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref}, each such ball belongs to $\mathcal B_{\mathrm w}$, and therefore so does every countable union of them.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-5}

Therefore
$$
\boxed{
\mathcal B_{\mathrm w}
=
\mathcal B_{\norm{\cdot}}
}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} gives
$$
\mathcal B_{\mathrm w}
\subset
\mathcal B_{\norm{\cdot}},
$$
while step [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} gives the reverse inclusion.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-5){.pf-ref} is the desired equality of Borel $\sigma$-algebras.

:::
