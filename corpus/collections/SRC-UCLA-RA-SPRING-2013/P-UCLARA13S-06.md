::: {.problem}
Define
\[
X=\left\{\xi\longmapsto\int_{\mathbb R}e^{i\xi x}f(x)\,dx:\ f\in L^1(\mathbb R)\right\}.
\]
Show that

(a) $X\subset C_0(\mathbb R)$;

(b) $X$ is dense in $C_0(\mathbb R)$;

(c) $X\ne C_0(\mathbb R)$.
:::
