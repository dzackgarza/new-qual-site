::: {.problem}
Let $P$ be a nonconstant polynomial all of whose zeros lie in the half-plane
\[
\{z\in\mathbb C:\operatorname{Re}z<\sigma\}.
\]
Show that every zero of $P'$ also lies in this half-plane.
:::
