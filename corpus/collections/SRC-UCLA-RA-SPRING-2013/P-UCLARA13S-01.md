::: {.problem}
Suppose $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ is bounded and Lebesgue measurable, and
\[
\lim_{h\to0^+}\frac1h\int_0^1 |f(x+h)-f(x)|\,dx=0.
\]
Show that $f$ is almost everywhere constant on $[0,1]$.
:::
