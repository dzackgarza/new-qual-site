::: {.problem}
Let
\[
f(z)=-\pi z\cot(\pi z).
\]

(a) Locate all poles of $f$ and determine their residues.

(b) Show that for every $n\ge1$, the coefficient of $z^{2n}$ in the Taylor expansion of $f$ about $0$ is
\[
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{2}{k^{2n}}.
\]
:::
