::: {.problem}
Find the eigenvalues of the $n\times n$ matrix $(a_{ij})$ with
\[
a_{ij}=\begin{cases}
1,&i=j+1,\\
-1,&i=j-1,\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\]
