::: problem
Let
\[
\cdots\subset X_2\subset X_1
\]
be a nested sequence of closed nonempty connected subsets of a compact metric space $X$. Prove that $\bigcap_{i=1}^{\infty}X_i$ is nonempty and connected.
:::
