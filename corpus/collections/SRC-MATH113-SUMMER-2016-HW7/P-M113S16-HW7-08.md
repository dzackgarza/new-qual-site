::: {.problem}
Consider the extension
\[
\mathbb Q(\sqrt2)\subset\mathbb Q(\sqrt[4]{2}).
\]

(a) Prove that every automorphism of $\mathbb Q(\sqrt[4]{2})$ fixes $\mathbb Q(\sqrt2)$ pointwise.

(b) Deduce that
\[
\operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb Q(\sqrt[4]{2}):\mathbb Q(\sqrt2))
=
\operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb Q(\sqrt[4]{2}):\mathbb Q).
\]

(c) Show that $\mathbb Q(\sqrt2)\subset\mathbb Q(\sqrt[4]{2})$ is a normal extension, but $\mathbb Q\subset\mathbb Q(\sqrt[4]{2})$ is not.

(d) Using (b), or otherwise, compute $\operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb Q(\sqrt[4]{2}):\mathbb Q)$.
:::
