::: {.problem}
Compute the minimal polynomials and find the degrees of the following simple extensions of $\mathbb Q$:

(a) $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})$;

(b) $\mathbb Q(\sqrt3+i)$;

(c) $\mathbb Q(\sqrt2-\sqrt{10})$;

(d) $\mathbb Q(e^{2\pi i/p})$, for $p$ an odd prime.
:::
