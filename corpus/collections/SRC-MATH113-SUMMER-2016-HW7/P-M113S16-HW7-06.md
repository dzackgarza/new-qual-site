::: {.problem}
Define
\[
\mathcal A=\{\alpha\in\mathbb C: \text{there exists } f\in\mathbb Q[x]\text{ such that }f(\alpha)=0\},
\]
the set of algebraic numbers. For example, $\sqrt2\in\mathcal A$ since it is a root of $x^2-2$, and $\sqrt{-2}+\sqrt3\in\mathcal A$ since it is a root of $x^4-2x^2+25$.

(a) Show that $\mathbb Q\subset\mathcal A$.

(b) Let $\mathbb Q\subset L$ be an algebraic extension. Prove that $L\subset\mathcal A$.

(c) Prove that $\mathcal A$ is a field. Deduce that it is the largest algebraic extension of $\mathbb Q$ in $\mathbb C$.

(d) Explain, using a single sentence, why $\mathcal A\ne\mathbb C$.
:::
