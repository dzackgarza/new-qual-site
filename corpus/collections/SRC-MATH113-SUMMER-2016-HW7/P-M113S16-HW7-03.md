::: {.problem}
Prove that, up to isomorphism, there are no finite extensions of $\mathbb C$ except $\mathbb C$ itself.
In other words, if $L$ is a finite extension of $\mathbb C$, prove that $L\cong\mathbb C$.

Does there exist an algebraic extension of $\mathbb C$?
Explain your answer.
:::
