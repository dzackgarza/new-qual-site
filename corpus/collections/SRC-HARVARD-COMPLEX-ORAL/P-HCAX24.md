::: problem
Define the modular group and describe its action on the upper half-plane.
:::

::: solution
The modular group is
\[
PSL_2(\mathbb Z)=SL_2(\mathbb Z)/\{\pm I\}.
\]
Thus an element is represented by a matrix
\[
\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\ c&d\end{pmatrix},
\qquad a,b,c,d\in\mathbb Z,
\quad ad-bc=1,
\]
with a matrix identified with its negative.

It acts on the upper half-plane
\[
\mathbb H=\{z\in\mathbb C:\operatorname{Im}z>0\}
\]
by Möbius transformations
\[
\gamma z=\frac{az+b}{cz+d}.
\]
Indeed,
\[
\operatorname{Im}(\gamma z)
=\frac{\operatorname{Im}z}{|cz+d|^2}>0,
\]
so the action preserves $\mathbb H$.

The group is generated by
\[
T(z)=z+1,
\qquad
S(z)=-\frac1z,
\]
corresponding respectively to
\[
\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]
A standard fundamental domain is
\[
\left\{z\in\mathbb H:
|\operatorname{Re}z|\le\frac12,
\ |z|\ge1\right\},
\]
with the usual identifications of its boundary arcs.
:::
