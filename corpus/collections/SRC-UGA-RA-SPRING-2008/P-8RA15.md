::: problem
(a) Let $E\subseteq[0,1)$ be a Lebesgue measurable set.
Prove that
\[
\int_E\sin(2\pi nx)\,dx\to 0\quad\text{as }n\to\infty
\]
by exploiting the fact that $\{e^{2\pi inx}\}_{n\in\mathbb{Z}}$ is an orthonormal set in $L^2[0,1)$.

(b) Show that the above also holds for any measurable set $E\subseteq\mathbb{R}$ of finite measure.
:::
