::: {.problem}
Let $z_1,\ldots,z_n$ be distinct complex numbers, and let $a_1,\ldots,a_n$ be nonzero complex numbers. Suppose
\[
S_p=\sum_{j=1}^n a_jz_j^p=0
\qquad(p=0,1,\ldots,m-1),
\]
but $S_m\ne0$, where $1\le m\le n-1$.

How many zeros does
\[
f(z)=\sum_{j=1}^n\frac{a_j}{z-z_j}
\]
have in $\mathbb C$? Why is $m\ge n$ impossible?
:::
