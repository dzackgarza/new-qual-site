::: {.problem}
Let $A$ and $B$ be $n\times n$ matrices. Show that $AB$ and $BA$ have the same eigenvalues.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #det-identity-swap}
For arbitrary $n\times n$ matrices $X,Y$,
$$
\det(I-XY)
=
\det(I-YX).
$$

::: pf-proof
Consider the block matrix
$$
M
=
\begin{pmatrix}
I&X\\
Y&I
\end{pmatrix}.
$$
Left multiplication by
$$
\begin{pmatrix}
I&-X\\
0&I
\end{pmatrix}
$$
has determinant $1$ and gives
$$
\begin{pmatrix}
I-XY&0\\
Y&I
\end{pmatrix}.
$$
Therefore
$$
\det M
=
\det(I-XY).
$$

On the other hand, left multiplication by
$$
\begin{pmatrix}
I&0\\
-Y&I
\end{pmatrix}
$$
also has determinant $1$ and gives
$$
\begin{pmatrix}
I&X\\
0&I-YX
\end{pmatrix}.
$$
Hence
$$
\det M
=
\det(I-YX).
$$
Equating the two expressions for $\det M$ proves the identity.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #char-poly-agree-nonzero-lambda}
For every nonzero scalar $\lambda$,
$$
\det(\lambda I-AB)
=
\det(\lambda I-BA).
$$

::: pf-proof
Factor out $\lambda$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\det(\lambda I-AB)
&=
\lambda^n
\det\left(
I-\lambda^{-1}AB
\right)\\
&=
\lambda^n
\det\left(
I-B\lambda^{-1}A
\right)
\end{aligned}
$$
by step [](#det-identity-swap){.pf-ref}, with
$$
X=\lambda^{-1}A,
\qquad
Y=B.
$$
Since scalars commute with matrices,
$$
B\lambda^{-1}A
=
\lambda^{-1}BA.
$$
Factoring $\lambda$ back in gives the desired equality.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #char-polys-equal}
The characteristic polynomials of $AB$ and $BA$ are equal.

::: pf-proof
Both
$$
\det(\lambda I-AB)
$$
and
$$
\det(\lambda I-BA)
$$
are polynomials in $\lambda$ of degree $n$. Step [](#char-poly-agree-nonzero-lambda){.pf-ref} shows that they agree
for every nonzero scalar $\lambda$. Over $\RR$ or $\CC$, this is an
infinite set, so the two polynomials are identical.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #same-eigenvalues-and-multiplicities}
The matrices $AB$ and $BA$ have the same eigenvalues, with the same
algebraic multiplicities.

::: pf-proof
The eigenvalues, counted with algebraic multiplicity, are precisely the
roots of the characteristic polynomial. Apply step [](#char-polys-equal){.pf-ref}.
:::

:::

::: pf-qed
Step [](#same-eigenvalues-and-multiplicities){.pf-ref} proves the requested statement.
:::
