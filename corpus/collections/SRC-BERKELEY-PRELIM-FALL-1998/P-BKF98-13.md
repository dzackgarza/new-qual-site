::: {.problem}
Let $f$ be entire and let
\[
\Omega=\mathbb C\setminus(-\infty,0].
\]
Suppose that for every $z\in\Omega$,
\[
|f(z)|\le |\log z|,
\]
where $\log z$ is the principal branch. What can be concluded about $f$?
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. One has
$$
f(1)=0.
$$

::: {.proof}
The point $1$ lies in $\Omega$, and
$$
\log1=0.
$$
The hypothesis therefore gives
$$
\abs{f(1)}
\leq0,
$$
so $f(1)=0$.
:::

<1>2. Fix $R>1$. For every point
$$
z=Re^{i\theta}
$$
on the circle with
$$
-\pi<\theta<\pi,
$$
one has
$$
\abs{f(z)}
\leq
\sqrt{(\log R)^2+\pi^2}.
$$

::: {.proof}
On the principal branch,
$$
\log z
=
\log R+i\theta.
$$
Hence
$$
\abs{\log z}^2
=
(\log R)^2+\theta^2
\leq
(\log R)^2+\pi^2.
$$
Apply the assumed inequality for $f$.
:::

<1>3. The same bound holds at the remaining point
$$
z=-R
$$
of the circle.

::: {.proof}
Approach $-R$ along the circle from within $\Omega$, for example with
$$
z_\theta=Re^{i\theta},
\qquad
\theta\uparrow\pi.
$$
Since $f$ is entire,
$$
f(z_\theta)\longrightarrow f(-R).
$$
Step <1>2 bounds every $\abs{f(z_\theta)}$ by the same constant. Passing
to the limit gives
$$
\abs{f(-R)}
\leq
\sqrt{(\log R)^2+\pi^2}.
$$
:::

<1>4. If
$$
f(z)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}a_nz^n,
$$
then for every $n\geq1$ and every $R>1$,
$$
\abs{a_n}
\leq
\frac{\sqrt{(\log R)^2+\pi^2}}{R^n}.
$$

::: {.proof}
Steps <1>2 and <1>3 give
$$
\max_{\abs z=R}\abs{f(z)}
\leq
\sqrt{(\log R)^2+\pi^2}.
$$
Cauchy's coefficient estimate at the origin gives the displayed bound.
:::

<1>5. For every $n\geq1$,
$$
a_n=0.
$$

::: {.proof}
Let $R\to\infty$ in step <1>4. Since
$$
\frac{\sqrt{(\log R)^2+\pi^2}}{R^n}
\longrightarrow0,
$$
one obtains $\abs{a_n}=0$.
:::

<1>6. The entire function $f$ is constant.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>5 shows that every positive-degree Taylor coefficient of $f$ at the
origin vanishes. Hence
$$
f(z)=a_0
$$
for all $z$.
:::

<1>7. One has
$$
\boxed{f\equiv0}.
$$

::: {.proof}
Step <1>6 makes $f$ constant, and step <1>1 shows that this constant is
zero.
:::

<1>8. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>7 is the conclusion forced by the hypotheses.
:::
:::
