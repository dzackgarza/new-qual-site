::: {.problem}
Find a function $y(x)$ for $x\ge0$ such that
\[
y^{(4)}+y=0,
\qquad
y(0)=0,
\qquad
y'(0)=1,
\]
and
\[
\lim_{x\to\infty}y(x)=
\lim_{x\to\infty}y'(x)=0.
\]
:::
