::: {.problem}
Let $\varphi(x,y)$ have continuous second partial derivatives and suppose on $\mathbb R^2\setminus\{0\}$ that
\[
\varphi_{xx}+\varphi_{yy}+\varphi_x=0.
\]
Writing $r=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}$, suppose also that as $r\to0$,
\[
r\varphi_x\longrightarrow \frac{x}{2\pi r},
\qquad
r\varphi_y\longrightarrow \frac{y}{2\pi r}.
\]
Let $C_R$ be the circle $x^2+y^2=R^2$. Show that
\[
\int_{C_R}e^x(-\varphi_y\,dx+\varphi_x\,dy)
\]
is independent of $R$, and evaluate it.
:::
