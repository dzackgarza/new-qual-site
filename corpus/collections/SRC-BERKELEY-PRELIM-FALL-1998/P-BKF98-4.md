::: {.problem}
Let $f$ be analytic on a neighborhood of the closed unit disk, suppose
\[
f(-\log2)=0,
\]
and assume
\[
|f(z)|\le |e^z|
\]
for every $|z|=1$. How large can
\[
|f(\log2)|
\]
be?
:::
