::: {.problem}
Let $(f_n)$ and $(g_n)$ converge uniformly on a set $A$.
Suppose both sequences are uniformly bounded: there is $M$ such that $|f_n(z)|,|g_n(z)|\le M$ for every $z\in A$ and $n$.
Prove that $(f_ng_n)$ converges uniformly on $A$.
:::
