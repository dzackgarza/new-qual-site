::: {.problem}
Let $\lambda\in\mathbb R$. Evaluate
\[
\int_0^\infty (\operatorname{sech}x)^2\cos(\lambda x)\,dx,
\]
where
\[
\operatorname{sech}x=\frac{2}{e^x+e^{-x}}.
\]
:::
