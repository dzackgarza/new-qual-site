::: {.problem}
1. Let $f$ be holomorphic on an open set containing the closed unit disk and suppose $f$ is real-valued on the unit circle. Prove that $f$ is constant.

2. Find a nonconstant function holomorphic on the complex plane except for a single point of the unit circle and real-valued at every other point of the unit circle.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. Let $U_0$ be the connected component of the given open set that contains
the closed unit disk. Then $f$ is constant on $U_0$.

::: {.proof}
Write
$$
f=u+iv
$$
on the open unit disk $\DD$. Since $f$ is holomorphic, the function
$v=\operatorname{Im}f$ is harmonic on $\DD$. It is continuous on
$\overline{\DD}$ and vanishes on $\partial\DD$, because $f$ is
real-valued on the unit circle.

The maximum principle for harmonic functions applied to $v$ gives
$$
v\leq0
$$
on $\DD$, while applying it to $-v$ gives
$$
v\geq0.
$$
Thus $v\equiv0$ on $\DD$, so
$$
f(\DD)\subseteq\RR.
$$
If $f|_{\DD}$ were nonconstant, the open mapping theorem would make
$f(\DD)$ open in $\CC$, which is impossible for a subset of $\RR$.
Therefore $f$ is constant on $\DD$.

Since $\DD\subseteq U_0$ and $U_0$ is connected, the identity theorem
implies that $f$ is constant on all of $U_0$.
:::

<1>2. In Part (2), one may take
$$
\boxed{
F(z)=i\frac{1+z}{1-z}
}.
$$

::: {.proof}
The function $F$ is rational, its denominator vanishes only at $z=1$, and
$$
F'(z)=\frac{2i}{(1-z)^2},
$$
so $F$ is holomorphic on $\CC\sm\{1\}$ and nonconstant.

If $\abs{z}=1$ and $z\neq1$, then $\bar z=1/z$, and hence
$$
\begin{aligned}
\overline{F(z)}
&=
-i\frac{1+\bar z}{1-\bar z}\\
&=
-i\frac{1+1/z}{1-1/z}\\
&=
-i\frac{z+1}{z-1}\\
&=
i\frac{1+z}{1-z}\\
&=
F(z).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus $F(z)$ is real for every point of the unit circle other than its single
singularity at $z=1$.
:::

<1>3. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>1 proves the intended conclusion of Part (1) on the connected
component containing the closed unit disk, and step <1>2 supplies the required
example for Part (2).
:::
:::

::: {.remark}
Taken literally, Part (1) needs the ambient open set to be connected if
"constant" is intended to mean constant on the whole open set. For example,
on
$$
U=\{\abs z<2\}\union\{\abs{z-4}<1/2\},
$$
the function that is $0$ on the first component and $1$ on the second is
holomorphic on $U$, is real-valued on the unit circle, and is not constant on
$U$. The argument above proves the source's intended conclusion on the
connected component containing the closed unit disk.
:::
