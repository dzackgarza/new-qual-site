::: {.problem}
Let $f,g:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ be smooth, with
\[
f(0)=0,
\qquad
f'(0)\ne0.
\]
Consider
\[
f(x)=t\,g(x).
\]

1. Show that for $|t|<\delta$, for some $\delta>0$, there is a unique continuous solution $x(t)$ with $x(0)=0$.
2. Find the first-order Taylor expansion of $x(t)$ at $t=0$.
:::
