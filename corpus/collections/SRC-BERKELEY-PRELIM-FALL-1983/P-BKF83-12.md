::: {.problem}
Let $r_1,\dots,r_n$ be distinct complex numbers. Show that every rational function of the form
\[
f(z)=\frac{b_0+b_1z+\cdots+b_{n-1}z^{n-1}}
{(z-r_1)(z-r_2)\cdots(z-r_n)}
\]
can be written
\[
f(z)=\frac{A_1}{z-r_1}+\frac{A_2}{z-r_2}+\cdots+\frac{A_n}{z-r_n}
\]
for suitable constants $A_1,\dots,A_n$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Set
$$
P(z)\coloneqq b_0+b_1z+\cdots+b_{n-1}z^{n-1},
\qquad
Q(z)\coloneqq\prod_{j=1}^n(z-r_j).
$$

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

For each $i$, define
$$
A_i
\coloneqq
\frac{P(r_i)}
{\displaystyle\prod_{\substack{1\le j\le n\\j\ne i}}(r_i-r_j)}.
$$
These constants are well-defined.

::: pf-proof

The numbers $r_1,\ldots,r_n$ are distinct, so every factor
$$
r_i-r_j
$$
with $j\ne i$ is nonzero. Hence the denominator in the definition of
$A_i$ is nonzero.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

The polynomial identity
$$
P(z)
=
\sum_{i=1}^n
A_i
\prod_{\substack{1\le j\le n\\j\ne i}}(z-r_j)
$$
holds for every $z\in\mathbb C$.

::: pf-proof

Let
$$
H(z)
\coloneqq
P(z)
-
\sum_{i=1}^n
A_i
\prod_{j\ne i}(z-r_j).
$$
Each summand has degree at most $n-1$, so
$$
\deg H\le n-1
$$
unless $H=0$ already.

Fix $k\in\{1,\ldots,n\}$. Evaluating at $z=r_k$, every summand in the sum
vanishes except the one with $i=k$. Thus
$$
\begin{aligned}
H(r_k)
&=
P(r_k)
-
A_k\prod_{j\ne k}(r_k-r_j)\\
&=
P(r_k)-P(r_k)
=0,
\end{aligned}
$$
by the definition in step [](#s1){.pf-ref}.

Hence $H$ has the $n$ distinct roots
$$
r_1,\ldots,r_n.
$$
A nonzero polynomial of degree at most $n-1$ cannot have $n$ distinct
roots, so $H\equiv0$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

Dividing the identity in step [](#s2){.pf-ref} by $Q(z)$ gives
$$
\boxed{
f(z)
=
\frac{A_1}{z-r_1}
+\cdots+
\frac{A_n}{z-r_n}
}.
$$

::: pf-proof

For $z\notin\{r_1,\ldots,r_n\}$,
$$
\frac1{Q(z)}
\prod_{j\ne i}(z-r_j)
=
\frac1{z-r_i}.
$$
Dividing step [](#s2){.pf-ref} by $Q(z)$ therefore yields the displayed identity on
the common domain of the rational functions.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s3){.pf-ref} gives the required partial-fraction decomposition with the
explicit constants from step [](#s1){.pf-ref}.

:::
