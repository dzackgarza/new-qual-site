::: {.problem}
Let $m<n$, and identify linear maps $\mathbb R^m\to\mathbb R^n$ with real $n\times m$ matrices. Let $L$ be the set of maps of rank $m$.

1. Show that $L$ is open.
2. Show that there is a continuous assignment
\[
A\longmapsto T(A)\in M_{m\times n}(\mathbb R)
\]
such that
\[
T(A)A=I_m
\]
for every $A\in L$.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

A matrix $A\in M_{n\times m}(\RR)$ lies in $L$ if and only if
$A^T A$ is invertible.

::: pf-proof

For every $v\in\RR^m$,
$$
v^T A^T A v
=
\norm{Av}^2.
$$
Hence
$$
A^T A v=0
$$
implies
$$
0
=
v^T A^T A v
=
\norm{Av}^2,
$$
so $Av=0$. Conversely, $Av=0$ immediately implies $A^TAv=0$. Therefore
$$
\ker(A^TA)=\ker(A).
$$
Since $A$ has $m$ columns, it has rank $m$ exactly when
$\ker(A)=\{0\}$, and this is equivalent to invertibility of the
$m\times m$ matrix $A^TA$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

The set $L$ is open in $M_{n\times m}(\RR)$.

::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1){.pf-ref},
$$
L
=
\left\{
A\in M_{n\times m}(\RR):
\det(A^TA)\neq0
\right\}.
$$
The map
$$
A\longmapsto\det(A^TA)
$$
is a polynomial in the entries of $A$, hence continuous. Therefore $L$ is
the inverse image of the open set $\RR\sm\{0\}$ and is open.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

Define, for $A\in L$,
$$
\boxed{
T(A)\coloneqq(A^TA)^{-1}A^T
}.
$$
Then $T(A)A=I_m$.

::: pf-proof

Step [](#s1){.pf-ref} makes $A^TA$ invertible for every $A\in L$, so the formula is
well-defined. Directly,
$$
T(A)A
=
(A^TA)^{-1}A^TA
=
I_m.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s4}

The assignment $T:L\to M_{m\times n}(\RR)$ is continuous.

::: pf-proof

Transpose and matrix multiplication are polynomial operations in the matrix
entries, hence continuous. Matrix inversion is continuous on
$\operatorname{GL}_m(\RR)$, since
$$
B^{-1}
=
\frac{\operatorname{adj}(B)}{\det B}
$$
there. Thus
$$
A
\longmapsto
A^TA
\longmapsto
(A^TA)^{-1}
\longmapsto
(A^TA)^{-1}A^T
$$
is continuous on $L$.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s2){.pf-ref} proves Part (1), while steps [](#s3){.pf-ref} and [](#s4){.pf-ref} construct the continuous
left inverse required in Part (2).

:::
