::: {.problem}
Let ξ and η be two independent random variables taking values 1 and 0 with the probabilities p and $q = 1 - p$ correspondingly.
Find

$$
\mathbf { E } ( \xi + \eta | \eta ) .
$$

$$
\mathbf { E } ( \xi | \xi + \eta ) .
$$
