::: {.problem}
A helix is written in a parametric form as follows,

$$
x = r \cos \phi , y = r \sin \phi , z = a \phi ,\tag{12}
$$

where $r , a > 0$ are constants and $\phi$ varies from $0$ to $\infty$.
Find the length of the helix as a function of $\phi$
