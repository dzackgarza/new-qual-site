::: {.problem}
Describe $\mathbb P^2$ and the standard embedding $\mathbb A^2\hookrightarrow\mathbb P^2$.
Let
\[
Z:=V(y^2-x^3)\subset\mathbb A^2.
\]
Answer the following questions about $Z$.

1. What is $A(Z)$?
   Is $Z$ irreducible?

2. What is the projective closure $\overline Z$ of $Z$, and what is $\overline Z-Z$?
   Depict the result.

3. Does the morphism
   \[
   f:Z-\{0\}\longrightarrow\mathbb P^1,\qquad f(\alpha,\beta)=[\alpha,\beta]
   \]
   extend to a morphism $Z\to\mathbb P^1$?

4. Is $f$ bijective?
   Is it an isomorphism?
   Is it a birational equivalence?

5. What is the function field of $Z$?

6. Compute the blow-up of $Z$ at $\{0\}$.

7. Where is $Z$ nonsingular?

8. What is the integral closure of $A(Z)$?
   What is the normalization of $Z$?
:::
