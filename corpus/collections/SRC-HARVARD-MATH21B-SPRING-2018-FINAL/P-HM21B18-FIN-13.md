::: {.problem}
(a) Solve
\[
u_t=9u_{xx}
\]
with initial condition
\[
u(x,0)=4\sin x+5\sin(2x).
\]

(b) Use Parseval's equality to compute $\|u(x,1)\|^2$.

(c) Solve
\[
u_{tt}=9u_{xx}-2u+1
\]
with
\[
u(x,0)=7\sin(5x),
\qquad
u_t(x,0)=x.
\]
:::
