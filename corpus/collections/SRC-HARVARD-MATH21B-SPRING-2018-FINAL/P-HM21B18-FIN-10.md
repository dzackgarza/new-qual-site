::: {.problem}
Find the general solution to each differential equation:

1. $f'(t)=1/(t+1)$;
2. $f''(t)=e^t+t$;
3. $f''(t)+f(t)=t+2$;
4. $f''(t)-2f'(t)+f(t)=e^t$;
5. $f''(t)-f(t)=e^t+\sin t$;
6. $f''(t)-f(t)=e^{-3t}$.
:::
