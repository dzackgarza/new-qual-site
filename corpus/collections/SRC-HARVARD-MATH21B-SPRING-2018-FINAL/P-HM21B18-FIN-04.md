::: {.problem}
Find all $(x,y,z,w)\in\mathbb R^4$ satisfying
\[
x+y+z+w=20,
\qquad
x-y+z-w=10,
\]
by row-reducing the augmented matrix. Then find two linearly independent vectors perpendicular to the kernel of the coefficient matrix.
:::
