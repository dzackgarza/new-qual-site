::: {.problem}
On $[-\pi,\pi]$, let
\[
f(x)=
\begin{cases}
1,&|x|>1,\\
-1,&|x|\le1.
\end{cases}
\]

(a) Find the Fourier series of $f$.

(b) Find the sum of the squares of all Fourier coefficients of $f$.
:::
