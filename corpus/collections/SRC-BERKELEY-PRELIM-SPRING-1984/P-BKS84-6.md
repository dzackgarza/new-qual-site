:::{.problem}
Let $a>0$ and define
\[
x_0=0,
\qquad
x_{n+1}=a+x_n^2.
\]
Find a necessary and sufficient condition on $a$ for the finite limit
\[
\lim_{n\to\infty}x_n
\]
to exist.
:::
