::: {.problem}
Let $I$ be an open interval containing $0$. Suppose $f'$ exists in a neighborhood of $0$ and $f''(0)$ exists. Show that
\[
f(x)=f(0)+f'(0)\sin x+\frac12f''(0)\sin^2x+o(x^2)
\qquad(x\to0).
\]
:::
