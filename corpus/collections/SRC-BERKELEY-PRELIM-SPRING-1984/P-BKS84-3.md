::: {.problem}
Let $f:[0,1]\to\mathbb R$ be continuous with
\[
f(0)=f(1)=0.
\]
Suppose $f''$ exists on $(0,1)$ and
\[
f''+2f'+f\ge0.
\]
Prove that
\[
f(x)\le0
\]
for every $0\le x\le1$.
:::
