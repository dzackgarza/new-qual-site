::: {.problem}
Determine all finitely generated abelian groups $G$ for which
\[
\operatorname{Aut}(G)
\]
is finite.
:::

::: {.solution}
By the structure theorem for finitely generated abelian groups, write
$$
G\cong\ZZ^r\oplus T,
$$
where $r\geq0$ and $T$ is finite.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #rank-two-infinite}
If $r\geq2$, then $\operatorname{Aut}(G)$ is infinite.

::: pf-proof
Let $e_1,e_2,\ldots,e_r$ be the standard basis of the free summand.
For each $n\in\ZZ$, define an automorphism of $\ZZ^r\oplus T$ by
$$
e_1\longmapsto e_1+n e_2,
\qquad
e_j\longmapsto e_j\quad(j\geq2),
$$
and let it act as the identity on $T$. Its inverse is obtained by
replacing $n$ with $-n$. Distinct integers $n$ give distinct
automorphisms, so the automorphism group is infinite.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #rank-zero-finite}
If $r=0$, then $\operatorname{Aut}(G)$ is finite.

::: pf-proof
In this case $G=T$ is a finite set. Hence the set of all maps
$G\to G$ is finite, and $\operatorname{Aut}(G)$ is a subset of it.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #rank-one-form}
Suppose $r=1$, so
$$
G\cong\ZZ e\oplus T.
$$
Every automorphism $\varphi$ has the form
$$
\varphi(e)=\epsilon e+t,
\qquad
\varphi|_T=\alpha,
$$
where
$$
\epsilon\in\{1,-1\},
\qquad
t\in T,
\qquad
\alpha\in\operatorname{Aut}(T).
$$

::: pf-proof
The torsion subgroup $T$ consists intrinsically of all elements of
finite order, so it is characteristic. Thus any automorphism
$\varphi$ restricts to an automorphism
$$
\alpha=\varphi|_T
$$
of $T$ and induces an automorphism of
$$
G/T\cong\ZZ.
$$
Every automorphism of $\ZZ$ is multiplication by $1$ or $-1$.
Therefore, for some $\epsilon\in\{1,-1\}$,
$$
\varphi(e)+T
=
\epsilon e+T,
$$
which means
$$
\varphi(e)=\epsilon e+t
$$
for some $t\in T$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #rank-one-finite}
If $r=1$, then $\operatorname{Aut}(G)$ is finite.

::: pf-proof
By step [](#rank-one-form){.pf-ref}, an automorphism is determined by the three pieces of data
$$
\epsilon\in\{1,-1\},
\qquad
t\in T,
\qquad
\alpha\in\operatorname{Aut}(T).
$$
All three sets of choices are finite because $T$ is finite. Thus
$\operatorname{Aut}(G)$ is finite.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #classification-boxed}
Consequently, the required groups are exactly the finitely
generated abelian groups of free rank at most one:
$$
\boxed{
G\cong T
\quad\text{or}\quad
G\cong\ZZ\oplus T,
\qquad T\text{ finite}.
}
$$

::: pf-proof
Steps [](#rank-zero-finite){.pf-ref} and [](#rank-one-finite){.pf-ref} prove finiteness when $r\leq1$, while step [](#rank-two-infinite){.pf-ref}
proves infinitude when $r\geq2$.
:::

:::

::: pf-qed
Step [](#classification-boxed){.pf-ref} gives the complete classification.
:::
