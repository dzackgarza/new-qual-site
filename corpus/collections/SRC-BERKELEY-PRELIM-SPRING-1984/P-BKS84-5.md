:::{.problem}
Let $A,B$ be complex $n\times n$ matrices satisfying
\[
AB=BA^2,
\]
and suppose $A$ has no eigenvalue of absolute value $1$. Prove that $A$ and $B$ have a common nonzero eigenvector.
:::
