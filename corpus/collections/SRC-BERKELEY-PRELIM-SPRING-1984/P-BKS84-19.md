:::{.problem}
For $x>0$, let
\[
f(x)=x\log(1+x^{-1}).
\]

1. Show that $f$ is strictly increasing.
2. Compute
   \[
   \lim_{x\to0^+}f(x)
   \qquad\text{and}\qquad
   \lim_{x\to\infty}f(x).
   \]
:::
