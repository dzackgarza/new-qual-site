::: {.problem}
Let $A$, $B$, $C$, and $D$ be well-formed sentences. Suppose $A\to C$, $B\to D$, $\neg C\to\neg D$, and $\neg A\to\neg C$. Which of the following statements must be true?

I. $A\to D$

II. $A$ and $C$ are logically equivalent.

III. $\neg A\to\neg B$

(A) I only
(B) II only
(C) III only
(D) I and II only
(E) II and III only
:::
