::: {.problem}
A fair coin is flipped $10$ times. What's the probability of getting between $40$ and $50$ "heads"?

(A) $10\%$
(B) $38\%$
(C) $41\%$
(D) $47\%$
(E) $53\%$
:::

::: {.remark}
As printed, ten flips cannot produce forty heads; the book's solution treats one hundred flips.
:::
