::: {.problem}
What is the order of the permutation
\[
\sigma=\begin{pmatrix}1&2&3&4&5&6\\5&6&2&3&1&4\end{pmatrix}
\]
on the set $S=\{1,2,3,4,5,6\}$?

(A) $1$
(B) $2$
(C) $3$
(D) $4$
(E) $6$
:::
