::: {.problem}
Let $f(z)$ be a complex analytic function such that $f(z) = (5x - 3y) + i v(x, y)$, where $v(x, y)$ is a real-valued function and $x, y, \in \mathbb{R}$. If $v(4, 1) = 7$, what is $v(3, 2)$?

(A) $-10$
(B) $-9$
(C) $1$
(D) $9$
(E) $14$
:::
