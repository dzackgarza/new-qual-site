Which of the following is a harmonic conjugate $u(x, y)$ of the harmonic function $v = x - 3x^2y + y^3$?

(A) $x^3 - 3xy^2 + y$
(B) $-x^3 + 3xy^2 - y$
(C) $-y^3 + 3x^2y - x$
(D) $y^3 - 3x^2y + x$
(E) $-x^3 + 3xy^2$
:::
