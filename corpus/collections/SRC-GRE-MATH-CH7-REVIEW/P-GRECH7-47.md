::: {.problem}
If $A \subseteq B$, which of the following conditions must be true?

I. $A \cup B = B$

II. $A - B = \emptyset$

III. $C - B \subseteq C - A$

(A) I only
(B) I and II only
(C) I and III only
(D) II and III only
(E) I, II, and III
:::
