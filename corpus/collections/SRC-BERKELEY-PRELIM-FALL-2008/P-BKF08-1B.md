::: {.problem}
Let $V$ be a nonzero vector space over an infinite field. Show that $V$ is not the union of finitely many cosets
\[
a_1+V_1,\ldots,a_n+V_n
\]
of proper subspaces $V_1,\ldots,V_n$.
:::
