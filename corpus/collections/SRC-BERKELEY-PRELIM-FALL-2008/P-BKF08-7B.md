::: {.problem}
(a) Prove that $\alpha=\sqrt3+\sqrt2$ is algebraic over $\mathbb Q$ by writing down a polynomial $f(x)\in\mathbb Q[x]$ of degree $4$ having $\alpha$ as a root.

(b) Show that $f(x)$ is irreducible over $\mathbb Q$.
