::: {.problem}
Suppose $(f_n)_{n>0}$ is a sequence of continuous real-valued functions on $[0,1]$ such that $f_n(x)\to0$ for every $x$.
Prove or give a counterexample to
\[
\lim_{n\to\infty}\int_0^1 f_n(x)\,dx=0.
\]
:::
