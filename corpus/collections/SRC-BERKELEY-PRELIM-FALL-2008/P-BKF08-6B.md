::: {.problem}
Show that
\[
\int_0^1\frac{1}{x^x}\,dx=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac1{n^n}.
\]

*Hint.* Write $x^x$ in terms of the exponential and logarithm functions, and evaluate $\int_0^1x^s\log(x)^n\,dx$.
:::
