::: problem
For $r>0$, let
\[
\gamma_r(t)=re^{it},\qquad 0\le t\le\pi.
\]
Calculate
\[
\lim_{r\to\infty}\int_{\gamma_r}\frac{e^{iz}}{z^2}\,dz.
\]
