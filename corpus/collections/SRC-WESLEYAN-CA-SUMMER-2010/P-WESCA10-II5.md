::: {.problem}
Let $f$ be entire.

(a) Prove that $f$ has a removable singularity at infinity if and only if $f$ is constant.

(b) Prove that $f$ has a pole at infinity of order $m$ if and only if $f$ is a polynomial of degree $m$.
:::
