::: {.problem}
Suppose $f$ is analytic in the unit disk, $f(0)=f'(0)=0$, and
\[
|f'(z)|\le1
\]
for every $|z|<1$.
Prove
\[
|f(z)|\le \frac{|z|^2}{2}.
\]
Determine the functions for which equality can hold at some nonzero $z$.
:::
