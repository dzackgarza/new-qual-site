::: {.problem}
Outline a proof, starting from basic properties of the real numbers, of the following theorem.

Let $f:[a,b]\to\mathbb R$ be continuous and differentiable on $(a,b)$, with
\[
f'(x)=0\qquad(x\in(a,b)).
\]
Then $f(b)=f(a)$.
