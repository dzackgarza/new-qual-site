::: {.problem}
Let $A$ be an $n\times n$ complex matrix with characteristic polynomial $\chi$ and minimal polynomial $\mu$. Suppose
\[
\chi(x)=\mu(x)(x-i),
\qquad
\mu(x)^2=\chi(x)(x^2+1).
\]
Determine the Jordan canonical form of $A$.
:::
