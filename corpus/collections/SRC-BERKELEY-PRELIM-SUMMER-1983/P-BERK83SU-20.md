::: {.problem}
Let $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ be continuously differentiable, nonnegative, and periodic of period $1$. Prove that
\[
\frac{d}{dx}\left(\frac{f(x)}{1+cf(x)}\right)\longrightarrow0
\qquad(c\to\infty)
\]
uniformly in $x$.
:::
