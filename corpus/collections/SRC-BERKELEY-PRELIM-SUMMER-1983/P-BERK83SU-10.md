::: {.problem}
Let $f:[0,2\pi]\to\mathbb R$ be twice differentiable and satisfy
\[
\int_0^{2\pi}f(x)\,dx=0,
\qquad
f(2\pi)=f(0).
\]
Prove that
\[
\int_0^{2\pi}f(x)^2\,dx
\le
\int_0^{2\pi}f'(x)^2\,dx.
\]
:::
