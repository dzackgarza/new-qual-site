::: {.problem}
Let $0<b_1<b_2<\cdots$ be real numbers such that
\[
b_n\to\infty,
\qquad
\frac{b_n}{b_{n+1}}\to1.
\]
Prove that
\[
\left\{\frac{b_m}{b_n}:1\le n<m\right\}
\]
is dense in $(1,\infty)$.
:::
