::: {.problem}
Let G be a finite group, $p > 0$ a prime number, and H a normal subgroup of G. Prove the following assertions.

(a) Any Sylow p-subgroup of H is the intersection $P \cap H$ of a Sylow p-subgroup P of G and subgroup H.

(b) Any Sylow p-subgroup of G/H is the is the quotient $P H / H$ where P is a Sylow p-subgroup of G.
:::
