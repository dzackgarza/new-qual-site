::: {.problem}
Given $n$ distinct points $z_j \in \mathbb{C}$ and $n$ values $f_j \in \mathbb{C}$, show that there is a unique polynomial $P$ of degree at most $n - 1$ such that

$$
P(z_j) = f_j
$$

for $1 \leq j \leq n$.
:::
