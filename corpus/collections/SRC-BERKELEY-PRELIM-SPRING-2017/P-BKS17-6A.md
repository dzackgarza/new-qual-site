::: {.problem}
Let $A$ be an $n \times n$ matrix over the complex numbers. Let $e^A = 1 + A + A^2/2 + \cdots + A^m/m! + \cdots$. Show this series converges and $\det(e^A) = e^{\operatorname{Tr}(A)}$.
:::
