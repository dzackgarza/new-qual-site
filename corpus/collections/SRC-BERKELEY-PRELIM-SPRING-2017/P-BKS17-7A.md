::: {.problem}
Given two vectors $x$ and $y$ in $\mathbb{R}^n$ with $\|x\|_2 = \|y\|_2$, construct an orthogonal matrix $Q$ such that $Qx = y$. Can there be such a matrix if $\|x\|_2 \neq \|y\|_2$?
:::
