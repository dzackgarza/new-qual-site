::: {.problem}
Given two vectors $x$ and $y$ in $\RR^n$ with $\norm{x}_2 = \norm{y}_2$, construct an orthogonal matrix $Q$ such that $Qx = y$. Can there be such a matrix if $\norm{x}_2 \neq \norm{y}_2$?
:::
