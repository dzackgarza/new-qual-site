::: {.problem}
Suppose $f : [-1, 1] \to \mathbb{C}$ is a continuous complex-valued function, and for all non-negative integers $n$

$$
\int_{-1}^{1} x^n f(x) \, dx = 0.
$$

Prove that $f = 0$.
:::
