::: {.problem}
A topological space $X$ has the $T_{2\frac12}$ property if for each pair $a,b$ of distinct points of $X$, there are open sets $U,V$ of $X$ such that $a\in U$, $b\in V$, and the closures satisfy $\operatorname{Cl}_X(U)\cap\operatorname{Cl}_X(V)=\varnothing$.
Show that if $A$ is a subspace of a $T_{2\frac12}$ space, then $A$ is also $T_{2\frac12}$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $a,b\in A$ be distinct.

<1>1. There are open sets $U,V\subseteq X$ such that
\[
a\in U,
\qquad
b\in V,
\qquad
\operatorname{Cl}_X(U)\cap\operatorname{Cl}_X(V)=\varnothing.
\]
::: {.proof}
The points $a$ and $b$ are also distinct points of $X$.
Apply the $T_{2\frac12}$ property of $X$.
:::

<1>2. For every subset $S\subseteq A$,
\[
\operatorname{Cl}_A(S)=A\cap\operatorname{Cl}_X(S).
\]
::: {.proof}
Let $x\in A$.
By the definition of closure in the subspace topology,
\[
x\in\operatorname{Cl}_A(S)
\]
if and only if every open neighborhood of $x$ in $A$ meets $S$.
Every such neighborhood has the form $A\cap W$ for some open neighborhood $W$ of $x$ in $X$.
Since $S\subseteq A$,
\[
(A\cap W)\cap S=W\cap S.
\]
Thus every open neighborhood of $x$ in $A$ meets $S$ if and only if every open neighborhood of $x$ in $X$ meets $S$, which is equivalent to
\[
x\in\operatorname{Cl}_X(S).
\]
Because $x\in A$ throughout, this proves the formula.
:::

<1>3. Put
\[
U_A=U\cap A,
\qquad
V_A=V\cap A.
\]
Then $U_A,V_A$ are open in $A$, contain $a,b$ respectively, and satisfy
\[
\operatorname{Cl}_A(U_A)
\subseteq
A\cap\operatorname{Cl}_X(U),
\qquad
\operatorname{Cl}_A(V_A)
\subseteq
A\cap\operatorname{Cl}_X(V).
\]
::: {.proof}
The two sets are open in the subspace topology and contain the required points by <1>1.
By <1>2,
\[
\operatorname{Cl}_A(U_A)=A\cap\operatorname{Cl}_X(U_A).
\]
Since $U_A\subseteq U$, monotonicity of closure gives
\[
\operatorname{Cl}_X(U_A)\subseteq\operatorname{Cl}_X(U),
\]
which proves the first inclusion.
The second is identical.
:::

<1>4. The closures of $U_A$ and $V_A$ in $A$ are disjoint.
::: {.proof}
Using <1>3 and then <1>1,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Cl}_A(U_A)\cap\operatorname{Cl}_A(V_A)
&\subseteq
A\cap\operatorname{Cl}_X(U)\cap\operatorname{Cl}_X(V)\\
&=\varnothing.
\end{aligned}
\]
:::

<1>5. The subspace $A$ is $T_{2\frac12}$.
::: {.proof}
For arbitrary distinct $a,b\in A$, <1>3--<1>4 provide open neighborhoods in $A$ whose closures in $A$ are disjoint.
This is exactly the defining $T_{2\frac12}$ property.
:::
:::
