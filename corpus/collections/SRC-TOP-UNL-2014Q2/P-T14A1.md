::: {.problem}
A topological space $X$ has the $T_{2\frac12}$ property if for each pair $a,b$ of distinct points of $X$, there are open sets $U,V$ of $X$ such that $a\in U$, $b\in V$, and the closures satisfy $\operatorname{Cl}_X(U)\cap\operatorname{Cl}_X(V)=\varnothing$.
Show that if $A$ is a subspace of a $T_{2\frac12}$ space, then $A$ is also $T_{2\frac12}$.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. Distinct points in the subspace:
<2>1. Let $X$ be a $T_{2\frac{1}{2}}$ space and let $A \subseteq X$ be endowed with the subspace topology.
Let $a, b \in A$ be two distinct points ($a \neq b$).
Proof: setup.
<2>2. Since $a, b \in X$ with $a \neq b$ and $X$ is $T_{2\frac{1}{2}}$, there exist open sets $U, V \subseteq X$ such that:
\[
a \in U, \qquad b \in V, \qquad \text{and} \qquad \operatorname{Cl}_X(U) \cap \operatorname{Cl}_X(V) = \emptyset.
\]
Proof: definition of $T_{2\frac{1}{2}}$ space.

<1>2. Construction and properties of open neighborhoods in $A$:
<2>1. Define the sets:
\[
U_A = U \cap A, \qquad V_A = V \cap A.
\]
By definition of the subspace topology, $U_A$ and $V_A$ are open in $A$.
Furthermore, $a \in U_A$ and $b \in V_A$.
Proof: definition of subspace topology.
<2>2. The closure in the subspace topology is given by $\operatorname{Cl}_A(S) = \operatorname{Cl}_X(S) \cap A$ for any $S \subseteq A$.
Because $U_A \subseteq U$, monotonicity of closure in $X$ gives:
\[
\operatorname{Cl}_A(U_A) = \operatorname{Cl}_X(U_A) \cap A \subseteq \operatorname{Cl}_X(U) \cap A.
\]
Proof: closure formula in subspace topology.
<2>3. Similarly for $V_A$:
\[
\operatorname{Cl}_A(V_A) = \operatorname{Cl}_X(V_A) \cap A \subseteq \operatorname{Cl}_X(V) \cap A.
\]
Proof: monotonicity of closure.

<1>3. Disjointness of subspace closures:
<2>1. Taking the intersection of the closures in $A$:
\[
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Cl}_A(U_A) \cap \operatorname{Cl}_A(V_A) &\subseteq (\operatorname{Cl}_X(U) \cap A) \cap (\operatorname{Cl}_X(V) \cap A) \\
&= (\operatorname{Cl}_X(U) \cap \operatorname{Cl}_X(V)) \cap A \\
&= \emptyset \cap A = \emptyset.
\end{aligned}
\]
Thus $\operatorname{Cl}_A(U_A) \cap \operatorname{Cl}_A(V_A) = \emptyset$.
Proof: intersection of subsets.

<1>4. Conclusion:
For every pair of distinct points $a, b \in A$, there exist open sets $U_A, V_A \subseteq A$ containing $a$ and $b$ with disjoint closures in $A$.
Thus $A$ is $T_{2\frac{1}{2}}$. Q.E.D.
Proof: <1>1 through <1>3.
:::
