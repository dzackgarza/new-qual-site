:::{.problem}
Suppose $u(x,t)$ is continuous with continuous second partial derivatives, and $u$ together with its first partial derivatives is periodic in $x$ with period $1$. Assume
\[
u_{xx}=u_{tt}.
\]
Prove that
\[
E(t)=\frac12\int_0^1\left(u_t^2+u_x^2\right)\,dx
\]
is independent of $t$.
:::
