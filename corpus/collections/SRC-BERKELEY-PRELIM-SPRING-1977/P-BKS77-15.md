:::{.problem}
Let $f$ be a nonconstant meromorphic function. A complex number $w$ is a period if
\[
f(z+w)=f(z)
\]
for all $z$ where the expression is defined.

1. Show that if $w_1,w_2$ are periods, then so is $n_1w_1+n_2w_2$ for all integers $n_1,n_2$.
2. Show that every bounded region of the complex plane contains at most finitely many periods of $f$.
:::
