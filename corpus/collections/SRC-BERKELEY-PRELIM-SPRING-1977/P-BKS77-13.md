:::{.problem}
Let $A(\theta)$ solve
\[
\frac{dA}{d\theta}=BA(\theta),
\qquad
A(0)=I,
\]
where $B$ is a constant square matrix.

1. Find a necessary and sufficient condition on $B$ for $A(\theta)$ to be orthogonal for every $\theta$.
2. Find $A(\theta)$ when
   \[
   B=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix},
   \]
   and give a geometric interpretation.
:::
