:::{.problem}
Let $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ be differentiable and suppose
\[
f'(x)>f(x)
\]
for every $x\in\mathbb R$. If $f(x_0)=0$, prove that
\[
f(x)>0
\]
for every $x>x_0$.
:::
