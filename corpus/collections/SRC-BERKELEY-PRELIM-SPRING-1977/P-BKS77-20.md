::: {.problem}
Let $h:[0,1)\to\mathbb R$ be uniformly continuous.
Prove that there exists a unique continuous function $g:[0,1]\to\mathbb R$ such that
\[
g(x)=h(x)
\]
for every $x\in[0,1)$.
:::
