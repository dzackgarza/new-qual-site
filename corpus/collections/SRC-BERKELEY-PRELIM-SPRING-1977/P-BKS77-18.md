::: {.problem}
1. In $\mathbb R[x]$, consider the polynomials satisfying
   \[
   f(2)=f'(2)=f''(2)=0.
   \]
   Prove that they form an ideal and find its monic generator.
2. Do the polynomials satisfying
   \[
   f(2)=0,
   \qquad
   f'(3)=0
   \]
   form an ideal?
:::
