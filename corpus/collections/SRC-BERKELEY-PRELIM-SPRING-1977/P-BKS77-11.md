:::{.problem}
Let $(a_n)$ be defined by
\[
1-x^2+x^4-x^6+\cdots
=
\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}a_n(x-3)^n,
\qquad 0<x<1.
\]
Find
\[
\lim_{n\to\infty}|a_n|^{1/n}.
\]
:::
