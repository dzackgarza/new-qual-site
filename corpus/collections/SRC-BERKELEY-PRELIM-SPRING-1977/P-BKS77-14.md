::: {.problem}
A square matrix is nilpotent if $A^k=0$ for some positive integer $k$.

1. If $A$ and $B$ are nilpotent, must $A+B$ be nilpotent?
2. Prove that if $A$ and $B$ are nilpotent and commute, then $A+B$ is nilpotent.
3. Prove that if $A$ is nilpotent, then both $I+A$ and $I-A$ are invertible.
:::
