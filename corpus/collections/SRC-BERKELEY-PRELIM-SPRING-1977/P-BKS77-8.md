:::{.problem}
Find as long a list as possible of pairwise nonsimilar real matrices such that every matrix in the list has characteristic polynomial
\[
(x-1)^5(x+1)
\]
and minimal polynomial
\[
(x-1)^2(x+1).
\]
:::
