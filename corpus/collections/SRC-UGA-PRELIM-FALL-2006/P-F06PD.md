::: problem
Consider the vector space of polynomials over $\mathbb{Q}$ spanned by $p_1(x) = x^2 + x + 1$, $p_2(x) = x^2 + 2x$, $p_3(x) = x^2 + 2$, $p_4(x) = x - 1$.
Find the dimension of this vector space.
:::
