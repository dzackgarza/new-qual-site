::: {.problem}
For continuous functions $f,g:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$, define the relation $\sim$ by $f\sim g$ iff
\[
\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{f(x)}{g(x)}=1.
\]
Suppose that $f\sim g$. Which of these does NOT necessarily follow?

(a) $f^2\sim g^2$
(b) $\sqrt f\sim\sqrt g$
(c) $e^f\sim e^g$
(d) $f+g\sim 2g$
(e) $g\sim f$
:::
