::: {.problem}
Let $f, g : \mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{C}$ be entire functions. Write $\Re$ for the real part of a complex number. Assume $\Re(f(z)) \geq \Re(g(z))$ for all $z$ such that $|z| = 1$. Show $\Re(f(z)) \geq \Re(g(z))$ for all $z$ such that $|z| < 1$.
:::
