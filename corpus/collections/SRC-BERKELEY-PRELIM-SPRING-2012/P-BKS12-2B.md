::: {.problem}
Let G be the group $( Z / 2012 Z ) ^ { * }$ (this is the group whose elements are classes a (mod 2012) with gcd $( a , 2012 ) = 1$ , and whose group operation is multiplication modulo 2012).

Determine the structure of G as an abstract abelian group. When doing so, break it down into as many (nontrivial) pieces as possible.

Note that 2012 has prime factorization $2 ^ { 2 }$ · 503, and that 502 has prime factorization $2 \cdot 251$
:::
