::: {.problem}
Suppose that X is a compact metric space. If Y is another metric space (possibly noncompact), let $p : X \times Y  Y$ be the map $p ( x , y ) = y$ . Show that if Z is a closed subset of $X \times Y$ then $p ( Z )$ is closed in Y .
:::
