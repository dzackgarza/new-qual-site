::: problem
Let $f_n : \mathbb{D} \to \mathbb{C}$ be holomorphic functions such that
\[
\int_{\mathbb{D}} |f_n|\, dx\, dy \le 1, \qquad \forall n \ge 1.
\]
Show that there exists a subsequence of $\{f_n\}$ that converges locally uniformly on $\mathbb{D}$.
:::
