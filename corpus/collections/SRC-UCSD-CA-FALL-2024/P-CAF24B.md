::: problem
Let $f : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}$ be an entire function.
Assume that for any $a \in \mathbb{R}$, at least one coefficient in the Taylor expansion of $f$ around $a$ is a rational number.
Prove that $f$ is a polynomial.
:::
