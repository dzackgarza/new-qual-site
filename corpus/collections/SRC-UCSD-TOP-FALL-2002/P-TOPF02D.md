::: problem
Let $X$ be a finite-dimensional cell complex with only even-dimensional cells.
Prove that $H_*(X; \mathbb{Z})$ is torsion-free.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. In the cellular chain complex of $X$, every odd chain group vanishes:
$$
C_{2k+1}(X;\mathbb Z)=0.
$$
::: {.proof}
By hypothesis there are no odd-dimensional cells, and cellular chains are the free abelian groups generated by cells of the corresponding dimension.
:::

<1>2. Every cellular differential is zero.
::: {.proof}
A differential $\partial_{2k}$ has target $C_{2k-1}=0$, while a differential $\partial_{2k+1}$ has source $C_{2k+1}=0$.
:::

<1>3. Hence
$$
H_{2k}(X;\mathbb Z)\cong C_{2k}(X;\mathbb Z),
\qquad
H_{2k+1}(X;\mathbb Z)=0.
$$
::: {.proof}
With all differentials zero, homology equals the chain group in each degree.
:::

<1>4. Therefore $H_*(X;\mathbb Z)$ is torsion-free.
::: {.proof}
Each nonzero cellular chain group is free abelian, hence torsion-free, and the odd homology groups vanish.
:::
:::
