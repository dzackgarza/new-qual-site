::: {.problem}
Let G be a non-empty set with a binary operation which is associative and fully cancellative (i.e.$x z = y z \implies x = y$and$z x = z y \implies x = y$for all$x , y , z \in G )$. Further assume that$\{ x ^ { n } : n = 1 , 2 , 3 , \ldots \}$is finite for all$x \in G$. Show that G is a group.
:::
