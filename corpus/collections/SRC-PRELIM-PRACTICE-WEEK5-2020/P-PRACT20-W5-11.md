::: {.problem}
Let G be a group and$H \subset G$be a subgroup. H is said to be a normal subgroup of G if$g h g ^ { - 1 } \in H$whenever$h \in H$and$g \in G$. If$\phi : G \to G ^ { \prime }$is a homomorphism (where$G ^ { \prime }$is some other arbitrary group), show that ker φ is a normal subgroup of G.
:::
