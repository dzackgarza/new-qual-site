::: {.problem}
Suppose that $f(x)$, $-\infty<x<\infty$, is a continuous real valued function, that $f'(x)$ exists for $x\neq0$, and that $\lim_{x\to0}f'(x)$ exists.
Prove that $f'(0)$ exists.
:::
