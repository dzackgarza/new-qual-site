::: {.problem}
Let $X$ be a set and $V$ a real vector space of real valued functions on $X$ of dimension $n$, $0<n<\infty$. Prove that there are $n$ points $x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_n$ in $X$ such that the map $f\mapsto(f(x_1),\ldots,f(x_n))$ of $V$ to $\RR^n$ is an isomorphism.
:::
