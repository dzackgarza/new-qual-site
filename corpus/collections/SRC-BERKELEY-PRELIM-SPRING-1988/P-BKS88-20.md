::: {.problem}
Does there exist a continuous real valued function $f(x)$, $0\leq x\leq1$, such that

$$
\int_0^1 xf(x)\,dx=1\quad\text{and}\quad\int_0^1 x^nf(x)\,dx=0
$$

for $n=0,2,3,4,\ldots$? Give an example or a proof that no such $f$ exists.
:::
