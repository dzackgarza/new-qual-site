::: problem
Let $S _ { 9 }$ denote the group of permutations of nine objects and let $A _ { 9 }$ be the subgroup consisting of all even permutations.
Denote by $1 \in S _ { 9 }$ the identity permutation.
Determine the minimum of all positive integers m such that every $\sigma \in S _ { 9 }$ satisfies $\sigma ^ { m } = 1$ . Determine also the minimum of all positive integers m such that every $\sigma \in A _ { 9 }$ satisfies $\sigma ^ { m } = 1$
:::
