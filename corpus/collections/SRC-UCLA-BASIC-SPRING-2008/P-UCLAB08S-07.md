::: {.problem}
Let $a\colon\mathbb R\to[0,\infty)$ satisfy the following bounded-finite-sum condition: there exists $M<\infty$ such that
\[
\sum_{x\in F}a(x)\le M
\]
for every finite set $F\subset\mathbb R$.
Prove that
\[
\{x\in\mathbb R:a(x)>0\}
\]
is countable.
:::
