::: {.problem}
Let $(f_n)$ be a sequence of continuous nonnegative functions on $[0,1]$ such that $f_n(x)\to0$ for every $x\in[0,1]$.
Prove or give a counterexample to the assertion
\[
\lim_{n\to\infty}\int_0^1 f_n(x)\,dx=0.
\]
:::
