::: {.problem}
Let $V$ be an $n$-dimensional vector space over $\mathbb Q$, and let $T\colon V\to V$ be linear with $T^2=T$. Prove that every $v\in V$ can be written uniquely as
\[
v=v_1+v_2
\]
with $T(v_1)=v_1$ and $T(v_2)=0$.
:::
