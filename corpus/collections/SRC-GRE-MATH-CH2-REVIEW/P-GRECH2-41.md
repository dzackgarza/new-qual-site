::: {.problem}
Which of the following infinite series converge?

I. $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{\cos^4(\arctan n)}{n\sqrt[4]{n}}$

II. $\sum_{n=2}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n \log n}$

III. $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{(n+1)^3}{5(n+2)(n+3)(n+4)}$

(A) I only
(B) I and II only
(C) II only
(D) I and III only
(E) II and III only
:::
