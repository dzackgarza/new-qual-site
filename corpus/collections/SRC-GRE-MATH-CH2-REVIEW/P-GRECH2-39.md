::: {.problem}
Find the positive value of $a$ that satisfies the equation:

\[
\int_{0}^{a} \frac{dx}{\sqrt{a^2 - x^2}} = \int_{0}^{a} \frac{x \, dx}{\sqrt{a^2 - x^2}}
\]

(A) $\frac{2\sqrt{2}}{\pi}$
(B) $1$
(C) $\frac{\pi}{2\sqrt{2}}$
(D) $\sqrt{2}$
(E) $\frac{\pi}{2}$
:::
