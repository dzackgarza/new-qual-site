::: {.problem}
Let $f(x) = x^k e^{-x}$, where $k$ is a positive constant. For $x > 0$, what is the maximum value attained by $f$?

(A) $\left(\frac{e}{k}\right)^k$
(B) $\sqrt[k]{\frac{e}{k^k}}$
(C) $\frac{(\log k)^k}{k}$
(D) $\left(\frac{e}{\log k}\right)^k$
(E) $\left(\frac{k}{e}\right)^k$
:::
