::: {.problem}
Evaluate the following limit:

\[
\lim_{n \to \infty} \sum_{k=1}^{n} \left[ \frac{k}{n^2} - \frac{k^2}{n^3} \right]
\]

(A) $\frac{2}{3}$
(B) $\frac{1}{2}$
(C) $\frac{1}{3}$
(D) $\frac{1}{6}$
(E) $\frac{1}{12}$
:::
