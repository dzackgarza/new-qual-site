::: {.problem}
There is exactly one value of the constant $k$ such that

$$\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{(e^{x^2} - x^2 - 1)(\cos x - 1)}{x^k}$$

is finite and nonzero. What is this value of $k$, and what is the limit, $L$?

(A) $k = 4$, $L = -\frac{1}{2}$
(B) $k = 6$, $L = \frac{1}{4}$
(C) $k = 4$, $L = 1$
(D) $k = 6$, $L = -\frac{1}{4}$
(E) $k = 4$, $L = \frac{1}{2}$
:::
