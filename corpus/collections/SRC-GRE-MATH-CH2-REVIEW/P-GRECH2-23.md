::: {.problem}
The radius of a circle is decreasing at a rate of 0.5 cm per second. At what rate, in cm$^2$/sec, is the circle's area decreasing when the radius is 4 cm?

(A) $4\pi$
(B) $2\pi$
(C) $\pi$
(D) $\frac{1}{2}\pi$
(E) $\frac{1}{4}\pi$
:::
