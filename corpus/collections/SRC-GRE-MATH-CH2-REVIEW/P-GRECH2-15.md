::: {.problem}

\[
f(x) = \frac{e^x \arccos x}{\cos x}
\]

then the slope of the line tangent to the graph of $f$ at its $y$-intercept is

(A) $-\frac{\pi}{2}$
(B) $-1$
(C) $\frac{\pi}{2} - 1$
(D) $1$
(E) $\frac{\pi}{2} + 1$
