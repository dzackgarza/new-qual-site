::: {.problem}
Let $y = \frac{1}{\sqrt{x^3 + 1}}$. If $x$ increases from 2 to 2.09, which of the following most closely approximates the change in $y$?

(A) $0.08$
(B) $0.04$
(C) $-0.02$
(D) $-0.06$
(E) $-0.09$
:::
