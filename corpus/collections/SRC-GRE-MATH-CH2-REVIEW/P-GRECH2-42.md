::: {.problem}
Find the smallest value of b that makes the following statement true:

If $0 \le a < b$, then the series $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{(n!)^2 a^n}{(2n)!}$ converges.

(A) $1$
(B) $2 \log 2$
(C) $2$
(D) $\sqrt{2}$
(E) $4$
