::: {.problem}
Let $[x]$ denote the greatest integer $\le x$. If $n$ is a positive integer, then

\[
\lim_{x \to -n^-} (|x| - [x]) - \lim_{x \to n^-} (|x| - [x]) = ?
\]

(A) $-2$
(B) $0$
(C) $2$
(D) $2n - 1$
(E) $2n$
:::
