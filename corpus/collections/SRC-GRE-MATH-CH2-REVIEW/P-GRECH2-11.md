::: {.problem}
If $f(x)$ is a function that's differentiable everywhere, what is the value of this limit?

\[
\lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(x + 3h^2) - f(x - h^2)}{2h^2}
\]

(A) $4f'(x)$
(B) $2f'(x)$
(C) $f'(x)$
(D) $\frac{1}{2}f'(x)$
(E) The limit does not exist.
:::
