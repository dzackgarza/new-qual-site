::: {.problem}
The curve whose equation is

\[
2x^2 + 3x - 2xy - y = 6
\]

has two asymptotes. Identify these lines.

(A) $x = -1$ and $y = -2$
(B) $x = -2$ and $y = 1$
(C) $x = -\frac{1}{2}$ and $y = x$
(D) $x = -\frac{1}{2}$ and $y = x + 1$
(E) $x = \frac{1}{2}$ and $y = 1 - x$
