::: {.problem}
Calculate the length of the portion of the hypocycloid $x^{2/3} + y^{2/3} = 1$ in the first quadrant from the point $\left(\frac{1}{8}, \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{8}\right)$, to the point $(1, 0)$.

(A) $\frac{9}{8}$
(B) $\frac{3\sqrt{2}}{4}$
(C) $1$
(D) $\frac{5\sqrt{2}}{8}$
(E) $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
:::
