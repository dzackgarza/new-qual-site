::: {.problem}
Let $n$ be a number for which the improper integral

\[
\int_{e}^{\infty} \frac{dx}{x(\log x)^n}
\]

converges. Determine the value of the integral.

(A) $\frac{1}{n+1}$
(B) $\frac{1}{n}$
(C) $\frac{1}{n-1}$
(D) $\frac{\log n}{n+1}$
(E) $\frac{\log n}{n-1}$
:::
