::: {.problem}
The following function is defined for all positive $x$:

\[
f(x) = \int_{x}^{2x} \frac{\sin t}{t} dt
\]

At what value of $x$ on the interval $(0, \frac{3\pi}{2})$ does this function attain a local maximum?

(A) $\frac{\pi}{6}$
(B) $\frac{\pi}{3}$
(C) $\frac{\pi}{2}$
(D) $\pi$
(E) $\frac{2\pi}{3}$
:::
