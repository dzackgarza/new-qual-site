::: {.problem}
Evaluate the following limit:

\[
\lim_{x \to 0} \left[ \frac{1}{x^2} \int_0^x \frac{t + t^2}{1 + \sin t} dt \right]
\]

(A) $\frac{1}{2\pi}$
(B) $\frac{1}{\pi}$
(C) $\frac{1}{2}$
(D) $1$
(E) $\frac{\pi}{2}$
