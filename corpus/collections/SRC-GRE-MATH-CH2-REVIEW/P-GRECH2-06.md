::: {.problem}
If the function

\[
f(x) = \begin{cases} \frac{x^2 - 6x + 8}{x^3 - 2x^2 + 2x - 4} & \text{if } x \neq 2 \\ k & \text{if } x = 2 \end{cases}
\]

is continuous everywhere, what is the value of $k$?

(A) $1$
(B) $\frac{1}{2}$
(C) $\frac{1}{8}$
(D) $-\frac{1}{3}$
(E) $-1$
:::
