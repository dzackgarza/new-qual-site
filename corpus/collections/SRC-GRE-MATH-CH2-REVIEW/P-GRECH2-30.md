::: {.problem}
Simplify the following:

\[
\exp \int_{3}^{5} \frac{dx}{x^2 - 3x + 2}
\]

[Note: Recall that $\exp x$ is a standard, alternate notation for $e^x$.]

(A) $\frac{3}{8}$
(B) $\frac{2}{3}$
(C) $\frac{4}{3}$
(D) $\frac{3}{2}$
(E) $\frac{5}{3}$
:::
