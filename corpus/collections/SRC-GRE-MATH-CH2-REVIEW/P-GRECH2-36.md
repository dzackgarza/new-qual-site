::: {.problem}
What positive value of $a$ satisfies the following equation?

\[
\int_{e}^{a^e} \frac{dx}{x \int_{a}^{ax} \frac{dy}{y}} = 1
\]

(A) $\frac{1}{e}$
(B) $\sqrt[4]{e}$
(C) $\sqrt{e}$
(D) $e$
(E) $e^2$
:::
