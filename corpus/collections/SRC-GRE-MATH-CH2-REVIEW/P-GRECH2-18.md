::: {.problem}
If $n$ is a positive integer, what is the value of the $n^{\text{th}}$ derivative of $f(x) = \frac{1}{1 - 2x}$ at $x = -\frac{1}{2}$?

(A) $\frac{1}{2}(n^n)$
(B) $\frac{1}{2}(n!)$
(C) $\frac{1}{2}n$
(D) $n$
(E) $\frac{n^n}{n!}$
:::
