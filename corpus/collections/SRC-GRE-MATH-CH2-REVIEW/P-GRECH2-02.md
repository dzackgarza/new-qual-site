::: {.problem}
Let $(x_n)$ be the sequence with $x_1 = 2$ and $x_n = \sqrt{5x_{n-1} + 6}$ for every integer $n \ge 2$. Given that this sequence converges, what is its limit?

(A) $4$
(B) $6$
(C) $8$
(D) $10$
(E) $16$
:::
