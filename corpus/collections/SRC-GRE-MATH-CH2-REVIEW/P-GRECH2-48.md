::: {.problem}
If $k_i$ ($i = 0, 1, 2, 3, 4$) are constants such that $x^4 = k_0 + k_1(x+1) + k_2(x+1)^2 + k_3(x+1)^3 + k_4(x+1)^4$ is an identity in $x$, what is the value of $k_3$?

(A) $-4$
(B) $-3$
(C) $-2$
(D) $3$
(E) $4$
:::
