::: {.problem}
Suppose that $X$ and $Y$ are connected, nonempty topological spaces.
Show that $X\times Y$ is also connected.
:::

::: {.solution}
Fix $y_0\in Y$, and for each $x\in X$ put $C_x=(X\times\{y_0\})\cup(\{x\}\times Y)$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #cx-connected}
Each $C_x$ is connected.

::: pf-proof
$X\times\{y_0\}\cong X$ and $\{x\}\times Y\cong Y$ are connected and meet at $(x,y_0)$.
:::

:::

::: pf-qed
Every $(x,y)\in X\times Y$ lies in $\{x\}\times Y\subseteq C_x$, so $X\times Y=\bigcup_{x\in X}C_x$.
Fix $x_1\in X$; every $C_x$ contains $(x_1,y_0)$.
A union of connected subspaces with a common point is connected, so by step [](#cx-connected){.pf-ref}, $X\times Y$ is connected.
:::
