::: {.problem}
Suppose that $X$ and $Y$ are connected, nonempty topological spaces.
Show that $X\times Y$ is also connected.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. Fix a point $y_0\in Y$ and set
$$
C=(X\times\{y_0\})\cup(\{x\}\times Y)
$$
for each $x\in X$.
::: {.proof}
We use connected slices to build the whole product from connected subsets with common intersection.
:::

<1>2. For each $x\in X$, the set
$$
C_x=(X\times\{y_0\})\cup(\{x\}\times Y)
$$
is connected.
::: {.proof}
The slice $X\times\{y_0\}$ is homeomorphic to the connected space $X$, and $\{x\}\times Y$ is homeomorphic to the connected space $Y$. They intersect at $(x,y_0)$, so their union is connected.
:::

<1>3. The family $\{C_x:x\in X\}$ has a common connected subset.
::: {.proof}
Every $C_x$ contains the entire slice $X\times\{y_0\}$.
:::

<1>4. Their union is all of $X\times Y$.
::: {.proof}
If $(x,y)\in X\times Y$, then $(x,y)\in\{x\}\times Y\subseteq C_x$.
:::

<1>5. Therefore $X\times Y$ is connected.
::: {.proof}
A union of connected subspaces having a common point, indeed the common connected slice from <1>3, is connected. Apply this to <1>2--<1>4.
:::
:::
