::: {.problem}
Let $A$ be the annulus $\{re^{i\theta}\in\CC:1\le r\le 2\}$, and let $B$ be the quotient space obtained from $A$ by identifying each point $e^{i\theta}$ on the circle $r=1$ with the point $2e^{i(\theta+\pi)}$ on the circle $r=2$.
Determine $\pi_1(B)$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Identify $A$ with $S^1\times[0,1]$ by $(z,t)\mapsto(1+t)z$.
The boundary relation becomes $(z,0)\sim(-z,1)$, so $B$ is the mapping torus of the rotation $z\mapsto-z$ of $S^1$.

<1>1. $\Phi(z,t)=(e^{\pi it}z,\,e^{2\pi it})$ induces a continuous bijection $\bar\Phi\colon B\to S^1\times S^1$.

::: {.proof}
$\Phi(z,0)=(z,1)$ and $\Phi(-z,1)=(e^{\pi i}(-z),1)=(z,1)$, so $\Phi$ is constant on classes and induces a continuous $\bar\Phi$.
Given $(w,e^{2\pi it})$ with $t\in[0,1)$, the class of $(e^{-\pi it}w,t)$ is its unique preimage: $e^{2\pi it}$ determines $t\in(0,1)$ uniquely, and for $e^{2\pi it}=1$ the two representatives $(w,0)$ and $(-w,1)$ form one class.
:::

<1>2. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
$B$ is compact as a quotient of $S^1\times[0,1]$, and $S^1\times S^1$ is Hausdorff, so the continuous bijection $\bar\Phi$ of step <1>1 is a homeomorphism.
Hence $\pi_1(B)\cong\pi_1(S^1)\times\pi_1(S^1)\cong\boxed{\ZZ^2}$.
:::
:::
