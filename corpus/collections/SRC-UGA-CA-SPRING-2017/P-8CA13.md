::: problem
Describe all entire functions $f(z)$ such that
\[
|f(z)|\le|\sin(z)|
\]
for all $z$.
:::

::: solution
Since $\sin(n\pi)=0$, the inequality forces
\[
f(n\pi)=0\qquad(n\in\mathbb Z).
\]
All zeros of $\sin z$ are simple, so
\[
g(z)=\frac{f(z)}{\sin z}
\]
has removable singularities at every $n\pi$ and therefore extends to an entire function. The given inequality becomes
\[
|g(z)|\le1
\]
away from the zeros of $\sin z$, hence everywhere by continuity. Liouville's theorem gives $g\equiv c$ for some constant with $|c|\le1$.

Therefore exactly the functions
\[
\boxed{f(z)=c\sin z,\qquad |c|\le1}
\]
satisfy the inequality.
:::
