::: problem
Exhibit a sequence of explicit conformal mappings, the composite of which gives a one-to-one conformal map from the region
\[
R=\{z:\ |z|<2\text{ and }|z-1|>1\}
\]
onto the upper half-plane.
:::

::: solution
The two boundary circles of $R$ are tangent at $z=2$. Set
\[
w_1=\frac1{z-2}.
\]
For $|z|=2$ one computes
\[
|2+w_1^{-1}|=2\iff \operatorname{Re}w_1=-\frac14,
\]
while for $|z-1|=1$,
\[
|1+w_1^{-1}|=1\iff \operatorname{Re}w_1=-\frac12.
\]
Thus $w_1$ maps $R$ biholomorphically onto the vertical strip
\[
-\frac12<\operatorname{Re}w_1<-\frac14.
\]
Now put
\[
w_2=4\pi i\left(w_1+\frac12\right).
\]
Then $0<\operatorname{Im}w_2<\pi$. Finally
\[
w_3=e^{w_2}
\]
maps this horizontal strip biholomorphically onto the upper half-plane. Hence the desired map is
\[
\boxed{z\longmapsto \exp\!\left(4\pi i\left(\frac1{z-2}+\frac12\right)\right)}.
\]
:::
