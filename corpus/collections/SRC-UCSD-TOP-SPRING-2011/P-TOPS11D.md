::: problem
Consider the set $L$ of $3$-manifolds which can be formed by gluing together the boundaries of two solid tori $S^1 \times B^2$ using a homeomorphism.
Consider the function $d : L \to \mathbb{N}$ given by the total dimension of the mod-$2$ homology: $d(M) = \sum_i \dim H_i(M; \mathbb{Z}_2)$.
What is the maximal value of $d$ on $L$?
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. Every manifold in $L$ is a closed orientable $3$-manifold obtained from a genus-one Heegaard splitting.
::: {.proof}
It is formed by gluing two oriented solid tori along their boundaries by a homeomorphism, reversing boundary orientation as needed to obtain the closed oriented manifold.
:::

<1>2. Its first homology is cyclic (possibly infinite), so
$$
\dim_{\mathbb F_2}H_1(M;\mathbb F_2)\le1.
$$
::: {.proof}
The Mayer--Vietoris sequence for two solid tori shows that $H_1(M;\mathbb Z)$ is generated by one class; equivalently these manifolds are lens spaces, $S^1\times S^2$, or $S^3$. Tensoring and applying the mod-$2$ universal coefficient theorem gives mod-$2$ first Betti number at most one.
:::

<1>3. Mod-$2$ Poincaré duality gives
$$
b_0=b_3=1,\qquad b_2=b_1.
$$
::: {.proof}
Every closed connected $3$-manifold is mod-$2$ orientable, so Poincaré duality over $\mathbb F_2$ applies.
:::

<1>4. Hence
$$
d(M)=\sum_i b_i=2+2b_1\le4.
$$
::: {.proof}
Substitute the identities from <1>3 and the bound $b_1\le1$ from <1>2.
:::

<1>5. The bound is attained, for example by $S^1\times S^2$, which admits a genus-one Heegaard splitting and has $b_1=b_2=1$ over $\mathbb F_2$.
::: {.proof}
Its mod-$2$ homology has dimension one in each degree $0,1,2,3$.
:::

<1>6. Therefore
$$
\boxed{\max_{M\in L}d(M)=4.}
$$
::: {.proof}
Combine <1>4 and <1>5.
:::
:::
