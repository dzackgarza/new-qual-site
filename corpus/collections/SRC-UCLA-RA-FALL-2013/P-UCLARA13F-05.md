::: {.problem}
Let $(f_n)$ be holomorphic functions on $\mathbb D$ satisfying
\[
\int_{\mathbb D}|f_n(z)|\,d\lambda(z)\le1
\]
for every $n$. Show that some subsequence converges uniformly on every compact subset of $\mathbb D$.
:::
