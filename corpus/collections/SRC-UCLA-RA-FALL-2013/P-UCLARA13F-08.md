::: {.problem}
Compute the following limits.

(a) For $n\in\mathbb N$,
\[
\lim_{k\to\infty}\int_0^k x^n\left(1-\frac{x}{k}\right)^k\,dx.
\]

(b)
\[
\lim_{k\to\infty}\int_0^\infty\left(1+\frac{x}{k}\right)^{-k}\cos(x/k)\,dx.
\]
:::
