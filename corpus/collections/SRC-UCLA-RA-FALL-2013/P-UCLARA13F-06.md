::: {.problem}
Let $U\subset\mathbb C$ be bounded and open with $0\in U$, and let $f:U\to\mathbb C$ be holomorphic with $f(U)\subset U$ and $f(0)=0$. Show that
\[
|f'(0)|\le1.
\]
Hint: consider the iterates $f^n=f\circ\cdots\circ f$.
:::
