::: {.problem}
Does there exist a holomorphic function $f:\mathbb D\to\mathbb C$ such that
\[
|f(z_n)|\longrightarrow\infty
\]
for every sequence $(z_n)\subset\mathbb D$ with $|z_n|\to1$? Justify your answer.
:::
