::: {.problem}
Let $S$ be a subspace of $C([0,1])$. Write
\[
\|f\|_\infty=\sup_{x\in[0,1]}|f(x)|,
\qquad
\|f\|_2=\left(\int_0^1|f(x)|^2\,dx\right)^{1/2}.
\]
Suppose there is $K\ge0$ such that
\[
\|f\|_\infty\le K\|f\|_2
\]
for every $f\in S$. Show that $S$ is finite-dimensional.
:::
