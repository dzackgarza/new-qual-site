::: {.problem}
Show that the set of functions $f\in L^2([0,1])$ such that $x\mapsto x^{-1/2}f(x)$ lies in $L^1([0,1])$ and
\[
\int_0^1 x^{-1/2}f(x)\,dx=0
\]
is dense in $L^2([0,1])$.
:::
