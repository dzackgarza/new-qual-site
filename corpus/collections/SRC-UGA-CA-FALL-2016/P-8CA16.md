::: {.problem}
Let $\Delta\subset\mathbb{C}$ denote the open unit disk.

(a) Prove that for each $\phi\in\operatorname{Aut}(\Delta)$ and each $z\in\Delta$,
\[
\frac{|\phi'(z)|}{1-|\phi(z)|^2}=\frac{1}{1-|z|^2}.
\]

(b) Let $f:\Delta\to\Delta$ be an analytic map having two different fixed points $z_1,z_2\in\Delta$.
Prove that $f(z)=z$ for all $z\in\Delta$.
:::

::: {.solution}
(a) Fix $z\in\Delta$. Let
\[
A_z(\zeta)=\frac{\zeta-z}{1-\overline z\,\zeta}
\]
and similarly let $A_{\phi(z)}$ send $\phi(z)$ to $0$. Then
\[
\psi=A_{\phi(z)}\circ\phi\circ A_z^{-1}
\]
is an automorphism of $\Delta$ fixing $0$. By Schwarz's lemma and its equality
case,
\[
\psi(\zeta)=e^{i\theta}\zeta,
\qquad |\psi'(0)|=1.
\]
Using the chain rule and
\[
|A_z'(z)|=\frac1{1-|z|^2},
\qquad
|(A_z^{-1})'(0)|=1-|z|^2,
\]
we obtain
\[
1=|\psi'(0)|
=\frac{|\phi'(z)|(1-|z|^2)}{1-|\phi(z)|^2}.
\]
Thus
\[
\boxed{\frac{|\phi'(z)|}{1-|\phi(z)|^2}
=\frac1{1-|z|^2}}.
\]

(b) Let $z_1\ne z_2$ be fixed points of $f$. Choose an automorphism
$A$ of $\Delta$ with $A(z_1)=0$, and set
\[
g=A\circ f\circ A^{-1}.
\]
Then $g:\Delta\to\Delta$ is holomorphic, fixes $0$, and fixes the nonzero
point $A(z_2)$. Schwarz's lemma gives $|g(\zeta)|\le|\zeta|$, and equality at
the nonzero fixed point forces
\[
g(\zeta)=e^{i\theta}\zeta.
\]
Since $A(z_2)$ is fixed and nonzero, $e^{i\theta}=1$. Hence $g$ is the identity,
and therefore so is $f$.
:::
