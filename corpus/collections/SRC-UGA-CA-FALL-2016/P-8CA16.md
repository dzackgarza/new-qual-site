::: {.problem}
Let $\Delta\subset\CC$ denote the open unit disk.

(a) Prove that for each $\phi\in\operatorname{Aut}(\Delta)$ and each $z\in\Delta$,
$$
\frac{\abs{\phi'(z)}}{1-\abs{\phi(z)}^2}=\frac{1}{1-\abs{z}^2}.
$$

(b) Let $f:\Delta\to\Delta$ be an analytic map having two different fixed points $z_1,z_2\in\Delta$.
Prove that $f(z)=z$ for all $z\in\Delta$.
:::

::: {.solution}

No retained solution set covers this exercise: the card's own Schwarz-lemma argument for (a) and the normalisation for (b) follow, with the conjugation, the chain rule and the equality case as separate steps.

Let $\Delta$ be the unit disc and $A_{w}(\zeta)=\dfrac{\zeta-w}{1-\overline w\zeta}$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #conjugate}
For $\phi\in\operatorname{Aut}(\Delta)$ and $z\in\Delta$, the map $\psi=A_{\phi(z)}\circ\phi\circ A_{z}^{-1}$ is an automorphism of $\Delta$ fixing $0$, hence a rotation: $\psi(\zeta)=e^{i\theta}\zeta$ and $\abs{\psi'(0)}=1$.

::: pf-proof
$\psi$ is a composite of biholomorphisms of $\Delta$, and $\psi(0)=A_{\phi(z)}\big(\phi(z)\big)=0$. Schwarz's lemma gives $\abs{\psi(\zeta)}\le\abs{\zeta}$, and applying it to $\psi^{-1}$ gives equality, so the equality case gives $\psi(\zeta)=e^{i\theta}\zeta$ with $\abs{\psi'(0)}=\abs{e^{i\theta}}=1$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #chain}
$\abs{\phi'(z)}=\abs{\psi'(0)}\cdot\abs{(A_{z}^{-1})'(0)}\big/\abs{A_{\phi(z)}'(\phi(z))}$, with $\abs{(A_{z}^{-1})'(0)}=1-\abs{z}^{2}$ and $\abs{A_{w}'(w)}=\dfrac{1}{1-\abs{w}^{2}}$.

::: pf-proof
Differentiating $\psi=A_{\phi(z)}\circ\phi\circ A_{z}^{-1}$ at $0$ and using $A_{z}^{-1}(0)=z$, the chain rule gives $\psi'(0)=A_{\phi(z)}'(\phi(z))\cdot\phi'(z)\cdot(A_{z}^{-1})'(0)$, hence the displayed formula. For $A_{w}(\zeta)=\frac{\zeta-w}{1-\bar w\zeta}$ one computes $A_{w}'(\zeta)=\frac{1-\abs{w}^{2}}{(1-\bar w\zeta)^{2}}$, so $\abs{A_{w}'(w)}=\frac{1-\abs{w}^{2}}{(1-\abs{w}^{2})^{2}}=\frac{1}{1-\abs{w}^{2}}$; the inverse is $A_{-w}$ up to a unimodular factor, and $\abs{(A_{z}^{-1})'(0)}=\abs{A_{-z}'(0)}^{-1}=1-\abs{z}^{2}$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #part-a}
(a) $\dfrac{\abs{\phi'(z)}}{1-\abs{\phi(z)}^{2}}=\dfrac{1}{1-\abs{z}^{2}}$.

::: pf-proof
Substituting step [](#chain){.pf-ref} into $\abs{\psi'(0)}=1$ of step [](#conjugate){.pf-ref}: $\abs{\phi'(z)}=\big(1-\abs{z}^{2}\big)\Big/\Big(\frac{1}{1-\abs{\phi(z)}^{2}}\Big)=\big(1-\abs{z}^{2}\big)\big(1-\abs{\phi(z)}^{2}\big)$, which is the assertion after dividing.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #part-b}
(b) An analytic $f:\Delta\to\Delta$ with two distinct fixed points is the identity.

::: pf-proof
Let $F=A_{z_{1}}\circ f\circ A_{z_{1}}^{-1}$; then $F$ is an analytic self-map of $\Delta$ fixing $0$ and fixing the point $w=A_{z_{1}}(z_{2})\ne0$ (since $A_{z_{1}}$ is injective and fixes $z_{1}$ in the sense $A_{z_{1}}(z_{1})=0$). Schwarz gives $\abs{F(\zeta)}\le\abs{\zeta}$, with equality at $w\ne0$, so the equality case gives $F(\zeta)=e^{i\theta}\zeta$; then $w=F(w)=e^{i\theta}w$ forces $e^{i\theta}=1$, so $F=\mathrm{id}$ and $f=A_{z_{1}}^{-1}\circ F\circ A_{z_{1}}=\mathrm{id}$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-qed}
Part (a) is step [](#part-a){.pf-ref}, the invariance of the quantity $\abs{\phi'(z)}/(1-\abs{\phi(z)}^{2})$ under automorphisms; part (b) is step [](#part-b){.pf-ref}.
:::
