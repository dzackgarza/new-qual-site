::: problem
Find all holomorphic functions $f:\mathbb{C}\setminus\{0\}\to\mathbb{C}$ such that
\[
|f(z)|<\bigl(|z|^{1/2}+|z|^{-1/2}\bigr)
\]
for all $z\neq 0$.
:::

::: solution
Write the Laurent expansion on $\mathbb C^*$ as
\[
f(z)=\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}a_nz^n.
\]
For every $R>0$, Cauchy's coefficient estimate on $|z|=R$ gives
\[
|a_n|\le \left(R^{1/2}+R^{-1/2}\right)R^{-n}.
\]
If $n\ge1$, letting $R\to\infty$ gives $a_n=0$. If $n\le-1$, letting
$R\to0$ gives $a_n=0$. Thus $f$ is constant, say $f\equiv c$.

The inequality then becomes
\[
|c|<R^{1/2}+R^{-1/2}\qquad(R>0).
\]
The right-hand side has minimum $2$ at $R=1$, so necessarily $|c|<2$.
Conversely every constant with $|c|<2$ works, since
$R^{1/2}+R^{-1/2}\ge2$ for all $R>0$.

Hence the complete family is
\[
\boxed{f(z)\equiv c,\qquad |c|<2.}
\]
:::
