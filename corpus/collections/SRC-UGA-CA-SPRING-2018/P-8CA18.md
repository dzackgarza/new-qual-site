::: {.problem}
Find all holomorphic functions $f:\CC\setminus\{0\}\to\CC$ such that
$$
\abs{f(z)}<\bigl(\abs{z}^{1/2}+\abs{z}^{-1/2}\bigr)
$$
for all $z\neq 0$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Write the Laurent expansion on $\CC^*$ as
$$
f(z)=\sum_{n\in\ZZ}a_nz^n.
$$
For every $R>0$, Cauchy's coefficient estimate on $\abs{z}=R$ gives
$$
\abs{a_n}\le \left(R^{1/2}+R^{-1/2}\right)R^{-n}.
$$
If $n\ge1$, letting $R\to\infty$ gives $a_n=0$. If $n\le-1$, letting
$R\to0$ gives $a_n=0$. Thus $f$ is constant, say $f\equiv c$.

The inequality then becomes
$$
\abs{c}<R^{1/2}+R^{-1/2}\qquad(R>0).
$$
The right-hand side has minimum $2$ at $R=1$, so necessarily $\abs{c}<2$.
Conversely every constant with $\abs{c}<2$ works, since
$R^{1/2}+R^{-1/2}\ge2$ for all $R>0$.

Hence the complete family is
$$
\boxed{f(z)\equiv c,\qquad \abs{c}<2.}
$$
:::
