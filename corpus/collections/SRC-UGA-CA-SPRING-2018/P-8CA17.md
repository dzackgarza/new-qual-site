::: {.problem}
Does there exist a conformal mapping of the region
\[
\Omega_1=\{z\in\mathbb{C}:\ |z-1|<\tfrac12\}
\]
onto the region
\[
\Omega_2=\{z\in\mathbb{C}:\ |z+1|>1\}?
\]
:::

::: {.solution}
No. The disk $\Omega_1$ is simply connected. On the other hand,
\[
z\longmapsto \frac1{z+1}
\]
maps $\Omega_2$ conformally onto the punctured unit disk
\[
0<|w|<1,
\]
which is not simply connected. A conformal equivalence is in particular a
homeomorphism and therefore preserves simple connectedness. Hence no conformal
equivalence $\Omega_1\to\Omega_2$ exists.
:::
