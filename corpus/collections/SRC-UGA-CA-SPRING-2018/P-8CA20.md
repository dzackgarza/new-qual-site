::: problem
Let $\mathbb{D}$ denote the open unit disk in the complex plane.
Does there exist an analytic function $f:\mathbb{D}\to\mathbb{D}$ such that

(i) $f(1/2)=3/4$,

(ii) $f'(1/2)=2/3$?
:::

::: solution
No. Schwarz--Pick gives, at $a=1/2$,
\[
|f'(a)|\le
\frac{1-|f(a)|^2}{1-|a|^2}.
\]
Using $f(a)=3/4$,
\[
|f'(1/2)|\le
\frac{1-9/16}{1-1/4}
=\frac{7/16}{3/4}
=\frac7{12}.
\]
But $2/3>7/12$. Hence no analytic self-map of the disk can satisfy both
conditions.
:::
