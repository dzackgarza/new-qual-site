::: {.problem}
Let $I\subset\mathbb R$ be an interval and let $f:I\to\mathbb R$ satisfy
$f(x)>0$ for every $x\in I$. Suppose that for every real constant $c$, the
function
$$
x\longmapsto e^{cx}f(x)
$$
is convex. Prove that
$$
\log f
$$
is convex.
:::

::: {.solution}
::: pf

::: {.pf-step #equal-case}
Fix $x,y\in I$ and $t\in[0,1]$, and put
$$
z\coloneqq tx+(1-t)y.
$$
If $x=y$, then
$$
\log f(z)
=
t\log f(x)+(1-t)\log f(y).
$$

::: pf-proof
When $x=y$, one has $z=x=y$, so both sides of the displayed equality are
$\log f(x)$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #tilt-equalizes-endpoints}
Suppose $x\ne y$. Define
$$
c
\coloneqq
\frac{\log f(y)-\log f(x)}{x-y}.
$$
Then
$$
e^{cx}f(x)=e^{cy}f(y).
$$

::: pf-proof
By the definition of $c$,
$$
c(x-y)
=
\log f(y)-\log f(x).
$$
Hence
$$
cx+\log f(x)
=
cy+\log f(y).
$$
Exponentiating gives the claimed equality.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #unequal-case}
Under the hypotheses of step [](#tilt-equalizes-endpoints){.pf-ref},
$$
\log f(z)
\le
t\log f(x)+(1-t)\log f(y).
$$

::: pf-proof
Let
$$
h_c(u)\coloneqq e^{cu}f(u).
$$
By hypothesis, $h_c$ is convex on $I$. Since $z=tx+(1-t)y$,
$$
h_c(z)
\le
t h_c(x)+(1-t)h_c(y).
$$
Step [](#tilt-equalizes-endpoints){.pf-ref} says that the two endpoint values are equal. Denote their common
value by $M>0$. Then
$$
e^{cz}f(z)\le M.
$$
Taking logarithms gives
$$
cz+\log f(z)\le\log M.
$$
Moreover,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\log M
&=
t\bigl(cx+\log f(x)\bigr)
+
(1-t)\bigl(cy+\log f(y)\bigr)\\
&=
cz+t\log f(x)+(1-t)\log f(y).
\end{aligned}
$$
Subtracting $cz$ yields the desired inequality.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #log-f-convex-boxed}
The function $\log f$ is convex on $I$.

::: pf-proof
For arbitrary $x,y\in I$ and $t\in[0,1]$, step [](#equal-case){.pf-ref} proves the convexity
inequality when $x=y$, and step [](#unequal-case){.pf-ref} proves it when $x\ne y$. Thus
$$
\boxed{
\log f\bigl(tx+(1-t)y\bigr)
\le
t\log f(x)+(1-t)\log f(y)
}.
$$
This is exactly convexity of $\log f$ on $I$.
:::

:::

::: pf-qed
Step [](#log-f-convex-boxed){.pf-ref} proves the source statement.
:::
