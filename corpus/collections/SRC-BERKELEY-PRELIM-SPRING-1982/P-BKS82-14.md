::: {.problem}
Let $f:\mathbb R\to(0,\infty)$ be continuous. Suppose that for every real constant $c$, the function
\[
x\longmapsto e^{cx}f(x)
\]
is convex. Prove that
\[
\log f
\]
is convex.
:::
