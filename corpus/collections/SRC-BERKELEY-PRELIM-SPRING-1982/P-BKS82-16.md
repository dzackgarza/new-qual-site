::: {.problem}
Let $G$ be a finite group with no subgroup of index $2$. Prove that every subgroup of index $3$ is normal in $G$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $H\le G$ have index $3$. Let $G$ act by left multiplication on the
three cosets $G/H$, and let
$$
\varphi:G\longrightarrow S_3
$$
be the corresponding homomorphism.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #image-order-three-or-six}
The subgroup $\varphi(G)\le S_3$ acts transitively on three points,
so
$$
\abs{\varphi(G)}\in\{3,6\}.
$$

::: pf-proof
The action of $G$ on its left cosets is transitive, so the induced action
of $\varphi(G)$ is transitive. By orbit--stabilizer, the order of
$\varphi(G)$ is divisible by the orbit size $3$. Since
$\varphi(G)\le S_3$, its order also divides $6$. Hence its order is
$3$ or $6$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #order-three-case-normal}
If $\abs{\varphi(G)}=3$, then $H$ is normal in $G$.

::: pf-proof
In this case $\varphi(G)$ is a transitive group of order $3$ on three
points. Orbit--stabilizer shows that every point stabilizer in
$\varphi(G)$ is trivial.

The stabilizer in $G$ of the coset $H$ is exactly $H$, so
$$
\varphi(H)
$$
is the stabilizer of $H$ in $\varphi(G)$ and is therefore trivial. Thus
$$
H\subseteq\ker\varphi.
$$
Conversely, every element of $\ker\varphi$ fixes the coset $H$, so it
lies in $H$. Hence
$$
H=\ker\varphi,
$$
which is normal.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #order-six-case-impossible}
The case $\abs{\varphi(G)}=6$ is impossible.

::: pf-proof
If $\abs{\varphi(G)}=6$, then
$$
\varphi(G)=S_3.
$$
The subgroup $A_3\le S_3$ has index $2$. Therefore
$$
\varphi^{-1}(A_3)
$$
is a subgroup of $G$ of index $2$, contradicting the hypothesis on $G$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #every-index-three-normal}
Therefore every subgroup of index $3$ in $G$ is normal.

::: pf-proof
For an arbitrary index-three subgroup $H$, step [](#image-order-three-or-six){.pf-ref} leaves only the two
cases considered in steps [](#order-three-case-normal){.pf-ref} and [](#order-six-case-impossible){.pf-ref}. The second is impossible, and
the first makes $H$ normal.
:::

:::

::: pf-qed
Step [](#every-index-three-normal){.pf-ref} proves the claim.
:::
