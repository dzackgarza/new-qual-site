:::{.problem}
Let $f(x)$ be a polynomial with real coefficients, and let $a\in\mathbb R$ satisfy $f(a)\ne0$. Prove that there is a real polynomial $g(x)$ such that, for
\[
p(x)=f(x)g(x),
\]
one has
\[
p(a)=1,
\qquad
p'(a)=0,
\qquad
p''(a)=0.
\]
:::
