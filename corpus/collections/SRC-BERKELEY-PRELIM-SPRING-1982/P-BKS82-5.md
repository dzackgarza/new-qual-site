::: {.problem}
Let $(g_n)$ be twice differentiable functions on $[0,1]$ such that
\[
g_n(0)=g_n'(0)=0
\]
for every $n$, and suppose
\[
|g_n''(x)|\le1
\]
for every $n$ and every $x\in[0,1]$. Prove that $(g_n)$ has a subsequence converging uniformly on $[0,1]$.
:::
