::: {.problem}
Prove that
\[
x^4+x+1
\]
is irreducible over $\mathbb Q$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let
$$
f(x)\coloneqq x^4+x+1\in\mathbb Z[x].
$$
Reduce $f$ modulo $2$ and write
$$
\overline f(x)=x^4+x+1\in\mathbb F_2[x].
$$

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #no-linear-factor}
The polynomial $\overline f$ has no linear factor over
$\mathbb F_2$.

::: pf-proof
The only elements of $\mathbb F_2$ are $0$ and $1$, and
$$
\overline f(0)=1,
\qquad
\overline f(1)=1+1+1=1
$$
in $\mathbb F_2$. Thus $\overline f$ has no root in $\mathbb F_2$ and
hence no linear factor.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #unique-irreducible-quadratic}
The only monic irreducible quadratic over $\mathbb F_2$ is
$$
q(x)=x^2+x+1.
$$

::: pf-proof
The monic quadratics over $\mathbb F_2$ are
$$
x^2,
\qquad
x^2+1,
\qquad
x^2+x,
\qquad
x^2+x+1.
$$
The first three are reducible:
$$
x^2=x\cdot x,
\qquad
x^2+1=(x+1)^2,
\qquad
x^2+x=x(x+1).
$$
The last has no root in $\mathbb F_2$, so it is irreducible.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #reduction-irreducible}
The polynomial $\overline f$ is irreducible over $\mathbb F_2$.

::: pf-proof
If the monic quartic $\overline f$ were reducible, then by step [](#no-linear-factor){.pf-ref} it
could not have a linear factor. Therefore it would have to factor as a
product of two monic irreducible quadratics. By step [](#unique-irreducible-quadratic){.pf-ref}, both factors
would have to equal $q$, so
$$
\overline f(x)
=
q(x)^2
=
(x^2+x+1)^2
=
x^4+x^2+1
$$
in characteristic $2$. But
$$
x^4+x^2+1\ne x^4+x+1.
$$
This contradiction proves irreducibility of $\overline f$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #f-irreducible-over-q}
The polynomial $f=x^4+x+1$ is irreducible over $\mathbb Q$.

::: pf-proof
The polynomial $f$ is primitive in $\mathbb Z[x]$. Its reduction modulo
$2$ has the same degree and is irreducible by step [](#reduction-irreducible){.pf-ref}. The standard
reduction criterion, equivalently Gauss's lemma followed by reduction
modulo $2$, therefore implies that $f$ is irreducible over $\mathbb Q$.
:::

:::

::: pf-qed
Step [](#f-irreducible-over-q){.pf-ref} is the required conclusion.
:::
