::: {.problem}
$X$ is the space obtained by gluing a disk to a sphere as follows: Let $\alpha:S^1\to S^2$ be the map that “wraps $S^1$ around the equator of $S^2$ three times”—more explicitly, $\alpha$ is the composition of the map $S^1\to S^1$ sending $z$ to $z^3$ (where $S^1$ is identified with complex numbers of norm one) and the canonical inclusion $S^1\subseteq S^2$.
We glue $D^2$ onto $S^2$ by attaching its boundary $\partial D^2=S^1$ according to $\alpha$: $$X=(S^2\sqcup D^2)/\sim,$$ where $\sim$ is the equivalence relation generated by $\alpha(z)\sim z$ for all $z\in\partial D^2=S^1$.
Compute the singular homology of $X$.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. $X$ is a CW complex with one $0$-cell $v$ and one $1$-cell $e$ forming the equator, the open northern and southern hemispheres as $2$-cells $u$ and $w$, and the glued disk as a third $2$-cell $d$. With suitable orientations, $\partial_2(u)=\partial_2(w)=e$ and $\partial_2(d)=3e$.

::: {.proof}
The closure of each hemisphere is a disc whose boundary maps homeomorphically onto the equator; orient $u$ and $w$ so that these attaching maps have degree $+1$.
The disk is attached by $S^1\xrightarrow{z\mapsto z^3}S^1\hookrightarrow S^2$, which has degree $3$ onto the equatorial $1$-cell.
:::

<1>2. In the bases $(u,w,d)$, $e$, $v$, the cellular chain complex is
$$0\to\ZZ^3\xrightarrow{\partial_2}\ZZ\xrightarrow{\partial_1}\ZZ\to0,\qquad \partial_2(a,b,c)=a+b+3c,\qquad \partial_1=0.$$

::: {.proof}
The formula for $\partial_2$ is step <1>1.
The $1$-cell is a loop at the unique $0$-cell, so $\partial_1=0$.
:::

<1>3. $H_0(X)\cong\ZZ$, $H_1(X)=0$, and $H_2(X)\cong\ZZ^2$.

::: {.proof}
By step <1>2, $H_0(X)=C_0\cong\ZZ$.
$\partial_2$ is surjective because $\partial_2(1,0,0)=1$, so $H_1(X)=\ZZ/\ZZ=0$.
There are no $3$-cells, so $H_2(X)=\ker\partial_2=\{(a,b,c)\in\ZZ^3:a+b+3c=0\}$, which is free with basis $(1,-1,0)$, $(-3,0,1)$.
:::

<1>4. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Cellular homology computes singular homology, and there are no cells in dimension $\ge3$, so by step <1>3
$$H_k(X;\ZZ)\cong\begin{cases}\ZZ,&k=0,\\\ZZ^2,&k=2,\\0,&k\neq0,2.\end{cases}$$
:::
