::: {.problem}
Suppose $G$ is a finite group acting on a Hausdorff space $X$ and assume the action is fixed point free (i.e., if $g\in G$ is not the identity element, then $g\cdot x\ne x$ for all $x\in X$).

(a) Prove that the action is necessarily a covering space action.
(Note: Recall that this means every point $x\in X$ has an open neighborhood $U$ such that $U\cap g\cdot U=\varnothing$ for all non-identity elements $g\in G$.)

(b) Assume $X$ is simply connected and let $X/G$ be the quotient space.
Prove every map from $X/G$ to $S^1$ is contractible.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #covering-space-action}
(a) Every $x\in X$ has an open neighborhood $U$ with $U\cap gU=\varnothing$ for all $g\in G\setminus\{e\}$.

::: pf-proof

::: {.pf-step #separate-x-from-gx}
For each $g\in G\setminus\{e\}$ there is an open neighborhood $U_g$ of $x$ with $U_g\cap gU_g=\varnothing$.

::: pf-proof
Freeness gives $x\neq gx$.
Since $X$ is Hausdorff, choose disjoint open sets $A_g\ni x$ and $B_g\ni gx$, and set $U_g=A_g\cap g^{-1}B_g$.
Then $U_g$ is an open neighborhood of $x$, $U_g\subseteq A_g$, and $gU_g\subseteq B_g$, so $U_g\cap gU_g=\varnothing$.
:::

:::

::: pf-qed
Let $U=\bigcap_{g\neq e}U_g$, an open neighborhood of $x$ because $G$ is finite.
For $g\neq e$, $U\cap gU\subseteq U_g\cap gU_g=\varnothing$ by step [](#separate-x-from-gx){.pf-ref}.
:::

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #part-b}
(b) If $X$ is also locally path-connected, then every map $f\colon X/G\to S^1$ is nullhomotopic.

::: pf-proof
By step [](#covering-space-action){.pf-ref}, $p\colon X\to X/G$ is a covering map with deck group $G$.
A covering map is a local homeomorphism, so $X/G$ is path-connected and locally path-connected, and $X$ is its universal cover.
Hence $\pi_1(X/G)\cong G$ is finite.
The image of $f_*\colon\pi_1(X/G)\to\pi_1(S^1)\cong\ZZ$ is a finite subgroup of $\ZZ$, hence trivial.
By the lifting criterion, $f$ lifts through the universal covering $\RR\to S^1$ to a map $\widetilde f\colon X/G\to\RR$.
The straight-line homotopy from $\widetilde f$ to a constant map, composed with $\RR\to S^1$, is a nullhomotopy of $f$.
:::

:::

::: pf-qed
Step [](#covering-space-action){.pf-ref} proves (a), and step [](#part-b){.pf-ref} proves (b) for locally path-connected $X$.
:::

:::

:::

::: {.remark}
Part (b) is false without local path-connectedness.
With $G$ trivial, it asserts that every map from a simply connected Hausdorff space to $S^1$ is nullhomotopic.
The quasi-circle $Y$ is a compact, path-connected, simply connected subspace of $\RR^2$, and the quotient map $Y\to S^1$ collapsing its limit segment to a point does not lift to $\RR$ [@Hat02, §1.3, Exercise 7].
A nullhomotopic map to $S^1$ lifts to $\RR$ by the homotopy lifting property, so this map is not nullhomotopic.
:::
