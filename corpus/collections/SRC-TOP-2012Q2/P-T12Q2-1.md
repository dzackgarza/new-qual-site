::: {.problem}
Assume that $(X,\tau)$ is a topological space with the property that for every open set $G\subseteq X$, the closure of $G$, $\overline G$, is open.
Such topological spaces are called *extremally disconnected*. Prove the following.

(a) If $F\subseteq X$ is a closed set, then the interior of $F$, $F^\circ$, is closed.

(b) If $G\subseteq X$ is an open set, then $G$ equipped with the subspace topology is extremally disconnected.

(c) If $X$ is Hausdorff and $f:\RR\to X$ is continuous, prove that $f$ is a constant function.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #part-a}
(a) If $F\subseteq X$ is closed, then $F^\circ$ is closed.

::: pf-proof
The interior of $F$ is the complement of the closure of the complement: $F^\circ=X\setminus\overline{X\setminus F}$.
Since $X\setminus F$ is open, $\overline{X\setminus F}$ is open by hypothesis, so its complement $F^\circ$ is closed.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #part-b}
(b) If $G\subseteq X$ is open, then $G$ with the subspace topology is extremally disconnected.

::: pf-proof
Let $U\subseteq G$ be open in $G$.
Then $U=G\cap V$ for some open $V\subseteq X$, so $U$ is open in $X$ because $G$ is.
The closure of $U$ in $G$ is $\overline U\cap G$, where $\overline U$ is the closure in $X$.
By hypothesis $\overline U$ is open in $X$, so $\overline U\cap G$ is open in $G$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #connected-subset-one-point}
If $X$ is Hausdorff, every connected subset of $X$ has at most one point.

::: pf-proof
Let $C\subseteq X$ be connected, and suppose $x,y\in C$ are distinct.
Choose disjoint open sets $U\ni x$ and $V\ni y$.
By hypothesis $\overline U$ is open, and it is closed, so $\overline U$ is clopen and $C\cap\overline U$ is clopen in $C$.
It contains $x$, and it omits $y$ because $V$ is an open neighborhood of $y$ disjoint from $U$.
This contradicts connectedness of $C$.
:::

:::

::: pf-qed
Steps [](#part-a){.pf-ref} and [](#part-b){.pf-ref} prove (a) and (b).
For (c), $f(\RR)$ is connected as a continuous image of the connected space $\RR$, so by step [](#connected-subset-one-point){.pf-ref} it is a single point, and $f$ is constant.
:::
