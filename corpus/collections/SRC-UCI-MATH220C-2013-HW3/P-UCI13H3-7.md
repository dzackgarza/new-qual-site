::: {.problem}
If $f$ is an entire function satisfying the estimate
$$
\abs{f(z)}\le 1+\abs z^{\sqrt{2010}}\quad\text{for every }z\in\CC,
$$
show that $f$ is a polynomial and determine the best upper bound for the degree of $f$.
:::
