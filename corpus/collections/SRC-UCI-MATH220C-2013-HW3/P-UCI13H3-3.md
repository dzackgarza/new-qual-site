::: {.problem}
Let $f$ be analytic in the punctured unit disk $U_0=\{0<\abs z<1\}$, and suppose there is a positive integer $n$ with $\abs{f^{(n)}(z)}\le\abs z^{-n}$ for all $z\in U_0$.
Show that $z=0$ is a removable singularity of $f$.
:::
