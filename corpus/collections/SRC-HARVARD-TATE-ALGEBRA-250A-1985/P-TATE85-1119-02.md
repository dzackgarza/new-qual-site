::: {.problem}
(2) Consider the cubic polynomials
\[
f_1(X)=X^3+X^2+7X-8,
\qquad
f_2(X)=X^3-8X+15,
\]
\[
f_3(X)=X^3+X^2-7X+12,
\qquad
f_4(X)=X^3+10X+1.
\]
They satisfy
\[
f_1(X)\equiv (X-6)(X+5)(X+2)\pmod{13},
\]
and $f_1$ is irreducible modulo $17$, $19$, and $29$;
\[
f_2(X)\equiv (X+4)(X+6)(X+7)\pmod{17},
\]
and $f_2$ is irreducible modulo $13$, $29$, and $29$;
\[
f_3(X)\equiv (X-8)(X+8)(X+1)\pmod{19},
\]
and $f_3$ is irreducible modulo $13$, $17$, and $29$; and
\[
f_4(X)\equiv (X-2)(X-3)(X-5)\pmod{29},
\]
and $f_4$ is irreducible modulo $13$, $17$, and $19$.

Each of the four polynomials has discriminant $-4027$, a prime.
Nevertheless, the fields $\mathbb Q(\alpha_i)$, where $\alpha_i$ is a root of $f_i(X)$, are pairwise non-isomorphic.
Why?
:::
