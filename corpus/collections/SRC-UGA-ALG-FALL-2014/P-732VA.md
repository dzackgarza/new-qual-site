::: {.problem}
Let $G$ be a group and let $H, K \le G$ be subgroups of finite index. Show that
$$
[G : H \cap K] \le [G : H] [G : K].
$$
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

There is an injective map of left-coset sets
$$
\Phi:H/(H\cap K)\longrightarrow G/K,\qquad h(H\cap K)\longmapsto hK.
$$

::: pf-proof

For $h_1,h_2\in H$, equality $h_1(H\cap K)=h_2(H\cap K)$ implies $h_2^{-1}h_1\in K$ and hence $h_1K=h_2K$, so the assignment is well-defined. Conversely, if $h_1K=h_2K$, then $h_2^{-1}h_1\in K$; because both elements lie in $H$, this element also lies in $H$, so it belongs to $H\cap K$. Thus the original cosets agree and $\Phi$ is injective.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

The subgroup indices satisfy
$$
\boxed{[G:H\cap K]\leq[G:H]\,[G:K]}.
$$

::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1){.pf-ref}, $[H:H\cap K]\leq[G:K]$. The multiplicativity of indices in the chain $H\cap K\leq H\leq G$ gives
$$
[G:H\cap K]=[G:H]\,[H:H\cap K]\leq[G:H]\,[G:K].
$$
In particular, $H\cap K$ has finite index in $G$.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s2){.pf-ref} proves the required inequality.

:::
