::: {.problem}
Let $f ( x ) = x ^ { 3 } + 6 \in \mathbb { Z } _ { 7 } [ x ]$ . Write $f ( x )$ as a product of irreducible polynomials over $\mathbb { Z } _ { 7 }$ .
:::

Let $k=\mathbb F_7$ and $f=x^3+6\in k[x]$.

::: pf

::: pf-step

The elements $1,2,4\in k$ are three distinct roots of $f$.


::: pf-proof

Direct substitution gives
\[
f(1)=7=0,\qquad f(2)=14=0,\qquad f(4)=70=0
\]
in $k$. The elements $1,2,4$ are pairwise distinct modulo $7$.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

The factorization into irreducible polynomials is
\[
\boxed{f(x)=(x-1)(x-2)(x-4)\quad\text{in }\mathbb F_7[x]}.
\]


::: pf-proof

By the factor theorem and step <1>1, each of the pairwise coprime monic linear polynomials $x-1$, $x-2$, and $x-4$ divides $f$. Their product is monic of degree three and divides the monic degree-three polynomial $f$, so equality follows. Every polynomial of degree one over a field is irreducible.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step <1>2 gives the requested factorization into irreducibles.

:::

:::
