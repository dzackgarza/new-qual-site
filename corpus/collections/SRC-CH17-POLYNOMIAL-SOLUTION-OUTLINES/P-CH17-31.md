::: {.problem}
Let F be a field and let $p ( x )$ be irreducible over F . If E is a field that contains F and there is an element a in E such that $p ( a ) = 0$ , show that the mapping $\phi : F [ x ] \to E$ given by $f ( x ) \mapsto f ( a )$ is a ring homomorphism with kernel $< p ( x ) >$ .
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $F\hookrightarrow E$ be the given inclusion of fields, let $p\in F[x]$ be irreducible, and let $a\in E$ satisfy $p(a)=0$.

<1>1. Evaluation at $a$ defines a unital ring homomorphism $\phi:F[x]\to E$.

::: {.proof}
For polynomials $f,g\in F[x]$, evaluation gives $(f+g)(a)=f(a)+g(a)$ and $(fg)(a)=f(a)g(a)$, as follows by distributing finite sums of powers of $a$. Moreover, $\phi(1)=1_E$, so the map preserves addition, multiplication, and the identity.
:::

<1>2. The kernel of $\phi$ is
\[
\boxed{\ker\phi=(p)}.
\]

::: {.proof}
Since $\phi(p)=p(a)=0$, the ideal $(p)$ is contained in $\ker\phi$. The polynomial $p$ is irreducible in the Euclidean domain $F[x]$, so $(p)$ is maximal. As $\phi(1)=1_E\neq0$, its kernel is a proper ideal. By maximality, the only proper ideal containing $(p)$ is $(p)$ itself; thus equality holds.
:::

<1>3. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>1 establishes the homomorphism property and step <1>2 determines its kernel.
:::
:::
