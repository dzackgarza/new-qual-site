::: {.problem}
Construct a field of order 25.
:::

::: {.solution}
Put $k=\mathbb F_5$ and $p(x)=x^2+x+1\in k[x]$.

<1>1. The polynomial $p$ is irreducible over $k$.

::: {.proof}
Its values at the five elements of $k$ are
\[
p(0)=1,\quad p(1)=3,\quad p(2)=2,\quad p(3)=3,\quad p(4)=1.
\]
None is zero in $k$. A polynomial of degree two over a field is reducible if and only if it has a root in that field; hence $p$ is irreducible.
:::

<1>2. A field of order $25$ is
\[
\boxed{K=\mathbb F_5[x]/(x^2+x+1)}.
\]

::: {.proof}
By step <1>1 and Bézout's identity in $k[x]$, every nonzero class modulo $(p)$ admits a multiplicative inverse, so $K$ is a field. Polynomial division by the monic degree-two polynomial $p$ gives each class a unique representative $a+bx$ with $a,b\in\mathbb F_5$. There are five choices for each coefficient and hence exactly $5^2=25$ elements.
:::

<1>3. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>2 constructs the requested field and establishes its cardinality.
:::
:::
