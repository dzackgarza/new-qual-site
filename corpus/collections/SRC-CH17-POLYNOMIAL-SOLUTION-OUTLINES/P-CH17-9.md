::: {.problem}
Construct a field of order 25.
:::

::: {.solution}
No retained solution set covers this exercise; the argument is the card's own, on the outline's plan. Write $\pi$ for the class of $x$ in $\ZZ_{5}[x]/\langle p(x)\rangle$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #quotient-field}
If $p(x)\in\ZZ_{5}[x]$ is irreducible of degree $2$, then $\ZZ_{5}[x]/\langle p(x)\rangle$ is a field.

::: pf-proof
This is the theorem that $\ZZ_{p}[x]/\langle f(x)\rangle$ is a field exactly when $f$ is irreducible over $\ZZ_{p}$ (Theorem 17.5): in a principal ideal domain the ideal $\langle p\rangle$ is maximal precisely when $p$ is irreducible, and a quotient by a maximal ideal is a field. Note $\ZZ_{5}$ is a field, so $\ZZ_{5}[x]$ is a principal ideal domain.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #twenty-five}
If $p$ has degree $2$, then $\ZZ_{5}[x]/\langle p(x)\rangle$ has $5^{2}=25$ elements.

::: pf-proof
By the division algorithm, each class in the quotient has a unique representative of degree less than $2$, that is of the form $a+bx$ with $a,b\in\ZZ_{5}$: division of any $f$ by the monic $p$ gives $f=qp+r$ with $\deg r<2$, and two such representatives differ by a multiple of $p$ only if they are equal, since a nonzero difference of degree below $2$ cannot be a nonzero multiple of $p$. There are $5\cdot5=25$ such pairs, so the quotient has $25$ elements.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #no-roots}
$x^{2}+x+1$ has no zero in $\ZZ_{5}$.

::: pf-proof
Evaluate at the five residues: $1^{2}+1+1=3$, $2^{2}+2+1=7\equiv2$, $3^{2}+3+1=13\equiv3$, $4^{2}+4+1=21\equiv1$, and at $0$ the value is $1$. None is $0$ modulo $5$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #irreducible}
$x^{2}+x+1$ is irreducible over $\ZZ_{5}$.

::: pf-proof
A polynomial of degree $2$ or $3$ over a field is irreducible exactly when it has no zero in that field, since a factorization of such a polynomial into lower-degree factors includes a linear factor, whose root would be a zero. Degree $2$ and step [](#no-roots){.pf-ref} give irreducibility. Independently, the discriminant is $1-4=-3\equiv2\pmod 5$, and the nonzero squares modulo $5$ are $1$ and $4$, so the discriminant is not a square and the quadratic has no root.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #answer}
$\boxed{\ZZ_{5}[x]/\langle x^{2}+x+1\rangle}$ is a field of order $25$.

::: pf-proof
Steps [](#irreducible){.pf-ref} and [](#quotient-field){.pf-ref} make the quotient a field, and step [](#twenty-five){.pf-ref} gives it $25$ elements; its elements are the $25$ classes $a+b\pi$ with $a,b\in\ZZ_{5}$, and $\pi^{2}=-\pi-1=4\pi+4$. This is the field the exercise asks for.
:::
