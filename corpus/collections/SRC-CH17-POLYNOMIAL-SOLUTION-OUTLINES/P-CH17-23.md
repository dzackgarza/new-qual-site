::: {.problem}
Find all monic irreducible polynomials of degree 2 over $\mathbb { Z } _ { 3 }$ .
:::

::: {.solution}
Put $k=\mathbb F_3$. Each monic quadratic is uniquely $f_{a,b}=x^2+ax+b$ with $a,b\in k$.

<1>1. Such a polynomial is irreducible precisely when it has no root in $k$.

::: {.proof}
Over a field, a reducible polynomial of degree two has a factor of degree one, and a linear factor exists precisely when the polynomial has a root in the field.
:::

<1>2. If $b=1$, then $f_{a,b}$ is irreducible exactly when $a=0$.

::: {.proof}
The values at $0,1,2$ are $1$, $a+2$, and $2+2a$, respectively. The second vanishes exactly when $a=1$, and the third exactly when $a=2$. Hence none vanishes precisely for $a=0$. Apply step <1>1.
:::

<1>3. If $b=2$, then $f_{a,b}$ is irreducible exactly when $a=1$ or $a=2$.

::: {.proof}
The values at $0,1,2$ are $2$, $a$, and $2a$, respectively. The polynomial has a root exactly when $a=0$. Apply step <1>1.
:::

<1>4. The complete list of monic irreducible quadratics over $k$ is
\[
\boxed{x^2+1,\quad x^2+x+2,\quad x^2+2x+2}.
\]

::: {.proof}
If $b=0$, then $0$ is a root, so step <1>1 rules out the polynomial. For $b=1,2$, steps <1>2 and <1>3 give precisely the three displayed polynomials. All three are monic quadratics without roots and therefore irreducible.
:::

<1>5. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>4 establishes the exhaustive classification.
:::
:::
