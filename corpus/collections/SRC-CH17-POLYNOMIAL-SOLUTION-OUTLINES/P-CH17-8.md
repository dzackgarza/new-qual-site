::: {.problem}
Suppose that $f(x) \in \ZZ_{p}[x]$ and $f(x)$ is irreducible over $\ZZ_{p}$, where $p$ is a prime.
If $\deg(f(x)) = n$, prove that $\ZZ_{p}[x]/\langle f(x) \rangle$ is a field with $p^{n}$ elements.
:::

::: {.solution}

The exercise is Gallian, *Contemporary Abstract Algebra* (7th ed.), Chapter 17 exercise 6,
printed p. 317, and is item 8 of the retained Chapter 17 solution outline
(`assets/attachments/Ch17Sltns.pdf`, PDF p. 1). Its two parts — the quotient is a field, and it
has $p^{n}$ elements — are proved below with the degree bound made explicit.

Throughout, $p$ is prime, $F=\ZZ_{p}$ is the field of $p$ elements (Corollary to
Theorem 13.4, printed p. 251), and $f\in F[x]$ is irreducible over $F$ of degree $n$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #nonconstant}

$n\ge1$.

::: pf-proof

Irreducibility is defined only for nonconstant polynomials (printed p. 305): a nonconstant
$f\in F[x]$ is irreducible when $f$ is not the product of two polynomials of positive
degree. So $f$ is nonconstant and $\deg f=n\ge1$. This matters, since for a nonzero constant
$f$ the ideal $\langle f\rangle$ is all of $F[x]$ and the quotient is the zero ring, which is
not a field.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #field}

$F[x]/\langle f\rangle$ is a field.

::: pf-proof

$f$ is irreducible over the field $F$, so $\langle f\rangle$ is a maximal ideal of $F[x]$ by
Theorem 17.5 (printed p. 311), and equivalently $F[x]/\langle f\rangle$ is a field by its
Corollary 1, or by Theorem 14.4 (printed p. 268).

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #representatives}

Every coset of $\langle f\rangle$ in $F[x]$ is $r+\langle f\rangle$ for some $r\in F[x]$ with
$r=0$ or $\deg r<n$.

::: pf-proof

By the Division Algorithm for $F[x]$ (Theorem 16.2, printed p. 296), applied to $g$ and the
nonzero polynomial $f$, there are $q,r\in F[x]$ with $g=fq+r$ and $r=0$ or $\deg r<\deg f=n$.
Then $g-r=fq\in\langle f\rangle$, so $g+\langle f\rangle=r+\langle f\rangle$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #count}

$F[x]/\langle f\rangle$ has exactly $p^{n}$ elements.

::: pf-proof

($\le$) By step [](#representatives){.pf-ref} every coset has a representative
$r=a_{0}+a_{1}x+\cdots+a_{n-1}x^{n-1}$ with each $a_{i}\in F$ — the zero polynomial
included, with all coefficients $0$. There are $p$ choices for each of the $n$ coefficients,
so at most $p^{n}$ cosets.
($\ge$) Distinct such representatives give distinct cosets: if $r\ne r'$ both have degree
$<n$ (or are zero), then $r-r'$ is nonzero of degree $<n$; if $r+\langle f\rangle
=r'+\langle f\rangle$ then $r-r'\in\langle f\rangle$, say $r-r'=fh$, and comparing degrees
$0\le\deg(r-r')<n=\deg f\le\deg(fh)$ contradicts $r-r'\ne0$. Hence the $p^{n}$ representatives
give $p^{n}$ distinct cosets.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #answer}

$\ZZ_{p}[x]/\langle f(x)\rangle$ is a field with $p^{n}$ elements.

::: pf-proof

Steps [](#field){.pf-ref} and [](#count){.pf-ref} together.

:::
