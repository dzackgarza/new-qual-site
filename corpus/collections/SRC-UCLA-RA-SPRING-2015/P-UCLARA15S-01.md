::: {.problem}
Let $f\in L^1(\mathbb R)$. Show that
\[
\lim_{n\to\infty}\sum_{k=-n^2}^{n^2}\left|\int_{k/n}^{(k+1)/n}f(x)\,dx\right|=\int_{\mathbb R}|f(x)|\,dx.
\]
:::
