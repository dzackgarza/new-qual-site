::: {.problem}
Let $f\in L^1_{\mathrm{loc}}(\mathbb R)$ be $2\pi$-periodic. Show that linear combinations of the translates $f(x-a)$, $a\in\mathbb R$, are dense in $L^1((0,2\pi))$ if and only if each Fourier coefficient of $f$ is nonzero.
:::
