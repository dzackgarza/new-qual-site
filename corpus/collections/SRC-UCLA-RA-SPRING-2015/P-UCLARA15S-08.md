::: {.problem}
Let $f:\mathbb C\to\mathbb C$ be holomorphic and suppose
\[
\sup_{x\in\mathbb R}\bigl(|f(x)|^2+|f(ix)|^2\bigr)<\infty
\]
and
\[
|f(z)|\le e^{|z|}\qquad\text{for all }z\in\mathbb C.
\]
Deduce that $f(z)$ is constant.
:::
