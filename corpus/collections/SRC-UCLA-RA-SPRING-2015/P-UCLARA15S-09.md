::: {.problem}
Let
\[
\Omega=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|>1\text{ and }\operatorname{Re}z>-2\}.
\]
Suppose $u:\Omega\to\mathbb R$ is bounded, continuous, and harmonic on $\Omega$, with $u(z)=1$ when $|z|=1$ and $u(z)=0$ when $\operatorname{Re}z=-2$. Determine $u(2)$.
:::
