Problem 9. Let Ω = {z ∈ C : |z| > 1 and Re z > −2}. Suppose u : Ω → R is bounded, continuous, and harmonic on Ω and also that u(z) = 1 when |z| = 1 and that u(z) = 0 when Re(z) = −2. Determine u(2).
