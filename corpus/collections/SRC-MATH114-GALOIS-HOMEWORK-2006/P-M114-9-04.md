::: {.problem}
Let $F \subset E$ be an extension of finite fields.
Prove that
$$
\cardinality{E} = \cardinality{F}^{(E/F)}.
$$
:::

::: {.remark}
The source writes $(E/F)$ for the degree $[E : F]$ of a field extension $E/F$.
:::

::: {.solution}

Put
$$
q=\cardinality{F},
\qquad
n=[E:F].
$$

::: pf

::: pf-step

The field $E$ is an $n$-dimensional vector space over $F$.


::: pf-proof

This is the definition of the extension degree $[E:F]=n$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

Choose an $F$-basis
$$
e_1,\ldots,e_n
$$
of $E$. Every element $x\in E$ has a unique expression
$$
x=a_1e_1+\cdots+a_ne_n,
\qquad a_i\in F.
$$


::: pf-proof

Existence and uniqueness of such coordinates are exactly the spanning and linear-independence properties of a basis.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

Hence the coordinate map
$$
F^n\longrightarrow E,
\qquad
(a_1,\ldots,a_n)\longmapsto
\sum_{i=1}^na_ie_i
$$
is a bijection.


::: pf-proof

Surjectivity follows from the spanning property in step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref}, and injectivity follows from uniqueness of the basis coordinates.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

Since each coordinate has $q=\cardinality{F}$ possible values,
$$
\cardinality{F^n}=q^n.
$$


::: pf-proof

There are $q$ independent choices for each of the $n$ entries of an ordered tuple in $F^n$, so the multiplication principle gives $q^n$ tuples.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-5}

Therefore
$$
\boxed{\cardinality{E}=\cardinality{F}^{[E:F]}}.
$$


::: pf-proof

By the bijection in step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref}, $\cardinality{E}=\cardinality{F^n}$. Apply step [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} and substitute $q=\cardinality{F}$, $n=[E:F]$.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-5){.pf-ref} is the desired cardinality formula.

:::
