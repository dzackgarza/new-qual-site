::: {.problem}
Let $q = p ^ { k }$ for some prime $p , n$ be a number relatively prime to $p ,$ m be the minimal positive integer such that

$$
q ^ { m } \equiv 1 \mod n .
$$

Show that the Galois group of $x ^ { n } - 1$ over $\mathbb { F } _ { q }$ is isomorphic to $\mathbb { Z } _ { m }$
:::
