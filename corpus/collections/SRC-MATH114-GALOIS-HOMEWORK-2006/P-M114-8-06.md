::: {.problem}
Assume that $f(x) = g(x)h(x)$ for some separable polynomials $f(x), g(x), h(x) \in F[x]$.
Denote by $E_f$, $E_g$ and $E_h$ the splitting fields of the polynomials $f(x)$, $g(x)$ and $h(x)$ respectively.
Let
$$
(E_f/F) = (E_g/F)(E_h/F).
$$
Prove that the Galois group of $f(x)$ is isomorphic to the direct product of the Galois groups of $g(x)$ and $h(x)$.
:::

::: {.remark}
The source writes $(E/F)$ for the degree $[E : F]$ of a field extension $E/F$.
:::
