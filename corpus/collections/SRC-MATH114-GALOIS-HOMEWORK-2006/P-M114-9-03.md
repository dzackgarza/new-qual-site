::: {.problem}
Find the Galois groups of $x^6-1$ over $\FF_5 , \FF _ { 2 5 }$ and $\FF_{125}$
:::

::: {.solution}

For $q\in\{5,25,125\}$, let $K_q$ be the splitting field of $x^6-1$ over $\FF_q$.

::: pf

::: pf-step

Since the characteristic is $5$, the polynomial $x^6-1$ is separable over each base field.


::: pf-proof

Its derivative is $6x^5=x^5$ in characteristic $5$. A common root of $x^6-1$ and $x^5$ would have to be both nonzero and zero, which is impossible. Thus the polynomial has no repeated roots.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

For a finite field $\FF_q$, all sixth roots of unity lie in $\FF_{q^m}$ exactly when
$$
6\mid q^m-1.
$$


::: pf-proof

The multiplicative group $\FF_{q^m}^{\times}$ is cyclic of order $q^m-1$. It contains all sixth roots of unity exactly when it contains an element of order $6$, which is equivalent to $6\mid q^m-1$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

For $q=5$, the least such $m$ is $2$.


::: pf-proof

Modulo $6$,
$$
5\equiv-1,
\qquad
5^2\equiv1.
$$
Thus $6\nmid5-1=4$, while $6\mid5^2-1=24$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

Hence
$$
K_5=\FF_{25}
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\boxed{\operatorname{Gal}(K_5/\FF_5)\cong\ZZ_2}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} and step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} identify the splitting field as the quadratic extension $\FF_{5^2}$. Every finite-field extension $\FF_{q^m}/\FF_q$ is Galois with cyclic group of order $m$, generated by the Frobenius $x\mapsto x^q$. Here $m=2$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-5}

Over $\FF_{25}$, the polynomial already splits, so
$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Gal}(K_{25}/\FF_{25})=1}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Since $25-1=24$ is divisible by $6$, step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} with $m=1$ shows that all sixth roots of unity already lie in $\FF_{25}$.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

For $q=125$, the least extension degree is again $2$.


::: pf-proof

Modulo $6$,
$$
125\equiv5\equiv-1,
$$
so $6\nmid124$, whereas
$$
125^2\equiv1\pmod6.
$$
Thus step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} gives minimal degree $2$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-7}

Hence
$$
K_{125}=\FF_{125^2}=\FF_{15625}
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\boxed{\operatorname{Gal}(K_{125}/\FF_{125})\cong\ZZ_2}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Apply the finite-field Galois-group statement from step [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} with $q=125$ and extension degree $2$. The group is generated by $x\mapsto x^{125}$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-8}

Therefore the three requested groups are
$$
\boxed{\ZZ_2,\qquad 1,\qquad\ZZ_2}
$$
over $\FF_5,\FF_{25},\FF_{125}$, respectively.


::: pf-proof

Combine steps [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} and [](#s1-5){.pf-ref}, and [](#s1-7){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-8){.pf-ref} gives all three Galois groups.

:::
