::: problem
Let $p(x)\in F[x]$ be irreducible. Suppose $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are distinct roots of $p$ in an extension field. Must $F(\alpha)$ and $F(\beta)$ be isomorphic as extensions of $F$? Justify your answer.
:::
