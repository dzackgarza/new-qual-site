::: problem
Let $F$ be a field, and let $\theta$ lie in an extension field.
There is an isomorphism $F[\theta]\cong F[x]/A$ for an ideal $A\subseteq F[x]$.
Describe $A$ when $\theta$ is algebraic and when $\theta$ is transcendental.
:::
