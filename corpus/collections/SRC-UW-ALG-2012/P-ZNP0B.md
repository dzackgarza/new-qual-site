::: {.problem}
Prove that if $A$ is a finite abelian group, then $\Hom_\ZZ(A,\QQ/\ZZ)\cong\Ext^1_\ZZ(A,\ZZ)\cong A$.
(Here, $\Ext^1_\ZZ(-,-)$ is also sometimes written as $\Ext(-,-)$.)
:::
