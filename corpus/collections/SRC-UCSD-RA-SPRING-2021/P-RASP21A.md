::: problem
Let $A, B \subseteq \mathbb{R}$ be Lebesgue measurable with $m(B) = 0$.
Prove that either $m(A) = 0$ or $\{a + b : a \in A, b \in B\} \subseteq \mathbb{R} \setminus \mathbb{Q}$.
:::
