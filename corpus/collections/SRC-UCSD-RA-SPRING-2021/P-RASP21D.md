::: problem
Let $X, Y, Z$ be Banach spaces and $B : X \times Y \to Z$ be a map such that for any fixed $x \in X$, $B(x, \cdot) \in L(Y, Z)$ and for any fixed $y \in Y$, $B(\cdot, y) \in L(X, Z)$.
Show that there is $C$ such that $\|B(x,y)\| \leq C\|x\|\|y\|$.
:::
