::: {.problem}
Let
\[
A=\begin{pmatrix}2&2\\0&2\end{pmatrix}.
\]
(a) Compute $A^2$ and $A^{-1}$; (b) find the eigenvalues of $A^T+A$; (c) find the QR decomposition; (d) find the characteristic polynomial; (e) determine algebraic and geometric multiplicities and decide whether $A$ is diagonalizable.
:::
