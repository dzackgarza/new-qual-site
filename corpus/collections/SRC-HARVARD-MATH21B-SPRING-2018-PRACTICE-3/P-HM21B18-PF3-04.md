\[
x+y+z+u+v+w=6,
\]
\[
x-y+z-u+v-w=0,
\]
\[
x+y+z+u-v-w=2,
\]
(a) find all solutions; (b) find a basis for the kernel of the coefficient matrix; (c) find a basis for its image.
