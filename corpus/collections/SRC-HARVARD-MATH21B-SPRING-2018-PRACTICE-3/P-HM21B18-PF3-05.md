::: {.problem}
Using least squares, find the hyperbola
$$
xy+ax+by=1
$$
that best fits
$$
(1,0),(-1,1),(1,2),(1,-1),(2,1).
$$
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: pf-step

The five data points give the overdetermined linear system
$$
M\begin{pmatrix}a\\b\end{pmatrix}=c,
$$
where
$$
M=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&0\\
-1&1\\
1&2\\
1&-1\\
2&1
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
c=
\begin{pmatrix}
1\\2\\-1\\2\\-1
\end{pmatrix}.
$$


::: pf-proof

At a data point $(x,y)$, the equation
$$
xy+ax+by=1
$$
is equivalent to
$$
ax+by=1-xy.
$$
Substitution of the five given points produces the five rows of $M$ and the
five entries of $c$.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

The normal equations are
$$
\begin{pmatrix}
8&2\\
2&7
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}a\\b\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}-2\\-3\end{pmatrix}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Direct multiplication gives
$$
M^TM=
\begin{pmatrix}
8&2\\
2&7
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
M^Tc=
\begin{pmatrix}-2\\-3\end{pmatrix}.
$$
The least-squares minimizer satisfies $M^TM(a,b)^T=M^Tc$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

The unique least-squares parameters are
$$
a=-\frac2{13},
\qquad
b=-\frac5{13}.
$$


::: pf-proof

The determinant of $M^TM$ is
$$
8\cdot7-2^2=52\ne0,
$$
so the normal equations have a unique solution. Moreover,
$$
(M^TM)^{-1}
=
\frac1{52}
\begin{pmatrix}
7&-2\\
-2&8
\end{pmatrix}.
$$
Hence
$$
\begin{pmatrix}a\\b\end{pmatrix}
=
\frac1{52}
\begin{pmatrix}
7&-2\\
-2&8
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}-2\\-3\end{pmatrix}
=
\frac1{52}
\begin{pmatrix}-8\\-20\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}-2/13\\-5/13\end{pmatrix}.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

The best-fitting hyperbola is
$$
\boxed{
xy-\frac2{13}x-\frac5{13}y=1.}
$$


::: pf-proof

Substitute the parameters from step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} into the model
$xy+ax+by=1$.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} gives the requested least-squares hyperbola.
