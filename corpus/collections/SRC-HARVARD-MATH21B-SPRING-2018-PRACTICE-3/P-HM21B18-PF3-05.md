::: {.problem}
Using least squares, find the hyperbola
\[
xy+ax+by=1
\]
that best fits
\[
(1,0),(-1,1),(1,2),(1,-1),(2,1).
\]
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. The five data points give the overdetermined linear system
\[
M\begin{pmatrix}a\\b\end{pmatrix}=c,
\]
where
\[
M=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&0\\
-1&1\\
1&2\\
1&-1\\
2&1
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
c=
\begin{pmatrix}
1\\2\\-1\\2\\-1
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

::: {.proof}
At a data point $(x,y)$, the equation
\[
xy+ax+by=1
\]
is equivalent to
\[
ax+by=1-xy.
\]
Substitution of the five given points produces the five rows of $M$ and the
five entries of $c$.
:::

<1>2. The normal equations are
\[
\begin{pmatrix}
8&2\\
2&7
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}a\\b\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}-2\\-3\end{pmatrix}.
\]

::: {.proof}
Direct multiplication gives
\[
M^TM=
\begin{pmatrix}
8&2\\
2&7
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
M^Tc=
\begin{pmatrix}-2\\-3\end{pmatrix}.
\]
The least-squares minimizer satisfies $M^TM(a,b)^T=M^Tc$.
:::

<1>3. The unique least-squares parameters are
\[
a=-\frac2{13},
\qquad
b=-\frac5{13}.
\]

::: {.proof}
The determinant of $M^TM$ is
\[
8\cdot7-2^2=52\ne0,
\]
so the normal equations have a unique solution. Moreover,
\[
(M^TM)^{-1}
=
\frac1{52}
\begin{pmatrix}
7&-2\\
-2&8
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Hence
\[
\begin{pmatrix}a\\b\end{pmatrix}
=
\frac1{52}
\begin{pmatrix}
7&-2\\
-2&8
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}-2\\-3\end{pmatrix}
=
\frac1{52}
\begin{pmatrix}-8\\-20\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}-2/13\\-5/13\end{pmatrix}.
\]
:::

<1>4. The best-fitting hyperbola is
\[
\boxed{
xy-\frac2{13}x-\frac5{13}y=1.}
\]

::: {.proof}
Substitute the parameters from step <1>3 into the model
$xy+ax+by=1$.
:::

<1>5. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>4 gives the requested least-squares hyperbola.
:::
