::: {.problem}
Let
\[
f(x)=
\begin{cases}
1,&\pi/2<x<\pi,\\
0,&-\pi/2<x<\pi/2,\\
-1,&-\pi<x<-\pi/2.
\end{cases}
\]
(a) Find its Fourier series on $[-\pi,\pi]$. (b) If $a_0,a_n,b_n$ are its Fourier coefficients, compute
\[
\sum_{n=0}^\infty a_n^2+\sum_{n=1}^\infty b_n^2.
\]
:::
