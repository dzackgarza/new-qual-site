::: {.problem}
Let
\[
A=\begin{pmatrix}2&3&1\\3&2&1\\0&0&3\end{pmatrix}.
\]
(a) Find a diagonal matrix $B$ similar to $A$; (b) find $S$ with $B=S^{-1}AS$; (c) solve $v(t+1)=Av(t)$ with $v(0)=(11,11,2)^T$; (d) decide whether the discrete system is stable.
:::
