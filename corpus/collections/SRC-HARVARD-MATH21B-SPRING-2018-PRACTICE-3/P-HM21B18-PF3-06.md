::: {.problem}
Let
$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}2&3&1\\3&2&1\\0&0&3\end{pmatrix}.
$$

(a) Find a diagonal matrix $B$ similar to $A$;

(b) find $S$ with $B=S^{-1}AS$;

(c) solve $v(t+1)=Av(t)$ with $v(0)=(11,11,2)^T$;

(d) decide whether the discrete system is stable.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

Three eigenpairs of $A$ are
$$
5:\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
-1:\begin{pmatrix}-1\\1\\0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
3:\begin{pmatrix}-1\\-1\\2\end{pmatrix}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Direct multiplication gives
$$
A\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\0\end{pmatrix}
=
5\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\0\end{pmatrix},
$$
$$
A\begin{pmatrix}-1\\1\\0\end{pmatrix}
=
-\begin{pmatrix}-1\\1\\0\end{pmatrix},
$$
and
$$
A\begin{pmatrix}-1\\-1\\2\end{pmatrix}
=
3\begin{pmatrix}-1\\-1\\2\end{pmatrix}.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

A diagonal matrix similar to $A$ is
$$
\boxed{
B=\begin{pmatrix}
5&0&0\\
0&-1&0\\
0&0&3
\end{pmatrix}.}
$$


::: pf-proof

The eigenvectors in step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} are linearly independent: the matrix having
them as columns has determinant $4\ne0$. Therefore they form an eigenbasis,
and the diagonal entries of the corresponding diagonal form are the three
eigenvalues from step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

One matrix satisfying $B=S^{-1}AS$ is
$$
\boxed{
S=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&-1&-1\\
1&1&-1\\
0&0&2
\end{pmatrix}.}
$$


::: pf-proof

The columns of $S$ are the three eigenvectors in step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref}, in the same order
as the diagonal entries of $B$ in step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref}. Thus
$$
AS=SB.
$$
Since $\det S=4\ne0$, multiplication on the left by $S^{-1}$ gives
$S^{-1}AS=B$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

The initial vector decomposes as
$$
\begin{pmatrix}11\\11\\2\end{pmatrix}
=
12\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\0\end{pmatrix}
+\begin{pmatrix}-1\\-1\\2\end{pmatrix}.
$$


::: pf-proof

The right-hand side equals
$$
\begin{pmatrix}12\\12\\0\end{pmatrix}
+
\begin{pmatrix}-1\\-1\\2\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}11\\11\\2\end{pmatrix}.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-5}

The solution of the discrete system is
$$
\boxed{
v(t)
=
12\,5^t\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\0\end{pmatrix}
+3^t\begin{pmatrix}-1\\-1\\2\end{pmatrix},
\qquad t=0,1,2,\ldots.}
$$


::: pf-proof

For an eigenvector $u$ with eigenvalue $\lambda$,
$$
A^tu=\lambda^tu.
$$
Apply this to the decomposition in step [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} using the eigenpairs from step
[](#s1-1){.pf-ref}. At $t=0$ the formula gives the required initial vector, and
multiplication by $A$ advances the formula from time $t$ to time $t+1$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-6}

The discrete system is
$$
\boxed{\text{not stable}.}
$$


::: pf-proof

Step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} exhibits the eigenvalue $5$, whose modulus exceeds $1$. Along its
eigenvector, the orbit satisfies
$$
A^t\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\0\end{pmatrix}
=
5^t\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\0\end{pmatrix},
$$
so arbitrarily small initial data in that eigendirection grow without bound.
Thus the zero equilibrium is not stable. In particular, the orbit in step
[](#s1-5){.pf-ref} also grows without bound.

:::

respectively.
