::: {.problem}
Suppose that $f \colon \RR^{2} \to \RR$ is linear.
If $f(1, 1) = 1$ and $f(-1, 0) = 2$, what is $f(3, 5)$?
:::

::: {.solution}
Since $(3,5)=5(1,1)+2(-1,0)$, linearity gives $\boxed{f(3,5)=5f(1,1)+2f(-1,0)=9}$.
:::
