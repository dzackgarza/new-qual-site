::: {.problem}
Suppose that $f : \mathbb { R } ^ { 2 } \to \mathbb { R }$ is linear.
If $f ( 1 , 1 ) = 1$ and $f ( - 1 , 0 ) = 2$ , what is $f ( 3 , 5 ) ?$
:::

::: {.solution}
By linearity $\Big | f ( 3 , 5 ) = f ( 5 \cdot ( 1 , 1 ) + 2 \cdot ( - 1 , 0 ) ) = 5 f ( 1 , 1 ) + 2 f ( - 1 , 0 ) = 9 .$
:::
