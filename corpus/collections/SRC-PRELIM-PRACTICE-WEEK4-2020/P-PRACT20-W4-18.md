::: {.problem}
If $A,B$ are subspaces of $V$, which of the following are necessarily subspaces of $V$?

(a) $A+B=\{x+y:x\in A,\ y\in B\}$
(b) $A\cup B$
(c) $A\cap B$
(d) $A^c=\{x\in V:x\notin A\}$
:::
