::: {.problem}
Let $M_n$ be the vector space of complex $n\times n$ matrices. For $A\in M_n$, define
\[
T_A(X)=AX-XA.
\]
Prove that
\[
\operatorname{rank}T_A\le n^2-n.
\]
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

The rank of the commutator map is invariant under similarity of
$A$.

::: pf-proof

Suppose
$$
J=P^{-1}AP.
$$
Define the vector-space automorphism
$$
\Phi:M_n\longrightarrow M_n,
\qquad
\Phi(X)=P^{-1}XP.
$$
Then
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Phi(T_A(X))
&=
P^{-1}(AX-XA)P\\
&=
J\Phi(X)-\Phi(X)J\\
&=
T_J(\Phi(X)).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus
$$
\Phi\circ T_A
=
T_J\circ\Phi.
$$
Since $\Phi$ is invertible, $T_A$ and $T_J$ have the same rank.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

Put $A$ in Jordan normal form
$$
J
=
\operatorname{diag}(J_1,\ldots,J_r),
$$
where
$$
J_i
=
\lambda_i I_{m_i}+N_i
$$
is a Jordan block of size $m_i$. For each $i$ and
$$
0\leq k<m_i,
$$
let $X_{i,k}$ be the block-diagonal matrix whose $i$-th diagonal block
is $N_i^k$ and whose other blocks are zero. Then
$$
X_{i,k}\in\ker T_J.
$$

::: pf-proof

On the $i$-th block,
$$
J_iN_i^k
=
(\lambda_iI_{m_i}+N_i)N_i^k
=
N_i^k(\lambda_iI_{m_i}+N_i)
=
N_i^kJ_i.
$$
All other blocks of $X_{i,k}$ are zero, so $JX_{i,k}=X_{i,k}J$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

The matrices
$$
X_{i,k},
\qquad
1\leq i\leq r,
\quad
0\leq k<m_i,
$$
are linearly independent.

::: pf-proof

Matrices belonging to different values of $i$ have disjoint block
support. For a fixed $i$, the matrices
$$
I_{m_i},N_i,N_i^2,\ldots,N_i^{m_i-1}
$$
are linearly independent: for the standard nilpotent Jordan block,
$N_i^k$ has ones precisely on the $k$-th superdiagonal. These supports
are pairwise disjoint as $k$ varies, so every coefficient in a linear
relation must vanish.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s4}

One has
$$
\dim\ker T_J\geq n.
$$

::: pf-proof

By steps [](#s2){.pf-ref} and [](#s3){.pf-ref}, the kernel contains
$$
\sum_{i=1}^r m_i
$$
linearly independent matrices. The Jordan block sizes partition the
dimension, so
$$
\sum_{i=1}^r m_i=n.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s5}

Therefore
$$
\boxed{
\operatorname{rank}T_A\leq n^2-n
}.
$$

::: pf-proof

The vector space $M_n$ has dimension $n^2$. By step [](#s1){.pf-ref} and
rank--nullity applied to $T_J$,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{rank}T_A
&=
\operatorname{rank}T_J\\
&=
n^2-\dim\ker T_J\\
&\leq
n^2-n
\end{aligned}
$$
by step [](#s4){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s5){.pf-ref} is the required rank bound.
