:::{.problem}
Let $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ be continuous and periodic with period $1$.
Prove that for every $c\in\mathbb R$ there exists $x_0\in\mathbb R$ such that
\[
f(x_0+c)=f(x_0).
\]
:::
