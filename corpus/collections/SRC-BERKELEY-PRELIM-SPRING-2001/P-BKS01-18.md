:::{.problem}
Let $f$ be entire and suppose that for some positive integer $k$ and constant $A>0$,
\[
\int_0^{2\pi}|f(re^{i\theta})|^2\,d\theta\le Ar^{2k}
\qquad(r>0).
\]
Prove that $f$ is a constant multiple of $z^k$.
:::
