::: {.problem}
Prove that
\[
16x^5-125x^4+50x^3-100x^2+75x+25
\]
is irreducible over $\mathbb Q$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Set
$$
f(x)
\coloneqq
16x^5-125x^4+50x^3-100x^2+75x+25.
$$

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

It is enough to prove that the reduction of $f$ modulo $3$ is
irreducible in $\FF_3[x]$.

::: pf-proof

The coefficients of $f$ have greatest common divisor $1$, so $f$ is
primitive in $\ZZ[x]$. Its leading coefficient is
$$
16\equiv1\pmod3,
$$
so reduction modulo $3$ preserves its degree. By Gauss's lemma and the
standard reduction criterion, irreducibility of the reduction modulo
$3$ implies irreducibility of $f$ over $\QQ$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

The reduction
$$
g(x)
\coloneqq
x^5+x^4+2x^3+2x^2+1
\in\FF_3[x]
$$
has no linear factor.

::: pf-proof

The three possible roots are $0,1,-1$, and
$$
g(0)=1,
\qquad
g(1)=1,
\qquad
g(-1)=1
$$
in $\FF_3$. Thus $g$ has no root in $\FF_3$, hence no linear factor.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

The polynomial $g$ has no irreducible quadratic factor.

::: pf-proof

A monic quadratic
$$
x^2+bx+c
$$
over $\FF_3$ is irreducible exactly when its discriminant
$$
b^2-c
$$
is the nonsquare $2$. Thus the monic irreducible quadratics are exactly
$$
q_1=x^2+1,
\qquad
q_2=x^2+x+2,
\qquad
q_3=x^2+2x+2.
$$
Direct reduction gives
$$
g\equiv2x\pmod{q_1},
\qquad
g\equiv x\pmod{q_2},
\qquad
g\equiv2x+1\pmod{q_3}.
$$
All three remainders are nonzero, so none of $q_1,q_2,q_3$ divides
$g$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s4}

The polynomial $g$ is irreducible in $\FF_3[x]$.

::: pf-proof

If the degree-$5$ polynomial $g$ were reducible, then in a factorization
into monic irreducibles at least one factor would have degree at most
$2$. Step [](#s2){.pf-ref} excludes irreducible factors of degree $1$, and step
[](#s3){.pf-ref} excludes irreducible factors of degree $2$. Therefore no such
factorization exists.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s5}

The polynomial $f$ is irreducible over $\QQ$.

::: pf-proof

This follows from steps [](#s1){.pf-ref} and [](#s4){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s5){.pf-ref} is the required conclusion.

:::
