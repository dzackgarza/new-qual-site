:::{.problem}
Let $f_n:[0,1]\to[0,1]$ satisfy
\[
|f_n(x)-f_n(y)|\le |x-y|
\]
whenever $|x-y|\ge1/n$.
Prove that $(f_n)$ has a uniformly convergent subsequence.
:::
