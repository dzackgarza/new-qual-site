:::{.problem}
Let $S$ be a real special orthogonal $n\times n$ matrix: $S^TS=I$ and $\det S=1$.

1. Prove that if $n$ is odd, then $1$ is an eigenvalue of $S$.
2. Show that if $n$ is even, then $1$ need not be an eigenvalue.
:::
