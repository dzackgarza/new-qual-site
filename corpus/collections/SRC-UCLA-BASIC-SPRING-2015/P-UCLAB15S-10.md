::: {.problem}
Let
\[
M=\begin{pmatrix}A&B\\C&D\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
M^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}P&Q\\R&S\end{pmatrix},
\]
where $A,\ldots,S$ are $k\times k$ matrices.
Show that
\[
\det(M)\det(S)=\det(A).
\]
:::
