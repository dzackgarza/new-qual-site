::: {.problem}
Let $f:[0,\infty)\to[0,\infty)$ be continuous with $f(0)=0$. Show that if
\[
f(t)\le 1+\frac1{10}f(t)^2\qquad\text{for all }t\in[0,\infty),
\]
then $f$ is uniformly bounded throughout $[0,\infty)$.
:::
