::: {.problem}
Let
\[
M=\begin{pmatrix}3&5\\1&-1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

1. Compute $\exp(M)$.

2. Does there exist a real $2\times2$ matrix $A$ such that $M=\exp(A)$?
:::
