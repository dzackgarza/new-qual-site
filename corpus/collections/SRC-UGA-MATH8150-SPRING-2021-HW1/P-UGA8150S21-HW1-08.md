::: {.problem}
Let f (z) be analytic in a domain.
Prove that f (z) is a constant if it satisfies any of the following conditions:

(a) |f (z)| is constant;

(b) $\operatorname { R e } ( f ( z ) )$ is constant;

(c) arg(f (z)) is constant;

(d) f (z) is analytic;

How do you generalize (a) and (b)?
:::
