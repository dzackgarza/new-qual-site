::: {.problem}
Let $f(z)$ be analytic.
Show that $\overline{f(\bar{z})}$ is also analytic.
:::

::: {.solution}

Suppose $f$ is analytic on a domain $D$. Define the reflected domain
$$
\overline D
=
\{z\in\CC:\overline z\in D\},
$$
and define
$$
g(z)
=
\overline{f(\overline z)}
\qquad
(z\in\overline D).
$$

::: pf

::: pf-step

The set $\overline D$ is a domain.


::: pf-proof

Complex conjugation
$$
z\longmapsto\overline z
$$
is a homeomorphism of $\CC$. Hence it sends the open connected set
$D$ to the open connected reflected set $\overline D$.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Fix
$$
z_0\in\overline D
$$
and set
$$
w_0=\overline{z_0}\in D.
$$


::: pf-proof

This is exactly the defining property of $\overline D$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

For sufficiently small $h\ne0$,
$$
\frac{g(z_0+h)-g(z_0)}{h}
=
\frac{
\overline{
f(w_0+\overline h)-f(w_0)
}
}{h}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Since
$$
\overline{z_0+h}
=
\overline{z_0}+\overline h
=
w_0+\overline h,
$$
substitute the definition of $g$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

The quotient in step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} can be rewritten as
$$
\boxed{
\frac{g(z_0+h)-g(z_0)}{h}
=
\overline{
\frac{
f(w_0+\overline h)-f(w_0)
}{
\overline h
}
}}.
$$


::: pf-proof

For $h\ne0$,
$$
\overline{
\frac{A}{\overline h}
}
=
\frac{\overline A}{h},
$$
because
$$
\overline{\overline h}=h.
$$
Apply this with
$$
A=f(w_0+\overline h)-f(w_0).
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-5}

As
$$
h\to0,
$$
one also has
$$
\overline h\to0.
$$


::: pf-proof

Complex conjugation preserves modulus:
$$
\abs{\overline h}=\abs{h}.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-6}

Since $f$ is analytic at $w_0$,
$$
\lim_{h\to0}
\frac{
f(w_0+\overline h)-f(w_0)
}{
\overline h
}
=
f'(w_0).
$$


::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1-5){.pf-ref}, the increment $\overline h$ approaches $0$. The defining
difference quotient for $f'(w_0)$ therefore applies.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-7}

Taking conjugates in step [](#s1-6){.pf-ref},
$$
\lim_{h\to0}
\overline{
\frac{
f(w_0+\overline h)-f(w_0)
}{
\overline h
}
}
=
\overline{f'(w_0)}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Complex conjugation is continuous.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-8}

Combining steps [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} and [](#s1-7){.pf-ref},
$$
\boxed{
g'(z_0)
=
\overline{f'(\overline{z_0})}}.
$$


::: pf-proof

The left-hand side is the limit of the difference quotient for $g$ at
$z_0$, and
$$
w_0=\overline{z_0}.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-9}

Since $z_0\in\overline D$ was arbitrary,
$$
\boxed{
g(z)
=
\overline{f(\overline z)}
\text{ is analytic on }\overline D}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Step [](#s1-8){.pf-ref} proves the existence of the complex derivative at every point of
$\overline D$.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

In particular, if $f$ is entire, then
$$
\boxed{
\overline{f(\overline z)}
}
$$
is entire.


::: pf-proof

For $D=\CC$,
$$
\overline D=\CC.
$$
Apply step [](#s1-9){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-9){.pf-ref} proves the required analyticity.

:::
