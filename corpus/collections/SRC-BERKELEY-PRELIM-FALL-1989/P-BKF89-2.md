::: {.problem}
Let $f:[0,1]\to\mathbb R$ be continuously differentiable with $f(0)=0$. Suppose there is a constant $M>0$ such that
\[
0\le f'(x)\le Mf(x)
\qquad(0\le x\le1).
\]
Prove that $f(x)=0$ for all $x\in[0,1]$.
:::
