::: {.problem}
Let $X\subset\mathbb R^n$ be closed and let $r>0$. Define
\[
Y=\{y\in\mathbb R^n:|x-y|=r\text{ for some }x\in X\}.
\]
Show that $Y$ is closed.
:::
