::: {.problem}
Let $F$ be a field and let $R$ be a subring of $F[x]$ with $F\subset R$. Prove that there exist finitely many polynomials $f_1,\ldots,f_n\in F[x]$ such that
\[
R=F[f_1,\ldots,f_n].
\]
:::
