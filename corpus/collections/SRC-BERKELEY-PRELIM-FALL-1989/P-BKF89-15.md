::: {.problem}
Let $G_n$ be the free group on $n$ generators. Show that $G_2$ and $G_3$ are not isomorphic.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $C_2$ denote the cyclic group of order $2$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

For every positive integer $n$,
$$
\abs{\Hom(G_n,C_2)}=2^n.
$$

::: pf-proof

Fix a free basis $x_1,\ldots,x_n$ of $G_n$. By the universal property of the free group, a homomorphism
$$
\varphi:G_n\to C_2
$$
is determined uniquely by the freely chosen values
$$
\varphi(x_1),\ldots,\varphi(x_n)\in C_2.
$$
Each generator has two possible images, independently of the others, so there are exactly $2^n$ homomorphisms.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

Isomorphic groups have the same number of homomorphisms to $C_2$.

::: pf-proof

If
$$
\psi:H\xrightarrow{\sim}K
$$
is an isomorphism, then precomposition with $\psi$ gives a bijection
$$
\Hom(K,C_2)\longrightarrow\Hom(H,C_2),
\qquad
\alpha\longmapsto\alpha\circ\psi.
$$
Its inverse is precomposition with $\psi^{-1}$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

The groups $G_2$ and $G_3$ are not isomorphic.

::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1){.pf-ref},
$$
\abs{\Hom(G_2,C_2)}=2^2=4,
\qquad
\abs{\Hom(G_3,C_2)}=2^3=8.
$$
If $G_2\cong G_3$, step [](#s2){.pf-ref} would force these cardinalities to be equal, a contradiction. Thus
$$
\boxed{G_2\not\cong G_3}.
$$

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s3){.pf-ref} proves the required non-isomorphism.

:::
