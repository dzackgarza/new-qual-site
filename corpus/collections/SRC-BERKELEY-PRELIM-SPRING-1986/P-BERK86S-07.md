::: {.problem}
For a real parameter $\lambda$, find all solutions on $0\le x\le1$ of
\[
\varphi(x)=e^x+\lambda\int_0^x e^{x-y}\varphi(y)\,dy
\]
and
\[
\psi(x)=e^x+\lambda\int_0^1 e^{x-y}\psi(y)\,dy.
\]
:::
