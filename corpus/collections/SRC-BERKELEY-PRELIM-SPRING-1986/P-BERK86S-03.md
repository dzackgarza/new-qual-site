:::{.problem}
Let $C$ be a positively oriented simple closed contour enclosing the points $0,1,\dots,k$. Evaluate, for $k=0,1,2,\dots$,
\[
I_k=\int_C\frac{dz}{z(z-1)\cdots(z-k)}
\]
and
\[
J_k=\int_C\frac{(z-1)\cdots(z-k)}{z}\,dz.
\]
:::
