:::{.problem}
Let $f:(0,\infty)\to(0,\infty)$ be differentiable. Prove that for every $x>0$ the finite nonzero limit
\[
\lim_{\delta\to0}
\left(\frac{f(x+\delta x)}{f(x)}\right)^{1/\delta}
\]
exists.
:::
