::: {.problem}
Let $T:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R^2$ be an isometry for the Euclidean metric. Prove that
\[
T(x)=a+U(x)
\]
for some $a\in\mathbb R^2$ and some orthogonal linear transformation $U$.
:::
