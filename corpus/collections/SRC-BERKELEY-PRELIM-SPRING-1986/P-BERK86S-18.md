::: {.problem}
Let $f,g_1,g_2,\dots$ be entire. Suppose
\[
|g_n^{(k)}(0)|\le|f^{(k)}(0)|
\]
for all $n,k$, and suppose
\[
\lim_{n\to\infty}g_n^{(k)}(0)
\]
exists for every $k$. Prove that $(g_n)$ converges uniformly on compact subsets of $\mathbb C$, and that its limit is entire.
:::
