::: {.problem}
Let $H_1\le\mathbb Z^2$ be generated by $(1,2)$ and $(4,1)$, and let $H_2\le\mathbb Z^2$ be generated by $(3,2)$ and $(1,3)$. Are the quotient groups
\[
\mathbb Z^2/H_1
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\mathbb Z^2/H_2
\]
isomorphic?
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $e_1=(1,0)$ and $e_2=(0,1)$.

<1>1. The quotient $\ZZ^2/H_1$ is generated by the class of $e_2$, and
that class has order dividing $7$.

::: {.proof}
In $\ZZ^2/H_1$, the generator $(1,2)=e_1+2e_2$ of $H_1$ gives
$$
[e_1]=-2[e_2].
$$
Hence every class is a multiple of $[e_2]$. The second generator
$(4,1)=4e_1+e_2$ then gives
$$
0
=
4[e_1]+[e_2]
=
-8[e_2]+[e_2]
=
-7[e_2].
$$
Thus $7[e_2]=0$.
:::

<1>2. In fact,
$$
\ZZ^2/H_1\cong\ZZ/7\ZZ.
$$

::: {.proof}
Define
$$
\phi_1:\ZZ^2\to\ZZ/7\ZZ,
\qquad
\phi_1(m,n)=5m+n\pmod7.
$$
Then
$$
\phi_1(1,2)=5+2=0
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\phi_1(4,1)=20+1=0
$$
in $\ZZ/7\ZZ$. Thus $H_1\subseteq\ker\phi_1$, so $\phi_1$ induces
$$
\overline{\phi}_1:\ZZ^2/H_1\to\ZZ/7\ZZ.
$$
Since
$$
\overline{\phi}_1([e_2])=1,
$$
this induced map is surjective. Step <1>1 shows that its domain is cyclic
generated by an element of order dividing $7$, while surjectivity forces
that generator to have order at least $7$. Hence its order is exactly
$7$, and the induced map is an isomorphism.
:::

<1>3. The quotient $\ZZ^2/H_2$ is generated by the class of $e_2$, and
that class has order dividing $7$.

::: {.proof}
In $\ZZ^2/H_2$, the relation
$$
e_1+3e_2=0
$$
from the generator $(1,3)$ gives
$$
[e_1]=-3[e_2].
$$
Thus $[e_2]$ generates the quotient. The other generator
$(3,2)=3e_1+2e_2$ gives
$$
0
=
3[e_1]+2[e_2]
=
-9[e_2]+2[e_2]
=
-7[e_2].
$$
Therefore $7[e_2]=0$.
:::

<1>4. In fact,
$$
\ZZ^2/H_2\cong\ZZ/7\ZZ.
$$

::: {.proof}
Define
$$
\phi_2:\ZZ^2\to\ZZ/7\ZZ,
\qquad
\phi_2(m,n)=4m+n\pmod7.
$$
Then
$$
\phi_2(3,2)=12+2=0
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\phi_2(1,3)=4+3=0
$$
in $\ZZ/7\ZZ$. Hence $H_2\subseteq\ker\phi_2$, and $\phi_2$ induces a
surjection
$$
\overline{\phi}_2:\ZZ^2/H_2\to\ZZ/7\ZZ
$$
because $\overline{\phi}_2([e_2])=1$. Step <1>3 now shows, exactly as in
step <1>2, that $[e_2]$ has order exactly $7$ and the induced map is an
isomorphism.
:::

<1>5. Therefore
$$
\boxed{\ZZ^2/H_1\cong\ZZ^2/H_2}.
$$

::: {.proof}
By steps <1>2 and <1>4, both groups are isomorphic to $\ZZ/7\ZZ$.
:::

<1>6. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>5 answers the question.
