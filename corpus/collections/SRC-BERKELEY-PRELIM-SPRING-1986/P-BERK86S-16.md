::: {.problem}
Let
\[
R_1=\mathbb F_p[x]/(x^2-2),
\qquad
R_2=\mathbb F_p[x]/(x^2-3).
\]
Determine whether $R_1$ and $R_2$ are isomorphic in each of the cases
\[
p=2,\qquad p=5,\qquad p=11.
\]
:::
