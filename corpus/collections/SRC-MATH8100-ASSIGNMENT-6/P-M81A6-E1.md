::: problem
Use the specified repeated integral involving $xe^{-x^2(1+y^2)}$ to prove that
$$
\int_0^\infty e^{-x^2}\,dx=\frac{\sqrt\pi}{2},
$$
and hence that $\int_{\mathbb R}e^{-\pi x^2}\,dx=1$.
:::
