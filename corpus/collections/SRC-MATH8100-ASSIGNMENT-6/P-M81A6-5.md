::: problem
Compute the Fourier transforms of the indicator of $[-1/2,1/2]$, the tent function $(1-|x|)_+$, and $\pi^{-1}(1+x^2)^{-1}$.
Also find the Fourier transform of $(\sin(\pi x)/(\pi x))^2$, with full justification.
:::

::: solution
Write
\[
D(x)=\mathbf 1_{[-1/2,1/2]}(x),
\qquad
F(x)=(1-|x|)_+,
\qquad
P(x)=\frac1{\pi(1+x^2)}.
\]

<1>1. The Dirichlet kernel profile.
::: proof
For $\xi\ne0$,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\widehat D(\xi)
&=\int_{-1/2}^{1/2}e^{-2\pi i x\xi}\,dx\\
&=\frac{e^{-\pi i\xi}-e^{\pi i\xi}}{-2\pi i\xi}
=\frac{\sin(\pi\xi)}{\pi\xi}.
\end{aligned}
\]
At $\xi=0$ the value is $1$, which is also the continuous extension of the displayed formula.
:::

<1>2. The tent function.
::: proof
For every $x$,
\[
(D*D)(x)
=m\bigl([-1/2,1/2]\cap[x-1/2,x+1/2]\bigr)
=(1-|x|)_+
=F(x).
\]
Therefore the convolution theorem gives
\[
\widehat F(\xi)
=\widehat D(\xi)^2
=\left(\frac{\sin(\pi\xi)}{\pi\xi}\right)^2.
\]
:::

<1>3. The Fourier transform of the squared sinc function.
::: proof
Set
\[
f(x)=\left(\frac{\sin(\pi x)}{\pi x}\right)^2.
\]
By <1>2, $f=\widehat F$. Moreover $F\in L^1(\mathbb R)$ and $f\in L^1(\mathbb R)$: near the origin $f$ is bounded, while for $|x|\ge1$,
\[
0\le f(x)\le \frac1{\pi^2x^2}.
\]
Thus the Fourier inversion theorem applies to $F$ and yields
\[
\widehat f(\xi)
=\widehat{\widehat F}(\xi)
=F(-\xi).
\]
Since $F$ is even,
\[
\boxed{\widehat f(\xi)=(1-|\xi|)_+.}
\]
:::

<1>4. The Poisson kernel.
::: proof
Let
\[
q(\xi)=e^{-2\pi|\xi|}.
\]
Then $q\in L^1(\mathbb R)$ and, for every $x\in\mathbb R$,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\int_{\mathbb R}q(\xi)e^{2\pi i x\xi}\,d\xi
&=2\int_0^\infty e^{-2\pi\xi}\cos(2\pi x\xi)\,d\xi\\
&=2\,\frac{2\pi}{(2\pi)^2+(2\pi x)^2}\\
&=\frac1{\pi(1+x^2)}
=P(x).
\end{aligned}
\]
Since also $P\in L^1(\mathbb R)$, Fourier inversion gives
\[
\boxed{\widehat P(\xi)=e^{-2\pi|\xi|}.}
\]
:::
:::
