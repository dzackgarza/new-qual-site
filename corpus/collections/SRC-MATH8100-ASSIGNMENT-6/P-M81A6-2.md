::: problem
1. Prove that $\widehat{f*g}=\widehat f\,\widehat g$ for $f,g\in L^1(\mathbb R^n)$.

2. Deduce that convolution is commutative and associative almost everywhere.

3. Prove that $L^1(\mathbb R^n)$ has no convolution identity.
:::
