::: problem
1. If $xf(x)\in L^1(\mathbb R)$, prove that $(\widehat f)'=-2\pi i\widehat{xf}$.
2. If $f\in C_0^1(\mathbb R)$ and $f'\in L^1$, prove that $\widehat{f'}(\xi)=2\pi i\xi\widehat f(\xi)$.
3. Prove that the Fourier transform of $G(x)=e^{-\pi x^2}$ equals $G$.
:::
