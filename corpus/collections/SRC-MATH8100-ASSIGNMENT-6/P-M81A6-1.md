::: problem
Prove that $\widehat f(\xi)\to0$ as $|\xi|\to\infty$ for every $f\in L^1(\mathbb R^n)$.
:::

::: solution
Fix $\xi\ne0$ and set
\[
h=\frac{\xi}{2|\xi|^2}.
\]
Then
\[
e^{2\pi i h\cdot\xi}=e^{\pi i}=-1.
\]
By the change of variables $x=y+h$,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\widehat f(\xi)
&=\int_{\mathbb R^n}f(y+h)e^{-2\pi i(y+h)\cdot\xi}\,dy\\
&=-\int_{\mathbb R^n}f(y+h)e^{-2\pi i y\cdot\xi}\,dy.
\end{aligned}
\]
Adding this identity to the defining formula for $\widehat f(\xi)$ gives
\[
2\widehat f(\xi)
=\int_{\mathbb R^n}\bigl(f(y)-f(y+h)\bigr)e^{-2\pi i y\cdot\xi}\,dy.
\]
Hence
\[
|\widehat f(\xi)|
\le \frac12\|f-\tau_{-h}f\|_1,
\]
where $(\tau_{-h}f)(y)=f(y+h)$.

Translations are continuous in $L^1(\mathbb R^n)$: if $g\in C_c(\mathbb R^n)$ approximates $f$ in $L^1$, then
\[
\|\tau_{-h}f-f\|_1
\le 2\|f-g\|_1+\|\tau_{-h}g-g\|_1,
\]
and the last term tends to $0$ as $h\to0$ by uniform continuity of $g$ and compact support.

As $|\xi|\to\infty$,
\[
|h|=\frac1{2|\xi|}\longrightarrow0.
\]
Therefore
\[
|\widehat f(\xi)|
\le \frac12\|f-\tau_{-h}f\|_1\longrightarrow0.
\]
This is the Riemann--Lebesgue lemma.
:::
