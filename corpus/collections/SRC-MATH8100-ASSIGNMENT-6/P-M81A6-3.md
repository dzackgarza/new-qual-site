::: problem
For $f\in L^1(\mathbb R^n)$, prove the Fourier-transform formulas for translating $f$, modulating $f$, and composing $f$ with a nonsingular linear map $T$.
In the last formula, use the inverse transpose of $T$ and the factor $|\det T|^{-1}$.
:::

::: {.solution}
**Goal.** Prove the Fourier transform formulas for translation, modulation, and composition with a nonsingular linear map.

<1>1. Translation: $\widehat{f(\cdot - a)}(\xi) = e^{-2\pi i a \cdot \xi} \hat f(\xi)$.
<2>1. $\widehat{f(\cdot - a)}(\xi) = \int f(x - a) e^{-2\pi i x \cdot \xi}\,dx$.
::: {.proof}
definition of the Fourier transform.
:::
<2>2. Substitute $y = x - a$: $\int f(y) e^{-2\pi i (y + a)\cdot \xi}\,dy = e^{-2\pi i a \cdot \xi}\int f(y) e^{-2\pi i y \cdot \xi}\,dy$.
::: {.proof}
change of variables.
:::
<2>3. Hence $\widehat{f(\cdot - a)}(\xi) = e^{-2\pi i a \cdot \xi}\hat f(\xi)$.
::: {.proof}
the last integral is $\hat f(\xi)$.
:::

<1>2. Modulation: $\widehat{e^{2\pi i b \cdot x} f(x)}(\xi) = \hat f(\xi - b)$.
<2>1. $\widehat{e^{2\pi i b \cdot x} f}(\xi) = \int e^{2\pi i b \cdot x} f(x) e^{-2\pi i x \cdot \xi}\,dx$.
::: {.proof}
definition.
:::
<2>2. $= \int f(x) e^{-2\pi i x \cdot (\xi - b)}\,dx = \hat f(\xi - b)$.
::: {.proof}
combine the exponentials.
:::

<1>3. Composition with a nonsingular linear map $T$: $\widehat{f \circ T}(\xi) = |\det T|^{-1} \hat f((T^{-1})^T \xi)$.
<2>1. $\widehat{f \circ T}(\xi) = \int f(Tx) e^{-2\pi i x \cdot \xi}\,dx$.
::: {.proof}
definition.
:::
<2>2. Substitute $y = Tx$: $x = T^{-1}y$, $dx = |\det T|^{-1} dy$.
::: {.proof}
change of variables for a linear map.
:::
<2>3. $= |\det T|^{-1}\int f(y) e^{-2\pi i (T^{-1}y) \cdot \xi}\,dy$.
::: {.proof}
substitute.
:::
<2>4. $(T^{-1}y) \cdot \xi = y \cdot (T^{-1})^T \xi$.
::: {.proof}
the adjoint of $T^{-1}$ is $(T^{-1})^T$.
:::
<2>5. Hence $\widehat{f \circ T}(\xi) = |\det T|^{-1}\hat f((T^{-1})^T \xi)$.
::: {.proof}
the integral is $\hat f$ evaluated at $(T^{-1})^T \xi$.
:::

<1>4. Q.E.D.
::: {.proof}
<1>1, <1>2, <1>3 are the three formulas.
:::
:::
