::: problem
For $f\in L^1(\mathbb R^n)$, prove the Fourier-transform formulas for translating $f$, modulating $f$, and composing $f$ with a nonsingular linear map $T$.
In the last formula, use the inverse transpose of $T$ and the factor $|\det T|^{-1}$.
:::
