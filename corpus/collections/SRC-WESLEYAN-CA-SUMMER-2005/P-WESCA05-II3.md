::: {.problem}
Let $f(z)=e^{z^2}$ and
\[
S=\{x+iy:0\le x\le2,\ 0\le y\le2\}.
\]

1. Compute the maximum value of $|f(z)|$ on $S$.

2. Find the set of points in $S$ where this maximum is attained.
:::
