::: {.problem}
Let
\[
f(z)=a_0+a_1z+\cdots+a_nz^n,
\qquad n\ge1,\quad a_n\ne0.
\]
Prove that there exists $z\in\mathbb C$ such that $f(z)=0$.

Hint: use Liouville's theorem.
:::
