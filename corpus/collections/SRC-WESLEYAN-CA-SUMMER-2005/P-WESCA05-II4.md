::: {.problem}
Let $f$ be entire.
Suppose there are a constant $M$ and a fixed positive integer $n$ such that
\[
|f(z)|\le M|z|^n
\qquad(z\in\mathbb C).
\]
Prove that $f$ is a polynomial of degree at most $n$.
:::
