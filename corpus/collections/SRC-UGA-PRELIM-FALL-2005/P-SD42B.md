::: {.problem}
Let $a_0, a_1, a_2, \ldots$ be a decreasing sequence of positive real numbers.
Prove that for every nonnegative integer $m$, $$0 \le \sum_{i=0}^{m}(-1)^i a_i \le a_0.$$ (Hint: $\sum_{i=0}^{m+1}(-1)^i a_i = a_0 - \sum_{i=0}^{m}(-1)^i a_{i+1}$.)
:::
