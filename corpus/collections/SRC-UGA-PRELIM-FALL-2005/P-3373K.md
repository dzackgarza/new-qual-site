::: {.problem}
Find all cube roots of $8$ in the complex plane.
Write your answers in the form $a + bi$, where $a$ and $b$ are real numbers, and justify your answer.
:::

::: solution
Write $8=8e^{2\pi i k}$ for $k\in\mathbb Z$. Its cube roots are
\[
2e^{2\pi i k/3},\qquad k=0,1,2.
\]
Thus the three roots are
\[
2,
\qquad
2\left(-\frac12+\frac{\sqrt3}{2}i\right)=-1+\sqrt3\,i,
\qquad
2\left(-\frac12-\frac{\sqrt3}{2}i\right)=-1-\sqrt3\,i.
\]
Cubing each gives $8$, and a degree-$3$ polynomial has at most three roots, so this list is complete.
:::
