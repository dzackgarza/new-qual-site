::: problem
Let $Z$ be a set and let $X_1, X_2, \dots$ be subsets of $Z$.
Prove the formula $\bigl(\bigcap_i X_i\bigr)^c = \bigcup_i X_i^c$, where $(\cdot)^c$ denotes complement.
:::
