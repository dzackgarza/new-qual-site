::: {.problem}
A space $(X,\mathcal{T})$ is called *locally compact* if, for every $x \in X$ and $x \in U \in \mathcal{T}$, there is a compact set $C \subseteq U$ containing an open neighborhood of $x$.
Show that the Cartesian product of two locally compact spaces is locally compact.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $X$ and $Y$ be locally compact.
Fix
\[
(x,y)\in X\times Y
\]
and an open set $W\subseteq X\times Y$ with $(x,y)\in W$.

<1>1. There are open sets $U\subseteq X$ and $V\subseteq Y$ such that
\[
(x,y)\in U\times V\subseteq W.
\]
::: {.proof}
The sets $U\times V$, with $U$ open in $X$ and $V$ open in $Y$, form a basis for the product topology.
Since $W$ is an open neighborhood of $(x,y)$, one such basic open set lies between $(x,y)$ and $W$.
:::

<1>2. There are compact sets $K\subseteq U$ and $L\subseteq V$ and open sets $U'\subseteq X$, $V'\subseteq Y$ such that
\[
x\in U'\subseteq K\subseteq U
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
y\in V'\subseteq L\subseteq V.
\]
::: {.proof}
Apply the definition of local compactness in $X$ to the point $x$ and the open neighborhood $U$.
It gives a compact set $K\subseteq U$ containing an open neighborhood $U'$ of $x$.

Apply the same definition in $Y$ to $y\in V$ to obtain a compact set $L\subseteq V$ containing an open neighborhood $V'$ of $y$.
:::

<1>3. The set
\[
C=K\times L
\]
is compact and satisfies
\[
(x,y)\in U'\times V'\subseteq C\subseteq W,
\]
where $U'\times V'$ is open in $X\times Y$.
::: {.proof}
The finite product of compact spaces is compact, so $K\times L$ is compact.
The product of the open sets $U'$ and $V'$ is open, and by <1>2,
\[
(x,y)
\in
U'\times V'
\subseteq
K\times L
\subseteq
U\times V.
\]
By <1>1, $U\times V\subseteq W$.
Thus $C$ is a compact subset of $W$ containing an open neighborhood of $(x,y)$.
:::

<1>4. Therefore $X\times Y$ is locally compact.
::: {.proof}
The point $(x,y)$ and its open neighborhood $W$ were arbitrary.
By <1>3, every such pair admits a compact subset of $W$ containing an open neighborhood of $(x,y)$, which is exactly the stated definition of local compactness.
:::
:::
