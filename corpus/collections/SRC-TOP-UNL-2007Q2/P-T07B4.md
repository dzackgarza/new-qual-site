::: {.problem}
Find the Euler characteristic of $X = (\Delta^5)^{(2)}$, the 2-skeleton of the 5-simplex.
Show that $H_1(X) = 0$, $H_2(X)$ is free abelian, and compute the rank of $H_2(X)$.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. $X = (\Delta^5)^{(2)}$ has $\binom{6}{1} = 6$ vertices, $\binom{6}{2} = 15$ edges, and $\binom{6}{3} = 20$ faces.
::: {.proof}
the $k$-simplices of $\Delta^5$ are the $(k+1)$-element subsets of the $6$ vertices.
:::

<1>2. $\chi(X) = 6 - 15 + 20 = 11$.
::: {.proof}
Euler characteristic of a $2$-dimensional complex is $V - E + F$.
:::

<1>3. $X$ is simply connected (it is the $2$-skeleton of a contractible space, and the $2$-skeleton of a simply connected CW complex is simply connected).
::: {.proof}
$\Delta^5$ is contractible, and attaching cells of dimension $\ge 3$ does not affect $\pi_1$, so $\pi_1(X) = \pi_1(\Delta^5) = 1$.
:::

<1>4. Hence $H_1(X) = 0$.
::: {.proof}
<1>3 (Hurewicz: $H_1 = \pi_1^{\mathrm{ab}} = 0$).
:::

<1>5. $H_2(X)$ is free abelian.
::: {.proof}
$H_2$ of a $2$-dimensional CW complex is a subgroup of the free abelian group $C_2$ (the cellular $2$-chains), hence free abelian.
:::

<1>6. $\chi(X) = \operatorname{rank} H_0 - \operatorname{rank} H_1 + \operatorname{rank} H_2 = 1 - 0 + \operatorname{rank} H_2$.
::: {.proof}
<1>2 and <1>4 (and $H_0 = \ZZ$ since $X$ is connected).
:::

<1>7. Hence $\operatorname{rank} H_2 = \chi(X) - 1 = 11 - 1 = 10$.
::: {.proof}
<1>2 and <1>6.
:::

<1>8. Q.E.D.
::: {.proof}
<1>2, <1>4, <1>5, <1>7.
:::
:::
