::: {.problem}
Find the Euler characteristic of $X = (\Delta^5)^{(2)}$, the 2-skeleton of the 5-simplex.
Show that $H_1(X) = 0$, $H_2(X)$ is free abelian, and compute the rank of $H_2(X)$.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. The complex $X=(\Delta^5)^{(2)}$ has $6$ vertices, $15$ edges, and $20$ two-simplices.
Hence
\[
\chi(X)=6-15+20=11.
\]
::: {.proof}
The $k$-simplices of $\Delta^5$ correspond to the $(k+1)$-element subsets of its $6$ vertices.
Therefore
\[
c_0=\binom61=6,
\qquad
c_1=\binom62=15,
\qquad
c_2=\binom63=20.
\]
Since $X$ has no cells above dimension $2$, its Euler characteristic is the alternating sum shown.
:::

<1>2. The complex $X$ is simply connected, so
\[
H_1(X;\mathbb Z)=0.
\]
::: {.proof}
The full simplex $\Delta^5$ is contractible and therefore has trivial fundamental group.
The complex $\Delta^5$ is obtained from its $2$-skeleton $X$ by attaching cells of dimensions $3,4,$ and $5$.
Attaching cells of dimension at least $3$ does not change the fundamental group, since the fundamental group of a CW complex depends only on its $2$-skeleton.
Thus
\[
\pi_1(X)\cong\pi_1(\Delta^5)=0.
\]
The first homology group is the abelianization of the fundamental group, hence
\[
H_1(X;\mathbb Z)=0.
\]
:::

<1>3. The group $H_2(X;\mathbb Z)$ is free abelian.
::: {.proof}
Because $X$ is $2$-dimensional, its cellular chain complex has $C_3(X)=0$.
Therefore
\[
H_2(X;\mathbb Z)=\ker\bigl(\partial_2:C_2(X)\to C_1(X)\bigr).
\]
The cellular group $C_2(X)$ is free abelian on the $20$ two-simplices, so
\[
C_2(X)\cong\mathbb Z^{20}.
\]
Every subgroup of a finitely generated free abelian group is free abelian, hence so is $H_2(X;\mathbb Z)$.
:::

<1>4. The rank of $H_2(X;\mathbb Z)$ is $10$.
::: {.proof}
The complex $X$ is connected, so
\[
H_0(X;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z.
\]
There are no homology groups above degree $2$, and <1>2 gives $H_1(X)=0$.
The Euler-characteristic formula therefore yields
\[
11
=\chi(X)
=\operatorname{rank}H_0(X)-\operatorname{rank}H_1(X)+\operatorname{rank}H_2(X)
=1+\operatorname{rank}H_2(X).
\]
Consequently
\[
\operatorname{rank}H_2(X;\mathbb Z)=10.
\]
:::

<1>5. Thus
\[
\chi(X)=11,
\qquad
H_1(X;\mathbb Z)=0,
\qquad
H_2(X;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^{10}.
\]
::: {.proof}
The Euler characteristic is <1>1, the vanishing of $H_1$ is <1>2, and <1>3--<1>4 show that $H_2$ is free abelian of rank $10$.
:::
