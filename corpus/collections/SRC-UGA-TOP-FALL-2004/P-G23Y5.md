::: problem
Let $M_g^2$ be the compact oriented surface of genus $g$.

Show that there exists a continuous map $f : M_g^2 \to S^2$ which is not homotopic to a constant map.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. $M_g$ has $H_2(M_g)=\Z$.
Proof: closed oriented surface.

<1>2. Collapse $M_g$ to $S^2$ by pinching: map $M_g\to S^2$ of degree $1$ exists (collapse complement of disk).
Proof: $M_g$ contains disk, collapse rest to point gives $S^2$.

<1>3. This map induces iso on $H_2$, so not null-homotopic.
Proof: degree $1$ nontrivial.

<1>4. Q.E.D.
Proof: <1>3.
:::
