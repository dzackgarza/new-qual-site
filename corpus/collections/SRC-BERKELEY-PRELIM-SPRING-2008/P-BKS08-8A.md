::: {.problem}
Let \(p\) and \(q\) be distinct primes, and let \(R\) be a commutative ring with identity of characteristic \(pq\). Show that there are rings \(S,T\) of characteristics \(p,q\), respectively, such that
\[
R\cong S\times T.
\]
:::
