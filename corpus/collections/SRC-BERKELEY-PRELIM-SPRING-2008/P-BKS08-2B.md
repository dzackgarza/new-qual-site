::: {.problem}
(a) Let \(G\) be a finite abelian group, and let \(c\) be the product of all elements of \(G\). Show that \(c^2=1\).

(b) Let \(F\) be a finite field, and let \(c\) be the product of all nonzero elements of \(F\). Show that \(c=-1\).
:::
