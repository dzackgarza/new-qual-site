::: {.problem}
Compute the sequence \(\{x_n\}_{n=0}^\infty\) satisfying
\[
x_n=x_{n-1}-\frac12x_{n-2}\qquad(n\ge2),
\]
with
\[
x_0=1,\qquad x_1=1.
\]
:::
