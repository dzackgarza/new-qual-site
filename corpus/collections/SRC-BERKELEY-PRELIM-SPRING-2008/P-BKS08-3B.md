::: {.problem}
Let \(G\) be a group and \(H\le G\) a subgroup of finite index \(n\). Show that \(G\) contains a normal subgroup \(N\) such that
\[
N\subseteq H
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
[G:N]\le n!.
\]
:::
