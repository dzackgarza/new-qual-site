::: {.problem}
Let \(M\) be an \(n\times n\) nilpotent matrix over \(\mathbb C\). Let
\[
C(M)=\{A\in M_n(\mathbb C):AM=MA\}.
\]
Show that
\[
C(M)=\mathbb C[M]
\]
if and only if the null space of \(M\) has dimension one.
:::
