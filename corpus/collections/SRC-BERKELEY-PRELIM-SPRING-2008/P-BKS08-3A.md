::: {.problem}
Find, without proof, the number of subgroups of each possible order of the symmetric group \(S_4\).
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. The possible subgroup orders and the number of subgroups of each
order are

| order | number of subgroups |
| ---: | ---: |
| $1$ | $1$ |
| $2$ | $9$ |
| $3$ | $4$ |
| $4$ | $7$ |
| $6$ | $4$ |
| $8$ | $3$ |
| $12$ | $1$ |
| $24$ | $1$ |

::: {.proof}
For identification of the entries, the nine order-$2$ subgroups consist
of six generated by transpositions
and three generated by double transpositions; the seven order-$4$
subgroups consist of three cyclic groups, the normal Klein four-group,
and three nonnormal Klein four-groups. The four order-$6$ subgroups are
the point stabilizers, the three order-$8$ subgroups are the Sylow
$2$-subgroups, the unique order-$12$ subgroup is $A_4$, and the
order-$1$ and order-$24$ subgroups are the trivial group and $S_4$
itself.
:::

<1>2. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>1 gives every requested count.
:::
