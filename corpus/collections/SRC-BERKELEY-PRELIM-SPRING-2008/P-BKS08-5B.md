::: {.problem}
Let
\[
\zeta=e^{2\pi i/5},\qquad \alpha=\sqrt[5]{2}\in\mathbb R,
\]
and let
\[
E=\mathbb Q(\zeta,\alpha)\subset\mathbb C.
\]

(a) Show that \(E/\mathbb Q\) is Galois.

(b) Compute \([E:\mathbb Q]\).
:::
