::: {.problem}
Use Laplace transforms to solve the differential equation

$$
y ^ { \prime \prime \prime } - 3 y ^ { \prime \prime } + 3 y ^ { \prime } - y = t ^ { 2 } e ^ { t }
$$

with initial conditions $y ( 0 ) = 1 , y ^ { \prime } ( 0 ) = 0 , y ^ { \prime \prime } ( 0 ) = - 2 .$
:::

::: {.solution}
Let
$$
Y(p)=\mathcal L\{y\}(p).
$$

<1>1. The transformed differential equation is
$$
(p-1)^3Y(p)-p^2+3p-1
=
\frac{2}{(p-1)^3}.
$$

::: {.proof}
Using the initial conditions,
$$
\mathcal L\{y'\}=pY-1,
$$
$$
\mathcal L\{y''\}=p^2Y-p,
$$
and
$$
\mathcal L\{y'''\}=p^3Y-p^2+2.
$$
Therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal L\{y'''-3y''+3y'-y\}
&=
(p^3-3p^2+3p-1)Y-p^2+3p-1 \\
&=
(p-1)^3Y-p^2+3p-1.
\end{aligned}
$$
Also,
$$
\mathcal L\{t^2e^t\}(p)
=
\frac{2}{(p-1)^3},
$$
by exponential shifting of
$\mathcal L\{t^2\}(p)=2/p^3$.
:::

<1>2. The transformed solution is
$$
Y(p)
=
\frac1{p-1}
-\frac1{(p-1)^2}
-\frac1{(p-1)^3}
+\frac2{(p-1)^6}.
$$

::: {.proof}
Step <1>1 gives
$$
Y(p)
=
\frac{p^2-3p+1}{(p-1)^3}
+\frac2{(p-1)^6}.
$$
Putting $q=p-1$ gives
$$
p^2-3p+1=q^2-q-1,
$$
so
$$
\frac{p^2-3p+1}{(p-1)^3}
=
\frac1q-\frac1{q^2}-\frac1{q^3},
$$
which is the claimed expression.
:::

<1>3. The solution is
$$
\boxed{
y(t)
=
e^t\left(
1-t-\frac{t^2}{2}+\frac{t^5}{60}
\right)}.
$$

::: {.proof}
For $n\geq1$,
$$
\mathcal L^{-1}
\left\{
\frac1{(p-1)^n}
\right\}(t)
=
e^t\frac{t^{n-1}}{(n-1)!}.
$$
Applying this termwise to step <1>2 gives
$$
y(t)
=
e^t
-
te^t
-
\frac{t^2}{2}e^t
+
\frac{2t^5}{5!}e^t,
$$
and $2/5!=1/60$.
:::

<1>4. The function in step <1>3 satisfies the differential equation and
the three initial conditions.

::: {.proof}
Write
$$
y(t)=e^t u(t),
\qquad
u(t)=1-t-\frac{t^2}{2}+\frac{t^5}{60}.
$$
Then
$$
(D-1)^3y=e^t u'''.
$$
Since
$$
u'''(t)=t^2,
$$
the differential equation is satisfied. Moreover,
$$
u(0)=1,
\qquad
u'(0)=-1,
\qquad
u''(0)=-1,
$$
so
$$
y(0)=u(0)=1,
$$
$$
y'(0)=u(0)+u'(0)=0,
$$
and
$$
y''(0)=u(0)+2u'(0)+u''(0)=-2.
$$
:::

<1>5. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Steps <1>1--<1>3 obtain the solution by Laplace transforms, and step
<1>4 verifies both the differential equation and the initial data.
:::
