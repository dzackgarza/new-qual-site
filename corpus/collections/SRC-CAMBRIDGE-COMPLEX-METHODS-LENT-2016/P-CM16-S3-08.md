::: {.problem}
Use Laplace transforms to solve the differential equation
$$
y''' - 3y'' + 3y' - y = t^{2}e^{t}
$$
with initial conditions $y(0) = 1$, $y'(0) = 0$, $y''(0) = -2$.
:::

::: {.solution}

Let
$$
Y(p)=\mathcal L\{y\}(p).
$$

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

The transformed differential equation is
$$
(p-1)^3Y(p)-p^2+3p-1
=
\frac{2}{(p-1)^3}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Using the initial conditions,
$$
\mathcal L\{y'\}=pY-1,
$$
$$
\mathcal L\{y''\}=p^2Y-p,
$$
and
$$
\mathcal L\{y'''\}=p^3Y-p^2+2.
$$
Therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal L\{y'''-3y''+3y'-y\}
&=
(p^3-3p^2+3p-1)Y-p^2+3p-1 \\
&=
(p-1)^3Y-p^2+3p-1.
\end{aligned}
$$
Also,
$$
\mathcal L\{t^2e^t\}(p)
=
\frac{2}{(p-1)^3},
$$
by exponential shifting of
$\mathcal L\{t^2\}(p)=2/p^3$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

The transformed solution is
$$
Y(p)
=
\frac1{p-1}
-\frac1{(p-1)^2}
-\frac1{(p-1)^3}
+\frac2{(p-1)^6}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} gives
$$
Y(p)
=
\frac{p^2-3p+1}{(p-1)^3}
+\frac2{(p-1)^6}.
$$
Putting $q=p-1$ gives
$$
p^2-3p+1=q^2-q-1,
$$
so
$$
\frac{p^2-3p+1}{(p-1)^3}
=
\frac1q-\frac1{q^2}-\frac1{q^3},
$$
which is the claimed expression.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

The solution is
$$
\boxed{
y(t)
=
e^t\left(
1-t-\frac{t^2}{2}+\frac{t^5}{60}
\right)}.
$$


::: pf-proof

For $n\geq1$,
$$
\mathcal L^{-1}
\left\{
\frac1{(p-1)^n}
\right\}(t)
=
e^t\frac{t^{n-1}}{(n-1)!}.
$$
Applying this termwise to step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} gives
$$
y(t)
=
e^t
-
te^t
-
\frac{t^2}{2}e^t
+
\frac{2t^5}{5!}e^t,
$$
and $2/5!=1/60$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

The function in step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} satisfies the differential equation and
the three initial conditions.


::: pf-proof

Write
$$
y(t)=e^t u(t),
\qquad
u(t)=1-t-\frac{t^2}{2}+\frac{t^5}{60}.
$$
Then
$$
(D-1)^3y=e^t u'''.
$$
Since
$$
u'''(t)=t^2,
$$
the differential equation is satisfied. Moreover,
$$
u(0)=1,
\qquad
u'(0)=-1,
\qquad
u''(0)=-1,
$$
so
$$
y(0)=u(0)=1,
$$
$$
y'(0)=u(0)+u'(0)=0,
$$
and
$$
y''(0)=u(0)+2u'(0)+u''(0)=-2.
$$

:::

:::

Steps [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} through [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} obtain the solution by Laplace transforms, and step
[](#s1-4){.pf-ref} verifies both the differential equation and the initial data.
