::: {.problem}
Evaluate $\oint_{\gamma} \bar{z} \, \mathrm{d}z$ when $\gamma$ is the circle $\abs{z} = 1$, and when $\gamma$ is the circle $\abs{z - 1} = 1$.
:::

::: {.solution}

Assume both circles have the positive, counterclockwise orientation.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

For $\gamma:\abs{z}=1$,
$$
\boxed{\oint_\gamma \bar z\,dz=2\pi i}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Parametrize the circle by
$$
z(t)=e^{it},
\qquad 0\leq t\leq2\pi.
$$
Then
$$
\bar z(t)=e^{-it},
\qquad
dz=ie^{it}\,dt,
$$
and hence
$$
\oint_\gamma\bar z\,dz
=\int_0^{2\pi}e^{-it}ie^{it}\,dt
=i\int_0^{2\pi}dt
=2\pi i.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

For $\gamma:\abs{z-1}=1$,
$$
\boxed{\oint_\gamma \bar z\,dz=2\pi i}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Parametrize this circle by
$$
z(t)=1+e^{it},
\qquad 0\leq t\leq2\pi.
$$
Because the center is real,
$$
\bar z(t)=1+e^{-it},
\qquad
dz=ie^{it}\,dt.
$$
Therefore
$$
\oint_\gamma\bar z\,dz
=i\int_0^{2\pi}\left(e^{it}+1\right)dt
=2\pi i,
$$
since the integral of $e^{it}$ over one period is zero.

:::
