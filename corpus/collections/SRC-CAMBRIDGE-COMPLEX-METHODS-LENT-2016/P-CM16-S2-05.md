::: {.problem}
Evaluate $\oint _ { \gamma } \bar { z } \mathrm { d } z$ when γ is the circle $| z | = 1 ,$ and when $\gamma$ is the circle $| z - 1 | = 1$
:::

Assume both circles have the positive, counterclockwise orientation.

::: pf

::: pf-step

For $\gamma:|z|=1$,
$$
\boxed{\oint_\gamma \bar z\,dz=2\pi i}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Parametrize the circle by
$$
z(t)=e^{it},
\qquad 0\leq t\leq2\pi.
$$
Then
$$
\bar z(t)=e^{-it},
\qquad
dz=ie^{it}\,dt,
$$
and hence
$$
\oint_\gamma\bar z\,dz
=\int_0^{2\pi}e^{-it}ie^{it}\,dt
=i\int_0^{2\pi}dt
=2\pi i.
$$

:::

:::

::: pf-step

For $\gamma:|z-1|=1$,
$$
\boxed{\oint_\gamma \bar z\,dz=2\pi i}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Parametrize this circle by
$$
z(t)=1+e^{it},
\qquad 0\leq t\leq2\pi.
$$
Because the center is real,
$$
\bar z(t)=1+e^{-it},
\qquad
dz=ie^{it}\,dt.
$$
Therefore
$$
\oint_\gamma\bar z\,dz
=i\int_0^{2\pi}\left(e^{it}+1\right)dt
=2\pi i,
$$
since the integral of $e^{it}$ over one period is zero.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Steps <1>1 and <1>2 evaluate the integral on the two circles.
