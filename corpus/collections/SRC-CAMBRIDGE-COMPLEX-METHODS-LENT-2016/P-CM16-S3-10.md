::: {.problem}
Solve the system of differential equations
$$
\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}\begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -5 & 10 \\ -1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \end{pmatrix} \quad \text{with} \quad \begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} \text{ at } t = 0.
$$
:::

::: {.solution}

Let
$$
X(p)=\mathcal L\{x\}(p),
\qquad
Y(p)=\mathcal L\{y\}(p).
$$

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

The transformed system is
$$
(p+5)X-10Y=3,
\qquad
X+(p-1)Y=1.
$$


::: pf-proof

The differential equations are
$$
x'=-5x+10y,
\qquad
y'=-x+y,
$$
with $x(0)=3$ and $y(0)=1$. Taking Laplace transforms gives
$$
pX-3=-5X+10Y
$$
and
$$
pY-1=-X+Y,
$$
which are exactly the displayed equations.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

The transforms are
$$
Y(p)=\frac{p+2}{(p+2)^2+1},
\qquad
X(p)=\frac{3(p+2)+1}{(p+2)^2+1}.
$$


::: pf-proof

From the second equation in step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref},
$$
X=1-(p-1)Y.
$$
Substitution in the first equation gives
$$
(p+5)-\bigl((p+5)(p-1)+10\bigr)Y=3.
$$
Since
$$
(p+5)(p-1)+10=p^2+4p+5=(p+2)^2+1,
$$
it follows that
$$
Y(p)=\frac{p+2}{(p+2)^2+1}.
$$
Then
$$
X
=
1-(p-1)Y
=
\frac{3p+7}{(p+2)^2+1}
=
\frac{3(p+2)+1}{(p+2)^2+1}.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

The solution is
$$
\boxed{
x(t)=e^{-2t}(3\cos t+\sin t),
\qquad
y(t)=e^{-2t}\cos t}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Using
$$
\mathcal L\{e^{-2t}\cos t\}(p)
=
\frac{p+2}{(p+2)^2+1}
$$
and
$$
\mathcal L\{e^{-2t}\sin t\}(p)
=
\frac1{(p+2)^2+1},
$$
step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} inverts termwise to the displayed formulas.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

The functions in step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} satisfy both differential equations and
the initial condition.


::: pf-proof

At $t=0$,
$$
x(0)=3,
\qquad
y(0)=1.
$$
Also,
$$
y'(t)=e^{-2t}(-2\cos t-\sin t),
$$
while
$$
-x(t)+y(t)
=
e^{-2t}(-2\cos t-\sin t),
$$
so the second equation holds. Finally,
$$
x'(t)
=
e^{-2t}(-5\cos t-5\sin t),
$$
and
$$
-5x(t)+10y(t)
=
e^{-2t}(-5\cos t-5\sin t),
$$
so the first equation holds as well.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Steps [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} through [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} solve the system by Laplace transforms, and step [](#s1-4){.pf-ref}
verifies the resulting solution.

:::
