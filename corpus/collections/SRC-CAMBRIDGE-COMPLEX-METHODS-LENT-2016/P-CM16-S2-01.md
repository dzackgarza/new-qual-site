::: {.problem}
Find the first two non-vanishing coefficients in the Taylor expansion about the origin of the following functions, assuming principal branches when there is a choice.
You may make use of standard series expansions for $\log ( 1 + z )$ , etc.

$$
z / \log ( 1 + z )
$$

$$
( \cos z ) ^ { 1 / 2 } - 1
$$

$$
\log ( 1 + e ^ { z } )
$$

$$
e ^ { e ^ { z } } ,
$$

State the range of values of z for which each series converges.

How would your answers differ if you assumed branches different from the principal branch?
:::
