::: problem
Let $\{r_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$ be a sequence with $r_n \in [0,1]$ and define the function
$$
f(x) := \sum_{r_n < x} \frac{1}{2^n}.
$$
Show that $f$ is Borel measurable, find all its points of discontinuity, and find $\int_0^1 f(x)\,dx$.
:::
