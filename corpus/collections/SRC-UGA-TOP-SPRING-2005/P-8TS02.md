::: {.problem}
Let $n\geq 2$.
Prove that the covering map $\pi:S^n\to\mathbb{RP}^n$ is not nullhomotopic.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

Suppose for contradiction that the covering map
\[
\pi:S^n\longrightarrow\RP^n
\]
is nullhomotopic.
Then there is a homotopy
\[
H:S^n\times I\longrightarrow\RP^n
\]
with
\[
H(x,0)=\pi(x)
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
H(x,1)=y_0
\]
for some $y_0\in\RP^n$.

::: pf-proof

This is the definition of nullhomotopy.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

The homotopy $H$ lifts through $\pi$ to a homotopy
\[
\widetilde H:S^n\times I\longrightarrow S^n
\]
whose initial map is the identity:
\[
\widetilde H(x,0)=x,
\qquad
\pi\circ\widetilde H=H.
\]

::: pf-proof

At time $0$ the identity map
\[
\id_{S^n}:S^n\longrightarrow S^n
\]
is a lift of $H(-,0)=\pi$, since
\[
\pi\circ\id_{S^n}=\pi.
\]
The homotopy lifting property for covering maps therefore gives a unique lift of the entire homotopy beginning with $\id_{S^n}$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

The terminal map $\widetilde H_1:S^n\to S^n$ is constant.

::: pf-proof

Since $H(-,1)=y_0$, the lifting identity gives
\[
\pi(\widetilde H(x,1))=y_0
\qquad(x\in S^n).
\]
Hence
\[
\widetilde H_1(S^n)\subseteq \pi^{-1}(y_0),
\]
and this fiber consists of exactly two antipodal points.
The sphere $S^n$ is connected for $n\ge1$, while the fiber is discrete, so the continuous image $\widetilde H_1(S^n)$ is a singleton.
Thus $\widetilde H_1$ is constant.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s4}

This is impossible because the identity map of $S^n$ is not nullhomotopic.

::: pf-proof

By steps [](#s2){.pf-ref} and [](#s3){.pf-ref}, $\widetilde H$ is a homotopy from $\id_{S^n}$ to a constant map.
Homotopic maps induce the same map on integral homology.
But on
\[
H_n(S^n;\ZZ)\cong\ZZ,
\]
the identity induces the identity homomorphism, whereas a constant map factors through a point and hence induces the zero homomorphism in degree $n>0$.
These maps are different, a contradiction.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Therefore
\[
\boxed{\pi:S^n\longrightarrow\RP^n\text{ is not nullhomotopic}.}
\]

::: pf-proof

The assumption of a nullhomotopy in step [](#s1){.pf-ref} led to the contradiction in step [](#s4){.pf-ref}.

:::
