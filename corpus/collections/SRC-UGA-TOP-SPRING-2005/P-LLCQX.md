::: problem
Let $X$ be the topological space formed by filling in two circles $S^1 \times \theset{p_1 }$ and $S^1 \times \theset{p_2 }$ in the torus $S^1 \times S^1$ with disks.

Calculate the fundamental group and the homology groups of $X$.
:::

::: {.solution}
**Goal.** Compute $\pi_1(X)$ and $H_*(X)$ for the torus with two circles filled by disks.

<1>1. $X$ is obtained from $T^2 = S^1 \times S^1$ by attaching two $2$-cells along the circles $S^1 \times \theset{p_1}$ and $S^1 \times \theset{p_2}$.
::: {.proof}
filling a circle with a disk is attaching a $2$-cell along that circle.
:::

<1>2. $\pi_1(T^2) = \ZZ \oplus \ZZ$, generated by $a = S^1 \times \theset{p_1}$ (longitude) and $b = \theset{p_1} \times S^1$ (meridian).
::: {.proof}
the fundamental group of the torus.
:::

<1>3. Both circles $S^1 \times \theset{p_1}$ and $S^1 \times \theset{p_2}$ represent the same class $a$.
::: {.proof}
both are longitudes (first factor varies, second fixed), homotopic to each other.
:::

<1>4. Attaching a $2$-cell along $a$ kills $a$ in $\pi_1$.
::: {.proof}
the attaching map makes $a$ null-homotopic.
:::

<1>5. Hence $\pi_1(X) = \langle a, b \mid [a,b] = 1, a = 1\rangle = \langle b \rangle = \ZZ$.
::: {.proof}
killing $a$ in $\ZZ \oplus \ZZ$ leaves $\ZZ$ (generated by $b$).
:::

<1>6. Homology groups.
<2>1. $H_0(X) = \ZZ$.
::: {.proof}
$X$ is path-connected.
:::
<2>2. $H_1(X) = \ZZ$.
::: {.proof}
$H_1 = \pi_1^{\mathrm{ab}} = \ZZ$ (by <1>5).
:::
<2>3. $H_2(X) = \ZZ \oplus \ZZ$.
::: {.proof}
$X \simeq S^1 \vee S^2 \vee S^2$: the first disk kills $a$, giving $T^2 \cup_a D^2 \simeq S^1 \vee S^2$; the second disk is attached along a now-null-homotopic circle, which is equivalent to wedging with another $S^2$. Hence $H_2 = H_2(S^1) \oplus H_2(S^2) \oplus H_2(S^2) = \ZZ \oplus \ZZ$.
:::
<2>4. $H_n(X) = 0$ for $n \ge 3$.
::: {.proof}
$X$ is a $2$-dimensional CW complex.
:::

<1>7. Q.E.D.
::: {.proof}
$\pi_1(X) = \ZZ$; $H_0 = \ZZ$, $H_1 = \ZZ$, $H_2 = \ZZ^2$, $H_n = 0$ for $n \ge 3$.
:::
:::
