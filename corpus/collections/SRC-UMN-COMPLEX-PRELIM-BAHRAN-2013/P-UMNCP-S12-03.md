::: {.problem}
Let \(f\) be entire.
Suppose there are positive real numbers \(a,b,k\) such that
\[
|f(z)|\le a+b|z|^k
\]
for every \(z\in\mathbb C\). Prove that \(f\) is a polynomial.
:::
