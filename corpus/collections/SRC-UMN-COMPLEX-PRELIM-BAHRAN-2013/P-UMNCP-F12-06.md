::: {.problem}
Let \(f\) be analytic on the upper half-plane \(\{z:\operatorname{Im}z>0\}\). Suppose \(|f(z)|<1\) throughout the domain and \(f(i)=0\). Find the largest possible value of \(|f(2i)|\).
:::
