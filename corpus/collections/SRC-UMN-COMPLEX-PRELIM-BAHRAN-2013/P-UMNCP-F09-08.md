::: {.problem}
Let \(f:\mathbb D\to\mathbb D\) be holomorphic.
Suppose there are distinct points \(p,q\in\mathbb D\) such that \(f(p)=p\) and \(f(q)=q\). Show that \(f(z)=z\) for every \(z\in\mathbb D\).
:::
