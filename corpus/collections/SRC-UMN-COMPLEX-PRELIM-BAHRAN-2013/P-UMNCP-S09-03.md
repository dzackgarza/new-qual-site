::: {.problem}
Find an explicit conformal equivalence from the region bounded by the circles
\[
\left|z-\frac{i}{2}\right|=\frac12
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
|z-i|=1
\]
onto the upper half-plane. The intended region is the part inside \(|z-i|<1\) and outside \(|z-i/2|\le 1/2\).
:::
