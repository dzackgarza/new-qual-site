::: {.problem}
Let \(g:\mathbb C\to\mathbb C\) be entire, and let \(k,n\) be integers such that
\[
\frac{g^{(n)}(z)}{2+|z|^k}
\]
is bounded on \(\mathbb C\). Prove that \(g\) is a polynomial and estimate its degree in terms of \(k\) and \(n\).
:::
