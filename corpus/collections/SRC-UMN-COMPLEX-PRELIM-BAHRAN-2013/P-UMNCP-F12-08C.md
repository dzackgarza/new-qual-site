::: {.problem}
Let \(f\) be holomorphic on the unit disk \(\mathbb D\), with \(f(0)=0\) and \(|f(z)|\le1\). Prove that for every integer \(m\ge1\),
\[
f(z)-2^m z^m
\]
has exactly \(m\) zeros, counted with multiplicity, in \(|z|<\frac12\).
:::
