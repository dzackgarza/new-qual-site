::: {.problem}
Suppose \(f\) is entire and there is \(M>0\) such that
\[
|\operatorname{Re}f(z)|\ge |\operatorname{Im}f(z)|
\]
whenever \(|z|\ge M\). Prove that \(f\) is constant.
:::
