::: {.problem}
Suppose \(f\) is analytic on the punctured unit disk \(\mathbb D\setminus\{0\}\) and
\[
\operatorname{Re} f(z)>0.
\]
Prove that \(f\) has a removable singularity at \(0\).
:::
