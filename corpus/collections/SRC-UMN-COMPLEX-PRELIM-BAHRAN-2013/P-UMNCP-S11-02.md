::: {.problem}
Let \(h\) be holomorphic on a connected open set \(V\). Prove that if
\[
h(z)^2=\overline{h(z)}
\]
for every \(z\in V\), then \(h\) is constant on \(V\). Find all possible values of such a function.
:::
