::: {.problem}
Let \(f:\mathbb D\to\mathbb D\) be holomorphic. Suppose \(c\in\mathbb D\) is a fixed point and \(|f'(c)|<1\). For any \(z_0\in\mathbb D\), define \(z_n=f(z_{n-1})\). Prove that \(z_n\to c\).
:::
