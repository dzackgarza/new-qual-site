::: {.problem}
Let \(f\) be holomorphic on the right half-plane \(\{z:\operatorname{Re}z>0\}\). Suppose \(|f(z)|<1\) throughout the domain and \(f(1)=0\). Find the largest possible value of \(|f(2)|\).
:::
