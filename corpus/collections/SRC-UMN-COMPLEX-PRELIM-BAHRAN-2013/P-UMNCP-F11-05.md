::: {.problem}
Let \(f\) be entire. Suppose
\[
f(z+1)=f(z)
\]
for every \(z\in\mathbb C\), and
\[
|f(z)|\le e^{|z|}
\]
for every \(z\in\mathbb C\). Prove that \(f\) is constant.
:::
