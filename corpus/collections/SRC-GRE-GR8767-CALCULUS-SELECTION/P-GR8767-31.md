::: {.problem}
$\lim_{x \to \pi} \frac{e^{-\pi} - e^{-x}}{\sin x} =$

(A) $-\infty$
(B) $-e^{-\pi}$
(C) $0$
(D) $e^{-\pi}$
(E) $1$
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

The limit is of the indeterminate form $0/0$.


::: pf-proof

At $x=\pi$,
$$
e^{-\pi}-e^{-x}=0,
\qquad
\sin x=0.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

The limit equals
$$
\boxed{-e^{-\pi}},
$$
so the answer is (B).


::: pf-proof

By l'Hôpital's rule,
$$
\lim_{x\to\pi}\frac{e^{-\pi}-e^{-x}}{\sin x}
=
\lim_{x\to\pi}\frac{e^{-x}}{\cos x}
=\frac{e^{-\pi}}{-1}
=-e^{-\pi}.
$$

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} gives the requested limit and choice.
