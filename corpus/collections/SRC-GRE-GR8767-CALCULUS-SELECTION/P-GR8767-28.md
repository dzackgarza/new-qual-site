::: {.problem}
Questions 28–30 are based on the following information. Let $f$ be a function such that the graph of $f$ is a semicircle $S$ with end points $(a, 0)$ and $(b, 0)$ where $a < b$.

$\abs{\int_a^b f(x) \, dx} =$

(A) $f(b) - f(a)$
(B) $\frac{f(b) - f(a)}{b - a}$
(C) $(b - a)\frac{\pi}{4}$
(D) $(b - a)^2 \pi$
(E) $(b - a)^2 \frac{\pi}{8}$
:::
