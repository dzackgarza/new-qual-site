::: {.problem}
Let $f$ be a continuous, strictly decreasing, real-valued function such that $\int_0^{+\infty} f(x) \, dx$ is finite and $f(0) = 1$. In terms of $f^{-1}$ (the inverse function of $f$), $\int_0^{+\infty} f(x) \, dx$ is

(A) less than $\int_1^{+\infty} f^{-1}(y) \, dy$
(B) greater than $\int_0^1 f^{-1}(y) \, dy$
(C) equal to $\int_1^{+\infty} f^{-1}(y) \, dy$
(D) equal to $\int_0^1 f^{-1}(y) \, dy$
(E) equal to $\int_0^{+\infty} f^{-1}(y) \, dy$
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

One has $f(x)>0$ for every $x\geq0$ and
$$
\lim_{x\to\infty}f(x)=0.
$$


::: pf-proof

Because $f$ is strictly decreasing and $f(0)=1$, if $f(x_0)\leq0$ for some
$x_0$, then strict decrease gives $f(x)<0$ for all sufficiently large $x$ and
in fact $f(x)\leq c<0$ on a tail. The improper integral would then diverge to
$-\infty$, contrary to finiteness. Hence $f(x)>0$ everywhere.

As a positive decreasing function, $f$ has a limit $L\geq0$ at infinity. If
$L>0$, then $f(x)\geq L$ for all $x$, so the improper integral diverges to
$+\infty$. Therefore $L=0$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

The inverse $f^{-1}$ is defined on $(0,1]$, and for
$0<y\leq1$,
$$
f(x)\geq y
\iff
0\leq x\leq f^{-1}(y).
$$


::: pf-proof

By continuity, strict decrease, $f(0)=1$, and step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref}, the range of $f$ is
exactly $(0,1]$. Strict decrease makes $f$ one-to-one. For a fixed
$y\in(0,1]$, monotonicity gives the displayed equivalence.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

One has
$$
\boxed{\int_0^{\infty}f(x)\,dx
=\int_0^1 f^{-1}(y)\,dy}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Consider the subgraph
$$
A=\{(x,y):x\geq0,\ 0\leq y\leq f(x)\}.
$$
Its vertical slice at $x$ has length $f(x)$. By step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref}, its horizontal slice
at $y\in(0,1]$ has length $f^{-1}(y)$. Tonelli's theorem applied to the
nonnegative indicator function of $A$ therefore gives
$$
\int_0^{\infty}f(x)\,dx
=\iint_A1\,d(x,y)
=\int_0^1 f^{-1}(y)\,dy.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

The answer is $\boxed{\text{(D)}}$.


::: pf-proof

Step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} is exactly option (D).

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} identifies the requested choice.

:::
