::: {.problem}
The shortest distance from the curve $xy = 8$ to the origin is

(A) $4$
(B) $8$
(C) $16$
(D) $2\sqrt{2}$
(E) $4\sqrt{2}$
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

Every point $(x,y)$ on the curve $xy=8$ satisfies
$$
x^2+y^2\geq16.
$$


::: pf-proof

Since $(\abs{x}-\abs{y})^2\geq0$,
$$
x^2+y^2\geq2\abs{xy}=16.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

Equality in step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} occurs on the curve.


::: pf-proof

Equality requires $\abs{x}=\abs{y}$. Since $xy=8>0$, $x$ and $y$ have the same
sign. Taking
$$
x=y=2\sqrt2
$$
gives $xy=8$ and $x^2+y^2=16$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

The shortest distance is
$$
\boxed{4},
$$
so the answer is (A).


::: pf-proof

The distance from $(x,y)$ to the origin is $\sqrt{x^2+y^2}$. Steps [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} and [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} show that its minimum squared value is $16$, hence its minimum value is
$4$.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} gives the requested distance and choice.

:::
