::: {.problem}
Suppose that an arrow is shot from a point $p$ and lands at a point $q$ such that at one and only one point in its flight is the arrow parallel to the line of sight between $p$ and $q$. Of the following, which is the best mathematical model for the phenomenon described above?

(A) A function $f$ differentiable on $[a, b]$ such that there is one and only one point $c$ in $[a, b]$ with $\int_a^b f'(x) \, dx = c(b - a)$
(B) A function $f$ whose second derivative is at all points negative such that there is one and only one point $c$ in $[a, b]$ with $f'(c) = \frac{f(b) - f(a)}{b - a}$
(C) A function $f$ whose first derivative is at all points positive such that there is one and only one point $c$ in $[a, b]$ with $\int_a^b f(x) \, dx = f(c) \cdot (b - a)$
(D) A function $f$ continuous on $[a, b]$ such that there is one and only one point $c$ in $[a, b]$ with $\int_a^b f(x) \, dx = f(c) \cdot (b - a)$
(E) A function $f$ continuous on $[a, b]$ and $f(a) < d < f(b)$ such that there is one and only one point $c$ in $[a, b]$ with $f(c) = d$
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

If the arrow's trajectory is modeled by a graph $y=f(x)$ from
$p=(a,f(a))$ to $q=(b,f(b))$, then the arrow is parallel to the line of sight
exactly when
$$
f'(c)=\frac{f(b)-f(a)}{b-a}.
$$


::: pf-proof

The tangent vector to the trajectory at abscissa $c$ has slope $f'(c)$, while
the line through the endpoints $p$ and $q$ has the secant slope
$(f(b)-f(a))/(b-a)$. Two nonvertical lines are parallel exactly when their
slopes agree.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

If $f''<0$ throughout $(a,b)$, then there is exactly one
$c\in(a,b)$ satisfying the equation in step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref}.


::: pf-proof

The mean value theorem gives at least one $c\in(a,b)$ with
$$
f'(c)=\frac{f(b)-f(a)}{b-a}.
$$
Since $f''<0$, the derivative $f'$ is strictly decreasing on $(a,b)$, so it
cannot take the same value at two distinct points. Hence this $c$ is unique.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

The best model is $\boxed{\text{(B)}}$.


::: pf-proof

Option (B) states exactly the secant--tangent equation from step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} and uses
$f''<0$ to guarantee the uniqueness proved in step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} identifies the requested model.

:::
