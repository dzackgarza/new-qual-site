::: {.problem}
In the $xy$-plane, the graph of $x^{\log y} = y^{\log x}$ is

(A) empty
(B) a single point
(C) a ray in the open first quadrant
(D) a closed curve
(E) the open first quadrant
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. The equation is defined exactly when $x>0$ and $y>0$.

::: {.proof}
Both $\log x$ and $\log y$ occur, so real-valued logarithms require
$x,y>0$. For positive bases, the real powers in the equation are then defined.
:::

<1>2. Every pair $(x,y)$ with $x>0$ and $y>0$ satisfies the equation.

::: {.proof}
Using $a^t=e^{t\log a}$ for $a>0$,
$$
x^{\log y}
=e^{(\log y)(\log x)}
=e^{(\log x)(\log y)}
=y^{\log x}.
$$
:::

<1>3. The graph is
$$
\boxed{\{(x,y)\in\RR^2:x>0,\ y>0\}},
$$
the open first quadrant, so the answer is (E).

::: {.proof}
Step <1>1 gives the full domain and step <1>2 shows that every point of that
domain satisfies the equation.
:::

<1>4. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>3 identifies the requested graph and choice.
:::
:::
