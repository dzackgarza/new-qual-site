::: {.problem}
For all $x > 0$, if $f(\log x) = \sqrt{x}$, then $f(x) =$

(A) $e^{\frac{x}{2}}$
(B) $\log \sqrt{x}$
(C) $e^{\sqrt{x}}$
(D) $\sqrt{\log x}$
(E) $\frac{\log x}{2}$
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

For every real $t$,
$$
f(t)=e^{t/2}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Since $t\in\RR$ can be written as $t=\log x$ with $x=e^t>0$, the hypothesis
gives
$$
f(t)=f(\log(e^t))=\sqrt{e^t}=e^{t/2}.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

Therefore
$$
\boxed{f(x)=e^{x/2}},
$$
so the answer is (A).


::: pf-proof

Rename the real variable $t$ in step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} as $x$.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} gives the requested formula and choice.

:::

:::

:::
