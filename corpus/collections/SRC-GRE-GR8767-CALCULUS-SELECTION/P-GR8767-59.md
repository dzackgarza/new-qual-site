::: {.problem}
![Source page for Question 59: the graph of f on [0,12], starting at 1, rising to 3, falling to -2 near x = 5, rising to 5 near x = 8.5 and decreasing to 3 at x = 12.](../../../assets/attachments/gr8767-calculus-figures/page-19.png)

If $f$ is the function whose graph is indicated in the figure above, then the least upper bound (supremum) of

$$\left\{ \sum_{k=1}^n \abs{f(x_k) - f(x_{k-1})} : 0 = x_0 < x_1 < \cdots < x_{n-1} < x_n = 12 \right\}$$

appears to be

(A) $2$
(B) $7$
(C) $12$
(D) $16$
(E) $21$
:::

::: {.solution}

Let $c_1<c_2<c_3$ denote the three interior turning points visible in the
graph. The displayed values are
$$
f(0)=1,
\qquad
f(c_1)=3,
\qquad
f(c_2)=-2,
\qquad
f(c_3)=5,
\qquad
f(12)=3.
$$

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

On each of the four intervals
$$
[0,c_1],\quad[c_1,c_2],\quad[c_2,c_3],\quad[c_3,12],
$$
the total variation equals the absolute change of $f$ between the endpoints.


::: pf-proof

The source graph shows $f$ monotone on each of these intervals. If a function
is monotone on $[u,v]$, then for every partition
$u=t_0<\cdots<t_m=v$, all increments $f(t_j)-f(t_{j-1})$ have the same sign.
Hence
$$
\sum_{j=1}^m\abs{f(t_j)-f(t_{j-1})}
=\abs{f(v)-f(u)}.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

The total variation on $[0,12]$ is
$$
\boxed{16}.
$$


::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref}, inserting the three turning points into a partition gives the full
variation, namely
$$
\abs{3-1}
+\abs{-2-3}
+\abs{5-(-2)}
+\abs{3-5}
=2+5+7+2
=16.
$$
Any further subdivision lies inside one of the monotone pieces and does not
increase its contribution, so this value is the supremum over all partitions.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

The answer is $\boxed{\text{(D)}}$.


::: pf-proof

Step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} gives the listed value $16$.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} identifies the requested choice.

:::
