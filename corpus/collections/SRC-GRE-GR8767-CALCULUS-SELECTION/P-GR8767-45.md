::: {.problem}
The radius of convergence of the series $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{e^n}{n!} x^n$ is

(A) $0$
(B) $\frac{1}{e}$
(C) $1$
(D) $e$
(E) $+\infty$
:::

::: pf

::: pf-step

For every fixed $x\in\RR$, the ratio of successive absolute terms is
$$
\frac{e^{n+1}\abs{x}^{n+1}/(n+1)!}{e^n\abs{x}^n/n!}
=\frac{e\abs{x}}{n+1}.
$$


::: pf-proof

This follows by cancelling the common factors $e^n\abs{x}^n$ and using
$(n+1)!=(n+1)n!$.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

The series converges absolutely for every real $x$.


::: pf-proof

For each fixed $x$, the ratio in step <1>1 tends to $0<1$. The ratio test
therefore gives absolute convergence.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

The radius of convergence is
$$
\boxed{+\infty},
$$
so the answer is (E).


::: pf-proof

Step <1>2 shows that the power series converges for every real $x$, so its
interval of convergence is unbounded in both directions and its radius is
infinite.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step <1>3 gives the requested radius and choice.

:::

:::
