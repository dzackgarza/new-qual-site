::: {.problem}
Let $n$ be odd, and let $G\le S_n$ have order a power of $2$. Prove that there is some
\[
i\in\{1,\dots,n\}
\]
such that
\[
\sigma(i)=i
\]
for every $\sigma\in G$.
:::
