:::{.problem}
Let $W\subset V$ be finite-dimensional vector spaces over a field, and let $L:V\to V$ satisfy
\[
L(V)\subset W.
\]
Let $L_W:W\to W$ be the restriction of $L$. Prove that
\[
\det(I_V-tL)=\det(I_W-tL_W).
\]
:::
