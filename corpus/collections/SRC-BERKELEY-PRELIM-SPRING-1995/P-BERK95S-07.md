::: {.problem}
Let $f,g:[0,1]\to[0,\infty)$ be continuous and suppose
\[
\sup_{0\le x\le1}f(x)=\sup_{0\le x\le1}g(x).
\]
Prove that there exists $t\in[0,1]$ such that
\[
f(t)^2+3f(t)=g(t)^2+3g(t).
\]
:::
