::: {.problem}
Let $y:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ be three times differentiable and satisfy
\[
y'''-y=0.
\]
Suppose
\[
\lim_{x\to\infty}y(x)=0.
\]
Find real numbers $a,b,c,d$, not all zero, such that
\[
ay(0)+by'(0)+cy''(0)=d.
\]
:::
