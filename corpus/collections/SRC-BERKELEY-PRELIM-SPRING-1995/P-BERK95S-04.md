:::{.problem}
Let $F$ be a finite field of cardinality $p^n$, where $p$ is prime and $n>0$, and let
\[
G=GL_2(F).
\]

1. Prove that
   \[
   |G|=(p^{2n}-1)(p^{2n}-p^n).
   \]
2. Show that every Sylow-$p$ subgroup of $G$ is isomorphic to the additive group of $F$.
:::
