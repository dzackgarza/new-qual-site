::: {.problem}
Let $V$ be finite-dimensional over a field $F$, and let $L:V\to V$ have characteristic polynomial
\[
\chi=\chi_1\chi_2,
\]
where $\chi_1,\chi_2\in F[x]$ are relatively prime. Prove that
\[
V=V_1\oplus V_2
\]
for subspaces $V_1,V_2$ satisfying
\[
\chi_i(L)V_i=0,
\qquad i=1,2.
\]
:::
