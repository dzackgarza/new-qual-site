::: {.problem}
Let
\[
A=\begin{pmatrix}
1&-1&0\\
-1&2&-1\\
0&-1&1
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Determine all real numbers $a$ for which
\[
\lim_{n\to\infty}a^nA^n
\]
exists and is nonzero as a matrix.
:::
