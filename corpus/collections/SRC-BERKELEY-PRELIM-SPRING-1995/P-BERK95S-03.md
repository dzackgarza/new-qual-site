::: {.problem}
Let $n$ be a positive integer and $0<\theta<\pi$. Prove that
\[
\frac1{2\pi i}\int_{|z|=2}
\frac{z^n}{1-2z\cos\theta+z^2}\,dz
=\frac{\sin(n\theta)}{\sin\theta},
\]
where the circle $|z|=2$ is positively oriented.
:::
