::: {.problem}
Let $G = \langle a, b \rangle$ be a free group on two generators, let $H$ be the subgroup generated by the elements
$$
(a^2 b)^3, \quad (ab)^3, \quad bab, \quad a^2 ba^{-1},
$$
and let $N(H)$ be the normaliser of $H$ in $G$.
By considering the deck translations of a suitable covering space, identify the quotient group $N(H)/H$.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #graph}

Let $\Gamma$ be the graph with vertices $0,1,\dots,7$, directed $a$-edges
$$
0\to1,\quad 1\to2,\quad 2\to3,\quad 4\to5,\quad 5\to6,\quad 7\to0,
$$
and directed $b$-edges
$$
0\to5,\quad 1\to7,\quad 2\to1,\quad 3\to4,\quad 6\to0.
$$
Then $\Gamma$ is the Stallings core graph of $H$ at the base vertex $0$: no vertex has two outgoing or two incoming edges with the same label, and $H$ is the set of reduced words that read closed paths at $0$.

::: pf-proof

Each generator reads a closed path at $0$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
(a^2b)^3&:\ 0\xrightarrow{a}1\xrightarrow{a}2\xrightarrow{b}1\xrightarrow{a}2\xrightarrow{a}3\xrightarrow{b}4\xrightarrow{a}5\xrightarrow{a}6\xrightarrow{b}0,\\
(ab)^3&:\ 0\xrightarrow{a}1\xrightarrow{b}7\xrightarrow{a}0\xrightarrow{b}5\xrightarrow{a}6\xrightarrow{b}0,\\
bab&:\ 0\xrightarrow{b}5\xrightarrow{a}6\xrightarrow{b}0,\\
a^2ba^{-1}&:\ 0\xrightarrow{a}1\xrightarrow{a}2\xrightarrow{b}1\xrightarrow{a^{-1}}0.
\end{aligned}
$$
$\Gamma$ is the folding of the wedge of four circles labelled by these words, and the edge lists show that no two edges with the same label leave or enter one vertex. By Stallings' theorem the closed reduced paths at $0$ in a folded graph are exactly the elements of $H$. The graph has $8$ vertices and $11$ edges, so $H$ is free of rank $11-8+1=4$, and it has infinite index because vertex $2$ has no incoming $b$-edge.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #cover}

The covering space $p:\widetilde X\to X=S^1\vee S^1$ with $p_*\pi_1(\widetilde X,\tilde x_0)=H$ is $\Gamma$ with an infinite tree attached at each missing edge end, and every deck transformation of $\widetilde X$ restricts to a label-preserving automorphism of $\Gamma$.

::: pf-proof

Completing $\Gamma$ to a covering of the wedge by hanging trees at the missing edge ends adds no new closed reduced paths, so the based covering corresponds to $H$. The core $\Gamma$ is the union of all closed reduced edge paths in $\widetilde X$, so a deck transformation, which is a label-preserving graph automorphism, carries $\Gamma$ to itself.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #rigid}

The only label-preserving automorphism of $\Gamma$ is the identity.

::: pf-proof

A label-preserving automorphism $\phi$ preserves at each vertex the set of labels of incoming and outgoing edges. Vertices $0$ and $1$ are the only vertices with all four edges $a$-in, $a$-out, $b$-in, $b$-out, so $\phi$ permutes $\{0,1\}$. Suppose $\phi(0)=1$. The $a$-edge into $0$ starts at $7$ and the $a$-edge into $1$ starts at $0$, so $\phi(7)=0$. The $b$-edge into $7$ starts at $1$ and the $b$-edge into $0$ starts at $6$, so $\phi(1)=6$. This contradicts $\phi(1)\in\{0,1\}$. Hence $\phi(0)=0$. A label-preserving automorphism of a connected folded graph that fixes one vertex fixes every vertex reached from it by an edge path, so $\phi=\operatorname{id}$.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

The deck group of $p$ is isomorphic to $N(H)/H$. By Steps [](#cover){.pf-ref} and [](#rigid){.pf-ref} a deck transformation fixes the core and hence fixes the base point, so it is the identity. Therefore
$$
\boxed{N(H)/H=1,\qquad N(H)=H.}
$$

:::
