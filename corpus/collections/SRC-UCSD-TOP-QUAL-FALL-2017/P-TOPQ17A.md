::: problem
Let $G = \langle a, b \rangle$ be a free group on two generators, let $H$ be the subgroup generated by the elements $(a^2 b)^3 (ab)^3 bab\, a^2 ba^{-1}$, and let $N(H)$ be the normaliser of $H$ in $G$.
By considering the deck translations of a suitable covering space, identify the quotient group $N(H)/H$.
:::
