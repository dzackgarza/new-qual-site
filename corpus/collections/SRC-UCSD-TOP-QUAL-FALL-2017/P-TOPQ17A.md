::: {.problem}
Let $G = \langle a, b \rangle$ be a free group on two generators, let $H$ be the subgroup generated by the elements $(a^2 b)^3 (ab)^3 bab\, a^2 ba^{-1}$, and let $N(H)$ be the normaliser of $H$ in $G$.
By considering the deck translations of a suitable covering space, identify the quotient group $N(H)/H$.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: pf-step

Let
$$w=(a^2b)^3(ab)^3bab\,a^2ba^{-1},\qquad H=\langle w\rangle.$$
The cyclic reduction of $w$ is
$$u=abaabaababababbabaab,$$
which has length $20$.

::: pf-proof

Free reduction gives $w=a u a^{-1}$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

The cyclically reduced word $u$ is not a proper power.

::: pf-proof

If a cyclically reduced word of length $20$ were a proper power, its cyclic word would have a period among the proper divisors $1,2,4,5,10$ of $20$. Direct comparison of the letters of $u$ with each corresponding periodic pattern fails; equivalently, the least period of the displayed word is $20$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

In a free group, the normalizer of a nontrivial cyclic subgroup $\langle w\rangle$ is the maximal cyclic subgroup generated by the primitive root of $w$.

::: pf-proof

The centralizer of a nontrivial element of a free group is infinite cyclic generated by its primitive root. If $g$ normalizes $\langle w\rangle$, then $gwg^{-1}=w^{\pm1}$; free groups have unique roots and the same axis in the Cayley tree, forcing $g$ to lie in that maximal cyclic subgroup.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

By step [](#s2){.pf-ref}, $w$ itself is primitive as a power, so $N(H)=H$ and
$$\boxed{N(H)/H=1.}$$

::: pf-proof

Apply step [](#s3){.pf-ref} with primitive root $w$.

:::
