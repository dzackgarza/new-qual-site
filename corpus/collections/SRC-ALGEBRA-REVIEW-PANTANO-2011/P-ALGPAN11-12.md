::: {.problem}
Which of the following subsets are subrings of the ring of real numbers?

I. $\{a+b\sqrt2:a,b\in\mathbb Q\}$.

II. $\{n/3^m:n\in\mathbb Z,\ m\ge0\}$.

III. $\{a+b\sqrt5:a,b\in\mathbb R,\ a^2+b^2\le1\}$.
