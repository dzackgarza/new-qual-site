::: {.problem}
Let $K$ be a CW-complex with one 0-cell $v$, and let $f \colon (K, v) \to (K, v)$ be a basepoint-preserving map.
Let $T_f$ be the mapping torus of $f$: that is, the space obtained from $K \times I$ by gluing $(x, 1) \sim (f(x), 0)$ for all $x$.
Show that a presentation of $\pi_1(T_f)$ may be obtained from a presentation of $\pi_1(K)$ by adjoining one new generator $t$ and certain relations involving $f_*$.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. Let
$$\pi_1(K,v)\cong\langle S\mid R\rangle.$$
Adjoin one generator $t$, represented by the loop $s\mapsto(v,s)$ around the mapping-torus direction.
::: {.proof}
Because $f(v)=v$, the segment $\{v\}\times I$ closes up in $T_f$ and defines a based loop.
:::

<1>2. For every generator $a\in S$, the mapping torus supplies the relation
$$t^{-1}at=f_*(a)$$
(up to replacing $t$ by $t^{-1}$, depending on the chosen orientation of the circle direction).
::: {.proof}
The square traced by a loop representing $a$ as it moves through $K\times I$ has bottom edge $a$, top edge identified with $f\circ a$, and the two vertical basepoint edges represented by $t$ and $t^{-1}$. Its boundary is null-homotopic, giving the displayed conjugacy relation.
:::

<1>3. Hence
$$\boxed{\pi_1(T_f)\cong\left\langle S,t\ \middle|\ R,\ t^{-1}at=f_*(a)\text{ for }a\in S\right\rangle.}$$
::: {.proof}
Apply van Kampen to the mapping torus cut open near the identified end. The old relations $R$ remain, and the only new relations are those identifying each loop at the top with its image under $f$ at the bottom. Relations for arbitrary words follow from those for the generators.
:::

<1>4. If $f_*$ is an automorphism, this is the usual semidirect-product/HNN presentation
$$\pi_1(T_f)\cong\pi_1(K)\rtimes_{f_*}\mathbb Z.$$
::: {.proof}
When $f_*$ is invertible, the displayed presentation is exactly the standard presentation of the semidirect product by the infinite cyclic group generated by $t$.
:::
:::
