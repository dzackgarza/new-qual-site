::: {.problem}
(a) Show that the algebraic closure of $\FF_p$ is the union of its finite subfields: $\overline{\FF}_p=\bigcup_{n=1}^\infty\FF_{p^n}$.

(b) Show that $\overline{\FF}_p$ contains proper infinite subfields.
:::
