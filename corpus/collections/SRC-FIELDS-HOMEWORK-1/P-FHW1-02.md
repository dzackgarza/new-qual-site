::: {.problem}
Consider field extensions $E\subset F\subset K$. Suppose that $F$ is algebraic over $E$ and $K$ is algebraic over $F$. Show that $K$ is algebraic over $E$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $a\in K$, let $m_a(x)=x^n+b_{n-1}x^{n-1}+\cdots+b_0\in F[x]$ be its minimal polynomial over $F$, and let $E'=E(b_0,\ldots,b_{n-1})$ be the subfield of $F$ generated by $E$ and the coefficients of $m_a$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #coeffs-are-algebraic}
Each $b_i$ is algebraic over $E$.

::: pf-proof
Each $b_i$ lies in $F$, and $F$ is algebraic over $E$ by hypothesis.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #prime-is-finite}
$E'$ is a finite extension of $E$.

::: pf-proof
By step [](#coeffs-are-algebraic){.pf-ref} each $b_i$ is algebraic over $E$, so $E(b_0)$ is finite over $E$; adjoining the algebraic element $b_1$ makes $E(b_0,b_1)$ finite over $E(b_0)$, and repeating for $b_2,\ldots,b_{n-1}$ makes $E'$ finite over $E$ by the tower law.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #a-over-prime}
$a$ is algebraic over $E'$.

::: pf-proof
By construction $m_a\in E'[x]$, and $m_a$ is a nonzero polynomial with $m_a(a)=0$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #finite-degree}
$[E(a):E]$ is finite for every $a\in K$.

::: pf-proof
By step [](#a-over-prime){.pf-ref} the extension $E'(a)$ is finite over $E'$, and by step [](#prime-is-finite){.pf-ref} the extension $E'$ is finite over $E$; the tower law then makes $E'(a)$ finite over $E$, and $E(a)$ is an intermediate field of $E\subseteq E'(a)\subseteq E(b_0,\ldots,b_{n-1})(a)$, hence finite over $E$ as well.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #algebraic}
$K$ is algebraic over $E$.

::: pf-proof
An element of an extension of $E$ is algebraic over $E$ exactly when the degree of the field it generates is finite over $E$.
Every $a\in K$ has $[E(a):E]$ finite by step [](#finite-degree){.pf-ref}, so every element of $K$ is algebraic over $E$.
:::
