::: {.problem}
(a) Suppose $[F:K]=2$ and $\operatorname{char}K\neq2$. Show that there exists $D\in K$ such that $F=K(\sqrt D)$.

(b) Show that (a) can fail if $[F:K]=\operatorname{char}K=2$.
:::
