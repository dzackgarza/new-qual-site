::: {.problem}
(a) Show that $f(x)=x^3+x^2+x+3$ is irreducible over $\QQ$.

(b) Consider the field $F=\QQ(\alpha)$, where $\alpha$ is a root of $f(x)$. Express $\dfrac{1}{2-\alpha+\alpha^2}$ as a $\QQ$-linear combination of $1$, $\alpha$, and $\alpha^2$.
:::
