::: {.problem}
Let $f(x)\in K[x]$ be an irreducible polynomial and let $g(x)\in K[x]$ be any non-constant polynomial. Let $p(x)$ be a non-constant polynomial that divides $f(g(x))$. Show that $\deg f$ divides $\deg p$.
:::
