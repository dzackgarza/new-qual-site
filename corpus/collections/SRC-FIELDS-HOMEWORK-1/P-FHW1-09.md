::: {.problem}
Let $\alpha,\beta\in F$ be algebraic over $K$, let $f(x)$ and $g(x)$ be their minimal polynomials, and suppose that $\deg f$ and $\deg g$ are coprime. Prove that $f(x)$ is irreducible in $K(\beta)[x]$.
:::
