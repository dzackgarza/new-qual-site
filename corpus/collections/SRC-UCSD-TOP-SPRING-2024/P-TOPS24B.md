::: problem
The classification of closed connected surfaces says that every such surface is homeomorphic to either a connect-sum of $g \geq 0$ tori (denoted $\Sigma_g$) or a connect-sum of $h \geq 1$ projective planes (denoted $N_h$). Commensurability is the equivalence relation on spaces generated by saying that $X \sim Y$ if $X$ is a finite cover of $Y$, or vice versa.
What are the commensurability classes of closed connected surfaces?
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. The sign of Euler characteristic is invariant under commensurability.
::: {.proof}
If $\widetilde S\to S$ is a finite $d$-sheeted cover, then $\chi(\widetilde S)=d\chi(S)$ with $d>0$. Thus positive, zero, and negative Euler characteristic cannot mix under the equivalence relation generated by finite coverings.
:::

<1>2. The positive-Euler-characteristic class is
$$\boxed{\{S^2,\mathbb{RP}^2\}}.$$
::: {.proof}
The antipodal quotient gives a double cover $S^2\to\mathbb{RP}^2$. These are exactly the closed connected surfaces with positive Euler characteristic.
:::

<1>3. The zero-Euler-characteristic class is
$$\boxed{\{T^2,K\}},$$
where $K=N_2$ is the Klein bottle.
::: {.proof}
The torus is the orientation double cover of the Klein bottle. These are exactly the closed connected surfaces with Euler characteristic zero.
:::

<1>4. All closed connected surfaces with negative Euler characteristic lie in one commensurability class.
::: {.proof}
Every nonorientable $N_h$ with $h\ge3$ has orientation double cover $\Sigma_{h-1}$, so it is commensurable with an orientable surface of genus at least $2$. For orientable surfaces $\Sigma_g,\Sigma_h$ with $g,h\ge2$, let $L=\operatorname{lcm}(g-1,h-1)$. A connected cover of degree $L/(g-1)$ of $\Sigma_g$ and one of degree $L/(h-1)$ of $\Sigma_h$ both have genus $1+L$, since a degree-$d$ cover of $\Sigma_r$ has genus $1+d(r-1)$. Such connected covers of every positive degree exist, for example via epimorphisms of the surface group onto a cyclic group of that order.
:::

<1>5. Hence there are exactly three commensurability classes:
$$\boxed{\{S^2,\mathbb{RP}^2\},\qquad\{T^2,K\},\qquad\{\Sigma_g:g\ge2\}\cup\{N_h:h\ge3\}.}$$
::: {.proof}
Combine <1>1--<1>4.
:::
:::
