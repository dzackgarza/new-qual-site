::: problem
Let $X$ be the CW complex formed by attaching $k$ two-cells $e^2_1, \ldots, e^2_k$ to the circle $S^1\ (= e^0 \cup e^1)$ via attaching maps with degrees $n_1, n_2, \ldots, n_k$.
Compute $\pi_2(X)$ in terms of $n_1, \ldots, n_k$.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. $\pi_1(X) = \ZZ/d$ where $d = \gcd(n_1, \ldots, n_k)$.
::: {.proof}
attaching a $2$-cell along a degree-$n_i$ map kills the $n_i$-th power of the generator of $\pi_1(S^1) = \ZZ$, so $\pi_1(X) = \ZZ/\langle n_1, \ldots, n_k \rangle = \ZZ/d$.
:::

<1>2. $\pi_2(X) = \pi_2(\tilde X) = H_2(\tilde X)$, where $\tilde X$ is the universal cover.
::: {.proof}
the universal cover induces isomorphisms on $\pi_n$ for $n \ge 2$, and by the Hurewicz theorem $\pi_2(\tilde X) = H_2(\tilde X)$ (since $\tilde X$ is simply connected).
:::

<1>3. $\tilde X$ is a $2$-dimensional CW complex with $d$ $0$-cells, $d$ $1$-cells, and $dk$ $2$-cells.
::: {.proof}
$\tilde X$ is the $d$-sheeted cover of $X$; each of the $k$ $2$-cells of $X$ has $d$ lifts, and the $1$-skeleton is a circle of $d$ edges.
:::

<1>4. The boundary map $d_2: C_2(\tilde X) = \ZZ^{dk} \to C_1(\tilde X) = \ZZ^d$ sends each lift of the $i$-th $2$-cell to $\frac{n_i}{d}$ times the fundamental cycle $(1, 1, \ldots, 1)$.
::: {.proof}
the attaching map of the $i$-th $2$-cell has degree $n_i$, and after passing to the $d$-sheeted cover, each lift wraps $\frac{n_i}{d}$ times around the $d$-cycle.
:::

<1>5. The image of $d_2$ is $\ZZ \cdot (1, 1, \ldots, 1)$, of rank $1$.
::: {.proof}
$\gcd(n_1/d, \ldots, n_k/d) = 1$, so the image is generated by the fundamental cycle.
:::

<1>6. Hence $H_2(\tilde X) = \ker d_2$ is free abelian of rank $dk - 1$.
::: {.proof}
$d_2: \ZZ^{dk} \to \ZZ^d$ has image of rank $1$, so its kernel has rank $dk - 1$.
:::

<1>7. Therefore $\pi_2(X) \cong \ZZ^{dk - 1}$, where $d = \gcd(n_1, \ldots, n_k)$.
::: {.proof}
<1>2 and <1>6.
:::

<1>8. Q.E.D.
::: {.proof}
<1>7.
:::
