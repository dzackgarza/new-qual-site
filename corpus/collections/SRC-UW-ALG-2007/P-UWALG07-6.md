::: {.problem}
Let $V$ be a finite dimensional vector space over $\QQ$ and $A:V\to V$ a linear map such that $A^7=\operatorname{id}$, the identity map.
Suppose that $1$ is not an eigenvalue of $A$.
Prove that $\dim V$ is divisible by $6$.
:::
