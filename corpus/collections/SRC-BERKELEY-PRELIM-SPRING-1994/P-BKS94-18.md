::: {.problem}
Let u be a real valued harmonic function in the complex plane such that

$$
u ( z ) \leqslant a \left| \log \left| z \right| \right| + b
$$

for all $z ,$ where a and b are positive constants. Prove that u is constant.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. There is an entire function
$$
F:\CC\longrightarrow\CC
$$
such that
$$
\Re F=u.
$$

::: {.proof}
The plane $\CC$ is simply connected, and $u$ is harmonic on all of $\CC$.
Therefore $u$ has a global harmonic conjugate $v$, and
$$
F\coloneqq u+iv
$$
is entire.
:::

<1>2. The entire function
$$
G(z)\coloneqq e^{F(z)}
$$
has no zeros and satisfies
$$
\abs{G(z)}
\leq
e^b\abs{z}^a
$$
whenever $\abs{z}\geq1$.

::: {.proof}
The exponential function never vanishes, so $G$ is zero-free. Also
$$
\abs{G(z)}
=
e^{\Re F(z)}
=
e^{u(z)}.
$$
For $\abs{z}\geq1$, one has
$$
\abs{\log\abs{z}}
=
\log\abs{z}.
$$
The given bound therefore yields
$$
u(z)\leq a\log\abs{z}+b,
$$
and exponentiating gives
$$
\abs{G(z)}
\leq
e^b\abs{z}^a.
$$
:::

<1>3. The function $G$ is a polynomial.

::: {.proof}
Write its power series at the origin as
$$
G(z)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}c_nz^n.
$$
For $R\geq1$, step <1>2 gives
$$
\max_{\abs{z}=R}\abs{G(z)}
\leq
e^bR^a.
$$
By Cauchy's coefficient estimate,
$$
\abs{c_n}
\leq
\frac{e^bR^a}{R^n}
=
e^bR^{a-n}.
$$
If $n>a$, letting $R\to\infty$ gives $c_n=0$. Hence only finitely many
coefficients are nonzero, so $G$ is a polynomial.
:::

<1>4. The polynomial $G$ is constant.

::: {.proof}
By step <1>2, $G$ has no zeros. A nonconstant complex polynomial has a root
by the fundamental theorem of algebra. Therefore the polynomial in step
<1>3 must be constant.
:::

<1>5. The function $F$ is constant.

::: {.proof}
Differentiate
$$
G=e^F.
$$
Since $G$ is constant by step <1>4,
$$
0=G'=e^F F'.
$$
The factor $e^F$ never vanishes, so $F'=0$ everywhere. Hence $F$ is
constant.
:::

<1>6. The harmonic function $u$ is constant.

::: {.proof}
By step <1>1,
$$
u=\Re F,
$$
and step <1>5 shows that $F$ is constant.
:::

<1>7. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>6 proves the claim.
:::
