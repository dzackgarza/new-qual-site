::: {.problem}
Let $W$ be a real $3 \times 3$ antisymmetric matrix, i.e., $W^t = -W$. Let the function

$$
X(t) = \begin{pmatrix} x_1(t) \\ x_2(t) \\ x_3(t) \end{pmatrix}
$$

be a real solution of the vector differential equation $\frac{dX}{dt} = WX$.

1. Prove that $\|X(t)\|$, the Euclidean norm of $X(t)$, is independent of $t$.

2. Prove that if $v$ is a vector in the null space of $W$, then $X(t) \cdot v$ is independent of $t$.

3. Prove that the values $X(t)$ all lie on a fixed circle in $\mathbb{R}^3$.
:::
