::: {.problem}
Let $T : \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}^n$ be a diagonalizable linear transformation. Prove that there is an orthonormal basis for $\mathbb{R}^n$ with respect to which $T$ has an upper-triangular matrix.
:::
