::: {.problem}
Suppose $f_n\in C^2([0,1])$, $f_n(x)\to f(x)$ pointwise, and
\[
|f_n'(x)|\le1,\qquad |f_n''(x)|\le1
\]
for every $x\in[0,1]$ and $n\ge1$. Prove that $f$ is continuously differentiable on $[0,1]$.
:::
