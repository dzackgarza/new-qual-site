:::{.problem}
Let
\[
F=\{a+b\sqrt[3]{2}+c\sqrt[3]{4}:a,b,c\in\mathbb Q\}.
\]
Prove that $F$ is a field, that every element of $F$ has a unique representation of this form, and find
\[
(1-\sqrt[3]{2})^{-1}
\]
in $F$.
:::
