::: {.problem}
Let $R>1$ and let $f$ be analytic on $|z|<R$ except for a simple pole at $z=1$. Suppose
\[
f(z)=\sum_{n=0}^\infty a_nz^n
\qquad(|z|<1).
\]
Show that
\[
\lim_{n\to\infty}a_n
\]
exists.
:::
