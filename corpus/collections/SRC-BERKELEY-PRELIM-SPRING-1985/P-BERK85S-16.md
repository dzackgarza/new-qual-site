:::{.problem}
Let $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ be continuous and define
\[
f_n(x)=\frac1n\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}f\left(x+\frac{k}{n}\right).
\]
Prove that $(f_n)$ converges uniformly to a limit on every finite interval $[a,b]$.
:::
