::: {.problem}
Let $A,B$ be Hermitian $n\times n$ matrices. Suppose every eigenvalue of $A$ lies in $[a,a']$ and every eigenvalue of $B$ lies in $[b,b']$. Prove that every eigenvalue of $A+B$ lies in
\[
[a+b,a'+b'].
\]
:::
