::: {.problem}
Solve the wave equation
\[
f_{tt}=16f_{xx}
\]
on $[0,\pi]$ with initial position
\[
f(x,0)=\sin x
\]
and initial velocity
\[
f_t(x,0)=
\begin{cases}
1,&\pi/3\le x\le2\pi/3,\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\]
:::
