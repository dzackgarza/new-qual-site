::: {.problem}
Find all solutions of
$$
\begin{aligned}
x+y+z+v&=4,\\
2y+z+v&=4,\\
3z+v&=4,\\
x+3y+2z+2v&=8.
\end{aligned}
$$
:::

::: {.solution}

Use the variable order $x,y,z,v$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

The augmented matrix row reduces to
$$
\boxed{
\begin{pmatrix}
1&0&0&1/3&\arrayrule&4/3\\
0&1&0&1/3&\arrayrule&4/3\\
0&0&1&1/3&\arrayrule&4/3\\
0&0&0&0&\arrayrule&0
\end{pmatrix}.}
$$


::: pf-proof

Starting from
$$
\begin{pmatrix}
1&1&1&1&\arrayrule&4\\
0&2&1&1&\arrayrule&4\\
0&0&3&1&\arrayrule&4\\
1&3&2&2&\arrayrule&8
\end{pmatrix},
$$
apply $R_4\leftarrow R_4-R_1$ and then $R_4\leftarrow R_4-R_2$, obtaining a zero fourth row. Scaling the third row by $1/3$, eliminating its entries from rows $1$ and $2$, scaling the second pivot to $1$, and then eliminating the second-column entry from row $1$ gives the displayed reduced row-echelon form.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

All solutions are
$$
\boxed{
(x,y,z,v)
=\left(\frac43,\frac43,\frac43,0\right)
+t\left(-\frac13,-\frac13,-\frac13,1\right),
\qquad t\in\RR.}
$$


::: pf-proof

Step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} gives
$$
x+\frac13v=\frac43,
\qquad
y+\frac13v=\frac43,
\qquad
z+\frac13v=\frac43.
$$
The variable $v$ is free. Setting $v=t$ yields exactly the displayed family. Conversely, every vector in that family satisfies the reduced system, and row operations preserve the solution set.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} gives exactly all solutions of the system.

:::
