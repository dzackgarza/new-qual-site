::: {.problem}
The recurrence
\[
x_{n+1}=6x_n-5x_{n-1}
\]
can be written as
\[
\begin{pmatrix}x_{n+1}\\x_n\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}6&-5\\1&0\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}x_n\\x_{n-1}\end{pmatrix}.
\]
With $x_0=0$ and $x_1=4$, find an explicit formula for $x_n$.
:::
