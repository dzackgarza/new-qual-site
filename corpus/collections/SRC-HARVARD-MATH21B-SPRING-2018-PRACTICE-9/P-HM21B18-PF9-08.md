::: {.problem}
The recurrence
$$
x_{n+1}=6x_n-5x_{n-1}
$$
can be written as
$$
\begin{pmatrix}x_{n+1}\\x_n\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}6&-5\\1&0\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}x_n\\x_{n-1}\end{pmatrix}.
$$
With $x_0=0$ and $x_1=4$, find an explicit formula for $x_n$.
:::

::: {.solution}

Let
$$
M=\begin{pmatrix}6&-5\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

::: pf

::: pf-step

The eigenvalues of $M$ are $5$ and $1$, with eigenvectors
$$
w_5=\begin{pmatrix}5\\1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
w_1=\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\end{pmatrix}.
$$


::: pf-proof

The characteristic polynomial is
$$
\det(\lambda I-M)
=\lambda^2-6\lambda+5
=(\lambda-5)(\lambda-1).
$$
Direct multiplication gives
$$
Mw_5=5w_5,
\qquad
Mw_1=w_1.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

The initial state decomposes as
$$
\begin{pmatrix}x_1\\x_0\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}4\\0\end{pmatrix}
=w_5-w_1.
$$


::: pf-proof

Indeed,
$$
\begin{pmatrix}5\\1\end{pmatrix}
-\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}4\\0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

For every $n\ge0$,
$$
\boxed{x_n=5^n-1.}
$$


::: pf-proof

Iterating the state equation and using step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} gives
$$
\begin{pmatrix}x_{n+1}\\x_n\end{pmatrix}
=M^n(w_5-w_1)
=5^n w_5-w_1.
$$
Taking the second component yields
$$
x_n=5^n-1.
$$
It also gives $x_0=0$ and $x_1=4$, so the formula has the prescribed initial data.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} is the required explicit formula.

:::
