::: {.problem}
Find the general solutions of

1. $f'(t)=t$;
2. $f'(t)+f(t)=t^2$;
3. $f''(t)-f(t)=t$;
4. $f''(t)+9f(t)=t$;
5. $f''(t)-2f'(t)+f(t)=t$.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

The general solution of equation 1 is
$$
\boxed{f(t)=\frac{t^2}{2}+C.}
$$


::: pf-proof

Integrating $f'(t)=t$ once gives $f(t)=t^2/2+C$, and differentiating this family recovers $f'(t)=t$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

The general solution of equation 2 is
$$
\boxed{f(t)=Ce^{-t}+t^2-2t+2.}
$$


::: pf-proof

The homogeneous equation $f'+f=0$ has solution $Ce^{-t}$. For a particular solution $f_p=at^2+bt+c$,
$$
f_p'+f_p
=a t^2+(2a+b)t+(b+c).
$$
Matching $t^2$ gives $a=1$, $b=-2$, and $c=2$. Adding the homogeneous solution gives the displayed family.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

The general solution of equation 3 is
$$
\boxed{f(t)=C_1e^t+C_2e^{-t}-t.}
$$


::: pf-proof

The homogeneous characteristic polynomial is $r^2-1=(r-1)(r+1)$, giving $C_1e^t+C_2e^{-t}$. The function $f_p=-t$ satisfies
$$
f_p''-f_p=0-(-t)=t.
$$
Therefore the displayed family is exactly the general solution.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

The general solution of equation 4 is
$$
\boxed{f(t)=C_1\cos(3t)+C_2\sin(3t)+\frac t9.}
$$


::: pf-proof

The homogeneous characteristic equation $r^2+9=0$ gives the basis $\cos(3t),\sin(3t)$. For $f_p=t/9$,
$$
f_p''+9f_p=0+t=t,
$$
so adding the homogeneous solution gives the displayed family.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-5}

The general solution of equation 5 is
$$
\boxed{f(t)=C_1e^t+C_2te^t+t+2.}
$$


::: pf-proof

The homogeneous operator factors as
$$
D^2-2D+1=(D-1)^2,
$$
so the homogeneous solutions are $C_1e^t+C_2te^t$. For $f_p=at+b$,
$$
f_p''-2f_p'+f_p
=at+(b-2a).
$$
Matching $t$ gives $a=1$ and $b=2$, yielding the stated particular solution.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Steps [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} through [](#s1-5){.pf-ref} give the complete solution families for equations 1--5.
