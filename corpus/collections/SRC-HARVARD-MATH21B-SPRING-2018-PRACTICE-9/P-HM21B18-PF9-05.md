::: {.problem}
Let $A$ be the $6\times6$ matrix with diagonal entries $-6$ and every off-diagonal entry equal to $1$, and set
\[
B=A+7I_6.
\]

(a) Find all eigenvalues of $B$.

(b) Find all eigenvectors of $B$.

(c) Find all eigenvalues of $A$.

(d) Decide the stability of the continuous system $\dot x=Ax$.
:::
