::: {.problem}
Let $A$ be the $6\times6$ matrix with diagonal entries $-6$ and every off-diagonal entry equal to $1$, and set
$$
B=A+7I_6.
$$

(a) Find all eigenvalues of $B$.

(b) Find all eigenvectors of $B$.

(c) Find all eigenvalues of $A$.

(d) Decide the stability of the continuous system $\dot x=Ax$.
:::

::: {.solution}

Let $\mathbf 1=(1,1,1,1,1,1)^T$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

The matrix $B$ is the $6\times6$ all-ones matrix, and
$$
Bx=\left(\sum_{i=1}^6x_i\right)\mathbf 1.
$$


::: pf-proof

Since $A$ has diagonal entries $-6$ and off-diagonal entries $1$, adding $7I_6$ changes every diagonal entry to $1$ and leaves every off-diagonal entry equal to $1$. Multiplying this all-ones matrix by $x$ gives the displayed formula.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

The eigenvalues of $B$ are
$$
\boxed{6\text{ with multiplicity }1,\qquad 0\text{ with multiplicity }5.}
$$


::: pf-proof

Step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} gives
$$
B\mathbf 1=6\mathbf 1,
$$
so $6$ is an eigenvalue. It also gives
$$
\ker B=\left\{x\in\RR^6:\sum_{i=1}^6x_i=0\right\},
$$
which is a five-dimensional hyperplane. Hence $0$ is an eigenvalue with geometric multiplicity $5$. These eigenspaces have dimensions $1$ and $5$ and intersect trivially, so they span $\RR^6$; therefore there are no other eigenvalues and the stated algebraic multiplicities follow.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

All eigenvectors of $B$ are the nonzero vectors in
$$
\boxed{
E_6=\operatorname{span}\{\mathbf 1\},
\qquad
E_0=\left\{x\in\RR^6:\sum_{i=1}^6x_i=0\right\}.}
$$


::: pf-proof

The two eigenspaces were identified in step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref}. For example, a basis of $E_0$ is
$$
e_1-e_2,\ e_1-e_3,\ e_1-e_4,\ e_1-e_5,\ e_1-e_6.
$$
Every eigenvector is by definition a nonzero vector in one of the eigenspaces belonging to an eigenvalue, and step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} shows that $0$ and $6$ are the only eigenvalues.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

The eigenvalues of $A$ are
$$
\boxed{-1\text{ with multiplicity }1,\qquad -7\text{ with multiplicity }5.}
$$


::: pf-proof

Since $A=B-7I_6$, every $B$-eigenvector with eigenvalue $\lambda$ is an $A$-eigenvector with eigenvalue $\lambda-7$. Applying this to the spectrum in step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} gives $6-7=-1$ and $0-7=-7$ with the same multiplicities.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-5}

The origin of $\dot x=Ax$ is $\boxed{\text{asymptotically stable}}$.


::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1-4){.pf-ref}, all eigenvalues of $A$ are real and strictly negative. Equivalently, decomposing an initial vector as
$$
x_0=c\mathbf1+y,
\qquad
\sum_i y_i=0,
$$
gives the solution
$$
x(t)=ce^{-t}\mathbf1+e^{-7t}y,
$$
which tends to $0$ exponentially as $t\to\infty$. Thus the origin is asymptotically stable.

:::
