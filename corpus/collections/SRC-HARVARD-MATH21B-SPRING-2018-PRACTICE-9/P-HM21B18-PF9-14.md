::: {.problem}
Use the Fourier expansion
\[
x=\sum_{n=1}^\infty 2(-1)^{n+1}\frac{\sin(nx)}{n}
\qquad(-\pi\le x\le\pi)
\]
to verify
\[
\sum_{n=1}^\infty\frac1{n^4}=\frac{\pi^4}{90}.
\]

(a) Write the Fourier-coefficient formula that gives the displayed sine coefficients and explain why no cosine coefficients occur.

(b) Integrate the series term-by-term to obtain a cosine series for $g(x)=x^2/2$ and determine its zeroth Fourier coefficient.

(c) Compute $\|g\|^2$ and use Parseval's identity to deduce the value of $\sum_{n\ge1}n^{-4}$.
:::
