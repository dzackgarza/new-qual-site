::: {.problem}
A real number $\alpha$ is called *algebraic* if there are integers $a_0,\ldots,a_n$, not all zero, such that
\[
a_0+a_1\alpha+\cdots+a_n\alpha^n=0.
\]
Prove that the set of algebraic real numbers is countable.
:::
