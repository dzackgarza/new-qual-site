::: {.problem}
(a) For each $n=2,3,4,\ldots$, is there an $n\times n$ matrix $A$ with
\[
A^{n-1}\ne0
\qquad\text{but}\qquad
A^n=0?
\]
Give an example or a proof of nonexistence.

(b) Is there an $n\times n$ upper triangular matrix $A$ with
\[
A^n\ne0
\qquad\text{but}\qquad
A^{n+1}=0?
\]
Give an example or a proof of nonexistence.
:::
