::: {.problem}
Let
\[
f(z)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}a_nz^n
\]
converge uniformly on the closed unit disk.
Show that
\[
\max_{|z|\le1}|f(z)|=\max_{|z|=1}|f(z)|.
\]
:::
