::: {.problem}
Consider the Taylor series
\[
\cos z=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^kz^{2k}}{(2k)!}.
\]
Show that at least one partial sum has a zero in the disk
\[
|z|<\frac{\pi}{2}.
\]
:::
