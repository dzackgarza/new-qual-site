::: {.problem}
Let $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb C$ be open and define
\[
|\Omega|=\{|z|:z\in\Omega\}\subseteq\mathbb R_{\ge0}.
\]
Show that $|\Omega|$ is relatively open in $\mathbb R_{\ge0}$.
:::
