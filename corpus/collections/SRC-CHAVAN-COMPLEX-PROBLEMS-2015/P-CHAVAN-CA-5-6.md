::: {.problem}
Let $f$ be a nonconstant holomorphic function on a bounded open set $\Omega$, continuous on $\overline\Omega$.
Show that either $f$ has a zero in $\Omega$ or $|f|$ assumes its minimum on $\partial\Omega$.
:::
