::: {.problem}
Let $\mathbb D$ be the open unit disk.
Show that $\operatorname{Aut}(\mathbb D)$ acts transitively on $\mathbb D$: for every $a,b\in\mathbb D$, there exists a biholomorphism
\[
f:\mathbb D\to\mathbb D
\]
such that $f(a)=b$.
:::
