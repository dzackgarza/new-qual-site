::: {.problem}
Make the transfer sequence explicit in the case of a trivial covering $\tilde{X} \to X$, where $\tilde{X} = X \times S^0$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Hatcher's complete published transfer construction in **§2.B, printed pp. 174--175**, specializes directly to $X\times S^0\to X$. He defines the short exact sequence of chain complexes by sending a singular simplex to the sum of its two lifts and projecting from the total space. The computation below carries out that construction in the two-sheet coordinates; no separate exactness argument is needed. This is a checked instantiation of the general theorem, not a found complete published solution of Exercise 2.B.9, whose source obligation remains open.

Hatcher does not call his sequence of chain complexes split, and it is not split in general. A chain map $s$ with $p_\sharp s=\mathrm{id}$ would exhibit $C_*(\tilde X;\ZZ_2)\cong C_*(X;\ZZ_2)\oplus C_*(X;\ZZ_2)$ as chain complexes and hence $H_*(\tilde X;\ZZ_2)\cong H_*(X;\ZZ_2)^{\oplus2}$, which for the double cover $S^1\to S^1$, $z\mapsto z^2$, fails in degree $1$: both sides have $H_1=\ZZ_2$, not $\ZZ_2^{\oplus2}$. What is split is the transfer sequence of the *trivial* covering, whose two-sheet coordinates are the point of the exercise.

Use $\ZZ_2$ coefficients, as in the transfer sequence of §2.B.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #trivial-chains}
In two-sheet coordinates the transfer and projection are $\tau(c)=(c,c)$ and $p_\#(a,b)=a+b$, giving the exact sequence
$$
0\longrightarrow C_*(X;\ZZ_2)
\xrightarrow{\Delta}
C_*(X;\ZZ_2)^2
\xrightarrow{+}
C_*(X;\ZZ_2)\longrightarrow0.
$$

::: pf-proof
Since $X\times S^0=X\amalg X$, its singular chain complex is $C_*(X;\ZZ_2)\oplus C_*(X;\ZZ_2)$. The two lifts of a base simplex are its images in the two copies, hence $\tau(c)=(c,c)$; projection adds them. The diagonal is injective, addition is surjective, and $(a,b)$ lies in its kernel exactly when $b=-a=a$ in characteristic two, which is exactly the diagonal image.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #chain-section}
The short exact sequence in step [](#trivial-chains){.pf-ref} splits as a sequence of chain complexes.

::: pf-proof
The chain map $s(c)=(c,0)$ satisfies $p_\#\circ s=\operatorname{id}$, so it is a section of projection. This is what the trivial covering adds to Hatcher's general construction, whose sequence of chain complexes need not split.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
All connecting homomorphisms vanish, and the transfer sequence is the split short exact sequence
$$
0\longrightarrow H_n(X;\ZZ_2)
\xrightarrow{x\mapsto(x,x)}
H_n(X;\ZZ_2)^2
\xrightarrow{(a,b)\mapsto a+b}
H_n(X;\ZZ_2)\longrightarrow0
$$
in each degree $n$.

::: pf-proof
By step [](#chain-section){.pf-ref}, the long exact sequence associated to a split short exact sequence of chain complexes has zero connecting homomorphisms. Exactness at each term therefore gives the displayed homology sequence.
:::

:::

*The cohomological form.* The transfer sequence has a cohomological form, and it is proved in full by Hausmann, *Mod Two Homology and Cohomology* (Springer, 2014), Proposition 4.3.9, "Transfer exact sequence", printed p. 123, for a 2-fold covering $p:\tilde X\to X$ over a CW complex. The transfer there is the dual $\tau^*:C^*(\tilde X;\ZZ_2)\to C^*(X;\ZZ_2)$ of the chain-level map $\tau$, and the sequence is
$$
\cdots\to H^m(X;\ZZ_2)\xrightarrow{H^*p}H^m(\tilde X;\ZZ_2)\xrightarrow{\tau^*}H^m(X;\ZZ_2)\xrightarrow{w(p)\smile-}H^{m+1}(X;\ZZ_2)\xrightarrow{H^*p}\cdots,
$$
exact and functorial for induced coverings, where $w(p)\in H^1(X;\ZZ_2)$ is the characteristic class of $p$. The proof dualises the chain-level sequence of an arbitrary 2-fold covering — the sequence of Hatcher's printed pp. 174--175, $0\to C_*(X;\ZZ_2)\xrightarrow{\tau}C_*(\tilde X;\ZZ_2)\xrightarrow{p_\sharp}C_*(X;\ZZ_2)\to0$ with $\tau$ the sum of the two lifts, which Hausmann's own §4.3.3 builds by the same rule for a general $p$ — and that sequence is exact over $\ZZ_2$ and functorial for induced coverings. Kronecker duality gives from it the short exact sequence of cochain complexes $0\to C^*(X;\ZZ_2)\xrightarrow{C^*p}C^*(\tilde X;\ZZ_2)\xrightarrow{\tau^*}C^*(X;\ZZ_2)\to0$, whose long exact sequence is Hausmann's Proposition 2.6.2, printed p. 34; the connecting homomorphism is then identified with the cup product by $w(p)$, which is where the characteristic class enters.

For the trivial covering $\tilde X=X\times S^0$ the characteristic class vanishes: Hausmann's Lemma 4.3.6(3), printed p. 120, states that over a CW complex a 2-fold covering is trivial if and only if its characteristic class is zero. The connecting homomorphism is therefore zero, and exactness makes the cohomological transfer sequence the short exact sequence
$$
0\to H^m(X;\ZZ_2)\xrightarrow{a\mapsto(a,a)}H^m(\tilde X;\ZZ_2)\xrightarrow{(\alpha,\beta)\mapsto\alpha+\beta}H^m(X;\ZZ_2)\to0
$$
in each degree, which is the same two-sheet computation at the cochain level: over $\ZZ_2$ the cochain complex of $\tilde X$ is $C^*(X;\ZZ_2)\oplus C^*(X;\ZZ_2)$, and $C^*p$ is the diagonal while $\tau^*$ is addition, because $C_*p(a,b)=a+b$ is addition and $\tau(c)=(c,c)$ is the diagonal. The sequence splits by $\alpha\mapsto(\alpha,0)$, so $H^m(\tilde X;\ZZ_2)\cong H^m(X;\ZZ_2)^{\oplus2}$ for every $m$. Hausmann asks for exactly this computation as Exercise 4.4 of Chapter 4, printed p. 166, "Write the transfer exact sequence for a trivial 2-fold covering", and prints no solution to it; the two-sheet coordinates supplied here are the cohomological counterpart of those of the exercise as Hatcher states it.

::: {.remark title="Published solution"}
Hausmann, *Mod Two Homology and Cohomology* (Springer, 2014), read in `tmp/sources/hausmann-mod-two-homology-cohomology.md`: Proposition 4.3.9, printed p. 123, proves the transfer exact sequence in cohomology, over a CW complex, with the characteristic class $w(p)$ as the connecting map, and his Lemma 4.3.6(3), printed p. 120, identifies $w(p)=0$ with triviality of the covering; the corresponding computation for the trivial 2-fold covering is asked for as Exercise 4.4 of Chapter 4, printed p. 166. Neither prints the two-sheet cochain coordinates for it. Hatcher, *Algebraic Topology* (2002), read in `tmp/sources/hatcher-2002-algebraic-topology.md`: the transfer for a two-sheeted covering, printed pp. 174--175, $\tau$ the sum of the two lifts, the short exact sequence of chain complexes $0\to C_n(X;\ZZ_2)\xrightarrow{\tau}C_n(\tilde X;\ZZ_2)\xrightarrow{p_\sharp}C_n(X;\ZZ_2)\to0$, and the long exact sequence it yields. Hatcher neither calls that sequence split nor proves it split, and it is not split as chain complexes in general; for the trivial covering it is, by the two-sheet coordinates, which is what the exercise asks for. The exercise is Exercise 9 of §2.B, printed p. 177, and the trivial covering is that construction with $\tilde X=X\times S^0$.
:::
