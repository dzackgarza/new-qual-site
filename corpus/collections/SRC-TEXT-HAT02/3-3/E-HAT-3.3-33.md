Show that if $M$ is a compact contractible $n$-manifold then $\partial M$ is a homology $(n-1)$-sphere, that is, $H_i(\partial M; \mathbb{Z}) \approx H_i(S^{n-1}; \mathbb{Z})$ for all $i$.
