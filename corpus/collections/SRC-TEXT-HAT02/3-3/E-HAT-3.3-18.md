::: {.problem}
Show that a direct limit $\varinjlim G_\alpha$ of torsionfree abelian groups $G_\alpha$ is torsionfree.
More generally, show that any finitely generated subgroup of $\varinjlim G_\alpha$ is realized as a subgroup of some $G_\alpha$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $G=\varinjlim G_\alpha$.

First suppose $x\in G$ and $mx=0$ for some nonzero integer $m$. Represent $x$ by $g_\alpha\in G_\alpha$. The equality $mx=0$ means that for some $\beta\ge\alpha$ the image $g_\beta$ satisfies
\[
mg_\beta=0.
\]
Since $G_\beta$ is torsionfree, $g_\beta=0$, hence $x=0$ in the direct limit. Thus $G$ is torsionfree.

For the stronger statement, let $H\le G$ be generated by $x_1,\ldots,x_r$. Choose one stage $G_\alpha$ containing representatives $g_1,\ldots,g_r$. Let
\[
\phi:\mathbb Z^r\to H,
\qquad e_i\mapsto x_i.
\]
Its kernel $K$ is a subgroup of the finitely generated free abelian group $\mathbb Z^r$, hence is finitely generated. Choose generators $k_1,\ldots,k_s$ of $K$.

Each relation $k_j$ becomes zero at some later stage. By directedness there is a single $\beta\ge\alpha$ at which all these relations vanish. Let $H_\beta\le G_\beta$ be the subgroup generated by the images of the $g_i$. Every relation among these images is certainly a relation in the direct limit, so its relation lattice is contained in $K$; conversely, by construction all generators of $K$ already vanish in $G_\beta$. Hence the two relation lattices coincide. Therefore
\[
H_\beta\cong\mathbb Z^r/K\cong H.
\]
Thus every finitely generated subgroup of the direct limit is realized as a subgroup of some stage $G_\beta$.
:::
