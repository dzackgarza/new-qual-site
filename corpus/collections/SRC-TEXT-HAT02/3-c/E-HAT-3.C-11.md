::: {.problem}
If $T^n$ is the $n$-dimensional torus, the product of $n$ circles, show that the Pontryagin ring $H_*(T^n; \mathbb{Z})$ is the exterior algebra $\Lambda_\mathbb{Z}[x_1, \dots, x_n]$ with $|x_i| = 1$.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

$H_*(S^1; \ZZ) = \ZZ \oplus \ZZ x$ with $|x| = 1$, and the Pontryagin product (induced by the group structure of $S^1$) satisfies $x^2 = 0$.

::: pf-proof

$H_1(S^1) = \ZZ$ generated by $x$, and $x \cdot x = 0$ since $H_2(S^1) = 0$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

By the Künneth theorem, $H_*(T^n) \cong H_*(S^1)^{\otimes n}$.

::: pf-proof

$T^n = (S^1)^n$, and the Künneth theorem computes the homology of a product.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

The Pontryagin product on $H_*(T^n)$ is the tensor product of the Pontryagin products on each factor (the group structure of $T^n$ is the product of the group structures).

::: pf-proof

the multiplication on $T^n$ is coordinatewise.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s4}

Hence $H_*(T^n) \cong \Lambda_\ZZ[x_1, \ldots, x_n]$, the exterior algebra on generators $x_1, \ldots, x_n$ of degree $1$ (where $x_i$ is the generator of $H_1$ of the $i$-th circle factor).

::: pf-proof

Steps [](#s1){.pf-ref}, [](#s2){.pf-ref} and [](#s3){.pf-ref} (the tensor product of exterior algebras on one generator each is the exterior algebra on all the generators).

:::
