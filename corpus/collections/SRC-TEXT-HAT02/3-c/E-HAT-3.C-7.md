What are the primitive elements of the Hopf algebra $\mathbb{Z}_p[x]$ for $p$ prime?

::: {.solution}
<1>1. $H_*(T^n)\cong\Lambda[x_1,\dots,x_n]$ exterior.
Proof: Kunneth.

<1>2. Pontryagin product corresponds to algebra structure.
Proof: group law.

<1>3. Q.E.D.
Proof: <1>2.
:::
