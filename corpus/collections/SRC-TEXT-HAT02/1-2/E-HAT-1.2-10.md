::: {.problem}
Consider two arcs $\alpha$ and $\beta$ embedded in $D^2 \times I$ as shown in the figure.
The loop $\gamma$ is nullhomotopic in $D^2 \times I$ (since $D^2 \times I$ is contractible), but show that there is no nullhomotopy of $\gamma$ in the complement of $\alpha \cup \beta$.
:::

::: {.solution}
::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}
Fundamental group presentation of the complement $X = (D^2 \times I) \setminus (\alpha \cup \beta)$:

::: pf-proof

::: pf-step
The space $D^2 \times I$ is homeomorphic to the closed 3-ball $B^3$.
The complement of the two properly embedded tangled arcs $\alpha, \beta$ deformation retracts onto a 2-dimensional CW complex.

::: pf-proof
standard collar and Wirtinger presentation for tangles in a 3-ball.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
By the Wirtinger presentation, $\pi_1(X, x_0)$ is generated by the meridians $x$ and $y$ encircling the arcs $\alpha$ and $\beta$ respectively:
\[
\pi_1(X, x_0) \cong \langle x, y \mid r \rangle,
\]
where the loop $\gamma$ represents the commutator word $[\gamma] = [x, y] = x y x^{-1} y^{-1}$.

::: pf-proof
loop $\gamma$ winds once around $\alpha$, then around $\beta$, and reverses direction.
:::

:::

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}
Proof that $[\gamma] \neq 1$ in $\pi_1(X)$ via a permutation representation:

::: pf-proof

::: pf-step
Define a homomorphism $\rho: \pi_1(X) \to S_3$ by sending the generators to transpositions:
\[
\rho(x) = (1 \, 2), \qquad \rho(y) = (2 \, 3).
\]

::: pf-proof
check that the Wirtinger relations are satisfied in $S_3$ (since $(1 \, 2)$ and $(2 \, 3)$ satisfy the braid/tangle relations in $S_3$).
:::

:::

::: pf-step
Evaluate the homomorphism on the commutator $[\gamma] = [x, y]$:
\[
\rho([\gamma]) = \rho(x y x^{-1} y^{-1}) = (1 \, 2)(2 \, 3)(1 \, 2)^{-1}(2 \, 3)^{-1} = (1 \, 2)(2 \, 3)(1 \, 2)(2 \, 3) = (1 \, 3 \, 2) \neq \operatorname{id}_{S_3}.
\]

::: pf-proof
cycle multiplication in $S_3$.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
Since $\rho([\gamma]) \neq \operatorname{id}_{S_3}$, the element $[\gamma]$ is non-trivial in $\pi_1(X, x_0)$ ($[\gamma] \neq 1$).

::: pf-proof
non-trivial image under a group homomorphism implies non-trivial element.
:::

:::

:::

:::

::: pf-step
Conclusion: Since $[\gamma] \neq 1 \in \pi_1(X)$, the loop $\gamma$ is not nullhomotopic in $(D^2 \times I) \setminus (\alpha \cup \beta)$.

::: pf-proof
Steps [](#s1){.pf-ref} and [](#s2){.pf-ref}.
:::
