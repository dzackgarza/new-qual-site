::: {.problem}
Finish the homotopy classification of lens spaces begun in Exercise 2 of §3.E by showing that two lens spaces $L_m(\ell_1, \ldots, \ell_n)$ and $L_m(\ell_1', \ldots, \ell_n')$ are homotopy equivalent if $\ell_1 \cdots \ell_n \equiv \pm k^n \ell_1' \cdots \ell_n' \pmod{m}$ for some integer $k$, via the following steps:

(a) Reduce to the case $k = 1$ by showing that $L_m(\ell_1', \ldots, \ell_n') = L_m(k\ell_1', \ldots, k\ell_n')$ if $k$ is relatively prime to $m$. [Rechoose the generator of the $\ZZ_m$ action on $S^{2n-1}$.]

(b) Let $f: L \to L'$ be a map constructed as in part (b) of the exercise in §3.E. Construct a map $g: L \to L'$ as a composition $L \to L \vee S^{2n-1} \to L \vee S^{2n-1} \to L'$ where the first map collapses the boundary of a small ball to a point, the second map is the wedge of the identity on $L$ and a map of some degree $d$ on $S^{2n-1}$, and the third map is $f$ on $L$ and the projection $S^{2n-1} \to L'$ on $S^{2n-1}$. Show that $g$ has degree $k_1 \cdots k_n + dm$, that is, $g$ induces multiplication by $k_1 \cdots k_n + dm$ on $H_{2n-1}(-; \ZZ)$. [Show first that a lift of $g$ to the universal cover $S^{2n-1}$ has this degree.]

(c) If $\ell_1 \cdots \ell_n \equiv \pm \ell_1' \cdots \ell_n' \pmod{m}$, choose $d$ so that $k_1 \cdots k_n + dm = \pm 1$ and show this implies that $g$ induces an isomorphism on all homotopy groups, hence is a homotopy equivalence. [For $\pi_i$ with $i > 1$, consider a lift of $g$ to the universal cover.]
:::

::: {.solution}
No retained solution set covers this exercise, so the argument is the card's own, following the three printed steps. Put $P=\ell_1\cdots\ell_n$ and $P'=\ell_1'\cdots\ell_n'$, and suppose $P\equiv\pm k^nP'\pmod m$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #part-a}
(a) Replacing the generator of the $\ZZ_m$-action on either factor by its $k$-th power, with $k$ coprime to $m$, replaces every $\ell_j$ by $k\ell_j$ and leaves the lens space unchanged; since $P\equiv\pm k^nP'$ makes $k$ a unit modulo $m$, we may assume $P\equiv\pm P'\pmod m$.

::: pf-proof
A lens space is the quotient of $S^{2n-1}\subset\CC^n$ by the action of $\ZZ_m$ generated by $(\zeta^{\ell_1},\ldots,\zeta^{\ell_n})$ with $\zeta=e^{2\pi i/m}$; choosing the generator $\zeta^k$ of the same group replaces the generator of the action by its $k$-th power, hence replaces each $\ell_j$ by $k\ell_j$ and gives the same subgroup of the torus, that is, the same quotient. Replacing $P$ by $k^nP$ and $P'$ by $k^nP'$ is exactly what this does to the two products. Since $P$ and $P'$ are units modulo $m$ by definition of a lens space and $P\equiv\pm k^nP'$, also $k^n$ is a unit, so $k$ is coprime to $m$ and the reduction is legitimate.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #part-b}
(b) The composite $g\colon L\to L\vee S^{2n-1}\to L\vee S^{2n-1}\to L'$ of the pinch, the wedge of the identity with a degree-$d$ self-map of the sphere, and $f$ with the covering projection induces multiplication by $k_1\cdots k_n+dm$ on $H_{2n-1}(-;\ZZ)$.

::: pf-proof
The collapse of the boundary of a small ball in $L$ sends the fundamental class $[L]$ to $[L]+[S^{2n-1}]$, the ball becoming the complementary cell of the wedge. The second map fixes the $L$ summand and multiplies the sphere's class by $d$. The third is $f$ on $L$, which multiplies by $\deg f=d_0=k_1\cdots k_n$, and the universal covering $S^{2n-1}\to L'$ on the sphere, which is $m$-sheeted and so multiplies the top class by $m$. Composing, $[L]\mapsto d_0[L']+dm[L']$, so the degree is $d_0+dm$. The same count is obtained on the lift to universal covers, where the added sphere contributes $dm$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #part-c}
(c) If $P\equiv\pm P'\pmod m$ then $d_0\equiv\pm1\pmod m$; choosing $d$ with $d_0+dm=\pm1$ makes $g$ a homotopy equivalence.

::: pf-proof
The maps $f$ of Exercise 2 of §3.E are built from integers $k_j$ with $k_j\ell_j\equiv\ell_j'\pmod m$ and have degree $d_0=k_1\cdots k_n$, whence $d_0P\equiv P'\pmod m$; with $P\equiv\pm P'$ and $P$ a unit this gives $d_0\equiv\pm1\pmod m$, so some $d$ satisfies $d_0+dm=\pm1$. For that $d$, step [](#part-b){.pf-ref} gives $\deg g=\pm1$. For $n=1$ there is nothing to prove: $L_m(\ell)=S^1$ for every $\ell$, since $\ZZ_m$ acts on $S^1$ by rotations with quotient $S^1$. For $n\ge2$, $S^{2n-1}$ is simply connected and a lift $\widetilde g\colon S^{2n-1}\to S^{2n-1}$ exists; the two coverings both have degree $m$, so $\deg\widetilde g=\deg g=\pm1$ and $\widetilde g$ is a homotopy equivalence. Hence $g$ induces isomorphisms on $\pi_i$ for $i\ge2$ through $\widetilde g$, and on $\pi_1$ because $g$ agrees with $f$ on the $1$-skeleton and $f$ induces an isomorphism there.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step}
$\boxed{L_m(\ell_1,\dots,\ell_n)\simeq L_m(\ell'_1,\dots,\ell'_n)}$ whenever $P\equiv\pm k^nP'\pmod m$.

::: pf-proof
Steps [](#part-a){.pf-ref} and [](#part-c){.pf-ref} produce a map $g$ between the two lens spaces inducing isomorphisms on all homotopy groups. Lens spaces are finite CW complexes, so Whitehead's theorem makes $g$ a homotopy equivalence. This proves the sufficiency of the congruence, which is the half of the classification left open by Exercise 2 of §3.E.
:::
