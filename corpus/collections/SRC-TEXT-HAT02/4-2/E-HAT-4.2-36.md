::: {.problem}
For basepoint-preserving maps $f: S^1 \to X$ and $g: S^n \to X$ with $n > 1$, show that the Whitehead product $[f, g]$ is $\pm(g - fg)$, where $fg$ denotes the action of $f$ on $g$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let
\[
f:S^1\to X,\qquad g:S^n\to X,
\quad n>1.
\]
The product \(S^1\times S^n\) is obtained from the wedge
\[
S^1\vee S^n
\]
by attaching one \((n+1)\)-cell. Its attaching map is, up to the standard sign convention, the Whitehead product
\[
[\iota_1,\iota_n].
\]

Lift the wedge to the universal cover. The lift is a bi-infinite string of \(n\)-spheres indexed by the deck group generated by the loop \(\iota_1\). A lift of the top cell joins two consecutive \(n\)-spheres, so its boundary represents the difference
\[
\iota_n-(\iota_1\cdot\iota_n)
\]
in \(\pi_n\) of the lifted wedge, up to orientation sign. Applying the map induced by \(f\vee g\) gives
\[
[f,g]=\pm\bigl(g-fg\bigr),
\]
where \(fg\) denotes the action of \([f]\in\pi_1(X)\) on \([g]\in\pi_n(X)\). Hence
\[
\boxed{[f,g]=\pm(g-fg).}
\]
:::
