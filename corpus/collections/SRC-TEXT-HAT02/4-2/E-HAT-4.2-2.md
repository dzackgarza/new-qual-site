::: {.problem}
Show the action of $\pi_1(\RP^n)$ on $\pi_n(\RP^n) \approx \ZZ$ is trivial for $n$ odd and nontrivial for $n$ even.
:::

::: {.solution}
For $n=1$, $\RP^1\cong S^1$ and the action of $\pi_1$ on itself is conjugation in $\ZZ$, hence trivial.

For $n\ge2$, the universal covering is
$$
p:S^n\to\RP^n,
$$
with deck group $\ZZ_2$ generated by the antipodal map
$$
a:S^n\to S^n,
\qquad a(x)=-x.
$$
Under the canonical identification
$$
\pi_n(\RP^n)\cong\pi_n(S^n)\cong\ZZ,
$$
the $\pi_1$-action is the action induced by this deck transformation. The antipodal map has degree
$$
\deg a=(-1)^{n+1}.
$$
Hence its action on $\pi_n(S^n)\cong\ZZ$ is multiplication by $(-1)^{n+1}$.

Thus when $n$ is odd the action is multiplication by $+1$, hence trivial, while when $n$ is even it is multiplication by $-1$, hence nontrivial:
$$
\boxed{
\gamma\cdot m=
\begin{cases}
m,&n\text{ odd},\\
-m,&n\text{ even}.
\end{cases}}
$$
:::
