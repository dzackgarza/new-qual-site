Show the action of $\pi_1(\mathbb{RP}^n)$ on $\pi_n(\mathbb{RP}^n) \approx \mathbb{Z}$ is trivial for $n$ odd and nontrivial for $n$ even.

::: {.solution}
For \(n\ge2\), the universal covering is
\[
p:S^n\to\mathbb{RP}^n,
\]
with deck group \(\mathbb Z_2\) generated by the antipodal map
\[
a:S^n\to S^n,
\qquad a(x)=-x.
\]
Under the canonical identification
\[
\pi_n(\mathbb{RP}^n)\cong\pi_n(S^n)\cong\mathbb Z,
\]
the \(\pi_1\)-action is the action induced by this deck transformation. The antipodal map has degree
\[
\deg a=(-1)^{n+1}.
\]
Hence its action on \(\pi_n(S^n)\cong\mathbb Z\) is multiplication by \((-1)^{n+1}\).

Thus when \(n\) is odd the action is multiplication by \(+1\), hence trivial, while when \(n\) is even it is multiplication by \(-1\), hence nontrivial:
\[
\boxed{
\gamma\cdot m=
\begin{cases}
m,&n\text{ odd},\\
-m,&n\text{ even}.
\end{cases}}
\]
:::
