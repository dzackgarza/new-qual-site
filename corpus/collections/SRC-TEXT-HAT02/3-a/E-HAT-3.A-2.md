Show that $\operatorname{Tor}(A, \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z})$ is isomorphic to the torsion subgroup of $A$.
Deduce that $A$ is torsionfree if $\operatorname{Tor}(A, B) = 0$ for all $B$.
