Show that $\otimes$ and Tor commute with direct limits: $(\varinjlim_\alpha A_\alpha) \otimes B = \varinjlim(A_\alpha \otimes B)$ and $\operatorname{Tor}(\varinjlim A_\alpha, B) = \varinjlim \operatorname{Tor}(A_\alpha, B)$.
