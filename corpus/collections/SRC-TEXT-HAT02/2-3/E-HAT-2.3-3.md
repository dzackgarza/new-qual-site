Show that if $\tilde{h}$ is a reduced homology theory, then $\tilde{h}_n(\text{point}) = 0$ for all $n$.
Deduce that there are suspension isomorphisms $\tilde{h}_n(X) \approx \tilde{h}_{n+1}(SX)$ for all $n$.
