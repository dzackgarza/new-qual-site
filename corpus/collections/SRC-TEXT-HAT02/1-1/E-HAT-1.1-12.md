::: {.problem}
Show that every homomorphism $\pi_1(S^1) \to \pi_1(S^1)$ can be realized as the induced homomorphism $\varphi_*$ of a map $\varphi: S^1 \to S^1$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Use $1\in S^1\subset\CC$ as the basepoint, and write $\omega(t)=e^{2\pi i t}$ for the positive generator of $\pi_1(S^1,1)\cong\ZZ$.

::: pf

::: pf-step
Every homomorphism $u:\pi_1(S^1,1)\to\pi_1(S^1,1)$ is multiplication by an integer $n$.

::: pf-proof
The group $\pi_1(S^1,1)$ is generated by $[\omega]$, so $u$ is determined by $u([\omega])=n[\omega]$ for a unique integer $n$.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
The based map $\varphi_n:S^1\to S^1$ defined by $\varphi_n(z)=z^n$ induces multiplication by $n$.

::: pf-proof
The map is continuous for every integer $n$ and fixes $1$. Its composite with the generator is
$$
(\varphi_n\circ\omega)(t)=e^{2\pi i n t},
$$
which represents $n[\omega]$ in $\pi_1(S^1,1)$. Thus $(\varphi_n)_*([\omega])=n[\omega]$, and equality on the generator gives $(\varphi_n)_*=u$.
:::
