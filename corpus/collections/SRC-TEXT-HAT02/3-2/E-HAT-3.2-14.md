::: {.problem}
Let $q: \mathbb{RP}^\infty \to \mathbb{CP}^\infty$ be the natural quotient map obtained by regarding both spaces as quotients of $S^\infty$, modulo multiplication by real scalars in one case and complex scalars in the other.
Show that the induced map $q^*: H^*(\mathbb{CP}^\infty; \mathbb{Z}) \to H^*(\mathbb{RP}^\infty; \mathbb{Z})$ is surjective in even dimensions by showing first by a geometric argument that the restriction $q: \mathbb{RP}^2 \to \mathbb{CP}^1$ induces a surjection on $H^2$ and then appealing to cup product structures.

Next, form a quotient space $X$ of $\mathbb{RP}^\infty \amalg \mathbb{CP}^n$ by identifying each point $x \in \mathbb{RP}^{2n}$ with $q(x) \in \mathbb{CP}^n$.
Show there are ring isomorphisms $H^*(X; \mathbb{Z}) \approx \mathbb{Z}[\alpha]/(2\alpha^{n+1})$ and $H^*(X; \mathbb{Z}_2) \approx \mathbb{Z}_2[\alpha, \beta]/(\beta^2 - \alpha^{2n+1})$, where $|\alpha| = 2$ and $|\beta| = 2n+1$.
Make a similar construction and analysis for the quotient map $q: \mathbb{CP}^\infty \to \mathbb{HP}^\infty$.
:::

::: {.solution}
**Goal.** Show $q^*: H^*(\CP^\infty;\ZZ) \to H^*(\RP^\infty;\ZZ)$ is surjective in even degrees, compute the cohomology of the quotient space $X$, and analyze the $\CP^\infty \to \HP^\infty$ analogue.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

$q^*$ is surjective in even dimensions.

::: pf-proof

::: pf-step

$H^*(\CP^\infty;\ZZ) = \ZZ[\alpha]$ with $|\alpha| = 2$, and $H^*(\RP^\infty;\ZZ) = \ZZ[\beta]/(2\beta)$ with $|\beta| = 2$.

::: pf-proof

standard cohomology rings of infinite projective spaces.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

The restriction $q: \RP^2 \to \CP^1$ induces a surjection on $H^2$.

::: pf-proof

$\RP^2$ and $\CP^1$ are the $2$-skeleta; $q$ maps $\RP^2$ onto $\CP^1$ with degree $2$ (the map $S^2 \to S^2$ given by $z \mapsto z^2$ has degree $2$), so $q^*: H^2(\CP^1;\ZZ) = \ZZ \to H^2(\RP^2;\ZZ) = \ZZ/2$ is the surjection $\ZZ \to \ZZ/2$.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Hence $q^*(\alpha) = \beta$ (the generator of $H^2(\RP^\infty;\ZZ) = \ZZ/2$).

::: pf-proof

$q^*$ on $H^2$ is the reduction mod $2$.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Since $H^*(\RP^\infty;\ZZ) = \ZZ[\beta]/(2\beta)$ is generated by $\beta = q^*(\alpha)$, the map $q^*$ is surjective in all even degrees.

::: pf-proof

$q^*(\alpha^k) = \beta^k$, and the $\beta^k$ generate $H^{2k}(\RP^\infty;\ZZ)$.

:::

:::

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

The quotient space $X$ and its cohomology.

::: pf-proof

::: pf-step

$X = \RP^\infty \amalg \CP^n / (x \sim q(x))$ for $x \in \RP^{2n}$.

::: pf-proof

definition.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

$H^*(X;\ZZ) \cong \ZZ[\alpha]/(2\alpha^{n+1})$.

::: pf-proof

$X$ is built from $\CP^n$ (with $H^* = \ZZ[\alpha]/(\alpha^{n+1})$) by attaching $\RP^\infty$ along $\RP^{2n}$; the attaching map identifies $\alpha$ with the generator of $H^2(\RP^\infty)$, and the relation $2\alpha = 0$ in $\RP^\infty$ combined with the truncation at $\CP^n$ gives $2\alpha^{n+1} = 0$.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

$H^*(X;\ZZ_2) \cong \ZZ_2[\alpha, \beta]/(\beta^2 - \alpha^{2n+1})$.

::: pf-proof

over $\ZZ_2$, $H^*(\RP^\infty;\ZZ_2) = \ZZ_2[\beta]$ with $|\beta| = 1$, and $H^*(\CP^n;\ZZ_2) = \ZZ_2[\alpha]/(\alpha^{n+1})$; the identification of $\RP^{2n}$ with the $2n$-skeleton of $\CP^n$ gives the relation $\beta^2 = \alpha^{2n+1}$ (the generator of $H^{2n+1}$).

:::

:::

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

The $\CP^\infty \to \HP^\infty$ analogue.

::: pf-proof

::: pf-step

$H^*(\HP^\infty;\ZZ) = \ZZ[\gamma]$ with $|\gamma| = 4$.

::: pf-proof

standard.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

The quotient map $q: \CP^\infty \to \HP^\infty$ (from $S^\infty$ modulo complex scalars to modulo quaternionic scalars) induces $q^*(\gamma) = \alpha^2$.

::: pf-proof

the map $\CP^1 \to \HP^1$ has degree $1$ on $H^4$, and $H^4(\CP^\infty) = \ZZ\alpha^2$.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

The analogous quotient space $Y = \CP^\infty \amalg \HP^n / (x \sim q(x))$ has $H^*(Y;\ZZ) \cong \ZZ[\alpha]/(\alpha^{2n+2})$ and $H^*(Y;\ZZ_2) \cong \ZZ_2[\alpha, \beta]/(\beta^2 - \alpha^{2n+2})$.

::: pf-proof

same Mayer–Vietoris / cell-attachment argument with the roles of $\CP$ and $\HP$ swapped.

:::
