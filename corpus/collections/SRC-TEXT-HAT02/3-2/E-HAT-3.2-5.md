# E-HAT-3.2-5

Show the ring $H^*(\mathbb{RP}^\infty; \mathbb{Z}_{2k})$ is isomorphic to $\mathbb{Z}_{2k}[\alpha, \beta] / (2\alpha, 2\beta, \alpha^2 - k\beta)$ where $|\alpha| = 1$ and $|\beta| = 2$.
