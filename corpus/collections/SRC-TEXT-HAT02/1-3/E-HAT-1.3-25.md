Let $\varphi: \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}^2$ be the linear transformation $\varphi(x,y) = (2x, y/2)$.
This generates an action of $\mathbb{Z}$ on $X = \mathbb{R}^2 - \{0\}$.
Show this action is a covering space action and compute $\pi_1(X/\mathbb{Z})$.
Show the orbit space $X/\mathbb{Z}$ is non-Hausdorff, and describe how it is a union of four subspaces homeomorphic to $S^1 \times \mathbb{R}$, coming from the complementary components of the $x$-axis and the $y$-axis.
