::: {.problem}
Show that $\operatorname{Ext}(\ZZ_{p^\infty}, \ZZ_p) \approx \ZZ_p$.
:::

::: {.solution}

The statement is Hatcher, *Algebraic Topology* (2002), §3.F Exercise 6, printed p. 320.
The computation is the card's own: the standard free resolution of the Prüfer group, and the cokernel of the dual of its first differential. Coefficients are $\ZZ$.

Let $\ZZ_{p^{\infty}}$ be the Prüfer group, generated by elements $x_{n}$ of order $p^{n}$ with $p x_{n+1}=x_{n}$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #resolution}
$0\to F_{1}\xrightarrow{d}F_{0}\to\ZZ_{p^{\infty}}\to0$ is a free resolution, where $F_{0}$ is free on $e_{1},e_{2},\ldots$ with $e_{n}\mapsto x_{n}$, $F_{1}$ is free on $r_{0},r_{1},\ldots$, and $d(r_{0})=pe_{1}$, $d(r_{n})=pe_{n+1}-e_{n}$ for $n\ge1$.

::: pf-proof
The map $F_{0}\to\ZZ_{p^{\infty}}$, $e_{n}\mapsto x_{n}$, is onto because the $x_{n}$ generate. Each $d(r_{n})$ lies in the kernel: $px_{1}=0$ and $px_{n+1}-x_{n}=0$. Conversely let $K$ be the kernel and $K'=\bigoplus_{n\ge0}\ZZ r_{n}\subseteq K$. Modulo $K'$ one has $e_{n}\equiv p e_{n+1}$, hence $e_{n}\equiv p^{N-n}e_{N}$ for $n\le N$; so an element $w=\sum_{n\le N}a_{n}e_{n}$ of $K$ satisfies $w\equiv c\,e_{N}\pmod{K'}$ with $c=\sum_{n}a_{n}p^{N-n}$. Since $w\in K$, $cx_{N}=0$, so $p^{N}\mid c$, say $c=p^{N}m$. Iterating $pe_{n+1}=d(r_{n+1})+e_{n}$ gives
$$
p^{N}e_{N}=\sum_{j=1}^{N}p^{j-1}d(r_{j})+d(r_{0})\in K',
$$
so $w\equiv mp^{N}e_{N}\equiv0\pmod{K'}$ and $K=K'$. The $r_{n}$ are independent because $r_{n}$ is the only one involving $e_{n+1}$ with coefficient $p$ and $r_{n-1}$ the only other involving $e_{n}$; hence $F_{1}\to K$ is an isomorphism and the sequence is exact with both terms free.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #duals}
$\operatorname{Hom}(F_{0},\ZZ_{p})\cong\prod_{n\ge1}\ZZ_{p}$ and $\operatorname{Hom}(F_{1},\ZZ_{p})\cong\prod_{n\ge0}\ZZ_{p}$.

::: pf-proof
A free module is a direct sum of copies of $\ZZ$, and $\operatorname{Hom}$ out of a direct sum is the product of the $\operatorname{Hom}$'s from the summands (the universal property of the product); $\operatorname{Hom}(\ZZ,\ZZ_{p})=\ZZ_{p}$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #dual-map}
Under these identifications $d^{*}$ sends $(a_{1},a_{2},\ldots)$ to $(0,-a_{1},-a_{2},\ldots)$, so $\operatorname{im}d^{*}=\{(b_{0},b_{1},\ldots):b_{0}=0\}$.

::: pf-proof
If $\varphi$ is represented by $a_{n}=\varphi(e_{n})$, then $d^{*}\varphi$ is determined by its values on the $r_{n}$:
$$
(d^{*}\varphi)(r_{0})=\varphi(pe_{1})=pa_{1}=0,\qquad (d^{*}\varphi)(r_{n})=\varphi(pe_{n+1}-e_{n})=pa_{n+1}-a_{n}=-a_{n},
$$
because $p=0$ in $\ZZ_{p}$. So the image is exactly the set of tuples with vanishing first coordinate, the map being onto that subgroup since $(a_{n})$ is arbitrary.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #ext}
$\operatorname{Ext}(\ZZ_{p^{\infty}},\ZZ_{p})=\operatorname{coker}d^{*}\cong\ZZ_{p}$.

::: pf-proof
With the free resolution of step [](#resolution){.pf-ref}, $\operatorname{Ext}^{1}_{\ZZ}(\ZZ_{p^{\infty}},\ZZ_{p})$ is computed by applying $\operatorname{Hom}(-,\ZZ_{p})$ and taking the cokernel of $d^{*}$, because $\operatorname{Ext}^{0}=\ker d^{*}$ and $\operatorname{Ext}^{1}=\operatorname{coker}d^{*}$ for a length-one free resolution. By step [](#dual-map){.pf-ref}
$$
\operatorname{coker}d^{*}=\prod_{n\ge0}\ZZ_{p}\big/\{(b_{n}):b_{0}=0\}\cong\ZZ_{p},
$$
projection onto the coordinate of $r_{0}$ being the isomorphism. Hence $\operatorname{Ext}(\ZZ_{p^{\infty}},\ZZ_{p})\cong\ZZ_{p}$.
:::
