Show that the subalgebra of $\mathcal{A}_2$ generated by $Sq^i$ for $i \leq 2$ has dimension 8 as a vector space over $\mathbb{Z}_2$, with multiplicative structure encoded in the following diagram, where diagonal lines indicate left-multiplication by $Sq^1$ and horizontal lines indicate left-multiplication by $Sq^2$.

::: {.solution}
Write
\[
a=Sq^1,
\qquad b=Sq^2.
\]
The mod-2 Adem relations give
\[
a^2=0,
\qquad
aba=b^2.
\tag{1}
\]
From these two relations we obtain
\[
b^2a=0,
\qquad
ab^2=0,
\qquad
b^4=0,
\tag{2}
\]
and
\[
abab=b^3=baba.
\tag{3}
\]
Consequently every word in \(a,b\) reduces to a linear combination of
\[
\boxed{
1,\ a,\ b,\ ab,\ ba,\ b^2,\ bab,\ b^3.}
\tag{4}
\]

These eight elements are linearly independent in the Steenrod algebra. To see this, use the admissible basis. We have
\[
ab=Sq^3,
\qquad
ba=Sq^2Sq^1,
\qquad
b^2=Sq^3Sq^1,
\]
while another Adem relation gives
\[
bab=Sq^2Sq^3=Sq^5+Sq^4Sq^1,
\]
and hence
\[
b^3=(bab)a=Sq^5Sq^1.
\]
These are nonzero combinations of distinct admissible basis elements, and the grading separates all cases except degree three, where \(Sq^3\) and \(Sq^2Sq^1\) are distinct admissible monomials. Thus (4) is a basis and the generated subalgebra has dimension \(8\).

It remains to record left multiplication. From (1)--(3):
\[
\begin{array}{c|cccccccc}
z&1&a&b&ab&ba&b^2&bab&b^3\\ \hline
az&a&0&ab&0&b^2&0&b^3&0\\
bz&b&ba&b^2&bab&0&b^3&0&0.
\end{array}
\]
Thus left multiplication by \(Sq^1=a\) and \(Sq^2=b\) gives exactly the diagonal and horizontal arrows in Hatcher's diagram. Therefore
\[
\boxed{\dim_{\mathbb Z_2}A(1)=8,}
\]
with the stated multiplicative structure.
:::
