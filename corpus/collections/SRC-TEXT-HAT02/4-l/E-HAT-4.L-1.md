::: {.problem}
Determine all cohomology operations $H^1(X; \ZZ) \to H^n(X; \ZZ)$, $H^2(X; \ZZ) \to H^n(X; \ZZ)$, and $H^1(X; \ZZ_p) \to H^n(X; \ZZ_p)$ for $p$ prime.
:::

::: {.solution}
Cohomology operations
$$
H^m(-;G)\longrightarrow H^n(-;H)
$$
are represented by classes in
$$
H^n(K(G,m);H).
$$
We apply this to the three cases.

For $H^1(-;\ZZ)$, the representing space is
$$
K(\ZZ,1)=S^1.
$$
Hence
$$
H^n(S^1;\ZZ)=
\begin{cases}
\ZZ,&n=1,\\
0,&n>1.
\end{cases}
$$
Thus the only positive-degree operations are
$$
\boxed{u\longmapsto a\,u\qquad(a\in\ZZ)}
$$
in degree $1$; all operations to $H^n$ for $n>1$ are zero.

For $H^2(-;\ZZ)$, use
$$
K(\ZZ,2)=\CP^\infty,
\qquad
H^*(\CP^\infty;\ZZ)=\ZZ[x],\quad \gradeddegree{x}=2.
$$
Therefore
$$
H^n(K(\ZZ,2);\ZZ)=
\begin{cases}
\ZZ\,x^j,&n=2j,\\
0,&n\text{ odd}.
\end{cases}
$$
Hence every operation is
$$
\boxed{u\longmapsto a\,u^j\quad(n=2j),\qquad a\in\ZZ,}
$$
and there are no nonzero operations in odd target degree.

Finally consider $H^1(-;\ZZ_p)$. The representing space is $K(\ZZ_p,1)$. For $p=2$,
$$
H^*(K(\ZZ_2,1);\ZZ_2)=\ZZ_2[x],
\qquad \gradeddegree{x}=1.
$$
Thus in every degree $n>0$ the group of operations is one-dimensional, generated by
$$
\boxed{u\longmapsto u^n.}
$$

For odd $p$,
$$
H^*(K(\ZZ_p,1);\ZZ_p)
=\Lambda(x)\otimes\ZZ_p[y],
\qquad \gradeddegree{x}=1,\quad \gradeddegree{y}=2,\quad y=\beta x,
$$
where $\beta$ is the mod-$p$ Bockstein. Thus there is again one basis operation in every positive degree:
$$
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
H^1(-;\ZZ_p)&\to H^{2j}(-;\ZZ_p),
&u&\mapsto (\beta u)^j,\\
H^1(-;\ZZ_p)&\to H^{2j+1}(-;\ZZ_p),
&u&\mapsto u(\beta u)^j.
\end{aligned}}
$$
All operations are scalar multiples of these by elements of $\ZZ_p$.
:::
