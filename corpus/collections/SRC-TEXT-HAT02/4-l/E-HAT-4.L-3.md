::: {.problem}
Since there is a fiber bundle $S^2 \to \CP^5 \to \quatprojective^2$ by Exercise 35 in §4.2, one might ask whether there is an analogous bundle $S^4 \to \quatprojective^5 \to \OP^2$.
Use Steenrod powers for the prime 3 to show that such a bundle cannot exist. [The Gysin sequence can be used to determine the map on cohomology induced by the bundle projection $\quatprojective^5 \to \OP^2$.]
:::

::: {.solution}

The argument is the card's own; no retained solution set in `tmp/sources` covers Section 4.L Exercise 3. The statement was checked against Hatcher, *Algebraic Topology* (2002), §4.L Exercise 3, and the tools used are the Gysin sequence (printed p. 438) and the Steenrod power operations at the prime 3 (Theorem 4L.12, printed p. 504).

Throughout cohomology has coefficients $\ZZ_{3}$, and $P^{1}$ is the reduced power operation, which raises degree by $2\cdot1\cdot(3-1)=4$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #cohomology}
$H^{*}(\quatprojective^{5})=\ZZ_{3}[x]/(x^{6})$ with $\gradeddegree{x}=4$, and $H^{*}(\OP^{2})=\ZZ_{3}[y]/(y^{3})$ with $\gradeddegree{y}=8$.

::: pf-proof
For a division algebra $K$ of dimension $d$ over $\RR$ one has $H^{*}(KP^{n};\ZZ)=\ZZ[z]/(z^{n+1})$ with $\gradeddegree{z}=d$, and the universal coefficient theorem gives the same over $\ZZ_{3}$ since the integral cohomology is free. Here the quaternions have dimension $4$ and the octonions dimension $8$, with $n=5$ and $n=2$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #gysin}
$p^{*}\colon H^{8}(\OP^{2})\to H^{8}(\quatprojective^{5})$ is an isomorphism, so after rescaling $y$ we may take $p^{*}y=x^{2}$.

::: pf-proof
The Gysin sequence of the bundle $S^{4}\to\quatprojective^{5}\xrightarrow{p}\OP^{2}$ reads
$$
\cdots\to H^{3}(\OP^{2})\to H^{8}(\OP^{2})\xrightarrow{p^{*}}H^{8}(\quatprojective^{5})\to H^{4}(\OP^{2})\to\cdots,
$$
the fibre having dimension $4$ so that the degree shift is $4$. By step [](#cohomology){.pf-ref} the groups $H^{3}(\OP^{2})$ and $H^{4}(\OP^{2})$ vanish, so $p^{*}$ is injective and surjective in degree $8$. Both groups are $\ZZ_{3}$, generated by $y$ and $x^{2}$ respectively, so $p^{*}y=\lambda x^{2}$ with $\lambda\in\ZZ_{3}^{\times}$; replacing $y$ by $\lambda^{-1}y$ gives $p^{*}y=x^{2}$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #p1y}
$P^{1}y=0$.

::: pf-proof
$P^{1}y$ lies in $H^{8+4}(\OP^{2})=H^{12}(\OP^{2})$, which vanishes because $H^{*}(\OP^{2})=\ZZ_{3}[y]/(y^{3})$ with $\gradeddegree{y}=8$ has nothing in degree $12$ (the nonzero degrees are $0,8,16$).
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #p1x}
$P^{1}x=x^{2}$.

::: pf-proof
Write $x_{\infty}\in H^{4}(\quatprojective^{\infty})$ and $u\in H^{2}(\CP^{\infty})$ for the generators. The inclusion $\CP^{\infty}\to\quatprojective^{\infty}$ pulls $x_{\infty}$ back to $-u^{2}$, and $P^{1}$ on a degree-$2$ class is $P^{1}(u)=u^{3}$, since the operation raises degree by $4$ and by the standard computation on $H^{*}(\CP^{\infty};\ZZ_{3})$ the answer is $u^{3}$. By the Cartan formula $P^{1}(u^{2})=P^{1}(u)\,u+u\,P^{1}(u)=2u^{4}=-u^{4}$, so $P^{1}(-u^{2})=u^{4}=(-u^{2})^{2}$ over $\ZZ_{3}$. Hence $P^{1}$ and squaring agree on the generator of $H^{4}(\CP^{\infty})$, and by naturality of $P^{1}$ under the inclusion $\CP^{\infty}\subset\quatprojective^{\infty}$ and the collapse $\quatprojective^{5}\subset\quatprojective^{\infty}$ (which is an isomorphism on $H^{4}$ and $H^{8}$ by step [](#cohomology){.pf-ref}) they agree on $x$ as well.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #p1x2}
$P^{1}(x^{2})=2x^{3}\neq0$.

::: pf-proof
By the Cartan formula $P^{1}(x^{2})=P^{1}(x)\,x+x\,P^{1}(x)=x^{2}x+xx^{2}=2x^{3}$ using step [](#p1x){.pf-ref}. In $H^{*}(\quatprojective^{5})=\ZZ_{3}[x]/(x^{6})$ the class $x^{3}$ is nonzero, and $2\neq0$ in $\ZZ_{3}$, so $2x^{3}\neq0$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #contradiction}
No fiber bundle $S^{4}\to\quatprojective^{5}\to\OP^{2}$ exists.

::: pf-proof
Suppose such a bundle exists. Naturality of $P^{1}$ under $p$ gives $p^{*}(P^{1}y)=P^{1}(p^{*}y)=P^{1}(x^{2})$, the last by step [](#gysin){.pf-ref}. The left side is $p^{*}(0)=0$ by step [](#p1y){.pf-ref}, while the right side is $2x^{3}\neq0$ by step [](#p1x2){.pf-ref}. Contradiction.
:::
