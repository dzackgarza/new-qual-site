::: {.problem}
Consider the equivalence relation $\simeq_w$ generated by weak homotopy equivalence: $X \simeq_w Y$ if there are spaces $X = X_1, X_2, \ldots, X_n = Y$ with weak homotopy equivalences $X_i \to X_{i+1}$ or $X_i \gets X_{i+1}$ for each $i$.
Show that $X \simeq_w Y$ if and only if $X$ and $Y$ have a common CW approximation.
:::

::: {.solution}

Recall that a **CW approximation** of a space $X$ is a CW complex $K$ together with a weak homotopy equivalence $K\to X$, and that every space has one.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

If $X$ and $Y$ have a common CW approximation then $X\simeq_wY$.

::: pf-proof

Let $K$ be a common CW approximation, so there are weak homotopy equivalences $K\xrightarrow{u}X$ and $K\xrightarrow{v}Y$, giving a zigzag
$$
X\xleftarrow{\ u\ }K\xrightarrow{\ v\ }Y
$$
of weak homotopy equivalences, which is one of the zigzags generating $\simeq_w$. Hence $X\simeq_wY$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

Weakly equivalent CW complexes are homotopy equivalent.

::: pf-proof

Let $K$ and $L$ be CW complexes with a zigzag of weak homotopy equivalences $K=K_1\leftrightarrow K_2\leftrightarrow\cdots\leftrightarrow K_n=L$. Passing to CW approximations of everything in sight, or more simply noting that a weak homotopy equivalence between CW complexes is a homotopy equivalence by Whitehead's theorem (Hatcher, Theorem 4.5, whose hypothesis is that $f_*$ is an isomorphism on $\pi_n$ for **all** $n$, which is exactly what weak homotopy equivalence gives for a map between CW complexes), each arrow of the zigzag is a homotopy equivalence, and a zigzag of homotopy equivalences composes to a homotopy equivalence.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

If $X\simeq_wY$ then $X$ and $Y$ have a common CW approximation.

::: pf-proof

Suppose first that $X=W_1,W_2,\dots,W_m=Y$ is a zigzag with a weak homotopy equivalence $W_i\to W_{i+1}$ or $W_i\leftarrow W_{i+1}$ for each $i$. For each $i$ choose a CW approximation $K_i\to W_i$.

*Claim: $K_i$ and $K_{i+1}$ are homotopy equivalent.* If $W_i\to W_{i+1}$ is a weak homotopy equivalence, then $K_i\to W_i\to W_{i+1}$ is a weak homotopy equivalence, so $K_i$ is also a CW approximation of $W_{i+1}$; and any two CW approximations of one space are homotopy equivalent, by step [](#s2){.pf-ref} applied to the zigzag $K_i\to W_{i+1}\leftarrow K_{i+1}$. The case of $W_i\leftarrow W_{i+1}$ is the same with the roles exchanged.

By transitivity of homotopy equivalence, $K_1\simeq K_m$, say through a homotopy equivalence $h:K_1\to K_m$. Now $K_1\to X$ is a weak homotopy equivalence, and $K_1\xrightarrow{h}K_m\to Y$ is a weak homotopy equivalence because $h$ is a homotopy equivalence and $K_m\to Y$ is a weak homotopy equivalence. So $K_1$ is a common CW approximation of $X$ and $Y$.

:::
