Let $X$ be the smash product of $k$ copies of a Moore space $M(\mathbb{Z}_p, n)$ with $p$ prime.
Compute the Bockstein homomorphisms in $H^*(X; \mathbb{Z}_p)$ and use this to describe $H^*(X; \mathbb{Z})$.
