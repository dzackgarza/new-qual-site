::: {.problem}
Show that if $M$ is a manifold of dimension $2n$ for which there exists a fiber bundle $S^1 \to S^{2n+1} \to M$, then $M$ is simply-connected and $H^*(M; \mathbb{Z}) \approx H^*(\mathbb{CP}^n; \mathbb{Z})$ as rings.
Conversely, if $M$ is simply-connected and $H^*(M; \mathbb{Z}) \approx H^*(\mathbb{CP}^n; \mathbb{Z})$ as rings, show there is a bundle $S^1 \to E \to M$ where $E \simeq S^{2n+1}$.
:::

::: {.solution}
**Goal.** Prove the two directions relating a circle bundle $S^1 \to S^{2n+1} \to M$ to the cohomology ring of $\CP^n$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}
($\Rightarrow$) Given $S^1 \to S^{2n+1} \to M$, show $M$ is simply-connected and $H^*(M;\ZZ) \cong H^*(\CP^n;\ZZ)$.

::: pf-proof

::: pf-step
$M$ is simply-connected.

::: pf-proof
the long exact sequence of the fibration gives $\pi_1(S^{2n+1}) = 0 \to \pi_1(M) \to \pi_0(S^1) = 0$, so $\pi_1(M) = 0$ (using that $S^{2n+1}$ is simply-connected and the fiber $S^1$ is connected).
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}
The Gysin sequence of the circle bundle gives $H^*(M;\ZZ) \cong \ZZ[\alpha]/(\alpha^{n+1})$ with $|\alpha| = 2$.

::: pf-proof
the Gysin sequence for the $S^1$-bundle $S^1 \to S^{2n+1} \to M$ (with Euler class $e \in H^2(M)$) gives $H^*(M) \cong \ZZ[e]/(e^{n+1})$, matching $H^*(\CP^n;\ZZ) = \ZZ[\alpha]/(\alpha^{n+1})$.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
Hence $H^*(M;\ZZ) \cong H^*(\CP^n;\ZZ)$ as rings.

::: pf-proof
step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref}.
:::

:::

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}
($\Leftarrow$) Given $M$ simply-connected with $H^*(M;\ZZ) \cong H^*(\CP^n;\ZZ)$, construct $S^1 \to E \to M$ with $E \simeq S^{2n+1}$.

::: pf-proof

::: pf-step
$H^2(M;\ZZ) = \ZZ$, generated by a class $\alpha$.

::: pf-proof
$H^2(\CP^n;\ZZ) = \ZZ$.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
Let $E$ be the total space of the principal $S^1$-bundle with Euler class $\alpha$ (the generator of $H^2(M;\ZZ)$).

::: pf-proof
principal $S^1$-bundles over $M$ are classified by $H^2(M;\ZZ) = \ZZ$, and we take the bundle corresponding to the generator.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
The Gysin sequence shows $H^*(E;\ZZ) \cong H^*(S^{2n+1};\ZZ)$.

::: pf-proof
the Gysin sequence for this bundle, with Euler class $\alpha$ (a generator), gives $H^*(E) \cong H^*(S^{2n+1})$ (the cohomology of a sphere).
:::

:::

::: pf-step
Since $E$ is simply-connected (by the LES, $\pi_1(E) = 0$), and $H^*(E) \cong H^*(S^{2n+1})$, Whitehead's theorem gives $E \simeq S^{2n+1}$.

::: pf-proof
a simply-connected space with the cohomology of $S^{2n+1}$ is homotopy equivalent to $S^{2n+1}$.
:::

:::

:::

:::

::: pf-qed
Steps [](#s1){.pf-ref} and [](#s2){.pf-ref} give both directions.
:::
