For the flag space $F_n(\mathbb{C}^n)$ show that $H^*(F_n(\mathbb{C}^n); \mathbb{Z}) \approx \mathbb{Z}[x_1, \ldots, x_n]/(\sigma_1, \ldots, \sigma_n)$ where $\sigma_i$ is the $i$th elementary symmetric polynomial.
