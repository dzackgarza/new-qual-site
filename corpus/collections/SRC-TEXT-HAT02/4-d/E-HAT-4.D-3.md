Use the Leray–Hirsch theorem as in Corollary 4D.3 to compute $H^*(V_n(\mathbb{C}^k); \mathbb{Z}) \approx \Lambda_\mathbb{Z}[x_{2k-2n+1}, x_{2k-2n+3}, \ldots, x_{2k-1}]$ and similarly in the quaternionic case.

::: {.solution}
<1>1. $V_n(\mathbb{C}^k)$ is the Stiefel manifold of $n$-frames in $\mathbb{C}^k$, and it fibers over $V_{n-1}(\mathbb{C}^k)$ with fiber $S^{2k-2n+1}$.
::: {.proof}
the standard fibration $S^{2k-2n+1} \to V_n(\mathbb{C}^k) \to V_{n-1}(\mathbb{C}^k)$.
:::

<1>2. The Leray–Hirsch theorem applies: the cohomology of the fiber $S^{2k-2n+1}$ is generated by a class $x_{2k-2n+1}$ that extends to a class on the total space (the Euler class of the associated bundle).
::: {.proof}
the fibration satisfies the Leray–Hirsch condition (the fiber cohomology is generated by classes that are restrictions of global classes).
:::

<1>3. Hence $H^*(V_n(\mathbb{C}^k)) \cong H^*(V_{n-1}(\mathbb{C}^k)) \otimes H^*(S^{2k-2n+1})$.
::: {.proof}
Leray–Hirsch theorem.
:::

<1>4. Iterating (since $V_1(\mathbb{C}^k) = S^{2k-1}$), we get
$$H^*(V_n(\mathbb{C}^k)) \cong H^*(S^{2k-1}) \otimes H^*(S^{2k-3}) \otimes \cdots \otimes H^*(S^{2k-2n+1}).$$
::: {.proof}
<1>3, applied inductively.
:::

<1>5. Each $H^*(S^{2m-1}) = \Lambda_\mathbb{Z}[x_{2m-1}]$ (exterior algebra on one generator of odd degree $2m-1$).
::: {.proof}
cohomology of an odd sphere.
:::

<1>6. Hence $H^*(V_n(\mathbb{C}^k)) \cong \Lambda_\mathbb{Z}[x_{2k-2n+1}, x_{2k-2n+3}, \ldots, x_{2k-1}]$.
::: {.proof}
<1>4 and <1>5.
:::

<1>7. In the quaternionic case, the fiber is $S^{4k-4n+3}$ (a quaternionic sphere), so
$$H^*(V_n(\mathbb{H}^k)) \cong \Lambda_\mathbb{Z}[x_{4k-4n+3}, x_{4k-4n+7}, \ldots, x_{4k-1}].$$
::: {.proof}
the same argument with the quaternionic Stiefel manifold, whose fibers are spheres of dimension $4k - 4n + 3, 4k - 4n + 7, \ldots, 4k - 1$.
:::

<1>8. Q.E.D.
::: {.proof}
<1>6 and <1>7.
:::
:::
