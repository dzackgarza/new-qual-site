Use the Gysin sequence to show that for a fiber bundle $S^k \to S^m \xrightarrow{p} S^n$ we must have $k = n-1$ and $m = 2n-1$.
Then use the Thom isomorphism to show that the Hopf invariant of $p$ must be $\pm 1$.
