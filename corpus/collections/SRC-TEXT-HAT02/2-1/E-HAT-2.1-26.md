Show that $H_1(X, A)$ is not isomorphic to $\tilde{H}_1(X/A)$ if $X = [0, 1]$ and $A$ is the sequence $1, {^1_2}, {^1_3}, \cdots$ together with its limit 0. [See Example 1.25.]
