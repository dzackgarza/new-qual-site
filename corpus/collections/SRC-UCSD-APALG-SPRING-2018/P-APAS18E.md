::: {.problem}
Let $A_4$ be the index two subgroup of $S_4$ consisting of even permutations.
Find the character table of $A_4$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $\omega\coloneqq e^{2\pi i/3}$ and $V_4\coloneqq\{e,(12)(34),(13)(24),(14)(23)\}$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #classes}

$A_4$ has four conjugacy classes: $\{e\}$, the three double transpositions, $C_3\coloneqq\{(123),(134),(142),(243)\}$ and $C_4\coloneqq C_3^{-1}$.

::: pf-proof

The double transpositions form one class already in $S_4$, and that class lies in $A_4$; it stays one class in $A_4$ because conjugation by $(123)$ permutes it cyclically. The centralizer of $(123)$ in $A_4$ is $\langle(123)\rangle$, of order $3$, so its class has $12/3=4$ elements; conjugating $(123)$ by the elements of $V_4$ gives the four listed. The eight $3$-cycles therefore form two classes of size $4$, and $(132)\notin C_3$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #linear}

$A_4$ has three linear characters $\chi_1,\chi_2,\chi_3$, equal to $1$ on $V_4$, with values $1,\omega,\omega^2$ on $C_3$ and $1,\omega^2,\omega$ on $C_4$.

::: pf-proof

$V_4$ is normal with $A_4/V_4\cong\ZZ/3\ZZ$, generated by the image of $(123)$; inflating the three characters of $\ZZ/3\ZZ$ gives these characters. The commutator subgroup of $A_4$ is $V_4$, so there are no other linear characters.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #degree-three}

The fourth irreducible character is $\chi_4=(3,-1,0,0)$.

::: pf-proof

By step [](#classes){.pf-ref} there are four irreducible characters, and $12=1+1+1+d^2$ gives $d=3$. The permutation character on four letters is $(4,0,1,1)$; subtracting $\chi_1$ gives $\psi=(3,-1,0,0)$, and $\langle\psi,\psi\rangle=\frac1{12}(9+3\cdot1)=1$, so $\psi$ is irreducible.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

The character table of $A_4$ is
$$
\begin{array}{c|cccc}
 & e&(12)(34)&(123)&(132)\\
\text{class size}&1&3&4&4\\ \hline
\chi_1&1&1&1&1\\
\chi_2&1&1&\omega&\omega^2\\
\chi_3&1&1&\omega^2&\omega\\
\chi_4&3&-1&0&0
\end{array}
$$

::: pf-proof

Steps [](#linear){.pf-ref} and [](#degree-three){.pf-ref} give the four rows, on the classes of step [](#classes){.pf-ref}.

:::
