Problem 12. Let $A$ be an $m\times n$ real matrix and let $b\in\mathbb R^m$. Suppose $Ax$ and $Ay$ both have minimal distance to $b$ among all vectors in $\operatorname{image}(A)$. Prove that
\[
x-y\in\ker A.
\]
