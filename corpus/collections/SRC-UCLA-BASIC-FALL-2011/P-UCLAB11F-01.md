::: {.problem}
Problem 1. Let $(X,d)$ be a compact metric space and let $f:X\to X$ satisfy
\[
d(f(x),f(y))<d(x,y)\qquad\text{for all }x\ne y.
\]
Prove that there is a unique point $x\in X$ such that $f(x)=x$.
:::
