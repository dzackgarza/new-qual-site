::: problem
Suppose $A$ is a unital commutative ring and $\operatorname{Spec}(A)$ is the set of all prime ideals of $A$.
For an $A$-module $M$ and $\mathfrak{p} \in \operatorname{Spec}(A)$, let $M_\mathfrak{p}$ be the localization of $M$ at $\mathfrak{p}$.
Let
$$
\operatorname{supp} M := \{\mathfrak{p} \in \operatorname{Spec}(A) \mid M_\mathfrak{p} \neq 0\}.
$$

(a) Prove that for a finitely generated $A$-module $M$,
$$
\operatorname{supp} M = \{\mathfrak{p} \in \operatorname{Spec}(A) \mid \operatorname{ann}(M) \subseteq \mathfrak{p}\},
$$
where $\operatorname{ann}(M)$ is the annihilator of $M$.

(b) For a positive integer $d$, let $U_d(M) := \{\mathfrak{p} \in \operatorname{Spec}(A) \mid M_\mathfrak{p} \text{ can be generated by } d \text{ elements}\}$.
Prove that if $M$ is finitely generated and $\mathfrak{p}_0 \in U_d(M)$, then there exists $a_0 \in A \setminus \mathfrak{p}_0$ such that $\{\mathfrak{q} \in \operatorname{Spec}(A) \mid a_0 \notin \mathfrak{q}\} \subseteq U_d(M)$.

Hint: Suppose $N_{\mathfrak{p}_0} = M_{\mathfrak{p}_0}$ where $N = \langle x_1, \ldots, x_d \rangle \subseteq M$.
Consider $\operatorname{supp}(M/N)$.
:::
