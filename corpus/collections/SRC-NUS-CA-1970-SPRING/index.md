::: {.remark}
The document this collection needs is a 1970 examination paper in complex analysis, sat at the University of Singapore, the institution that became NUS in 1980. It was searched for and not found, and the list above is deliberately empty.

The list previously named two files under `dzackgarza/make-me-a-qual`: `Combined_Questions.yaml`, an importer wrapper, and `Questions/Complex_Analysis/NUS/NUS_Complex_Analysis.yaml`, the dataset record for these questions. Neither is a document — the YAML holds transcribed question text with the label `year: 1970, season: Spring, exam: Complex_Analysis, university: NUS` and nothing that identifies or reproduces a paper — so both were removed rather than repointed. The label remains unverified: the collection title records that it is imported and unchecked.

What the search covered. NUS examination papers are held in the NUS Libraries Examination Papers database, and the library's own answer to where to find them (Library FAQ 71278, `https://libfaq.nus.edu.sg/faq/71278`, last updated 22 September 2026) states that access is restricted to NUS staff and students, and that from academic year 2012/13 only the five most recent years are made accessible, on the ground that older papers are obsolete — so a 1970 paper is outside what that database offers, and it is not reachable from outside NUS in any case. The URL that FAQ gives for the database, `https://digitalgems.nus.edu.sg/browse/collection/31`, now answers 404. Searches for the paper by institution, year, season and subject found no copy: no scan, transcript, or departmental archive of 1970 papers is published, and NUS ScholarBank holds theses, not examination papers.

The list stays empty until the paper itself is produced.
:::
