::: {.problem}
In $\SL(2, \ZZ)$, the multiplicative group of all $2$ by $2$ matrices with integer entries whose determinant is $1$, what is the order of the cyclic subgroup generated by the following element?

$$A = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ -1 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$$

(A) $2$
(B) $3$
(C) $4$
(D) $6$
(E) $\infty$
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

The square of $A$ is
$$
A^2=
\begin{pmatrix}
-1&-1\\
1&0
\end{pmatrix}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Direct multiplication gives
$$
\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&-1\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&-1\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}-1&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

One has
$$
A^3=I.
$$


::: pf-proof

Multiplying the matrix in step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} by $A$ gives
$$
A^3=
\begin{pmatrix}-1&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&-1\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

Neither $A$ nor $A^2$ is the identity matrix.


::: pf-proof

The displayed matrix $A$ has upper-left entry $0$, while $I$ has upper-left
entry $1$. The matrix $A^2$ from step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} has upper-left entry $-1$.
Therefore neither power is $I$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

Hence the cyclic subgroup generated by $A$ has order
$$
\boxed{3},
$$
so the answer is (B).


::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref}, the order of $A$ divides $3$. Step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} excludes orders $1$
and $2$, so the least positive exponent with $A^n=I$ is $3$.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} gives the requested order.

:::
