::: {.problem}
(a) Let $q\ne0$ be rational and $x$ irrational. Prove that $q+x$ and $qx$ are irrational.

(b) Use the Archimedean property of $\mathbb R$ to prove that between any two distinct real numbers there is both a rational and an irrational number.
:::
