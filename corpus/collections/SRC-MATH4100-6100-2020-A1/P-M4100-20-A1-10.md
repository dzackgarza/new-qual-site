::: {.problem}
(a) Determine convergence or divergence of
\[
\sum_{n=1}^\infty(\sqrt{n+1}-\sqrt n),
\qquad
\sum_{n=1}^\infty\frac{\sqrt{n+1}-\sqrt n}{n},
\]
and
\[
\sum_{n=1}^\infty\left(n^{1/n}-1\right)^n.
\]

(b) Let $a_n>0$. Prove that if $\lim na_n$ exists and is nonzero, then $\sum a_n$ diverges, while if $\lim n^2a_n$ exists, then $\sum a_n$ converges.

(c) If $a_n>0$ and $\sum a_n$ converges, prove that both
\[
\sum a_n^2
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\sum\frac{\sqrt{a_n}}{n}
\]
converge.
:::
