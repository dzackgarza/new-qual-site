::: {.problem}
(a) Let $A\subseteq\mathbb R$ be nonempty and bounded below. Prove
\[
\inf A=-\sup(-A)
\]
and
\[
\inf A=\sup\{b:b\text{ is a lower bound for }A\}.
\]

(b) Let $A,B\subseteq\mathbb R$ be nonempty and bounded above. Prove that $A\subseteq B$ implies $\sup A\le\sup B$, and that if $\sup A<\sup B$, then some $b\in B$ is an upper bound for $A$.
:::
