::: {.problem}
Suppose one reverses the usual order of quantifiers and says that a sequence $a_n$ “verconges” to $a$ when there exists $\varepsilon>0$ such that for every $N\in\mathbb N$, $n\ge N$ implies
\[
|a_n-a|<\varepsilon.
\]
Give an example of a vercongent sequence, give a vercongent sequence that diverges in the usual sense, and characterize exactly what this altered definition describes.
:::
