::: {.problem}
Prove directly that the open unit disk
\[
\{(x,y)\in\mathbb R^2:x^2+y^2<1\}
\]
is connected.

You may assume that intervals in $\mathbb R$ are connected, but should not simply quote more general connectedness results.
:::
