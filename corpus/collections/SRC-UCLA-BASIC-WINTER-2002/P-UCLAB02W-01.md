::: {.problem}
(a) State reasonably general conditions under which
\[
\frac{d}{dx}\int_a^b f(x,y)\,dy
=
\int_a^b \frac{\partial f}{\partial x}(x,y)\,dy
\]
is valid.

(b) Prove the formula under the conditions you stated in part (a).
:::
