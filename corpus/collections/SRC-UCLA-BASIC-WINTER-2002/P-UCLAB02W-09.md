::: {.problem}
Let $V$ be a real vector space and $T:V\to V$ linear.
Let $\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_m$ be distinct eigenvalues of $T$, and for each $i$ let $0\ne v_i$ be an eigenvector with eigenvalue $\lambda_i$.
Show that
\[
\{v_1,\ldots,v_m\}
\]
is linearly independent.
:::
