::: {.problem}
Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional complex inner-product space and let $f:V\to\mathbb C$ be linear.
Show that there exists $w\in V$ such that
\[
f(v)=\langle v,w\rangle
\]
for every $v\in V$.
:::
