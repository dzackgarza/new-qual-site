::: {.problem}
Suppose $f:\DD\to\DD$ is holomorphic and $f(0)=0$.
Prove that either $f$ is a rotation of the unit disc, or the sequence of iterates $\{f^n\}$ converges to zero uniformly on compact subsets of $\DD$.
:::
