:::{.problem}
Using properties of the Riemann integral, prove that if $f:[0,1]\to\mathbb R$ is continuous and nonnegative and
\[
\int_0^1f(x)\,dx=0,
\]
then $f(x)=0$ for every $x\in[0,1]$.
:::
