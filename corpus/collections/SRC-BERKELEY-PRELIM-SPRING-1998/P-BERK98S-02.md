:::{.problem}
Let $f$ be analytic on an open set containing the closed unit disk. Suppose
\[
|f(z)|>m\quad\text{for }|z|=1,
\qquad
|f(0)|<m.
\]
Prove that $f$ has at least one zero in the open unit disk.
:::
