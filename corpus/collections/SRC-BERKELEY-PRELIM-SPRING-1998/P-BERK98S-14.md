:::{.problem}
Let $K\in\mathbb R$ and let $y(t)>0$ be differentiable for $t\ge0$. Suppose
\[
y'(t)\le Ky(t)
\]
for $t\ge0$. Prove that
\[
y(t)\le e^{Kt}y(0)
\]
for every $t\ge0$.
:::
