:::{.problem}
Let $a>0$. Show that
\[
f(z)=\frac{1+z+az^2}{1-z+az^2}
\]
satisfies
\[
|f(z)|<1
\]
for every $z$ in the open left half-plane $\operatorname{Re}z<0$.
:::
