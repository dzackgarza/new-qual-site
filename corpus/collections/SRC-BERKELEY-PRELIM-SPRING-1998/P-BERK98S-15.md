:::{.problem}
For continuous real-valued functions on $[-1,1]$, define
\[
\langle f,g\rangle=\int_{-1}^1f(x)g(x)\,dx.
\]
Find the polynomial
\[
p(x)=a+bx^2-x^4
\]
that is orthogonal to every polynomial of lower degree.
:::
