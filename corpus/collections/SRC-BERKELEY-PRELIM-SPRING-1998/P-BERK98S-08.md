:::{.problem}
Let $m\ge0$ be an integer, let $a_1,\dots,a_m\in\mathbb Z$, and set
\[
f(x)=\sum_{i=1}^m\frac{a_ix^i}{i!}.
\]
Show that for every integer $d\ge0$ there are integers $b_0,\dots,b_{md}$ such that
\[
\frac{f(x)^d}{d!}
=\sum_{i=0}^{md}\frac{b_ix^i}{i!}.
\]
:::
