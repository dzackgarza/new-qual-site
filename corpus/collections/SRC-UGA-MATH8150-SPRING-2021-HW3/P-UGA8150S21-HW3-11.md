::: {.problem}
Let f be a holomorphic function on the strip $- 1 < y < 1 , x \in \mathbb { R }$ such that

$$
| f ( z ) | \leq A ( 1 + | z | ) ^ { \eta } , \quad \eta \mathrm { ~ i s ~ a ~ f x e d ~ r e a l ~ n u m b e r }
$$

for all z in the strip.
Show that for each integer $n \geq 0$ , there exists $A _ { n }$ such that

$$
| f ^ { ( n ) } ( x ) | \leq A _ { n } ( 1 + | x | ) ^ { \eta } , \quad { \mathrm { ~ f o r ~ a l l ~ } } x \in \mathbb { R } .
$$

[Hint: Use Cauchy inequalities]
:::

::: {.solution}
Fix the radius
$$
\rho=\frac12.
$$
For every real $x$, the closed disk $\overline{B(x,\rho)}$ lies inside the strip $-1<\operatorname{Im}z<1$.

<1>1. If $\abs{z-x}=1/2$ with $x\in\mathbb R$, then
$$
\frac23(1+\abs x)
\leq
1+\abs z
\leq
\frac32(1+\abs x).
$$

::: {.proof}
The triangle inequality gives
$$
\abs z\leq\abs x+\frac12,
$$
so
$$
1+\abs z
\leq1+\abs x+\frac12
\leq\frac32(1+\abs x).
$$
Interchanging $x$ and $z$ gives
$$
1+\abs x
\leq1+\abs z+\frac12
\leq\frac32(1+\abs z),
$$
which is equivalent to the lower bound.
:::

<1>2. For every real $\eta$ and every $z$ with $\abs{z-x}=1/2$,
$$
(1+\abs z)^\eta
\leq
\left(\frac32\right)^{\abs\eta}(1+\abs x)^\eta.
$$

::: {.proof}
If $\eta\geq0$, apply the upper bound from step <1>1 and raise it to the power $\eta$. If $\eta<0$, apply the lower bound from step <1>1; raising a positive inequality to a negative power reverses it and gives
$$
(1+\abs z)^\eta
\leq
\left(\frac23(1+\abs x)\right)^\eta
=\left(\frac32\right)^{-\eta}(1+\abs x)^\eta.
$$
Both cases are the displayed estimate.
:::

<1>3. For every integer $n\geq1$ and every real $x$,
$$
\abs{f^{(n)}(x)}
\leq
A\,n!\,2^n
\left(\frac32\right)^{\abs\eta}
(1+\abs x)^\eta.
$$

::: {.proof}
Cauchy's inequality on the circle $\abs{z-x}=1/2$ gives
$$
\abs{f^{(n)}(x)}
\leq
\frac{n!}{(1/2)^n}
\max_{\abs{z-x}=1/2}\abs{f(z)}.
$$
The assumed growth bound and step <1>2 imply
$$
\max_{\abs{z-x}=1/2}\abs{f(z)}
\leq
A\left(\frac32\right)^{\abs\eta}(1+\abs x)^\eta.
$$
Combining the two inequalities yields the claim.
:::

<1>4. For each integer $n\geq0$, there is a constant $A_n$ such that
$$
\boxed{
\abs{f^{(n)}(x)}
\leq A_n(1+\abs x)^\eta
\qquad\text{for every }x\in\mathbb R.}
$$

::: {.proof}
For $n=0$, the original hypothesis gives the inequality with $A_0=A$. For $n\geq1$, take
$$
A_n
=A\,n!\,2^n\left(\frac32\right)^{\abs\eta}.
$$
Then step <1>3 gives the required bound uniformly for all real $x$.
:::

<1>5. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>4 is the required conclusion for every derivative order.
:::
:::
