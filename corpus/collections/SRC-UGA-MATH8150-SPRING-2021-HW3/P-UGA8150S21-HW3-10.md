Suppose $f ^ { \prime } ( z )$ exists in a region Ω except possibly at point w in Ω. Let $T \subset \Omega$ b e a triangle whose interior is also contained in Ω and contains w. Show that

$$
\int _ { T } f ( z ) d z = 0 .
$$
