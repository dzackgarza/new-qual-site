::: {.problem}
Let $V$ be an $n$-dimensional real vector space and let $T\in\operatorname{End}(V)$.
Let $W\subseteq\operatorname{End}(V)$ be the vector subspace spanned by the nonnegative powers $T^k$, $k\ge0$.
Prove that
\[
\dim W\le \dim V=n.
\]
:::
