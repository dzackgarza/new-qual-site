::: {.problem}
Let $S$ be the Sorgenfrey line, the real numbers with basis
\[
\{[a,b):a<b\}.
\]

1. Explain why $S$ is completely regular, separable, Lindelöf, and not metrizable.
2. Explain why $S\times S$ is separable, not normal, and not Lindelöf.
:::
