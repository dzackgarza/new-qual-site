::: {.problem}
The fundamental group $\pi_1(X,x)$ can be described as pointed homotopy classes of pointed maps
\[
(S^1,p)\to(X,x).
\]
Describe the set of pointed homotopy classes of pointed maps
\[
(S^0,p)\to(X,x),
\]
where
\[
S^0=\{-1,1\},
\qquad p=1.
\]
:::
