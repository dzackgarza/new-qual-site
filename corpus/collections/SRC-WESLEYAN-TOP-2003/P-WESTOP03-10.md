::: {.problem}
Let
\[
f:X\to Y
\]
be continuous and onto, where $X$ is compact Hausdorff and $Y$ is Hausdorff. Prove that there is a subspace $F\subseteq X$ minimal, under inclusion, among compact subspaces $Z\subseteq X$ satisfying
\[
f(Z)=Y.
\]
:::
