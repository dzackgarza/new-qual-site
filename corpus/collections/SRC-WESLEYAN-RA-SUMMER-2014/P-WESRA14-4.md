::: {.problem}
Let $X=Y=\RR$ with their Borel Lebesgue measures $m_X,m_Y$.
Prove that every open subset of
$$
X\times Y=\RR^2
$$
is measurable for the product measure $m_X\times m_Y$; equivalently, the product measure is a Borel measure.
:::

::: {.solution}

Let
$$
\mathcal B(\RR)
$$
denote the Borel $\sigma$-algebra on $\RR$.
The product measure
$$
m_X\times m_Y
$$
is defined on the product $\sigma$-algebra
$$
\mathcal B(\RR)
\otimes
\mathcal B(\RR).
$$

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

Every open rectangle
$$
U\times V
\subseteq
\RR^2
$$
with $U,V$ open in $\RR$ belongs to
$$
\mathcal B(\RR)
\otimes
\mathcal B(\RR).
$$


::: pf-proof

Open subsets of $\RR$ are Borel.
Hence
$$
U,V
\in
\mathcal B(\RR).
$$
By definition, the product $\sigma$-algebra is generated by measurable
rectangles
$$
A\times B
$$
with
$$
A,B\in\mathcal B(\RR).
$$
Thus every open rectangle belongs to it.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

Let
$$
\mathcal Q
\coloneqq
\{
(a,b)\times(c,d):
a,b,c,d\in\QQ,
\ a<b,
\ c<d
\}.
$$
Then
$$
\mathcal Q
$$
is a countable basis for the Euclidean topology on
$$
\RR^2.
$$


::: pf-proof

The rational numbers are countable, so
$$
\QQ^4
$$
is countable.
Hence $\mathcal Q$ is countable.

Let
$$
O\subseteq\RR^2
$$
be open and let
$$
(x,y)\in O.
$$
There exists
$$
\varepsilon>0
$$
such that
$$
(x-\varepsilon,x+\varepsilon)
\times
(y-\varepsilon,y+\varepsilon)
\subseteq
O.
$$
Using density of $\QQ$, choose rational numbers
$$
a<x<b,
\qquad
c<y<d
$$
with
$$
(a,b)
\subseteq
(x-\varepsilon,x+\varepsilon)
$$
and
$$
(c,d)
\subseteq
(y-\varepsilon,y+\varepsilon).
$$
Then
$$
(x,y)
\in
(a,b)\times(c,d)
\subseteq
O.
$$
Thus $\mathcal Q$ is a basis.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

Every open set
$$
O\subseteq\RR^2
$$
is a countable union of members of
$$
\mathcal Q.
$$


::: pf-proof

Define
$$
\mathcal Q_O
\coloneqq
\{
Q\in\mathcal Q:
Q\subseteq O
\}.
$$
Because
$$
\mathcal Q
$$
is countable, so is
$$
\mathcal Q_O.
$$

By the basis property from step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref}, every point of $O$ lies in some
$$
Q\in\mathcal Q_O.
$$
Therefore
$$
O
=
\bigcup_{Q\in\mathcal Q_O}Q,
$$
a countable union.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

Every open subset of
$$
\RR^2
$$
belongs to
$$
\mathcal B(\RR)
\otimes
\mathcal B(\RR).
$$


::: pf-proof

Each
$$
Q\in\mathcal Q
$$
is an open rectangle, hence belongs to the product $\sigma$-algebra by
step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref}.

By step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref}, any open set is a countable union of such rectangles.
A $\sigma$-algebra is closed under countable unions.
Hence every open set belongs to
$$
\mathcal B(\RR)
\otimes
\mathcal B(\RR).
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-5}

Consequently
$$
\mathcal B(\RR^2)
\subseteq
\mathcal B(\RR)
\otimes
\mathcal B(\RR).
$$


::: pf-proof

The Borel $\sigma$-algebra on
$$
\RR^2
$$
is generated by the open subsets.
Step [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} shows all open subsets already lie in the product
$\sigma$-algebra.
Therefore the $\sigma$-algebra they generate is contained in it.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-6}

Conversely,
$$
\mathcal B(\RR)
\otimes
\mathcal B(\RR)
\subseteq
\mathcal B(\RR^2).
$$


::: pf-proof

Let
$$
\pi_1,\pi_2:
\RR^2\to\RR
$$
be the coordinate projections.
They are continuous.
Hence for every Borel set
$$
A\subseteq\RR,
$$
$$
\pi_1^{-1}(A)
=
A\times\RR
$$
is Borel in
$$
\RR^2,
$$
and similarly
$$
\pi_2^{-1}(B)
=
\RR\times B
$$
is Borel.

Therefore
$$
A\times B
=
\pi_1^{-1}(A)
\cap
\pi_2^{-1}(B)
$$
is Borel in $\RR^2$.
Since such rectangles generate the product $\sigma$-algebra, the stated
containment follows.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-7}

Thus
$$
\boxed{
\mathcal B(\RR^2)
=
\mathcal B(\RR)
\otimes
\mathcal B(\RR).
}
$$


::: pf-proof

Combine steps [](#s1-5){.pf-ref} through [](#s1-6){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-8}

Hence every open subset of
$$
\RR^2
$$
is measurable for the product measure
$$
m_X\times m_Y.
$$
Equivalently,
$$
\boxed{
m_X\times m_Y
\text{ is a Borel measure on }\RR^2.
}
$$


::: pf-proof

The product measure is defined on
$$
\mathcal B(\RR)
\otimes
\mathcal B(\RR),
$$
which equals
$$
\mathcal B(\RR^2)
$$
by step [](#s1-7){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-8){.pf-ref} is the desired conclusion.

:::
