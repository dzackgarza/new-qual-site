::: {.problem}
Suppose $\mu(\Omega)=1$ and $f,g$ are positive functions on $\Omega$ satisfying $fg\ge1$.
Prove
\[
\left(\int_\Omega f\,d\mu\right)
\left(\int_\Omega g\,d\mu\right)\ge1.
\]
:::
