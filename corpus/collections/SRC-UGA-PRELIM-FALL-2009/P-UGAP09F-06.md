::: {.problem}
Let $V$ be the real vector space spanned by
\[
1,\qquad \sin^2x,\qquad \cos^2x,\qquad \sin(2x),\qquad \cos(2x).
\]
Find $\dim_{\mathbb R}V$ and exhibit a basis for $V$ over $\mathbb R$.
:::
