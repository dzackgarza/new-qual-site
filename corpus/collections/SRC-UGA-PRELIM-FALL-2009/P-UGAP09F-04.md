::: {.problem}
Give the $\varepsilon$-$\delta$ definition for the existence of
\[
\lim_{x\to x_0}f(x).
\]
Then use the definition to show that
\[
\lim_{x\to1/4}\sqrt{x}=\frac12.
\]
:::
