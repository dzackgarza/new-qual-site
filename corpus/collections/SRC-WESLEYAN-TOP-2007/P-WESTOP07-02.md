::: {.problem}
In this problem a *triangle* consists only of its three edges and three vertices. Let $T_1,T_2,T_3\subset\mathbb R^2$ be the triangles with vertices
\[
(5,5),(7,5),(6,6),
\]
\[
(-5,-5),(-6,-6),(-4,-6),
\]
and
\[
(17,-5),(18,-6),(16,-6),
\]
respectively. Let $P$ be the closed segment from $(-5,-5)$ to $(5,5)$ and let $Q$ be the closed segment from $(7,5)$ to $(17,-5)$. Put
\[
X=T_1\cup T_2\cup T_3\cup P\cup Q.
\]
Compute, with proof,
\[
\pi_1(X,(0,0)).
\]
:::
