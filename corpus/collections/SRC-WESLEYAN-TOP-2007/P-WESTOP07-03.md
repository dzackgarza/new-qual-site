::: {.problem}
Let $(X_i)_{i\in I}$ be a family of topological spaces.

1. Define the product and box topologies on $\prod_i X_i$. If the $X_i$ are metric spaces, define the uniform topology on the product.
2. State the characteristic property that makes the product topology the distinguished topology on $\prod_i X_i$.
3. Give, with justification, a sequence that converges in the product topology but not in the uniform or box topologies.
:::
