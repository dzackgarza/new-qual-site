::: {.problem}
Let $R = \ZZ_{30}$ and let $A \in M_{2,3}(R)$ be the matrix

$$
A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 & -1 \\ 0 & 2 & 3 \end{bmatrix}.
$$

Show that the two rows of $A$ are linearly independent over $R$, but that any two of the three columns are linearly dependent over $R$.
:::

::: {.solution}
The collection's provenance is the exercise sheet, so no retained solution set covers the problem and the computation is the card's own. Write $R=\ZZ_{30}$, and let $r_1=(1,1,-1)$, $r_2=(0,2,3)$ be the rows and $c_1=(1,0)$, $c_2=(1,2)$, $c_3=(-1,3)$ be the columns of $A$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #rows}
The rows of $A$ are linearly independent over $R$.

::: pf-proof
Suppose $r\,r_1+s\,r_2=(0,0,0)$ with $r,s\in R$. The first coordinate gives $r\cdot1+s\cdot0=r=0$; the remaining coordinates then read $2s\equiv0$ and $3s\equiv0$ modulo $30$, that is $15\mid s$ and $10\mid s$. Since $\operatorname{lcm}(15,10)=30$, this gives $30\mid s$, that is $s=0$ in $R$. Both coefficients vanish, so the two rows are linearly independent.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #columns}
Any two of the three columns are linearly dependent over $R$.

::: pf-proof
The three identities
$$
15c_1+15c_2=(15+15,\,0+30)=(30,30)=(0,0),
$$
$$
10c_1+10c_3=(10-10,\,0+30)=(0,30)=(0,0),
$$
$$
6c_2+6c_3=(6-6,\,12+18)=(0,30)=(0,0)
$$
exhibit a nontrivial dependence of each pair: the coefficients $15$, $10$ and $6$ are nonzero in $\ZZ_{30}$, since none of them is divisible by $30$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step}
$\boxed{\text{the rows are independent over }\ZZ_{30}\text{ and every pair of columns is dependent}}$.

::: pf-proof
The two clauses are steps [](#rows){.pf-ref} and [](#columns){.pf-ref}. This is the contrast the exercise asks for: the rank of a matrix over a ring with zero divisors need not behave as over a field, the coefficients $15,10,6$ being the zero divisors of $\ZZ_{30}$ that kill the column combinations.
:::
