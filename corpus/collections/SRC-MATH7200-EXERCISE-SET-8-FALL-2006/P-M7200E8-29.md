::: {.problem}
Let $R = \ZZ_{30}$ and let $A \in M_{2,3}(R)$ be the matrix

$$
A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 & -1 \\ 0 & 2 & 3 \end{bmatrix}.
$$

Show that the two rows of $A$ are linearly independent over $R$, but that any two of the three columns are linearly dependent over $R$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Suppose that
$$
r\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 & -1 \end{bmatrix} + s\begin{bmatrix} 0 & 2 & 3 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix}
$$
with $r, s \in R$.
The first entry gives $r = 0$, and then the other entries give $2s = 0$ and $3s = 0$, so $s = 3s - 2s = 0$.
Thus the rows are linearly independent over $R$.

For the columns, in $R = \ZZ_{30}$,
$$
15\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix} + 15\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix},
\qquad
10\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix} + 10\begin{bmatrix} -1 \\ 3 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix},
\qquad
6\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix} + 6\begin{bmatrix} -1 \\ 3 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix},
$$
and the coefficients $15$, $10$ and $6$ are nonzero in $\ZZ_{30}$.
Thus any two of the three columns are linearly dependent over $R$.
:::
