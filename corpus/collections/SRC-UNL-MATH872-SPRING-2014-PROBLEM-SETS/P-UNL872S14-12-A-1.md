::: {.problem}
Hatcher p. 132 #11.

Show that if $A$ is a retract of $X$, then the map
$$
i_*:H_n(A)\to H_n(X)
$$
induced by the inclusion
$$
i:A\hookrightarrow X
$$
is injective for every $n$.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: pf-step

Since $A$ is a retract of $X$, there is a continuous map
$$
r:X\to A
$$
such that
$$
\boxed{r\circ i=\operatorname{id}_A}.
$$


::: pf-proof

This is the definition of a retraction.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

Applying homology gives
$$
\boxed{
r_*\circ i_*
=
(\operatorname{id}_A)_*
=
\operatorname{id}_{H_n(A)}}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Singular homology is functorial, so
$$
(r\circ i)_*
=
r_*\circ i_*.
$$
The identity map induces the identity homomorphism on homology.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

Therefore $i_*$ has a left inverse.


::: pf-proof

Step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} says exactly that
$$
r_*i_*=\operatorname{id}.
$$
Thus $r_*$ is a left inverse for $i_*$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

Any homomorphism with a left inverse is injective.


::: pf-proof

Suppose
$$
i_*(\alpha)=i_*(\beta).
$$
Apply $r_*$:
$$
\alpha
=
r_*i_*(\alpha)
=
r_*i_*(\beta)
=
\beta.
$$
Hence $i_*$ is injective.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-5}

Consequently,
$$
\boxed{
i_*:H_n(A)\hookrightarrow H_n(X)}
$$
is injective for every $n$.


::: pf-proof

Combine steps [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} and [](#s1-4){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-5){.pf-ref} is the required conclusion.

:::
