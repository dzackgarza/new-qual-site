::: {.problem}
Consider the map
\[
h:S^1\to S^1,
\qquad
h(z)=\frac1{z^n}=z^{-n},
\]
where \(n\in\mathbb Z\) and points of \(S^1\) are viewed as complex numbers.

Under the standard identification
\[
\pi_1(S^1,(1,0))\cong\mathbb Z,
\]
determine the induced homomorphism
\[
h_*:\mathbb Z\to\mathbb Z.
\]
Prove the answer.
:::

::: {.solution}
For \(m\in\mathbb Z\), let
\[
\omega_m(s)=e^{2\pi ims}.
\]
Under the standard winding-number isomorphism, \([\omega_m]\leftrightarrow m\).

<1>1. For every \(m\in\mathbb Z\),
\[
\boxed{(h\circ\omega_m)(s)=e^{-2\pi inms}=\omega_{-nm}(s)}.
\]

::: {.proof}
By definition, \(\omega_m(s)=e^{2\pi ims}\). Hence
\[
(h\circ\omega_m)(s)
=\left(e^{2\pi ims}\right)^{-n}
=e^{-2\pi inms}
=\omega_{-nm}(s).
\]
:::

<1>2. Therefore
\[
\boxed{h_*([\omega_m])=[\omega_{-nm}]}.
\]

::: {.proof}
The induced map on the fundamental group is defined by composition:
\[
h_*([\gamma])=[h\circ\gamma].
\]
Apply step <1>1 to \(\gamma=\omega_m\).
:::

<1>3. Under \(\pi_1(S^1)\cong\mathbb Z\), the induced homomorphism is
\[
\boxed{h_*:\mathbb Z\to\mathbb Z,\qquad m\mapsto -nm}.
\]

::: {.proof}
The winding-number identification sends \([\omega_m]\mapsto m\) and \([\omega_{-nm}]\mapsto -nm\). Combine this with step <1>2.
:::

<1>4. In particular, the generator \(1\in\mathbb Z\) is sent to \(\boxed{-n}\).

::: {.proof}
Set \(m=1\) in step <1>3.
:::

<1>5. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>3 is the requested induced homomorphism.
:::
:::
