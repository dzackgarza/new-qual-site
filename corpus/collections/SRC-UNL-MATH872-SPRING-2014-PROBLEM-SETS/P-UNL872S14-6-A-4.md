::: {.problem}
Consider the map
$$
h:S^1\to S^1,
\qquad
h(z)=\frac1{z^n}=z^{-n},
$$
where $n\in\ZZ$ and points of $S^1$ are viewed as complex numbers.

Under the standard identification
$$
\pi_1(S^1,(1,0))\cong\ZZ,
$$
determine the induced homomorphism
$$
h_*:\ZZ\to\ZZ.
$$
Prove the answer.
:::

::: {.solution}

For $m\in\ZZ$, let
$$
\omega_m(s)=e^{2\pi ims}.
$$
Under the standard winding-number isomorphism, $[\omega_m]\leftrightarrow m$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

For every $m\in\ZZ$,
$$
\boxed{(h\circ\omega_m)(s)=e^{-2\pi inms}=\omega_{-nm}(s)}.
$$


::: pf-proof

By definition, $\omega_m(s)=e^{2\pi ims}$. Hence
$$
(h\circ\omega_m)(s)
=\left(e^{2\pi ims}\right)^{-n}
=e^{-2\pi inms}
=\omega_{-nm}(s).
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

Therefore
$$
\boxed{h_*([\omega_m])=[\omega_{-nm}]}.
$$


::: pf-proof

The induced map on the fundamental group is defined by composition:
$$
h_*([\gamma])=[h\circ\gamma].
$$
Apply step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} to $\gamma=\omega_m$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

Under $\pi_1(S^1)\cong\ZZ$, the induced homomorphism is
$$
\boxed{h_*:\ZZ\to\ZZ,\qquad m\mapsto -nm}.
$$


::: pf-proof

The winding-number identification sends $[\omega_m]\mapsto m$ and $[\omega_{-nm}]\mapsto -nm$. Combine this with step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

In particular, the generator $1\in\ZZ$ is sent to $\boxed{-n}$.


::: pf-proof

Set $m=1$ in step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} is the requested induced homomorphism.

:::
