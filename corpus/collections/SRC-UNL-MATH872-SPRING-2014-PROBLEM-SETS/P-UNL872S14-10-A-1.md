::: {.problem}
Hatcher p. 79 #1.

Let
$$
p:\widetilde X\to X
$$
be a covering space and let $A\subset X$. Put
$$
\widetilde A=p^{-1}(A).
$$
Show that the restriction
$$
p|_{\widetilde A}:\widetilde A\to A
$$
is a covering space.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: pf-step

Fix $a\in A$. Since $p:\widetilde X\to X$ is a covering, there is an open neighborhood $U\subset X$ of $a$ that is evenly covered.


::: pf-proof

This is the defining local property of a covering map.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

Write
$$
p^{-1}(U)
=
\bigsqcup_{\lambda\in\Lambda}U_\lambda,
$$
where each $U_\lambda$ is open in $\widetilde X$ and
$$
p|_{U_\lambda}:U_\lambda\xrightarrow{\sim}U
$$
is a homeomorphism.


::: pf-proof

This is what it means for $U$ to be evenly covered.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

The set
$$
V=U\cap A
$$
is an open neighborhood of $a$ in the subspace $A$.


::: pf-proof

Because $U$ is open in $X$, its intersection with $A$ is open in the subspace topology on $A$. Since $a\in U\cap A$, it is a neighborhood of $a$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

One has
$$
\boxed{
(p|_{\widetilde A})^{-1}(V)
=
\bigsqcup_{\lambda\in\Lambda}
(U_\lambda\cap\widetilde A)}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Since
$$
\widetilde A=p^{-1}(A),
$$
one has
$$
\begin{aligned}
(p|_{\widetilde A})^{-1}(V)
&=
p^{-1}(U\cap A)\cap\widetilde A\\
&=
p^{-1}(U)\cap p^{-1}(A)\\
&=
\left(
\bigsqcup_{\lambda}U_\lambda
\right)
\cap\widetilde A\\
&=
\bigsqcup_{\lambda}
(U_\lambda\cap\widetilde A).
\end{aligned}
$$
The union remains disjoint because the $U_\lambda$ are pairwise disjoint.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-5}

Each
$$
U_\lambda\cap\widetilde A
$$
is open in $\widetilde A$.


::: pf-proof

The set $U_\lambda$ is open in $\widetilde X$. Intersecting it with the subspace $\widetilde A$ gives an open subset of $\widetilde A$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-6}

For every $\lambda$,
$$
\boxed{
p|_{U_\lambda\cap\widetilde A}:
U_\lambda\cap\widetilde A
\xrightarrow{\sim}
V}
$$
is a homeomorphism.


::: pf-proof

The map
$$
p|_{U_\lambda}:U_\lambda\to U
$$
is a homeomorphism by step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref}. Under this homeomorphism,
$$
U_\lambda\cap\widetilde A
=
U_\lambda\cap p^{-1}(A)
$$
is exactly the inverse image of
$$
U\cap A=V.
$$
Restricting a homeomorphism to the inverse image of a subspace gives a homeomorphism onto that subspace.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-7}

Thus $V$ is evenly covered by
$$
p|_{\widetilde A}:\widetilde A\to A.
$$


::: pf-proof

Steps [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} through [](#s1-6){.pf-ref} give a disjoint union of open sheets over $V$, each mapped homeomorphically onto $V$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-8}

Therefore
$$
\boxed{
p|_{\widetilde A}:\widetilde A\to A
\text{ is a covering map}.}
$$


::: pf-proof

Every point $a\in A$ has an evenly covered neighborhood by step [](#s1-7){.pf-ref}. This is exactly the definition of a covering map.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-8){.pf-ref} is the required conclusion.

:::
