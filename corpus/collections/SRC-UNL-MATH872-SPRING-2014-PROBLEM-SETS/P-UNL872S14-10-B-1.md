::: {.problem}
Let (Y) be path connected and locally path connected, and suppose a group (G)
acts on (Y) by a covering-space action. Fix (y_0in Y), and let
[
p:Y	o Y/G
]
be the quotient covering map. Prove that
[
p_*pi_1(Y,y_0)
]
is a normal subgroup of
[
pi_1(Y/G,[y_0]).
]
:::

::: {.solution}
Let
[
H=p_*pi_1(Y,y_0).
]

<1>1. A based loop (lambda) in (Y/G) represents an element of (H) if and only if its lift starting at (y_0) is a loop.

::: {.proof}
For any covering map, the image of the induced map on fundamental groups consists exactly of the based loops whose lifts from the chosen point close up.
:::

<1>2. Let
[
[gamma]inpi_1(Y/G,[y_0])
]
and
[
[eta]in H
]
be arbitrary. Let (widetildegamma) be the lift of (gamma) beginning at (y_0), and set
[
y_1=widetildegamma(1).
]

::: {.proof}
The path lifting theorem gives the unique lift.
:::

<1>3. There is some (gin G) such that
[
oxed{y_1=gy_0}.
]

::: {.proof}
Since (gamma) is a loop,
[
p(y_1)=gamma(1)=[y_0]=p(y_0).
]
The fiber of the quotient map over ([y_0]) is exactly the orbit (Gy_0).
:::

<1>4. Let (widetildeeta) be the lift of (eta) starting at (y_0). Then (widetildeeta) is a loop at (y_0).

::: {.proof}
This follows from step <1>1 because ([eta]in H).
:::

<1>5. The path
[
gwidetildeeta(t)=g(widetildeeta(t))
]
is the lift of (eta) starting at (y_1), and it is a loop at (y_1).

::: {.proof}
Every (gin G) is a deck transformation for the quotient covering, so
[
p(gz)=p(z).
]
Hence
[
p(gwidetildeeta(t))=eta(t).
]
Its initial and final points are both
[
gy_0=y_1.
]
Uniqueness of path lifts identifies it with the lift of (eta) from (y_1).
:::

<1>6. The lift of
[
gamma*eta*overlinegamma
]
starting at (y_0) is
[
oxed{
widetildegamma*(gwidetildeeta)*overline{widetildegamma}}.
]

::: {.proof}
The first segment lifts (gamma) from (y_0) to (y_1). Step <1>5 gives the lift of (eta) beginning and ending at (y_1). The reverse path (overline{widetildegamma}) is the lift of (overlinegamma) beginning at (y_1). Uniqueness of path lifts gives the concatenated lift.
:::

<1>7. The lift in step <1>6 is a loop at (y_0).

::: {.proof}
It starts at (y_0), reaches (y_1), traverses a loop at (y_1), and returns along (overline{widetildegamma}) to (y_0).
:::

<1>8. Therefore
[
oxed{
[gamma][eta][gamma]^{-1}in H}.
]

::: {.proof}
By step <1>7, the conjugate loop has a lift from (y_0) that closes. Step <1>1 therefore puts its class in (H).
:::

<1>9. Hence
[
oxed{
p_*pi_1(Y,y_0)
	rianglelefteq
pi_1(Y/G,[y_0])}.
]

::: {.proof}
The elements ([gamma]) of the ambient group and ([eta]in H) were arbitrary. Step <1>8 proves invariance of (H) under conjugation.
:::

<1>10. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>9 is the required conclusion.
:::
