::: {.problem}
Let $Y$ be path connected and locally path connected, and suppose a group $G$ acts on $Y$ by a covering-space action.
Fix $y_0\in Y$, and let
$$
p:Y\to Y/G
$$
be the quotient covering map.
Prove that
$$
p_*\pi_1(Y,y_0)
$$
is a normal subgroup of
$$
\pi_1(Y/G,[y_0]).
$$
:::

::: {.solution}

Let
$$
H=p_*\pi_1(Y,y_0).
$$

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

A based loop $\lambda$ in $Y/G$ represents an element of $H$ if and only if its lift starting at $y_0$ is a loop.


::: pf-proof

For any covering map, the image of the induced map on fundamental groups consists exactly of the based loops whose lifts from the chosen point close up.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Let
$$
[\gamma]\in\pi_1(Y/G,[y_0])
$$
and
$$
[\eta]\in H
$$
be arbitrary.
Let $\widetilde\gamma$ be the lift of $\gamma$ beginning at $y_0$, and set
$$
y_1=\widetilde\gamma(1).
$$


::: pf-proof

The path lifting theorem gives the unique lift.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

There is some $g\in G$ such that
$$
\boxed{y_1=gy_0}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Since $\gamma$ is a loop,
$$
p(y_1)=\gamma(1)=[y_0]=p(y_0).
$$
The fiber of the quotient map over $[y_0]$ is exactly the orbit $Gy_0$.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Let $\widetilde\eta$ be the lift of $\eta$ starting at $y_0$. Then $\widetilde\eta$ is a loop at $y_0$.


::: pf-proof

This follows from step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} because $[\eta]\in H$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-5}

The path
$$
g\widetilde\eta(t)=g(\widetilde\eta(t))
$$
is the lift of $\eta$ starting at $y_1$, and it is a loop at $y_1$.


::: pf-proof

Every $g\in G$ is a deck transformation for the quotient covering, so
$$
p(gz)=p(z).
$$
Hence
$$
p(g\widetilde\eta(t))=\eta(t).
$$
Its initial and final points are both
$$
gy_0=y_1.
$$
Uniqueness of path lifts identifies it with the lift of $\eta$ from $y_1$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-6}

The lift of
$$
\gamma*\eta*\overline\gamma
$$
starting at $y_0$ is
$$
\boxed{
\widetilde\gamma*(g\widetilde\eta)*\overline{\widetilde\gamma}}.
$$


::: pf-proof

The first segment lifts $\gamma$ from $y_0$ to $y_1$. Step [](#s1-5){.pf-ref} gives the lift of $\eta$ beginning and ending at $y_1$. The reverse path $\overline{\widetilde\gamma}$ is the lift of $\overline\gamma$ beginning at $y_1$. Uniqueness of path lifts gives the concatenated lift.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-7}

The lift in step [](#s1-6){.pf-ref} is a loop at $y_0$.


::: pf-proof

It starts at $y_0$, reaches $y_1$, traverses a loop at $y_1$, and returns along $\overline{\widetilde\gamma}$ to $y_0$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-8}

Therefore
$$
\boxed{
[\gamma][\eta][\gamma]^{-1}\in H}.
$$


::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1-7){.pf-ref}, the conjugate loop has a lift from $y_0$ that closes.
Step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} therefore puts its class in $H$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-9}

Hence
$$
\boxed{
p_*\pi_1(Y,y_0)
\trianglelefteq
\pi_1(Y/G,[y_0])}.
$$


::: pf-proof

The elements $[\gamma]$ of the ambient group and $[\eta]\in H$ were arbitrary.
Step [](#s1-8){.pf-ref} proves invariance of $H$ under conjugation.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-9){.pf-ref} is the required conclusion.

:::
