::: {.problem}
Construct a Δ-complex structure for the connected sum of two tori.
(Give an explanation with pictures, formal proof not needed.
Be sure to label all simplices of all dimensions.)
:::

::: {.solution}
Start with an oriented octagon (P) whose vertices, in cyclic order, are
[
p_0,p_1,dots,p_7.
]

<1>1. Label the oriented boundary edges
[
[p_i,p_{i+1}]
]
cyclically by
[
oxed{
a, b, a^{-1}, b^{-1}, c, d, c^{-1}, d^{-1}}.
]

::: {.proof}
Thus the boundary word of the octagon is
[
aba^{-1}b^{-1}cdc^{-1}d^{-1},
]
the standard fundamental polygon word for a closed orientable surface of genus (2).
:::

<1>2. Identify the two (a)-edges, the two (b)-edges, the two (c)-edges, and the two (d)-edges by orientation-preserving affine maps after using the displayed inverse orientations.

::: {.proof}
For example, the edge labeled (a^{-1}) is glued to the edge labeled (a) with the opposite boundary orientation, so the gluing preserves the chosen orientation of the abstract edge (a). The same rule is used for (b,c,d).
:::

<1>3. After these boundary identifications, all eight polygon vertices become one vertex
[
oxed{v}.
]

::: {.proof}
The standard genus-two polygon identifications successively identify the endpoints of paired edges; following the boundary word shows every (p_i) lies in the same equivalence class.
:::

<1>4. Triangulate the octagon by the five diagonals
[
oxed{
x_2=[p_0,p_2],x_3=[p_0,p_3],x_4=[p_0,p_4],x_5=[p_0,p_5],x_6=[p_0,p_6]}.
]

::: {.proof}
A fan triangulation of an (8)-gon from one vertex uses exactly (8-3=5) diagonals.
:::

<1>5. The six (2)-simplices are
[
oxed{
egin{aligned}
sigma_1&=[p_0,p_1,p_2],\
sigma_2&=[p_0,p_2,p_3],\
sigma_3&=[p_0,p_3,p_4],\
sigma_4&=[p_0,p_4,p_5],\
sigma_5&=[p_0,p_5,p_6],\
sigma_6&=[p_0,p_6,p_7].
end{aligned}}
]

::: {.proof}
These are exactly the six triangles in the fan from (p_0), and their interiors are pairwise disjoint with union equal to the octagon interior.
:::

<1>6. The (0)-simplices of the quotient (Delta)-complex consist of the single vertex
[
oxed{{v}}.
]

::: {.proof}
This is step <1>3.
:::

<1>7. The (1)-simplices are
[
oxed{
a,b,c,d,x_2,x_3,x_4,x_5,x_6}.
]

::: {.proof}
The eight boundary edges are identified in four pairs, producing the four edge classes (a,b,c,d). The five fan diagonals lie in the polygon interior and are not identified with one another or with boundary edges. Thus there are (4+5=9) open (1)-simplices.
:::

<1>8. The oriented boundary of (sigma_1) is
[
oxed{a,b,x_2^{-1}}.
]

::: {.proof}
Traversing
[
p_0	o p_1	o p_2	o p_0
]
uses the boundary edges (a,b), followed by the reverse of (x_2).
:::

<1>9. The oriented boundary of (sigma_2) is
[
oxed{x_2,a^{-1},x_3^{-1}}.
]

::: {.proof}
Traverse
[
p_0	o p_2	o p_3	o p_0.
]
The three edges are (x_2), the boundary edge labeled (a^{-1}), and the reverse of (x_3).
:::

<1>10. The oriented boundary of (sigma_3) is
[
oxed{x_3,b^{-1},x_4^{-1}}.
]

::: {.proof}
Traverse
[
p_0	o p_3	o p_4	o p_0.
]
:::

<1>11. The oriented boundary of (sigma_4) is
[
oxed{x_4,c,x_5^{-1}}.
]

::: {.proof}
Traverse
[
p_0	o p_4	o p_5	o p_0.
]
:::

<1>12. The oriented boundary of (sigma_5) is
[
oxed{x_5,d,x_6^{-1}}.
]

::: {.proof}
Traverse
[
p_0	o p_5	o p_6	o p_0.
]
:::

<1>13. The oriented boundary of (sigma_6) is
[
oxed{x_6,c^{-1},d^{-1}}.
]

::: {.proof}
Traverse
[
p_0	o p_6	o p_7	o p_0.
]
The last two boundary edges of the octagon are labeled (c^{-1}) and (d^{-1}).
:::

<1>14. These cells and face identifications define a (Delta)-complex.

::: {.proof}
Each triangle is mapped homeomorphically on its interior to the quotient. Every edge face is identified affinely with one of the nine (1)-simplices listed in step <1>7, and every vertex maps to the unique (0)-simplex (v). These are exactly the compatibility conditions for a (Delta)-complex; distinct vertices of one abstract simplex are allowed to have the same image in the quotient.
:::

<1>15. The underlying quotient space is the connected sum of two tori,
[
oxed{T^2#T^2}.
]

::: {.proof}
The quotient of an octagon by the side-pairing word
[
aba^{-1}b^{-1}cdc^{-1}d^{-1}
]
is the standard fundamental-polygon construction of the closed orientable genus-two surface, equivalently (T^2#T^2). The fan triangulation does not change the quotient space.
:::

<1>16. The cell count is
[
oxed{
f_0=1,qquad f_1=9,qquad f_2=6}.
]

::: {.proof}
These are steps <1>6, <1>7, and <1>5.
:::

<1>17. Its Euler characteristic is
[
oxed{
chi=1-9+6=-2},
]
as expected for a genus-two closed orientable surface.

::: {.proof}
For an orientable closed surface of genus (g),
[
chi=2-2g.
]
At (g=2), this is (-2), agreeing with the explicit (Delta)-complex count.
:::

<1>18. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Steps <1>1--<1>15 construct and identify the requested (Delta)-complex, while steps <1>16--<1>17 check the complete simplex labeling and cell count.
:::
