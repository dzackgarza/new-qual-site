::: {.problem}
Construct a Δ-complex structure for the connected sum of two tori.
(Give an explanation with pictures, formal proof not needed.
Be sure to label all simplices of all dimensions.)
:::

::: {.solution}

Start with an oriented octagon $P$ whose vertices, in cyclic order, are
$$
p_0,p_1,\dots,p_7.
$$

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

Label the oriented boundary edges
$$
[p_i,p_{i+1}]
$$
cyclically by
$$
\boxed{
a, b, a^{-1}, b^{-1}, c, d, c^{-1}, d^{-1}}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Thus the boundary word of the octagon is
$$
aba^{-1}b^{-1}cdc^{-1}d^{-1},
$$
the standard fundamental polygon word for a closed orientable surface of genus $2$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

Identify the two $a$-edges, the two $b$-edges, the two $c$-edges, and the two $d$-edges by orientation-preserving affine maps after using the displayed inverse orientations.


::: pf-proof

For example, the edge labeled $a^{-1}$ is glued to the edge labeled $a$ with the opposite boundary orientation, so the gluing preserves the chosen orientation of the abstract edge $a$. The same rule is used for $(b,c,d)$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

After these boundary identifications, all eight polygon vertices become one vertex
$$
\boxed{v}.
$$


::: pf-proof

The standard genus-two polygon identifications successively identify the endpoints of paired edges; following the boundary word shows every $p_i$ lies in the same equivalence class.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

Triangulate the octagon by the five diagonals
$$
\boxed{
x_2=[p_0,p_2],x_3=[p_0,p_3],x_4=[p_0,p_4],x_5=[p_0,p_5],x_6=[p_0,p_6]}.
$$


::: pf-proof

A fan triangulation of an $8$-gon from one vertex uses exactly $8-3=5$ diagonals.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-5}

The six $2$-simplices are
$$
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\sigma_1&=[p_0,p_1,p_2],\\
\sigma_2&=[p_0,p_2,p_3],\\
\sigma_3&=[p_0,p_3,p_4],\\
\sigma_4&=[p_0,p_4,p_5],\\
\sigma_5&=[p_0,p_5,p_6],\\
\sigma_6&=[p_0,p_6,p_7].
\end{aligned}}
$$


::: pf-proof

These are exactly the six triangles in the fan from $p_0$, and their interiors are pairwise disjoint with union equal to the octagon interior.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-6}

The $0$-simplices of the quotient $\Delta$-complex consist of the single vertex
$$
\boxed{{v}}.
$$


::: pf-proof

This is step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-7}

The $1$-simplices are
$$
\boxed{
a,b,c,d,x_2,x_3,x_4,x_5,x_6}.
$$


::: pf-proof

The eight boundary edges are identified in four pairs, producing the four edge classes $(a,b,c,d)$. The five fan diagonals lie in the polygon interior and are not identified with one another or with boundary edges.
Thus there are $4+5=9$ open $1$-simplices.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-8}

The oriented boundary of $\sigma_1$ is
$$
\boxed{a,b,x_2^{-1}}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Traversing
$$
p_0\to p_1\to p_2\to p_0
$$
uses the boundary edges $(a,b)$, followed by the reverse of $x_2$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-9}

The oriented boundary of $\sigma_2$ is
$$
\boxed{x_2,a^{-1},x_3^{-1}}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Traverse
$$
p_0\to p_2\to p_3\to p_0.
$$
The three edges are $x_2$, the boundary edge labeled $a^{-1}$, and the reverse of $x_3$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-10}

The oriented boundary of $\sigma_3$ is
$$
\boxed{x_3,b^{-1},x_4^{-1}}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Traverse
$$
p_0\to p_3\to p_4\to p_0.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-11}

The oriented boundary of $\sigma_4$ is
$$
\boxed{x_4,c,x_5^{-1}}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Traverse
$$
p_0\to p_4\to p_5\to p_0.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-12}

The oriented boundary of $\sigma_5$ is
$$
\boxed{x_5,d,x_6^{-1}}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Traverse
$$
p_0\to p_5\to p_6\to p_0.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-13}

The oriented boundary of $\sigma_6$ is
$$
\boxed{x_6,c^{-1},d^{-1}}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Traverse
$$
p_0\to p_6\to p_7\to p_0.
$$
The last two boundary edges of the octagon are labeled $c^{-1}$ and $d^{-1}$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-14}

These cells and face identifications define a $\Delta$-complex.


::: pf-proof

Each triangle is mapped homeomorphically on its interior to the quotient.
Every edge face is identified affinely with one of the nine $1$-simplices listed in step [](#s1-7){.pf-ref}, and every vertex maps to the unique $0$-simplex $v$. These are exactly the compatibility conditions for a $\Delta$-complex; distinct vertices of one abstract simplex are allowed to have the same image in the quotient.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-15}

The underlying quotient space is the connected sum of two tori,
$$
\boxed{T^2#T^2}.
$$


::: pf-proof

The quotient of an octagon by the side-pairing word
$$
aba^{-1}b^{-1}cdc^{-1}d^{-1}
$$
is the standard fundamental-polygon construction of the closed orientable genus-two surface, equivalently $T^2#T^2$. The fan triangulation does not change the quotient space.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-16}

The cell count is
$$
\boxed{
f_0=1,\qquad f_1=9,\qquad f_2=6}.
$$


::: pf-proof

These are steps [](#s1-6){.pf-ref} and [](#s1-7){.pf-ref}, and [](#s1-5){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-17}

Its Euler characteristic is
$$
\boxed{
\chi=1-9+6=-2},
$$
as expected for a genus-two closed orientable surface.


::: pf-proof

For an orientable closed surface of genus $g$,
$$
\chi=2-2g.
$$
At $g=2$, this is $-2$, agreeing with the explicit $\Delta$-complex count.

:::
