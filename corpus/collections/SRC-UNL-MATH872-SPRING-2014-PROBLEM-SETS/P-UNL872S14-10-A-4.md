Lifting criterion and unique lifting property, proof deconstruction.
The following questions refer to the proofs of the lifting criterion (LC) and the unique lifting property (ULP) in Hatcher, pp. 62--63.
Paragraph 1 of LC begins "The 'only if' statement is", paragraph 2 line 3 of LC begins "path-connected open neighborhood", and line 4 of ULP begins "continuity of $\tilde f_1$".

(a) LC paragraph 1, line 3: what theorem is used in the definition of the function $\tilde f \colon Y \to \tilde X$?

(b) LC paragraph 1, lines 6--7: what theorem is Hatcher applying in order to know that a loop $h_1$ exists such that $h_1$ is path homotopic to $h_0$ and the lift $\tilde h_1$ at $\tilde x_0$ is a loop?

(c) LC paragraph 1: where is the hypothesis that $\operatorname{im} f_* \subseteq \operatorname{im} p_*$ used in the proof that $\tilde f$ is well-defined?

(d) LC paragraph 2: in order to prove that $\tilde f$ is continuous, Hatcher is proving another condition that is equivalent to the definition of continuity.
State this condition, and give a reference to the theorem in Munkres that shows the equivalence.
Also, Hatcher does not show the condition for all open neighborhoods of $f(y)$, only for evenly covered ones; write the missing sentences that show the condition holds for all open neighborhoods of $f(y)$.

(e) LC paragraphs 1--2: exactly where are the hypotheses that $Y$ is path-connected and locally path-connected, respectively, used in this proof?

(f) ULP lines 4--5: Hatcher is again applying the theorem from Munkres you found in part (d).
Write a couple of extra lines explaining more clearly why an open set $N$ exists with the properties listed in these lines.

(g) ULP: exactly where is the hypothesis that $Y$ is connected used in this proof?
