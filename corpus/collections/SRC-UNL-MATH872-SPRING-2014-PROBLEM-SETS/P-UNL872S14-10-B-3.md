::: {.problem}
Let Y be a simply connected and LPC space.
Let G and H be subgroups of the group Homeo(Y) of all homeomorphisms of Y. Suppose that G and H are conjugate in Homeo(Y), and that both G and H give covering space actions on Y. Show that Y/G and Y/H are homeomorphic spaces.
:::
