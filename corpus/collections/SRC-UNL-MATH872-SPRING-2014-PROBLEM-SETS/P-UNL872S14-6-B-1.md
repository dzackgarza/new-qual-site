Hints for (f): The boundary of $X$ is the set of points that have an open neighborhood homeomorphic to the half disc
\[
\{(x,y)\in\mathbb R^2\mid x^2+y^2<1\text{ and }x\ge0\}.
\]
Let $x_0$ be a basepoint of $X$ on the boundary circle. Let $f$ be the loop in $X$ at $x_0$ that goes around the center circle of $X$ once, and let $g$ be the loop at $x_0$ that goes around the boundary circle of $X$ once. How are $f$ and $g$ related? Where does the map induced by a retraction $r:X\to A$ send $[f]$ and $[g]$?
