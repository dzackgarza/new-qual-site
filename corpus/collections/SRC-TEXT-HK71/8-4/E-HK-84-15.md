::: {.exercise}
On $\RR^4$ define the quadratic form
$$
q(x,y,z,t)=t^2-x^2-y^2-z^2.
$$
A *Lorentz transformation* is a real linear operator preserving $q$. Let $H$ be the real vector space of self-adjoint $2\times2$ complex matrices, and define
$$
U(x,y,z,t)=\begin{pmatrix}t+x&y+iz\\y-iz&t-x\end{pmatrix}.
$$

(a) Show that $U:\RR^4\to H$ is a real vector space isomorphism.

(b) Show that $q(\alpha)=\det(U\alpha)$.

(c) If $T:H\to H$ is real linear, show that $L=U^{-1}TU$ is real linear on $\RR^4$.

(d) For a complex $2\times2$ matrix $M$, show that $T_M(A)=M^*AM$ defines a real linear operator on $H$.

(e) If $\abs{\det M}=1$, show that $L_M=U^{-1}T_MU$ is a Lorentz transformation.

(f) Find a Lorentz transformation that is not of the form $L_M$.
:::
