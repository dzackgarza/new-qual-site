::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ and $W$ be finite-dimensional inner product spaces having the same dimension.
Let $U$ be an isomorphism of $V$ onto $W$.
Show that:

(a) The mapping $T \to UTU^{-1}$ is an isomorphism of the vector space $L(V, V)$ onto the vector space $L(W, W)$.

(b) $\operatorname{trace}(UTU^{-1}) = \operatorname{trace}(T)$ for each $T$ in $L(V, V)$.

(c) $UT_{\alpha,\beta}U^{-1} = T_{U\alpha, U\beta}$ ($T_{\alpha,\beta}$ defined in Exercise 10).

(d) $(UTU^{-1})^* = UT^*U^{-1}$.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. $T\mapsto UTU^{-1}$ linear and bijective with inverse $S\mapsto U^{-1}SU$.
Proof: check.

<1>2. $\operatorname{tr}(UTU^{-1})=\operatorname{tr}(T)$.
Proof: trace cyclic.

<1>3. $T_{\alpha,\beta}(v)=\langle v,\beta\rangle\alpha$, so $UT_{\alpha,\beta}U^{-1}=T_{U\alpha,U\beta}$.
Proof: compute.

<1>4. $(UTU^{-1})^*=UT^*U^{-1}$ (since $U$ unitary, $U^*=U^{-1}$).
Proof: $(UTU^{-1})^*=(U^{-1})^*T^*U^*=UT^*U^{-1}$.

<1>5. Q.E.D.
Proof: <1>1 and <1>4.
:::
