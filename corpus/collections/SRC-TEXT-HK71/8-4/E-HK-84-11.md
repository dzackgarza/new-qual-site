::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be an $n$-dimensional inner product space over the field $F$, and let $L(V, V)$ be the space of linear operators on $V$.
Show that there is a unique inner product on $L(V, V)$ with the property that $||T_{\alpha, \beta}||^2 = ||\alpha||^2 ||\beta||^2$ for all $\alpha, \beta$ in $V$.
($T_{\alpha, \beta}$ is the operator defined in Exercise 10.) Find an isomorphism between $L(V, V)$ with this inner product and the space of $n \times n$ matrices over $F$, with the inner product $(A \mid B) = \operatorname{tr}(AB^*)$.
:::
