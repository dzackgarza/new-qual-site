::: {.exercise}
Construct a linear operator $T$ with minimal polynomial $x^2(x-1)^2$ and characteristic polynomial $x^3(x-1)^4$.
Describe the primary decomposition of the vector space under $T$ and find the projections on the primary components.
Find a basis in which the matrix of $T$ is in Jordan form.
Also find an explicit direct sum decomposition of the space into $T$-cyclic subspaces as in Theorem 3 and give the invariant factors.
:::

::: {.solution}
Take $V=F^7$ and, in the standard basis, let
$$
T=J_2(0)\oplus J_1(0)\oplus J_2(1)\oplus J_2(1),
$$
where $J_r(c)$ is the $r\times r$ Jordan block with eigenvalue $c$.
Then
$$
\chi_T(x)=x^3(x-1)^4,
\qquad
m_T(x)=x^2(x-1)^2,
$$
because the algebraic multiplicities are $3$ and $4$ and the largest block at
each eigenvalue has size $2$ [@HK71].

<1>1. Primary decomposition and projections.
::: {.proof}
Let
$$
W_0=\ker T^2,
\qquad
W_1=\ker(T-I)^2.
$$
For the displayed block matrix,
$$
W_0=F^3\oplus0,
\qquad
W_1=0\oplus F^4,
\qquad
V=W_0\oplus W_1.
$$
The polynomials
$$
e_0(x)=1-3x^2+2x^3,
\qquad
e_1(x)=3x^2-2x^3
$$
satisfy
$$
e_0\equiv1\pmod{x^2},\quad e_0\equiv0\pmod{(x-1)^2},
$$
and the reverse congruences for $e_1$. Hence the primary projections are
$$
\boxed{E_0=e_0(T),\qquad E_1=e_1(T)}.
$$
They satisfy $E_0+E_1=I$, $E_i^2=E_i$, $E_0E_1=0$, with ranges $W_0,W_1$
respectively, by the primary-decomposition/CRT construction [@HK71].
:::

<1>2. Jordan basis.
::: {.proof}
The standard basis is already a Jordan basis for the displayed $T$; its Jordan
form is therefore
$$
\boxed{J_2(0)\oplus J_1(0)\oplus J_2(1)\oplus J_2(1)}.
$$
This also directly verifies the characteristic and minimal polynomials above.
:::

<1>3. Cyclic decomposition and invariant factors.
::: {.proof}
Write the standard basis in the four displayed Jordan blocks as
$(e_1,e_2)$, $(e_3)$, $(e_4,e_5)$, $(e_6,e_7)$, with the convention that the
first vector of each size-two block is cyclic for that block. Define
$$
\alpha_1=e_3+e_4,
\qquad
\alpha_2=e_1+e_6.
$$
Because the annihilators of $e_3$ and $e_4$ are respectively $x$ and
$(x-1)^2$, which are coprime, the annihilator of $\alpha_1$ is
$$
q_1=x(x-1)^2.
$$
Likewise the annihilators of $e_1$ and $e_6$ are $x^2$ and $(x-1)^2$, so
the annihilator of $\alpha_2$ is
$$
q_2=x^2(x-1)^2.
$$
Their cyclic subspaces use disjoint Jordan blocks, hence
$$
V=Z(\alpha_1;T)\oplus Z(\alpha_2;T).
$$
Their dimensions are $\deg q_1=3$ and $\deg q_2=4$, totaling $7$, and
$q_1\mid q_2$. Therefore the invariant factors are
$$
\boxed{x(x-1)^2\mid x^2(x-1)^2}.
$$
Their product is $x^3(x-1)^4$ and the last is the minimal polynomial, as
required.
:::
