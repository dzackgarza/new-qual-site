::: {.exercise}
Prove an analogue of Exercise 17 in Section 10.2 for skew-symmetric forms.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. Let $f$ be a skew-symmetric bilinear form on a finite-dimensional vector space $V$ over a field of characteristic $\neq 2$.
Proof: setup.

<1>2. If $f = 0$, the result is trivial (the zero form is represented by the zero matrix).
Proof: base case.

<1>3. Otherwise, choose $u, v \in V$ with $f(u, v) \neq 0$; rescaling, assume $f(u, v) = 1$.
Proof: $f$ is nonzero, so some pair has nonzero value; scale $u$ by $1/f(u,v)$.

<1>4. The subspace $W = \operatorname{span}\{u, v\}$ is nondegenerate, and $f|_W$ has matrix $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ -1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$ in the basis $(u, v)$.
Proof: $f(u,u) = f(v,v) = 0$ (skew-symmetry) and $f(u,v) = 1$, so $f(v,u) = -1$.

<1>5. $V = W \oplus W^\perp$.
Proof: $W$ is nondegenerate, so $V = W \oplus W^\perp$.

<1>6. By induction on $\dim V$, $f|_{W^\perp}$ has a basis in which its matrix is a block diagonal sum of $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ -1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$ blocks (and possibly a zero block).
Proof: <1>5 and induction.

<1>7. Hence there is a basis of $V$ in which the matrix of $f$ is
$$\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ -1 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \oplus \cdots \oplus \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ -1 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \oplus 0,$$
i.e. a block diagonal matrix with $2 \times 2$ hyperbolic blocks $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ -1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$ and a zero block.
Proof: <1>4 and <1>6.

<1>8. In particular, the rank of $f$ is even, and $f$ is nondegenerate iff $\dim V$ is even and there is no zero block.
Proof: <1>7.

<1>9. Q.E.D.
Proof: <1>7 and <1>8.
:::
