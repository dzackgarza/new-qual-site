::: {.exercise}
Let $f$ be a bilinear form on a vector space $V$.
Let $L_f$ and $R_f$ be the mappings of $V$ into $V^*$ associated with $f$ in Section 10.1. Prove that $f$ is skew-symmetric if and only if $L_f = -R_f$.
:::
