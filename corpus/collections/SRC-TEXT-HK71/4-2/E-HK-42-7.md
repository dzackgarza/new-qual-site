::: {.exercise}
If $a$ and $b$ are elements of a field $F$ and $a \neq 0$, show that the polynomials $1$, $ax + b$, $(ax + b)^2$, $(ax + b)^3$, $\ldots$ form a basis of $F[x]$.
:::

::: solution
The polynomial $(ax+b)^k$ has degree $k$ and leading coefficient $a^k\ne0$. Hence the family
\[
1,\ ax+b,\ (ax+b)^2,\ldots
\]
is linearly independent because its elements have distinct degrees.

To show that it spans $F[x]$, note that
\[
x=\frac{(ax+b)-b}{a}.
\]
Therefore every monomial $x^n$ is a polynomial in $ax+b$, namely
\[
x^n=a^{-n}\big((ax+b)-b\big)^n.
\]
Thus every standard monomial belongs to the span of the displayed family. Since the monomials span $F[x]$, so does this family. Hence it is a basis of $F[x]$.
:::
