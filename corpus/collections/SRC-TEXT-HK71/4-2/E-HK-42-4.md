::: {.exercise}
If $f$ and $g$ are independent polynomials over a field $F$ and $h$ is a non-zero polynomial over $F$, show that $fh$ and $gh$ are independent.
:::

::: {.solution}
Suppose
\[
a(fh)+b(gh)=0
\]
for scalars $a,b\in F$. Then
\[
(af+bg)h=0.
\]
Since $F[x]$ is an integral domain and $h\ne0$, it follows that
\[
af+bg=0.
\]
Because $f$ and $g$ are linearly independent, $a=b=0$. Hence $fh$ and $gh$ are linearly independent.
:::
