::: {.exercise}
Let $T$ be the linear operator on $\RR^3$ defined by
\[
T(x_1, x_2, x_3) = (x_1,\; x_3,\; -2x_2 - x_3).
\]
Let $f$ be the polynomial over $\RR$ defined by $f = -x^3 + 2$.
Find $f(T)$.
:::

::: solution
Relative to the standard basis,
\[
[T]=
\begin{bmatrix}
1&0&0\\
0&0&1\\
0&-2&-1
\end{bmatrix}.
\]
A direct computation gives
\[
[T]^3=
\begin{bmatrix}
1&0&0\\
0&2&-1\\
0&2&3
\end{bmatrix}.
\]
Therefore
\[
[f(T)]=- [T]^3+2I
=
\begin{bmatrix}
1&0&0\\
0&0&1\\
0&-2&-1
\end{bmatrix}
=[T].
\]
Thus
\[
\boxed{f(T)=T}.
\]
:::
