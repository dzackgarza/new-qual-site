::: {.exercise}
If $F$ is a field, show that the product of two non-zero elements of $F^{\infty}$ is non-zero.
:::

::: solution
Let
\[
f=(f_0,f_1,\ldots),\qquad g=(g_0,g_1,\ldots)
\]
be nonzero elements of $F^\infty$. Let $m$ be the least index with $f_m\ne0$ and $n$ the least index with $g_n\ne0$. By the convolution definition of multiplication in $F^\infty$,
\[
(fg)_{m+n}=\sum_{i=0}^{m+n}f_i g_{m+n-i}.
\]
If $i<m$, then $f_i=0$; if $i>m$, then $m+n-i<n$, so $g_{m+n-i}=0$. Thus only the term $i=m$ survives:
\[
(fg)_{m+n}=f_mg_n\ne0.
\]
Hence $fg\ne0$.
:::
