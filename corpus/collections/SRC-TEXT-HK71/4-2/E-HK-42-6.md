::: {.exercise}
Let $S$ be a set of non-zero polynomials over a field $F$.
If no two elements of $S$ have the same degree, show that $S$ is an independent set in $F[x]$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Take a finite linear relation
\[
c_1f_1+\cdots+c_rf_r=0
\]
with distinct $f_i\in S$. Since their degrees are pairwise distinct, choose $f_k$ of largest degree. The coefficient of the highest-degree term in the sum comes only from $c_kf_k$, so it can vanish only if $c_k=0$. Removing that term and repeating the argument shows successively that every $c_i=0$.

Thus every finite subset of $S$ is linearly independent, so $S$ is an independent set in $F[x]$.
:::
