::: {.exercise}
Let $F$ be a subfield of the complex numbers and let $A$ be the following $2 \times 2$ matrix over $F$
$$
A = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ -1 & 3 \end{bmatrix}.
$$
For each of the following polynomials $f$ over $F$, compute $f(A)$.

(a) $f = x^2 - x + 2$;

(b) $f = x^3 - 1$;

(c) $f = x^2 - 5x + 7$.
:::

::: {.solution}
No retained solution set covers this exercise; the argument is the card's own, checked against Hoffman--Kunze, *Linear Algebra* (1971), §4.2 Exercise 1.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #powers}
$A^2=\begin{pmatrix}3&5\\-5&8\end{pmatrix}$ and $A^3=\begin{pmatrix}1&18\\-18&19\end{pmatrix}$.

::: pf-proof
$A^2$ has entries $2\cdot2+1\cdot(-1)=3$, $2\cdot1+1\cdot3=5$, $(-1)\cdot2+3\cdot(-1)=-5$ and $(-1)\cdot1+3\cdot3=8$. Then $A^3=A^2A$ has entries $3\cdot2+5\cdot(-1)=1$, $3\cdot1+5\cdot3=18$, $(-5)\cdot2+8\cdot(-1)=-18$ and $(-5)\cdot1+8\cdot3=19$.
:::
:::

::: {.pf-step #a}
(a) $f(A)=A^2-A+2I=\begin{pmatrix}3&4\\-4&7\end{pmatrix}$.

::: pf-proof
Subtract $A=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\-1&3\end{pmatrix}$ from the $A^2$ of step [](#powers){.pf-ref} and add $2I$: the entries are $3-2+2=3$, $5-1=4$, $-5+1=-4$ and $8-3+2=7$.
:::
:::

::: {.pf-step #b}
(b) $f(A)=A^3-I=\begin{pmatrix}0&18\\-18&18\end{pmatrix}$.

::: pf-proof
Subtract the identity from the $A^3$ of step [](#powers){.pf-ref}: the diagonal entries become $0$ and $18$, and the off-diagonal entries are unchanged.
:::
:::

::: {.pf-step #c}
(c) $f(A)=A^2-5A+7I=0$.

::: pf-proof
$5A=\begin{pmatrix}10&5\\-5&15\end{pmatrix}$ and $7I=\begin{pmatrix}7&0\\0&7\end{pmatrix}$, so the entries are $3-10+7=0$, $5-5+0=0$, $-5+5+0=0$ and $8-15+7=0$. This is also what the Cayley--Hamilton theorem predicts, $x^2-(\operatorname{tr}A)x+(\det A)=x^2-5x+7$ being the characteristic polynomial of $A$.
:::
:::

::: {.pf-step}
The three values are $\begin{pmatrix}3&4\\-4&7\end{pmatrix}$, $\begin{pmatrix}0&18\\-18&18\end{pmatrix}$ and the zero matrix.

::: pf-proof
Steps [](#a){.pf-ref}, [](#b){.pf-ref} and [](#c){.pf-ref} compute them.
:::
