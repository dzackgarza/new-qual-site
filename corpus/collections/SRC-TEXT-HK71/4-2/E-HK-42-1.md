::: {.exercise}
Let $F$ be a subfield of the complex numbers and let $A$ be the following $2 \times 2$ matrix over $F$
\[
A = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ -1 & 3 \end{bmatrix}.
\]
For each of the following polynomials $f$ over $F$, compute $f(A)$.

(a) $f = x^2 - x + 2$;

(b) $f = x^3 - 1$;

(c) $f = x^2 - 5x + 7$.
:::
