::: {.exercise}
Let $F$ be a subfield of the complex numbers and let $A$ be the following $2 \times 2$ matrix over $F$
\[
A = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ -1 & 3 \end{bmatrix}.
\]
For each of the following polynomials $f$ over $F$, compute $f(A)$.

(a) $f = x^2 - x + 2$;

(b) $f = x^3 - 1$;

(c) $f = x^2 - 5x + 7$.
:::

::: solution
A direct multiplication gives
\[
A^2=\begin{bmatrix}3&5\\-5&8\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
A^3=\begin{bmatrix}1&18\\-18&19\end{bmatrix}.
\]
Hence
\[
(A^2-A+2I)=
\begin{bmatrix}3&4\\-4&7\end{bmatrix},
\]
\[
(A^3-I)=
\begin{bmatrix}0&18\\-18&18\end{bmatrix},
\]
and
\[
A^2-5A+7I
=\begin{bmatrix}0&0\\0&0\end{bmatrix}.
\]
:::
