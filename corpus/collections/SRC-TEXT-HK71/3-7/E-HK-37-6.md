::: {.exercise}
Let $n$ be a positive integer and let $V$ be the space of all polynomial functions over the field of real numbers which have degree at most $n$, i.e., functions of the form
\[
f(x) = c_0 + c_1 x + \cdots + c_n x^n.
\]
Let $D$ be the differentiation operator on $V$.
Find a basis for the null space of the transpose operator $D^t$.
:::

::: {.solution}
**Goal.** For $V$ the space of polynomials of degree $\le n$ and $D$ differentiation, find a basis for $\ker D^t$.

<1>1. $D^t: V^* \to V^*$ is defined by $(D^t \ell)(f) = \ell(Df)$.
::: {.proof}
definition of the transpose (dual) map.
:::

<1>2. $\ker D^t = \theset{\ell \in V^* : \ell(Df) = 0 \text{ for all } f \in V}$.
::: {.proof}
$\ell \in \ker D^t$ iff $D^t \ell = 0$ iff $\ell(Df) = 0$ for all $f$.
:::

<1>3. $\im D = \theset{\text{polynomials of degree } \le n-1}$.
::: {.proof}
$D$ maps $V$ onto the polynomials of degree $\le n-1$ (the derivative of a degree-$\le n$ polynomial has degree $\le n-1$, and every such is a derivative).
:::

<1>4. $\ker D^t = (\im D)^\perp = \theset{\ell \in V^* : \ell|_{\im D} = 0}$.
::: {.proof}
$\ell(Df) = 0$ for all $f$ iff $\ell$ vanishes on $\im D$.
:::

<1>5. $\im D$ has codimension $1$ in $V$ (it is the subspace of polynomials of degree $\le n-1$).
::: {.proof}
$\dim V = n+1$ and $\dim \im D = n$.
:::

<1>6. Hence $\ker D^t$ is $1$-dimensional.
::: {.proof}
$\dim \ker D^t = \dim V - \dim \im D = (n+1) - n = 1$.
:::

<1>7. A basis for $\ker D^t$ is $\theset{\ell}$ where $\ell(f) = f^{(n)}(0)$ (the $n$-th derivative evaluated at $0$), or equivalently the functional $\ell(f) = c_n$ (the leading coefficient).
::: {.proof}
$\ell(Df) = (Df)^{(n)}(0) = f^{(n+1)}(0) = 0$ for all $f$ (since $f$ has degree $\le n$), so $\ell \in \ker D^t$; and $\ell \neq 0$ (e.g. $\ell(x^n) = n! \neq 0$), so it spans the $1$-dimensional kernel.
:::

<1>8. Q.E.D.
::: {.proof}
<1>7 gives the basis $\theset{\ell}$ with $\ell(f) = c_n$ (the coefficient of $x^n$).
:::
:::
