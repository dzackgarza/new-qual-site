::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be the space of all $n \times n$ matrices over a field $F$ and let $B$ be a fixed $n \times n$ matrix.
If $T$ is the linear operator on $V$ defined by $T(A) = AB - BA$, and if $f$ is the trace function, what is $T^t f$?
:::

::: {.solution}

The argument is the card's own: no copy of Hoffman and Kunze and no solution set for it is retained in `tmp/sources`, so nothing here is transcribed. The statement is the exercise the collection index records as Hoffman and Kunze, *Linear Algebra* §3.7 Exercise 3.

Let $V=M_{n}(F)$, let $B\in V$ be fixed, let $T(A)=AB-BA$, and let $f\in V^{*}$ be the trace, $f(A)=\operatorname{tr}A=\sum_{i}A_{ii}$. The transpose $T^{t}\colon V^{*}\to V^{*}$ is defined by $(T^{t}f)(A)=f\bigl(T(A)\bigr)$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #swap}
$\operatorname{tr}(AB)=\operatorname{tr}(BA)$ for all $A,B\in V$.

::: pf-proof
$\operatorname{tr}(AB)=\sum_{i}(AB)_{ii}=\sum_{i}\sum_{j}A_{ij}B_{ji}$ and $\operatorname{tr}(BA)=\sum_{i}\sum_{j}B_{ij}A_{ji}$; the second double sum is the first with the names of the indices interchanged, a finite sum being unchanged by reindexing along the bijection $(i,j)\mapsto(j,i)$, and the factors commute in the field.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #evaluate}
For every $A\in V$, $(T^{t}f)(A)=0$.

::: pf-proof
By the definitions, $(T^{t}f)(A)=f(T(A))=\operatorname{tr}(AB-BA)=\operatorname{tr}(AB)-\operatorname{tr}(BA)$, linearity of the trace being immediate from $\operatorname{tr}C=\sum_{i}C_{ii}$. Step [](#swap){.pf-ref} makes the last expression $0$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step}
$T^{t}f=0$, the zero functional on $V$.

::: pf-proof
Two linear functionals are equal when they agree at every vector of $V$; by step [](#evaluate){.pf-ref} $T^{t}f$ agrees with the zero functional everywhere. So the answer to the exercise is the zero functional.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step}
Equivalently: $\operatorname{tr}\bigl(T(A)\bigr)=0$ for every $A$, so the trace is annihilated by every commutator operator, whatever $B$ is.

::: pf-proof
$\operatorname{tr}(T(A))=\operatorname{tr}(AB-BA)=0$ is exactly step [](#evaluate){.pf-ref} read as a statement about $f$ rather than about $T^{t}f$. Since $B$ was arbitrary, the same holds for every choice of the fixed matrix.
:::
