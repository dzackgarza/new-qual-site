::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be the space of all $n \times n$ matrices over a field $F$ and let $B$ be a fixed $n \times n$ matrix.
If $T$ is the linear operator on $V$ defined by $T(A) = AB - BA$, and if $f$ is the trace function, what is $T^t f$?
:::

::: {.solution}
For $A\in V$,
\[
(T^tf)(A)=f(T(A))=\operatorname{tr}(AB-BA).
\]
Since $\operatorname{tr}(AB)=\operatorname{tr}(BA)$,
\[
(T^tf)(A)=0
\]
for every $A$. Hence
\[
T^tf=0.
\]
:::
