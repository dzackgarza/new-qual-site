::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be the vector space of all polynomial functions over the field of real numbers.
Let $a$ and $b$ be fixed real numbers and let $f$ be the linear functional on $V$ defined by
\[
f(p) = \int_a^b p(x)\,dx.
\]
If $D$ is the differentiation operator on $V$, what is $D^t f$?
:::

::: solution
For any polynomial $p$,
\[
(D^tf)(p)=f(Dp)=\int_a^b p'(x)\,dx.
\]
By the fundamental theorem of calculus,
\[
(D^tf)(p)=p(b)-p(a).
\]
Thus $D^tf$ is the linear functional
\[
\boxed{p\longmapsto p(b)-p(a)}.
\]
:::
