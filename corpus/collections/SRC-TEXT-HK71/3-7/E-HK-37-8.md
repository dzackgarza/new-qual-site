::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be the vector space of $n \times n$ matrices over the field $F$.

(a) If $B$ is a fixed $n \times n$ matrix, define a function $f_B$ on $V$ by $f_B(A) = \operatorname{trace}(B^t A)$.
Show that $f_B$ is a linear functional on $V$.

(b) Show that every linear functional on $V$ is of the above form, i.e., is $f_B$ for some $B$.

(c) Show that $B \to f_B$ is an isomorphism of $V$ onto $V^*$.
:::

::: {.solution}
For (a), linearity of trace gives, for $A,C\in V$ and $c\in F$,
\[
f_B(cA+C)=\operatorname{tr}(B^t(cA+C))
=c f_B(A)+f_B(C).
\]
Hence $f_B$ is linear.

For (b), let $E_{ij}$ denote the matrix units. If $f\in V^*$, define $B=(b_{ij})$ by
\[
b_{ij}=f(E_{ij}).
\]
For $A=(a_{ij})$,
\[
\operatorname{tr}(B^tA)=\sum_{i,j}b_{ij}a_{ij}
=\sum_{i,j}a_{ij}f(E_{ij})
=f(A).
\]
Thus $f=f_B$.

For (c), the map
\[
\Phi:V\to V^*,\qquad B\mapsto f_B
\]
is linear by part (a), and it is surjective by part (b). If $f_B=0$, then evaluating on $E_{ij}$ gives
\[
0=f_B(E_{ij})=b_{ij}
\]
for all $i,j$, so $B=0$. Hence $\Phi$ is injective as well. Therefore $B\mapsto f_B$ is an isomorphism $V\cong V^*$.
:::
