::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional vector space over the field $F$.
Show that $T \to T^t$ is an isomorphism of $L(V, V)$ onto $L(V^*, V^*)$.
:::

::: solution
Choose an ordered basis $\mathcal B$ of $V$ and let $\mathcal B^*$ be its dual basis. If $A=[T]_{\mathcal B}$, then
\[
[T^t]_{\mathcal B^*}=A^t.
\]
Thus, under the standard identifications
\[
L(V,V)\cong M_n(F),\qquad L(V^*,V^*)\cong M_n(F),
\]
the map
\[
T\longmapsto T^t
\]
corresponds exactly to matrix transposition
\[
A\longmapsto A^t.
\]
Matrix transposition is linear and satisfies $(A^t)^t=A$, so it is an isomorphism. Therefore $T\mapsto T^t$ is an isomorphism of $L(V,V)$ onto $L(V^*,V^*)$.
:::
