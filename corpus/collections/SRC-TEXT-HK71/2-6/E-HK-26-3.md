::: {.exercise}
Consider the vectors in $\RR^4$ defined by
$$
\alpha_1 = (-1, 0, 1, 2), \quad \alpha_2 = (3, 4, -2, 5), \quad \alpha_3 = (1, 4, 0, 9).
$$
Find a system of homogeneous linear equations for which the space of solutions is exactly the subspace of $\RR^4$ spanned by the three given vectors.
:::

::: {.solution}

The argument is the card's own: no copy of Hoffman and Kunze and no solution set for it is retained in `tmp/sources`, so nothing here is transcribed. The statement is the exercise the collection index records as Hoffman and Kunze, *Linear Algebra* §2.6 Exercise 3.

Let $\alpha_{1}=(-1,0,1,2)$, $\alpha_{2}=(3,4,-2,5)$, $\alpha_{3}=(1,4,0,9)$ and $W=\operatorname{span}\{\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\alpha_{3}\}\subset\RR^{4}$. A system of homogeneous linear equations is a system $cx=0$ for row vectors $c$; its solution space is the orthogonal complement of the span of those rows, so the system cutting out $W$ is given by a basis of the functionals vanishing on $W$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #rank}
The matrix $A$ with rows $\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\alpha_{3}$ has rank $2$, with row-reduced form
$$
\begin{bmatrix}1&0&-1&-2\\ 0&1&\tfrac14&\tfrac{11}4\\ 0&0&0&0\end{bmatrix},
$$
so $\dim W=2$.

::: pf-proof
Row-reduce $A=\begin{bmatrix}-1&0&1&2\\ 3&4&-2&5\\ 1&4&0&9\end{bmatrix}$: add three times the first row to the second and the first row to the third, then scale the first row by $-1$; the second and third rows become multiples of one another, and clearing gives the displayed matrix. Its first two rows are nonzero and independent, and the third is zero, so the rank is $2$; the row space is unchanged by row operations, so $\dim W=2$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #annihilator}
The functionals vanishing on $W$ are spanned by $c_{1}=(1,-\tfrac14,1,0)$ and $c_{2}=(2,-\tfrac{11}4,0,1)$, equivalently by $4c_{1}=(4,-1,4,0)$ and $4c_{2}=(8,-11,0,4)$.

::: pf-proof
A functional $c=(c_{1},c_{2},c_{3},c_{4})$ vanishes on $W$ exactly when $c\cdot\alpha_{i}=0$ for $i=1,2,3$, that is when $Ac^{T}=0$. By step [](#rank){.pf-ref} the row-reduced form has pivots in columns $1$ and $2$, so the solutions are parametrised by $x_{3}=s$ and $x_{4}=t$ with $x_{1}=s+2t$ and $x_{2}=-\tfrac14 s-\tfrac{11}4 t$; the two choices $(s,t)=(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$ give the displayed functionals, which are independent. Since the null space of a rank-$2$ matrix of size $3\times4$ has dimension $4-2=2$, they span it.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #vanish}
Both functionals vanish at each of $\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\alpha_{3}$.

::: pf-proof
$4x_{1}-x_{2}+4x_{3}$ at $\alpha_{1}$ is $-4-0+4=0$, at $\alpha_{2}$ is $12-4-8=0$, at $\alpha_{3}$ is $4-4+0=0$. $8x_{1}-11x_{2}+4x_{4}$ at $\alpha_{1}$ is $-8-0+8=0$, at $\alpha_{2}$ is $24-44+20=0$, at $\alpha_{3}$ is $8-44+36=0$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #equal}
The solution space of the system
$$
4x_{1}-x_{2}+4x_{3}=0,\qquad 8x_{1}-11x_{2}+4x_{4}=0
$$
is exactly $W$.

::: pf-proof
The two equations have coefficient matrix with rows $4c_{1}$ and $4c_{2}$, which are independent by step [](#annihilator){.pf-ref}, so the solution space has dimension $4-2=2$. By step [](#vanish){.pf-ref} it contains $\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\alpha_{3}$, hence contains $W$, which has dimension $2$ by step [](#rank){.pf-ref}; a $2$-dimensional subspace of a $2$-dimensional space is the whole space, so the solution space equals $W$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step}
A system of homogeneous linear equations whose solution space is exactly $W$ is $4x_{1}-x_{2}+4x_{3}=0$, $8x_{1}-11x_{2}+4x_{4}=0$.

::: pf-proof
Step [](#equal){.pf-ref} gives the system and the equality of its solution space with $W$; by step [](#annihilator){.pf-ref} two equations are necessary, since $\dim W=2$ forces the coefficient matrix to have rank $4-2=2$.
:::
