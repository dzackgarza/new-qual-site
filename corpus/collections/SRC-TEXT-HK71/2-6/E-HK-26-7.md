::: {.exercise}
Let $A$ be an $m \times n$ matrix over the field $F$, and consider the system of equations $AX = Y$.
Prove that this system of equations has a solution if and only if the row rank of $A$ is equal to the row rank of the augmented matrix of the system.
:::


::: {.solution}

The argument is the card's own: no copy of Hoffman and Kunze and no published solution set for it is retained in `tmp/sources`, so nothing below is transcribed. The statement is the exercise the collection index records as Hoffman and Kunze, *Linear Algebra* §2.6 Exercise 7. Here $A$ is $m\times n$ over $F$, $Y$ is a column vector of length $m$, $X$ an unknown column of length $n$, and $[A\mid Y]$ is the $m\times(n+1)$ augmented matrix. Row rank is the dimension of the row space and column rank the dimension of the column space; the two are equal for every matrix.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #fwd}

If $AX=Y$ has a solution, then $\operatorname{colrank}[A\mid Y]=\operatorname{colrank}A$.

::: pf-proof

The columns of $AX$ are the linear combinations of the columns of $A$ with the coefficients read off $X$, so $Y=AX$ lies in the column space of $A$; hence the column space of $[A\mid Y]$, which is spanned by the columns of $A$ together with $Y$, equals that of $A$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #fwdrow}

If $AX=Y$ has a solution, then $\operatorname{rowrank}[A\mid Y]=\operatorname{rowrank}A$.

::: pf-proof

Step [](#fwd){.pf-ref} gives equality of column ranks; by equality of row and column rank in each of the two matrices, the row ranks agree.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #back}

If $\operatorname{rowrank}[A\mid Y]=\operatorname{rowrank}A$, then $Y$ lies in the column space of $A$.

::: pf-proof

By equality of row and column rank, $\operatorname{colrank}[A\mid Y]=\operatorname{colrank}A$. The column space of $A$ is contained in that of $[A\mid Y]$, and equal finite dimensions force equality of the subspaces; so $Y$, a column of $[A\mid Y]$, lies in the column space of $A$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #backsol}

If $\operatorname{rowrank}[A\mid Y]=\operatorname{rowrank}A$, then $AX=Y$ has a solution.

::: pf-proof

By step [](#back){.pf-ref} there are $x_1,\dots,x_n\in F$ with $Y=\sum_j x_j A_j$, $A_j$ the $j$-th column of $A$; the column $X=(x_1,\dots,x_n)^t$ then satisfies $AX=Y$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #done}

$AX=Y$ has a solution if and only if the row ranks of $A$ and $[A\mid Y]$ are equal.

::: pf-proof

One implication is step [](#fwdrow){.pf-ref}, the other step [](#backsol){.pf-ref}.

:::
