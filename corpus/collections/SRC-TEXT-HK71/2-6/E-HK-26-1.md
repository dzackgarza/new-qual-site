::: {.exercise}
Let $s < n$ and $A$ an $s \times n$ matrix with entries in the field $F$.
Use Theorem 4 (not its proof) to show that there is a non-zero $X$ in $F^{n \times 1}$ such that $AX = 0$.
:::


::: solution
The linear map
\[
T:F^n\longrightarrow F^s,
\qquad T(X)=AX,
\]
has rank at most $s$. Since $s<n$, rank-nullity gives
\[
\dim\ker T=n-\operatorname{rank}T\ge n-s>0.
\]
Therefore $\ker T$ contains a nonzero vector $X$. For that vector,
\[
AX=T(X)=0.
\]
:::
