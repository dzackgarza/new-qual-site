::: {.exercise}
Let $s < n$ and $A$ an $s \times n$ matrix with entries in the field $F$.
Use Theorem 4 (not its proof) to show that there is a non-zero $X$ in $F^{n \times 1}$ such that $AX = 0$.
:::
