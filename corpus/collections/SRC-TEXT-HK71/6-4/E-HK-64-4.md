::: {.exercise}
Let
\[
A = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 2 & -2 & 2 \\ 2 & -3 & 2 \end{bmatrix}.
\]
Is $A$ similar over the field of real numbers to a triangular matrix?
If so, find such a triangular matrix.
:::
