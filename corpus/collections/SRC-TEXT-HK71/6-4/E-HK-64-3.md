::: {.exercise}
Let $c$ be a characteristic value of $T$ and let $W$ be the space of characteristic vectors associated with the characteristic value $c$.
What is the restriction operator $T_W$?
:::

::: {.solution}
By definition,
\[
W=\ker(T-cI).
\]
Thus every \(w\in W\) satisfies \(Tw=cw\). Therefore the restriction is simply
\[
T_W=cI_W.
\]
:::
