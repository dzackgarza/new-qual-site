::: {.exercise}
Let $T$ be a linear operator on a finite-dimensional vector space over an algebraically closed field $F$.
Let $f$ be a polynomial over $F$.
Prove that $c$ is a characteristic value of $f(T)$ if and only if $c = f(t)$, where $t$ is a characteristic value of $T$.
:::
