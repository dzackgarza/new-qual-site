::: {.exercise}
Let $W$ be an invariant subspace for $T$.
Prove that the minimal polynomial for the restriction operator $T_W$ divides the minimal polynomial for $T$, without referring to matrices.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let \(m_T\) be the minimal polynomial of \(T\). Since \(m_T(T)=0\), for every \(w\in W\) we have
\[
m_T(T_W)w=m_T(T)w=0,
\]
because \(W\) is invariant and therefore every power of \(T_W\) is the restriction to \(W\) of the corresponding power of \(T\). Hence \(m_T\) annihilates \(T_W\). By the defining divisibility property of the minimal polynomial,
\[
m_{T_W}\mid m_T.
\]
No matrices are involved.
:::
