::: {.exercise}
Let $T$ be the linear operator on $\RR^2$, the matrix of which in the standard ordered basis is
\[
A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -1 \\ 2 & 2 \end{bmatrix}.
\]

(a) Prove that the only subspaces of $\RR^2$ invariant under $T$ are $\RR^2$ and the zero subspace.

(b) If $U$ is the linear operator on $C^2$, the matrix of which in the standard ordered basis is $A$, show that $U$ has 1-dimensional invariant subspaces.
:::
