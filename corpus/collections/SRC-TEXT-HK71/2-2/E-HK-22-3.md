::: {.exercise}
Is the vector $(3, -1, 0, -1)$ in the subspace of $\RR^4$ spanned by the vectors $(2, -1, 3, 2)$, $(-1, 1, 1, -3)$, and $(1, 1, 9, -5)$?
:::


::: solution
Suppose
\[
a(2,-1,3,2)+b(-1,1,1,-3)+c(1,1,9,-5)=(3,-1,0,-1).
\]
The first three coordinates give
\[
2a-b+c=3,
\]
\[
-a+b+c=-1,
\]
\[
3a+b+9c=0.
\]
Solving these yields
\[
a=1,\qquad b=0,\qquad c=1.
\]
But then the fourth coordinate of the linear combination is
\[
2a-3b-5c=2-5=-3,
\]
whereas the target fourth coordinate is $-1$. This contradiction shows that $(3,-1,0,-1)$ is not in the span of the three given vectors.
:::
