::: {.exercise}
Is the vector $(3, -1, 0, -1)$ in the subspace of $\RR^4$ spanned by the vectors $(2, -1, 3, 2)$, $(-1, 1, 1, -3)$, and $(1, 1, 9, -5)$?
:::


::: {.solution}
Suppose
$$
a(2,-1,3,2)+b(-1,1,1,-3)+c(1,1,9,-5)=(3,-1,0,-1).
$$
The first three coordinates give
$$
2a-b+c=3,
$$
$$
-a+b+c=-1,
$$
$$
3a+b+9c=0.
$$
Adding the first two equations gives
$$
a+2c=2.
$$
The second equation gives $b=a-c-1$; substituting into the third gives $4a+8c-1=0$, that is,
$$
a+2c=\frac14.
$$
These two conditions are incompatible, so already the first three coordinates admit no solution. Hence $(3,-1,0,-1)$ is not in the span of the three given vectors.
:::
