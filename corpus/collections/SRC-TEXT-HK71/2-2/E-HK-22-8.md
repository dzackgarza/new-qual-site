::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be the vector space of all functions from $\RR$ into $\RR$; let $V_e$ be the subset of even functions, $f(-x) = f(x)$; let $V_o$ be the subset of odd functions, $f(-x) = -f(x)$.

(a) Prove that $V_e$ and $V_o$ are subspaces of $V$.

(b) Prove that $V_e + V_o = V$.

(c) Prove that $V_e \cap V_o = \{0\}$.
:::


::: solution
<1>1. $V_e$ and $V_o$ are subspaces of $V$.
::: proof
The zero function is both even and odd. If $f,g$ are even and $a,b\in\RR$, then
\[
(af+bg)(-x)=af(-x)+bg(-x)=af(x)+bg(x),
\]
so $af+bg$ is even. If $f,g$ are odd, then
\[
(af+bg)(-x)=-af(x)-bg(x)=-(af+bg)(x),
\]
so $af+bg$ is odd.
:::

<1>2. $V_e+V_o=V$.
::: proof
For $f\in V$, define
\[
f_e(x)=\frac{f(x)+f(-x)}2,
\qquad
f_o(x)=\frac{f(x)-f(-x)}2.
\]
Then $f_e$ is even, $f_o$ is odd, and
\[
f=f_e+f_o.
\]
Thus every function belongs to $V_e+V_o$.
:::

<1>3. $V_e\cap V_o=\{0\}$.
::: proof
If $f$ is both even and odd, then for every $x$,
\[
f(x)=f(-x)=-f(x).
\]
Hence $2f(x)=0$, so over $\RR$ one has $f(x)=0$ for all $x$. Thus $f=0$.
:::
:::
