::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be the vector space of all functions from $\RR$ into $\RR$; let $V_e$ be the subset of even functions, $f(-x) = f(x)$; let $V_o$ be the subset of odd functions, $f(-x) = -f(x)$.

(a) Prove that $V_e$ and $V_o$ are subspaces of $V$.

(b) Prove that $V_e + V_o = V$.

(c) Prove that $V_e \cap V_o = \{0\}$.
:::
