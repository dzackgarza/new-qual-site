::: {.exercise}
Which of the following sets of vectors $\alpha = (a_1, \ldots, a_n)$ in $\RR^n$ ($n \geq 3$) are subspaces of $\RR^n$?

(a) all $\alpha$ such that $a_1 \geq 0$;

(b) all $\alpha$ such that $a_1 + 3a_2 = a_3$;

(c) all $\alpha$ such that $a_2 = a_1^2$;

(d) all $\alpha$ such that $a_1 a_2 = 0$;

(e) all $\alpha$ such that $a_2$ is rational.
:::
