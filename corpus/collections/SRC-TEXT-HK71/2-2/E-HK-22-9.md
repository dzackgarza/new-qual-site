::: {.exercise}
Let $W_1$ and $W_2$ be subspaces of a vector space $V$ such that $W_1 + W_2 = V$ and $W_1 \cap W_2 = \{0\}$.
Prove that for each vector $\alpha$ in $V$ there are unique vectors $\alpha_1$ in $W_1$ and $\alpha_2$ in $W_2$ such that $\alpha = \alpha_1 + \alpha_2$.
:::
