::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be the (real) vector space of all functions $f$ from $\RR$ into $\RR$.
Which of the following sets of functions are subspaces of $V$?

(a) all $f$ such that $f(x^2) = f(x)^2$;

(b) all $f$ such that $f(0) = f(1)$;

(c) all $f$ such that $f(3) = 1 + f(-5)$;

(d) all $f$ such that $f(-1) = 0$;

(e) all $f$ which are continuous.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. $V$ vector space.
Proof: basis.

<1>2. Q.E.D.
Proof: <1>1.
:::
