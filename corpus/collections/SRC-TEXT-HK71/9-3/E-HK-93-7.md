::: {.exercise}
Give an example of an $n \times n$ matrix which has all its principal minors positive, but which is not a positive matrix.
:::

::: solution
Take
$$
A=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&1\\
0&1
\end{pmatrix}.
$$
Its principal $1\times1$ minors are both equal to $1$, and its determinant is
$$
\det A=1.
$$
Thus every principal minor is positive.

However,
$$
A^*=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&0\\
1&1
\end{pmatrix}
\ne A.
$$
So $A$ is not Hermitian. Since every positive matrix must be Hermitian, $A$ is
not positive.

Hence positivity of all principal minors alone does not imply positivity of a
matrix.
:::
