::: {.exercise}
Prove that every entry on the main diagonal of a positive matrix is positive.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

Let $A$ be a positive matrix, i.e. $A$ is the matrix of a positive (positive-definite) Hermitian form, so $x^* A x > 0$ for all nonzero $x$.

::: pf-proof

definition of a positive matrix.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Let $e_i$ be the $i$-th standard basis vector.

::: pf-proof

choose a basis vector.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

Then $A_{ii} = e_i^* A e_i$.

::: pf-proof

the $(i,i)$-entry of $A$ is $e_i^* A e_i$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s4}

Since $e_i \neq 0$, $e_i^* A e_i > 0$.

::: pf-proof

Step [](#s1){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s5}

Hence $A_{ii} > 0$ for every $i$.

::: pf-proof

Steps [](#s3){.pf-ref} and [](#s4){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

Step [](#s5){.pf-ref}.
