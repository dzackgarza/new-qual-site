::: {.exercise}
Prove that every entry on the main diagonal of a positive matrix is positive.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. Let $A$ be a positive matrix, i.e. $A$ is the matrix of a positive (positive-definite) Hermitian form, so $x^* A x > 0$ for all nonzero $x$.
::: {.proof}
definition of a positive matrix.
:::

<1>2. Let $e_i$ be the $i$-th standard basis vector.
::: {.proof}
choose a basis vector.
:::

<1>3. Then $A_{ii} = e_i^* A e_i$.
::: {.proof}
the $(i,i)$-entry of $A$ is $e_i^* A e_i$.
:::

<1>4. Since $e_i \neq 0$, $e_i^* A e_i > 0$.
::: {.proof}
<1>1.
:::

<1>5. Hence $A_{ii} > 0$ for every $i$.
::: {.proof}
<1>3 and <1>4.
:::

<1>6. Q.E.D.
::: {.proof}
<1>5.
:::
:::
