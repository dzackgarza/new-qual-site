::: {.exercise}
Use the classical adjoint formula to compute the inverses of each of the following $3 \times 3$ real matrices.
\[
\begin{bmatrix} -2 & 3 & 2 \\ 6 & 0 & 3 \\ 4 & 1 & -1 \end{bmatrix}, \qquad \begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta & 0 & -\sin\theta \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ \sin\theta & 0 & \cos\theta \end{bmatrix}
\]
:::

::: {.solution}
For
\[
A=\begin{bmatrix}-2&3&2\\6&0&3\\4&1&-1\end{bmatrix}
\]
one computes $\det A=72$ and the cofactor matrix
\[
C=\begin{bmatrix}-3&18&6\\5&-6&14\\9&18&-18\end{bmatrix}.
\]
Hence
\[
A^{-1}=\frac1{72}C^t
=\begin{bmatrix}-1/24&5/72&1/8\\1/4&-1/12&1/4\\1/12&7/36&-1/4\end{bmatrix}.
\]

For
\[
R=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta&0&-\sin\theta\\0&1&0\\\sin\theta&0&\cos\theta\end{bmatrix},
\]
$\det R=\cos^2\theta+\sin^2\theta=1$. Its classical adjoint is
\[
\operatorname{adj}R=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta&0&\sin\theta\\0&1&0\\-\sin\theta&0&\cos\theta\end{bmatrix},
\]
so
\[
R^{-1}=\operatorname{adj}R=R^t.
\]
:::
