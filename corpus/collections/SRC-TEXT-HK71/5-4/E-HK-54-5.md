::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 5.4.5"}
An $n \times n$ matrix $A$ over the field of complex numbers is said to be unitary if $AA^* = I$ ($A^*$ denotes the conjugate transpose of $A$). If $A$ is unitary, show that $|\det A| = 1$.
:::
