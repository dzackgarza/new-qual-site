::: {.exercise}
Let $A$ be an $n \times n$ matrix over the field $F$.
Prove that there are at most $n$ distinct scalars $c$ in $F$ such that $\det(cI - A) = 0$.
:::

::: {.solution}
The function
\[
p(x)=\det(xI-A)
\]
is the characteristic polynomial of $A$. It is a monic polynomial of degree $n$, hence is nonzero. Over a field, a nonzero polynomial of degree $n$ has at most $n$ distinct roots. Therefore there are at most $n$ scalars $c\in F$ satisfying
\[
\det(cI-A)=p(c)=0.
\]
:::
