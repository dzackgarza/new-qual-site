::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be the vector space of $n \times n$ matrices over the field $F$.
Let $B$ be a fixed element of $V$ and let $T_B$ be the linear operator on $V$ defined by $T_B(A) = AB - BA$.
Show that $\det T_B = 0$.
:::
