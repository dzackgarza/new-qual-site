::: {.exercise}
An $n \times n$ matrix $A$ over a field $F$ is called orthogonal if $AA^t = I$.
If $A$ is orthogonal, show that $\det A = \pm 1$.
Give an example of an orthogonal matrix for which $\det A = -1$.
:::
