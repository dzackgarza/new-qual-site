::: {.exercise}
An $n \times n$ matrix $A$ over a field $F$ is called orthogonal if $AA^t = I$.
If $A$ is orthogonal, show that $\det A = \pm 1$.
Give an example of an orthogonal matrix for which $\det A = -1$.
:::

::: {.solution}
From $AA^t=I$ and determinant multiplicativity,
\[
1=\det I=\det(A)\det(A^t)=(\det A)^2.
\]
Hence $\det A=\pm1$.

For example,
\[
A=\operatorname{diag}(-1,1,\ldots,1)
\]
is orthogonal and has determinant $-1$.
:::
