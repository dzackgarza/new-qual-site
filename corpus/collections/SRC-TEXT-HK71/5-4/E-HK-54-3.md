::: {.exercise}
An $n \times n$ matrix $A$ over a field $F$ is skew-symmetric if $A^t = -A$.
If $A$ is a skew-symmetric $n \times n$ matrix with complex entries and $n$ is odd, prove that $\det A = 0$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Since $A^t=-A$,
\[
\det A=\det(A^t)=\det(-A)=(-1)^n\det A=-\det A
\]
because $n$ is odd. Over $\mathbb C$ this implies $2\det A=0$, hence $\det A=0$.
:::
