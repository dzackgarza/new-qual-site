::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 2.1.5"}
On $\RR^n$, define two operations
\[
\alpha \oplus \beta = \alpha - \beta, \qquad c \cdot \alpha = -c\alpha.
\]
The operations on the right are the usual ones.
Which of the axioms for a vector space are satisfied by $(\RR^n, \oplus, \cdot)$?
:::
