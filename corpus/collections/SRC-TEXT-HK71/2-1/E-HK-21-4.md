::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be the set of all pairs $(x, y)$ of real numbers, and let $F$ be the field of real numbers.
Define
\[
(x, y) + (x_1, y_1) = (x + x_1,\; y + y_1), \qquad c(x, y) = (cx,\; y).
\]
Is $V$, with these operations, a vector space over the field of real numbers?
:::
