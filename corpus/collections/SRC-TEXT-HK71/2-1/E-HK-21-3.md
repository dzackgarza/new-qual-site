::: {.exercise}
If $C$ is the field of complex numbers, which vectors in $C^3$ are linear combinations of $(1, 0, -1)$, $(0, 1, 1)$, and $(1, 1, 1)$?
:::


::: solution
Put the three vectors as columns:
\[
M=
\begin{bmatrix}
1&0&1\\
0&1&1\\
-1&1&1
\end{bmatrix}.
\]
Its determinant is
\[
\det M=1.
\]
Hence the columns are linearly independent. Since there are three of them in the three-dimensional space $\CC^3$, they form a basis. Therefore every vector in $\CC^3$ is a linear combination of the three given vectors.
:::
