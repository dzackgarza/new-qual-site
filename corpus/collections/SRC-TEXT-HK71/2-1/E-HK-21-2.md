::: {.exercise}
If $V$ is a vector space over the field $F$, verify that
$$
(\alpha_1 + \alpha_2) + (\alpha_3 + \alpha_4) = [\alpha_2 + (\alpha_3 + \alpha_1)] + \alpha_4
$$
for all vectors $\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3$, and $\alpha_4$ in $V$.
:::


::: {.solution}
No retained solution set covers this exercise; the argument is the card's own.

The axioms of a vector space over $F$ used below are associativity of addition, $(\alpha+\beta)+\gamma=\alpha+(\beta+\gamma)$, commutativity, $\alpha+\beta=\beta+\alpha$, and the fact that a sum of more than two terms may be re-associated and re-ordered freely, since these two laws allow it.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #left}
$(\alpha_{1}+\alpha_{2})+(\alpha_{3}+\alpha_{4})=\alpha_{1}+\alpha_{2}+\alpha_{3}+\alpha_{4}$.

::: pf-proof
Write the sum without parentheses from two applications of associativity: $(\alpha_{1}+\alpha_{2})+(\alpha_{3}+\alpha_{4})$ equals $\bigl((\alpha_{1}+\alpha_{2})+\alpha_{3}\bigr)+\alpha_{4}$, which equals $\alpha_{1}+\alpha_{2}+\alpha_{3}+\alpha_{4}$ read left to right.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #right}
$[\alpha_{2}+(\alpha_{3}+\alpha_{1})]+\alpha_{4}=\alpha_{1}+\alpha_{2}+\alpha_{3}+\alpha_{4}$.

::: pf-proof
By associativity $[\alpha_{2}+(\alpha_{3}+\alpha_{1})]+\alpha_{4}=\alpha_{2}+\alpha_{3}+\alpha_{1}+\alpha_{4}$, and commutativity inside that sum turns $\alpha_{2}+\alpha_{3}+\alpha_{1}$ into $\alpha_{1}+\alpha_{2}+\alpha_{3}$; so the right side is $\alpha_{1}+\alpha_{2}+\alpha_{3}+\alpha_{4}$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step}
$(\alpha_{1}+\alpha_{2})+(\alpha_{3}+\alpha_{4})=[\alpha_{2}+(\alpha_{3}+\alpha_{1})]+\alpha_{4}$.

::: pf-proof
Both sides equal $\alpha_{1}+\alpha_{2}+\alpha_{3}+\alpha_{4}$ by steps [](#left){.pf-ref} and [](#right){.pf-ref}, so by transitivity of equality they are equal to each other, which is the identity the exercise asks for. No property beyond associativity and commutativity of vector addition is used.
:::
