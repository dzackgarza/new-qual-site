::: {.exercise}
If $V$ is a vector space over the field $F$, verify that
\[
(\alpha_1 + \alpha_2) + (\alpha_3 + \alpha_4) = [\alpha_2 + (\alpha_3 + \alpha_1)] + \alpha_4
\]
for all vectors $\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3$, and $\alpha_4$ in $V$.
:::
