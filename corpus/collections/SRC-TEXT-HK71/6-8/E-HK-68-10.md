::: {.exercise}
Let $T$ be a linear operator on the finite-dimensional space $V$, let $p = p_1^{r_1} \cdots p_k^{r_k}$ be the minimal polynomial for $T$, and let $V = W_1 \oplus \cdots \oplus W_k$ be the primary decomposition for $T$, i.e., $W_i$ is the null space of $p_i(T)^{r_i}$.
Let $W$ be any subspace of $V$ which is invariant under $T$.
Prove that
\[
W = (W \cap W_1) \oplus (W \cap W_2) \oplus \cdots \oplus (W \cap W_k).
\]
:::

::: solution
Let
$$
q_i(x)=p_i(x)^{r_i},
\qquad
p(x)=q_1(x)\cdots q_k(x).
$$
The $q_i$ are pairwise relatively prime. By the polynomial Chinese remainder
theorem, there are polynomials $e_i(x)$ such that
$$
e_i\equiv1\pmod{q_i},
\qquad
e_i\equiv0\pmod{q_j}\quad(j\ne i),
$$
and
$$
e_1+\cdots+e_k\equiv1\pmod p
$$
[@HK71]. Put
$$
E_i=e_i(T).
$$
These are the primary projections: $E_i$ has range $W_i$, the operators
$E_i$ are pairwise annihilating, and
$$
E_1+\cdots+E_k=I.
$$

<1>1. Each primary component of a vector in $W$ still lies in $W$.
::: proof
Since $W$ is invariant under $T$, it is invariant under every power of $T$,
hence under every polynomial in $T$. Thus
$$
E_i(W)\subseteq W
$$
for every $i$. Also $\operatorname{range}E_i=W_i$, so
$$
E_i(W)\subseteq W\cap W_i.
$$
For any $w\in W$,
$$
w=(E_1+\cdots+E_k)w
=E_1w+\cdots+E_kw,
$$
and each summand lies in $W\cap W_i$. Hence
$$
W\subseteq (W\cap W_1)+\cdots+(W\cap W_k).
$$
The reverse inclusion is immediate because every $W\cap W_i$ is contained in
$W$.
:::

<1>2. The sum of the intersections is direct.
::: proof
The primary decomposition
$$
V=W_1\oplus\cdots\oplus W_k
$$
is direct. Therefore if
$$
w_1+\cdots+w_k=0,
\qquad
w_i\in W\cap W_i,
$$
then in particular $w_i\in W_i$, so directness in $V$ forces every $w_i=0$.
Thus the sum in step <1>1 is direct, and
$$
\boxed{
W=(W\cap W_1)\oplus\cdots\oplus(W\cap W_k).}
$$
:::
:::
