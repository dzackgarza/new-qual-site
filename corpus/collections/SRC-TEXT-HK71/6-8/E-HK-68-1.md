::: {.exercise}
Let $T$ be a linear operator on $\RR^3$ which is represented in the standard ordered basis by the matrix
\[
\begin{bmatrix} 6 & -3 & -2 \\ 4 & -1 & -2 \\ 10 & -5 & -3 \end{bmatrix}.
\]
Express the minimal polynomial $p$ for $T$ in the form $p = p_1p_2$, where $p_1$ and $p_2$ are monic and irreducible over the field of real numbers.
Let $W_i$ be the null space of $p_i(T)$.
Find bases $B_i$ for the spaces $W_1$ and $W_2$.
If $T_i$ is the operator induced on $W_i$ by $T$, find the matrix of $T_i$ in the basis $B_i$ (above).
:::
