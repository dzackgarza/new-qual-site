::: {.exercise}
If $V$ is the space of all polynomials of degree less than or equal to $n$ over a field $F$, prove that the differentiation operator on $V$ is nilpotent.
:::

::: solution
Let $D:V\to V$ be differentiation. For a monomial $x^k$ with
$0\le k\le n$,
$$
D^{n+1}(x^k)=0,
$$
because after at most $k+1\le n+1$ differentiations the monomial becomes zero.
Since
$$
1,x,\ldots,x^n
$$
form a basis of $V$, the operator $D^{n+1}$ vanishes on a basis and therefore
$$
D^{n+1}=0.
$$
Hence $D$ is nilpotent [@HK71].

More precisely, if the scalar field has characteristic zero, then
$D^n(x^n)=n!\ne0$, so the nilpotence index is exactly $n+1$; the exercise only
requires nilpotence, which holds over every field.
:::
