::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be the vector space of all functions from $\RR$ into $\RR$ which are continuous, i.e., the space of continuous real-valued functions on the real line.
Let $T$ be the linear operator on $V$ defined by
\[
(Tf)(x) = \int_0^x f(t)\,dt.
\]
Prove that $T$ has no characteristic values.
:::
