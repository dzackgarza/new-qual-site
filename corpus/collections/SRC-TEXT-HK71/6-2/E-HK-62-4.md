::: {.exercise}
Let $T$ be the linear operator on $\RR^3$ which is represented in the standard ordered basis by the matrix
\[
\begin{bmatrix} -9 & 4 & 4 \\ -8 & 3 & 4 \\ -16 & 8 & 7 \end{bmatrix}.
\]
Prove that $T$ is diagonalizable by exhibiting a basis for $\RR^3$, each vector of which is a characteristic vector of $T$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let
\[
A=\begin{bmatrix}-9&4&4\\-8&3&4\\-16&8&7\end{bmatrix}.
\]
Its characteristic polynomial is
\[
\chi_A(x)=(x-3)(x+1)^2.
\]
For $\lambda=-1$, direct multiplication shows
\[
A(1,2,0)^t=-(1,2,0)^t,
\qquad
A(1,0,2)^t=-(1,0,2)^t.
\]
For $\lambda=3$,
\[
A(1,1,2)^t=3(1,1,2)^t.
\]
The three vectors
\[
v_1=(1,2,0),\qquad v_2=(1,0,2),\qquad v_3=(1,1,2)
\]
are linearly independent, since
\[
\det\begin{bmatrix}1&1&1\\2&0&1\\0&2&2\end{bmatrix}=-2\ne0.
\]
Therefore they form a basis of $\mathbb R^3$ consisting of characteristic vectors, so $T$ is diagonalizable.
:::
