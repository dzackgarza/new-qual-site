::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be an $n$-dimensional vector space over $F$.
What is the characteristic polynomial of the identity operator on $V$?
What is the characteristic polynomial for the zero operator?
:::

::: {.solution}
If $I_V$ is the identity operator, then in any ordered basis its matrix is $I_n$. Hence
\[
\chi_{I_V}(x)=\det(xI_n-I_n)=\det((x-1)I_n)=(x-1)^n.
\]
For the zero operator, the matrix is $0$, so
\[
\chi_0(x)=\det(xI_n)=x^n.
\]
:::
