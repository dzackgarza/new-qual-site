::: {.exercise}
Let $A$ and $B$ be $n \times n$ matrices over the field $F$.
Prove that if $(I - AB)$ is invertible, then $I - BA$ is invertible and
\[
(I - BA)^{-1} = I + B(I - AB)^{-1}A.
\]
:::

::: {.solution}
Set $C=I+B(I-AB)^{-1}A$. Since $(I-BA)B=B(I-AB)$,
\[
(I-BA)C=I-BA+B(I-AB)(I-AB)^{-1}A=I.
\]
Also $A(I-BA)=(I-AB)A$, so
\[
C(I-BA)=I-BA+B(I-AB)^{-1}(I-AB)A=I.
\]
Thus $C=(I-BA)^{-1}$.
:::
