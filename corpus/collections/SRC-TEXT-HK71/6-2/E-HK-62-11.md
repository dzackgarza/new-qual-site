::: {.exercise}
Let $N$ be a $2 \times 2$ complex matrix such that $N^2 = 0$.
Prove that either $N = 0$ or $N$ is similar over $C$ to
\[
\begin{bmatrix} 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 0 \end{bmatrix}.
\]
:::
