::: {.exercise}
Let $N$ be a $2 \times 2$ complex matrix such that $N^2 = 0$.
Prove that either $N = 0$ or $N$ is similar over $C$ to
\[
\begin{bmatrix} 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 0 \end{bmatrix}.
\]
:::

::: {.solution}
Assume $N\ne0$. Choose $v$ with $Nv\ne0$ and put $w=Nv$. Then $Nw=N^2v=0$. The vectors $v,w$ are independent, since $v=\lambda w$ would imply $Nv=\lambda Nw=0$. In the ordered basis $(v,w)$,
\[
Nv=w,\qquad Nw=0,
\]
so the matrix of $N$ is
\[
\begin{bmatrix}0&0\\1&0\end{bmatrix}.
\]
:::
