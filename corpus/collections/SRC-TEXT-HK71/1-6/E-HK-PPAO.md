::: {.exercise}
Let

$$
A = \left[ \begin{array}{c c c} 5 & 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 5 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 5 \end{array} \right].
$$

For which $X$ does there exist a scalar $c$ such that $AX = cX$ ?
:::
