::: {.exercise}
Let

$$
A = \left[ \begin{array}{r r r r} 1 & 2 & 1 & 0 \\ - 1 & 0 & 3 & 5 \\ 1 & - 2 & 1 & 1 \end{array} \right].
$$

Find a row-reduced echelon matrix R which is row-equivalent to A and an invertible $3 \times 3$ matrix P such that R = PA.
:::
