::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be the vector space of all $2 \times 2$ matrices over the field $F$.
Let $W_1$ be the set of matrices of the form
\[
\begin{bmatrix} x & -x \\ y & z \end{bmatrix}
\]
and let $W_2$ be the set of matrices of the form
\[
\begin{bmatrix} a & b \\ -a & c \end{bmatrix}.
\]

(a) Prove that $W_1$ and $W_2$ are subspaces of $V$.

(b) Find the dimensions of $W_1$, $W_2$, $W_1 + W_2$, and $W_1 \cap W_2$.
:::


::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: pf-step
$W_1$ and $W_2$ are subspaces of $V$.

::: pf-proof
One has
\[
W_1=\left\{
x\begin{bmatrix}1&-1\\0&0\end{bmatrix}
+y\begin{bmatrix}0&0\\1&0\end{bmatrix}
+z\begin{bmatrix}0&0\\0&1\end{bmatrix}:x,y,z\in F
\right\},
\]
so $W_1$ is a span. Likewise
\[
W_2=\left\{
a\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\-1&0\end{bmatrix}
+b\begin{bmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{bmatrix}
+c\begin{bmatrix}0&0\\0&1\end{bmatrix}:a,b,c\in F
\right\}.
\]
Hence both are subspaces.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
$\dim W_1=\dim W_2=3$.

::: pf-proof
The three displayed spanning matrices for each space have disjoint independent coordinate parameters, hence are linearly independent.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
The intersection is
\[
W_1\cap W_2
=\left\{
\begin{bmatrix}x&-x\\-x&z\end{bmatrix}:x,z\in F
\right\},
\]
so $\dim(W_1\cap W_2)=2$.

::: pf-proof
A matrix lies in both spaces exactly when
\[
\begin{bmatrix}x&-x\\y&z\end{bmatrix}
=\begin{bmatrix}a&b\\-a&c\end{bmatrix}.
\]
Comparing entries gives $a=x$, $b=-x$, $y=-x$, and $c=z$, with $x,z$ free.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
$W_1+W_2=V$, so $\dim(W_1+W_2)=4$.

::: pf-proof
The dimension formula gives
\[
\dim(W_1+W_2)=3+3-2=4.
\]
Since $V=M_2(F)$ has dimension $4$, the subspace $W_1+W_2$ must equal $V$.
:::
