::: {.exercise}
Prove that the space of all $m \times n$ matrices over the field $F$ has dimension $mn$, by exhibiting a basis for this space.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: pf-step
Let $E_{ij}$ be matrix with $1$ in $(i,j)$ and $0$ elsewhere.

::: pf-proof
definition.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #e-ij-span}
$\{E_{ij}:1\le i\le m,1\le j\le n\}$ spans.

::: pf-proof
any matrix $A=(a_{ij})=\sum a_{ij}E_{ij}$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #e-ij-independent}
They are linearly independent: $\sum c_{ij}E_{ij}=0$ implies $c_{ij}=0$.

::: pf-proof
entrywise.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #e-ij-basis}
Hence basis, dimension $mn$.

::: pf-proof
Steps [](#e-ij-span){.pf-ref} and [](#e-ij-independent){.pf-ref}.
:::
