::: {.exercise}
Prove that the space of all $m \times n$ matrices over the field $F$ has dimension $mn$, by exhibiting a basis for this space.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. Let $E_{ij}$ be matrix with $1$ in $(i,j)$ and $0$ elsewhere.
Proof: definition.

<1>2. $\{E_{ij}:1\le i\le m,1\le j\le n\}$ spans.
Proof: any matrix $A=(a_{ij})=\sum a_{ij}E_{ij}$.

<1>3. They are linearly independent: $\sum c_{ij}E_{ij}=0$ implies $c_{ij}=0$.
Proof: entrywise.

<1>4. Hence basis, dimension $mn$.
Proof: <1>2 and <1>3.

<1>5. Q.E.D.
Proof: <1>4.
:::
