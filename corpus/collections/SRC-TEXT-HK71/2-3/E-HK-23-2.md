::: {.exercise}
Are the vectors
\[
\alpha_1 = (1, 1, 2, 4), \quad \alpha_2 = (2, -1, -5, 2),
\]
\[
\alpha_3 = (1, -1, -4, 0), \quad \alpha_4 = (2, 1, 1, 6)
\]
linearly independent in $\RR^4$?
:::


::: solution
They are linearly dependent. Indeed,
\[
\frac13\alpha_1-rac23\alpha_2+\alpha_3=0.
\]
Equivalently,
\[
\alpha_1-2\alpha_2+3\alpha_3=0.
\]
The coefficients are not all zero, so the four vectors are not linearly independent.
:::
