::: {.exercise}

\[
\alpha_1 = (1, 1, 2, 4), \quad \alpha_2 = (2, -1, -5, 2),
\]
\[
\alpha_3 = (1, -1, -4, 0), \quad \alpha_4 = (2, 1, 1, 6)
\]
linearly independent in $\RR^4$?
:::
