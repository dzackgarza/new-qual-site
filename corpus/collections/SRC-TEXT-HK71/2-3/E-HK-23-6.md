::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be the vector space of all $2 \times 2$ matrices over the field $F$.
Prove that $V$ has dimension 4 by exhibiting a basis for $V$ which has four elements.
:::


::: solution
Let
\[
E_{11}=\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{bmatrix},\quad
E_{12}=\begin{bmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{bmatrix},\quad
E_{21}=\begin{bmatrix}0&0\\1&0\end{bmatrix},\quad
E_{22}=\begin{bmatrix}0&0\\0&1\end{bmatrix}.
\]
Every matrix
\[
A=\begin{bmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{bmatrix}
\]
has the unique expression
\[
A=aE_{11}+bE_{12}+cE_{21}+dE_{22}.
\]
Thus the four matrix units span $V$ and are linearly independent. They form a basis, so
\[
\dim_F V=4.
\]
:::
