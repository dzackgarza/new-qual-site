::: {.exercise}
Discuss Exercise 9 when $V$ is a vector space over the field with two elements described in Exercise 5, Section 1.1.
:::


::: solution
Let the scalar field be $\FF_2$, and let $\alpha,\beta,\gamma$ be linearly independent. Then
\[
(\alpha+\beta)+(\beta+\gamma)+(\gamma+\alpha)
=2\alpha+2\beta+2\gamma=0
\]
because $2=0$ in $\FF_2$. The three pairwise sums are therefore linearly dependent.

In fact, any two of them remain linearly independent. For example, if
\[
a(\alpha+\beta)+b(\beta+\gamma)=0,
\qquad a,b\in\FF_2,
\]
then
\[
a\alpha+(a+b)\beta+b\gamma=0.
\]
Independence of $\alpha,\beta,\gamma$ gives $a=b=0$. The other pairs are identical by symmetry. Thus Exercise 2.3.9 fails precisely at the three-vector level in characteristic two.
:::
