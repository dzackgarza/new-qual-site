::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 2.3.4"}
Show that the vectors
\[
\alpha_1 = (1, 0, -1), \quad \alpha_2 = (1, 2, 1), \quad \alpha_3 = (0, -3, 2)
\]
form a basis for $\RR^3$.
Express each of the standard basis vectors as linear combinations of $\alpha_1$, $\alpha_2$, and $\alpha_3$.
:::
