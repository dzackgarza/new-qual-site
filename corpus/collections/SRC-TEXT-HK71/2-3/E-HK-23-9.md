::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be a vector space over a subfield $F$ of the complex numbers.
Suppose $\alpha$, $\beta$, and $\gamma$ are linearly independent vectors in $V$.
Prove that $(\alpha + \beta)$, $(\beta + \gamma)$, and $(\gamma + \alpha)$ are linearly independent.
:::


::: solution
Suppose
\[
a(\alpha+\beta)+b(\beta+\gamma)+c(\gamma+\alpha)=0.
\]
Collecting coefficients of the linearly independent vectors $\alpha,\beta,\gamma$ gives
\[
a+c=0,
\qquad
a+b=0,
\qquad
b+c=0.
\]
From the first two equations, $b=c=-a$. Substituting into the third gives
\[
-2a=0.
\]
Because $F$ is a subfield of $\CC$, it has characteristic zero, so $2\ne0$ and hence $a=0$. Therefore $b=c=0$ as well. Thus the three vectors are linearly independent.
:::
