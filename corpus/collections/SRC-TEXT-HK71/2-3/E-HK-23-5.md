::: {.exercise}
Find three vectors in $\RR^3$ which are linearly dependent, and are such that any two of them are linearly independent.
:::


::: solution
Take
\[
v_1=(1,0,0),\qquad v_2=(0,1,0),\qquad v_3=(1,1,0).
\]
They are linearly dependent because
\[
v_1+v_2-v_3=0.
\]
Any two are linearly independent: $v_1,v_2$ are standard basis vectors; $v_3$ is not a scalar multiple of either $v_1$ or $v_2$. Hence every pair is independent while the three-vector set is dependent.
:::
