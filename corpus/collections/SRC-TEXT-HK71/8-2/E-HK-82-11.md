::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional inner product space, and let $\{\alpha_1, \ldots, \alpha_n\}$ be an orthonormal basis for $V$.
Show that for any vectors $\alpha, \beta$ in $V$
\[
(\alpha \mid \beta) = \sum_{k=1}^{n} (\alpha \mid \alpha_k)\overline{(\beta \mid \alpha_k)}.
\]
:::

::: {.solution}
Because $\{\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_n\}$ is an orthonormal basis, every vector
$\alpha$ has the expansion
$$
\alpha=\sum_{k=1}^n(\alpha\mid\alpha_k)\alpha_k
$$
[@HK71]. Likewise,
$$
\beta=\sum_{j=1}^n(\beta\mid\alpha_j)\alpha_j.
$$
Using linearity in the first argument and conjugate linearity in the second,
$$
\begin{aligned}
(\alpha\mid\beta)
&=\sum_{k,j}
(\alpha\mid\alpha_k)
\overline{(\beta\mid\alpha_j)}
(\alpha_k\mid\alpha_j)\\
&=\sum_{k=1}^n
(\alpha\mid\alpha_k)
\overline{(\beta\mid\alpha_k)},
\end{aligned}
$$
because orthonormality gives
$(\alpha_k\mid\alpha_j)=\delta_{kj}$. Hence
$$
\boxed{
(\alpha\mid\beta)
=\sum_{k=1}^n
(\alpha\mid\alpha_k)
\overline{(\beta\mid\alpha_k)}.}
$$
:::
