::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional inner product space, and let $\{\alpha_1, \ldots, \alpha_n\}$ be an orthonormal basis for $V$.
Show that for any vectors $\alpha, \beta$ in $V$
$$
(\alpha \mid \beta) = \sum_{k=1}^{n} (\alpha \mid \alpha_k)\overline{(\beta \mid \alpha_k)}.
$$

:::

::: {.solution}
There is no retained solution set for this exercise and no text version of Hoffman and Kunze in the checkout; the argument is the card's own.

Throughout, $V$ is a finite-dimensional inner product space over $F\subseteq\CC$ with orthonormal basis $\{\alpha_1,\dots,\alpha_n\}$, so $(\alpha_k\mid\alpha_j)=\delta_{kj}$, and the inner product is linear in the first argument and conjugate linear in the second.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #expansion}
Every $\gamma\in V$ equals $\sum_{k}(\gamma\mid\alpha_k)\alpha_k$.

::: pf-proof
$\{\alpha_1,\dots,\alpha_n\}$ is a basis, so $\gamma=\sum_jc_j\alpha_j$ for unique scalars $c_j$. Taking the inner product with $\alpha_k$ and using linearity in the first argument and orthonormality,
$$
(\gamma\mid\alpha_k)=\sum_jc_j(\alpha_j\mid\alpha_k)=c_k,
$$
since $(\alpha_j\mid\alpha_k)=\delta_{jk}$. Hence $c_k=(\gamma\mid\alpha_k)$ for every $k$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #computation}
$(\alpha\mid\beta)=\sum_{k,j}(\alpha\mid\alpha_k)\overline{(\beta\mid\alpha_j)}(\alpha_k\mid\alpha_j)$.

::: pf-proof
By step [](#expansion){.pf-ref}, $\alpha=\sum_k(\alpha\mid\alpha_k)\alpha_k$ and $\beta=\sum_j(\beta\mid\alpha_j)\alpha_j$. Bilinearity — linearity in the first argument and conjugate linearity in the second — gives
$$
\big(\sum_kc_k\alpha_k\mid\sum_jd_j\alpha_j\big)=\sum_{k,j}c_k\overline{d_j}(\alpha_k\mid\alpha_j)
$$
with $c_k=(\alpha\mid\alpha_k)$ and $d_j=(\beta\mid\alpha_j)$; a finite double sum may be rearranged term by term, which is what the display records.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #orthonormal}
The double sum collapses to $\sum_k(\alpha\mid\alpha_k)\overline{(\beta\mid\alpha_k)}$.

::: pf-proof
$(\alpha_k\mid\alpha_j)=\delta_{kj}$ vanishes unless $j=k$, so only the diagonal terms survive in step [](#computation){.pf-ref}, and each surviving term has the form $(\alpha\mid\alpha_k)\overline{(\beta\mid\alpha_k)}$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #conclusion}
$(\alpha\mid\beta)=\sum_{k=1}^{n}(\alpha\mid\alpha_k)\overline{(\beta\mid\alpha_k)}$.

::: pf-proof
Combine steps [](#computation){.pf-ref} and [](#orthonormal){.pf-ref}.

:::
