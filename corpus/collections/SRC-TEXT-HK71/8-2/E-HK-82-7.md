::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be the inner product space consisting of $\RR^2$ and the inner product whose quadratic form is defined by
\[
||(x_1, x_2)||^2 = (x_1 - x_2)^2 + 3x_2^2.
\]
Let $E$ be the orthogonal projection of $V$ onto the subspace $W$ spanned by the vector $(3, 4)$.
Now answer the four questions of Exercise 6.
:::

::: solution
The quadratic form
$$
\|(x_1,x_2)\|^2
=(x_1-x_2)^2+3x_2^2
=x_1^2-2x_1x_2+4x_2^2
$$
has Gram matrix
$$
G=\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\-1&4\end{pmatrix},
$$
so
$$
(x\mid y)=x^tGy.
$$
Let $v=(3,4)$.

<1>1. The orthogonal projection onto $W=\operatorname{span}\{v\}$ is
$$
\boxed{
E(x_1,x_2)
=\frac{-x_1+13x_2}{49}(3,4).}
$$
::: proof
One computes
$$
Gv=(-1,13)^t,
$$
hence
$$
(x\mid v)=-x_1+13x_2.
$$
Also
$$
(v\mid v)=v^tGv=49.
$$
The line-projection formula therefore gives the result [@HK71].
:::

<1>2. In the standard basis,
$$
\boxed{
[E]=\frac1{49}
\begin{pmatrix}-3&39\\-4&52\end{pmatrix}.}
$$
::: proof
This is obtained by expanding the formula in step <1>1 coordinatewise.
:::

<1>3. The orthogonal complement is
$$
\boxed{W^\perp=\operatorname{span}\{(13,1)\}.}
$$
::: proof
The condition $(x\mid v)=0$ is
$$
-x_1+13x_2=0,
$$
whose solution space is the displayed line.
:::

<1>4. An orthonormal basis adapted to $W\oplus W^\perp$ is
$$
\boxed{
\mathcal B=\left(
\frac17(3,4),
\frac1{7\sqrt3}(13,1)
\right).}
$$
::: proof
The first vector has norm $1$ because $(v\mid v)=49$. For
$w=(13,1)$,
$$
\|w\|^2=(13-1)^2+3=147=49\cdot3,
$$
so the second displayed vector also has norm one. Step <1>3 makes them
orthogonal. The projection fixes the first and kills the second, hence
$$
[E]_{\mathcal B}
=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$
:::
:::
