::: {.exercise}
Let $W$ be the subspace of $\RR^2$ spanned by the vector $(3, 4)$.
Using the standard inner product, let $E$ be the orthogonal projection of $\RR^2$ onto $W$.
Find

(a) a formula for $E(x_1, x_2)$;

(b) the matrix of $E$ in the standard ordered basis;

(c) $W^{\perp}$;

(d) an orthonormal basis in which $E$ is represented by the matrix
\[
\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix}.
\]
:::

::: solution
Let
$$
v=(3,4).
$$
Then $W=\operatorname{span}\{v\}$ and
$$
(v\mid v)=25.
$$

<1>1. The orthogonal projection is
$$
\boxed{
E(x_1,x_2)
=\frac{3x_1+4x_2}{25}(3,4).}
$$
::: proof
The orthogonal projection onto a line spanned by a nonzero vector $v$ is
$$
E(x)=\frac{(x\mid v)}{(v\mid v)}v
$$
[@HK71]. Here
$$
(x\mid v)=3x_1+4x_2,
$$
which gives the displayed formula.
:::

<1>2. Its matrix in the standard basis is
$$
\boxed{
[E]=\frac1{25}
\begin{pmatrix}9&12\\12&16\end{pmatrix}.}
$$
::: proof
Expanding step <1>1 gives
$$
E(x_1,x_2)
=\left(
\frac{9x_1+12x_2}{25},
\frac{12x_1+16x_2}{25}
\right),
$$
so the displayed coefficient matrix is immediate.
:::

<1>3. The orthogonal complement is
$$
\boxed{W^\perp=\operatorname{span}\{(-4,3)\}.}
$$
::: proof
A vector $(a,b)$ is perpendicular to $(3,4)$ exactly when
$$
3a+4b=0.
$$
The solution space is the line spanned by $(-4,3)$.
:::

<1>4. An orthonormal basis diagonalizing $E$ is
$$
\boxed{
\mathcal B=\left(
\frac15(3,4),
\frac15(-4,3)
\right).}
$$
::: proof
Both vectors have norm one and are orthogonal. The first spans $W$, so $E$
fixes it. The second spans $W^\perp$, so $E$ kills it. Therefore
$$
[E]_{\mathcal B}
=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$
:::
:::
