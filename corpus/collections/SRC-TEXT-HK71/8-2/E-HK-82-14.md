::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional inner product space, and let $\mathfrak{B} = \{\alpha_1, \ldots, \alpha_n\}$ be an orthonormal basis for $V$.
Let $T$ be a linear operator on $V$ and $A$ the matrix of $T$ in the ordered basis $\mathfrak{B}$.
Prove that
\[
A_{ij} = (T\alpha_j \mid \alpha_i).
\]
:::

::: solution
By definition of the matrix $A=[T]_{\mathfrak B}$, its $j$th column is the
coordinate vector of $T\alpha_j$. Thus
$$
T\alpha_j=\sum_{k=1}^n A_{kj}\alpha_k.
$$
Take the inner product with $\alpha_i$. Using orthonormality,
$$
\begin{aligned}
(T\alpha_j\mid\alpha_i)
&=\sum_{k=1}^n A_{kj}(\alpha_k\mid\alpha_i)\\
&=\sum_{k=1}^n A_{kj}\delta_{ki}\\
&=A_{ij}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Therefore
$$
\boxed{A_{ij}=(T\alpha_j\mid\alpha_i)}
$$
for all $i,j$ [@HK71].
:::
