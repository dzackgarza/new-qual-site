::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be the vector space of all $n \times n$ matrices over $C$, with the inner product $(A \mid B) = \operatorname{tr}(AB^*)$.
Find the orthogonal complement of the subspace of diagonal matrices.
:::

::: solution
Let $\mathcal D$ be the subspace of diagonal matrices. We claim
$$
\boxed{
\mathcal D^\perp
=\{A=(a_{ij})\in M_n(\mathbb C):a_{11}=\cdots=a_{nn}=0\}.}
$$

Indeed, if
$$
D=\operatorname{diag}(d_1,\ldots,d_n),
$$
then
$$
D^*=\operatorname{diag}(\overline d_1,\ldots,\overline d_n)
$$
and therefore
$$
(A\mid D)
=\operatorname{tr}(AD^*)
=\sum_{i=1}^n a_{ii}\overline{d_i}.
$$
If every $a_{ii}=0$, this is zero for every diagonal $D$, so
$A\in\mathcal D^\perp$.

Conversely, suppose $A\in\mathcal D^\perp$. For each $k$, take $D=E_{kk}$,
the diagonal matrix unit. Then
$$
0=(A\mid E_{kk})=a_{kk}.
$$
Thus every diagonal entry of $A$ is zero. This proves the claimed description
[@HK71].
:::
