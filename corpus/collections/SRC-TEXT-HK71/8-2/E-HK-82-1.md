::: {.exercise}
Consider $\RR^4$ with the standard inner product.
Let $W$ be the subspace of $\RR^4$ consisting of all vectors which are orthogonal to both $\alpha = (1, 0, -1, 1)$ and $\beta = (2, 3, -1, 2)$.
Find a basis for $W$.
:::
