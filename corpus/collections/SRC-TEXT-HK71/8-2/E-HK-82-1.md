::: {.exercise}
Consider $\RR^4$ with the standard inner product.
Let $W$ be the subspace of $\RR^4$ consisting of all vectors which are orthogonal to both $\alpha = (1, 0, -1, 1)$ and $\beta = (2, 3, -1, 2)$.
Find a basis for $W$.
:::

::: solution
Let $x=(x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4)$. Orthogonality to
$$
\alpha=(1,0,-1,1),
\qquad
\beta=(2,3,-1,2)
$$
gives
$$
x_1-x_3+x_4=0
$$
and
$$
2x_1+3x_2-x_3+2x_4=0.
$$
Subtracting twice the first equation from the second yields
$$
3x_2+x_3=0,
$$
so $x_3=-3x_2$. Substituting into the first equation gives
$$
x_1=-3x_2-x_4.
$$
Thus, with $s=x_2$ and $t=x_4$,
$$
x=s(-3,1,-3,0)+t(-1,0,0,1).
$$
The two displayed vectors are linearly independent, hence
$$
\boxed{
W=\operatorname{span}\{(-3,1,-3,0),\,(-1,0,0,1)\}.}
$$
Therefore they form a basis of $W$ [@HK71].
:::
