::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be an inner product space and $W$ a finite-dimensional subspace of $V$.
There are (in general) many projections which have $W$ as their range.
One of these, the orthogonal projection on $W$, has the property that $||E\alpha|| \leq ||\alpha||$ for every $\alpha$ in $V$.
Prove that if $E$ is a projection with range $W$, such that $||E\alpha|| \leq ||\alpha||$ for all $\alpha$ in $V$, then $E$ is the orthogonal projection on $W$.
:::
