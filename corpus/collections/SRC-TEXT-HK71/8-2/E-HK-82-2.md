::: {.exercise}
Apply the Gram-Schmidt process to the vectors $\beta_1 = (1, 0, 1)$, $\beta_2 = (1, 0, -1)$, $\beta_3 = (0, 3, 4)$, to obtain an orthonormal basis for $\RR^3$ with the standard inner product.
:::

::: solution
Set
$$
\beta_1=(1,0,1),\qquad
\beta_2=(1,0,-1),\qquad
\beta_3=(0,3,4).
$$
Since
$$
\beta_1\cdot\beta_2=0,
\qquad
\|\beta_1\|=\|\beta_2\|=\sqrt2,
$$
the first two normalized vectors are
$$
u_1=\frac1{\sqrt2}(1,0,1),
\qquad
u_2=\frac1{\sqrt2}(1,0,-1).
$$

For the third vector,
$$
(\beta_3\mid u_1)=2\sqrt2,
\qquad
(\beta_3\mid u_2)=-2\sqrt2.
$$
Therefore the Gram--Schmidt residual is
$$
\begin{aligned}
v_3
&=\beta_3-(\beta_3\mid u_1)u_1-(\beta_3\mid u_2)u_2\\
&=(0,3,4)-(2,0,2)-(-2,0,2)\\
&=(0,3,0).
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence $u_3=(0,1,0)$. Thus an orthonormal basis obtained from the prescribed
order is
$$
\boxed{
\left\{
\frac1{\sqrt2}(1,0,1),
\frac1{\sqrt2}(1,0,-1),
(0,1,0)
\right\}.}
$$
Each pair has zero dot product and each vector has norm one, so the result is
indeed orthonormal [@HK71].
:::
