::: {.exercise}
Let $W$ be a finite-dimensional subspace of an inner product space $V$, and let $E$ be the orthogonal projection of $V$ on $W$.
Prove that $(E\alpha \mid \beta) = (\alpha \mid E\beta)$ for all $\alpha, \beta$ in $V$.
:::
