::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 8.2.9"}
Let $V$ be the subspace of $R[x]$ of polynomials of degree at most 3. Equip $V$ with the inner product
\[
(f \mid g) = \int_0^1 f(t)g(t)\,dt.
\]

(a) Find the orthogonal complement of the subspace of scalar polynomials.

(b) Apply the Gram-Schmidt process to the basis $\{1, x, x^2, x^3\}$.
:::
