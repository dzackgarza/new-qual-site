::: {.exercise}
Find an inner product on $\RR^2$ such that $(\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2) = 2$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Define
$$
((x_1,x_2)\mid(y_1,y_2))
=x_1y_1+2x_1y_2+2x_2y_1+5x_2y_2.
$$
Equivalently, its Gram matrix in the standard basis is
$$
G=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\2&5\end{pmatrix}.
$$
This form is symmetric and bilinear. Moreover,
$$
(x\mid x)
=x_1^2+4x_1x_2+5x_2^2
=(x_1+2x_2)^2+x_2^2,
$$
which is strictly positive for every nonzero $x$. Hence it is an inner product
[@HK71]. Finally,
$$
(\epsilon_1\mid\epsilon_2)=G_{12}=2.
$$
Thus this inner product has the required property.
:::
