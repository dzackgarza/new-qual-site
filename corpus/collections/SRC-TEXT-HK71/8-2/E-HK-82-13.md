::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 8.2.13"}
Let $S$ be a subset of an inner product space $V$.
Show that $(S^{\perp})^{\perp}$ contains the subspace spanned by $S$.
When $V$ is finite-dimensional, show that $(S^{\perp})^{\perp}$ is the subspace spanned by $S$.
:::
