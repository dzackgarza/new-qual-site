::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ and $W$ be vector spaces over the field $F$ and let $U$ be an isomorphism of $V$ onto $W$.
Prove that $T \rightarrow UTU^{-1}$ is an isomorphism of $L(V, V)$ onto $L(W, W)$.
:::
