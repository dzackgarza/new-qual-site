::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be a vector space over the field of complex numbers, and suppose there is an isomorphism $T$ of $V$ onto $C^3$.
Let $\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3, \alpha_4$ be vectors in $V$ such that
\[
T\alpha_1 = (1, 0, i), \quad T\alpha_2 = (-2, 1 + i, 0),
\]
\[
T\alpha_3 = (-1, 1, 1), \quad T\alpha_4 = (\sqrt{2}, i, 3).
\]

(a) Is $\alpha_1$ in the subspace spanned by $\alpha_2$ and $\alpha_3$?

(b) Let $W_1$ be the subspace spanned by $\alpha_1$ and $\alpha_2$, and let $W_2$ be the subspace spanned by $\alpha_3$ and $\alpha_4$.
What is the intersection of $W_1$ and $W_2$?

(c) Find a basis for the subspace of $V$ spanned by the four vectors $\alpha_i$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Because $T$ is an isomorphism, linear relations among the $\alpha_i$ are exactly the corresponding linear relations among their images.

For part (a), direct calculation gives
\[
(1,0,i)=-\frac{1+i}{2}(-2,1+i,0)+i(-1,1,1).
\]
Hence
\[
\alpha_1=-\frac{1+i}{2}\alpha_2+i\alpha_3,
\]
so $\alpha_1\in\operatorname{span}\{\alpha_2,\alpha_3\}$.

For part (b), suppose
\[
a\alpha_1+b\alpha_2=c\alpha_3+d\alpha_4.
\]
Applying $T$ and comparing coordinates gives the homogeneous system whose solutions are
\[
a=-ic,\qquad b=\frac{1-i}{2}c,\qquad d=0.
\]
Thus every vector in $W_1\cap W_2$ is a scalar multiple of $\alpha_3$. Conversely,
\[
\alpha_3=-i\alpha_1+\frac{1-i}{2}\alpha_2,
\]
so $\alpha_3\in W_1$, while of course $\alpha_3\in W_2$. Therefore
\[
W_1\cap W_2=\operatorname{span}\{\alpha_3\}.
\]

For part (c), the three vectors $T\alpha_1,T\alpha_2,T\alpha_4$ are linearly independent, since
\[
\det\begin{bmatrix}
1&-2&\sqrt2\\
0&1+i&i\\
i&0&3
\end{bmatrix}
=5+\sqrt2+i(3-\sqrt2)\ne0.
\]
They therefore form a basis of $\mathbb C^3$. Since $\alpha_3$ is already a linear combination of $\alpha_1$ and $\alpha_2$, a basis for the span of all four vectors is
\[
\{\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_4\}.
\]
:::
