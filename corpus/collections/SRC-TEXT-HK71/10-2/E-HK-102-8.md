::: {.exercise}
Let $A$ be a symmetric $n \times n$ matrix over $\RR$, and let $q$ be the quadratic form on $\RR^n$ given by
\[
q(x_1, \ldots, x_n) = \sum_{i,j} A_{ij}x_ix_j.
\]
Generalize the method used in Exercise 7 to show that there is an invertible linear operator $U$ on $\RR^n$ such that
\[
(U^{\dagger}q)(x_1, \ldots, x_n) = \sum_{i=1}^{n} c_ix_i^2
\]
where $c_i$ is $1$, $-1$, or $0$, $i = 1, \ldots, n$.
:::
