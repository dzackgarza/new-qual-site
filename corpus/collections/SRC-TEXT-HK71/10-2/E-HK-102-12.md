::: {.exercise}
Let $F$ be a field and let $V$ be the space of $n\times1$ matrices over $F$.
Suppose $A$ is a fixed $n\times n$ matrix over $F$ and
\[
f(X,Y)=X^tAY
\]
is a symmetric, non-degenerate bilinear form on $V$.
Let $B$ be an $n\times n$ matrix over $F$, and let $T$ be the linear operator
\[
T(X)=BX.
\]
Find the adjoint $T'$ relative to $f$ from Exercise 11.
:::

::: {.solution}
Because $f$ is symmetric,
$$
A^t=A,
$$
and because $f$ is non-degenerate, $A$ is invertible.

<1>1. Translate the adjoint identity into a matrix equation.
::: {.proof}
Suppose $T'$ has matrix $C$, so $T'(Y)=CY$. Then
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(TX,Y)
&=(BX)^tAY\\
&=X^tB^tAY,
\end{aligned}
$$
whereas
$$
f(X,T'Y)=X^tACY.
$$
Thus
$$
f(TX,Y)=f(X,T'Y)
$$
for every $X,Y$ if and only if
$$
B^tA=AC.
$$
:::

<1>2. Solve for the adjoint matrix.
::: {.proof}
Since $A$ is invertible,
$$
C=A^{-1}B^tA.
$$
Therefore
$$
\boxed{T'(Y)=A^{-1}B^tAY.}
$$

Direct substitution verifies the formula:
$$
f(X,T'Y)
=X^tAA^{-1}B^tAY
=X^tB^tAY
=f(TX,Y).
$$
By the uniqueness proved in Exercise 11, this is the relative adjoint.
:::
:::
