::: {.exercise}
Let $q(x_1, x_2) = ax_1^2 + bx_1x_2 + cx_2^2$ be the quadratic form associated with a symmetric bilinear form $f$ on $\RR^2$.
Show that $f$ is non-degenerate if and only if $b^2 - 4ac \neq 0$.
:::

::: solution
<1>1. Recover the symmetric matrix of $f$ from the quadratic form.
::: proof
If
$$
M=
\begin{pmatrix}
m_{11}&m_{12}\\
m_{12}&m_{22}
\end{pmatrix}
$$
is the standard matrix of $f$, then
$$
q(x_1,x_2)
=f(x,x)
=m_{11}x_1^2+2m_{12}x_1x_2+m_{22}x_2^2.
$$
Comparing coefficients with
$$
q(x_1,x_2)=a x_1^2+b x_1x_2+c x_2^2
$$
gives
$$
M=
\begin{pmatrix}
a&b/2\\
b/2&c
\end{pmatrix}.
$$
:::

<1>2. Nondegeneracy is equivalent to nonvanishing of the discriminant.
::: proof
A bilinear form on a finite-dimensional space is non-degenerate exactly when
its representing matrix is nonsingular. Here
$$
\det M
=ac-\frac{b^2}{4}
=-\frac14(b^2-4ac).
$$
Since $-1/4\ne0$ in $\mathbb R$,
$$
\det M\ne0
\iff
b^2-4ac\ne0.
$$
Therefore
$$
\boxed{f\text{ is non-degenerate}\iff b^2-4ac\ne0.}
$$
:::
:::
