::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional vector space with a non-degenerate symmetric bilinear form $f$.
Let
\[
\mathfrak B=\{\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_n\}
\]
be a basis of $V$, and let
\[
\mathfrak B'=\{\alpha'_1,\ldots,\alpha'_n\}
\]
be the $f$-dual basis from Exercise 13, so that
\[
f(\alpha_i,\alpha'_j)=\delta_{ij}.
\]
Let $T$ be a linear operator on $V$, and let $T'$ be its adjoint relative to $f$ as in Exercise 11.

(a) Show that
\[
[T']_{\mathfrak B'}=[T]_{\mathfrak B}^{\,t}.
\]

(b) Show that
\[
\operatorname{tr}T=\operatorname{tr}T'
=\sum_{i=1}^n f(T\alpha_i,\alpha'_i).
\]
:::

::: solution
Write
$$
[T]_{\mathfrak B}=(a_{ij}),
\qquad
[T']_{\mathfrak B'}=(c_{ij}).
$$
Thus
$$
T\alpha_j=\sum_i a_{ij}\alpha_i,
\qquad
T'\alpha'_k=\sum_i c_{ik}\alpha'_i.
$$

<1>1. Compare the matrix coefficients of $T$ and $T'$.
::: proof
Using the defining identity for the relative adjoint and the $f$-dual-basis
relations,
$$
\begin{aligned}
c_{jk}
&=f(\alpha_j,T'\alpha'_k)\\
&=f(T\alpha_j,\alpha'_k)\\
&=f\left(\sum_i a_{ij}\alpha_i,\alpha'_k\right)\\
&=\sum_i a_{ij}\delta_{ik}\\
&=a_{kj}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Therefore
$$
\boxed{[T']_{\mathfrak B'}=[T]_{\mathfrak B}^{\,t}.}
$$
:::

<1>2. Deduce the trace identities.
::: proof
Taking traces in step <1>1 gives
$$
\operatorname{tr}T'
=\operatorname{tr}([T']_{\mathfrak B'})
=\operatorname{tr}([T]_{\mathfrak B}^t)
=\operatorname{tr}T.
$$

Also, for each $i$,
$$
f(T\alpha_i,\alpha'_i)
=f\left(\sum_j a_{ji}\alpha_j,\alpha'_i\right)
=a_{ii}.
$$
Hence
$$
\sum_i f(T\alpha_i,\alpha'_i)
=\sum_i a_{ii}
=\operatorname{tr}T.
$$
Combining the two equalities gives
$$
\boxed{
\operatorname{tr}T=\operatorname{tr}T'
=\sum_{i=1}^n f(T\alpha_i,\alpha'_i).}
$$
:::
:::
