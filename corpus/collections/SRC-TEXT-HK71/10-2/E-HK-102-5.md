::: {.exercise}
Let $q$ be the quadratic form on $\RR^2$ given by
\[
q(x_1, x_2) = ax_1^2 + 2bx_1x_2 + cx_2^2, \quad a \neq 0.
\]
Find an invertible linear operator $U$ on $\RR^2$ such that
\[
(U^{\dagger}q)(x_1, x_2) = ax_1^2 + \left(c - \frac{b^2}{a}\right)x_2^2.
\]
(Hint: To find $U^{-1}$ (and hence $U$), complete the square.
For the definition of $U^{\dagger}$, see part (e) of Exercise 4.)
:::
