::: {.exercise}
Find a row-reduced echelon matrix which is row-equivalent to

$$
A = \left[ \begin{array}{c c} 1 & - i \\ 2 & 2 \\ i & 1 + i \end{array} \right].
$$

What are the solutions of $AX = 0$ ?
:::
