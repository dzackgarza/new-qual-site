::: {.exercise}
Show that the system

$$
\begin{array}{l} x _ {1} - 2 x _ {2} + x _ {3} + 2 x _ {4} = 1 \\ x _ {1} + x _ {2} - x _ {3} + x _ {4} = 2 \\ x _ {1} + 7 x _ {2} - 5 x _ {3} - x _ {4} = 3 \end{array}
$$

has no solution.
:::
