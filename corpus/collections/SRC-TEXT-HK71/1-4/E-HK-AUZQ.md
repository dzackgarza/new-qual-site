::: {.exercise}
Let

$$
A = \left[ \begin{array}{c c c c} 3 & - 6 & 2 & - 1 \\ - 2 & 4 & 1 & 3 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & - 2 & 1 & 0 \end{array} \right].
$$

For which $(y_{1}, y_{2}, y_{3}, y_{4})$ does the system of equations AX = Y have a solution?
:::
