::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be the vector space over the complex numbers of all functions from $\RR$ into $C$, i.e., the space of all complex-valued functions on the real line.
Let $f_1(x) = 1$, $f_2(x) = e^{ix}$, $f_3(x) = e^{-ix}$.

(a) Prove that $f_1$, $f_2$, and $f_3$ are linearly independent.

(b) Let $g_1(x) = 1$, $g_2(x) = \cos x$, $g_3(x) = \sin x$.
Find an invertible $3 \times 3$ matrix $P$ such that
\[
g_j = \sum_{i=1}^{3} P_{ij} f_i.
\]
:::
