::: {.exercise}
Find the coordinate matrix of the vector $(1, 0, 1)$ in the basis of $\CC^3$ consisting of the vectors $(2i, 1, 0)$, $(2, -1, 1)$, $(0, 1+i, 1-i)$, in that order.
:::


::: {.solution}
Seek scalars $a,b,c\in\CC$ such that
$$
a(2i,1,0)+b(2,-1,1)+c(0,1+i,1-i)=(1,0,1).
$$
The coordinates give
$$
2ia+2b=1,
\qquad
a-b+(1+i)c=0,
\qquad
b+(1-i)c=1.
$$
The third equation gives $b=1-(1-i)c$, and then the second gives $a=b-(1+i)c=1-2c$. Substituting into the first gives $2i(1-2c)+2-2(1-i)c=1$, that is, $(2+2i)c=1+2i$, so $c=\frac{(1+2i)(2-2i)}{8}=\frac{3+i}{4}$. Hence
$$
a=-\frac12-\frac{i}{2},
\qquad
b=\frac{i}{2},
\qquad
c=\frac34+\frac{i}{4}.
$$
Therefore the coordinate matrix of $(1,0,1)$ in the given ordered basis is
$$
\begin{bmatrix}
-\frac12-\frac{i}{2}\\
\frac{i}{2}\\
\frac34+\frac{i}{4}
\end{bmatrix}.
$$
:::
