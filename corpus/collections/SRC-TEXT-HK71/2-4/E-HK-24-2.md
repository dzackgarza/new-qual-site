::: {.exercise}
Find the coordinate matrix of the vector $(1, 0, 1)$ in the basis of $C^3$ consisting of the vectors $(2i, 1, 0)$, $(2, -1, 1)$, $(0, 1+i, 1-i)$, in that order.
:::


::: solution
Seek scalars $a,b,c\in\CC$ such that
\[
a(2i,1,0)+b(2,-1,1)+c(0,1+i,1-i)=(1,0,1).
\]
Solving the resulting system gives
\[
a=2-i,
\qquad
b=-\frac12-\frac{3i}{2},
\qquad
c=-1+\frac{i}{2}.
\]
Therefore the coordinate matrix of $(1,0,1)$ in the given ordered basis is
\[
\begin{bmatrix}
2-i\\
-\frac12-\frac{3i}{2}\\
-1+\frac{i}{2}
\end{bmatrix}.
\]
:::
