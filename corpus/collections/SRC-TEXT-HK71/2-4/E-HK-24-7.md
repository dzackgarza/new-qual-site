::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be the (real) vector space of all polynomial functions from $\RR$ into $\RR$ of degree 2 or less, i.e., the space of all functions $f$ of the form
\[
f(x) = c_0 + c_1 x + c_2 x^2.
\]
Let $t$ be a fixed real number and define
\[
g_1(x) = 1, \quad g_2(x) = x + t, \quad g_3(x) = (x + t)^2.
\]
Prove that $\mathfrak{B} = \{g_1, g_2, g_3\}$ is a basis for $V$.
If
\[
f(x) = c_0 + c_1 x + c_2 x^2,
\]
what are the coordinates of $f$ in this ordered basis $\mathfrak{B}$?
:::


::: solution
The functions
\[
g_1(x)=1,
\qquad
g_2(x)=x+t,
\qquad
g_3(x)=(x+t)^2
\]
span $V$: if
\[
f(x)=c_0+c_1x+c_2x^2,
\]
then
\[
f(x)=a+b(x+t)+c(x+t)^2
\]
with
\[
c=c_2,
\qquad
b=c_1-2tc_2,
\qquad
a=c_0-tc_1+t^2c_2.
\]
Indeed, expanding the right side gives
\[
(a+bt+ct^2)+(b+2ct)x+cx^2,
\]
whose coefficients match $f$ for exactly these values of $a,b,c$.

The representation is unique because the coefficient comparison determines $a,b,c$ uniquely. Therefore $\{g_1,g_2,g_3\}$ is a basis, and
\[
[f]_{\mathfrak B}
=
\begin{bmatrix}
c_0-tc_1+t^2c_2\\
c_1-2tc_2\\
c_2
\end{bmatrix}.
\]
:::
