::: {.exercise}
Let $\mathfrak{B} = \{\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3\}$ be the ordered basis for $\RR^3$ consisting of
\[
\alpha_1 = (1, 0, -1), \quad \alpha_2 = (1, 1, 1), \quad \alpha_3 = (1, 0, 0).
\]
What are the coordinates of the vector $(a, b, c)$ in the ordered basis $\mathfrak{B}$?
:::


::: solution
Write
\[
(a,b,c)=x\alpha_1+y\alpha_2+z\alpha_3.
\]
Comparing coordinates gives
\[
x+y+z=a,
\qquad
y=b,
\qquad
-x+y=c.
\]
Thus
\[
y=b,
\qquad
x=b-c,
\qquad
z=a-2b+c.
\]
Therefore
\[
[(a,b,c)]_{\mathfrak B}
=
\begin{bmatrix}
b-c\\
b\\
a-2b+c
\end{bmatrix}.
\]
:::
