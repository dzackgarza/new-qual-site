::: {.exercise}
If $A$ is an invertible $n \times n$ matrix over a field, show that $\det A \neq 0$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Since $A$ is invertible, there is a matrix $A^{-1}$ with
\[
AA^{-1}=I.
\]
By multiplicativity of the determinant,
\[
1=\det I=\det(AA^{-1})=(\det A)(\det A^{-1}).
\]
Thus $\det A$ has a multiplicative inverse in the field, namely $\det(A^{-1})$, and therefore $\det A\ne0$.
:::
