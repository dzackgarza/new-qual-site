::: {.exercise}
List explicitly the six permutations of degree 3, state which are odd and which are even, and use this to give the complete formula (5-15) for the determinant of a $3 \times 3$ matrix.
:::

::: solution
The six permutations of $\{1,2,3\}$ are
\[
\mathrm{id},\quad (123),\quad(132),\quad(12),\quad(13),\quad(23).
\]
The identity and the two $3$-cycles are even; the three transpositions are odd. Therefore, for $A=(A_{ij})$, the permutation definition of determinant gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
\det A={}&A_{11}A_{22}A_{33}+A_{12}A_{23}A_{31}+A_{13}A_{21}A_{32}\\
&-A_{12}A_{21}A_{33}-A_{13}A_{22}A_{31}-A_{11}A_{23}A_{32}.
\end{aligned}
\]
These are exactly the three even-permutation terms minus the three odd-permutation terms.
:::
