::: {.exercise}
If $K$ is a commutative ring with identity and $A$ is the matrix over $K$ given by
\[
A = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & a & b \\ -a & 0 & c \\ -b & -c & 0 \end{bmatrix}
\]
show that $\det A = 0$.
:::
