::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 5.3.2"}
Prove that the determinant of the Vandermonde matrix
\[
\begin{bmatrix} 1 & a & a^2 \\ 1 & b & b^2 \\ 1 & c & c^2 \end{bmatrix}
\]
is $(b - a)(c - a)(c - b)$.
:::
