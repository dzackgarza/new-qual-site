::: {.exercise}
Let $A$ be a $2 \times 2$ matrix over a field.
Prove that $\det(I + A) = 1 + \det A$ if and only if $\operatorname{trace}(A) = 0$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Write
\[
A=\begin{bmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{bmatrix}.
\]
Then
\[
\det(I+A)=(1+a)(1+d)-bc
=1+(a+d)+(ad-bc)
=1+\operatorname{trace}(A)+\det A.
\]
Therefore
\[
\det(I+A)=1+\det A
\]
holds exactly when $\operatorname{trace}(A)=0$.
:::
