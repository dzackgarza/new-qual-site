::: {.exercise}
Let $F$ be a field, $A$ a $2 \times 3$ matrix over $F$, and $(c_1, c_2, c_3)$ the vector in $F^3$ defined by
\[
c_1 = \begin{vmatrix} A_{12} & A_{13} \\ A_{22} & A_{23} \end{vmatrix}, \quad c_2 = \begin{vmatrix} A_{13} & A_{11} \\ A_{23} & A_{21} \end{vmatrix}, \quad c_3 = \begin{vmatrix} A_{11} & A_{12} \\ A_{21} & A_{22} \end{vmatrix}.
\]
Show that

(a) $\operatorname{rank}(A) = 2$ if and only if $(c_1, c_2, c_3) \neq 0$;

(b) if $A$ has rank 2, then $(c_1, c_2, c_3)$ is a basis for the solution space of the system of equations $AX = 0$.
:::
