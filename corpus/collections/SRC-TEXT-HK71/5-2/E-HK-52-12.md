::: {.exercise}
Let $F$ be a field and $D$ a function on $n \times n$ matrices over $F$ (with values in $F$). Suppose $D(AB) = D(A)D(B)$ for all $A, B$.
Show that either $D(A) = 0$ for all $A$, or $D(I) = 1$.
In the latter case show that $D(A) \neq 0$ whenever $A$ is invertible.
:::
