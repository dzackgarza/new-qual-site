::: {.exercise}
Let $K$ be a subfield of the complex numbers and $n$ a positive integer.
Let $j_1, \ldots, j_n$ and $k_1, \ldots, k_n$ be positive integers not exceeding $n$.
For an $n \times n$ matrix $A$ over $K$ define
\[
D(A) = A(j_1, k_1)A(j_2, k_2) \cdots A(j_n, k_n).
\]
Prove that $D$ is $n$-linear if and only if the integers $j_1, \ldots, j_n$ are distinct.
:::
