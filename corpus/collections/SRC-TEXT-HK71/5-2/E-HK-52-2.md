::: {.exercise}
Let $K$ be a commutative ring with identity, let $A = (A_{ij})$ be a $3 \times 3$ matrix over $K$, and write $\abs{B} = B_{11}B_{22} - B_{12}B_{21}$ for a $2 \times 2$ matrix $B$.
Define
$$
\begin{aligned}
E_1(A) &= A_{11} \begin{vmatrix} A_{22} & A_{23} \\ A_{32} & A_{33} \end{vmatrix} - A_{21} \begin{vmatrix} A_{12} & A_{13} \\ A_{32} & A_{33} \end{vmatrix} + A_{31} \begin{vmatrix} A_{12} & A_{13} \\ A_{22} & A_{23} \end{vmatrix}, \\
E_2(A) &= -A_{12} \begin{vmatrix} A_{21} & A_{23} \\ A_{31} & A_{33} \end{vmatrix} + A_{22} \begin{vmatrix} A_{11} & A_{13} \\ A_{31} & A_{33} \end{vmatrix} - A_{32} \begin{vmatrix} A_{11} & A_{13} \\ A_{21} & A_{23} \end{vmatrix}, \\
E_3(A) &= A_{13} \begin{vmatrix} A_{21} & A_{22} \\ A_{31} & A_{32} \end{vmatrix} - A_{23} \begin{vmatrix} A_{11} & A_{12} \\ A_{31} & A_{32} \end{vmatrix} + A_{33} \begin{vmatrix} A_{11} & A_{12} \\ A_{21} & A_{22} \end{vmatrix}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Verify directly that the three functions $E_1$, $E_2$, $E_3$ are identical.
:::

::: {.solution}
No retained solution set covers this exercise; the argument is the card's own, and the three expansions were redone for this pass.

Throughout, $\abs{B}=B_{11}B_{22}-B_{12}B_{21}$ for a $2\times2$ matrix $B$ over the commutative ring $K$ with identity, and $A=(A_{ij})$ is $3\times3$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #E1}
$E_{1}(A)$ equals $-A_{13}A_{22}A_{31}+A_{11}A_{22}A_{33}-A_{11}A_{23}A_{32}-A_{12}A_{21}A_{33}+A_{13}A_{21}A_{32}+A_{12}A_{23}A_{31}$.

::: pf-proof
Expand the three $2\times2$ determinants in the definition of $E_{1}$:
$$
E_{1}(A)=A_{11}(A_{22}A_{33}-A_{23}A_{32})-A_{21}(A_{12}A_{33}-A_{13}A_{32})+A_{31}(A_{12}A_{23}-A_{13}A_{22}),
$$
and distribute each product over the difference: the nine terms are $A_{11}A_{22}A_{33}$, $-A_{11}A_{23}A_{32}$, $-A_{21}A_{12}A_{33}$, $A_{21}A_{13}A_{32}$, $A_{31}A_{12}A_{23}$ and $-A_{31}A_{13}A_{22}$. Commutativity of $K$ writes each of these with the first index running $1,2,3$ within each monomial, as displayed.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #E2}
$E_{2}(A)$ equals the same six signed monomials.

::: pf-proof
Expanding the determinants in the definition of $E_{2}$ gives
$$
E_{2}(A)=-A_{12}(A_{21}A_{33}-A_{23}A_{31})+A_{22}(A_{11}A_{33}-A_{13}A_{31})-A_{32}(A_{11}A_{23}-A_{13}A_{21}),
$$
whose terms are $-A_{12}A_{21}A_{33}$, $A_{12}A_{23}A_{31}$, $A_{22}A_{11}A_{33}$, $-A_{22}A_{13}A_{31}$, $-A_{32}A_{11}A_{23}$ and $A_{32}A_{13}A_{21}$. Reordering the factors inside each monomial by commutativity gives exactly the six terms of step [](#E1){.pf-ref}.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #E3}
$E_{3}(A)$ equals the same six signed monomials.

::: pf-proof
Expanding the determinants in the definition of $E_{3}$ gives
$$
E_{3}(A)=A_{13}(A_{21}A_{32}-A_{22}A_{31})-A_{23}(A_{11}A_{32}-A_{12}A_{31})+A_{33}(A_{11}A_{22}-A_{12}A_{21}),
$$
whose terms are $A_{13}A_{21}A_{32}$, $-A_{13}A_{22}A_{31}$, $-A_{23}A_{11}A_{32}$, $A_{23}A_{12}A_{31}$, $A_{33}A_{11}A_{22}$ and $-A_{33}A_{12}A_{21}$. Reordering the factors by commutativity gives the same six terms as before.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step}
$E_{1}=E_{2}=E_{3}$ as functions on $3\times3$ matrices over $K$.

::: pf-proof
By steps [](#E1){.pf-ref}, [](#E2){.pf-ref} and [](#E3){.pf-ref} each of the three is the sum of the six monomials
$$
A_{11}A_{22}A_{33},\ -A_{11}A_{23}A_{32},\ -A_{12}A_{21}A_{33},\ A_{12}A_{23}A_{31},\ A_{13}A_{21}A_{32},\ -A_{13}A_{22}A_{31},
$$
so the three agree on every matrix, which is the assertion that $E_{1}$, $E_{2}$ and $E_{3}$ are identical. Only the ring axioms are used: distributivity to expand, associativity to regroup, and commutativity to reorder the factors.
:::
