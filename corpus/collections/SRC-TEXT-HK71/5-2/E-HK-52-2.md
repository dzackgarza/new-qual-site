::: {.exercise}
Let $K$ be a commutative ring with identity, let $A = (A_{ij})$ be a $3 \times 3$ matrix over $K$, and write $\abs{B} = B_{11}B_{22} - B_{12}B_{21}$ for a $2 \times 2$ matrix $B$.
Define
$$
\begin{aligned}
E_1(A) &= A_{11} \begin{vmatrix} A_{22} & A_{23} \\ A_{32} & A_{33} \end{vmatrix} - A_{21} \begin{vmatrix} A_{12} & A_{13} \\ A_{32} & A_{33} \end{vmatrix} + A_{31} \begin{vmatrix} A_{12} & A_{13} \\ A_{22} & A_{23} \end{vmatrix}, \\
E_2(A) &= -A_{12} \begin{vmatrix} A_{21} & A_{23} \\ A_{31} & A_{33} \end{vmatrix} + A_{22} \begin{vmatrix} A_{11} & A_{13} \\ A_{31} & A_{33} \end{vmatrix} - A_{32} \begin{vmatrix} A_{11} & A_{13} \\ A_{21} & A_{23} \end{vmatrix}, \\
E_3(A) &= A_{13} \begin{vmatrix} A_{21} & A_{22} \\ A_{31} & A_{32} \end{vmatrix} - A_{23} \begin{vmatrix} A_{11} & A_{12} \\ A_{31} & A_{32} \end{vmatrix} + A_{33} \begin{vmatrix} A_{11} & A_{12} \\ A_{21} & A_{22} \end{vmatrix}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Verify directly that the three functions $E_1$, $E_2$, $E_3$ are identical.
:::

::: {.solution}
Each $E_j$ is the expansion along column $j$.
Expanding the $2 \times 2$ determinants and using commutativity of $K$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
E_1(A) &= A_{11}A_{22}A_{33} - A_{11}A_{23}A_{32} - A_{12}A_{21}A_{33} + A_{13}A_{21}A_{32} + A_{12}A_{23}A_{31} - A_{13}A_{22}A_{31}, \\
E_2(A) &= -A_{12}A_{21}A_{33} + A_{12}A_{23}A_{31} + A_{11}A_{22}A_{33} - A_{13}A_{22}A_{31} - A_{11}A_{23}A_{32} + A_{13}A_{21}A_{32}, \\
E_3(A) &= A_{13}A_{21}A_{32} - A_{13}A_{22}A_{31} - A_{11}A_{23}A_{32} + A_{12}A_{23}A_{31} + A_{11}A_{22}A_{33} - A_{12}A_{21}A_{33}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Each line consists of the same six signed monomials
$$
A_{11}A_{22}A_{33},\ -A_{11}A_{23}A_{32},\ -A_{12}A_{21}A_{33},\ A_{12}A_{23}A_{31},\ A_{13}A_{21}A_{32},\ -A_{13}A_{22}A_{31},
$$
so $E_1 = E_2 = E_3$.
:::
