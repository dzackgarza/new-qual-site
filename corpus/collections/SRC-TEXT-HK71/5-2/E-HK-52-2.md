::: {.exercise}
Verify directly that the three functions $E_1$, $E_2$, $E_3$ defined by (5-6), (5-7), and (5-8) are identical.
:::

::: solution
**Goal:** Verify directly by algebraic expansion that the three cofactor formulas $E_1, E_2, E_3$ for the $3 \times 3$ matrix $A = (A_{ij})$ from Hoffman and Kunze are identical.

<1>1. Definition and expansion of $E_1(A)$ (Laplace expansion along row 1, formula 5-6):
    *Proof:*
    <2>1. Formula (5-6) defines
    $$E_1(A) = A_{11}(A_{22} A_{33} - A_{23} A_{32}) - A_{12}(A_{21} A_{33} - A_{23} A_{31}) + A_{13}(A_{21} A_{32} - A_{22} A_{31}).$$
    <2>2. Distributing the terms:
    $$E_1(A) = A_{11} A_{22} A_{33} - A_{11} A_{23} A_{32} - A_{12} A_{21} A_{33} + A_{12} A_{23} A_{31} + A_{13} A_{21} A_{32} - A_{13} A_{22} A_{31}.$$

<1>2. Definition and expansion of $E_2(A)$ (Laplace expansion along row 2, formula 5-7):
    *Proof:*
    <2>1. Formula (5-7) defines
    $$E_2(A) = -A_{21}(A_{12} A_{33} - A_{13} A_{32}) + A_{22}(A_{11} A_{33} - A_{13} A_{31}) - A_{23}(A_{11} A_{32} - A_{12} A_{31}).$$
    <2>2. Distributing the terms:
    $$E_2(A) = -A_{21} A_{12} A_{33} + A_{21} A_{13} A_{32} + A_{22} A_{11} A_{33} - A_{22} A_{13} A_{31} - A_{23} A_{11} A_{32} + A_{23} A_{12} A_{31}.$$
    <2>3. Rearranging into standard factor order:
    $$E_2(A) = A_{11} A_{22} A_{33} - A_{11} A_{23} A_{32} - A_{12} A_{21} A_{33} + A_{12} A_{23} A_{31} + A_{13} A_{21} A_{32} - A_{13} A_{22} A_{31}.$$

<1>3. Definition and expansion of $E_3(A)$ (Laplace expansion along row 3, formula 5-8):
    *Proof:*
    <2>1. Formula (5-8) defines
    $$E_3(A) = A_{31}(A_{12} A_{23} - A_{13} A_{22}) - A_{32}(A_{11} A_{23} - A_{13} A_{21}) + A_{33}(A_{11} A_{22} - A_{12} A_{21}).$$
    <2>2. Distributing the terms:
    $$E_3(A) = A_{31} A_{12} A_{23} - A_{31} A_{13} A_{22} - A_{32} A_{11} A_{23} + A_{32} A_{13} A_{21} + A_{33} A_{11} A_{22} - A_{33} A_{12} A_{21}.$$
    <2>3. Rearranging into standard factor order:
    $$E_3(A) = A_{11} A_{22} A_{33} - A_{11} A_{23} A_{32} - A_{12} A_{21} A_{33} + A_{12} A_{23} A_{31} + A_{13} A_{21} A_{32} - A_{13} A_{22} A_{31}.$$

<1>4. Conclusion:
    *Proof:*
    All three expanded expressions consist of the exact same 6 signed monomials:
    $$E_1(A) = E_2(A) = E_3(A) = \sum_{\sigma \in S_3} \operatorname{sgn}(\sigma) A_{1\sigma(1)} A_{2\sigma(2)} A_{3\sigma(3)}.$$
:::
