::: {.exercise}
Test the following systems of equations as in Exercise 2.
\[
\begin{array}{rl}
-x_1 + x_2 + 4x_3 = 0, & x_1 - x_3 = 0, \\
x_1 + 3x_2 + 8x_3 = 0, & x_2 + 3x_3 = 0, \\
\frac{1}{2}x_1 + x_2 + \frac{5}{2}x_3 = 0. &
\end{array}
\]
:::

::: {.solution}
Let
\[
A_1=-x_1+x_2+4x_3,
\quad A_2=x_1+3x_2+8x_3,
\quad A_3=\frac12x_1+x_2+\frac52x_3,
\]
and
\[
B_1=x_1-x_3,
\qquad B_2=x_2+3x_3.
\]

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #first-system-in-span-of-second}
Every equation of the first system lies in the span of the second.

::: pf-proof
One has
\[
A_1=-B_1+B_2,
\qquad
A_2=B_1+3B_2,
\qquad
A_3=\frac12B_1+B_2.
\]
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #second-system-in-span-of-first}
Every equation of the second system lies in the span of the first.

::: pf-proof
The identities
\[
B_1=-\frac34A_1+\frac14A_2,
\qquad
B_2=\frac14A_1+\frac14A_2
\]
hold by comparing coefficients of $x_1,x_2,x_3$.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
Therefore the two systems are equivalent.

::: pf-proof
By steps [](#first-system-in-span-of-second){.pf-ref} and [](#second-system-in-span-of-first){.pf-ref} the two sets of equations span the same subspace of the dual
space, so their common zero sets coincide.
:::
