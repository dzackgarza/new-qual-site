::: {.exercise}
Prove that each subfield of the field of complex numbers contains every rational number.
:::

::: solution
Let $F\subseteq\CC$ be a subfield.

<1>1. Every integer belongs to $F$.
::: proof
Because $F$ is a subfield, $0,1\in F$. Closure under addition gives
\[
n=\underbrace{1+\cdots+1}_{n\text{ times}}\in F
\]
for every positive integer $n$, and closure under additive inverses gives every
negative integer as well. Hence $\ZZ\subseteq F$.
:::

<1>2. Every rational number belongs to $F$.
::: proof
Let $m/n\in\QQ$ with $n\ne0$. By <1>1, $m,n\in F$. Since $F\subseteq\CC$,
the element $n$ is nonzero in $F$, so $n^{-1}\in F$. Therefore
\[
\frac mn=m n^{-1}\in F.
\]
Thus $\QQ\subseteq F$.
:::
:::
