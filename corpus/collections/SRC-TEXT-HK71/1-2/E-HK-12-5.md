::: {.exercise}
Let $F$ be a set which contains exactly two elements, $0$ and $1$. Define an addition and multiplication by the tables
\[
\begin{array}{c|cc}
+ & 0 & 1 \\
\hline
0 & 0 & 1 \\
1 & 1 & 0
\end{array}
\qquad
\begin{array}{c|cc}
\cdot & 0 & 1 \\
\hline
0 & 0 & 0 \\
1 & 0 & 1
\end{array}
.\]
Verify that the set $F$, together with these two operations, is a field.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #addition-commutative-associative}
Addition is commutative and associative, with identity $0$ and each element its own inverse.

::: pf-proof
the table is symmetric; $0 + a = a$; $1 + 1 = 0$ so $1$ is its own inverse.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #multiplication-commutative-associative}
Multiplication is commutative and associative, with identity $1$.

::: pf-proof
the table is symmetric; $1 \cdot a = a$; associativity is checked directly (the only nontrivial case is $1 \cdot 1 \cdot 1 = 1$).
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #multiplication-distributes}
Multiplication distributes over addition.

::: pf-proof
$a(b + c) = ab + ac$ holds for all $a, b, c \in \{0, 1\}$ by direct check (the only case needing care is $1 \cdot (1 + 1) = 1 \cdot 0 = 0 = 1 + 1 = 1 \cdot 1 + 1 \cdot 1$).
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #nonzero-has-inverse}
Every nonzero element has a multiplicative inverse.

::: pf-proof
the only nonzero element is $1$, and $1 \cdot 1 = 1$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #f-is-field}
Hence $F$ is a field.

::: pf-proof
Steps [](#addition-commutative-associative){.pf-ref}, [](#multiplication-commutative-associative){.pf-ref}, [](#multiplication-distributes){.pf-ref}, and [](#nonzero-has-inverse){.pf-ref} verify the field axioms; this is the field $\FF_2$ with two elements.
:::

:::
