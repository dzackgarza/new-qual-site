::: {.exercise}
Prove that each field of characteristic zero contains a copy of the rational number field.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $F$ be a field of characteristic zero, with multiplicative identity $1_F$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #iota-injective-ring-hom}
The map
\[
\iota:\ZZ\longrightarrow F,
\qquad
\iota(n)=n\cdot1_F,
\]
is an injective ring homomorphism.

::: pf-proof
The ring-homomorphism identities follow from repeated addition. Its kernel is
the ideal
\[
\{n\in\ZZ:n\cdot1_F=0\}.
\]
Characteristic zero means this kernel is $0$, hence $\iota$ is injective.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #iota-extends-to-q}
The map extends uniquely to an injective field homomorphism
\[
\widetilde\iota:\QQ\longrightarrow F,
\qquad
\widetilde\iota\!\left(\frac mn\right)
=\iota(m)\iota(n)^{-1}.
\]

::: pf-proof
If $n\ne0$, injectivity of $\iota$ gives $\iota(n)\ne0$, so the displayed
expression is defined. If $m/n=m'/n'$, then $mn'=m'n$, and applying $\iota$
shows the two displayed values are equal, so the map is well-defined. The field
operations are preserved by the usual common-denominator calculation. Finally,
if $\widetilde\iota(m/n)=0$, then $\iota(m)=0$, hence $m=0$ by step [](#iota-injective-ring-hom){.pf-ref}; therefore
the map is injective.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
Hence $F$ contains a subfield isomorphic to $\QQ$.

::: pf-proof
The image of the injective field homomorphism in step [](#iota-extends-to-q){.pf-ref} is such a subfield.
:::
