::: {.exercise}
Which of the following functions $f$, defined on vectors $\alpha = (x_1, x_2)$ and $\beta = (y_1, y_2)$ in $\RR^2$, are bilinear forms?

(a) $f(\alpha, \beta) = 1$.

(b) $f(\alpha, \beta) = (x_1 - y_1)^2 + x_2y_2$.

(c) $f(\alpha, \beta) = (x_1 + y_1)^2 - (x_1 - y_1)^2$.

(d) $f(\alpha, \beta) = x_1y_2 - x_2y_1$.
:::
