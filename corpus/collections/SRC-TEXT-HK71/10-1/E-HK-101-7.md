::: {.exercise}
Let $f$ be the bilinear form defined in Exercise 6. Show that $f$ is degenerate (not non-degenerate).
Let $V_1$ be the subspace of $V$ consisting of the matrices of trace 0, and let $f_1$ be the restriction of $f$ to $V_1$.
Show that $f_1$ is non-degenerate.
:::

::: solution
Exercise 6 defines
$$
f(A,B)
=n\operatorname{tr}(AB)
-\operatorname{tr}(A)\operatorname{tr}(B)
$$
on $V=M_n(\mathbb C)$.

<1>1. The form $f$ is degenerate on $V$.
::: proof
Let $I$ be the identity matrix. It is nonzero, and for every $B\in V$,
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(I,B)
&=n\operatorname{tr}(IB)
-\operatorname{tr}(I)\operatorname{tr}(B)\\
&=n\operatorname{tr}(B)-n\operatorname{tr}(B)\\
&=0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus the nonzero vector $I$ belongs to the radical of $f$, so $f$ is
degenerate.
:::

<1>2. On the trace-zero subspace the form simplifies to a trace pairing.
::: proof
If $A,B\in V_1$, then
$$
\operatorname{tr}(A)=\operatorname{tr}(B)=0.
$$
Hence
$$
f_1(A,B)=n\operatorname{tr}(AB).
$$
Since $n>0$, the scalar $n$ is nonzero in $\mathbb C$. Therefore it is enough
to prove that
$$
\operatorname{tr}(AB)=0\quad\text{for every }B\in V_1
$$
forces $A=0$.
:::

<1>3. Vanishing against all trace-zero matrices forces every off-diagonal entry of $A$ to vanish.
::: proof
Assume $A\in V_1$ satisfies
$$
\operatorname{tr}(AB)=0
$$
for every $B\in V_1$. For $i\ne j$, the matrix unit $E_{ji}$ has trace zero,
so it belongs to $V_1$. Moreover
$$
\operatorname{tr}(AE_{ji})=A_{ij}.
$$
Thus
$$
A_{ij}=0
\qquad(i\ne j).
$$
Hence $A$ is diagonal.
:::

<1>4. Its diagonal entries are all equal, and trace zero then forces them all to vanish.
::: proof
Assume first $n\ge2$. For any $i\ne j$, the matrix
$$
B=E_{ii}-E_{jj}
$$
has trace zero. Therefore
$$
0=\operatorname{tr}(AB)=A_{ii}-A_{jj}.
$$
All diagonal entries of $A$ are equal, say to $c$. Since $A\in V_1$,
$$
0=\operatorname{tr}(A)=nc.
$$
Thus $c=0$ and $A=0$.

If $n=1$, then $V_1=\{0\}$, so the restriction is non-degenerate
vacuously. Consequently, for every positive integer $n$,
$$
\boxed{f_1\text{ is non-degenerate on }V_1.}
$$
:::
:::
