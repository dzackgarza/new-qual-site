::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be the space of all $2 \times 3$ matrices over $\RR$, and let $f$ be the bilinear form on $V$ defined by $f(X, Y) = \operatorname{trace}(X^tAY)$, where
\[
A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 4 \end{bmatrix}.
\]
Find the matrix of $f$ in the ordered basis
\[
\{E^{11}, E^{12}, E^{13}, E^{21}, E^{22}, E^{23}\}
\]
where $E^{ij}$ is the matrix whose only non-zero entry is a 1 in row $i$ and column $j$.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. $f(E^{ij}, E^{kl}) = A_{ik} \delta_{jl}$.
Proof: $X^t A Y$ has $(j,l)$-entry $\sum_{r,s} X_{rj} A_{rs} Y_{sl}$; for $X = E^{ij}$ and $Y = E^{kl}$, this is $A_{ik}$ when $j = l$ and $0$ otherwise, so its trace is $A_{ik}\delta_{jl}$.

<1>2. The matrix of $f$ in the given basis is
$$\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 0 & 0 & 2 & 0 & 0 \\
0 & 1 & 0 & 0 & 2 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 1 & 0 & 0 & 2 \\
3 & 0 & 0 & 4 & 0 & 0 \\
0 & 3 & 0 & 0 & 4 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 3 & 0 & 0 & 4
\end{bmatrix}.$$
Proof: <1>1, evaluated for all pairs $(i,j), (k,l)$ in the basis order $E^{11}, E^{12}, E^{13}, E^{21}, E^{22}, E^{23}$.

<1>3. Q.E.D.
Proof: <1>2.
:::
