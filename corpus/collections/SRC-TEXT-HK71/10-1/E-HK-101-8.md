::: {.exercise}
Let $f$ be the bilinear form defined in Exercise 6, and let $V_2$ be the subspace of $V$ consisting of all matrices $A$ such that $\operatorname{trace}(A) = 0$ and $A^* = -A$ ($A^*$ is the conjugate transpose of $A$). Denote by $f_2$ the restriction of $f$ to $V_2$.
Show that $f_2$ is negative definite, i.e., that $f_2(A, A) < 0$ for each non-zero $A$ in $V_2$.
:::
