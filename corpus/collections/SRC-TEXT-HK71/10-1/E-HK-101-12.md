::: {.exercise}
Let $f, g$ be bilinear forms on a finite-dimensional vector space $V$.
Suppose $g$ is non-singular.
Show that there exist unique linear operators $T_1, T_2$ on $V$ such that
\[
f(\alpha, \beta) = g(T_1\alpha, \beta) = g(\alpha, T_2\beta)
\]
for all $\alpha, \beta$.
:::
