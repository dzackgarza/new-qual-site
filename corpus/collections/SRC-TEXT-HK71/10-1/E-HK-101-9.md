::: {.exercise}
Let $f$ be the bilinear form defined in Exercise 6. Let $W$ be the set of all matrices $A$ in $V$ such that $f(A, B) = 0$ for all $B$.
Show that $W$ is a subspace of $V$.
Describe $W$ explicitly and find its dimension.
:::

::: {.solution}
Recall
$$
f(A,B)
=n\operatorname{tr}(AB)
-\operatorname{tr}(A)\operatorname{tr}(B).
$$

<1>1. The set $W$ is a subspace of $V$.
::: {.proof}
If $A,C\in W$ and $a,b\in\mathbb C$, then for every $B\in V$,
$$
f(aA+bC,B)
=a f(A,B)+b f(C,B)
=0.
$$
Thus $aA+bC\in W$. Also $0\in W$. Hence $W$ is a vector subspace of
$V$.
:::

<1>2. Every scalar matrix lies in $W$.
::: {.proof}
For $A=cI$ and arbitrary $B$,
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(cI,B)
&=n\operatorname{tr}(cB)
-\operatorname{tr}(cI)\operatorname{tr}(B)\\
&=nc\operatorname{tr}(B)-nc\operatorname{tr}(B)\\
&=0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Therefore
$$
\mathbb CI\subseteq W.
$$
:::

<1>3. Every element of $W$ is scalar.
::: {.proof}
Let $A\in W$ and set
$$
c=\frac{\operatorname{tr}(A)}{n},
\qquad
C=A-cI.
$$
Then $\operatorname{tr}(C)=0$. Since the scalar part $cI$ belongs to $W$ by
step <1>2 and $W$ is a subspace, $C\in W$. Thus for every $B\in V$,
$$
0=f(C,B)=n\operatorname{tr}(CB),
$$
so
$$
\operatorname{tr}(CB)=0.
$$

Take $B=E_{ji}$. Then
$$
\operatorname{tr}(CE_{ji})=C_{ij}.
$$
Hence every entry $C_{ij}$ is zero. Thus $C=0$ and
$$
A=cI.
$$
Consequently
$$
W=\mathbb CI.
$$
:::

<1>4. Compute the dimension.
::: {.proof}
Since $I\ne0$, the subspace $\mathbb CI$ is one-dimensional. Therefore
$$
\boxed{W=\mathbb CI,
\qquad \dim W=1.}
$$
:::
