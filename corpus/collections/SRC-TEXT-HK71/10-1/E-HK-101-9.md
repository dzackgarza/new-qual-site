::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 10.1.9"}
Let $f$ be the bilinear form defined in Exercise 6. Let $W$ be the set of all matrices $A$ in $V$ such that $f(A, B) = 0$ for all $B$.
Show that $W$ is a subspace of $V$.
Describe $W$ explicitly and find its dimension.
:::
