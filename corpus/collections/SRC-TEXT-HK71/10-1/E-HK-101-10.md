::: {.exercise}
Let $f$ be any bilinear form on a finite-dimensional vector space $V$.
Let $W$ be the subspace of all $\beta$ such that $f(\alpha, \beta) = 0$ for every $\alpha$.
Show that
\[
\operatorname{rank} f = \dim V - \dim W.
\]
Use this result and the result of Exercise 9 to compute the rank of the bilinear form defined in Exercise 6.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. Construct the associated linear map into the dual space $V^*$:
<2>1. For each $\beta \in V$, define $L_\beta: V \to F$ by $L_\beta(\alpha) = f(\alpha, \beta)$.
::: {.proof}
linearity of $f$ in the first slot implies $L_\beta \in V^*$.
:::
<2>2. Define the map $T: V \to V^*$ by $T(\beta) = L_\beta$.
::: {.proof}
definition of $T$.
:::
<2>3. $T$ is linear: for all $\beta_1, \beta_2 \in V$, $c \in F$, and $\alpha \in V$:
\[
T(c\beta_1 + \beta_2)(\alpha) = f(\alpha, c\beta_1 + \beta_2) = c f(\alpha, \beta_1) + f(\alpha, \beta_2) = c T(\beta_1)(\alpha) + T(\beta_2)(\alpha).
\]
::: {.proof}
linearity of $f$ in the second slot.
:::

<1>2. Identify the kernel and rank of $T$:
<2>1. Compute the kernel of $T$:
\[
\ker(T) = \{\beta \in V : T(\beta) = 0\} = \{\beta \in V : f(\alpha, \beta) = 0 \text{ for all } \alpha \in V\} = W.
\]
Thus $\operatorname{nullity}(T) = \dim W$.
::: {.proof}
definition of $W$.
:::
<2>2. Let $\mathcal{B} = \{v_1, \dots, v_n\}$ be an ordered basis of $V$, with dual basis $\mathcal{B}^* = \{v_1^*, \dots, v_n^*\}$.
The matrix of the bilinear form $f$ relative to $\mathcal{B}$ is $A = [a_{ij}]$ with $a_{ij} = f(v_i, v_j)$.
::: {.proof}
definition of matrix of a bilinear form.
:::
<2>3. For each basis vector $v_j$, $T(v_j) = \sum_{i=1}^n f(v_i, v_j) v_i^* = \sum_{i=1}^n a_{ij} v_i^*$.
Thus the coordinate vector $[T(v_j)]_{\mathcal{B}^*}$ is the $j$-th column of $A$.
::: {.proof}
coordinate evaluation on dual basis.
:::
<2>4. Therefore $\dim(\operatorname{im} T) = \operatorname{rank}(A) = \operatorname{rank}(f)$.
::: {.proof}
rank of a linear map equals the column rank of its matrix.
:::

<1>3. Apply the Rank-Nullity Theorem:
<2>1. By the Rank-Nullity Theorem applied to $T: V \to V^*$:
\[
\dim V = \operatorname{rank}(T) + \operatorname{nullity}(T) = \operatorname{rank}(f) + \dim W.
\]
::: {.proof}
Rank-Nullity Theorem for linear transformations on finite-dimensional spaces.
:::
<2>2. Rearranging gives:
\[
\operatorname{rank}(f) = \dim V - \dim W.
\]
::: {.proof}
algebraic rearrangement.
:::

<1>4. Conclusion:
$\operatorname{rank}(f) = \dim V - \dim W$. Q.E.D.
::: {.proof}
<1>1 through <1>3.
:::
:::
