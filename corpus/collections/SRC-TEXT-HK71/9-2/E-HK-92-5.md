::: {.exercise}
Let $f$ be the form on $\RR^2$ given by
\[
f((x_1, x_2), (y_1, y_2)) = x_1y_1 + 4x_2y_2 + 2x_1y_2 + 2x_2y_1.
\]
Find an ordered basis in which $f$ is represented by a diagonal matrix.
:::

::: solution
The form factors as
$$
f((x_1,x_2),(y_1,y_2))
=(x_1+2x_2)(y_1+2y_2).
$$
Choose
$$
v_1=(1,0),
\qquad
v_2=(-2,1).
$$
These vectors form a basis because
$$
\det\begin{pmatrix}1&-2\\0&1\end{pmatrix}=1.
$$
Moreover
$$
v_{1,1}+2v_{1,2}=1,
\qquad
v_{2,1}+2v_{2,2}=0.
$$
Hence
$$
f(v_1,v_1)=1,
\qquad
f(v_1,v_2)=f(v_2,v_1)=f(v_2,v_2)=0.
$$
Therefore, in the ordered basis
$$
\mathfrak B=((1,0),(-2,1)),
$$
the matrix of $f$ is
$$
\boxed{[f]_{\mathfrak B}=
\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}.}
$$
:::
