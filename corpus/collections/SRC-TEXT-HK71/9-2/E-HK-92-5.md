::: {.exercise}
Let $f$ be the form on $\RR^2$ given by
\[
f((x_1, x_2), (y_1, y_2)) = x_1y_1 + 4x_2y_2 + 2x_1y_2 + 2x_2y_1.
\]
Find an ordered basis in which $f$ is represented by a diagonal matrix.
:::
