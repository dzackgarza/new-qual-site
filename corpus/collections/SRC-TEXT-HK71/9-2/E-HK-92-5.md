::: {.exercise}
Let $f$ be the form on $\RR^2$ given by
$$
f((x_1, x_2), (y_1, y_2)) = x_1y_1 + 4x_2y_2 + 2x_1y_2 + 2x_2y_1.
$$
Find an ordered basis in which $f$ is represented by a diagonal matrix.

:::

::: {.solution}
There is no retained solution set for this exercise ; the book's text is retained as `assets/attachments/Hoffman-Kunze-Linear-Algebra.pdf` (Hoffman and Kunze, *Linear Algebra*, 2nd ed., 1971), where it is §9.2 Exercise 5 on printed p. 324; the computation is the card's own.

Throughout, $f$ is the form on $\RR^{2}$ given by $f((x_1,x_2),(y_1,y_2))=x_1y_1+4x_2y_2+2x_1y_2+2x_2y_1$, and $[f]_{\mathfrak B}$ is the matrix with entries $f(v_i,v_j)$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #factor}
$f((x_1,x_2),(y_1,y_2))=(x_1+2x_2)(y_1+2y_2)$.

::: pf-proof
Expanding the right side gives $x_1y_1+2x_1y_2+2x_2y_1+4x_2y_2$, which is the displayed form.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #basis}
$v_1=(1,0)$ and $v_2=(-2,1)$ form a basis of $\RR^{2}$, and $v_{1,1}+2v_{1,2}=1$ while $v_{2,1}+2v_{2,2}=0$.

::: pf-proof
The matrix with these vectors as columns is $\begin{pmatrix}1&-2\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$, whose determinant is $1\neq0$, so the two vectors are linearly independent and, being two in a two-dimensional space, form a basis. The two displayed values are $1+2\cdot0=1$ and $-2+2\cdot1=0$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #matrix}
$[f]_{\mathfrak B}=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$ for $\mathfrak B=(v_1,v_2)$.

::: pf-proof
By step [](#factor){.pf-ref}, $f(v_i,v_j)=(v_{i,1}+2v_{i,2})(v_{j,1}+2v_{j,2})$. By step [](#basis){.pf-ref} the first factor is $1$ for $i=1$ and $0$ for $i=2$, and likewise for $j$. So $f(v_1,v_1)=1\cdot1=1$ and $f(v_1,v_2)=f(v_2,v_1)=f(v_2,v_2)=1\cdot0=0$, which is the displayed matrix.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #diagonal}
$f$ is represented by a diagonal matrix in the ordered basis $\mathfrak B$.

::: pf-proof
The matrix of step [](#matrix){.pf-ref} is diagonal, so $\mathfrak B$ is the basis asked for; the form is degenerate, which is consistent with the zero diagonal entry and is not excluded by the exercise.

:::
