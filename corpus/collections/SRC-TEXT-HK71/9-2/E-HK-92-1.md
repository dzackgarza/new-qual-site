::: {.exercise}
Which of the following functions $f$, defined on vectors $\alpha = (x_1, x_2)$ and $\beta = (y_1, y_2)$ in $C^2$, are (sesqui-linear) forms on $C^2$?

(a) $f(\alpha, \beta) = 1$.

(b) $f(\alpha, \beta) = (x_1 - \bar{y}_1)^2 + x_2\bar{y}_2$.

(c) $f(\alpha, \beta) = (x_1 + \overline{y}_1)^2 - (x_1 - \overline{y}_1)^2$.

(d) $f(\alpha, \beta) = x_1\overline{y}_2 - \overline{x}_2 y_1$.
:::
