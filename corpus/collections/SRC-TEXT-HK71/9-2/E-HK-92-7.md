::: {.exercise}
Let $f$ be a form on a finite-dimensional vector space $V$.
Look at the definition of left non-degeneracy given in Exercise 6. Define right non-degeneracy and prove that the form $f$ is left non-degenerate if and only if $f$ is right non-degenerate.
:::

::: {.solution}
The form is called **right non-degenerate** if the only vector $\beta$ such
that
$$
f(\alpha,\beta)=0
$$
for every $\alpha\in V$ is $\beta=0$.

Choose an ordered basis of $V$ and let $A$ be the matrix of $f$ in that basis.
With coordinate columns $X,Y$, the form has the shape
$$
f(X,Y)=Y^*AX.
$$

<1>1. Left non-degeneracy is equivalent to $\ker A=\{0\}$.
::: {.proof}
Fix $X$. The equality
$$
f(X,Y)=Y^*AX=0
$$
for every $Y$ holds exactly when $AX=0$. Thus the left radical corresponds to
$\ker A$. Hence left non-degeneracy is equivalent to
$$
\ker A=\{0\}.
$$
:::

<1>2. Right non-degeneracy is equivalent to $\ker A^*=\{0\}$.
::: {.proof}
Fix $Y$. We have
$$
f(X,Y)=Y^*AX=(A^*Y)^*X.
$$
This vanishes for every $X$ exactly when
$$
A^*Y=0.
$$
Thus the right radical corresponds to $\ker A^*$, and right non-degeneracy is
equivalent to
$$
\ker A^*=\{0\}.
$$
:::

<1>3. The two conditions are equivalent.
::: {.proof}
For every finite matrix,
$$
\operatorname{rank}A^*=\operatorname{rank}A.
$$
Since $A$ and $A^*$ are both $n\times n$, rank-nullity gives
$$
\dim\ker A^*=\dim\ker A.
$$
Therefore
$$
\ker A=\{0\}
\iff
\ker A^*=\{0\}.
$$
Combining steps <1>1 and <1>2,
$$
\boxed{f\text{ is left non-degenerate}\iff f\text{ is right non-degenerate}.}
$$
:::
:::
