::: {.exercise}
Call the form $f$ (left) non-degenerate if 0 is the only vector $\alpha$ such that $f(\alpha, \beta) = 0$ for all $\beta$.
Let $f$ be a form on an inner product space $V$.
Prove that $f$ is non-degenerate if and only if the associated linear operator $T_f$ (Theorem 1) is non-singular.
:::
