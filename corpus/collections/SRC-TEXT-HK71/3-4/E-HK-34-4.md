::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be a two-dimensional vector space over the field $F$, and let $\mathfrak{B}$ be an ordered basis for $V$.
If $T$ is a linear operator on $V$ and
\[
[T]_{\mathfrak{B}} = \begin{bmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{bmatrix}
\]
prove that $T^2 - (a + d)T + (ad - bc)I = 0$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let
\[
A=[T]_{\mathfrak B}=\begin{bmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{bmatrix}.
\]
A direct multiplication gives
\[
A^2-(a+d)A+(ad-bc)I_2=0.
\]
Indeed, the four entries cancel separately. Matrix representation in a fixed basis respects sums, scalar multiples, products, and the identity, so
\[
[T^2-(a+d)T+(ad-bc)I]_{\mathfrak B}=0.
\]
The zero matrix represents only the zero operator. Hence
\[
T^2-(a+d)T+(ad-bc)I=0.
\]
:::
