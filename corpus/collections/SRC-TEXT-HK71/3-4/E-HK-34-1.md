::: {.exercise}
Let $T$ be the linear operator on $C^2$ defined by $T(x_1, x_2) = (x_1, 0)$.
Let $\mathfrak{B}$ be the standard ordered basis for $C^2$ and let $\mathfrak{B}' = \{\alpha_1, \alpha_2\}$ be the ordered basis defined by $\alpha_1 = (1, i)$, $\alpha_2 = (-i, 2)$.

(a) What is the matrix of $T$ relative to the pair $\mathfrak{B}$, $\mathfrak{B}'$?

(b) What is the matrix of $T$ relative to the pair $\mathfrak{B}'$, $\mathfrak{B}$?

(c) What is the matrix of $T$ in the ordered basis $\mathfrak{B}'$?

(d) What is the matrix of $T$ in the ordered basis $\{\alpha_2, \alpha_1\}$?
:::

::: {.solution}
Let
\[
A=\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{bmatrix}
\]
be the matrix of $T$ in the standard basis, and let
\[
P=\begin{bmatrix}1&-i\\ i&2\end{bmatrix}
\]
be the matrix whose columns are $\alpha_1,\alpha_2$. Since $\det P=1$, $P$ is invertible.

For (a), the domain basis is standard and the codomain basis is $\mathfrak B'$, so
\[
[T]_{\mathfrak B,\mathfrak B'}=P^{-1}A
=\begin{bmatrix}2&0\\-i&0\end{bmatrix}.
\]
For (b),
\[
[T]_{\mathfrak B',\mathfrak B}=AP
=\begin{bmatrix}1&-i\\0&0\end{bmatrix}.
\]
For (c),
\[
[T]_{\mathfrak B'}=P^{-1}AP
=\begin{bmatrix}2&-2i\\-i&-1\end{bmatrix}.
\]
If $\mathfrak C=\{\alpha_2,\alpha_1\}$, then using the change-of-basis matrix obtained by swapping the columns of $P$ gives
\[
[T]_{\mathfrak C}
=\begin{bmatrix}-1&-i\\-2i&2\end{bmatrix}.
\]
:::
