::: {.exercise}
Let $T$ be a linear operator on $F^n$, let $A$ be the matrix of $T$ in the standard ordered basis for $F^n$, and let $W$ be the subspace of $F^n$ spanned by the column vectors of $A$.
What does $W$ have to do with $T$?
:::

::: solution
The columns of $A$ are precisely the vectors $T(e_1),\ldots,T(e_n)$, where $e_1,\ldots,e_n$ is the standard basis of $F^n$. Hence
\[
W=\operatorname{span}\{T(e_1),\ldots,T(e_n)\}.
\]
Every vector $x=\sum_j x_je_j$ satisfies
\[
T(x)=\sum_j x_jT(e_j),
\]
so $\operatorname{range}T\subseteq W$. The reverse inclusion is immediate because every $T(e_j)$ lies in the range. Therefore
\[
W=\operatorname{range}T.
\]
In particular, the column rank of $A$ is the rank of $T$.
:::
