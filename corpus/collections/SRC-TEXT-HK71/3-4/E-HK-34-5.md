::: {.exercise}
Let $T$ be the linear operator on $\RR^3$, the matrix of which in the standard ordered basis is
\[
A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \\ -1 & 3 & 4 \end{bmatrix}.
\]
Find a basis for the range of $T$ and a basis for the null space of $T$.
:::

::: {.solution}
The matrix is
\[
A=\begin{bmatrix}1&2&1\\0&1&1\\-1&3&4\end{bmatrix}.
\]
Row reduction gives
\[
\operatorname{rref}(A)=
\begin{bmatrix}1&0&-1\\0&1&1\\0&0&0\end{bmatrix}.
\]
Thus the pivot columns are the first two columns of the original matrix, so a basis for the range is
\[
\left\{(1,0,-1),(2,1,3)\right\}.
\]
The null equations are $x_1-x_3=0$ and $x_2+x_3=0$. Hence
\[
(x_1,x_2,x_3)=t(1,-1,1),
\]
so a basis for the null space is
\[
\{(1,-1,1)\}.
\]
:::
