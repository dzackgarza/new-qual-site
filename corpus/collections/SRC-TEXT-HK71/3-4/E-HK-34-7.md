::: {.exercise}
Let $T$ be the linear operator on $\RR^3$ defined by
\[
T(x_1, x_2, x_3) = (3x_1 + x_3,\; -2x_1 + x_2,\; -x_1 + 2x_2 + 4x_3).
\]

(a) What is the matrix of $T$ in the standard ordered basis for $\RR^3$?

(b) What is the matrix of $T$ in the ordered basis
\[
\{\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3\}
\]
where $\alpha_1 = (1, 0, 1)$, $\alpha_2 = (-1, 2, 1)$, and $\alpha_3 = (2, 1, 1)$?

(c) Prove that $T$ is invertible and give a rule for $T^{-1}$ like the one which defines $T$.
:::

::: solution
In the standard basis,
\[
A=[T]=\begin{bmatrix}3&0&1\\-2&1&0\\-1&2&4\end{bmatrix}.
\]
Let
\[
P=\begin{bmatrix}1&-1&2\\0&2&1\\1&1&1\end{bmatrix}
\]
be the matrix whose columns are the given basis vectors. Then
\[
[T]_{\mathfrak B}=P^{-1}AP
=\begin{bmatrix}
\frac{17}{4}&\frac{35}{4}&\frac{11}{2}\\
-\frac34&\frac{15}{4}&-\frac32\\
-\frac12&-\frac72&0
\end{bmatrix}.
\]

Since $\det A=9\ne0$, $T$ is invertible. Solving $T(x_1,x_2,x_3)=(y_1,y_2,y_3)$, equivalently using
\[
A^{-1}=\begin{bmatrix}
\frac49&\frac29&-\frac19\\
\frac89&\frac{13}{9}&-\frac29\\
-\frac13&-\frac23&\frac13
\end{bmatrix},
\]
gives
\[
T^{-1}(y_1,y_2,y_3)=
\left(
\frac{4y_1+2y_2-y_3}{9},
\frac{8y_1+13y_2-2y_3}{9},
\frac{-y_1-2y_2+y_3}{3}
\right).
\]
:::
