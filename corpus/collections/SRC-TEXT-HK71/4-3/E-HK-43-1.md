::: {.exercise}
Use the Lagrange interpolation formula to find a polynomial $f$ with real coefficients such that $f$ has degree $\leq 3$ and $f(-1) = -6$, $f(0) = 2$, $f(1) = -2$, $f(2) = 6$.
:::

::: {.solution}
The Lagrange interpolation polynomial is
\[
\begin{aligned}
f(x)=&-6\frac{x(x-1)(x-2)}{(-1)(-2)(-3)}
+2\frac{(x+1)(x-1)(x-2)}{(1)(-1)(-2)}\\
&-2\frac{(x+1)x(x-2)}{(2)(1)(-1)}
+6\frac{(x+1)x(x-1)}{(3)(2)(1)}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Simplifying gives
\[
\boxed{f(x)=4x^3-6x^2-2x+2}.
\]
Direct substitution gives $f(-1)=-6$, $f(0)=2$, $f(1)=-2$, and $f(2)=6$.
:::
