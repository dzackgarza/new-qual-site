::: {.exercise}
Let $F$ be the field of real numbers,
\[
A = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 3 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}, \qquad p = (x - 2)(x - 3)(x - 1).
\]

(a) Show that $p(A) = 0$.

(b) Let $P_1, P_2, P_3$ be the Lagrange polynomials for $t_1 = 2$, $t_2 = 3$, $t_3 = 1$.
Compute $E_i = P_i(A)$, $i = 1, 2, 3$.

(c) Show that $E_1 + E_2 + E_3 = I$, $E_iE_j = 0$ if $i \neq j$, $E_i^2 = E_i$.

(d) Show that $A = 2E_1 + 3E_2 + E_3$.
:::
