::: {.exercise}
Let $n$ be a positive integer and $F$ a field.
Suppose $A$ is an $n \times n$ matrix over $F$ and $P$ is an invertible $n \times n$ matrix over $F$.
If $f$ is any polynomial over $F$, prove that
\[
f(P^{-1}AP) = P^{-1}f(A)P.
\]
:::
