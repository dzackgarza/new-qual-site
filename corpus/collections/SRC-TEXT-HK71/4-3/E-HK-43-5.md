::: {.exercise}
Let $n$ be a positive integer and $F$ a field.
Suppose $A$ is an $n \times n$ matrix over $F$ and $P$ is an invertible $n \times n$ matrix over $F$.
If $f$ is any polynomial over $F$, prove that
\[
f(P^{-1}AP) = P^{-1}f(A)P.
\]
:::

::: {.solution}
For every integer $k\ge0$,
\[
(P^{-1}AP)^k=P^{-1}A^kP.
\]
This follows by induction: the case $k=0$ is immediate, and
\[
(P^{-1}A^kP)(P^{-1}AP)=P^{-1}A^{k+1}P.
\]
Now let
\[
f(x)=\sum_{k=0}^r c_kx^k.
\]
Then
\[
\begin{aligned}
f(P^{-1}AP)
&=\sum_{k=0}^r c_k(P^{-1}AP)^k\\
&=\sum_{k=0}^r c_kP^{-1}A^kP\\
&=P^{-1}\left(\sum_{k=0}^r c_kA^k\right)P\\
&=P^{-1}f(A)P.
\end{aligned}
\]
:::
