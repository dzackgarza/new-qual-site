::: {.exercise}
Let $p = (x - 2)(x - 3)(x - 1)$ and let $T$ be any linear operator on $\RR^4$ such that $p(T) = 0$.
Let $P_1, P_2, P_3$ be the Lagrange polynomials of Exercise 3, and let $E_i = P_i(T)$, $i = 1, 2, 3$.
Prove that
\[
E_1 + E_2 + E_3 = I, \qquad E_iE_j = 0 \quad \text{if} \quad i \neq j,
\]
\[
E_i^2 = E_i, \quad \text{and} \quad T = 2E_1 + 3E_2 + E_3.
\]
:::

::: {.solution}
The Lagrange polynomials satisfy
\[
P_i(t_j)=\delta_{ij}
\qquad(t_1,t_2,t_3)=(2,3,1).
\]
The polynomial
\[
P_1+P_2+P_3-1
\]
has degree at most $2$ and vanishes at the three distinct points $1,2,3$, so it is identically zero. Hence
\[
E_1+E_2+E_3=I.
\]
Similarly,
\[
x-(2P_1+3P_2+P_3)
\]
has degree at most $2$ and vanishes at $1,2,3$, so
\[
T=2E_1+3E_2+E_3.
\]

For $i\ne j$, the polynomial $P_iP_j$ vanishes at each of $1,2,3$, hence is divisible by
\[
p(x)=(x-1)(x-2)(x-3).
\]
Therefore $P_i(T)P_j(T)=0$, i.e.
\[
E_iE_j=0.
\]
Likewise, $P_i^2-P_i$ vanishes at $1,2,3$, so it is divisible by $p$ and
\[
E_i^2-E_i=0.
\]
Thus each $E_i$ is idempotent and the $E_i$ are pairwise orthogonal, with the stated decomposition of $I$ and $T$.
:::
