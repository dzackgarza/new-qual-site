::: {.exercise}
Let $p = (x - 2)(x - 3)(x - 1)$ and let $T$ be any linear operator on $\RR^4$ such that $p(T) = 0$.
Let $P_1, P_2, P_3$ be the Lagrange polynomials of Exercise 3, and let $E_i = P_i(T)$, $i = 1, 2, 3$.
Prove that
\[
E_1 + E_2 + E_3 = I, \qquad E_iE_j = 0 \quad \text{if} \quad i \neq j,
\]
\[
E_i^2 = E_i, \quad \text{and} \quad T = 2E_1 + 3E_2 + E_3.
\]
:::
