::: {.exercise}
Let $F$ be a field, $n$ a positive integer, and let $V$ be the space of $n \times n$ matrices over $F$.
If $A$ is a fixed $n \times n$ matrix over $F$, let $T_A$ be the linear operator on $V$ defined by $T_A(B) = AB - BA$.
Consider the family of linear operators $T_A$ obtained by letting $A$ vary over all diagonal matrices.
Prove that the operators in that family are simultaneously diagonalizable.
:::

::: solution
Let $E_{ij}$, $1\leq i,j\leq n$, be the standard matrix units. These $n^2$
matrices form a basis of $V$.

<1>1. Every $E_{ij}$ is an eigenvector for every operator $T_A$ in the family.
::: proof
Fix a diagonal matrix
$$
A=\operatorname{diag}(a_1,\ldots,a_n).
$$
Left multiplication by $A$ scales the $i$th row of $E_{ij}$ by $a_i$, while
right multiplication scales its $j$th column by $a_j$. Explicitly,
$$
AE_{ij}=a_iE_{ij},
\qquad
E_{ij}A=a_jE_{ij}.
$$
Therefore
$$
T_A(E_{ij})=AE_{ij}-E_{ij}A=(a_i-a_j)E_{ij}.
$$
Thus $E_{ij}$ is an eigenvector of $T_A$ for every diagonal $A$; only its
eigenvalue changes with $A$ [@HK71].
:::

<1>2. The matrix-unit basis diagonalizes the whole family simultaneously.
::: proof
Step <1>1 shows that the single basis
$$
\{E_{ij}:1\leq i,j\leq n\}
$$
consists of eigenvectors for every operator $T_A$ with $A$ diagonal. Hence the
matrix of every such $T_A$ in this same basis is diagonal, with diagonal
entries $a_i-a_j$ indexed by the ordered pairs $(i,j)$. Therefore the entire
family is simultaneously diagonalizable.
:::
:::
