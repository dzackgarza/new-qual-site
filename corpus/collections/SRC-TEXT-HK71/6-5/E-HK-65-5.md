::: {.exercise}
Let $F$ be a field, $n$ a positive integer, and let $V$ be the space of $n \times n$ matrices over $F$.
If $A$ is a fixed $n \times n$ matrix over $F$, let $T_A$ be the linear operator on $V$ defined by $T_A(B) = AB - BA$.
Consider the family of linear operators $T_A$ obtained by letting $A$ vary over all diagonal matrices.
Prove that the operators in that family are simultaneously diagonalizable.
:::
