::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 6.5.1"}
Find an invertible real matrix $P$ such that $P^{-1}AP$ and $P^{-1}BP$ are both diagonal, where $A$ and $B$ are the real matrices

(a)
\[
A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 2 \end{bmatrix}, \quad B = \begin{bmatrix} 3 & -8 \\ 0 & -1 \end{bmatrix}
\]

(b)
\[
A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix}, \qquad B = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & a \\ a & 1 \end{bmatrix}.
\]
:::
