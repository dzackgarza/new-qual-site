::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 3.1.5"}
If
\[
\alpha_1 = (1, -1), \quad \beta_1 = (1, 0),
\]
\[
\alpha_2 = (2, -1), \quad \beta_2 = (0, 1),
\]
\[
\alpha_3 = (-3, 2), \quad \beta_3 = (1, 1),
\]
is there a linear transformation $T$ from $\RR^2$ into $\RR^2$ such that $T\alpha_i = \beta_i$ for $i = 1, 2$ and 3?
:::
