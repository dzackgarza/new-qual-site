::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be a vector space and $T$ a linear transformation from $V$ into $V$.
Prove that the following two statements about $T$ are equivalent.

(a) The intersection of the range of $T$ and the null space of $T$ is the zero subspace of $V$.

(b) If $T(T\alpha) = 0$, then $T\alpha = 0$.
:::


::: solution
Assume (a). If $T(T\alpha)=0$, then $T\alpha\in\ker T$. Also $T\alpha\in\operatorname{range}T$ by definition. Hence
\[
T\alpha\in\operatorname{range}T\cap\ker T=\{0\},
\]
so $T\alpha=0$. Thus (b) holds.

Conversely, assume (b). Let $v\in\operatorname{range}T\cap\ker T$. Since $v$ lies in the range, $v=T\alpha$ for some $\alpha$. Since $v$ lies in the nullspace,
\[
T(T\alpha)=T(v)=0.
\]
By (b), $T\alpha=0$, hence $v=0$. Therefore
\[
\operatorname{range}T\cap\ker T=\{0\},
\]
which is (a).
:::
