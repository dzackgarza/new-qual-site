::: {.exercise}
Find the range, rank, null space, and nullity for the zero transformation and the identity transformation on a finite-dimensional space $V$.
:::


::: solution
Let $\dim V=n$.

For the zero transformation $Z(v)=0$:
\[
\operatorname{range}Z=\{0\},
\qquad
\operatorname{rank}Z=0,
\]
\[
\ker Z=V,
\qquad
\operatorname{nullity}Z=n.
\]

For the identity transformation $I(v)=v$:
\[
\operatorname{range}I=V,
\qquad
\operatorname{rank}I=n,
\]
\[
\ker I=\{0\},
\qquad
\operatorname{nullity}I=0.
\]
:::
