::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be an $n$-dimensional vector space over the field $F$ and let $T$ be a linear transformation from $V$ into $V$ such that the range and null space of $T$ are identical.
Prove that $n$ is even.
(Can you give an example of such a linear transformation $T$?)
:::


::: solution
Let
\[
R=\operatorname{range}T=\ker T.
\]
Then
\[
\operatorname{rank}T=\dim R
\]
and
\[
\operatorname{nullity}T=\dim\ker T=\dim R.
\]
By rank-nullity,
\[
n=\operatorname{rank}T+\operatorname{nullity}T=2\dim R,
\]
so $n$ is even.

For example, on $F^2$ define
\[
T(x,y)=(y,0).
\]
Then
\[
\operatorname{range}T=F(1,0)=\ker T.
\]
:::
