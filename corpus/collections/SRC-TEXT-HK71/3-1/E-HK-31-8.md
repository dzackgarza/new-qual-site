::: {.exercise}
Describe explicitly a linear transformation from $\RR^3$ into $\RR^3$ which has as its range the subspace spanned by $(1, 0, -1)$ and $(1, 2, 2)$.
:::


::: solution
Let
\[
v_1=(1,0,-1),
\qquad
v_2=(1,2,2).
\]
Define
\[
T(x,y,z)=xv_1+yv_2.
\]
Explicitly,
\[
T(x,y,z)=(x+y,\;2y,\;-x+2y).
\]
This is linear. Its range is contained in $\operatorname{span}\{v_1,v_2\}$ by construction. Conversely,
\[
T(1,0,0)=v_1,
\qquad
T(0,1,0)=v_2,
\]
so both spanning vectors lie in the range. Hence
\[
\operatorname{range}T=\operatorname{span}\{(1,0,-1),(1,2,2)\}.
\]
:::
