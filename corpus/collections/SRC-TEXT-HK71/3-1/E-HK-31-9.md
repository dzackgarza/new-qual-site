::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be the vector space of all $n \times n$ matrices over the field $F$, and let $B$ be a fixed $n \times n$ matrix.
If
$$
T(A) = AB - BA
$$
verify that $T$ is a linear transformation from $V$ into $V$.
:::


::: {.solution}
No retained solution set covers this exercise; the argument is the card's own, checked against Hoffman--Kunze, *Linear Algebra* (1971), §3.1 Exercise 9.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #closed}
$T(A)=AB-BA$ lies in $V$ for every $A\in V$.

::: pf-proof
$V$ is the space of $n\times n$ matrices over $F$; $AB$ and $BA$ are again $n\times n$ matrices over $F$, and $V$ is closed under subtraction.
:::
:::

::: {.pf-step #additive}
$T(A+C)=T(A)+T(C)$ for $A,C\in V$.

::: pf-proof
$(A+C)B-B(A+C)=AB+CB-BA-BC=(AB-BA)+(CB-BC)=T(A)+T(C)$, using distributivity of matrix multiplication over matrix addition on both sides.
:::
:::

::: {.pf-step #homogeneous}
$T(cA)=cT(A)$ for $c\in F$ and $A\in V$.

::: pf-proof
$(cA)B-B(cA)=c(AB)-c(BA)=c(AB-BA)=cT(A)$, using that scalars commute with matrix multiplication.
:::
:::

::: {.pf-step}
$T$ is a linear transformation from $V$ into $V$.

::: pf-proof
Step [](#closed){.pf-ref} shows that $T$ maps $V$ into $V$, and steps [](#additive){.pf-ref} and [](#homogeneous){.pf-ref} are the two defining properties of a linear transformation.
:::
:::
