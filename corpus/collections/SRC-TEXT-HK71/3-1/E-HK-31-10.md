::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be the set of all complex numbers regarded as a vector space over the field of real numbers (usual operations).
Find a function from $V$ into $V$ which is a linear transformation on the above vector space, but which is not a linear transformation on $C^1$, i.e., which is not complex linear.
:::


::: solution
Take complex conjugation,
\[
T(z)=\overline z.
\]
For real scalars $a,b$ and complex numbers $z,w$,
\[
T(az+bw)=\overline{az+bw}=a\overline z+b\overline w=aT(z)+bT(w),
\]
so $T$ is real-linear.

It is not complex-linear, since
\[
T(i\cdot1)=T(i)=-i,
\]
whereas complex linearity would require
\[
iT(1)=i.
\]
Thus $T$ is linear over $\RR$ but not over $\CC$.
:::
