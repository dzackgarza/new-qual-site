::: {.exercise}
Let $A$ be an $n \times n$ matrix with complex entries such that $A^* = -A$, and let $B = e^A$.
Show that

(a) $\det B = e^{\operatorname{tr} A}$;

(b) $B^* = e^{-A}$;

(c) $B$ is unitary.
:::
