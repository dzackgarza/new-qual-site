::: {.exercise}
Let $\mathfrak{F}$ be a commuting family of diagonalizable normal operators on a finite-dimensional inner product space $V$ and $\mathfrak{A}$ the self-adjoint algebra generated by $\mathfrak{F}$.
Let $\mathfrak{Q}$ be the self-adjoint algebra generated by $\mathfrak{F}$ and the identity operator $I$.
Show that

(a) $\mathfrak{Q}$ is the set of all operators on $V$ of the form $cI + T$ where $c$ is a scalar and $T$ an operator in $\mathfrak{A}$;

(b) There is at most one root $r$ of $\mathfrak{Q}$ such that $r(T) = 0$ for all $T$ in $\mathfrak{A}$.

(c) If one of the roots of $\mathfrak{Q}$ is 0 on $\mathfrak{A}$, the projections $P_1, \ldots, P_k$ in the resolution of the identity defined by $\mathfrak{F}$ may be indexed in such a way that $\mathfrak{A}$ consists of all operators on $V$ of the form
\[
T = \sum_{j=2}^{k} c_jP_j
\]
where $c_2, \ldots, c_k$ are arbitrary scalars.

(d) $\mathfrak{Q} = \mathfrak{A}$ if and only if for each root $r$ of $\mathfrak{Q}$ there exists an operator $T$ in $\mathfrak{A}$ such that $r(T) \neq 0$.
:::
