::: {.exercise}
Let $T$ be the linear operator on $C^2$ defined by $T\epsilon_1 = (1 + i, 2)$, $T\epsilon_2 = (i, i)$.
Using the standard inner product, find the matrix of $T^*$ in the standard ordered basis.
Does $T$ commute with $T^*$?
:::
