::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be the space of $n \times n$ matrices over the complex numbers, with the inner product $(A, B) = \operatorname{tr}(AB^*)$.
Let $P$ be a fixed invertible matrix in $V$, and let $T_P$ be the linear operator on $V$ defined by $T_P(A) = P^{-1}AP$.
Find the adjoint of $T_P$.
:::

::: solution
The adjoint is
$$
\boxed{
T_P^*(B)=(P^*)^{-1}BP^*.}
$$
Equivalently,
$$
T_P^*=T_{P^*}.
$$

<1>1. Move the factors around the trace.
::: proof
For arbitrary matrices $A,B$,
$$
\begin{aligned}
(T_P(A),B)
&=\operatorname{tr}\!\left(P^{-1}APB^*\right)\\
&=\operatorname{tr}\!\left(APB^*P^{-1}\right),
\end{aligned}
$$
where the second equality uses cyclicity of trace [@HK71].
:::

<1>2. Identify the matrix whose adjoint appears in the second slot.
::: proof
Set
$$
S(B)=(P^*)^{-1}BP^*.
$$
Then
$$
S(B)^*
=P B^* P^{-1},
$$
because $((P^*)^{-1})^*=P^{-1}$ and $(P^*)^*=P$. Hence
$$
\operatorname{tr}\!\left(APB^*P^{-1}\right)
=\operatorname{tr}\!\left(A S(B)^*\right)
=(A,S(B)).
$$
Thus
$$
(T_P(A),B)=(A,S(B))
$$
for every $A,B$. By uniqueness of the adjoint,
$$
T_P^*=S,
$$
which is the displayed formula.
:::
:::
