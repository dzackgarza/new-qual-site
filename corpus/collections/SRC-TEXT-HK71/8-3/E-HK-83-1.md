::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 8.3.1"}
Let $V$ be the space $C^2$, with the standard inner product.
Let $T$ be the linear operator defined by $T\epsilon_1 = (1, -2)$, $T\epsilon_2 = (i, -1)$.
If $\alpha = (x_1, x_2)$, find $T^*\alpha$.
:::
