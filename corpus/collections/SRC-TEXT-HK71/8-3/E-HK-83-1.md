::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be the space $C^2$, with the standard inner product.
Let $T$ be the linear operator defined by $T\epsilon_1 = (1, -2)$, $T\epsilon_2 = (i, -1)$.
If $\alpha = (x_1, x_2)$, find $T^*\alpha$.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. Matrix representation of $T$ in the standard orthonormal basis:
<2>1. The standard basis $\mathcal{B} = \{e_1, e_2\}$ is an orthonormal basis of $\mathbb{C}^2$ under the standard inner product:
\[
\langle (u_1, u_2), (v_1, v_2) \rangle = u_1 \overline{v_1} + u_2 \overline{v_2}.
\]
<2>2. From $T e_1 = (1, -2) = 1 e_1 - 2 e_2$ and $T e_2 = (i, -1) = i e_1 - 1 e_2$, the matrix of $T$ with respect to $\mathcal{B}$ is:
\[
[T]_\mathcal{B} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & i \\ -2 & -1 \end{pmatrix}.
\]

<1>2. Computation of the adjoint matrix $[T^*]_\mathcal{B}$:
<2>1. With respect to an orthonormal basis, the matrix of the adjoint operator $T^*$ is the conjugate transpose of $[T]_\mathcal{B}$:
\[
[T^*]_\mathcal{B} = [T]_\mathcal{B}^* = \overline{[T]_\mathcal{B}}^T = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & \overline{-2} \\ \overline{i} & \overline{-1} \end{pmatrix}^T = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -2 \\ -i & -1 \end{pmatrix}^T = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -2 \\ -i & -1 \end{pmatrix}.
\]
<2>2. For any vector $\alpha = (x_1, x_2) \in \mathbb{C}^2$:
\[
[T^*\alpha]_\mathcal{B} = [T^*]_\mathcal{B} [\alpha]_\mathcal{B} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -2 \\ -i & -1 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} x_1 \\ x_2 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} x_1 - 2x_2 \\ -i x_1 - x_2 \end{pmatrix}.
\]
Thus:
\[
T^*\alpha = (x_1 - 2x_2, \, -i x_1 - x_2).
\]

<1>3. Verification of the adjoint identity $\langle T\beta, \alpha \rangle = \langle \beta, T^*\alpha \rangle$:
<2>1. For $\beta = (y_1, y_2)$:
\[
\langle T\beta, \alpha \rangle = (y_1 + i y_2)\overline{x_1} + (-2y_1 - y_2)\overline{x_2} = y_1(\overline{x_1} - 2\overline{x_2}) + y_2(i \overline{x_1} - \overline{x_2}).
\]
\[
\langle \beta, T^*\alpha \rangle = y_1 \overline{(x_1 - 2x_2)} + y_2 \overline{(-i x_1 - x_2)} = y_1(\overline{x_1} - 2\overline{x_2}) + y_2(i \overline{x_1} - \overline{x_2}).
\]
Both expressions are equal.

<1>4. Conclusion:
$T^*\alpha = (x_1 - 2x_2, -i x_1 - x_2)$. Q.E.D.
:::
