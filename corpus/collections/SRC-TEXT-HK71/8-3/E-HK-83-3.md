::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be $C^3$ with the standard inner product.
Let $T$ be the linear operator on $V$ whose matrix in the standard ordered basis is defined by
\[
A_{jk} = i^{j+k}, \quad (i^2 = -1).
\]
Find a basis for the null space of $T^*$.
:::

::: {.solution}
**Goal.** For $T$ with matrix $A_{jk} = i^{j+k}$ ($j,k = 1,2,3$), find a basis for $\ker T^*$.

<1>1. Compute the matrix $A$.
<2>1. $A_{jk} = i^{j+k}$, so
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} i^2 & i^3 & i^4 \\ i^3 & i^4 & i^5 \\ i^4 & i^5 & i^6 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -1 & -i & 1 \\ -i & 1 & i \\ 1 & i & -1 \end{pmatrix}.
$$
::: {.proof}
$i^2 = -1$, $i^3 = -i$, $i^4 = 1$, $i^5 = i$, $i^6 = -1$.
:::

<1>2. $T^*$ has matrix $A^* = \bar A^T$.
::: {.proof}
the adjoint of an operator with matrix $A$ (in an orthonormal basis) has matrix the conjugate transpose.
:::

<1>3. Compute $A^*$.
<2>1. $\bar A = \begin{pmatrix} -1 & i & 1 \\ i & 1 & -i \\ 1 & -i & -1 \end{pmatrix}$.
::: {.proof}
conjugate each entry.
:::
<2>2. $A^* = \bar A^T = \begin{pmatrix} -1 & i & 1 \\ i & 1 & -i \\ 1 & -i & -1 \end{pmatrix}$.
::: {.proof}
$A$ is symmetric ($A_{jk} = A_{kj}$), so $\bar A^T = \bar A$.
:::

<1>4. Find $\ker A^*$.
<2>1. Solve $A^* x = 0$: the rows are $(-1, i, 1)$, $(i, 1, -i)$, $(1, -i, -1)$.
::: {.proof}
write out the system.
:::
<2>2. The rows are linearly dependent: row 3 = $-1 \cdot$ row 1, and row 2 = $-i \cdot$ row 1.
::: {.proof}
$(-1)(-1, i, 1) = (1, -i, -1)$ and $(-i)(-1, i, 1) = (i, 1, -i)$.
:::
<2>3. So the null space is the set of $x$ with $-x_1 + i x_2 + x_3 = 0$, i.e. $x_3 = x_1 - i x_2$.
::: {.proof}
the single independent equation is $-x_1 + i x_2 + x_3 = 0$.
:::
<2>4. A basis is $\theset{(1, 0, 1), (0, 1, -i)}$.
::: {.proof}
setting $(x_1, x_2) = (1, 0)$ gives $(1, 0, 1)$; setting $(x_1, x_2) = (0, 1)$ gives $(0, 1, -i)$; these are independent and span the $2$-dimensional null space.
:::

<1>5. Q.E.D.
::: {.proof}
<1>4.4 gives the basis $\theset{(1,0,1), (0,1,-i)}$ for $\ker T^*$.
:::
