::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be $C^3$ with the standard inner product.
Let $T$ be the linear operator on $V$ whose matrix in the standard ordered basis is defined by
\[
A_{jk} = i^{j+k}, \quad (i^2 = -1).
\]
Find a basis for the null space of $T^*$.
:::
