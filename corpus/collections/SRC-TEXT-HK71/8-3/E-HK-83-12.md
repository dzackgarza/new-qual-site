::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional complex inner product space, and let $T$ be a linear operator on $V$.
Prove that $T$ is self-adjoint if and only if $(T\alpha \mid \alpha)$ is real for every $\alpha$ in $V$.
:::
