::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional inner product space and $T$ a linear operator on $V$.
Show that the range of $T^*$ is the orthogonal complement of the null space of $T$.
:::

::: solution
Let
$$
N=\ker T,
\qquad R^*=\operatorname{range}T^*.
$$
We prove
$$
\boxed{R^*=N^\perp.}
$$

<1>1. Every vector in the range of $T^*$ is orthogonal to the null space of $T$.
::: proof
Take $y\in R^*$. Then $y=T^*x$ for some $x\in V$. If $n\in N$, then
$Tn=0$, and the defining identity for the adjoint gives
$$
(n\mid y)=(n\mid T^*x)=(Tn\mid x)=0.
$$
Thus $y$ is orthogonal to every vector in $N$, so
$$
R^*\subseteq N^\perp.
$$
:::

<1>2. The two subspaces have the same dimension.
::: proof
In an orthonormal basis, the matrix of $T^*$ is the conjugate transpose of the
matrix of $T$, so
$$
\operatorname{rank}T^*=\operatorname{rank}T
$$
[@HK71]. Hence rank-nullity gives
$$
\dim R^*
=\operatorname{rank}T
=\dim V-\dim\ker T.
$$
For a subspace of a finite-dimensional inner product space,
$$
\dim N^\perp=\dim V-\dim N
$$
[@HK71]. Therefore
$$
\dim R^*=\dim N^\perp.
$$
Combined with the inclusion from step <1>1, this yields
$$
R^*=N^\perp.
$$
:::
:::
