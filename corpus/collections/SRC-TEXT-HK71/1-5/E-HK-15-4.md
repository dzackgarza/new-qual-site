::: {.exercise}
For the matrix $A$ of Exercise 2, find elementary matrices $E_1, E_2, \ldots, E_k$ such that
\[
E_k \cdots E_2E_1A = I.
\]
:::


::: solution
For
\[
A=\begin{bmatrix}1&-1&1\\2&0&1\\3&0&1\end{bmatrix},
\]
perform the row operations
\[
R_2\leftarrow R_2-2R_1,\quad
R_3\leftarrow R_3-3R_1,\quad
R_2\leftarrow \tfrac12R_2,\quad
R_3\leftarrow R_3-3R_2,
\]
\[
R_3\leftarrow -2R_3,\quad
R_2\leftarrow R_2+\tfrac12R_3,\quad
R_1\leftarrow R_1-R_3,\quad
R_1\leftarrow R_1+R_2.
\]
They reduce $A$ to $I_3$. The corresponding elementary matrices are
\[
E_1=\begin{bmatrix}1&0&0\\-2&1&0\\0&0&1\end{bmatrix},\quad
E_2=\begin{bmatrix}1&0&0\\0&1&0\\-3&0&1\end{bmatrix},\quad
E_3=\begin{bmatrix}1&0&0\\0&\frac12&0\\0&0&1\end{bmatrix},
\]
\[
E_4=\begin{bmatrix}1&0&0\\0&1&0\\0&-3&1\end{bmatrix},\quad
E_5=\begin{bmatrix}1&0&0\\0&1&0\\0&0&-2\end{bmatrix},\quad
E_6=\begin{bmatrix}1&0&0\\0&1&\frac12\\0&0&1\end{bmatrix},
\]
\[
E_7=\begin{bmatrix}1&0&-1\\0&1&0\\0&0&1\end{bmatrix},\qquad
E_8=\begin{bmatrix}1&1&0\\0&1&0\\0&0&1\end{bmatrix}.
\]
Therefore
\[
E_8E_7E_6E_5E_4E_3E_2E_1A=I_3.
\]
:::
