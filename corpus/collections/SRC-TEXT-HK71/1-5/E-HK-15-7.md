::: {.exercise}
Let $A$ and $B$ be $2 \times 2$ matrices such that $AB = I$.
Prove that $BA = I$.
:::
\n::: solution\nFrom $AB=I_2$, taking determinants gives\n\[\n\det(A)\det(B)=1.\n\]\nHence $\det(A)\ne0$, so $A$ is invertible. Multiplying $AB=I_2$ on the left by $A^{-1}$ gives\n\[\nB=A^{-1}.\n\]\nTherefore\n\[\nBA=A^{-1}A=I_2.\n\]\n:::\n
