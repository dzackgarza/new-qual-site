::: {.exercise}
Let $A$ and $B$ be $2 \times 2$ matrices such that $AB = I$.
Prove that $BA = I$.
:::


::: solution
From $AB=I_2$, taking determinants gives
\[
\det(A)\det(B)=1.
\]
Hence $\det(A)\ne0$, so $A$ is invertible. Multiplying $AB=I_2$ on the left by $A^{-1}$ gives
\[
B=A^{-1}.
\]
Therefore
\[
BA=A^{-1}A=I_2.
\]
:::
