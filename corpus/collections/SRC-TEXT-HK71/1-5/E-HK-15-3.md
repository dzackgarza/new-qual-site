::: {.exercise}
Find two different $2 \times 2$ matrices $A$ such that $A^2 = 0$ but $A \neq 0$.
:::

::: solution
Two examples are
\[
A_1=\begin{bmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
A_2=\begin{bmatrix}0&0\\1&0\end{bmatrix}.
\]
Both are nonzero, and direct multiplication gives
\[A_1^2=A_2^2=0.\]
Since $A_1\ne A_2$, these are two different required matrices.
:::
