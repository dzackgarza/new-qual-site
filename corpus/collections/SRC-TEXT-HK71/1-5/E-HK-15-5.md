::: {.exercise}
Let
\[
A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -1 \\ 2 & 2 \\ 1 & 0 \end{bmatrix}, \qquad B = \begin{bmatrix} 3 & 1 \\ -4 & 4 \end{bmatrix}.
\]
Is there a matrix $C$ such that $CA = B$?
:::
