::: {.exercise}
Let
\[
A = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & -1 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 & 1 \end{bmatrix}, \qquad B = \begin{bmatrix} 3 \\ 1 \\ -1 \end{bmatrix}, \qquad C = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -1 \end{bmatrix}.
\]
Compute $ABC$ and $CAB$.
:::

::: solution
First compute
\[
AB=
\begin{bmatrix}2&-1&1\\1&2&1\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}3\\1\\-1\end{bmatrix}
=\begin{bmatrix}4\\4\end{bmatrix}.
\]
Hence
\[
ABC=\begin{bmatrix}4\\4\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}1&-1\end{bmatrix}
=\begin{bmatrix}4&-4\\4&-4\end{bmatrix}.
\]
Also
\[
CA=\begin{bmatrix}1&-1\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}2&-1&1\\1&2&1\end{bmatrix}
=\begin{bmatrix}1&-3&0\end{bmatrix},
\]
so
\[
CAB=\begin{bmatrix}1&-3&0\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}3\\1\\-1\end{bmatrix}=0.
\]
Therefore
\[ABC=\begin{bmatrix}4&-4\\4&-4\end{bmatrix},\qquad CAB=\begin{bmatrix}0\end{bmatrix}.\]
:::
