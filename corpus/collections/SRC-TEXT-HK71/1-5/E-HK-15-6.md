::: {.exercise}
Let $A$ be an $m \times n$ matrix and $B$ an $n \times k$ matrix.
Show that the columns of $C = AB$ are linear combinations of the columns of $A$.
If $\alpha_1, \ldots, \alpha_n$ are the columns of $A$ and $\gamma_1, \ldots, \gamma_k$ are the columns of $C$, then
\[
\gamma_j = \sum_{r=1}^{n} B_{rj}\alpha_r.
\]
:::
