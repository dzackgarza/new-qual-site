::: {.exercise}
Let
\[
A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -1 & 1 \\ 2 & 0 & 1 \\ 3 & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}, \quad B = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & -2 \\ 1 & 3 \\ 4 & 4 \end{bmatrix}.
\]
Verify directly that $A(AB) = A^2B$.
:::

::: solution
Compute
\[
AB=\begin{bmatrix}5&-1\\8&0\\10&-2\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
A^2=\begin{bmatrix}2&-1&1\\5&-2&3\\6&-3&4\end{bmatrix}.
\]
Then
\[
A(AB)=
\begin{bmatrix}7&-3\\20&-4\\25&-5\end{bmatrix}
\]
and
\[
A^2B=
\begin{bmatrix}7&-3\\20&-4\\25&-5\end{bmatrix}.
\]
Thus $A(AB)=A^2B$, as required.
:::
