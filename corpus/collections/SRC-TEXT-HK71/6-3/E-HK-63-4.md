::: {.exercise}
Is the matrix $A$ of Exercise 3 similar over the field of complex numbers to a diagonal matrix?
:::

::: {.solution}
No. A diagonalizable matrix has a minimal polynomial which is a product of distinct linear factors: if \(A=SDS^{-1}\) with \(D\) diagonal, then the product of \(x-\lambda\) over the distinct diagonal entries annihilates \(D\), hence \(A\).

By Exercise 3,
\[
m_A(x)=x^2(x-1)^2,
\]
which has repeated factors. Therefore \(A\) is not diagonalizable over \(\mathbb C\).
:::
