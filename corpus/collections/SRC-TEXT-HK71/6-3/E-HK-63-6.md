::: {.exercise}
Find a $3 \times 3$ matrix for which the minimal polynomial is $x^2$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Take
\[
A=\begin{bmatrix}
0&1&0\\
0&0&0\\
0&0&0
\end{bmatrix}.
\]
Then \(A\ne0\) but \(A^2=0\). Hence \(x^2\) annihilates \(A\), while neither the constant polynomial \(1\) nor the degree-one polynomial \(x\) annihilates \(A\). Therefore the minimal polynomial is
\[
m_A(x)=x^2.
\]
:::
