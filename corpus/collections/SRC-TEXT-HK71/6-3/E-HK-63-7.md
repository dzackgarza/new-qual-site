::: {.exercise}
Let $n$ be a positive integer, and let $V$ be the space of polynomials over $\RR$ which have degree at most $n$ (throw in the 0-polynomial).
Let $D$ be the differentiation operator on $V$.
What is the minimal polynomial for $D$?
:::

::: {.solution}
On \(V=\mathbb R[x]_{\le n}\), repeated differentiation gives
\[
D^{n+1}=0,
\]
so the minimal polynomial divides \(x^{n+1}\). On the other hand,
\[
D^n(x^n)=n!\ne0,
\]
so \(D^n\ne0\). Therefore no smaller power of \(x\) annihilates \(D\), and
\[
m_D(x)=x^{n+1}.
\]
:::
