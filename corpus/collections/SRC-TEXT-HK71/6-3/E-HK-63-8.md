::: {.exercise}
Let $P$ be the operator on $\RR^2$ which projects each vector onto the $x$-axis, parallel to the $y$-axis: $P(x, y) = (x, 0)$.
Show that $P$ is linear.
What is the minimal polynomial for $P$?
:::

::: {.solution}
For \((x_1,y_1),(x_2,y_2)\in\mathbb R^2\) and \(a,b\in\mathbb R\),
\[
P\bigl(a(x_1,y_1)+b(x_2,y_2)\bigr)
=(ax_1+bx_2,0)
=aP(x_1,y_1)+bP(x_2,y_2),
\]
so \(P\) is linear.

Also
\[
P^2=P,
\]
so \(P(P-I)=0\), and therefore the minimal polynomial divides \(x(x-1)\). Since \(P\ne0\) and \(P\ne I\), neither \(x\) nor \(x-1\) annihilates \(P\). Thus
\[
m_P(x)=x(x-1).
\]
:::
