::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be the vector space of $n \times n$ matrices over the field $F$.
Let $A$ be a fixed $n \times n$ matrix.
Let $T$ be the linear operator on $V$ defined by
\[
T(B) = AB.
\]
Show that the minimal polynomial for $T$ is the minimal polynomial for $A$.
:::

::: {.solution}
For every polynomial \(f(x)=\sum_{j=0}^r c_jx^j\) and every matrix \(B\), repeated application of \(T(B)=AB\) gives
\[
T^j(B)=A^jB,
\]
and hence
\[
f(T)(B)=\sum_{j=0}^r c_jA^jB=f(A)B.
\]
Therefore \(f(T)=0\) if \(f(A)=0\).

Conversely, if \(f(T)=0\), evaluate at \(B=I\):
\[
0=f(T)(I)=f(A)I=f(A).
\]
Thus the polynomials annihilating \(T\) are exactly the polynomials annihilating \(A\). Their monic generators are therefore equal, so
\[
m_T=m_A.
\]
:::
