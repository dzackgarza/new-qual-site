::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be the vector space of $n \times n$ matrices over the field $F$.
Let $A$ be a fixed $n \times n$ matrix.
Let $T$ be the linear operator on $V$ defined by
\[
T(B) = AB.
\]
Show that the minimal polynomial for $T$ is the minimal polynomial for $A$.
:::
