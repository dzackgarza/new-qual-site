::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be an $n$-dimensional vector space over the field $F$, and let $N$ be a nilpotent linear operator on $V$.
Suppose $N^{n-1} \neq 0$, and let $\alpha$ be any vector in $V$ such that $N^{n-1}\alpha \neq 0$.
Prove that $\alpha$ is a cyclic vector for $N$.
What exactly is the matrix of $N$ in the ordered basis $\{\alpha, N\alpha, \ldots, N^{n-1}\alpha\}$?
:::
