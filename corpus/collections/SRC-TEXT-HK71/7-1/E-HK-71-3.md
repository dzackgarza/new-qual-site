::: {.exercise}
Let $T$ be the linear operator on $C^3$ which is represented in the standard ordered basis by the matrix
\[
\begin{bmatrix} 1 & i & 0 \\ -1 & 2 & -i \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix}.
\]
Find the $T$-annihilator of the vector $(1, 0, 0)$.
Find the $T$-annihilator of $(1, 0, i)$.
:::

::: solution
**Goal:** Compute each cyclic subspace and the first polynomial relation that kills each generating vector.

<1> Let
$$
T=\begin{bmatrix}
1 & i & 0\\
-1 & 2 & -i\\
0 & 1 & 1
\end{bmatrix}.
$$

<1> For $v_1=(1,0,0)$, set $v=T v_1$ and $v_2=T^2 v_1$.
    Then
    $$
    T v_1=(1,-1,0),\quad T^2 v_1=(1-i,-3,-1),\quad T^3 v_1=(1-4i,-7+2i,-4).
    $$
    Solve
    $$
    (T^3+aT^2+bT+c)v_1=0
    $$
    to get
    $$
    T^3v_1=4T^2v_1-(5+2i)Tv_1+(2+2i)v_1.
    $$
    So
    $$
    p_{v_1}(t)=t^3-4t^2+(5+2i)t-(2+2i)
    $$
    annihilates $v_1$ and is minimal.

<1> For $v_2=(1,0,i)$,
    $$
    Tv_2=v_2.
    $$
    Hence $(T-1)v_2=0$, so the annihilator is $t-1$.

:::
