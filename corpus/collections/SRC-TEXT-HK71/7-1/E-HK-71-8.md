::: {.exercise}
Let $T$ be a linear operator on the finite-dimensional vector space $V$.
Suppose $T$ has a cyclic vector.
Prove that if $U$ is any linear operator which commutes with $T$, then $U$ is a polynomial in $T$.
:::
