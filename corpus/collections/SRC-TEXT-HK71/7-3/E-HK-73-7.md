::: {.exercise}
If $A$ is an $n \times n$ matrix over the field $F$ with characteristic polynomial
\[
f = (x - c_1)^{d_1} \cdots (x - c_k)^{d_k}
\]
what is the trace of $A$?
:::

::: solution
The trace is
$$
\boxed{\operatorname{tr}(A)=d_1c_1+\cdots+d_kc_k.}
$$

Indeed, over a splitting field the Jordan form of $A$ has exactly $d_i$
diagonal entries equal to $c_i$ for each $i$. Similarity preserves trace, and
the trace of a triangular matrix is the sum of its diagonal entries [@HK71].
Therefore
$$
\operatorname{tr}(A)=\sum_{i=1}^k d_i c_i.
$$
Equivalently, this is the negative of the coefficient of $x^{n-1}$ in the
monic characteristic polynomial.
:::
