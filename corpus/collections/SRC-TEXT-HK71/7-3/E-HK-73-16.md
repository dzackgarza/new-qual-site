::: {.exercise}
Use the result of Exercise 15 to prove that if $c$ is a non-zero complex number and $N$ is a nilpotent complex matrix, then $(cI + N)$ has a square root.
Now use the Jordan form to prove that every non-singular complex $n \times n$ matrix has a square root.
:::
