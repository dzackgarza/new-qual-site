::: {.exercise}
Let $N_1$ and $N_2$ be $3 \times 3$ nilpotent matrices over the field $F$.
Prove that $N_1$ and $N_2$ are similar if and only if they have the same minimal polynomial.
:::

::: {.solution}
No retained solution set covers this exercise; the argument is the card's own, checked against Hoffman--Kunze, *Linear Algebra* (1971), §7.3 Exercise 1. A nilpotent matrix has its only eigenvalue $0$ in the field, so its Jordan form exists over $F$; the minimal polynomial is $x^r$ with $r$ the largest block size, and the three partitions of $3$ give the three similarity classes.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #forward}
If $N_1$ and $N_2$ are similar, they have the same minimal polynomial.

::: pf-proof
For an invertible $P$, $N_2=P^{-1}N_1P$ gives $N_2^k=P^{-1}N_1^kP$ for every $k$, so $N_2^k=0$ exactly when $N_1^k=0$; the least such $k$ is the degree of the minimal polynomial in each case.
:::
:::

::: {.pf-step #blocks}
A nilpotent $3\times3$ matrix over $F$ has a Jordan form over $F$, and the minimal polynomial is $x^r$ where $r$ is the size of its largest Jordan block.

::: pf-proof
Its characteristic polynomial is $x^3$, which splits over $F$, so the Jordan form exists over $F$; all blocks are $J_k(0)$. The minimal polynomial of $J_k(0)^{\oplus\cdots}$ is the least power of $x$ annihilating every block, that is $x^r$ with $r$ the largest block size.
:::
:::

::: {.pf-step #partitions}
The possible block-size multisets for dimension $3$ are $1+1+1$, $2+1$ and $3$, giving minimal polynomials $x$, $x^2$ and $x^3$ respectively.

::: pf-proof
The block sizes are positive integers summing to $3$; the three multisets listed are exactly those, and the largest part is $1$, $2$ and $3$ respectively.
:::
:::

::: {.pf-step #converse}
If $N_1$ and $N_2$ have the same minimal polynomial, they are similar.

::: pf-proof
By steps [](#blocks){.pf-ref} and [](#partitions){.pf-ref} the degree of the minimal polynomial determines the block-size multiset: $x$ gives three blocks of size $1$, $x^2$ a block of size $2$ and one of size $1$, and $x^3$ a single block of size $3$. Two matrices with the same block sizes have the same Jordan form, and matrices with the same Jordan form are similar.
:::
:::

::: {.pf-step}
$N_1$ and $N_2$ are similar if and only if they have the same minimal polynomial.

::: pf-proof
Step [](#forward){.pf-ref} is the necessity and step [](#converse){.pf-ref} the sufficiency.
:::
:::
