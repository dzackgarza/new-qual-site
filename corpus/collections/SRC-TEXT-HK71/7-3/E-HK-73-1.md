::: {.exercise}
Let $N_1$ and $N_2$ be $3 \times 3$ nilpotent matrices over the field $F$.
Prove that $N_1$ and $N_2$ are similar if and only if they have the same minimal polynomial.
:::

::: {.solution}
Similarity always preserves the minimal polynomial, so only the converse needs
proof.

For a nilpotent $3\times3$ matrix, the Jordan blocks all have eigenvalue zero.
If the largest Jordan block has size $r$, then the minimal polynomial is
$$
x^r
$$
[@HK71]. Since the total dimension is three, there are only three possibilities:
$$
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
m_N(x)&\text{Jordan block sizes}&\text{Jordan form}\\ \hline
x&1+1+1&0_3\\
x^2&2+1&J_2(0)\oplus J_1(0)\\
x^3&3&J_3(0).
\end{array}
$$
Thus the minimal polynomial uniquely determines the Jordan form. If $N_1$ and
$N_2$ have the same minimal polynomial, they have the same Jordan form and are
therefore similar. Hence
$$
\boxed{N_1\sim N_2\iff m_{N_1}=m_{N_2}.}
$$
:::
