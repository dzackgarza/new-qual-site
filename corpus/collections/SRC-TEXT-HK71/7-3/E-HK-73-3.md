::: {.exercise}
If $A$ is a complex $5 \times 5$ matrix with characteristic polynomial
\[
f = (x - 2)^3(x + 7)^2
\]
and minimal polynomial $p = (x - 2)^2(x + 7)$, what is the Jordan form for $A$?
:::

::: solution
For the eigenvalue $2$, the characteristic polynomial gives total multiplicity
$3$, while the exponent $2$ in the minimal polynomial says the largest Jordan
block has size $2$. Hence the block sizes are $2+1$.

For the eigenvalue $-7$, the total multiplicity is $2$, while the exponent $1$
in the minimal polynomial says every Jordan block has size $1$. Hence the
block sizes are $1+1$ [@HK71].

Therefore, up to permutation of Jordan blocks,
$$
\boxed{
J(A)=J_2(2)\oplus J_1(2)\oplus J_1(-7)\oplus J_1(-7).}
$$
Explicitly, one possible ordering is
$$
\begin{pmatrix}
2&1&0&0&0\\
0&2&0&0&0\\
0&0&2&0&0\\
0&0&0&-7&0\\
0&0&0&0&-7
\end{pmatrix}.
$$
:::
