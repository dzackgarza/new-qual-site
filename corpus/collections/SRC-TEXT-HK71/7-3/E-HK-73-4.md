::: {.exercise}
How many possible Jordan forms are there for a $6 \times 6$ complex matrix with characteristic polynomial $(x + 2)^4(x - 1)^2$?
:::

::: {.solution}
Jordan forms are determined, for each eigenvalue separately, by a partition of
its algebraic multiplicity into Jordan block sizes [@HK71].

For eigenvalue $-2$, multiplicity $4$, the five partitions are
$$
4,\qquad 3+1,\qquad 2+2,\qquad 2+1+1,\qquad 1+1+1+1.
$$
For eigenvalue $1$, multiplicity $2$, the two partitions are
$$
2,\qquad 1+1.
$$
The choices are independent. Therefore the number of Jordan forms, up to
permutation of blocks, is
$$
\boxed{5\cdot2=10}.
$$
:::
