::: {.exercise}
Use the result of Exercise 11 and the Jordan form to prove the following.
Let $A$ and $B$ be $n \times n$ matrices over the field $F$ which have the same characteristic polynomial
\[
f = (x - c_1)^{d_1} \cdots (x - c_k)^{d_k}
\]
and the same minimal polynomial.
Suppose also that for each $i$ the solution spaces of $(A - c_iI)$ and $(B - c_iI)$ have the same dimension.
If no $d_i$ is greater than 6, then $A$ and $B$ are similar.
:::
