::: {.exercise}
Find the minimal polynomials and the rational forms of each of the following real matrices.
$$
\begin{bmatrix} 0 & -1 & -1 \\ 1 & 0 & 0 \\ -1 & 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix}, \qquad \begin{bmatrix} c & 0 & -1 \\ 0 & c & 1 \\ -1 & 1 & c \end{bmatrix}, \qquad \begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta & \sin\theta \\ -\sin\theta & \cos\theta \end{bmatrix}.
$$
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

$A_1=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1&-1\\1&0&0\\-1&0&0\end{pmatrix}$ has minimal polynomial $x^3$ and rational form $C(x^3)$.

::: pf-proof

Expanding along the first row,
$$
\chi_{A_1}=\det\begin{pmatrix}x&1&1\\-1&x&0\\1&0&x\end{pmatrix}
=x\cdot x^2-1\cdot(-x)+1\cdot(-x)=x^3.
$$
Direct multiplication gives
$$
A_1^2=\begin{pmatrix}0&0&0\\0&-1&-1\\0&1&1\end{pmatrix}\neq0,
\qquad A_1^3=0.
$$
So $m_{A_1}=x^3=\chi_{A_1}$, there is one invariant factor, and the rational form is
$$
C(x^3)=\begin{pmatrix}0&0&0\\1&0&0\\0&1&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

$A_2=\begin{pmatrix}c&0&-1\\0&c&1\\-1&1&c\end{pmatrix}$ has minimal polynomial $(x-c)\bigl((x-c)^2-2\bigr)$ for every real $c$, and its rational form is the companion matrix of that cubic.

::: pf-proof

Expanding along the first row,
$$
\chi_{A_2}=\det\begin{pmatrix}x-c&0&1\\0&x-c&-1\\1&-1&x-c\end{pmatrix}
=(x-c)\bigl((x-c)^2-1\bigr)-(x-c)
=(x-c)\bigl((x-c)^2-2\bigr).
$$
Its roots $c$, $c+\sqrt2$, $c-\sqrt2$ are distinct, so $m_{A_2}=\chi_{A_2}$. Expanding,
$$
\chi_{A_2}=x^3-3cx^2+(3c^2-2)x-(c^3-2c),
$$
and the rational form is
$$
\begin{pmatrix}0&0&c^3-2c\\1&0&2-3c^2\\0&1&3c\end{pmatrix}.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

For $A_3=\begin{pmatrix}\cos\theta&\sin\theta\\-\sin\theta&\cos\theta\end{pmatrix}$, the answer depends on whether $\sin\theta=0$.

::: pf-proof

Here $\chi_{A_3}=x^2-2\cos\theta\,x+1$.
If $\sin\theta\neq0$, then $A_3$ is not a scalar matrix, so $m_{A_3}$ has degree $2$ and equals $\chi_{A_3}$; the rational form is
$$
\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&2\cos\theta\end{pmatrix}.
$$
If $\sin\theta=0$, then $A_3=\varepsilon I$ with $\varepsilon=\cos\theta=\pm1$, so $m_{A_3}=x-\varepsilon$, the invariant factors are $x-\varepsilon$ twice, and the rational form is $\varepsilon I$.

:::
