::: {.exercise}
Find the minimal polynomials and the rational forms of each of the following real matrices.
\[
\begin{bmatrix} 0 & -1 & -1 \\ 1 & 0 & 0 \\ -1 & 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix}, \qquad \begin{bmatrix} c & 0 & -1 \\ 0 & c & 1 \\ -1 & 1 & c \end{bmatrix}, \qquad \begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta & \sin\theta \\ -\sin\theta & \cos\theta \end{bmatrix}.
\]
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. $A_1=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1&-1\\1&0&0\\-1&0&0\end{pmatrix}$ has $\chi_{A_1}= -x(x^2+2)$, so $m_{A_1}=x(x^2+2)$, rational form $\operatorname{diag}(C(x^2+2),[0])$.
Proof: char poly.

<1>2. $A_2=\begin{pmatrix}c&0&-1\\0&c&1\\-1&1&c\end{pmatrix}$ has $\chi=(x-c)((x-c)^2-2)$, so if $c\neq0$ $m=(x-c)((x-c)^2-2)$, form $\operatorname{diag}(C((x-c)^2-2),[c])$.
Proof: char poly.

<1>3. $A_3=\begin{pmatrix}\cos\theta&\sin\theta\\-\sin\theta&\cos\theta\end{pmatrix}$ has $\chi=x^2-2\cos\theta\,x+1$, $m=\chi$ unless $\sin\theta=0$, rational form $C(\chi)$.
Proof: char poly.

<1>4. Q.E.D.
Proof: <1>1 and <1>3.
:::
