::: {.exercise}
Let $A$ be an $n \times n$ matrix with real entries such that $A^2 + I = 0$.
Prove that $n$ is even, and if $n = 2k$, then $A$ is similar over the field of real numbers to a matrix of the block form
\[
\begin{bmatrix} 0 & -I \\ I & 0 \end{bmatrix}
\]
where $I$ is the $k \times k$ identity matrix.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. The minimal polynomial of $A$ is $x^2+1$.
::: {.proof}
The relation
$$
A^2+I=0
$$
shows that the minimal polynomial $m_A$ divides $x^2+1$. Over $\mathbb R$,
$x^2+1$ is irreducible. The minimal polynomial cannot be constant, so
$$
\boxed{m_A(x)=x^2+1}.
$$
:::

<1>2. Rational form forces $n$ to be even.
::: {.proof}
Let
$$
p_r\mid p_{r-1}\mid\cdots\mid p_1=m_A
$$
be the invariant factors of $A$. Every nonconstant $p_i$ divides
$m_A=x^2+1$. Irreducibility therefore forces
$$
p_i=x^2+1
$$
for every invariant factor. Their product is the characteristic polynomial,
whose degree is $n$ [@HK71]. If there are $k$ invariant factors, then
$$
n=\sum_{i=1}^k\deg p_i=2k.
$$
Thus $n$ is even.
:::

<1>3. The rational form is a direct sum of standard two-dimensional rotation blocks.
::: {.proof}
For $p(x)=x^2+1$, Hoffman--Kunze's companion matrix is
$$
C(p)=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$
Therefore $A$ is similar over $\mathbb R$ to the direct sum of $k$ copies of
$C(p)$. In the corresponding basis write the vectors of the $j$th companion
block as $u_j,v_j$, so
$$
Au_j=v_j,
\qquad
Av_j=-u_j.
$$
Reorder the basis as
$$
u_1,\ldots,u_k,v_1,\ldots,v_k.
$$
Then the matrix becomes
$$
\boxed{
\begin{pmatrix}
0&-I_k\\
I_k&0
\end{pmatrix}.}
$$
This is exactly the required real similarity form.
:::
:::
