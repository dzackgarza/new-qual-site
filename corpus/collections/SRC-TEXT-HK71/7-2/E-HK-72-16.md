::: {.exercise}
Let $A$ be an $n \times n$ matrix with real entries such that $A^2 + I = 0$.
Prove that $n$ is even, and if $n = 2k$, then $A$ is similar over the field of real numbers to a matrix of the block form
\[
\begin{bmatrix} 0 & -I \\ I & 0 \end{bmatrix}
\]
where $I$ is the $k \times k$ identity matrix.
:::
