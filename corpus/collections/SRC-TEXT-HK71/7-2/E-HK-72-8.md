::: {.exercise}
Let $T$ be the linear operator on $\RR^3$ which is represented in the standard ordered basis by
\[
\begin{bmatrix} 3 & -4 & -4 \\ -1 & 3 & 2 \\ 2 & -4 & -3 \end{bmatrix}.
\]
Find non-zero vectors $\alpha_1, \ldots, \alpha_r$ satisfying the conditions of Theorem 3.
:::
