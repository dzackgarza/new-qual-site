::: {.exercise}
Let $T$ be the linear operator on $F^4$ which is represented in the standard ordered basis by the matrix
\[
\begin{bmatrix} c & 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 1 & c & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & c & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & c \end{bmatrix}.
\]
Let $W$ be the null space of $T - cI$.

(a) Prove that $W$ is the subspace spanned by $\epsilon_4$.

(b) Find the monic generators of the ideals $S(\epsilon_4; W)$, $S(\epsilon_3; W)$, $S(\epsilon_2; W)$, $S(\epsilon_1; W)$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Put
$$
N=T-cI.
$$
Then the given matrix shows
$$
N\epsilon_1=\epsilon_2,
\qquad
N\epsilon_2=\epsilon_3,
\qquad
N\epsilon_3=\epsilon_4,
\qquad
N\epsilon_4=0.
$$

<1>1. The null space $W$ is spanned by $\epsilon_4$.
::: {.proof}
For
$$
v=a_1\epsilon_1+a_2\epsilon_2+a_3\epsilon_3+a_4\epsilon_4,
$$
one has
$$
Nv=a_1\epsilon_2+a_2\epsilon_3+a_3\epsilon_4.
$$
Thus $Nv=0$ if and only if $a_1=a_2=a_3=0$. Hence
$$
\boxed{W=\ker(T-cI)=\operatorname{span}\{\epsilon_4\}.}
$$
:::

<1>2. Membership in $S(\epsilon_j;W)$ is controlled by the vanishing order at $c$.
::: {.proof}
By definition,
$$
S(\alpha;W)=\{f\in F[x]:f(T)\alpha\in W\}
$$
[@HK71]. Write any polynomial uniquely as
$$
f(x)=a_0+a_1(x-c)+a_2(x-c)^2+a_3(x-c)^3+(x-c)^4q(x).
$$
Since $N^4=0$, this gives
$$
f(T)=a_0I+a_1N+a_2N^2+a_3N^3.
$$
Applying this to the four chain vectors yields
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(T)\epsilon_4&=a_0\epsilon_4,\\
f(T)\epsilon_3&=a_0\epsilon_3+a_1\epsilon_4,\\
f(T)\epsilon_2&=a_0\epsilon_2+a_1\epsilon_3+a_2\epsilon_4,\\
f(T)\epsilon_1&=a_0\epsilon_1+a_1\epsilon_2+a_2\epsilon_3+a_3\epsilon_4.
\end{aligned}
$$
Because $W=\operatorname{span}\{\epsilon_4\}$, these vectors lie in $W$
respectively with no restriction, with $a_0=0$, with $a_0=a_1=0$, and with
$a_0=a_1=a_2=0$.
:::

<1>3. Read off the four monic generators.
::: {.proof}
The conditions in step <1>2 are exactly divisibility by successive powers of
$x-c$. Therefore
$$
\boxed{
S(\epsilon_4;W)=(1),\qquad
S(\epsilon_3;W)=(x-c),\qquad
S(\epsilon_2;W)=((x-c)^2),\qquad
S(\epsilon_1;W)=((x-c)^3).}
$$
Thus the requested monic generators are
$$
\boxed{1,\ x-c,\ (x-c)^2,\ (x-c)^3.}
$$
:::
