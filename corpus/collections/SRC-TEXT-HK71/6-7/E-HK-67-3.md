::: {.exercise}
Let $T$ be a linear operator on a finite-dimensional vector space $V$.
Let $R$ be the range of $T$ and let $N$ be the null space of $T$.
Prove that $R$ and $N$ are independent if and only if $V = R \oplus N$.
:::

::: solution
Because $V$ is finite dimensional, rank-nullity gives
$$
\dim R+\dim N=\dim V
$$
[@HK71]. For two subspaces,
$$
\dim(R+N)=\dim R+\dim N-\dim(R\cap N).
$$
Therefore
$$
\dim(R+N)=\dim V-\dim(R\cap N).
$$

If $R$ and $N$ are independent, then $R\cap N=\{0\}$, so
$\dim(R+N)=\dim V$. Since $R+N\subseteq V$, equality of dimensions gives
$$
R+N=V.
$$
Together with the trivial intersection, this says
$$
V=R\oplus N.
$$

Conversely, if $V=R\oplus N$, the definition of direct sum gives
$R\cap N=\{0\}$. Thus $R$ and $N$ are independent. Hence the two conditions
are equivalent.
:::
