::: {.exercise}
Let $T$ be a linear operator on a finite-dimensional vector space $V$.
Let $R$ be the range of $T$ and let $N$ be the null space of $T$.
Prove that $R$ and $N$ are independent if and only if $V = R \oplus N$.
:::
