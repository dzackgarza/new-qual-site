::: {.exercise}
Let $A$ be the $4 \times 4$ matrix in Example 6 of Section 6.3. Find matrices $E_1, E_2, E_3$ such that $A = c_1E_1 + c_2E_2 + c_3E_3$, $E_1 + E_2 + E_3 = I$, and $E_iE_j = 0$, $i \neq j$.
:::
