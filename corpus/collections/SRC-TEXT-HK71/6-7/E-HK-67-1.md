::: {.exercise}
Let $E$ be a projection of $V$ and let $T$ be a linear operator on $V$.
Prove that the range of $E$ is invariant under $T$ if and only if $ETE = TE$.
Prove that both the range and null space of $E$ are invariant under $T$ if and only if $ET = TE$.
