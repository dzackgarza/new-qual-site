::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be the vector space of all $2 \times 2$ matrices over the field of real numbers, and let
\[
B = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & -2 \\ -1 & 1 \end{bmatrix}.
\]
Let $W$ be the subspace of $V$ consisting of all $A$ such that $AB = 0$.
Let $f$ be a linear functional on $V$ which is in the annihilator of $W$.
Suppose that $f(I) = 0$ and $f(C) = 3$, where $I$ is the $2 \times 2$ identity matrix and
\[
C = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}.
\]
Find $f(B)$.
:::

::: solution
Write
\[
A=\begin{bmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{bmatrix}.
\]
Since
\[
AB=
\begin{bmatrix}
2a-b&-2a+b\\
2c-d&-2c+d
\end{bmatrix},
\]
we have $AB=0$ exactly when $b=2a$ and $d=2c$. Thus
\[
W=\left\{\begin{bmatrix}a&2a\\c&2c\end{bmatrix}:a,c\in\RR\right\}.
\]
Let
\[
f\!\left(\begin{bmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{bmatrix}\right)
=pa+qb+rc+sd.
\]
Because $f$ annihilates $W$,
\[
p+2q=0,\qquad r+2s=0.
\]
The conditions $f(I)=0$ and $f(C)=3$ give
\[
p+s=0,\qquad s=3.
\]
Hence
\[
(p,q,r,s)=\left(-3,\frac32,-6,3\right).
\]
Therefore
\[
f(B)=2p-2q-r+s=-6-3+6+3=0.
\]
:::
