::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be the vector space of all $2 \times 2$ matrices over the field of real numbers, and let
$$
B = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & -2 \\ -1 & 1 \end{bmatrix}.
$$
Let $W$ be the subspace of $V$ consisting of all $A$ such that $AB = 0$.
Let $f$ be a linear functional on $V$ which is in the annihilator of $W$.
Suppose that $f(I) = 0$ and $f(C) = 3$, where $I$ is the $2 \times 2$ identity matrix and
$$
C = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}.
$$
Find $f(B)$.
:::

::: {.solution}
No retained solution set covers this exercise; the argument is the card's own, checked against Hoffman--Kunze, *Linear Algebra* (1971), §3.5 Exercise 9. The computation was redone exactly: $AB=0$ forces $b=2a$ and $d=2c$; the four linear conditions on the coefficients of $f$ determine them uniquely as $(-3,\tfrac32,-6,3)$; and $f(B)=2p-2q-r+s=0$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #w}
$AB=0$ if and only if $b=2a$ and $d=2c$, so $W=\left\{\begin{pmatrix}a&2a\\c&2c\end{pmatrix}\st a,c\in\RR\right\}$.

::: pf-proof
Multiplying, $AB=\begin{pmatrix}2a-b&-2a+b\\2c-d&-2c+d\end{pmatrix}$; all four entries vanish exactly when $b=2a$ and $d=2c$.
:::
:::

::: {.pf-step #form}
Every linear functional on $V$ has the form $f\left(\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}\right)=pa+qb+rc+sd$ with $p,q,r,s\in\RR$.

::: pf-proof
The four matrices $E_{ij}$ form a basis of $V$, so $f$ is determined by its values $p=f(E_{11})$, $q=f(E_{12})$, $r=f(E_{21})$, $s=f(E_{22})$, and linearity gives the displayed expression.
:::
:::

::: {.pf-step #annihilator}
$f$ annihilates $W$ if and only if $p+2q=0$ and $r+2s=0$.

::: pf-proof
Evaluating the form of step [](#form){.pf-ref} on the general element $\begin{pmatrix}a&2a\\c&2c\end{pmatrix}$ of $W$ gives $(p+2q)a+(r+2s)c$. This vanishes for all $a,c$ exactly when the two coefficients vanish.
:::
:::

::: {.pf-step #values}
The conditions $f(I)=0$ and $f(C)=3$ are $p+s=0$ and $s=3$.

::: pf-proof
The identity has $a=d=1$ and $b=c=0$, so $f(I)=p+s$; the matrix $C$ has $d=1$ and the other entries $0$, so $f(C)=s$.
:::
:::

::: {.pf-step #coefficients}
$(p,q,r,s)=\left(-3,\tfrac32,-6,3\right)$.

::: pf-proof
From $s=3$ and $p+s=0$ we get $p=-3$; then $p+2q=0$ gives $q=\tfrac32$; and $r+2s=0$ gives $r=-6$. The four equations are triangular in this order, so the solution is unique.
:::
:::

::: {.pf-step #answer}
$f(B)=0$.

::: pf-proof
Here $B=\begin{pmatrix}2&-2\\-1&1\end{pmatrix}$, so $a=2$, $b=-2$, $c=-1$, $d=1$ and $f(B)=2p-2q-r+s=-6-3+6+3=0$ by step [](#coefficients){.pf-ref}.
:::
:::
