::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional vector space over the field $F$ and let $W$ be a subspace of $V$.
If $f$ is a linear functional on $W$, prove that there is a linear functional $g$ on $V$ such that $g(\alpha) = f(\alpha)$ for each $\alpha$ in the subspace $W$.
:::

::: solution
Choose a basis $\{\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_r\}$ of $W$ and extend it to a basis
\[
\{\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_r,\alpha_{r+1},\ldots,\alpha_n\}
\]
of $V$. Define $g$ on this basis by
\[
g(\alpha_i)=f(\alpha_i)\quad(1\le i\le r),
\qquad
g(\alpha_i)=0\quad(r<i\le n),
\]
and extend linearly to $V$.

If $w\in W$, then $w=\sum_{i=1}^r c_i\alpha_i$, and hence
\[
g(w)=\sum_{i=1}^r c_i g(\alpha_i)
=\sum_{i=1}^r c_i f(\alpha_i)=f(w).
\]
Thus $g$ is a linear functional on $V$ extending $f$.
:::
