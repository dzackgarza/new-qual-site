::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be the vector space of all polynomial functions $p$ from $\RR$ into $\RR$ which have degree 2 or less:
$$
p(x) = c_0 + c_1 x + c_2 x^2.
$$
Define three linear functionals on $V$ by
$$
f_1(p) = \int_0^1 p(x)\,dx, \quad f_2(p) = \int_0^2 p(x)\,dx, \quad f_3(p) = \int_0^{-1} p(x)\,dx.
$$
Show that $\{f_1, f_2, f_3\}$ is a basis for $V^*$ by exhibiting the basis for $V$ of which it is the dual.
:::

::: {.solution}
Relative to the standard basis $\{1,x,x^2\}$, the three functionals have matrix
$$
M=
\begin{bmatrix}
1&\frac12&\frac13\\
2&2&\frac83\\
-1&\frac12&-\frac13
\end{bmatrix}.
$$
Its determinant is $-2$, so the functionals are linearly independent and hence form a basis of $V^*$.

To exhibit the basis of $V$ of which they are dual, use the columns of $M^{-1}$:
$$
M^{-1}=
\begin{bmatrix}
1&-\frac16&-\frac13\\
1&0&1\\
-\frac32&\frac12&-\frac12
\end{bmatrix}.
$$
Thus set
$$
p_1(x)=1+x-\frac32x^2,
\qquad
p_2(x)=-\frac16+\frac12x^2,
\qquad
p_3(x)=-\frac13+x-\frac12x^2.
$$
The identity $MM^{-1}=I$ gives
$$
f_i(p_j)=\delta_{ij}.
$$
Hence $\{p_1,p_2,p_3\}$ is a basis of $V$ and $\{f_1,f_2,f_3\}$ is its dual basis.
:::
