::: {.exercise}
If $A$ and $B$ are $n \times n$ matrices over the field $F$, show that $\operatorname{trace}(AB) = \operatorname{trace}(BA)$.
Now show that similar matrices have the same trace.
:::

::: {.solution}
Write $A=(a_{ij})$ and $B=(b_{ij})$. Then
\[
\operatorname{tr}(AB)
=\sum_i(AB)_{ii}
=\sum_{i,j}a_{ij}b_{ji}.
\]
Interchanging the dummy indices $i,j$ gives
\[
\sum_{i,j}a_{ij}b_{ji}
=\sum_{i,j}b_{ij}a_{ji}
=\operatorname{tr}(BA).
\]
Thus $\operatorname{tr}(AB)=\operatorname{tr}(BA)$.

If $B=P^{-1}AP$ is similar to $A$, then
\[
\operatorname{tr}(B)
=\operatorname{tr}(P^{-1}AP)
=\operatorname{tr}(APP^{-1})
=\operatorname{tr}(A).
\]
Therefore similar matrices have the same trace.
:::
