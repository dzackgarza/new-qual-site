::: {.exercise}
Let $m$ and $n$ be positive integers and $F$ a field.
Let $f_1, \ldots, f_m$ be linear functionals on $F^n$.
For $\alpha$ in $F^n$ define
\[
T\alpha = (f_1(\alpha), \ldots, f_m(\alpha)).
\]
Show that $T$ is a linear transformation from $F^n$ into $F^m$.
Then show that every linear transformation from $F^n$ into $F^m$ is of the above form, for some $f_1, \ldots, f_m$.
:::

::: solution
For $\alpha,\beta\in F^n$ and $c\in F$,
\[
T(\alpha+c\beta)
=(f_1(\alpha+c\beta),\ldots,f_m(\alpha+c\beta))
=T\alpha+cT\beta,
\]
so $T$ is linear.

Conversely, let $T:F^n\to F^m$ be linear. For $1\le i\le m$, let
$\pi_i:F^m\to F$ be the $i$th coordinate functional and put
\[
f_i=\pi_i\circ T.
\]
Each $f_i$ is a linear functional on $F^n$, and by construction
\[
T\alpha=(f_1(\alpha),\ldots,f_m(\alpha)).
\]
Thus every linear transformation $F^n\to F^m$ has the stated form.
:::
