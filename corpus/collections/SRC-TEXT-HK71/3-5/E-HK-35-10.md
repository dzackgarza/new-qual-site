::: {.exercise}
Let $F$ be a subfield of the complex numbers.
We define $n$ linear functionals on $F^n$ ($n \geq 2$) by
\[
f_k(x_1, \ldots, x_n) = \sum_{j=1}^{n} (k - j)x_j, \quad 1 \leq k \leq n.
\]
What is the dimension of the subspace annihilated by $f_1, \ldots, f_n$?
:::

::: {.solution}
A vector $x=(x_1,\ldots,x_n)$ is annihilated by every $f_k$ exactly when
\[
0=f_k(x)=k\sum_{j=1}^n x_j-\sum_{j=1}^n jx_j
\qquad(1\le k\le n).
\]
Since $n\ge2$ and $F$ has characteristic zero, subtracting the equations for two distinct values of $k$ gives
\[
\sum_{j=1}^n x_j=0.
\]
Substituting back then gives
\[
\sum_{j=1}^n jx_j=0.
\]
These are two independent linear equations: their coefficient vectors
\[
(1,\ldots,1),\qquad(1,2,\ldots,n)
\]
are not scalar multiples. Hence the common annihilator has codimension $2$ in $F^n$, and therefore dimension
\[
\boxed{n-2}.
\]
:::
