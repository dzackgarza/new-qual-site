::: {.exercise}
Let $W$ be the subspace of $\RR^5$ which is spanned by the vectors
\[
\alpha_1 = \epsilon_1 + 2\epsilon_2 + \epsilon_3, \quad \alpha_2 = \epsilon_2 + 3\epsilon_3 + 3\epsilon_4 + \epsilon_5,
\]
\[
\alpha_3 = \epsilon_1 + 4\epsilon_2 + 6\epsilon_3 + 4\epsilon_4 + \epsilon_5.
\]
Find a basis for $W^0$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Identify a linear functional on $\RR^5$ with its coefficient vector
$c=(c_1,\ldots,c_5)$. The condition $c\in W^0$ is
\[
\begin{bmatrix}
1&2&1&0&0\\
0&1&3&3&1\\
1&4&6&4&1
\end{bmatrix}c^{\mathsf T}=0.
\]
Row reduction gives a two-dimensional nullspace with basis
\[
(-4,3,-2,1,0),\qquad (-3,2,-1,0,1).
\]
Therefore a basis for $W^0$ is
\[
f_1(x)=-4x_1+3x_2-2x_3+x_4,
\]
\[
f_2(x)=-3x_1+2x_2-x_3+x_5.
\]
Direct substitution in each $\alpha_i$ verifies that both functionals annihilate $W$; their coefficient vectors are independent, and $\dim W^0=5-\dim W=2$.
:::
