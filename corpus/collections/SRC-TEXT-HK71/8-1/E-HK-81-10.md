::: {.exercise}
In Example 2 the inner product on $\mathbb R^2$ is
\[
((x_1,x_2)\mid(y_1,y_2))
=x_1y_1-x_2y_1-x_1y_2+4x_2y_2.
\]
Let $B$ be the standard ordered basis for $\RR^2$.
Find the matrix of this inner product relative to $B$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let
$$
e_1=(1,0),\qquad e_2=(0,1).
$$
The Gram matrix relative to $B=(e_1,e_2)$ is
$$
G=\bigl((e_i\mid e_j)\bigr)_{i,j=1}^2.
$$
Directly from the defining formula,
$$
(e_1\mid e_1)=1,
\qquad
(e_1\mid e_2)=-1,
\qquad
(e_2\mid e_1)=-1,
\qquad
(e_2\mid e_2)=4.
$$
Therefore
$$
\boxed{
G=\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\-1&4\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

Indeed, if $X=(x_1,x_2)^t$ and $Y=(y_1,y_2)^t$, then
$$
X^tGY
=x_1y_1-x_1y_2-x_2y_1+4x_2y_2,
$$
which recovers the given inner product exactly [@HK71].
:::
