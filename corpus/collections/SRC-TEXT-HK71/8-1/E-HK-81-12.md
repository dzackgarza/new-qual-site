::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional vector space and let $\mathfrak{B} = \{\alpha_1, \ldots, \alpha_n\}$ be a basis for $V$.
Let $(\quad | \quad)$ be an inner product on $V$.
If $c_1, \ldots, c_n$ are any $n$ scalars, show that there is exactly one vector $\alpha$ in $V$ such that $(\alpha \mid \alpha_j) = c_j$, $j = 1, \ldots, n$.
:::
