::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be a real or complex vector space with an inner product.
Show that the quadratic form determined by the inner product satisfies the parallelogram law
\[
||\alpha + \beta||^2 + ||\alpha - \beta||^2 = 2||\alpha||^2 + 2||\beta||^2.
\]
:::


::: {.solution}
By definition $\|\gamma\|^2=(\gamma\mid\gamma)$. Therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
\|\alpha+\beta\|^2
&=(\alpha\mid\alpha)+(\alpha\mid\beta)
 +(\beta\mid\alpha)+(\beta\mid\beta),\\
\|\alpha-\beta\|^2
&=(\alpha\mid\alpha)-(\alpha\mid\beta)
 -(\beta\mid\alpha)+(\beta\mid\beta).
\end{aligned}
$$
Adding cancels the two mixed terms and yields
$$
\boxed{
\|\alpha+\beta\|^2+\|\alpha-\beta\|^2
=2\|\alpha\|^2+2\|\beta\|^2.}
$$
The computation uses only additivity and conjugate symmetry, so it is valid
for both real and complex inner-product spaces [@HK71].
:::
