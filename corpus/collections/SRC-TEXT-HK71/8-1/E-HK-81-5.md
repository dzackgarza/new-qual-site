::: {.exercise}
Let $( \mid )$ be the standard inner product on $\RR^2$.

(a) Let $\alpha = (1, 2)$, $\beta = (-1, 1)$.
If $\gamma$ is a vector such that $(\alpha \mid \gamma) = -1$ and $(\beta \mid \gamma) = 3$, find $\gamma$.

(b) Show that for any $\alpha$ in $\RR^2$ we have $\alpha = (\alpha \mid \epsilon_1)\epsilon_1 + (\alpha \mid \epsilon_2)\epsilon_2$.
:::
