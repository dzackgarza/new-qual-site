::: {.exercise}
Find a row-reduced matrix which is row-equivalent to

$$
A = \left[ \begin{array}{c c c} i & - (1 + i) & 0 \\ 1 & - 2 & 1 \\ 1 & 2 i & - 1 \end{array} \right].
$$
:::
