::: {.exercise}
Let $S$ be a set, $F$ a field, and $V(S; F)$ the space of all functions from $S$ into $F$:
\[
(f + g)(x) = f(x) + g(x), \qquad (cf)(x) = cf(x).
\]
Let $W$ be any $n$-dimensional subspace of $V(S; F)$.
Show that there exist points $x_1, \ldots, x_n$ in $S$ and functions $f_1, \ldots, f_n$ in $W$ such that $f_i(x_j) = \delta_{ij}$.
:::

::: solution
For each $x\in S$, let
\[
e_x:W\to F,\qquad e_x(f)=f(x)
\]
be the evaluation functional. We claim that the family $\{e_x:x\in S\}$ spans $W^*$.

If its span were a proper subspace of $W^*$, then, because $W$ is finite-dimensional, there would exist a nonzero $f\in W$ annihilated by every $e_x$. But then
\[
f(x)=e_x(f)=0
\]
for every $x\in S$, so $f$ would be the zero function, a contradiction. Hence the evaluation functionals span $W^*$.

Since $\dim W^*=n$, choose points $x_1,\ldots,x_n\in S$ such that
\[
e_{x_1},\ldots,e_{x_n}
\]
form a basis of $W^*$. Let $f_1,\ldots,f_n$ be the basis of $W$ dual to this basis of $W^*$. Then
\[
f_i(x_j)=e_{x_j}(f_i)=\delta_{ij},
\]
which is the desired conclusion.
:::
