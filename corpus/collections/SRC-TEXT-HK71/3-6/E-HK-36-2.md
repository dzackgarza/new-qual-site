::: {.exercise}
Use Theorem 20 to prove the following.
If $W$ is a subspace of a finite-dimensional vector space $V$ and if $\{g_1, \ldots, g_r\}$ is any basis for $W^0$, then
\[
W = \bigcap_{i=1}^{r} N_{g_i}.
\]
:::

::: {.solution}
Let
\[
K=\bigcap_{i=1}^r N_{g_i}.
\]
Every $w\in W$ is annihilated by every $g_i\in W^0$, so $W\subseteq K$.

Conversely, if $v\in K$, then $g_i(v)=0$ for every basis vector $g_i$ of $W^0$. Hence every $g\in W^0$ satisfies $g(v)=0$, because $g$ is a linear combination of the $g_i$. Thus
\[
v\in (W^0)^0.
\]
By the finite-dimensional double-annihilator theorem,
\[
(W^0)^0=W.
\]
Therefore $K\subseteq W$, and hence
\[
W=\bigcap_{i=1}^r N_{g_i}.
\]
:::
