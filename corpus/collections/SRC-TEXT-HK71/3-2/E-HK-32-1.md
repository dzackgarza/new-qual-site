::: {.exercise}
Let $T$ and $U$ be the linear operators on $\RR^2$ defined by
\[
T(x_1, x_2) = (x_2, x_1) \quad \text{and} \quad U(x_1, x_2) = (x_1, 0).
\]

(a) How would you describe $T$ and $U$ geometrically?

(b) Give rules like the ones defining $T$ and $U$ for each of the transformations $(U + T)$, $UT$, $TU$, $T^2$, $U^2$.
:::

::: solution
The map $T$ interchanges the two coordinates, so geometrically it is reflection across the line $x_1=x_2$. The map $U$ is projection onto the $x_1$-axis.

For $(x_1,x_2)\in\RR^2$,
\[
(U+T)(x_1,x_2)=(x_1+x_2,x_1),
\]
\[
(UT)(x_1,x_2)=U(x_2,x_1)=(x_2,0),
\qquad
(TU)(x_1,x_2)=T(x_1,0)=(0,x_1).
\]
Also
\[
T^2(x_1,x_2)=(x_1,x_2),
\qquad
U^2(x_1,x_2)=(x_1,0).
\]
Hence $T^2=I$ and $U^2=U$.
:::
