::: {.exercise}
Let $C^{2 \times 2}$ be the complex vector space of $2 \times 2$ matrices with complex entries.
Let
\[
B = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -1 \\ -4 & 4 \end{bmatrix}
\]
and let $T$ be the linear operator on $C^{2 \times 2}$ defined by $T(A) = BA$.
What is the rank of $T$?
Can you describe $T^2$?
:::
