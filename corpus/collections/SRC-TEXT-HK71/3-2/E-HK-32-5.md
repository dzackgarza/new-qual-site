::: {.exercise}
Let $C^{2 \times 2}$ be the complex vector space of $2 \times 2$ matrices with complex entries.
Let
\[
B = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -1 \\ -4 & 4 \end{bmatrix}
\]
and let $T$ be the linear operator on $C^{2 \times 2}$ defined by $T(A) = BA$.
What is the rank of $T$?
Can you describe $T^2$?
:::

::: solution
The matrix
\[
B=\begin{bmatrix}1&-1\\-4&4\end{bmatrix}
\]
has rank $1$: its second row is $-4$ times its first row, and $B\ne0$.
For $A=[a_1\ a_2]$, with columns $a_1,a_2\in\mathbb C^2$,
\[
T(A)=BA=[Ba_1\ Ba_2].
\]
Each column $Ba_j$ can be any vector in the one-dimensional space $\operatorname{im}B$, independently of the other column. Hence
\[
\operatorname{range}T
=\{[u\ v]:u,v\in\operatorname{im}B\}
\]
has dimension $2$. Therefore $\operatorname{rank}T=2$.

A direct multiplication gives
\[
B^2=
\begin{bmatrix}5&-5\\-20&20\end{bmatrix}=5B.
\]
Consequently, for every $A$,
\[
T^2(A)=B(BA)=B^2A=5BA=5T(A).
\]
Thus $T^2=5T$.
:::
