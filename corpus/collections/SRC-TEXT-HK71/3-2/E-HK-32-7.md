::: {.exercise}
Find two linear operators $T$ and $U$ on $\RR^2$ such that $TU = 0$ but $UT \neq 0$.
:::

::: solution
Define
\[
T(x,y)=(x,0),\qquad U(x,y)=(0,x).
\]
Then
\[
TU(x,y)=T(0,x)=(0,0)
\]
for every $(x,y)$, so $TU=0$. On the other hand,
\[
UT(x,y)=U(x,0)=(0,x),
\]
which is not the zero operator; for example, $UT(1,0)=(0,1)$.
:::
