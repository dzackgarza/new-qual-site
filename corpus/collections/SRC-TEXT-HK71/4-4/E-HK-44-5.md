::: {.exercise}
Let $F$ be a field.
Show that the intersection of any number of ideals in $F[x]$ is an ideal.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $\{I_\lambda\}_{\lambda\in\Lambda}$ be any family of ideals in $F[x]$ and put
\[
I=\bigcap_{\lambda\in\Lambda}I_\lambda.
\]
Since every $I_\lambda$ contains $0$, so does $I$. If $f,g\in I$, then $f,g\in I_\lambda$ for every $\lambda$, hence
\[
f-g\in I_\lambda
\]
for every $\lambda$, so $f-g\in I$. If $h\in F[x]$ and $f\in I$, then
\[
hf\in I_\lambda
\]
for every $\lambda$, so $hf\in I$. Thus $I$ is an ideal.
:::
