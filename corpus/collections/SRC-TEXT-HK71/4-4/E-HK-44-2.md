::: {.exercise}
Find the g.c.d. of each of the following pairs of polynomials

(a) $2x^5 - x^3 - 3x^2 - 6x + 4$, $\;x^4 + x^3 - x^2 - 2x - 2$;

(b) $3x^4 + 8x^2 - 3$, $\;x^3 + 2x^2 + 3x + 6$;

(c) $x^4 - 2x^3 - 2x^2 - 2x - 3$, $\;x^3 + 6x^2 + 7x + 1$.
:::

::: {.solution}
For each pair, apply the Euclidean algorithm over $\QQ[x]$ and normalize the last nonzero remainder to be monic.

(a) For
$$
f=2x^5-x^3-3x^2-6x+4,
\qquad
g=x^4+x^3-x^2-2x-2,
$$
division gives $f = (2x - 2)g + (3x^3 - x^2 - 6x)$.
Up to nonzero scalar factors, which do not change the gcd, the following remainders are $13x^2 + 6x - 18$ and $7x + 31$.
The value of $13x^2 + 6x - 18$ at $x = -31/7$ is $10309/49 \neq 0$, so the next remainder is a nonzero constant. Hence
$$
\gcd(f,g)=1.
$$

(b) For
$$
f=3x^4+8x^2-3,
\qquad
g=x^3+2x^2+3x+6,
$$
one finds
$$
f=(3x-6)g+11(x^2+3),
$$
and
$$
g=(x+2)(x^2+3).
$$
Therefore
$$
\gcd(f,g)=x^2+3.
$$

(c) For
$$
f=x^4-2x^3-2x^2-2x-3,
\qquad
g=x^3+6x^2+7x+1,
$$
division gives $f = (x - 8)g + (39x^2 + 53x + 5)$.
Up to a nonzero scalar factor, the next remainder is $859x + 616$.
The value of $39x^2 + 53x + 5$ at $x = -616/859$ is $-9556443/737881 \neq 0$, so the next remainder is a nonzero constant. Hence
$$
\gcd(f,g)=1.
$$

Thus the three monic gcds are
$$
\boxed{1,\quad x^2+3,\quad 1}.
$$
:::
