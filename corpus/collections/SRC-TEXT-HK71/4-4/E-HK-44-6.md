::: {.exercise}
Let $F$ be a field.
Show that the ideal generated by a finite number of polynomials $f_1, \ldots, f_n$ in $F[x]$ is the intersection of all ideals containing $f_1, \ldots, f_n$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let
\[
J=(f_1,\ldots,f_n)
=\left\{\sum_{i=1}^n h_i f_i:h_i\in F[x]\right\}.
\]
Then $J$ is an ideal containing every $f_i$.

If $I$ is any ideal containing $f_1,\ldots,f_n$, then for arbitrary $h_i\in F[x]$ each product $h_if_i$ lies in $I$, and therefore so does their sum. Hence
\[
J\subseteq I.
\]
Thus $J$ is contained in every ideal containing all the $f_i$. Since $J$ itself is one such ideal,
\[
J=\bigcap_{\substack{I\text{ ideal}\\ f_1,\ldots,f_n\in I}} I.
\]
:::
