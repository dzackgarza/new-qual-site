::: {.exercise}
Let $F$ be a subfield of the complex numbers, and let
\[
A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -2 \\ 0 & 3 \end{bmatrix}.
\]
Find the monic generator of the ideal of all polynomials $f$ in $F[x]$ such that $f(A) = 0$.
:::

::: solution
The matrix $A$ has two distinct eigenvalues, $1$ and $3$. Indeed,
\[
Ae_1=e_1,
\qquad
A\begin{bmatrix}1\\-1\end{bmatrix}
=3\begin{bmatrix}1\\-1\end{bmatrix}.
\]
If $f(A)=0$, applying $f(A)$ to these two eigenvectors gives
\[
f(1)=0,
\qquad
f(3)=0.
\]
Hence $(x-1)(x-3)$ divides $f$.

Conversely,
\[
(A-I)(A-3I)=0,
\]
so the polynomial $(x-1)(x-3)$ annihilates $A$. Therefore the ideal of annihilating polynomials is
\[
((x-1)(x-3)),
\]
and its monic generator is
\[
\boxed{(x-1)(x-3)}.
\]
:::
