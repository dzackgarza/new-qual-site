::: {.exercise}
Let $F$ be a subfield of the complex numbers, and let
\[
A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -2 \\ 0 & 3 \end{bmatrix}.
\]
Find the monic generator of the ideal of all polynomials $f$ in $F[x]$ such that $f(A) = 0$.
:::
