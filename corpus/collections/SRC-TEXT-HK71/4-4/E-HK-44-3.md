::: {.exercise}
Let $A$ be an $n \times n$ matrix over a field $F$.
Show that the set of all polynomials $f$ in $F[x]$ such that $f(A) = 0$ is an ideal.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let
\[
I=\{f\in F[x]:f(A)=0\}.
\]
Certainly $0\in I$. If $f,g\in I$, then
\[
(f-g)(A)=f(A)-g(A)=0,
\]
so $f-g\in I$. If $h\in F[x]$ and $f\in I$, then
\[
(hf)(A)=h(A)f(A)=0,
\]
so $hf\in I$. Therefore $I$ is an ideal of $F[x]$.
:::
