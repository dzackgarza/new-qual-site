::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be a vector space, let $W_1, \ldots, W_k$ be subspaces of $V$, and let
\[
V_j = W_1 + \cdots + W_{j-1} + W_{j+1} + \cdots + W_k.
\]
Suppose that $V = W_1 \oplus \cdots \oplus W_k$.
Prove that the dual space $V^*$ has the direct-sum decomposition $V^* = V_1^0 \oplus \cdots \oplus V_k^0$.
:::
