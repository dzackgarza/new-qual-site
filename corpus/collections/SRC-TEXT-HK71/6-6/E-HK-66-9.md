::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 6.6.9"}
Let $V$ be a real vector space and $E$ an idempotent linear operator on $V$, i.e., a projection.
Prove that $(I + E)$ is invertible.
Find $(I + E)^{-1}$.
:::
