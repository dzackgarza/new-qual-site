::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be a real vector space and $E$ an idempotent linear operator on $V$, i.e., a projection.
Prove that $(I + E)$ is invertible.
Find $(I + E)^{-1}$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Because $E^2=E$,
$$
\left(I-\frac12E\right)(I+E)
=I+E-\frac12E-\frac12E^2
=I.
$$
The same computation in the opposite order gives
$$
(I+E)\left(I-\frac12E\right)=I.
$$
Since the scalar field is $\mathbb R$, the scalar $2$ is invertible. Therefore
$$
\boxed{(I+E)^{-1}=I-\frac12E.}
$$
Thus $I+E$ is invertible [@HK71].
:::
