::: {.exercise}
If $E$ is a projection and $f$ is a polynomial, then $f(E) = aI + bE$.
What are $a$ and $b$ in terms of the coefficients of $f$?
:::

::: {.solution}
Write
$$
f(t)=c_0+c_1t+\cdots+c_mt^m.
$$
Since $E$ is a projection, $E^2=E$, and therefore
$$
E^k=E\qquad(k\ge1)
$$
by induction. Hence
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(E)
&=c_0I+c_1E+\cdots+c_mE^m\\
&=c_0I+(c_1+\cdots+c_m)E.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus one may take
$$
\boxed{a=c_0=f(0),\qquad b=c_1+\cdots+c_m=f(1)-f(0).}
$$
This proves the claimed form [@HK71].
:::
