::: {.exercise}
True or false?
If a diagonalizable operator has only the characteristic values 0 and 1, it is a projection.
:::

::: solution
The assertion is $\boxed{\text{true}}$.

Since $T$ is diagonalizable, there is a basis of eigenvectors
$v_1,\ldots,v_n$ with
$$
Tv_j=\lambda_jv_j,
\qquad
\lambda_j\in\{0,1\}
$$
for every $j$ [@HK71]. Therefore
$$
T^2v_j=\lambda_j^2v_j=\lambda_jv_j=Tv_j,
$$
because $0^2=0$ and $1^2=1$. Thus $T^2$ and $T$ agree on a basis, hence
$$
T^2=T.
$$
An idempotent linear operator is a projection, so $T$ is a projection.
:::
