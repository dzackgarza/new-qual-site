::: {.exercise}
Use Exercise 6 to prove the following.
If $f$, $g$, $h$, and $p$ are polynomials over the field $F$ and $p \neq 0$, and if $f \equiv g \bmod p$, then $h(f) \equiv h(g) \bmod p$.
:::

::: solution
Write
\[
h(x)=a_0+a_1x+\cdots+a_rx^r.
\]
From $f\equiv g\pmod p$, repeated application of Exercise 6 gives
\[
f^k\equiv g^k\pmod p
\]
for every $k\ge0$. Multiplication by the scalar $a_k$ preserves congruence, and adding the resulting congruences gives
\[
\sum_{k=0}^r a_k f^k
\equiv
\sum_{k=0}^r a_k g^k
\pmod p.
\]
Therefore
\[
h(f)\equiv h(g)\pmod p.
\]
:::
