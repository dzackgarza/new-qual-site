::: {.exercise}
Use Exercise 6 to prove the following.
If $f$, $g$, $h$, and $p$ are polynomials over the field $F$ and $p \neq 0$, and if $f \equiv g \bmod p$, then $h(f) \equiv h(g) \bmod p$.
:::
