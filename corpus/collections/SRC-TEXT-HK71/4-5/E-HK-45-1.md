::: {.exercise}
Let $p$ be a monic polynomial over the field $F$, and let $f$ and $g$ be relatively prime polynomials over $F$.
Prove that the g.c.d. of $pf$ and $pg$ is $p$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Since $f$ and $g$ are relatively prime, there exist $a,b\in F[x]$ such that
\[
af+bg=1.
\]
Multiplying by $p$ gives
\[
a(pf)+b(pg)=p.
\]
Thus every common divisor of $pf$ and $pg$ divides $p$. On the other hand, $p$ itself divides both $pf$ and $pg$. Hence the monic greatest common divisor of $pf$ and $pg$ is exactly
\[
\boxed{p}.
\]
:::
