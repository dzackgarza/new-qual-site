::: {.exercise}
For each of the following real symmetric matrices $A$, find a real orthogonal matrix $P$ such that $P^tAP$ is diagonal.
\[
\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix}, \qquad \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 1 \end{bmatrix}, \qquad \begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta & \sin\theta \\ \sin\theta & -\cos\theta \end{bmatrix}
\]
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. Diagonalize the first matrix.
::: {.proof}
Let
$$
A_1=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$
The orthonormal vectors
$$
u_1=\frac1{\sqrt2}\binom11,
\qquad
u_2=\frac1{\sqrt2}\binom1{-1}
$$
satisfy
$$
A_1u_1=2u_1,
\qquad
A_1u_2=0.
$$
Thus with
$$
P_1=\frac1{\sqrt2}
\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix},
$$
we have $P_1^tP_1=I$ and
$$
\boxed{P_1^tA_1P_1=\begin{pmatrix}2&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}.}
$$
:::

<1>2. The same orthogonal matrix diagonalizes the second matrix.
::: {.proof}
For
$$
A_2=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\2&1\end{pmatrix},
$$
the same two vectors satisfy
$$
A_2u_1=3u_1,
\qquad
A_2u_2=-u_2.
$$
Hence
$$
\boxed{P_1^tA_2P_1=\begin{pmatrix}3&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}.}
$$
:::

<1>3. Diagonalize the parameterized matrix by a half-angle rotation.
::: {.proof}
Write
$$
A_3=
\begin{pmatrix}
\cos\theta&\sin\theta\\
\sin\theta&-\cos\theta
\end{pmatrix}.
$$
Set
$$
v_+=\binom{\cos(\theta/2)}{\sin(\theta/2)},
\qquad
v_-=\binom{-\sin(\theta/2)}{\cos(\theta/2)}.
$$
These vectors are orthonormal. The angle-difference identities give
$$
A_3v_+=v_+,
\qquad
A_3v_-=-v_-.
$$
Therefore, for
$$
P_3=
\begin{pmatrix}
\cos(\theta/2)&-\sin(\theta/2)\\
\sin(\theta/2)&\cos(\theta/2)
\end{pmatrix},
$$
we have $P_3^tP_3=I$ and
$$
\boxed{P_3^tA_3P_3=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}.}
$$
:::
:::
