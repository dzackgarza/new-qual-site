::: {.exercise}
For each of the following real symmetric matrices $A$, find a real orthogonal matrix $P$ such that $P^tAP$ is diagonal.
\[
\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix}, \qquad \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 1 \end{bmatrix}, \qquad \begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta & \sin\theta \\ \sin\theta & -\cos\theta \end{bmatrix}
\]
:::
