::: {.exercise}
Prove that a normal and nilpotent operator is the zero operator.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: pf-step

Let $T$ be normal and nilpotent on a finite-dimensional inner product space $V$.

::: pf-proof

setup.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

A normal operator is diagonalizable (over $\CC$).

::: pf-proof

the spectral theorem for normal operators.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

A nilpotent operator has all eigenvalues equal to $0$.

::: pf-proof

if $Tv = \lambda v$ with $\lambda \neq 0$, then $T^n v = \lambda^n v \neq 0$ for all $n$, contradicting nilpotency.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Hence $T$ is diagonalizable with all eigenvalues $0$.

::: pf-proof

Steps [](#s2){.pf-ref} and [](#s3){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s5}

Therefore $T = 0$.

::: pf-proof

a diagonalizable operator with all eigenvalues $0$ is the zero operator.

:::
