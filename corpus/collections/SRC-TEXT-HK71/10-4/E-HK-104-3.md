::: {.exercise}
Let
\[
y_j = \sum_{k=1}^{n} M_{jk}x_k
\]
where $M$ is a member of $O(n, C)$.
Show that
\[
\sum_j y_j^2 = \sum_j x_j^2.
\]
:::


::: {.solution}
Let
$$
x=\begin{pmatrix}x_1\\ \vdots\\ x_n\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
y=\begin{pmatrix}y_1\\ \vdots\\ y_n\end{pmatrix}.
$$
The defining equations for the $y_j$ say exactly that
$$
y=Mx.
$$
Since $M\in O(n,\mathbb C)$,
$$
M^tM=I.
$$
Therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
\sum_{j=1}^n y_j^2
&=y^ty\\
&=(Mx)^t(Mx)\\
&=x^tM^tMx\\
&=x^tx\\
&=\sum_{j=1}^n x_j^2.
\end{aligned}
$$
Hence
$$
\boxed{\sum_j y_j^2=\sum_j x_j^2.}
$$
:::
