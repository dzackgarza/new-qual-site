::: {.exercise}
Let $F$ be a subfield of $C$, $V$ the space of $n \times 1$ matrices over $F$, $A$ an invertible $n \times n$ matrix over $F$, and $f$ the bilinear form on $V$ given by $f(X, Y) = X^tAY$.
If $M$ is an $n \times n$ matrix over $F$, show that $M$ preserves $f$ if and only if $A^{-1}M^tA = M^{-1}$.
:::


::: {.solution}
<1>1. If $M$ preserves $f$, then $A^{-1}M^tA=M^{-1}$.
::: {.proof}
Preservation means
$$
f(MX,MY)=f(X,Y)
$$
for all $X,Y$. Thus
$$
X^tM^tAMY=X^tAY
$$
for all $X,Y$, and hence
$$
M^tAM=A.
$$
Taking determinants and using $\det A\ne0$ gives
$$
(\det M)^2=1,
$$
so $M$ is invertible. Now multiply
$$
M^tAM=A
$$
on the left by $A^{-1}$ and on the right by $M^{-1}$ to obtain
$$
\boxed{A^{-1}M^tA=M^{-1}.}
$$
:::

<1>2. If $A^{-1}M^tA=M^{-1}$, then $M$ preserves $f$.
::: {.proof}
The displayed equation already implies that $M$ is invertible. Multiply it on
the left by $A$ and on the right by $M$:
$$
M^tAM=A.
$$
Therefore, for all $X,Y$,
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(MX,MY)
&=X^tM^tAMY\\
&=X^tAY\\
&=f(X,Y).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus $M$ preserves $f$.
:::

Hence
$$
\boxed{M\text{ preserves }f\iff A^{-1}M^tA=M^{-1}.}
$$
:::
