::: {.exercise}
Let $f$ be the bilinear form on $C^2$ defined by
\[
f((x_1, x_2), (y_1, y_2)) = x_1y_2 - x_2y_1.
\]
Show that

(a) if $T$ is a linear operator on $C^2$, then $f(T\alpha, T\beta) = (\det T)f(\alpha, \beta)$ for all $\alpha, \beta$ in $C^2$.

(b) $T$ preserves $f$ if and only if $\det T = +1$.

(c) What does (b) say about the group of $2 \times 2$ matrices $M$ such that $M^tAM = A$ where
\[
A = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ -1 & 0 \end{bmatrix}?
\]
:::


::: solution
For column vectors $\alpha,\beta\in\mathbb C^2$, let
$$
[\alpha\ \beta]
$$
denote the $2\times2$ matrix having these vectors as columns. Then
$$
f(\alpha,\beta)=\det[\alpha\ \beta].
$$

<1>1. Compute the effect of an arbitrary linear operator.
::: proof
Let $M$ be the matrix of $T$ in the standard basis. Then
$$
[T\alpha\ T\beta]=M[\alpha\ \beta].
$$
Therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(T\alpha,T\beta)
&=\det(M[\alpha\ \beta])\\
&=(\det M)\det[\alpha\ \beta]\\
&=(\det T)f(\alpha,\beta).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus
$$
\boxed{f(T\alpha,T\beta)=(\det T)f(\alpha,\beta).}
$$
:::

<1>2. Characterize the operators preserving $f$.
::: proof
If
$$
\det T=1,
$$
step <1>1 immediately gives
$$
f(T\alpha,T\beta)=f(\alpha,\beta)
$$
for all $\alpha,\beta$, so $T$ preserves $f$.

Conversely, if $T$ preserves $f$, take the standard basis vectors
$e_1,e_2$. Since
$$
f(e_1,e_2)=1,
$$
step <1>1 gives
$$
1=f(Te_1,Te_2)=(\det T)f(e_1,e_2)=\det T.
$$
Hence
$$
\boxed{T\text{ preserves }f\iff\det T=1.}
$$
:::

<1>3. Identify the matrix group preserving $A$.
::: proof
The form $f$ has matrix
$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix},
$$
so the equation
$$
M^tAM=A
$$
is exactly the statement that the linear operator with matrix $M$ preserves
$f$. By step <1>2, this happens exactly when
$$
\det M=1.
$$
Therefore
$$
\boxed{
\{M\in GL_2(\mathbb C):M^tAM=A\}=SL_2(\mathbb C).}
$$
In particular, the complex symplectic group in dimension two is
$SL_2(\mathbb C)$.
:::
:::
