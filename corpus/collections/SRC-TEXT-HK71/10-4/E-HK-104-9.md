::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be the space of all $n \times 1$ matrices over $C$, $A$ an $n \times n$ matrix over $C$, and $f$ the bilinear form on $V$ given by $f(X, Y) = X^tAY$.
Show that $f$ is invariant under $O(n, C)$, i.e., $f(MX, MY) = f(X, Y)$ for all $X, Y$ in $V$ and $M$ in $O(n, C)$, if and only if $A$ commutes with each member of $O(n, C)$.
:::


::: solution
For $M\in O(n,\mathbb C)$ and $X,Y\in V$,
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(MX,MY)
&=(MX)^tA(MY)\\
&=X^tM^tAMY.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus
$$
f(MX,MY)=f(X,Y)
$$
for all $X,Y$ if and only if
$$
M^tAM=A.
$$
Indeed, equality of the two bilinear forms for all $X,Y$ forces equality of
their representing matrices.

Since $M\in O(n,\mathbb C)$,
$$
M^t=M^{-1}.
$$
Therefore
$$
M^tAM=A
\iff M^{-1}AM=A
\iff AM=MA.
$$
This equivalence holds for every $M\in O(n,\mathbb C)$. Hence
$$
\boxed{
f\text{ is }O(n,\mathbb C)\text{-invariant}
\iff
AM=MA\text{ for every }M\in O(n,\mathbb C).}
$$
:::
