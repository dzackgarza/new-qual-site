::: {.exercise}
Let $S$ be any set of $n \times n$ matrices over $C$ and $S'$ the set of all $n \times n$ matrices over $C$ which commute with each element of $S$.
Show that $S'$ is an algebra over $C$.
:::


::: {.solution}
Let
$$
S'=\{A\in M_n(\mathbb C): AM=MA\text{ for every }M\in S\}.
$$

<1>1. The set $S'$ is a complex vector subspace of $M_n(\mathbb C)$.
::: {.proof}
The zero matrix commutes with every $M\in S$. If $A,B\in S'$ and
$c,d\in\mathbb C$, then for every $M\in S$,
$$
\begin{aligned}
(cA+dB)M
&=cAM+dBM\\
&=cMA+dMB\\
&=M(cA+dB).
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus $cA+dB\in S'$.
:::

<1>2. The set $S'$ is closed under multiplication.
::: {.proof}
If $A,B\in S'$ and $M\in S$, then
$$
(AB)M=A(BM)=A(MB)=(AM)B=(MA)B=M(AB).
$$
Hence $AB\in S'$.
:::

<1>3. The identity belongs to $S'$.
::: {.proof}
For every $M\in S$,
$$
IM=M=MI.
$$
Thus $I\in S'$.
:::

Therefore $S'$ is a unital subalgebra of $M_n(\mathbb C)$ over $\mathbb C$.
:::
