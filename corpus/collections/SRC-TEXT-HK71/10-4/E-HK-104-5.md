::: {.exercise}
Let $X$ be an $n \times 1$ matrix over $C$.
Under what conditions does $O(n, C)$ contain a matrix $M$ whose first column is $X$?
:::


::: {.solution}
Write
$$
\langle Y,Z\rangle=Y^tZ
$$
for the standard non-degenerate symmetric bilinear form on $\mathbb C^n$.

<1>1. A necessary condition is $X^tX=1$.
::: {.proof}
If $M\in O(n,\mathbb C)$ has first column $X$, then Exercise 4 gives
$$
X^tX=M_1^tM_1=1.
$$
Thus every possible first column must satisfy
$$
\sum_{j=1}^n x_j^2=1.
$$
:::

<1>2. The condition $X^tX=1$ is sufficient.
::: {.proof}
Assume
$$
X^tX=1.
$$
Then the line
$$
L=\mathbb C X
$$
is non-degenerate for the form $\langle\ ,\ \rangle$. Hence
$$
\mathbb C^n=L\oplus L^\perp,
$$
where
$$
L^\perp=\{Y:X^tY=0\}.
$$
The restriction of the form to $L^\perp$ is non-degenerate: if
$Y\in L^\perp$ is orthogonal to all of $L^\perp$, then it is orthogonal both
to $L$ and to $L^\perp$, hence to all of $\mathbb C^n$, so $Y=0$.

Over $\mathbb C$, every non-degenerate symmetric bilinear form admits an
orthonormal basis. Thus choose
$$
X_2,\ldots,X_n
$$
in $L^\perp$ such that
$$
X_j^tX_k=\delta_{jk}
$$
for $2\le j,k\le n$. Together with $X_1=X$, these vectors satisfy
$$
X_j^tX_k=\delta_{jk}
$$
for all $1\le j,k\le n$.

Let $M$ be the matrix with columns
$$
X_1,X_2,\ldots,X_n.
$$
By Exercise 4,
$$
M\in O(n,\mathbb C),
$$
and its first column is $X$.
:::

Therefore
$$
\boxed{\text{such an }M\text{ exists}\iff X^tX=1.}
$$
:::
