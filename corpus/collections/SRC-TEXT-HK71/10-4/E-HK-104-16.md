::: {.exercise}
Let $n$ be a positive integer, $I$ the $n \times n$ identity matrix over $C$, and $J$ the $2n \times 2n$ matrix given by
\[
J = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & I \\ -I & 0 \end{bmatrix}.
\]
Let $M$ be a $2n \times 2n$ matrix over $C$ of the form
\[
M = \begin{bmatrix} A & B \\ C & D \end{bmatrix}
\]
where $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$ are $n \times n$ matrices over $C$.
Find necessary and sufficient conditions on $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$ in order that $M^tJM = J$.
:::


::: {.solution}
First compute
$$
JM
=\begin{pmatrix}0&I\\-I&0\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}A&B\\C&D\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}C&D\\-A&-B\end{pmatrix}.
$$
Since
$$
M^t=\begin{pmatrix}A^t&C^t\\B^t&D^t\end{pmatrix},
$$
we obtain
$$
M^tJM
=
\begin{pmatrix}
A^tC-C^tA & A^tD-C^tB\\
B^tC-D^tA & B^tD-D^tB
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Therefore
$$
M^tJM=J
$$
if and only if
$$
A^tC-C^tA=0,
$$
$$
B^tD-D^tB=0,
$$
$$
A^tD-C^tB=I,
$$
and
$$
B^tC-D^tA=-I.
$$

The last equation is redundant: transposing
$$
A^tD-C^tB=I
$$
gives
$$
D^tA-B^tC=I,
$$
which is equivalent to
$$
B^tC-D^tA=-I.
$$

Hence the necessary and sufficient conditions can be written compactly as
$$
\boxed{
A^tC=C^tA,\qquad
B^tD=D^tB,\qquad
A^tD-C^tB=I.}
$$
Equivalently, $A^tC$ and $B^tD$ are symmetric and the mixed block equation
$A^tD-C^tB=I$ holds.
:::
