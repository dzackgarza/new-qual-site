::: {.exercise}
Let $n$ be a positive integer, $I$ the $n \times n$ identity matrix over $\CC$, and $J$ the $2n \times 2n$ matrix given by
$$
J = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & I \\ -I & 0 \end{bmatrix}.
$$
Let $M$ be a $2n \times 2n$ matrix over $\CC$ of the form
$$
M = \begin{bmatrix} A & B \\ C & D \end{bmatrix}
$$
where $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$ are $n \times n$ matrices over $\CC$.
Find necessary and sufficient conditions on $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$ in order that $M^tJM = J$.
:::


::: {.solution}
No retained solution set covers this exercise; the argument is the card's own, and the block products below were recomputed for this pass. Here $J=\begin{bmatrix}0&I\\-I&0\end{bmatrix}$ with $I$ the $n\times n$ identity over $\CC$, and $M=\begin{bmatrix}A&B\\C&D\end{bmatrix}$ with $n\times n$ blocks.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #jm}
$JM=\begin{bmatrix}C&D\\-A&-B\end{bmatrix}$ and $M^{t}=\begin{bmatrix}A^{t}&C^{t}\\B^{t}&D^{t}\end{bmatrix}$.

::: pf-proof
Multiplying blockwise, the first block row of $J$ times $M$ is $(0\cdot A+I\cdot C,\ 0\cdot B+I\cdot D)=(C,D)$, and the second is $(-I\cdot A+0\cdot C,\ -I\cdot B+0\cdot D)=(-A,-B)$. The transpose of a block matrix interchanges the block rows and columns and transposes each block, giving $M^{t}$ as displayed.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #product}
$M^{t}JM=\begin{bmatrix}A^{t}C-C^{t}A & A^{t}D-C^{t}B\\ B^{t}C-D^{t}A & B^{t}D-D^{t}B\end{bmatrix}$.

::: pf-proof
Multiply the two matrices of step [](#jm){.pf-ref} blockwise:
$$
M^{t}(JM)=\begin{bmatrix}A^{t}&C^{t}\\B^{t}&D^{t}\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}C&D\\-A&-B\end{bmatrix}
=\begin{bmatrix}A^{t}C-C^{t}A & A^{t}D-C^{t}B\\ B^{t}C-D^{t}A & B^{t}D-D^{t}B\end{bmatrix},
$$
the four entries being $A^{t}C+C^{t}(-A)$, $A^{t}D+C^{t}(-B)$, $B^{t}C+D^{t}(-A)$ and $B^{t}D+D^{t}(-B)$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #equations}
$M^{t}JM=J$ if and only if $A^{t}C=C^{t}A$, $B^{t}D=D^{t}B$, $A^{t}D-C^{t}B=I$ and $B^{t}C-D^{t}A=-I$.

::: pf-proof
Two matrices are equal exactly when their blocks in the same position are equal. Comparing step [](#product){.pf-ref} with $J=\begin{bmatrix}0&I\\-I&0\end{bmatrix}$ gives the four block equations $A^{t}C-C^{t}A=0$, $B^{t}D-D^{t}B=0$, $A^{t}D-C^{t}B=I$ and $B^{t}C-D^{t}A=-I$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #redundant}
The equation $B^{t}C-D^{t}A=-I$ is a consequence of $A^{t}D-C^{t}B=I$.

::: pf-proof
Transposing $A^{t}D-C^{t}B=I$ gives $D^{t}A-B^{t}C=I^{t}=I$, and multiplying by $-1$ gives $B^{t}C-D^{t}A=-I$. So the fourth block equation carries no information beyond the third.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step}
$M^{t}JM=J$ if and only if $A^{t}C=C^{t}A$, $B^{t}D=D^{t}B$ and $A^{t}D-C^{t}B=I$.

::: pf-proof
By step [](#equations){.pf-ref} the condition is the conjunction of the four equations, and by step [](#redundant){.pf-ref} the fourth follows from the third; conversely the three displayed equations are among the four, so together they are equivalent to all four. Hence the necessary and sufficient conditions are that $A^{t}C$ and $B^{t}D$ be symmetric and that $A^{t}D-C^{t}B=I$, which is exactly $M^{t}JM=J$, that is $M$ symplectic.
:::
