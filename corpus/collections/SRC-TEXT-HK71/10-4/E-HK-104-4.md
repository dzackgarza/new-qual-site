::: {.exercise}
Let $M$ be an $n \times n$ matrix over $C$ with columns $M_1, M_2, \ldots, M_n$.
Show that $M$ belongs to $O(n, C)$ if and only if
\[
M_j^tM_k = \delta_{jk}.
\]
:::


::: {.solution}
Let $M_j$ denote the $j$th column of $M$. The $(j,k)$ entry of $M^tM$ is
$$
(M^tM)_{jk}
=\sum_{r=1}^n M_{rj}M_{rk}
=M_j^tM_k.
$$
Therefore
$$
M^tM=I
$$
if and only if
$$
M_j^tM_k=\delta_{jk}
$$
for every pair $j,k$.

But $M\in O(n,\mathbb C)$ is defined exactly by $M^tM=I$. Hence
$$
\boxed{M\in O(n,\mathbb C)\iff M_j^tM_k=\delta_{jk}.}
$$
:::
