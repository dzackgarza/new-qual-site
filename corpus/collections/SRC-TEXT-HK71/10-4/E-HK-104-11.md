::: {.exercise}
Let $F$ be a subfield of $C$, $V$ a finite-dimensional vector space over $F$, and $f$ a non-singular bilinear form on $V$.
If $T$ is a linear operator on $V$ preserving $f$, prove that $\det T = \pm 1$.
:::


::: {.solution}
Choose an ordered basis of $V$. Let $A$ be the matrix of $f$ and $P$ the
matrix of $T$ in this basis. Since $f$ is non-singular,
$$
\det A\ne0.
$$

Preservation of $f$ means
$$
f(T\alpha,T\beta)=f(\alpha,\beta)
$$
for all $\alpha,\beta$. In matrix form this is
$$
P^tAP=A.
$$
Taking determinants gives
$$
\det(P^t)\det(A)\det(P)=\det(A).
$$
Because
$$
\det(P^t)=\det P
$$
and $\det A\ne0$, we may cancel $\det A$ to obtain
$$
(\det P)^2=1.
$$
Over the subfield $F\subseteq\mathbb C$, the only roots of $x^2-1$ are
$1$ and $-1$. Hence
$$
\boxed{\det T=\det P=\pm1.}
$$
:::
