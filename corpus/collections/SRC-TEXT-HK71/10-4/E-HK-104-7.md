::: {.exercise}
Let $V$ be the space of all $n \times 1$ matrices over $C$ and $f$ the bilinear form on $V$ given by $f(X, Y) = X^tY$.
Let $M$ belong to $O(n, C)$.
What is the matrix of $f$ in the basis of $V$ consisting of the columns $M_1, M_2, \ldots, M_n$ of $M$?
:::


::: {.solution}
Because $M\in O(n,\mathbb C)$, it is invertible, so its columns
$$
\mathfrak B=(M_1,\ldots,M_n)
$$
form a basis of $V$.

The $(j,k)$ entry of the matrix of $f$ in this basis is
$$
f(M_j,M_k)=M_j^tM_k.
$$
Since $M$ is orthogonal,
$$
M^tM=I,
$$
and therefore
$$
M_j^tM_k=\delta_{jk}.
$$
Hence
$$
\boxed{[f]_{\mathfrak B}=I_n.}
$$
:::
