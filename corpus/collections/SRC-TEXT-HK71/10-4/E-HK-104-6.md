::: {.exercise}
Find a matrix in $O(3, C)$ whose first row is $(2i, 2i, 3)$.
:::


::: {.solution}
The prescribed row already has transpose norm one:
$$
(2i)^2+(2i)^2+3^2=-4-4+9=1.
$$
Choose
$$
r_1=(2i,2i,3),
\qquad
r_2=\frac1{\sqrt2}(1,-1,0).
$$
Then
$$
r_1r_2^t=0,
\qquad
r_2r_2^t=1.
$$

To find a third row orthogonal to $r_2$, take its first two entries equal. The
condition of orthogonality to $r_1$ then gives
$$
4ia+3z=0.
$$
Choosing $a=3/\sqrt2$ gives $z=-2\sqrt2\,i$, and hence
$$
r_3=\left(\frac3{\sqrt2},\frac3{\sqrt2},-2\sqrt2\,i\right).
$$
Now
$$
r_3r_3^t
=\frac92+\frac92+(-2\sqrt2\,i)^2
=9-8
=1,
$$
and directly
$$
r_1r_3^t=r_2r_3^t=0.
$$

Therefore the matrix
$$
\boxed{
M=
\begin{pmatrix}
2i&2i&3\\
\frac1{\sqrt2}&-\frac1{\sqrt2}&0\\
\frac3{\sqrt2}&\frac3{\sqrt2}&-2\sqrt2\,i
\end{pmatrix}}
$$
has mutually transpose-orthonormal rows, so
$$
MM^t=I.
$$
Thus $M$ is invertible with $M^{-1}=M^t$, and consequently
$$
M^tM=I.
$$
Hence
$$
M\in O(3,\mathbb C),
$$
and its first row is the required $(2i,2i,3)$.
:::
