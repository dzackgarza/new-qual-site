::: problem
Let $a,b\in\operatorname{PGL}_2(\mathbb C)$ have the same finite order $n$. Prove that there exists $c\in\operatorname{PGL}_2(\mathbb C)$ such that $cac^{-1}$ is a power of $b$.
:::
