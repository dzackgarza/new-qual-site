::: problem
Let $C,D\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ be nonempty closed sets and assume $C$ is bounded. Prove there exist $x_0\in C$ and $y_0\in D$ such that
\[
d(x_0,y_0)\le d(x,y)
\]
for all $x\in C$ and $y\in D$.
:::
