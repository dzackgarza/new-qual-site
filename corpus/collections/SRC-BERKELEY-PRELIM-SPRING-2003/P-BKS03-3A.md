::: problem
Let $R$ be the subring of $M_2(\mathbb Q)$ consisting of matrices commuting with
\[
\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

(a) Prove that $R$ is a subring of $M_2(\mathbb Q)$.

(b) Prove that $R\cong\mathbb Q[x]/(x^2)$.
:::
