::: {.problem}
Problem 2. Let µ be a finite positive Borel measure on R that is singular to
Lebesgue measure. Show that
                               µ([x − r, x + r])
                           lim                   = +∞
                          r→0+       2r
for µ-almost every x ∈ R.
:::
