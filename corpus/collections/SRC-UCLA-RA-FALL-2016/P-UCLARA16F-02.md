::: {.problem}
Let $\mu$ be a finite positive Borel measure on $\mathbb R$ that is singular to Lebesgue measure. Show that
\[
\lim_{r\to0^+}\frac{\mu([x-r,x+r])}{2r}=+\infty
\]
for $\mu$-almost every $x\in\mathbb R$.
:::
