Problem 12. Let U ⊂ C be an open set and K ⊂ U be a compact subset of U .
   (a) Prove that there exists a bounded open set V with K ⊂ V ⊂ V ⊂ U such
that ∂V consists of finitely many closed line segments. Hint: Consider a fine square
grid.
   (b) Let f be a holomorphic function on U . Show that there exists a sequence
{Rn } of rational functions such that (i) Rn → f uniformly on K and (ii) none of
the functions Rn has a pole in K. Hint: First represent f (z) for z ∈ K as a suitable
integral over the set ∂V and then notice that the integrand is equicontinuous in z.
:::
