::: problem
Prove that $|x|^{-p}$ is integrable on the unit ball in $\mathbb R^n$ exactly when $p<n$.
Prove that it is integrable outside the unit ball exactly when $p>n$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let
\[
\omega_n=m(B(0,1)).
\]

<1>1. Integrability near the origin.
::: {.proof}
For $k\ge0$, define the dyadic annulus
\[
A_k=\{x:2^{-k-1}<|x|\le2^{-k}\}.
\]
These annuli are pairwise disjoint and cover the punctured unit ball. Their measures are
\[
m(A_k)
=\omega_n\bigl(2^{-kn}-2^{-(k+1)n}\bigr)
=c_n\,2^{-kn},
\]
where
\[
c_n=\omega_n(1-2^{-n})>0.
\]

On $A_k$,
\[
2^{kp}\le |x|^{-p}<2^{(k+1)p}.
\]
Hence
\[
c_n2^{k(p-n)}
\le
\int_{A_k}|x|^{-p}\,dx
\le
c_n2^p\,2^{k(p-n)}.
\]
Therefore
\[
\int_{|x|\le1}|x|^{-p}\,dx<\infty
\iff
\sum_{k=0}^\infty2^{k(p-n)}<\infty.
\]
This geometric series converges exactly when
\[
p-n<0,
\]
i.e. exactly when
\[
\boxed{p<n}.
\]
:::

<1>2. Integrability at infinity.
::: {.proof}
For $k\ge0$, define
\[
B_k=\{x:2^k\le |x|<2^{k+1}\}.
\]
Then
\[
m(B_k)=c_n\,2^{kn}.
\]
On $B_k$,
\[
2^{-(k+1)p}<|x|^{-p}\le2^{-kp}.
\]
Thus
\[
c_n2^{-p}\,2^{k(n-p)}
\le
\int_{B_k}|x|^{-p}\,dx
\le
c_n2^{k(n-p)}.
\]
Consequently
\[
\int_{|x|\ge1}|x|^{-p}\,dx<\infty
\iff
\sum_{k=0}^\infty2^{k(n-p)}<\infty.
\]
This geometric series converges exactly when
\[
n-p<0,
\]
i.e. exactly when
\[
\boxed{p>n}.
\]
:::
:::
