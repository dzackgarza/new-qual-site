::: problem
1. If $f$ and $xf(x)$ are integrable on $\mathbb R$, prove that $F(t)=\int f(x)\cos(tx)\,dx$ is differentiable and find $F'(t)$.

2. Evaluate $\lim_{t\to0}\int_0^1(e^{t\sqrt x}-1)/t\,dx$ with full justification.
:::
