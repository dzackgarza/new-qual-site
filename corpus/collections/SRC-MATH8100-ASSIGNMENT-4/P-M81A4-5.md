::: {.problem}
For $f\in L^1(\RR^n)$, prove that
$$
\widehat f(\xi)=\int_{\RR^n}f(x)e^{-2\pi i x\cdot\xi}\,dx
$$
is bounded and continuous.
:::

::: {.solution}
For every $\xi\in\RR^n$,
$$
\abs{\widehat f(\xi)}
\le
\int_{\RR^n}\abs{f(x)}\,\abs{e^{-2\pi i x\cdot\xi}}\,dx
=\int_{\RR^n}\abs{f(x)}\,dx
=\norm{f}_1.
$$
Hence
$$
\norm{\widehat f}_\infty\le\norm{f}_1,
$$
so $\widehat f$ is bounded.

Now let $\xi_j\to\xi$ in $\RR^n$. For each fixed $x$,
$$
e^{-2\pi i x\cdot\xi_j}
\longrightarrow
e^{-2\pi i x\cdot\xi}.
$$
Therefore
$$
f(x)e^{-2\pi i x\cdot\xi_j}
\longrightarrow
f(x)e^{-2\pi i x\cdot\xi}
$$
pointwise. Moreover,
$$
\abs{f(x)e^{-2\pi i x\cdot\xi_j}}
=\abs{f(x)},
$$
and $\abs{f}\in L^1(\RR^n)$. By the dominated convergence theorem,
$$
\widehat f(\xi_j)
=\int f(x)e^{-2\pi i x\cdot\xi_j}\,dx
\longrightarrow
\int f(x)e^{-2\pi i x\cdot\xi}\,dx
=\widehat f(\xi).
$$
Thus $\widehat f$ is continuous on $\RR^n$.
:::
