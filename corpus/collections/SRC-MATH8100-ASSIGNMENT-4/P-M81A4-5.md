::: problem
For $f\in L^1(\mathbb R^n)$, prove that
$$
\widehat f(\xi)=\int_{\mathbb R^n}f(x)e^{-2\pi i x\cdot\xi}\,dx
$$
is bounded and continuous.
:::

::: {.solution}
For every $\xi\in\mathbb R^n$,
\[
|\widehat f(\xi)|
\le
\int_{\mathbb R^n}|f(x)|\,|e^{-2\pi i x\cdot\xi}|\,dx
=\int_{\mathbb R^n}|f(x)|\,dx
=\|f\|_1.
\]
Hence
\[
\|\widehat f\|_\infty\le\|f\|_1,
\]
so $\widehat f$ is bounded.

Now let $\xi_j\to\xi$ in $\mathbb R^n$. For each fixed $x$,
\[
e^{-2\pi i x\cdot\xi_j}
\longrightarrow
e^{-2\pi i x\cdot\xi}.
\]
Therefore
\[
f(x)e^{-2\pi i x\cdot\xi_j}
\longrightarrow
f(x)e^{-2\pi i x\cdot\xi}
\]
pointwise. Moreover,
\[
\left|f(x)e^{-2\pi i x\cdot\xi_j}\right|
=|f(x)|,
\]
and $|f|\in L^1(\mathbb R^n)$. By the dominated convergence theorem,
\[
\widehat f(\xi_j)
=\int f(x)e^{-2\pi i x\cdot\xi_j}\,dx
\longrightarrow
\int f(x)e^{-2\pi i x\cdot\xi}\,dx
=\widehat f(\xi).
\]
Thus $\widehat f$ is continuous on $\mathbb R^n$.
:::
