::: problem
Assume Fatou's theorem and deduce the monotone convergence theorem.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $(f_n)$ be a sequence of nonnegative measurable functions such that
\[
f_n\uparrow f
\qquad\text{pointwise}.
\]
We must prove
\[
\int f_n\longrightarrow\int f,
\]
with the value $+\infty$ allowed.

Since $f_n\le f$ for every $n$, monotonicity of the integral gives
\[
\int f_n\le\int f.
\]
Therefore
\[
\limsup_{n\to\infty}\int f_n\le\int f.
\]

On the other hand, Fatou's theorem gives
\[
\int f
=\int\liminf_{n\to\infty}f_n
\le
\liminf_{n\to\infty}\int f_n.
\]

Combining the two inequalities,
\[
\int f
\le
\liminf_n\int f_n
\le
\limsup_n\int f_n
\le
\int f.
\]
Hence the limit exists and
\[
\boxed{\lim_{n\to\infty}\int f_n=\int f.}
\]
This is the monotone convergence theorem.
:::
