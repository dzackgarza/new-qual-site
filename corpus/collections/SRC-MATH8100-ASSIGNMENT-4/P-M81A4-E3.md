::: problem
Let $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ have finite measure.
Let $(f_k)$ be uniformly integrable in $L^1(\Omega)$ and suppose $f_k\to f$ almost everywhere.

1. If $f\in L^1(\Omega)$, prove that $f_k\to f$ in $L^1$.

2. Decide whether the assumption $f\in L^1(\Omega)$ is necessary.
:::
