::: problem
1. Prove that $L^1$ convergence implies convergence in measure, and give a counterexample to the converse.

2. Suppose $|f_k|\leq g\in L^1$ and $f_k\to f$ in measure.
   Prove that $f\in L^1$ and $f_k\to f$ in $L^1$.
:::
