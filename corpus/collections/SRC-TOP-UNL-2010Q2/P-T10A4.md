::: {.problem}
Let $Z=X\cup Y$, for $X$ and $Y$ connected subspaces of $Z$ with $X\cap Y=\varnothing$.
Let $x_0\in X$ and $y_0\in Y$.
Let $\sim$ be the equivalence relation generated by the equivalence $x_0\sim y_0$.
Show that the quotient space $Z/\sim$ is connected.
:::

::: solution
**Theorem.**  
Let $q:Z\to Z/\!\sim$ be the quotient map identifying $x_0$ and $y_0$.
Then $Z/\!\sim$ is connected.

*Proof.*  
Consider the continuous images
$$
q(X)\subset Z/\!\sim,\qquad q(Y)\subset Z/\!\sim.
$$

**Lemma 1.**  
$q(X)$ and $q(Y)$ are connected sets.

*Proof.*  
The restrictions $q|_X:X\to q(X)$ and $q|_Y:Y\to q(Y)$ are continuous surjections.
Continuous images of connected spaces are connected. ∎

**Lemma 2.**  
The sets $q(X)$ and $q(Y)$ intersect.

*Proof.*  
Since $x_0\sim y_0$, we have $q(x_0)=q(y_0)$, so the common point
$q(x_0)=q(y_0)$ lies in both $q(X)$ and $q(Y)$. ∎

From Lemma 1, $q(X)$ and $q(Y)$ are connected.
From Lemma 2, $q(X)\cap q(Y)\neq\varnothing$.
Because
$$
Z/\!\sim=q(X)\cup q(Y),
$$
the quotient is the union of two connected sets with nonempty intersection.
Hence $Z/\!\sim$ is connected. ∎
:::
