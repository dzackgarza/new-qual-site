::: {.problem}
Attach a 2-disc $D^2$ to a torus $S^1\times S^1$ by the attaching map $e^{2\pi it}\mapsto(e^{2\pi it},e^{2\pi it})$, thinking of the boundary $\partial D^2$ and each $S^1$ factor as the unit circle in the complex plane, and let $X$ be the resulting space.
Compute a presentation for the fundamental group of $X$.
:::

::: solution
**Theorem.**  
Let $T=S^1\times S^1$ and let $X$ be obtained by attaching a 2-cell by
$\gamma(t)=(e^{2\pi it},e^{2\pi it})$.
Then
$$\pi_1(X)\cong\mathbb Z.$$

*Proof.*
Let $a,b\in\pi_1(T,x_0)\cong \mathbb Z^2$ be the standard meridian and longitude loops.

1. The attaching map of $\partial D^2$ has homotopy class $ab\in\pi_1(T)$ because it traverses both $S^1$ factors once with the same parameter.

2. By van Kampen, attaching one 2-cell forces the loop $\gamma$ to vanish in $\pi_1(X)$:
$$
\pi_1(X)\cong\langle a,b\mid [a,b],ab\rangle.
$$
But $T$ already has relation $[a,b]=1$, so adding $ab=1$ gives
$$
ab=1\implies b=a^{-1}.
$$

3. Every word in $a,b$ reduces to a power of $a$.
Hence
$$
\pi_1(X)\cong\langle a\rangle\cong\mathbb Z.
$$

So a presentation is
$$
\pi_1(X)\cong \langle a,b\mid ab=1,\ [a,b]=1\rangle\cong\mathbb Z.
$$
:::
