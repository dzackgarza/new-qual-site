::: {.problem title="?"}
Show that if $A$ is a path-connected subspace of $X$, $x_0\in A$, and the homomorphism $i_*:\pi_1(A,x_0)\to\pi_1(X,x_0)$ induced by the inclusion map $i:A\to X$ is surjective, then every path in $X$ with endpoints in $A$ is homotopic relative to the endpoints (i.e., via a homotopy that fixes each endpoint) to a path in $A$.
:::
