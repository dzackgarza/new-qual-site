::: {.problem}
Show that if $A$ is a path-connected subspace of $X$, $x_0\in A$, and the homomorphism $i_*:\pi_1(A,x_0)\to\pi_1(X,x_0)$ induced by the inclusion map $i:A\to X$ is surjective, then every path in $X$ with endpoints in $A$ is homotopic relative to the endpoints (i.e., via a homotopy that fixes each endpoint) to a path in $A$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let
\[
\alpha:[0,1]\longrightarrow X
\]
be a path with
\[
\alpha(0)=a\in A,
\qquad
\alpha(1)=b\in A.
\]

<1>1. There are paths
\[
\lambda:[0,1]\to A,
\qquad
\mu:[0,1]\to A
\]
with
\[
\lambda(0)=x_0,
\quad
\lambda(1)=a,
\qquad
\mu(0)=b,
\quad
\mu(1)=x_0.
\]
::: {.proof}
The points $x_0,a,b$ all lie in the path-connected space $A$.
Hence there is a path in $A$ from $x_0$ to $a$ and a path in $A$ from $b$ to $x_0$.
Choose these as $\lambda$ and $\mu$.
:::

<1>2. The concatenation
\[
\ell=\lambda*\alpha*\mu
\]
is a loop in $X$ based at $x_0$, and there is a loop
\[
\gamma:[0,1]\to A
\]
based at $x_0$ such that, as loops in $X$,
\[
\gamma\simeq\ell
\quad\text{relative to the basepoint}.
\]
::: {.proof}
The endpoint equalities in <1>1 and those for $\alpha$ make the concatenation well-defined and give
\[
\ell(0)=\ell(1)=x_0.
\]
Thus $[\ell]\in\pi_1(X,x_0)$.

Surjectivity of
\[
i_*:\pi_1(A,x_0)\to\pi_1(X,x_0)
\]
gives a class $[\gamma]\in\pi_1(A,x_0)$ satisfying
\[
i_*[\gamma]=[\ell].
\]
Choose a loop $\gamma$ in $A$ representing this class.
Equality of the two classes in $\pi_1(X,x_0)$ means precisely that, after viewing $\gamma$ as a loop in $X$, it is homotopic to $\ell$ through loops based at $x_0$.
:::

<1>3. The path
\[
\beta=\lambda^{-1}*\gamma*\mu^{-1}
\]
lies entirely in $A$ and runs from $a$ to $b$.
::: {.proof}
The reversed path $\lambda^{-1}$ runs in $A$ from $a$ to $x_0$, the loop $\gamma$ lies in $A$ and begins and ends at $x_0$, and $\mu^{-1}$ runs in $A$ from $x_0$ to $b$.
Hence their concatenation is an $A$-valued path from $a$ to $b$.
:::

<1>4. For any path $\eta$ from $u$ to $v$, the loops
\[
\eta^{-1}*\eta
\quad\text{at }v
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\eta*\eta^{-1}
\quad\text{at }u
\]
are homotopic relative to their endpoints to the corresponding constant loops.
::: {.proof}
With the usual equal-speed parametrization,
\[
(\eta^{-1}*\eta)(t)=\eta(|2t-1|).
\]
The formula
\[
H(t,s)=\eta\bigl(s+(1-s)|2t-1|\bigr)
\]
is a continuous homotopy from $\eta^{-1}*\eta$ at $s=0$ to the constant loop at $v=\eta(1)$ at $s=1$, and
\[
H(0,s)=H(1,s)=v
\]
for all $s$.

Similarly,
\[
(\eta*\eta^{-1})(t)=\eta(1-|2t-1|),
\]
and
\[
K(t,s)=\eta\bigl((1-s)(1-|2t-1|)\bigr)
\]
contracts it relative to its endpoints to the constant loop at $u=\eta(0)$.
:::

<1>5. The paths $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are homotopic relative to the endpoints $a,b$.
::: {.proof}
By <1>2, concatenate the based homotopy
\[
\gamma\simeq\lambda*\alpha*\mu
\]
on the left with the fixed path $\lambda^{-1}$ and on the right with the fixed path $\mu^{-1}$.
This gives a homotopy relative to $a,b$ from
\[
\beta
=
\lambda^{-1}*\gamma*\mu^{-1}
\]
to
\[
\lambda^{-1}*(\lambda*\alpha*\mu)*\mu^{-1}.
\]
The two parenthesizations of a fixed finite concatenation traverse the same component paths in the same order and differ only by an increasing piecewise-linear reparametrization $\varphi:[0,1]\to[0,1]$ fixing $0$ and $1$.
The straight-line interpolation
\[
\varphi_s(t)=(1-s)\varphi(t)+st
\]
also fixes $0,1$, so composing the path with $\varphi_s$ gives a homotopy relative to endpoints between the two parametrizations.
Consequently the latter path is homotopic relative to $a,b$ to
\[
(\lambda^{-1}*\lambda)*\alpha*(\mu*\mu^{-1}).
\]
By <1>4, the first parenthesized loop contracts at $a$ and the second contracts at $b$, both with their endpoints fixed.
Concatenating those contractions with the fixed path $\alpha$ yields a homotopy relative to $a,b$ from the displayed path to $\alpha$.
Therefore
\[
\beta\simeq\alpha\pmod{\text{endpoints}}.
\]
By <1>3, $\beta$ lies in $A$, which proves the claim.
:::
