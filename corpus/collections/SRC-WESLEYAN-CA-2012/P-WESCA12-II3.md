::: problem
Let $D\subset\mathbb C$ be a domain containing $0$ and let $f,g$ be analytic on $D$.
Suppose both have zeros of order $7$ at $0$.
Prove that
\[
\lim_{z\to0}\frac{f'(z)}{g'(z)}
\quad\text{and}\quad
\lim_{z\to0}\frac{f(z)}{g(z)}
\]
both exist and are equal.
:::
