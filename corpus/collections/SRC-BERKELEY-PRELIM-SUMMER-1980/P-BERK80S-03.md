::: {.problem}
Let $T : V  V$ be an invertible linear transformation of a vector space V . Denote by G the group of all maps $f _ { k , a } : V \to V$ where $k \in \mathbb { Z } , a \in V$ , and for $x \in V$

$$
f _ { k , a } ( x ) = T ^ { k } x + a \quad ( x \in V ) .
$$

Prove that the commutator subgroup $G ^ { \prime }$ of G is isomorphic to the additive group of the vector space $( T - I ) V$ , the image of $T - I$ . (G0 is generated by all $g h g ^ { - 1 } h ^ { - 1 }$ , g and h in G.)
:::
