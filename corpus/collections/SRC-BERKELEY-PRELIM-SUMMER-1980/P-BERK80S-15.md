::: {.problem}
Let E be a finite-dimensional vector space over a field F. Suppose $B : E { \times } E {  } \mathbf { F }$ is a bilinear map (not necessarily symmetric). Define subspaces

$$
E _ { 1 } = \{ x \in E \mid B ( x , y ) = 0 \ f o r a l l \ y \in E \} ,
$$

$$
E _ { 2 } = \{ y \in E \mid B ( x , y ) = 0 \ f o r a l l \ x \in E \}
$$

Prove that dim $E _ { 1 } = \dim E _ { 2 }$
