::: {.problem}
Suppose
\[
f(x_1,\ldots,x_n)=\sum_{j,k}a_{jk}x_jx_k
\]
for real numbers $a_{jk}$.
If $f$ is nonnegative for all real arguments, show that $f$ can be written as a finite sum of squares of linear forms in $x_1,\ldots,x_n$.
:::
