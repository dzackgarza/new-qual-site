::: {.problem}
Let $f:[0,\infty)\to\mathbb R$ satisfy:

- $f$ is continuous;

- $f(0)=0$;

- $f$ is differentiable on $(0,\infty)$; and

- $f'$ is increasing on $(0,\infty)$.

Define $g:(0,\infty)\to\mathbb R$ by
\[
g(x)=\frac{f(x)}x.
\]
Show that $g$ is increasing.

*Hint.* Differentiate.
:::
