::: {.problem}
Show that the splitting field of
\[
x^5-10x+5
\]
over $\mathbb Q$ has Galois group the symmetric group $S_5$ on five points.

You may assume that any subgroup of $S_5$ containing a $5$-cycle and a $2$-cycle is all of $S_5$.
:::
