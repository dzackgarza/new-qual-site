::: {.problem}
Suppose that $f$ is a positive continuous function on $[a,b]$.
Prove that there are polynomials $p_n$, for $n=0,1,2,\ldots$, such that $p_n$ is monic of degree $n$ and
\[
\int_a^b p_m(x)p_n(x)f(x)\,dx=0
\]
whenever $m\ne n$.
:::
