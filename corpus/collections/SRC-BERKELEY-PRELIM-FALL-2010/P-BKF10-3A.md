::: {.problem}
Suppose that $f$ is bounded and analytic on a deleted neighborhood $0<|z|<\varepsilon$ of the origin.
Let
\[
f(z)=\sum_{j=-\infty}^{\infty}c_jz^j
\]
be the Laurent expansion of $f$.
Show that if $j<0$, then $c_j=0$.
:::
