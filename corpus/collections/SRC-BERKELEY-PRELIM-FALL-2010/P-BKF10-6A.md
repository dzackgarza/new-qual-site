::: {.problem}
For nonzero integers $a,b,c$, show that
\[
\gcd\{a,\operatorname{lcm}\{b,c\}\}
=
\operatorname{lcm}\{\gcd\{a,b\},\gcd\{a,c\}\}.
\]
