::: {.problem}
Suppose $f:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$ is a bounded continuous function. Calculate the limit
\[
\lim_{\epsilon\to0^+}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f(t)\frac{\epsilon}{\epsilon^2+t^2}\,dt.
\]
:::
