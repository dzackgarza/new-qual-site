::: {.problem}
Consider finite directed graphs with no loops and no multiple directed edges, but allowing both $\overrightarrow{xy}$ and $\overrightarrow{yx}$.

A *monotone tournament* is an orientation of a complete graph for which some ordering of the vertices directs every edge from the smaller vertex to the larger. A *complete digraph* has both directed edges between every two distinct vertices.

Given $m\ge1$, prove that for all sufficiently large $N$, every digraph on $N$ vertices has an $m$-vertex subset inducing either an empty digraph, a complete digraph, or a monotone tournament.
:::
