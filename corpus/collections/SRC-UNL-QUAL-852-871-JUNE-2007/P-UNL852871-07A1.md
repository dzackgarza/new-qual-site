::: {.problem}
Let
\[
\mathcal A=\{A_1,\ldots,A_n\}
\]
be a family of distinct subsets of an $n$-element set $X$. Define a graph $G$ on $\mathcal A$ by joining $A_i$ and $A_j$ whenever
\[
|A_i\triangle A_j|=1.
\]
If $A_i\triangle A_j=\{x\}$, label the edge $A_iA_j$ by $x$.

Prove that there is a forest $F\subseteq G$ whose edges use every label that occurs on an edge of $G$. Deduce that there exists $x\in X$ such that
\[
A_1\setminus\{x\},\ldots,A_n\setminus\{x\}
\]
are all distinct.
:::
