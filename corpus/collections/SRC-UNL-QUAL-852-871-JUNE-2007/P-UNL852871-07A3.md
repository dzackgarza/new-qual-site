::: {.problem}
Let $x$ be a vertex of a connected graph $G$. For $r\ge0$, let
$$
G_r=G\bigl[\{y\in V(G):d_G(x,y)=r\}\bigr]
$$
be the subgraph induced by the vertices at distance $r$ from $x$. Prove that
$$
\chi(G)\le \max_r\bigl(\chi(G_r)+\chi(G_{r+1})\bigr).
$$
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

Every edge of $G$ has both endpoints in one layer $G_r$ or in two consecutive layers $G_r,G_{r+1}$.


::: pf-proof

If $uv$ is an edge, the triangle inequality for graph distance gives
$$
d_G(x,u)\le d_G(x,v)+1
$$
and symmetrically
$$
d_G(x,v)\le d_G(x,u)+1.
$$
Thus
$$
|d_G(x,u)-d_G(x,v)|\le1,
$$
which proves the claim.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

Put
$$
M\coloneqq\max_r\bigl(\chi(G_r)+\chi(G_{r+1})\bigr).
$$
There are sets of colors $C_r\subseteq\{1,\ldots,M\}$ such that
$$
|C_r|=\chi(G_r)
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
C_r\cap C_{r+1}=\varnothing
$$
for every $r$.


::: pf-proof

Choose any $C_0$ of size $\chi(G_0)$. Suppose $C_r$ has been chosen. Since
$$
\chi(G_r)+\chi(G_{r+1})\le M,
$$
the complement of $C_r$ in the $M$-element color set has at least $\chi(G_{r+1})$ elements. Choose $C_{r+1}$ of that size inside the complement. Induction constructs the required palettes.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

Then
$$
\boxed{\chi(G)\le M
=\max_r\bigl(\chi(G_r)+\chi(G_{r+1})\bigr).}
$$


::: pf-proof

For each $r$, properly color $G_r$ using only the palette $C_r$ from step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref}. Edges with both endpoints in the same layer are properly colored because the coloring of $G_r$ is proper. By step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref}, every remaining edge joins consecutive layers, and such layers use disjoint palettes. Hence these layer colorings combine to a proper coloring of all of $G$ using at most $M$ colors.

:::
