::: problem
Let $\mathcal{B}$ denote the set of all Borel subsets of $\mathbb{R}$.
Let $\mu:\mathcal{B}\to[0,\infty)$ be a finite Borel measure on $\mathbb R$.

(a) Prove that if $\{F_k\}$ is a sequence of Borel sets for which $F_k\supset F_{k+1}$ for all $k$, then
\[
\lim_{k\to\infty}\mu(F_k)=\mu\Bigl(\bigcap_{k=1}^\infty F_k\Bigr).
\]

(b) Suppose that for every $E\in\mathcal{B}$ with Lebesgue measure $m(E)=0$, it follows that $\mu(E)=0$.
Prove that for every $\varepsilon>0$ there exists $\delta>0$ so that if $E\in\mathcal{B}$ with $m(E)<\delta$, then $\mu(E)<\varepsilon$.
:::

::: solution
<1>1. Prove continuity from above.
::: proof
Let
\[
F:=\bigcap_{k=1}^\infty F_k.
\]
For $k\ge1$, set
\[
E_k:=F_k\setminus F_{k+1}.
\]
The sets $F,E_1,E_2,\ldots$ are pairwise disjoint, and for every $n$,
\[
F_n=F\cup\bigcup_{k=n}^\infty E_k.
\]
Because $\mu$ is finite,
\[
\mu(F_1)=\mu(F)+\sum_{k=1}^\infty\mu(E_k)<\infty.
\]
Hence the series on the right converges, and
\[
\mu(F_n)
=\mu(F)+\sum_{k=n}^\infty\mu(E_k)
\longrightarrow \mu(F).
\]
Thus
\[
\boxed{\lim_{n\to\infty}\mu(F_n)
=\mu\!\left(\bigcap_{n=1}^\infty F_n\right).}
\]
:::

<1>2. Prove the epsilon-delta form of absolute continuity.
::: proof
Suppose, toward a contradiction, that the conclusion fails. Then there exists $\varepsilon_0>0$ such that for every $n$ one can choose a Borel set $E_n$ satisfying
\[
m(E_n)<2^{-n}
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\mu(E_n)\ge\varepsilon_0.
\]
Define the tail unions
\[
F_n:=\bigcup_{k=n}^\infty E_k.
\]
Then $F_{n+1}\subseteq F_n$ and
\[
m(F_n)
\le\sum_{k=n}^\infty2^{-k}
=2^{1-n}.
\]
Therefore, for
\[
F:=\bigcap_{n=1}^\infty F_n,
\]
we have
\[
m(F)=0.
\]
By the assumed absolute continuity on null sets,
\[
\mu(F)=0.
\]

Part (a), applied to the decreasing sequence $(F_n)$, now gives
\[
\mu(F_n)\longrightarrow\mu(F)=0.
\]
But $E_n\subseteq F_n$, so
\[
\mu(F_n)\ge\mu(E_n)\ge\varepsilon_0
\]
for every $n$, a contradiction.

Hence for every $\varepsilon>0$ there exists $\delta>0$ such that
\[
m(E)<\delta\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\mu(E)<\varepsilon.
\]
:::
:::
