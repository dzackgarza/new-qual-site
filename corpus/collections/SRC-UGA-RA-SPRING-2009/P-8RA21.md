::: problem
Let $\mathcal{H}$ be a Hilbert space and $\{\varphi_k\}_{k=1}^\infty$ be a subset of $\mathcal{H}$ with the property that for every $f\in\mathcal{H}$
\[
\sum_{k=1}^\infty\bigl|\langle f,\varphi_k\rangle\bigr|^2=\|f\|^2,
\]
where $\langle f,\varphi_k\rangle$ denotes the inner product of $f$ and $\varphi_k$ in $\mathcal{H}$ and $\|f\|$ denotes the norm of $f$ on $\mathcal{H}$.

(a) Show that for all $f,g$ in $\mathcal{H}$,
\[
\langle f,g\rangle=\sum_{k=1}^\infty\langle f,\varphi_k\rangle\overline{\langle g,\varphi_k\rangle}.
\]
[Hint: Consider $\|f+g\|^2$ and $\|f+ig\|^2$.]

(b) Show that for all $f$ in $\mathcal{H}$,
\[
\lim_{N\to\infty}\Bigl\|f-\sum_{k=1}^N\langle f,\varphi_k\rangle\varphi_k\Bigr\|=0.
\]
[Hint: Use the fact that $\|h\|=\sup_{\|g\|\le 1}|\langle h,g\rangle|$ for all $h$ in $\mathcal{H}$.]
:::

::: solution
<1>1. Recover the inner product from the Parseval identity.
::: proof
For $f,g\in\mathcal H$, write
\[
a_k:=\langle f,\varphi_k\rangle,
\qquad
b_k:=\langle g,\varphi_k\rangle.
\]
By hypothesis,
\[
\sum_k|a_k|^2=\|f\|^2,
\qquad
\sum_k|b_k|^2=\|g\|^2.
\]
Applying the same identity to $f+g$ gives
\[
\|f+g\|^2
=\sum_k|a_k+b_k|^2.
\]
Subtracting the identities for $f$ and $g$ yields
\[
\operatorname{Re}\langle f,g\rangle
=\operatorname{Re}\sum_{k=1}^\infty a_k\overline{b_k}.
\]
Similarly, applying the identity to $f+ig$ yields equality of the imaginary parts. Therefore
\[
\boxed{
\langle f,g\rangle
=\sum_{k=1}^\infty
\langle f,\varphi_k\rangle
\overline{\langle g,\varphi_k\rangle}.}
\]
The series is absolutely convergent by Cauchy--Schwarz in $\ell^2$.
:::

<1>2. Estimate the reconstruction error against an arbitrary test vector.
::: proof
Let
\[
S_Nf:=\sum_{k=1}^N
\langle f,\varphi_k\rangle\varphi_k.
\]
For $g\in\mathcal H$, part (a) gives
\[
\langle f,g\rangle
=\sum_{k=1}^\infty
\langle f,\varphi_k\rangle
\overline{\langle g,\varphi_k\rangle}.
\]
Hence
\[
\langle f-S_Nf,g\rangle
=\sum_{k>N}
\langle f,\varphi_k\rangle
\overline{\langle g,\varphi_k\rangle}.
\]
By Cauchy--Schwarz,
\[
\begin{aligned}
|\langle f-S_Nf,g\rangle|
&\le
\left(\sum_{k>N}|\langle f,\varphi_k\rangle|^2\right)^{1/2}
\left(\sum_{k>N}|\langle g,\varphi_k\rangle|^2\right)^{1/2}\\
&\le
\left(\sum_{k>N}|\langle f,\varphi_k\rangle|^2\right)^{1/2}\|g\|.
\end{aligned}
\]
:::

<1>3. Pass from the dual estimate to norm convergence.
::: proof
Using
\[
\|h\|=\sup_{\|g\|\le1}|\langle h,g\rangle|,
\]
Step 2 gives
\[
\|f-S_Nf\|
\le
\left(\sum_{k>N}|\langle f,\varphi_k\rangle|^2\right)^{1/2}.
\]
The full series of squared coefficients converges to $\|f\|^2$, so its tails tend to $0$. Therefore
\[
\boxed{
\lim_{N\to\infty}
\left\|f-\sum_{k=1}^N
\langle f,\varphi_k\rangle\varphi_k\right\|=0.}
\]
:::
:::
