::: {.problem}
Let $F_2 = \langle a, b \rangle$ be the free group of rank 2, let $\theta$ be the homomorphism $F_2 \to \mathbb{Z}_4$ given by $a \mapsto 1$, $b \mapsto 2$, and let $K = \ker \theta$.
Find a minimal set of generators for $K$ as a subgroup of $F_2$.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. The homomorphism $\theta:F_2\to\mathbb Z/4$ is surjective, so $K$ has index $4$.
::: {.proof}
The generator $a$ maps to $1$, which generates $\mathbb Z/4$.
:::

<1>2. By Nielsen--Schreier, $K$ is free of rank
$$1+4(2-1)=5.$$
::: {.proof}
An index-$d$ subgroup of a free group of rank $r$ has rank $1+d(r-1)$.
:::

<1>3. Using the Schreier transversal $T=\{1,a,a^2,a^3\}$, a Schreier basis is
$$\boxed{a^4,\ ba^{-2},\ aba^{-3},\ a^2b,\ a^3ba^{-1}}.$$
::: {.proof}
For $t\in T$ and $x\in\{a,b\}$, the Schreier generator is $tx\overline{tx}^{-1}$, where $\overline{tx}$ is the representative of the coset determined by $\theta(tx)$. The $a$-generators are trivial except at $t=a^3$, giving $a^4$. Since $\theta(b)=2$, the four $b$-generators are exactly the remaining displayed words.
:::

<1>4. This set is minimal.
::: {.proof}
It is a free basis of the rank-$5$ free group $K$ by Schreier's theorem, and a free group of rank $5$ cannot be generated by fewer than five elements, as seen from its abelianization $\mathbb Z^5$.
:::
:::
