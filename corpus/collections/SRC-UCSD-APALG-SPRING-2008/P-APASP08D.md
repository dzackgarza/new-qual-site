::: problem
Give the expansion of the Garnir polynomial $G_T(x)$ corresponding to the tableau
$$
T = \begin{array}{ccc}
7 & & \\
2 & 3 & 5 \\
1 & 4 & 6
\end{array}
$$
in terms of the Garnir polynomials of the standard tableaux of the same shape.
:::
