::: {.problem}
Let $P(T)$ be the Young idempotent corresponding to the row group of the tableau
$$
T = \begin{array}{ccc}
5 & & \\
2 & 6 & 7 \\
1 & 3 & 4
\end{array}.
$$
Let $\chi$ be the character of the representation resulting from the action of $S_7$ on the left ideal $\mathbb{C}[S_7]P(T)$.
Give the expansion of $\chi$ in terms of the character basis $\{\chi^\lambda\}_{\lambda \vdash 7}$.
Hint: Use the Frobenius map.
:::

::: {.solution}
The row lengths of $T$ are $(3,3,1)$, so its row group is
\[
R_T\cong S_3\times S_3\times S_1.
\]
The left ideal generated by the normalized row symmetrizer $P(T)$ is the permutation module
\[
\mathbb C[S_7]P(T)
\cong
\operatorname{Ind}_{S_3\times S_3\times S_1}^{S_7}\mathbf 1.
\]
Under the Frobenius characteristic map, induction from a Young subgroup corresponds to multiplication of complete symmetric functions. Since $h_r=s_{(r)}$, we get
\[
\operatorname{ch}(\chi)
=h_3h_3h_1
=s_{(3)}s_{(3)}s_{(1)}.
\]

Apply the Pieri rule first to $s_{(3)}s_{(3)}$. Adding a horizontal $3$-strip to $(3)$ gives
\[
s_{(3)}s_{(3)}
=s_{(6)}+s_{(5,1)}+s_{(4,2)}+s_{(3,3)}.
\]
Now multiply by $s_{(1)}$ and add one box in every possible position:
\[
\begin{aligned}
s_{(6)}s_{(1)}&=s_{(7)}+s_{(6,1)},\\
s_{(5,1)}s_{(1)}&=s_{(6,1)}+s_{(5,2)}+s_{(5,1,1)},\\
s_{(4,2)}s_{(1)}&=s_{(5,2)}+s_{(4,3)}+s_{(4,2,1)},\\
s_{(3,3)}s_{(1)}&=s_{(4,3)}+s_{(3,3,1)}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Therefore
\[
\operatorname{ch}(\chi)
=
s_{(7)}
+2s_{(6,1)}
+2s_{(5,2)}
+s_{(5,1,1)}
+2s_{(4,3)}
+s_{(4,2,1)}
+s_{(3,3,1)}.
\]
Since the Frobenius map sends irreducible characters $\chi^\lambda$ to Schur functions $s_\lambda$, the required character expansion is
\[
\boxed{
\chi
=
\chi^{(7)}
+2\chi^{(6,1)}
+2\chi^{(5,2)}
+\chi^{(5,1,1)}
+2\chi^{(4,3)}
+\chi^{(4,2,1)}
+\chi^{(3,3,1)}.
}
\]

As a dimension check, the dimensions of these irreducibles are respectively
\[
1,6,14,15,14,35,21,
\]
so the total dimension is
\[
1+2(6)+2(14)+15+2(14)+35+21=140.
\]
On the other hand,
\[
\dim \operatorname{Ind}_{S_3\times S_3\times S_1}^{S_7}\mathbf1
=[S_7:S_3\times S_3\times S_1]
=\frac{7!}{3!3!}=140,
\]
as required.
:::
