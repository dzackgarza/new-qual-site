::: problem
Let
\[
A=\begin{pmatrix} 0 & -1 \\ 1 & -1 \end{pmatrix}.
\]

(a) Show that $A$ generates a matrix group $G$ of order three.

(b) Find a set of homogeneous $G$-invariant polynomials which generate $\mathbb{C}[x,y]^G$.

(c) Compute the Hilbert series of $\mathbb{C}[x,y]^G$.
:::

::: solution
First compute
\[
A^2=
\begin{pmatrix}-1&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
A^3=I.
\]
Since \(A\ne I\), the subgroup generated by \(A\) is
\[
G=\{I,A,A^2\},
\]
so \(|G|=3\).

Let \(\omega=e^{2\pi i/3}\), so
\[
1+\omega+\omega^2=0.
\]
Consider the linear forms
\[
u=x+\omega y,
\qquad
v=x+\omega^2y.
\]
Under the substitution
\[
(x,y)\longmapsto(-y,x-y)
\]
induced by \(A\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
u&\longmapsto -y+\omega(x-y)
=\omega x-(1+\omega)y
=\omega x+\omega^2y
=\omega u,\\
v&\longmapsto -y+\omega^2(x-y)
=\omega^2x-(1+\omega^2)y
=\omega^2x+\omega y
=\omega^2v.
\end{aligned}
\]
Thus \(u,v\) diagonalize the action. Since the change of variables from \((x,y)\) to \((u,v)\) is invertible,
\[
\mathbb C[x,y]=\mathbb C[u,v].
\]

A monomial \(u^av^b\) is invariant exactly when
\[
\omega^{a+2b}=1,
\]
i.e. exactly when
\[
a+2b\equiv0\pmod3.
\]
If \(a\ge b\), write \(a=b+(a-b)\). The congruence gives \(a-b\equiv0\pmod3\), so
\[
u^av^b=(uv)^b(u^3)^{(a-b)/3}.
\]
If \(b\ge a\), similarly
\[
u^av^b=(uv)^a(v^3)^{(b-a)/3}.
\]
Hence every invariant monomial, and therefore every invariant polynomial, lies in
\[
\mathbb C[u^3,uv,v^3].
\]
The reverse inclusion is immediate because each of \(u^3,uv,v^3\) is invariant. Therefore
\[
\boxed{\mathbb C[x,y]^G=\mathbb C[u^3,uv,v^3].}
\]
In the original variables one may take the three homogeneous generators
\[
I_1=(x+\omega y)^3,
\qquad
I_2=(x+\omega y)(x+\omega^2y)=x^2-xy+y^2,
\qquad
I_3=(x+\omega^2y)^3.
\]
Their degrees are \(3,2,3\), and they satisfy the single relation
\[
I_2^3=I_1I_3.
\]
Thus
\[
\mathbb C[x,y]^G
\cong
\mathbb C[X,Y,Z]/(Y^3-XZ),
\]
where \(\deg X=\deg Z=3\) and \(\deg Y=2\). Consequently
\[
\begin{aligned}
H_{\mathbb C[x,y]^G}(t)
&=\frac{1-t^6}{(1-t^3)^2(1-t^2)}\\
&=\boxed{\frac{1+t^3}{(1-t^2)(1-t^3)}}.
\end{aligned}
\]
:::
