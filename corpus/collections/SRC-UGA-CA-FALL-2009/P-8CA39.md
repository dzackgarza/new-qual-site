::: problem
Let $D$ be the domain in the complex plane consisting of the interior of the unit disk, with the subdisk of radius $\frac12$ and center $\frac12$ removed.
Construct an analytic function that maps $D$ conformally onto the first quadrant $\operatorname{Re} z>0$, $\operatorname{Im} z>0$.

:::
