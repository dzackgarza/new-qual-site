::: {.problem}
Let $D$ be the domain in the complex plane consisting of the interior of the unit disk, with the subdisk of radius $\frac12$ and center $\frac12$ removed.
Construct an analytic function that maps $D$ conformally onto the first quadrant $\operatorname{Re} z>0$, $\operatorname{Im} z>0$.
(A figure appears in the original exam.)
:::

::: {.solution}

Here $D=\{z:\abs{z}<1,\ \abs{z-\frac12}>\frac12\}$, the unit disk with the closed subdisk removed. The two boundary circles are tangent at $z=1$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #cayley}

$w=\dfrac{1+z}{1-z}$ maps $D$ conformally onto the strip $S=\{w:0<\operatorname{Re}w<1\}$.

::: pf-proof

This Möbius map sends $\DD$ onto $\{\operatorname{Re}w>0\}$, because $\operatorname{Re}w=\frac{1-\abs{z}^2}{\abs{1-z}^2}$. The circle $\abs{z-\frac12}=\frac12$ is the set where $\abs{z}^2=\operatorname{Re}z$, and there
$$
\operatorname{Re}w=\frac{1-\abs{z}^2}{1-2\operatorname{Re}z+\abs{z}^2}=\frac{1-\operatorname{Re}z}{1-\operatorname{Re}z}=1.
$$
The same computation shows that $\abs{z-\frac12}<\frac12$, that is $\abs{z}^2<\operatorname{Re}z$, gives $\operatorname{Re}w>1$, and $\abs{z-\frac12}>\frac12$ gives $\operatorname{Re}w<1$. The map is a bijection of $\CC\setminus\{1\}$ onto $\CC\setminus\{-1\}$, so it maps $D$ onto $S$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #exp}

$\zeta=e^{i\pi w/2}$ maps $S$ conformally onto the first quadrant $Q$.

::: pf-proof

For $w=s+it$, $e^{i\pi w/2}=e^{-\pi t/2}e^{i\pi s/2}$. As $s$ ranges over $(0,1)$ and $t$ over $\RR$, the argument $\pi s/2$ ranges over $(0,\pi/2)$ and the modulus $e^{-\pi t/2}$ over $(0,\infty)$, and $(s,t)$ is determined by the argument and the modulus. So the map is a holomorphic bijection of $S$ onto $Q$.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

By Steps [](#cayley){.pf-ref} and [](#exp){.pf-ref},
$$
\boxed{f(z)=\exp\left(\frac{i\pi}{2}\cdot\frac{1+z}{1-z}\right)}
$$
maps $D$ conformally onto the first quadrant.

:::
