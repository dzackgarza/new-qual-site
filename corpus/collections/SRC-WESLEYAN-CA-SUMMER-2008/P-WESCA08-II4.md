::: problem
Let $D\subset\mathbb C$ be a domain and let $f,g:D\to\mathbb C$ be analytic.
Suppose $z_0\in D$, $f(z_0)=g(z_0)=0$, and
\[
\lim_{z\to z_0}\frac{f'(z)}{g'(z)}\in\mathbb C
\]
exists.
Show that
\[
\lim_{z\to z_0}\frac{f(z)}{g(z)}
\]
exists and calculate it.
:::
