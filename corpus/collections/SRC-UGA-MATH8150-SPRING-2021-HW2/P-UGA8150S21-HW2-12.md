::: {.problem}
Assume f is continuous in the region: $x \ge x _ { 0 } , 0 \le y \le b$ and the limit

$$
\operatorname* { l i m } _ { x \to + \infty } f ( x + i y ) = A
$$

exists uniformly with respect to y (independent of y). Show that

$$
\operatorname* { l i m } _ { x  + \infty } \int _ { \gamma _ { x } } f ( z ) d z = i A b ,
$$

where $\gamma _ { x } : = \{ z \mid z = x + i t , 0 \leq t \leq b \}$
:::

::: {.solution}
Orient $\gamma_x$ from $x$ to $x+ib$ and parametrize it by
$$
z(t)=x+it,
\qquad
0\le t\le b.
$$

<1>1. The contour integral is
$$
\int_{\gamma_x}f(z)\,dz
=
i\int_0^b f(x+it)\,dt.
$$

::: {.proof}
Under the stated parametrization,
$$
dz=i\,dt.
$$
Substitution into the definition of the contour integral gives the identity.
:::

<1>2. The difference from $iAb$ satisfies
$$
\abs{
\int_{\gamma_x}f(z)\,dz-iAb}
\le
b\sup_{0\le t\le b}\abs{f(x+it)-A}.
$$

::: {.proof}
By step <1>1,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\int_{\gamma_x}f(z)\,dz-iAb
&=
i\int_0^b(f(x+it)-A)\,dt.
\end{aligned}
$$
Therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
\abs{
\int_{\gamma_x}f(z)\,dz-iAb}
&\le
\int_0^b\abs{f(x+it)-A}\,dt\\
&\le
b\sup_{0\le t\le b}\abs{f(x+it)-A}.
\end{aligned}
$$
:::

<1>3. Consequently
$$
\boxed{
\lim_{x\to+\infty}
\int_{\gamma_x}f(z)\,dz
=
iAb}.
$$

::: {.proof}
The assumed convergence
$$
f(x+iy)\longrightarrow A
$$
is uniform for $0\le y\le b$. Hence
$$
\sup_{0\le t\le b}\abs{f(x+it)-A}
\longrightarrow0.
$$
The estimate in step <1>2 then shows that the difference between the contour integral and $iAb$ tends to zero.
:::

<1>4. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>3 is the required limit.
:::
:::
