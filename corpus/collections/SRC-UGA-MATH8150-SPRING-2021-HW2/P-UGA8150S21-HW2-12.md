::: {.problem}
Assume $f$ is continuous in the region $x \geq x_0$, $0 \leq y \leq b$, and the limit
$$
\lim_{x \to +\infty} f(x + iy) = A
$$
exists uniformly with respect to $y$ (independent of $y$). Show that
$$
\lim_{x \to +\infty} \int_{\gamma_x} f(z)\,dz = iAb,
$$
where $\gamma_x \coloneqq \theset{z \suchthat z = x + it,\ 0 \leq t \leq b}$.
:::

::: {.solution}

Orient $\gamma_x$ from $x$ to $x+ib$ and parametrize it by
$$
z(t)=x+it,
\qquad
0\le t\le b.
$$

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

The contour integral is
$$
\int_{\gamma_x}f(z)\,dz
=
i\int_0^b f(x+it)\,dt.
$$


::: pf-proof

Under the stated parametrization,
$$
dz=i\,dt.
$$
Substitution into the definition of the contour integral gives the identity.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

The difference from $iAb$ satisfies
$$
\abs{
\int_{\gamma_x}f(z)\,dz-iAb}
\le
b\sup_{0\le t\le b}\abs{f(x+it)-A}.
$$


::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref},
$$
\begin{aligned}
\int_{\gamma_x}f(z)\,dz-iAb
&=
i\int_0^b(f(x+it)-A)\,dt.
\end{aligned}
$$
Therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
\abs{
\int_{\gamma_x}f(z)\,dz-iAb}
&\le
\int_0^b\abs{f(x+it)-A}\,dt\\
&\le
b\sup_{0\le t\le b}\abs{f(x+it)-A}.
\end{aligned}
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

Consequently
$$
\boxed{
\lim_{x\to+\infty}
\int_{\gamma_x}f(z)\,dz
=
iAb}.
$$


::: pf-proof

The assumed convergence
$$
f(x+iy)\longrightarrow A
$$
is uniform for $0\le y\le b$. Hence
$$
\sup_{0\le t\le b}\abs{f(x+it)-A}
\longrightarrow0.
$$
The estimate in step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} then shows that the difference between the contour integral and $iAb$ tends to zero.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} is the required limit.

:::
