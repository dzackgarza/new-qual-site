::: {.problem}
(a) Express sin z in the form $u + i v$ . Do the same for cos z.

(b) Show that there exists a sequence $z _ { n }$ such that sin $z _ { n } \to \infty$ . Do the same for cos z.

(This shows that unlike their real counter parts, sin z and cos z are unbounded.)
:::
