:::{.problem}
For each $t\in\mathbb R$, let $P(t)$ be a real symmetric $n\times n$ matrix whose entries depend continuously on $t$. Suppose every eigenvalue of $P(t)$ is at most $-1$ for every $t$.
If $x(t)$ solves
\[
x'(t)=P(t)x(t),
\]
prove that
\[
\lim_{t\to\infty}x(t)=0.
\]
:::
