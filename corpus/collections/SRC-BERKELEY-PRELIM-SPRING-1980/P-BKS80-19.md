:::{.problem}
Let
\[
f(z)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}c_nz^n
\]
be analytic and one-to-one on the unit disk $\mathbb D$, mapping it onto a domain $G$ of area $A$.
Prove that
\[
A=\pi\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}n|c_n|^2.
\]
:::
