::: {.problem}
Let $S_9$ be the symmetric group on nine letters.

1. Exhibit an element of $S_9$ of order $20$.
2. Prove that no element of $S_9$ has order $18$.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

An element of order $20$ is
$$
\boxed{\sigma=(12345)(6789)}.
$$

::: pf-proof

The two displayed cycles are disjoint and have lengths $5$ and $4$.
Therefore
$$
|\sigma|
=
\operatorname{lcm}(5,4)
=
20.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

No element of $S_9$ has order $18$.

::: pf-proof

Suppose that $\tau\in S_9$ had order $18$. Write $\tau$ as a product of
disjoint cycles, with cycle lengths $d_1,\ldots,d_r$. The order of $\tau$
is
$$
\operatorname{lcm}(d_1,\ldots,d_r)=18.
$$
Since $9\mid18$, at least one cycle length $d_i$ must be divisible by $9$.
Every cycle in $S_9$ has length at most $9$, so $d_i=9$. A $9$-cycle uses
all nine letters, leaving only fixed points. Hence $\tau$ itself has order
$9$, contradicting the assumption that its order is $18$.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1){.pf-ref} proves part (1), and step [](#s2){.pf-ref} proves part (2).
