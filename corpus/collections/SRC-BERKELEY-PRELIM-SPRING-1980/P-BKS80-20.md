:::{.problem}
Does there exist an analytic function mapping the annulus
\[
A=\{z:1\le |z|\le4\}
\]
onto
\[
B=\{z:1\le |z|\le2\}
\]
and taking the boundary circles $C_1$ to $C_1$ and $C_4$ to $C_2$, where $C_r=\{z:|z|=r\}$?
:::
