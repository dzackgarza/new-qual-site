:::{.problem}
For each $n\ge1$, let $f_n:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ be differentiable with
\[
|f_n'(x)|\le1
\]
for all $n,x$. Suppose $f_n(x)\to g(x)$ for every $x\in\mathbb R$.
Prove that $g$ is continuous.
:::
