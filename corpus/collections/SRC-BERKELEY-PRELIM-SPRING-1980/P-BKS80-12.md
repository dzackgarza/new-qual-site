::: {.problem}
Let $M_{3\times3}(\mathbb R)$ be the vector space of real $3\times3$ matrices. For $A\in M_{3\times3}(\mathbb R)$ define
\[
L_A(B)=AB.
\]
Suppose
\[
\det A=32
\]
and the minimal polynomial of $A$ is
\[
(t-4)(t-2).
\]
What is $\operatorname{tr}(L_A)$?
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

The matrix $A$ is diagonalizable over $\RR$, and its eigenvalues
are $2$ and $4$.

::: pf-proof

The minimal polynomial
$$
(t-4)(t-2)
$$
splits over $\RR$ into distinct linear factors, so $A$ is
diagonalizable. For a diagonalizable operator, the minimal polynomial is the
product of the distinct factors $t-\lambda$ as $\lambda$ ranges over its
eigenvalues. Since both factors occur here, the eigenvalues are precisely $2$
and $4$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

The eigenvalue $4$ has multiplicity $2$, the eigenvalue $2$ has
multiplicity $1$, and therefore
$$
\operatorname{tr}(A)=10.
$$

::: pf-proof

Let $m$ be the multiplicity of the eigenvalue $4$. By step [](#s1){.pf-ref}, both
eigenvalues occur, so $m\in\{1,2\}$ and the multiplicity of $2$ is $3-m$.
Because $A$ is diagonalizable,
$$
32
=
\det A
=
4^m2^{3-m}
=
2^{m+3}.
$$
Hence $m+3=5$, so $m=2$. The trace is therefore
$$
\operatorname{tr}(A)=2\cdot4+1\cdot2=10.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

The operator $L_A$ has trace
$$
\operatorname{tr}(L_A)=3\operatorname{tr}(A).
$$

::: pf-proof

Write a matrix $B\in M_{3\times3}(\RR)$ by its columns as
$$
B=[b_1\ b_2\ b_3].
$$
Then
$$
L_A(B)=AB=[Ab_1\ Ab_2\ Ab_3].
$$
Thus, under the column-space identification
$$
M_{3\times3}(\RR)
\cong
\RR^3\oplus\RR^3\oplus\RR^3,
$$
the operator $L_A$ is $A\oplus A\oplus A$. Its matrix is block diagonal
with three copies of $A$, so its trace is the sum of the traces of those
three blocks.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s4}

The requested trace is
$$
\boxed{\operatorname{tr}(L_A)=30}.
$$

::: pf-proof

By step [](#s2){.pf-ref}, $\operatorname{tr}(A)=10$, and step [](#s3){.pf-ref} gives
$\operatorname{tr}(L_A)=3\operatorname{tr}(A)=30$.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s4){.pf-ref} gives the requested value.

:::
