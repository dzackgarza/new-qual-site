::: {.problem}
Let
\[
f(z)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}a_nz^n
\]
be analytic in the unit disk. Assume $a_1\ne0$ and
\[
\sum_{n=2}^{\infty}n|a_n|\le |a_1|.
\]
Prove that $f$ is injective.
:::
