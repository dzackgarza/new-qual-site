:::{.problem}
Let $P_2$ be the real polynomials of degree at most two and define
\[
J(f)=\int_0^1 f(x)^2\,dx.
\]
Let
\[
Q=\{f\in P_2:f(1)=1\}.
\]
Show that $J$ attains a minimum on $Q$ and determine the minimizing polynomial.
:::
