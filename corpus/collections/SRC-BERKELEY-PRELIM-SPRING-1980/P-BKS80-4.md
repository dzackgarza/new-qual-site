::: {.problem}
Let $a>0$, $a\ne2$, and let $C_a$ be the positively oriented circle $|z|=a$.
Evaluate
\[
\int_{C_a}\frac{z^2+e^z}{z^2(z-2)}\,dz.
\]
:::
