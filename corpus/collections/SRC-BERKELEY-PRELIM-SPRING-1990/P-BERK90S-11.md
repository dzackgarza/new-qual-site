:::{.problem}
Let $f$ be bounded and analytic in the half-plane
\[
\operatorname{Re}z>0.
\]
Prove that for every $c>0$, the restriction of $f$ to
\[
\operatorname{Re}z>c
\]
is uniformly continuous.
:::
