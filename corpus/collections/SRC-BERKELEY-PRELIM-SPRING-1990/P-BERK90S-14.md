::: {.problem}
Let $A,B$ be subspaces of a finite-dimensional vector space $V$ with
\[
A+B=V.
\]
Write
\[
n=\dim V,
\qquad
a=\dim A,
\qquad
b=\dim B.
\]
Let $S$ be the set of endomorphisms $f$ of $V$ such that
\[
f(A)\subset A,
\qquad
f(B)\subset B.
\]
Prove that $S$ is a vector subspace of $\operatorname{End}(V)$, and express $\dim S$ in terms of $n,a,b$.
:::
