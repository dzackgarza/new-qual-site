::: {.problem}
Let $L>0$ and let $y:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ be smooth with
\[
y''+y'-y=0
\]
on $[0,L]$. Suppose
\[
y(0)=y(L)=0.
\]
Prove that $y\equiv0$ on $[0,L]$.
:::
