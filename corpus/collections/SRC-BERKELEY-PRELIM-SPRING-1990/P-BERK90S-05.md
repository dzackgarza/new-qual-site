:::{.problem}
Let $(x_n)$ be a sequence of nonnegative real numbers satisfying
\[
x_{n+1}\le x_n+\frac1{n^2}
\]
for every $n\ge1$. Prove that
\[
\lim_{n\to\infty}x_n
\]
exists.
:::
