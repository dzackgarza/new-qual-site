::: {.problem}
Let $A=(a_{ij})$ be the real $n\times n$ matrix with
\[
a_{ii}=2,
\qquad
a_{i,i+1}=a_{i+1,i}=-1,
\]
and all other entries zero. Prove that every eigenvalue of $A$ is a positive real number.
:::
