:::{.problem}
Let $z_1,\dots,z_n\in\mathbb C$. Prove that there is a subset
\[
J\subset\{1,\dots,n\}
\]
such that
\[
\left|\sum_{j\in J}z_j\right|
\ge
\frac{1}{4\sqrt2}\sum_{j=1}^n|z_j|.
\]
:::
