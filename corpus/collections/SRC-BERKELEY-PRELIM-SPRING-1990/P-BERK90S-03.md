:::{.problem}
Let $c_0,\dots,c_{n-1}\in\mathbb C$. Prove that every zero of
\[
z^n+c_{n-1}z^{n-1}+\cdots+c_1z+c_0
\]
lies in the open disk centered at $0$ of radius
\[
\sqrt{1+|c_{n-1}|^2+\cdots+|c_1|^2+|c_0|^2}.
\]
:::
