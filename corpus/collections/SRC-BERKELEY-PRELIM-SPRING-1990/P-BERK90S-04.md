:::{.problem}
Let $R$ be a commutative ring with identity, and let $R^*$ be its group of units. Suppose the additive group of $R$ is generated by
\[
\{u^2:u\in R^*\}.
\]
Prove that there is at most one ideal $I$ such that
\[
|R/I|=3.
\]
:::
