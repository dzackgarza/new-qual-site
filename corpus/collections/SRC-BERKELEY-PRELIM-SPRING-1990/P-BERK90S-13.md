::: {.problem}
Let $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ be infinitely differentiable. Suppose for some positive integer $n$ that
\[
f(1)=f(0)=f'(0)=f''(0)=\cdots=f^{(n)}(0)=0.
\]
Prove that there is $x\in(0,1)$ such that
\[
f^{(n+1)}(x)=0.
\]
:::
