:::{.problem}
Give an example of a continuous function
\[
v:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R^3
\]
such that $v(t_1),v(t_2),v(t_3)$ form a basis of $\mathbb R^3$ whenever $t_1,t_2,t_3$ are distinct real numbers.
