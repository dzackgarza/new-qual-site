::: {.problem}
Let $a_1,\dots,a_{10}$ be integers with
\[
1\le a_i\le25.
\]
Prove that there are integers $n_1,\dots,n_{10}$, not all zero, such that
\[
\prod_{i=1}^{10}a_i^{n_i}=1.
\]
:::
