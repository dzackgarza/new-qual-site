::: {.problem}
Let
\[
x_0=1,
\qquad
x_{n+1}=\frac{3+2x_n}{3+x_n}
\quad(n\ge0).
\]
Prove that
\[
x_\infty=\lim_{n\to\infty}x_n
\]
exists, and find its value.
:::
