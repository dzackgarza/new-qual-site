:::{.problem}
Let $p$ be prime with
\[
p\equiv3\pmod4.
\]
Suppose
\[
x^4+1=g(x)h(x)
\]
in $\mathbb F_p[x]$, where $g,h$ are quadratic. Prove that both $g$ and $h$ are irreducible over $\mathbb F_p$.
:::
