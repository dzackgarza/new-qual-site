:::{.problem}
Let $f$ be entire, real-valued on the real axis, and suppose
\[
\operatorname{Im}f(z)>0
\]
whenever $\operatorname{Im}z>0$. Prove that
\[
f'(x)>0
\]
for every real $x$.
:::
