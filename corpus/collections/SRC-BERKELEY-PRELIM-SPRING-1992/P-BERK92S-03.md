:::{.problem}
Let $f$ be analytic on a connected open subset $G\subset\mathbb C$. Suppose that for every $z\in G$ there is a positive integer $n$ such that
\[
f^{(n)}(z)=0.
\]
Prove that $f$ is a polynomial.
:::
