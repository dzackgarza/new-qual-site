::: problem
Consider the function
$$
f(z) = \frac{z^2 + 1}{z^2 - 1}
$$
in $G = \{z : |z| > 2\}$.
Does $f$ have a primitive in $G$ (i.e., $F \in H(G)$ such that $F' = f$)? Prove your assertion.
:::
