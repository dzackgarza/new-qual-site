::: problem
Let $G \subset \mathbb{C}$ be a bounded, simply connected region and let $a \in G$.
Let $f$ be an analytic self-map of $G$ (i.e., $f(G) \subset G$) such that $f(a) = a$ and $f'(a) = 1$.
Show that $f(z) = z$.
:::
