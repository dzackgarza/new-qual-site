::: problem
Construct a basepointed covering space of $X = S^1 \vee S^1$ corresponding to the subgroup of the free group $\langle a, b \rangle = \pi_1(X)$ generated by the elements $aba^{-1}b^{-1}$, $ab^{-1}a^{-1}b$, $a^{-1}bab^{-1}$, and $a^{-1}b^{-1}ab$.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. Let $R=S^1_a\vee S^1_b$. Begin with the labeled graph whose vertices are the nine lattice points
$$
(i,j),\qquad i,j\in\{-1,0,1\},
$$
and whose edges are the twelve sides of the four unit squares meeting the origin: horizontal edges are labeled $a$ and vertical edges are labeled $b$, oriented in the positive coordinate directions. Take $(0,0)$ as basepoint.
::: {.proof}
The four given words are exactly the four oriented unit-square loops based at the origin:
$$
aba^{-1}b^{-1},\quad ab^{-1}a^{-1}b,\quad a^{-1}bab^{-1},\quad a^{-1}b^{-1}ab.
$$
Their union is precisely this $3\times3$ square-grid core.
:::

<1>2. The labeled map from this graph to $R$ is an immersion: at each vertex there is at most one outgoing and at most one incoming edge of each label.
::: {.proof}
Horizontal $a$-edges and vertical $b$-edges occur exactly as in the integer lattice, so no two edges with the same oriented label need to be folded together.
:::

<1>3. The core graph has
$$
V=9,\qquad E=12,
$$
so its fundamental group has rank $E-V+1=4$, generated by the four square boundaries above.
::: {.proof}
The graph is connected. Its four bounded squares form a basis for its cycle space, and those cycles are exactly the four prescribed subgroup generators.
:::

<1>4. Complete this immersed core to a covering graph by attaching rooted trees wherever an $a^{\pm1}$- or $b^{\pm1}$-edge is missing, continuing until every vertex has exactly one incoming and one outgoing edge of each label.
::: {.proof}
The standard completion of a connected labeled immersion into a rose produces the Schreier covering graph of the represented subgroup; attaching trees introduces no new loops, hence does not enlarge the subgroup represented by the basepointed core.
:::

<1>5. The resulting basepointed covering $\widetilde R\to R$ corresponds exactly to
$$
\boxed{H=\langle aba^{-1}b^{-1},\ ab^{-1}a^{-1}b,\ a^{-1}bab^{-1},\ a^{-1}b^{-1}ab\rangle.}
$$
::: {.proof}
By <1>3 the based loops in the core generate exactly $H$, and by <1>4 the attached trees add no fundamental group. The subgroup carried by the completed based cover is therefore $H$.
:::
:::
