::: {.problem}
Consider the natural inclusion of $V = S^1 \vee S^1$ in the torus $T = S^1 \times S^1$.
Show that there does not exist a retraction $T \to V$.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. Let $i:V=S^1\vee S^1\hookrightarrow T^2$ be the natural inclusion. On fundamental groups,
$$
i_*:F(a,b)\longrightarrow\mathbb Z^2
$$
is the abelianization map.
::: {.proof}
The two circle generators map to the two coordinate loops of the torus, and the torus fundamental group is generated by them subject to the commutativity relation.
:::

<1>2. Hence $i_*$ is not injective: for example,
$$
[a,b]=aba^{-1}b^{-1}\ne1\quad\text{in }F(a,b)
$$
but $i_*([a,b])=0$ in $\mathbb Z^2$.
::: {.proof}
The commutator is a nontrivial reduced word in the free group, while every commutator vanishes in an abelian group.
:::

<1>3. If a retraction $r:T^2\to V$ existed, then
$$
r\circ i=\operatorname{id}_V
$$
and therefore
$$
r_*\circ i_*=\operatorname{id}_{\pi_1(V)}.
$$
::: {.proof}
Functoriality of $\pi_1$ preserves compositions and identities.
:::

<1>4. This would force $i_*$ to be injective, contradicting <1>2. Thus
$$
\boxed{\text{there is no retraction }T^2\to S^1\vee S^1.}
$$
::: {.proof}
A homomorphism admitting a left inverse is injective.
:::
:::
