::: problem
Let $K$ be the Klein bottle.
Compute the homology groups $H_*(K \times K; \mathbb{Z})$ and cohomology groups $H^*(K \times K; \mathbb{Z})$ (you don't need to work out the ring structure on the cohomology).
:::
