::: {.problem}
1. Let G be a non-abelian finite group.
   Show that $G / Z ( G )$ is not cyclic, where $Z ( G )$ is the center of G.

2. If $| G | = p ^ { n }$ , with p prime and $n > 0$ , show that $Z ( G )$ is not trivial.

3. If $| G | = p ^ { 2 }$ , show that G is abelian.
:::
