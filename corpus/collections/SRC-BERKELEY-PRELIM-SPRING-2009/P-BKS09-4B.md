::: {.problem}
Consider a polynomial expression $H ( \alpha ) = A + B \alpha + C \alpha ^ { 2 } + \cdot \cdot \cdot + D \alpha ^ { N }$ with rational coefficients A, $B , C , \ldots , D$ , where α is an algebraic number, in other words a root of some polynomial with rational coefficients. Prove that if $H ( \alpha ) \neq 0$ , then the reciprocal $1 / H ( \alpha )$ can be expressed as a polynomial in α with rational coefficients.
:::
