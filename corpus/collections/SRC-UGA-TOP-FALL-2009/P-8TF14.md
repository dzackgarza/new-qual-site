::: problem
State the classification theorem for compact surfaces and identify the surface drawn on the exam on your list.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. Statement of the Classification Theorem for Compact Surfaces:
<2>1. **Closed Surfaces (without boundary):**
Every compact, connected 2-dimensional manifold without boundary is homeomorphic to exactly one of:
1. The 2-sphere $S^2$ (orientable, genus $g = 0$, $\chi = 2$),
2. The orientable surface of genus $g \ge 1$: $\Sigma_g = \underbrace{T^2 \# \cdots \# T^2}_{g \text{ times}}$, with Euler characteristic $\chi(\Sigma_g) = 2 - 2g$,
3. The non-orientable surface of genus $k \ge 1$: $N_k = \underbrace{\mathbb{RP}^2 \# \cdots \# \mathbb{RP}^2}_{k \text{ times}}$, with Euler characteristic $\chi(N_k) = 2 - k$.
<2>2. **Surfaces with Boundary:**
Every compact, connected 2-manifold with $b \ge 1$ boundary components is homeomorphic to a closed surface with $b$ open disks removed:
- $\Sigma_{g, b}$ (orientable, genus $g \ge 0$, $b$ boundary components), with $\chi = 2 - 2g - b$,
- $N_{k, b}$ (non-orientable, genus $k \ge 1$, $b$ boundary components), with $\chi = 2 - k - b$.

<1>2. Invariant identification procedure:
<2>1. To identify any compact surface $S$ from a cell diagram or polyhedral representation:
1. Count the number of boundary components $b = |\pi_0(\partial S)|$.
2. Determine orientability: $S$ is non-orientable if and only if it contains an embedded Möbius strip (an orientation-reversing loop), and orientable otherwise.
3. Compute the Euler characteristic $\chi(S) = V - E + F$.
4. Determine the genus:
   - If orientable: $g = \frac{2 - b - \chi(S)}{2}$, identifying $S \cong \Sigma_{g, b}$.
   - If non-orientable: $k = 2 - b - \chi(S)$, identifying $S \cong N_{k, b}$.

<1>3. Conclusion:
The classification of compact surfaces is uniquely determined by orientability, genus, and number of boundary components. Q.E.D.
:::
