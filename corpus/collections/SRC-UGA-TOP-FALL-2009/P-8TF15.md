::: problem
Find the universal cover of $\RP^2\times S^1$ and explicitly describe its group of deck transformations.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. A universal covering map is
\[
p:S^2\times\RR\longrightarrow \RP^2\times S^1,
\qquad
p(x,t)=([x],e^{2\pi i t}).
\]
::: {.proof}
The antipodal quotient
\[
q:S^2\longrightarrow\RP^2,
\qquad
q(x)=[x],
\]
is a covering map, and
\[
\exp:\RR\longrightarrow S^1,
\qquad
\exp(t)=e^{2\pi i t},
\]
is a covering map. Their product is therefore a covering map
\[
q\times\exp:S^2\times\RR\longrightarrow\RP^2\times S^1.
\]

Both $S^2$ and $\RR$ are simply connected, so their product $S^2\times\RR$ is simply connected. Hence this covering is universal.
:::

<1>2. For every
\[
(\epsilon,n)\in\ZZ/2\ZZ\times\ZZ,
\]
define
\[
T_{\epsilon,n}:S^2\times\RR\longrightarrow S^2\times\RR,
\qquad
T_{\epsilon,n}(x,t)=((-1)^\epsilon x,t+n).
\]
Then $T_{\epsilon,n}$ is a deck transformation of $p$.
::: {.proof}
Since $[(-1)^\epsilon x]=[x]$ in $\RP^2$ and
\[
e^{2\pi i(t+n)}=e^{2\pi i t},
\]
one has
\[
p\circ T_{\epsilon,n}=p.
\]
Each $T_{\epsilon,n}$ is a homeomorphism, with inverse $T_{\epsilon,-n}$ because $\epsilon=-\epsilon$ in $\ZZ/2\ZZ$. Thus it is a deck transformation.
:::

<1>3. Every deck transformation of $p$ is equal to exactly one $T_{\epsilon,n}$.
::: {.proof}
Let
\[
F:S^2\times\RR\longrightarrow S^2\times\RR
\]
be a deck transformation. Fix $(x_0,t_0)\in S^2\times\RR$. Since
\[
p(F(x_0,t_0))=p(x_0,t_0),
\]
the point $F(x_0,t_0)$ lies in the fiber of $p$ over $p(x_0,t_0)$. That fiber is
\[
\{((-1)^\epsilon x_0,t_0+n):\epsilon\in\ZZ/2\ZZ,\ n\in\ZZ\}.
\]
Hence for a unique pair $(\epsilon,n)$,
\[
F(x_0,t_0)=T_{\epsilon,n}(x_0,t_0).
\]

Both $F$ and $T_{\epsilon,n}$ are lifts of the same map
\[
p:S^2\times\RR\longrightarrow\RP^2\times S^1
\]
through the covering $p$, because
\[
p\circ F=p=p\circ T_{\epsilon,n}.
\]
The domain $S^2\times\RR$ is connected, and the two lifts agree at $(x_0,t_0)$. Uniqueness of lifts therefore gives
\[
F=T_{\epsilon,n}.
\]
The pair is unique because distinct pairs send $(x_0,t_0)$ to distinct points of its fiber.
:::

<1>4. The group law is
\[
T_{\epsilon,n}\circ T_{\delta,m}
=T_{\epsilon+\delta,n+m},
\]
so
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{Deck}(p)\cong\ZZ/2\ZZ\times\ZZ}.
\]
::: {.proof}
For $(x,t)\in S^2\times\RR$,
\[
T_{\epsilon,n}(T_{\delta,m}(x,t))
=T_{\epsilon,n}((-1)^\delta x,t+m)
=((-1)^{\epsilon+\delta}x,t+n+m).
\]
Thus composition corresponds exactly to addition in $\ZZ/2\ZZ\times\ZZ$.
:::
:::
