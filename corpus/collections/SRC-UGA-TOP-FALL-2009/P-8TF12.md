::: problem
a. Show that a path-connected space is connected.

b. A topological space $X$ is locally path-connected if for every point $x\in X$ and every neighbourhood $V$ of $x$, there is a path-connected open set $U$ with $x\in U\subset V$.
Show that a connected and locally path-connected space is path-connected.
:::
