::: problem
Prove that $\int_0^\infty|\sin x/x|\,dx=\infty$. Use the specified repeated integral with $xe^{-xy}(1-\cos y)$ to prove that $\lim_{A\to\infty}\int_0^A\sin x/x\,dx=\pi/2$.
:::
