::: problem
Let $A,B\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ be bounded measurable sets of positive measure.
Set $g(t)=m(A\cap(t-B))$.

1. Prove that $g$ is continuous and $\int g=m(A)m(B)$.

2. Deduce that $A+B$ contains a nonempty open set.
:::
