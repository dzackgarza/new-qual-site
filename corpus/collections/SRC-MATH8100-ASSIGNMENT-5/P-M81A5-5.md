::: problem
For $f,g\in L^1([0,1])$, set $F(x)=\int_0^xf$ and $G(x)=\int_0^xg$.
Prove that
$$
\int_0^1F(x)g(x)\,dx=F(1)G(1)-\int_0^1f(x)G(x)\,dx.
$$
:::
