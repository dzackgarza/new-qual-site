::: problem
For $f\in L^1(\mathbb R)$ and $h>0$, set $A_hf(x)=(2h)^{-1}\int_{x-h}^{x+h}f(y)\,dy$.

1. Prove that $\|A_hf\|_1\leq\|f\|_1$.

2. Prove that $A_hf\to f$ in $L^1$ as $h\to0^+$.
:::
