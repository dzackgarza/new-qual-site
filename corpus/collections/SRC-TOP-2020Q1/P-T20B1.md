::: {.problem}
Prove that every contractible space is path-connected.
**Caution:** $X$ being contractible does *not* imply that $X$ strong deformation retracts to a point.
:::

::: {.solution}
Since $X$ is contractible, there are $x_0\in X$ and a homotopy $H\colon X\times[0,1]\to X$ with $H(x,0)=x$ and $H(x,1)=x_0$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #path-to-basepoint}
For every $x\in X$, the path $t\mapsto H(x,t)$ runs from $x$ to $x_0$.

::: pf-proof
Its endpoints are $H(x,0)=x$ and $H(x,1)=x_0$.
:::

:::

::: pf-qed
For $x,y\in X$, the path of step [](#path-to-basepoint){.pf-ref} from $x$ to $x_0$ followed by the reverse of the path from $y$ to $x_0$ joins $x$ to $y$.
:::
