::: {.problem}
Suppose that $X$ is compact and nonempty, and $Y$ is Hausdorff and connected.
Prove that any continuous, open map $f:X\to Y$ is onto.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. The image $f(X)$ is compact.
::: {.proof}
The space $X$ is compact and $f$ is continuous, so its image is compact.
:::

<1>2. The subset $f(X)$ is closed in $Y$.
::: {.proof}
Compact subsets of Hausdorff spaces are closed, and $Y$ is Hausdorff.
:::

<1>3. The subset $f(X)$ is open in $Y$.
::: {.proof}
The whole space $X$ is open in itself. Since $f$ is an open map, its image $f(X)$ is open in $Y$.
:::

<1>4. The subset $f(X)$ is nonempty.
::: {.proof}
The domain $X$ is nonempty.
:::

<1>5. Therefore $f(X)=Y$.
::: {.proof}
By <1>2--<1>4, $f(X)$ is a nonempty clopen subset of the connected space $Y$. Hence it is all of $Y$.
:::

<1>6. Thus $f$ is surjective.
::: {.proof}
This is precisely the conclusion of <1>5.
:::
:::
