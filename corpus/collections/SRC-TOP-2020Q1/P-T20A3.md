::: {.problem}
Suppose that $X$ is compact and nonempty, and $Y$ is Hausdorff and connected.
Prove that any continuous, open map $f:X\to Y$ is onto.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #image-closed}
$f(X)$ is closed in $Y$.

::: pf-proof
$f(X)$ is compact as a continuous image of a compact space, and compact subsets of the Hausdorff space $Y$ are closed.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #image-open}
$f(X)$ is open in $Y$.

::: pf-proof
$X$ is open in itself and $f$ is an open map.
:::

:::

::: pf-qed
$f(X)$ is nonempty because $X$ is, and by steps [](#image-closed){.pf-ref} and [](#image-open){.pf-ref} it is clopen in the connected space $Y$, so $f(X)=Y$.
:::
