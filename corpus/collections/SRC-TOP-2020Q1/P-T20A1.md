::: {.problem}
Suppose $Y$ is Hausdorff, and $f:X\to Y$ and $g:X\to Y$ are continuous functions.
Prove that the set $A=\{x\in X:f(x)=g(x)\}$ is closed in $X$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $F\colon X\to Y\times Y$, $F(x)=(f(x),g(x))$, which is continuous because its coordinates are, and let $\Delta_Y=\{(y,y):y\in Y\}$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #diagonal-closed}
$\Delta_Y$ is closed in $Y\times Y$.

::: pf-proof
Let $(y_1,y_2)\notin\Delta_Y$.
Choose disjoint open $U\ni y_1$ and $V\ni y_2$; then $U\times V$ is an open neighborhood of $(y_1,y_2)$ disjoint from $\Delta_Y$.
:::

:::

::: pf-qed
$x\in F^{-1}(\Delta_Y)$ exactly when $f(x)=g(x)$, so $A=F^{-1}(\Delta_Y)$, which is closed by step [](#diagonal-closed){.pf-ref} and continuity of $F$.
:::
