::: {.problem}
The free group $F(a,b)$ on two generators is the fundamental group of the space $Z=S^1\vee S^1$, using the wedge point as basepoint, with $a$ and $b$ corresponding to each of the loops $S^1$.
Construct the covering space of $Z$ corresponding to the subgroup $H=\langle a^2b,aba,ab^2,b^2a\rangle$, determine the index of $H$ in $F(a,b)$, and decide if $H$ is a normal subgroup of $F(a,b)$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $\phi\colon F(a,b)\to\ZZ/3$ be the surjective homomorphism with $\phi(a)=\phi(b)=1$, and $K=\ker\phi$, of index $3$.

<1>1. $H\le K$.

::: {.proof}
Each of $a^2b$, $aba$, $ab^2$, $b^2a$ has total exponent sum $3$.
:::

<1>2. $K$ is free on $s_1=ba^{-1}$, $s_2=aba^{-2}$, $s_3=a^3$, $s_4=a^2b$.

::: {.proof}
These are the nontrivial Reidemeister--Schreier generators for the Schreier transversal $\{1,a,a^2\}$: for $a$ they are $1$, $1$, $a^3$, and for $b$ they are $ba^{-1}$, $aba^{-2}$, $a^2b$.
:::

<1>3. $K\le H$.

::: {.proof}
Put $u=a^2b$, $w=aba$, $x=ab^2$, $y=b^2a$.
Then $u=s_4$, $w=s_2s_3$, $x=s_2s_4$, and $y=s_1s_2s_3=s_1w$.
Hence $s_4=u$, $s_2=xu^{-1}$, $s_3=s_2^{-1}w$, and $s_1=yw^{-1}$ lie in $H$, and by step <1>2 so does $K$.
:::

<1>4. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
By steps <1>1 and <1>3, $H=K$, so $[F(a,b):H]=\boxed{3}$, and $H$ is normal as the kernel of $\phi$.
The covering space is the graph with vertices $v_0,v_1,v_2$ indexed by $\ZZ/3$, basepoint $v_0$, and for each label $c\in\{a,b\}$ oriented $c$-edges $v_0\to v_1\to v_2\to v_0$, mapped to $Z$ by sending each $c$-edge around the loop $c$.
Each vertex has one incoming and one outgoing edge of each label, so this is a connected $3$-sheeted covering, and a word lifts to a loop at $v_0$ exactly when $\phi$ vanishes on it, so its subgroup is $H$.
:::
:::
