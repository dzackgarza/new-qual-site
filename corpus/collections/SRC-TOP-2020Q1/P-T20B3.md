::: {.problem}
The free group $F(a,b)$ on two generators is the fundamental group of the space $Z=S^1\vee S^1$, using the wedge point as basepoint, with $a$ and $b$ corresponding to each of the loops $S^1$.
Construct the covering space of $Z$ corresponding to the subgroup $H=\langle a^2b,aba,ab^2,b^2a\rangle$, determine the index of $H$ in $F(a,b)$, and decide if $H$ is a normal subgroup of $F(a,b)$.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. Let
$$
\phi:F(a,b)\longrightarrow\mathbb Z/3,
\qquad
\phi(a)=\phi(b)=1.
$$
Every displayed generator of $H$ lies in $K=\ker\phi$.
::: {.proof}
Each word $a^2b$, $aba$, $ab^2$, and $b^2a$ has total exponent sum $3$, hence has image $0$ modulo $3$. Thus $H\le K$. Since $\phi$ is surjective, $K$ has index $3$.
:::

<1>2. With Schreier transversal $\{1,a,a^2\}$, the subgroup $K$ has free basis
$$
s_1=ba^{-1},\qquad
s_2=aba^{-2},\qquad
s_3=a^3,\qquad
s_4=a^2b.
$$
::: {.proof}
This is the Reidemeister--Schreier basis for the kernel of the exponent-sum map modulo $3$ using the three coset representatives $1,a,a^2$.
:::

<1>3. All four basis elements $s_i$ belong to $H$.
::: {.proof}
Set
$$
u=a^2b,\qquad v=aba,\qquad w=ab^2,\qquad y=b^2a.
$$
Then
$$
u=s_4,\qquad v=s_2s_3,\qquad w=s_2s_4,\qquad y=s_1s_2s_3=s_1v.
$$
Hence
$$
s_4=u,\qquad s_2=wu^{-1},\qquad s_3=s_2^{-1}v,\qquad s_1=yv^{-1},
$$
so every $s_i$ lies in $H$.
:::

<1>4. Therefore $H=K$, so $[F(a,b):H]=3$ and $H$ is normal.
::: {.proof}
We have $H\le K$ by <1>1 and $K\le H$ by <1>2--<1>3. Since $K$ is a kernel, it is normal.
:::

<1>5. The corresponding covering graph has three vertices $v_0,v_1,v_2$, and both the $a$-edges and the $b$-edges run cyclically
$$
v_0\longrightarrow v_1\longrightarrow v_2\longrightarrow v_0.
$$
::: {.proof}
The vertices are the three cosets of $H=K$, identified with $\mathbb Z/3$. Traversing either an $a$-edge or a $b$-edge adds $1$ modulo $3$. Thus the label-preserving graph map to the rose $S^1\vee S^1$ is a connected $3$-sheeted covering. Its based loops are exactly the words in $\ker\phi=H$.
:::
