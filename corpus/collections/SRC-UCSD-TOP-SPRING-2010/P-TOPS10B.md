::: {.problem}
How many distinct double covers does $\RP^3 \times S^1$ have?
Can you identify any of them?
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: pf-step
We have
$$
\pi_1(\RP^3\times S^1)\cong \ZZ/2\oplus\ZZ.
$$

::: pf-proof
Use the product formula for fundamental groups, with $\pi_1(\RP^3)=\ZZ/2$ and $\pi_1(S^1)=\ZZ$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #three-index-two-subgroups}
Connected double covers correspond to index-$2$ subgroups, equivalently kernels of nonzero homomorphisms
$$
\ZZ/2\oplus\ZZ\longrightarrow\ZZ/2.
$$
There are exactly three.

::: pf-proof
A homomorphism is determined by the images of the two standard generators, each independently in $\ZZ/2$. Of the four possibilities, exactly three are nonzero; every nonzero map onto $\ZZ/2$ has index-$2$ kernel.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #three-kernel-formulas}
The three kernels are those of
$$
(\epsilon,n)\mapsto\epsilon,\qquad
(\epsilon,n)\mapsto n\bmod2,\qquad
(\epsilon,n)\mapsto\epsilon+n\bmod2.
$$

::: pf-proof
These are the three nonzero elements of $\operatorname{Hom}(\ZZ/2\oplus\ZZ,\ZZ/2)$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #first-two-double-covers}
The first gives the product covering
$$
S^3\times S^1\longrightarrow\RP^3\times S^1,
$$
and the second gives
$$
\RP^3\times S^1\xrightarrow{\operatorname{id}\times(z\mapsto z^2)}\RP^3\times S^1.
$$

::: pf-proof
Their induced subgroups are respectively $0\oplus\ZZ$ and $\ZZ/2\oplus2\ZZ$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #third-double-cover}
The third is the mixed double cover associated to the subgroup
$$
\{(\epsilon,n):\epsilon\equiv n\pmod2\}\cong\ZZ.
$$
It can be modeled as the mapping torus of the antipodal map on $S^3$, with its evident projection to $\RP^3\times S^1$.

::: pf-proof
The subgroup is generated by $(1,1)$. Pulling the universal cover $S^3\times\RR$ down by this subgroup identifies $(x,t)$ with $(-x,t+1)$, which is precisely that mapping torus.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
Thus there are
$$
\boxed{3\text{ connected double covers}.}
$$
If disconnected covers are allowed, there is one additional trivial double cover $X\sqcup X\to X$, for a total of $4$.

::: pf-proof
The connected classification is steps [](#three-index-two-subgroups){.pf-ref}, [](#three-kernel-formulas){.pf-ref}, [](#first-two-double-covers){.pf-ref} and [](#third-double-cover){.pf-ref}. The zero homomorphism corresponds to the disconnected trivial two-sheeted covering.
:::
