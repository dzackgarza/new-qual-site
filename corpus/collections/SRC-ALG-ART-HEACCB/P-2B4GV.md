::: problem
Consider the $\ZZ\dash$submodule $N$ of $\ZZ^3$ spanned by 
\[
f_1 &= [-1, 0, 1], \\
f_2 &= [2,-3,1], \\
f_3 &= [0, 3, 1], \\
f_4 &= [3,1,5]
.\] 
Find a basis for $N$ and describe $\ZZ^3/N$.
:::
