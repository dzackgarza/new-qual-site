::: problem
Consider the $\ZZ\dash$submodule $N$ of $\ZZ^3$ spanned by 
\[
f_1 &= [-1, 0, 1], \\
f_2 &= [2,-3,1], \\
f_3 &= [0, 3, 1], \\
f_4 &= [3,1,5]
.\] 
Find a basis for $N$ and describe $\ZZ^3/N$.
:::


::: {.solution}
<1>1. Let \(L_{134}=\langle f_1,f_3,f_4
angle\) and \(L_{234}=\langle f_2,f_3,f_4
angle\). Then both are full-rank sublattices of \(\mathbb Z^3\) contained in \(N\).
::: {.proof}
Using the given vectors as columns,
\[
\det\begin{pmatrix}
-1&0&3\\
0&3&1\\
1&1&5
\end{pmatrix}=-23,
\qquad
\det\begin{pmatrix}
2&0&3\\
-3&3&1\\
1&1&5
\end{pmatrix}=10.
\]
Both determinants are nonzero, so each triple is linearly independent over \(\mathbb Q\) and spans a finite-index sublattice of \(\mathbb Z^3\). Since all of the generators lie in \(N\), both sublattices are contained in \(N\).
:::

<1>2. The index \([\mathbb Z^3:N]\) divides both \(23\) and \(10\).
::: {.proof}
For a full-rank sublattice generated by the columns of an integer \(3\times3\) matrix, its index in \(\mathbb Z^3\) is the absolute value of the determinant. Hence
\[
[\mathbb Z^3:L_{134}]=23,
\qquad
[\mathbb Z^3:L_{234}]=10.
\]
Because \(L_{134}\subseteq N\subseteq\mathbb Z^3\), multiplicativity of index gives
\[
23=[\mathbb Z^3:N]\,[N:L_{134}],
\]
so \([\mathbb Z^3:N]\mid23\). Likewise \([\mathbb Z^3:N]\mid10\).
:::

<1>3. Therefore \(N=\mathbb Z^3\).
::: {.proof}
Since \(\gcd(23,10)=1\), the positive integer \([\mathbb Z^3:N]\) must equal \(1\). A subgroup of index \(1\) is the whole group.
:::

<1>4. Thus one basis for \(N\) is the standard basis
\[
e_1=(1,0,0),\qquad e_2=(0,1,0),\qquad e_3=(0,0,1),
\]
and
\[
\mathbb Z^3/N=0.
\]
::: {.proof}
This follows immediately from <1>3.
:::
:::
