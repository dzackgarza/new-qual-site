::: {.problem}
Consider the equations
\begin{align*}
xy+x^2&=1,\\
y^2-2x^2&=-2.
\end{align*}

(a) Let $I$ be the ideal of $\mathbb{C}[x,y]$ generated by these equations.
Find the Gröbner basis for $I$ relative to lexicographic order where $y>x$.

(b) Find a Gröbner basis for $\mathbb{C}[x]\cap I$.

(c) Find all solutions to these equations that lie in $\mathbb{C}^2$.

(d) Find a vector space basis for $\mathbb{C}[x,y]/I$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let
\[
f_1=xy+x^2-1,\qquad f_2=y^2-2x^2+2.
\]
With lexicographic order $y>x$, a reduced Gröbner basis is
\[
G=\{\,y-x^3+x,\ x^4-1\,\}.
\]
Indeed, from $y=x^3-x$ we get
\[
xy+x^2-1=x(x^3-x)+x^2-1=x^4-1,
\]
so the original first equation is equivalent modulo $G$ to $x^4-1=0$. Conversely, using $y=x^3-x$ and $x^4=1$,
\[
y^2-2x^2+2=(x^3-x)^2-2x^2+2
=x^6-4x^4+3x^2
=x^2(x^4-1)-3(x^4-1),
\]
so it also lies in the ideal generated by $G$. Since the leading monomials are $y$ and $x^4$, which are relatively prime, Buchberger's criterion shows that $G$ is a Gröbner basis.

For (b), the elimination theorem gives
\[
I\cap\mathbb C[x]=(x^4-1),
\]
so a Gröbner basis for the elimination ideal is simply
\[
\{x^4-1\}.
\]

For (c), $x^4=1$, so
\[
x\in\{1,-1,i,-i\}.
\]
For each such $x$,
\[
y=x^3-x.
\]
Thus the four solutions are
\[
(1,0),\quad (-1,0),\quad (i,-2i),\quad (-i,2i).
\]

For (d), the standard monomials are those not divisible by either leading monomial $y$ or $x^4$. Hence they are
\[
1,x,x^2,x^3.
\]
Their residue classes form a basis of $\mathbb C[x,y]/I$. Therefore
\[
\dim_\mathbb C \mathbb C[x,y]/I=4.
\]
:::
