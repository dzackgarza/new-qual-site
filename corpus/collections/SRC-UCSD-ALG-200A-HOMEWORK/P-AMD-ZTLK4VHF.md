::: {.problem}
Given: $R$ a commutative ring, $x,y \in R$ nilpotent

- Show: $x+y$ is nilpotent

- Show: $\{x\in R : x \text{ is nilpotent}\} \normal R$

- Given: $u\in R^\times, x\in R$ nilpotent

- Show: $u+x\in R^\times$

- Show: An counterexample to 1 when $R$ is noncommutative.
:::
