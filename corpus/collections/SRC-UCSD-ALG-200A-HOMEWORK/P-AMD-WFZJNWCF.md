::: {.problem}
Given: $|G| < \infty$, $H \leq G$ maximal $\implies [G:H] = p$, a prime.

- Show: $|G|$ is solvable

- Given: $P \in Syl_p(G) \text{ and } \exists H \ni N_G(P) \leq H \leq G$

  - Show: $[G:H] = 1 \mod p$

- Given: $p \divides o(G)$, the largest such prime

  - Show: $\exists P \normal G \in Syl_p(G),$
:::
