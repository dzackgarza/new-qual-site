::: problem
Let $(X,\mathcal A,\mu)$ be a measure space and let $f_n,f$ be integrable real-valued functions.
Prove that if $f_n\to f$ almost everywhere and
\[
\int_X|f_n|\,d\mu\to\int_X|f|\,d\mu,
\]
then
\[
\int_X|f_n-f|\,d\mu\to0.
\]
:::
