::: {.problem}
Let $L^n$ denote the tautological complex line bundle over $\mathbb{CP}^n$,
\[
L^n=\{(\ell,z)\in \mathbb{CP}^n\times\mathbb C^{n+1}:z\in\ell\}.
\]
Compute the Euler class of $L^1$ by writing down transition functions for $L^1$.
:::
