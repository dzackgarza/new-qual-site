::: {.problem}
Suppose a continuous multiplication on $\mathbb R^n$ makes it a real division algebra.
Write $e_i$ for the $i$th standard basis vector.

1. For $p\in S^{n-1}\subset\mathbb R^n$, show that there is a unique $a\in\mathbb R^n$ such that
   \[
   p=ae_1.
   \]

2. If $b\in\mathbb R^n$ is nonzero, show that
   \[
   be_1,\ldots,be_n
   \]
   are linearly independent.

3. For $p=ae_1$ as above, orthogonally project $ae_2,\ldots,ae_n$ to $T_pS^{n-1}$.
   Describe these projections and show that they remain linearly independent.

4. Deduce that $TS^{n-1}$ is trivial.
   Conclude that $\mathbb R^3$ cannot admit a real division-algebra structure.
:::
