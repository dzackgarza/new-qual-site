::: {.problem}
Let $\pi:E\to M$ be a rank-two vector bundle, and let $\Phi$ be its Thom class.
Wedge product with $\Phi$ gives an isomorphism
\[
H^*(M)\longrightarrow H_{cv}^{*+2}(E).
\]
Thus every class $\omega\in H_{cv}^{*+2}(E)$ can be written as
\[
\omega=\Phi\wedge\pi^*u
\]
for some $u\in H^*(M)$.

Give a natural description of the class $u$ for which
\[
\Phi\wedge\pi^*u=\Phi\wedge\Phi.
\]
:::
