::: {.exercise}
If $f: R \to S$ is a ring map and $I$ an ideal of $S$, then $f^{-1}(I)$ is an ideal of $R$, the induced map $R/f^{-1}(I) \to S/I$ is injective, and $f^{-1}(I)$ is prime if $I$ is prime.
:::
