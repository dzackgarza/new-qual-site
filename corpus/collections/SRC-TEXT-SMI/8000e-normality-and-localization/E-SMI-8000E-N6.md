::: {.exercise}
Prove that the ideal $PR_P$ generated by $P$ in $R_P$ is maximal, and that all elements of $R_P$ not in this ideal are units.
Conclude that there is only this one maximal ideal in $R_P$.
[$R_P$ is called a "local ring".]
:::
