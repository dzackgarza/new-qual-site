::: {.exercise}
The "normalizer" $N(H)$ of a subgroup $H$ of $G$ is

$$
N(H) = \ts{g \in G : gHg^{-1} = H}.
$$

If $P$ is a Sylow subgroup of $G$, prove that $N(N(P)) = N(P)$.
:::
