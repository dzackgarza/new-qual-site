::: {.exercise}
Prove that, for a subgroup $H$ of $S(n)$, setting $j \sim k$ if and only if the 2-cycle $(jk)$ belongs to $H$ defines an equivalence relation on $\ts{1, 2, \ldots, n}$.
:::
