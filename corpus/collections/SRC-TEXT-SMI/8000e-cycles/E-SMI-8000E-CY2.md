::: {.exercise}
Prove disjoint cycles commute — i.e. if no $a_i$ equals any $b_j$, then

$$
(a_1 a_2 \ldots a_r)(b_1 b_2 \ldots b_s) = (b_1 b_2 \ldots b_s)(a_1 a_2 \ldots a_r).
$$
:::
