::: {.exercise}
Prove: if $s$ is any permutation in $S_n$, and $(a_1\,a_2\,\cdots\,a_k)$ is the cycle taking $a_1$ to $a_2$, etc., then
\[
s(a_1\,a_2\,\cdots\,a_k)s^{-1} = \bigl(s(a_1)\,s(a_2)\,\cdots\,s(a_k)\bigr).
\]
:::
