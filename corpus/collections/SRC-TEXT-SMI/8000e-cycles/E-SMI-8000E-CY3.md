::: {.exercise}
Prove that $S(n)$ is generated by 2-cycles.
[Hint: $(a_1 a_k)(a_1 a_2 \ldots a_{k-1}) = (a_1 a_2 \ldots a_k)$.]
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. Every permutation is a product of disjoint cycles.
::: {.proof}
standard fact (cycle decomposition).
:::

<1>2. Every cycle is a product of transpositions (2-cycles).
<2>1. $(a_1 a_2 \cdots a_k) = (a_1 a_k)(a_1 a_{k-1}) \cdots (a_1 a_2)$.
::: {.proof}
by the hint, $(a_1 a_k)(a_1 a_2 \cdots a_{k-1}) = (a_1 a_2 \cdots a_k)$; iterating this identity expresses the $k$-cycle as a product of the transpositions $(a_1 a_k), (a_1 a_{k-1}), \ldots, (a_1 a_2)$.
:::
<2>2. Hence every cycle is a product of transpositions.
::: {.proof}
<2>1.
:::

<1>3. Therefore every permutation is a product of transpositions.
::: {.proof}
<1>1 and <1>2.
:::

<1>4. Q.E.D.
::: {.proof}
<1>3.
:::
:::
