::: {.exercise}
Prove that if $f: M \to N$ is any $R$ module map, and $g: M \to M$ and $h: N \to N$ are isomorphisms, then $h \circ f \circ g: M \to N$ has kernel and cokernel isomorphic to those of $f$.
:::
