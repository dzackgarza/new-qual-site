::: {.problem}
Let $V$ be the vector space of real $3\times3$ skew-symmetric matrices. Prove that
\[
\langle A,B\rangle=\frac12\operatorname{Tr}(AB^T)
\]
defines an inner product on $V$, and exhibit an orthonormal basis.
:::
