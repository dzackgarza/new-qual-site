::: {.problem}
Let $V$ be finite-dimensional and let $T:V\to V$ be linear.
Suppose $T(W)\subseteq W$ for every subspace $W\subset V$ with $\dim W=\dim V-1$.
Prove that $T$ is a scalar multiple of the identity.
:::
