::: {.problem}
Let $f:[0,1]\to\mathbb R$ be continuous on $[0,1]$, differentiable on $(0,1)$, with $f(0)=0$. Suppose $f'$ is decreasing on $(0,1)$. Show that
\[
g(x)=\frac{f(x)}x
\]
is decreasing on $(0,1)$.
:::
