::: {.problem}
Let $f:[0,1]\to\mathbb R$ and $g:[0,1]\to[0,1]$ be Riemann integrable.
Suppose that for some $\alpha\in(0,1)$,
\[
|g(x)-g(y)|\ge \alpha|x-y|\qquad(x,y\in[0,1]).
\]
Show that $f\circ g$ is Riemann integrable.
:::
