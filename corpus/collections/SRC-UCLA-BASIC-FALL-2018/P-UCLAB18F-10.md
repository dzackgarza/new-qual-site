\[
A=\begin{pmatrix}
1&0&0\\[2pt]
\tfrac12&\tfrac12&0\\[2pt]
\tfrac13&\tfrac13&\tfrac13
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Compute $\lim_{n\to\infty}A^n$.
