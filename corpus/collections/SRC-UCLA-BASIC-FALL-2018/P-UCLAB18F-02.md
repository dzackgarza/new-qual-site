::: {.problem}
Let $A\subset\mathbb R^n$ be connected and suppose
\[
\mathbb R^n\setminus A=B\cup C,
\]
where $B,C$ are separated, meaning
\[
\overline B\cap C=B\cap\overline C=\varnothing.
\]
Show that $A\cup B$ is connected.
:::
