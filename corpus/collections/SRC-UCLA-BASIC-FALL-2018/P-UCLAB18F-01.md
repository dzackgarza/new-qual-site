::: {.problem}
Let $(a_n)_{n\ge1}$ be nonnegative and suppose $\sum_{n\ge1}a_n$ diverges. Show that
\[
\sum_{n\ge1}\frac{a_n}{2a_n+1}
\]
also diverges.
:::
