::: {.problem}
Let $T:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^n$ be linear and satisfy $T^2=T$.
Show that there is a basis in which the matrix of $T$ is diagonal with entries consisting only of $1$'s followed by $0$'s.
:::
