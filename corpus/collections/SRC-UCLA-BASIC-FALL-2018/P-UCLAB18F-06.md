::: {.problem}
For $x>0$ define
\[
F(x)=\int_0^\infty\frac{1-e^{-tx}}{t^{3/2}}\,dt.
\]
Show that $F:(0,\infty)\to(0,\infty)$ is well-defined, bijective, of class $C^1$, and has a $C^1$ inverse.
:::
