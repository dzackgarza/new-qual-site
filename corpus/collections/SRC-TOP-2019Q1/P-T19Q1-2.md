::: {.problem}
Let $(X,\mathcal T)$ be a path-connected space and $\mathcal C=\{U_\alpha\}_{\alpha\in I}$ an open covering of $X$.
Show that for every $x,y\in X$ there is an $n\in\mathbb N$ and a sequence of sets $U_{\alpha_1},\ldots,U_{\alpha_n}$ in $\mathcal C$ with $x\in U_{\alpha_1}$, $y\in U_{\alpha_n}$ and $U_{\alpha_{i-1}}\cap U_{\alpha_i}\ne\varnothing$ for all $i=2,\ldots,n$.
:::
