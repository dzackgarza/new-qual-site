::: {.problem}
Let $(X,\mathcal T)$ be a path-connected space and $\mathcal C=\{U_\alpha\}_{\alpha\in I}$ an open covering of $X$.
Show that for every $x,y\in X$ there is an $n\in\mathbb N$ and a sequence of sets $U_{\alpha_1},\ldots,U_{\alpha_n}$ in $\mathcal C$ with $x\in U_{\alpha_1}$, $y\in U_{\alpha_n}$ and $U_{\alpha_{i-1}}\cap U_{\alpha_i}\ne\varnothing$ for all $i=2,\ldots,n$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Since $X$ is path-connected, choose a path $\gamma\colon[0,1]\to X$ from $x$ to $y$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #partition-along-path}
There is a partition $0=t_0<t_1<\cdots<t_n=1$ and sets $U_{\alpha_1},\ldots,U_{\alpha_n}\in\mathcal C$ with $\gamma([t_{i-1},t_i])\subseteq U_{\alpha_i}$ for each $i$.

::: pf-proof
$\{\gamma^{-1}(U_\alpha)\}_{\alpha\in I}$ is an open cover of the compact metric space $[0,1]$, so it has a Lebesgue number $\delta>0$.
Take an equal subdivision with $t_i-t_{i-1}<\delta$; each $[t_{i-1},t_i]$ then lies in some $\gamma^{-1}(U_{\alpha_i})$.
:::

:::

::: pf-qed
With the sets of step [](#partition-along-path){.pf-ref}, $x=\gamma(t_0)\in U_{\alpha_1}$ and $y=\gamma(t_n)\in U_{\alpha_n}$, and for $2\le i\le n$ the point $\gamma(t_{i-1})$ lies in $U_{\alpha_{i-1}}\cap U_{\alpha_i}$.
:::
