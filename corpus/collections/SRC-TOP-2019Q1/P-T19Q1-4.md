::: {.problem}
Show that if $f,g:(X,\mathcal T)\to(Y,\mathcal T')$ and $h,k:(Y,\mathcal T')\to(Z,\mathcal T'')$ are continuous and $f\simeq g$ and $h\simeq k$, then $h\circ f\simeq k\circ g$.
Show, however, that the converse ($h\circ f\simeq k\circ g$ implies $f\simeq g$ and $h\simeq k$) need not be true.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #compose-homotopic}
If $f\simeq g$ and $h\simeq k$, then $h\circ f\simeq k\circ g$.

::: pf-proof
Let $F\colon X\times[0,1]\to Y$ be a homotopy from $f$ to $g$ and $K\colon Y\times[0,1]\to Z$ a homotopy from $h$ to $k$.
Then $(x,t)\mapsto h(F(x,t))$ is a homotopy from $h\circ f$ to $h\circ g$, and $(x,t)\mapsto K(g(x),t)$ is a homotopy from $h\circ g$ to $k\circ g$.
Homotopy is transitive.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #converse-fails}
With $X=Y=S^1$, $Z$ a point, $f=\id_{S^1}$, $g$ constant, and $h=k$ the unique map $S^1\to Z$, we have $h\circ f=k\circ g$ but $f\not\simeq g$.

::: pf-proof
Both composites are the unique map $S^1\to Z$.
If $f\simeq g$, the induced homomorphisms $\pi_1(S^1,1)\to\pi_1(S^1,f(1))$ and $\pi_1(S^1,1)\to\pi_1(S^1,g(1))$ would agree after the change-of-basepoint isomorphism along the track of the homotopy, which for the abelian group $\pi_1(S^1)\cong\ZZ$ does not depend on the path.
But $f_*$ is the identity of $\ZZ$ and $g_*=0$.
:::

:::

::: pf-qed
Step [](#compose-homotopic){.pf-ref} proves the first assertion, and step [](#converse-fails){.pf-ref} shows the converse fails.
:::
