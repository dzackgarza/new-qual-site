::: {.problem}
Suppose $g:Y\to S^1$ is a path-connected 3-sheeted covering space.
Prove that this covering space is unique up to isomorphism.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #covers-correspond-subgroups}
Connected covering spaces of $S^1$ correspond, up to isomorphism, to subgroups of $\pi_1(S^1)\cong\ZZ$, and the number of sheets equals the index of the subgroup.

::: pf-proof
$S^1$ is path-connected, locally path-connected, and semilocally simply connected, so the classification of connected covering spaces applies; conjugacy classes of subgroups of the abelian group $\ZZ$ are single subgroups.
The number of sheets of a connected covering equals the index of $p_*\pi_1$ of the total space.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #unique-index-3-subgroup}
The only subgroup of index $3$ in $\ZZ$ is $3\ZZ$.

::: pf-proof
Every subgroup of $\ZZ$ is $n\ZZ$ for a unique $n\ge0$, of index $n$ when $n\ge1$.
:::

:::

::: pf-qed
By steps [](#covers-correspond-subgroups){.pf-ref} and [](#unique-index-3-subgroup){.pf-ref}, every path-connected $3$-sheeted covering of $S^1$ is isomorphic to the covering with subgroup $3\ZZ$, namely $S^1\to S^1$, $z\mapsto z^3$.
:::
