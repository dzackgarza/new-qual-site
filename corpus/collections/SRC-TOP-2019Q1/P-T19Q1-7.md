::: {.problem}
Suppose $g:Y\to S^1$ is a path-connected 3-sheeted covering space.
Prove that this covering space is unique up to isomorphism.
:::
::: {.solution}
<1>1. Connected covering spaces of $S^1$ are classified, up to covering isomorphism, by conjugacy classes of subgroups of $\pi_1(S^1)\cong\mathbb Z$.
::: {.proof}
The circle is path-connected, locally path-connected, and semilocally simply connected, so the standard classification theorem for connected covering spaces applies.
:::

<1>2. A connected $3$-sheeted covering corresponds to a subgroup of index $3$ in $\mathbb Z$.
::: {.proof}
For a connected covering, the number of sheets equals the index of the image of the fundamental group of the total space in the fundamental group of the base.
:::

<1>3. The only subgroup of index $3$ in $\mathbb Z$ is $3\mathbb Z$.
::: {.proof}
Every subgroup of $\mathbb Z$ is $n\mathbb Z$ for a unique $n\ge0$, and its finite index is $n$.
:::

<1>4. Therefore every path-connected $3$-sheeted cover of $S^1$ is isomorphic to the standard cover
$$
S^1\longrightarrow S^1,\qquad z\longmapsto z^3.
$$
::: {.proof}
The standard cover has associated subgroup $3\mathbb Z$, and <1>1--<1>3 show that any other connected $3$-sheeted cover has the same subgroup. Since $\mathbb Z$ is abelian, there is no distinction from conjugacy.
:::
