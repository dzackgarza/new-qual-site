::: {.problem title="?"}
Recalling that $S^2=\{(x,y,z)\in\mathbb R^3:x^2+y^2+z^2=1\}$, define the equator of $S^2$ to be $\{(x,y,z)\in S^2:z=0\}$, so that the equator is homeomorphic to $S^1$.
Let $Z_1$ and $Z_2$ be disjoint copies of the $2$-sphere $S^2$, let $f$ be a homeomorphism from the equator of $Z_1$ to the equator of $Z_2$, and let $Z$ be the quotient space obtained from $Z_1\sqcup Z_2$ by identifying the equator of $Z_1$ to the equator of $Z_2$ via $f$.
Find a $\Delta$-complex structure on the space $Z$, and compute the simplicial homology groups of $Z$.
:::
