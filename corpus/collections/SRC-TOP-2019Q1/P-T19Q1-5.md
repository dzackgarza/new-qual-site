::: {.problem}
Let $U=\{(x,y,0)\in\RR^3:x^2+y^2=1\}$, and let $W=\RR^3-U$.
Let $V=\{(x,y,z)\in\RR^3:x^2+y^2+z^2=4\}\cup\{(0,0,z)\in\RR^3:-2\le z\le2\}$.

(a) Describe a deformation retraction of $W$ onto $V$.

(b) Use (a) and the Seifert--van Kampen Theorem to compute $\pi_1(W)$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Write points of $\RR^3$ in cylindrical coordinates $(r,\theta,z)$ with $r\ge0$.
Rotation about the $z$-axis preserves $U$ and $V$, and a rotation orbit is determined by its image $(r,z)$ in the half-plane $H=\{(r,z):r\ge0\}$.
$U$ is the orbit of $p=(1,0)$, and $V$ is the union of the orbits of $\partial_HD=\{r^2+z^2=4\}\cup\{r=0,\ \abs{z}\le2\}$, the boundary of the closed half-disk $D=\{(r,z)\in H:r^2+z^2\le4\}$ relative to $\RR^2$; $p$ lies in the interior of $D$.

<1>1. $H\setminus\{p\}$ strongly deformation retracts onto $D\setminus\{p\}$ by a homotopy preserving each ray from the origin.

::: {.proof}
For $u\in H$ let $q(u)=u$ if $\norm{u}\le2$ and $q(u)=2u/\norm{u}$ if $\norm{u}>2$, and put $Q_s(u)=(1-s)u+sq(u)$.
$Q_s$ fixes $D$ and moves a point outside $D$ along its ray while keeping its norm at least $2$, so it never meets $p$, whose norm is $1$.
:::

<1>2. $D\setminus\{p\}$ strongly deformation retracts onto $\partial_HD$ by a homotopy fixing $\partial_HD$ pointwise.

::: {.proof}
$D$ is compact and convex with $p$ in its interior, so each ray from $p$ meets $\partial_HD$ in exactly one point $\rho(u)$, and $\rho\colon D\setminus\{p\}\to\partial_HD$ is continuous.
$R_s(u)=(1-s)u+s\rho(u)$ stays on the ray from $p$ through $u$, so it never meets $p$, and $\rho(u)=u$ for $u\in\partial_HD$.
:::

<1>3. $W$ strongly deformation retracts onto $V$.

::: {.proof}
Apply $Q_s$ and then $R_s$ to $(r,z)$ while keeping $\theta$ fixed.
$Q_s$ preserves rays from the origin, so points on the $z$-axis stay on it; $R_s$ fixes the diameter $\{r=0,\ \abs{z}\le2\}$ pointwise, and a point with $r>0$ reaches $r=0$ only at $s=1$.
So the lifted homotopies are well defined and continuous on $W$, fix $V$ pointwise, and end with image the rotation of $\partial_HD$, which is $V$.
:::

<1>4. $\pi_1(V)\cong\ZZ$.

::: {.proof}
Let $S$ be the sphere of radius $2$ and $I$ the polar segment.
A meridian arc $\mu\subset S$ joining the poles is contractible, and $V/\mu\cong S^2\vee S^1$: the sphere becomes a sphere, and $I$, whose endpoints are identified, becomes a circle attached at one point.
Collapsing the contractible subcomplex $\mu$ is a homotopy equivalence, so $V\simeq S^2\vee S^1$.
By the Seifert--van Kampen theorem for wedge sums, $\pi_1(S^2\vee S^1)\cong\pi_1(S^2)*\pi_1(S^1)\cong\ZZ$.
:::

<1>5. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>3 answers (a).
For (b), the deformation retraction of step <1>3 induces an isomorphism $\pi_1(W)\cong\pi_1(V)$, so by step <1>4, $\pi_1(W)\cong\boxed{\ZZ}$.
:::
