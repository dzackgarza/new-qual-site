::: {.problem}
Suppose $X$ and $Y$ are spaces such that
\[
X\times Y\simeq\mathbb{CP}^n,
\qquad n\ge1.
\]
Prove that either
\[
H^i(X)=0\quad\text{for all }i>0,
\]
or
\[
H^i(Y)=0\quad\text{for all }i>0.
\]
:::
