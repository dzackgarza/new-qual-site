8. $\displaystyle \int \frac {x+1}{x^2+2x+3} ~dx = \color{blue} {\frac 1 2 \ln(x^2 + 2x + 3)}​$

- **Solution:** $u = x^2 + 2x + 3$, $du = 2x + 2 ~dx$
