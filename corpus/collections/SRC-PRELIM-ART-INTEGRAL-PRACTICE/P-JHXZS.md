::: problem
Evaluate
\[
\int 2018^x\,dx.
\]
:::

::: solution
Since
\[
\frac{d}{dx}2018^x=(\ln 2018)2018^x,
\]
we have
\[
\boxed{\int 2018^x\,dx=\frac{2018^x}{\ln 2018}+C.}
\]
:::
