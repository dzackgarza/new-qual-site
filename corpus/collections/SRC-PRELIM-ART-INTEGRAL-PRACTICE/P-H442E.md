::: problem
Evaluate the Gaussian integrals
\[
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-(x-\mu)^2/(2\sigma^2)}\,dx,
\qquad \sigma\ne0,
\]
\[
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-x^2}\,dx,
\qquad
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-x^2/2}\,dx,
\]
and
\[
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-x^2+6x-3}\,dx.
\]
:::

::: solution
The standard Gaussian integral is
\[
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-u^2}\,du=\sqrt\pi.
\]
For the shifted-scaled Gaussian, put $u=(x-\mu)/(\sqrt2\,|\sigma|)$:
\[
\boxed{\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-(x-\mu)^2/(2\sigma^2)}\,dx=\sqrt{2\pi}\,|\sigma|.}
\]
Thus
\[
\boxed{\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-x^2}\,dx=\sqrt\pi},
\qquad
\boxed{\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-x^2/2}\,dx=\sqrt{2\pi}}.
\]
Finally,
\[
-x^2+6x-3=-(x-3)^2+6,
\]
so
\[
\boxed{\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-x^2+6x-3}\,dx=e^6\sqrt\pi.}
\]
:::
