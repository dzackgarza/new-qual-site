::: {.problem}
Problem 10. Define
\[
f(x,y)=\frac{xy}{\sqrt{x^2+y^2}}
\]
for $(x,y)\ne(0,0)$ and set $f(0,0)=0$.

(a) Compute the directional derivatives of $f$ at $(0,0)$ in all directions where they exist.

(b) Is $f$ differentiable at $(0,0)$?
Prove your answer.
:::
