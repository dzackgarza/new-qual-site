::: {.problem}
Problem 1. Let $u_1,\ldots,u_n$ be an orthonormal basis of $\mathbb R^n$, and let $y_1,\ldots,y_n\in\mathbb R^n$ satisfy
\[
\sum_i\|y_i\|^2<1.
\]
Prove that $u_1+y_1,\ldots,u_n+y_n$ are linearly independent.
:::
