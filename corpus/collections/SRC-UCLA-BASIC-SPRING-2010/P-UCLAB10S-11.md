::: {.problem}
Problem 11. Suppose
\[
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}|a_n|<\infty.
\]
Let $\sigma:\mathbb N\to\mathbb N$ be a bijection. Prove that every rearrangement
\[
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_{\sigma(n)}
\]
converges and has the same sum as $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n$.
:::
