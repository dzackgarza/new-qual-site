::: {.problem}
Problem 6. Let
\[
A=\begin{pmatrix}4&-4\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]

(i) Find the Jordan form $J$ of $A$ and a matrix $P$ such that $P^{-1}AP=J$.

(ii) Compute $A^{100}$ and $J^{100}$.

(iii) Find a formula for $a_n$ when
\[
a_{n+1}=4a_n-4a_{n-1},\qquad a_0=a,\quad a_1=b.
\]
:::
