::: {.problem}
Problem 9. Let $f(x,y,z)$ be a real-valued continuously differentiable function such that
\[
f(x_0,y_0,z_0)=0,
\qquad
\nabla f(x_0,y_0,z_0)\ne0.
\]
Show that there is a differentiable parametrized surface
\[
(x(s,t),y(s,t),z(s,t))
\]
through $(x_0,y_0,z_0)$ on which $f=0$.
:::
