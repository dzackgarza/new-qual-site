::: {.problem}
How many isomorphisms are there from $\ZZ_{12}$ to $\ZZ_{4} \oplus \ZZ_{3}$?
:::

::: {.solution}
We know that isomorphisms map generators to generators.
So the isomorphisms are completely determined by where 1 maps.
The generators of $\ZZ _ { 4 } \oplus \ZZ _ { 3 }$ (the elements with order 12) are: (1,1), (3,1), (1,2), and (3,2). Thus there are four isomorphisms.
:::
