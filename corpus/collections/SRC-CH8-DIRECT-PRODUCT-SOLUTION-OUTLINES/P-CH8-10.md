::: {.problem}
How many elements of order $9$ does $\ZZ_{3} \oplus \ZZ_{9}$ have?
:::

::: {.solution}
We know that $( a , b )$ has order 9 if $\operatorname { l c m } ( \elementorder{ a } , \elementorder{ b } ) = 9$ . Thus $b$ must have order 9 and $\elementorder{ a }$ can be 1 or 3. Everything in $\ZZ _ { 3 }$ has order 1 or 3, so a can be any element in $\ZZ _ { 3 }$ . In $\ZZ _ { 9 }$ , there are $\phi ( 9 ) = 3 ^ { 2 } - 3 = 6$ elements of order 9. Thus there are $3 \cdot 6 = 1 8$ elements of order 9 in $\ZZ _ { 3 } \oplus \ZZ _ { 9 }$ .
:::
