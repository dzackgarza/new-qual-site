::: {.problem}
Find a subgroup of $\ZZ_{12} \oplus \ZZ_{18}$ that is isomorphic to $\ZZ_{9} \oplus \ZZ_{4}$.
:::

::: {.solution}
First notice that $\ZZ _ { 9 } \oplus \ZZ _ { 4 }$ is isomorphic to $\ZZ _ { 4 } \oplus \ZZ _ { 9 }$ . This second presentation is, by the previous problem, isomorphic to $< 3 > \oplus < 2 >$ .
:::

::: {.remark}
Certificate. `proofs/cards/CH8DirectProduct.g` (`P-CH8-20`) checks in GAP that
$\langle(3,0),(0,2)\rangle\le\ZZ_{12}\oplus\ZZ_{18}$ has order $36$ and abelian
type $\ZZ_{4}\oplus\ZZ_{9}$: the element $3$ generates the order-$4$ subgroup of
$\ZZ_{12}$ and $2$ the order-$9$ subgroup of $\ZZ_{18}$.
:::
