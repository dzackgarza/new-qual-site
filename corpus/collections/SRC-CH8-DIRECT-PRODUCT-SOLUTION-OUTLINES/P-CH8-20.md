::: {.problem}
Find a subgroup of $\ZZ_{12} \oplus \ZZ_{18}$ that is isomorphic to $\ZZ_{9} \oplus \ZZ_{4}$.
:::

::: {.solution}
First notice that $\ZZ _ { 9 } \oplus \ZZ _ { 4 }$ is isomorphic to $\ZZ _ { 4 } \oplus \ZZ _ { 9 }$ . This second presentation is, by the previous problem, isomorphic to $< 3 > \oplus < 2 >$ .
:::
