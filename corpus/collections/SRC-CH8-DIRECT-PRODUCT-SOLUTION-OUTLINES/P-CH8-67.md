::: {.problem}
Express $U(165)$ as an external direct product of $U$-groups in four different ways.
:::

::: {.solution}
First note that $1 6 5 = 3 \cdot 5 \cdot 1 1$ . Then $U ( 1 6 5 ) \approx U ( 3 ) \oplus U ( 5 5 ) \approx U ( 5 ) \oplus U ( 3 3 ) \approx U ( 1 1 ) \oplus U ( 1 5 ) \approx U ( 3 ) \oplus U ( 5 ) \oplus U ( 1 1 )$ .
:::

::: {.remark}
Certificate. `proofs/cards/CH8DirectProduct.g` (`P-CH8-67`) checks in GAP that
$U(165)$ has order $80$ and is isomorphic to each of
$U(11)\oplus U(15)$, $U(3)\oplus U(55)$, $U(5)\oplus U(33)$ and
$U(3)\oplus U(5)\oplus U(11)$, the four groupings of $3,5,11$ into at least two
blocks.
:::
