::: {.problem}
Express U(165) as an external direct product of U-groups in four different ways.
:::

::: {.solution}
First note that $1 6 5 = 3 \cdot 5 \cdot 1 1$ . Then $U ( 1 6 5 ) \approx U ( 3 ) \oplus U ( 5 5 ) \approx U ( 5 ) \oplus U ( 3 3 ) \approx U ( 1 1 ) \oplus U ( 1 5 ) \approx U ( 3 ) \oplus U ( 5 ) \oplus U ( 1 1 )$ .
:::
