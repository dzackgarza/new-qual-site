::: {.problem}
For each integer $n > 1$, give two examples of two non isomorphic groups of order $n^{2}$.
:::

::: {.solution}
$\ZZ _ { n ^ { 2 } }$ and $\ZZ _ { n } \oplus \ZZ _ { n }$ (Note: we know these are different since n is not relatively prime to $n$ ) .
:::
