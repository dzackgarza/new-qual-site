::: {.problem}
Let $A,B\in M_5(\mathbb R)$ satisfy $A^2=A$, $B^2=B$, and suppose $I-(A+B)$ is invertible.
Prove that
\[
\operatorname{rank}(A)=\operatorname{rank}(B).
\]
:::
