::: {.problem}
Let $A\in M_n(\mathbb C)$. Recall that
\[
e^A=I+A+\frac{A^2}{2!}+\frac{A^3}{3!}+\cdots.
\]
Suppose
\[
e^A=I+A+A^2.
\]
Prove or disprove that $A$ must be the zero matrix.
:::
