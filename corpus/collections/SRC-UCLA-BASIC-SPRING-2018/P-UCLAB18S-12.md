::: {.problem}
Let $\mathcal F$ be an equicontinuous family of real-valued functions on a compact metric space $X$, with every $f\in\mathcal F$ taking values in $[-1,1]$.
Prove that
\[
g(x)=\sup\{f(x):f\in\mathcal F\}
\]
is continuous.
:::
