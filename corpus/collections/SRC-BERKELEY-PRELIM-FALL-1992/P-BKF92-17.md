::: {.problem}
Let $s\in\mathbb R$ and define on $\mathbb C\setminus(-\infty,0]$
\[
u(re^{i\theta})=r^s\cos(s\theta),
\qquad r>0,\ -\pi<\theta<\pi.
\]
Prove that $u$ is harmonic.
:::
