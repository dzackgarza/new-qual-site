::: {.problem}
Let $y:[0,\infty)\to\mathbb R$ solve
\[
y''=-|y|,
\qquad
y(0)=1,
\qquad
y'(0)=0.
\]
Prove that there is exactly one $t>0$ such that $y(t)=0$.
:::
