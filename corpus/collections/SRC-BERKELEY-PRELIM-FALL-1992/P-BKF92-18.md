::: {.problem}
Let $k$ be a positive integer. Determine all real numbers $c$ such that every real sequence $(x_n)$ satisfying
\[
\frac12(x_{n+1}+x_{n-1})=cx_n
\]
has period $k$, i.e.
\[
x_{n+k}=x_n
\]
for every $n$.
:::
