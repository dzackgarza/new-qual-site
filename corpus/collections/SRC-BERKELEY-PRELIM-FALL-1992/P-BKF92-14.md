::: {.problem}
Let $f,g:\mathbb R\to(0,\infty)$ be continuous with $g\le f$. Suppose the initial-value problem
\[
x'=f(x),
\qquad
x(0)=0,
\]
has a solution defined on all of $\mathbb R$. Prove that
\[
x'=g(x),
\qquad
x(0)=0,
\]
also has a solution defined on all of $\mathbb R$.
:::
