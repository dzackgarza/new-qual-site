::: {.problem}
Let $f$ be holomorphic on a neighborhood of the closed unit disk and write
$$
u(x,y)=\operatorname{Re}f(x+iy).
$$
Prove that
$$
\int_C u_y\,dx-u_x\,dy=0,
$$
where $C$ is the unit circle.
:::

::: {.solution}
Write
$$
f(x+iy)=u(x,y)+iv(x,y).
$$

<1>1. On a neighborhood of the closed unit disk,
$$
u_y\,dx-u_x\,dy=-dv.
$$

::: {.proof}
Since $f$ is holomorphic, the Cauchy--Riemann equations give
$$
u_x=v_y,
\qquad
u_y=-v_x.
$$
Therefore
$$
u_y\,dx-u_x\,dy
=-v_x\,dx-v_y\,dy
=-dv.
$$
:::

<1>2. $\displaystyle\int_C u_y\,dx-u_x\,dy=0$.

::: {.proof}
Let $\gamma:[0,2\pi]\to C$ be any smooth parametrization with $\gamma(0)=\gamma(2\pi)$. By step <1>1,
$$
\int_C u_y\,dx-u_x\,dy
=-\int_0^{2\pi}\frac{d}{dt}(v\circ\gamma)(t)\,dt
=-v(\gamma(2\pi))+v(\gamma(0))
=0.
$$
:::

<1>3. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>2 is the required identity.
:::
:::
