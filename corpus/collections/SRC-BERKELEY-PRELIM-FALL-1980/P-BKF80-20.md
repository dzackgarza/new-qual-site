::: {.problem}
Let $R$ be a ring with identity, and let $G(R)$ denote its set of units.

1. Prove that $G(R)$ is a group under multiplication.
2. Let $R=\mathbb Z[i]$. Prove that
\[
G(R)\cong\mathbb Z/4\mathbb Z.
\]
:::
