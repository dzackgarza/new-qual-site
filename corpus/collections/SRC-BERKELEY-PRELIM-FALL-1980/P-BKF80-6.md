::: {.problem}
Let $M_2(\mathbb R)$ be the ring of real $2\times2$ matrices, and let
\[
S=\left\{
\begin{pmatrix}a&-b\\ b&a\end{pmatrix}:a,b\in\mathbb R
\right\}.
\]

1. Exhibit an isomorphism $S\cong\mathbb C$.
2. Prove that
\[
A=\begin{pmatrix}0&3\\-4&1\end{pmatrix}
\]
lies in a subring of $M_2(\mathbb R)$ isomorphic to $S$.
3. Prove that there exists $X\in M_2(\mathbb R)$ such that
\[
X^4+13X=A.
\]
:::
