::: {.problem}
Let
\[
f(x)=\frac14+x-x^2.
\]
For $x\in\mathbb R$, define $x_0=x$ and
\[
x_{n+1}=f(x_n).
\]
If the sequence converges, write its limit as $x_\infty$.

1. For $x=0$, show that $(x_n)$ is bounded and nondecreasing and find its limit $\lambda$.
2. Determine all $y\in\mathbb R$ for which $y_\infty=\lambda$.
:::
