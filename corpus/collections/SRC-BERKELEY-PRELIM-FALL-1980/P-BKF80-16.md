::: {.problem}
Let $A,B$ be real $n\times n$ matrices. Suppose
\[
A^T=A,
\qquad
v^TAv\ge0\quad(v\in\mathbb R^n),
\]
and
\[
AB+BA=0.
\]
Prove that
\[
AB=BA=0,
\]
and give an example in which neither $A$ nor $B$ is zero.
:::
