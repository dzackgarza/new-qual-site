::: {.problem}
Show that there exists $\varepsilon>0$ such that, whenever $A$ is a real $2\times2$ matrix whose entries satisfy
\[
|a_{ij}|\le\varepsilon,
\]
there exists a real $2\times2$ matrix $X$ with
\[
X^2+X^T=A.
\]
Is $X$ unique?
:::
