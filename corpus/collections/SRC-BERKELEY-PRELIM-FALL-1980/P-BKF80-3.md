::: {.problem}
Determine whether there exist functions $f$ and $g$, analytic at $0$, such that for every positive integer $n$,
\[
f(1/n)=f(-1/n)=1/n^2,
\]
and
\[
g(1/n)=g(-1/n)=1/n^3.
\]
:::
