::: {.problem}
Let $f$ be holomorphic on a neighborhood of the closed unit disk and suppose
\[
|f(z)|<1
\qquad(|z|=1).
\]
Show that
\[
f(z)=z^3
\]
has exactly three solutions, counted with multiplicity, in $|z|<1$.
:::
