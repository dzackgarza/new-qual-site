::: problem
Let $\tau$ be the map that in cylindrical coordinates takes $(r,\theta,z)$ to $(r,\theta+\pi,z)$, thus $\tau$ maps the torus $T$ to itself by a half-turn about its vertical axis, as shown on the exam.

a. Find a cell structure on $T$ such that $\tau$ maps cells to cells.

b. Let $Q$ be the quotient of $T$ given by identifying $x$ and $\tau(x)$ for all $x\in T$.
What is $\chi(Q)$?

c. Is $Q$ a surface?
:::
