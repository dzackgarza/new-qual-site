::: problem
Explicitly give a collection of deck transformations on $\{(x,y)\mid -1\leq x\leq 1,\ -\infty<y<\infty\}$ such that the quotient is a Möbius band.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let
\[
S=[-1,1]\times\RR.
\]

<1>1. Define
\[
\tau:S\longrightarrow S,
\qquad
\tau(x,y)=(-x,y+1).
\]
Then the required collection is the infinite cyclic group
\[
G=\langle\tau\rangle
=\{\tau^n:n\in\ZZ\},
\qquad
\tau^n(x,y)=((-1)^n x,y+n).
\]
::: {.proof}
The displayed formula for $\tau^n$ follows by induction for $n\ge0$ and from
\[
\tau^{-1}(x,y)=(-x,y-1)
\]
for negative $n$.
Each $\tau^n$ is a homeomorphism of $S$, and
\[
\tau^m\circ\tau^n=\tau^{m+n},
\]
so these maps form an action of $\ZZ$ on the strip.
:::

<1>2. The action of $G$ on $S$ is free and properly discontinuous.
::: {.proof}
If
\[
\tau^n(x,y)=(x,y),
\]
then comparison of second coordinates gives $y+n=y$, hence $n=0$.
Thus no nonidentity element fixes a point.

For proper discontinuity, fix $(x_0,y_0)\in S$ and choose a relative-open neighborhood whose $y$-coordinates lie in
\[
(y_0-1/4,y_0+1/4).
\]
Its image under $\tau^n$ has $y$-coordinates in
\[
(y_0+n-1/4,y_0+n+1/4),
\]
which is disjoint from the original interval for every $n\ne0$.
Hence this neighborhood is disjoint from all of its nontrivial translates.
:::

<1>3. The rectangle
\[
F=[-1,1]\times[0,1]
\]
is a fundamental domain, and its only quotient identification between the two horizontal boundary levels is
\[
(x,0)\sim(-x,1).
\]
::: {.proof}
Given $(x,y)\in S$, choose $n\in\ZZ$ so that $y-n\in[0,1]$.
Then $\tau^{-n}(x,y)$ lies in $F$, so every orbit meets $F$.
An orbit meeting the interior $[-1,1]\times(0,1)$ meets it only once because every nontrivial $\tau^n$ changes the second coordinate by a nonzero integer.

The two remaining representatives occur at $y=0$ and $y=1$.
Since
\[
\tau(x,0)=(-x,1),
\]
the bottom point $(x,0)$ is identified with the top point $(-x,1)$.
No interior point of either vertical side $x=\pm1$ is identified with another point of $F$; those sides remain boundary.
:::

<1>4. Therefore
\[
\boxed{S/G\cong\text{the Möbius band}.}
\]
::: {.proof}
The standard rectangle model of the Möbius band is obtained from
\[
[-1,1]\times[0,1]
\]
by identifying the two horizontal edges with reversed transverse coordinate,
\[
(x,0)\sim(-x,1),
\]
while leaving the vertical sides as boundary.
This is exactly the quotient relation on the fundamental domain from <1>3. The endpoints of the two vertical sides are paired by the horizontal-edge identification, so those sides concatenate to the single boundary circle of the Möbius band.

By <1>2, the quotient map
\[
q:S\longrightarrow S/G
\]
is a covering map, and every element of $G$ satisfies $q\circ\tau^n=q$.
Thus each of the maps
\[
\tau^n(x,y)=((-1)^n x,y+n),
\qquad n\in\ZZ,
\]
is a deck transformation, and their common quotient is the Möbius band.
:::
