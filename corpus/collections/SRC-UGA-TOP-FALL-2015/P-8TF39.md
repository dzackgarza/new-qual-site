::: problem
Explicitly give a collection of deck transformations on $\{(x,y)\mid -1\leq x\leq 1,\ -\infty<y<\infty\}$ such that the quotient is a Möbius band.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. Let $S = \{(x,y) : -1 \le x \le 1,\ -\infty < y < \infty\}$ be the infinite strip.
Proof: the given space.

<1>2. Define the deck transformation $\tau : S \to S$ by $\tau(x, y) = (-x, y + 1)$.
Proof: reflect across the vertical axis and translate up by $1$.

<1>3. $\tau$ is a homeomorphism of $S$ with no fixed points.
Proof: $\tau$ is a composition of a reflection and a translation, both homeomorphisms; $\tau(x,y) = (x,y)$ would force $x = -x$ (so $x = 0$) and $y = y + 1$ (impossible).

<1>4. The group $\langle \tau \rangle \cong \ZZ$ acts freely and properly discontinuously on $S$.
Proof: <1>3, and the action is by integer translations in the $y$-direction composed with reflection.

<1>5. The quotient $S/\langle \tau \rangle$ is the Möbius band.
Proof: the fundamental domain is the rectangle $[-1,1] \times [0,1]$, and $\tau$ identifies the top edge $(x, 1)$ with the bottom edge $(-x, 0)$ — i.e. the two vertical edges are glued with a half-twist, which is exactly the Möbius band.

<1>6. Q.E.D.
Proof: <1>5.
:::
