::: {.problem}
Let $f:[0,\infty)\to[0,\infty)$ be continuous and strictly increasing onto $[0,\infty)$, and let $g=f^{-1}$.
Prove that for all positive $a,b$,
\[
\int_0^a f(x)\,dx+\int_0^b g(y)\,dy\ge ab,
\]
and determine the condition for equality.
:::
