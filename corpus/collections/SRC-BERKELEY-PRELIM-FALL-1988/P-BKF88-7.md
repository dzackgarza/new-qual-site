::: {.problem}
Find all abelian groups of order $8$, up to isomorphism.
Then identify which type occurs in each case:

1. $(\mathbb Z_{15})^\times$;

2. $(\mathbb Z_{17})^\times/\{\pm1\}$;

3. the roots of $z^8-1$ in $\mathbb C$, under multiplication;

4. the additive group $\mathbb F_8^+$;

5. $(\mathbb Z_{16})^\times$.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

Up to isomorphism, the abelian groups of order $8$ are
$$
\boxed{C_8,\qquad C_4\times C_2,\qquad C_2\times C_2\times C_2}.
$$

::: pf-proof

By the classification theorem for finite abelian groups, the isomorphism types of abelian groups of order
$$
8=2^3
$$
are indexed by the partitions of $3$:
$$
3,\qquad 2+1,\qquad 1+1+1.
$$
These give exactly the three displayed groups.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

One has
$$
\boxed{(\ZZ/15\ZZ)^\times\cong C_4\times C_2}.
$$

::: pf-proof

The Chinese remainder theorem gives
$$
(\ZZ/15\ZZ)^\times
\cong
(\ZZ/3\ZZ)^\times\times(\ZZ/5\ZZ)^\times.
$$
The first factor is cyclic of order $2$, and the second is cyclic of order $4$. Hence
$$
(\ZZ/15\ZZ)^\times\cong C_2\times C_4.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

One has
$$
\boxed{(\ZZ/17\ZZ)^\times/\{\pm1\}\cong C_8}.
$$

::: pf-proof

The multiplicative group of the finite field $\ZZ/17\ZZ$ is cyclic of order $16$. In a cyclic group of order $16$, the subgroup $\{\pm1\}$ is the unique subgroup of order $2$. The quotient is therefore cyclic of order
$$
16/2=8.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s4}

The roots of $z^8-1$ under multiplication form
$$
\boxed{C_8}.
$$

::: pf-proof

The eighth roots of unity are generated by
$$
\zeta_8=e^{2\pi i/8}.
$$
Since $\zeta_8$ has order $8$, the group of all eighth roots of unity is cyclic of order $8$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s5}

The additive group of $\FF_8$ is
$$
\boxed{C_2\times C_2\times C_2}.
$$

::: pf-proof

The field $\FF_8$ has characteristic $2$ and cardinality
$$
8=2^3.
$$
Hence it is a three-dimensional vector space over its prime field $\FF_2$. Its additive group is therefore the direct sum of three copies of the additive group of $\FF_2$, each isomorphic to $C_2$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s6}

One has
$$
\boxed{(\ZZ/16\ZZ)^\times\cong C_4\times C_2}.
$$

::: pf-proof

The element $3$ has order $4$ modulo $16$, since
$$
3^2\equiv9\pmod{16},
\qquad
3^4\equiv1\pmod{16},
$$
while $3^2\not\equiv1\pmod{16}$. Thus
$$
\langle3\rangle=\{1,3,9,11\}.
$$
The subgroup generated by $-1$ is
$$
\langle-1\rangle=\{1,15\},
$$
and its intersection with $\langle3\rangle$ is trivial. Since
$$
\abs{(\ZZ/16\ZZ)^\times}=\varphi(16)=8
$$
and the two subgroups commute, their product has order $4\cdot2=8$ and equals the whole unit group. Hence
$$
(\ZZ/16\ZZ)^\times
\cong
\langle3\rangle\times\langle-1\rangle
\cong
C_4\times C_2.
$$

:::
