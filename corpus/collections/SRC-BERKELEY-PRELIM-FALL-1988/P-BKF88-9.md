::: {.problem}
Let $A,B$ be real symmetric $n\times n$ matrices.
Assume all eigenvalues of $A$ lie in $[a_1,a_2]$ and all eigenvalues of $B$ lie in $[b_1,b_2]$.
Prove that every eigenvalue of $A+B$ lies in
\[
[a_1+b_1,a_2+b_2].
\]
:::
