::: {.problem}
Prove that a real-valued $C^3$ function $f$ on $\mathbb R^2$ whose Laplacian
\[
\frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x^2}+\frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial y^2}
\]
is everywhere positive cannot have a local maximum.
:::
