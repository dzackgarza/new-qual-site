::: {.problem}
Let $f$ be holomorphic on the unit disk, with $f(0)=0$ and $|f(z)|\le1$ for $|z|<1$.
Prove, using the maximum principle, that
\[
|f(z)|\le|z|
\]
for $|z|<1$.
:::
