::: problem
If $A$ and $B$ are the radii of convergence of
\[
\sum_{n=0}^\infty a_nz^n
\quad\text{and}\quad
\sum_{n=0}^\infty b_nz^n,
\]
prove that the radius of convergence of
\[
\sum_{n=0}^\infty a_nb_nz^n
\]
is at least $AB$.
:::
