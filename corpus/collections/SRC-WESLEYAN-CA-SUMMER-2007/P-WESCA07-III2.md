::: {.problem}
Let $f$ be analytic on the unit disk $D$, with
\[
f(0)=f'(0)=0,
\qquad
|f'(z)|<1\quad(z\in D).
\]
Show that
\[
|f(z)|\le\frac{|z|^2}{2}
\qquad(z\in D).
\]

Hint: represent $f(z)$ as a path integral along a convenient path.
:::
