::: {.problem}
Let
\[
\gamma(t)=te^{it},\qquad 0\le t\le\pi.
\]
Show that
\[
\left|\int_\gamma z\,dz\right|
\le \frac{\pi^2}{2}+\frac{\pi^3}{3}.
\]
:::
