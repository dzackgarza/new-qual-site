::: {.problem}
Let
\[
f(z)=\frac{z-a}{1-\overline a z},
\qquad |a|<1,
\]
be defined on the unit disk $D=\{z:|z|<1\}$.
Using the Maximum Principle, show that $f$ maps $D$ onto $D$.
:::
