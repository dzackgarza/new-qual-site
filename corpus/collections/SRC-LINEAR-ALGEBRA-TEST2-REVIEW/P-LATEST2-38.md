::: {.problem}
Let
\[
M=\begin{pmatrix}0&1&0\\0&0&1\\1&0&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Determine $M^{100}$ from the following choices:

(A) $M$

(B) $\begin{pmatrix}0&0&1\\1&0&0\\0&1&0\end{pmatrix}$

(C) $I_3$

(D) the zero matrix

(E) none of the above
:::
