::: {.problem}
Let $V$ be the real vector space of polynomials of degree at most $3$.
Let
\[
W=\{p\in V:p(0)=p(1)=p(-1)=0\}.
\]
Find $\dim V+\dim W$.

(A) $4$  (B) $5$  (C) $6$  (D) $7$  (E) $8$
:::
