::: {.problem}
Let $f:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R$ be linear, with $f(1,1)=1$ and $f(-1,0)=2$.
Find $f(3,5)$.

(A) $-6$  (B) $-5$  (C) $0$  (D) $8$  (E) $9$
