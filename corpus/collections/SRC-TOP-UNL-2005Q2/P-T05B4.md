::: {.problem}
A space $X$ is called *unicoherent* if whenever $X=U\cup V$ with $U,V$ nonempty, open, and path-connected, then $U\cap V$ is path-connected.
Use the Mayer--Vietoris sequence to show that, for $n\ge 2$, $S^n$ is unicoherent.
:::

::: {.solution}
Fix $n\ge2$ and suppose $S^n=U\cup V$ with $U$, $V$ nonempty, open, and path-connected.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

$U\cap V\neq\varnothing$.

::: pf-proof

Otherwise $U$ and $V$ would be disjoint nonempty open sets covering the connected space $S^n$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

$\widetilde H_0(U\cap V)=0$.

::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1){.pf-ref} the reduced Mayer--Vietoris sequence of the open cover $\{U,V\}$ applies and contains the exact segment
$$\widetilde H_1(S^n)\to\widetilde H_0(U\cap V)\to\widetilde H_0(U)\oplus\widetilde H_0(V).$$
$\widetilde H_1(S^n)=0$ because $n\ge2$, and $\widetilde H_0(U)=\widetilde H_0(V)=0$ because $U$ and $V$ are nonempty and path-connected.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

For a nonempty space $Y$, $H_0(Y)$ is free abelian on the path components of $Y$, so $\widetilde H_0(Y)=0$ exactly when $Y$ is path-connected.
By steps [](#s1){.pf-ref} and [](#s2){.pf-ref}, $U\cap V$ is path-connected, so $S^n$ is unicoherent.

:::
