::: {.problem}
Find a cell structure for the quotient space obtained by identifying two distinct points $a,b$ in a 2-torus to a third point $c$ in a 2-sphere, and compute a presentation for the fundamental group of this space.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $Q$ denote the quotient space.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

The torus has a CW structure with $0$-cells $a,b$, $1$-cells $r\colon a\to b$, $s\colon b\to a$, $t\colon a\to a$, and one $2$-cell attached along $(rs)t(rs)^{-1}t^{-1}$.

::: pf-proof

Take the standard CW structure on $T^2$ with vertex $a$, loops $x,t$, and one $2$-cell attached along $xtx^{-1}t^{-1}$, chosen so that $b$ lies in the interior of the $x$-circle.
Subdividing $x$ at $b$ into edges $r$ and $s$ writes $x$ as the edge path $rs$, which gives the stated attaching word.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

$Q$ is a CW complex with one $0$-cell $v$, loops $r,s,t$ at $v$, a $2$-cell $e_T$ attached along $(rs)t(rs)^{-1}t^{-1}$, and a $2$-cell $e_S$ attached by the constant map at $v$.

::: pf-proof

$S^2=D^2/\partial D^2$ has a CW structure with $0$-cell $c$ and one $2$-cell attached by the constant map.
The quotient identifies only the three $0$-cells $a,b,c$ to $v$, so $r,s,t$ become loops at $v$, and each $2$-cell keeps its attaching map from step [](#s1){.pf-ref} or from the sphere.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

$\pi_1(Q,v)\cong\langle r,s,t\mid(rs)t(rs)^{-1}t^{-1}\rangle$.

::: pf-proof

The $1$-skeleton is a wedge of three circles, with fundamental group free on $r,s,t$.
By the Seifert--van Kampen theorem, attaching $e_T$ imposes its attaching word as a relation, and the constant attaching loop of $e_S$ imposes the trivial relation.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s2){.pf-ref} gives the cell structure.
In the presentation of step [](#s3){.pf-ref}, substitute $x=rs$, $y=t$, $z=r$; then $s=z^{-1}x$, so $x,y,z$ generate freely subject to the single relation $[x,y]=1$.
Hence
$$\pi_1(Q)\cong\langle x,y,z\mid[x,y]\rangle\cong\langle x,y\mid[x,y]\rangle*\langle z\rangle\cong\ZZ^2*\ZZ.$$

:::
