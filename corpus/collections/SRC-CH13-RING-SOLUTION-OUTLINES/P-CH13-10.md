::: {.problem}
Describe all zero-divisors and units of $\ZZ \oplus \QQ \oplus \ZZ$
:::

::: {.solution}
The zero divisors are elements with one or two entries that are zero.
Formally, $\{ ( a , b , c ) \mid$ exactly 1 or 2 of the entries are zero $\}$ .

The units are elements composed of units so they have ±1 in the first and last coordinate, and the middle coordinate is not 0. So $\{ ( \pm 1 , b , \pm 1 ) \st b \neq 0 \}$ .
:::
