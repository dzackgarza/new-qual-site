::: {.problem}
Let $f$ be the function defined for all $(x,y)$ as follows:
\[
f(x,y)=\begin{cases}xy\,\dfrac{x^2-y^2}{x^2+y^2} & \text{if } (x,y)\ne(0,0)\\ 0 & \text{if } (x,y)=(0,0)\end{cases}
\]
What is the value of $f_{xy}$ at the point $(0,0)$?

(A) $-1$
(B) $0$
(C) $\frac{1}{2}$
(D) $1$
(E) Undefined
:::
