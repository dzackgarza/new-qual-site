::: {.problem}
If $\mathbf{F}=(3y-2x)\hat{\mathbf{i}}+(x^2+y)\hat{\mathbf{j}}$, find the value of $\int_C\mathbf{F}\cdot d\mathbf{r}$, where $C$ is the portion of the parabola $y=x^2$, directed from $(-1,1)$ to the origin.

(A) $-1$
(B) $0$
(C) $1$
(D) $2$
(E) $3$
:::
