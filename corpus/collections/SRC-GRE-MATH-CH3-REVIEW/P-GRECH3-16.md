::: {.problem}
The plane P is tangent to the surface

$$
y ^ { 2 } z - 2 x z ^ { 2 } + 3 x ^ { 2 } y = 2
$$

at the point $Q = ( 1 , 1 , 1 )$ . Which of the following points also lies in P? (A) $(4, 5, -3)$ (B) $(6, 4, 3)$ (C) $(3, -1, 5)$ (D) $(5, 3, 2)$ (E) $(-2, 4, 2)$
:::
