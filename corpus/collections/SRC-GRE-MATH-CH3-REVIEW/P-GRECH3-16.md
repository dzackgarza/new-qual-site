::: {.problem}
Let $P$ be the tangent plane to the surface
\[
y^2z-2xz^2+3x^2y=2
\]
at the point $Q=(1,1,1)$. Which of the following points also lies in $P$?

(A) $(4,5,-3)$
(B) $(6,-4,3)$
(C) $(3,-1,5)$
(D) $(5,3,-2)$
(E) $(-2,4,2)$
:::
