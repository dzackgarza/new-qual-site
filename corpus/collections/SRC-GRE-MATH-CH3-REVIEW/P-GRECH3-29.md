::: {.problem}
If $a$ is a positive number, what's the value of the following double integral?
\[
\int_0^{2a}\int_{-\sqrt{2ay-y^2}}^{0}\sqrt{x^2+y^2}\,dx\,dy
\]

(A) $\frac{16}{9}a^3$
(B) $\frac{32}{9}a^3$
(C) $\frac{\pi}{2}a^2$
(D) $\frac{8\pi}{3}a^2$
(E) $2a^4$
:::
