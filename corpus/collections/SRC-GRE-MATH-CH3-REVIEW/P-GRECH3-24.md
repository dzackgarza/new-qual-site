::: {.problem}
A vertical fence is constructed whose base is the curve $y=x\sqrt{x}$, from $(0,0)$ to $(1,1)$, and whose height above each point $(x,y)$ along the curve is $x^3-y^2+27$. Find the area of this fence.

(A) $\frac{1}{9}(5\sqrt{5}-2)$
(B) $5\sqrt{13}-6$
(C) $9\sqrt{3}$
(D) $13\sqrt{13}-8$
(E) $27$
:::
