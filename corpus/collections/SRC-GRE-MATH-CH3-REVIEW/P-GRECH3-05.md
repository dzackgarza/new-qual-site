::: {.problem}
Let $L$ be the line in space that passes through the points $P=(-1,-2,4)$ and $Q=(4,2,1)$. At what point does $L$ intersect the plane $x+y+2z=11$?

(A) $(5,-4,1)$
(B) $(1,2,4)$
(C) $(8,9,-3)$
(D) $(6,7,-1)$
(E) $(9,6,-2)$
:::
