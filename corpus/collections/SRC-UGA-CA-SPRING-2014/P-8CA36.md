::: {.problem}
Suppose $f$ has an isolated singularity at $a\in\CC$ and $\operatorname{Re} f$ is bounded above in a deleted neighborhood of $a$.
Prove that the singularity is removable.
:::

::: {.solution}

No retained solution set covers this exercise: the card's own argument follows, with the bounded exponential, its removability and the exclusion of a zero at the centre as separate steps.

Let $f$ have an isolated singularity at $a$, with $\operatorname{Re}f(z)\le M$ for $0<\abs{z-a}<r$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #bounded}
$g(z)=e^{f(z)-M}$ is holomorphic on the punctured disc with $\abs{g}\le1$.

::: pf-proof
$f$ is holomorphic there, so $g$ is, and $\abs{g}=e^{\operatorname{Re}f-M}\le e^{0}=1$ by the hypothesis $\operatorname{Re}f\le M$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #removable}
$g$ extends holomorphically across $a$.

::: pf-proof
$g$ is bounded on a punctured neighbourhood of $a$, so $a$ is a removable singularity by Riemann's theorem; let $\widetilde g$ be the extension.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #nonzero}
$\widetilde g(a)\ne0$.

::: pf-proof
Suppose $\widetilde g(a)=0$. Then near $a$ one has $\widetilde g(z)=(z-a)^{m}h(z)$ with $m\ge1$, $h$ holomorphic and $h(a)\ne0$; shrinking the disc if necessary, $h$ has a holomorphic logarithm $H$ there. On the punctured disc $g=(z-a)^{m}e^{H}$, and a branch of the logarithm exists on it, so $f=M+m\log(z-a)+H(z)$ for a suitable branch; taking real parts, $\operatorname{Re}f(z)=m\log\abs{z-a}+\operatorname{Re}H(z)$. As $z\to a$ the first term tends to $-\infty$ while $\operatorname{Re}H$ stays bounded, so $\operatorname{Re}f\to-\infty$, contradicting $\operatorname{Re}f\le M$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #extends}
$f$ extends holomorphically across $a$.

::: pf-proof
By steps [](#removable){.pf-ref} and [](#nonzero){.pf-ref} the extension $\widetilde g$ is holomorphic and nonzero near $a$, so a holomorphic logarithm $\ell$ of $\widetilde g$ exists near $a$; then $f=M+\ell$ on the punctured disc, which extends holomorphically across $a$. So the singularity is removable.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-qed}
The singularity of $f$ at $a$ is removable by step [](#extends){.pf-ref}; the same argument with $f$ replaced by $-f$ gives the corresponding statement for $\operatorname{Re}f$ bounded below.
:::
