::: {.problem}
Let $c_x:I\to X$ be the constant path at $x$, and let $f$ be a path from $x_0$ to $x_1$. Construct explicit path homotopies, algebraically and with diagrams, proving
\[
[f]*[c_{x_1}]=[f]
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
[c_{x_0}]*[f]=[f].
\]
:::
