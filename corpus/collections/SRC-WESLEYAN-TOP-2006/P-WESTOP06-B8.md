::: {.problem}
1. State the Lebesgue Number Lemma.
2. Let $p:E\to B$ be a covering map and let $p(e_0)=b_0$. Prove that every path
   \[
   f:[0,1]\to B,
   \qquad f(0)=b_0,
   \]
   has a unique lift $\widetilde f:[0,1]\to E$ beginning at $e_0$.
:::
