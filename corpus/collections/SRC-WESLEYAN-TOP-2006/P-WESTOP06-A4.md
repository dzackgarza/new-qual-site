::: {.problem}
Let $C$ be the class of compact Hausdorff spaces. Call $I\in C$ *injective in $C$* if whenever $X,Y\in C$, $X\subseteq Y$, and $f:X\to I$ is continuous, there is a continuous extension
\[
\overline f:Y\to I.
\]
Explain why $[0,1]$ is injective in $C$.
:::
