::: {.problem}
Problem 11. For $R>1$, let
\[
A_R=\{z\in\mathbb C:1<|z|<R\}.
\]
Assume there is a conformal (i.e. injective, holomorphic) mapping $F$ from $A_{R_1}$ onto $A_{R_2}$. Prove that $R_1=R_2$.
:::
