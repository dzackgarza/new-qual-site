::: {.problem}
Problem 5. Let $\mu$ be a real-valued Borel measure on $[0,1]$ such that
\[
\int_0^1\frac1{x+t}\,d\mu(t)=0
\qquad\text{for all }x>1.
\]
Show that $\mu=0$.
:::
