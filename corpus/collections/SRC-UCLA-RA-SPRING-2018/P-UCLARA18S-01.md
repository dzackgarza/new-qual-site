::: {.problem}
Problem 1. Suppose $f\in L^1(\mathbb R)$ satisfies
\[
\limsup_{h\to0}\int_{\mathbb R}\left|\frac{f(x+h)-f(x)}{h}\right|\,dx=0.
\]
Show that $f=0$ almost everywhere.
:::
