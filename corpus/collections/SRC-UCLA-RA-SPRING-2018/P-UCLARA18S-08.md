::: {.problem}
Problem 8. Determine the supremum of
\[
\left|\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}(0,0)\right|
\]
among all harmonic functions $u:\mathbb D\to[0,1]$, where
\[
\mathbb D=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<1\}.
\]
Prove that your answer is correct.
:::
