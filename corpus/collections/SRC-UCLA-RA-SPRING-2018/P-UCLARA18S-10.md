Problem 10. Let C∗ = C ∪ {∞} be the Riemann sphere and let Ω = C∗ \ {0, 1}. Let f : Ω → Ω be a holomorphic function.
(a) Prove that if f is injective then f (Ω) = Ω. (b) Make a list of all such injective functions f .
