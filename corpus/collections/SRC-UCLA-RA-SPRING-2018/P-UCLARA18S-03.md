::: {.problem}
Problem 3. Suppose $f\in L^1(\mathbb R)$ satisfies
\[
\limsup_{\varepsilon\to0}\int_{\mathbb R}\int_{\mathbb R}
\frac{|f(x)f(y)|}{|x-y|^2+\varepsilon^2}\,dx\,dy<\infty.
\]
Show that $f=0$ almost everywhere.
:::
