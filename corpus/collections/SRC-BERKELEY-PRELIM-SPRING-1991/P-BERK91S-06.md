:::{.problem}
Let
\[
F(r)=g(\|r\|)r,
\qquad r\ne0,
\]
be a vector field on $\mathbb R^3\setminus\{0\}$, where $g:(0,\infty)\to\mathbb R$ is smooth. Prove that
\[
\int_C F\cdot ds=0
\]
for every smooth closed path $C$ in $\mathbb R^3$ that avoids the origin.
:::
