::: {.problem}
Let $f$ be analytic in the unit disk with
\[
|f(z)|\le1,
\qquad f(0)=0.
\]
Suppose there is $r\in(0,1)$ such that
\[
f(r)=f(-r)=0.
\]
Prove that
\[
|f(z)|
\le
|z|\left|\frac{z^2-r^2}{1-r^2z^2}\right|.
\]
:::
