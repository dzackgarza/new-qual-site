::: {.problem}
Let $f$ be real-valued on an open interval containing $a$, and suppose $f$ is differentiable at $a$. If $(x_n)$ is increasing, $(y_n)$ is decreasing, and both sequences converge to $a$, prove that
\[
\lim_{n\to\infty}
\frac{f(y_n)-f(x_n)}{y_n-x_n}
=f'(a).
\]
:::
