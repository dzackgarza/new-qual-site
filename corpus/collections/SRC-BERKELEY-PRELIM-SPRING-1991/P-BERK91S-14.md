::: {.problem}
Let $x(t)$ be a nontrivial solution of
\[
\frac{dx}{dt}=Ax,
\qquad
A=\begin{pmatrix}
1&6&1\\
-4&4&11\\
-3&-9&8
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Prove that the Euclidean norm $\|x(t)\|$ is an increasing function of $t$.
:::
