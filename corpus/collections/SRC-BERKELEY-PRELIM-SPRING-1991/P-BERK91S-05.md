::: {.problem}
Let $A=(a_{ij})_{i,j=1}^r$ be a square matrix with integer entries.

1. Prove that if an integer $n$ is an eigenvalue of $A$, then $n$ divides $\det A$.
2. Suppose every row of $A$ has sum $n$:
   \[
   \sum_{j=1}^r a_{ij}=n
   \qquad(1\le i\le r).
   \]
   Prove that $n$ divides $\det A$.
:::
