::: {.problem}
Consider the set of functions $f:[0,1]\to\mathbb R$ satisfying
\[
|f(x)-f(y)|\leq|x-y|
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\int_0^1 f(x)\,dx=1.
\]
Show that this is a compact subset of $C([0,1])$.
:::
