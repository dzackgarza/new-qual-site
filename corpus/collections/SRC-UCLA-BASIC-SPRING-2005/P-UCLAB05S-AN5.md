::: {.problem}
(a) Suppose
\[
\lim_{n\to\infty}a_n=A.
\]
Show that
\[
\lim_{N\to\infty}\frac1N\sum_{n=1}^N a_n=A.
\]

(b) Show by example that the converse is false.
:::
