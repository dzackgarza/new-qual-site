::: {.problem}
Let $A$ be a Hermitian-symmetric $n\times n$ complex matrix.
Show that if
\[
\langle Av,v\rangle\geq0
\qquad\text{for every }v\in\mathbb C^n,
\]
then there exists an $n\times n$ matrix $T$ such that
\[
A=T^*T.
\]
:::
