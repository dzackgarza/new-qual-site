::: {.problem}
Let A and B be ideals of a ring.
Prove that $A B \subseteq A \cap B$
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $x \in A B$ . Then $x = a_1b_1 + \cdots + a_kb_k$ for some $a_i \in A$ and $b_i \in B$ . Since A is an ideal, each $a_ib_i \in A$ . Similarly, each $a_ib_i \in B$ . Since $A \cap B$ is closed under addition, $x \in A \cap B$.
:::
