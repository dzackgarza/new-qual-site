::: {.problem}
Let A and B be ideals of a ring. Prove that $A B \subseteq A \cap B$
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $x \in A B$ . Then x is of the form ab for some $a \in A$ and $b \in B$ . Since A is an ideal, $a b \in A$ . Similarly, $a b \in B$ . Hence $x \in A \cap B$
:::
