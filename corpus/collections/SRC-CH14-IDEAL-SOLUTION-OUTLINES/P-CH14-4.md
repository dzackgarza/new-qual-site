::: {.problem}
Find a subring of $\ZZ \oplus \ZZ$ that is not an ideal of $\ZZ \oplus \ZZ$.
:::

::: {.solution}
$I = \{ ( a , a ) \st a \in \ZZ \}$ is a subring but not an ideal since $( 1 , 2 ) ( a , a ) = ( a , 2 a ) \not \in I .$
:::
