::: {.problem}
Find all maximal ideals in:

(a) $\ZZ_8$.

(b) $\ZZ_{10}$.

(c) $\ZZ_{12}$.

(d) $\ZZ_n$.
:::

::: {.solution}
(a) $( 2 )$

(b) $( 2 ) , ( 5 )$

(c) $( 2 ) , ( 3 )$

(d) The maximal ideals are of the form $( p )$ where p is a prime that divides n.
:::
