::: {.problem}
In the ring of integers, find a positive integer a such that:
:::

::: {.solution}
$$
1 . \ < a > = < 2 > + < 3 > \text { Since } 1 = - 2 + 3 , a = 1 .
$$

$$
2 . \ < a > = < 6 > + < 8 > , \ a = 2
$$

$$
3 . \ < a > = < m > + < n > , \ a = \operatorname { g c d } ( m , n )
$$
:::
