::: {.problem}
Prove that $I = \langle 2 + 2i \rangle$ is not a prime ideal of $\ZZ[i]$. How many elements are in $\ZZ[i]/I$? What is the characteristic of $\ZZ[i]/I$?
:::

::: {.solution}
The product $2 ( 1 + i ) = 2 + 2 i \in I$ but $2 \notin I$ and $1 + i \notin I$ . This shows I is not a prime ideal.
For the order, $( 2 + 2 i ) ( 1 - i ) = 2 - 2 i + 2 i + 2 = 4 \in I$ and $2i + I = -2 + I = 2 + I$ because $2 + 2i \in I$ and $4 \in I$. So $a + b i + I$ would have $a$ in $\{ 0 , 1 , 2 , 3 \}$ and $b$ in $\{ 0 , 1 \}$ . Thus there are $4 \cdot 2 = 8$ elements in the quotient ring.
The characteristic is n such that $n ( a + b i ) + I = I$ , and hence it is 4.
:::
