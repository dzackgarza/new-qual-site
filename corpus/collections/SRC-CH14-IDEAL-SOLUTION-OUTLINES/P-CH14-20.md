::: {.problem}
Suppose that R is a commutative ring and $| R | = 3 0$ . If I is an ideal of R and $| I | = 1 0$ , prove that I is a maximal ideal.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let R be an order 30 commutative ring, and I be an ideal of R with order 10. Let J be an ideal with $I \subsetneq J \subseteq R$. By Lagrange's theorem for the additive groups, $\abs{J}$ is a multiple of $10$ dividing $30$ and exceeding $10$, so $\abs{J} = 30$ and $J = R$. Hence I is a maximal ideal.
:::
