::: {.problem}
Suppose that $R$ is a commutative ring and $\cardinality{R} = 30$. If $I$ is an ideal of $R$ and $\cardinality{I} = 10$, prove that $I$ is a maximal ideal.
:::

::: {.solution}

The exercise is Gallian, *Contemporary Abstract Algebra* (7th ed.), Chapter 14 exercise 20,
printed p. 270. The argument is the retained solution outline's item 20
(`assets/attachments/Ch14Sltns.pdf`) in its counting form, restated as numbered
steps; the outline's route through the factor ring is not used, for the reason recorded on
the audit.

Throughout, $R$ is a commutative ring with $\cardinality{R}=30$ and $I$ is an ideal of $R$
with $\cardinality{I}=10$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #proper}

$I$ is a proper ideal of $R$.

::: pf-proof

$I\subseteq R$ and $\cardinality{I}=10<30=\cardinality{R}$, so $I\ne R$; an ideal that is
not all of $R$ is proper (Definition, printed p. 262).

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #orders}

If $J$ is an ideal of $R$ with $I\subseteq J\subseteq R$ and $J\ne I$, then $J=R$.

::: pf-proof

An ideal is a subring (Definition, printed p. 262), so $(I,+)$ is a subgroup of $(J,+)$ and
$(J,+)$ is a subgroup of the additive group $(R,+)$. That additive group is abelian of order
$30$, hence a finite group, so Lagrange's Theorem (Theorem 7.1) gives
$\cardinality{I}$ divides $\cardinality{J}$ and $\cardinality{J}$ divides
$\cardinality{R}$: that is, $10$ divides $\cardinality{J}$ and $\cardinality{J}$ divides
$30$. The multiples of $10$ that divide $30$ are $10$ and $30$, so
$\cardinality{J}\in\theset{10,30}$. If $J\ne I$ with $I\subseteq J$ then
$\cardinality{J}>\cardinality{I}=10$, leaving $\cardinality{J}=30=\cardinality{R}$; since
$J\subseteq R$ is a subset of the same finite cardinality, $J=R$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #maximal}

$I$ is a maximal ideal of $R$.

::: pf-proof

By step [](#proper){.pf-ref} $I$ is proper, and by step [](#orders){.pf-ref} no ideal lies
properly between $I$ and $R$. These are the two clauses of the definition of a maximal ideal
for a commutative ring (printed p. 267), so $I$ is maximal.

:::
