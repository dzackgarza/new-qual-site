::: {.problem}
Let $R = \mathbb{Z}_{27}$. Find:

(a) $N(\langle 0 \rangle)$.

(b) $N(\langle 3 \rangle)$.

(c) $N(\langle 9 \rangle)$.

Here, for an ideal $A$ of $R$, $N(A) = \{a \in R \mid a^n \in A \text{ for some } n\}$, the definition the source's solution applies to $N(\langle 0 \rangle)$.
:::
