::: {.problem}
If $f\in L^1(\RR^n)$, prove that its Fourier transform
$$
\widehat f(\xi)=\int_{\RR^n}f(x)e^{-2\pi i x\cdot\xi}\,dx
$$
is uniformly continuous on $\RR^n$.
:::

::: {.solution}
The collection's provenance is the homework sheet, so no retained solution set covers the problem and the argument is the card's own. Write $\widehat f(\xi)=\int_{\RR^n}f(x)e^{-2\pi ix\cdot\xi}\,dx$ for $f\in L^1(\RR^n)$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #bounded}
$\widehat f$ is well defined, with $\abs{\widehat f(\xi)}\le\norm{f}_1$ for every $\xi$.

::: pf-proof
$\abs{f(x)e^{-2\pi ix\cdot\xi}}=\abs{f(x)}$ for real $x,\xi$, so the integrand is dominated by the integrable function $\abs f$, and the integral exists with the stated bound by the triangle inequality for the integral.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #difference}
For all $h,y\in\RR^n$,
$$
\widehat f(y+h)-\widehat f(y)=\int_{\RR^n}f(x)e^{-2\pi ix\cdot y}\bigl(e^{-2\pi ix\cdot h}-1\bigr)\,dx.
$$

::: pf-proof
Both integrals exist by step [](#bounded){.pf-ref}, and the difference of two integrals of integrable functions is the integral of the difference; factoring out $e^{-2\pi ix\cdot y}$ gives the displayed integrand.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #sup-bound}
$$
\sup_{y\in\RR^n}\abs{\widehat f(y+h)-\widehat f(y)}\le\int_{\RR^n}\abs{f(x)}\,\abs{e^{-2\pi ix\cdot h}-1}\,dx.
$$

::: pf-proof
By step [](#difference){.pf-ref} and the triangle inequality, $\abs{\widehat f(y+h)-\widehat f(y)}\le\int\abs{f(x)}\abs{e^{-2\pi ix\cdot h}-1}\,dx$, whose right-hand side does not involve $y$; the supremum over $y$ is therefore at most it.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #tends-to-zero}
$\int_{\RR^n}\abs{f(x)}\abs{e^{-2\pi ix\cdot h}-1}\,dx\to0$ as $h\to0$.

::: pf-proof
For each fixed $x$ one has $\abs{e^{-2\pi ix\cdot h}-1}\to0$ as $h\to0$, by continuity of the exponential and $e^0=1$; and $\abs{e^{-2\pi ix\cdot h}-1}\le2$ always. So the integrands are dominated by the fixed integrable function $2\abs f$ and converge pointwise to $0$, and the dominated convergence theorem gives convergence of the integrals to $0$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step}
$\boxed{\widehat f\text{ is uniformly continuous on }\RR^n}$.

::: pf-proof
Given $\varepsilon>0$, step [](#tends-to-zero){.pf-ref} supplies $\delta>0$ with $\int\abs{f(x)}\abs{e^{-2\pi ix\cdot h}-1}\,dx<\varepsilon$ whenever $\abs h<\delta$. Then step [](#sup-bound){.pf-ref} gives $\abs{\widehat f(y+h)-\widehat f(y)}<\varepsilon$ for all $y$ and all such $h$, which is uniform continuity, the same $\delta$ serving every $y$.
:::
