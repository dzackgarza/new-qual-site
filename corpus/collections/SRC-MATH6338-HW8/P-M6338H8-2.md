::: problem
Let $f\in L^2(\mathbb R^n)$ and define
\[
g_N(\xi)=\int_{|x|\le N}f(x)e^{-2\pi i x\cdot\xi}\,dx.
\]
Prove that $g_N\to\widehat f$ in $L^2(\mathbb R^n)$ as $N\to\infty$.
:::

::: solution
Put $f_N=f\mathbf1_{\{|x|\le N\}}$. Since the ball has finite measure, $f_N\in L^1\cap L^2$, and its ordinary Fourier transform is exactly $g_N$.

Moreover,
\[
\|f-f_N\|_2^2
=\int_{|x|>N}|f(x)|^2\,dx\longrightarrow0
\]
by monotone convergence applied to the complementary truncations (equivalently, absolute continuity of the integral of $|f|^2$). Plancherel's theorem therefore gives
\[
\|\widehat f-g_N\|_2
=\|\widehat{f-f_N}\|_2
=\|f-f_N\|_2\longrightarrow0.
\]
:::
