::: {.problem}
Let $A$ be a real symmetric positive-definite $n\times n$ matrix and let $0\ne y\in\mathbb R^n$.
Prove that
\[
\lim_{m\to\infty}\frac{y^TA^{m+1}y}{y^TA^my}
\]
exists and is an eigenvalue of $A$.
:::
