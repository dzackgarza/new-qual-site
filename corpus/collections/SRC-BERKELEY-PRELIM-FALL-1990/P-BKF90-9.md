::: {.problem}
Let $(a,b,c)\in\mathbb R^3$ have length $1$, and let
\[
W=\{(x,y,z):ax+by+cz=0\}.
\]
Find, in the standard basis, the matrix of the orthogonal projection of $\mathbb R^3$ onto $W$.
