::: {.problem}
Let $A$ be an additively written abelian group and let
\[
u,v:A\to A
\]
be homomorphisms.
Define $f,g:A\to A$ by
\[
f(a)=a-v(u(a)),
\qquad
g(a)=a-u(v(a)).
\]
Prove that $\ker f\cong\ker g$.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: pf-step

The homomorphism $u:A\to A$ restricts to a homomorphism
$$
u_0:\ker f\longrightarrow\ker g.
$$

::: pf-proof

If $a\in\ker f$, then
$$
a-v(u(a))=0,
$$
so $v(u(a))=a$. Therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
g(u(a))
&=u(a)-u(v(u(a)))\\
&=u\bigl(a-v(u(a))\bigr)\\
&=0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus $u(a)\in\ker g$. Since $u$ is a homomorphism, so is its restriction $u_0$.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

The homomorphism $v:A\to A$ restricts to a homomorphism
$$
v_0:\ker g\longrightarrow\ker f.
$$

::: pf-proof

If $b\in\ker g$, then
$$
b-u(v(b))=0,
$$
so $u(v(b))=b$. Therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(v(b))
&=v(b)-v(u(v(b)))\\
&=v\bigl(b-u(v(b))\bigr)\\
&=0.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus $v(b)\in\ker f$, and the restriction $v_0$ is a homomorphism.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

The restrictions $u_0$ and $v_0$ are mutually inverse.

::: pf-proof

If $a\in\ker f$, then the kernel equation gives
$$
(v_0\circ u_0)(a)=v(u(a))=a.
$$
If $b\in\ker g$, then similarly
$$
(u_0\circ v_0)(b)=u(v(b))=b.
$$
Hence both compositions are identity maps on their respective kernels.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s4}

Therefore
$$
\boxed{\ker f\cong\ker g}.
$$

::: pf-proof

By step [](#s3){.pf-ref}, $u_0$ is an isomorphism with inverse $v_0$.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s4){.pf-ref} is the required isomorphism.

:::
