::: {.problem}
Let $A$ be an additively written abelian group and let
\[
u,v:A\to A
\]
be homomorphisms.
Define $f,g:A\to A$ by
\[
f(a)=a-v(u(a)),
\qquad
g(a)=a-u(v(a)).
\]
Prove that $\ker f\cong\ker g$.
:::
