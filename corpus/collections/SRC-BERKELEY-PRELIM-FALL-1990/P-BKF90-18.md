::: {.problem}
Let $f$ be entire and suppose that for every $r>0$,
\[
\int_0^{2\pi}|f(re^{i\theta})|\,d\theta\le r^{17/3}.
\]
Prove that $f$ is identically zero.
:::

::: {.solution}
Write the Taylor expansion of $f$ at the origin as
$$
f(z)=\sum_{m=0}^\infty a_m z^m.
$$

<1>1. For every integer $m\ge0$ and every $r>0$,
$$
\abs{a_m}
\le
\frac{1}{2\pi}r^{17/3-m}.
$$

::: {.proof}
By the Cauchy coefficient formula on the circle $\abs z=r$,
$$
a_m
=
\frac{1}{2\pi i}\int_{\abs z=r}\frac{f(z)}{z^{m+1}}\,dz.
$$
With $z=re^{i\theta}$ this becomes
$$
a_m
=
\frac{1}{2\pi r^m}
\int_0^{2\pi}f(re^{i\theta})e^{-im\theta}\,d\theta.
$$
Therefore the hypothesis gives
$$
\abs{a_m}
\le
\frac{1}{2\pi r^m}
\int_0^{2\pi}\abs{f(re^{i\theta})}\,d\theta
\le
\frac{1}{2\pi}r^{17/3-m}.
$$
:::

<1>2. One has
$$
a_m=0
$$
for $0\le m\le5$.

::: {.proof}
For such $m$,
$$
\frac{17}{3}-m>0.
$$
The estimate in step <1>1 holds for every $r>0$, so letting $r\downarrow0$ forces $\abs{a_m}=0$.
:::

<1>3. One has
$$
a_m=0
$$
for every $m\ge6$.

::: {.proof}
For such $m$,
$$
\frac{17}{3}-m<0.
$$
Again using step <1>1, now let $r\to\infty$. The right-hand side tends to $0$, so $\abs{a_m}=0$.
:::

<1>4. Hence
$$
\boxed{f\equiv0}.
$$

::: {.proof}
Steps <1>2 and <1>3 show that every Taylor coefficient $a_m$ is zero. Since $f$ is entire, its Taylor series represents $f$ on all of $\CC$, so $f$ is identically zero.
:::

<1>5. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>4 is the required conclusion.
:::
