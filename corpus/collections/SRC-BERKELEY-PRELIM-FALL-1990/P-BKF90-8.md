::: {.problem}
Let $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ be continuous, nonnegative, and satisfy
\[
\int_0^\infty f(x)\,dx<\infty.
\]
Prove that
\[
\frac1n\int_0^n x f(x)\,dx\longrightarrow0
\qquad(n\to\infty).
\]
:::
