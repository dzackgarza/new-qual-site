::: {.problem}
Evaluate
\[
\lim_{n\to\infty}
\prod_{k=2}^{n}\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{2^k}\right).
\]
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

For every $n\ge2$,
$$
1
=
2^{n-1}\sin\left(\frac{\pi}{2^n}\right)
\prod_{k=2}^n\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{2^k}\right).
$$

::: pf-proof

The double-angle identity gives
$$
\sin x=2\sin\left(\frac x2\right)\cos\left(\frac x2\right).
$$
Starting with $x=\pi/2$ and applying this identity successively to the sine factor gives
$$
\sin\left(\frac\pi2\right)
=
2^{n-1}\sin\left(\frac{\pi}{2^n}\right)
\prod_{k=2}^n\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{2^k}\right).
$$
Since $\sin(\pi/2)=1$, the displayed identity follows.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

Hence
$$
\prod_{k=2}^n\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{2^k}\right)
=
\frac{2}{\pi}
\frac{\pi/2^n}{\sin(\pi/2^n)}.
$$

::: pf-proof

Solving the identity in step [](#s1){.pf-ref} for the product gives
$$
\prod_{k=2}^n\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{2^k}\right)
=
\frac{1}{2^{n-1}\sin(\pi/2^n)}.
$$
Since
$$
2^{n-1}=\frac{\pi}{2(\pi/2^n)},
$$
this is the displayed expression.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

Therefore
$$
\boxed{
\lim_{n\to\infty}
\prod_{k=2}^{n}\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{2^k}\right)
=
\frac{2}{\pi}}.
$$

::: pf-proof

As $n\to\infty$, one has $\pi/2^n\to0$, and the standard limit
$$
\frac{\sin t}{t}\longrightarrow1
$$
implies
$$
\frac{\pi/2^n}{\sin(\pi/2^n)}\longrightarrow1.
$$
Apply this to step [](#s2){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s3){.pf-ref} gives the requested limit.

:::
