::: {.problem}
Let $a$ and $b$ be real numbers.
Prove that there are two orthogonal unit vectors $u$ and $v$ in $\mathbb R^3$ such that $u=(u_1,u_2,a)$ and $v=(v_1,v_2,b)$ if and only if $a^2+b^2\le1$.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

If such orthogonal unit vectors $u$ and $v$ exist, then
$$
a^2+b^2\leq1.
$$

::: pf-proof

Write
$$
u'=(u_1,u_2),
\qquad
v'=(v_1,v_2).
$$
Since $u$ and $v$ are unit vectors,
$$
\norm{u'}^2=1-a^2,
\qquad
\norm{v'}^2=1-b^2.
$$
Their orthogonality gives
$$
\inner{u'}{v'}=-ab.
$$
By the Cauchy--Schwarz inequality,
$$
a^2b^2
=
\abs{\inner{u'}{v'}}^2
\leq
\norm{u'}^2\norm{v'}^2
=
(1-a^2)(1-b^2).
$$
Expanding the last inequality and cancelling $a^2b^2$ gives
$$
a^2+b^2\leq1.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

Assume $a^2+b^2\leq1$ and $a^2<1$. Then there are orthogonal unit vectors with third coordinates $a$ and $b$.

::: pf-proof

Set
$$
r=\sqrt{1-a^2}>0
$$
and define
$$
u=(r,0,a),
$$
$$
v=
\left(
-\frac{ab}{r},
\sqrt{\frac{1-a^2-b^2}{1-a^2}},
b
\right).
$$
The square root is real because $a^2+b^2\leq1$. Clearly
$$
\norm{u}^2=r^2+a^2=1.
$$
Also
$$
\begin{aligned}
\norm{v}^2
&=
\frac{a^2b^2}{1-a^2}
+
\frac{1-a^2-b^2}{1-a^2}
+b^2\\
&=
1.
\end{aligned}
$$
Finally,
$$
\inner{u}{v}
=
r\left(-\frac{ab}{r}\right)+ab
=
0.
$$
Thus $u$ and $v$ have all the required properties.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

Assume $a^2+b^2\leq1$ and $a^2=1$. Then there are orthogonal unit vectors with third coordinates $a$ and $b$.

::: pf-proof

The inequality $a^2+b^2\leq1$ and $a^2=1$ force $b=0$. Take
$$
u=(0,0,a),
\qquad
v=(1,0,0).
$$
Then both vectors are unit vectors, their inner product is $0$, and their third coordinates are $a$ and $b$, respectively.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s4}

If $a^2+b^2\leq1$, then the required orthogonal unit vectors exist.

::: pf-proof

The inequality implies $a^2\leq1$. If $a^2<1$, apply step [](#s2){.pf-ref}; if $a^2=1$, apply step [](#s3){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s5}

The required vectors exist if and only if
$$
\boxed{a^2+b^2\leq1}.
$$

::: pf-proof

Step [](#s1){.pf-ref} proves necessity, and step [](#s4){.pf-ref} proves sufficiency.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s5){.pf-ref} proves the stated equivalence.

:::
