::: {.problem}
Let $\mathbf F$ be a field containing $\mathbb Q$ such that $[\mathbf F:\mathbb Q]=2$. Prove that there exists a unique integer $m$ such that $m$ has no multiple prime factors and $\mathbf F$ is isomorphic to $\mathbb Q(\sqrt m)$.
:::
