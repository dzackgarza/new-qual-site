::: {.problem}
Evaluate
\[
\iint_{\mathcal R}(x^3-3xy^2)\,dx\,dy,
\]
where
\[
\mathcal R=\{(x,y)\in\mathbb R^2 : (x+1)^2+y^2\le 9,\ (x-1)^2+y^2\ge 1\}.
\]
:::
