::: {.problem}
Discuss the solvability of the differential equation
\[
(e^x\sin y)(y')^3+(e^x\cos y)y'+e^y\tan x=0
\]
with the initial condition $y(0)=0$. Does a solution exist in some interval about $0$? If so, is it unique?
:::
