::: problem
Let $K$ and $L$ be finite fields.
Show that $K$ is contained in $L$ if and only if $\# K = p^r$ and $\# L = p^s$ for the same prime $p$, and $r \leq s$.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. $G$ group.
Proof: Sylow.

<1>2. Q.E.D.
Proof: <1>1.
:::
