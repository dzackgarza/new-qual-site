::: {.problem}
Let $a,b$ be the generators of
\[
\pi_1(S^1\vee S^1).
\]
Draw a picture of the covering space of $S^1\vee S^1$ corresponding to the normal subgroup generated by
\[
a^2,\qquad b^2,\qquad ab.
\]
:::

::: {.solution}
Let
$$
F=\pi_1(S^1\vee S^1)=\langle a,b\rangle
$$
and let
$$
N=\langle\!\langle a^2,b^2,ab\rangle\!\rangle
$$
be their normal closure.

<1>1. The quotient group $F/N$ is isomorphic to $\ZZ/2\ZZ$.

::: {.proof}
In the quotient, the relations are
$$
a^2=1,
\qquad
b^2=1,
\qquad
ab=1.
$$
The last relation gives
$$
b=a^{-1}.
$$
Since $a^2=1$, one has $a^{-1}=a$, so $b=a$. Thus the quotient is generated by one element $a$ with the single relation $a^2=1$:
$$
F/N\cong\langle a\mid a^2=1\rangle\cong\ZZ/2\ZZ.
$$
:::

<1>2. Equivalently, $N$ is the kernel of the homomorphism
$$
\phi:F\longrightarrow\ZZ/2\ZZ,
\qquad
\phi(a)=\phi(b)=1.
$$

::: {.proof}
The elements $a^2,b^2,ab$ all lie in $\ker\phi$, so
$$
N\subseteq\ker\phi.
$$
The map $\phi$ is surjective, so $F/\ker\phi$ has order $2$. Step <1>1 shows that $F/N$ also has order $2$. Since $N\subseteq\ker\phi$, equality follows.
:::

<1>3. The corresponding covering has two vertices, say $v_0$ and $v_1$, indexed by the two cosets of $N$ in $F$.

::: {.proof}
For a subgroup of the fundamental group of a graph, the vertices of the associated Schreier covering graph are the right cosets of the subgroup. By step <1>1,
$$
[F:N]=2.
$$
Thus there are exactly two vertices.
:::

<1>4. Both the $a$-edge and the $b$-edge interchange the two vertices.

::: {.proof}
In the quotient $F/N\cong\ZZ/2\ZZ$, both $a$ and $b$ represent the nonzero element by step <1>2. Right multiplication by either generator therefore swaps the two cosets.
:::

<1>5. Hence the covering graph is the two-sheeted graph with vertices $v_0,v_1$ and four edges:
$$
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{edge}&\text{initial vertex}&\text{terminal vertex}\\ \hline
a_0&v_0&v_1\\
a_1&v_1&v_0\\
b_0&v_0&v_1\\
b_1&v_1&v_0
\end{array}
$$
where each $a_j$ maps homeomorphically to the $a$-circle and each $b_j$ maps homeomorphically to the $b$-circle.

::: {.proof}
At each vertex of a covering of $S^1\vee S^1$, there must be exactly one outgoing lift of the positively oriented $a$-edge and exactly one outgoing lift of the positively oriented $b$-edge. Step <1>4 says that each such lift ends at the other sheet. Thus the four listed edges give the entire covering.

Ignoring orientation, this is a graph with two vertices joined by four parallel edges, two labeled $a$ and two labeled $b$.
:::

<1>6. This graph is a connected regular two-sheeted covering of $S^1\vee S^1$, and its associated subgroup is exactly $N$.

::: {.proof}
The graph is connected because each generator edge joins $v_0$ to $v_1$. The local star at each vertex maps bijectively to the local star at the wedge point, so the map is a covering. Its monodromy action is precisely the quotient action of
$$
F\longrightarrow F/N\cong\ZZ/2\ZZ,
$$
whose stabilizer of the identity coset is $N$. Since $N$ is normal, the covering is regular, with deck group $F/N\cong\ZZ/2\ZZ$.
:::

<1>7. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Steps <1>3--<1>6 completely specify the requested picture and verify that it corresponds to the given normal subgroup.
:::
