::: {.problem}
Is there a ring homomorphism from the reals to some ring whose kernel is the integers?
:::

::: {.solution}
No. The kernel of a ring homomorphism is an ideal but the integers are not an ideal of the real numbers.
For instance, $\pi \cdot 1 = \pi \notin \mathbb { Z }$
:::
