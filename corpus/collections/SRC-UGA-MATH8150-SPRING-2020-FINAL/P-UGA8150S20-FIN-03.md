::: {.problem}
(1) Show that if $f$ is analytic in an open set containing the disk $|z-a|\le R$, then
\[
|f(a)|^2\le \frac{1}{\pi R^2}\int_0^{2\pi}\int_0^R
|f(a+re^{i\theta})|^2 r\,dr\,d\theta.
\]

(2) Let $\Omega$ be a region and $M>0$. Let $\mathcal F$ be the family of all analytic functions $f$ on $\Omega$ such that
\[
\iint_\Omega |f(z)|^2\,dx\,dy\le M.
\]
Show that $\mathcal F$ is a normal family.
:::
