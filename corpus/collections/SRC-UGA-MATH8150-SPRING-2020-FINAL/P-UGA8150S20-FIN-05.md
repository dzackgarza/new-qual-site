::: {.problem}
Consider
\[
f(z)=\frac12\left(z+\frac1z\right),\qquad z\in\mathbb C\setminus\{0\}.
\]

(a) Show that $f$ is univalent on the punctured disk $\mathbb D\setminus\{0\}$. What is the image of $|z|=r<1$ under this map?

(b) Show that $f$ is univalent on $\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|>1\}$. What is the image of this domain?

(c) What is the inverse mapping
\[
f^{-1}:\mathbb C\setminus[-1,1]\longrightarrow \mathbb D\setminus\{0\}?
\]
:::
