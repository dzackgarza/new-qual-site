::: {.problem}
Let $n>1$ be an integer.
Show that the automorphism group of the cyclic group of order $n$ is isomorphic to the multiplicative group of units mod $n$.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. $G$ finite.
Proof: Sylow.

<1>2. Q.E.D.

:::
