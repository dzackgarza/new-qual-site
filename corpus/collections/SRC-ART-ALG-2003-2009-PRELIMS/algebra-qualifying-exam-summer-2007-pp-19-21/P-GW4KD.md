::: {.problem}
Let $n>1$ be an integer.
Show that the automorphism group of the cyclic group of order $n$ is isomorphic to the multiplicative group of units mod $n$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let
$$
C_n=\langle g\rangle.
$$

::: pf

::: pf-step

Every endomorphism of $C_n$ has the form
$$
\phi_k(g^a)=g^{ka}
$$
for a uniquely determined residue class $k\pmod n$.

::: pf-proof

A homomorphism $\phi:C_n\to C_n$ is determined by the image of the generator $g$. Every element of $C_n$ is $g^k$ for some integer $k$, so
$$
\phi(g)=g^k.
$$
Then for every integer $a$,
$$
\phi(g^a)=\phi(g)^a=g^{ka}.
$$
If $k\equiv \ell\pmod n$, then $g^k=g^\ell$, so the endomorphism depends only on $k\pmod n$; conversely, equality of the endomorphisms forces equality of the images of $g$, hence $k\equiv\ell\pmod n$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

The endomorphism $\phi_k$ is an automorphism if and only if
$$
\gcd(k,n)=1.
$$

::: pf-proof

The image of $\phi_k$ is the cyclic subgroup generated by
$$
\phi_k(g)=g^k.
$$
Thus $\phi_k$ is surjective, and hence bijective because $C_n$ is finite, exactly when $g^k$ has order $n$.

The order of $g^k$ is
$$
\frac{n}{\gcd(n,k)}.
$$
Therefore $g^k$ has order $n$ exactly when $\gcd(n,k)=1$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

Define
$$
\Psi:(\ZZ/n\ZZ)^\times\longrightarrow\Aut(C_n),
\qquad
[k]\longmapsto\phi_k.
$$
Then $\Psi$ is a group isomorphism.

::: pf-proof

By step [](#s2){.pf-ref}, $\phi_k$ is an automorphism exactly for unit classes $[k]\in(\ZZ/n\ZZ)^\times$, so the map is well-defined.

For units $[k],[\ell]$,
$$
(\phi_k\circ\phi_\ell)(g)
=\phi_k(g^\ell)
=g^{k\ell}
=\phi_{k\ell}(g).
$$
Since homomorphisms from $C_n$ are determined by the image of $g$,
$$
\phi_k\circ\phi_\ell=\phi_{k\ell},
$$
so $\Psi$ is a homomorphism.

If $\Psi([k])$ is the identity, then
$$
g^k=g,
$$
so
$$
g^{k-1}=e,
$$
which implies $k\equiv1\pmod n$. Hence the kernel is trivial and $\Psi$ is injective.

Finally, every automorphism of $C_n$ sends $g$ to another generator $g^k$, and by step [](#s2){.pf-ref} such a $k$ is relatively prime to $n$. Hence every automorphism is in the image of $\Psi$, so $\Psi$ is surjective.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s4}

Therefore
$$
\boxed{\Aut(C_n)\cong(\ZZ/n\ZZ)^\times}.
$$

::: pf-proof

This is step [](#s3){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s4){.pf-ref} is the required conclusion.

:::
