::: {.problem}
a. List all abelian groups of size 90.
b. List all $\mathbf{R}[x]$ modules $M$ such that $\dim_{\mathbf{R}} M=4$ and $M$ is annihilated by $x^4-1$.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

Up to isomorphism, the abelian groups of order $90$ are $\ZZ/90$ and $\ZZ/3\times\ZZ/30$.

::: pf-proof

Since $90=2\cdot3^2\cdot5$, the structure theorem for finite abelian groups gives a unique decomposition into cyclic groups of prime-power order whose exponents for each prime $p$ form a partition of the exponent of $p$ in $90$ [@DF04].
The $2$-part and $5$-part are $\ZZ/2$ and $\ZZ/5$; the $3$-part is $\ZZ/9$ or $\ZZ/3\times\ZZ/3$.
By the Chinese remainder theorem, $\ZZ/2\times\ZZ/9\times\ZZ/5\cong\ZZ/90$ and $\ZZ/2\times\ZZ/3\times\ZZ/3\times\ZZ/5\cong\ZZ/3\times\ZZ/30$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

Up to isomorphism, the $\RR[x]$-modules $M$ with $\dim_\RR M=4$ and $(x^4-1)M=0$ are the nine modules
$$
M_{a,b,c}=\bigl(\RR[x]/(x-1)\bigr)^{a}\oplus\bigl(\RR[x]/(x+1)\bigr)^{b}\oplus\bigl(\RR[x]/(x^2+1)\bigr)^{c},
\qquad a,b,c\ge0,\ a+b+2c=4.
$$

::: pf-proof

Over $\RR$, $x^4-1=(x-1)(x+1)(x^2+1)$ with pairwise distinct irreducible factors.
The module $M$ is finitely generated and torsion over the principal ideal domain $\RR[x]$, so by the structure theorem it is a direct sum of cyclic modules $\RR[x]/(p^k)$ with $p$ monic irreducible, and the multiset of these summands is determined by $M$ [@DF04].
Each $p^k$ divides $x^4-1$, which is squarefree, so $k=1$ and $p\in\{x-1,x+1,x^2+1\}$.
Hence $M\cong M_{a,b,c}$ for unique $a,b,c\ge0$, and $\dim_\RR M=a+b+2c$.
The solutions of $a+b+2c=4$ are the five triples with $c=0$, the three with $c=1$ and $a+b=2$, and $(0,0,2)$.
Each $M_{a,b,c}$ is annihilated by $x^4-1$ and has dimension $4$.

:::
