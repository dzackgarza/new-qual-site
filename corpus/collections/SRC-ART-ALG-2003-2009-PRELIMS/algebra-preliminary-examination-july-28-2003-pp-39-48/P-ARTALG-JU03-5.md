::: {.problem}
Find the Galois group of $x^4 + x^3 + x^2 + x + 1$

(a) over $\QQ$.

(b) over $\FF_5$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Put $f(x)=x^4+x^3+x^2+x+1$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

Over $\QQ$, the Galois group is cyclic of order $4$.

::: pf-proof

Let $\zeta=e^{2\pi i/5}$. The identity
$(x-1)f(x)=x^5-1$ shows that the roots of $f$ are
$\zeta,\zeta^2,\zeta^3,\zeta^4$. They are distinct and all
belong to $L=\QQ(\zeta)$, which they generate.
Thus $L$ is the splitting field.

The translated polynomial is
$$
f(y+1)=y^4+5y^3+10y^2+10y+5.
$$
It is Eisenstein at $5$, so it is irreducible over $\QQ$
[@DF04]. Translation is an automorphism of the polynomial ring,
so $f$ is irreducible as well and $[L:\QQ]=4$.

For each $a\in\{1,2,3,4\}$, sending $\zeta$ to $\zeta^a$
defines an embedding of $\QQ(\zeta)$ into $L$, because
$\zeta^a$ has the same minimal polynomial $f$. It is onto:
choose $b$ with $ab\equiv1\pmod5$, and then
$(\zeta^a)^b=\zeta$. Denote this automorphism by $\sigma_a$.
Conversely, every automorphism must send $\zeta$ to a root of
$f$, so these are all the automorphisms. Their composition is
$$
\sigma_a\sigma_b(\zeta)=\zeta^{ab}.
$$
Therefore $\operatorname{Gal}(L/\QQ)$ is isomorphic to
$(\ZZ/5\ZZ)^\times$. The residues
$1,2,2^2=4,2^3=3$ are all its elements and $2^4=1$ modulo $5$.
Hence the group is $C_4$, generated by $\zeta\mapsto\zeta^2$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

Over $\FF_5$, the Galois group is trivial.

::: pf-proof

In characteristic $5$,
$$
(x-1)^4=x^4-4x^3+6x^2-4x+1
       =x^4+x^3+x^2+x+1=f(x).
$$
The only root is $1$, already in $\FF_5$.
The splitting field is therefore $\FF_5$ itself, and its
only automorphism fixing the base field pointwise is the identity.
The repeated roots of this polynomial do not make the identity
extension inseparable: every element of the splitting field has
a linear minimal polynomial over the base field.

:::
