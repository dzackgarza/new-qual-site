::: {.problem}
a. Let $F$ be a field and let $K$ be a field containing $F$.
Suppose that $\alpha, \beta \in K$ are both algebraic over $F$.
Prove that $\alpha + \beta$ is algebraic over $F$.

b. What is the minimal polynomial of $\sqrt{5} + 2\sqrt{7}$ over $\mathbb{Q}$?
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

In part (a), $\alpha+\beta$ is algebraic over $F$.

::: pf-proof

Since $\alpha$ is algebraic over $F$, $[F(\alpha):F]<\infty$.
Since $\beta$ is algebraic over $F\subseteq F(\alpha)$, $[F(\alpha,\beta):F(\alpha)]<\infty$.
By the tower law, $[F(\alpha,\beta):F]=[F(\alpha,\beta):F(\alpha)]\,[F(\alpha):F]<\infty$.
Every element of a finite extension is algebraic [@DF04], and $\alpha+\beta\in F(\alpha,\beta)$.

Let $\gamma=\sqrt5+2\sqrt7$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

$\gamma$ is a root of $f(x)=x^4-66x^2+529$.

::: pf-proof

$\gamma^2=5+4\sqrt{35}+28=33+4\sqrt{35}$, so $(\gamma^2-33)^2=16\cdot35=560$, which expands to $\gamma^4-66\gamma^2+529=0$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

$\QQ(\gamma)=\QQ(\sqrt5,\sqrt7)$, a field of degree $4$ over $\QQ$.

::: pf-proof

The field $\QQ(\gamma)$ contains $\sqrt{35}=(\gamma^2-33)/4$ and hence $\sqrt{35}\,\gamma=14\sqrt5+5\sqrt7$.
The linear system $\sqrt5+2\sqrt7=\gamma$, $14\sqrt5+5\sqrt7=\sqrt{35}\,\gamma$ has determinant $5-28=-23\ne0$, so $\sqrt5,\sqrt7\in\QQ(\gamma)$; the reverse inclusion is clear.
Finally $[\QQ(\sqrt5):\QQ]=2$, and $\sqrt7\notin\QQ(\sqrt5)$: if $(a+b\sqrt5)^2=7$ with $a,b\in\QQ$, then $ab=0$, and neither $a^2=7$ nor $5b^2=7$ has a rational solution. Thus $[\QQ(\sqrt5,\sqrt7):\QQ]=4$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s4}

The minimal polynomial of $\gamma$ over $\QQ$ is $\boxed{x^4-66x^2+529}$.

::: pf-proof

By step [](#s3){.pf-ref} the minimal polynomial has degree $4$, and by step [](#s2){.pf-ref} it divides the monic quartic $f$, so it equals $f$.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1){.pf-ref} answers part (a) and step [](#s4){.pf-ref} answers part (b).
