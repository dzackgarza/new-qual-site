:::{.problem}
Let $f=u+iv$ be analytic on a connected open set $D\subset\mathbb C$. Suppose there are real constants $a,b,c$ with
\[
a^2+b^2\ne0
\]
and
\[
au+bv=c
\]
throughout $D$. Prove that $f$ is constant on $D$.
:::
