:::{.problem}
Let $A,B$ be real $n\times n$ matrices. Suppose
\[
A^T=A,
\qquad
v^TAv\ge0\quad\text{for every }v\in\mathbb R^n,
\]
and
\[
AB+BA=0.
\]
Show that
\[
AB=BA=0.
\]
Give an example in which neither $A$ nor $B$ is zero.
:::
