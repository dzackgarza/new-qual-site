::: {.problem}
Identify the real vector space $M_{2\times2}(\mathbb R)$ with $\mathbb R^4$ and define
\[
F:M_{2\times2}(\mathbb R)\to M_{2\times2}(\mathbb R),
\qquad
F(X)=X+X^2.
\]
Prove that the range of $F$ contains a neighborhood of the zero matrix.
:::
