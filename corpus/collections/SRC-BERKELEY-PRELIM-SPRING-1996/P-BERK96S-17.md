::: {.problem}
Let $G$ be the group of $2\times2$ determinant-one matrices over the four-element field $\mathbb F_4$, and let $S$ be the set of one-dimensional subspaces of $\mathbb F_4^2$. Show that the natural action of $G$ on $S$ is faithful.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let
$$
e_1=(1,0)^T,
\qquad
e_2=(0,1)^T
$$
be the standard basis of $\FF_4^2$.

<1>1. If $T\in G$ fixes every line in $S$, then
$$
T=\lambda I
$$
for some $\lambda\in\FF_4^\times$.

::: {.proof}
Since $T$ fixes the lines $\FF_4e_1$ and $\FF_4e_2$, there are
$a,b\in\FF_4^\times$ such that
$$
Te_1=ae_1,
\qquad
Te_2=be_2.
$$
The line
$$
\FF_4(e_1+e_2)
$$
is also fixed, so there is $c\in\FF_4^\times$ with
$$
T(e_1+e_2)=c(e_1+e_2).
$$
But linearity gives
$$
T(e_1+e_2)=ae_1+be_2.
$$
Since $e_1,e_2$ are linearly independent,
$$
a=b=c.
$$
Thus $T=aI$.
:::

<1>2. The only scalar matrix in $G$ is the identity.

::: {.proof}
Let $\lambda I\in G$. Since its determinant is $1$,
$$
\lambda^2=1.
$$
The field $\FF_4$ has characteristic $2$, so
$$
(\lambda-1)^2
=
\lambda^2-2\lambda+1
=
0.
$$
A field has no nonzero nilpotents, hence
$$
\lambda-1=0.
$$
Therefore $\lambda=1$ and $\lambda I=I$.
:::

<1>3. The kernel of the action of $G$ on $S$ is trivial.

::: {.proof}
An element of the kernel fixes every line in $S$. By step <1>1 it is scalar,
and by step <1>2 it is the identity. Hence the kernel consists only of $I$.
:::

<1>4. The natural action of $G$ on $S$ is faithful.

::: {.proof}
An action is faithful exactly when its kernel is trivial. This is step
<1>3.
:::

<1>5. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>4 is the required conclusion.
:::
:::
