::: {.problem}
Let $\varphi\in C^1(\mathbb R)$ and suppose
\[
\varphi(x)\to a,
\qquad
\varphi'(x)\to b
\qquad(x\to\infty).
\]
Prove or give a counterexample: must $b=0$?
:::
