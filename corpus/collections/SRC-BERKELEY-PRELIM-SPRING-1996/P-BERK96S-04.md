:::{.problem}
Let $r<1<R$. Show that for all sufficiently small $\varepsilon>0$, the polynomial
\[
p(z)=\varepsilon z^7+z^2+1
\]
has exactly five roots, counted with multiplicity, in the annulus
\[
r\varepsilon^{-1/5}<|z|<R\varepsilon^{-1/5}.
\]
:::
