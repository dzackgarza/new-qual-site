::: problem
Let $A = \begin{pmatrix} 7 & -3 \\ 1 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$.
Find an invertible matrix $P$ and a diagonal matrix $D$ with $P^{-1}AP = D$.
[You should not have to compute $P^{-1}$.]
:::
