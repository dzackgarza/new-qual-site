::: {.problem}
How many roots does the equation
\[
z^7-4z^3+z-1=0
\]
have in the open disk $|z|<1$?
:::

::: {.solution}
On the unit circle,
\[
|z^7+z-1|\le |z^7|+|z|+1=3<4=|-4z^3|.
\]
Hence, by Rouché's theorem,
\[
z^7-4z^3+z-1
\]
and $-4z^3$ have the same number of zeros in $|z|<1$, counted with
multiplicity. The latter has exactly three zeros there. Therefore the given
polynomial has
\[
\boxed{3}
\]
zeros in the open unit disk, counted with multiplicity.
:::
