::: {.problem}
Let $1<p,q<\infty$ with $1/p+1/q=1$.
Let $f\in L^p(\mathbb R^3)$ and $g\in L^q(\mathbb R^3)$.
Show that:

(a) $f*g$ is continuous on $\mathbb R^3$;

(b) $(f*g)(x)\to0$ as $|x|\to\infty$.
:::
