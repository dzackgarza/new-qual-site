::: {.problem}
Let $f:\mathbb C\to\mathbb C$ be holomorphic with $f(z)\ne0$ for every $z\in\mathbb C$.
Define
\[
U=\{z\in\mathbb C:|f(z)|<1\}.
\]
Show that every connected component of $U$ is unbounded.
:::
