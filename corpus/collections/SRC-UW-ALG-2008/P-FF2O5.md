::: problem
- Let $R$ be a commutative principal ideal domain.
  Show that any $R$-module $M$ generated by two elements takes the form $R/(a)\oplus R/(b)$ for some $a,b\in R$.
  What more can you say about $a$ and $b$?

- Give a necessary and sufficient condition for two direct sums as in part (a) to be isomorphic as $R$-modules.
:::
