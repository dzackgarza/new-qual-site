::: problem
Assume that $f_n$ is a sequence of measurable functions on $(X, \mathcal{M}, \mu)$.
Assume that there exists an integrable function $F$ such that $|f_n| \leq F$ $\mu$-a.e., and $f_n \to f$ $\mu$-a.e. Show that $f_n \to f$ in measure.
:::
