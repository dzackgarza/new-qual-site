::: {.problem}
Let $A,B$ be real $2\times2$ matrices satisfying
\[
A^2=B^2=I,
\qquad
AB+BA=0.
\]
Show that there exists a real invertible $2\times2$ matrix $T$ such that
\[
TAT^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
TBT^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]
:::
