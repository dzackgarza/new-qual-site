::: {.problem}
Let $M$ be the space of real-valued functions $f$ on $[0,1]$ for which $f$ and $f'$ are continuous, with norm
\[
\|f\|=\sup_{0\le x\le1}|f(x)|+\sup_{0\le x\le1}|f'(x)|.
\]
Which subsets of $M$ are compact?
:::
