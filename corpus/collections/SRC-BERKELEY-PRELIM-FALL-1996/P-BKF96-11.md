::: {.problem}
Let $f$ be continuous and nonnegative on $[0,1]$, and suppose
\[
f(t)^2\le 1+2\int_0^t f(s)\,ds.
\]
Prove that
\[
f(t)\le1+t
\qquad(0\le t\le1).
\]
:::
