::: {.problem}
For positive integers $a,b,c$, prove that
\[
\gcd\bigl(a,\operatorname{lcm}(b,c)\bigr)
=
\operatorname{lcm}\bigl(\gcd(a,b),\gcd(a,c)\bigr).
\]
:::
