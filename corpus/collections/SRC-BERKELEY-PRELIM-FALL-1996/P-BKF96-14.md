::: {.problem}
Let $f$ be holomorphic on a neighborhood of the closed unit disk, and let $L$ be the length of the image of the unit circle under $f$. Show that
\[
L\ge2\pi|f'(0)|.
\]
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. The length $L$ is
$$
L=\int_0^{2\pi}\abs{f'(e^{it})}\,dt.
$$

::: {.proof}
Parametrize the unit circle by
$$
\gamma(t)=e^{it},
\qquad
0\le t\le2\pi.
$$
The image curve is $f\circ\gamma$, whose derivative is
$$
(f\circ\gamma)'(t)
=
ie^{it}f'(e^{it}).
$$
Since $\abs{ie^{it}}=1$, its speed is $\abs{f'(e^{it})}$. The
arc-length formula therefore gives the displayed identity.
:::

<1>2. One has
$$
2\pi f'(0)
=
\int_0^{2\pi} f'(e^{it})\,dt.
$$

::: {.proof}
Since $f$ is holomorphic on a neighborhood of the closed unit disk,
$f'$ is holomorphic there as well. Cauchy's integral formula applied
to $f'$ at $0$ gives
$$
f'(0)
=
\frac{1}{2\pi i}
\int_{\abs{z}=1}\frac{f'(z)}{z}\,dz.
$$
With $z=e^{it}$ and $dz=ie^{it}\,dt$, this becomes
$$
f'(0)
=
\frac{1}{2\pi}
\int_0^{2\pi} f'(e^{it})\,dt,
$$
which is the claimed identity.
:::

<1>3. The required estimate is
$$
\boxed{L\ge2\pi\abs{f'(0)}}.
$$

::: {.proof}
By step <1>2 and the triangle inequality,
$$
2\pi\abs{f'(0)}
=
\abs{\int_0^{2\pi}f'(e^{it})\,dt}
\le
\int_0^{2\pi}\abs{f'(e^{it})}\,dt.
$$
The right-hand side is $L$ by step <1>1.
:::

<1>4. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>3 is the required inequality.
:::
