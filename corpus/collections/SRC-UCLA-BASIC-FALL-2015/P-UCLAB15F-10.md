::: {.problem}
Let $F,G:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^n$ be linear operators, with
\[
\exp(F)=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac1{k!}F^k.
\]

1. Prove that if $FG=GF$, then
\[
\exp(F+G)=\exp(F)\exp(G).
\]

2. Give an example of non-commuting linear operators for which this equality fails.
:::
