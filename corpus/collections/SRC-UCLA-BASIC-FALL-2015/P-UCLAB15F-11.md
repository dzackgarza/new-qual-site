::: {.problem}
Let $T:V\to V$ be linear with
\[
T^{18}=0,\qquad T^5\ne0.
\]
Suppose $V\cong\mathbb R^6$. Prove that there is no linear operator $S:V\to V$ such that $S^2=T$.

Does the answer change if $V\cong\mathbb R^{12}$?
:::
