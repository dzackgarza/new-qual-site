::: {.problem}
Prove that the $n\times n$ matrix
\[
M=\begin{pmatrix}
2&1&1&\cdots&1\\
1&3&1&\cdots&1\\
1&1&4&\cdots&1\\
\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\ddots&\vdots\\
1&1&1&\cdots&n+1
\end{pmatrix}
\]
is positive definite.
:::
