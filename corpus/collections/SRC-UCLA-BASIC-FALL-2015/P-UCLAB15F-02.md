::: {.problem}
Let $a,b\in\mathbb R$ with $a<b$.
Show that if $g,h:[a,b]\to\mathbb R$ are continuous and $h\ge0$, then there is $c\in[a,b]$ such that
\[
\int_a^b g(x)h(x)\,dx=g(c)\int_a^b h(x)\,dx.
\]
:::
