::: {.problem}
Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional real vector space and let $W\subset V$ be a subspace.
Define
\[
W^0=\{f:V\to\mathbb R\text{ linear}:f|_W=0\}.
\]
Prove that
\[
\dim V=\dim W+\dim W^0.
\]
:::
