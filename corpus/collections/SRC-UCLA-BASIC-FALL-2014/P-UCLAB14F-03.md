Problem 3. Let $f_n:[0,1]\to\mathbb R$ be monotonically increasing continuous functions. Assume that $f_n$ converge pointwise to a continuous function $f:[0,1]\to\mathbb R$. Prove that $f_n$ converge uniformly to $f$.
