::: {.problem}
Problem 8. Compute the eigenvalues of the following $n\times n$ matrix:
\[
M=
\begin{pmatrix}
k&1&1&\cdots&1\\
1&k&1&\cdots&1\\
1&1&k&\cdots&1\\
\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\ddots&\vdots\\
1&1&1&\cdots&k
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Use the eigenvalues to compute $\det(M)$.
:::
