::: {.problem}
Problem 11. Suppose a $4\times4$ integer matrix has four distinct real eigenvalues
\[
\lambda_1>\lambda_2>\lambda_3>\lambda_4.
\]
Prove that
\[
\lambda_1^2+\lambda_2^2+\lambda_3^2+\lambda_4^2\in\mathbb Z.
\]
:::
