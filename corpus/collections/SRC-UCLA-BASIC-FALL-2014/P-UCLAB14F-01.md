::: {.problem}
Problem 1. Show that the function
\[
H(x,y)=x^2+y^2+|x-y|^{-1}
\]
achieves its global minimum somewhere on the set
\[
\{(x,y)\in\mathbb R^2:x\neq y\}.
\]
:::
