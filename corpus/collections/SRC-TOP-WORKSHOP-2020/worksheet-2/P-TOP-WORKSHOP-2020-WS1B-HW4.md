::: {.problem}
Give 4--6 different topologies on $\mathbb R$.
:::

::: solution
**Goal:** List six distinct topologies on $\mathbb R$.

<1> Give examples.
    *Proof:*
    <2>1. Standard topology $\tau_{\mathrm{std}}$ (Euclidean intervals).
    <2>2. Discrete topology $\tau_{\mathrm{disc}}=\mathcal P(\mathbb R)$.
    <2>3. Trivial topology $\tau_{\mathrm{triv}}=\{\emptyset,\mathbb R\}$.
    <2>4. Cofinite topology $\tau_{\mathrm{cof}}=\{\emptyset\}\cup\{U:\mathbb R\setminus U\text{ finite}\}$.
    <2>5. Countable-co-countable topology $\tau_{\mathrm{cc}}=\{\emptyset\}\cup\{U:\mathbb R\setminus U\text{ countable}\}\cup\{\mathbb R\}$.
    <2>6. Lower-limit (Sorgenfrey) topology $\tau_{\mathrm{S}}$ generated by rays $[a,b)$.

<1> Verify they are all different.
    *Proof:*
    <2>1. $\tau_{\mathrm{triv}}\neq\tau_{\mathrm{disc}}$ since $\{0\}\in\tau_{\mathrm{disc}}$ but $\{0\}\notin\tau_{\mathrm{triv}}$.
    <2>2. $\tau_{\mathrm{std}}\neq\tau_{\mathrm{triv}}$ because $\{0\}\subsetneq \mathbb R$ and $(0,1)\in\tau_{\mathrm{std}}$ are nontrivial opens.
    <2>3. $\tau_{\mathrm{cof}}\neq\tau_{\mathrm{std}}$ since $\{0\}$ is open in $\tau_{\mathrm{cof}}$ but not in $\tau_{\mathrm{std}}$.
    <2>4. $\tau_{\mathrm{cof}}\neq\tau_{\mathrm{cc}}$ since $\mathbb R\setminus\{0\}\in\tau_{\mathrm{cc}}$ but $\mathbb R\setminus\{0\}\notin\tau_{\mathrm{cof}}$.
    <2>5. $\tau_{\mathrm{cc}}\neq\tau_{\mathrm{S}}$ since $[0,1)\in\tau_{\mathrm{S}}$ and the complement $\mathbb R\setminus[0,1)$ is uncountable, so $[0,1)\notin\tau_{\mathrm{cc}}$.
    <2>6. $\tau_{\mathrm{S}}\neq\tau_{\mathrm{std}}$ since $[0,1)\in\tau_{\mathrm{S}}$ while $[0,1)\notin\tau_{\mathrm{std}}$.

:::
