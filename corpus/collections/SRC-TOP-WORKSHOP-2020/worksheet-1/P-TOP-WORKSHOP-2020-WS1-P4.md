::: {.problem}
(May 2016) Suppose that the space $X$ has the fixed point property (that is, for any continuous function $f\colon X\to X$ there is a point $p\in X$ with $f(p)=p$).
Suppose also that $A\subset X$ is a subspace admitting a retraction $r\colon X\to A$.
Show that $A$ also has the fixed point property.
:::
