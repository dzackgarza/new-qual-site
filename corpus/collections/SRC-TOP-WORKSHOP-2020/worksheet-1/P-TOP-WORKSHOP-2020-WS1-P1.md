::: {.problem}
(May 2016) Given any topological space $Z$ and subset $D\subseteq Z$, let $\operatorname{Cl}_Z(D)$ denote the closure of $D$ in $Z$.
Show that if $X$ and $Y$ are topological spaces and $A\subseteq X$, $B\subseteq Y$, then
$$\operatorname{Cl}_{X\times Y}(A\times B)=\operatorname{Cl}_X(A)\times\operatorname{Cl}_Y(B).$$
:::
