::: {.problem}
(May 2016) Let $X$ be a connected space and $A,B\subseteq X$ be closed subsets of $X$ with $X=A\cup B$ and $A\cap B$ a connected subset of $X$.
Show that both $A$ and $B$ are connected.
:::
