::: {.problem}
Let $a_n>0$ and define
\[
P_n=\prod_{j=1}^n(1+a_j).
\]
Prove that $\lim_{n\to\infty}P_n$ exists and is a nonzero real number if and only if
\[
\sum_n a_n<\infty.
\]
:::
