::: {.problem}
Let $X$ be a compact metric space, let $(x_n)$ be a sequence in $X$, and let $x\in X$. Assume that every subsequence $(y_n)$ of $(x_n)$ has a subsequence $(z_n)$ converging to $x$. Prove that $x_n\to x$.
:::
