::: {.problem}
If $U , ~ V , ~ W$ are subspaces of a vector space such that any two have intersection zero, prove that

$$
\dim ( U + V + W ) + \dim U + \dim V + \dim W \leq \dim ( U + V ) + \dim ( V + W ) + \dim ( W + U )
$$

and give an example where equality does not hold.
:::
