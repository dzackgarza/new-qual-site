::: {.problem}
Let $I _ { n }$ denote the $n \times n$ identity matrix, and $J _ { n }$ the $n \times n$ matrix with all entries equal to 1.\
Determine for which real numbers a the matrix $I _ { n } + a J _ { n }$ is invertible, and find its inverse.
:::
