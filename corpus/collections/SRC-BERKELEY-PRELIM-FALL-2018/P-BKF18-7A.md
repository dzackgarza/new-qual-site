::: problem
Suppose $A=(a_{ij})$ is a real symmetric $n\times n$ matrix with nonnegative eigenvalues. Show that
\[
|a_{ij}|\le \sqrt{a_{ii}a_{jj}}
\]
for all distinct $i,j\le n$.
:::
