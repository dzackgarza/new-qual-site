::: problem
Let $a\in\mathbb R$ with $|a|<1$. Prove that
\[
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}a^k\cos(k\theta)
=\frac{-a^2+a\cos\theta}{1+a^2-2a\cos\theta}.
\]
:::
