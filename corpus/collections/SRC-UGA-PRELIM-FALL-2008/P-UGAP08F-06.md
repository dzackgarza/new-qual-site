::: {.problem}
Suppose $f:[0,1]\to\mathbb R$ is continuous, $f(x)\ge0$ for all $x\in[0,1]$, and
\[
f(1/2)>0.
\]
Prove that
\[
\int_0^1 f(x)\,dx>0.
\]
Give an example showing that the conclusion need not hold when $f$ is not continuous.
:::
