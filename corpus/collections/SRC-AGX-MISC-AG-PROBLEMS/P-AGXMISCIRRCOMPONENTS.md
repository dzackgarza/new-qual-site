::: {.problem}
Find the irreducible components of the following algebraic sets over $\CC$.

a. $V\big(y^4 - x^2,\ y^4 - x^2 y^2 + x y^2 - x^3\big) \subseteq \AA^2$.

b. $V\big(y^2 - xz,\ z^2 - y^3\big) \subseteq \AA^3$.
:::
