::: {.problem}
Let $X$ be an integral scheme.
Prove that every nonzero morphism of invertible sheaves on $X$ is injective, and that every generically injective morphism of locally free sheaves on $X$ is injective.
:::

::: {.hint}
First prove that a locally free sheaf on $X$ has no nonzero torsion subsheaf, where a torsion sheaf is one whose support has positive codimension.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $\eta$ be the generic point of the integral scheme $X$ and put
$$
K=K(X)=\OO_{X,\eta}.
$$

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #torsion-subsheaf-vanishes}
If $\mce$ is locally free and $\mct\subseteq\mce$ satisfies
$$
\mct_\eta=0,
$$
then $\mct=0$.

::: pf-proof
It suffices to work on an affine open
$$
U=\Spec A
$$
on which
$$
\restrictionof{\mce}{U}\cong\widetilde{A^r}.
$$
Because $X$ is integral, $A$ is a domain and its fraction field is $K$.

Let
$$
s\in\mct(U)\subseteq A^r.
$$
The condition $\mct_\eta=0$ says that the image of $s$ in
$$
A^r\otimes_AK\cong K^r
$$
is zero.
By the definition of localization, there is a nonzero
$$
a\in A
$$
such that
$$
as=0
$$
in $A^r$.
Since $A$ is a domain, the free module $A^r$ is torsion-free, so $s=0$.

Thus $\mct(U)=0$ on every such trivializing affine open.
These opens cover $X$, hence $\mct=0$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #generically-injective-implies-injective}
Every generically injective morphism of locally free sheaves
$$
\varphi:\mce\longrightarrow\mcf
$$
is injective.

::: pf-proof
Let
$$
\mck=\ker\varphi.
$$
Generic injectivity means precisely that
$$
\varphi_\eta:\mce_\eta\longrightarrow\mcf_\eta
$$
is injective, so
$$
\mck_\eta=0.
$$
Since $\mck\subseteq\mce$ and $\mce$ is locally free, step [](#torsion-subsheaf-vanishes){.pf-ref} gives
$$
\mck=0.
$$
Therefore $\varphi$ is injective.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #nonzero-implies-nonzero-generic-stalk}
A nonzero morphism of invertible sheaves is nonzero at the generic point.

::: pf-proof
Let
$$
\varphi:\mcl\longrightarrow\mcm
$$
be a morphism of invertible sheaves.
Such a morphism is equivalently a global section
$$
s_\varphi\in
H^0\bigl(X,\mcm\tensor\dualof{\mcl}\bigr),
$$
where $\mcm\tensor\dualof{\mcl}$ is again invertible.

Suppose $\varphi_\eta=0$.
Then the corresponding section has zero germ at $\eta$.
The image subsheaf generated by $s_\varphi$ is a subsheaf of the locally free sheaf $\mcm\tensor\dualof{\mcl}$ with zero generic stalk, so step [](#torsion-subsheaf-vanishes){.pf-ref} implies that this image subsheaf is zero.
Hence
$$
s_\varphi=0
$$
and therefore $\varphi=0$.

Thus a nonzero $\varphi$ must satisfy
$$
\varphi_\eta\ne0.
$$
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #nonzero-invertible-morphism-injective}
Every nonzero morphism of invertible sheaves on $X$ is injective.

::: pf-proof
At the generic point, $\mcl_\eta$ and $\mcm_\eta$ are one-dimensional $K$-vector spaces.
By step [](#nonzero-implies-nonzero-generic-stalk){.pf-ref}, a nonzero morphism
$$
\varphi:\mcl\longrightarrow\mcm
$$
induces a nonzero linear map
$$
\varphi_\eta:\mcl_\eta\longrightarrow\mcm_\eta.
$$
A nonzero map between one-dimensional vector spaces is injective.
Therefore $\varphi$ is generically injective, and step [](#generically-injective-implies-injective){.pf-ref} gives
$$
\boxed{\varphi\text{ is injective}.}
$$
:::

:::

::: pf-qed
Step [](#torsion-subsheaf-vanishes){.pf-ref} shows that a subsheaf of a locally free sheaf with zero generic stalk is zero.
Step [](#generically-injective-implies-injective){.pf-ref} proves the locally free assertion, and steps [](#nonzero-implies-nonzero-generic-stalk){.pf-ref} and [](#nonzero-invertible-morphism-injective){.pf-ref} prove the invertible-sheaf assertion.
:::
