::: {.problem}
Prove that the cuspidal cubic $Y \subseteq \AA^2_\CC$ with equation $x^3 - y^2 = 0$ is irreducible.
:::

::: {.hint}
Express $Y$ as the image of $\AA^1$ under a continuous map.
:::
