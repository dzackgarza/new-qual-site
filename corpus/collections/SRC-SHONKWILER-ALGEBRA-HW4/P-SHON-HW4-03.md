::: {.problem}
Prove that subgroups and quotient groups of a solvable group are solvable.
:::

::: {.solution}
Recall the derived series
\[
G^{(0)}=G,
\qquad
G^{(m+1)}=[G^{(m)},G^{(m)}].
\]
A group \(G\) is solvable if
\[
G^{(r)}=1
\]
for some \(r\).

## Subgroups

Let
\[
H\le G.
\]

<1>1. One has
\[
\boxed{H^{(0)}\le G^{(0)}}.
\]

::: {.proof}
This is exactly the inclusion \(H\le G\).
:::

<1>2. If
\[
H^{(m)}\le G^{(m)},
\]
then
\[
\boxed{
H^{(m+1)}\le G^{(m+1)}}.
\]

::: {.proof}
Every commutator of elements of \(H^{(m)}\) is also a commutator of elements of \(G^{(m)}\). Hence
\[
[H^{(m)},H^{(m)}]
\le
[G^{(m)},G^{(m)}].
\]
:::

<1>3. Therefore, for every \(m\ge0\),
\[
\boxed{
H^{(m)}\le G^{(m)}}.
\]

::: {.proof}
Apply induction using steps <1>1--<1>2.
:::

<1>4. If \(G\) is solvable, choose \(r\) with
\[
G^{(r)}=1.
\]
Then
\[
\boxed{H^{(r)}=1}.
\]

::: {.proof}
By step <1>3,
\[
H^{(r)}\le G^{(r)}=1.
\]
:::

<1>5. Hence every subgroup of a solvable group is solvable.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>4 shows the derived series of \(H\) terminates.
:::

## Quotients

Let
\[
N\triangleleft G.
\]

<1>6. The commutator subgroup of \(G/N\) is
\[
\boxed{
(G/N)'=G'N/N}.
\]

::: {.proof}
For \(g,h\in G\),
\[
[gN,hN]=[g,h]N.
\]
Thus the subgroup generated by all quotient commutators is exactly the image of \(G'\) in \(G/N\), namely
\[
G'N/N.
\]
:::

<1>7. More generally,
\[
\boxed{
(G/N)^{(m)}
=
G^{(m)}N/N}
\]
for every \(m\ge0\).

::: {.proof}
Use induction.

For \(m=0\),
\[
G^{(0)}N/N
=
GN/N
=
G/N.
\]

Assume the formula for \(m\). Then
\[
\begin{aligned}
(G/N)^{(m+1)}
&=
[(G/N)^{(m)},(G/N)^{(m)}]\\
&=
[G^{(m)}N/N,\ G^{(m)}N/N]\\
&=
G^{(m+1)}N/N,
\end{aligned}
\]
by the quotient commutator formula from step <1>6 applied to the subgroup image.
:::

<1>8. If \(G\) is solvable and
\[
G^{(r)}=1,
\]
then
\[
\boxed{
(G/N)^{(r)}=1}.
\]

::: {.proof}
By step <1>7,
\[
(G/N)^{(r)}
=
G^{(r)}N/N
=
N/N
=
1.
\]
:::

<1>9. Hence every quotient group of a solvable group is solvable.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>8 shows the quotient derived series terminates.
:::

<1>10. Therefore
\[
\boxed{
\text{subgroups and quotient groups of a solvable group are solvable}.}
\]

::: {.proof}
The subgroup statement is step <1>5 and the quotient statement is step <1>9.
:::

<1>11. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>10 is the required conclusion.
:::
:::
