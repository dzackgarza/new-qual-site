::: {.problem}
Prove that subgroups and quotient groups of a solvable group are solvable.
:::

::: {.solution}

Recall the derived series
$$
G^{(0)}=G,
\qquad
G^{(m+1)}=[G^{(m)},G^{(m)}].
$$
A group $G$ is solvable if
$$
G^{(r)}=1
$$
for some $r$.

## Subgroups

Let
$$
H\le G.
$$

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

One has
$$
\boxed{H^{(0)}\le G^{(0)}}.
$$


::: pf-proof

This is exactly the inclusion $H\le G$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

If
$$
H^{(m)}\le G^{(m)},
$$
then
$$
\boxed{
H^{(m+1)}\le G^{(m+1)}}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Every commutator of elements of $H^{(m)}$ is also a commutator of elements of $G^{(m)}$. Hence
$$
[H^{(m)},H^{(m)}]
\le
[G^{(m)},G^{(m)}].
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

Therefore, for every $m\ge0$,
$$
\boxed{
H^{(m)}\le G^{(m)}}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Apply induction using steps [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} through [](#s1-2){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

If $G$ is solvable, choose $r$ with
$$
G^{(r)}=1.
$$
Then
$$
\boxed{H^{(r)}=1}.
$$


::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref},
$$
H^{(r)}\le G^{(r)}=1.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-5}

Hence every subgroup of a solvable group is solvable.


::: pf-proof

Step [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} shows the derived series of $H$ terminates.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-6}

The commutator subgroup of $G/N$ is
$$
\boxed{
(G/N)'=G'N/N}.
$$


::: pf-proof

For $g,h\in G$,
$$
[gN,hN]=[g,h]N.
$$
Thus the subgroup generated by all quotient commutators is exactly the image of $G'$ in $G/N$, namely
$$
G'N/N.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-7}

More generally,
$$
\boxed{
(G/N)^{(m)}
=
G^{(m)}N/N}
$$
for every $m\ge0$.


::: pf-proof

Use induction.

For $m=0$,
$$
G^{(0)}N/N
=
GN/N
=
G/N.
$$

Assume the formula for $m$. Then
$$
\begin{aligned}
(G/N)^{(m+1)}
&=
[(G/N)^{(m)},(G/N)^{(m)}]\\
&=
[G^{(m)}N/N,\ G^{(m)}N/N]\\
&=
G^{(m+1)}N/N,
\end{aligned}
$$
by the quotient commutator formula from step [](#s1-6){.pf-ref} applied to the subgroup image.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-8}

If $G$ is solvable and
$$
G^{(r)}=1,
$$
then
$$
\boxed{
(G/N)^{(r)}=1}.
$$


::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1-7){.pf-ref},
$$
(G/N)^{(r)}
=
G^{(r)}N/N
=
N/N
=
1.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-9}

Hence every quotient group of a solvable group is solvable.


::: pf-proof

Step [](#s1-8){.pf-ref} shows the quotient derived series terminates.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-10}

Therefore
$$
\boxed{
\text{subgroups and quotient groups of a solvable group are solvable}.}
$$


::: pf-proof

The subgroup statement is step [](#s1-5){.pf-ref} and the quotient statement is step [](#s1-9){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-10){.pf-ref} is the required conclusion.

:::
