::: {.problem}
Let $N\triangleleft G$ be a normal subgroup of a finite group $G$.
Assume
\[
\gcd(|N|,[G:N])=1.
\]
Prove that $N$ is the unique subgroup of $G$ of order $|N|$.
:::
