::: {.problem}
Let $G^{\mathrm{op}}$ be the group with the same underlying set as $G$ and multiplication
\[
\mu^{\prime}(x,y)=\mu_G(y,x).
\]
Prove that $G^{\mathrm{op}}\cong G$.
:::
