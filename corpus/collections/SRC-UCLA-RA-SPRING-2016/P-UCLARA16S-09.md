::: {.problem}
Let $f:\mathbb C\to\mathbb C$ be entire and assume that $|f(z)|=1$ when $|z|=1$. Show that
\[
f(z)=Cz^m
\]
for some integer $m\ge0$ and $C\in\mathbb C$ with $|C|=1$.
:::
