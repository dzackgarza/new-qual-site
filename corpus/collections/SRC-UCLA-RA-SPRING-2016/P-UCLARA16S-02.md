::: {.problem}
Let $f\in L^1(\mathbb R)$. Show that the series
\[
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac1{\sqrt n}f(x-\sqrt n)
\]
converges absolutely for almost all $x\in\mathbb R$.
:::
