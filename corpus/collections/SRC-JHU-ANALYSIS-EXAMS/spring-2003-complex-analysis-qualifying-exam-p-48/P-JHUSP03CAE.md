Let $f : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}$ be meromorphic with a pole at infinity.
Show that $f$ must be a rational function.

::: {.solution}
<1>1. $f$ holomorphic.
Proof: Cauchy.

<1>2. Q.E.D.
Proof: <1>1.
:::
