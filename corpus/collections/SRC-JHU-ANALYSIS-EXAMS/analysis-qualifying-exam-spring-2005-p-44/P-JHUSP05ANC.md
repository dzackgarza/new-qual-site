Prove or find a counterexample to each of the following statements:

a) $L^{2}(\mathbb{R}) \subset L^{1}(\mathbb{R})$

b) $L^{1}(\mathbb{R}) \subset L^{2}(\mathbb{R})$

c) $L^{2}([0,1]) \subset L^{1}([0,1])$

d) $L^{1}([0,1]) \subset L^{2}([0,1])$
