Suppose that $f \in L^{1}[0,1]$ and let

$$
g(x) = \int_{x}^{1} \frac{f(t)}{t} \, dt.
$$

Show that $g \in L^{1}[0,1]$ and that

$$
\int_{0}^{1} g(x)\, dx = \int_{0}^{1} f(x)\, dx.
$$
