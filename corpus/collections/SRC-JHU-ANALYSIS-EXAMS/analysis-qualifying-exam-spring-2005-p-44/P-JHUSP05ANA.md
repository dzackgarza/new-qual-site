Notation: $\mathcal{C}^{\infty}(\mathbb{R})$ denotes the complex-valued $\mathcal{C}^{\infty}$ functions on $\mathbb{R}$, and $\widehat{f}$ denotes the Fourier transform of $f$.

Let $f(x) = e^{-|x|}$ for $x \in \mathbb{R}$.

a) Is $\widehat{f} \in \mathcal{C}^{\infty}(\mathbb{R})$?
Prove that your answer is correct.

b) Show that $|\widehat{f}(\xi)| \to 0$ as $|\xi| \to \infty$.
