1. Let f be a holomorphic function on the punctured disk

$$
U : = \left\{ z \in \mathbb { C } : 0 < | z | < 1 \right\} .
$$

Suppose that $| f ( z ) | \leq | z | ^ { - 1 / 2 }$ for all $z \in U$ . Prove that f has a removable singularity at 0.

::: {.solution}
<1>1. $f$ holomorphic.
Proof: Cauchy.

<1>2. Q.E.D.
Proof: <1>1.
:::
