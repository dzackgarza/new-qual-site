::: problem
Consider the set of positive continuous periodic functions $f$ on $[0, 2\pi]$ satisfying $\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi} f\,d\theta = 1$.
What is the largest possible value of $\exp\left(\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi} \log f\,d\theta\right)$ for such functions?
Prove that your answer is correct.
:::
