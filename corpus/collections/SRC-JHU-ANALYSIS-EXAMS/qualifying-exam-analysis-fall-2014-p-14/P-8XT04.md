1. Let Q be the unit square in $\mathbb { R } ^ { 2 }$ . Consider functions $f _ { n } \in L ^ { 1 } ( Q )$ such that

$f _ { n } \to f$ almost everywhere in Q and $\int _ { Q } | f _ { n } | \to \int _ { Q } | f | < \infty .$

(a) Prove that $\textstyle \int _ { A } | f _ { n } | \to \int _ { A } | f |$ for every measurable subset A of $Q$

(b) Prove that $f _ { n } \to f$ in $L ^ { 1 }$

::: {.solution}
<1>1. Part (a): Convergence of integrals over any measurable subset $A \subseteq Q$:
<2>1. Let $A \subseteq Q$ be any Lebesgue measurable subset, and let $A^c = Q \setminus A$.
::: {.proof}
set decomposition $Q = A \sqcup A^c$.
:::
<2>2. Since $|f_n| \mathbf{1}_A \ge 0$ and $|f_n| \mathbf{1}_A \to |f| \mathbf{1}_A$ a.e., Fatou’s Lemma on $A$ gives:
\[
\int_A |f| \, dx = \int_Q \liminf_{n \to \infty} |f_n| \mathbf{1}_A \, dx \le \liminf_{n \to \infty} \int_A |f_n| \, dx.
\]
::: {.proof}
Fatou's Lemma applied to non-negative functions on $A$.
:::
<2>3. Symmetrically, applying Fatou’s Lemma to $|f_n| \mathbf{1}_{A^c}$ on $A^c$:
\[
\int_{A^c} |f| \, dx \le \liminf_{n \to \infty} \int_{A^c} |f_n| \, dx.
\]
::: {.proof}
Fatou's Lemma applied to non-negative functions on $A^c$.
:::
<2>4. Using the total convergence $\int_Q |f_n| \to \int_Q |f|$:
\[
\limsup_{n \to \infty} \int_A |f_n| \, dx = \limsup_{n \to \infty} \left( \int_Q |f_n| \, dx - \int_{A^c} |f_n| \, dx \right)
= \int_Q |f| \, dx - \liminf_{n \to \infty} \int_{A^c} |f_n| \, dx.
\]
::: {.proof}
$\limsup(a_n - b_n) = \lim a_n - \liminf b_n$.
:::
<2>5. Substituting the bound from <2>3 into <2>4:
\[
\limsup_{n \to \infty} \int_A |f_n| \, dx \le \int_Q |f| \, dx - \int_{A^c} |f| \, dx = \int_A |f| \, dx.
\]
::: {.proof}
<2>3 and additivity of integration over disjoint sets.
:::
<2>6. Combining <2>2 and <2>5:
\[
\int_A |f| \, dx \le \liminf_{n \to \infty} \int_A |f_n| \, dx \le \limsup_{n \to \infty} \int_A |f_n| \, dx \le \int_A |f| \, dx.
\]
Thus $\lim_{n \to \infty} \int_A |f_n| \, dx = \int_A |f| \, dx$.
::: {.proof}
squeeze principle for limit infimum and supremum.
:::

<1>2. Part (b): $L^1$-convergence $f_n \to f$:
<2>1. By the triangle inequality, $|f_n(x) - f(x)| \le |f_n(x)| + |f(x)|$, so the functions:
\[
g_n(x) = |f_n(x)| + |f(x)| - |f_n(x) - f(x)|
\]
are non-negative ($g_n \ge 0$) on $Q$.
::: {.proof}
triangle inequality for absolute values.
:::
<2>2. Since $f_n(x) \to f(x)$ a.e., we have $g_n(x) \to 2|f(x)|$ almost everywhere.
::: {.proof}
pointwise a.e. limit.
:::
<2>3. Applying Fatou’s Lemma to the non-negative sequence $\{g_n\}$:
\[
\int_Q 2|f| \, dx = \int_Q \liminf_{n \to \infty} g_n \, dx \le \liminf_{n \to \infty} \int_Q g_n \, dx.
\]
::: {.proof}
Fatou's Lemma.
:::
<2>4. Expanding the integral on the right-hand side using linearity:
\[
\int_Q g_n \, dx = \int_Q |f_n| \, dx + \int_Q |f| \, dx - \int_Q |f_n - f| \, dx.
\]
Since $\int_Q |f_n| \to \int_Q |f|$, taking the $\liminf$ yields:
\[
\liminf_{n \to \infty} \int_Q g_n \, dx = 2 \int_Q |f| \, dx - \limsup_{n \to \infty} \int_Q |f_n - f| \, dx.
\]
::: {.proof}
$\liminf(c_n - d_n) = \lim c_n - \limsup d_n$.
:::
<2>5. Comparing <2>3 and <2>4 and subtracting the finite quantity $2 \int_Q |f| \, dx < \infty$:
\[
2 \int_Q |f| \, dx \le 2 \int_Q |f| \, dx - \limsup_{n \to \infty} \int_Q |f_n - f| \, dx \implies \limsup_{n \to \infty} \int_Q |f_n - f| \, dx \le 0.
\]
::: {.proof}
algebraic cancellation of $2\int_Q |f|$.
:::
<2>6. Since $\int_Q |f_n - f| \, dx \ge 0$, it follows that:
\[
\lim_{n \to \infty} \|f_n - f\|_{L^1(Q)} = \lim_{n \to \infty} \int_Q |f_n - f| \, dx = 0.
\]
Thus $f_n \to f$ in $L^1(Q)$.
::: {.proof}
non-negativity and squeeze theorem.
:::

<1>3. Conclusion:
$\int_A |f_n| \to \int_A |f|$ for all measurable $A \subseteq Q$, and $f_n \to f$ in $L^1(Q)$. Q.E.D.
::: {.proof}
<1>1 and <1>2.
:::
:::
