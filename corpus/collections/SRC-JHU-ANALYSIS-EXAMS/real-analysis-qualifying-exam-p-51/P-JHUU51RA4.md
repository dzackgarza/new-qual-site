::: problem
Suppose that $f \in L^{1}(\mathbb{R}^n, m)$ satisfies

$$\left| \int_E f\, dm \right| \leq m(E)
$$

for all Lebesgue measurable sets $E$ (where $m$ denotes Lebesgue measure on $\mathbb{R}^n$). Prove that $|f| \leq 1$ almost everywhere.
:::
