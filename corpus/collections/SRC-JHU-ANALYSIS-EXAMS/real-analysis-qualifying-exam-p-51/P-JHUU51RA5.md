::: problem
Let $(X, \mathcal{M}, \mu)$ be a measure space, and let $f \in L^{1}(\mu) \cap L^{\infty}(\mu)$. Prove that

$$\lim_{p \to \infty} \|f\|_p = \|f\|_{\infty}.
$$
:::
