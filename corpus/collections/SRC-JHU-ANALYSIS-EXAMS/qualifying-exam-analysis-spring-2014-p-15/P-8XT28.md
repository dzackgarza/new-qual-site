Prove that smooth compactly supported functions are dense in $L^2(\mathbb{R}^n)$.

::: {.solution}
Let $f\in L^2(\mathbb R^n)$ and let $\varepsilon>0$.

First truncate both the domain and the size of $f$. Define
\[
f_N(x):=f(x)\mathbf1_{B(0,N)}(x)\mathbf1_{\{|f(x)|\le N\}}.
\]
Then
\[
|f-f_N|^2\to0
\]
pointwise and
\[
|f-f_N|^2\le |f|^2\in L^1.
\]
By dominated convergence,
\[
\|f-f_N\|_2\to0.
\]
Choose $N$ so large that
\[
\|f-f_N\|_2<\frac\varepsilon2.
\]
The function $f_N$ is bounded and compactly supported, hence belongs to both $L^1$ and $L^2$.

Now let $\rho\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb R^n)$ be a standard mollifier with $\rho\ge0$ and $\int\rho=1$, and set
\[
\rho_\delta(x)=\delta^{-n}\rho(x/\delta).
\]
Then
\[
\varphi_\delta:=f_N*\rho_\delta
\]
belongs to $C_c^\infty(\mathbb R^n)$. Moreover,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\|\varphi_\delta-f_N\|_2
&=\left\|\int \rho_\delta(y)
\bigl(f_N(\cdot-y)-f_N(\cdot)\bigr)\,dy\right\|_2\\
&\le \int \rho_\delta(y)
\|\tau_y f_N-f_N\|_2\,dy.
\end{aligned}
\]
Translations are continuous in $L^2$, so the last expression tends to $0$ as $\delta\downarrow0$. Choose $\delta$ so small that
\[
\|\varphi_\delta-f_N\|_2<\frac\varepsilon2.
\]

Therefore
\[
\|f-\varphi_\delta\|_2
\le \|f-f_N\|_2+\|f_N-\varphi_\delta\|_2
<\varepsilon.
\]
Thus $C_c^\infty(\mathbb R^n)$ is dense in $L^2(\mathbb R^n)$.
:::
