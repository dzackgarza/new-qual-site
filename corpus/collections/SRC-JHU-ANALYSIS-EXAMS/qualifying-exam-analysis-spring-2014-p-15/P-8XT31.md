Determine all entire functions $f$ (i.e., $f(z)$ is holomorphic on the whole $z$-plane) that satisfy the inequality

$$|f(z)| \leq |z|^2 |\mathrm{Im}\, z|^2$$

for $|z|$ sufficiently large.
