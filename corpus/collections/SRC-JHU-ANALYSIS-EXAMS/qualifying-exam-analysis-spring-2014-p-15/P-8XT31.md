Determine all entire functions $f$ (i.e., $f(z)$ is holomorphic on the whole $z$-plane) that satisfy the inequality

$$|f(z)| \leq |z|^2 |\mathrm{Im}\, z|^2$$

for $|z|$ sufficiently large.

::: solution
The only possibility is $\boxed{f\equiv0}$.

<1>1. The inequality forces zeros on a real interval.

::: proof
Choose $R>0$ so that the given bound holds whenever
$|z|>R$. For every real $x>R$,
$$
0\leq|f(x)|\leq|x|^2|\operatorname{Im}x|^2=0.
$$
Thus $f(x)=0$ for all such $x$. In particular the
distinct zeros $x_n=R+1+1/n$ accumulate at $R+1$,
a point of the domain $\mathbb C$.
:::

<1>2. The identity theorem and the converse finish the classification.

::: proof
The entire function $f$ has zeros accumulating inside
the connected plane, so the identity theorem gives
$f\equiv0$ [@SS03]. Conversely, the zero function is
entire and satisfies the given inequality at every
point, since its right-hand side is nonnegative.
:::
:::
