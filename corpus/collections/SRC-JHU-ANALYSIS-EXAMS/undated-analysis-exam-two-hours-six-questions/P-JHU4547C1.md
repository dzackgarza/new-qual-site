::: problem
Suppose that $f \in L^1(\mathbb{R})$.
Prove that given $\epsilon > 0$, there exists $\delta > 0$ so that
\[
\int_A |f| < \epsilon
\]
for every measurable set $A$ with $|A| < \delta$, where $|A|$ denotes the measure of $A$.
:::
