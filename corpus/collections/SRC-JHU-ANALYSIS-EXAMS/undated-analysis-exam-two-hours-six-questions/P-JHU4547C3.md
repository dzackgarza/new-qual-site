::: problem
Suppose that $1 < p < \infty$ and the linear mapping $T$ is defined by
\[
Tf(x) = x^{-1/p} \int_0^x f(t)\,dt.
\]
Show that $T$ is a bounded map from $L^q((0,\infty))$ to $C^0((0,\infty))$, where $q$ satisfies $1/p + 1/q = 1$.
:::
