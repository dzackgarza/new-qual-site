::: problem
Suppose that $f$ is holomorphic on the punctured disk $0 < |z| < 2$.

(A) Prove that if there is a real constant $C$ such that $|f(z)| \leq C$, then
\[
\int_{|z|<1} |f'(z)|^2\,dz < \infty.
\]

(B) What happens when $|f|$ is unbounded?
:::
