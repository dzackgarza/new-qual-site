::: {.problem}
Suppose that $f$ is holomorphic on the punctured disk $0 < |z| < 2$.

(A) Prove that if there is a real constant $C$ such that $|f(z)| \leq C$, then
\[
\int_{|z|<1} |f'(z)|^2\,dz < \infty.
\]

(B) What happens when $|f|$ is unbounded?
:::

::: {.solution}
The integral is taken with respect to area. Write $f(z)=\sum_{n\in\ZZ}a_nz^n$, the Laurent series on $0<\abs z<2$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

For $0<r<1$, $\int_{r<\abs z<1}\abs{f'}^2\,dA=\pi\sum_{n\ne0}n\abs{a_n}^2(1-r^{2n})$.

::: pf-proof

In polar coordinates, orthogonality of $e^{i(n-1)\theta}$ on each circle gives $\int_0^{2\pi}\abs{f'(\rho e^{i\theta})}^2\,d\theta=2\pi\sum_nn^2\abs{a_n}^2\rho^{2n-2}$, and $\int_r^1 2\pi n^2\rho^{2n-1}\,d\rho=\pi n(1-r^{2n})$ for $n\ne0$. Each term $n(1-r^{2n})$ is nonnegative, for $n<0$ as well.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

(A) If $\abs f\le C$, the integral is finite.

::: pf-proof

By the removable singularity theorem $a_n=0$ for $n<0$. Then the terms of step [](#s1){.pf-ref} increase to $\pi n\abs{a_n}^2$ as $r\to0$, and $\pi\sum_{n>0}n\abs{a_n}^2\le\pi\sum_nn^2\abs{a_n}^2=\int_0^{2\pi}\abs{f'(e^{i\theta})}^2\,\frac{d\theta}2<\infty$, since $f'$ is continuous on $\abs z=1$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

(B) If $\abs f$ is unbounded near $0$, the integral is $\boxed{\infty}$.

::: pf-proof

The singularity is then not removable, so $a_n\ne0$ for some $n<0$, and its term in step [](#s1){.pf-ref} is $\abs n\abs{a_n}^2\pi(r^{-2\abs n}-1)\to\infty$ as $r\to0$. For example $f(z)=1/z$ gives $\int_{\abs z<1}\abs z^{-4}\,dA=2\pi\int_0^1r^{-3}\,dr=\infty$.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Steps [](#s2){.pf-ref} and [](#s3){.pf-ref} answer parts (A) and (B).

:::
