::: {.problem}
Let $U\subseteq\mathbb C$ be an open set, let $D=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<1\}$, and let $\mathcal F$ be the set of all holomorphic functions $f\colon U\to D$.
Given $z_0\in U$, show that there exists $f_0\in\mathcal F$ such that
\[
|f_0''(z_0)|=\sup_{f\in\mathcal F}|f''(z_0)|.
\]
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. $M=\sup_{f\in\mathcal F}|f''(z_0)|$ is finite.

::: {.proof}
Choose $r>0$ with $\overline{D(z_0,r)}\subset U$. Every
$f\in\mathcal F$ satisfies $|f|<1$, so Cauchy's estimate
gives $|f''(z_0)|\leq2/r^2$ [@SS03].
:::

<1>2. Some holomorphic $f_0:U\to\overline D$ satisfies $|f_0''(z_0)|=M$.

::: {.proof}
Choose $f_n\in\mathcal F$ with $|f_n''(z_0)|\to M$.
The family $\mathcal F$ is uniformly bounded, so Montel's
theorem gives a subsequence $(f_{n_k})$ converging uniformly
on compact subsets of $U$ to a holomorphic $f_0$ [@SS03].
Derivatives of all orders then converge locally uniformly,
so $f_{n_k}''(z_0)\to f_0''(z_0)$ and $|f_0''(z_0)|=M$.
Pointwise limits give $|f_0|\leq1$ on $U$.
:::

<1>3. On each connected component $W$ of $U$, either $|f_0|<1$ on $W$ or $f_0$ is a constant of modulus one on $W$.

::: {.proof}
If $|f_0(w)|=1$ for some $w\in W$, then $|f_0|$ attains
its maximum over $W$ at an interior point. The maximum
modulus principle makes $f_0$ constant on $W$ [@SS03].
:::

<1>4. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Define $g$ to equal $f_0$ on every component of $U$ on
which $|f_0|<1$, and to equal $0$ on every other component.
Components are open, so $g$ is holomorphic on $U$, and
step <1>3 gives $g(U)\subset D$. Thus $g\in\mathcal F$.
If $|f_0|<1$ on the component of $z_0$, then $g=f_0$ near
$z_0$ and $|g''(z_0)|=M$. Otherwise $f_0$ is constant near
$z_0$, so $M=|f_0''(z_0)|=0=|g''(z_0)|$. In both cases
$g$ attains the supremum.
:::
:::
