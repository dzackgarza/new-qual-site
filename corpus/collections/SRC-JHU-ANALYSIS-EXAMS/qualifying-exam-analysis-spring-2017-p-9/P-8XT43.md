3. Let $\{ E _ { k } \}$ be a sequence of (Lebesgue) measurable sets in $\mathbb { R } ^ { k }$ such that

$$
\sum _ { k = 1 } ^ { \infty } m ( E _ { k } ) < \infty .
$$

Prove that almost every $\boldsymbol { x } \in \mathbb { R } ^ { k }$ lie in at most finitely many sets $E _ { k }$
