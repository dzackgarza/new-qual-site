Let $f$ be an entire function.
Suppose that for each $z_0 \in \mathbb{C}$, the power series expansion

$$f(z) = \sum_{n=0}^\infty c_n(z - z_0)^n$$

has at least one coefficient $c_n = 0$.
Show that $f$ is a polynomial.
