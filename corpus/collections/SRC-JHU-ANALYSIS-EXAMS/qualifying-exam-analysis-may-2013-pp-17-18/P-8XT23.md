::: {.problem}
Let $f$ be a holomorphic function on the unit disc $\{z : \abs{z} < 1\}$ satisfying $f(0) = 0$ and $\mathrm{Re}\, f(z) \leq A$ for some positive number $A > 0$.
Prove:

$$\abs{f(z)} \leq \frac{2A\abs{z}}{1 - \abs{z}}.$$
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

A fractional transformation of $f$ satisfies the Schwarz estimate.

::: pf-proof

Put $D=\{z:\abs{z}<1\}$ and
$$
h(z)=\frac{f(z)}{2A-f(z)}.
$$
The denominator cannot vanish: $f(z)=2A$ would contradict
$\operatorname{Re}f(z)\leq A$ and $A>0$. Thus $h$ is
holomorphic on $D$ and $h(0)=0$. Moreover,
$$
\abs{2A-f(z)}^2-\abs{f(z)}^2=4A\bigl(A-\operatorname{Re}f(z)\bigr)\geq0,
$$
so $\abs{h(z)}\leq1$.

The Schwarz bound $\abs{h(z)}\leq\abs{z}$ holds under the
non-strict hypothesis $\abs{h}\leq1$. The quotient $H(z)=h(z)/z$ extends
holomorphically to zero by the Taylor expansion of $h$.
For $0<\rho<1$, it satisfies $\abs{H}\leq1/\rho$ on
$\abs{z}=\rho$. The maximum modulus principle gives this
bound throughout that closed disk [@SS03]. For fixed
$z\in D$, let $\rho\uparrow1$ with $\rho>\abs{z}$ to obtain
$\abs{H(z)}\leq1$. Therefore $\abs{h(z)}\leq\abs{z}$ on $D$.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Inverting the transformation yields the required modulus bound.

::: pf-proof

The identity defining $h$ rearranges to
$f(1+h)=2Ah$. Step [](#s1){.pf-ref} gives $\abs{h(z)}\leq\abs{z}<1$,
so $1+h(z)$ is nonzero and
$$
\abs{f(z)}=\frac{2A\abs{h(z)}}{\abs{1+h(z)}}
\leq\frac{2A\abs{z}}{1-\abs{z}}.
$$
Here the denominator estimate is
$\abs{1+h(z)}\geq1-\abs{h(z)}\geq1-\abs{z}>0$.
This also covers $z=0$, where both sides vanish.

:::
