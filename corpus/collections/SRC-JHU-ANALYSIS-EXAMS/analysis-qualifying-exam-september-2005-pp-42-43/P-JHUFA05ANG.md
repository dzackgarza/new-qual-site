7. How many zeros does the polynomial

$$
z ^ { 9 } + z ^ { 5 } - 8 z ^ { 3 } - z + 2
$$

have between the circles $\{ | z | = 1 \}$ and $\{ | z | = 2 \}$ . Justify your answer.
