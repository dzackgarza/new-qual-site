Let $\{g_k\}_{k=1}^\infty$ be a sequence in $L^1(\mathbb{R}^n)$ with $\sum \|g_k\|_{L^1(\mathbb{R}^n)} < \infty$.

a. Show that $\sum_{k=1}^\infty g_k$ converges a.e. to a function $g \in L^1(\mathbb{R}^n)$.

b. Show that $\lim_{N \to \infty} \|g - \sum_{k=1}^N g_k\|_{L^1(\mathbb{R}^n)} = 0$.
