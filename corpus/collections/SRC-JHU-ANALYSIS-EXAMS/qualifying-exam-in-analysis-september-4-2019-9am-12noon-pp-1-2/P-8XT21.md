::: {.problem}
Let $k\geq0$ and let $f$ be entire with
$$
\max_{\abs{z}=R}\abs{f(z)}\leq AR^k+B
$$
for positive constants $A,B$ and all $R>1000$.
Show that $f$ is a polynomial of degree at most $k$.
:::

::: {.solution}
::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}
The growth bound makes every Taylor coefficient of order
greater than $k$ vanish.

::: pf-proof
Write $f(z)=\sum_{m\geq0}a_mz^m$, the Taylor expansion
valid on the entire plane. Cauchy's coefficient formula
and the length of the circle give, for $R>1000$,
$$
\abs{a_m}=\abs{\frac{1}{2\pi i}
\int_{\abs{\zeta}=R}\frac{f(\zeta)}{\zeta^{m+1}}\,d\zeta}
\leq AR^{k-m}+BR^{-m}
$$
[@SS03]. For an integer $m>k\geq0$, both terms tend
to zero as $R\to\infty$. Hence $a_m=0$. All terms
above $\lfloor k\rfloor$ vanish, so $f$ is a polynomial
of degree at most $k$.
:::

:::

:::
:::

::: {.remark}
For $k<0$ the estimate in step [](#s1){.pf-ref} kills every coefficient
with $m\geq1$, so $f$ is constant, but $f$ need not have
degree at most $k$: $f=1$ with $A=B=1$ satisfies the bound
for every negative $k$.
:::
