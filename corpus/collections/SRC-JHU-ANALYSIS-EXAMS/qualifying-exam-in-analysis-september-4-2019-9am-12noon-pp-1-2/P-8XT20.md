::: {.problem}
3. Define
$$
f * g(x) \coloneqq \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f(y)g(x - y) \, dy.
$$
Prove that if $f, g \in L^{2}(\RR)$, then $f * g$ is a continuous function on $\RR$.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: pf-step

$f\ast g$ is defined everywhere and bounded by $\norm f_2\norm g_2$.

::: pf-proof

By the Cauchy--Schwarz inequality, $\abs{\int f(y)g(x-y)\,dy}\le\norm f_2\norm g_2$.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

By the Cauchy--Schwarz inequality, $\abs{(f\ast g)(x+h)-(f\ast g)(x)}\le\norm f_2\norm{g(\cdot+h)-g}_2$, which tends to $0$ as $h\to0$ by continuity of translation in $L^2$ [@Fol99].

:::
