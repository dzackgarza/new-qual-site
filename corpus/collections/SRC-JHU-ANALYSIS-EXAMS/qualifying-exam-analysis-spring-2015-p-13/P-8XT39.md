::: {.problem}
Let $D(0,r) = \{z \in \mathbb{C} : |z| < r\}$.
Assume $r > 1$ and $f : \overline{D(0,r)} \setminus D(0,1) \to \mathbb{C}$ is a continuous function, holomorphic on $D(0,r) \setminus \overline{D(0,1)}$ that satisfies

$$\max_{\partial D(0,1)} |f(z)| = 1 \quad \text{and} \quad \max_{\partial D(0,r)} |f(z)| = R.$$

Prove $\log |f(z)| \leq \frac{\log R}{\log r} \log |z|$.
:::

::: solution
Write $A=\{z:1<|z|<r\}$. We prove
$|f(z)|\leq |z|^{\log R/\log r}$ on $\overline A$.
At zeros of $f$, the requested logarithmic form uses
$\log0=-\infty$.

<1>1. The outer boundary maximum satisfies $R>0$.

::: proof
Continuity on the compact outer circle gives $0\leq R<\infty$.
Suppose $R=0$. For each positive integer $n$, the function
$z^n f(z)$ is continuous on $\overline A$ and holomorphic
on $A$. Its modulus is at most one on the inner boundary
and zero on the outer boundary. The maximum modulus
principle gives $|z|^n|f(z)|\leq1$ throughout $A$ [@SS03].
For fixed $|z|>1$, letting $n\to\infty$ forces $f(z)=0$.
Continuity then makes $f=0$ on the inner boundary as well,
contradicting its maximum modulus one. Thus $R>0$.
:::

<1>2. Integer powers give the bound without choosing a logarithm branch on the annulus.

::: proof
Set $a=\log R/\log r$ and, for each positive integer $n$,
let $m_n=\lfloor na\rfloor$. The function
$$
H_n(z)=z^{-m_n}f(z)^n
$$
is single-valued and holomorphic on $A$, and continuous on
$\overline A$, even when $m_n$ is negative. On $|z|=1$
its modulus is at most one. On $|z|=r$ it is at most
$$
r^{-m_n}R^n=r^{na-m_n}\leq r,
$$
since $0\leq na-m_n<1$. The maximum modulus principle
therefore gives $|H_n(z)|\leq r$ on $\overline A$.
Taking $n$th roots yields
$$
|f(z)|\leq r^{1/n}|z|^{m_n/n}.
$$
Because $m_n/n\to a$ and $r^{1/n}\to1$, passage to
the limit gives $|f(z)|\leq|z|^a$ at every point of
$\overline A$. At nonzero values of $f$, taking logarithms
gives the required inequality. At zeros it holds with
the stated extended-real convention. This includes both
boundary circles and all signs of $\log R$.
:::
:::
