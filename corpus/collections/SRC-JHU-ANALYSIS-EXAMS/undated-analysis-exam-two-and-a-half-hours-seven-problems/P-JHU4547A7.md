::: problem
Let $\gamma$ denote the curve $|z - 1| = 2$, oriented counterclockwise.
Evaluate
\[
\int_{\gamma} \frac{e^z\,dz}{z^3}.
\]
:::

::: solution
The value is $\boxed{\pi i}$.

<1>1. Cauchy's derivative formula applies at zero.
::: proof
The circle bounds $B=\{|z-1|<2\}$, and zero belongs
to $B$ because $|0-1|=1<2$. The function $F(z)=e^z$
is holomorphic on a neighborhood of the closed disk.
For the specified counterclockwise orientation, Cauchy's
formula for the second derivative gives
$$
F''(0)=\frac{2!}{2\pi i}\int_\gamma\frac{F(z)}{(z-0)^3}\,dz
$$
[@SS03]. Since $F''(0)=e^0=1$, rearranging gives
$\int_\gamma e^z/z^3\,dz=(2\pi i)/2=\pi i$.
:::
:::
