::: problem
Let $f \in L^1(\mathbb{R})$.
Show directly (i.e., do not cite properties of the Fourier transform) that the function
\[
\widehat{f}(\xi) = \int_{\mathbb{R}} e^{-ix\xi} f(x)\,dx
\]
is uniformly continuous and $\widehat{f}(\xi) \to 0$ as $|\xi| \to \infty$.
:::
