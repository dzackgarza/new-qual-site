::: problem
(a) Let $C([0,1])$ denote the space of continuous functions on $[0,1]$, endowed with the "sup" norm.
Show that $C([0,1])$ is a Banach space.

(b) Let $B_p = L^p([0,1])$, with $1 < p < \infty$.
Define weak and strong convergence in $B_p$.
Then, show that the sequence $f_n(x) = \sin nx$ converges weakly to $0$, but not strongly to $0$, in $B_2$.
:::
