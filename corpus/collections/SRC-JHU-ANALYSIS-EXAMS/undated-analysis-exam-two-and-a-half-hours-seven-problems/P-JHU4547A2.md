::: problem
(a) Let $f$ be integrable over a set $A$ and suppose $A = \bigcup_{n=1}^{\infty} A_n$, where the $A_n$ are pairwise disjoint.
Show that
\[
\int_A f = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \int_{A_n} f
\]
and that the sum on the right-hand side is absolutely convergent.

(b) Let $\mu$ be Lebesgue measure on $\mathbb{R}^2$ and let $f \in L^1(\mathbb{R}^2)$.
Show there is a Borel measure $\lambda$ for which $d\lambda = f\,d\mu$ (verify that it is a measure).

(c) For $f = x^2 + y^2$ and $D$ the unit disc, compute $\lambda(D)$.
:::
