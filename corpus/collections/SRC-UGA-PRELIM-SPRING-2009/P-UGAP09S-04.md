::: {.problem}
Prove that if $\sum a_n$ converges and $\sum b_n$ converges absolutely, then
\[
\sum a_nb_n
\]
converges absolutely. What can be said if $\sum a_n$ and $\sum b_n$ are both only conditionally convergent?
:::
