::: {.problem}
Determine, with proof, which of the following functions $\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R$ are continuous:
\[
f(x,y)=
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{x^3y}{x^4+y^4},&(x,y)\ne(0,0),\\
0,&(x,y)=(0,0),
\end{cases}
\]
and
\[
g(x,y)=
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{x^3y^2}{(x^2+y^2)^2},&(x,y)\ne(0,0),\\
0,&(x,y)=(0,0).
\end{cases}
\]
:::
