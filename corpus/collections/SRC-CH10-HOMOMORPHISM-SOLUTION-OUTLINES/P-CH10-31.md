::: {.problem}
Suppose that $\phi$ is a homomorphism from $U ( 3 0 )$ to $U ( 3 0 )$ and that $K e r \phi = \{ 1 , 1 1 \}$ . If $\phi ( 7 ) = 7$, find all elements of $U ( 3 0 )$ that map to 7.
:::

::: {.solution}
$$
\phi ^ { - 1 } ( 7 ) = 7 K e r \phi = \{ 7 , 1 7 \} .
$$
:::
