::: {.problem}
Let $p$ be a prime.
Determine the number of homomorphisms from $\ZZ_{p} \oplus \ZZ_{p}$ into $\ZZ_{p}$.
:::

::: {.solution}

The argument is the card's own: a homomorphism on a direct sum of two cyclic groups is determined by two values, and every pair of values is allowed.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #determined}

$\varphi(a,b)=a\varphi(1,0)+b\varphi(0,1)$ for every homomorphism $\varphi:\ZZ_{p}\oplus\ZZ_{p}\to\ZZ_{p}$.

::: pf-proof

$(a,b)=a(1,0)+b(0,1)$ in the direct sum, and a homomorphism is additive, so $\varphi(a,b)=a\varphi(1,0)+b\varphi(0,1)$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #allowed}

Every choice of $u=\varphi(1,0)$ and $v=\varphi(0,1)$ in $\ZZ_{p}$ occurs.

::: pf-proof

For arbitrary $u,v\in\ZZ_{p}$, the formula $\varphi(a,b)=au+bv$ is well defined on $\ZZ_{p}\oplus\ZZ_{p}$ and additive in $(a,b)$, and it is additive in the $\ZZ_{p}$-scalars because $a\mapsto au$ is; the values at $(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$ are $u$ and $v$. No condition restricts $u$ and $v$: the order of $u$ in the additive group $\ZZ_{p}$ divides $p$, and $p\cdot u=0$ holds automatically.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #count}

There are $p^{2}$ homomorphisms.

::: pf-proof

By step [](#determined){.pf-ref} the map $\varphi\mapsto(\varphi(1,0),\varphi(0,1))$ is injective into $\ZZ_{p}\times\ZZ_{p}$, and by step [](#allowed){.pf-ref} it is surjective. So the homomorphisms correspond bijectively to the $p^{2}$ pairs $(u,v)$:
$$
\boxed{\cardinality{\operatorname{Hom}(\ZZ_{p}\oplus\ZZ_{p},\ZZ_{p})}=p^{2}.}
$$

:::
