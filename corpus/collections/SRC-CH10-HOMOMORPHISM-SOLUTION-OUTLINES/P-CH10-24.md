::: {.problem}
Suppose that $\phi : \mathbb{Z}_{50} \to \mathbb{Z}_{15}$ is a group homomorphism with $\phi(7) = 6$.

(a) Determine $\phi(x)$.

(b) Determine the image of $\phi$.

(c) Determine the kernel of $\phi$.

(d) Determine $\phi^{-1}(3)$.
:::

::: {.solution}
(a) Let $\phi ( 1 ) = k$ . Then $\phi ( x ) = k x$ . In particular, $\phi ( 7 ) = 7 k$ mod $1 5 = 6$ . So $k = 3$ Hence, $\phi ( x ) = 3 x$

(b) The image of $\phi$ is $< 3 >$ in $\mathbb { Z } _ { 1 5 }$ , which is {3, 6, 9, 12, 0}.

(c) The $K e r \phi$ has order $\frac { 5 0 } { 5 } = 1 0$ in $\mathbb { Z } _ { 5 0 }$ . So $K e r \phi = < 5 > = \{ 5 , 1 0 , 1 5 , 2 0 , 2 5 , 3 0 , 3 5 , 4 0 , 4 5 , 0 \}$ in $\mathbb { Z } _ { 5 0 }$

(d)
$$
\phi ^ { - 1 } ( 3 ) = 1 + \ker \phi = 1 + < 5 > = \{ 6 , 1 1 , 1 6 , 2 1 , 2 6 , 3 1 , 3 6 , 4 1 , 4 6 , 1 \} .
$$
:::
