::: {.problem}
Suppose that $\ZZ_{10}$ and $\ZZ_{15}$ are both homomorphic images of a finite group $G$. What can be said about $\cardinality{G}$?
Generalize.
:::

::: {.solution}
If $\ZZ _ { 1 0 }$ is a homomorphic image of $G$, 10 divides the order of G. Similarly, 15 divides the order of $G$ . Hence the order of G is divisible by $\operatorname{lcm}(10, 15) = 30$ . In general, the order of G is divisible by the least common multiple of the orders of all its homomorphic images.
:::
