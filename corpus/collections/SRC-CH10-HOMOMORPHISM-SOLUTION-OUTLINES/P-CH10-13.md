::: {.problem}
Prove that $(A \oplus B)/(A \oplus \{e\}) \approx B$.
:::

::: {.solution}

The argument is that of the retained solution outline for Chapter 10, Exercise 13, PDF p. 1 of `assets/attachments/Ch10Sltns.pdf`, restructured into numbered steps. Here $A$ and $B$ are groups written multiplicatively, $A\oplus B$ is their direct product with componentwise operation $(a,b)(c,d)=(ac,bd)$, and $A\oplus\{e\}=\theset{(a,e)\mid a\in A}$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #hom}

$\phi:(A\oplus B)\to B$, $\phi(a,b)=b$, is a homomorphism.

::: pf-proof

For $(a,b),(c,d)\in A\oplus B$, $\phi((a,b)(c,d))=\phi(ac,bd)=bd=\phi(a,b)\phi(c,d)$, the middle equality being the componentwise product and the last the definition of $\phi$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #surj}

$\phi$ is surjective.

::: pf-proof

For $y\in B$ and any $a\in A$, $\phi(a,y)=y$; taking $a=e$ exhibits a preimage.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #ker}

$\ker\phi=A\oplus\{e\}$.

::: pf-proof

$\phi(a,b)=e$ holds exactly when $b=e$, that is, exactly when $(a,b)=(a,e)$ for some $a\in A$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #done}

$(A\oplus B)/(A\oplus\{e\})\approx B$.

::: pf-proof

By the first isomorphism theorem, a homomorphism of groups induces an isomorphism from the quotient by its kernel onto its image; by steps [](#hom){.pf-ref} and [](#ker){.pf-ref} the kernel is $A\oplus\{e\}$ and by step [](#surj){.pf-ref} the image is $B$.

:::
