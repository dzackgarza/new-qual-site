::: problem
Let $f:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$ be a continuously differentiable function.
Let $g:\mathbb{R}^2\to\mathbb{R}$ be defined by
\[
g(x,y)=\begin{cases}
\dfrac{f(x)-f(y)}{x-y} & x\neq y,\\
f'(x) & x=y.
\end{cases}
\]
Prove that $g$ is continuous.
:::
