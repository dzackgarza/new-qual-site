::: problem
Let $X$ be a Banach space.
State the definition of the dual Banach space $X^*$, and prove that $X^*$ is complete.
:::

::: solution
The continuous dual of $X$ is
\[
X^*:=\{\varphi:X\to\mathbb F:\varphi\text{ is linear and continuous}\},
\]
where $\mathbb F=\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$, equipped with the operator norm
\[
\|\varphi\|
:=\sup_{\|x\|\le1}|\varphi(x)|.
\]

<1>1. Construct the pointwise limit of an operator-norm Cauchy sequence.
::: proof
Let $(\varphi_n)$ be Cauchy in $X^*$. For fixed $x\in X$,
\[
|\varphi_n(x)-\varphi_m(x)|
\le \|\varphi_n-\varphi_m\|\,\|x\|.
\]
Hence $(\varphi_n(x))$ is Cauchy in the complete scalar field $\mathbb F$. Define
\[
\varphi(x):=\lim_{n\to\infty}\varphi_n(x).
\]
Pointwise passage to the limit in
\[
\varphi_n(ax+by)=a\varphi_n(x)+b\varphi_n(y)
\]
shows that $\varphi$ is linear.
:::

<1>2. Show that the pointwise limit is bounded.
::: proof
Every Cauchy sequence is bounded, so there exists $M<\infty$ with
\[
\|\varphi_n\|\le M
\qquad\text{for all }n.
\]
Therefore, for every $x\in X$,
\[
|\varphi(x)|
=\lim_{n\to\infty}|\varphi_n(x)|
\le M\|x\|.
\]
Thus $\varphi\in X^*$ and $\|\varphi\|\le M$.
:::

<1>3. Prove convergence in operator norm.
::: proof
Let $\varepsilon>0$. Since $(\varphi_n)$ is Cauchy, choose $N$ such that
\[
\|\varphi_n-\varphi_m\|<\varepsilon
\qquad(n,m\ge N).
\]
Fix $n\ge N$ and $x$ with $\|x\|\le1$. Letting $m\to\infty$ gives
\[
|\varphi_n(x)-\varphi(x)|
\le\varepsilon.
\]
Taking the supremum over the unit ball,
\[
\|\varphi_n-\varphi\|\le\varepsilon.
\]
Hence $\varphi_n\to\varphi$ in $X^*$, so
\[
\boxed{X^*\text{ is complete}.}
\]
:::
:::
