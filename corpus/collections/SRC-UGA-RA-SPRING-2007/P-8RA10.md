::: problem
Let $(\Omega,\mathcal{M},\mu)$ be a measure space, and let $f\in L^\infty(\Omega)\cap L^1(\Omega)$.
Show that $\lim_{p\to\infty}\|f\|_p=\|f\|_\infty$.
(Hint: $|f|^p=|f|\,|f|^{p-1}$.)
:::
