::: problem
Let $(\Omega,\mathcal{M},\mu)$ be a measure space, and let $f\in L^\infty(\Omega)\cap L^1(\Omega)$.
Show that $\lim_{p\to\infty}\|f\|_p=\|f\|_\infty$.
(Hint: $|f|^p=|f|\,|f|^{p-1}$.)
:::

::: solution
Let
\[
M:=\|f\|_\infty.
\]
If $M=0$, then $f=0$ almost everywhere and the conclusion is immediate. Assume $M>0$.

<1>1. Prove the upper bound.
::: proof
For every $p\ge1$,
\[
|f|^p=|f|\,|f|^{p-1}\le |f|M^{p-1}
\]
almost everywhere. Hence
\[
\|f\|_p^p
\le M^{p-1}\|f\|_1.
\]
Taking $p$th roots gives
\[
\|f\|_p
\le M^{1-1/p}\|f\|_1^{1/p},
\]
so
\[
\limsup_{p\to\infty}\|f\|_p\le M.
\]
:::

<1>2. Prove the lower bound.
::: proof
Fix $a$ with $0<a<M$. By the definition of essential supremum,
\[
E_a:=\{x:|f(x)|>a\}
\]
has positive measure. Since $f\in L^1$,
\[
a\mu(E_a)\le \int_{E_a}|f|\,d\mu\le\|f\|_1,
\]
so $0<\mu(E_a)<\infty$.

Therefore
\[
\|f\|_p^p
\ge \int_{E_a}|f|^p\,d\mu
\ge a^p\mu(E_a),
\]
and hence
\[
\|f\|_p\ge a\mu(E_a)^{1/p}.
\]
Letting $p\to\infty$ yields
\[
\liminf_{p\to\infty}\|f\|_p\ge a.
\]
Since this holds for every $a<M$,
\[
\liminf_{p\to\infty}\|f\|_p\ge M.
\]
Combining with Step 1 gives
\[
\boxed{\lim_{p\to\infty}\|f\|_p=\|f\|_\infty.}
\]
:::
:::
