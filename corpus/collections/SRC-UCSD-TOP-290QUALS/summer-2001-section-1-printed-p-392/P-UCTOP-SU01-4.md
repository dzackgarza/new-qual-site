Let $X$ be a (path-connected) simply-connected CW-complex with $H_2(X) \cong \mathbb{Z} \oplus \mathbb{Z}$ and $H_{\geq 3}(X) = 0$.
Prove that $X$ is homotopy-equivalent to the "bouquet of two spheres" $S^2 \vee S^2$.
