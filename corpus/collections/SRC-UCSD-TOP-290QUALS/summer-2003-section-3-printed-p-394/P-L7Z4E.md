::: {.problem}
Consider the standard embedding $\CP^1 \subseteq \CP^2$.
Show that there is no homeomorphism $f\colon \CP^2 \to \CP^2$ such that $f(\CP^1)$ is disjoint from $\CP^1$.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #h2}

$H_2(\CP^2;\ZZ) \cong \ZZ$ is generated by $[\CP^1]$, and the intersection number satisfies $[\CP^1]\cdot[\CP^1] = 1$.

::: pf-proof

$\CP^2$ has a CW structure with one cell in each of the dimensions $0,2,4$, and $\CP^1$ is its 2-skeleton, so $[\CP^1]$ generates $H_2$.
Two distinct lines in $\CP^2$ are homologous to $\CP^1$ and meet transversally in exactly one point with positive sign, because both are complex submanifolds.
Equivalently, the Poincaré dual $\alpha \in H^2$ of $[\CP^1]$ generates $H^2$, and $\alpha^2$ generates $H^4$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #fstar}

If $f$ is a homeomorphism, then $f_*[\CP^1] = \pm[\CP^1]$, so $[\CP^1]\cdot f_*[\CP^1] = \pm 1$.

::: pf-proof

$f_*$ is an automorphism of $H_2 \cong \ZZ$, hence multiplication by $\pm 1$. Then $[\CP^1]\cdot(\pm[\CP^1]) = \pm 1$ by Step [](#h2){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #disjoint}

If $f(\CP^1) \cap \CP^1 = \emptyset$, then $[\CP^1]\cdot f_*[\CP^1] = 0$.

::: pf-proof

Let $\alpha \in H^2(\CP^2)$ be the Poincaré dual of $[\CP^1]$, let $\beta = (f^{-1})^*\alpha$, which is the Poincaré dual of $f_*[\CP^1]$ up to sign, and let $U = \CP^2 \setminus \CP^1$ and $V = \CP^2 \setminus f(\CP^1) = f(U)$.
$U \cong \CC^2$ is contractible, and so is $V$, so $\restrictionof{\alpha}{U} = 0$ and $\restrictionof{\beta}{V} = 0$.
By the long exact sequences of the pairs, $\alpha$ comes from $H^2(\CP^2, U)$ and $\beta$ comes from $H^2(\CP^2, V)$.
Hence $\alpha\smile\beta$ comes from $H^4(\CP^2, U\cup V) = H^4(\CP^2,\CP^2) = 0$, because $U \cup V = \CP^2$ when the two spheres are disjoint.
The intersection number is $\langle \alpha\smile\beta, [\CP^2]\rangle = 0$.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Steps [](#fstar){.pf-ref} and [](#disjoint){.pf-ref} give $\pm 1 = [\CP^1]\cdot f_*[\CP^1] = 0$, a contradiction. So no such homeomorphism exists.

:::
