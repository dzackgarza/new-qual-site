::: {.problem}
Show that a closed connected orientable surface $\Sigma$ of genus $g\geq 1$ has $\pi_i(\Sigma_g)=0$ for $i\geq 2$, and deduce that the fundamental group of $\Sigma_g$ is not a free group.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #universal-cover-contractible}
For $g=1$, the universal cover of $\Sigma_g=T^2$ is $\mathbb R^2$; for $g\ge2$, the universal cover is the open disk.

::: pf-proof
The torus is $\mathbb R^2/\mathbb Z^2$. A closed orientable surface of genus at least two admits a hyperbolic structure, whose universal cover is $\mathbb H^2$, homeomorphic to an open disk.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #higher-homotopy-vanishes}
Hence
$$
\pi_i(\Sigma_g)=0\qquad(i\ge2).
$$

::: pf-proof
The universal cover is contractible by step [](#universal-cover-contractible){.pf-ref}, and a covering map induces isomorphisms on homotopy groups in all degrees at least two.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #free-rank-2g}
Suppose $\pi_1(\Sigma_g)$ were free. Its rank would have to be $2g$.

::: pf-proof
The abelianization is
$$
H_1(\Sigma_g;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^{2g}.
$$
The abelianization of a free group of rank $r$ is $\mathbb Z^r$, so $r=2g$.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
Then both $\Sigma_g$ and $\bigvee^{2g}S^1$ would be CW models for $K(F_{2g},1)$ and hence homotopy-equivalent.

::: pf-proof
By step [](#higher-homotopy-vanishes){.pf-ref}, $\Sigma_g$ is aspherical. A bouquet of circles has contractible universal cover and fundamental group $F_{2g}$, so it is also a $K(F_{2g},1)$. CW models of a fixed Eilenberg--Mac Lane space are homotopy-equivalent.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #homology-contradiction}
This is impossible because
$$
H_2(\Sigma_g;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z,
\qquad
H_2\!\left(\bigvee^{2g}S^1;\mathbb Z\right)=0.
$$

::: pf-proof
The closed orientable surface has a fundamental class, whereas a graph has no homology above degree one.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
Therefore $\pi_1(\Sigma_g)$ is not free.

::: pf-proof
The assumption in step [](#free-rank-2g){.pf-ref} yields the contradiction in step [](#homology-contradiction){.pf-ref}.
:::
