Consider the set $L$ of 3-manifolds which can be formed by gluing together the boundaries of two solid tori $S^1 \times B^2$ using a homeomorphism.
Consider the function $d : L \to \mathbb{N}$ given by the total dimension of its mod-5 homology: $d(M) = \sum \dim H_i(M; \mathbb{Z}_5)$.
What is the maximal value of $d$?

::: {.solution}
<1>1. Every manifold obtained by gluing two solid tori is a closed connected orientable $3$-manifold admitting a genus-$1$ Heegaard splitting.
::: {.proof}
The two solid tori are compact connected orientable $3$-manifolds with common torus boundary. Gluing the boundaries by a homeomorphism produces a closed connected $3$-manifold; reversing the orientation on one solid torus if necessary makes the gluing orientation-compatible.
:::

<1>2. Over $\mathbb F_5$, Poincaré duality gives
$$
b_0=b_3=1,\qquad b_1=b_2.
$$
::: {.proof}
Apply Poincaré duality to the closed orientable $3$-manifold with field coefficients.
:::

<1>3. One has $b_1\le1$.
::: {.proof}
Van Kampen applied to the genus-$1$ Heegaard splitting shows that the fundamental group is generated by the core loop of either solid torus, hence is cyclic. Therefore
$$
H_1(M;\mathbb F_5)\cong \pi_1(M)^{\mathrm{ab}}\otimes\mathbb F_5
$$
has dimension at most $1$.
:::

<1>4. Consequently
$$
d(M)=\sum_i b_i=2+2b_1\le4.
$$
::: {.proof}
Use <1>2--<1>3.
:::

<1>5. The value $4$ is attained.
::: {.proof}
Glue meridian to meridian so as to obtain $S^1\times S^2$. Then
$$
H_i(S^1\times S^2;\mathbb F_5)\cong\mathbb F_5
$$
for $i=0,1,2,3$, so the total dimension is $4$.
:::

<1>6. Thus the maximal value is
$$
\boxed{4}.
$$
::: {.proof}
Combine <1>4 and <1>5.
:::
:::
