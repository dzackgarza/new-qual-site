::: {.problem}
Consider a solid tetrahedron $ABCD$.
The face $ABC$ is glued to $ABD$ by an affine map preserving the order of vertices (i.e. $A$ goes to $A$, $B$ goes to $B$, $C$ goes to $D$.)
Similarly, $BCD$ is glued to $ACD$.
Compute the fundamental group of the resulting quotient space.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: pf-step
The face identifications leave two vertex classes, namely $A\sim B$ and $C\sim D$.

::: pf-proof
The gluing $ABC\to ABD$ sends $C$ to $D$, while the gluing $BCD\to ACD$ sends $B$ to $A$. No further vertex identifications are needed.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
In the quotient $1$-skeleton, the edges $AC,AD,BC,BD$ all become one edge $e$ joining the two vertex classes, while $AB$ and $CD$ become loops $a$ and $c$ at the two vertices.

::: pf-proof
The first face gluing identifies $AC$ with $AD$ and $BC$ with $BD$. The second identifies $BC$ with $AC$ and $BD$ with $AD$. Hence all four cross-edges form one class. The edges $AB$ and $CD$ are each fixed by the relevant face pairing.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #face-abc-kills-a}
The quotient of the face $ABC$ is a $2$-cell whose attaching loop is homotopic to $a$.

::: pf-proof
Traversing $ABC$ gives the edge path
$$
AB\cdot BC\cdot CA=aee^{-1}=a.
$$
Thus this $2$-cell kills the loop $a$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #face-bcd-kills-c}
The quotient of the face $BCD$ is a $2$-cell whose attaching loop is $ece^{-1}$.

::: pf-proof
Traversing $BCD$ gives
$$
BC\cdot CD\cdot DB=ece^{-1}.
$$
Thus this $2$-cell kills the conjugate of $c$, hence kills $c$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #boundary-simply-connected}
Therefore the quotient of the boundary tetrahedron is simply connected, and attaching the interior $3$-cell does not change the fundamental group.

::: pf-proof
Choose a maximal tree consisting of the edge $e$. Before attaching the $2$-cells, the fundamental group is freely generated by the two loops $a,c$. By steps [](#face-abc-kills-a){.pf-ref} and [](#face-bcd-kills-c){.pf-ref} both generators are killed. Cells of dimension at least $3$ do not affect $\pi_1$.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
Hence the resulting quotient space has
$$
\boxed{\pi_1=1}.
$$

::: pf-proof
This is the conclusion of step [](#boundary-simply-connected){.pf-ref}.
:::
