::: problem
Find the Jordan canonical form for the matrix
\[
\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}.
\]

(a) Over the complex numbers.

(b) Over the algebraic closure of the field of three elements.
:::
