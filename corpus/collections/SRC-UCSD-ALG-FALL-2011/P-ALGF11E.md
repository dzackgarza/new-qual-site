::: problem
Let $F$ be a field and $f(x) = x^4 + bx^2 + c \in F[x]$, for some $b,c \in F$.

If $K$ is the splitting field of $f(x)$ over $F$, prove that the Galois group $\mathrm{Gal}(K/F)$ is isomorphic to a subgroup of the dihedral group $D_4$ of order $8$.
:::
