::: {.problem}
Let $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ be $C^\infty$ and suppose $f$ has a local minimum at $0$.
Prove that there is a disk centered on the $y$-axis which lies above the graph of $f$ and is tangent to the graph at
\[
(0,f(0)).
\]
:::
