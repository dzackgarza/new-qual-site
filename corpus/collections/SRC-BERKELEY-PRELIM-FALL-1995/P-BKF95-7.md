::: {.problem}
Let
\[
g(z)=\sum_{n=0}^\infty g_nz^n,
\qquad
h(z)=\sum_{n=0}^\infty h_nz^n
\]
be entire.
Find a formula for the coefficients $f_n$ in the Taylor expansion at $0$ of
\[
f(z)=\frac1{2\pi i}\int_{|w|=1}g(z/w)h(w)\frac{dw}{w}.
\]
:::
