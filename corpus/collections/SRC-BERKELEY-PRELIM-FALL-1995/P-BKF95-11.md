::: {.problem}
Let $f=u+iv$ be holomorphic on $|z|<1$, with $u,v$ real-valued.
Assume
\[
u(0)^2=v(0)^2.
\]
Show that for every $0<r<1$,
\[
\int_0^{2\pi}u(re^{i\theta})^2\,d\theta
=
\int_0^{2\pi}v(re^{i\theta})^2\,d\theta.
\]
:::
