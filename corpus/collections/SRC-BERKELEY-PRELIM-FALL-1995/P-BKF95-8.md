::: {.problem}
Let $A=(a_{ij})$ be an $n\times n$ complex matrix satisfying
\[
|a_{ii}|>\sum_{j\ne i}|a_{ij}|
\]
for every $1\le i\le n$.
Prove that $A$ is invertible.
:::
