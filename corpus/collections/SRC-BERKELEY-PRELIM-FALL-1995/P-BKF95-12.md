::: {.problem}
Let $f$ and $f'$ be continuous on $[0,\infty)$, and suppose
\[
f(x)=0\qquad(x\ge10^{10}).
\]
Show that
\[
\int_0^\infty f(x)^2\,dx
\le
2\sqrt{\int_0^\infty x^2f(x)^2\,dx}
\sqrt{\int_0^\infty f'(x)^2\,dx}.
\]
:::
