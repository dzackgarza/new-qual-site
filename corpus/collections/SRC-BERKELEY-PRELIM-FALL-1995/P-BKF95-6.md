::: {.problem}
Determine all real numbers $L>1$ for which
\[
x^2y''(x)+y(x)=0,
\qquad 1\le x\le L,
\]
with boundary conditions
\[
y(1)=y(L)=0
\]
has a nonzero solution.
:::
