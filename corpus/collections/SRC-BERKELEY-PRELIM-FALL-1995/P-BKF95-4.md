::: {.problem}
Suppose $A,B$ are real $n\times n$ matrices and $C$ is an invertible complex $n\times n$ matrix such that
\[
CAC^{-1}=B.
\]
Find an invertible real $n\times n$ matrix $D$ such that
\[
DAD^{-1}=B.
\]
:::
