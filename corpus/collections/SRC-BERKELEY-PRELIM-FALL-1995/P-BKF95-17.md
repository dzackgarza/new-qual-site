::: {.problem}
Let $f_1,\ldots,f_n$ be continuous real-valued functions on $[a,b]$.
Show that $\{f_1,\ldots,f_n\}$ is linearly dependent on $[a,b]$ if and only if
\[
\det\left(\int_a^b f_i(x)f_j(x)\,dx\right)_{i,j=1}^n=0.
\]
:::
