::: {.problem}
Let $P_n$ be the vector space of real polynomials of degree at most $n$, and let
\[
D:P_n\to P_n,
\qquad
D(p)=p'.
\]
Let $\pi$ be a real polynomial. What is the minimal polynomial of the transformation $\pi(D)$?
