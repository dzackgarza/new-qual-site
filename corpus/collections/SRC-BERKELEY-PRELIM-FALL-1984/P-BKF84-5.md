::: {.problem}
Consider
\[
\frac{dy}{dx}=3xy+\frac{y}{1+y^2}.
\]
Prove:

1. for each $n=1,2,\ldots$ there is a unique solution $y=f_n(x)$ on $0\le x\le1$ satisfying $f_n(0)=1/n$;

2.
\[
\lim_{n\to\infty}f_n(1)=0.
\]
:::
