::: {.problem}
Consider
\[
\frac{dx}{dt}=y,
\qquad
\frac{dy}{dt}=-ay-x^3-x^5,
\qquad a>0.
\]

1. Show that
\[
F(x,y)=\frac{y^2}{2}+\frac{x^4}{4}+\frac{x^6}{6}
\]
decreases along solutions.

2. Show that for every $\varepsilon>0$ there is a $\delta>0$ such that whenever
\[
\|(x(0),y(0))\|<\delta,
\]
there is a unique solution with that initial condition, defined for all $t\ge0$, satisfying
\[
\|(x(t),y(t))\|<\varepsilon
\qquad\text{for all }t\ge0.
\]
:::
