::: {.problem}
Prove or give a counterexample: if $f:[0,1]\to\mathbb R$ is nondecreasing, then there is a sequence of continuous functions $f_n:[0,1]\to\mathbb R$ such that
\[
\lim_{n\to\infty}f_n(x)=f(x)
\]
for every $x\in[0,1]$.
:::
