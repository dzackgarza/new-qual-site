::: {.problem}
Let $a,b,c,d\in\mathbb R$, not all zero. Find the eigenvalues of
\[
\begin{pmatrix}
a^2&ab&ac&ad\\
ab&b^2&bc&bd\\
ac&bc&c^2&cd\\
ad&bd&cd&d^2
\end{pmatrix}
\]
and describe the eigenspace decomposition of $\mathbb R^4$.
:::
