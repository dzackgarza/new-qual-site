::: {.problem}
Fix $0\le\theta,\varphi\le2\pi$. Let $R:\mathbb R^3\to\mathbb R^3$ have matrix
\[
\begin{pmatrix}
1&0&0\\
0&\cos\theta&\sin\theta\\
0&-\sin\theta&\cos\theta
\end{pmatrix}
\]
in the standard basis $\mathbf i,\mathbf j,\mathbf k$.

Let $S:\mathbb R^3\to\mathbb R^3$ have matrix
\[
\begin{pmatrix}
\cos\varphi&\sin\varphi&0\\
-\sin\varphi&\cos\varphi&0\\
0&0&1
\end{pmatrix}
\]
with respect to the basis
\[
\left\{
\frac1{\sqrt2}(\mathbf i+\mathbf k),
\mathbf j,
\frac1{\sqrt2}(\mathbf i-\mathbf k)
\right\}.
\]
Prove that $T=R\circ S$ leaves a line invariant.
:::
