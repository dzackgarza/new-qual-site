::: {.problem}
Let $G$ be a group and let $H\le G$ have finite index $n$. Prove or disprove:

1. If $a\in G$, then $a^n\in H$.

2. If $a\in G$, then for some $k$ with $1\le k\le n$ one has $a^k\in H$.
:::
