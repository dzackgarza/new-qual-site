::: {.problem}
Let $T:\mathbb C^n\to\mathbb C^n$ be a linear transformation and let $P(X)$ be a polynomial such that
\[
P(T)=0.
\]
Prove that every eigenvalue of $T$ is a root of $P(X)$.
:::
