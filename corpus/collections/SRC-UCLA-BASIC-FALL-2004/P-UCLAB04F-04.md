::: {.problem}
Suppose $(\mathcal M,\rho)$ is a metric space, $x,y\in\mathcal M$, and $(x_n)$ is a sequence in $\mathcal M$ such that $x_n\to x$.
Prove that
\[
\rho(x_n,y)\longrightarrow\rho(x,y).
\]
:::
