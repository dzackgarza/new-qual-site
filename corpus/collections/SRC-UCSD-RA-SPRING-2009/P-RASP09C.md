::: problem
Assume that $1 \leq p < \infty$ and $(X, \mathcal{M}, \mu)$ is a measure space.
If $f_n \to f$ in measure and $|f_n| \leq g \in L^p(X, d\mu)$ for all $n$, then $f_n \to f$ in $L^p$-norm.
:::
