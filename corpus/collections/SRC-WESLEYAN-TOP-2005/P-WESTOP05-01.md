::: {.problem}
Determine the cardinalities of the following sets:
\[
\mathcal P(\mathbb N),
\qquad
\{0,1\},
\qquad
[0,1],
\qquad
(0,1),
\]
and
\[
\{f:\mathbb N\to[0,1]\}.
\]
Here $\mathcal P$ denotes power set.
:::
