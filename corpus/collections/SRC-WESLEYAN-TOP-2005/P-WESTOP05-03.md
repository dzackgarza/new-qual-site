::: {.problem}
Explain why each of the following is not metrizable:

1. the Sorgenfrey line;
2. the one-point compactification of an uncountable discrete space;
3. the product of uncountably many copies of $\mathbb N$ with the discrete topology.

Then explain:

1. why every compact Hausdorff space is completely regular;
2. why every completely regular Hausdorff space is homeomorphic to a subspace of a compact Hausdorff space.
:::
