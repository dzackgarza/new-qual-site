::: {.problem}
For a space $X$, define its suspension $\Sigma X$ as the quotient of $X\times I$ obtained by collapsing $X\times\{0\}$ to one point and $X\times\{1\}$ to another point. Assuming $X$ is path-connected, compute with proof
\[
\pi_1(\Sigma X).
\]
:::
