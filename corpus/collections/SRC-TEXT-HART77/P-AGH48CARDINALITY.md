::: problem
1. Show that any variety of positive dimension over $k$ has the same cardinality as $k$.
2. Deduce that any two curves over $k$ are homeomorphic.

*Hint:* Treat $\AA^n$ and $\PP^n$ first. Then for any $X$, use induction on the dimension $n$: make $X$ birational to a hypersurface $H \subseteq \PP^{n+1}$, and show that the projection of $H$ to $\PP^n$ from a point not on $H$ is finite-to-one and surjective.
:::
