::: problem
Let $\varphi: \PP^n_k \to \PP^m_k$ be a morphism.
Then

a. either $\varphi(\PP^n) = \pt$, or $m \geq n$ and $\dim \varphi(\PP^n) = n$;

b. in the second case, $\varphi$ can be obtained as the composition of

    1. a $d\dash$uple embedding $\PP^n \to \PP^N$ for a uniquely determined $d \geq 1$,
    2. a linear projection $\PP^N - L \to \PP^m$, and
    3. an automorphism of $\PP^m$.

   Also, $\varphi$ has finite fibres.
:::
