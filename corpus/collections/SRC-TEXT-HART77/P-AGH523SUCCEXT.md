::: {.problem}
a. If $\mathcal{E}$ is a locally free sheaf of rank $r$ on a (nonsingular) curve $C$, then there is a sequence
\[
0=\mathcal{E}_0 \subseteq \mathcal{E}_1 \subseteq \ldots \subseteq \mathcal{E}_r=\mathcal{E}
\]
of subsheaves such that $\mathcal{E}_i / \mathcal{E}_{i-1}$ is an invertible sheaf for each $i=1, \ldots, r$.
We say that $\mathcal{E}$ is a successive extension of invertible sheaves.

Hint: Use (II, Ex.
8.2).

b. Show that this is false for varieties of dimension $\geqslant 2$.
In particular, the sheaf of differentials $\Omega$ on $\PP^2$ is not an extension of invertible sheaves.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

Let $\mathcal E$ be locally free of rank $r\geq2$ on the nonsingular
projective curve $C$. There is an invertible subsheaf
$$
\mathcal L\hookrightarrow\mathcal E
$$
whose quotient is locally free of rank $r-1$.

::: pf-proof

Fix a very ample invertible sheaf $\OO_C(1)$. For $n\gg0$, Serre's theorem
makes
$$
\mathcal E(n)=\mathcal E\tensor\OO_C(n)
$$
generated by global sections. Since
$$
\operatorname{rank}\mathcal E(n)=r>1=\dim C,
$$
Exercise II.8.2, proved on [[P-AGH282GENERICSECTION]], gives a nowhere-vanishing
global section and an exact sequence
$$
0\longrightarrow\OO_C
\longrightarrow\mathcal E(n)
\longrightarrow\mathcal F
\longrightarrow0,
$$
where $\mathcal F$ is locally free of rank $r-1$. Tensoring by
$\OO_C(-n)$ gives
$$
0\longrightarrow\OO_C(-n)
\longrightarrow\mathcal E
\longrightarrow\mathcal F(-n)
\longrightarrow0.
$$
Thus one may take
$$
\mathcal L=\OO_C(-n).
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

Every locally free sheaf of rank $r$ on $C$ is a successive extension
of invertible sheaves.

::: pf-proof

Proceed by induction on $r$. For $r=1$ there is nothing to prove.

Assume $r\geq2$. By step [](#s1){.pf-ref} there is an exact sequence
$$
0\longrightarrow\mathcal L
\longrightarrow\mathcal E
\xrightarrow{\pi}\mathcal Q
\longrightarrow0
$$
with $\mathcal L$ invertible and $\mathcal Q$ locally free of rank $r-1$.
By induction, choose a filtration
$$
0=\mathcal Q_0\subseteq\mathcal Q_1\subseteq\cdots
\subseteq\mathcal Q_{r-1}=\mathcal Q
$$
whose successive quotients are invertible.

Define
$$
\mathcal E_0=0,
\qquad
\mathcal E_1=\mathcal L,
\qquad
\mathcal E_{i+1}=\pi^{-1}(\mathcal Q_i)
\quad(1\leq i\leq r-1).
$$
Then
$$
\mathcal E_1/\mathcal E_0\cong\mathcal L
$$
and, for $1\leq i\leq r-1$,
$$
\mathcal E_{i+1}/\mathcal E_i
\cong
\mathcal Q_i/\mathcal Q_{i-1}.
$$
Every quotient on the right is invertible. Hence
$$
0=\mathcal E_0\subseteq\mathcal E_1\subseteq\cdots
\subseteq\mathcal E_r=\mathcal E
$$
is the required filtration. This proves part (a).

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

The cotangent bundle on $\PP^2$ has total Chern class
$$
c\qty(\Omega^1_{\PP^2})=1-3H+3H^2,
$$
where
$$
H=c_1\qty(\OO_{\PP^2}(1)).
$$

::: pf-proof

The cotangent Euler sequence is
$$
0\longrightarrow\Omega^1_{\PP^2}
\longrightarrow\OO_{\PP^2}(-1)^{\oplus3}
\longrightarrow\OO_{\PP^2}
\longrightarrow0.
$$
Multiplicativity of total Chern classes in a short exact sequence gives
$$
c\qty(\Omega^1_{\PP^2})
=
c\qty(\OO(-1)^{\oplus3})
=(1-H)^3.
$$
Since $H^3=0$ in the Chow ring of $\PP^2$,
$$
(1-H)^3=1-3H+3H^2.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s4}

The bundle $\Omega^1_{\PP^2}$ is not an extension of invertible
sheaves.

::: pf-proof

Because $\Omega^1_{\PP^2}$ has rank two, a successive-extension filtration
would give an exact sequence
$$
0\longrightarrow\mathcal L
\longrightarrow\Omega^1_{\PP^2}
\longrightarrow\mathcal M
\longrightarrow0
$$
with $\mathcal L,\mathcal M$ invertible. The Picard group of $\PP^2$ is
generated by $\OO(1)$ [@Har10a, Proposition II.6.4 and Corollary II.6.16], so
for some integers $a,b$,
$$
\mathcal L\cong\OO(a),
\qquad
\mathcal M\cong\OO(b).
$$
Multiplicativity of Chern classes and step [](#s3){.pf-ref} would then give
$$
1-3H+3H^2
=
(1+aH)(1+bH)
=
1+(a+b)H+abH^2.
$$
Thus
$$
a+b=-3,
\qquad
ab=3.
$$
The integers $a,b$ would therefore be roots of
$$
t^2+3t+3=0,
$$
whose discriminant is
$$
9-12=-3.
$$
This is impossible over $\ZZ$. Hence
$$
\boxed{\Omega^1_{\PP^2}\text{ is not a successive extension of line bundles}.}
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s5}

For every integer $n\geq2$, there is a smooth projective $n$-fold with
a locally free sheaf that is not a successive extension of invertible sheaves.

::: pf-proof

For $n=2$, use step [](#s4){.pf-ref}. For $n>2$, put
$$
Y=\PP^2\times\PP^{n-2}
$$
and let
$$
\mathcal G=\operatorname{pr}_1^*\Omega^1_{\PP^2}.
$$
Suppose $\mathcal G$ admitted a filtration with invertible successive
quotients. Restrict it to a closed fibre
$$
\PP^2\times\{y\}\cong\PP^2.
$$
Because every successive quotient is locally free, restriction preserves the
short exact sequences in the filtration. It would therefore give a filtration
of
$$
\mathcal G|_{\PP^2\times\{y\}}
\cong
\Omega^1_{\PP^2}
$$
with invertible successive quotients, contradicting step [](#s4){.pf-ref}. Thus the
curve statement does not extend to any dimension $n\geq2$, proving part (b).

:::

the stated example on $\PP^2$.
