::: {.problem}
Let $f: X \to Y$ be a morphism of schemes.

(a) Show by example that if $\mcf$ is coherent on $X$, then $f_* \mcf$ need not be coherent on $Y$, even if $X$ and $Y$ are varieties over a field $k$.

(b) Show that a closed immersion is a finite morphism.

(c) If $f$ is a finite morphism of noetherian schemes, and if $\mcf$ is coherent on $X$, then $f_* \mcf$ is coherent on $Y$.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

For part (a), an example is
$$
\boxed{f:\AA_k^2\longrightarrow\AA_k^1,\quad (x,y)\longmapsto x,\quad \mcf=\OO_{\AA_k^2}}.
$$

::: pf-proof

Take $k$ algebraically closed, $A=k[x]$, and $B=k[x,y]$.
The morphism $f$ is induced by the inclusion $A\hookrightarrow B$.
Both affine spaces are varieties, and $\OO_{\AA_k^2}$ is [[D-QNTZY|coherent]] because $B$ is noetherian and free of rank one over itself.

For every $g\in A$, the inverse image of $D_Y(g)$ is $D_X(g)$, so
$$
(f_*\OO_X)(D_Y(g))=B_g.
$$
The restriction maps are the localization maps.
Thus $f_*\OO_X\cong\widetilde B$, with $B$ regarded as an $A$-module.
This module is not finitely generated: the $y$-degrees of any finite list of polynomials have an upper bound $d$, and their $k[x]$-linear combinations still have $y$-degree at most $d$, whereas $y^{d+1}$ does not.
By the affine characterization of [[D-QNTZY|coherent]] sheaves over a noetherian ring, $f_*\OO_X$ is not [[D-QNTZY|coherent]] [@Har10a, Proposition II.5.4].

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

Every [[D-MORIMM|closed immersion]] is [[D-MORFIN|finite]], proving part (b).

::: pf-proof

Let $f:X\to Y$ be a [[D-MORIMM|closed immersion]], and take an affine open $U=\Spec A\subseteq Y$.
The affine closed-subscheme theorem gives an ideal $I\subseteq A$ and an isomorphism
$$
f^{-1}(U)\cong\Spec(A/I)
$$
over $U$; under this isomorphism the restricted morphism is induced by $A\to A/I$ [@Har10a, Corollary II.5.10].
The $A$-module $A/I$ is generated by $1+I$.
Thus every member of an affine cover of $Y$ has affine inverse image with coordinate ring finite as an $A$-module, which is the definition of a [[D-MORFIN|finite morphism]].

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

A [[D-MORFIN|finite morphism]] of noetherian schemes carries [[D-QNTZY|coherent]] sheaves to [[D-QNTZY|coherent]] sheaves under direct image, proving part (c).

::: pf-proof

Choose an affine cover of $Y$ as in the definition of [[D-MORFIN|finiteness]].
On a member $U=\Spec A$, write $V=f^{-1}(U)=\Spec B$, where the structural map is $\varphi:A\to B$ and $B$ is a finite $A$-module.
Since $\mcf$ is [[D-QNTZY|coherent]], its restriction to $V$ is $\widetilde M$ for a finite $B$-module $M$ [@Har10a, Proposition II.5.4].
Let $N$ be $M$ regarded as an $A$-module through $\varphi$.

For $a\in A$, direct image and the sections of an associated sheaf give
$$
\begin{aligned}
(f_*\mcf)(D_U(a))
&=\mcf(D_V(\varphi(a)))\\
&\cong M_{\varphi(a)}\cong N_a.
\end{aligned}
$$
The last isomorphism identifies the fraction $m/\varphi(a)^n$ with $m/a^n$.
These identifications commute with restriction, so $(f_*\mcf)|_U\cong\widetilde N$.

Choose $A$-module generators $b_1,\ldots,b_r$ of $B$ and $B$-module generators $m_1,\ldots,m_s$ of $M$.
Writing each coefficient in a sum $\sum_j c_jm_j$ as an $A$-linear combination of the $b_i$ shows that the finitely many products $b_im_j$ generate $N$ over $A$.
Since $A$ is noetherian, $\widetilde N$ is [[D-QNTZY|coherent]].
This holds on the chosen affine cover, so $f_*\mcf$ is [[D-QNTZY|coherent]] on $Y$.

:::
