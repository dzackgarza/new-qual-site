::: {.problem}
Let $f: X \to Y$ be a morphism of schemes.

a. Show by example that if $\mcf$ is coherent on $X$, then $f_* \mcf$ need not be coherent on $Y$, even if $X$ and $Y$ are varieties over a field $k$.

b. Show that a closed immersion is a finite morphism.

c. If $f$ is a finite morphism of noetherian schemes, and if $\mcf$ is coherent on $X$, then $f_* \mcf$ is coherent on $Y$.
:::
