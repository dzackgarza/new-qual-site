::: problem
A curve of degree 5 and genus 2 in $\PP^3$ is contained in a unique quadric surface $Q$. Show that for any abstract curve $X$ of genus 2, there exist embeddings of degree 5 in $\PP^3$ for which $Q$ is nonsingular, and there exist other embeddings of degree 5 for which $Q$ is singular.
:::
