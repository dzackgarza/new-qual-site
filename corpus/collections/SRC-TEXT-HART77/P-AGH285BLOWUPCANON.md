::: {.problem}
As in (8.24), let $X$ be a nonsingular integral variety over an algebraically closed field $k$, let $Y$ be a nonempty nonsingular integral closed subvariety of codimension $r \geq 2$, let $\pi: \tilde X \to X$ be the blowing-up of $X$ along $Y$, and let $Y' = \inverseof{\pi}(Y)$.

(a) Show that the maps $\pi^*: \Pic X \to \Pic \tilde X$, and $\ZZ \to \Pic \tilde X$ defined by $n \mapsto$ the class of $nY'$, give rise to an isomorphism $\Pic \tilde X \cong \Pic X \oplus \ZZ$.

(b) Show that $$ \omega_{\tilde X} \cong \pi^* \omega_X \tensor \mcl((r-1) Y')
.
$$
:::

::: {.hint}
By (a), write $\omega_{\tilde X}\cong\pi^*\mcm\tensor\mcl(qY')$.
Restrict to $\tilde X\setminus Y'\cong X\setminus Y$ to show that $\mcm\cong\omega_X$.
Adjunction should give $\omega_{Y'}\cong(\pi|_{Y'})^*\omega_X\tensor\OO_{Y'}(-q-1)$.
For a closed point $y\in Y$ and its fibre $Z\subseteq Y'$, show that $\omega_Z\cong\OO_Z(-q-1)$.
Since $Z\cong\PP^{r-1}$ and $\omega_Z\cong\OO_Z(-r)$, this determines $q$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Write $E=Y'$, $U=X\setminus Y$, and $\widetilde U=\widetilde X\setminus E$.
Let $i:Y\hookrightarrow X$ and $p:E\to Y$ be the natural maps.
All divisor classes are identified with invertible sheaf classes by $D\mapsto\OO(D)$ on the nonsingular varieties.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

The varieties $\widetilde X$ and $E$ are nonsingular and integral, and
$$
E\cong\PP_Y(\mathcal I_Y/\mathcal I_Y^2),\qquad
\OO_{\widetilde X}(E)|_E\cong\OO_E(-1),\qquad
\widetilde U\cong U.
$$

::: pf-proof

The [blowup theorem for a smooth center](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0FUT) gives smoothness, the displayed exceptional projective bundle in the quotient convention, and $\OO_{\widetilde X}(-E)|_E\cong\OO_E(1)$ [@Har10a, Proposition II.8.24].
Dualizing gives the asserted sign for $\OO(E)|_E$.
The conormal bundle $\mathcal I_Y/\mathcal I_Y^2$ has rank $r$.
Since $Y$ is integral, its projective bundle is integral: local trivializations are projective spaces over integral opens and overlap over the generic point of $Y$.
Thus $E$ is an irreducible prime divisor.
The blowup of the integral $X$ along the proper center is integral, and is an isomorphism off that center, by the [[D-SCHBLOWUP|blowup construction]]. For every closed point $y\in Y$, its fibre $Z=p^{-1}(y)$ is $\PP_k^{r-1}$ and
$$
\OO_{\widetilde X}(E)|_Z\cong\OO_{\PP^{r-1}}(-1).
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

Restriction gives an isomorphism $\Pic(X)\to\Pic(U)$, and the kernel of $\Pic(\widetilde X)\to\Pic(\widetilde U)$ is generated by $[\OO_{\widetilde X}(E)]$.

::: pf-proof

On a regular integral noetherian variety, the [[D-5PQ5W|Cartier--Weil comparison]] identifies $\Pic$ with the Weil divisor class group [@Har10a, Corollary II.6.16].
Removing $Y$, whose codimension is at least two, removes no prime divisor of $X$.
Every prime divisor on $U$ closes to one on $X$, and the common function field gives the same principal-divisor relations.
Thus $\Cl(X)\to\Cl(U)$ is an isomorphism, and so is the Picard restriction.

For $\widetilde X$, the omitted set is the single prime divisor $E$.
The divisor restriction sequence [@Har10a, Proposition II.6.5] gives
$$
\ZZ[E]\longrightarrow\Cl(\widetilde X)
\longrightarrow\Cl(\widetilde U)\longrightarrow0.
$$
Applying the same Cartier--Weil identifications proves the kernel assertion.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

The isomorphism in (a) is
$$
\boxed{\Pic(X)\oplus\ZZ\xrightarrow{\cong}\Pic(\widetilde X),\qquad
([M],q)\longmapsto[\pi^*M\otimes\OO_{\widetilde X}(qE)].}
$$

::: pf-proof

It is a homomorphism by compatibility of pullback and tensor products.
Given an invertible sheaf $L$ on $\widetilde X$, use step [](#s2){.pf-ref} and $\widetilde U\cong U$ to choose $M$ on $X$ with $\pi^*M|_{\widetilde U}\cong L|_{\widetilde U}$.
Then $L\otimes(\pi^*M)^{-1}$ has trivial restriction and is $\OO(qE)$ for some $q$, again by step [](#s2){.pf-ref}. This proves surjectivity.

If $\pi^*M\otimes\OO(qE)$ is trivial, restriction to $\widetilde U$ and injectivity of $\Pic(X)\to\Pic(U)$ give $M\cong\OO_X$.
Restrict the remaining trivial sheaf $\OO(qE)$ to a fibre $Z=\PP_k^{r-1}$ from step [](#s1){.pf-ref}. This gives $\OO_Z(-q)\cong\OO_Z$.
Since $r-1\ge1$, $\Pic(Z)=\ZZ$ with generator $\OO_Z(1)$, so $q=0$ [@Har10a, Proposition II.6.4].
Thus the kernel is zero.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s4}

There is an integer $q$ with
$$
\omega_{\widetilde X}\cong\pi^*\omega_X\otimes\OO_{\widetilde X}(qE),
\qquad
\omega_E\cong(i\circ p)^*\omega_X\otimes\OO_E(-q-1).
$$

::: pf-proof

Step [](#s3){.pf-ref} first writes $\omega_{\widetilde X}=\pi^*M\otimes\OO(qE)$.
On $\widetilde U\cong U$, the differential sheaves and their top exterior powers agree under the isomorphism.
Thus $M|_U\cong\omega_X|_U$.
Injectivity in step [](#s2){.pf-ref} implies $M\cong\omega_X$ on $X$.

Apply [[D-MODCONORM|adjunction]] to the nonsingular divisor $E\subseteq\widetilde X$:
$$
\omega_E\cong(\omega_{\widetilde X}\otimes\OO_{\widetilde X}(E))|_E.
$$
Substitute the first formula and use $\OO(E)|_E=\OO_E(-1)$ from step [](#s1){.pf-ref}. This gives the second formula, including the exponent $-q-1$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s5}

Restricting to a fibre determines $q=r-1$ and proves (b).

::: pf-proof

Take a closed point $y\in Y$, put $m=\dim Y$, and let $Z=p^{-1}(y)\cong\PP_k^{r-1}$.
A projective-bundle trivialization over a neighborhood of $y$ identifies $p$ with projection from a product.
Consequently the conormal sheaf of this fibre is
$$
\mathcal I_{Z/E}/\mathcal I_{Z/E}^2
\cong(\mathfrak m_y/\mathfrak m_y^2)\otimes_k\OO_Z
\cong\OO_Z^{\oplus m}.
$$
This follows directly by pulling back the ideal of $y$ through the flat product projection; the residue cotangent space has dimension $m$ because $Y$ is nonsingular.
The exact conormal sequence for the nonsingular closed immersion $Z\hookrightarrow E$, followed by determinants, therefore gives
$$
\omega_E|_Z\cong\omega_Z,
$$
up to tensoring with the constant one-dimensional vector space $\det(\mathfrak m_y/\mathfrak m_y^2)$, whose associated invertible sheaf is trivial.

On $Z$, the pullback of $\omega_X$ from step [](#s4){.pf-ref} is also a constant invertible sheaf, since the map to $X$ factors through $y$.
Hence $\omega_Z\cong\OO_Z(-q-1)$.
But the canonical sheaf of $\PP^{r-1}$ is $\OO_Z(-r)$ [@Har10a, Example II.8.20.1].
Its Picard group is freely generated by $\OO_Z(1)$, so $-q-1=-r$ and $q=r-1$.
Substituting into step [](#s4){.pf-ref} gives
$$
\boxed{\omega_{\widetilde X}\cong\pi^*\omega_X\otimes\OO_{\widetilde X}((r-1)E).}
$$

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s3){.pf-ref} proves (a), and steps [](#s4){.pf-ref} and [](#s5){.pf-ref} prove (b) through the stated adjunction and exceptional-fibre comparison.
