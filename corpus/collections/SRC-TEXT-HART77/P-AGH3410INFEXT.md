::: problem
Let $X$ be a nonsingular variety over an algebraically closed field $k$, and let $\mcf$ be a coherent sheaf on $X$. Show that there is a one-to-one correspondence between the set of infinitesimal extensions of $X$ by $\mcf$ (II, Ex. 8.7) up to isomorphism, and the group $H^1(X, \mcf \tensor \mct)$, where $\mct$ is the tangent sheaf of $X$, see (II, §8).

Hint: Use (II, Ex. 8.6) and (4.5).
:::
