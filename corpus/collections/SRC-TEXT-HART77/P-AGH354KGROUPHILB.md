::: problem
Recall from (II, Ex. 6.10) the definition of the Grothendieck group $K(X)$ of a noetherian scheme $X$.

a. Let $X$ be a projective scheme over a field $k$, and let $\mco_X(1)$ be a very ample invertible sheaf on $X$. Show that there is a (unique) additive homomorphism
\[
P: K(X) \to \QQ[z]
\]
such that for each coherent sheaf $\mcf$ on $X$, $P(\gamma(\mcf))$ is the Hilbert polynomial of $\mcf$ (Ex. 5.2).

b. Now let $X=\PP_k^r$. For each $i=0,1, \ldots, r$, let $L_i$ be a linear space of dimension $i$ in $X$. Then show that

    (1) $K(X)$ is the free abelian group generated by $\ts{\gamma(\mco_{L_i}) \st i=0, \ldots, r}$, and

    (2) the map $P: K(X) \to \QQ[z]$ is injective.

Hint: Show that (1) $\Rightarrow$ (2). Then prove (1) and (2) simultaneously, by induction on $r$, using (II, Ex. 6.10c).
:::
