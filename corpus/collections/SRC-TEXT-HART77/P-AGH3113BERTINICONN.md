::: problem
Let $X$ be a normal, projective variety over an algebraically closed field $k$. Let $D$ be a linear system of effective Cartier divisors without base points, and assume that $D$ is **not composite with a pencil**, which means that if $f: X \to \PP_k^n$ is the morphism determined by $D$, then $\dim f(X) \geq 2$.

Show that every divisor in $D$ is connected.

Hints: see (10.9.1); use (11.5), (Ex. 5.7) and (7.9).
:::
