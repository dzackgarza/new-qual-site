::: {.problem}
Let $\ts{\mcf_i}$ be a direct system of sheaves on a noetherian topological space $X$.
Show that the presheaf $U \mapsto \varinjlim \mcf_i(U)$ is already a sheaf.
In particular,
\[
\Gamma\qty{X, \varinjlim \mcf_i} = \varinjlim \Gamma(X, \mcf_i).
\]
:::
