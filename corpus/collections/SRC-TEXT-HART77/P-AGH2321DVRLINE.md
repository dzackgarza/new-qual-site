::: problem
Let $R$ be a discrete valuation ring containing its residue field $k$.
Let $X = \Spec R[t]$ be the affine line over $\Spec R$.
Show that statements (a), (d), and (e) of Hartshorne II.3.20 are false for $X$.
:::
