::: {.problem}
Let $Y \subseteq \AA^3$ be the curve given parametrically by $x = t^3$, $y = t^4$, $z = t^5$.
Show that $I(Y)$ is a prime ideal of height $2$ in $k[x,y,z]$ which cannot be generated by $2$ elements.
We say $Y$ is not a local complete intersection; cf. (Ex. 2.17).
:::
