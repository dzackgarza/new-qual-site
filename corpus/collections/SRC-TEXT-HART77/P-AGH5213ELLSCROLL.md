::: problem
For every $e \geqslant-1$ and $n \geqslant e+3$, there is an elliptic scroll of degree $d=2 n-e$ in $\PP^{d-1}$.
In particular, there is an elliptic scroll of degree 5 in $\PP^4$.
:::
