::: problem
Let $\phi: \AA^n \to \AA^n$ be a morphism given by $n$ polynomials $f_1,\ldots,f_n$ in the variables $x_1,\ldots,x_n$, and let
$J = \det\qty{ \frac{\partial f_i}{\partial x_j} }$
be the **Jacobian polynomial** of $\phi$.

1. If $\phi$ is an isomorphism, in which case $\phi$ is called an **automorphism** of $\AA^n$, show that $J$ is a nonzero constant polynomial.
2. The converse is an unsolved problem, even for $n = 2$.
:::
