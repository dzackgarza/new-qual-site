::: {.problem}
Let $f: X \to Y$ be a proper, flat morphism of varieties over $k$.
Suppose that for some point $y \in Y$ the fibre $X_y$ is smooth over $k(y)$.
Show that there is an open neighborhood $U$ of $y$ in $Y$ such that $f: f^{-1}(U) \to U$ is smooth.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let
$$
V=\{x\in X \st f\text{ is smooth at }x\}.
$$

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

Every point of the fibre $X_y$ lies in $V$, and
$$
V=\bigl\{x\in X \st \dim_{\kappa(x)}\bigl(\Omega_{X/Y}\tensor_{\OO_X}\kappa(x)\bigr)=n\bigr\},
\qquad n=\dim X-\dim Y .
$$

::: pf-proof

Both $X$ and $Y$ are varieties over $k$, so $X$ is equidimensional of
dimension $\dim X$ and $Y$ is irreducible; with $n=\dim X-\dim Y$, condition (i)
of Corollary III.9.6 holds, and $f$ is flat, so by (ii) of that corollary every
irreducible component of every fibre of $f$ has dimension $n$; in particular
$\dim X_y=n$.

Fix $x\in X_y$. The fibre $X_y$ is a variety over $k(y)$ of dimension $n$, and it
is smooth over $k(y)$ by hypothesis. Relative differentials commute with base
change, Proposition II.8.10:
$$
\Omega_{X_y/k(y)}=\Omega_{X/Y}\tensor_{\OO_X}\OO_{X_y},
$$
so at $x$
$$
\Omega_{X/Y}\tensor_{\OO_X}\kappa(x)=\Omega_{X_y/k(y)}\tensor_{\OO_{X_y}}\kappa(x).
$$
A smooth morphism of relative dimension $n$ has locally free relative
differentials of rank $n$ (Theorem III.10.2, condition (2)), so this
$\kappa(x)$-vector space has dimension $n$; hence $x$ lies in the displayed set.

Conversely, if $f$ is smooth at $x$ then by Theorem III.10.2 the sheaf
$\Omega$ is locally free of rank equal to the relative dimension of $f$ at $x$,
and that relative dimension is $n$ because it equals $\dim X-\dim Y$; so
$x$ lies in $S$. Conversely, if $x\in S$ then $\Omega\otimes\kappa(x)$ has
dimension $n$, and $f$ is flat everywhere by hypothesis, so Theorem III.10.2
says that $f$ is smooth of relative dimension $n$ at $x$, i.e. $x\in V$.
Hence $S=V$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

The subset $V$ is open in $X$.

::: pf-proof

Write $\Omega=\Omega_{X/Y}$ and put
$$
S=\bigl\{x\in X \st \dim_{\kappa(x)}(\Omega\tensor_{\OO_X}\kappa(x))=n\bigr\}.
$$
The coherent sheaf $\Omega$ has rank at least $n$ at the generic point of $X$:
for the generic point $\eta_X$ of $X$, with $f(\eta_X)=\eta_Y$, the base-change
formula of Proposition II.8.10 gives
$$
\Omega\tensor\kappa(\eta_X)=\Omega_{\kappa(\eta_X)/\kappa(\eta_Y)},
$$
and $\dim X-\dim Y=n$ gives $\operatorname{tr\,deg}\kappa(\eta_X)/\kappa(\eta_Y)=n$, so
$\dim\kappa(\eta_X)\ge n$ by Theorem II.8.6A.

Now let $x\in S$. By Lemma II.8.9 (Nakayama applied to the finite
$\OO_{X,x}$-module $\Omega_x$), the stalk $\Omega_x$ is freely generated by $n$
elements, so $\Omega$ is free of rank $n$ on an open neighborhood of $x$
(Exercise II.5.7). On that neighborhood every point lies in $S$. Hence $S$ is
open, and by step [](#s1){.pf-ref} $S=V$. Therefore
$$
Z=X\setminus V
$$
is closed.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

The image $f(Z)$ is closed in $Y$ and does not contain $y$.

::: pf-proof

The morphism $f$ is proper, so it is closed. Since $Z$ is closed in $X$,
$$
f(Z)\subseteq Y
$$
is closed.

If $y\in f(Z)$, then some point of the fibre $X_y$ would lie in $Z$,
contradicting step [](#s1){.pf-ref}. Therefore
$$
y\notin f(Z).
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s4}

The required neighborhood is
$$
U=Y\setminus f(Z).
$$

::: pf-proof

By step [](#s3){.pf-ref}, $U$ is an open neighborhood of $y$.
Moreover, by definition of $U$,
$$
f^{-1}(U)\cap Z=\varnothing,
$$
so
$$
f^{-1}(U)\subseteq V.
$$
Thus $f$ is smooth at every point of $f^{-1}(U)$, which says precisely that
the restricted morphism
$$
f:f^{-1}(U)\longrightarrow U
$$
is smooth.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Steps [](#s1){.pf-ref} and [](#s2){.pf-ref} identify the smooth locus with the open set where
$\Omega_{X/Y}$ has the expected rank, and show that it contains the whole fibre over
$y$; steps [](#s3){.pf-ref} and [](#s4){.pf-ref} use properness to remove the image of its complement
from the base.

:::
