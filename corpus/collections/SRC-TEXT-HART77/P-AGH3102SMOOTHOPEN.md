::: problem
Let $f: X \to Y$ be a proper, flat morphism of varieties over $k$. Suppose that for some point $y \in Y$ the fibre $X_y$ is smooth over $k(y)$. Show that there is an open neighborhood $U$ of $y$ in $Y$ such that $f: f^{-1}(U) \to U$ is smooth.
:::
