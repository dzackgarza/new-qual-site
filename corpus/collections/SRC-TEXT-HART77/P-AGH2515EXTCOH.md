::: {.problem}
We prove the following theorem in several steps: let $X$ be a noetherian scheme, let $U$ be an open subset, and let $\mcf$ be a coherent sheaf on $U$.
Then there is a coherent sheaf $\mcf'$ on $X$ such that $\ro{\mcf'}{U} \cong \mcf$.

a. On a noetherian affine scheme, every quasi-coherent sheaf is the union of its coherent subsheaves.
We say a sheaf $\mcf$ is the union of subsheaves $\mcf_\alpha$ if for every open set $U$ the group $\mcf(U)$ is the union of the subgroups $\mcf_\alpha(U)$.

b. Let $X$ be an affine noetherian scheme, $U$ an open subset, and $\mcf$ coherent on $U$.
Then there exists a coherent sheaf $\mcf'$ on $X$ with $\ro{\mcf'}{U} \cong \mcf$.
*Hint:* let $i: U \to X$ be the inclusion; show $i_* \mcf$ is quasi-coherent, then use (a).

c. With $X, U, \mcf$ as in (b), suppose further we are given a quasi-coherent sheaf $\mcg$ on $X$ such that $\mcf \subseteq \ro{\mcg}{U}$.
Show that we can find $\mcf'$ a coherent subsheaf of $\mcg$ with $\ro{\mcf'}{U} \cong \mcf$.
*Hint:* same method, but replace $i_* \mcf$ by $\rho\inv(i_* \mcf)$, where $\rho$ is the natural map $\mcg \to i_*(\ro{\mcg}{U})$.

d. Now let $X$ be any noetherian scheme, $U$ an open subset, $\mcf$ a coherent sheaf on $U$, and $\mcg$ a quasi-coherent sheaf on $X$ with $\mcf \subseteq \ro{\mcg}{U}$.
Show that there is a coherent subsheaf $\mcf' \subseteq \mcg$ on $X$ with $\ro{\mcf'}{U} \cong \mcf$.
Taking $\mcg = i_* \mcf$ proves the result announced above.
*Hint:* cover $X$ with open affines, and extend over one of them at a time.

e. As an extra corollary, show that on a noetherian scheme any quasi-coherent sheaf $\mcf$ is the union of its coherent subsheaves.
*Hint:* if $s$ is a section of $\mcf$ over an open set $U$, apply (d) to the subsheaf of $\ro{\mcf}{U}$ generated by $s$.
:::
