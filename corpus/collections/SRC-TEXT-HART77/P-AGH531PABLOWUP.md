::: {.problem}
Let $X$ be a nonsingular projective variety of any dimension, let $Y$ be a nonsingular subvariety, and let $\pi: \tilde{X} \rightarrow X$ be obtained by blowing up $Y$.
Show that $p_a(\tilde{X})=p_a(X)$.
:::
