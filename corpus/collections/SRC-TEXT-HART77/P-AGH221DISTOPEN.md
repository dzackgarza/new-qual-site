::: problem
Let $A$ be a ring, let $X = \Spec A$, let $f \in A$, and let $D(f) \subseteq X$ be the open complement of $V(f)$.
Show that the locally ringed space $\qty{D(f), \ro{\OO_X}{D(f)}}$ is isomorphic to $\Spec A_f$.
:::
