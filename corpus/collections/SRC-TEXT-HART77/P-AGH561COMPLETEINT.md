::: problem
Let $X$ be a surface in $\PP^n$, $n \geqslant 3$, defined as the complete intersection of hypersurfaces of degrees $d_1, \ldots, d_{n-2}$, with each $d_i \geqslant 2$. Show that for all but finitely many choices of $\left(n, d_1, \ldots, d_{n-2}\right)$, the surface $X$ is of general type. List the exceptional cases, and where they fit into the classification picture.
:::
