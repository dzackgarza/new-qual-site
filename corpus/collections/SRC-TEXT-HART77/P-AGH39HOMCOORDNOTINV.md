::: problem
The homogeneous coordinate ring of a projective variety is not invariant under isomorphism.
Let $X = \PP^1$ and let $Y$ be the $2$-uple embedding of $\PP^1$ in $\PP^2$.
Then $X \cong Y$, but show that $S(X) \not\cong S(Y)$.
:::
