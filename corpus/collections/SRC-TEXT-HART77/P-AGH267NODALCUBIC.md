::: problem
Let $X$ be the nodal cubic curve $y^2 z = x^3 + x^2 z$ in $\PP^2$.
Show that the group of Cartier divisors of degree $0$, written $\mathrm{CaCl}^\circ X$, is naturally isomorphic to the multiplicative group $\GG_m$.
:::
