::: problem
1. Show that an irreducible curve $Y$ of degree $d > 1$ in $\PP^2$ cannot have a point of multiplicity $\geq d$.
2. If $Y$ is an irreducible curve of degree $d > 1$ having a point of multiplicity $d - 1$, show that $Y$ is a rational curve.
:::
