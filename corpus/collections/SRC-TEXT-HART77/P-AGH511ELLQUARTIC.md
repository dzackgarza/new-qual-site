::: problem
Let $Y$ be the algebraic set in $\PP^3$ defined by the equations
$$
x^2 - xz - yw = 0, \qquad yz - xw - zw = 0.
$$
Let $P$ be the point $(x,y,z,w) = (0,0,0,1)$, and let $\varphi$ denote the projection from $P$ to the plane $w = 0$. Show that $\varphi$ induces an isomorphism of $Y \sm\ts{P}$ with the plane cubic curve $y^2 z - x^3 + x z^2 = 0$ minus the point $(1,0,-1)$. Then show that $Y$ is an irreducible nonsingular curve.

This $Y$ is called the *elliptic quartic curve* in $\PP^3$. Since it is defined by two equations it is another example of a complete intersection.
:::
