::: problem
A homogeneous polynomial $f$ of degree $d$ in three variables $x, y, z$ has $\binom{d+2}{2}$ coefficients.
Let these coefficients represent a point in $\PP^N$, where
$$
N = \binom{d+2}{2} - 1 = \frac{1}{2} d(d+3).
$$

1. Show that this gives a correspondence between points of $\PP^N$ and algebraic sets in $\PP^2$ that can be defined by an equation of degree $d$.
   The correspondence is one-to-one except in some cases where $f$ has a multiple factor.

2. Show that under this correspondence the irreducible nonsingular curves of degree $d$ correspond one-to-one to the points of a nonempty Zariski-open subset of $\PP^N$.

*Hint:* Use elimination theory applied to the homogeneous polynomials $\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_0}, \ldots, \frac{\partial f}{\partial x_n}$.
:::
