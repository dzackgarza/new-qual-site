::: {.problem}
Let $S$ be a graded ring, generated by $S_1$ as an $S_0\dash$algebra.
For any integer $d > 0$, let $S^{(d)}$ be the graded ring $\bigoplus_{n \geq 0} S^{(d)}_n$ where $S^{(d)}_n = S_{nd}$.
Let $X = \Proj S$.
Show that $\Proj S^{(d)} \cong X$, and that the sheaf $\OO(1)$ on $\Proj S^{(d)}$ corresponds via this isomorphism to $\OO_X(d)$.

This construction is related to the $d\dash$uple embedding as follows.
If $x_0, \ldots, x_r$ generate $S_1$, corresponding to an embedding $X \injects \PP^r_A$, then the monomials of degree $d$ in the $x_i$ generate $S^{(d)}_1 = S_d$.
These define a projective embedding of $\Proj S^{(d)}$ which is the image of $X$ under the $d\dash$uple embedding of $\PP^r_A$.
:::
