::: problem
1. Show that any conic in $\AA^2$ is isomorphic either to $\AA^1$ or to $\AA^1 \sm \ts{0}$.
2. Show that $\AA^1$ is not isomorphic to any proper open subset of itself.
3. Show that any conic in $\PP^2$ is isomorphic to $\PP^1$.
4. Show that $\AA^2$ is not even homeomorphic to $\PP^2$.
5. Show that if an affine variety is isomorphic to a projective variety, then it consists of a single point.
:::
