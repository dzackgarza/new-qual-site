::: problem
Show that a composition of projective morphisms is projective.
Conclude that projective morphisms have the stability properties (a)--(f) of the preceding exercise.

*Hint:* use the Segre embedding, and show that it gives a closed immersion $\PP^r \times \PP^s \injects \PP^{r + s + rs}$.
:::
