::: {.problem}
Let $X$ be a noetherian scheme.
Show that $X$ is affine if and only if $X_{\mathrm{red}}$ (II, Ex.
2.3) is affine.

Hint: Use (3.7), and for any coherent sheaf $\mcf$ on $X$, consider the filtration $\mcf \supseteq \mcn \cdot \mcf \supseteq \mcn^2 \cdot \mcf \supseteq \cdots$, where $\mcn$ is the sheaf of nilpotent elements on $X$.
:::

::::: {.solution}
Let $\mcn \subseteq \OO_X$ be the sheaf of nilpotent sections, the ideal sheaf of the closed immersion $X_{\mathrm{red}} \to X$.

<1>1. If $X = \Spec A$ is affine, then $X_{\mathrm{red}} = \Spec A/N$ for the nilradical $N$ of $A$, so $X_{\mathrm{red}}$ is affine.

::: {.proof}
On an affine scheme the reduced induced structure is defined by the nilradical.
:::

<1>2. $\mcn^n = 0$ for some $n \geq 1$.

::: {.proof}
Cover $X$ by finitely many affine opens $\Spec A_j$ with $A_j$ Noetherian; $\mcn$ restricts on $\Spec A_j$ to the sheaf of the nilradical $N_j$.
$N_j$ is generated by finitely many nilpotent elements $x_1, \ldots, x_s$, with $x_i^m = 0$ for all $i$, and every product of $s(m-1) + 1$ generators contains some $x_i$ at least $m$ times, so $N_j^{s(m-1)+1} = 0$.
Take $n$ to be the largest of these finitely many exponents.
:::

<1>3. If $X_{\mathrm{red}}$ is affine, then $H^1(X, \mcf) = 0$ for every coherent sheaf $\mcf$ on $X$.

::: {.proof}
For $0 \leq i < n$, the coherent sheaf $\mcn^i \mcf / \mcn^{i+1} \mcf$ is annihilated by $\mcn$, so it is the pushforward of a coherent sheaf on $X_{\mathrm{red}}$ along the closed immersion, which is a homeomorphism; its $H^1$ on $X$ is its $H^1$ on the affine Noetherian scheme $X_{\mathrm{red}}$, which is $0$.
The exact sequences $0 \to \mcn^{i+1} \mcf \to \mcn^i \mcf \to \mcn^i \mcf / \mcn^{i+1} \mcf \to 0$ give $H^1(X, \mcn^{i+1} \mcf) = 0 \Rightarrow H^1(X, \mcn^i \mcf) = 0$.
Since $\mcn^n \mcf = 0$ by step <1>2, descending induction on $i$ gives $H^1(X, \mcf) = 0$.
:::

<1>4. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>1 is the forward implication.
Conversely, if $X_{\mathrm{red}}$ is affine, step <1>3 gives $H^1(X, \mci) = 0$ for every coherent sheaf of ideals $\mci$, so $X$ is affine by Serre's criterion [[T-5IOUR]].
:::
