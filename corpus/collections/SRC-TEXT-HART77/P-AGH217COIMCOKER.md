::: problem
Let $\varphi: \mcf \to \mcg$ be a morphism of sheaves.

a. Show that $\im \varphi \cong \mcf / \ker \varphi$.

b. Show that $\coker \varphi \cong \mcg / \im \varphi$.
:::
