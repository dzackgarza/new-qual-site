::: problem
Use (4.11) to show that if $D$ is any ample divisor on the cubic surface $X$, then $H^1\left(X, \mathcal{O}_X(-D)\right)=0$. This is Kodaira's vanishing theorem for the cubic surface (III, 7.15).
:::
