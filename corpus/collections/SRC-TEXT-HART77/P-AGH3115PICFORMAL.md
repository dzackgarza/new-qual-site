::: problem
Let $Y$ be a hypersurface in $X = \PP_k^N$ with $N \geq 4$.
Let $\hat{X}$ be the formal completion of $X$ along $Y$ (II, §9). Prove that the natural map
\[
\Pic \hat{X} \to \Pic Y
\]
is an isomorphism.

Hint: use (II, Ex.
9.6), and then study the maps $\Pic X_{n+1} \to \Pic X_n$ for each $n$ using (Ex.
4.6) and (Ex.
5.5).
:::
