::: {.problem}
Let $X$ be a noetherian scheme.
Define $K(X)$ to be the quotient of the free abelian group generated by all coherent sheaves on $X$, by the subgroup generated by all expressions $\mcf - \mcf' - \mcf''$ whenever there is an exact sequence $0 \to \mcf' \to \mcf \to \mcf'' \to 0$ of coherent sheaves on $X$.
If $\mcf$ is a coherent sheaf, denote by $\gamma(\mcf)$ its image in $K(X)$.

(a) If $X = \AA^1_k$, then $K(X) \cong \ZZ$.

(b) If $X$ is any integral scheme and $\mcf$ a coherent sheaf, define the **rank** of $\mcf$ to be $\dim_K \mcf_\xi$, where $\xi$ is the generic point of $X$ and $K = \OO_\xi$ is the function field of $X$.
Show that the rank function defines a surjective homomorphism $\rank: K(X) \to \ZZ$.

(c) If $Y$ is a closed subscheme of $X$, there is an exact sequence
$$
K(Y) \to K(X) \to K(X - Y) \to 0,
$$
where the first map is extension by zero and the second is restriction.

For further information about $K(X)$, and its applications to the generalized Riemann--Roch theorem, see Borel--Serre, Manin, and Hartshorne Appendix A.
:::

::: {.hint}
For exactness in the middle of part (c), show that if $\mcf$ is a coherent sheaf on $X$ whose support is contained in $Y$, then there is a finite filtration $\mcf = \mcf_0 \supseteq \mcf_1 \supseteq \cdots \supseteq \mcf_n = 0$ such that each $\mcf_i/\mcf_{i+1}$ is an $\OO_Y$-module.
To show surjectivity on the right, use (Ex. 5.15).
:::

::: {.solution}
Write $\gamma_X(\mathcal F)$ for the class of a coherent sheaf in $K(X)$.
All schemes in the problem remain noetherian; the letter $K$ in part (b) denotes the function field, not the group $K(X)$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

On the affine line, rank gives $\boxed{K(\AA_k^1)\cong\ZZ}$, with inverse $n\mapsto n\gamma_{\AA^1}(\OO_{\AA^1})$.

::: pf-proof

Coherent sheaves on $\Spec k[t]$ correspond exactly to finite $k[t]$-modules, and this correspondence is exact [@Har10a, Proposition II.5.4].
The structure theorem over the PID $R=k[t]$ writes every such module as
$$
M\cong R^r\oplus\bigoplus_{i=1}^sR/(f_i),\qquad f_i\ne0.
$$
The exact sequence
$$
0\longrightarrow R\xrightarrow{\cdot f_i}R\longrightarrow R/(f_i)\longrightarrow0
$$
shows that each torsion summand has zero Grothendieck class.
Thus $\gamma(\widetilde M)=r\gamma(\OO_{\AA^1})$, so this one class generates the group.
Tensoring with $k(t)$ is exact and sends it to a one-dimensional vector space.
Consequently $[M]\mapsto\dim_{k(t)}(M\otimes_Rk(t))$ is an additive inverse to the asserted map from $\ZZ$; in particular, the generator has infinite order.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

For any integral noetherian $X$, rank induces a surjective homomorphism $K(X)\to\ZZ$.

::: pf-proof

Taking the stalk at the generic point $\xi$ is exact.
For a coherent sheaf this stalk is finite-dimensional over the field $\OO_{X,\xi}$.
The dimension formula for a short exact sequence of finite-dimensional vector spaces therefore shows that rank respects every defining relation of $K(X)$.
Since $\rank\OO_X=1$, every integer is the rank of an element $n\gamma_X(\OO_X)$.
This proves part (b) and also splits the rank homomorphism.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

In part (c), let $i:Y\hookrightarrow X$, $j:U=X\setminus Y\hookrightarrow X$, and let $\mathcal I$ be the ideal sheaf of $Y$.
If $\mathcal F$ is coherent with support contained in $Y$, then $\mathcal I^N\mathcal F=0$ for some integer $N\ge1$.

::: pf-proof

On an affine open $\Spec A$, write $\mathcal F=\widetilde M$ and $\mathcal I=\widetilde I$, with $M$ and $I$ finite.
The support condition gives
$$
V(\Ann_A M)\subseteq V(I),\qquad I\subseteq\sqrt{\Ann_A M}.
$$
The support formula for finite modules is proved in [[P-AGH256SUPPORT]], step [](#s2){.pf-ref}.
Choose generators $a_1,\ldots,a_s$ of $I$ and positive integers $e_i$ with $a_i^{e_i}M=0$.
For $N=1+\sum_i(e_i-1)$, every monomial of total degree $N$ in these generators is divisible by some $a_i^{e_i}$.
Hence $I^NM=0$.
When $I=0$, take $N=1$.
Taking the maximum of these exponents over a finite affine cover proves the global assertion.

The finite filtration
$$
\mathcal F\supseteq\mathcal I\mathcal F\supseteq\cdots\supseteq\mathcal I^N\mathcal F=0
$$
has coherent quotients annihilated by $\mathcal I$.
Each quotient is therefore $i_*\mathcal G$ for a coherent $\OO_Y$-module $\mathcal G$: on affines this is the identification of finite $A$-modules annihilated by $I$ with finite $A/I$-modules, and these identifications glue.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s4}

Let $H\subseteq K(X)$ be the subgroup generated by classes of coherent sheaves supported on $Y$.
Then $\im(i_*:K(Y)\to K(X))=H$, and restriction induces a surjection
$$
\overline{j^*}:K(X)/H\longrightarrow K(U).
$$

::: pf-proof

Direct image under a closed immersion is exact and preserves coherence, so it induces $i_*$ on Grothendieck groups.
Every $i_*\mathcal G$ is supported on $Y$.
Conversely, the filtration in step [](#s3){.pf-ref} expresses the class of every supported coherent sheaf as a sum of such direct-image classes.
This proves $\im i_*=H$.

Restriction to $U$ is exact and annihilates supported sheaves, so $j^*$ factors through $K(X)/H$.
Every coherent sheaf on $U$ extends to a coherent sheaf on $X$ by [[P-AGH2515EXTCOH]], step [](#s5){.pf-ref}.
Its class is therefore in the image of $\overline{j^*}$, proving surjectivity.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s5}

Two coherent sheaves $\mathcal F_1,\mathcal F_2$ on $X$ whose restrictions to $U$ are isomorphic have the same class in $K(X)/H$.

::: pf-proof

Choose an isomorphism $\alpha:\mathcal F_1|_U\to\mathcal F_2|_U$.
Its graph is a coherent subsheaf of $(\mathcal F_1\oplus\mathcal F_2)|_U$.
The subsheaf-extension assertion of [[P-AGH2515EXTCOH]], step [](#s5){.pf-ref}, extends it to a coherent subsheaf
$$
\mathcal G\subseteq\mathcal F_1\oplus\mathcal F_2.
$$
Both projections $p_i:\mathcal G\to\mathcal F_i$ are isomorphisms on $U$.
Their kernels and cokernels are coherent and supported on $Y$.
Factoring $p_i$ through its image and using the two short exact sequences gives
$$
\gamma_X(\mathcal G)-\gamma_X(\mathcal F_i)
=\gamma_X(\ker p_i)-\gamma_X(\operatorname{coker}p_i)\in H.
$$
Thus both sheaves have the same class as $\mathcal G$ modulo $H$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s6}

Extending coherent sheaves from $U$ defines a homomorphism $s:K(U)\to K(X)/H$ inverse to $\overline{j^*}$.

::: pf-proof

For a coherent sheaf $\mathcal E$ on $U$, choose a coherent extension $\mathcal F$ on $X$ and assign the class $\gamma_X(\mathcal F)+H$.
Step [](#s5){.pf-ref} proves independence of the extension and of the chosen identification on $U$.

To verify additivity, consider an exact sequence
$$
0\longrightarrow\mathcal E'\longrightarrow\mathcal E\longrightarrow\mathcal E''\longrightarrow0
$$
on $U$.
Choose an extension $\mathcal F$ of the middle sheaf.
Extend $\mathcal E'\subseteq\mathcal F|_U$ to a coherent subsheaf $\mathcal F'\subseteq\mathcal F$, again by [[P-AGH2515EXTCOH]], step [](#s5){.pf-ref}.
The coherent quotient $\mathcal F''=\mathcal F/\mathcal F'$ restricts to $\mathcal E''$.
The exact sequence on $X$ then gives
$$
\gamma_X(\mathcal F)+H
=(\gamma_X(\mathcal F')+H)+(\gamma_X(\mathcal F'')+H).
$$
Thus the assignment respects every defining relation and induces $s$.

Restricting an extension returns the original sheaf, so $\overline{j^*}\circ s=\id$.
Conversely, for a coherent sheaf $\mathcal F$ on $X$, its own class is one permitted extension of $\mathcal F|_U$.
Hence $s\circ\overline{j^*}=\id$ on the generators of $K(X)/H$, and therefore on the whole group.

:::
