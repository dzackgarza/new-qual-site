::: {.problem}
For any closed subscheme $X \subseteq \PP^n$, we denote by $C(X) \subseteq \PP^{n+1}$ the projective cone over $X$ (I, Ex.
2.10). If $I \subseteq k[x_0, \ldots, x_n]$ is the largest homogeneous ideal of $X$, then $C(X)$ is defined by the ideal generated by $I$ in $k[x_0, \ldots, x_{n+1}]$.

a. Give an example to show that if $\ts{X_t}$ is a flat family of closed subschemes of $\PP^n$, then $\ts{C(X_t)}$ need not be a flat family in $\PP^{n+1}$.

b. To remedy this situation, we make the following definition.
Let $X \subseteq \PP_T^n$ be a closed subscheme, where $T$ is a noetherian integral scheme.
For each $t \in T$, let $I_t \subseteq S_t = k(t)[x_0, \ldots, x_n]$ be the homogeneous ideal of $X_t$ in $\PP_{k(t)}^n$.
We say that the family $\ts{X_t}$ is **very flat** if for all $d \geq 0$ the dimension
\[
\dim_{k(t)} (S_t / I_t)_d
\]
is independent of $t$.
Here $(\cdot)_d$ means the homogeneous part of degree $d$.

c. If $\ts{X_t}$ is a very flat family in $\PP^n$, show that it is flat.
Show also that $\ts{C(X_t)}$ is a very flat family in $\PP^{n+1}$, and hence flat.

d. If $\ts{X_{(t)}}$ is an algebraic family of projectively normal varieties in $\PP_k^n$, parametrized by a nonsingular curve $T$ over an algebraically closed field $k$, then $\ts{X_{(t)}}$ is a very flat family of schemes.
:::
