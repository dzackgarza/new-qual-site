::: {.problem}
Let $S$ be a scheme, let $X$ be a reduced scheme over $S$, and let $Y$ be a separated scheme over $S$.
Let $f$ and $g$ be two $S\dash$morphisms from $X$ to $Y$ which agree on an open dense subset of $X$.
Show that $f = g$.

Give examples to show that this result fails if either

a. $X$ is nonreduced, or

b. $Y$ is nonseparated.

*Hint:* consider the map $h: X \to \fiberprod{Y}{S}{Y}$ obtained from $f$ and $g$.
:::
