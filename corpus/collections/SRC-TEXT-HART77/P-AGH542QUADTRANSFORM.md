::: problem
Let $\varphi$ be the quadratic transformation of $(4.2.3)$, centered at $P_1, P_2, P_3$. If $C$ is an irreducible curve of degree $d$ in $\PP^2$, with points of multiplicity $r_1, r_2, r_3$ at $P_1, P_2, P_3$, then the strict transform $C^{\prime}$ of $C$ by $\varphi$ has degree
\[
d^{\prime}=2 d-r_1-r_2-r_3
\]
and has points of multiplicity

- $d-r_2-r_3$ at $Q_1$,
- $d-r_1-r_3$ at $Q_2$ and
- $d-r_1-r_2$ at $Q_3$.

The curve $C$ may have arbitrary singularities.

Hint: Use (Ex. 3.2).
:::
