::: problem
A variety $Y$ is *rational* if it is birationally equivalent to $\PP^n$ for some $n$; equivalently, if $K(Y)$ is a purely transcendental extension of $k$.

1. Show that any conic in $\PP^2$ is a rational curve.

2. Show that the cuspidal cubic $y^2 = x^3$ is a rational curve.

3. Let $Y$ be the nodal cubic curve $y^2 z = x^2(x+z)$ in $\PP^2$.
   Show that the projection $\varphi$ from the point $P = (0,0,1)$ to the line $z = 0$ induces a birational map from $Y$ to $\PP^1$.
   Thus $Y$ is a rational curve.
:::
