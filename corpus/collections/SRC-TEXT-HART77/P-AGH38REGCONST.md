::: problem
Let $H_i$ and $H_j$ be the hyperplanes in $\PP^n$ defined by $x_i = 0$ and $x_j = 0$, with $i \neq j$.
Show that any regular function on $\PP^n \sm (H_i \intersect H_j)$ is constant.
:::
