::: problem
Let $Y^r \subseteq \PP^n$ be a variety of degree $2$. Show that $Y$ is contained in a linear subspace $L$ of dimension $r + 1$ in $\PP^n$. Thus $Y$ is isomorphic to a quadric hypersurface in $\PP^{r+1}$.
:::
