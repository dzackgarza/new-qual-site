::: problem
Use the result of (Ex. 9.1) to prove the following geometric result.
Let $Y \subseteq X = \PP^n_k$ be as above, and let $f: X \to Z$ be a morphism of $k\dash$varieties.
Suppose that $f(Y)$ is a single closed point $P \in Z$.
Then $f(X) = P$ also.
:::
