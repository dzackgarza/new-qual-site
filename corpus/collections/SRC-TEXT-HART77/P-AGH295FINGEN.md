::: {.problem}
If $\mcf$ is a coherent sheaf on a noetherian formal scheme $\mathfrak{X}$ which can be generated by global sections, show in fact that it can be generated by a finite number of its global sections.
:::

::: {.solution}
Choose a family $(s_\lambda)_{\lambda\in\Lambda}$ of global sections generating $\mcf$.
We will find a finite subfamily which still generates it.
The [[D-SCHFORMAL|noetherian hypothesis]] includes quasi-compactness of the underlying space.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

For each $x\in\mathfrak X$, finitely many of the $s_\lambda$ generate $\mcf$ on an open neighborhood of $x$.

::: pf-proof

The coherent sheaf $\mcf$ is locally of finite type, so $\mcf_x$ is a finite $\OO_{\mathfrak X,x}$-module [@Har10a, Chapter II, §9].
Express a finite generating set of this module as finite linear combinations of the germs $(s_\lambda)_x$.
Only finitely many indices occur; let $\Lambda_x\subseteq\Lambda$ be their union.
The corresponding map
$$
\OO_{\mathfrak X}^{\oplus\Lambda_x}\longrightarrow\mcf
$$
is surjective at $x$.

Its cokernel $Q_x$ is locally of finite type because it is a quotient of the locally finite-type sheaf $\mcf$.
Choose an open neighborhood $V$ of $x$ and finitely many sections generating $Q_x|_V$.
Their germs at $x$ are all zero, since $(Q_x)_x=0$.
Each section therefore vanishes on a neighborhood of $x$; intersect these finitely many neighborhoods to obtain an open $U_x\subseteq V$ on which all the generators vanish.
It follows that $Q_x|_{U_x}=0$.
The sections indexed by $\Lambda_x$ thus generate $\mcf$ throughout $U_x$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

A finite subfamily generates $\mcf$ on all of $\mathfrak X$.

::: pf-proof

The open sets $U_x$ of step [](#s1){.pf-ref} cover $\mathfrak X$.
By quasi-compactness, choose a finite subcover $U_{x_1},\ldots,U_{x_t}$.
The set
$$
\Lambda_0=\Lambda_{x_1}\cup\cdots\cup\Lambda_{x_t}
$$
is finite.
At every point, one of these finite subsets already generates the stalk, so the sections indexed by $\Lambda_0$ generate every stalk.
Equivalently, with $N=\#\Lambda_0$, their evaluation map is a surjection
$$
\boxed{\OO_{\mathfrak X}^{\oplus N}\twoheadrightarrow\mcf.}
$$
For the empty formal scheme or the zero sheaf, an empty generating family suffices.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1){.pf-ref} gives finite generating subfamilies on neighborhoods, and step [](#s2){.pf-ref} selects finitely many of those neighborhoods to give the required global finite family.

:::

:::

:::

::: {.remark title="Quasi-compactness cannot simply be omitted"}
Let $\mathfrak X=\coprod_{j\ge1}\Spec k$, viewed as a formal scheme with the zero ideal of definition, and let $\mcf$ restrict to $\OO^{\oplus j}$ on its $j$th component.
This formal scheme is locally noetherian but not quasi-compact, and $\mcf$ is coherent because coherence is local and each component has a finite free module.
The sections given by one coordinate vector on one component and zero on all other components generate $\mcf$ globally.
However, any $N$ global sections give at most $N$ vectors on the $(N+1)$st component, and cannot span its $(N+1)$-dimensional fibre.
Thus no finite global generating family exists in this locally noetherian example.
:::
