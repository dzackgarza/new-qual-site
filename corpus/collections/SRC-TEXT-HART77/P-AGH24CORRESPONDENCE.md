::: problem
Let $S \da k[x_0,\ldots,x_n]$.

1. Show that $Y \mapsto I(Y)$ and $\mfa \mapsto Z(\mfa)$ give a one-to-one inclusion-reversing correspondence between algebraic sets in $\PP^n$ and homogeneous radical ideals of $S$ not equal to $S_+$.
   Because $S_+$ does not occur in this correspondence, it is called the **irrelevant maximal ideal** of $S$.
2. An algebraic set $Y \subseteq \PP^n$ is irreducible if and only if $I(Y)$ is a prime ideal.
3. Show that $\PP^n$ is itself irreducible.
:::
