::: problem
Let $\pi: \tilde{X} \rightarrow X$ be a monoidal transformation, and let $D$ be a very ample divisor on $X$. Show that $2 \pi^* D-E$ is ample on $\tilde{X}$.

Hint: Use a suitable generalization of (I, Ex. 7.5) to curves in $\PP^n$.
:::
