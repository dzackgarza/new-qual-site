::: {.problem}
For a point $P$ on a variety $X$, let $\mfm$ be the maximal ideal of the local ring $\mco_P$.
Define the *Zariski tangent space* $T_P(X)$ of $X$ at $P$ to be the dual $k\da$vector space of $\mfm/\mfm^2$.

1. For any point $P \in X$, show that $\dim T_P(X) \geq \dim X$, with equality if and only if $P$ is nonsingular.

2. For any morphism $\varphi: X \to Y$, show that there is a natural induced $k\da$linear map $T_P(\varphi): T_P(X) \to T_{\varphi(P)}(Y)$.

3. If $\varphi$ is the vertical projection of the parabola $x = y^2$ onto the $x\da$axis, show that the induced map $T_0(\varphi)$ of tangent spaces at the origin is the zero map.
:::
