::: problem
Let $X$ be a regular noetherian scheme, and $\mce$ a locally free coherent sheaf of rank $\geq 2$ on $X$.

a. Show that $\Pic \PP(\mce) \cong \Pic X \times \ZZ$.

b. If $\mce'$ is another locally free coherent sheaf on $X$, show that $\PP(\mce) \cong \PP(\mce')$ over $X$ if and only if there is an invertible sheaf $\mcl$ on $X$ with $\mce' \cong \mce \tensor \mcl$.
:::
