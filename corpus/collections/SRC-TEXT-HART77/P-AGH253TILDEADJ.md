::: problem
Let $X = \Spec A$ be an affine scheme.
Show that the functors $\sim$ and $\Gamma$ are adjoint in the following sense: for any $A\dash$module $M$, and for any sheaf of $\OO_X\dash$modules $\mcf$, there is a natural isomorphism
\[
\Hom_A(M, \Gamma(X, \mcf)) \cong \Hom_{\OO_X}(\tilde M, \mcf)
.\]
:::
