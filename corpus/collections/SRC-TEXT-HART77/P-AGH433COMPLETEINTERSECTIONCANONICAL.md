::: problem
If $X$ is a curve of genus $\geq 2$ which is a complete intersection (II, Ex. 8.4) in some $\PP^n$, show that the canonical divisor $K$ is very ample. Conclude that a curve of genus 2 can never be a complete intersection in any $\PP^n$. Cf. (Ex. 5.1).
:::
