::: problem
Let $X$ be a noetherian scheme, and suppose that every coherent sheaf on $X$ is a quotient of a locally free sheaf. In this case we say $\Coh(X)$ has enough locally frees. Then for any $\mcg \in \Mod(X)$, show that the $\delta$-functor $(\mathcal{E}xt^i(\wait, \mcg))$, from $\Coh(X)$ to $\Mod(X)$ is a contravariant universal $\delta$-functor.

Hint: Show $\mathcal{E}xt^i(\wait, \mcg)$ is coeffaceable for $i>0$.
:::
