::: problem
Let $X=\PP_k^1$, with $k$ an infinite field.

a. Show that there does not exist a projective object $\mcp \in \Mod(X)$, together with a surjective map $\mcp \to \mco_X \to 0$.

Hint: Consider surjections of the form $\mco_V \to k(x) \to 0$, where $x \in X$ is a closed point, $V$ is an open neighborhood of $x$, and $\mco_V=j_{!}(\ro{\mco_X}{V})$, where $j: V \to X$ is the inclusion.

b. Show that there does not exist a projective object $\mcp$ in either $\QCoh(X)$ or $\Coh(X)$ together with a surjection $\mcp \to \mco_X \to 0$.

Hint: Consider surjections of the form $\mcl \to \mcl \tensor k(x) \to 0$, where $x \in X$ is a closed point, and $\mcl$ is an invertible sheaf on $X$.
:::
