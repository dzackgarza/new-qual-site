::: {.problem}
Let $Y$ be an integral scheme of finite type over an algebraically closed field $k$.
Let $\mce$ be a locally free sheaf on $Y$, and let $X = \PP(\mce)$; see (II, §7).

Show that $\Pic X \cong (\Pic Y) \times \ZZ$.
This strengthens (II, Ex.
7.9).
:::
