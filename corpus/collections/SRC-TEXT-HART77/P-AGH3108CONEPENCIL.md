::: problem
In affine $3\dash$space with coordinates $x, y, z$, let $C$ be the conic $(x-1)^2 + y^2 = 1$ in the $xy\dash$plane, and let $P$ be the point $(0,0,t)$ on the $z\dash$axis. Let $Y_t$ be the closure in $\PP^3$ of the cone over $C$ with vertex $P$.

Show that as $t$ varies, the surfaces $\ts{Y_t}$ form a linear system of dimension $1$, with a moving singularity at $P$. The base locus of this linear system is the conic $C$ plus the $z\dash$axis.

This happens in any characteristic.
:::
