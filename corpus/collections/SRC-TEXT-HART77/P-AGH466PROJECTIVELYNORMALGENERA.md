::: {.problem}
Let $X$ be a projectively normal curve in $\PP^3$, not contained in any plane.
If $d=6$, then $g=3$ or 4. If $d=7$, then $g=5$ or 6. Cf.
(II, Ex.
8.4) and (III, Ex.
5.6).
:::
