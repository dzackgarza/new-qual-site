::: {.problem}
a. If $X$ is a surface of degree $d$ in $\PP^3$, then
\[
K^2=d(d-4)^2
.\]

b. If $X$ is a product of two nonsingular curves $C, C^{\prime}$, of genus $g, g^{\prime}$ respectively, then
\[
K^2=8(g-1)\left(g^{\prime}-1\right)
.\]
Cf. (II, Ex. 8.3).
:::
