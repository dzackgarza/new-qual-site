::: problem
Let $Y \subseteq \PP^2$ be a nonsingular plane curve of degree $> 1$, defined by the equation $f(x,y,z) = 0$. Let $X \subseteq \AA^3$ be the affine variety defined by $f$; this is the cone over $Y$. Let $P = (0,0,0)$ be the vertex of the cone, and let $\varphi: \tilde{X} \to X$ be the blowing-up of $X$ at $P$.

1. Show that $X$ has just one singular point, namely $P$.
2. Show that $\tilde{X}$ is nonsingular, by covering it with open affines.
3. Show that $\varphi^{-1}(P)$ is isomorphic to $Y$.
:::
