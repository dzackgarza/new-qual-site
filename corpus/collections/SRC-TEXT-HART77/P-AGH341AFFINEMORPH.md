::: problem
Let $f: X \to Y$ be an affine morphism of noetherian separated schemes (II, Ex. 5.17). Show that for any quasi-coherent sheaf $\mcf$ on $X$, there are natural isomorphisms for all $i \geq 0$,
\[
H^i(X, \mcf) \cong H^i(Y, f_* \mcf).
\]

Hint: Use (II, 5.8).
:::
