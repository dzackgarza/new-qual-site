::: problem
The line, the conic, the twisted cubic curve and the elliptic quartic curve in $\PP^3$ have no multisecants. Every other curve in $\PP^3$ has infinitely many multisecants.

Hint: Consider a projection from a point of the curve to $\PP^2$.
:::
