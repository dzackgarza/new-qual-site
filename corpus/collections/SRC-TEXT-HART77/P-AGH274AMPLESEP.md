::: problem
a. Show that if $X$ is a scheme of finite type over a noetherian ring $A$, and if $X$ admits an ample invertible sheaf, then $X$ is separated.

b. Let $X$ be the affine line over a field $k$ with the origin doubled.
   Calculate $\Pic X$, determine which invertible sheaves are generated by global sections, and then show directly, without using (a), that there is no ample invertible sheaf on $X$.
:::
