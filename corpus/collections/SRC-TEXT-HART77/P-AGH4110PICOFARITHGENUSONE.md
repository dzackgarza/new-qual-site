::: problem
Let $X$ be an integral projective scheme of dimension 1 over $k$, which is locally complete intersection, and has $p_a=1$. Fix a point $P_0 \in X_{\reg}$. Imitate (1.3.7) to show that the map $P \mapsto \mcl(P-P_0)$ gives a one-to-one correspondence between the points of $X_{\reg}$ and the elements of the group $\Pic X$.

This generalizes (II, 6.11.4) and (II, Ex. 6.7).
:::
