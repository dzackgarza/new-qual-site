::: {.problem}
Let $X$ be an integral projective scheme of dimension $\geq 1$ over a field $k$, and let $\mcl$ be an ample invertible sheaf on $X$. Show that
\[
H^0(X, \mcl^{-1}) = 0
.\]

This is a special case of Kodaira vanishing.
:::
