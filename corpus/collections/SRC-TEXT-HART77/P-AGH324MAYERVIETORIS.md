::: problem
Let $Y_1, Y_2$ be two closed subsets of $X$. Then there is a long exact sequence of cohomology with supports
\[
\begin{aligned}
\cdots & \to H_{Y_1 \intersect Y_2}^i(X, \mcf) \to H_{Y_1}^i(X, \mcf) \oplus H_{Y_2}^i(X, \mcf) \to H_{Y_1 \union Y_2}^i(X, \mcf) \to \\
& \to H_{Y_1 \intersect Y_2}^{i+1}(X, \mcf) \to \cdots
\end{aligned}
\]
:::
