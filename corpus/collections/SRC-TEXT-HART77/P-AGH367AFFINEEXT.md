::: {.problem}
Let $X=\Spec A$ be an affine noetherian scheme.
Let $M, N$ be $A$-modules, with $M$ finitely generated.
Then
\[
\Ext_X^i(\tilde{M}, \tilde{N}) \cong \Ext_A^i(M, N)
\]
and
\[
\mathcal{E}xt_X^i(\tilde{M}, \tilde{N}) \cong \Ext_A^i(M, N)^{\sim}.
\]
:::
