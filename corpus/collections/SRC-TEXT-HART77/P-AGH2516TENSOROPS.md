First recall the tensor operations on a module.
Let $A$ be a ring and $M$ an $A\dash$module.

- Let $T^n(M)$ be the tensor product of $M$ with itself $n$ times, for $n \geq 1$, and $T^0(M) = A$.
  Then $T(M) = \bigoplus_{n \geq 0} T^n(M)$ is a noncommutative $A\dash$algebra, the **tensor algebra** of $M$.

- The **symmetric algebra** $S(M) = \bigoplus_{n \geq 0} S^n(M)$ is the quotient of $T(M)$ by the two-sided ideal generated by all $x \tensor y - y \tensor x$ for $x, y \in M$.
  Then $S(M)$ is a commutative $A\dash$algebra, and $S^n(M)$ is the $n$th **symmetric product**. If $M$ is free of rank $r$, then $S(M) \cong A[x_1, \ldots, x_r]$.

- The **exterior algebra** $\bigwedge(M) = \bigoplus_{n \geq 0} \bigwedge^n(M)$ is the quotient of $T(M)$ by the two-sided ideal generated by all $x \tensor x$ for $x \in M$.
  This ideal contains all $x \tensor y + y \tensor x$, so $\bigwedge(M)$ is skew commutative: if $u \in \bigwedge^r(M)$ and $v \in \bigwedge^s(M)$, then $u \wedge v = (-1)^{rs} v \wedge u$.
  The $n$th component $\bigwedge^n(M)$ is the $n$th **exterior power**.

Now let $(X, \OO_X)$ be a ringed space and $\mcf$ a sheaf of $\OO_X\dash$modules.
Define the tensor, symmetric, and exterior algebras of $\mcf$ by sheafifying the presheaf which assigns to each open set $U$ the corresponding operation applied to $\mcf(U)$ as an $\OO_X(U)\dash$module.

a. Suppose $\mcf$ is locally free of rank $n$.
Then $T^r(\mcf)$, $S^r(\mcf)$, and $\bigwedge^r(\mcf)$ are also locally free, of ranks $n^r$, $\binom{n + r - 1}{n - 1}$, and $\binom{n}{r}$ respectively.

b. Again let $\mcf$ be locally free of rank $n$.
Then the multiplication map $\bigwedge^r \mcf \tensor \bigwedge^{n-r} \mcf \to \bigwedge^n \mcf$ is a perfect pairing for any $r$: it induces an isomorphism of $\bigwedge^r \mcf$ with $(\bigwedge^{n-r} \mcf)\dual \tensor \bigwedge^n \mcf$.
As a special case, if $\mcf$ has rank $2$ then $\mcf \cong \mcf\dual \tensor \bigwedge^2 \mcf$.

c. Let $0 \to \mcf' \to \mcf \to \mcf'' \to 0$ be an exact sequence of locally free sheaves.
Then for any $r$ there is a finite filtration
\[
S^r(\mcf) = F^0 \supseteq F^1 \supseteq \cdots \supseteq F^r \supseteq F^{r+1} = 0
\]
with quotients $F^p/F^{p+1} \cong S^p(\mcf') \tensor S^{r-p}(\mcf'')$ for each $p$.

d. The same statement as (c), with exterior powers instead of symmetric powers.
In particular, if $\mcf', \mcf, \mcf''$ have ranks $n', n, n''$, there is an induced isomorphism on top exterior powers.

e. Let $f: X \to Y$ be a morphism of ringed spaces and $\mcf$ an $\OO_Y\dash$module.
Then $f^*$ commutes with all the tensor operations, e.g. $f^*(S^n(\mcf)) = S^n(f^* \mcf)$.
