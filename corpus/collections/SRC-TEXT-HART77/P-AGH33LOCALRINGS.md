::: problem
1. Let $\phi: X \to Y$ be a morphism.
   Show that for each $P \in X$, $\phi$ induces a homomorphism of local rings
   $\phi_P^* : \mco_{\phi(P), Y} \to \mco_{P, X}$.
2. Show that a morphism $\phi$ is an isomorphism if and only if $\phi$ is a homeomorphism and the induced map $\phi_P^*$ on local rings is an isomorphism for all $P \in X$.
3. Show that if $\phi(X)$ is dense in $Y$, then $\phi_P^*$ is injective for all $P \in X$.
:::
