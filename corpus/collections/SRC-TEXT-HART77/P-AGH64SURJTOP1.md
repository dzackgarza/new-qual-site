::: problem
Let $Y$ be a nonsingular projective curve. Show that every nonconstant rational function $f$ on $Y$ defines a surjective morphism $\varphi: Y \to \PP^1$, and that for every $P \in \PP^1$ the fibre $\varphi^{-1}(P)$ is a finite set of points.
:::
