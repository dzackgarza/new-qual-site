::: {.problem}
Let
\[
S=\left\{f\in L^1(\mathbb R^3):\int_{\mathbb R^3}f(x)\,dx=0\right\}.
\]

(a) Show that $S$ is closed in the $L^1$ topology.

(b) Show that $S\cap L^2(\mathbb R^3)$ is dense in $L^2(\mathbb R^3)$.
:::
