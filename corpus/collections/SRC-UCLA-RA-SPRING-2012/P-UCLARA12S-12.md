::: {.problem}
Show that the only entire function $f$ satisfying both
\[
|f'(z)|\le e^{|z|}
\]
and
\[
f\!\left(\frac{n}{\sqrt{1+|n|}}\right)=0
\qquad\text{for every }n\in\mathbb Z
\]
is the zero function.
:::
