::: {.problem}
Let $(u_n)$ be a sequence of real-valued harmonic functions on the unit disc $\mathbb D$ satisfying
\[
u_1(z)\ge u_2(z)\ge\cdots\ge0
\]
for every $z\in\mathbb D$. Prove that $z\mapsto\inf_n u_n(z)$ is harmonic on $\mathbb D$.
:::
