::: {.problem}
Let $f$ be a twice continuously differentiable function on $[0,1]$ such that $f(0)=f(1)=0$.
Prove that
\[
\max_{x\in[0,1]}|f(x)|\le \frac18\max_{x\in[0,1]}|f''(x)|,
\]
and find an example where equality holds.
:::
