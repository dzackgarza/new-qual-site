::: {.problem}
Let $c_0,c_1,\ldots,c_{n-1}$ be complex numbers.
Prove that all the zeroes of the polynomial
\[
z^n+c_{n-1}z^{n-1}+\cdots+c_1z+c_0
\]
lie in the open disc with center $0$ and radius
\[
1+|c_{n-1}|+\cdots+|c_1|+|c_0|.
\]
:::
