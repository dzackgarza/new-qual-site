::: {.problem}
Recall that $S_6$ and $A_6$ are the symmetric and alternating groups on six letters.
Prove or give a counterexample, with explanation: for every $\sigma\in A_6$ there is a $\tau\in S_6$ such that $\tau^2=\sigma$.
