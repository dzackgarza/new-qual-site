::: {.problem}
For $t\ge0$ let
\[
F(t)=\int_0^t e^{-x^2}\,dx
\]
and
\[
G(t)=\int_0^1\frac{e^{-t^2(1+x^2)}}{1+x^2}\,dx.
\]
Show that $F(t)^2+G(t)$ is constant and deduce the value of $F(\infty)$.
:::
