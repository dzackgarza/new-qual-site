::: {.problem}
If $f(z)$ is analytic in the open disc
\[
\mathbb D=\{z:|z|<1\},
\]
and
\[
|f(z)|<\frac1{1-|z|}\qquad(z\in\mathbb D),
\]
show that
\[
\left|\frac{f^{(n)}(0)}{n!}\right|\le (n+1)\left(1+\frac1n\right)^n<e(n+1).
\]
:::
