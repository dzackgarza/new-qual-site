::: {.problem}
Let $V,W$ be finite-dimensional real inner-product spaces, let $A:V\to W$ be linear, and let $w\in W$. Show that the vectors $v\in V$ minimizing
\[
\|Av-w\|
\]
are exactly the solutions of the normal equation
\[
A^*Av=A^*w.
\]
:::
