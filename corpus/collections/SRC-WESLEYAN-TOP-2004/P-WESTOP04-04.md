::: {.problem}
Define a covering space. Let
\[
p:(E,e_0)\to(X,x_0)
\]
be a covering map and let
\[
f:(Y,y_0)\to(X,x_0)
\]
be continuous, where $Y$ is path-connected. Prove that there is at most one based lift
\[
\widetilde f:(Y,y_0)\to(E,e_0)
\]
satisfying
\[
p\circ\widetilde f=f.
\]
:::
