::: {.problem}
Let $\Gamma(z)=\int_0^\infty e^{-t}t^{z-1}\,dt$.
Using the polar coordinate formula, prove the following:

(a) $\int_{\mathbb{R}^d} e^{-\pi|x|^2}\,dx=1$ when $d=2$.
Deduce from this that the same identity holds for any $d$.

(b) $\Bigl(\int_0^\infty e^{-\pi r^2}r^{d-1}\,dr\Bigr)\sigma(S^{d-1})=1$, and show that $\sigma(S^{d-1})=\dfrac{2\pi^{d/2}}{\Gamma(d/2)}$.

(c) If $B$ is the unit ball, show that $\mathrm{Vol}(B)=\dfrac{\pi^{d/2}}{\Gamma(d/2+1)}$.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. Compute the Gaussian integral in dimension two.
::: {.proof}
By polar coordinates in $\mathbb R^2$,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\int_{\mathbb R^2}e^{-\pi|x|^2}\,dx
&=2\pi\int_0^\infty e^{-\pi r^2}r\,dr\\
&=2\pi\left[-\frac{1}{2\pi}e^{-\pi r^2}\right]_{0}^{\infty}=1.
\end{aligned}
\]
Thus
\[
\boxed{\int_{\mathbb R^2}e^{-\pi|x|^2}\,dx=1.}
\]
:::

<1>2. Deduce the Gaussian integral in every dimension.
::: {.proof}
Tonelli's theorem gives
\[
\int_{\mathbb R^2}e^{-\pi(x_1^2+x_2^2)}\,dx_1dx_2
=\left(\int_{\mathbb R}e^{-\pi t^2}\,dt\right)^2.
\]
The one-dimensional integral is positive, so Step 1 implies
\[
\int_{\mathbb R}e^{-\pi t^2}\,dt=1.
\]
Applying Tonelli again in $\mathbb R^d$,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\int_{\mathbb R^d}e^{-\pi|x|^2}\,dx
&=\prod_{j=1}^d\int_{\mathbb R}e^{-\pi x_j^2}\,dx_j\\
&=1.
\end{aligned}
\]
Hence
\[
\boxed{\int_{\mathbb R^d}e^{-\pi|x|^2}\,dx=1.}
\]
:::

<1>3. Compute the surface area of the unit sphere.
::: {.proof}
The polar-coordinate formula in $\mathbb R^d$ gives
\[
1
=\int_{\mathbb R^d}e^{-\pi|x|^2}\,dx
=\sigma(S^{d-1})\int_0^\infty e^{-\pi r^2}r^{d-1}\,dr.
\]
Thus
\[
\left(\int_0^\infty e^{-\pi r^2}r^{d-1}\,dr\right)
\sigma(S^{d-1})=1.
\]

With the substitution
\[
t=\pi r^2,
\qquad
dr=\frac{1}{2\sqrt\pi}t^{-1/2}\,dt,
\]
we obtain
\[
\begin{aligned}
\int_0^\infty e^{-\pi r^2}r^{d-1}\,dr
&=\frac12\pi^{-d/2}
\int_0^\infty e^{-t}t^{d/2-1}\,dt\\
&=\frac12\pi^{-d/2}\Gamma(d/2).
\end{aligned}
\]
Therefore
\[
\boxed{
\sigma(S^{d-1})
=\frac{2\pi^{d/2}}{\Gamma(d/2)}.}
\]
:::

<1>4. Compute the volume of the unit ball.
::: {.proof}
If $B=\{x:|x|<1\}$, then polar coordinates give
\[
\operatorname{Vol}(B)
=\sigma(S^{d-1})\int_0^1r^{d-1}\,dr
=\frac{\sigma(S^{d-1})}{d}.
\]
Using Step 3 and the identity
\[
\Gamma(d/2+1)=\frac d2\Gamma(d/2),
\]
we find
\[
\operatorname{Vol}(B)
=\frac{2\pi^{d/2}}{d\Gamma(d/2)}
=\boxed{\frac{\pi^{d/2}}{\Gamma(d/2+1)}}.
\]
:::
