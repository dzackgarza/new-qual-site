::: {.problem}
Let
\[
f(z)=\frac{z-1}{z+1}.
\]
Determine the image under $f$ of each of the following:

1. the real axis (hint: this is a line);

2. the circle $|z|=2$ (hint: this is a circle);

3. the circle $|z|=1$;

4. the imaginary axis.
:::
