::: {.problem}
Let
\[
f(z)=\frac{z-1}{z+1}.
\]
Determine the image under $f$ of each of the following:

1. the real axis;

2. the circle $|z|=2$;

3. the circle $|z|=1$;

4. the imaginary axis.
:::
