::: {.problem}
Show informally that if $\gamma$ is a simple closed curve traveled counterclockwise, then
\[
\int_\gamma f(z)\,dz
=-2\pi i\sum(\text{residues of $f$ outside $\gamma$, including $\infty$}).
\]
:::
