::: {.problem}
Show that there is exactly one point $z$ in the right half-plane $\{z:\operatorname{Re}z>0\}$ satisfying
\[
z+e^{-z}=2.
\]

Hint: use a contour in the right half-plane bounded by a large semicircle and a vertical line $\operatorname{Re}z=\varepsilon$, with $R>3$ and $\varepsilon>0$ small.
:::
