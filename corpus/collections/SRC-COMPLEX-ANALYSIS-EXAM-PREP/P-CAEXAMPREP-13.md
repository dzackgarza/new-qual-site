::: {.problem}
Let $A$ be the upper half of the unit disk $|z|<1$.
Find the temperature $T$ inside $A$ if the circular portion of the boundary is insulated, while on the diameter
\[
T=0\quad(0<x<1),
\qquad
T=10\quad(-1<x<0).
\]
:::
