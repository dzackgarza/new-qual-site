:::{.problem}
Let $X$ be a topological space and let $V$ be an $n$-dimensional subspace of the real vector space of continuous functions on $X$. Prove that there are a basis
\[
\{f_1,\dots,f_n\}
\]
of $V$ and points $x_1,\dots,x_n\in X$ such that
\[
f_i(x_j)=\delta_{ij}.
\]
:::
