:::{.problem}
Let $A$ be a finite-dimensional commutative algebra with identity over $\mathbb C$, with
\[
\dim_{\mathbb C}A\ge2.
\]
Suppose
\[
a^2\ne0
\]
for every nonzero $a\in A$. Show that the equation
\[
a^2=a
\]
has at least three distinct solutions in $A$.
:::
