::: {.problem}
Suppose $f:\mathbb C\to\mathbb C$ is injective and entire. Prove that there are $a,b\in\mathbb C$ with $a\ne0$ such that
\[
f(z)=az+b
\]
for every $z\in\mathbb C$.
:::
