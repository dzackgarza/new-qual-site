::: {.problem}
Let $GL_2(\mathbb Z_m)$ be the multiplicative group of invertible $2\times2$ matrices over the ring of integers modulo $m$. Find
\[
|GL_2(\mathbb Z_{p^n})|
\]
for every prime $p$ and positive integer $n$.
:::
