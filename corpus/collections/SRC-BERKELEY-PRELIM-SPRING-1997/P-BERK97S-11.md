::: {.problem}
Suppose $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ satisfies
\[
f''(x)=(x^2-1)f(x)
\]
for every $x\in\mathbb R$, with
\[
f(0)=1,
\qquad
f'(0)=0.
\]
Show that
\[
f(x)\longrightarrow0
\qquad(x\to\infty).
\]
:::
