:::{.problem}
Let $f,g$ be entire functions and suppose there is a real constant $k$ such that
\[
\operatorname{Re}f(z)\le k\operatorname{Re}g(z)
\]
for every $z\in\mathbb C$. Show that there are constants $a,b$ such that
\[
f(z)=ag(z)+b.
\]
:::
