::: {.problem}
Let $P,Q$ be $n\times n$ matrices satisfying
\[
P^2=P,
\qquad
Q^2=Q,
\]
and suppose
\[
I-(P+Q)
\]
is invertible. Prove that $P$ and $Q$ have the same rank.
:::
