::: {.problem}
Find all solutions of
$$
f''(t)-2f'(t)+f(t)=4e^{3t},
$$
then find the unique solution satisfying
$$
f(0)=1,\qquad f'(0)=1.
$$
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

All solutions of the differential equation are
$$
\boxed{f(t)=ae^t+bte^t+e^{3t},\qquad a,b\in\RR.}
$$


::: pf-proof

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

The homogeneous equation has solution space
$$
f_h(t)=ae^t+bte^t.
$$


::: pf-proof

The homogeneous characteristic polynomial is
$$
r^2-2r+1=(r-1)^2.
$$
Thus $r=1$ is a double root, giving the linearly independent solutions $e^t$ and $te^t$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

A particular solution is $f_p(t)=e^{3t}$.


::: pf-proof

For the trial function $f_p(t)=ce^{3t}$,
$$
f_p''-2f_p'+f_p
=(9c-6c+c)e^{3t}
=4ce^{3t}.
$$
Matching the right-hand side $4e^{3t}$ gives $c=1$.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Every solution of the inhomogeneous equation is a particular solution plus a solution of the homogeneous equation. Steps [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} and [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} therefore give exactly the family displayed in step [](#s1){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

The unique solution satisfying the initial conditions is
$$
\boxed{f(t)=-2te^t+e^{3t}.}
$$


::: pf-proof

For the family in step [](#s1){.pf-ref},
$$
f(0)=a+1,
$$
so $f(0)=1$ gives $a=0$. Differentiating gives
$$
f'(t)=ae^t+be^t+bte^t+3e^{3t},
$$
hence
$$
f'(0)=a+b+3.
$$
With $a=0$, the condition $f'(0)=1$ gives $b=-2$. These two scalar conditions determine the two constants $a,b$ uniquely.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1){.pf-ref} gives all solutions, and step [](#s2){.pf-ref} gives the unique member satisfying the prescribed initial data.

:::
