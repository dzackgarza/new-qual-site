::: {.problem}
Find a single $3\times3$ matrix $A$ such that

1. $\ker A$ is the line spanned by $(1,1,1)^T$;
2. $(1,0,1)^T$ is an eigenvector of $A$;
3. $(1,2,-1)^T\in\operatorname{im}A$.
:::
