::: {.problem}
Find the ellipse
$$
ax^2+by^2=1
$$
that best fits, in the least-squares sense, the four points
$$
(2,2),\quad(-1,1),\quad(-1,-1),\quad(2,-1).
$$
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: pf-step

The least-squares problem is
$$
M\begin{pmatrix}a\\b\end{pmatrix}\approx
\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\1\\1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
M=
\begin{pmatrix}
4&4\\
1&1\\
1&1\\
4&1
\end{pmatrix}.
$$


::: pf-proof

At a data point $(x_i,y_i)$, the residual in the equation $ax^2+by^2=1$ is
$$
a x_i^2+b y_i^2-1.
$$
For the four given points, the rows $(x_i^2,y_i^2)$ are $(4,4)$, $(1,1)$, $(1,1)$, and $(4,1)$, which gives the displayed system. Minimizing the sum of squared residuals is therefore exactly this linear least-squares problem.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

The unique least-squares coefficients are
$$
\boxed{a=\frac29,\qquad b=\frac19.}
$$


::: pf-proof

The normal equations are
$$
M^TM\begin{pmatrix}a\\b\end{pmatrix}
=M^T\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\1\\1\end{pmatrix}.
$$
Direct calculation gives
$$
M^TM=
\begin{pmatrix}
34&22\\
22&19
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
M^T\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\1\\1\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}10\\7\end{pmatrix}.
$$
Since
$$
\det(M^TM)=34\cdot19-22^2=162\ne0,
$$
the least-squares solution is unique. Solving
$$
34a+22b=10,
\qquad
22a+19b=7
$$
gives $a=2/9$ and $b=1/9$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

The best-fitting ellipse is
$$
\boxed{2x^2+y^2=9.}
$$


::: pf-proof

Substituting the coefficients from step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} into $ax^2+by^2=1$ gives
$$
\frac29x^2+\frac19y^2=1.
$$
Multiplying by $9$ gives the displayed equation. Both coefficients are positive, so this conic is an ellipse.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} is the ellipse determined by the unique least-squares minimizer from step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref}.

:::
