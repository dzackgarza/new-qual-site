::: {.problem}
Let
\[
A=\begin{pmatrix}1/2&-1/2\\1/2&1/2\end{pmatrix}.
\]

(a) Find the eigenvalues of $A$.

(b) Is the origin stable for the continuous system $\dot x=Ax$?

(c) Describe the solution curves of the continuous system.

(d) Is the origin stable for the discrete system $x_{n+1}=Ax_n$?
