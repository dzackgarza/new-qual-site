::: {.problem}
Let
$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}1/2&-1/2\\1/2&1/2\end{pmatrix}.
$$

(a) Find the eigenvalues of $A$.

(b) Is the origin stable for the continuous system $\dot x=Ax$?

(c) Describe the solution curves of the continuous system.

(d) Is the origin stable for the discrete system $x_{n+1}=Ax_n$?
:::

::: {.solution}

Let
$$
J=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},
$$
so that $A=\frac12I+\frac12J$ and $J^2=-I$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

The eigenvalues of $A$ are
$$
\boxed{\lambda_\pm=\frac{1\pm i}{2}.}
$$


::: pf-proof

The characteristic polynomial is
$$
\det(\lambda I-A)
=\lambda^2-\lambda+\frac12.
$$
Its discriminant is $1-2=-1$, so the quadratic formula gives the displayed pair.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

The origin is $\boxed{\text{unstable}}$ for the continuous system.


::: pf-proof

Since $I$ and $J$ commute,
$$
e^{At}
=e^{t/2}e^{(t/2)J}
=e^{t/2}
\begin{pmatrix}
\cos(t/2)&-\sin(t/2)\\
\sin(t/2)&\cos(t/2)
\end{pmatrix}.
$$
Thus every nonzero solution satisfies
$$
\norm{x(t)}=e^{t/2}\norm{x(0)},
$$
which grows without bound. In particular, arbitrarily small nonzero initial conditions eventually leave any fixed neighborhood of the origin.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

Every nonzero continuous-system trajectory is an outward counterclockwise logarithmic spiral, with
$$
\boxed{x(t)=e^{t/2}R_{t/2}x(0),}
$$
where $R_\theta$ denotes counterclockwise rotation through angle $\theta$.


::: pf-proof

The formula is exactly the matrix exponential computed in step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref}. Its factor $e^{t/2}$ multiplies the radius, while $R_{t/2}$ increases the polar angle at constant rate $1/2$. Therefore every nonzero trajectory spirals counterclockwise away from the origin.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

The origin is $\boxed{\text{asymptotically stable}}$ for the discrete system.


::: pf-proof

One has
$$
A^TA=\frac12I.
$$
Hence
$$
\norm{Ax}=\frac1{\sqrt2}\norm{x}
$$
for every $x$, and induction gives
$$
\norm{A^nx_0}=2^{-n/2}\norm{x_0}\longrightarrow0.
$$
Thus all sufficiently small initial conditions remain small and in fact converge to the origin.

:::

::: pf-qed

Steps [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} through [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} answer parts (a)--(d), respectively.
