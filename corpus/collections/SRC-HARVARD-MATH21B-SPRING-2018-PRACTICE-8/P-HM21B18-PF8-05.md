::: {.problem}
Let $f$ be $1$ on $[\pi/3,2\pi/3]$ and $0$ elsewhere on $[0,\pi]$.

(a) Write $f$ as a Fourier sine series.

(b) Solve $u_t=\mu u_{xx}$ with $u(x,0)=f(x)$.

(c) Solve $u_{tt}=c^2u_{xx}$ with $u(x,0)=f(x)$ and $u_t(x,0)=0$.
:::
