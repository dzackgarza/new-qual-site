::: {.problem}
Find all solutions of

(a) $(D^2-3D+2)f=60e^{7x}$;

(b) $(D^2-2D+1)f=x$;

(c) $(D^2+1)f=x^2$.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

The solutions of part (a) are
\[
\boxed{f(x)=C_1e^x+C_2e^{2x}+2e^{7x},\qquad C_1,C_2\in\RR.}
\]


::: pf-proof

The homogeneous operator factors as
\[
D^2-3D+2=(D-1)(D-2),
\]
so the homogeneous solutions are $C_1e^x+C_2e^{2x}$. For a particular solution $f_p(x)=ce^{7x}$,
\[
(D^2-3D+2)f_p
=(49-21+2)ce^{7x}
=30ce^{7x}.
\]
Matching $60e^{7x}$ gives $c=2$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

The solutions of part (b) are
\[
\boxed{f(x)=C_1e^x+C_2xe^x+x+2,\qquad C_1,C_2\in\RR.}
\]


::: pf-proof

Here
\[
D^2-2D+1=(D-1)^2,
\]
so the homogeneous solutions are $C_1e^x+C_2xe^x$. For a particular solution $f_p(x)=ax+b$,
\[
(D^2-2D+1)f_p
=ax+(b-2a).
\]
Equating this with $x$ gives $a=1$ and $b=2$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

The solutions of part (c) are
\[
\boxed{f(x)=C_1\cos x+C_2\sin x+x^2-2,\qquad C_1,C_2\in\RR.}
\]


::: pf-proof

The homogeneous equation $f''+f=0$ has solution space spanned by $\cos x$ and $\sin x$. For a particular solution
\[
f_p(x)=ax^2+bx+c,
\]
one has
\[
f_p''+f_p=ax^2+bx+(c+2a).
\]
Matching $x^2$ gives $a=1$, $b=0$, and $c=-2$.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Steps [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} through [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} give the complete solution families for parts (a)--(c).

:::
