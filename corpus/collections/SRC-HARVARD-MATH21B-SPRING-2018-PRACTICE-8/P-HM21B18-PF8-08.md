::: {.problem}
Find the matrix for rotation in $\RR^3$ by $90^\circ$ about the line spanned by
$$
\begin{pmatrix}2\\2\\1\end{pmatrix},
$$
clockwise as viewed facing the origin from the point $(2,2,1)$. You may leave the answer as a product of matrices and inverses.
:::

::: {.solution}

Let
$$
e_1=\frac13\begin{pmatrix}2\\2\\1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
e_2=\frac1{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}1\\-1\\0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
e_3=\frac1{3\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\-4\end{pmatrix},
$$
and let $E=[e_1\ e_2\ e_3]$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

The ordered basis $(e_1,e_2,e_3)$ is a positively oriented orthonormal basis, with $e_1$ along the rotation axis.


::: pf-proof

Each vector has norm $1$, and their pairwise dot products are zero. Moreover,
$$
e_1\times e_2=e_3,
$$
so the basis is positively oriented. By definition, $e_1$ spans the same line as $(2,2,1)^T$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

In the adapted basis $(e_1,e_2,e_3)$, the required rotation has matrix
$$
R_0=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&0&0\\
0&0&1\\
0&-1&0
\end{pmatrix}.
$$


::: pf-proof

The rotation fixes $e_1$. Viewed from the point on the positive $e_1$-axis while facing the origin, a clockwise quarter-turn sends $e_2$ to $-e_3$ and $e_3$ to $e_2$. Thus
$$
e_1\longmapsto e_1,
\qquad
e_2\longmapsto-e_3,
\qquad
e_3\longmapsto e_2,
$$
so the adapted-coordinate columns are $(1,0,0)^T$, $(0,0,-1)^T$, and $(0,1,0)^T$, which gives the displayed matrix.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

In standard coordinates, the rotation matrix is
$$
\boxed{
A=ER_0E^T
=\frac19
\begin{pmatrix}
4&7&-4\\
1&4&8\\
8&-4&1
\end{pmatrix}.}
$$


::: pf-proof

The columns of $E$ are the adapted basis vectors written in standard coordinates, so $E$ sends adapted coordinate columns to standard coordinate columns. Since $E$ is orthogonal by step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref}, $E^{-1}=E^T$. Therefore the standard-coordinate matrix is $ER_0E^{-1}=ER_0E^T$. Multiplying the matrices gives the displayed explicit form.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} aligns the first adapted basis vector with the required axis, step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} encodes the specified viewing-direction sense of rotation, and step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} converts that rotation back to standard coordinates.

:::
