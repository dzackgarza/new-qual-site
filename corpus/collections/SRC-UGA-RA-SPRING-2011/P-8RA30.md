::: problem
Let $A,B\subseteq\mathbb{R}$ be bounded measurable sets with positive Lebesgue measure.
For each $t\in\mathbb{R}$ define
\[
g(t)=m\bigl(A\cap(t-B)\bigr),
\]
where $t-B=\{t-b:b\in B\}$.

(a) Prove that $g$ is a continuous function and
\[
\int_{\mathbb{R}}g(t)\,dt=m(A)m(B).
\]

(b) Conclude that the sumset
\[
A+B=\{a+b:a\in A\text{ and }b\in B\}
\]
contains a non-empty open subset of $\mathbb{R}$.
:::

::: solution
<1>1. Express $g$ as a convolution and prove continuity.
::: proof
For $t\in\mathbb R$,
\[
\begin{aligned}
g(t)
&=\int_{\mathbb R}\mathbf1_A(x)\mathbf1_{t-B}(x)\,dx\\
&=\int_{\mathbb R}\mathbf1_A(x)\mathbf1_B(t-x)\,dx.
\end{aligned}
\]
Thus
\[
g=\mathbf1_A*\mathbf1_B.
\]
Because $A$ and $B$ have finite measure,
\[
\mathbf1_A,\mathbf1_B\in L^2(\mathbb R).
\]
For $h\in\mathbb R$, Cauchy--Schwarz gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
|g(t+h)-g(t)|
&\le \|\mathbf1_A\|_2
\,\|\mathbf1_B(t+h-\cdot)-\mathbf1_B(t-\cdot)\|_2\\
&=\|\mathbf1_A\|_2
\,\|\tau_h\mathbf1_B-\mathbf1_B\|_2.
\end{aligned}
\]
Translations are continuous in $L^2$, so the right-hand side tends to $0$ as $h\to0$, uniformly in $t$. Hence $g$ is continuous.
:::

<1>2. Compute the integral of $g$.
::: proof
By Tonelli's theorem,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\int_{\mathbb R}g(t)\,dt
&=\int_{\mathbb R}\int_{\mathbb R}
\mathbf1_A(x)\mathbf1_B(t-x)\,dx\,dt\\
&=\int_{\mathbb R}\mathbf1_A(x)
\left(\int_{\mathbb R}\mathbf1_B(t-x)\,dt\right)dx\\
&=m(B)\int_{\mathbb R}\mathbf1_A(x)\,dx\\
&=m(A)m(B).
\end{aligned}
\]
Thus
\[
\boxed{\int_{\mathbb R}g(t)\,dt=m(A)m(B).}
\]
:::

<1>3. Deduce that $A+B$ has nonempty interior.
::: proof
Since $g\ge0$ and
\[
\int_{\mathbb R}g(t)\,dt=m(A)m(B)>0,
\]
there exists $t_0\in\mathbb R$ such that $g(t_0)>0$. By continuity, there is an open interval $I$ containing $t_0$ such that
\[
g(t)>0
\qquad(t\in I).
\]
If $g(t)>0$, then
\[
m(A\cap(t-B))>0,
\]
so $A\cap(t-B)$ is nonempty. Choose $a\in A\cap(t-B)$. Then $a=t-b$ for some $b\in B$, hence
\[
t=a+b\in A+B.
\]
Therefore
\[
I\subseteq A+B.
\]
Thus $A+B$ contains a nonempty open interval.
:::
