::: problem
Let $0<p<\infty$ and $\{x_n\}_{n=1}^\infty$ be a bounded sequence of complex numbers.
Prove the equivalence of the following two statements:

(i) $\displaystyle\lim_{N\to\infty}\frac1N\sum_{n=1}^N|x_n|^p=0$;

(ii) for every $\varepsilon>0$,
\[
\lim_{N\to\infty}\frac1N\bigl|\{1\le n\le N:|x_n|\ge\varepsilon\}\bigr|=0.
\]
:::

::: solution
<1>1. Prove (i) implies (ii).
::: proof
Fix $\varepsilon>0$ and let
\[
E_N(\varepsilon):=
\{1\le n\le N:|x_n|\ge\varepsilon\}.
\]
For every $n\in E_N(\varepsilon)$,
\[
|x_n|^p\ge\varepsilon^p.
\]
Hence
\[
\varepsilon^p|E_N(\varepsilon)|
\le \sum_{n=1}^N|x_n|^p.
\]
Dividing by $N\varepsilon^p$ gives
\[
\frac{|E_N(\varepsilon)|}{N}
\le
\frac1{\varepsilon^p}
\frac1N\sum_{n=1}^N|x_n|^p.
\]
If (i) holds, the right-hand side tends to $0$, proving (ii).
:::

<1>2. Prove (ii) implies (i).
::: proof
Since $(x_n)$ is bounded, choose $M<\infty$ such that
\[
|x_n|\le M
\qquad\text{for all }n.
\]
Fix $\varepsilon>0$. Split the sum according to whether $|x_n|<\varepsilon$ or $|x_n|\ge\varepsilon$:
\[
\begin{aligned}
\frac1N\sum_{n=1}^N|x_n|^p
&\le \varepsilon^p
+M^p\frac{|E_N(\varepsilon)|}{N}.
\end{aligned}
\]
By (ii), the second term tends to $0$. Thus
\[
\limsup_{N\to\infty}
\frac1N\sum_{n=1}^N|x_n|^p
\le\varepsilon^p.
\]
Since $\varepsilon>0$ is arbitrary and the averages are nonnegative,
\[
\boxed{
\lim_{N\to\infty}
\frac1N\sum_{n=1}^N|x_n|^p=0.}
\]
Hence (i) and (ii) are equivalent.
:::
:::
