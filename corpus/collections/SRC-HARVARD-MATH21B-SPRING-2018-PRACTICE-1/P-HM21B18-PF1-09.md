::: {.problem}
Find the determinants of
\[
A=\begin{pmatrix}1&1&1&1\\2&1&1&1\\3&2&1&1\\4&3&2&1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
B=\begin{pmatrix}9&2&2&2\\2&9&2&2\\2&2&9&2\\2&2&2&9\end{pmatrix},
\]
\[
C=\begin{pmatrix}2&8&5&2\\2&9&2&2\\2&8&9&2\\2&2&1&2\end{pmatrix},
\quad
D=\begin{pmatrix}0&0&0&2\\0&0&2&5\\0&2&5&4\\2&5&4&3\end{pmatrix},
\]
and
\[
E=\begin{pmatrix}2&3&0&0\\4&1&0&0\\2&3&2&3\\4&1&4&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Show the reasoning used for each determinant.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. $\det A=-1$.

::: {.proof}
Starting from the bottom, apply
\[
R_4\leftarrow R_4-R_3,\qquad
R_3\leftarrow R_3-R_2,\qquad
R_2\leftarrow R_2-R_1.
\]
These row additions do not change the determinant and give
\[
\begin{pmatrix}
1&1&1&1\\
1&0&0&0\\
1&1&0&0\\
1&1&1&0
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Expanding along the fourth column gives
\[
\det A
=(-1)^{1+4}
\det\begin{pmatrix}
1&0&0\\
1&1&0\\
1&1&1
\end{pmatrix}
=-1.
\]
:::

<1>2. $\det B=5145$.

::: {.proof}
Let $J$ be the $4\times4$ all-ones matrix. Then
\[
B=7I+2J.
\]
The vector $\mathbf1=(1,1,1,1)^T$ satisfies
$J\mathbf1=4\mathbf1$, while every vector in
$\mathbf1^\perp$ satisfies $Jv=0$. Hence the eigenvalues of $B$ are
\[
7+2\cdot4=15
\]
on $\operatorname{span}\{\mathbf1\}$ and $7$ with multiplicity $3$ on
$\mathbf1^\perp$. Therefore
\[
\det B=15\cdot7^3=5145.
\]
:::

<1>3. $\det C=0$.

::: {.proof}
The first and fourth columns of $C$ are both
\[
(2,2,2,2)^T.
\]
A matrix with two equal columns has determinant zero.
:::

<1>4. $\det D=16$.

::: {.proof}
In the Leibniz formula
\[
\det D=\sum_{\sigma\in S_4}
\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)\prod_{i=1}^4D_{i,\sigma(i)},
\]
row $1$ forces $\sigma(1)=4$ for a nonzero product. Then row $2$ forces
$\sigma(2)=3$, row $3$ forces $\sigma(3)=2$, and finally
$\sigma(4)=1$. Thus the reverse permutation is the only nonzero term.
It has $6$ inversions, hence sign $+1$, so
\[
\det D=2\cdot2\cdot2\cdot2=16.
\]
:::

<1>5. $\det E=100$.

::: {.proof}
Write
\[
M=\begin{pmatrix}2&3\\4&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Then
\[
E=
\begin{pmatrix}
M&0\\
M&M
\end{pmatrix}
\]
is block lower triangular. Therefore
\[
\det E=(\det M)^2
=(2\cdot1-3\cdot4)^2
=(-10)^2
=100.
\]
:::

<1>6. The five determinants are
\[
\boxed{
\det A=-1,\qquad
\det B=5145,\qquad
\det C=0,\qquad
\det D=16,\qquad
\det E=100.}
\]

::: {.proof}
These are exactly the values established in steps <1>1 through <1>5.
:::

<1>7. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>6 records all requested determinants.
