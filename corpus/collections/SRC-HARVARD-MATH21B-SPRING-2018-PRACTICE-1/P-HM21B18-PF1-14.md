::: {.problem}
Find the determinant of the $124\times124$ diagonal matrix
$$
2I_{124}.
$$
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

The matrix $2I_{124}$ is diagonal with all $124$ diagonal entries
equal to $2$.


::: pf-proof

By definition, $I_{124}$ has $1$ in every diagonal position and $0$
off the diagonal. Multiplication by $2$ replaces every diagonal entry by
$2$ and leaves every off-diagonal entry equal to $0$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

Therefore
$$
\boxed{\det(2I_{124})=2^{124}}.
$$


::: pf-proof

The determinant of a diagonal matrix is the product of its diagonal
entries. By step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref}, there are $124$ such entries and each is $2$.
Hence
$$
\det(2I_{124})
=\prod_{j=1}^{124}2
=2^{124}.
$$

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} gives the requested determinant.
