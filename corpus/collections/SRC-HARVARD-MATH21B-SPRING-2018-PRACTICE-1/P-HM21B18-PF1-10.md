::: {.problem}
(a) Solve $f''(t)=t$.

(b) Solve $f''(t)+f(t)=t$.

(c) Solve $f''(t)-f(t)=t$.

(d) Decide, for each of
\[
f''+f'=\cos t,
\qquad
f''-f'=\cos t,
\]
whether every solution remains bounded as $t\to+\infty$.
:::
