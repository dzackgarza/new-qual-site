::: {.problem}
(a) Solve $f''(t)=t$.

(b) Solve $f''(t)+f(t)=t$.

(c) Solve $f''(t)-f(t)=t$.

(d) Decide, for each of
\[
f''+f'=\cos t,
\qquad
f''-f'=\cos t,
\]
whether every solution remains bounded as $t\to+\infty$.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. The general solution of $f''(t)=t$ is
\[
f(t)=C_1+C_2t+\frac{t^3}{6}.
\]

::: {.proof}
Integrating once gives
\[
f'(t)=C_2+\frac{t^2}{2},
\]
and integrating again gives the displayed formula.
:::

<1>2. The general solution of $f''+f=t$ is
\[
f(t)=C_1\cos t+C_2\sin t+t.
\]

::: {.proof}
The homogeneous equation $f''+f=0$ has basis
$\cos t,\sin t$. The function $f_p(t)=t$ satisfies
$f_p''+f_p=t$, so adding the homogeneous solution gives the formula.
:::

<1>3. The general solution of $f''-f=t$ is
\[
f(t)=C_1e^t+C_2e^{-t}-t.
\]

::: {.proof}
The homogeneous equation has characteristic polynomial
$r^2-1=(r-1)(r+1)$, giving $e^t,e^{-t}$. The particular solution
$f_p(t)=-t$ satisfies
\[
f_p''-f_p=0-(-t)=t.
\]
:::

<1>4. Every solution of $f''+f'=\cos t$ remains bounded as
$t\to+\infty$.

::: {.proof}
The homogeneous characteristic equation is
\[
r^2+r=r(r+1)=0,
\]
so
\[
f_h(t)=C_1+C_2e^{-t}.
\]
A particular solution is
\[
f_p(t)=\frac12\sin t-\frac12\cos t,
\]
because $f_p''+f_p'=\cos t$. Thus every solution is a sum of bounded
functions on $[0,\infty)$.
:::

<1>5. Not every solution of $f''-f'=\cos t$ remains bounded as
$t\to+\infty$.

::: {.proof}
The homogeneous characteristic equation is
\[
r^2-r=r(r-1)=0,
\]
so
\[
f_h(t)=C_1+C_2e^t.
\]
A particular solution is
\[
f_p(t)=-\frac12\cos t-\frac12\sin t.
\]
Therefore any solution with $C_2\neq0$ contains the unbounded term
$C_2e^t$.
:::

<1>6. The requested answers are
\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\text{(a)}\quad& C_1+C_2t+\frac{t^3}{6},\\
\text{(b)}\quad& C_1\cos t+C_2\sin t+t,\\
\text{(c)}\quad& C_1e^t+C_2e^{-t}-t,\\
\text{(d)}\quad&
f''+f'=\cos t:\ \text{all solutions bounded};\\
&f''-f'=\cos t:\ \text{not all solutions bounded}.
\end{aligned}}
\]

::: {.proof}
Steps <1>1--<1>5 establish the corresponding formulas and boundedness
claims.
:::

<1>7. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>6 records all requested answers.
:::
