::: {.problem}
Consider
$$
\dot x=(x-1)^2+y^2-10,
\qquad
\dot y=(x+1)^2+y^2-10.
$$

(a) Find the equilibria.

(b) Find the Jacobian at each equilibrium.

(c) Analyze stability at each equilibrium.

(d) Choose the matching source phase
portrait A--D. In the source, candidate A is the portrait with a saddle at
the upper equilibrium and an attracting spiral at the lower equilibrium.
:::

::: {.solution}

Let
$$
F(x,y)=(x-1)^2+y^2-10,
\qquad
G(x,y)=(x+1)^2+y^2-10.
$$

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

The equilibrium points are
$$
(0,3)
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
(0,-3).
$$


::: pf-proof

At an equilibrium, $F=G=0$. Subtracting the two equations gives
$$
G-F=(x+1)^2-(x-1)^2=4x=0,
$$
so $x=0$. Substituting into either equation gives
$$
1+y^2-10=0,
$$
hence $y^2=9$ and $y=\pm3$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

The Jacobian is
$$
J(x,y)=
\begin{pmatrix}
2(x-1)&2y\\
2(x+1)&2y
\end{pmatrix}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Differentiate the two components:
$$
F_x=2(x-1),\quad F_y=2y,\qquad
G_x=2(x+1),\quad G_y=2y.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

At $(0,3)$,
$$
J(0,3)=
\begin{pmatrix}
-2&6\\
2&6
\end{pmatrix},
$$
and the equilibrium is an unstable saddle.


::: pf-proof

The determinant is
$$
(-2)(6)-6(2)=-24<0.
$$
Hence the two eigenvalues are real and have opposite signs. The
linearization is therefore a saddle. Since neither eigenvalue is zero,
the equilibrium is hyperbolic, so the nonlinear equilibrium has the same
local saddle type and is unstable.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

At $(0,-3)$,
$$
J(0,-3)=
\begin{pmatrix}
-2&-6\\
2&-6
\end{pmatrix},
$$
and the equilibrium is a locally asymptotically stable spiral.


::: pf-proof

The trace and determinant are
$$
\operatorname{tr}J=-8,
\qquad
\det J=24.
$$
Thus the characteristic polynomial is
$$
\lambda^2+8\lambda+24,
$$
whose roots are
$$
\lambda=-4\pm2\sqrt2\,i.
$$
Both have negative real part, so the hyperbolic equilibrium is locally
asymptotically stable; the nonzero imaginary parts give the spiral type.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-5}

The matching phase portrait is A.


::: pf-proof

Steps [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} and [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} require a saddle at the upper equilibrium $(0,3)$
and an attracting spiral at the lower equilibrium $(0,-3)$. The source
description identifies candidate A as precisely that portrait.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-6}

Therefore
$$
\boxed{
\begin{gathered}
\text{equilibria }(0,3),(0,-3),\\
J(0,3)=\begin{pmatrix}-2&6\\2&6\end{pmatrix}
\text{ is a saddle linearization},\\
J(0,-3)=\begin{pmatrix}-2&-6\\2&-6\end{pmatrix}
\text{ is an attracting-spiral linearization},\\
\text{and the source phase portrait is A.}
\end{gathered}}
$$


::: pf-proof

Steps [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} through [](#s1-5){.pf-ref} establish parts (a)--(d).

:::
