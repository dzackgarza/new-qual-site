::: {.problem}
On $[-\pi,\pi]$, let
\[
f(x)=
\begin{cases}
1,&0<x\le1,\\
-1,&-1\le x\le0,\\
0,&|x|>1.
\end{cases}
\]
(a) Find the Fourier series of $f$. (b) Find the sum of the squares of all Fourier coefficients.
:::
