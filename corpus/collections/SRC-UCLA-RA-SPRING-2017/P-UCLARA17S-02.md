Let $f_n:[0,1]\to[0,\infty)$ be a sequence of functions, each non-decreasing on $[0,1]$. Suppose the sequence is uniformly bounded in $L^2([0,1])$. Show that there exists a subsequence that converges in $L^1([0,1])$.
