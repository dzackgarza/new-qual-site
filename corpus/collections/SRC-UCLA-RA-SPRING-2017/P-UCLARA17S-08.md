Problem 8. Let a1 , a2 , ...., an be n ≥ 1 points in the disc D = {|z| < 1} (possibly with repetitions), so that the function n Y z − aj B(z) = j=1 1 − aj z has n zeros in D. Prove that the derivative B 0 (z) has n − 1 zeros in D.
