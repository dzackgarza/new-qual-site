::: {.problem}
Let $f(z)$ be a one-to-one continuous mapping from the closed annulus
\[
\{1\le|z|\le R\}
\]
onto the closed annulus
\[
\{1\le|z|\le S\}
\]
such that $f$ is analytic on the open annulus $\{1<|z|<R\}$. Prove that $S=R$.
:::
