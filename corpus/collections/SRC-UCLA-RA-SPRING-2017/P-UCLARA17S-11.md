::: {.problem}
Let $1\le p<\infty$ and let $U(z)$ be harmonic on $\mathbb C$ such that
\[
\iint_{\mathbb R\times\mathbb R}|U(x+iy)|^p\,dx\,dy<\infty.
\]
Prove that $U(z)=0$ for all $z=x+iy\in\mathbb C$.
:::
