::: {.problem}
Assume that $f(z)$ is analytic in $\{z : |z| < 1\}$ and continuous on $\{z : |z| \leq 1\}$. If $f(z) = f(1/z)$ when $|z| = 1$, prove that $f(z)$ is constant.
:::
