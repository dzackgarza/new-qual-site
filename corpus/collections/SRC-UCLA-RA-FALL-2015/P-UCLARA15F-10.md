::: {.problem}
Use the Residue Theorem to prove that
\[
\int_0^\infty e^{\cos x} \sin(\sin x)\,\frac{dx}{x} = \frac{\pi}{2}(e - 1).
\]
Use a large semicircle as part of the contour.
:::
