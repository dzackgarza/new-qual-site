::: {.problem}
Let $\omega$ be a linear $2$-form on a finite-dimensional vector space $V$, and define
$$
V^\omega=\{v\in V:(\forall w\in V)\,\omega(v,w)=0\}=\ker\theta_\omega.
$$
Prove that there is a linear symplectic form $\bar\omega$ on $V/V^\omega$, uniquely characterized by
$$
\pi^*\bar\omega=\omega,
$$
where $\pi:V\to V/V^\omega$ is the quotient projection.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

Define
$$
\bar\omega([v],[w])
\coloneqq
\omega(v,w),
$$
where $[v]=v+V^\omega$ and $[w]=w+V^\omega$.


::: pf-proof

This gives a candidate bilinear form on the quotient. The next step proves that it is independent of the chosen representatives.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

The formula in step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} is well defined.


::: pf-proof

Suppose
$$
v'=v+k,
\qquad
w'=w+\ell,
$$
with $k,\ell\in V^\omega$. Then
$$
\begin{aligned}
\omega(v',w')
&=
\omega(v,w)
+\omega(k,w)
+\omega(v,\ell)
+\omega(k,\ell).
\end{aligned}
$$
By definition of $V^\omega$,
$$
\omega(k,w)=\omega(k,\ell)=0.
$$
Since $\omega$ is alternating,
$$
\omega(v,\ell)=-\omega(\ell,v)=0.
$$
Thus
$$
\omega(v',w')=\omega(v,w).
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

The form $\bar\omega$ is alternating and bilinear.


::: pf-proof

Both properties follow immediately from the corresponding properties of $\omega$ and the definition in step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

The form $\bar\omega$ is nondegenerate.


::: pf-proof

Suppose $[v]\in V/V^\omega$ satisfies
$$
\bar\omega([v],[w])=0
$$
for every $[w]\in V/V^\omega$. By step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref}, this means
$$
\omega(v,w)=0
$$
for every $w\in V$. Hence
$$
v\in V^\omega,
$$
so $[v]=0$ in the quotient. Therefore the kernel of $\bar\omega$ is zero.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-5}

The quotient projection satisfies
$$
\boxed{\pi^*\bar\omega=\omega}.
$$


::: pf-proof

For $v,w\in V$,
$$
\begin{aligned}
(\pi^*\bar\omega)(v,w)
&=
\bar\omega(\pi(v),\pi(w))\\
&=
\bar\omega([v],[w])\\
&=
\omega(v,w)
\end{aligned}
$$
by step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-6}

The form $\bar\omega$ is uniquely characterized by
$$
\pi^*\bar\omega=\omega.
$$


::: pf-proof

Let $\eta$ be any bilinear form on $V/V^\omega$ satisfying
$$
\pi^*\eta=\omega.
$$
Since $\pi$ is surjective, every pair of quotient vectors has the form $([v],[w])$. Then
$$
\eta([v],[w])
=
(\pi^*\eta)(v,w)
=
\omega(v,w)
=
\bar\omega([v],[w]).
$$
Thus $\eta=\bar\omega$.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Steps [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} through [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} show that $\bar\omega$ is a well-defined linear symplectic form, step [](#s1-5){.pf-ref} proves the pullback identity, and step [](#s1-6){.pf-ref} proves uniqueness.

:::
