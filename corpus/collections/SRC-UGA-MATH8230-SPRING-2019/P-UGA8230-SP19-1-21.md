::: {.problem}
Let $x\in\RR^{2n}$ and $v\in T_x\RR^{2n}$.
Prove that there is a smooth function $H:\RR^{2n}\to\RR$ such that $X_H(x)=v$.
:::

::: {.solution}

Use the coordinates
$$
x=(p_0,q_0)\in\RR^n\times\RR^n
$$
and write
$$
v=(a,b)\in T_x\RR^{2n}\cong\RR^n\times\RR^n.
$$
With the convention of the notes,
$$
X_H(p,q)
=
\left(-\nabla_qH(p,q),\nabla_pH(p,q)\right).
$$

::: pf

::: pf-step

Define the affine function
$$
H(p,q)
\coloneqq
b\cdot(p-p_0)-a\cdot(q-q_0).
$$


::: pf-proof

This is a globally defined smooth real-valued function on $\RR^{2n}$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

Its partial gradients are
$$
\nabla_pH=b,
\qquad
\nabla_qH=-a.
$$


::: pf-proof

The $p$-dependence of $H$ is the linear function $b\cdot p$ up to an additive constant, while the $q$-dependence is $-a\cdot q$ up to an additive constant.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

The Hamiltonian vector field of $H$ is the constant vector field $v$:
$$
X_H(p,q)
=
(a,b)
$$
for every $(p,q)\in\RR^{2n}$.


::: pf-proof

Substituting step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} into the defining formula for $X_H$ gives
$$
X_H
=
\left(-(-a),b\right)
=
(a,b).
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

In particular,
$$
\boxed{X_H(x)=v}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Evaluate the identity of step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} at the prescribed point $x=(p_0,q_0)$.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} is the required conclusion.

:::

:::

:::
