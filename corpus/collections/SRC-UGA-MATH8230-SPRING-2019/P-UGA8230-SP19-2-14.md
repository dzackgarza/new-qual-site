::: {.problem}
(a) Prove that in the symplectic vector space $(\mathbb R^4,\omega_0)$ every subspace is symplectic, isotropic, or coisotropic.
Hint: if $V$ is the subspace, consider the possible values of the rank of $\omega_0|_{V\times V}$.

(b) Give an example of a subspace of $\mathbb R^6$ that is neither symplectic, nor isotropic, nor coisotropic with respect to $\omega_0$.
:::
