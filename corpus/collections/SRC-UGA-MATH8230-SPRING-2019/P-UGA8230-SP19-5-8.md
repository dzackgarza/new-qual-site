::: {.problem}
Let
$$
D^*=\{z\in\CC:0<\abs{z}<1\}
$$
with the standard symplectic structure $dp\wedge dq$, where $z=p+iq$.
Using
$$
dp\wedge dq=d\!\left(\frac12 r^2\right)\wedge d\theta,
$$
give an explicit symplectomorphism $\phi:D^*\to D^*$ that turns $D^*$ inside out: if $z_n\to0$ in $D^*$, then $\abs{\phi(z_n)}\to1$.
:::

::: {.solution}

Write
$$
z=re^{i\theta},
\qquad
0<r<1,
$$
and set
$$
s\coloneqq\frac12r^2.
$$
Then
$$
0<s<\frac12
$$
and
$$
dp\wedge dq=ds\wedge d\theta.
$$

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

Define
$$
\Phi\left(s,\theta\right)
=
\left(\frac12-s,-\theta\right).
$$
This is a diffeomorphism of
$$
\left(0,\frac12\right)\times S^1
$$
and satisfies
$$
\Phi^*(ds\wedge d\theta)=ds\wedge d\theta.
$$


::: pf-proof

The map is an involution:
$$
\Phi(\Phi(s,\theta))
=
(s,\theta).
$$
Hence it is a diffeomorphism.

Moreover,
$$
\Phi^*ds=-ds,
\qquad
\Phi^*d\theta=-d\theta.
$$
Therefore
$$
\Phi^*(ds\wedge d\theta)
=
(-ds)\wedge(-d\theta)
=
ds\wedge d\theta.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

In polar coordinates, the corresponding map on $D^*$ is
$$
\boxed{
\phi(re^{i\theta})
=
\sqrt{1-r^2}\,e^{-i\theta}}.
$$


::: pf-proof

If
$$
s'=\frac12-s,
$$
then
$$
\frac12(r')^2
=
\frac12-\frac12r^2,
$$
so
$$
r'=\sqrt{1-r^2}.
$$
The angular coordinate is
$$
\theta'=-\theta.
$$
Thus the formula is exactly the map induced by $\Phi$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

The formula in step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} defines a smooth diffeomorphism
$$
\phi:D^*\longrightarrow D^*.
$$


::: pf-proof

For
$$
0<r<1,
$$
one has
$$
0<\sqrt{1-r^2}<1,
$$
so the image lies in $D^*$. The expression is independent of the chosen representative of the angle modulo $2\pi$.

Equivalently, in Cartesian notation,
$$
\phi(z)
=
\sqrt{1-\abs{z}^2}\,
\frac{\overline z}{\abs{z}},
$$
which is smooth for $0<\abs{z}<1$.

Since the coordinate map $\Phi$ is an involution, so is $\phi$. Hence $\phi$ is a diffeomorphism.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

The map $\phi$ is symplectic:
$$
\boxed{\phi^*(dp\wedge dq)=dp\wedge dq}.
$$


::: pf-proof

In the coordinates
$$
(s,\theta),
$$
the standard symplectic form is
$$
ds\wedge d\theta.
$$
Step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} shows that $\Phi$ preserves this form. Step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} identifies $\phi$ with $\Phi$ under the polar action–angle coordinates, so $\phi$ preserves $dp\wedge dq$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-5}

The map turns the punctured disk inside out.


::: pf-proof

If
$$
z_n\to0,
$$
then
$$
r_n\coloneqq\abs{z_n}\to0.
$$
By step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref},
$$
\abs{\phi(z_n)}
=
\sqrt{1-r_n^2}
\longrightarrow1.
$$

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Steps [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} through [](#s1-5){.pf-ref} show that the explicit map in step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} is a symplectomorphism of $D^*$ with the required inside-out behavior.

:::
