::: {.problem}
Let $F,G\in C^\infty(M)$ on a symplectic manifold. Prove that
\[
X_{\{F,G\}}=[X_F,X_G],
\]
where the right-hand side is the commutator $X_F\circ X_G-X_G\circ X_F$ under the identification of vector fields with derivations on $C^\infty(M)$.
:::
