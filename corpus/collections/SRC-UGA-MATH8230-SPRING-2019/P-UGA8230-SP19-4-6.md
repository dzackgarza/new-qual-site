::: {.problem}
Suppose that $V=(V_t)_{t\in[0,1]}$ is a time-dependent vector field whose time-one flow $\psi_{V,1}$ is defined on all of $M$. Construct another time-dependent vector field $W=(W_t)_{t\in[0,1]}$ such that
\[
\psi_{W,1}=(\psi_{V,1})^{-1}.
\]
Suggestion: arrange that whenever $\gamma:[0,1]\to M$ satisfies $\dot\gamma(t)=V_t(\gamma(t))$, the reversed curve $\bar\gamma(t)=\gamma(1-t)$ satisfies $\dot{\bar\gamma}(t)=W_t(\bar\gamma(t))$.
:::
