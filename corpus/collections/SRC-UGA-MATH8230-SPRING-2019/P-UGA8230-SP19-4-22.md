::: {.problem}
Let $\omega_0$ be the standard area form on $S^2$, so that for $x\in S^2$ and $v,w\in T_xS^2=\{x\}^\perp\subset\mathbb R^3$,
\[
(\omega_0)_x(v,w)=x\cdot(v\times w).
\]
Let $M=S^2\times S^2$ with product symplectic form
\[
\omega=\pi_1^*\omega_0+\pi_2^*\omega_0.
\]
Use without proof that $H_2(M;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z\oplus\mathbb Z$, generated by
\[
A=[S^2\times\{y\}],\qquad B=[\{x\}\times S^2].
\]

(a) If $\alpha:S^2\to S^2$ is the antipodal map, prove that
\[
L=\{(x,\alpha(x)):x\in S^2\}
\]
is a Lagrangian submanifold of $M$.
Determine its homology class in terms of $A$ and $B$.

(b) Prove that for positive integers $m,n$ there is a symplectic submanifold of $M$ representing $mA+nB$.
Hint: start with a union of horizontal and vertical spheres and smooth their transverse intersections symplectically.
:::
