::: {.problem}
Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional real vector space and let $B:V\times V\to\mathbb R$ be bilinear.
Define $\theta_B:V\to V^*$ by
\[
(\theta_B(v))(w)=B(v,w).
\]

(a) Using the canonical identification $V\cong V^{**}$, show that the adjoint $\theta_B^*:V\to V^*$ is given by
\[
(\theta_B^*(v))(w)=B(w,v).
\]
In particular, prove that $B$ is alternating if and only if $\theta_B^*=-\theta_B$.

(b) Choose a basis $E=\{e_1,\dots,e_k\}$ of $V$, and let $M$ be the matrix of $\theta_B:V\to V^*$ with respect to $E$ and the dual basis $E^*$.
Show that $B$ is alternating if and only if $M$ is skew-symmetric, i.e. $M=-M^T$.
:::
