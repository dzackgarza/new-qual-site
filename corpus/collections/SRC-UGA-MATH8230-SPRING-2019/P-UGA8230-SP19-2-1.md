::: {.problem}
Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional real vector space and let $B:V\times V\to\RR$ be bilinear.
Define $\theta_B:V\to V^*$ by
$$
(\theta_B(v))(w)=B(v,w).
$$

(a) Using the canonical identification $V\cong V^{**}$, show that the adjoint $\theta_B^*:V\to V^*$ is given by
$$
(\theta_B^*(v))(w)=B(w,v).
$$
In particular, prove that $B$ is alternating if and only if $\theta_B^*=-\theta_B$.

(b) Choose a basis $E=\{e_1,\dots,e_k\}$ of $V$, and let $M$ be the matrix of $\theta_B:V\to V^*$ with respect to $E$ and the dual basis $E^*$.
Show that $B$ is alternating if and only if $M$ is skew-symmetric, i.e. $M=-M^T$.
:::

::: {.solution}

Let
$$
\iota:V\longrightarrow V^{**},
\qquad
\iota(v)(\lambda)=\lambda(v),
$$
be the canonical isomorphism.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

Under the identification $V\cong V^{**}$,
$$
\boxed{
(\theta_B^*(v))(w)=B(w,v)}.
$$


::: pf-proof

By definition of the dual map,
$$
\theta_B^*:V^{**}\longrightarrow V^*
$$
satisfies
$$
(\theta_B^*\Phi)(w)
=
\Phi(\theta_B(w)).
$$
Taking $\Phi=\iota(v)$ gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
(\theta_B^*(v))(w)
&=
\iota(v)(\theta_B(w))\\
&=
(\theta_B(w))(v)\\
&=
B(w,v).
\end{aligned}
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

A real bilinear form $B$ is alternating if and only if
$$
B(v,w)=-B(w,v)
$$
for all $v,w\in V$.


::: pf-proof

If $B$ is alternating, then
$$
0
=
B(v+w,v+w)
=
B(v,w)+B(w,v),
$$
because $B(v,v)=B(w,w)=0$. Thus $B(v,w)=-B(w,v)$.

Conversely, if $B(v,w)=-B(w,v)$ for all $v,w$, then
$$
B(v,v)=-B(v,v).
$$
Over $\RR$ this implies $B(v,v)=0$, so $B$ is alternating.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

One has
$$
\boxed{
B\text{ alternating}
\iff
\theta_B^*=-\theta_B}.
$$


::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref},
$$
(\theta_B^*(v))(w)=B(w,v),
$$
while by definition
$$
(\theta_B(v))(w)=B(v,w).
$$
Thus $\theta_B^*=-\theta_B$ exactly when
$$
B(w,v)=-B(v,w)
$$
for all $v,w$, which is equivalent to alternation by step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

If $M$ is the matrix of $\theta_B$ in the bases $E$ and $E^*$, then
$$
M_{ij}=B(e_j,e_i).
$$


::: pf-proof

The $j$th column of $M$ is the coordinate vector of
$$
\theta_B(e_j)\in V^*
$$
in the dual basis. Its $i$th coordinate is
$$
(\theta_B(e_j))(e_i)=B(e_j,e_i).
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-5}

The form $B$ is alternating if and only if
$$
\boxed{M=-M^T}.
$$


::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1-4){.pf-ref},
$$
(M^T)_{ij}
=
M_{ji}
=
B(e_i,e_j).
$$
Hence
$$
M=-M^T
$$
is equivalent to
$$
B(e_j,e_i)=-B(e_i,e_j)
$$
for all basis vectors. By bilinearity this is equivalent to skew-symmetry on all of $V\times V$, and by step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} that is equivalent to $B$ being alternating.

:::
