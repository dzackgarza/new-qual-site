::: {.problem}
Suppose that $\lambda\in\Omega^k(M)$ and that $V$ is a vector field on $M$ satisfying
$$
\iota_V d\lambda=\lambda.
$$
Prove that
$$
\psi_{V,t}^*d\lambda=e^t d\lambda
$$
for every $t$ for which the flow $\psi_{V,t}$ is defined.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

The Lie derivative of $d\lambda$ along $V$ is
$$
\boxed{\mathcal L_V(d\lambda)=d\lambda}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Cartan's formula gives
$$
\mathcal L_V(d\lambda)
=
d(\iota_Vd\lambda)
+\iota_V(d^2\lambda).
$$
By hypothesis,
$$
\iota_Vd\lambda=\lambda,
$$
and $d^2=0$. Hence
$$
\mathcal L_V(d\lambda)
=
d\lambda.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

If
$$
\alpha_t\coloneqq\psi_{V,t}^*d\lambda,
$$
then
$$
\frac{d}{dt}\alpha_t=\alpha_t
$$
and
$$
\alpha_0=d\lambda.
$$


::: pf-proof

The differentiation formula for pullback by a flow gives
$$
\frac d{dt}\psi_{V,t}^*d\lambda
=
\psi_{V,t}^*(\mathcal L_Vd\lambda).
$$
By step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref}, this becomes
$$
\frac d{dt}\alpha_t
=
\psi_{V,t}^*d\lambda
=
\alpha_t.
$$
Since $\psi_{V,0}=\operatorname{id}_M$,
$$
\alpha_0=d\lambda.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

Therefore
$$
\boxed{
\psi_{V,t}^*d\lambda=e^t d\lambda}
$$
for every time $t$ for which the flow is defined.


::: pf-proof

Consider
$$
\beta_t\coloneqq e^{-t}\alpha_t.
$$
By step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref},
$$
\frac d{dt}\beta_t
=
-e^{-t}\alpha_t
+e^{-t}\frac d{dt}\alpha_t
=0.
$$
Thus $\beta_t$ is constant in $t$, and
$$
\beta_t=\beta_0=d\lambda.
$$
Multiplying by $e^t$ gives the claimed identity.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} is exactly the required flow-scaling formula.

:::
