::: {.problem}
Let $F=\mathbb R^2/\mathbb Z^2$ and let
\[
\phi([x,y])=[x+y,y].
\]
Form the mapping torus
\[
Y_\phi=(\mathbb R\times F)/((t+2\pi,[x,y])\sim(t,\phi([x,y]))).
\]
Show that
\[
H_1(S^1\times Y_\phi;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^3.
\]
:::

Let
$$
a,b\in\pi_1(F)\cong\ZZ^2
$$
be the standard generators represented by the $x$- and $y$-coordinate circles.

::: pf

::: pf-step

The induced map
$$
\phi_*:\pi_1(F)\longrightarrow\pi_1(F)
$$
satisfies
$$
\boxed{
\phi_*(a)=a,
\qquad
\phi_*(b)=ab}.
$$


::: pf-proof

The $x$-circle is
$$
s\longmapsto[s,0].
$$
Applying $\phi$ gives
$$
[s,0]\longmapsto[s,0],
$$
so its class is fixed.

The $y$-circle is
$$
s\longmapsto[0,s].
$$
Applying $\phi$ gives
$$
[0,s]\longmapsto[s,s],
$$
which winds once around each coordinate circle. Since
$$
\pi_1(F)\cong\ZZ^2
$$
is abelian, this class is $ab$.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

The fundamental group of the mapping torus has presentation
$$
\pi_1(Y_\phi)
\cong
\left\langle
a,b,t
\middle|[a,b]=1,
tat^{-1}=a,
tbt^{-1}=ab
\right\rangle,
$$
where $t$ is the loop around the mapping-torus direction.


::: pf-proof

The mapping torus is obtained from
$$
F\times[0,2\pi]
$$
by identifying
$$
(x,2\pi)
\sim
(\phi(x),0).
$$
Thus its fundamental group is the semidirect product
$$
\pi_1(F)\rtimes_{\phi_*}\ZZ.
$$
Writing the generator of the $\ZZ$ factor as $t$ gives the standard presentation with conjugation by $t$ implementing $\phi_*$. Step <1>1 gives the two displayed conjugation relations.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

The abelianization of $\pi_1(Y_\phi)$ is
$$
\boxed{\ZZ^2},
$$
generated by the images of $b$ and $t$.


::: pf-proof

In the abelianization all generators commute. The relation
$$
tbt^{-1}=ab
$$
therefore becomes
$$
b=a+b
$$
in additive notation, which forces
$$
a=0.
$$
The relation
$$
tat^{-1}=a
$$
becomes tautological, and no relation remains between $b$ and $t$. Hence the abelianization is the free abelian group
$$
\ZZ b\oplus\ZZ t
\cong
\ZZ^2.
$$

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Therefore
$$
H_1(Y_\phi;\ZZ)
\cong
\ZZ^2.
$$


::: pf-proof

For every path-connected space,
$$
H_1(-;\ZZ)
$$
is canonically the abelianization of the fundamental group. Apply step <1>3.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

The first homology of the product is
$$
H_1(S^1\times Y_\phi;\ZZ)
\cong
H_1(S^1;\ZZ)
\oplus
H_1(Y_\phi;\ZZ).
$$


::: pf-proof

The degree-one part of the Künneth theorem gives
$$
H_1(X\times Y;\ZZ)
\cong
H_1(X;\ZZ)\otimes H_0(Y;\ZZ)
\oplus
H_0(X;\ZZ)\otimes H_1(Y;\ZZ)
$$
for path-connected spaces $X,Y$. Since both spaces here are connected and their $H_0$ groups are $\ZZ$, the displayed decomposition follows.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Consequently,
$$
\boxed{
H_1(S^1\times Y_\phi;\ZZ)
\cong
\ZZ^3}.
$$


::: pf-proof

By step <1>4,
$$
H_1(Y_\phi;\ZZ)
\cong
\ZZ^2,
$$
while
$$
H_1(S^1;\ZZ)
\cong
\ZZ.
$$
Substituting these groups into step <1>5 gives
$$
\ZZ\oplus\ZZ^2
\cong
\ZZ^3.
$$

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step <1>6 is the required computation.

:::

:::
