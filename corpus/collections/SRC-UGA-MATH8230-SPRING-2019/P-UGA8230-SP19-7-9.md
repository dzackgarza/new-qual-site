::: {.problem}
Let $F=\mathbb R^2/\mathbb Z^2$ and let
\[
\phi([x,y])=[x+y,y].
\]
Form the mapping torus
\[
Y_\phi=(\mathbb R\times F)/((t+2\pi,[x,y])\sim(t,\phi([x,y]))).
\]
Show that
\[
H_1(S^1\times Y_\phi;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^3.
\]
:::
