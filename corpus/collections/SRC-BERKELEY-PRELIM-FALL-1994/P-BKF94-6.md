::: problem
Let $A=(a_{ij})$ be a real $n\times n$ matrix with $a_{ii}\ge1$ for every $i$ and
\[
\sum_{i\ne j}a_{ij}^2<1.
\]
Prove that $A$ is invertible.
:::
