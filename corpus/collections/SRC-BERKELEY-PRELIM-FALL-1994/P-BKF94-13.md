::: {.problem}
Let $p$ be an odd prime. How many elements of $\mathbb F_p$ have square roots in $\mathbb F_p$? How many have cube roots in $\mathbb F_p$?
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. For every positive integer $k$, the map
$$
\varphi_k:\FF_p^\times\longrightarrow\FF_p^\times,
\qquad
x\longmapsto x^k
$$
has image of size
$$
\frac{p-1}{\gcd(k,p-1)}.
$$

::: {.proof}
The group $\FF_p^\times$ is cyclic of order $p-1$. Let $g$ be a generator.
Then
$$
\varphi_k(g^m)=g^{km}.
$$
Its kernel consists of the residue classes $m$ modulo $p-1$ satisfying
$$
km\equiv0\pmod{p-1}.
$$
There are exactly
$$
\gcd(k,p-1)
$$
such classes. The first isomorphism theorem therefore gives
$$
\abs{\operatorname{im}\varphi_k}
=
\frac{p-1}{\gcd(k,p-1)}.
$$
:::

<1>2. The number of elements of $\FF_p$ having square roots in $\FF_p$ is
$$
\boxed{\frac{p+1}{2}}.
$$

::: {.proof}
Since $p$ is odd,
$$
\gcd(2,p-1)=2.
$$
Step <1>1 therefore gives
$$
\frac{p-1}{2}
$$
nonzero squares. The element $0$ also has the square root $0$, so the total
is
$$
1+\frac{p-1}{2}
=
\frac{p+1}{2}.
$$
:::

<1>3. The number of elements of $\FF_p$ having cube roots in $\FF_p$ is
$$
\boxed{
1+\frac{p-1}{\gcd(3,p-1)}
}.
$$

::: {.proof}
Step <1>1 with $k=3$ counts the nonzero cubes, and $0$ contributes one more
element.
:::

<1>4. Equivalently, the number of cubes is
$$
\boxed{
\begin{cases}
p,&p=3\text{ or }p\equiv2\pmod3,\\[2mm]
\dfrac{p+2}{3},&p\equiv1\pmod3.
\end{cases}
}
$$

::: {.proof}
If $p=3$ or $p\equiv2\pmod3$, then
$$
\gcd(3,p-1)=1,
$$
so step <1>3 gives $p$. If $p\equiv1\pmod3$, then
$$
\gcd(3,p-1)=3,
$$
and step <1>3 gives
$$
1+\frac{p-1}{3}
=
\frac{p+2}{3}.
$$
:::

<1>5. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>2 gives the square count, and steps <1>3--<1>4 give the cube count.
:::
:::
