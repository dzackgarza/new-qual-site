::: {.problem}
For real $\theta$, let
\[
F_\theta=\mathbb Q(\sin\theta),\qquad E_\theta=\mathbb Q\!\left(\sin\frac\theta3\right).
\]
Show that $E_\theta$ is an extension of $F_\theta$ and determine all possibilities for $[E_\theta:F_\theta]$.
:::
