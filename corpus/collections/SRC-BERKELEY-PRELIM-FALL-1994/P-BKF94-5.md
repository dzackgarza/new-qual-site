::: problem
(a) Find a basis for the real solution space of
\[
\sum_{n=0}^{7}\frac{d^nx}{dt^n}=0.
\]

(b) Find a basis for the subspace of solutions satisfying $x(t)\to0$ as $t\to+\infty$.
:::
