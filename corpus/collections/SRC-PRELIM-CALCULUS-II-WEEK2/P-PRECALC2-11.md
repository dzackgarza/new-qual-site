::: {.problem}
Evaluate
\[
\lim_{n\to\infty}(3^n+5^n)^{1/n}.
\]
More generally, if $x_1,\ldots,x_k>0$, evaluate
\[
\lim_{n\to\infty}(x_1^n+\cdots+x_k^n)^{1/n}.
\]
:::
