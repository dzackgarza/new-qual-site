::: {.problem}
Let $K$ be a compact subset and $F$ a closed subset of a metric space $X$.
Suppose $K\cap F=\varnothing$.
Prove that
\[
0<\inf\{d(x,y):x\in K,\ y\in F\}.
\]
:::
