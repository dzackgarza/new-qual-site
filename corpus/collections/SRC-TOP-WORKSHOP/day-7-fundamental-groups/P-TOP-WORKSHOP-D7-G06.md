::: {.problem}
Attach a $2$-disk $D^2$ to a torus $S^1\times S^1$ by the attaching map $e^{2\pi it}\mapsto(e^{2\pi it},e^{2\pi it})$, thinking of the boundary $\partial D^2$ and each $S^1$ factor as the unit circle in the complex plane, and let $X$ be the resulting space.
Compute a presentation for the fundamental group of $X$.
:::

::: {.solution}

The argument is the card's own: van Kampen turns the attachment into one relation, and the two relations together present $\ZZ$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #torus}

$\pi_{1}(T^{2})=\langle a,b\mid[a,b]\rangle$, where $a,b$ are the classes of the two circle factors.

::: pf-proof

$T^{2}=S^{1}\times S^{1}$ with $\pi_{1}(S^{1})=\ZZ$ generated by the loop $z\mapsto z$ in each factor; by the product formula $\pi_{1}(T^{2})\cong\ZZ\times\ZZ$, generated by $a=(1,0)$ and $b=(0,1)$, and this group is abelian, so the single relation $[a,b]=1$ presents it.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #attaching}

The attaching map represents $ab\in\pi_{1}(T^{2})$, so $\pi_{1}(X)=\langle a,b\mid[a,b],ab\rangle$.

::: pf-proof

The attaching map sends $e^{2\pi it}$ to $(e^{2\pi it},e^{2\pi it})$, which traverses the first circle once positively and the second once positively as $t$ runs over $[0,1]$; its class is therefore the product $ab$. By the Seifert–van Kampen theorem applied to $T^{2}$ and the attached disk, the fundamental group of $X$ is $\pi_{1}(T^{2})$ with the image of the boundary class killed, i.e. the presentation of step [](#torus){.pf-ref} with the extra relation $ab=1$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #collapse}

$\langle a,b\mid[a,b],ab\rangle\cong\ZZ$.

::: pf-proof

The relation $ab=1$ gives $b=a^{-1}$; substituting, the commutator relation becomes $[a,a^{-1}]=aa^{-1}a^{-1}a=1$, which is automatic, so it imposes nothing further. Thus the presentation reduces to $\langle a\mid\ \rangle\cong\ZZ$, generated by the image of $a$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #conclusion}

$\pi_{1}(X)\cong\ZZ$.

::: pf-proof

Steps [](#attaching){.pf-ref} and [](#collapse){.pf-ref} give
$$
\boxed{\pi_{1}(X)\cong\langle a,b\mid[a,b],ab\rangle\cong\ZZ.}
$$
Geometrically the disk closes up the loop $ab$, leaving one free generator.

:::
