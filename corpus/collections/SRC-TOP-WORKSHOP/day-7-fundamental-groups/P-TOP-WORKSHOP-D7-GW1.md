::: {.problem}
Let $G=\langle x,y\mid x^2yxy=1\rangle$.
Describe a space $X$ that has $\pi_1(X)=G$.
:::

::: {.solution}
No retained solution set covers this item, the collection's provenance being the worksheet, so the construction is the card's own; it is the standard realisation of a finite presentation by a two-dimensional CW complex.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #rose}
The wedge $R=S^1_x\vee S^1_y$ of two circles has $\pi_1(R)=F(x,y)$, the free group on two generators.

::: pf-proof
The wedge of two path connected spaces with a common basepoint has fundamental group the free product of the two fundamental groups, by van Kampen's theorem applied to the two open halves; and $\pi_1(S^1)=\ZZ$ with generator the loop going once around. So $\pi_1(R)=\ZZ*\ZZ=F(x,y)$, the letters $x,y$ being the two generating loops.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #attach}
Let $X$ be $R$ with a $2$-cell attached along a loop $\gamma$ representing the word $x^2yxy$. Then $\pi_1(X)\cong F(x,y)/\langle\!\langle x^2yxy\rangle\!\rangle$.

::: pf-proof
The attaching map $S^1\to R$ represents the element $x^2yxy$ of $\pi_1(R)$. Van Kampen's theorem for the decomposition $X=R\cup e^2$ gives $\pi_1(X)\cong\pi_1(R)/N$, where $N$ is the normal subgroup generated by the class of the attaching loop; a loop going once around the attached disc bounds a disc in $X$ and so becomes null, and conversely every relation imposed by the cell is that one. Hence $N=\langle\!\langle x^2yxy\rangle\!\rangle$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step}
$\boxed{\pi_1(X)\cong G=\langle x,y\mid x^2yxy=1\rangle}$, realised by a two-petal rose with one $2$-cell attached along $x^2yxy$.

::: pf-proof
By definition $\langle x,y\mid x^2yxy=1\rangle=F(x,y)/\langle\!\langle x^2yxy\rangle\!\rangle$, so step [](#attach){.pf-ref} gives $\pi_1(X)\cong G$; step [](#rose){.pf-ref} identifies the $1$-skeleton. The complex is connected, has one vertex, two $1$-cells and one $2$-cell, so it is a finite CW complex and $\pi_1$ captures its fundamental group.
:::
