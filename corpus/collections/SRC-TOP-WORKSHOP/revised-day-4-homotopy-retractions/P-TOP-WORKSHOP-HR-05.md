::: {.problem}
Let $f:S^1\vee S^1\to T$ be a continuous map, where $T$ is the torus. Show that there is no continuous map
\[
g:T\to S^1\vee S^1
\]
such that $f\circ g$ is the identity map on $T$.
:::
