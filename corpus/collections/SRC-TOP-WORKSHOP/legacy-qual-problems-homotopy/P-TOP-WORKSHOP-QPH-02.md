::: {.problem}
Let $D^2$ be the closed $2$-disk and $S^1=\partial D^2$.
Is
\[
X=S^1\times S^1
\]
a retract of
\[
Y=D^2\times S^1?
\]
That is, does there exist a continuous map $r:Y\to X$ whose restriction to $X\subset Y$ is the identity?
:::
