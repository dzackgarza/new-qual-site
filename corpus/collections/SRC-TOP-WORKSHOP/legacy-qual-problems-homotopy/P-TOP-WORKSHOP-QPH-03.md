::: {.problem}
Suppose $\phi:S^1\to S^1$ and $\psi:S^1\to S^1$ are continuous maps.
Show that the compositions $\phi\circ\psi$ and $\psi\circ\phi$ are homotopic.

Hint: Is this true for $f(z)=z^n$, where $n\in\mathbb Z$?
:::
