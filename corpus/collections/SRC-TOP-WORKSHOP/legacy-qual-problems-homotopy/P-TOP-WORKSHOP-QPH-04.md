::: {.problem}
Let $f:D^2\to D^2$ be a homeomorphism, where $D^2$ is the closed unit disk in $\mathbb R^2$.
Show that every boundary point $x\in\partial D^2$ satisfies
\[
f(x)\in\partial D^2.
\]
:::
