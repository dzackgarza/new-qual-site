::: {.problem}
(Michigan Sept ’10) Consider the 2-dimensional torus $T$ and the topological space $$X=T\times[-1,1]/\sim$$ where $(x,t)\sim(x',t')$ if either $(x,t)=(x',t')$ or $t=t'\in\{-1,1\}$.
Compute $H_*(X,\mathbb Z)$.
:::
