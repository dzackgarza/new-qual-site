:::{.problem}
Let $G$ be a group of order $120$ and $H\le G$ a subgroup of order $24$. Assume that at least one left coset of $H$, other than $H$ itself, is equal to some right coset of $H$.
Prove that $H$ is normal in $G$.
:::
