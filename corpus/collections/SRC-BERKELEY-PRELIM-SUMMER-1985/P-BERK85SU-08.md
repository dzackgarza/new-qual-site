::: {.problem}
Let $f:[a,b]\to\mathbb R$ be continuous. Given $\varepsilon>0$, prove that there is a polynomial $p$ such that
\[
p(a)=f(a),\qquad p'(a)=0,
\]
and
\[
|p(x)-f(x)|<\varepsilon\qquad (x\in[a,b]).
\]
:::
