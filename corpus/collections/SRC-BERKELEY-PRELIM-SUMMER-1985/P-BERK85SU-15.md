::: {.problem}
For each $k>0$, let $X_k$ be the set of analytic functions $f$ on the open unit disk $\mathbb D$ such that
\[
\sup_{z\in\mathbb D}(1-|z|)^k|f(z)|<\infty.
\]
Prove that
\[
f\in X_k\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad f'\in X_{k+1}.
\]
:::
