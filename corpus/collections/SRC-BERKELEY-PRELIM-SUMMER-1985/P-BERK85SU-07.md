:::{.problem}
For $x>0$, let
\[
f(x)=e^{x^2/2}\int_x^\infty e^{-t^2/2}\,dt.
\]

1. Prove that
\[
0<f(x)<\frac1x.
\]
2. Prove that $f$ is strictly decreasing on $(0,\infty)$.
:::
