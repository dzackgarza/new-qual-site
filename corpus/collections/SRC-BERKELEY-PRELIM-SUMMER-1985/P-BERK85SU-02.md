:::{.problem}
1. For $0\le\theta\le\pi/2$, prove
\[
\sin\theta\ge \frac{2}{\pi}\theta.
\]

2. Using part 1 or otherwise, prove that if $\lambda<1$, then
\[
\lim_{R\to\infty}R^\lambda\int_0^{\pi/2}e^{-R\sin\theta}\,d\theta=0.
\]
:::
