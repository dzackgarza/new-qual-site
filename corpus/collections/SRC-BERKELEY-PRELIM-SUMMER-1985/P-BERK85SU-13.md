:::{.problem}
Let $P(z)$ be a polynomial of degree less than $k$ with complex coefficients, and let
\[
\omega_1,\ldots,\omega_k
\]
be the $k$th roots of unity in $\mathbb C$. Prove that
\[
\frac1k\sum_{i=1}^k P(\omega_i)=P(0).
\]
:::
