:::{.problem}
Let
\[
A_1\ge A_2\ge\cdots\ge A_k\ge0.
\]
Evaluate
\[
\lim_{n\to\infty}\left(A_1^n+A_2^n+\cdots+A_k^n\right)^{1/n}.
\]
