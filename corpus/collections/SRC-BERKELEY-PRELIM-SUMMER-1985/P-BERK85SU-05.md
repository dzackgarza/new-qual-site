::: {.problem}
By the Fundamental Theorem of Algebra, the polynomial
\[
x^3+2x^2+7x+1
\]
has three complex roots $\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3$, counted with multiplicity. Compute
\[
\alpha_1^3+\alpha_2^3+\alpha_3^3.
\]
:::
