::: {.problem}
Let $F$ be a field of characteristic $p>0$, with $p\ne3$. If $\alpha$ is a zero of
\[
f(x)=x^p-x+3
\]
in an extension field of $F$, prove that $f(x)$ has $p$ distinct zeros in $F(\alpha)$.
:::
