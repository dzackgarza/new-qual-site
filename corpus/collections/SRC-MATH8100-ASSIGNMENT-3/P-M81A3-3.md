::: problem
Prove that every upper semicontinuous function $f:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R$ is Borel measurable.
:::

::: {.solution}
Fix $a\in\mathbb R$. We claim that
\[
U_a:=\{x\in\mathbb R^n:f(x)<a\}
\]
is open.

Let $x_0\in U_a$. Then
\[
f(x_0)<a.
\]
Choose $\eta>0$ such that
\[
f(x_0)+\eta<a.
\]
Upper semicontinuity at $x_0$ means
\[
\limsup_{x\to x_0}f(x)\le f(x_0).
\]
Hence there exists $\delta>0$ such that
\[
|x-x_0|<\delta
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
f(x)<f(x_0)+\eta<a.
\]
Thus
\[
B_\delta(x_0)\subseteq U_a,
\]
so $U_a$ is open.

Therefore for every $a\in\mathbb R$,
\[
f^{-1}(( -\infty,a))
\]
is open, hence Borel. Since the intervals $(-\infty,a)$ generate the Borel $\sigma$-algebra on $\mathbb R$, it follows that $f$ is Borel measurable.
:::
