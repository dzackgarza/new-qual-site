::: problem
Let $E\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ be measurable with $m(E)>0$.

1. For every $\varepsilon>0$, prove that a closed cube $Q$ exists with $m(E\cap Q)\geq(1-\varepsilon)m(Q)$.
2. Prove that $E-E$ contains an open ball centered at the origin.
:::
