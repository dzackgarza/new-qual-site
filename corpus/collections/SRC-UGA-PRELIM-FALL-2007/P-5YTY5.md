::: {.problem}
Let $\Delta$ be the triangle in $\mathbb{R}^2$ with vertices $(0,1)$, $(2,0)$, and $(2,1)$, traversed counterclockwise.
Evaluate the line integral $$\int_{\Delta} y\,dx + 2x\,dy.$$
:::
