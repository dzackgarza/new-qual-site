::: {.problem}
Let $A$ be a symmetric real $n\times n$ matrix, where $n\ge4$, and let $v_1,\ldots,v_4\in\mathbb R^n$ be nonzero vectors. Suppose
\[
Av_k=(2k-1)v_k
\qquad (1\le k\le4).
\]
Prove that $v_1+2v_2$ is orthogonal to $3v_3+4v_4$.
:::
