::: {.problem}
Let $C$ be a binary code of length $n$ with minimum distance $d>n/2$ and $M$ codewords, enumerated as $C=\{c_1,\ldots,c_M\}$.

(a) Prove that
\[
M(M-1)d\le \sum_{i=1}^M\sum_{j=1}^M d(c_i,c_j)\le \frac{nM^2}{2}.
\]

(b) Deduce that $C$ has at most
\[
\frac{2d}{2d-n}
\]
codewords.
This is the Plotkin bound.
:::
