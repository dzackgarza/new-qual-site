::: {.problem}
(a) Compute
\[
\sum_{k=0}^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}\binom n{2k}.
\]

(b) Let $c_n$ be the number of monotonically increasing functions from $\{1,2,\ldots,n\}$ to itself satisfying $f(i)\le i$ for every $i$.
Find a recurrence relation satisfied by $(c_n)_{n\ge1}$.
:::
