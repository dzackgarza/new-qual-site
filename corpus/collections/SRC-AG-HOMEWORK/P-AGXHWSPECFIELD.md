::: problem
Let $X\in \Sch$, and for $x\in X$ let $\OO_x$ be the local ring at $x$ and $\mfm_x$ its maximal ideal.
Let $\kappa(x) \da \OO_x/\mfm_x$ be the residue field at $x$.

Then for $k$ any field, show that giving a morphism $\Spec(k) \to X \in \Sch$ is equivalent to giving a point $x\in X$ together with an inclusion $\kappa(x) \injects k$.
:::
