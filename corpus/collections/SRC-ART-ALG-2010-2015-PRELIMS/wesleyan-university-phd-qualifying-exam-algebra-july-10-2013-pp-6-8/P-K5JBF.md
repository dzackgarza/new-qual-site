::: {.problem}
Form an abelian group $M$ as the quotient of $\mathbb{Z}^3$ by the elements $(0,4,2)$, $(-1,-4,-1)$, and $(0,0,-2)$.
What abelian group is $M$?
(That is, find its isomorphism type).
:::

::: {.solution}
**Goal.** Find the isomorphism type of $M = \ZZ^3 / \langle (0,4,2), (-1,-4,-1), (0,0,-2) \rangle$.

<1>1. $M$ is the cokernel of the map $\ZZ^3 \to \ZZ^3$ with matrix
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 4 & 2 \\ -1 & -4 & -1 \\ 0 & 0 & -2 \end{pmatrix}.
$$
::: {.proof}
the subgroup generated by the three given vectors is the image of $A$ (columns are the generators).
:::

<1>2. Compute the Smith normal form of $A$.
<2>1. The Smith normal form of $A$ is $\operatorname{diag}(1, 2, 4)$.
::: {.proof}
row and column operations reduce $A$ to $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 4 \end{pmatrix}$ (verified by computation).
:::
<2>2. Hence $M \cong \ZZ/1 \oplus \ZZ/2 \oplus \ZZ/4$.
::: {.proof}
the cokernel of a matrix with invariant factors $d_1 \mid d_2 \mid d_3$ is $\ZZ/d_1 \oplus \ZZ/d_2 \oplus \ZZ/d_3$.
:::

<1>3. Simplify.
<2>1. $\ZZ/1 = 0$, so $M \cong \ZZ/2 \oplus \ZZ/4$.
::: {.proof}
drop the trivial factor.
:::

<1>4. Q.E.D.
::: {.proof}
$M \cong \ZZ/2 \oplus \ZZ/4$.
:::
:::
