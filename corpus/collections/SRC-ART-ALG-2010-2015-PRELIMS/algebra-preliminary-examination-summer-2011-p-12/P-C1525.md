::: {.problem}
a. Prove that every group of order 15 is cyclic.

b. Prove that every group of order $255 = 3 \cdot 5 \cdot 17$ is cyclic.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

Every group of order $15$ is cyclic.

::: pf-proof

Let $G$ have order $15=3\cdot5$. Sylow's theorems give
$$
n_5\mid3,\qquad n_5\equiv1\pmod5,
$$
so $n_5=1$, and
$$
n_3\mid5,\qquad n_3\equiv1\pmod3,
$$
so $n_3=1$. Thus the Sylow subgroups
$P_3$ and $P_5$ are both normal.

Their intersection is trivial. If $x\in P_3$ and $y\in P_5$, then the
commutator $xyx^{-1}y^{-1}$ lies in both normal subgroups and hence is
trivial. Therefore $P_3$ and $P_5$ commute elementwise, and
$$
G=P_3P_5\cong P_3\times P_5
\cong C_3\times C_5\cong C_{15}.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

If $G$ has order $255=3\cdot5\cdot17$, then its Sylow
$17$-subgroup $P$ is normal.

::: pf-proof

Sylow's theorems give
$$
n_{17}\mid15,
\qquad
n_{17}\equiv1\pmod{17}.
$$
Among the divisors $1,3,5,15$, only $1$ satisfies the congruence. Hence
$n_{17}=1$ and $P\lhd G$. Since $\cardinality{P}=17$, one has $P\cong C_{17}$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

The subgroup $P$ is contained in $Z(G)$.

::: pf-proof

Conjugation on the normal subgroup $P$ gives a homomorphism
$$
G\longrightarrow\Aut(P).
$$
Since $P\cong C_{17}$,
$$
\cardinality{\Aut(P)}=16.
$$
The image order divides both $\cardinality{G}=255$ and $16$. Their gcd is $1$, so the
image is trivial. Thus every element of $G$ centralizes $P$, proving
$P\subseteq Z(G)$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s4}

The quotient $G/P$ is cyclic of order $15$.

::: pf-proof

Its order is $255/17=15$, so step [](#s1){.pf-ref} applies.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s5}

The group $G$ is abelian.

::: pf-proof

Because $P\subseteq Z(G)$, there is a natural surjection
$$
G/P\twoheadrightarrow G/Z(G).
$$
By step [](#s4){.pf-ref} the source is cyclic, so $G/Z(G)$ is cyclic. A group whose
quotient by its center is cyclic is abelian: if
$G/Z(G)=\langle gZ(G)\rangle$, then every element has the form
$g^az$ with $z\in Z(G)$, and any two such elements commute.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s6}

Every group of order $255$ is cyclic.

::: pf-proof

By step [](#s5){.pf-ref}, $G$ is finite abelian. Its Sylow subgroups therefore form a
direct product:
$$
G\cong P_3\times P_5\times P_{17}.
$$
Each has prime order and is cyclic, so
$$
G\cong C_3\times C_5\times C_{17}
\cong C_{255},
$$
because the three orders are pairwise coprime.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1){.pf-ref} proves part (a), and steps [](#s2){.pf-ref}, [](#s3){.pf-ref}, [](#s4){.pf-ref}, [](#s5){.pf-ref} and [](#s6){.pf-ref} prove part (b).

:::
