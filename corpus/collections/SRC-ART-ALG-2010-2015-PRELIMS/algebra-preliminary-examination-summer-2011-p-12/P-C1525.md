::: {.problem}
a. Prove that every group of order 15 is cyclic.

b. Prove that every group of order $255 = 3 \cdot 5 \cdot 17$ is cyclic.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

In part (a), every group $G$ of order $15$ is cyclic.

::: pf-proof

By the Sylow theorems, $n_3\equiv1\pmod3$ and $n_3\mid5$, so $n_3=1$; and $n_5\equiv1\pmod5$ and $n_5\mid3$, so $n_5=1$.
Thus the Sylow subgroups $P\cong\ZZ/3$ and $Q\cong\ZZ/5$ are normal, and $P\cap Q=1$ because their orders are coprime.
Hence $G=PQ\cong P\times Q\cong\ZZ/3\times\ZZ/5\cong\ZZ/15$ by the Chinese remainder theorem.

Let $G$ be a group of order $255=3\cdot5\cdot17$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

$G$ has a normal Sylow $17$-subgroup $N\cong\ZZ/17$, and $N\subseteq Z(G)$.

::: pf-proof

By the Sylow theorems, $n_{17}\equiv1\pmod{17}$ and $n_{17}\mid15$; the divisors $1,3,5,15$ of $15$ leave $n_{17}=1$, so $N$ is normal.
Conjugation gives a homomorphism $G\to\Aut(N)\cong(\ZZ/17)^\times$, a group of order $16$.
Its image has order dividing $\gcd(255,16)=1$, so $G$ centralizes $N$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

$G$ is abelian.

::: pf-proof

The quotient $G/N$ has order $15$, so it is cyclic by step [](#s1){.pf-ref}.
By step [](#s2){.pf-ref}, $N\subseteq Z(G)$, so $G/Z(G)$ is a quotient of $G/N$ and is cyclic.
A group whose quotient by its center is cyclic is abelian: if $G/Z(G)=\langle gZ(G)\rangle$, then any two elements have the form $g^iz$ and $g^jz'$ with $z,z'\in Z(G)$, and these commute.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s4}

In part (b), $G$ is cyclic.

::: pf-proof

By step [](#s3){.pf-ref} and the structure theorem for finite abelian groups, $G$ is a product of cyclic groups of prime-power order whose orders multiply to $255=3\cdot5\cdot17$; since $255$ is squarefree, $G\cong\ZZ/3\times\ZZ/5\times\ZZ/17\cong\ZZ/255$ by the Chinese remainder theorem.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Steps [](#s1){.pf-ref} and [](#s4){.pf-ref} answer parts (a) and (b).

:::
