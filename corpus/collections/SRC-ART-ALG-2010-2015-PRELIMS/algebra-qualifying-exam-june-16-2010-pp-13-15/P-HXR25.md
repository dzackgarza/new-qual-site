::: {.problem}
Let $\mathbb{F}_p$ be the finite field with $p$ elements, and let $f(x) \in \mathbb{F}_p[x]$ be a polynomial of degree 4.

a. Show that $f(x)$ is irreducible if and only if the polynomials $f(x)$ and $x^{p^2} - x$ are relatively prime.

b. Assuming $f(x)$ is irreducible over $\mathbb{F}_p$, describe the Galois group of $f(x)$ over $\mathbb{F}_p$.
Include a generating set for the Galois group in your answer.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

The monic irreducible factors of $x^{p^2}-x$ over $\FF_p$ are exactly the monic irreducibles of degree $1$ and $2$.

::: pf-proof

In a field $\FF_{p^2}$ with $p^2$ elements, every element satisfies $\alpha^{p^2}=\alpha$, so $x^{p^2}-x=\prod_{\alpha\in\FF_{p^2}}(x-\alpha)$ has distinct roots.
The minimal polynomial over $\FF_p$ of $\alpha\in\FF_{p^2}$ has degree $[\FF_p(\alpha):\FF_p]$, which divides $2$.
Conversely, a monic irreducible $g$ of degree $d\in\{1,2\}$ has a root $\beta$ with $\FF_p(\beta)$ of order $p^d$, so $\beta^{p^d}=\beta$ and hence $\beta^{p^2}=\beta$; thus $g$, the minimal polynomial of $\beta$, divides $x^{p^2}-x$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

In part (a), $f$ is irreducible if and only if $\gcd(f,x^{p^2}-x)=1$.

::: pf-proof

If $f$ is reducible, it has a monic irreducible factor of degree $1$ or $2$, since $\deg f=4$; by step [](#s1){.pf-ref} that factor divides $x^{p^2}-x$, so the gcd is not $1$.
If $f$ is irreducible, a common monic irreducible factor with $x^{p^2}-x$ would be $f$ itself, of degree $4$, contradicting step [](#s1){.pf-ref}; so the gcd is $1$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

In part (b), the Galois group of an irreducible $f$ over $\FF_p$ is cyclic of order $4$, generated by the Frobenius automorphism $\sigma(x)=x^p$.

::: pf-proof

Adjoining one root $\alpha$ of $f$ gives $\FF_p(\alpha)$ of degree $4$, a field $\FF_{p^4}$ with $p^4$ elements.
The extension $\FF_{p^4}/\FF_p$ is Galois with cyclic group of order $4$ generated by $\sigma$ [@DF04], so it contains every root of the irreducible polynomial $f$ and is its splitting field.

:::
