::: problem
Let $K$ be the splitting field of $X^4 - 12$ over $\mathbb{Q}$.

a. Give an explicit description of $K$ and compute its degree over $\mathbb{Q}$.

b. What is the Galois group of $K$ over $\mathbb{Q}$?
You should identify the group as well as its action on the roots of $X^4 - 12$.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. Roots of $X^4-12$ are $\alpha, i\alpha, -\alpha, -i\alpha$ where $\alpha=12^{1/4}>0$.
Proof: fourth roots.

<1>2. $K=\Q(\alpha,i)$.
Proof: splitting field.

<1>3. $[\Q(\alpha):\Q]=4$ ($X^4-12$ Eisenstein at $3$).
Proof: $3\mid12$, $9\nmid12$.

<1>4. $i\notin\Q(\alpha)\subset\R$, so $[K:\Q]=8$.
Proof: tower.

<1>5. $G=\Gal(K/\Q)\cong D_8$ (dihedral of order $8$), generated by $\sigma:\alpha\mapsto i\alpha$, $\tau:i\mapsto -i$.
Proof: $\sigma^4=1$, $\tau^2=1$, $\tau\sigma\tau=\sigma^{-1}$.

<1>6. Q.E.D.
Proof: <1>4 and <1>5.
:::
