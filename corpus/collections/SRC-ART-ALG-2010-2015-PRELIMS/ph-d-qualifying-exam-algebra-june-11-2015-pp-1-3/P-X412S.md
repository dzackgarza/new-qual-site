::: {.problem}
Let $K$ be the splitting field of $X^4 - 12$ over $\mathbb{Q}$.

a. Give an explicit description of $K$ and compute its degree over $\mathbb{Q}$.

b. What is the Galois group of $K$ over $\mathbb{Q}$?
You should identify the group as well as its action on the roots of $X^4 - 12$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $\alpha=12^{1/4}>0$ be the real positive fourth root of $12$.

::: pf

::: pf-step

The roots of $X^4-12$ are $r_j=i^j\alpha$ for $j\in\ZZ/4\ZZ$, that is, $\alpha, i\alpha, -\alpha, -i\alpha$.

::: pf-proof

Each $r_j$ satisfies $r_j^4=i^{4j}\alpha^4=12$, and the four numbers are distinct, so they are all the roots of the quartic.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

$K=\QQ(\alpha,i)$.

::: pf-proof

The field $\QQ(\alpha,i)$ contains every $r_j$. Conversely, $K$ contains $\alpha=r_0$ and $i=r_1/r_0$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

$[\QQ(\alpha):\QQ]=4$.

::: pf-proof

Since $3\mid12$ and $9\nmid12$, the polynomial $X^4-12$ is Eisenstein at $3$, hence irreducible over $\QQ$, and it is the minimal polynomial of $\alpha$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s4}

$[K:\QQ]=8$.

::: pf-proof

The field $\QQ(\alpha)$ lies in $\RR$, so $i\notin\QQ(\alpha)$ and $[K:\QQ(\alpha)]=2$, with minimal polynomial $X^2+1$. The tower law and step [](#s3){.pf-ref} give $[K:\QQ]=4\cdot2=8$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s5}

$\Gal(K/\QQ)\cong D_8$, the dihedral group of order $8$. It is generated by $\sigma$ with $\sigma(\alpha)=i\alpha$, $\sigma(i)=i$, and $\tau$ with $\tau(\alpha)=\alpha$, $\tau(i)=-i$. On the roots, $\sigma(r_j)=r_{j+1}$ and $\tau(r_j)=r_{-j}$: $\sigma$ is a rotation and $\tau$ a reflection of the square with vertices $r_0,r_1,r_2,r_3$.

::: pf-proof

By step [](#s4){.pf-ref}, $[K:\QQ(i)]=4$, so $X^4-12$ is irreducible over $\QQ(i)$ and there is a $\QQ(i)$-automorphism $\sigma$ of $K$ with $\sigma(\alpha)=i\alpha$. Complex conjugation restricts to $\tau$. Then $\sigma(r_j)=\sigma(i)^j\sigma(\alpha)=i^{j+1}\alpha=r_{j+1}$ and $\tau(r_j)=(-i)^j\alpha=r_{-j}$. Hence $\sigma$ has order $4$, $\tau$ has order $2$, and $\tau\sigma\tau=\sigma^{-1}$, since both sides send $r_j$ to $r_{j-1}$. Every power of $\sigma$ fixes $i$ and $\tau$ does not, so $\tau\notin\langle\sigma\rangle$ and $\langle\sigma,\tau\rangle$ has order $8=\abs{\Gal(K/\QQ)}$. These are the defining relations of $D_8$.

:::
