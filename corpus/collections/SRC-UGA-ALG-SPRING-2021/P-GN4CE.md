::: {.problem}
a. Carefully state the definition of **Noetherian** for a commutative ring $R$.

b. Let $R$ be a subset of $\ZZ[x]$ consisting of all polynomials
$$
f(x) = a_ 0 + a_1 x + a_2 x^2 + \cdots + a_nx^n
$$
such that $a_k$ is even for $1\leq k \leq n$.
Show that $R$ is a subring of $\ZZ[x]$.

c. Show that $R$ is not Noetherian.

> *Hint: consider the ideal generated by $\theset{ 2x^k \st 1\leq k \in \ZZ }$.*
:::


::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #def}

A commutative ring $R$ is Noetherian if every ascending chain of ideals $I_1\subseteq I_2\subseteq\cdots$ of $R$ is eventually constant; equivalently, every ideal of $R$ is finitely generated.

:::

::: {.pf-step #subring}

$R$ is a subring of $\ZZ[x]$.

::: pf-proof

$1\in R$, and $R$ is closed under subtraction because a difference of even integers is even. Let $f=\sum a_kx^k$ and $g=\sum b_kx^k$ lie in $R$. For $n\geq1$, the coefficient of $x^n$ in $fg$ is $\sum_{k=0}^na_kb_{n-k}$. In each term $k\geq1$ or $n-k\geq1$, so each term has an even factor. Hence $fg\in R$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #chain}

For $n\geq1$, let $I_n$ be the ideal of $R$ generated by $2x,2x^2,\ldots,2x^n$. Then $2x^{n+1}\notin I_n$.

::: pf-proof

An element of $I_n$ is $h=\sum_{k=1}^nr_k\cdot2x^k$ with $r_k\in R$. Its coefficient of $x^{n+1}$ is $\sum_{k=1}^n2c_k$, where $c_k$ is the coefficient of $x^{n+1-k}$ in $r_k$. Since $n+1-k\geq1$ and $r_k\in R$, each $c_k$ is even. So the coefficient of $x^{n+1}$ in $h$ is divisible by $4$. The coefficient of $x^{n+1}$ in $2x^{n+1}$ is $2$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #notnoeth}

$R$ is not Noetherian.

::: pf-proof

$2x^{n+1}\in R$, so $I_n\subseteq I_{n+1}$, and Step [](#chain){.pf-ref} gives $I_n\neq I_{n+1}$. Hence $I_1\subsetneq I_2\subsetneq\cdots$ is an ascending chain of ideals that is not eventually constant.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Steps [](#def){.pf-ref}, [](#subring){.pf-ref} and [](#notnoeth){.pf-ref} prove parts (a), (b) and (c). The union of the chain is the ideal of the hint, which is therefore not finitely generated.

:::
