::: {.problem}
Define
\[
f(x) \da x^4 + 4x^2 + 64 \in \QQ[x]
.\]

a. Find the splitting field $K$ of $f$ over $\QQ$.

b. Find the Galois group $G$ of $f$.

c. Exhibit explicitly the correspondence between subgroups of $G$ and intermediate fields between $\QQ$ and $K$.
:::

::: {.concept}
\envlist

- Useful trick: given $a + \sqrt{b}$, try to rewrite this as $(\sqrt{c} + \sqrt{d})^2$ for some $c, d$ to get a better basis for $\SF(f)$.
:::


::: {.solution}
Put $u=\sqrt5$ and $v=i\sqrt3$.

<1>1. The splitting field of $f$ is
\[
\boxed{K=\mathbb Q(u,v),\qquad [K:\mathbb Q]=4}.
\]

::: {.proof}
The identity
\[
(x^2-(u+v)^2)(x^2-(u-v)^2)
=x^4-2(u^2+v^2)x^2+(u^2-v^2)^2
=x^4+4x^2+64
\]
shows that the four roots are $\pm(u+v)$ and $\pm(u-v)$. Their sums and differences yield $2u$ and $2v$, so the field generated by the roots is $\mathbb Q(u,v)$. The quadratic field $\mathbb Q(u)$ is real and $v$ is a root of $t^2+3$ with no real root. Thus $[K:\mathbb Q(u)]=2$ and $[K:\mathbb Q]=4$.
:::

<1>2. The Galois group is
\[
\boxed{G=\operatorname{Gal}(K/\mathbb Q)
=\{1,a,b,ab\}\cong C_2\times C_2},
\]
where $a(u)=-u$, $a(v)=v$, $b(u)=u$, and $b(v)=-v$.

::: {.proof}
The extension $K/\mathbb Q$ is the splitting field of a separable polynomial in characteristic zero, hence Galois of degree four. The two sign changes on the generators preserve their quadratic relations and determine commuting automorphisms $a,b$ of order two. These give four distinct automorphisms and therefore exhaust the group.
:::

<1>3. The subgroup–fixed-field correspondence is
\[
\boxed{\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{subgroup}&\text{fixed field}\\ \hline
\{1\}&K\\
\langle a\rangle&\mathbb Q(v)=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})\\
\langle b\rangle&\mathbb Q(u)=\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)\\
\langle ab\rangle&\mathbb Q(uv)=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-15})\\
G&\mathbb Q
\end{array}}.
\]

::: {.proof}
The Klein four-group has exactly the five displayed subgroups. The element $a$ fixes $v$, the element $b$ fixes $u$, and $ab$ fixes $uv$. Each displayed proper fixed subfield has degree two over $\mathbb Q$ since its generator has nonsquare square $-3$, $5$, or $-15$. By the Galois correspondence, each subgroup of order two fixes a field of degree two, so the containments are equalities. The trivial and full subgroups fix $K$ and $\mathbb Q$, respectively.
:::

<1>4. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Steps <1>1, <1>2, and <1>3 prove parts (a), (b), and (c), respectively.
:::
