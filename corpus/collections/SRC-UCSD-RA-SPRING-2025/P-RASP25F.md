::: problem
Let $(\xi_m)_{m \geq 1}$ be a sequence of vectors in a Hilbert space $H$.
Assume that $\|\xi_m\| \leq 1$ and $\lim_{n \to \infty} \langle \xi_n, \xi_m \rangle = 0$ for every $m \geq 1$.
Prove that $\lim_{n \to \infty} \langle \xi_n, \xi \rangle = 0$ for every $\xi \in H$.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. Let $M = \overline{\operatorname{span}\{\xi_m : m \ge 1\}}$ be the closed span of the sequence.
::: {.proof}
define the closed subspace generated by the sequence.
:::

<1>2. For $\xi \in M^\perp$, $\langle \xi_n, \xi \rangle = 0$ for all $n$.
::: {.proof}
$\xi$ is orthogonal to every $\xi_m$, hence to every $\xi_n$.
:::

<1>3. For $\xi = \xi_m$ (a generator), $\lim_{n \to \infty} \langle \xi_n, \xi_m \rangle = 0$ by hypothesis.
::: {.proof}
the given condition.
:::

<1>4. Hence $\lim_{n \to \infty} \langle \xi_n, \xi \rangle = 0$ for every $\xi$ in the span of the $\xi_m$.
::: {.proof}
<1>3 and linearity (finite linear combinations).
:::

<1>5. For $\xi \in M$ arbitrary, write $\xi = \lim_k \eta_k$ with $\eta_k$ in the span of the $\xi_m$.
::: {.proof}
<1>1 (density of the span in $M$).
:::

<1>6. Then $|\langle \xi_n, \xi \rangle| \le |\langle \xi_n, \xi - \eta_k \rangle| + |\langle \xi_n, \eta_k \rangle| \le \|\xi_n\|\,\|\xi - \eta_k\| + |\langle \xi_n, \eta_k \rangle| \le \|\xi - \eta_k\| + |\langle \xi_n, \eta_k \rangle|$.
::: {.proof}
Cauchy–Schwarz and $\|\xi_n\| \le 1$.
:::

<1>7. Given $\varepsilon > 0$, choose $k$ with $\|\xi - \eta_k\| < \varepsilon/2$; then by <1>4, for $n$ large, $|\langle \xi_n, \eta_k \rangle| < \varepsilon/2$.
::: {.proof}
<1>5 and <1>4.
:::

<1>8. Hence $|\langle \xi_n, \xi \rangle| < \varepsilon$ for $n$ large, so $\lim_n \langle \xi_n, \xi \rangle = 0$ for all $\xi \in M$.
::: {.proof}
<1>6 and <1>7.
:::

<1>9. Since $H = M \oplus M^\perp$, combining <1>2 and <1>8 gives the result for all $\xi \in H$.
::: {.proof}
<1>2 and <1>8.
:::

<1>10. Q.E.D.
::: {.proof}
<1>9.
:::
:::
