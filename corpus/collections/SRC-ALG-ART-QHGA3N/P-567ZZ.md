::: {.problem}
Let $L/K$ be a finite normal extension.

a. Show that if $L/K$ is cyclic and $E/K$ is normal with $L/E/K$ then $L/E$ and $E/K$ are cyclic.

b. Show that if $L/K$ is cyclic then there exists exactly one extension $E/K$ of degree $n$ with $L/E/K$ for each divisor $n$ of $[L:K]$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let
$$
G=\Gal(L/K).
$$

<1>1. Suppose $L/K$ is cyclic and $E/K$ is normal. Then $L/E$ is cyclic.
::: {.proof}
Let
$$
H=\Gal(L/E)\le G.
$$
Because $G$ is cyclic, every subgroup of $G$ is cyclic. Hence
$$
\Gal(L/E)=H
$$
is cyclic, so $L/E$ is a cyclic extension.
:::

<1>2. Under the same hypotheses, $E/K$ is cyclic.
::: {.proof}
Since $E/K$ is normal, the fundamental theorem of Galois theory gives
$$
H\trianglelefteq G
$$
and
$$
\Gal(E/K)\cong G/H.
$$
A quotient of a cyclic group is cyclic. Therefore $\Gal(E/K)$ is cyclic, so $E/K$ is cyclic.
:::

<1>3. This proves part (a).
::: {.proof}
Step <1>1 proves that $L/E$ is cyclic, and step <1>2 proves that $E/K$ is cyclic.
:::

<1>4. Suppose now that $L/K$ is cyclic of degree $m$. For every divisor $n\mid m$, there exists an intermediate field $E$ with
$$
[E:K]=n.
$$
::: {.proof}
Write
$$
G\cong C_m.
$$
There is a unique subgroup $H\le G$ of order $m/n$. Let
$$
E=L^H.
$$
By the fundamental theorem of Galois theory,
$$
[E:K]=[G:H]=\frac{m}{m/n}=n.
$$
Thus an intermediate extension of degree $n$ exists.
:::

<1>5. The intermediate field of degree $n$ is unique.
::: {.proof}
Let $E'$ be any intermediate field with $[E':K]=n$. Put
$$
H'=\Gal(L/E').
$$
Then
$$
[G:H']=[E':K]=n,
$$
so
$$
|H'|=\frac{m}{n}.
$$
The cyclic group $G$ has a unique subgroup of each order dividing $m$, hence
$$
H'=H.
$$
By the Galois correspondence,
$$
E'=L^{H'}=L^H=E.
$$
Therefore the intermediate field of degree $n$ is unique.
:::

<1>6. Hence for every divisor $n$ of $[L:K]$ there exists exactly one intermediate extension $E/K$ of degree $n$.
::: {.proof}
Existence is step <1>4 and uniqueness is step <1>5.
:::

<1>7. Q.E.D.
::: {.proof}
Step <1>3 proves part (a), and step <1>6 proves part (b).
:::
:::
