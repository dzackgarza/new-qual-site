::: {.problem}
(a) Let
\[
g(x)=
\begin{cases}
1,&x\in\mathbb Q,\\
0,&x\notin\mathbb Q.
\end{cases}
\]
Prove that $g$ is discontinuous at every real number.

(b) Let
\[
h(x)=
\begin{cases}
x,&x\in\mathbb Q,\\
0,&x\notin\mathbb Q.
\end{cases}
\]
Prove that $h$ is continuous at $0$ and discontinuous at every $x\ne0$.

(c) Define Thomae's function by
\[
t(x)=
\begin{cases}
1,&x=0,\\
1/n,&x=m/n\in\mathbb Q\setminus\{0\}\text{ in lowest terms with }n>0,\\
0,&x\notin\mathbb Q.
\end{cases}
\]
Prove that $t$ is continuous at every irrational point and has a removable discontinuity at every rational point.

Hint: show that
\[
\lim_{x\to c}t(x)=0
\]
for every $c\in\mathbb R$.
:::
