::: {.problem}
Suppose $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ satisfies
\[
\lim_{h\to0}\bigl(f(x+h)-f(x-h)\bigr)=0
\]
for every $x\in\mathbb R$. Must $f$ be continuous?
:::
