::: {.problem}
Let $G = \{1, 7, 17, 23, 49, 55, 65, 71\}$ under multiplication modulo $96$. Express $G$ as an external and an internal direct product of cyclic groups.
:::

::: {.solution}
G has 8 elements, so it is isomorphic to one of $\ZZ _ { 8 } , \ \ZZ _ { 4 } \oplus \ZZ _ { 2 }$ or $\ZZ _ { 2 } \oplus \ZZ _ { 2 } \oplus \ZZ _ { 2 }$ The order of 1 is 1, and there are four elements of order 4 (namely: 7, 23, 55, 71). The remaining three elements (17, 49, 65) have order 2. Since no elements have order 8, and there are elements of order 4, $G \approx \ZZ _ { 4 } \oplus \ZZ _ { 2 }$ as an external direct product.
As an internal direct product, G can be expressed as $\langle 7\rangle \times \langle 17\rangle$, since $\langle 7\rangle = \{1,7,49,55\}$ and $\langle 17\rangle = \{1,17\}$ meet trivially and $4 \cdot 2 = 8$. Every element of order 4 squares to $49$, so the order-2 factor must be $\langle 17\rangle$ or $\langle 65\rangle$, not $\langle 49\rangle$.
:::
