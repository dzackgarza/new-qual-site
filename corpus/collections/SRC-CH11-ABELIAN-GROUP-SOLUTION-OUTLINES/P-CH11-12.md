::: {.problem}
Suppose that the order of some finite Abelian group is divisible by 10. Prove that the group has a cyclic subgroup of order 10.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let G be an Abelian group whose order is finite and divisible by 10. Then, by the corollary on P 230, G has a subgroup of order 10. But a subgroup of order 10, has an element of order 10 - namely the element isomorphic to 1 in $\mathbb { Z } _ { 1 0 }$ . (Note: This is clear because all Abelian groups of order 10 are isomorphic to $\mathbb { Z } _ { 1 0 } )$
:::
