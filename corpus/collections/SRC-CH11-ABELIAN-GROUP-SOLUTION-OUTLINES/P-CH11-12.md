::: {.problem}
Suppose that the order of some finite Abelian group is divisible by 10. Prove that the group has a cyclic subgroup of order 10.
:::

::: {.solution}

The argument is the card's own: the structure theorem gives a subgroup of order $10$ inside $G$, and an abelian group of order $10$ is cyclic.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #subgroup}

$G$ has a subgroup $H$ of order $10$.

::: pf-proof

By the fundamental theorem of finite abelian groups $G\approx\ZZ_{n_{1}}\oplus\cdots\oplus\ZZ_{n_{k}}$ where $n_{i+1}\mid n_{i}$. As $10=2\cdot5$ divides $\abs{G}=n_{1}\cdots n_{k}$, the prime $5$ divides some $n_{j}$ and $2$ divides some $n_{i}$; taking the unique $\ZZ_{5}$ inside $\ZZ_{n_{j}}$ and the $\ZZ_{2}$ inside $\ZZ_{n_{i}}$ (possibly $i=j$) gives a subgroup isomorphic to $\ZZ_{2}\oplus\ZZ_{5}\approx\ZZ_{10}$, of order $10$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #cyclic}

Every abelian group of order $10$ is cyclic.

::: pf-proof

A finite abelian group is the direct sum of its Sylow subgroups, here a group of order $2$ and one of order $5$; each is cyclic because a group of prime order is cyclic, so the group is $\ZZ_{2}\oplus\ZZ_{5}\approx\ZZ_{10}$ since $\gcd(2,5)=1$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #conclusion}

$G$ has a cyclic subgroup of order $10$.

::: pf-proof

The subgroup $H$ of step [](#subgroup){.pf-ref} is abelian of order $10$, hence cyclic by step [](#cyclic){.pf-ref}; a generator maps to $1\in\ZZ_{10}$ under the isomorphism and has order $10$:
$$
\boxed{10\mid\abs{G},\ G\ \text{abelian}\implies G\ \text{has a cyclic subgroup of order } 10.}
$$

:::
