::: {.problem}
Suppose that the order of some finite Abelian group is divisible by 10. Prove that the group has a cyclic subgroup of order 10.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let G be an Abelian group whose order is finite and divisible by 10. Then, by the corollary on P 230, G has a subgroup of order 10. Every Abelian group of order 10 is isomorphic to $\mathbb { Z } _ { 1 0 }$, so this subgroup is cyclic: the element corresponding to $1 \in \mathbb { Z } _ { 1 0 }$ under such an isomorphism has order 10.
:::
