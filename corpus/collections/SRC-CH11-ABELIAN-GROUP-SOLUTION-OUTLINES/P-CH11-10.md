::: {.problem}
Find all the Abelian groups (up to isomorphism) of order 360.
:::

::: {.solution}
The prime factorization is $360 = 2^3 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5$. There are three abelian groups of order $2^3$ and two of order $3^2$, so there are six groups, up to isomorphism:
$$
\begin{gathered}
\ZZ_2 \oplus \ZZ_2 \oplus \ZZ_2 \oplus \ZZ_3 \oplus \ZZ_3 \oplus \ZZ_5, \qquad \ZZ_2 \oplus \ZZ_2 \oplus \ZZ_2 \oplus \ZZ_9 \oplus \ZZ_5, \\
\ZZ_2 \oplus \ZZ_4 \oplus \ZZ_3 \oplus \ZZ_3 \oplus \ZZ_5, \qquad \ZZ_2 \oplus \ZZ_4 \oplus \ZZ_9 \oplus \ZZ_5, \\
\ZZ_8 \oplus \ZZ_3 \oplus \ZZ_3 \oplus \ZZ_5, \qquad \ZZ_8 \oplus \ZZ_9 \oplus \ZZ_5.
\end{gathered}
$$
:::
