::: {.problem}
Let $F$ be a finite field of order $q$ and let $E/F$ be a field extension.
Suppose that an element $a \in E$ is algebraic over $F$.
Prove that $[F(a) : F]$ is the smallest positive integer $n$ such that $a^{q^n} = a$ and that it divides every other such positive integer.
:::

::: {.solution}
Set
\[
d:=[F(a):F].
\]
Because $a$ is algebraic over $F$, one has $d<\infty$.

<1>1. The field $F(a)$ has exactly $q^d$ elements, and every element $x\in F(a)$ satisfies
\[
x^{q^d}=x.
\]
::: {.proof}
As a $d$-dimensional vector space over the field $F$ of $q$ elements, $F(a)$ has
\[
q^d
\]
elements.
Its multiplicative group $F(a)^\times$ therefore has order $q^d-1$.
By Lagrange's theorem, every nonzero $x\in F(a)$ satisfies
\[
x^{q^d-1}=1,
\]
so
\[
x^{q^d}=x.
\]
The equality is also true for $x=0$.
:::

<1>2. In particular,
\[
a^{q^d}=a.
\]
::: {.proof}
This is <1>1 applied to the element $a\in F(a)$.
Thus $d$ is one positive integer for which the required Frobenius equality holds.
:::

<1>3. For every positive integer $n$, the roots of
\[
T^{q^n}-T
\]
in an algebraic closure of $F$ form a field with exactly $q^n$ elements.
::: {.proof}
Let
\[
K_n:=\{x:x^{q^n}=x\}
\]
inside a fixed algebraic closure of $F$.
In characteristic $p$, where $q$ is a power of $p$, the map
\[
x\longmapsto x^{q^n}
\]
is a ring homomorphism.
Hence if $x,y\in K_n$, then
\[
(x+y)^{q^n}=x^{q^n}+y^{q^n}=x+y
\]
and
\[
(xy)^{q^n}=x^{q^n}y^{q^n}=xy.
\]
If $0\neq x\in K_n$, then
\[
(x^{-1})^{q^n}=(x^{q^n})^{-1}=x^{-1}.
\]
Thus $K_n$ is a field.

The polynomial $T^{q^n}-T$ has derivative
\[
q^nT^{q^n-1}-1=-1,
\]
so it has no repeated roots.
Its splitting field contains all of its $q^n$ distinct roots, and those roots are precisely the elements of $K_n$.
Therefore
\[
|K_n|=q^n.
\]
:::

<1>4. If $a^{q^n}=a$, then
\[
d\mid n.
\]
::: {.proof}
Assume
\[
a^{q^n}=a.
\]
Then $a\in K_n$ by <1>3.
Also $F\subseteq K_n$, because every element $c\in F$ satisfies $c^q=c$, hence $c^{q^n}=c$.
Therefore the field generated by $F$ and $a$ satisfies
\[
F(a)\subseteq K_n.
\]
By <1>3,
\[
[K_n:F]=n,
\]
since $|K_n|=q^n$ and $|F|=q$.
The tower law gives
\[
n=[K_n:F]=[K_n:F(a)]\,[F(a):F].
\]
Thus
\[
d=[F(a):F]\mid n.
\]
:::

<1>5. Hence $d=[F(a):F]$ is the smallest positive integer $n$ such that $a^{q^n}=a$, and it divides every other such $n$.
::: {.proof}
By <1>2, $d$ itself has the required property.
By <1>4, every positive integer with that property is divisible by $d$.
Therefore none is smaller than $d$, and $d$ divides all of them.
:::
:::
