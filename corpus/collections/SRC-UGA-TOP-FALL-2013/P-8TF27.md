::: problem
Prove that the product of two compact spaces is compact.
:::

::: solution
**Goal:** Prove that $X\times Y$ is compact when $X$ and $Y$ are compact.

<1>1. Let $\mathcal U$ be an open cover of $X\times Y$.
    <2>1. Fix $x\in X$. The slice $\{x\}\times Y$ is compact (homeomorphic to $Y$).
    <2>2. For each $y\in Y$, pick $W\in\mathcal U$ with $(x,y)\in W$.
    <2>3. There exists a basic rectangle $N_y\times M_y\subseteq W$ with $(x,y)\in N_y\times M_y$.
    <2>4. The rectangles $\{N_y\times M_y:y\in Y\}$ cover $\{x\}\times Y$, and project to an open cover $\{M_y:y\in Y\}$ of $Y$.
    <2>5. By compactness of $Y$, choose finite points $y_1,\dots,y_{n_x}$ such that $\{M_{y_1},\dots,M_{y_{n_x}}\}$ covers $Y$.
    <2>6. Set
    $$W_x:=\bigcap_{i=1}^{n_x} N_{y_i} \quad\text{and}\quad
    \mathcal U_x:=\{N_{y_i}\times M_{y_i}\}_{i=1}^{n_x}.$$
    Each $N_{y_i}$ contains $x$ because $x\in N_{y_i}$.
    Then $W_x$ is open in $X$, $x\in W_x$, and
    $$W_x\times Y\subseteq\bigcup_{i=1}^{n_x}N_{y_i}\times M_{y_i}\subseteq\bigcup_{i=1}^{n_x}(N_{y_i}\times M_{y_i}).$$

<1>2. Compactness from a finite subcover of $X$.
    <2>1. $\{W_x:x\in X\}$ is an open cover of $X$.
    <2>2. By compactness of $X$, choose finite points $x_1,\dots,x_m$ with $W_{x_1},\dots,W_{x_m}$ covering $X$.
    <2>3. Let
    $$\mathcal U'=\bigcup_{k=1}^m \mathcal U_{x_k}.$$
    This is a finite subfamily of $\mathcal U$.

<1>3. Verify that $\mathcal U'$ covers $X\times Y$.
    <2>1. Take $(x,y)\in X\times Y$.
    <2>2. Choose $k$ with $x\in W_{x_k}$.
    <2>3. Then $(x,y)\in W_{x_k}\times Y\subseteq\bigcup_{i=1}^{n_{x_k}}N_{y_i}\times M_{y_i}\subseteq\bigcup\mathcal U_{x_k}\subseteq\bigcup\mathcal U'$.
    <2>4. Thus every point is covered by an element of $\mathcal U'$, so $X\times Y$ is compact.
:::
