::: problem
Use covering spaces to show that any free group is a subgroup of $F_2$, the free group on $2$ generators.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. Let
\[
R=S^1_a\vee S^1_b
\]
be the rose with two oriented loops $a,b$ and one vertex $v$.
Then
\[
\pi_1(R,v)\cong F_2=\langle a,b\rangle.
\]
::: {.proof}
The rose is a connected graph with one vertex and two edges.
Collapsing a maximal tree, which here is just the vertex, gives the standard computation
\[
\pi_1(S^1\vee S^1,v)\cong\mathbb Z*\mathbb Z=F_2.
\]
:::

<1>2. For every integer $n\ge2$, construct a connected $(n-1)$-sheeted covering
\[
p_n:Y_n\to R
\]
as follows.
::: {.proof}
Put
\[
d=n-1.
\]
Let $Y_n$ have vertices
\[
v_0,\dots,v_{d-1}.
\]
For each $i$ modulo $d$, include

- one oriented $a$-edge from $v_i$ to $v_{i+1}$, and

- one $b$-loop at $v_i$.

Map every $a$-edge homeomorphically to the $a$-loop of $R$, every $b$-loop homeomorphically to the $b$-loop, and every vertex to $v$.
At each vertex of $Y_n$ there is exactly one incoming and one outgoing lift of each oriented edge germ at $v$.
Hence a sufficiently small star neighborhood of each vertex maps homeomorphically to the corresponding star neighborhood of $v$.
Thus $p_n$ is a covering map.

The $a$-edges form a $d$-cycle through all the vertices, so $Y_n$ is connected.
Each point of the interior of either loop of $R$ has exactly $d$ preimages, so the covering has $d=n-1$ sheets.
:::

<1>3. The group $\pi_1(Y_n)$ is free of rank $n$.
::: {.proof}
The graph $Y_n$ has
\[
V=d
\]
vertices and
\[
E=2d
\]
edges.
For a connected finite graph, a maximal tree has $V-1$ edges, and collapsing it leaves a wedge of
\[
E-(V-1)=E-V+1
\]
circles.
Therefore
\[
\operatorname{rank}\pi_1(Y_n)
=
2d-d+1
=
d+1
=
n.
\]
Hence
\[
\pi_1(Y_n)\cong F_n.
\]
:::

<1>4. Consequently $F_n$ embeds in $F_2$ for every finite $n\ge2$.
::: {.proof}
A covering map induces an injective homomorphism on fundamental groups.
Indeed, if a loop in $Y_n$ projects to a null-homotopic loop in $R$, lift a null-homotopy of its projection starting with the given loop; uniqueness of lifts forces the original loop to be null-homotopic.
Thus
\[
(p_n)_*:\pi_1(Y_n,v_0)\hookrightarrow\pi_1(R,v)
\]
is injective.
Using <1>1 and <1>3 gives
\[
F_n\cong\pi_1(Y_n,v_0)
\hookrightarrow
\pi_1(R,v)
\cong F_2.
\]
:::

<1>5. The remaining finite ranks also occur: $F_1$ and the trivial group embed in $F_2$.
::: {.proof}
The cyclic subgroup
\[
\langle a\rangle\le F_2
\]
is isomorphic to $\mathbb Z=F_1$, and the trivial subgroup is the free group of rank $0$.
:::

<1>6. There is also a covering whose fundamental group is free of countably infinite rank.
::: {.proof}
Let $Y_\infty$ have vertices
\[
\{v_k:k\in\mathbb Z\},
\]
an oriented $a$-edge from $v_k$ to $v_{k+1}$ for every $k\in\mathbb Z$, and a $b$-loop at every $v_k$.
Map all $a$-edges to $a$, all $b$-loops to $b$, and all vertices to $v$.
The same local-star check as in <1>2 shows that
\[
p_\infty:Y_\infty\to R
\]
is a connected covering.

The union of all $a$-edges is a bi-infinite line and hence a maximal tree in $Y_\infty$.
Collapsing this tree leaves one circle for each $b$-loop, indexed by $k\in\mathbb Z$.
Therefore
\[
\pi_1(Y_\infty,v_0)
\cong
F_{\aleph_0}.
\]
Since $(p_\infty)_*$ is injective, this gives
\[
F_{\aleph_0}\hookrightarrow F_2.
\]
Concretely, its free generators may be represented in $F_2$ by
\[
a^kba^{-k},
\qquad
k\in\mathbb Z.
\]
:::

<1>7. The phrase ``any free group'' cannot literally include free groups of uncountable rank.
::: {.proof}
The group $F_2$ is countable: every element is represented by a finite reduced word in the four-letter alphabet
\[
\{a,a^{-1},b,b^{-1}\}.
\]
Hence every subgroup of $F_2$ is countable.

If $I$ is uncountable, the free group $F_I$ on the set $I$ contains $I$ itself as its set of free generators and is therefore uncountable.
Thus no $F_I$ with uncountable $I$ can embed in $F_2$.

So the strongest literal form of the intended result is:
\[
\boxed{F_2\text{ contains a subgroup isomorphic to every free group of finite or countable rank.}}
\]
In particular, it contains every finitely generated free group.
:::
:::
