::: problem
Let $n \geq 0$ be an integer.
Assume $f : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}$ is an entire function such that
$$
|f(z)| \leq 1 + (\log(1 + |z|))^n.
$$
Prove that $f$ is constant.
:::
