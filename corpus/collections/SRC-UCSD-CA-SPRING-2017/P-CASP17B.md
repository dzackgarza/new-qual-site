::: problem
Let $f : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}$ be entire.
Assume that the function $g(z) = f(z) \cdot f\!\left(\frac{1}{z}\right)$ is bounded on $\mathbb{C} \setminus \{0\}$.
Show that $f(z) = cz^m$.
:::
