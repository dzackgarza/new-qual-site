::: {.problem}
Given that $p ( x )$ is a real polynomial of degree $\leq 4$ such that one can find five distinct solutions to the equation $p ( x ) = 5 ,$ what is the value of $p ( 5 ) ?$ (A) $0$ (B) $1$ (C) $4$ (D) $5$

(E) Cannot be determined from the information given
