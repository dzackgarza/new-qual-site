::: {.problem}
Let $f$ be a real-valued function whose inverse is given by the equation:

\[
f^{-1}(x) = x(1 + x^2) + (1 - x^2)
\]

What's the value of $f(f^{-1}(f(2)))$?

(A) $-2$
(B) $-1$
(C) $1$
(D) $2$
(E) $7$
:::
