::: {.problem}
When the polynomial $p(x)$ is divided by $x - 1$, it leaves a remainder of 1, and when $p(x)$ is divided by $x + 1$, it leaves a remainder of $-1$. Find the remainder when $p(x)$ is divided by $x^2 - 1$.

(A) $-1$
(B) $0$
(C) $x$
(D) $-x$
(E) $2x$
:::
