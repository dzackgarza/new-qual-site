::: {.problem}
Find the largest value of $b$ such that $1 + bi$ satisfies the equation

\[
x^3 - 3x^2 + 6x - 4 = 0
\]

given that every root of this equation has the form $1 + bi$ (where $b$ is real).

(A) $1$
(B) $\sqrt{2}$
(C) $\sqrt{3}$
(D) $2$
(E) $3$
:::
