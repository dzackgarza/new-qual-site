If $a$ and $x$ are positive numbers and $A = a ^ { 2 }$ , express the following in its simplest form in terms of $x :$ $a ^ { ( \log _ { a } x ) + ( \log _ { A } x ) }$ (A) $2 x$ (B) $x ^ { 2 }$ (C) $\sqrt { x }$ (D) $x ^ { 3 }$ (E) $x \sqrt { x }$
