::: {.problem}
If $a$ and $x$ are positive numbers and $A = a^2$, express the following in its simplest form in terms of $x$:

\[
a^{(\log_a x) + (\log_A x)}
\]

(A) $2x$
(B) $x^2$
(C) $\sqrt{x}$
(D) $x^3$
(E) $x\sqrt{x}$
:::
