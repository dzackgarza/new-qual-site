::: {.problem}
Let $C$ be the curve in the $x y$ -plane described by the equation $x ^ { 2 } + 4 y ^ { 2 } = 1 6$ . If every point $( x , y )$ on $C$ is replaced by the point $\bigl ( \frac { 1 } { 2 } x , y \bigr )$ , what is the area enclosed by the resulting curve?
(A) $8$ (B) $4 \pi$ (C) $1 6$ (D) $8 \pi$ (E) $1 6 \pi$
:::
