::: {.problem}
Let $C$ be the curve in the $xy$-plane described by the equation $x^2 + 4y^2 = 16$. If every point $(x, y)$ on $C$ is replaced by the point $(\frac{1}{2}x, y)$, what is the area enclosed by the resulting curve?

(A) $8$
(B) $4\pi$
(C) $16$
(D) $8\pi$
(E) $16\pi$
:::
