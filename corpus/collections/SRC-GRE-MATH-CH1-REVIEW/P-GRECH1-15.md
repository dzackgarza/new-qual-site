::: {.problem}
If the roots of the equation $x ^ { 2 } + B x + 1 = 0$ are the squares of the roots of the equation $x ^ { 2 } + b x + 1 = 0 ,$ which of the following expresses $B$ in terms of $b$ ? (A) $2 - b ^ { 2 }$ (B) $1 - b ^ { 2 }$ (C) $b ^ { 2 } - 1$ (D) $b ^ { 2 }$ (E) $b ^ { 2 } - 2$
:::
