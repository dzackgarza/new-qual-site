::: {.problem}
Show that
\[
A \coloneqq \left\{ f \in L^3(\mathbb{R}) : \int_{\mathbb{R}} |f(x)|^2\,dx < \infty \right\}
\]
is a Borel subset of $L^3(\mathbb{R})$.
:::
