::: {.problem}
Let $(f_n)$ be a bounded sequence in $L^2(\mathbb{R})$ and suppose that $f_n \to 0$ Lebesgue almost everywhere. Show that $f_n \to 0$ in the weak topology on $L^2(\mathbb{R})$.
:::
