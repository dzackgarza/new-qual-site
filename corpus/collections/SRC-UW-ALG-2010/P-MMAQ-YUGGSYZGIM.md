::: problem
Let $f(x)=a_nx^n+a_{n-1}x^{n-1}+\dots+a_0\in\mathbb Z[x]$ (where $a_n\neq 0$) and let $R=\mathbb Z[x]/(f)$.
Prove that $R$ is a finitely generated module over $\mathbb Z$ if and only if $a_n=\pm 1$.
:::
