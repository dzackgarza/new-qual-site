::: {.problem}
Let $n$ be a positive integer and set
\[
c_n=\binom{2n}{n}.
\]

(a) Show that $c_n$ is even.

(b) Prove that $c_n$ is divisible by $4$ if and only if $n$ is not a power of $2$.
:::
