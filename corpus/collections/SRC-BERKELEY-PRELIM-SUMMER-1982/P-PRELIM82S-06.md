::: {.problem}
Suppose $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ is differentiable, $f'(x)>f(x)$ for every $x\in\mathbb R$, and $f(0)=0$.
Prove that
\[
f(x)>0
\]
for every $x>0$.
:::
