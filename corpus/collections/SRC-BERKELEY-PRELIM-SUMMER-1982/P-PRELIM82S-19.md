::: {.problem}
Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional vector space over $\mathbb Q$, and let $M\in\operatorname{Aut}(V)$ fix no nonzero vector.
Suppose
\[
M^p=I
\]
for a prime $p$.
Show that
\[
p-1\mid\dim_{\mathbb Q}V.
\]
:::
