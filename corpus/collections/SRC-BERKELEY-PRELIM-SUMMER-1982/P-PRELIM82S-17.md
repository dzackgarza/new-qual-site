::: {.problem}
Let $f:\mathbb R^3\to\mathbb R^2$, and suppose $0$ is a regular value of $f$; that is, $Df$ has rank $2$ at every point of $f^{-1}(0)$.
Prove that
\[
\mathbb R^3\setminus f^{-1}(0)
\]
is arcwise connected.
:::
