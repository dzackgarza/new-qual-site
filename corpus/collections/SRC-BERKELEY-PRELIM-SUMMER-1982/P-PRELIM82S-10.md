::: {.problem}
For complex numbers $\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_k$, prove that
\[
\limsup_{n\to\infty}
\left|\sum_{j=1}^k\alpha_j^n\right|^{1/n}
=\max_{1\le j\le k}|\alpha_j|.
\]
:::
