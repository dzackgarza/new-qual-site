::: {.problem}
Let $K$ be a nonempty compact subset of a metric space with distance $d$.
Suppose $\varphi:K\to K$ satisfies
\[
d(\varphi(x),\varphi(y))<d(x,y)
\]
for all distinct $x,y\in K$.
Show that $\varphi$ has exactly one fixed point in $K$.
:::
