::: {.problem}
Let $f:[0,\pi]\to\mathbb R$ be continuous and suppose
\[
\int_0^\pi f(x)\sin(nx)\,dx=0
\]
for every integer $n\ge1$.
Must $f$ be identically zero?
:::
