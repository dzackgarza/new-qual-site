::: {.problem}
Let $s,t:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ be differentiable and suppose
\[
f(x+iy)=e^x\bigl(s(y)+it(y)\bigr)
\]
is holomorphic on $\mathbb C$, with
\[
s(0)=1,
\qquad
t(0)=0.
\]
Determine $s(y)$ and $t(y)$.
:::
