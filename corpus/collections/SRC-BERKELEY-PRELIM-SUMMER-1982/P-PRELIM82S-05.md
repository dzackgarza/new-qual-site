::: {.problem}
Let
\[
0<a_0\le a_1\le\cdots\le a_n.
\]
Prove that
\[
a_0z^n+a_1z^{n-1}+\cdots+a_n=0
\]
has no roots in the disk $|z|<1$.
:::
