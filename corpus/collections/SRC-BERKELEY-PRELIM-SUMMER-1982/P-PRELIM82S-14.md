::: {.problem}
Let $A$ be a real $n\times n$ matrix such that
\[
\langle Ax,x\rangle\ge0
\]
for every $x\in\mathbb R^n$.
Show that, for every $u\in\mathbb R^n$,
\[
Au=0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
A^Tu=0.
\]
:::
