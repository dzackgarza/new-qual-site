::: {.problem}
A square matrix $A$ is nilpotent if $A^k=0$ for some positive integer $k$.

(a) If $A$ and $B$ are nilpotent, must $A+B$ be nilpotent?
Give a proof or counterexample.

(b) Prove that if $A$ is nilpotent, then $I-A$ is invertible.
:::
