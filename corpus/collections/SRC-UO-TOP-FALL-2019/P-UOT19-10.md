::: {.problem}
Let $Y$ be a $2$-connected space and let
\[
p:Y\to \operatorname{Gr}_3^+(\mathbb R^\infty)
\]
be a fibration such that $p_*:\pi_i(Y)\to\pi_i(\operatorname{Gr}_3^+(\mathbb R^\infty))$ is an isomorphism for $i>2$; thus $Y$ is a $2$-connected cover of $\operatorname{Gr}_3^+(\mathbb R^\infty)$.

Define the primary obstruction in cohomology to lifting a map
\[
f:X\to \operatorname{Gr}_3^+(\mathbb R^\infty)
\]
to a map $\widetilde f:X\to Y$, and give an example in which the obstruction does not vanish.
:::
