::: {.problem}
Recall that oriented $3$-dimensional vector bundles over $X$ are classified by
\[
[X,\operatorname{Gr}_3^+(\mathbb R^\infty)],
\]
where
\[
\operatorname{Gr}_3^+(\mathbb R^\infty)=V_3(\mathbb R^\infty)/SO(3).
\]
Compute $\pi_i(\operatorname{Gr}_3^+(\mathbb R^\infty))$ for $i\le 4$. (Hint: recall that $SO(3)\cong\mathbb{RP}^3$.)
:::
