::: problem
Let $f \in C^1([0,1])$ be such that $f(1) = 0$.
Assume
$$
\int_0^1 x^k f'(x)\,dx = 0 \qquad \forall k = 1, 2, \ldots.
$$
Prove that $f = 0$ identically on $[0,1]$.
:::
