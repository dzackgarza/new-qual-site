::: problem
Let $H$ be a real Hilbert space and $M$ a nonempty, closed subspace of $H$.
Suppose $x_0 \in H \setminus M$.
Prove that
$$
\min\{\|x - x_0\| : x \in M\} = \max\{\langle x_0, y \rangle : y \in M^\perp,\; \|y\| = 1\}.
$$
:::
