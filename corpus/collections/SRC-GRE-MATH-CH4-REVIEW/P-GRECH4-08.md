::: {.problem}
Let $y = f(x)$ be the solution of the equation

\[
\frac{d^4y}{dx^4} = \frac{d^2y}{dx^2}
\]

such that $f(0) = f'(0) = f''(0) = 0$ and $f'''(0) = -1$. What is $f(x)$?

(A) $x - \cosh x$
(B) $x - \sinh x$
(C) $x + \cosh x$
(D) $x + \sinh x$
(E) $\cosh x + \sinh x$
:::
