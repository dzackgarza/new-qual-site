::: {.problem}
Let $y=f(x)$ solve
\[
\frac{d^4y}{dx^4}=\frac{d^2y}{dx^2},
\]
with $f(0)=f'(0)=f''(0)=0$ and $f'''(0)=-1$.
Find $f(x)$.

(A) $x-\sinh x$  (B) $x-\sin x$  (C) $x+\cosh x$  (D) $x+\sin x$  (E) $\cosh x+\sinh x$
:::
