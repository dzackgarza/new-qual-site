::: {.problem}
If $w = f(x, y)$ is a solution of the partial differential equation

\[
2\frac{\partial w}{\partial x} - 3\frac{\partial w}{\partial y} = 0
\]

then $w$ could equal

(A) $(2x - 3y)^6$
(B) $\sin[\log(3x - 2y)]$
(C) $e^{\arctan(3x + 2y)}$
(D) $[\arccos(2y - 3x)]^2$
(E) $\sqrt{2x + 3y}$
:::
