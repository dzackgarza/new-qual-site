::: {.problem}
If $w=f(x,y)$ satisfies
\[
2\frac{\partial w}{\partial x}-3\frac{\partial w}{\partial y}=0,
\]
which of the following could equal $w$?

(A) $(2x-3y)^6$
(B) $\sin(\log(3x-2y))$
(C) $e^{\arctan(3x+2y)}$
(D) $[\arccos(2y-3x)]^2$
(E) $\sqrt{2x+3y}$
:::
