::: {.problem}
At every point $(x,y)$ on a curve, the slope is
\[
\frac{1-2xy}{x^2+3y^2+1}.
\]
Find the equation of the curve through $(1,1)$.

(A) $\frac13x^3+3xy^2+x+y-xy^2=\frac{13}{3}$

(B) $xy^2+y^3+x-y=2$

(C) $\frac13x^3+3xy^2-x+y+xy^2=\frac{13}{3}$

(D) $x^2y+y^3-x+y=2$

(E) $x^2y^2+xy^3+x-y=1$
:::
