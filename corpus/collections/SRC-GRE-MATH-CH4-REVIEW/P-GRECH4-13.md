::: {.problem}
Consider the family of circles
\[
(x-c)^2+y^2=c^2
\]
tangent to the $y$-axis at the origin. Which differential equation describes the family of curves orthogonal to it?

(A) $y'=\frac{x}{x-y}$
(B) $y'=\frac{x}{y-x}$
(C) $y'=\frac{xy}{x-y}$
(D) $y'=\frac{2xy}{x^2-y^2}$
(E) $y'=\frac{2xy}{y^2-x^2}$
:::
