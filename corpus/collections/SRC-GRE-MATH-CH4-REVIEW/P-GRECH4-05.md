::: {.problem}
If $a$ is a positive constant, let $y = f(x)$ be the solution of the equation

\[
y''' - ay'' + a^2y' - a^3y = 0
\]

such that $f(0) = 1$, $f'(0) = 0$, and $f''(0) = a^2$. How many positive values of $x$ satisfy the equation $f(x) = 0$?

(A) $0$
(B) $1$
(C) $2$
(D) $3$
(E) more than $3$
:::
