::: {.problem}
Let $a>0$ and let $y=f(x)$ solve
\[
y'''-ay''+a^2y'-a^3y=0,
\]
with $f(0)=1$, $f'(0)=0$, and $f''(0)=a^2$. How many positive values of $x$ satisfy $f(x)=0$?

(A) 0  (B) 1  (C) 2  (D) 3  (E) more than 3
:::
