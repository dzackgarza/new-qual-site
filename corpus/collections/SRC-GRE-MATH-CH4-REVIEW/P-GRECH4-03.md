::: {.problem}
Every curve in a family $y=f(x,c)$ has the property that the area in the first quadrant under the curve from $(0,0)$ to $(x,y)$ is one third of the area of the rectangle with corners $(0,0)$ and $(x,y)$.
Find $f(x,c)$.

(A) $c\cos^3 x$  (B) $cx^3+x$  (C) $cx^3-x$  (D) $cx^2$  (E) $c\sqrt{x}$
:::
