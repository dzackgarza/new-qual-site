::: {.problem}
Let $y = f(x)$ be the solution of the equation

\[
\frac{dy}{dx} + \frac{y}{x} = \sin x
\]

such that $f(\pi) = 1$. What is the value of $f(\frac{1}{2}\pi)$?

(A) $\frac{2}{\pi} - 1$
(B) $\frac{2}{\pi}$
(C) $\frac{2}{\pi} + 1$
(D) $\frac{\pi}{2}$
(E) $\frac{\pi}{2} + 1$
:::
