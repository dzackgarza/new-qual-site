::: {.problem}
Let $y=f(x)$ solve
\[
\frac{dy}{dx}+\frac{y}{x}=\sin x,\qquad f(\pi)=1.
\]
Find $f(\pi/2)$.

(A) $\frac2\pi-1$  (B) $\frac2\pi$  (C) $\frac2\pi+1$  (D) $\frac\pi2$  (E) $\frac\pi2+1$
:::
