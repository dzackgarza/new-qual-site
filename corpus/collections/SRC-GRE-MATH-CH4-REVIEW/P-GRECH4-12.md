\[
\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{x + y}{x}
\]

(A) $e^{y/x} = cx$
(B) $e^{y/x} = cy$
(C) $e^{x/y} = cx$
(D) $e^{x/y} = cy$
(E) $e^{-x/y} = cx$
:::
