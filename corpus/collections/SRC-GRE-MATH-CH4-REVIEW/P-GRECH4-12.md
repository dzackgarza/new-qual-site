::: {.problem}
Find the general solution of
\[
\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{x+y}{x}.
\]

(A) $e^{y/x}=cx$  (B) $e^{y/x}=cy$  (C) $e^{x/y}=x$  (D) $e^{x/y}=xy$  (E) $e^{-x/y}=-x$
:::
