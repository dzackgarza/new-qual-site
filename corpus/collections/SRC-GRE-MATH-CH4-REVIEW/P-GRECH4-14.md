::: {.problem}
Let $g(x,y)$ be defined for all $x$ and nonzero $y$ so that
\[
(\sin xy)\,dx+g(x,y)\,dy=0
\]
is exact and $g(0,y)=0$ for every $y\ne0$. Find $g(x,1)$.

(A) $\sin x+\cos x-1$
(B) $x\sin x+\cos x-1$
(C) $x\sin x-\cos x+1$
(D) $x\sin x+\cos x$
(E) $\sin x-x\cos x+1$
:::
