::: {.problem}
Let $y = f(x)$ be the solution of the equation

\[
\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{x^2}{x^2 + 1}
\]

such that $y = 0$ when $x = 0$. What is the value of $f(1)$?

(A) $1 - \log 2$
(B) $1 + \log 2$
(C) $1$
(D) $\log 2$
(E) $\frac{1}{4}(4 - \pi)$
:::
