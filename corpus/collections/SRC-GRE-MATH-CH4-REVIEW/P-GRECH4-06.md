::: {.problem}
Let $g:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ be differentiable and integrable. The integral curve of
\[
[y+g(x)]\,dx+[x-g(y)]\,dy=0
\]
through $(1,1)$ must also pass through which point?

(A) $(0,0)$  (B) $(2,\tfrac12)$  (C) $(\tfrac12,2)$  (D) $(-1,-1)$  (E) $(0,1)$
:::
