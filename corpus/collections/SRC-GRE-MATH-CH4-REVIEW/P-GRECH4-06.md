::: {.problem}
Let $g\colon \RR \to \RR$ be a differentiable and integrable function. The integral curve of the differential equation

\[
[y + g(x)]\,dx + [x - g(y)]\,dy = 0
\]

that passes through the point $(1, 1)$ must also pass through which of the following points?

(A) $(0, 0)$
(B) $(2, \frac{1}{2})$
(C) $(\frac{1}{2}, 2)$
(D) $(-1, -1)$
(E) $(0, 1)$
:::
