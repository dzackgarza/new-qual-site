::: {.problem}
Let G be a group of order 25. Prove that G is cyclic or $g ^ { 5 } = e$ for all $g$ in G. Generalize to any group of order $p ^ { 2 }$ where $p$ is prime. Does your proof work for this generalization?
:::

::: {.solution}
Let G be a group of order 25. Assume G is not cyclic.
Hence no element of G has order 25. But $| g |$ must divide $| G | = 2 5$ for all $g \in G$ , so every $g \in G$ has order 1 or 5.
Hence, $g ^ { 5 } = e$ for all $g \in G$ .

The same proof works for any group of order $p^2$ with $p$ prime: if G is not cyclic, every element has order 1 or $p$, so $g^p = e$ for all $g \in G$.
:::
