::: {.problem}
Prove that every subgroup of $D _ { n }$ of odd order is cyclic.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let G be a subgroup of $D _ { n }$ with odd order.
Then G can not contain any flips since the order of an element has to divide the order of a group.
Thus $G \subseteq < r >$ , which is cyclic.
We already know from the fundamental theorem of cyclic groups that all subgroups of cyclic groups are cyclic so G must be cyclic.
:::
