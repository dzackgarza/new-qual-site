::: {.problem}
Let G be a group with $| G | = p q$ , where p and q are prime. Prove that every proper subgroup of G is cyclic.
:::

::: {.solution}
Proof.
Let G be a group with $| G | = p q$ where $p , q$ are prime.
Let H be a proper subgroup of G. By Lagrange’s theorem, the order of H is either 1, p, or q (It can not be $p q$ since H is proper).
If $| H | = 1 , H = \{ e \} = < e >$ , and hence it is cyclic.
If H is not trivial, then the order of H is either p or q, which are both prime.
Hence, H is cyclic (by corollary of Lagrange’s theorem).
□
:::
