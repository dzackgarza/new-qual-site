::: {.problem}
The group $D_{4}$ acts as a group of permutations of the square regions shown on the following page (printed p. 152). For each square region, locate the points in the orbit of the indicated point under $D_{4}$. In each case, determine the stabilizer of the indicated point.
:::

::: {.solution}

The exercise is Gallian, *Contemporary Abstract Algebra* (7th ed.), Chapter 7 exercise 46,
printed pp. 151–152, and is item 60 of the retained Chapter 7 solution outline
(`assets/attachments/Ch7Sltns.pdf`). The outline answers with a list of stabilizers
keyed to the figure; the steps below supply the coordinates it leaves out and prove each
orbit and stabilizer by checking the eight symmetries, then read the six squares off the
figure.

Identify the square with $S=\theset{(x,y) : \abs{x}\le1,\ \abs{y}\le1}$, and write the eight
symmetries as
$\rho_{1}(x,y)=(-y,x)$, $\rho_{2}(x,y)=(-x,-y)$, $\rho_{3}(x,y)=(y,-x)$, $H(x,y)=(x,-y)$,
$V(x,y)=(-x,y)$, $D_{+}(x,y)=(y,x)$, $D_{-}(x,y)=(-y,-x)$, with $\rho_{0}$ the identity;
these are the three rotations about the centre, the reflections in the horizontal and
vertical midlines and in the two diagonals, and together they are $D_{4}$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #edge-midpoint}

The orbit of $(1,0)$ is $\theset{(\pm1,0),(0,\pm1)}$ and its stabilizer is
$\theset{\rho_{0},H}$.

::: pf-proof

Evaluating the eight symmetries at $(1,0)$: $\rho_{0}$ and $H$ give $(1,0)$; $\rho_{2}$ and
$V$ give $(-1,0)$; $\rho_{1}$ and $D_{+}$ give $(0,1)$; $\rho_{3}$ and $D_{-}$ give
$(0,-1)$. So the orbit is $\theset{(\pm1,0),(0,\pm1)}$, four points, and the two symmetries
fixing $(1,0)$ are $\rho_{0}$ and $H$. The count
$4\cdot2=8=\cardinality{D_{4}}$ agrees with the Orbit-Stabilizer Theorem (Theorem 7.3).

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #corner}

The orbit of $(1,1)$ is $\theset{(\pm1,\pm1)}$ and its stabilizer is $\theset{\rho_{0},D_{+}}$.

::: pf-proof

Evaluating the eight symmetries at $(1,1)$: $\rho_{0}$ and $D_{+}$ give $(1,1)$; $\rho_{1}$
and $V$ give $(-1,1)$; $\rho_{2}$ and $D_{-}$ give $(-1,-1)$; $\rho_{3}$ and $H$ give
$(1,-1)$. So the orbit is the four corners, and the symmetries fixing $(1,1)$ are
$\rho_{0}$ and the reflection $D_{+}$ in the diagonal through $(1,1)$ and $(-1,-1)$.
Again $4\cdot2=8$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #midline}

For $0<a<1$, the orbit of $(a,0)$ is $\theset{(\pm a,0),(0,\pm a)}$ and its stabilizer is
$\theset{\rho_{0},H}$.

::: pf-proof

The eight images are $\rho_{0}(a,0)=H(a,0)=(a,0)$, $\rho_{2}(a,0)=V(a,0)=(-a,0)$,
$\rho_{1}(a,0)=D_{+}(a,0)=(0,a)$ and $\rho_{3}(a,0)=D_{-}(a,0)=(0,-a)$. As $0<a<1$, the
four points $\theset{(\pm a,0),(0,\pm a)}$ are distinct, so this is the orbit, and only
$\rho_{0}$ and $H$ fix $(a,0)$. Again $4\cdot2=8$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #edge-off-midpoint}

For $0<b<1$, the orbit of $(1,b)$ is $\theset{(\pm1,\pm b),(\pm b,\pm1)}$ and its stabilizer is
$\theset{\rho_{0}}$.

::: pf-proof

The eight images are $\rho_{0}(1,b)=(1,b)$, $\rho_{1}(1,b)=(-b,1)$, $\rho_{2}(1,b)=(-1,-b)$,
$\rho_{3}(1,b)=(b,-1)$, $H(1,b)=(1,-b)$, $V(1,b)=(-1,b)$, $D_{+}(1,b)=(b,1)$ and
$D_{-}(1,b)=(-b,-1)$. As $0<b<1$ we have $b\ne1$ and $b\ne0$, so the eight points are
distinct — no two of them share a coordinate pattern with equal entries — and the orbit has
$8$ elements; the only symmetry fixing $(1,b)$ is $\rho_{0}$. Here $8\cdot1=8$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #interior}

For $0<b<a<1$, the orbit of $(a,b)$ is $\theset{(\pm a,\pm b),(\pm b,\pm a)}$ and its
stabilizer is $\theset{\rho_{0}}$.

::: pf-proof

The eight images are $\rho_{0}(a,b)=(a,b)$, $\rho_{1}(a,b)=(-b,a)$, $\rho_{2}(a,b)=(-a,-b)$,
$\rho_{3}(a,b)=(b,-a)$, $H(a,b)=(a,-b)$, $V(a,b)=(-a,b)$, $D_{+}(a,b)=(b,a)$ and
$D_{-}(a,b)=(-b,-a)$. Since $0<b<a$ the entries $a$ and $b$ are distinct and nonzero, so
these are eight distinct points of the form $(\pm a,\pm b)$ and $(\pm b,\pm a)$; no
nonidentity symmetry fixes $(a,b)$, since each of them negates a coordinate or swaps the two
and the swap does not fix $(a,b)$ because $a\ne b$. So the orbit has $8$ elements and the
stabilizer is $\theset{\rho_{0}}$, with $8\cdot1=8$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #squares}

Read left to right and top to bottom, the six indicated points of the figure are an edge
midpoint, a corner, a point of the horizontal midline inside the square, a point of an edge
off its midpoint, and two interior points; the corresponding stabilizers are
$\theset{\rho_{0},H}$, $\theset{\rho_{0},D_{+}}$, $\theset{\rho_{0},H}$, $\theset{\rho_{0}}$,
$\theset{\rho_{0}}$, $\theset{\rho_{0}}$.

::: pf-proof

This is the assignment the figure on printed p. 152 makes, and steps
[](#edge-midpoint){.pf-ref} to [](#interior){.pf-ref} compute the orbit and stabilizer of
each of those five kinds of point. In the outline's notation $\rho_{0}$ is written $id$,
$H$ is the reflection in the horizontal midline, and $D_{+}$ is the reflection in the
diagonal from the upper right corner to the lower left, which the outline calls $d_{2}$; so
the list of stabilizers here is the outline's list.

:::
