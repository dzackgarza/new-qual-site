::: {.problem}
The group $D _ { 4 }$ acts as a group of permutations of the square regions shown on page 159. For each square region, locate the points in the orbit of the indicated point under $D _ { 4 }$ . In each case, determine the stabilizer of the indicated point.
:::

::: {.solution}
The source draws six copies of the square, divided by its diagonals and midlines, with the indicated point in black and the rest of its orbit in blue.
Placing the square as $[-1,1]^2$, with $0<b<a<1$, the six orbits drawn are:

- the midpoint $(1,0)$ of an edge, with orbit $\{(\pm1,0),(0,\pm1)\}$;
- the corner $(1,1)$, with orbit $\{(\pm1,\pm1)\}$;
- a point $(a,0)$ on the horizontal midline, with orbit $\{(\pm a,0),(0,\pm a)\}$;
- a point $(1,b)$ on an edge, off its midpoint, with orbit $\{(\pm1,\pm b),(\pm b,\pm1)\}$;
- two interior points $(a,b)$ off the diagonals and midlines, each with orbit $\{(\pm a,\pm b),(\pm b,\pm a)\}$.

Left to right, top to bottom, the stabilizers are {id, h}, {id, d2}, {id, h}, {id}, {id}, {id} where id is the identity symmetry, h is the flip across the horizontal line and $d _ { 2 }$ is the diagonal from the upper right corner to the bottom left corner.
:::

The source does not reproduce the square regions shown on page 159 or the indicated points, so the statement is not self-contained.
:::
