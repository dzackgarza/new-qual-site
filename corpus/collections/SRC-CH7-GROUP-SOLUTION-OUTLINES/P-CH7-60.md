::: {.problem}
The group $D _ { 4 }$ acts as a group of permutations of the square regions shown on page 159. For each square region, locate the points in the orbit of the indicated point under $D _ { 4 }$ . In each case, determine the stabilizer of the indicated point.
:::

::: {.solution}
The six squares are divided by their diagonals and midlines, each with one indicated point.
Placing the square as $[-1,1]^2$, with $0<b<a<1$, the six indicated points and their orbits are:

- the midpoint $(1,0)$ of an edge, with orbit $\{(\pm1,0),(0,\pm1)\}$;
- the corner $(1,1)$, with orbit $\{(\pm1,\pm1)\}$;
- a point $(a,0)$ on the horizontal midline, with orbit $\{(\pm a,0),(0,\pm a)\}$;
- a point $(1,b)$ on an edge, off its midpoint, with orbit $\{(\pm1,\pm b),(\pm b,\pm1)\}$;
- two interior points $(a,b)$ off the diagonals and midlines, each with orbit $\{(\pm a,\pm b),(\pm b,\pm a)\}$.

Left to right, top to bottom, the stabilizers are {id, h}, {id, d2}, {id, h}, {id}, {id}, {id} where id is the identity symmetry, h is the reflection in the horizontal midline and $d _ { 2 }$ is the reflection in the diagonal from the upper right corner to the bottom left corner.:::

::: {.remark}
This problem refers to the figures of square regions on page 159 of the textbook; the solution locates the indicated points in coordinates.
:::
