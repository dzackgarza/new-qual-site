::: {.problem}
Let $\CC^{*}$ be the group of nonzero complex numbers under multiplication and let $H = \{ a + bi \in \CC^{*} \mid a^{2} + b^{2} = 1 \}$. Give a geometric description of the coset $(3 + 4i)H$. Give a geometric description of the coset $(c + di)H$.
:::

::: {.solution}

The exercise is Gallian, *Contemporary Abstract Algebra* (7th ed.), Chapter 7 exercise 12,
printed p. 149, and is item 14 of the retained Chapter 7 solution outline
(`assets/attachments/Ch7Sltns.pdf`, PDF p. 1). The outline's computation for $(3+4i)H$ is
replaced below by one general statement about $wH$, which gives both parts at once and
proves the reverse inclusion the outline's expansion does not address.

Throughout, $\abs{z}$ is the modulus of the nonzero complex number $z$, and for $w\in\CC^*$
the left coset is $wH=\ts{wz : z\in H}$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #circle}

$H=\ts{z\in\CC^* : \abs{z}=1}$, the circle of radius $1$ about the origin.

::: pf-proof

For $z=a+bi$ with $a,b$ real, $\abs{z}^{2}=a^{2}+b^{2}$. Hence $z\in H$ says exactly
$a^{2}+b^{2}=1$, that is $\abs{z}=1$; and $z\ne0$ is automatic when $\abs{z}=1$. So $H$ is
the set of points of the plane at distance $1$ from the origin.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #scaling}

For $w\in\CC^*$, $wH=\ts{\zeta\in\CC^* : \abs{\zeta}=\abs{w}}$, the circle of radius
$\abs{w}$ about the origin.

::: pf-proof

($\subseteq$) An element of $wH$ is $wz$ with $\abs{z}=1$, and
$\abs{wz}=\abs{w}\abs{z}=\abs{w}$, so it lies in the stated circle.
($\supseteq$) Let $\zeta\in\CC^*$ with $\abs{\zeta}=\abs{w}$ and put
$z=\zeta/w$, which is nonzero. Then $\abs{z}=\abs{\zeta}/\abs{w}=1$, so $z\in H$ by step
[](#circle){.pf-ref}, and $\zeta=wz\in wH$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #threefour}

$(3+4i)H$ is the circle of radius $5$ about the origin.

::: pf-proof

$\abs{3+4i}=\sqrt{3^{2}+4^{2}}=\sqrt{25}=5$, so by step [](#scaling){.pf-ref} the coset
$(3+4i)H$ is $\ts{\zeta : \abs{\zeta}=5}$. In coordinates this is the set of $(x,y)$ with
$x^{2}+y^{2}=25$, which is what the outline's expansion
$(3a-4b)^{2}+(3b+4a)^{2}=25(a^{2}+b^{2})$ computes for the image of the unit circle.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #general}

$(c+di)H$ is the circle of radius $\sqrt{c^{2}+d^{2}}$ about the origin.

::: pf-proof

As $c+di\in\CC^*$ we have $c^{2}+d^{2}>0$, and $\abs{c+di}=\sqrt{c^{2}+d^{2}}$; step
[](#scaling){.pf-ref} with $w=c+di$ gives $(c+di)H=\ts{\zeta : \abs{\zeta}=\sqrt{c^{2}+d^{2}}}$.
Geometrically, $H$ is the unit circle and the coset is the image of the unit circle under
multiplication by $c+di$, which rotates by the argument of $c+di$ and scales every modulus
by $\abs{c+di}$; the rotated circle is again centred at the origin.

:::
