::: {.problem}
Let $H = \{0, \pm 3, \pm 6, \pm 9, \ldots\}$. Find all the left cosets of $H$ in $\ZZ$.
:::

::: {.solution}
There are 3: H, 1 + H, and 2 + H. (Note: By the division algorithm, we know there are no other cosets.)
:::
