::: {.problem}
Let G be a group of order 60. What are the possible orders for the subgroups of G?
:::

::: {.solution}
The possible orders are the divisors of 60: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 10, 12, 15, 20, 30, 60.
:::
