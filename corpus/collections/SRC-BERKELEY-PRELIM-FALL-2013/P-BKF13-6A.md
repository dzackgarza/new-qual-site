::: {.problem}
Let V be the complex vector space of complex $2 \times 2$ matrices X. Find all quadratic forms $Q$ on V such that $Q ( X ) = Q ( A X A ^ { - 1 } )$ for any complex invertible $2 \times 2$ matrix A.
:::
