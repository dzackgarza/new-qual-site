::: {.problem}
Let \(F\) be a finite field with \(q\) elements. A complete flag in \(F^n\) is a nested sequence
\[
V^1\subset V^2\subset\cdots\subset V^{n-1}
\]
with \(\dim V^j=j\). Let \(f_n(q)\) be the number of complete flags in \(F^n\). Find
\[
\lim_{q\to1} f_n(q).
\]
:::
