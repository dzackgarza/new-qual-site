::: {.problem}
Let \(f\) be an injective holomorphic function on the open unit disk \(U\subset\mathbb C\). Show that the area of \(f(U)\) is
\[
\int_U |f'(z)|^2\,dx\,dy.
\]
Compute the area of the image of \(U\) under
\[
f(z)=z+\frac{z^2}{2}.
\]
:::
