::: {.problem}
Let
\[
A=\begin{pmatrix}
\frac52&0&-\frac12\\
0&3&0\\
\frac52&0&-\frac12
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Calculate \(A^{16}\). You may give your answer as a polynomial in \(A\) of degree at most \(2\).
:::
