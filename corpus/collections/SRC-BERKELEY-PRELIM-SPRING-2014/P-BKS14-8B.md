::: {.problem}
Let \(B=C^{-1}AC\), where \(A\) and \(C\) are \(n\times n\) matrices with integer entries, \(\det A=1\), and \(\det C\ne0\). Prove that there exists a positive integer \(m\) such that every entry of \(B^m\) is an integer.
:::
