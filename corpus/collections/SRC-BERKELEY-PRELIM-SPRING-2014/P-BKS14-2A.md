::: {.problem}
Prove or disprove that there is a sequence \(\{f_n\}\) of continuous functions on \(\mathbb R\) such that for every rational \(x\), the sequence \(f_n(x)\) is bounded, but the sequence \(f_n(x+\sqrt2)\) is unbounded.
:::
