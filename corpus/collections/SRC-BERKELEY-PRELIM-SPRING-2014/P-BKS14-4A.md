::: {.problem}
Let \(f\) be analytic on the closed unit disk and suppose \(|f(z)|\le 1\) there. Assume
\[
f\!\left(\frac12\right)=f\!\left(\frac i2\right)=0.
\]
Prove that \(|f(0)|\le \frac14\).
:::
