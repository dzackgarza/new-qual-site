::: {.problem}
Let $Y$ be a topological space and, for each natural number $n$, let $X_n$ be a connected subspace of $Y$ such that
\[
X_{n+1}\subseteq X_n.
\]
Must
\[
\bigcap_n X_n
\]
be connected? Prove your answer.
:::
