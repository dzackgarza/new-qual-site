::: {.problem}
Let $\{a_n\}_{n=0}^\infty$ be a sequence of real numbers satisfying
\[
|a_{n+1}-a_n|\le \frac12|a_n-a_{n-1}|.
\]
Show that the sequence converges. Hint: show that the sequence is Cauchy.
:::
