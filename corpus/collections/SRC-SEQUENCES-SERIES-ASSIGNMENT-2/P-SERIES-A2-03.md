::: {.problem}
Find the $\limsup$ and $\liminf$ of the sequence $\{a_n\}$ defined recursively by
\[
a_1=0,\qquad a_{2m}=\frac{a_{2m-1}}2,\qquad a_{2m+1}=\frac12+a_{2m}.
\]
Justify your answers with complete proofs.
:::
