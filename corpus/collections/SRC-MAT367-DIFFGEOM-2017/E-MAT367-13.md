::: {.problem}
Let $F\in C^\infty(M,N)$.

1. If $(U,\phi)$ is a chart on $M$, $(V,\psi)$ is a chart on $N$, and $F(U)\subseteq V$, prove that $U\cap F^{-1}(W)$ is open for every open $W\subseteq N$.

2. Deduce that $F$ is continuous.

3. Given $p\in M$ and a chart $(V,\psi)$ about $F(p)$, show that there is a chart $(U,\phi)$ about $p$ with $F(U)\subseteq V$.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. Under the hypotheses of part 1, $U\cap F^{-1}(W)$ is open in $M$ for every open subset $W\subseteq N$.
::: {.proof}
Since $W$ is open in $N$ and $V$ is open in $N$, the set
\[
V\cap W
\]
is open in $V$.  Because $\psi:V\to\psi(V)$ is a homeomorphism,
\[
\psi(V\cap W)
\]
is open in $\psi(V)$.

The coordinate expression
\[
G=\psi\circ F\circ\phi^{-1}:\phi(U)\longrightarrow\psi(V)
\]
is smooth and therefore continuous.  Hence
\[
G^{-1}(\psi(V\cap W))
\]
is open in $\phi(U)$.  Directly from the definitions,
\[
G^{-1}(\psi(V\cap W))
=\phi\bigl(U\cap F^{-1}(W)\bigr).
\]
Applying the homeomorphism $\phi^{-1}$ shows that $U\cap F^{-1}(W)$ is open in $U$.  Since the chart domain $U$ is open in $M$, this set is open in $M$.
:::

<1>2. The map $F:M\to N$ is continuous.
::: {.proof}
Let $W\subseteq N$ be open.  For every
\[
p\in F^{-1}(W),
\]
smoothness of $F$ at $p$ gives coordinate charts $(U_p,\phi_p)$ about $p$ and $(V_p,\psi_p)$ about $F(p)$ such that
\[
F(U_p)\subseteq V_p.
\]
By <1>1,
\[
U_p\cap F^{-1}(W)
\]
is an open subset of $M$ containing $p$.  Therefore
\[
F^{-1}(W)
=\bigcup_{p\in F^{-1}(W)}\bigl(U_p\cap F^{-1}(W)\bigr)
\]
is open.  Thus the inverse image of every open subset of $N$ is open, so $F$ is continuous.
:::

<1>3. Given $p\in M$ and a coordinate chart $(V,\psi)$ about $F(p)$, choose any coordinate chart $(U_1,\phi_1)$ about $p$ and set
\[
\boxed{U=U_1\cap F^{-1}(V).}
\]
Then $U$ is the domain of a coordinate chart about $p$ and $F(U)\subseteq V$.
::: {.proof}
By <1>2, $F$ is continuous.  Since $V$ is open in $N$, the set $F^{-1}(V)$ is open in $M$.  It contains $p$ because $F(p)\in V$.  Hence
\[
U=U_1\cap F^{-1}(V)
\]
is an open neighborhood of $p$ contained in the chart domain $U_1$.

Restricting $\phi_1$ gives a coordinate chart
\[
\phi=\phi_1|_U:U\longrightarrow\phi_1(U),
\]
because $\phi_1(U)$ is open in $\phi_1(U_1)\subseteq\mathbb R^{\dim M}$.  By the definition of $U$,
\[
F(U)\subseteq V.
\]
:::

<1>4. Q.E.D.
::: {.proof}
Step <1>1 proves part 1, step <1>2 proves part 2, and step <1>3 proves part 3.
