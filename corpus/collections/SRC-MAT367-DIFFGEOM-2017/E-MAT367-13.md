::: {.problem}
Let $F\in C^\infty(M,N)$.

1. If $(U,\phi)$ is a chart on $M$, $(V,\psi)$ is a chart on $N$, and $F(U)\subseteq V$, prove that $U\cap F^{-1}(W)$ is open for every open $W\subseteq N$.

2. Deduce that $F$ is continuous.

3. Given $p\in M$ and a chart $(V,\psi)$ about $F(p)$, show that there is a chart $(U,\phi)$ about $p$ with $F(U)\subseteq V$.
:::
