::: {.problem}
In the notation of the source's construction of the diffeomorphism $\mathbb{RP}^1\cong S^1$, work out the details of the displayed calculation of $F(w_0:w_1)$ in the standard projective charts.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. The inverse of the circle chart
\[
\phi_-:U_-\longrightarrow\mathbb R,
\qquad
\phi_-(x,y)=\frac{x}{1-y},
\]
is
\[
\phi_-^{-1}(u)
=\left(\frac{2u}{1+u^2},\frac{u^2-1}{1+u^2}\right).
\]
::: {.proof}
Set
\[
x=\frac{2u}{1+u^2},
\qquad
y=\frac{u^2-1}{1+u^2}.
\]
Then
\[
x^2+y^2
=\frac{4u^2+(u^2-1)^2}{(1+u^2)^2}
=1,
\]
so $(x,y)\in S^1$.  Moreover
\[
1-y=\frac{2}{1+u^2},
\qquad
\frac{x}{1-y}=u.
\]
Thus this point is exactly $\phi_-^{-1}(u)$.
:::

<1>2. On the projective chart
\[
U_1=\{(w_0:w_1):w_1\ne0\},
\qquad
\phi_1(w_0:w_1)=\frac{w_0}{w_1},
\]
the map $F=\phi_-^{-1}\circ\phi_1$ is
\[
F(w_0:w_1)
=\frac1{w_0^2+w_1^2}
\left(2w_0w_1,w_0^2-w_1^2\right).
\]
::: {.proof}
Put
\[
u=\frac{w_0}{w_1}.
\]
By <1>1,
\[
\begin{aligned}
F(w_0:w_1)
&=\left(\frac{2u}{1+u^2},\frac{u^2-1}{1+u^2}\right)\\
&=\left(
\frac{2w_0w_1}{w_0^2+w_1^2},
\frac{w_0^2-w_1^2}{w_0^2+w_1^2}
\right).
\end{aligned}
\]
:::

<1>3. The inverse of the second circle chart
\[
\phi_+:U_+\longrightarrow\mathbb R,
\qquad
\phi_+(x,y)=\frac{x}{1+y},
\]
is
\[
\phi_+^{-1}(u)
=\left(\frac{2u}{1+u^2},\frac{1-u^2}{1+u^2}\right).
\]
Consequently the same homogeneous formula for $F$ holds on
\[
U_0=\{(w_0:w_1):w_0\ne0\}.
\]
::: {.proof}
The displayed inverse is verified exactly as in <1>1: its coordinates lie on $S^1$, and
\[
1+y=\frac{2}{1+u^2},
\qquad
\frac{x}{1+y}=u.
\]
On $U_0$ we have
\[
u=\phi_0(w_0:w_1)=\frac{w_1}{w_0}.
\]
Therefore
\[
\begin{aligned}
F(w_0:w_1)
&=\phi_+^{-1}(u)\\
&=\left(
\frac{2w_0w_1}{w_0^2+w_1^2},
\frac{w_0^2-w_1^2}{w_0^2+w_1^2}
\right).
\end{aligned}
\]
:::

<1>4. Hence on all of $\mathbb{RP}^1$,
\[
\boxed{
F(w_0:w_1)
=\frac1{\|w\|^2}
\left(2w_1w_0,w_0^2-w_1^2\right),
\qquad
\|w\|^2=w_0^2+w_1^2.
}
\]
::: {.proof}
The two standard projective charts $U_0$ and $U_1$ cover $\mathbb{RP}^1$, and <1>2 and <1>3 give the same formula on both.  The expression is unchanged when $(w_0,w_1)$ is replaced by $(\lambda w_0,\lambda w_1)$ with $\lambda\ne0$, since both numerator and denominator are multiplied by $\lambda^2$.  Thus it depends only on the projective point $(w_0:w_1)$.
:::

<1>5. Q.E.D.
::: {.proof}
Step <1>4 is the displayed calculation requested in the exercise.
