::: {.problem}
Let $M$ be a set equipped with a maximal atlas.
Show that a subset $U\subseteq M$ is open if and only if $U=\varnothing$ or $U$ is a union of domains of coordinate charts.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

Every coordinate-chart domain is open in $M$.

::: pf-proof

Let $(V,\varphi)$ be a chart in the maximal atlas $\mathcal A$.  For any other chart $(W,\psi)\in\mathcal A$, compatibility gives that
\[
\psi(V\cap W)
\]
is open in $\mathbb R^m$.  This is exactly the defining condition that $V$ be open in $M$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

Any union of coordinate-chart domains is open.

::: pf-proof

Let
\[
U=\bigcup_{i\in I}V_i,
\]
where each $V_i$ is a chart domain.  For any chart $(W,\psi)\in\mathcal A$,
\[
\psi(U\cap W)
=\psi\left(\bigcup_{i\in I}(V_i\cap W)\right)
=\bigcup_{i\in I}\psi(V_i\cap W).
\]
Each set on the right is open by step [](#s1){.pf-ref} and chart compatibility, so their union is open in $\mathbb R^m$.  Hence $U$ is open in $M$.  The empty set is open as well.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

Every nonempty open subset of $M$ is a union of coordinate-chart domains.

::: pf-proof

Let $U\subseteq M$ be nonempty and open.  For each $p\in U$, choose a chart
\[
(V_p,\varphi_p)\in\mathcal A
\]
with $p\in V_p$.  Since $U$ is open,
\[
\varphi_p(U\cap V_p)
\]
is open in $\mathbb R^m$.  Therefore
\[
(U\cap V_p,\varphi_p|_{U\cap V_p})
\]
is a chart compatible with every chart of $\mathcal A$.  Maximality of $\mathcal A$ implies that this restricted chart itself belongs to $\mathcal A$.

Its domain contains $p$ and lies in $U$.  Hence
\[
U=\bigcup_{p\in U}(U\cap V_p),
\]
expressing $U$ as a union of coordinate-chart domains.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s2){.pf-ref} proves one implication, while step [](#s3){.pf-ref} proves the converse for nonempty $U$; the empty set is handled explicitly in step [](#s2){.pf-ref}.

:::
