::: {.problem}
For $u\in\mathbb R^n\setminus\{0\}$, consider the matrix
\[
A(u)_{ij}=\frac{1}{\lVert u\rVert^2}\delta_{ij}-\frac{2u_i u_j}{\lVert u\rVert^4}.
\]
Calculate $\det A(u)$.
The source suggests checking the eigenvalue on the span of $u$ and on $u^\perp$.
:::
