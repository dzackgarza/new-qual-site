::: {.problem}
For $u\in\mathbb R^n\setminus\{0\}$, consider the matrix
\[
A(u)_{ij}=\frac{1}{\lVert u\rVert^2}\delta_{ij}-\frac{2u_i u_j}{\lVert u\rVert^4}.
\]
Calculate $\det A(u)$.
The source suggests checking the eigenvalue on the span of $u$ and on $u^\perp$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Set
\[
r=\lVert u\rVert>0.
\]
Then
\[
A(u)=\frac1{r^2}I-\frac2{r^4}uu^T.
\]

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

The vector $u$ is an eigenvector of $A(u)$ with eigenvalue
\[
-\frac1{r^2}.
\]

::: pf-proof

Since
\[
u^Tu=r^2,
\]
we have
\[
A(u)u
=\frac1{r^2}u-\frac2{r^4}u(u^Tu)
=\frac1{r^2}u-\frac2{r^2}u
=-\frac1{r^2}u.
\]

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

Every vector in $u^\perp$ is an eigenvector of $A(u)$ with eigenvalue
\[
\frac1{r^2}.
\]

::: pf-proof

If $v\in u^\perp$, then $u^Tv=0$.  Therefore
\[
A(u)v
=\frac1{r^2}v-\frac2{r^4}u(u^Tv)
=\frac1{r^2}v.
\]

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

The determinant is
\[
\boxed{\det A(u)=-\lVert u\rVert^{-2n}}.
\]

::: pf-proof

The orthogonal decomposition
\[
\mathbb R^n=\mathbb Ru\oplus u^\perp
\]
has dimensions $1$ and $n-1$.  By steps [](#s1){.pf-ref} and [](#s2){.pf-ref}, the eigenvalues of $A(u)$ are
\[
-r^{-2}
\]
once and
\[
r^{-2}
\]
with multiplicity $n-1$.  Their product is
\[
\det A(u)
=(-r^{-2})(r^{-2})^{n-1}
=-r^{-2n}
=-\lVert u\rVert^{-2n}.
\]

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s3){.pf-ref} follows from the eigenspace decomposition established in steps [](#s1){.pf-ref} and [](#s2){.pf-ref}.

:::
