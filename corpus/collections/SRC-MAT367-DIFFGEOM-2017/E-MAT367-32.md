::: {.problem}
Let
\[
\alpha=y^2e^x\,dx+2ye^x\,dy
\]
on $\mathbb R^2$ and let
\[
\gamma:[0,1]\to\mathbb R^2,\qquad \gamma(t)=(\sin(\pi t/2),t^3).
\]
Find $\int_\gamma\alpha$.
:::
