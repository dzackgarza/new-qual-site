::: {.problem}
In the notation of the source's hypersurface construction, verify that
\[
\Gamma=i^*(\iota_X\Gamma_0)
\]
is a volume form, where $i$ is the hypersurface inclusion, $X$ is the chosen transverse vector field, and $\Gamma_0$ is the ambient volume form.
