::: {.problem}
In the notation of the source's hypersurface construction, verify that
\[
\Gamma=i^*(\iota_X\Gamma_0)
\]
is a volume form, where $i$ is the hypersurface inclusion, $X$ is the chosen transverse vector field, and $\Gamma_0$ is the ambient volume form.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $S\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ be the hypersurface, let
\[
i:S\hookrightarrow\mathbb R^n
\]
be the inclusion, and let $X\in\mathfrak X(\mathbb R^n)$ be a vector field such that
\[
X_p\notin T_pS
\qquad\text{for every }p\in S.
\]
The ambient Euclidean volume form is
\[
\Gamma_0=dx_1\wedge\cdots\wedge dx_n.
\]

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #Gamma-is-smooth-form}
The form
\[
\Gamma=i^*(\iota_X\Gamma_0)
\]
is a smooth $(n-1)$-form on $S$.

::: pf-proof
Contraction of the smooth $n$-form $\Gamma_0$ with the smooth vector field $X$ gives a smooth $(n-1)$-form $\iota_X\Gamma_0$ on $\mathbb R^n$.  Pullback by the smooth inclusion $i$ therefore gives
\[
i^*(\iota_X\Gamma_0)\in\Omega^{n-1}(S).
\]
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #extended-basis}
Fix $p\in S$ and a basis
\[
v_1,\ldots,v_{n-1}
\]
of $T_pS$.  Then
\[
X_p,v_1,\ldots,v_{n-1}
\]
is a basis of $T_p\mathbb R^n\cong\mathbb R^n$.

::: pf-proof
The vectors $v_1,\ldots,v_{n-1}$ span the $(n-1)$-dimensional subspace $T_pS$.  By transversality,
\[
X_p\notin T_pS.
\]
Thus adjoining $X_p$ increases the span by one dimension, so the resulting $n$ vectors form a basis of the $n$-dimensional ambient tangent space.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #Gamma-value-on-basis}
The value of $\Gamma$ on the basis from step [](#extended-basis){.pf-ref} is
\[
\Gamma_p(v_1,\ldots,v_{n-1})
=\Gamma_{0,p}(X_p,v_1,\ldots,v_{n-1}).
\]

::: pf-proof
By the definition of pullback,
\[
\Gamma_p(v_1,\ldots,v_{n-1})
=(\iota_X\Gamma_0)_p(T_pi(v_1),\ldots,T_pi(v_{n-1})).
\]
For the inclusion, $T_pi$ identifies each $v_j$ with the same vector in $T_p\mathbb R^n$.  By the definition of contraction,
\[
(\iota_X\Gamma_0)_p(v_1,\ldots,v_{n-1})
=\Gamma_{0,p}(X_p,v_1,\ldots,v_{n-1}).
\]
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #Gamma-nonvanishing}
The form $\Gamma_p$ is nonzero for every $p\in S$.

::: pf-proof
By step [](#extended-basis){.pf-ref}, the vectors
\[
X_p,v_1,\ldots,v_{n-1}
\]
form a basis of $T_p\mathbb R^n$.  The Euclidean volume form $\Gamma_{0,p}$ is a nonzero top-degree alternating form, so its value on any basis is nonzero.  Hence step [](#Gamma-value-on-basis){.pf-ref} gives
\[
\Gamma_p(v_1,\ldots,v_{n-1})\ne0.
\]
Therefore $\Gamma_p\ne0$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #Gamma-is-volume-form}
Consequently
\[
\boxed{\Gamma=i^*(\iota_X\Gamma_0)}
\]
is a volume form on $S$.

::: pf-proof
Step [](#Gamma-is-smooth-form){.pf-ref} shows that $\Gamma$ is a smooth form of top degree $n-1$ on the $(n-1)$-manifold $S$.  Step [](#Gamma-nonvanishing){.pf-ref} shows that it is nowhere vanishing.  This is exactly the definition of a volume form used in the source.
:::

:::

::: pf-qed
Step [](#Gamma-is-volume-form){.pf-ref} is the claim to be verified.
:::
