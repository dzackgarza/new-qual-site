::: {.problem}
Let $F:M\to N$ be smooth, $p\in M$, $q=F(p)$, and $v\in T_pM$.
Show that the map
\[
C^\infty(N)\to\mathbb R,\qquad g\longmapsto v(g\circ F),
\]
satisfies the product rule at $q$ and therefore defines an element of $T_qN$.
:::
