::: {.problem}
Let $F:M\to N$ be smooth, $p\in M$, $q=F(p)$, and $v\in T_pM$.
Show that the map
\[
C^\infty(N)\to\mathbb R,\qquad g\longmapsto v(g\circ F),
\]
satisfies the product rule at $q$ and therefore defines an element of $T_qN$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Define
\[
D:C^\infty(N)\longrightarrow\mathbb R,
\qquad
D(g)=v(g\circ F).
\]

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

The map $D$ is linear.

::: pf-proof

For $a,b\in\mathbb R$ and $g,h\in C^\infty(N)$,
\[
\begin{aligned}
D(ag+bh)
&=v\bigl((ag+bh)\circ F\bigr)\\
&=v\bigl(a(g\circ F)+b(h\circ F)\bigr)\\
&=aD(g)+bD(h),
\end{aligned}
\]
because $v\in T_pM$ is linear.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

The map $D$ satisfies the product rule at $q=F(p)$:
\[
\boxed{
D(gh)=g(q)D(h)+D(g)h(q).
}
\]

::: pf-proof

For $g,h\in C^\infty(N)$,
\[
\begin{aligned}
D(gh)
&=v\bigl((gh)\circ F\bigr)\\
&=v\bigl((g\circ F)(h\circ F)\bigr).
\end{aligned}
\]
Since $v$ is a tangent vector at $p$, it satisfies the product rule there.  Hence
\[
\begin{aligned}
D(gh)
&=(g\circ F)(p)\,v(h\circ F)
+v(g\circ F)\,(h\circ F)(p)\\
&=g(F(p))D(h)+D(g)h(F(p))\\
&=g(q)D(h)+D(g)h(q).
\end{aligned}
\]

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

Therefore $D$ defines a tangent vector in $T_qN$.

::: pf-proof

The source's derivation characterization of the tangent space says that $T_qN$ consists exactly of the linear maps
\[
C^\infty(N)\longrightarrow\mathbb R
\]
satisfying the product rule at $q$.  Steps [](#s1){.pf-ref} and [](#s2){.pf-ref} show that $D$ has these two properties.  Hence
\[
D\in T_qN.
\]

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s3){.pf-ref} proves that the formula
\[
(T_pF(v))(g)=v(g\circ F)
\]
is well-defined as a tangent vector at $q=F(p)$.

:::
