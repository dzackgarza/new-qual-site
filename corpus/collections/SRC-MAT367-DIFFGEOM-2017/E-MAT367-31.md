::: {.problem}
Let $F:M\to N$ be smooth and let $X\in\mathfrak X(M)$ and $Y\in\mathfrak X(N)$.
Show that $X$ and $Y$ are $F$-related if and only if the natural diagram formed by $X:M\to TM$, $Y:N\to TN$, $F:M\to N$, and $TF:TM\to TN$ commutes.
:::
