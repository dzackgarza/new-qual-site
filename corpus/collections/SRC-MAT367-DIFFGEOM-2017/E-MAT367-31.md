::: {.problem}
Let $F:M\to N$ be smooth and let $X\in\mathfrak X(M)$ and $Y\in\mathfrak X(N)$.
Show that $X$ and $Y$ are $F$-related if and only if the natural diagram formed by $X:M\to TM$, $Y:N\to TN$, $F:M\to N$, and $TF:TM\to TN$ commutes.
:::

::: {.solution}
The diagram in question is
\[
\begin{array}{ccc}
M&\xrightarrow{X}&TM\\
{\scriptstyle F}\downarrow&&\downarrow{\scriptstyle TF}\\
N&\xrightarrow{Y}&TN.
\end{array}
\]

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #TF-composed-with-X}
For every $p\in M$,
\[
(TF\circ X)(p)=T_pF(X_p).
\]

::: pf-proof
By definition, $X(p)=X_p\in T_pM$.  The tangent-bundle map $TF:TM\to TN$ restricts on the fiber $T_pM$ to the tangent map
\[
T_pF:T_pM\longrightarrow T_{F(p)}N.
\]
Therefore
\[
TF(X(p))=T_pF(X_p).
\]
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #Y-composed-with-F}
For every $p\in M$,
\[
(Y\circ F)(p)=Y_{F(p)}.
\]

::: pf-proof
This is the definition of $Y$ as a section $N\to TN$: at a point $q\in N$, its value is the tangent vector $Y_q\in T_qN$.  Substitute $q=F(p)$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #commuting-diagram-condition}
The diagram commutes if and only if
\[
T_pF(X_p)=Y_{F(p)}
\qquad\text{for every }p\in M.
\]

::: pf-proof
The diagram commutes exactly when the two maps from $M$ to $TN$ are equal:
\[
TF\circ X=Y\circ F.
\]
Two maps are equal exactly when their values agree at every point.  By steps [](#TF-composed-with-X){.pf-ref} and [](#Y-composed-with-F){.pf-ref}, equality at $p$ is the displayed condition.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #boxed-F-related-equivalence}
Hence
\[
\boxed{
X\sim_FY
\iff
TF\circ X=Y\circ F.
}
\]

::: pf-proof
The defining condition for $F$-related vector fields is precisely
\[
T_pF(X_p)=Y_{F(p)}
\]
for every $p\in M$.  Apply step [](#commuting-diagram-condition){.pf-ref}.
:::

:::

::: pf-qed
Step [](#boxed-F-related-equivalence){.pf-ref} is the desired equivalence.
:::
