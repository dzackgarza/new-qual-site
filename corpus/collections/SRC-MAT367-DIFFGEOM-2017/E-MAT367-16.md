::: {.problem}
In the notation of the source's construction of the diffeomorphism $\mathbb{CP}^1\cong S^2$ by stereographic charts, work out the details of the displayed coordinate calculation.
:::

::: {.solution}
Identify $\mathbb R^2$ with $\mathbb C$ by $(x,y)\leftrightarrow x+iy$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

The inverse of the stereographic chart
\[
\phi_+:U_+\longrightarrow\mathbb C,
\qquad
\phi_+(x,y,z)=\frac{x+iy}{1+z},
\]
is
\[
\phi_+^{-1}(u)
=\frac1{1+|u|^2}
\left(2\operatorname{Re}u,\,2\operatorname{Im}u,\,1-|u|^2\right).
\]

::: pf-proof

Let the displayed point be $(x,y,z)$.  Then
\[
x^2+y^2+z^2
=\frac{4|u|^2+(1-|u|^2)^2}{(1+|u|^2)^2}
=1,
\]
so it lies on $S^2$.  Also
\[
1+z=\frac{2}{1+|u|^2},
\qquad
x+iy=\frac{2u}{1+|u|^2},
\]
and therefore
\[
\frac{x+iy}{1+z}=u.
\]

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

On the projective chart
\[
U_0=\{(w_0:w_1):w_0\ne0\},
\qquad
\phi_0(w_0:w_1)=\frac{w_1}{w_0},
\]
the condition $\phi_+\circ F\circ\phi_0^{-1}=\operatorname{id}_{\mathbb C}$ gives
\[
F(w_0:w_1)
=\frac1{|w_0|^2+|w_1|^2}
\left(
2\operatorname{Re}(w_1\overline{w_0}),
2\operatorname{Im}(w_1\overline{w_0}),
|w_0|^2-|w_1|^2
\right).
\]

::: pf-proof

Put
\[
u=\frac{w_1}{w_0}.
\]
Then $F=\phi_+^{-1}\circ\phi_0$ on $U_0$.  By step [](#s1){.pf-ref},
\[
F(w_0:w_1)
=\frac1{1+|u|^2}
\left(2\operatorname{Re}u,\,2\operatorname{Im}u,\,1-|u|^2\right).
\]
Since
\[
u=\frac{w_1\overline{w_0}}{|w_0|^2},
\qquad
1+|u|^2=\frac{|w_0|^2+|w_1|^2}{|w_0|^2},
\]
multiplying numerator and denominator by $|w_0|^2$ gives the claimed homogeneous expression.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

The inverse of the other stereographic chart
\[
\phi_-:U_-\longrightarrow\mathbb C,
\qquad
\phi_-(x,y,z)=\frac{x+iy}{1-z},
\]
is
\[
\phi_-^{-1}(v)
=\frac1{1+|v|^2}
\left(2\operatorname{Re}v,\,2\operatorname{Im}v,\,|v|^2-1\right).
\]

::: pf-proof

The verification is the same as in step [](#s1){.pf-ref}.  The displayed point lies on $S^2$, and
\[
1-z=\frac{2}{1+|v|^2},
\qquad
x+iy=\frac{2v}{1+|v|^2},
\]
so $\phi_-(x,y,z)=v$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s4}

On the projective chart
\[
U_1=\{(w_0:w_1):w_1\ne0\},
\qquad
\phi_1(w_0:w_1)=\frac{w_0}{w_1},
\]
the condition $\phi_-\circ F\circ\phi_1^{-1}(u)=\overline u$ gives the same homogeneous formula as in step [](#s2){.pf-ref}.

::: pf-proof

Put
\[
u=\frac{w_0}{w_1},
\qquad
v=\overline u=\frac{\overline{w_0}}{\overline{w_1}}.
\]
By step [](#s3){.pf-ref},
\[
F(w_0:w_1)
=\frac1{1+|v|^2}
\left(2\operatorname{Re}v,\,2\operatorname{Im}v,\,|v|^2-1\right).
\]
Multiplying numerator and denominator by $|w_1|^2$ gives
\[
\frac1{|w_0|^2+|w_1|^2}
\left(
2\operatorname{Re}(w_1\overline{w_0}),
2\operatorname{Im}(w_1\overline{w_0}),
|w_0|^2-|w_1|^2
\right),
\]
because $|w_1|^2v=w_1\overline{w_0}$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s5}

Hence the displayed map is
\[
\boxed{
F(w_0:w_1)
=\frac1{|w_0|^2+|w_1|^2}
\left(
2\operatorname{Re}(w_1\overline{w_0}),
2\operatorname{Im}(w_1\overline{w_0}),
|w_0|^2-|w_1|^2
\right).
}
\]

::: pf-proof

The charts $U_0$ and $U_1$ cover $\mathbb{CP}^1$, and steps [](#s2){.pf-ref} and [](#s4){.pf-ref} give the same expression on them.  Replacing $(w_0,w_1)$ by $(\lambda w_0,\lambda w_1)$ multiplies every numerator coordinate and the denominator by $|\lambda|^2$, so the formula depends only on the projective point.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s5){.pf-ref} is the coordinate calculation requested in the exercise.
