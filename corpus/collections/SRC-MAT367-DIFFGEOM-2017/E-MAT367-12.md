::: {.problem}
Verify that
\[
\operatorname{Gr}(k,n)\longrightarrow M_n(\mathbb R),\qquad E\longmapsto P_E,
\]
where $P_E$ is orthogonal projection onto $E$, is smooth and injective.
Deduce that $\operatorname{Gr}(k,n)$ is Hausdorff.
Discuss the analogous map for the complex Grassmannian.
:::
