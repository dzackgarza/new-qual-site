::: {.problem}
Complete the last step in the source proof that the manifold topology on a submanifold $S\subseteq M$ agrees with the subspace topology: with the notation constructed there, verify that the constructed open set intersects $S$ in the original open subset.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $\dim M=m$ and $\dim S=k$.  For a submanifold chart $(V,\psi)$, write
\[
A_V=\{a\in\mathbb R^k:(a,0)\in\psi(U'\cap V)\}.
\]
Thus the source's notation $\psi(U'\cap V)\times\mathbb R^{m-k}$ means $A_V\times\mathbb R^{m-k}$ after identifying $\psi(V\cap S)$ with its first $k$ coordinates.  The source starts with an open subset $U'\subseteq S$ and defines
\[
U=
\bigcup_{(V,\psi)}
\psi^{-1}\!\left(A_V\times\mathbb R^{m-k}\right),
\]
where the union ranges over submanifold charts $(V,\psi)$ covering $S$ and the submanifold coordinates identify
\[
\psi(V\cap S)=\psi(V)\cap(\mathbb R^k\times\{0\}).
\]

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

One has $U'\subseteq U\cap S$.

::: pf-proof

Let $p\in U'$.  Since the chosen submanifold charts cover $S$, choose $(V,\psi)$ with $p\in V$.  Because $p\in S$, write
\[
\psi(p)=(a,0)
\]
with $a\in\mathbb R^k$.  Since $p\in U'\cap V$, the tangential coordinate $a$ lies in $A_V$.  Hence
\[
\psi(p)=(a,0)
\in
A_V\times\mathbb R^{m-k},
\]
so $p$ belongs to the corresponding summand of $U$.  Thus $p\in U$, and already $p\in S$, proving $p\in U\cap S$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

One has $U\cap S\subseteq U'$.

::: pf-proof

Let $p\in U\cap S$.  By the definition of $U$, there is a submanifold chart $(V,\psi)$ such that
\[
p\in V,
\qquad
\psi(p)\in A_V\times\mathbb R^{m-k}.
\]
Since $p\in S$, its normal coordinates vanish, so
\[
\psi(p)=(a,0)
\]
for some $a\in\mathbb R^k$.  Membership in the product above says
\[
a\in A_V.
\]
By the definition of $A_V$, there is $q\in U'\cap V\subseteq S$ with
\[
\psi(q)=(a,0)=\psi(p).
\]
The chart map $\psi$ is injective on $V$, hence $q=p$.  Therefore $p\in U'$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

Therefore
\[
\boxed{U\cap S=U'.}
\]

::: pf-proof

Combine steps [](#s1){.pf-ref} and [](#s2){.pf-ref}.

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s3){.pf-ref} is the last equality needed in the source proof.
