::: {.problem}
Let $\mathcal A$ be a maximal atlas on a manifold $M$, let $U\subseteq M$ be open, and let $\mathcal A_U$ be the atlas obtained by restricting every chart in $\mathcal A$ to $U$.
Verify that $\mathcal A_U$ is maximal.
If $\mathcal A$ satisfies the countability and Hausdorff conditions, show that $\mathcal A_U$ does also.
:::
