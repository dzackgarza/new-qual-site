::: {.problem}
For the torus example immediately preceding this exercise in the source, work out the formula for the graphing function $F(y,z)$ on the subset where $x^2+y^2<R^2$ and $x>0$.
:::

::: {.solution}
The source's surface is the torus
\[
S=\left\{(x,y,z)\in\mathbb R^3:
\bigl(\sqrt{x^2+y^2}-R\bigr)^2+z^2=r^2\right\},
\qquad 0<r<R.
\]

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

On the component where $x^2+y^2<R^2$, the torus equation is equivalent to
\[
\sqrt{x^2+y^2}=R-\sqrt{r^2-z^2}.
\]

::: pf-proof

The torus equation gives
\[
\bigl(\sqrt{x^2+y^2}-R\bigr)^2=r^2-z^2.
\]
On the specified component,
\[
\sqrt{x^2+y^2}-R<0.
\]
Therefore the correct square-root branch is
\[
\sqrt{x^2+y^2}-R=-\sqrt{r^2-z^2},
\]
which is the claimed identity.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

On the further component where $x>0$, one has
\[
\boxed{
F(y,z)
=\sqrt{\left(R-\sqrt{r^2-z^2}\right)^2-y^2}.
}
\]

::: pf-proof

Squaring the identity in step [](#s1){.pf-ref} gives
\[
x^2+y^2
=\left(R-\sqrt{r^2-z^2}\right)^2.
\]
Hence
\[
x^2
=\left(R-\sqrt{r^2-z^2}\right)^2-y^2.
\]
The condition $x>0$ selects the positive square root, yielding the displayed formula.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

The natural domain of this graph is
\[
D=\left\{(y,z):
|z|<r,
\quad
y^2<\left(R-\sqrt{r^2-z^2}\right)^2
\right\}.
\]
On $D$, the function $F$ in step [](#s2){.pf-ref} is smooth.

::: pf-proof

If a point of the requested component had $|z|=r$, then step [](#s1){.pf-ref} would give
\[
\sqrt{x^2+y^2}=R,
\]
contrary to $x^2+y^2<R^2$.  Thus $|z|<r$.  The condition $x>0$ is equivalent, by step [](#s2){.pf-ref}, to strict positivity of the outer radicand, namely
\[
y^2<\left(R-\sqrt{r^2-z^2}\right)^2.
\]
Both radicands are therefore strictly positive on $D$.  The ordinary square-root function is smooth on $(0,\infty)$, so the displayed composition is smooth on $D$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s4}

The requested component of the torus is exactly
\[
\boxed{
\{(F(y,z),y,z):(y,z)\in D\}.
}
\]

::: pf-proof

Steps [](#s1){.pf-ref} and [](#s2){.pf-ref} show that every point of the component has this form.  Conversely, for every $(y,z)\in D$, substituting $x=F(y,z)>0$ into the equations in step [](#s2){.pf-ref} and then step [](#s1){.pf-ref} recovers the torus equation and gives $\sqrt{x^2+y^2}<R$.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s4){.pf-ref} gives the local graph formula requested in the exercise.

:::
