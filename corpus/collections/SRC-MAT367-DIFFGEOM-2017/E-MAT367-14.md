::: {.problem}
Using the preceding local-coordinate exercise, prove that every smooth map $F\in C^\infty(M,N)$ is continuous.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

For every open subset $W\subseteq N$ and every point $p\in F^{-1}(W)$, there is an open neighborhood $O_p\subseteq M$ of $p$ such that
\[
O_p\subseteq F^{-1}(W).
\]

::: pf-proof

Since $F$ is smooth at $p$, there are coordinate charts $(U_p,\phi_p)$ about $p$ and $(V_p,\psi_p)$ about $F(p)$ such that
\[
F(U_p)\subseteq V_p.
\]
The preceding local-coordinate exercise shows that
\[
O_p:=U_p\cap F^{-1}(W)
\]
is open in $M$.  Because $p\in U_p$ and $F(p)\in W$, the point $p$ lies in $O_p$, and by definition $O_p\subseteq F^{-1}(W)$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

The inverse image $F^{-1}(W)$ is open in $M$ for every open subset $W\subseteq N$.

::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1){.pf-ref},
\[
F^{-1}(W)
=\bigcup_{p\in F^{-1}(W)}O_p.
\]
Every $O_p$ is open in $M$, so this union is open.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

Hence every smooth map $F\in C^\infty(M,N)$ is continuous.

::: pf-proof

Step [](#s2){.pf-ref} says that the inverse image under $F$ of every open subset of $N$ is open in $M$, which is the definition of continuity.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s3){.pf-ref} is the required conclusion.

:::
