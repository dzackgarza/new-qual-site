::: {.problem}
Complete the converse direction of the source proposition: if $A\subseteq M$ is contained in the domain of a coordinate chart $(U,\phi)$, prove that $A$ is compact in $M$ if and only if $\phi(A)$ is compact in Euclidean space.
:::

::: {.solution}
Assume $A$ is compact in $M$ and
\[
A\subseteq U.
\]
We prove that $\phi(A)$ is compact in $\mathbb R^n$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

Let $\{V_\alpha\}_{\alpha\in I}$ be an arbitrary open cover of $\phi(A)$ in $\mathbb R^n$.  The sets
\[
W_\alpha=\phi^{-1}(V_\alpha\cap\phi(U))
\]
are open subsets of $M$ and cover $A$.

::: pf-proof

Because $(U,\phi)$ is a coordinate chart, $\phi(U)$ is open in $\mathbb R^n$.  Hence
\[
V_\alpha\cap\phi(U)
\]
is open in $\phi(U)$, and its inverse image $W_\alpha$ is open in $U$.

The chart domain $U$ is open in $M$, so every subset open in $U$ is open in $M$.  Thus each $W_\alpha$ is open in $M$.

If $a\in A$, then $\phi(a)\in\phi(A)$, so $\phi(a)\in V_\alpha$ for some $\alpha$.  Since $a\in U$, this gives
\[
a\in\phi^{-1}(V_\alpha\cap\phi(U))=W_\alpha.
\]
Therefore $\{W_\alpha\}$ covers $A$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

The cover $\{V_\alpha\}$ has a finite subcover of $\phi(A)$.

::: pf-proof

Compactness of $A$ and step [](#s1){.pf-ref} give indices
\[
\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_N
\]
such that
\[
A\subseteq W_{\alpha_1}\cup\cdots\cup W_{\alpha_N}.
\]
Applying the injective map $\phi$ gives
\[
\phi(A)
\subseteq
\phi(W_{\alpha_1})\cup\cdots\cup\phi(W_{\alpha_N})
\subseteq
V_{\alpha_1}\cup\cdots\cup V_{\alpha_N}.
\]
Thus the original Euclidean open cover has a finite subcover.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

Hence $\phi(A)$ is compact in $\mathbb R^n$.

::: pf-proof

The cover in step [](#s1){.pf-ref} was arbitrary, and step [](#s2){.pf-ref} produced a finite subcover.  This is the definition of compactness of $\phi(A)$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s4}

Together with the forward implication proved in the source,
\[
\boxed{
A\text{ is compact in }M
\iff
\phi(A)\text{ is compact in }\mathbb R^n.
}
\]

::: pf-proof

The source proves that compactness of $\phi(A)$ implies compactness of $A$.  Step [](#s3){.pf-ref} proves the converse requested here.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s4){.pf-ref} is the claimed equivalence.

:::
