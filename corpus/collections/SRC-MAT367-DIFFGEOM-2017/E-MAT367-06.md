::: {.problem}
Let $A\in M_n(\mathbb C)$ and let $A_{\mathbb R}\in M_{2n}(\mathbb R)$ be the same linear transformation regarded as real-linear on $\mathbb R^{2n}\cong\mathbb C^n$.
Show that
\[
\det_{\mathbb R}(A_{\mathbb R})=|\det_{\mathbb C}(A)|^2.
\]
:::

::: {.solution}
Write
\[
A=B+iC,
\qquad
B,C\in M_n(\mathbb R).
\]

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

In the real basis obtained from the standard complex basis of $\mathbb C^n$, the matrix of $A_{\mathbb R}$ is
\[
M=
\begin{pmatrix}
B&-C\\
C&B
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

::: pf-proof

For $x,y\in\mathbb R^n$,
\[
A(x+iy)
=(B+iC)(x+iy)
=(Bx-Cy)+i(Cx+By).
\]
Thus the real and imaginary parts transform by the displayed block matrix.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

Over $\mathbb C$, the matrix $M$ is similar to
\[
\begin{pmatrix}
A&0\\
0&\overline A
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

::: pf-proof

The complexification of the underlying real vector space has the direct-sum decomposition
\[
\mathbb C^{2n}
=V_+\oplus V_-,
\]
where
\[
V_+=\{(z,-iz):z\in\mathbb C^n\},
\qquad
V_-=\{(z,iz):z\in\mathbb C^n\}.
\]
Using the block form from step [](#s1){.pf-ref},
\[
M(z,-iz)
=\bigl((B+iC)z,-i(B+iC)z\bigr),
\]
so the restriction to $V_+$ is represented by $A=B+iC$.  Similarly,
\[
M(z,iz)
=\bigl((B-iC)z,i(B-iC)z\bigr),
\]
so the restriction to $V_-$ is represented by $\overline A=B-iC$.  Hence $M$ has the asserted block-diagonal form in a basis adapted to $V_+\oplus V_-$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

The real determinant is
\[
\boxed{
\det_{\mathbb R}(A_{\mathbb R})
=|\det_{\mathbb C}(A)|^2.
}
\]

::: pf-proof

The determinant of a real matrix is unchanged when the same matrix is regarded over $\mathbb C$.  By step [](#s2){.pf-ref},
\[
\begin{aligned}
\det_{\mathbb R}(A_{\mathbb R})
&=\det_{\mathbb C}M\\
&=\det_{\mathbb C}(A)\det_{\mathbb C}(\overline A)\\
&=\det_{\mathbb C}(A)\,\overline{\det_{\mathbb C}(A)}\\
&=|\det_{\mathbb C}(A)|^2.
\end{aligned}
\]

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s3){.pf-ref} follows from the real block form in step [](#s1){.pf-ref} and its complex eigenspace decomposition in step [](#s2){.pf-ref}.

:::
