::: {.problem}
Let $P\in M_n(\mathbb R)$.
Show that $P$ is the matrix of an orthogonal projection onto a subspace $E\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ if and only if
\[
P^T=P,\qquad P^2=P.
\]
What is the matrix of the orthogonal projection onto $E^\perp$?
:::
