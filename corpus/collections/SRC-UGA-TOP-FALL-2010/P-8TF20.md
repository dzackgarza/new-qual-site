::: problem
a. State the classification theorem for compact connected surfaces (with or without boundary).

b. Show that any compact connected surface with a nonempty boundary is homotopy equivalent to a wedge of circles.
(Hint: you may assume that any compact connected surface without boundary is given by identifying edges of a polygon in pairs.)

c. For each surface in your classification, say how many circles are needed in the wedge from part (b). (Hint: you should be able to do this even if you have not done part (b).)
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. The classification of compact connected surfaces is as follows.
Every such surface is homeomorphic to exactly one surface in one of the two families
\[
\Sigma_{g,b}
\qquad(g\ge0,\ b\ge0)
\]
or
\[
N_{k,b}
\qquad(k\ge1,\ b\ge0),
\]
where $\Sigma_{g,b}$ is the orientable genus-$g$ surface with $b$ boundary components and $N_{k,b}$ is the nonorientable genus-$k$ surface with $b$ boundary components.
::: {.proof}
The surface $\Sigma_{g,b}$ is obtained from the connected sum of $g$ tori by removing the interiors of $b$ disjoint disks; for $g=0$ the closed model is $S^2$.
The surface $N_{k,b}$ is obtained from the connected sum of $k$ projective planes by removing the interiors of $b$ disjoint disks.

The classification theorem says that every compact connected surface is of exactly one of these forms, and that orientability, the genus $g$ or crosscap number $k$, and the number $b$ of boundary components determine its homeomorphism type.
This answers part (a).
:::

<1>2. If $b\ge1$, the surface $\Sigma_{g,b}$ has a handle decomposition with one $0$-handle, $2g+b-1$ $1$-handles, and no $2$-handles; the surface $N_{k,b}$ has one $0$-handle, $k+b-1$ $1$-handles, and no $2$-handles.
::: {.proof}
Use the standard polygon models from the classification theorem and leave one boundary disk unfilled.

For $\Sigma_{g,b}$, the $g$ orientable handles contribute $2g$ bands to the standard disk model, and each of the remaining $b-1$ boundary components is created by one additional band.
Thus the surface is a disk with
\[
2g+(b-1)=2g+b-1
\]
bands attached along disjoint pairs of boundary intervals.

For $N_{k,b}$, each of the $k$ crosscaps contributes one twisted band, and each of the remaining $b-1$ boundary components contributes one further band.
Thus the surface is a disk with
\[
k+(b-1)=k+b-1
\]
bands attached.

A disk is a $0$-handle and each band is a $1$-handle, giving the stated handle decompositions.
:::

<1>3. A compact connected surface built from one $0$-handle and $r$ $1$-handles is homotopy equivalent to a wedge of $r$ circles.
::: {.proof}
Inside the $0$-handle choose a point $v$ and disjoint arcs from $v$ to the attaching intervals of the $1$-handles.
In each $1$-handle choose its core interval.
The union of these arcs and core intervals is a graph $G$ with one essential vertex and one independent loop for each $1$-handle.
Hence
\[
G\cong\bigvee_{j=1}^{r}S^1.
\]

The given $0$- and $1$-handle decomposition exhibits the surface as a regular neighborhood of this core graph $G$.
A regular neighborhood of a graph deformation retracts onto the graph, by collapsing each rectangular strip transverse to its core and the remaining disk sectors toward the chosen arcs.
Therefore the surface is homotopy equivalent to $\bigvee_{j=1}^{r}S^1$.
:::

<1>4. Consequently every compact connected surface with nonempty boundary is homotopy equivalent to a wedge of circles.
More precisely,
\[
\Sigma_{g,b}\simeq\bigvee^{\,2g+b-1}S^1
\qquad(b\ge1)
\]
and
\[
N_{k,b}\simeq\bigvee^{\,k+b-1}S^1
\qquad(b\ge1).
\]
::: {.proof}
Apply <1>3 to the handle decompositions in <1>2. When $g=0$ and $b=1$, the number $2g+b-1$ is $0$; this is the disk, which is contractible, so the empty wedge is interpreted as a point.
This proves part (b) and gives the counts required in part (c).
:::

<1>5. The same counts are forced by Euler characteristic.
::: {.proof}
The classification gives
\[
\chi(\Sigma_{g,b})=2-2g-b,
\qquad
\chi(N_{k,b})=2-k-b.
\]
A wedge of $r$ circles has Euler characteristic
\[
1-r.
\]
Thus any wedge homotopy equivalent to $\Sigma_{g,b}$ must have
\[
r=1-\chi(\Sigma_{g,b})=2g+b-1,
\]
and any wedge homotopy equivalent to $N_{k,b}$ must have
\[
r=1-\chi(N_{k,b})=k+b-1.
\]
This independently verifies the numbers in <1>4.
:::
:::
