::: {.problem}
Compute the difference
\[
\int_{|z|=3}\frac{e^{\pi/z}}{z^2+4}\,dz-
\int_{|z|=1}\frac{e^{\pi/z}}{z^2+4}\,dz,
\]
where both integrals are taken counterclockwise.
:::
