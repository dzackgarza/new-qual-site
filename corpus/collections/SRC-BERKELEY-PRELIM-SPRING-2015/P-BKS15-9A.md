::: {.problem}
Find, with proof, a product of cyclic groups isomorphic to
$$
(\ZZ_{12}\times\ZZ_{12})/\langle(2,6)\rangle.
$$
Here $\ZZ_n$ means $\ZZ/n\ZZ$.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

The required group is the cokernel of the homomorphism $A:\ZZ^3\to\ZZ^2$ whose matrix is
$$
A
=
\begin{pmatrix}
12&0&2\\
0&12&6
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

::: pf-proof

The group $\ZZ_{12}\times\ZZ_{12}$ has the presentation
$$
\ZZ^2/
\left\langle
(12,0),(0,12)
\right\rangle.
$$
Quotienting further by the class of $(2,6)$ adds the relation $(2,6)$. Thus the full relation subgroup is generated by
$$
(12,0),\qquad (0,12),\qquad (2,6),
$$
which are exactly the three columns of $A$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

Integer elementary row and column operations reduce $A$ to
$$
\begin{pmatrix}
2&0&0\\
0&12&0
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

::: pf-proof

Starting from $A$, swap the first and third columns:
$$
\begin{pmatrix}
12&0&2\\
0&12&6
\end{pmatrix}
\longmapsto
\begin{pmatrix}
2&0&12\\
6&12&0
\end{pmatrix}.
$$
Then replace row $2$ by row $2-3$ times row $1$:
$$
\begin{pmatrix}
2&0&12\\
6&12&0
\end{pmatrix}
\longmapsto
\begin{pmatrix}
2&0&12\\
0&12&-36
\end{pmatrix}.
$$
Next replace column $3$ by column $3-6$ times column $1$:
$$
\begin{pmatrix}
2&0&12\\
0&12&-36
\end{pmatrix}
\longmapsto
\begin{pmatrix}
2&0&0\\
0&12&-36
\end{pmatrix}.
$$
Finally replace column $3$ by column $3+3$ times column $2$:
$$
\begin{pmatrix}
2&0&0\\
0&12&-36
\end{pmatrix}
\longmapsto
\begin{pmatrix}
2&0&0\\
0&12&0
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Column operations multiply $A$ on the right by an element of $\GL_3(\ZZ)$, so they do not change the subgroup generated by the columns. Row operations multiply on the left by an element of $\GL_2(\ZZ)$, hence apply an automorphism of the ambient group $\ZZ^2$ and therefore preserve the cokernel up to isomorphism.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

The cokernel of the reduced matrix is
$$
\ZZ_2\times\ZZ_{12}.
$$

::: pf-proof

The reduced relation subgroup is generated by $(2,0)$ and $(0,12)$. Therefore
$$
\ZZ^2/
\left\langle
(2,0),(0,12)
\right\rangle
\cong
\ZZ/2\ZZ\times\ZZ/12\ZZ
=
\ZZ_2\times\ZZ_{12}.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s4}

Hence
$$
\boxed{
(\ZZ_{12}\times\ZZ_{12})/\langle(2,6)\rangle
\cong
\ZZ_2\times\ZZ_{12}
}.
$$

::: pf-proof

Combine steps [](#s1){.pf-ref}, [](#s2){.pf-ref} and [](#s3){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s4){.pf-ref} gives the requested product of cyclic groups.
