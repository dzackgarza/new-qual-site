::: {.problem}
Let $(X,d)$ be a compact metric space.
Suppose that $f_n \colon X \to [0,\infty)$ is a sequence of continuous functions with $f_n(x) \geq f_{n+1}(x)$ for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $x \in X$, and such that $f_n \to 0$ pointwise on $X$.
Prove that $\{f_n\}_{n=1}^\infty$ converges uniformly on $X$.
:::
::: {.solution}
<1>1. For every $\varepsilon>0$ and $n\geq1$, the set $U_n:=\{x\in X:f_n(x)<\varepsilon\}$ is open in $X$.

::: {.proof}
Continuity of $f_n$ gives $U_n=f_n^{-1}(( -\infty,\varepsilon))$, which is open in $X$.
:::

<1>2. For every $\varepsilon>0$, the open sets $U_n$ are increasing and cover $X$.

::: {.proof}
The inequality $f_{n+1}(x)\leq f_n(x)$ gives $U_n\subseteq U_{n+1}$. For each $x\in X$, pointwise convergence $f_n(x)\to0$ gives an index $n$ with $f_n(x)<\varepsilon$. Hence $X=\bigcup_{n\geq1}U_n$.
:::

<1>3. For every $\varepsilon>0$, there is $N\geq1$ such that $U_N=X$.

::: {.proof}
By steps <1>1 and <1>2, the $U_n$ form an increasing open cover of compact $X$. Choose a finite subcover $U_{n_1},\ldots,U_{n_k}$ and take $N=\max_i n_i$. Then $X=\bigcup_iU_{n_i}\subseteq U_N\subseteq X$. If $X$ is empty, any $N$ works.
:::

<1>4. The sequence $f_n$ converges uniformly to zero on $X$.

::: {.proof}
Given $\varepsilon>0$, choose $N$ by step <1>3. For every $n\geq N$ and $x\in X$, monotonicity and nonnegativity give
\[
|f_n(x)-0|=f_n(x)\leq f_N(x)<\varepsilon,
\]
because $x\in U_N=X$. This is the uniform convergence criterion.
:::

<1>5. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>4 proves the asserted uniform convergence.
:::
:::
