::: {.problem}
Let $(X,d)$ be a compact metric space.
Suppose that $f_n \colon X \to [0,\infty)$ is a sequence of continuous functions with $f_n(x) \geq f_{n+1}(x)$ for all $n \in \NN$ and $x \in X$, and such that $f_n \to 0$ pointwise on $X$.
Prove that $\{f_n\}_{n=1}^\infty$ converges uniformly on $X$.
:::

::: {.solution}

Fix $\varepsilon>0$ and, for $n\ge1$, let $U_n\coloneqq\{x\in X:f_n(x)<\varepsilon\}$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

Each $U_n$ is open in $X$.

::: pf-proof

Continuity of $f_n$ gives that $U_n=f_n^{-1}((-\infty,\varepsilon))$ is open in $X$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

The sets $U_n$ are increasing and cover $X$.

::: pf-proof

The inequality $f_{n+1}(x)\leq f_n(x)$ gives $U_n\subseteq U_{n+1}$. For each $x\in X$, pointwise convergence $f_n(x)\to0$ gives an index $n$ with $f_n(x)<\varepsilon$. Hence $X=\bigcup_{n\geq1}U_n$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

There is $N\geq1$ such that $U_N=X$.

::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1){.pf-ref} and step [](#s2){.pf-ref}, the $U_n$ form an increasing open cover of compact $X$. A finite subcover $U_{n_1},\ldots,U_{n_k}$ exists; with $N=\max_i n_i$, we get $X=\bigcup_iU_{n_i}\subseteq U_N\subseteq X$. If $X$ is empty, any $N$ works.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

$f_n$ converges uniformly to zero on $X$.

::: pf-proof

Take $N$ from step [](#s3){.pf-ref}. For every $n\geq N$ and $x\in X$, monotonicity and nonnegativity give
$$
\abs{f_n(x)-0}=f_n(x)\leq f_N(x)<\varepsilon,
$$
because $x\in U_N=X$. Since $\varepsilon>0$ was arbitrary, this is uniform convergence.

:::
