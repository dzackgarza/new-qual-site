::: {.problem}
Let $U$ be the upper half plane $\{z\in\mathbb{C}\mid\operatorname{Im}(z)>0\}$ with the slit $\{iy\mid 0<y\le 1\}$ removed.
Find a conformal equivalence from $U$ to $\mathbb{D}$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let
\[
U=\{\operatorname{Im}z>0\}\setminus\{iy:0<y\le1\}.
\]
The squaring map is injective on the upper half-plane and maps it onto
$\mathbb C\setminus[0,\infty)$. The removed slit is mapped to $[-1,0)$.
Therefore
\[
z\longmapsto z^2
\]
maps $U$ biholomorphically onto $\mathbb C\setminus[-1,\infty)$, and hence
\[
z\longmapsto z^2+1
\]
maps $U$ biholomorphically onto $\mathbb C\setminus[0,\infty)$.

On $\mathbb C\setminus[0,\infty)$ choose the square-root branch with
$0<\arg w<2\pi$. Then
\[
S(w)=\sqrt w
\]
has argument in $(0,\pi)$, so it maps this slit plane biholomorphically onto
the upper half-plane $\mathbb H$.

Finally the Cayley map
\[
C(\zeta)=\frac{\zeta-i}{\zeta+i}
\]
maps $\mathbb H$ biholomorphically onto $\mathbb D$. Thus one explicit
conformal equivalence is
\[
\boxed{\displaystyle
\Phi(z)=\frac{\sqrt{z^2+1}-i}{\sqrt{z^2+1}+i},}
\]
where the square root is the branch just specified.
:::
