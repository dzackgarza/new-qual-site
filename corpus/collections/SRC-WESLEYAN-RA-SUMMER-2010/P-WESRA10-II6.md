::: {.problem}
Let $\mathcal H=L^2([0,1],m)$ and let $E\subset[0,1]$ be Lebesgue measurable.
Define
\[
H_E=\{f\in\mathcal H:f=f\chi_E\}.
\]

(a) Prove $H_E$ is a closed subspace of $\mathcal H$.

(b) Identify $H_E^\perp$.

(c) Let $P(f)=f\chi_E$.
Calculate $\|P\|$.
:::
