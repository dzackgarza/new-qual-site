(a) Show that if $\mu(X)<\infty$, then
\[
L^4(X,\mathcal B,\mu)\subseteq L^3(X,\mathcal B,\mu).
\]

(b) Give a measure space and a function $f$ such that $f\in L^4$ but $f\notin L^3$.
