::: problem
Let $A$ be a path-connected subset of a path-connected space $X$ and let $i:A\subset X$ be the inclusion map.
Let $p:\widetilde{X}\to X$ be the universal covering map.
Show that $p^{-1}(A)$ is path-connected if and only if $i_{\#}:\pi_1(A,*)\to\pi_1(X,*)$ is surjective.
:::

::: {.solution}
Fix a basepoint $a_0\in A$ and a lift $\widetilde a_0\in p^{-1}(a_0)$.

<1>1. Two loops at $a_0$ represent the same element of $\pi_1(X,a_0)$ if and only if their lifts beginning at $\widetilde a_0$ have the same endpoint.
::: {.proof}
Let $\alpha$ and $\beta$ be loops at $a_0$, with lifts $\widetilde\alpha$ and $\widetilde\beta$ beginning at $\widetilde a_0$.

If $[\alpha]=[\beta]$, the homotopy lifting property for coverings shows that the two lifts have the same endpoint.

Conversely, suppose their endpoints agree.
Then the lift of the loop
\[
\alpha*\overline\beta
\]
starting at $\widetilde a_0$ is a loop in $\widetilde X$.
Hence
\[
[\alpha*\overline\beta]
\in
p_*\pi_1(\widetilde X,\widetilde a_0).
\]
Because $p$ is the universal covering, $\widetilde X$ is simply connected, so this subgroup is trivial.
Thus $[\alpha]=[\beta]$.
:::

<1>2. Assume that
\[
i_*:\pi_1(A,a_0)\longrightarrow\pi_1(X,a_0)
\]
is surjective.
Every point of the fiber $p^{-1}(a_0)$ can be joined to $\widetilde a_0$ by a path in $p^{-1}(A)$.
::: {.proof}
Let $\widetilde b\in p^{-1}(a_0)$.
Since $\widetilde X$ is path-connected, choose a path $\widetilde\gamma$ from $\widetilde a_0$ to $\widetilde b$.
Its projection
\[
\gamma=p\circ\widetilde\gamma
\]
is a loop at $a_0$.

Surjectivity gives a loop $\lambda$ in $A$ based at $a_0$ such that
\[
i_*[\lambda]=[\gamma].
\]
Lift $\lambda$ from $\widetilde a_0$.
By <1>1 its endpoint is the same as the endpoint of $\widetilde\gamma$, namely $\widetilde b$.
Since $\lambda$ lies in $A$, its lift lies in $p^{-1}(A)$.
:::

<1>3. If $i_*$ is surjective, then $p^{-1}(A)$ is path-connected.
::: {.proof}
Let $\widetilde x\in p^{-1}(A)$ and put $x=p(\widetilde x)\in A$.
Because $A$ is path-connected, choose a path $\eta$ in $A$ from $x$ to $a_0$.
Lift $\eta$ starting at $\widetilde x$.
Its endpoint $\widetilde b$ lies in $p^{-1}(a_0)$, and the entire lifted path lies in $p^{-1}(A)$.

By <1>2, $\widetilde b$ can be joined to $\widetilde a_0$ inside $p^{-1}(A)$.
Concatenating the two paths joins $\widetilde x$ to $\widetilde a_0$ inside $p^{-1}(A)$.
Thus every point lies in the same path component as $\widetilde a_0$.
:::

<1>4. If $p^{-1}(A)$ is path-connected, then $i_*$ is surjective.
::: {.proof}
Let $[\gamma]\in\pi_1(X,a_0)$ and lift $\gamma$ from $\widetilde a_0$.
Write $\widetilde b$ for its endpoint.
Since $\gamma$ is a loop,
\[
\widetilde b\in p^{-1}(a_0)\subseteq p^{-1}(A).
\]

By path-connectedness of $p^{-1}(A)$, choose a path $\widetilde\lambda$ in $p^{-1}(A)$ from $\widetilde a_0$ to $\widetilde b$.
Then
\[
\lambda=p\circ\widetilde\lambda
\]
is a loop in $A$ based at $a_0$.
The lifts of $\gamma$ and $i\circ\lambda$ beginning at $\widetilde a_0$ have the same endpoint $\widetilde b$.
By <1>1,
\[
[\gamma]=i_*[\lambda].
\]
Thus every element of $\pi_1(X,a_0)$ lies in the image of $i_*$.
:::

<1>5. Therefore $p^{-1}(A)$ is path-connected if and only if $i_*$ is surjective.
::: {.proof}
The forward implication is <1>4 and the reverse implication is <1>3.
:::
