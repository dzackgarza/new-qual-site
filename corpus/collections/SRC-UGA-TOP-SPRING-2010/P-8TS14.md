::: problem
Prove or disprove: The fundamental group of a Klein bottle is an abelian group.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. The claim is **false**: the fundamental group of the Klein bottle $K$ is non-abelian.
::: {.proof}
statement of disproof.
:::

<1>2. Presentation of the fundamental group $\pi_1(K)$:
<2>1. The standard CW decomposition of the Klein bottle $K$ has one 0-cell, two 1-cells $a$ and $b$, and one 2-cell attached along the boundary loop $a b a^{-1} b$.
::: {.proof}
standard polygonal planar model for the Klein bottle.
:::
<2>2. By the Seifert–van Kampen Theorem / cellular presentation of $\pi_1$:
\[
\pi_1(K) \cong \langle a, b \mid a b a^{-1} b = 1 \rangle \cong \langle a, b \mid a b a^{-1} = b^{-1} \rangle.
\]
::: {.proof}
cellular fundamental group.
:::

<1>3. Prove that $a$ and $b$ do not commute:
<2>1. In $\pi_1(K)$, $a b = b^{-1} a$.
::: {.proof}
multiplying the relation $a b a^{-1} = b^{-1}$ on the right by $a$.
:::
<2>2. If $\pi_1(K)$ were abelian, then $a b = b a$.
Equating this with <2>1 yields $b a = b^{-1} a \implies b = b^{-1} \implies b^2 = 1$.
::: {.proof}
group cancellation.
:::
<2>3. Consider the faithful representation of $\pi_1(K)$ in the isometry group $\operatorname{Isom}(\mathbb{R}^2)$ by deck transformations on the universal covering space $\mathbb{R}^2 \to K$:
\[
A(x, y) = (x + 1, -y) \quad (\text{glide reflection}), \qquad B(x, y) = (x, y + 1) \quad (\text{vertical translation}).
\]
::: {.proof}
universal cover of the Klein bottle is $\mathbb{R}^2$.
:::
<2>4. Compute the compositions at the origin $(0, 0)$:
\[
(A \circ B)(0, 0) = A(0, 1) = (1, -1),
\]
\[
(B \circ A)(0, 0) = B(1, 0) = (1, 1).
\]
Since $(1, -1) \neq (1, 1)$, $A \circ B \neq B \circ A$.
::: {.proof}
evaluation of affine transformations.
:::
<2>5. Thus $a b \neq b a$ in $\pi_1(K)$, so $\pi_1(K)$ is not abelian.
::: {.proof}
existence of non-commuting elements.
:::

<1>4. Conclusion:
The fundamental group of the Klein bottle is non-abelian (disproved). Q.E.D.
::: {.proof}
<1>1 through <1>3.
:::
:::
