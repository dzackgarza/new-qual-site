::: problem
Let $B^3$ be a solid ball of radius $2$ centered at the origin in $\mathbb{R}^3$.
Let $S^1$ be the unit circle in the $xy$-plane.
Compute the homology groups of $B^3\setminus S^1$.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. The inclusion
\[
B^3\setminus S^1
\hookrightarrow
\RR^3\setminus S^1
\]
is a homotopy equivalence.
::: {.proof}
Write $B^3=\{x\in\RR^3:\lVert x\rVert\le2\}$.
Radially push every point outside $B^3$ onto the sphere of radius $2$, while fixing $B^3$ pointwise.
Explicitly, for $x\in\RR^3\setminus S^1$, define
\[
H_t(x)=
\begin{cases}
x,&\lVert x\rVert\le2,\\[2mm]
\left((1-t)+{2t\over\lVert x\rVert}\right)x,&\lVert x\rVert\ge2.
\end{cases}
\]
The two formulas agree when $\lVert x\rVert=2$.
If $\lVert x\rVert\ge2$, then
\[
\lVert H_t(x)\rVert=(1-t)\lVert x\rVert+2t\ge2,
\]
so this homotopy never meets the deleted unit circle.
At $t=1$ its image is contained in $B^3\setminus S^1$.
Thus $H$ is a strong deformation retraction of $\RR^3\setminus S^1$ onto $B^3\setminus S^1$.
:::

<1>2. Regard $\RR^3\setminus S^1$ as a complement in $S^3$.
::: {.proof}
Let $\infty$ be the point at infinity in the one-point compactification
\[
S^3=\RR^3\cup\{\infty\},
\]
and put
\[
K=S^1\sqcup\{\infty\}.
\]
Then
\[
\RR^3\setminus S^1=S^3\setminus K.
\]
Since $K$ is compact and locally contractible, Alexander duality gives
\[
\widetilde H_k(\RR^3\setminus S^1;\ZZ)
\cong
\widetilde H^{\,2-k}(K;\ZZ).
\]
:::

<1>3. The reduced cohomology of $K=S^1\sqcup\{\infty\}$ is
\[
\widetilde H^j(K;\ZZ)
\cong
\begin{cases}
\ZZ,&j=0,\\
\ZZ,&j=1,\\
0,&j\ge2.
\end{cases}
\]
::: {.proof}
The space $K$ has two connected components, so
\[
\widetilde H^0(K;\ZZ)\cong\ZZ.
\]
Its only positive-degree cohomology comes from the circle, giving
\[
H^1(K;\ZZ)\cong H^1(S^1;\ZZ)\cong\ZZ.
\]
All higher cohomology groups vanish.
:::

<1>4. Therefore
\[
H_k(B^3\setminus S^1;\ZZ)
\cong
\begin{cases}
\ZZ,&k=0,\\
\ZZ,&k=1,\\
\ZZ,&k=2,\\
0,&k\ge3.
\end{cases}
\]
::: {.proof}
By <1>2 and <1>3,
\[
\widetilde H_0(\RR^3\setminus S^1)=0,
\qquad
H_1(\RR^3\setminus S^1)\cong\ZZ,
\qquad
H_2(\RR^3\setminus S^1)\cong\ZZ,
\]
and all higher groups vanish.
Hence $H_0\cong\ZZ$.
Transport these groups across the homotopy equivalence from <1>1.
:::
:::
