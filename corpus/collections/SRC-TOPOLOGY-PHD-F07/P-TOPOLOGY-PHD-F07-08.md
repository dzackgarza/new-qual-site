::: {.problem}
Let $X=\prod_{\mu\in M}X_\mu$ and $Y=\prod_{\mu\in M}Y_\mu$ be the Cartesian products of the topological spaces $(X_\mu)_{\mu\in M}$ and $(Y_\mu)_{\mu\in M}$ and let $X$ and $Y$ have the product topologies, respectively.
Prove that if for each $\mu\in M$ the maps $f_\mu:X_\mu\to Y_\mu$ are continuous then $f:X\to Y$ defined by $f(x)_\mu=f_\mu(x_\mu)$ is continuous.
:::

::: {.solution}
For each $\mu\in M$, write
\[
p_\mu:X\to X_\mu,
\qquad
q_\mu:Y\to Y_\mu
\]
for the coordinate projections.

<1>1. For every $\mu\in M$ and every open set $V\subseteq Y_\mu$,
\[
f^{-1}\bigl(q_\mu^{-1}(V)\bigr)
=p_\mu^{-1}\bigl(f_\mu^{-1}(V)\bigr).
\]
::: {.proof}
For $x\in X$,
\[
x\in f^{-1}\bigl(q_\mu^{-1}(V)\bigr)
\]
if and only if
\[
q_\mu(f(x))\in V.
\]
By the definition of $f$,
\[
q_\mu(f(x))=f(x)_\mu=f_\mu(x_\mu)=f_\mu(p_\mu(x)).
\]
Thus the preceding condition is equivalent to
\[
p_\mu(x)\in f_\mu^{-1}(V),
\]
which is exactly
\[
x\in p_\mu^{-1}\bigl(f_\mu^{-1}(V)\bigr).
\]
:::

<1>2. The inverse image under $f$ of every subbasic open set of $Y$ is open in $X$.
::: {.proof}
The product topology on $Y$ has subbasis
\[
\mathcal S
=\{q_\mu^{-1}(V):\mu\in M,\ V\subseteq Y_\mu\text{ open}\}.
\]
Take $q_\mu^{-1}(V)\in\mathcal S$.
Because $f_\mu$ is continuous,
\[
f_\mu^{-1}(V)
\]
is open in $X_\mu$.
Because $X$ has the product topology, the coordinate projection $p_\mu$ is continuous, so
\[
p_\mu^{-1}\bigl(f_\mu^{-1}(V)\bigr)
\]
is open in $X$.
By <1>1 this set is precisely
\[
f^{-1}\bigl(q_\mu^{-1}(V)\bigr).
\]
:::

<1>3. Therefore $f:X\to Y$ is continuous.
::: {.proof}
Every open set in the topology generated by the subbasis $\mathcal S$ is a union of finite intersections of members of $\mathcal S$.
Inverse images commute with arbitrary unions and finite intersections.
By <1>2, the inverse image of each member of $\mathcal S$ is open in $X$, so the inverse image of every open subset of $Y$ is open in $X$.
Hence $f$ is continuous.
:::
:::
