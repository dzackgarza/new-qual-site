::: {.problem}
Let $D$ be the open unit disk in the complex plane that is $D:=\{z\mid |z|<1\}$.
Let $\sim$ be an equivalence relation on $D$ defined by $z_1\sim z_2$ if $|z_1|=|z_2|$.
Is the quotient space $D/\sim$ Hausdorff?
Prove or disprove.
:::
