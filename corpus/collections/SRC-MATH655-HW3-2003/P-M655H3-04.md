::: {.problem}
Show that if
$$
P(z)=z^n+a_{n-1}z^{n-1}+\cdots+a_0
$$
is a polynomial of degree $n\ge1$, then $\abs{P(z)}\to\infty$ as $\abs{z}\to\infty$.
In fact, show that if
$$
\abs{z}\ge\max\{1,2n\abs{a_{n-1}},\ldots,2n\abs{a_0}\},
$$
then
$$
\abs{P(z)}\ge\frac{\abs{z}^n}{2}.
$$
:::

::: {.solution}

Let
$$
M=
\max\{
1,
2n\abs{a_{n-1}},
\ldots,
2n\abs{a_0}
\}.
$$

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

If $\abs{z}\geq M$, then for every $k=1,\ldots,n$,
$$
\frac{\abs{a_{n-k}}}{\abs{z}^k}
\leq
\frac1{2n}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Since $\abs{z}\geq M\geq1$,
$$
\abs{z}^k\geq\abs{z}.
$$
Also,
$$
\abs{z}
\geq
M
\geq
2n\abs{a_{n-k}}.
$$
Therefore
$$
\frac{\abs{a_{n-k}}}{\abs{z}^k}
\leq
\frac{\abs{a_{n-k}}}{\abs{z}}
\leq
\frac1{2n}.
$$
If $a_{n-k}=0$, the same conclusion is immediate.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

If $\abs{z}\geq M$, then
$$
\abs{
\frac{a_{n-1}}z
+
\frac{a_{n-2}}{z^2}
+
\cdots
+
\frac{a_0}{z^n}
}
\leq
\frac12.
$$


::: pf-proof

By the triangle inequality and step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref},
$$
\begin{aligned}
\abs{
\sum_{k=1}^{n}
\frac{a_{n-k}}{z^k}
}
&\leq
\sum_{k=1}^{n}
\frac{\abs{a_{n-k}}}{\abs{z}^k}\\
&\leq
n\cdot\frac1{2n}\\
&=
\frac12.
\end{aligned}
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

If $\abs{z}\geq M$, then
$$
\boxed{
\abs{P(z)}
\geq
\frac{\abs{z}^n}{2}.
}
$$


::: pf-proof

Factor
$$
P(z)
=
z^n
\left(
1
+
\frac{a_{n-1}}z
+
\cdots
+
\frac{a_0}{z^n}
\right).
$$
Hence by the reverse triangle inequality and step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref},
$$
\begin{aligned}
\abs{P(z)}
&=
\abs{z}^n
\abs{
1
+
\frac{a_{n-1}}z
+
\cdots
+
\frac{a_0}{z^n}
}\\
&\geq
\abs{z}^n
\left(
1
-
\abs{
\frac{a_{n-1}}z
+
\cdots
+
\frac{a_0}{z^n}
}
\right)\\
&\geq
\frac{\abs{z}^n}{2}.
\end{aligned}
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

One has
$$
\abs{P(z)}\longrightarrow\infty
$$
as $\abs{z}\to\infty$.


::: pf-proof

For all sufficiently large $\abs{z}$, step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} gives
$$
\abs{P(z)}
\geq
\frac{\abs{z}^n}{2}.
$$
Since $n\geq1$,
$$
\frac{\abs{z}^n}{2}\longrightarrow\infty
$$
as $\abs{z}\to\infty$.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} proves the quantitative estimate, and step [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} gives the stated
growth at infinity.

:::
