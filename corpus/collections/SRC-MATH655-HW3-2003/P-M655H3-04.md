::: {.problem}
Show that if
\[
P(z)=z^n+a_{n-1}z^{n-1}+\cdots+a_0
\]
is a polynomial of degree $n\ge1$, then $|P(z)|\to\infty$ as $|z|\to\infty$. In fact, show that if
\[
|z|\ge\max\{1,2n|a_{n-1}|,\ldots,2n|a_0|\},
\]
then
\[
|P(z)|\ge\frac{|z|^n}{2}.
\]
:::
