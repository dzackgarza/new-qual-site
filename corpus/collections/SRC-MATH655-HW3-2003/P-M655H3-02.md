::: {.problem}
Let f be analytic on C and real valued on $| z | = 1$ . Show that $f$ is constant.
:::

::: {.solution}
Write
$$
f=u+iv.
$$

<1>1. The function $v$ is harmonic on $\CC$ and satisfies
$$
v(z)=0
$$
for every $|z|=1$.

::: {.proof}
Because $f$ is entire, its real and imaginary parts are harmonic. The
hypothesis that $f$ is real-valued on the unit circle means exactly that its
imaginary part vanishes there.
:::

<1>2. One has
$$
v\equiv0
$$
on the closed unit disk.

::: {.proof}
Apply the maximum principle for harmonic functions to $v$ on the unit disk.
Since $v=0$ on the boundary,
$$
v\leq0
$$
throughout the disk.

Apply the same principle to $-v$. Since $-v=0$ on the boundary,
$$
-v\leq0,
$$
so
$$
v\geq0.
$$
Hence $v=0$ throughout the disk.
:::

<1>3. The function $f$ is constant on the open unit disk.

::: {.proof}
By step <1>2, $f$ is real-valued on the connected open unit disk. The result
of Problem 1 therefore applies: a holomorphic real-valued function on a
connected open set is constant.
:::

<1>4. The function $f$ is constant on all of $\CC$.

::: {.proof}
Let $c$ be the constant value of $f$ on the unit disk. Then the entire
function
$$
f-c
$$
vanishes on the open unit disk, hence on a set with an accumulation point in
$\CC$. By the identity theorem,
$$
f-c\equiv0
$$
on all of $\CC$.
:::

<1>5. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>4 is the required conclusion.
:::
