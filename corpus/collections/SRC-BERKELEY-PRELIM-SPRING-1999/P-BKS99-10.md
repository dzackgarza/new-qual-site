::: {.problem}
Let $f$ be twice differentiable on $[a,b]$ and assume
\[
f''(x)>0\qquad(x\in[a,b]).
\]
For $c\in[a,b]$, consider the region between the graph $y=f(x)$, the tangent line to the graph at $(c,f(c))$, and the vertical lines $x=a$ and $x=b$.

Determine all $c\in[a,b]$ for which the area of this region is minimal.
:::
