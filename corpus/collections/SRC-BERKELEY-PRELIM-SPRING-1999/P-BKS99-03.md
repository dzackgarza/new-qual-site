::: {.problem}
Let $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ be twice differentiable and suppose
\[
f(0)=0,\qquad f'(0)>0,
\]
and
\[
f''(x)\ge f(x)\qquad(x\ge0).
\]
Prove that $f(x)>0$ for every $x>0$.
:::
