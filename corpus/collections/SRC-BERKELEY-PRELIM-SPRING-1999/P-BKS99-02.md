::: {.problem}
Suppose $f_n:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ converges uniformly on $\mathbb R$ to $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$, and that for every positive integer $n$ the limit
\[
c_n=\lim_{x\to\infty}f_n(x)
\]
exists.
Prove that both
\[
\lim_{n\to\infty}c_n
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\lim_{x\to\infty}f(x)
\]
exist and are equal.
:::
