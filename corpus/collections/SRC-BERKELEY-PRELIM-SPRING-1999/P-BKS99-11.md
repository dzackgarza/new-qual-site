::: {.problem}
Let $n$ be a positive integer, let $\alpha\in\mathbb R$, and let $\varepsilon>0$.
Prove that there exists a real $C^\infty$ function $f$ such that

1. for every $x\in\mathbb R$ and every $k=0,1,\ldots,n-1$,
   \[
   |f^{(k)}(x)|\le\varepsilon;
   \]
2. for $k=0,1,\ldots,n-1$,
   \[
   f^{(k)}(0)=0;
   \]
3. \[
   f^{(n)}(0)=\alpha.
   \]
:::
