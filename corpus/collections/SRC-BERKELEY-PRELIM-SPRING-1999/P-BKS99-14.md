::: {.problem}
Let $A=(a_{ij})$ be an $n\times n$ complex matrix such that
\[
a_{ij}\ne0\quad\text{if }i=j+1,
\]
while
\[
a_{ij}=0\quad\text{if }i\ge j+2.
\]
Prove that $A$ cannot have more than one Jordan block for any eigenvalue.
:::
