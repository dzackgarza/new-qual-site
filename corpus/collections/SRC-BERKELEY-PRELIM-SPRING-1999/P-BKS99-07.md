::: {.problem}
Let $T$ be a linear operator on a seven-dimensional vector space whose minimal polynomial is $x^2$.
What are the possible values of
$$
\dim\ker T?
$$
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

If $r:=\operatorname{rank}T$, then
$$
1\leq r\leq3.
$$


::: pf-proof

Because the minimal polynomial is $x^2$, one has $T^2=0$ but $T\neq0$. Thus
$$
\operatorname{im}T\subseteq\ker T
$$
and $r\geq1$. By rank-nullity,
$$
\dim\ker T=7-r.
$$
Since $\operatorname{im}T\subseteq\ker T$,
$$
r\leq7-r,
$$
so $r\leq3$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

Therefore
$$
\dim\ker T\in\{4,5,6\}.
$$


::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} and rank-nullity,
$$
\dim\ker T=7-r
$$
with $r\in\{1,2,3\}$, giving precisely the three listed values.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

Each of the values $4,5,6$ occurs.


::: pf-proof

For $r\in\{1,2,3\}$, choose a basis
$$
e_1,\ldots,e_r,
f_1,\ldots,f_r,
g_1,\ldots,g_{7-2r}
$$
and define
$$
T(f_i)=e_i,
\qquad
T(e_i)=0,
\qquad
T(g_j)=0.
$$
Then $T^2=0$ and $T\neq0$, so its minimal polynomial is exactly $x^2$. Its image is spanned by $e_1,\ldots,e_r$, hence $\operatorname{rank}T=r$ and
$$
\dim\ker T=7-r.
$$
Thus the nullities $6,5,4$ occur for $r=1,2,3$, respectively.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Steps [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} and [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} show that the possible values are exactly
$$
\boxed{4,5,6}.
$$

:::
