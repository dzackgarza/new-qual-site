::: {.problem}
Let $f:[0,1]\to\mathbb C$ be continuous and define
\[
g(z)=\int_0^1 f(t)e^{tz}\,dt,
\qquad z\in\mathbb C.
\]
Prove that $g$ is entire.
:::
