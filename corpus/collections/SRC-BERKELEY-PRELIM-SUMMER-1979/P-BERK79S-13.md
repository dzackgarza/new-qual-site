::: {.problem}
Let $F$ be a finite field with $q$ elements and let $V$ be an $n$-dimensional vector space over $F$.

1. Determine $|V|$.

2. Determine the order of $GL_n(F)$.

3. Determine the order of $SL_n(F)$.
:::
