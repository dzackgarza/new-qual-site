::: {.problem}
Let $U\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ be open.
Suppose $h:U\to\mathbb R^n$ is a uniformly continuous homeomorphism from $U$ onto $\mathbb R^n$.
Prove that
\[
U=\mathbb R^n.
\]
:::
