::: {.problem}
Let $F\subseteq K$ be fields and let $p,q\in F[x]$.
Prove that the greatest common divisor of $p$ and $q$ computed in $F[x]$ is the same, up to multiplication by a unit, as their greatest common divisor computed in $K[x]$.
:::
