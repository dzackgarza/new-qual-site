::: {.problem}
For each $n\ge1$, let $g_n$ be an entire function whose zeros are all real.
Suppose
\[
g_n\to g
\]
uniformly on compact subsets of $\mathbb C$, where $g$ is not identically zero.
Prove that every zero of $g$ is real.
:::
