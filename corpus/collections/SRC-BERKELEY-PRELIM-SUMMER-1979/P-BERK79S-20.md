::: {.problem}
Let $x:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ solve
\[
5x''+10x'+6x=0.
\]
Prove that the function
\[
f(t)=\frac{x(t)^2}{1+x(t)^4}
\]
attains a maximum value on $\mathbb R$.
:::
