::: {.problem}
Let $E$ be a three-dimensional vector space over $\mathbb Q$.
Suppose $T:E\to E$ is linear and
\[
Tx=y,
\qquad
Ty=z,
\qquad
Tz=x+y
\]
for some $x,y,z\in E$ with $x\ne0$.
Prove that $x,y,z$ are linearly independent.
:::
