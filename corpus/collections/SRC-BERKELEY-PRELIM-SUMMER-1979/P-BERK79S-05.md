1. Give an example of a differentiable function $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ whose derivative $f'$ is not continuous.

2. Let $f$ be differentiable and suppose
   \[
   f'(0)<2<f'(1).
   \]
   Prove that $f'(x)=2$ for some $x\in[0,1]$.
