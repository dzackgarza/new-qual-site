::: problem
Let $M, N$ be two closed, oriented, $n$-dimensional manifolds.
Show that the mapping degree of a continuous map $f : M \to N$ must be divisible by $[\pi_1(N), f_*(\pi_1(M))]$, the index of the subgroup $f_*(\pi_1(M))$ in $\pi_1(N)$.
:::
