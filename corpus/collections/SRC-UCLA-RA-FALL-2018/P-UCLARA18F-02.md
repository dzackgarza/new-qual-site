::: {.problem}
Problem 2. Let (X, ρ) be a compact metric space which has at least two points,
and let C(X) be the space of continuous functions X → R with the uniform norm
||f || = supX |f (x)|. Let D be a dense subset of X and for each y ∈ D define
fy ∈ C(X) by

                                  fy (x) = ρ(x, y).
Let A be the subalgebra of C(X) generated by the collection {fy : y ∈ D} (with
pointwise addition and multiplication of functions).
  (a) Prove that A is dense in C(X) under the uniform norm.
  (b) Prove that C(X) is separable.
:::
