::: {.problem}
Problem 12. If $\alpha\in\mathbb C$ satisfies $0<|\alpha|<1$ and if $n\in\mathbb N=\{1,2,3,\ldots\}$, show that the equation
\[
e^z(z-1)^n=\alpha
\]
has exactly $n$ simple roots in the half-plane
\[
\{z:\operatorname{Re}z>0\}.
\]
:::
