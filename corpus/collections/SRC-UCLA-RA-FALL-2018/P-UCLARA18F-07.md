::: {.problem}
Problem 7. Let $f(z)$ be an analytic function on the entire complex plane $\mathbb C$ such that
\[
U(z)=\log|f(z)|
\]
is Lebesgue-area integrable:
\[
\int_{\mathbb C}|U(z)|\,dx\,dy<\infty.
\]
Prove that $f$ is constant.
:::
