::: {.problem}
(a) How many increasing functions are there which map
\[
[n]=\{1,2,\ldots,n\}
\]
to $[m]$?
The functions are not required to be strictly increasing.

(b) Give a closed-form expression for
\[
\sum_{k=0}^{m}\binom{n-k}{m-k}.
\]
:::
