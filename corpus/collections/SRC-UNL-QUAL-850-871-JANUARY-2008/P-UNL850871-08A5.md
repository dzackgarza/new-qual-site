::: {.problem}
Given integers $0<d\le n$ and a prime power $q$, prove that there exists a linear code
\[
C\subseteq\mathbb F_q^n
\]
of minimum distance at least $d$, containing at least
\[
\frac{q^n}{\displaystyle\sum_{i=0}^{d-1}\binom ni(q-1)^i}
\]
codewords.
:::
