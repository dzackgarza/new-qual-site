::: {.problem}
(a) Let $A$ be a subset of a topological space $X$.
Prove that both
\[
\overline A\setminus A
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
A\setminus\operatorname{int}A
\]
have empty interior.

(b) A family $\mathcal A$ of subsets of a topological space $X$ is *locally finite* if for every $x\in X$ there exists a neighborhood $U$ of $x$ such that $U$ meets only finitely many members of $\mathcal A$.
If $\mathcal A$ is locally finite, prove that
\[
\overline{\bigcup_{A\in\mathcal A}A}
=
\bigcup_{A\in\mathcal A}\overline A.
\]
:::
