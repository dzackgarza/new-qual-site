::: {.problem}
Suppose $0\le t<n/2$ and $\mathcal A\subseteq\mathcal P(n)$ is an antichain such that $|A|\le t$ for all $A\in\mathcal A$.
Define
\[
\mathcal A_t
=
\left\{
B\in\binom{[n]}{t}:
\text{there is some }A\in\mathcal A\text{ with }A\subseteq B
\right\}.
\]
Prove that
\[
|\mathcal A|\le |\mathcal A_t|.
\]
:::
