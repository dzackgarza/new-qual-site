::: {.problem}
Let $\{F_p\}$ be a sequence of entire functions such that $F_p(1)=0$, each $F_p$ has no other zeros, and
\[
F_p(z)\longrightarrow1
\]
uniformly on compact subsets of $\mathbb C\setminus\{1\}$.
Let $a_n\ne0$ with $a_n\to\infty$.
Show that there exist indices $p_n$ such that
\[
G(z)=\prod_{n=1}^{\infty}F_{p_n}(z/a_n)
\]
converges absolutely on $\mathbb C$ and defines an entire function whose zeros are exactly the $a_n$.
:::
