::: {.problem}
Let $\mathcal S$ be the normalized univalent maps $f:\Delta\to\mathbb C$ with $f(0)=0$ and $f'(0)=1$.
Show that the maps $f\in\mathcal S$ whose images $f(\Delta)$ are Jordan domains are dense in $\mathcal S$ for uniform convergence on compact subsets of $\Delta$.
:::
