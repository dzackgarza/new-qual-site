::: {.problem}
Let $f:\mathbb R^{n+1}\to\mathbb R^{n+1}$ be continuous, and suppose $f$ does not vanish on the unit sphere $S^n$.
Define
\[
F:S^n\to S^n,
\qquad
F(x)=\frac{f(x)}{|f(x)|}.
\]
Prove that if $F$ has nonzero degree, then $f$ has a zero in the unit ball.
:::
