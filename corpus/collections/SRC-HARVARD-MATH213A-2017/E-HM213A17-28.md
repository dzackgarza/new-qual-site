::: {.problem}
For each integer $n>0$, give an example of an analytic function
\[
f:\Delta^*\to\mathbb C
\]
such that:

1. $f$ is nowhere zero;
2. $f$ has an essential singularity at $z=0$;
3. $\operatorname{Res}(f'/f,0)=n$.
:::
