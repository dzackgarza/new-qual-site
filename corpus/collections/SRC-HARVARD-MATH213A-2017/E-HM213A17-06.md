::: {.problem}
In the notation of the preceding exercise, give an example for which the covering $U_0\to U_1$ is normal (or Galois), i.e. for which
\[
f_*(\pi_1(U_0))\triangleleft \pi_1(U_1).
\]
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. Take
\[
f(z)=z^d
\]
for any integer $d\ge2$.
::: {.proof}
We have
\[
f'(z)=dz^{d-1},
\]
so the only critical point is $0$ and the only critical value is $0$. Hence, in the notation of the preceding exercise,
\[
V_1=\{0\},
\qquad
V_0=f^{-1}(V_1)=\{0\},
\]
and therefore
\[
U_0=U_1=\mathbb C^*.
\]
Thus the covering is
\[
f:\mathbb C^*\longrightarrow\mathbb C^*,
\qquad z\longmapsto z^d.
\]
:::

<1>2. On fundamental groups, $f_*$ is multiplication by $d$.
::: {.proof}
Use the standard generator
\[
\gamma(t)=e^{2\pi i t},
\qquad 0\le t\le1,
\]
of
\[
\pi_1(\mathbb C^*,1)\cong\mathbb Z.
\]
Then
\[
(f\circ\gamma)(t)=e^{2\pi i d t},
\]
which winds $d$ times around the origin. Hence
\[
f_*:\mathbb Z\longrightarrow\mathbb Z,
\qquad n\longmapsto dn,
\]
and so
\[
f_*(\pi_1(U_0))=d\mathbb Z.
\]
:::

<1>3. The covering is normal.
::: {.proof}
The group
\[
\pi_1(U_1)\cong\mathbb Z
\]
is abelian, so every subgroup is normal. In particular,
\[
d\mathbb Z\triangleleft\mathbb Z.
\]
Therefore the covering $z\mapsto z^d$ of $\mathbb C^*$ is normal (Galois). Its deck group is equivalently the cyclic group of $d$th roots of unity, acting by
\[
z\longmapsto\zeta z,
\qquad \zeta^d=1.
\]
:::
:::
