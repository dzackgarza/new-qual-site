::: {.problem}
In the notation of the preceding exercise, give an example for which the covering $U_0\to U_1$ is normal (or Galois), i.e. for which
$$
f_*(\pi_1(U_0))\triangleleft \pi_1(U_1).
$$
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: pf-step

Take
$$
f(z)=z^d
$$
for any integer $d\ge2$.

::: pf-proof

We have
$$
f'(z)=dz^{d-1},
$$
so the only critical point is $0$ and the only critical value is $0$. Hence, in the notation of [[E-HM213A17-05]],
$$
V_1=\{0\},
\qquad
V_0=f^{-1}(V_1)=\{0\},
$$
and therefore
$$
U_0=U_1=\CC^*.
$$
Thus the covering is
$$
f:\CC^*\longrightarrow\CC^*,
\qquad z\longmapsto z^d.
$$

:::

:::

::: pf-step

On fundamental groups, $f_*$ is multiplication by $d$.

::: pf-proof

Use the standard generator
$$
\gamma(t)=e^{2\pi i t},
\qquad 0\le t\le1,
$$
of
$$
\pi_1(\CC^*,1)\cong\ZZ.
$$
Then
$$
(f\circ\gamma)(t)=e^{2\pi i d t},
$$
which winds $d$ times around the origin. Hence
$$
f_*:\ZZ\longrightarrow\ZZ,
\qquad n\longmapsto dn,
$$
and so
$$
f_*(\pi_1(U_0))=d\ZZ.
$$

:::

:::

::: pf-step

The covering is normal.

::: pf-proof

The group
$$
\pi_1(U_1)\cong\ZZ
$$
is abelian, so every subgroup is normal. In particular,
$$
d\ZZ\triangleleft\ZZ.
$$
Therefore the covering $z\mapsto z^d$ of $\CC^*$ is normal (Galois). Its deck group is equivalently the cyclic group of $d$th roots of unity, acting by
$$
z\longmapsto\zeta z,
\qquad \zeta^d=1.
$$

:::
