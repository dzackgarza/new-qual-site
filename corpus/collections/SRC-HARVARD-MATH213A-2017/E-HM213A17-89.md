::: {.problem}
State and prove a necessary and sufficient condition for a meromorphic $1$-form
\[
\omega=\omega(z)\,dz
\]
on $\mathbb C$ to be the logarithmic derivative of a meromorphic function; that is, for
\[
\omega=d\log f=\frac{f'(z)}{f(z)}\,dz
\]
for some meromorphic $f$.
:::
