::: {.problem}
Let $E\subset(-1,1)$ be a compact set of zero linear measure, and let
\[
f:\Delta\setminus E\to\mathbb C
\]
be a bounded analytic function.
Show that $f$ extends to an analytic function on the whole unit disk $\Delta$.
:::
