::: {.problem}
Let
\[
E(a,b)=\left\{x+iy:\frac{x^2}{a^2}+\frac{y^2}{b^2}\le1\right\},
\qquad a>b>0.
\]
Find an explicit conformal homeomorphism
\[
\mathbb C\setminus\Delta\longrightarrow\mathbb C\setminus E(a,b).
\]
:::
