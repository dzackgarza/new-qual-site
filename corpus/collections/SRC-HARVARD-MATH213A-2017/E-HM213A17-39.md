::: {.problem}
Find a bounded harmonic function $u:\Delta\to\mathbb R$ such that
\[
\lim_{r\to1}u(re^{i\theta})=1
\quad\text{for }0<\theta<\pi,
\]
and
\[
\lim_{r\to1}u(re^{i\theta})=-1
\quad\text{for }\pi<\theta<2\pi.
\]
:::
