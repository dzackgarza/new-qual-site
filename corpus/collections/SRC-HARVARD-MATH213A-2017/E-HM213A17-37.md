::: {.problem}
Let $f$ be analytic in a region $U$, and suppose
\[
\int_U |f(z)|\,|dz|^2<\infty.
\]
Prove that there is a constant $M$ such that for every $z\in U$,
\[
|f(z)|\le \frac{M}{d(z,\partial U)^2}.
\]
:::
