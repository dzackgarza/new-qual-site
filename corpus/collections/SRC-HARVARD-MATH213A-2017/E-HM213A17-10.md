::: {.problem}
For $a,b\in\CC$, regard $a$ and $b$ as vectors in $\RR^2$.
Express the complex product $\overline a b$ in terms of the dot product and the planar cross product of those vectors.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

With the standard oriented planar cross product, $\overline a b=a\cdot b+i(a\times b)$.

::: pf-proof

Write
$$
a=x+iy,
\qquad
b=u+iv,
$$
and use
$$
a\cdot b=xu+yv,
\qquad
a\times b=xv-yu.
$$
Then
$$
\overline a b
=(x-iy)(u+iv)
=(xu+yv)+i(xv-yu),
$$
so
$$
\boxed{\overline a b=a\cdot b+i(a\times b)}.
$$
Equivalently,
$$
a\overline b=a\cdot b-i(a\times b).
$$

:::
