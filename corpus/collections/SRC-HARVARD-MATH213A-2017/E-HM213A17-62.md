::: {.problem}
Prove that there is no conformal metric
\[
\rho=\rho(z)|dz|
\]
on $\widehat{\mathbb C}$ that is invariant under the full automorphism group $\operatorname{Aut}(\widehat{\mathbb C})$.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. Invariance of a conformal metric under a holomorphic automorphism
$g$ means
\[
\rho(g(z))|g'(z)|=\rho(z)
\]
in any local coordinate.
::: {.proof}
The pullback of the metric $\rho(w)|dw|$ by $w=g(z)$ is
\[
g^*\rho
=\rho(g(z))|g'(z)|\,|dz|.
\]
Thus $g^*\rho=\rho$ is exactly the displayed equality.
:::

<1>2. Apply this identity to the Möbius automorphism
\[
g(z)=2z.
\]
::: {.proof}
This map is an automorphism of $\widehat{\mathbb C}$, fixes $0$, and has
$g'(0)=2$. If the metric were invariant under the full automorphism group,
then at $z=0$ one would have
\[
\rho(0)
=\rho(g(0))|g'(0)|
=2\rho(0).
\]
Hence $\rho(0)=0$.
:::

<1>3. This contradicts the definition of a conformal metric.
::: {.proof}
A conformal metric has strictly positive density in every local coordinate,
so in particular $\rho(0)>0$. This contradicts <1>2. Therefore no conformal
metric on $\widehat{\mathbb C}$ can be invariant under all Möbius
automorphisms.
:::
:::
