::: {.problem}
Prove that there is no nonzero holomorphic function
\[
f:\Delta\to\Delta
\]
whose zeros include
\[
a_n=1-\frac1{n+1},
\qquad n=1,2,3,\ldots.
\]

Hint: consider $f(0)/B_n(0)$, where $B_n:\Delta\to\Delta$ is a proper map of degree $n$ with zeros at $a_1,\ldots,a_n$.
:::
