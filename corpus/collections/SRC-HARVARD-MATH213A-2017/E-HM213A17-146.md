::: {.problem}
Let
\[
X=\widehat{\mathbb C}\setminus\{0,1,\infty\}.
\]
Let $\gamma$ be the closed hyperbolic geodesic on $X$ that makes a figure eight around $0$ and $1$.
Show that its length is
\[
L(\gamma)=\log(17+12\sqrt2).
\]

Hint: identify the corresponding element of
\[
\pi_1(X)\cong\Gamma(2)
\]
with a matrix of trace $6$.
:::
