::: {.problem}
Suppose $f$ is analytic.
Compute
\[
\frac{\partial}{\partial z}\frac{\partial}{\partial\overline z}|f(z)|^2.
\]
:::

::: {.solution}
\[
\boxed{
\frac{\partial}{\partial z}
\frac{\partial}{\partial\overline z}|f(z)|^2
=|f'(z)|^2.}
\]

<1>1. Since $f$ is analytic,
\[
\frac{\partial f}{\partial\overline z}=0,
\qquad
\frac{\partial\overline f}{\partial\overline z}
=\overline{f'(z)}.
\]
::: {.proof}
The first identity is the Cauchy--Riemann equation in Wirtinger notation.
Taking complex conjugates of
\[
\frac{\partial f}{\partial z}=f'(z)
\]
gives the second identity.
:::

<1>2. Therefore
\[
\frac{\partial}{\partial\overline z}|f|^2
=f\,\overline{f'}.
\]
::: {.proof}
Because $|f|^2=f\overline f$, the product rule and <1>1 give
\[
\frac{\partial}{\partial\overline z}(f\overline f)
=\frac{\partial f}{\partial\overline z}\overline f
+f\frac{\partial\overline f}{\partial\overline z}
=f\,\overline{f'}.
\]
:::

<1>3. Differentiating once more with respect to $z$ yields
\[
\frac{\partial}{\partial z}
\frac{\partial}{\partial\overline z}|f|^2
=|f'|^2.
\]
::: {.proof}
The function $f'$ is analytic, so $\overline{f'}$ is antiholomorphic and
\[
\frac{\partial}{\partial z}\overline{f'}=0.
\]
Thus, by <1>2,
\[
\frac{\partial}{\partial z}\bigl(f\,\overline{f'}\bigr)
=f'\overline{f'}
+f\frac{\partial\overline{f'}}{\partial z}
=|f'|^2.
\]
:::
:::
