::: {.problem}
Prove that
\[
\frac{\coth\pi}{1^7}+\frac{\coth(2\pi)}{2^7}+\frac{\coth(3\pi)}{3^7}+\cdots
=\frac{19\pi^7}{56700}.
\]

Hint: you may use
\[
\operatorname{Res}_{z=0}
\frac{\cot(\pi z)\coth(\pi z)}{z^7}
=-\frac{19\pi^6}{14175}.
\]
:::
