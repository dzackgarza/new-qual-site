::: {.problem}
Continue with the lattice
\[
\Lambda=\mathbb Z\lambda_1\oplus\mathbb Z\lambda_2
\]
and its Weierstrass zeta function $\zeta$.

(a) Show that there are constants $\eta_1,\eta_2\in\mathbb C$ such that
\[
\zeta(z+\lambda_i)=\zeta(z)+\eta_i
\qquad(i=1,2).
\]

(b) Using the residue theorem, prove the Legendre relation
\[
\det
\begin{pmatrix}
\eta_1&\eta_2\\
\lambda_1&\lambda_2
\end{pmatrix}
=2\pi i.
\]
:::
