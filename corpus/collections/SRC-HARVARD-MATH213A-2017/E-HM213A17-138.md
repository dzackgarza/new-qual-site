::: {.problem}
Let
\[
\Lambda=\mathbb Z\lambda_1\oplus\mathbb Z\lambda_2
\]
be a lattice in $\mathbb C$, with Weierstrass function $\wp$, and define the canonical product
\[
\sigma(z)
=z\prod_{\lambda\in\Lambda\setminus\{0\}}
\left(1-\frac z\lambda\right)
\exp\!\left(\frac z\lambda+\frac12\left(\frac z\lambda\right)^2\right).
\]

Prove that there is a unique odd meromorphic function $\zeta$ on $\mathbb C$ satisfying
\[
\zeta'(z)=-\wp(z),
\]
and relate $\zeta$ to $\sigma$.
:::
