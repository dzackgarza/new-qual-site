::: {.problem}
Let $f:\Delta\to\CC$ be analytic and suppose
$$
\operatorname{Re}f(z)\ge0.
$$
Show that
$$
\abs{f(z)}\le \abs{f(0)}\frac{1+\abs{z}}{1-\abs{z}}.
$$
:::

::: {.solution}
Set $a=f(0)$.

::: pf

::: pf-step

If $\Re a=0$, then $f$ is constant and the estimate holds.

::: pf-proof

The harmonic function $u=\Re f$ satisfies $u\ge0$ on $\Delta$ and
$u(0)=0$. Thus $u$ attains its minimum at an interior point, so the minimum
principle gives $u\equiv0$. A holomorphic function with identically zero real
part is constant, hence $f\equiv a$. Therefore
$$
\abs{f(z)}=\abs{a}
\le \abs{a}\frac{1+\abs{z}}{1-\abs{z}}.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

Suppose $\Re a>0$. Then
$$
\Phi(w)=\frac{w-a}{w+\overline a}
$$
maps the right half-plane holomorphically into $\Delta$ and satisfies
$\Phi(a)=0$.

::: pf-proof

For $\Re w>0$,
$$
\abs{w+\overline a}^2-\abs{w-a}^2
=4(\Re w)(\Re a)>0.
$$
Hence $\abs{\Phi(w)}<1$. The identity $\Phi(a)=0$ is immediate.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

For every $z\in\Delta$,
$$
\abs{\frac{f(z)-a}{f(z)+\overline a}}\le \abs{z}.
$$

::: pf-proof

By step [](#s2){.pf-ref}, the map
$$
g=\Phi\circ f:\Delta\to\Delta
$$
is holomorphic and satisfies $g(0)=0$. Schwarz's lemma therefore gives
$\abs{g(z)}\le\abs{z}$.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

The desired estimate follows.

::: pf-proof

Let $r=\abs{z}$. From step [](#s3){.pf-ref},
$$
\abs{f(z)-a}
\le r\,\abs{f(z)+\overline a}
\le r\bigl(\abs{f(z)}+\abs{a}\bigr).
$$
The triangle inequality also gives
$$
\abs{f(z)}\le \abs{f(z)-a}+\abs{a}.
$$
Combining these inequalities yields
$$
\abs{f(z)}
\le r\abs{f(z)}+(1+r)\abs{a},
$$
so
$$
(1-r)\abs{f(z)}\le(1+r)\abs{a}.
$$
Since $a=f(0)$,
$$
\abs{f(z)}\le \abs{f(0)}\frac{1+\abs{z}}{1-\abs{z}}.
$$

:::
