::: {.problem}
Let $\sum_{n\ge0}a_nz^n$ be the Taylor series for $\tanh z$ at $z=0$.

(a) What is the radius of convergence of this power series?

(b) Show that $a_5=2/15$.

(c) Give an explicit value of $N$ such that $\tanh(1)$ and $\sum_{n=0}^N a_n$ agree to $1000$ decimal places.
Justify your answer.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. The radius of convergence is $\pi/2$.
::: {.proof}
Since
\[
\tanh z=\frac{\sinh z}{\cosh z},
\]
its finite singularities occur at the zeros of $\cosh z$. The equation
\[
\cosh z=0
\]
is equivalent to $e^{2z}=-1$, and hence its solutions are
\[
z=\left(k+\frac12\right)\pi i,
\qquad k\in\mathbb Z.
\]
At each such point $\sinh z\ne0$, so these singularities are poles rather than removable singularities. The two nearest poles to the origin are $\pm\pi i/2$. Therefore the Taylor series at $0$ has radius of convergence $\pi/2$.
:::

<1>2. The coefficient of $z^5$ is $a_5=2/15$.
::: {.proof}
The function $y(z)=\tanh z$ is odd and satisfies
\[
y'=1-y^2.
\]
Write
\[
y(z)=a_1z+a_3z^3+a_5z^5+O(z^7).
\]
Then
\[
y'=a_1+3a_3z^2+5a_5z^4+O(z^6)
\]
and
\[
1-y^2=1-a_1^2z^2-2a_1a_3z^4+O(z^6).
\]
Comparing coefficients gives
\[
a_1=1,
\qquad
3a_3=-1,
\qquad
5a_5=-2a_3=\frac23.
\]
Thus
\[
a_5=\frac{2}{15}.
\]
:::

<1>3. On the circle $|z|=3/2$, one has the uniform bound $|\tanh z|<48$.
::: {.proof}
Write $z=x+iy$. Then
\[
|\cosh z|^2
=\sinh^2x+\cos^2y.
\]
Since $|y|\le3/2<\pi/2$,
\[
|\cosh z|\ge \cos(3/2).
\]
The cosine series gives the rigorous lower bound
\[
\cos(3/2)
>
1-\frac{(3/2)^2}{2!}
+\frac{(3/2)^4}{4!}
-\frac{(3/2)^6}{6!}
=\frac{359}{5120}
>\frac1{16};
\]
after the displayed negative term, the remaining alternating terms have decreasing magnitudes and begin with a positive term.

Also, by the power series for $\sinh$,
\[
|\sinh z|\le\sinh|z|=\sinh(3/2)<\frac{e^{3/2}}2<3.
\]
Indeed,
\[
e=1+1+\sum_{k\ge2}\frac1{k!}
<2+\sum_{k\ge2}2^{1-k}=3,
\]
so $e^{3/2}<3^{3/2}<6$. Therefore
\[
|\tanh z|=\frac{|\sinh z|}{|\cosh z|}<3\cdot16=48.
\]
:::

<1>4. The explicit choice $N=6017$ makes the truncation error less than $10^{-1000}$.
::: {.proof}
Apply Cauchy's coefficient estimate on the circle $|z|=3/2$. By <1>3,
\[
|a_n|\le48\left(\frac23\right)^n.
\]
Since $1<3/2$, the Taylor series converges absolutely at $z=1$, and therefore
\[
\left|\tanh(1)-\sum_{n=0}^N a_n\right|
\le
\sum_{n=N+1}^{\infty}|a_n|
\le
144\left(\frac23\right)^{N+1}.
\]
For $N=6017$ we have $N+1=6018=6\cdot1003$, while
\[
\left(\frac23\right)^6=\frac{64}{729}<\frac1{10}.
\]
Consequently
\[
144\left(\frac23\right)^{6018}
<144\cdot10^{-1003}
<10^{-1000}.
\]
Thus $N=6017$ is an explicit value for which the stated partial sum agrees with $\tanh(1)$ to at least $1000$ decimal places.
