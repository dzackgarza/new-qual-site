::: {.problem}
For a lattice $\Lambda$, define
\[
G_n(\Lambda)=\sum_{\lambda\in\Lambda\setminus\{0\}}\lambda^{-2n}.
\]
Assume that uniqueness of modular forms of weight $8$ gives a universal constant $A$ such that
\[
G_2(\Lambda)^2=A\,G_4(\Lambda)
\]
for every lattice $\Lambda$.
Determine $A$.

Hint: use
\[
\sum_{n\ne0}\frac1{n^8}=\frac{2\pi^8}{9450}=\frac{\pi^8}{4725}.
\]
:::
