::: {.problem}
Let $f:\Delta\to\Delta$ be a proper analytic map, and let $F:\overline\Delta\to\overline\Delta$ be its continuous extension.
Suppose $f(0)=0$.
Show that for every continuous function $\phi:S^1\to\mathbb R$, the functions $\phi$ and $\phi\circ F$ have the same average over $S^1$.

Hint: use the harmonic extension of $\phi$.
:::
