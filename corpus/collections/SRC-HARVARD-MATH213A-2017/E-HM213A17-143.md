::: {.problem}
Let $g_2(\tau),g_3(\tau)$ be the Weierstrass invariants and let $\lambda(\tau)$ be the cross-ratio of the three roots of
\[
4x^3-g_2(\tau)x-g_3(\tau)=0
\]
together with $\infty$, in the source's ordering.
Using
\[
J(\tau)=\frac4{27}\frac{(\lambda^2-\lambda+1)^3}{\lambda^2(1-\lambda)^2},
\]
prove that
\[
J(\tau)=\frac{g_2(\tau)^3}{g_2(\tau)^3-27g_3(\tau)^2}.
\]
:::
