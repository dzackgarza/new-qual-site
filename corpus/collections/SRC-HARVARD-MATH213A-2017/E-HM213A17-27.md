::: {.problem}
(a) Estimate $\log 2$ by evaluating
\[
S=\sum_{n=0}^5 a_n
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
E=\sum_{n=0}^5 b_n(1/2)^n,
\]
where
\[
f(x)=\log(1+x)=\sum a_nx^n
\]
and
\[
g(y)=f\!\left(\frac{y}{1-y}\right)=\sum b_ny^n.
\]
How do these approximations compare to $\log 2$?

(b) What is the radius of convergence of the power series for $g(y)$?
:::
