::: {.problem}
Let $f:\Delta\to\Delta$ be analytic and let $f^n=f\circ\cdots\circ f$ denote the $n$-fold iterate.
Show that either $f$ has a fixed point in $\Delta$, or
\[
|f^n(z)|\longrightarrow1
\]
for every $z\in\Delta$.
:::
