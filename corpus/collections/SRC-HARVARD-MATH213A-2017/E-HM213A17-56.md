::: {.problem}
Compute the hyperbolic metric on
$$
\Delta^*=\Delta\setminus\{0\}.
$$
:::

::: {.solution}
The hyperbolic metric is
$$
\boxed{
\rho_{\Delta^*}(z)
=\frac{\abs{dz}}{\abs{z}\log(1/\abs{z})}
=\frac{\abs{dz}}{\abs{z}\,\abs{\log\abs{z}}}.}
$$

::: pf

::: pf-step

The map
$$
\pi(\zeta)=e^{2\pi i\zeta}
$$
is a holomorphic covering $\halfplane\to\Delta^*$.

::: pf-proof

Write $\zeta=x+iy$ with $y>0$. Then
$$
\abs{\pi(\zeta)}=e^{-2\pi y}\in(0,1),
$$
so the image lies in $\Delta^*$. Conversely, if $0<\abs{z}<1$, choose an
argument $\theta$ of $z$ and set
$$
\zeta=\frac{\theta}{2\pi}
+i\frac{\log(1/\abs{z})}{2\pi}.
$$
Then $\zeta\in\halfplane$ and $\pi(\zeta)=z$. The deck transformations are
$\zeta\mapsto\zeta+k$, $k\in\ZZ$, so $\pi$ is the usual universal
covering of the punctured disk.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

If $z=\pi(\zeta)$ and $\zeta=x+iy$, then
$$
y=\frac{\log(1/\abs{z})}{2\pi}
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\abs{\pi'(\zeta)}=2\pi\abs{z}.
$$

::: pf-proof

The first identity follows from $\abs{z}=e^{-2\pi y}$. The second follows from
$$
\pi'(\zeta)=2\pi i e^{2\pi i\zeta}=2\pi i z.
$$

:::

:::

::: pf-step

The displayed metric is exactly the metric whose pullback under
$\pi$ is the hyperbolic metric $\abs{d\zeta}/\operatorname{Im}\zeta$ on
$\halfplane$.

::: pf-proof

Let the metric on $\Delta^*$ be $\lambda(z)\abs{dz}$. Since a universal
covering between hyperbolic Riemann surfaces is a local isometry, it must
satisfy
$$
\lambda(\pi(\zeta))\abs{\pi'(\zeta)}\,\abs{d\zeta}
=\frac{\abs{d\zeta}}{y}.
$$
Using step [](#s2){.pf-ref} gives
$$
\lambda(z)\,2\pi\abs{z}
=\frac{2\pi}{\log(1/\abs{z})}.
$$
Hence
$$
\lambda(z)=\frac{1}{\abs{z}\log(1/\abs{z})}.
$$
Because $0<\abs{z}<1$, one has $\log(1/\abs{z})=\abs{\log\abs{z}}$, giving the equivalent
form stated above.

:::
