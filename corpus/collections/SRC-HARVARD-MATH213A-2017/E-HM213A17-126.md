::: {.problem}
Given $A,B,C\in\mathbb C$, prove that there is a unique quadratic differential
\[
q=q(z)\,dz^2
\]
on $\widehat{\mathbb C}$ having double poles at $0$, $1$, and $\infty$, residues $A$, $B$, and $C$ there respectively, and no other singularities.

Here, if
\[
q=\left(\frac{a}{z^2}+O\!\left(\frac1z\right)\right)dz^2
\]
near $0$, then $\operatorname{Res}_0(q)=a$.
:::
