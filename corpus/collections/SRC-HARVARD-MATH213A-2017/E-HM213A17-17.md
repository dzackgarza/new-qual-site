::: {.problem}
Prove that the distributional derivative of $f(z)=1/z$ satisfies
\[
\frac{\partial f}{\partial\overline z}=C\delta_0,
\]
and evaluate the constant $C$.
Equivalently, show that
\[
-\int_{\mathbb C} f(z)\frac{\partial\phi}{\partial\overline z}\,|dz|^2=C\phi(0)
\]
for every $\phi\in C_0^\infty(\mathbb C)$.
:::
