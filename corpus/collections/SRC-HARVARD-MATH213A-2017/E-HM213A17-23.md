::: {.problem}
Let $p(n)$ be the number of partitions of $n$ into unequal parts.

(a) Show that
\[
f(z)=\prod_{n=1}^{\infty}(1+z^n)
\]
defines an analytic function on the unit disk $\Delta$.

(b) Show that
\[
f(z)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}p(n)z^n.
\]

(c) Show that $f$ cannot be analytically continued beyond the unit disk: if $g$ is analytic on a connected domain $U\supset\Delta$ and $g=f$ on $\Delta$, then $U=\Delta$.
:::
