::: {.problem}
Let $f_n\to f$ uniformly on compact subsets of an open connected set $\Omega\subset\mathbb C$, where every $f_n$ is analytic and $f$ is not identically zero.
Show that if $f(w)=0$, then there are points $z_n\to w$ such that $f_n(z_n)=0$ for all sufficiently large $n$.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. The limit function $f$ is analytic on $\Omega$, and its zero at $w$
is isolated.
::: {.proof}
Uniform convergence on compact subsets of analytic functions implies that
the limit is analytic. Since $\Omega$ is connected and $f$ is not identically
zero, the identity theorem implies that the zeros of $f$ are isolated.
:::

<1>2. Choose radii
\[
r_1>r_2>\cdots>0,
\qquad r_k\longrightarrow0,
\]
such that every closed disk
\[
\overline{D(w,r_k)}\subset\Omega
\]
and $w$ is the only zero of $f$ in each disk.
::: {.proof}
By <1>1, there is $r>0$ such that
\[
\overline{D(w,r)}\subset\Omega
\]
and $f$ has no zero in
$\overline{D(w,r)}\setminus\{w\}$. Any strictly decreasing sequence
$r_k\to0$ with $r_k<r$ has the stated properties.
:::

<1>3. For each $k$, there is an integer $N_k$ such that every
$n\ge N_k$ has at least one zero in $D(w,r_k)$.
::: {.proof}
On the circle
\[
C_k=\{z:|z-w|=r_k\}
\]
the continuous function $|f|$ has a positive minimum
\[
m_k:=\min_{C_k}|f|>0.
\]
Compact-uniform convergence gives $N_k$ such that, for $n\ge N_k$,
\[
|f_n(z)-f(z)|<m_k\le |f(z)|
\qquad(z\in C_k).
\]
Rouché's theorem therefore says that $f_n$ and $f$ have the same number
of zeros in $D(w,r_k)$, counted with multiplicity. Since $f(w)=0$, that
number is positive. Hence $f_n$ has at least one zero in the disk.
:::

<1>4. The thresholds $N_k$ may be chosen strictly increasing.
::: {.proof}
Replace the sequence from <1>3 recursively by
\[
N_1'=N_1,
\qquad
N_k'=\max\{N_k,N_{k-1}'+1\}
\quad(k\ge2).
\]
The property in <1>3 remains true with $N_k'$ in place of $N_k$, and the
new sequence is strictly increasing. Rename it $N_k$.
:::

<1>5. There are zeros $z_n$ of $f_n$, for all sufficiently large $n$,
such that $z_n\to w$.
::: {.proof}
For $n\ge N_1$, let $k(n)$ be the largest integer $k$ with
\[
N_k\le n.
\]
By <1>3, choose a zero $z_n$ of $f_n$ satisfying
\[
|z_n-w|<r_{k(n)}.
\]
Because the strictly increasing sequence $N_k$ tends to infinity,
$k(n)\to\infty$ as $n\to\infty$. Since $r_k\to0$,
\[
|z_n-w|<r_{k(n)}\longrightarrow0.
\]
Thus $z_n\to w$, as required.
:::
:::
