::: {.problem}
Let $f_n\to f$ uniformly on compact subsets of an open connected set $\Omega\subset\mathbb C$, where every $f_n$ is analytic and $f$ is not identically zero.
Show that if $f(w)=0$, then there are points $z_n\to w$ such that $f_n(z_n)=0$ for all sufficiently large $n$.
:::
