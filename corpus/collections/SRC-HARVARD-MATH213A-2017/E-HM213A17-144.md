::: {.problem}
For $\lambda\in\widehat{\mathbb C}\setminus\{0,1,\infty\}$, let
\[
S_\lambda=\widehat{\mathbb C}\setminus\{0,1,\infty,\lambda\}.
\]

(a) Prove that every holomorphic automorphism of $S_\lambda$ is the restriction of a Möbius transformation.

(b) Prove that $\operatorname{Aut}(S_\lambda)$ always contains a subgroup isomorphic to
\[
\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\times\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z,
\]
and determine how this subgroup permutes $\{0,1,\infty,\lambda\}$.

(c) Find all $\lambda$ for which
\[
|\operatorname{Aut}(S_\lambda)|>4,
\]
and identify the automorphism group in each case.
:::
