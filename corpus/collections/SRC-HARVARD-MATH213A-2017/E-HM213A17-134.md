::: {.problem}
Let $X=\mathbb C/\Lambda$ be a complex torus and define
\[
F:X\to X,
\qquad
[z]\mapsto[2z].
\]

(a) Show that $F$ has a dense forward orbit: there exists $p\in X$ such that
\[
\overline{\{F^n(p):n>0\}}=X.
\]

(b) Let $R$ be the rational function in the Weierstrass double-angle formula
\[
\wp(2z)=R(\wp(z)).
\]
Prove that $R$ has a dense forward orbit on $\widehat{\mathbb C}$.
:::

::: remark
The source's parenthetical description of a dense orbit writes $\{F^n(p):n>0\}=X$ without closure.
Literal equality is impossible because the forward orbit is countable while the torus is uncountable; density is the stated and intended property.
:::
