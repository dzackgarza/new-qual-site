::: {.problem}
Let $f,g\in\mathcal O(\mathbb C)$ have no common zeros.
Show that
\[
(f,g)=(1),
\]
i.e. there exist $r,s\in\mathcal O(\mathbb C)$ such that
\[
fr+gs=1.
\]

Hint: split $1/(fg)$ as $F+G$, with the poles of $F$ supported on the zeros of $f$ and the poles of $G$ supported on the zeros of $g$.
:::
