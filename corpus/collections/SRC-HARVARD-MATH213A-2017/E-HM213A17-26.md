::: {.problem}
Let $f_n(z)$ be a sequence of polynomials such that $f_n(z)\to f(z)$ pointwise on $\mathbb C$.
Show that there exists a dense open set $U\subset\mathbb C$ such that $f|_U$ is analytic.
You may quote the Baire category theorem.
:::
