::: {.problem}
Let $f:\mathbb C\to\mathbb C$ be a polynomial of degree at least $2$.
Let
\[
V_1=\{f(z):f'(z)=0\},\qquad V_0=f^{-1}(V_1),\qquad U_i=\mathbb C\setminus V_i\quad(i=0,1).
\]
Prove that $f:U_0\to U_1$ is a covering map.
:::
