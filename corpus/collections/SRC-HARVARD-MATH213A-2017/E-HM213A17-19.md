::: {.problem}
Let $f:\mathbb C\to\mathbb C$ be analytic and let $U\subset\mathbb C$ be a bounded region.
Suppose $|f(z)|$ is constant on $\partial U$.
Show that either $f$ is constant or $f$ has a zero in $U$.
:::
