::: {.problem}
State and prove an asymptotic formula for $\Gamma'(x)$ as $x\to+\infty$ along the real axis.
Equivalently, find an elementary function $A(x)$ such that
\[
\frac{\Gamma'(x)}{A(x)}\longrightarrow1.
\]
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. The function
\[
\phi(x)=\log\Gamma(x),\qquad x>0,
\]
is convex.
::: {.proof}
Using the integral representation
\[
\Gamma(x)=\int_0^\infty e^{-t}t^{x-1}\,dt,
\]
let $x,y>0$ and $0<\theta<1$. Then
\[
\Gamma(\theta x+(1-\theta)y)
=\int_0^\infty
\bigl(e^{-t}t^{x-1}\bigr)^\theta
\bigl(e^{-t}t^{y-1}\bigr)^{1-\theta}\,dt.
\]
Hölder's inequality gives
\[
\Gamma(\theta x+(1-\theta)y)
\le \Gamma(x)^\theta\Gamma(y)^{1-\theta}.
\]
Taking logarithms proves the convexity of $\phi$.
:::

<1>2. For every $x>1$,
\[
\log(x-1)
\le \frac{\Gamma'(x)}{\Gamma(x)}
\le \log x.
\]
::: {.proof}
For a differentiable convex function, the derivative at $x$ lies between the backward and forward secant slopes over intervals of length $1$. Hence
\[
\phi(x)-\phi(x-1)
\le \phi'(x)
\le \phi(x+1)-\phi(x).
\]
Using $\Gamma(x)=(x-1)\Gamma(x-1)$ and $\Gamma(x+1)=x\Gamma(x)$ gives
\[
\phi(x)-\phi(x-1)=\log(x-1),
\qquad
\phi(x+1)-\phi(x)=\log x.
\]
Since $\phi'(x)=\Gamma'(x)/\Gamma(x)$, the stated bounds follow.
:::

<1>3. The logarithmic derivative satisfies
\[
\frac{\Gamma'(x)}{\Gamma(x)}\sim\log x
\qquad(x\to+\infty).
\]
::: {.proof}
For $x>2$, divide the inequalities in <1>2 by $\log x>0$:
\[
\frac{\log(x-1)}{\log x}
\le
\frac{\Gamma'(x)}{\Gamma(x)\log x}
\le1.
\]
The left-hand side tends to $1$, so the squeeze theorem gives
\[
\frac{\Gamma'(x)}{\Gamma(x)\log x}\longrightarrow1.
\]
:::

<1>4. Therefore one may take
\[
\boxed{
A(x)=\sqrt{\frac{2\pi}{x}}\left(\frac{x}{e}\right)^x\log x
}.
\]
Equivalently,
\[
\Gamma'(x)
\sim
\sqrt{\frac{2\pi}{x}}\left(\frac{x}{e}\right)^x\log x.
\]
::: {.proof}
Stirling's formula gives
\[
\Gamma(x)
\sim
\sqrt{\frac{2\pi}{x}}\left(\frac{x}{e}\right)^x.
\]
Multiplying this by the asymptotic relation in <1>3 yields
\[
\frac{\Gamma'(x)}{
\sqrt{2\pi/x}(x/e)^x\log x}
=
\frac{\Gamma(x)}{\sqrt{2\pi/x}(x/e)^x}
\cdot
\frac{\Gamma'(x)}{\Gamma(x)\log x}
\longrightarrow1.
\]
:::
