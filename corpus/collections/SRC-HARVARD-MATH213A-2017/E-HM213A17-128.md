::: {.problem}
Let $\Lambda\subset\mathbb R^n$ be a lattice.
Choose $a_1,\ldots,a_n\in\Lambda$ successively so that $a_1$ is a shortest nonzero vector and, for $i>1$, $a_i$ is a shortest vector linearly independent from $a_1,\ldots,a_{i-1}$.

Must one always have
\[
\Lambda=\mathbb Za_1\oplus\cdots\oplus\mathbb Za_n?
\]
:::
