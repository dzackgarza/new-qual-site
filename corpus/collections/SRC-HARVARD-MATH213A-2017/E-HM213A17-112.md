::: {.problem}
Let $\mathcal S$ be the normalized univalent maps on $\Delta$.
Prove that the maps in $\mathcal S$ whose images are polygons are dense in $\mathcal S$ for uniform convergence on compact subsets of $\Delta$.
:::
