::: {.problem}
Determine the orbits of
\[
\operatorname{SL}_2(\mathbb Z)
\]
acting by fractional linear transformations on
\[
\mathbb Q\cup\{\infty\},
\]
and determine the orbits of the principal congruence subgroup $\Gamma(2)$ on the same set.
:::
