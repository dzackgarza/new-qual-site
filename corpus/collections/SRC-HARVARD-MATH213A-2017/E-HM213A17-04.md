::: {.problem}
Let $f:U\to V$ be a proper local homeomorphism between open sets $U,V\subset\mathbb C$.
Prove that $f$ is a covering map.
Here proper means that $f^{-1}(K)$ is compact whenever $K\subset V$ is compact.
:::
