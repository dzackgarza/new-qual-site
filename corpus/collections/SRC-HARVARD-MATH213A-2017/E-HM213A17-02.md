::: {.problem}
Let
\[
f(z)=\frac{az+b}{cz+d}
\]
be a Möbius transformation.
Show that the number of rational maps $g:\widehat{\mathbb C}\to\widehat{\mathbb C}$ satisfying
\[
g\circ g\circ g\circ g\circ g=f
\]
is $1$, $5$, or $\infty$.
Explain how to determine which alternative holds for a given $f$.
:::
