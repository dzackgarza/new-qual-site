::: {.problem}
Let
\[
f_n:\widehat{\mathbb C}\to\widehat{\mathbb C}
\]
be an unbounded sequence in $\operatorname{Aut}(\widehat{\mathbb C})$.
Prove that, after passing to a subsequence, there are $p,q\in\widehat{\mathbb C}$ such that
\[
f_n(z)\longrightarrow p
\]
uniformly on compact subsets of $\widehat{\mathbb C}\setminus\{q\}$.
Give an example for which $p=q=0$.
:::
