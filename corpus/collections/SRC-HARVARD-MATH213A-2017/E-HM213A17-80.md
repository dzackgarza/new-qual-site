::: {.problem}
Find the orders of the entire functions
\[
\sin^2(\sqrt z)
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\exp(\sin z).
\]
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. The function
\[
F(z)=\sin^2(\sqrt z)
\]
is entire.
::: {.proof}
Use the identity
\[
\sin^2w=\frac{1-\cos(2w)}2.
\]
Since the power series for $\cos(2\sqrt z)$ contains only even powers of
$\sqrt z$, it is a power series in $z$:
\[
F(z)
=\frac12\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}
(-1)^{n+1}\frac{(2\sqrt z)^{2n}}{(2n)!}.
\]
Hence $F$ is entire.
:::

<1>2. The order of $F$ is at most $1/2$.
::: {.proof}
For every $w\in\mathbb C$,
\[
|\sin w|\le e^{|w|}.
\]
Thus for $|z|=r$,
\[
|F(z)|
=|\sin^2(\sqrt z)|
\le e^{2\sqrt r}.
\]
Therefore
\[
\log M_F(r)\le2\sqrt r,
\]
which implies
\[
\limsup_{r\to\infty}
\frac{\log\log M_F(r)}{\log r}\le\frac12.
\]
:::

<1>3. The order of $F$ is at least $1/2$.
::: {.proof}
On the negative real axis, take $z=-r$ and $\sqrt z=i\sqrt r$. Then
\[
|F(-r)|
=|\sin^2(i\sqrt r)|
=\sinh^2(\sqrt r).
\]
For large $r$,
\[
\sinh^2(\sqrt r)\ge \frac1{16}e^{2\sqrt r}.
\]
Hence
\[
\log M_F(r)\ge2\sqrt r-O(1),
\]
and so the order is at least $1/2$.
:::

<1>4. Consequently
\[
\boxed{\rho\!\left(\sin^2(\sqrt z)\right)=\frac12.}
\]
::: {.proof}
Combine <1>2 and <1>3.
:::

<1>5. Let
\[
G(z)=e^{\sin z}.
\]
Then $G$ has infinite order.
::: {.proof}
Take
\[
z_r=\frac\pi2+ir.
\]
Since
\[
\sin\left(\frac\pi2+ir\right)=\cosh r,
\]
one has
\[
|G(z_r)|=e^{\cosh r}.
\]
Put $R_r=|z_r|$. Then $R_r\sim r$, while
\[
\log M_G(R_r)\ge\cosh r\ge\frac12e^r.
\]
Thus
\[
\log\log M_G(R_r)\ge r-O(1).
\]
Since $\log R_r\sim\log r$,
\[
\frac{\log\log M_G(R_r)}{\log R_r}
\longrightarrow\infty.
\]
Therefore
\[
\boxed{\rho(e^{\sin z})=\infty.}
\]
:::
:::
