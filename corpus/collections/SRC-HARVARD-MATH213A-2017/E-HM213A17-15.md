::: {.problem}
Let $f_1,\ldots,f_n$ be analytic functions on the unit disk $\Delta$ and suppose
\[
\sum_{i=1}^n |f_i(z)|^2=1
\qquad(z\in\Delta).
\]

(a) Show that all $f_i$ are constant by taking the Laplacian of both sides.

(b) Show that all $f_i$ are constant by applying the maximum principle, or the open mapping theorem, to suitable linear combinations of the functions.

(c) Let $H:\mathbb C^n\to\mathbb R$ be a strictly convex smooth function.
Using method (a) or (b), prove that if
\[
H(f_1(z),\ldots,f_n(z))
\]
is constant on $\Delta$, then each $f_i$ is constant.
:::
