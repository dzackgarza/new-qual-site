::: {.problem}
Let
\[
f(z)=z+\sum_{n=2}^{\infty}a_nz^n
\]
be univalent on $\Delta$.
Give an explicit bound
\[
|a_n|\le M_n
\]
valid for every such $f$, using only elementary methods such as the area theorem or the distortion theorems.
:::

::: {.solution}
For $n\ge2$, one explicit bound is
\[
\boxed{
|a_n|
\le M_n
:=
\frac{(n+1)^2}{4}
\left(\frac{n+1}{n-1}\right)^{n-1}
}.
\]

<1>1. The distortion theorem and Cauchy's coefficient estimate give, for every $0<r<1$,
\[
|a_n|
\le \frac{r^{1-n}}{(1-r)^2}.
\]
::: {.proof}
McMullen's distortion theorem gives, on $|z|=r$,
\[
|f(z)|\le\frac{r}{(1-r)^2}.
\]
By Cauchy's formula for the coefficient $a_n$,
\[
a_n=\frac{1}{2\pi i}\int_{|z|=r}\frac{f(z)}{z^{n+1}}\,dz,
\]
and therefore
\[
|a_n|
\le \frac{1}{r^n}\max_{|z|=r}|f(z)|
\le \frac{r^{1-n}}{(1-r)^2}.
\]
:::

<1>2. Optimizing this elementary estimate gives the displayed $M_n$.
::: {.proof}
For fixed $n\ge2$, set
\[
\Phi_n(r)=\frac{r^{1-n}}{(1-r)^2}.
\]
Then
\[
\frac{d}{dr}\log\Phi_n(r)
=\frac{1-n}{r}+\frac{2}{1-r}.
\]
The unique critical point in $(0,1)$ is
\[
r_n=\frac{n-1}{n+1}.
\]
Since $\Phi_n(r)\to\infty$ at both endpoints, this is its minimum. Substitution gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
\Phi_n(r_n)
&=\left(\frac{n+1}{n-1}\right)^{n-1}
  \left(\frac{n+1}{2}\right)^2\\
&=\frac{(n+1)^2}{4}
\left(\frac{n+1}{n-1}\right)^{n-1}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Thus $|a_n|\le M_n$ for every normalized univalent $f$.
:::
