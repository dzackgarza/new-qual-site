::: {.problem}
Determine the zeros of the Weierstrass $\wp$-function for each of the lattices
\[
\mathbb Z\oplus\mathbb Zi
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\mathbb Z\oplus\mathbb Z\omega,
\]
where
\[
\omega=\frac{1+\sqrt{-3}}2.
\]
:::
