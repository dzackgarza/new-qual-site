::: {.problem}
Let
\[
T=\{(x,y,z)\in\mathbb R^3:x^2+y^2+z^2=1,\ x,y,z\ge 0\}
\]
be the spherical triangle, and let $\alpha=z\,dx\wedge dz$.

(a) Find a smooth $1$-form $\beta$ on $\mathbb R^3$ such that $\alpha=d\beta$.

(b) Define consistent orientations for $T$ and $\partial T$.

(c) Using those orientations, compute $\int_T\alpha$ and $\int_{\partial T}\beta$ directly and check that they agree.
Explain why they should agree.
:::
