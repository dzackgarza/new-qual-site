::: {.problem}
Let $f:\mathbb C\to\mathbb C$ be given in polar coordinates by
\[
f(r,\theta)=(r^\alpha,n\theta),
\]
where $\alpha>0$ and $n\in\mathbb Z$.
Compute $\partial f/\partial z$ and $\partial f/\partial\overline z$.
:::
