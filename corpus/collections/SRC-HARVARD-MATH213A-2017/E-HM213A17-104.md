::: {.problem}
Let $f:\Delta\to\mathbb C$ be holomorphic and suppose
\[
\int_\Delta |f'(z)|^2\,|dz|^2<\infty.
\]
Prove that for almost every $\zeta\in S^1$, the radial limit
\[
F(\zeta)=\lim_{r\to1^-} f(r\zeta)
\]
exists and is finite.
:::
