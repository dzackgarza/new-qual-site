::: {.problem}
A circular triangle is a region $T\subset\widehat{\mathbb C}$ bounded by three circular arcs or lines.
Let $T_1,T_2\subset\widehat{\mathbb C}$ be two circular triangles with the same interior angles in the same cyclic order.
Prove that there exists a Möbius transformation sending $T_1$ to $T_2$.
:::
