::: {.problem}
For a locally univalent holomorphic map $f$, define
\[
S_f=N_f'-\frac12N_f^2,
\qquad
N_f=\frac{f''}{f'}.
\]
Prove the composition law
\[
S_{f\circ g}=S_g+(S_f\circ g)(g')^2.
\]
:::
