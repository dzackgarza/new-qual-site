::: {.problem}
For a locally univalent holomorphic map $f$, define
\[
S_f=N_f'-\frac12N_f^2,
\qquad
N_f=\frac{f''}{f'}.
\]
Prove the composition law
\[
S_{f\circ g}=S_g+(S_f\circ g)(g')^2.
\]
:::

::: {.solution}
Use the nonlinearity chain rule
\[
N_{f\circ g}=N_g+(N_f\circ g)g'.
\]
Differentiating gives
\[
N_{f\circ g}'
=N_g'
 +(N_f'\circ g)(g')^2
 +(N_f\circ g)g''.
\]
Also
\[
\begin{aligned}
\frac12N_{f\circ g}^2
&=\frac12N_g^2
 +N_g(N_f\circ g)g'\\
&\qquad
 +\frac12(N_f\circ g)^2(g')^2.
\end{aligned}
\]
Since
\[
N_g=\frac{g''}{g'},
\]
the two mixed terms cancel:
\[
(N_f\circ g)g''
=N_g(N_f\circ g)g'.
\]
Therefore
\[
\begin{aligned}
S_{f\circ g}
&=N_{f\circ g}'-\frac12N_{f\circ g}^2\\
&=\left(N_g'-\frac12N_g^2\right)
 +\left(N_f'-\frac12N_f^2\right)\!\circ g\,(g')^2\\
&=S_g+(S_f\circ g)(g')^2.
\end{aligned}
\]
Equivalently, for the quadratic differential $S_f(z)\,dz^2$,
\[
S(f\circ g)=S(g)+g^*S(f).
\]
:::
