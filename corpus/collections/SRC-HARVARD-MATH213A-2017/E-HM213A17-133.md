::: {.problem}
Let $\wp$ be the Weierstrass function of a lattice with invariants $g_2,g_3$.
Find a rational function $R(x)$, allowed to depend on $g_2,g_3$, such that
\[
\wp(2z)=R(\wp(z)).
\]
:::
