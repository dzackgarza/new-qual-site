::: {.problem}
Let $\wp$ be the Weierstrass function of a lattice with invariants $g_2,g_3$.
Find a rational function $R(x)$, allowed to depend on $g_2,g_3$, such that
\[
\wp(2z)=R(\wp(z)).
\]
:::

::: {.solution}
The required rational function is
\[
\boxed{
R(x)=
\frac{(x^2+g_2/4)^2+2g_3x}
{4x^3-g_2x-g_3}.
}
\]
Equivalently,
\[
R(x)
=-2x+
\frac{(12x^2-g_2)^2}
{16(4x^3-g_2x-g_3)}.
\]

<1>1. On the Weierstrass cubic
\[
E:\quad y^2=4x^3-g_2x-g_3,
\]
the slope of the tangent at
\[
(x,y)=(\wp(z),\wp'(z))
\]
is
\[
m=\frac{6x^2-g_2/2}{y}.
\]
::: {.proof}
Differentiate the cubic equation implicitly:
\[
2y\frac{dy}{dx}=12x^2-g_2.
\]
Hence, wherever $y\ne0$,
\[
\frac{dy}{dx}
=\frac{12x^2-g_2}{2y}
=\frac{6x^2-g_2/2}{y}.
\]
The resulting identity is rational on the curve and therefore extends across the exceptional points in the usual limiting sense.
:::

<1>2. If the tangent at $(x,y)$ meets $E$ again at a point with $x$-coordinate $x_3$, then
\[
x_3=\frac{m^2}{4}-2x.
\]
::: {.proof}
Write the tangent line as
\[
Y=m(X-x)+y.
\]
Substituting into the cubic gives a cubic polynomial equation in $X$:
\[
4X^3-g_2X-g_3-\bigl(m(X-x)+y\bigr)^2=0.
\]
Its leading coefficient is $4$, and its $X^2$ coefficient is $-m^2$. Thus the sum of its three roots is
\[
\frac{m^2}{4}.
\]
The tangency means that $X=x$ is a double root, while the third root is $x_3$. Hence
\[
2x+x_3=\frac{m^2}{4},
\]
which gives the formula.
:::

<1>3. The third intersection of the tangent represents $-2z$, so its $x$-coordinate is $\wp(2z)$.
::: {.proof}
McMullen's chord--tangent group law says that the three intersection points of a line with the cubic sum to zero in the elliptic-curve group. For a tangent at the point corresponding to $z$, the three points are
\[
z,
\qquad
z,
\qquad
-2z.
\]
Negation on the Weierstrass cubic changes $(x,y)$ to $(x,-y)$ and leaves the $x$-coordinate unchanged. Since $\wp$ is even,
\[
x_3=\wp(-2z)=\wp(2z).
\]
Combining with <1>2 and <1>1 gives
\[
\wp(2z)
=-2x
 +\frac14
 \frac{(6x^2-g_2/2)^2}{y^2}.
\]
Using
\[
y^2=4x^3-g_2x-g_3
\]
gives
\[
\wp(2z)
=-2x+
\frac{(12x^2-g_2)^2}
{16(4x^3-g_2x-g_3)}.
\]
:::

<1>4. Simplifying gives the displayed $R(x)$.
::: {.proof}
Put
\[
D=4x^3-g_2x-g_3.
\]
Then
\[
\begin{aligned}
-2x+\frac{(12x^2-g_2)^2}{16D}
&=\frac{-32xD+(12x^2-g_2)^2}{16D}\\
&=\frac{16x^4+8g_2x^2+32g_3x+g_2^2}{16D}\\
&=\frac{(x^2+g_2/4)^2+2g_3x}{D}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Thus
\[
\wp(2z)=R(\wp(z))
\]
as an identity of meromorphic functions.
:::
:::
