::: {.problem}
Let $D_n$ be the union of the open unit disks centered at $0$ and at $2-1/n$.
Let
\[
f_n:(\Delta,0)\longrightarrow(D_n,0)
\]
be the Riemann map normalized by $f_n'(0)>0$.
Prove that
\[
f_n(z)\longrightarrow z
\]
uniformly on compact subsets of $\Delta$.
In particular, show that the image of the limiting map is smaller than the limit of the images of the $f_n$.
:::
