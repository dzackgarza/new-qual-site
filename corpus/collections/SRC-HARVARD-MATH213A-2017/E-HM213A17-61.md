::: {.problem}
Let $\mathcal U$ be the set of positive harmonic functions $u$ on the unit disk $\Delta$, normalized by $u(0)=1$.
Show that $\mathcal U$ is compact: every sequence in $\mathcal U$ has a subsequence converging uniformly on compact subsets of $\Delta$.
:::
