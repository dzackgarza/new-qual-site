::: {.problem}
Let $H_d\subset\mathbb C[x,y]$ be the space of harmonic polynomials homogeneous of degree $d$.

1. Compute $\dim H_d$.
2. Give an explicit nonzero example
   \[
   p(x,y)=\sum a_{ij}x^iy^j\in H_d.
   \]
3. Show that the restrictions
   \[
   \bigoplus_d H_d\big|_{S^1}
   \]
   span a dense subset of $C(S^1)$ with the supremum norm.
4. Explain these results in terms of analytic functions.
:::
