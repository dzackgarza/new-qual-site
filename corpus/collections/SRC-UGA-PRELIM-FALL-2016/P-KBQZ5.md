::: {.problem}
Give an example of a linear map defined on $\RR^3$ with kernel generated by $(1,3,5)$.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

Let $T:\RR^3\to\RR^3$ be multiplication by
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -2 & 1 \\ 0 & 5 & -3 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}.
$$
Then $\ker T=\operatorname{span}\{(1,3,5)\}$.

::: pf-proof

$(x,y,z)\in\ker T$ exactly when $x-2y+z=0$ and $5y-3z=0$. The second equation gives $y=\frac35z$, and then the first gives $x=2y-z=\frac15z$. So
$$
\ker T=\theset{\tfrac z5(1,3,5)\suchthat z\in\RR}=\operatorname{span}\{(1,3,5)\}.
$$

:::
