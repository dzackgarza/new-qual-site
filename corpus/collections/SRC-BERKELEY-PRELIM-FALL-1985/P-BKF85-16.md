::: {.problem}
Let
\[
f(x)=x^5-8x^3+9x-3,
\qquad
g(x)=x^4-5x^2-6x+3.
\]
Prove that there is an integer $d$ such that $f$ and $g$ have a common root in
\[
\mathbb Q(\sqrt d).
\]
Determine $d$.
:::
