::: {.problem}
Find a product of cyclic groups of prime power order isomorphic to the group of units in the
ring of integers modulo 2016.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. One has
$$
2016=2^5\cdot3^2\cdot7=32\cdot9\cdot7.
$$
Hence
$$
(\ZZ/2016\ZZ)^\times
\cong
(\ZZ/32\ZZ)^\times
\times
(\ZZ/9\ZZ)^\times
\times
(\ZZ/7\ZZ)^\times.
$$

::: {.proof}
The integers $32,9,7$ are pairwise coprime, so the Chinese remainder theorem gives a ring isomorphism
$$
\ZZ/2016\ZZ
\cong
\ZZ/32\ZZ\times\ZZ/9\ZZ\times\ZZ/7\ZZ.
$$
Taking unit groups gives the displayed group isomorphism.
:::

<1>2. One has
$$
(\ZZ/32\ZZ)^\times
\cong
\ZZ_2\times\ZZ_8.
$$

::: {.proof}
The unit group modulo $32$ has
$$
\varphi(32)=16
$$
elements. The class of $-1$ has order $2$. The class of $3$ has order $8$, because
$$
3^2\equiv9,
\qquad
3^4\equiv17,
\qquad
3^8\equiv1
\pmod{32},
$$
while $3^4\not\equiv1\pmod{32}$.

Moreover $-1$ is not a power of $3$ modulo $32$: the powers
$$
1,3,9,27,17,19,25,11
$$
are the eight elements of $\langle3\rangle$, and none is $31$. Hence
$$
\langle-1\rangle\cap\langle3\rangle=\{1\}.
$$
Since the group is abelian, the subgroup generated by $-1$ and $3$ has order
$$
2\cdot8=16,
$$
so it is the whole unit group and is isomorphic to $\ZZ_2\times\ZZ_8$.
:::

<1>3. One has
$$
(\ZZ/9\ZZ)^\times
\cong
\ZZ_6
\cong
\ZZ_2\times\ZZ_3.
$$

::: {.proof}
The group has $\varphi(9)=6$ elements. The class of $2$ has order $6$: indeed,
$$
2^2=4\not\equiv1
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
2^3=8\equiv-1\pmod9,
$$
so its order is not $1$, $2$, or $3$, and
$$
2^6\equiv1\pmod9.
$$
Thus it generates the group. Since $2$ and $3$ are coprime, the cyclic group of order $6$ is isomorphic to $\ZZ_2\times\ZZ_3$.
:::

<1>4. One has
$$
(\ZZ/7\ZZ)^\times
\cong
\ZZ_6
\cong
\ZZ_2\times\ZZ_3.
$$

::: {.proof}
The group has $6$ elements. The class of $3$ has order $6$, since
$$
3^3=27\equiv-1\pmod7
$$
and hence $3^6\equiv1\pmod7$, while the order cannot be $1,2,$ or $3$. Thus the group is cyclic of order $6$, and the final decomposition follows from coprimality of $2$ and $3$.
:::

<1>5. Therefore
$$
\boxed{
(\ZZ/2016\ZZ)^\times
\cong
\ZZ_8
\times
\ZZ_2^3
\times
\ZZ_3^2
}.
$$

::: {.proof}
Combine steps <1>1--<1>4:
$$
(\ZZ/2016\ZZ)^\times
\cong
(\ZZ_2\times\ZZ_8)
\times
(\ZZ_2\times\ZZ_3)
\times
(\ZZ_2\times\ZZ_3).
$$
Reorder the direct factors. Every displayed cyclic factor has prime-power order.
:::

<1>6. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>5 gives the requested product of cyclic groups of prime-power order.
:::
