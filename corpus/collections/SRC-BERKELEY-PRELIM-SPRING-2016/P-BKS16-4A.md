::: {.problem}
Prove that a monic polynomial $p(z)$ with real coefficients is real-rooted if and only if $\Im(p'(z)/p(z)) < 0$ whenever $\Im(z) > 0$. ($\Im(z)$ denotes the imaginary part of $z$.)
:::
