::: {.problem}
Let
\[
f(z)=\sum f_n z^n\qquad\text{and}\qquad g(z)=\sum g_n z^n
\]
define holomorphic functions on a neighborhood of the closed unit disk $D=\{z:|z|\le 1\}$.
Prove that
\[
h(z)=\sum f_n g_n z^n
\]
also defines a holomorphic function on a neighborhood of $D$.
:::
