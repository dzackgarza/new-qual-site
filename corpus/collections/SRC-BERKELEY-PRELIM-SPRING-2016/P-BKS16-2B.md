::: {.problem}
Let $(f_i)_{i=1}^\infty$ and $g$ be twice-differentiable real-valued functions on $\mathbb R$, with $f_i''\ge 0$.
Suppose that
\[
\lim_{i\to\infty} f_i(x)=g(x)
\]
for all $x\in\mathbb R$.
Show that $g''\ge 0$.
:::
