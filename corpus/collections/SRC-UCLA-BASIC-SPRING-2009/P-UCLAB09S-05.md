::: {.problem}
Problem 5. Compute $e^{At}$ for
\[
A=\begin{pmatrix}2&1&5\\0&1&3\\1&0&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Recall that $e^{At}$ is characterized by
\[
\frac{dx}{dt}(t)=Ax(t)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
x(t)=e^{At}x(0).
\]
:::
