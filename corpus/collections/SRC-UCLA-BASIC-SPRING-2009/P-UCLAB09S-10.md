::: {.problem}
Problem 10. (a) Rigorously justify
\[
\int_0^1\frac{dx}{1+x^2}
=
\lim_{N\to\infty}\sum_{n=0}^N\frac{(-1)^n}{2n+1}.
\]

(b) Deduce the value of
\[
1-\frac13+\frac15-\frac17+\cdots.
\]
