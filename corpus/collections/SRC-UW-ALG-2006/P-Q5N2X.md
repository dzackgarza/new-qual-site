::: problem
Let $K$ be the field $\mathbb Q(z)$ of rational functions in a variable $z$ with coeffiecients in the rational field $\mathbb Q$.
Let $n$ be a positive integer.
Consider the polynomial $x^n-z\in K[x]$.

- Show that the polynomial $x^n-z$ is irreducible over $K$.

- Describe the splitting field of $x^n-z$ over $K$.

- Determine the Galois group of the splitting field of $x^5-z$ over the field $K$.
:::

::: {.solution}
**Goal.** For $K = \QQ(z)$, show $x^n - z$ is irreducible over $K$, describe its splitting field, and compute the Galois group for $n = 5$.

<1>1. $x^n - z$ is irreducible over $K = \QQ(z)$.
<2>1. $z$ is prime in $\QQ[z]$, and $x^n - z$ is Eisenstein at the prime $z$.
Proof: $z$ is irreducible in the UFD $\QQ[z]$; the polynomial $x^n - z$ has leading coefficient $1$, all non-leading coefficients $0$ (divisible by $z$), and constant term $-z$ not divisible by $z^2$.
<2>2. Hence $x^n - z$ is irreducible in $\QQ[z][x] = \QQ[z, x]$.
Proof: Eisenstein's criterion.
<2>3. Hence $x^n - z$ is irreducible in $\QQ(z)[x] = K[x]$.
Proof: Gauss's lemma — irreducibility in $\QQ[z][x]$ passes to the fraction field $\QQ(z)$.

<1>2. The splitting field of $x^n - z$ over $K$.
<2>1. Adjoin a root $\alpha$ with $\alpha^n = z$; then $x^n - z = \prod_{j=0}^{n-1} (x - \zeta_n^j \alpha)$.
Proof: the roots are $\zeta_n^j \alpha$ for $j = 0, \dots, n-1$, where $\zeta_n$ is a primitive $n$-th root of unity.
<2>2. The splitting field is $K(\alpha, \zeta_n) = \QQ(z)(\alpha, \zeta_n)$.
Proof: it is generated by one root $\alpha$ and the $n$-th roots of unity $\zeta_n$.

<1>3. The Galois group of the splitting field of $x^5 - z$ over $K$.
<2>1. The splitting field is $L = K(\alpha, \zeta_5)$ with $[K(\alpha) : K] = 5$ and $[K(\zeta_5) : K] = 4$.
Proof: $x^5 - z$ is irreducible of degree $5$ by <1>1, so $[K(\alpha):K] = 5$; the cyclotomic polynomial $\Phi_5$ has degree $4$ and is irreducible over $\QQ$, hence over $K$ (since $K \cap \QQ(\zeta_5) = \QQ$).
<2>2. $[L : K] = 20$.
Proof: $K(\alpha)$ and $K(\zeta_5)$ are linearly disjoint over $K$ (their degrees $5$ and $4$ are coprime), so $[L:K] = 5 \cdot 4 = 20$.
<2>3. $\Gal(L/K) \cong \ZZ/5 \rtimes (\ZZ/5)^\times \cong \ZZ/5 \rtimes \ZZ/4$.
Proof: an automorphism sends $\alpha \mapsto \zeta_5^a \alpha$ ($a \in \ZZ/5$) and $\zeta_5 \mapsto \zeta_5^b$ ($b \in (\ZZ/5)^\times$), giving the semidirect product $\ZZ/5 \rtimes (\ZZ/5)^\times$ of order $20$.

<1>4. Q.E.D.
Proof: <1>1, <1>2, and <1>3 answer the three parts.
:::
