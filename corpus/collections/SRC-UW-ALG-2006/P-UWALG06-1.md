::: {.problem}
Let $\ZZ$ be the ring of integers and let $\{0\}$ be the trivial $\ZZ$-module.
Let $G$ be a finitely generated $\ZZ$-module, i.e., a finitely generated Abelian group.
Show that if $G\otimes_{\ZZ}F=\{0\}$ for all fields $F$, then $G=\{0\}$.
:::
