::: {.problem}
Let $G$ be a finite group and $p$ a prime. Suppose $a,b\in G$ have order $p$ and
\[
b\notin\langle a\rangle.
\]
Prove that $G$ contains at least $p^2-1$ elements of order $p$.
:::
