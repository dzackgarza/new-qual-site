::: {.problem}
Let $\{f_n\}_{n=1}^\infty$ be a uniformly bounded equicontinuous sequence of real-valued functions on a compact metric space $(X,d)$. Define
\[
g_n(x)=\max\{f_1(x),\ldots,f_n(x)\}.
\]
Prove that $\{g_n\}$ converges uniformly on $X$.
:::
