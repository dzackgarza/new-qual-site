::: {.problem}
If $x$, $y$, and $z$ are positive integers such that $4x - 5y + 2z$ is divisible by $13$, then which one of the following must also be divisible by $13$?

(A) $x + 13y - z$
(B) $6x - 10y - z$
(C) $x - y - 2z$
(D) $-7x + 12y + 3z$
(E) $-5x + 3y - 4z$
:::
