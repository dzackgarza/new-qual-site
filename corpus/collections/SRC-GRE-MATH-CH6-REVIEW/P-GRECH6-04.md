::: {.problem}
Suppose positive integers $x,y,z$ satisfy $13\mid(4x-5y+2z)$.
Which expression must also be divisible by $13$?

(A) $x+13y-z$  (B) $6x-10y-z$  (C) $x-y-2z$  (D) $-7x+12y+3z$  (E) $-5x+3y-4z$
:::
