::: {.problem}
Let $G=\langle x,y\mid x^2=y^3,\ y^6=1,\ x^{-1}yx=y^{-1}\rangle$.
Express in simplest form the inverse of $z=x^{-2}yx^3y^3$.

(A) $y^{-2}x^{-1}$  (B) $xy^2$  (C) $xy$  (D) $yx$  (E) $y^2x$
:::
