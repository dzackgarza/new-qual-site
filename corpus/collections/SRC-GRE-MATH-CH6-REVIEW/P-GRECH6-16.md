::: {.problem}
Let $R$ be a ring; an element $x$ in $R$ is said to be idempotent if $x^2 = x$. How many idempotent elements does the ring $\ZZ_{20}$ contain?

(A) $2$
(B) $4$
(C) $5$
(D) $8$
(E) $10$
