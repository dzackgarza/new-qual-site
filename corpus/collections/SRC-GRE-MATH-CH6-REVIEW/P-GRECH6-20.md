::: {.problem}
Which of the following are subfields of $\mathbb C$?

I. $K_1=\{a+b\sqrt{2/3}:a,b\in\mathbb Q\}$.

II. $K_2=\{a+b\sqrt2:a,b\in\mathbb Q\text{ and }ab<\sqrt2\}$.

III. $K_3=\{a+bi:a,b\in\mathbb Z,\ i=\sqrt{-1}\}$.

(A) I only  (B) I and II only  (C) III only  (D) I and III only  (E) none of the $K_i$ are subfields of $\mathbb C$
:::
