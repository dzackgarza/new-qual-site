::: {.problem}
Which of the following are subfields of $\CC$?

I. $K_1 = \{a + b\sqrt{2/3} : a, b \in \QQ\}$

II. $K_2 = \{a + b\sqrt{2} : a, b \in \QQ \text{ and } ab < \sqrt{2}\}$

III. $K_3 = \{a + bi : a, b \in \ZZ \text{ and } i = \sqrt{-1}\}$

(A) I only
(B) I and II only
(C) III only
(D) I and III only
(E) None of the $K_i$ are subfields of $\CC$.
:::
