::: {.problem}
Let $S=\{a\in\mathbb R^+:a\ne1\}$ with $a\bullet b=a^{\log b}$, where $\log=\log_e$.
Then $(S,\bullet)$ is a group.
What is the inverse of $a\in S$?

(A) $1/(e\log a)$  (B) $e/\log a$  (C) $e^{-\log a}$  (D) $e^{\log(1/a)}$  (E) $e^{1/\log a}$
:::
