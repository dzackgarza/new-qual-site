::: {.problem}
Let $G$ be a group of order $9$ with identity $e$.
Which statement cannot be true?

(A) There is $x\ne e$ with $x^{-1}=x$.

(B) There is $x\ne e$ with $x^2=x^5$.

(C) There is $x$ with $|\langle x\rangle|=3$.

(D) $G$ is cyclic.

(E) $G$ is abelian.
:::
