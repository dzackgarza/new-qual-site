::: {.problem}
Which of the following rings are integral domains?

I. $\mathbb Z\oplus\mathbb Z$.

II. $\mathbb Z_p$, where $p$ is prime.

III. $\mathbb Z_{p^2}$, where $p$ is prime.

(A) I and II only  (B) II only  (C) II and III only  (D) III only  (E) I and III only
