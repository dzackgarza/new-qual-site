::: {.problem}
Let $x_1$ and $x_2$ be the two smallest positive integers for which the following statement is true: "$85x - 12$ is a multiple of $19$." Then $x_1 + x_2 =$

(A) $19$
(B) $27$
(C) $31$
(D) $38$
(E) $47$
