::: {.problem}
Consider
\[
x'=x^2+t^2,\qquad x(0)=1.
\]

1. Prove that there is a solution on $[0,b]$ for some $b>0$.
2. Give an explicit such $b$.
3. Find $c>0$ such that no solution exists on $[0,c]$.
:::
