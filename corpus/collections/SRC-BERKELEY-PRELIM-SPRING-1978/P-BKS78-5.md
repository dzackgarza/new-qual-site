:::{.problem}
Let $I=(x^2+x+1)\subset\mathbb Z_n[x]$.

1. For which $n$, $1\le n\le10$, is $\mathbb Z_n[x]/I$ a field?
2. Give the multiplication table for the resulting quotient when $n=2$.
