::: {.problem}
Let $f_n:[0,1]\to[0,1]$ be nondecreasing functions. Suppose $f_n(x)\to f(x)$ pointwise and $f$ is continuous. Prove that $f_n\to f$ uniformly on $[0,1]$. The functions $f_n$ need not be continuous.
:::
