::: {.problem}
Find all the subgroups of the dihedral group of order 12 (the group of symmetries of a regular hexagon).
:::

::: {.solution}
Write
$$
D_6
=
\langle r,s\mid r^6=s^2=1,\ srs=r^{-1}\rangle,
$$
where $r$ is rotation through $60^\circ$ and $s$ is one reflection.
The rotation subgroup is
$$
R\coloneqq\langle r\rangle.
$$

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

The subgroups contained in $R$ are exactly
$$
\{1\},
\qquad
\langle r^3\rangle,
\qquad
\langle r^2\rangle,
\qquad
\langle r\rangle.
$$

::: pf-proof

The group $R$ is cyclic of order $6$. A cyclic group has exactly one
subgroup for each divisor of its order. The displayed subgroups have
orders $1$, $2$, $3$, and $6$, respectively.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

Let $H\le D_6$ with $H\not\subseteq R$, and put
$$
K\coloneqq H\cap R.
$$
If $sr^j\in H$, then
$$
H=K\sqcup sr^jK.
$$

::: pf-proof

Every element of $D_6$ is either a rotation $r^a$ or a reflection
$sr^a$. Since $H\not\subseteq R$, choose a reflection $sr^j\in H$.

Every element of $K$ belongs to $H$. Conversely, if $sr^a\in H$, then
$$
(sr^j)^{-1}(sr^a)
=
r^{a-j}\in H\cap R=K.
$$
Hence $sr^a\in sr^jK$. Thus every element of $H$ lies in
$K\cup sr^jK$. The two sets are disjoint because one consists of
rotations and the other of reflections.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

If $K=\{1\}$, then
$$
H=\langle sr^j\rangle
$$
for a unique $j\in\{0,1,\ldots,5\}$. These give six distinct
reflection subgroups of order $2$.

::: pf-proof

By step [](#s2){.pf-ref},
$$
H=\{1,sr^j\}.
$$
Each reflection has order $2$. Distinct reflections give distinct
two-element subgroups, so the six values of $j$ yield six subgroups.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s4}

If
$$
K=\langle r^3\rangle,
$$
then
$$
H=\langle r^3,sr^j\rangle,
$$
and there are exactly three distinct such subgroups:
$$
\langle r^3,s\rangle,
\qquad
\langle r^3,sr\rangle,
\qquad
\langle r^3,sr^2\rangle.
$$

::: pf-proof

By step [](#s2){.pf-ref}, $H$ has $2|K|=4$ elements and is generated by $K$ and
$sr^j$. Since
$$
r^3(sr^j)=sr^{j+3},
$$
the choices $j$ and $j+3$ give the same subgroup. Thus only
$j\bmod 3$ matters, giving the three displayed subgroups. They are
distinct because their reflection sets are the disjoint pairs
$$
\{sr^j,sr^{j+3}\}.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s5}

If
$$
K=\langle r^2\rangle,
$$
then there are exactly two subgroups:
$$
\langle r^2,s\rangle
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\langle r^2,sr\rangle,
$$
each of order $6$.

::: pf-proof

Again step [](#s2){.pf-ref} gives
$$
H=\langle r^2,sr^j\rangle
$$
with order $2|K|=6$. Multiplying a reflection $sr^j$ by powers of
$r^2$ changes the exponent $j$ by an even integer. Hence the subgroup
depends only on the parity of $j$, yielding the two displayed choices.
Their reflection sets have exponents of opposite parity, so the two
subgroups are distinct.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s6}

If
$$
K=R,
$$
then
$$
H=D_6.
$$

::: pf-proof

The subgroup $H$ contains all rotations and at least one reflection.
These generate the full dihedral group.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s7}

The complete list of subgroups is
$$
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
&\{1\};\\
&\langle r^3\rangle,\quad
  \langle sr^j\rangle\ (0\le j<6);\\
&\langle r^2\rangle;\\
&\langle r^3,s\rangle,\quad
  \langle r^3,sr\rangle,\quad
  \langle r^3,sr^2\rangle;\\
&\langle r\rangle,\quad
  \langle r^2,s\rangle,\quad
  \langle r^2,sr\rangle;\\
&D_6.
\end{aligned}
}
$$

::: pf-proof

Step [](#s1){.pf-ref} lists all subgroups contained in the rotation subgroup.
Every remaining subgroup contains a reflection, and steps [](#s2){.pf-ref}, [](#s3){.pf-ref}, [](#s4){.pf-ref}, [](#s5){.pf-ref} and [](#s6){.pf-ref}
exhaust the four possibilities for
$$
H\cap R\in
\{\{1\},\langle r^3\rangle,\langle r^2\rangle,\langle r\rangle\}.
$$
Thus no other subgroups exist.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s8}

By order, the list consists of one subgroup of order $1$, seven
of order $2$, one of order $3$, three of order $4$, three of order
$6$, and one of order $12$.

::: pf-proof

This count follows directly from the explicit list in step [](#s7){.pf-ref} and
agrees with the order of each displayed generator set.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s7){.pf-ref} gives all subgroups, and step [](#s8){.pf-ref} records their counts.
