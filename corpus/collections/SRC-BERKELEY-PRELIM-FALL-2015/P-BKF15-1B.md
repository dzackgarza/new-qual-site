::: {.problem}
For a real, find a 2-dimensional space of real-valued solutions of $y ^ { \prime \prime } = a y / x ^ { 2 }$ for $x > 0$ . When $a = - 1 / 4$ find the solution with y = 0, y0 = 1 at x = 1.
