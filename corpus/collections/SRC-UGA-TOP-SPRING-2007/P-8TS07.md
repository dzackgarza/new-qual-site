::: problem
(a) State the Seifert–van Kampen theorem for the union of two spaces.

(b) Use this theorem to compute the fundamental group of the Klein bottle.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. Let $X=U\cup V$, where $U$, $V$, and $U\cap V$ are open and path-connected, and choose $x_0\in U\cap V$.
If
\[
i_U:\pi_1(U\cap V,x_0)\longrightarrow\pi_1(U,x_0),
\qquad
i_V:\pi_1(U\cap V,x_0)\longrightarrow\pi_1(V,x_0)
\]
are induced by inclusion, then Seifert--van Kampen gives
\[
\pi_1(X,x_0)
\cong
\pi_1(U,x_0)*_{\pi_1(U\cap V,x_0)}\pi_1(V,x_0).
\]
Equivalently,
\[
\pi_1(X,x_0)
\cong
\frac{\pi_1(U,x_0)*\pi_1(V,x_0)}
{\left\langle\!\left\langle
i_U(\gamma)i_V(\gamma)^{-1}:\gamma\in\pi_1(U\cap V,x_0)
\right\rangle\!\right\rangle}.
\]
::: {.proof}
This is the based two-set form of the Seifert--van Kampen theorem: the homomorphisms from the two factors induced by inclusion agree on $\pi_1(U\cap V,x_0)$, and the displayed amalgamated free product is their pushout in groups.
:::

<1>2. If $Y$ is obtained from a path-connected space $A$ by attaching one $2$-cell along a loop $\phi:S^1\to A$, then
\[
\pi_1(Y)
\cong
\pi_1(A)/\left\langle\!\left\langle[\phi]\right\rangle\!\right\rangle.
\]
::: {.proof}
Use the mapping-cylinder model for the attachment.
It has an open cover $U\cup V$ in which $U$ deformation retracts onto $A$, $V$ is contractible, and $U\cap V$ deformation retracts onto $S^1$.
Under these deformation retractions, the map
\[
\pi_1(U\cap V)\longrightarrow\pi_1(U)
\]
sends a generator to $[\phi]$, while the map to $\pi_1(V)=1$ is trivial.
Applying <1>1 therefore gives the stated quotient by the normal closure of $[\phi]$.
:::

<1>3. Give the Klein bottle $K$ its standard polygonal CW structure.
Its $1$-skeleton is $S^1\vee S^1$, with generators $a,b$, and its single $2$-cell is attached along the word
\[
aba^{-1}b.
\]
::: {.proof}
In the usual square model of the Klein bottle, one pair of opposite edges is identified with the same orientation and the other pair with opposite orientation.
After all four vertices are identified, the two edge classes give the two circles $a$ and $b$, and traversing the boundary of the square gives the attaching loop $aba^{-1}b$.
:::

<1>4. The fundamental group of the Klein bottle is
\[
\boxed{
\pi_1(K)
\cong
\left\langle a,b\mid aba^{-1}b=1\right\rangle
=
\left\langle a,b\mid aba^{-1}=b^{-1}\right\rangle.
}
\]
::: {.proof}
The group of the $1$-skeleton is
\[
\pi_1(S^1\vee S^1)\cong F(a,b).
\]
By <1>2 and <1>3, attaching the $2$-cell quotients this free group by the normal closure of $aba^{-1}b$.
This is exactly the displayed presentation.
:::
