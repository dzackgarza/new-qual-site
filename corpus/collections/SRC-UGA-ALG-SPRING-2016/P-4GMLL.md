::: problem
Let
\[
A=\left(\begin{array}{ccc}
-3 & 3 & -2 \\
-7 & 6 & -3 \\
1 & -1 & 2
\end{array}\right) \in M_{3}(\mathrm{C})
.\]

a.
Find the Jordan canonical form $J$ of $A$.

b.
Find an invertible matrix $P$ such that $P\inv A P = J$.
You do not need to compute $P\inv$.
:::
