::: {.problem}
Let $x = r \cos \theta$ and $y = r \sin \theta$. Which of the following expressions is equal to $\left(\frac{\partial \theta}{\partial r}\right)_x$, the partial derivative of $\theta$ with respect to $r$, keeping $x$ constant?

(A) $r^{-1} \tan \theta$
(B) $-r^{-1} \tan \theta$
(C) $r^{-1} \cot \theta$
(D) $-r^{-1} \cot \theta$
(E) $-r^{-1} \csc \theta$
:::
