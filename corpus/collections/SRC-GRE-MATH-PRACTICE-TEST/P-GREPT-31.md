::: {.problem}
Given that $\int_{0}^{\infty} e^{-x^2} \, dx = \frac{1}{2}\sqrt{\pi}$, what's the value of the following integral?

$$\int_{0}^{\infty} \frac{e^{-x}}{\sqrt{x}} \, dx$$

(A) $\frac{1}{4}\sqrt{\pi}$
(B) $\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{\pi}$
(C) $\sqrt{\pi}$
(D) $2\sqrt{\pi}$
(E) $\frac{1}{4}\pi$
:::
