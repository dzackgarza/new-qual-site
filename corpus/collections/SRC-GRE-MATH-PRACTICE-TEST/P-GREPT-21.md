::: {.problem}
For any real number $x$, let $[x]$ denote the unique integer, $n$, such that $n \le x < n + 1$. Evaluate the integral

$$\int_{-b}^{2b} (x - [x]) \, dx$$

for $b = \frac{1}{2}$.

(A) $\frac{3}{4}$
(B) $\frac{7}{8}$
(C) $\frac{5}{4}$
(D) $\frac{9}{8}$
(E) does not exist
:::
