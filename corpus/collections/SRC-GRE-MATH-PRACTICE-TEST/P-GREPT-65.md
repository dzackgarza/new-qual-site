::: {.problem}
If $\ZZ_n$ denotes the ring of integers modulo $n$, for which of the pairs of rings $(\ZZ_m, \ZZ_n)$ given below does there exist a ring homomorphism $\phi\colon \ZZ_m \to \ZZ_n$ such that $\phi(1) = 1$?

(A) $(\ZZ_4, \ZZ_6)$
(B) $(\ZZ_6, \ZZ_4)$
(C) $(\ZZ_3, \ZZ_6)$
(D) $(\ZZ_6, \ZZ_3)$
(E) $(\ZZ_3, \ZZ_4)$
:::
