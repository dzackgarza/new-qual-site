::: {.problem}
Let $T\colon \RR^2 \to \RR^2$ be the linear transformation that maps the point $(1, 2)$ to $(2, 3)$ and the point $(-1, 2)$ to $(2, -3)$. Then $T$ maps the point $(2, 1)$ to

(A) $(1, 6)$
(B) $(-1, 4)$
(C) $(3, 2)$
(D) $(-4, 3)$
(E) $(-3, 1)$
:::
