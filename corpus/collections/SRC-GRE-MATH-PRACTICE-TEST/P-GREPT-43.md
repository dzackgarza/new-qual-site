::: {.problem}
Let $L$ be the curve whose equation in the polar coordinates $r$ and $\theta$ is $r^2 = 4 \cos 2\theta$. Find the largest value of $y$ such that the point with rectangular coordinates $(x, y)$ is on $L$.

(A) $\frac{1+\sqrt{3}}{4}$
(B) $\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$
(C) $\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$
(D) $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
(E) $1$
:::
