::: {.problem}
Find the family of all nonzero solutions of the equation

$$\frac{d^2y}{dx^2} = \left(\frac{dy}{dx}\right)^2$$

(A) $y = \frac{1}{x + c_1} + c_2$
(B) $y = c_2 + \log(c_1 - x)$
(C) $y = c_2 - \log(c_1 - x^2)$
(D) $y = c_2 + \log(c_1 + x)$
(E) $y = c_2 - \log(c_1 + x)$
:::
