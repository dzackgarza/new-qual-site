::: {.problem}
For how many values of the parameter, $k$, will the following system have no solutions?

$$\begin{aligned} kx + y + z &= 1 \\ x + ky + z &= k \\ x + y + kz &= k^2 \end{aligned}$$

(A) $0$
(B) $1$
(C) $2$
(D) $4$
(E) Infinitely many
:::
