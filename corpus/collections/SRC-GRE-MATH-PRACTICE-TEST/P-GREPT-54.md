::: {.problem}
If $C$ is the circle in the complex plane whose equation is $\abs{z} = \pi$, oriented counterclockwise, find the value of the integral

$$\oint_{C} \left( \cos z - z \cos \frac{1}{z} \right) dz$$

(A) $-2\pi i$
(B) $-\pi i$
(C) $0$
(D) $\pi i$
(E) $2\pi i$
:::
