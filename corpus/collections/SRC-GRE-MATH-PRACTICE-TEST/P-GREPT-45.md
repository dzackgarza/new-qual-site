::: {.problem}
Let $C$ be the curve in the $xy$-plane given parametrically by the equations $x = te^{1-t}$ and $y = \arcsin t$, as the parameter $t$ increases from $0$ to $1$. Evaluate the integral

$$\int_C e^x (\sin y \, dx + \cos y \, dy)$$

(A) $1$
(B) $e$
(C) $\frac{\pi e}{2}$
(D) $2e$
(E) $\pi e$
:::
