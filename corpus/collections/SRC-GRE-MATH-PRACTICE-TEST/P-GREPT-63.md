::: {.problem}
Find the largest number $a$ that makes the following statement true:

"The series $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{(n!)^3}{(3n)!} x^{2n}$ converges for all $x$ with $\abs{x} < a$."

(A) $3$
(B) $3\sqrt{3}$
(C) $6$
(D) $6\sqrt{3}$
(E) $9$
:::
