::: {.problem}
If $m$ is an integer greater than $1$, let $\ZZ_m$ denote the ring of integers modulo $m$. Determine the smallest value of $n$ such that the set $A = \{\ZZ_n, \ZZ_{n+1}, \ZZ_{n+2}, \ZZ_{n+3}, \ZZ_{n+4}\}$ contains precisely one field.

(A) $2$
(B) $4$
(C) $6$
(D) $8$
(E) None of the above
:::
