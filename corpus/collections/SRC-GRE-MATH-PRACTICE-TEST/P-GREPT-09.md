::: {.problem}
Let $a$ be the smallest positive value of $x$ at which the function $f(x) = (\cos x^2)(\sin x^2)$ has a critical point. What is the value of $f(a)$?

(A) $\frac{\sqrt{2}}{4}$
(B) $\frac{1}{2}$
(C) $\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$
(D) $1$
(E) $\sqrt{2}$
:::
