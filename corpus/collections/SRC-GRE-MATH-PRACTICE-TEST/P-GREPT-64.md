::: {.problem}
If $A$ is the $2$ by $2$ matrix whose $(i, j)$ entry is equal to $i + j$, and $B$ is the $3$ by $3$ matrix whose $(i, j)$ entry is equal to $i + j$, find the value of the sum $\det A + \det B$.

(A) $-2$
(B) $-1$
(C) $0$
(D) $2$
(E) $5$
:::
