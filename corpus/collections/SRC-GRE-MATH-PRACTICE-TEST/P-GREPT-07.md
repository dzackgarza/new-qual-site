::: {.problem}
Let $f(x, y) = xy(x - y)$, where $x(t, u) = t(t - u)$ and $y(t, u) = u(u - t)$. What is the value of $\frac{\partial f}{\partial t}$ at the point $(t, u) = (1, -1)$?

(A) $-8$
(B) $-4$
(C) $2$
(D) $4$
(E) $8$
:::
