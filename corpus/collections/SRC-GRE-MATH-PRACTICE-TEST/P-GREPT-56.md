::: {.problem}
If $k$ is a nonzero constant, find the critical point of the function

$$f(x, y) = \frac{k^2}{x} + \frac{k}{y} + xy$$

(A) $(k, k)$
(B) $(-1, -k)$
(C) $(1, k)$
(D) $(-k, -1)$
(E) $(k, 1)$
:::
