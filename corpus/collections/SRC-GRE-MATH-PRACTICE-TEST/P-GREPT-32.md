::: {.problem}
Let $S$ be the surface whose equation is $x^2 - xy^2z + z^2 = 1$. The plane tangent to $S$ at the point $(1, 1, 1)$ contains which of the following lines?

(A) $x = y = z$
(B) $x = y$, $z = 1$
(C) $x = z$, $y = 1$
(D) $y = z$, $x = 1$
(E) $x - 1 = 2(y - 1) = z - 1$
:::
