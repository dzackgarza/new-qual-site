::: {.problem}
Define a function $f(x)$ for all positive $x$ by the equation

$$f(x) = \int_{x^2}^{x^3} (\log t) \, dt$$

Find the critical point(s) of this function.

(A) $x = \frac{4}{9}, 1$
(B) $x = 1$
(C) $x = \frac{3}{2}$
(D) $x = 1, \frac{3}{2}$
(E) $x = 1, \frac{9}{4}$
:::
