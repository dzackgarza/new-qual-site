::: {.problem}
Let $X$ be a random variable whose probability density function is

$$f(t) = \begin{cases} \frac{1}{2} e^{-(t-1)/2} & \text{if } 1 < t < \infty \\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$

What is the expectation (mean) of $X$?

(A) $1$
(B) $2$
(C) $3$
(D) $4$
(E) $6$
:::
