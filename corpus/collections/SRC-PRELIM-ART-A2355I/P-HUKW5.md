::: {.problem title="?"}
Define the Jacobian matrix of a mapping $f: \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}^m$ at a point $a = (a_1, \dots, a_n)$ of $\mathbb{R}^n$.
Compute the Jacobian matrix of $f(r,\theta) = (r\cos\theta, r\sin\theta)$ at $a = (0,0)$.
:::
