::: {.problem}
Let $a\in\mathbb R$ and let $I$ be an open interval containing $a$. For a function $f:I\to\mathbb R$, give the definition of continuity at $a$. Prove that
\[
f(x)=x^2
\]
is continuous at $a=2$.
:::
