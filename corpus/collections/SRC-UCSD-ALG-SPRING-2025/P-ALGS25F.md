::: {.problem}
Suppose $p$ is an odd prime.
Let $E$ be a splitting field of $x^p - 2x - 1$ over $\mathbb{F}_p$.
Prove that
\[
\operatorname{Gal}(E/\mathbb{F}_p) \simeq \langle 2 + p\mathbb{Z}\rangle \subseteq (\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z})^\times.
\]
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: pf-step

With the change of variables \(y=x+1\), one has
\[
x^p-2x-1=y^p-2y=y(y^{p-1}-2).
\]

::: pf-proof

Since the characteristic is \(p\),
\[
(y-1)^p=y^p-1.
\]
Substituting \(x=y-1\) gives
\[
(y-1)^p-2(y-1)-1=y^p-2y.
\]

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

Let \(\beta\ne0\) satisfy \(\beta^{p-1}=2\). Then the splitting field is
\[
E=\mathbb F_p(\beta).
\]

::: pf-proof

The nonzero roots of \(y^{p-1}-2\) are \(c\beta\) for \(c\in\mu_{p-1}\). But
\[
\mu_{p-1}=\mathbb F_p^\times,
\]
so adjoining one nonzero root \(\beta\) adjoins all of them. The remaining root \(y=0\) already lies in the base field. Translating back by \(x=y-1\) does not change the splitting field.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

The extension \(E/\mathbb F_p\) is finite Galois, and its Galois group is cyclic generated by Frobenius
\[
\operatorname{Fr}(z)=z^p.
\]

::: pf-proof

The field \(E\) is a finite extension of the finite field \(\mathbb F_p\), hence itself finite. Every finite extension of a finite field is Galois, with cyclic Galois group generated by Frobenius.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s4}

Frobenius acts on \(\beta\) by
\[
\operatorname{Fr}(\beta)=2\beta.
\]

::: pf-proof

Since \(\beta^{p-1}=2\),
\[
\beta^p=\beta^{p-1}\beta=2\beta.
\]

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s5}

For every \(k\ge0\),
\[
\operatorname{Fr}^k(\beta)=2^k\beta.
\]

::: pf-proof

Induct on \(k\), using step [](#s4){.pf-ref} and the fact that \(2\in\mathbb F_p\) is fixed by Frobenius.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s6}

The order of \(\operatorname{Fr}\) in \(\operatorname{Gal}(E/\mathbb F_p)\) is the multiplicative order of \(2\) in \(\mathbb F_p^\times\).

::: pf-proof

By step [](#s2){.pf-ref}, an automorphism of \(E\) is determined by its value on \(\beta\). By step [](#s5){.pf-ref},
\[
\operatorname{Fr}^k(\beta)=\beta
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
2^k=1\text{ in }\mathbb F_p^\times.
\]
Thus the least positive \(k\) for which Frobenius is the identity is exactly \(\operatorname{ord}_{\mathbb F_p^\times}(2)\).

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Hence
\[
\operatorname{Gal}(E/\mathbb F_p)
\cong
\langle 2+p\mathbb Z\rangle
\subseteq(\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z)^\times.
\]

::: pf-proof

By step [](#s3){.pf-ref} the Galois group is cyclic generated by Frobenius, and by step [](#s6){.pf-ref} its order equals the order of \(2+p\mathbb Z\) in \((\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z)^\times\). Two cyclic groups generated by elements of the same order are isomorphic, giving the displayed identification.

:::
