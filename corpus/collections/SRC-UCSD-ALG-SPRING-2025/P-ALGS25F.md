::: {.problem}
Suppose $p$ is an odd prime.
Let $E$ be a splitting field of $x^p - 2x - 1$ over $\FF_p$.
Prove that
$$
\operatorname{Gal}(E/\FF_p) \simeq \langle 2 + p\ZZ\rangle \subseteq (\ZZ/p\ZZ)^\times.
$$
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: pf-step

With the change of variables $y=x+1$, one has
$$
x^p-2x-1=y^p-2y=y(y^{p-1}-2).
$$

::: pf-proof

Since the characteristic is $p$,
$$
(y-1)^p=y^p-1.
$$
Substituting $x=y-1$ gives
$$
(y-1)^p-2(y-1)-1=y^p-2y.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

Let $\beta\ne0$ satisfy $\beta^{p-1}=2$. Then the splitting field is
$$
E=\FF_p(\beta).
$$

::: pf-proof

The nonzero roots of $y^{p-1}-2$ are $c\beta$ for $c\in\mu_{p-1}$. But
$$
\mu_{p-1}=\FF_p^\times,
$$
so adjoining one nonzero root $\beta$ adjoins all of them. The remaining root $y=0$ already lies in the base field. Translating back by $x=y-1$ does not change the splitting field.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

The extension $E/\FF_p$ is finite Galois, and its Galois group is cyclic generated by Frobenius
$$
\operatorname{Fr}(z)=z^p.
$$

::: pf-proof

The field $E$ is a finite extension of the finite field $\FF_p$, hence itself finite. Every finite extension of a finite field is Galois, with cyclic Galois group generated by Frobenius.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s4}

Frobenius acts on $\beta$ by
$$
\operatorname{Fr}(\beta)=2\beta.
$$

::: pf-proof

Since $\beta^{p-1}=2$,
$$
\beta^p=\beta^{p-1}\beta=2\beta.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s5}

For every $k\ge0$,
$$
\operatorname{Fr}^k(\beta)=2^k\beta.
$$

::: pf-proof

Induct on $k$, using step [](#s4){.pf-ref} and the fact that $2\in\FF_p$ is fixed by Frobenius.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s6}

The order of $\operatorname{Fr}$ in $\operatorname{Gal}(E/\FF_p)$ is the multiplicative order of $2$ in $\FF_p^\times$.

::: pf-proof

By step [](#s2){.pf-ref}, an automorphism of $E$ is determined by its value on $\beta$. By step [](#s5){.pf-ref},
$$
\operatorname{Fr}^k(\beta)=\beta
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
2^k=1\text{ in }\FF_p^\times.
$$
Thus the least positive $k$ for which Frobenius is the identity is exactly $\operatorname{ord}_{\FF_p^\times}(2)$.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Hence
$$
\operatorname{Gal}(E/\FF_p)
\cong
\langle 2+p\ZZ\rangle
\subseteq(\ZZ/p\ZZ)^\times.
$$

::: pf-proof

By step [](#s3){.pf-ref} the Galois group is cyclic generated by Frobenius, and by step [](#s6){.pf-ref} its order equals the order of $2+p\ZZ$ in $(\ZZ/p\ZZ)^\times$. Two cyclic groups generated by elements of the same order are isomorphic, giving the displayed identification.

:::
