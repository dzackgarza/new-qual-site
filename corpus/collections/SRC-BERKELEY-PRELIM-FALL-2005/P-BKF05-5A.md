::: {.problem}
Does there exist a differentiable function \(f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R\) such that
\[
f(0)=1
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
f'(x)\ge f(x)^2
\]
for every \(x\in\mathbb R\)? Prove your answer.
:::
