::: {.problem}
Let \(A\) be a real \(n\times n\) matrix such that
\[
(A-I)^m=0
\]
for some \(m\ge1\). Prove that there is a real \(n\times n\) matrix \(B\) satisfying
\[
B^2=A.
\]
:::
