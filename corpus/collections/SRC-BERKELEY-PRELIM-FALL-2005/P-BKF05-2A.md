::: {.problem}
Let
\[
f(z)=\frac{P(z)}{Q(z)}
\]
be a rational function with complex coefficients and \(\deg P<\deg Q\). Prove that \(f\) is a sum of terms of the form
\[
\frac{a}{(z-b)^k},\qquad a,b\in\mathbb C.
\]
:::
