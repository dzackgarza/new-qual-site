::: {.problem}
Let \(V\) be a finite-dimensional complex inner-product space and let \(T:V\to V\) be Hermitian.

(a) Prove that \(I+iT\) is invertible.

(b) Prove that
\[
(I-iT)(I+iT)^{-1}
\]
is unitary.
:::
