::: {.problem}
Let \(f:[0,\infty)\to\mathbb R\) be continuous, nonnegative, nonincreasing, and satisfy
\[
\lim_{x\to\infty}f(x)=0.
\]
Show that the improper integral
\[
\int_0^\infty f(x)\sin x\,dx
\]
converges.
:::
