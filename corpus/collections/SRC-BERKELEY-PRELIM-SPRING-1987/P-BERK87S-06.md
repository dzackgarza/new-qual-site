:::{.problem}
Let $f$ be analytic in the open unit disk and suppose
\[
|f(z)|\le\frac{C}{1-|z|}
\]
for all $z$ in the disk, where $C>0$. Prove that
\[
|f'(z)|\le\frac{4C}{(1-|z|)^2}.
\]
:::
