::: {.problem}
Let $p(z)$ be a nonconstant complex polynomial all of whose roots satisfy
\[
\operatorname{Re}z>0.
\]
Prove that every root of $p'$ also satisfies
\[
\operatorname{Re}z>0.
\]
:::
