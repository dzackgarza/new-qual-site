:::{.problem}
Let $A$ be an $m\times n$ matrix with rational entries and let $b\in\mathbb Q^m$. Prove or disprove: if
\[
Ax=b
\]
has a solution $x\in\mathbb C^n$, then it has a solution $x\in\mathbb Q^n$.
:::
