:::{.problem}
Let
\[
W=\{(u,v)\in\mathbb R^2:u>v\}
\]
and define
\[
T(u,v)=(u+v,u^2+v^2).
\]

1. Prove that $T$ is locally one-to-one.
2. Determine the range of $T$ and prove that $T$ is globally one-to-one.
:::
