::: {.problem}
Let $\mathcal F$ be a uniformly bounded, equicontinuous family of real-valued functions on a metric space $(X,d)$. Prove that
\[
g(x)=\sup\{f(x):f\in\mathcal F\}
\]
is continuous.
:::
