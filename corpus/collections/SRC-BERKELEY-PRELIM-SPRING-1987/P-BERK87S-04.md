:::{.problem}
Let $F$ be a finite field with $q$ elements. For $f\in F[x]$, write
\[
\varphi_f(a)=f(a)
\qquad(a\in F).
\]
Prove that every function $\varphi:F\to F$ equals $\varphi_f$ for some $f\in F[x]$, and that $f$ is determined by $\varphi$ up to addition of a multiple of
\[
x^q-x.
\]
:::
