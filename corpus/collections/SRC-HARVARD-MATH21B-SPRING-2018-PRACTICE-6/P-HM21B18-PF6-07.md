::: {.problem}
Consider
\[
x_{n+1}=2y_n-3x_n,\qquad y_{n+1}=x_n-2y_n,
\]
with $x_0=5$ and $y_0=3$.

(a) Find a closed-form solution.

(b) A system is called Lyapunov stable here if for every initial condition $(x_0,y_0)$ the sequence $(x_n,y_n)$ stays in a bounded region. Decide, without further justification, whether the system is Lyapunov stable and whether it is asymptotically stable.
:::
