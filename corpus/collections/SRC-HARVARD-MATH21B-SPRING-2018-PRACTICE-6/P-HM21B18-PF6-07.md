::: {.problem}
Consider
\[
x_{n+1}=2y_n-3x_n,\qquad y_{n+1}=x_n-2y_n,
\]
with $x_0=5$ and $y_0=3$.

(a) Find a closed-form solution.

(b) A system is called Lyapunov stable here if for every initial condition $(x_0,y_0)$ the sequence $(x_n,y_n)$ stays in a bounded region.
Decide, without further justification, whether the system is Lyapunov stable and whether it is asymptotically stable.
:::

::: solution
Let
$$
z_n=\binom{x_n}{y_n},
\qquad
A=\begin{pmatrix}-3&2\\1&-2\end{pmatrix}.
$$
Then $z_{n+1}=Az_n$.

<1>1. Diagonalize the recurrence matrix.
::: proof
The characteristic polynomial is
$$
\det(\lambda I-A)
=\lambda^2+5\lambda+4
=(\lambda+4)(\lambda+1).
$$
Thus the eigenvalues are $-4$ and $-1$. Corresponding eigenvectors are
$$
v_{-4}=\binom{-2}{1},
\qquad
v_{-1}=\binom11.
$$
Indeed,
$$
Av_{-4}=-4v_{-4},
\qquad
Av_{-1}=-v_{-1}.
$$
:::

<1>2. Expand the initial condition in the eigenbasis.
::: proof
Write
$$
\binom53=c_1\binom{-2}{1}+c_2\binom11.
$$
The two coordinate equations are
$$
-2c_1+c_2=5,
\qquad
c_1+c_2=3,
$$
which give
$$
c_1=-\frac23,
\qquad
c_2=\frac{11}{3}.
$$
Therefore
$$
\boxed{
\binom{x_n}{y_n}
=-\frac23(-4)^n\binom{-2}{1}
+\frac{11}{3}(-1)^n\binom11.}
$$
Equivalently,
$$
x_n=\frac43(-4)^n+\frac{11}{3}(-1)^n,
\qquad
y_n=-\frac23(-4)^n+\frac{11}{3}(-1)^n.
$$
:::

<1>3. Determine stability.
::: proof
The eigenvalue $-4$ has modulus $4>1$. Initial data with a nonzero component
along $v_{-4}$ therefore produce trajectories whose norms grow like $4^n$.
Hence the system is not Lyapunov stable in the stated sense. In particular it
cannot be asymptotically stable either.

Thus
$$
\boxed{\text{Lyapunov stable: no; asymptotically stable: no}.}
$$
:::
:::
