::: {.problem}
Consider
\[
u_{tt}=T(u)=u_{xx}+u_{yy}-u_{xxxx}
\]
on $-\pi\le x\le\pi$, $-\pi\le y\le\pi$.

(a) Verify that $\sin(nx)\sin(my)$ is an eigenfunction of $T$ and find the corresponding eigenvalue $\lambda_{nm}$.

(b) Find the solution when
\[
u(x,y,0)=\sum_{n,m=1}^{\infty}\frac1{nm}\sin(nx)\sin(my)
\]
and the initial velocity is zero.

(c) Find the solution when the initial position is identically zero and
\[
u_t(x,y,0)=\sin(4x)\sin(6y).
\]
:::
