::: {.problem}
Consider
\[
u_{tt}=T(u)=u_{xx}+u_{yy}-u_{xxxx}
\]
on $-\pi\le x\le\pi$, $-\pi\le y\le\pi$.

(a) Verify that $\sin(nx)\sin(my)$ is an eigenfunction of $T$ and find the corresponding eigenvalue $\lambda_{nm}$.

(b) Find the solution when
\[
u(x,y,0)=\sum_{n,m=1}^{\infty}\frac1{nm}\sin(nx)\sin(my)
\]
and the initial velocity is zero.

(c) Find the solution when the initial position is identically zero and
\[
u_t(x,y,0)=\sin(4x)\sin(6y).
\]
:::

::: {.solution}
For $n,m\ge1$, put
$$
\phi_{nm}(x,y)=\sin(nx)\sin(my).
$$

<1>1. Compute the spatial eigenvalue of $\phi_{nm}$.
::: {.proof}
The relevant derivatives are
$$
(\phi_{nm})_{xx}=-n^2\phi_{nm},
\qquad
(\phi_{nm})_{yy}=-m^2\phi_{nm},
$$
and
$$
(\phi_{nm})_{xxxx}=n^4\phi_{nm}.
$$
Therefore
$$
T(\phi_{nm})
=-(n^4+n^2+m^2)\phi_{nm}.
$$
Thus
$$
\boxed{\lambda_{nm}=-(n^4+n^2+m^2).}
$$
It is convenient to write
$$
\omega_{nm}=\sqrt{n^4+n^2+m^2},
$$
so that $\lambda_{nm}=-\omega_{nm}^2$.
:::

<1>2. Propagate the initial position from part (b).
::: {.proof}
If a single mode has time-dependent amplitude $a_{nm}(t)$, then
$$
u(x,y,t)=a_{nm}(t)\phi_{nm}(x,y)
$$
reduces the PDE to
$$
a_{nm}''+\omega_{nm}^2a_{nm}=0.
$$
Zero initial velocity and initial amplitude $1/(nm)$ give
$$
a_{nm}(t)=\frac1{nm}\cos(\omega_{nm}t).
$$
By linearity, the modal solution for the stated initial position is
$$
\boxed{
u(x,y,t)=
\sum_{n,m=1}^{\infty}
\frac1{nm}
\cos\!\left(\sqrt{n^4+n^2+m^2}\,t\right)
\sin(nx)\sin(my).}
$$
At $t=0$ this recovers the prescribed Fourier series, and differentiating in
$t$ gives zero initial velocity.
:::

<1>3. Solve the single-mode initial-velocity problem in part (c).
::: {.proof}
For $n=4$ and $m=6$,
$$
\omega_{46}^2=4^4+4^2+6^2=256+16+36=308.
$$
With zero initial displacement and initial modal velocity $1$, the oscillator
amplitude is
$$
a(t)=\frac{\sin(\sqrt{308}\,t)}{\sqrt{308}}.
$$
Hence
$$
\boxed{
u(x,y,t)=
\frac{\sin(\sqrt{308}\,t)}{\sqrt{308}}
\sin(4x)\sin(6y).}
$$
Indeed $u(x,y,0)=0$, while differentiating gives
$u_t(x,y,0)=\sin(4x)\sin(6y)$.
:::
