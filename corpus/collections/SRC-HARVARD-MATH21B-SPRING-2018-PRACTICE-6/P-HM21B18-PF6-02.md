::: {.problem}
No justifications are required.

(a) Circle all matrices similar to
\[
\begin{pmatrix}-1&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}
\]
among
\[
A=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&-1\end{pmatrix},\quad
B=\begin{pmatrix}-1&0\\1&1\end{pmatrix},\quad
C=\begin{pmatrix}2&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix},
\]
\[
D=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix},\quad
E=\begin{pmatrix}1&-2\\1&-2\end{pmatrix}.
\]

(b) Match the five displayed differential equations, with repetitions, to the five functions A--E so that each function is used exactly once:
\[
f''+f=t,\qquad f''-f=t,\qquad f''=t,\qquad f''-f=t,\qquad f''+f=t,
\]
where
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm A)&\ f(t)=e^t-t,\\
\mathrm B)&\ f(t)=t+\cos t-\sin t,\\
\mathrm C)&\ f(t)=1-t+t^3/6,\\
\mathrm D)&\ f(t)=e^t-e^{-t}-t,\\
\mathrm E)&\ f(t)=t.
\end{aligned}
\]
:::

::: solution
<1>1. The matrices similar to $\operatorname{diag}(-1,1)$ are exactly $A$ and $B$.
::: proof
The target matrix has characteristic polynomial
$$
(\lambda+1)(\lambda-1)=\lambda^2-1.
$$
Because its two eigenvalues are distinct, any $2\times2$ matrix with trace
$0$ and determinant $-1$ is diagonalizable with eigenvalues $-1,1$ and is
therefore similar to it.

For the five candidates,
$$
\begin{array}{c|cc}
&\operatorname{tr}&\det\\ \hline
A&0&-1\\
B&0&-1\\
C&2&0\\
D&2&0\\
E&-1&0
\end{array}
$$
so precisely
$$
\boxed{A\text{ and }B}
$$
are similar to $\operatorname{diag}(-1,1)$.
:::

<1>2. A valid matching for the five differential equations, in displayed order, is
$$
\boxed{B,\ A,\ C,\ D,\ E}.
$$
::: proof
Differentiate the candidates:

For $A(t)=e^t-t$,
$$
A''-A=e^t-(e^t-t)=t.
$$

For $B(t)=t+\cos t-\sin t$,
$$
B''+B=(-\cos t+\sin t)+(t+\cos t-\sin t)=t.
$$

For $C(t)=1-t+t^3/6$,
$$
C''=t.
$$

For $D(t)=e^t-e^{-t}-t$,
$$
D''-D=(e^t-e^{-t})-(e^t-e^{-t}-t)=t.
$$

For $E(t)=t$,
$$
E''+E=t.
$$
Thus the displayed equations
$$
f''+f=t,\quad f''-f=t,\quad f''=t,\quad f''-f=t,\quad f''+f=t
$$
may be matched as $B,A,C,D,E$. Since the first and last equations coincide,
$B$ and $E$ may be interchanged; similarly $A$ and $D$ may be interchanged
between the two $f''-f=t$ slots.
:::
:::
