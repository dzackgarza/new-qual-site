::: {.problem}
Find all solutions of
\[
\begin{aligned}
x+z-u+v&=2,\\
x+y+z+u+v&=6,\\
x+y-z-u+v&=0
\end{aligned}
\]
for the five variables $x,y,z,u,v$, using row reduction.
:::
