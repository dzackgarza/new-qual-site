::: {.problem}
Find all solutions of
\[
\begin{aligned}
x+z-u+v&=2,\\
x+y+z+u+v&=6,\\
x+y-z-u+v&=0
\end{aligned}
\]
for the five variables $x,y,z,u,v$, using row reduction.
:::

::: {.solution}
The augmented matrix is
$$
\left[
\begin{array}{ccccc|c}
1&0&1&-1&1&2\\
1&1&1&1&1&6\\
1&1&-1&-1&1&0
\end{array}
\right].
$$

<1>1. Row-reduce the augmented matrix.
::: {.proof}
Subtract the first row from the second and third:
$$
\left[
\begin{array}{ccccc|c}
1&0&1&-1&1&2\\
0&1&0&2&0&4\\
0&1&-2&0&0&-2
\end{array}
\right].
$$
Next replace the third row by the third minus the second:
$$
\left[
\begin{array}{ccccc|c}
1&0&1&-1&1&2\\
0&1&0&2&0&4\\
0&0&-2&-2&0&-6
\end{array}
\right].
$$
Dividing the last row by $-2$ and clearing the $z$-entry from the first row gives
$$
\operatorname{rref}=
\left[
\begin{array}{ccccc|c}
1&0&0&-2&1&-1\\
0&1&0&2&0&4\\
0&0&1&1&0&3
\end{array}
\right].
$$
:::

<1>2. Parametrize all solutions.
::: {.proof}
The pivot variables are $x,y,z$ and the free variables are $u,v$. Put
$$
u=r,\qquad v=s.
$$
Then the reduced equations give
$$
x=-1+2r-s,\qquad y=4-2r,\qquad z=3-r.
$$
Hence every solution is
$$
\boxed{
\begin{pmatrix}x\\y\\z\\u\\v\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}-1\\4\\3\\0\\0\end{pmatrix}
+r\begin{pmatrix}2\\-2\\-1\\1\\0\end{pmatrix}
+s\begin{pmatrix}-1\\0\\0\\0\\1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad r,s\in\mathbb R.}
$$
Substitution into the three original equations verifies the parametrization.
:::
:::
