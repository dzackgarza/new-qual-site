::: {.problem}
(a) Using the least-squares method, find the best fit of the form
\[
y=ax^2+b2^x
\]
to the data points $(1,2)$, $(0,1)$, and $(2,4)$.

(b) For the data points $(0,1)$, $(0,2)$, and $(0,0)$ there is more than one minimizing fit; for example, both $y=x^2+2^x$ and $y=2x^2+2^x$ minimize the error. Why does the least-squares formula fail here? Choose exactly one:

1. the matrix $A$ in the least-squares formula is undefined;
2. the matrix $A$ is not invertible;
3. the matrix $A^TA$ is not invertible.
:::
