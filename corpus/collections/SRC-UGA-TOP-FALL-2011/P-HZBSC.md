::: problem
Let 
$$
V = \DD^2 \times S^1 = \theset{ (z, e^{it}) \suchthat \norm z \leq 1,~~ 0 \leq t < 2\pi}
$$ 
be the "solid torus" with boundary given by the torus $T = S^1 \times S^1$ . 

For $n \in \ZZ$ define 
\begin{align*}
\phi_n : T &\to T \\
(e^{is} , e^{it} ) &\mapsto (e^{is} , e^{i(ns+t)})
.\end{align*}

Find the fundamental group of the identification space
$$
V_n = {V\disjoint V \over \sim n}
$$
where the equivalence relation $\sim_n$ identifies a point $x$ on the boundary $T$ of the first copy of $V$ with the point $\phi_n (x)$ on the boundary of the second copy of $V$.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. $\pi_1(V) \cong \mathbb{Z}$, generated by the $S^1$ factor (the core), while the meridian $\mu = S^1 \times \{\text{pt}\}$ (the boundary of the $D^2$ factor) is null-homotopic in $V$.
::: {.proof}
$V = D^2 \times S^1$ deformation retracts onto $\{0\} \times S^1$.
:::

<1>2. $\pi_1(T) \cong \mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}$, generated by $\mu$ and $\lambda = \{\text{pt}\} \times S^1$.
::: {.proof}
$T = S^1 \times S^1$.
:::

<1>3. The inclusion $T \hookrightarrow V$ induces $i_* : \mathbb{Z}\mu \times \mathbb{Z}\lambda \to \mathbb{Z}$, with $i_*(\mu) = 0$ and $i_*(\lambda) = 1$.
::: {.proof}
<1>1.
:::

<1>4. By van Kampen, $\pi_1(V_n) = \pi_1(V) * \pi_1(V) / N$, where $N$ is the normal closure of $\{i_{1*}(g) \, i_{2*}(\phi_{n*}(g))^{-1} : g \in \pi_1(T)\}$.
::: {.proof}
van Kampen for the union of two solid tori glued along $T$.
:::

<1>5. $\phi_{n*} : \pi_1(T) \to \pi_1(T)$ sends $\mu \mapsto \mu$ and $\lambda \mapsto n\mu + \lambda$.
::: {.proof}
$\phi_n(e^{is}, e^{it}) = (e^{is}, e^{i(ns+t)})$.
:::

<1>6. Let $a$ be the generator of $\pi_1$ of the first $V$ and $b$ that of the second $V$.
::: {.proof}
notation.
:::

<1>7. The relation from $g = \mu$ is $0 = 0$ (trivial).
::: {.proof}
$i_{1*}(\mu) = 0$ and $i_{2*}(\phi_{n*}(\mu)) = i_{2*}(\mu) = 0$.
:::

<1>8. The relation from $g = \lambda$ is $a = b$.
::: {.proof}
$i_{1*}(\lambda) = a$ and $i_{2*}(\phi_{n*}(\lambda)) = i_{2*}(n\mu + \lambda) = b$.
:::

<1>9. Hence $\pi_1(V_n) \cong \langle a, b \mid a = b \rangle \cong \mathbb{Z}$.
::: {.proof}
<1>4, <1>7, <1>8.
:::

<1>10. Q.E.D.
::: {.proof}
<1>9.
:::
:::
