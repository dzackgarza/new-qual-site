::: problem
Let $f(z) = \pi^2 z^5 e^{-2z} - 1$.
How many roots does $f$ have in $\mathbb{D}$?
How many simple roots does $f$ have in $\mathbb{D}$?

Hint: $e < \pi$.
:::
