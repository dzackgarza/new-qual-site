::: {.problem}
Let $(X,\mathcal B,\mu)$ be a measure space and let $f\ge0$ be integrable.

(a) Prove that for every $a>0$,
\[
\mu\{x:f(x)\ge a\}<\infty.
\]

(b) Prove that $\{x:f(x)>0\}$ is a countable union of sets of finite measure.
:::
