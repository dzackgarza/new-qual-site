::: {.problem}
Let $(X,\mathcal B,\mu)$ satisfy $\mu(X)<\infty$.
Let $(f_n)$ be a uniformly bounded sequence of measurable functions and suppose $f_n\to0$ in measure.
Prove
\[
\int_X|f_n|\,d\mu\to0.
\]
:::
