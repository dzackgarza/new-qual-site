::: {.problem}
Let $(X,\mathcal B,\mu)$ be a measure space and let $A_n\in\mathcal B$ satisfy
\[
\sum_{n=1}^\infty\mu(A_n)<\infty.
\]
Prove
\[
\mu\!\left(\bigcap_{n=1}^\infty\bigcup_{k=n}^\infty A_k\right)=0.
\]
:::
