::: {.problem}
Define paths $f,g:I\to S^2$ by
\[
f(s)=(\cos(2\pi s),\sin(2\pi s),0),
\qquad
g(s)=(1,0,0).
\]
Prove that $f$ is homotopic to $g$ relative to $\{0,1\}$; that is, prove that the two loops are path-homotopic.
:::
