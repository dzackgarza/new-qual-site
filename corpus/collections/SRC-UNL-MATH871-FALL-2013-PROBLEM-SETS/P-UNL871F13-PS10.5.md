::: {.problem}
Let $X=\{a,b\}$ have the indiscrete topology.
Compute the fundamental group
$$
\pi_1(X,a),
$$
and prove your answer.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

Every function from any topological space into $X$ is continuous.


::: pf-proof

The only open subsets of the indiscrete space $X$ are
$$
\varnothing
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
X.
$$
For any function
$$
f:Y\to X,
$$
$$
f^{-1}(\varnothing)
=
\varnothing
$$
and
$$
f^{-1}(X)
=
Y,
$$
both of which are open in $Y$.
Thus $f$ is continuous.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

In particular, every function
$$
\gamma:[0,1]\to X
$$
with
$$
\gamma(0)=\gamma(1)=a
$$
is a based loop at $a$.


::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref}, every such set map is continuous.
The endpoint equalities make it a loop based at $a$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

Let
$$
\gamma_0,\gamma_1:[0,1]\to X
$$
be two based loops at $a$.
Define
$$
H:[0,1]\times[0,1]\to X
$$
by
$$
H(s,t)
=
\begin{cases}
\gamma_0(s),&t=0,\\
\gamma_1(s),&t=1,\\
a,&0<t<1.
\end{cases}
$$
Then $H$ is continuous.


::: pf-proof

The displayed rule defines a set map into $X$.
By step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref}, every such map is continuous because the target is
indiscrete.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

The homotopy $H$ is relative to the endpoints.


::: pf-proof

For
$$
t=0,
$$
$$
H(s,0)
=
\gamma_0(s).
$$
For
$$
t=1,
$$
$$
H(s,1)
=
\gamma_1(s).
$$

Also, because both loops are based at $a$,
$$
\gamma_0(0)
=
\gamma_0(1)
=
\gamma_1(0)
=
\gamma_1(1)
=
a.
$$
Hence for every
$$
t\in[0,1],
$$
$$
H(0,t)
=
H(1,t)
=
a.
$$
Thus the endpoints remain fixed throughout the homotopy.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-5}

Therefore every two based loops at $a$ are path-homotopic.


::: pf-proof

Steps [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} through [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} construct a homotopy rel endpoints between arbitrary
based loops.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Hence there is exactly one element of
$$
\pi_1(X,a).
$$


::: pf-proof

The fundamental group consists of based loops modulo path homotopy.
By step [](#s1-5){.pf-ref}, all based loops lie in the same homotopy class.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-7}

Consequently
$$
\boxed{
\pi_1(X,a)=0.
}
$$


::: pf-proof

A group with one element is the trivial group.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-7){.pf-ref} is the required computation.

:::

:::
