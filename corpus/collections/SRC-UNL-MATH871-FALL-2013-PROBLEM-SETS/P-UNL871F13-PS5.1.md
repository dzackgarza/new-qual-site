::: {.problem}
Let $f:X\to Y$ and $g:Y\to Z$ be homeomorphisms.
Prove that the inverse map
\[
f^{-1}:Y\to X
\]
and the composite
\[
g\circ f:X\to Z
\]
are homeomorphisms.
:::
