::: {.problem}
Let $X_f$ be the mapping cylinder of a continuous map $f:X\to Y$, and let
\[
j:Y\to X_f,\qquad j(y)=[y]
\]
denote the canonical inclusion.
Write $\overline Y=j(Y)$.

1. Define
   \[
   r:X_f\to\overline Y,
   \qquad
   r([(x,s)])=[f(x)],\quad r([y])=[y].
   \]
   Show that $r$ is a retraction.
   Show that
   \[
   F_t([(x,s)])=[(x,t+(1-t)s)],\qquad F_t([y])=[y]
   \]
   defines a deformation retraction of $X_f$ onto $\overline Y$.

2. Show that $j$ is an embedding and conclude that $X_f$ is homotopy equivalent to $Y$.
:::
