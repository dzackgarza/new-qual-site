::: {.problem}
Let $X$ be a metrizable space, let $p\in X$, and let $C\subseteq X$ be closed with $p\notin C$.
Prove that there are disjoint open sets $U,V\subseteq X$ such that
\[
p\in U,\qquad C\subseteq V.
\]
:::
