::: {.problem}
Construct an explicit deformation retraction of
\[
\mathbb R^n\setminus\{0\}
\]
onto $S^{n-1}$.
:::

Define
\[
H:
(\mathbb R^n\setminus\{0\})\times[0,1]
\longrightarrow
\mathbb R^n\setminus\{0\}
\]
by
\[
\boxed{
H(x,t)
=
\left(
(1-t)
+
\frac{t}{\|x\|}
\right)x.
}
\]

::: pf

::: pf-step

The map $H$ is continuous.


::: pf-proof

On
\[
\mathbb R^n\setminus\{0\},
\]
the norm
\[
x\mapsto\|x\|
\]
is continuous and strictly positive.
Therefore
\[
x\mapsto\frac1{\|x\|}
\]
is continuous.

The scalar coefficient
\[
(x,t)
\longmapsto
(1-t)+\frac{t}{\|x\|}
\]
is continuous, and scalar multiplication in $\mathbb R^n$ is continuous.
Hence $H$ is continuous.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

For every
\[
x\ne0
\]
and every
\[
t\in[0,1],
\]
one has
\[
H(x,t)\ne0.
\]


::: pf-proof

The scalar
\[
(1-t)+\frac{t}{\|x\|}
\]
is strictly positive because both summands are nonnegative and at least one
is positive.
Multiplying a nonzero vector $x$ by a positive scalar cannot give zero.
Thus $H$ indeed takes values in
\[
\mathbb R^n\setminus\{0\}.
\]

:::

:::

::: pf-step

At homotopy time
\[
t=0,
\]
\[
H(x,0)=x.
\]


::: pf-proof

Substitute $t=0$:
\[
H(x,0)
=
(1+0)x
=
x.
\]
Thus
\[
H(-,0)=\operatorname{id}_{\mathbb R^n\setminus\{0\}}.
\]

:::

:::

::: pf-step

At homotopy time
\[
t=1,
\]
\[
H(x,1)
=
\frac{x}{\|x\|}
\in
S^{n-1}.
\]


::: pf-proof

Substitute $t=1$:
\[
H(x,1)
=
\frac1{\|x\|}x.
\]
Its norm is
\[
\left\|
\frac{x}{\|x\|}
\right\|
=
1.
\]
Hence
\[
H(-,1)
\]
is the radial retraction onto the unit sphere.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Every point of
\[
S^{n-1}
\]
is fixed throughout the homotopy.


::: pf-proof

Let
\[
x\in S^{n-1}.
\]
Then
\[
\|x\|=1.
\]
Therefore
\[
\begin{aligned}
H(x,t)
&=
\left(
(1-t)+t
\right)x\\
&=
x
\end{aligned}
\]
for every
\[
t\in[0,1].
\]

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Consequently
\[
\boxed{
H
\text{ is a deformation retraction of }
\mathbb R^n\setminus\{0\}
\text{ onto }
S^{n-1}.
}
\]


::: pf-proof

Step <1>3 gives the identity at time $0$.
Step <1>4 gives a retraction onto the sphere at time $1$.
Step <1>5 fixes the sphere pointwise for all times.
These are exactly the conditions for a deformation retraction.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step <1>6 is the desired conclusion.

:::

:::
