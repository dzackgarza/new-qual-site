::: {.problem}
Construct an explicit deformation retraction of
$$
\RR^n\setminus\{0\}
$$
onto $S^{n-1}$.
:::

::: {.solution}

Define
$$
H:
(\RR^n\setminus\{0\})\times[0,1]
\longrightarrow
\RR^n\setminus\{0\}
$$
by
$$
\boxed{
H(x,t)
=
\left(
(1-t)
+
\frac{t}{\|x\|}
\right)x.
}
$$

::: pf

::: pf-step

The map $H$ is continuous.


::: pf-proof

On
$$
\RR^n\setminus\{0\},
$$
the norm
$$
x\mapsto\|x\|
$$
is continuous and strictly positive.
Therefore
$$
x\mapsto\frac1{\|x\|}
$$
is continuous.

The scalar coefficient
$$
(x,t)
\longmapsto
(1-t)+\frac{t}{\|x\|}
$$
is continuous, and scalar multiplication in $\RR^n$ is continuous.
Hence $H$ is continuous.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

For every
$$
x\ne0
$$
and every
$$
t\in[0,1],
$$
one has
$$
H(x,t)\ne0.
$$


::: pf-proof

The scalar
$$
(1-t)+\frac{t}{\|x\|}
$$
is strictly positive because both summands are nonnegative and at least one
is positive.
Multiplying a nonzero vector $x$ by a positive scalar cannot give zero.
Thus $H$ indeed takes values in
$$
\RR^n\setminus\{0\}.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

At homotopy time
$$
t=0,
$$
$$
H(x,0)=x.
$$


::: pf-proof

Substitute $t=0$:
$$
H(x,0)
=
(1+0)x
=
x.
$$
Thus
$$
H(-,0)=\operatorname{id}_{\RR^n\setminus\{0\}}.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

At homotopy time
$$
t=1,
$$
$$
H(x,1)
=
\frac{x}{\|x\|}
\in
S^{n-1}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Substitute $t=1$:
$$
H(x,1)
=
\frac1{\|x\|}x.
$$
Its norm is
$$
\left\|
\frac{x}{\|x\|}
\right\|
=
1.
$$
Hence
$$
H(-,1)
$$
is the radial retraction onto the unit sphere.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-5}

Every point of
$$
S^{n-1}
$$
is fixed throughout the homotopy.


::: pf-proof

Let
$$
x\in S^{n-1}.
$$
Then
$$
\|x\|=1.
$$
Therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
H(x,t)
&=
\left(
(1-t)+t
\right)x\\
&=
x
\end{aligned}
$$
for every
$$
t\in[0,1].
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-6}

Consequently
$$
\boxed{
H
\text{ is a deformation retraction of }
\RR^n\setminus\{0\}
\text{ onto }
S^{n-1}.
}
$$


::: pf-proof

Step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} gives the identity at time $0$.
Step [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} gives a retraction onto the sphere at time $1$.
Step [](#s1-5){.pf-ref} fixes the sphere pointwise for all times.
These are exactly the conditions for a deformation retraction.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-6){.pf-ref} is the desired conclusion.

:::
