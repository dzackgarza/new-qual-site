::: {.problem}
Identify a familiar space homeomorphic to each of the following constructions, and prove your answer geometrically.

1. The mapping cylinder of
   \[
   f:I\to S^1,\qquad f(t)=(\cos(4\pi t),\sin(4\pi t)).
   \]

2. The mapping torus of
   \[
   f:S^1\to S^1,\qquad f(x,y)=(x,-y).
   \]
:::
