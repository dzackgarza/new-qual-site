::: problem
Let $K$ denote a splitting field of $x^7 - 1$ over $\mathbb{Q}$ inside $\mathbb{C}$.
Determine all subfields of $K$.
Express each subfield in the form $\mathbb{Q}(\alpha)$ for some $\alpha \in \mathbb{C}$, where you must justify that $\alpha$ is a primitive element for the subfield if it is not obvious.
:::
\n\n::: {.solution}\nLet $\zeta=e^{2\pi i/7}$.
Since\n\[\nx^7-1=(x-1)\Phi_7(x),\n\]\nthe splitting field is\n\[\nK=\mathbb Q(\zeta).\n\]\nThe cyclotomic polynomial $\Phi_7$ has degree $6$, and\n\[\n\operatorname{Gal}(K/\mathbb Q)\cong(\mathbb Z/7\mathbb Z)^\times\cong C_6.\n\]\nHence there is exactly one subgroup of each order $1,2,3,6$, so by the Galois correspondence there are exactly four subfields of $K$.\n\n<1>1. The fields corresponding to the trivial subgroup and the whole Galois group are $K=\mathbb Q(\zeta)$ and $\mathbb Q$.\n::: {.proof}\nThis is the two extreme cases of the Galois correspondence.\n:::\n\n<1>2. The unique cubic subfield is\n\[\nK_3=\mathbb Q(\zeta+\zeta^{-1}).\n\]\n::: {.proof}\nThe unique subgroup of order $2$ is generated by complex conjugation, so its fixed field is the maximal real subfield.
Put\n\[\nu=\zeta+\zeta^{-1}.\n\]\nThen $u$ is fixed by conjugation, hence $\mathbb Q(u)$ lies in the fixed field.
Moreover\n\[\n[\mathbb Q(\zeta):\mathbb Q(u)]=2,\n\]\nbecause $\zeta$ satisfies\n\[\nX^2-uX+1=0\n\]\nover $\mathbb Q(u)$ and $\zeta\notin\mathbb R$.
Thus $[\mathbb Q(u):\mathbb Q]=3$, so $u$ is a primitive element for the unique cubic subfield.\n:::\n\n<1>3. The unique quadratic subfield is\n\[\nK_2=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-7}).\n\]\n::: {.proof}\nLet\n\[\na=\zeta+\zeta^2+\zeta^4,\qquad b=\zeta^3+\zeta^5+\zeta^6.\n\]\nThe subgroup of squares $\{1,2,4\}\subset(\mathbb Z/7\mathbb Z)^\times$ has order $3$ and fixes both $a$ and $b$, so it fixes $a-b$.
Since\n\[\na+b=\zeta+\zeta^2+\cdots+\zeta^6=-1,\n\]\nand\n\[\nab=2,\n\]\nwe get\n\[\n(a-b)^2=(a+b)^2-4ab=1-8=-7.\n\]\nTo verify $ab=2$, expand $ab$: among the nine sums $r+s$ modulo $7$ with $r\in\{1,2,4\}$ and $s\in\{3,5,6\}$, each nonzero residue occurs once and $0$ occurs three times.
Hence\n\[\nab=(\zeta+\cdots+\zeta^6)+3=-1+3=2.\n\]\nThus $a-b$ is a square root of $-7$, and $\mathbb Q(a-b)=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-7})$.
Since this field has degree $2$, it is exactly the fixed field of the unique subgroup of order $3$.\n:::\n\n<1>4. Therefore the complete list of subfields is\n\[\n\boxed{\mathbb Q,\quad \mathbb Q(\sqrt{-7}),\quad \mathbb Q(\zeta+\zeta^{-1}),\quad \mathbb Q(\zeta)}.\n\]\n::: {.proof}\nA cyclic group of order $6$ has no other subgroups, so the Galois correspondence gives no other intermediate fields.\n:::\n:::\n
