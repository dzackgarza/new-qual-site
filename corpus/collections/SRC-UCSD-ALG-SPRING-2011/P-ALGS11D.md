::: problem
Let $K$ denote a splitting field of $x^7 - 1$ over $\mathbb{Q}$ inside $\mathbb{C}$.
Determine all subfields of $K$.
Express each subfield in the form $\mathbb{Q}(\alpha)$ for some $\alpha \in \mathbb{C}$, where you must justify that $\alpha$ is a primitive element for the subfield if it is not obvious.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $\zeta=e^{2\pi i/7}$.
Since
\[
x^7-1=(x-1)\Phi_7(x),
\]
the splitting field is $K=\mathbb Q(\zeta)$.
The cyclotomic polynomial $\Phi_7$ has degree $6$, and
\[
\operatorname{Gal}(K/\mathbb Q)\cong (\mathbb Z/7\mathbb Z)^\times\cong C_6.
\]
Hence there is exactly one subgroup of each order $1,2,3,6$, so by Galois correspondence there are exactly four subfields.

<1>1. The extreme subfields are $\mathbb Q$ and $K=\mathbb Q(\zeta)$.
::: {.proof}
These correspond to the whole Galois group and the trivial subgroup.
:::

<1>2. The unique cubic subfield is
\[
K_3=\mathbb Q(\zeta+\zeta^{-1}).
\]
::: {.proof}
The unique subgroup of order $2$ is generated by complex conjugation, so its fixed field is the maximal real subfield.
Put $u=\zeta+\zeta^{-1}$.
Then $u$ is fixed by conjugation.
Moreover $\zeta$ satisfies
\[
X^2-uX+1=0
\]
over $\mathbb Q(u)$, while $\zeta\notin\mathbb R$.
Thus $[\mathbb Q(\zeta):\mathbb Q(u)]=2$, so $[\mathbb Q(u):\mathbb Q]=3$.
Hence $u$ generates the unique cubic subfield.
:::

<1>3. The unique quadratic subfield is
\[
K_2=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-7}).
\]
::: {.proof}
Let
\[
a=\zeta+\zeta^2+\zeta^4,\qquad b=\zeta^3+\zeta^5+\zeta^6.
\]
The subgroup of squares $\{1,2,4\}\subset (\mathbb Z/7\mathbb Z)^\times$ has order $3$ and fixes $a-b$.
Also
\[
a+b=-1.
\]
Expanding $ab$, among the nine sums $r+s$ modulo $7$ with $r\in\{1,2,4\}$ and $s\in\{3,5,6\}$, residue $0$ occurs three times and each nonzero residue occurs once.
Hence
\[
ab=3+(\zeta+\zeta^2+\cdots+\zeta^6)=2.
\]
Therefore
\[
(a-b)^2=(a+b)^2-4ab=1-8=-7.
\]
Thus $\mathbb Q(a-b)=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-7})$, which has degree $2$, so it is the fixed field of the unique subgroup of order $3$.
:::

<1>4. Therefore the complete list of subfields is
\[
\boxed{\mathbb Q,\quad \mathbb Q(\sqrt{-7}),\quad \mathbb Q(\zeta+\zeta^{-1}),\quad \mathbb Q(\zeta)}.
\]
::: {.proof}
A cyclic group of order $6$ has no other subgroups.
:::
:::
