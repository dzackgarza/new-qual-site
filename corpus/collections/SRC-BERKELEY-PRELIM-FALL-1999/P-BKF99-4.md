::: {.problem}
Let $f$ be a rational function with no poles in the closed upper half-plane. Prove that
\[
\sup\{|f(z)|:\operatorname{Im}z\ge0\}
=
\sup\{|f(z)|:\operatorname{Im}z=0\}.
\]
:::
