::: {.problem}
Let
\[
A=\{0\}\cup\{1/n:n\in\mathbb Z,\ n>1\},
\]
and let $D$ be the open unit disk in $\mathbb C$. Prove that every bounded holomorphic function on $D\setminus A$ extends to a holomorphic function on $D$.
:::

::: {.solution}

Let $f$ be bounded and holomorphic on $D\setminus A$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #puncture-removable-at-1-over-n}
For every integer $n>1$, the singularity of $f$ at $1/n$ is removable.

::: pf-proof
Fix $n>1$. The point $1/n$ is isolated in $A$: there is $r_n>0$ such that
$$
B(1/n,r_n)\cap A=\{1/n\}.
$$
Thus $f$ is holomorphic and bounded on the punctured disk
$$
0<\abs{z-1/n}<r_n.
$$
By the removable singularity theorem, $f$ extends holomorphically across
$1/n$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #g-bounded-on-punctured-disk}
The extensions from step [](#puncture-removable-at-1-over-n){.pf-ref} define a bounded holomorphic function
$g$ on $D\setminus\{0\}$ that agrees with $f$ on $D\setminus A$.

::: pf-proof
Define $g=f$ off the points $1/n$, and at each $1/n$ give $g$ the value of
the holomorphic extension from step [](#puncture-removable-at-1-over-n){.pf-ref}. These local definitions agree with
$f$ on punctured neighborhoods, so they define a holomorphic function on
$D\setminus\{0\}$.

If $\abs{f}\leq M$ on $D\setminus A$, continuity of the extension at
$1/n$ gives
$$
\abs{g(1/n)}
=
\lim_{z\to1/n}\abs{f(z)}
\leq M.
$$
Hence $\abs{g}\leq M$ throughout $D\setminus\{0\}$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #singularity-at-zero-removable}
The singularity of $g$ at $0$ is removable.

::: pf-proof
By step [](#g-bounded-on-punctured-disk){.pf-ref}, $g$ is bounded and holomorphic on the punctured unit disk.
The removable singularity theorem therefore gives a holomorphic extension
$G$ of $g$ across $0$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #G-extends-f}
The function $G$ is a holomorphic extension of $f$ to all of $D$.

::: pf-proof
Step [](#singularity-at-zero-removable){.pf-ref} gives $G\in\mathcal O(D)$, and step [](#g-bounded-on-punctured-disk){.pf-ref} gives
$G=g=f$ on $D\setminus A$.
:::

:::

::: pf-qed
Step [](#G-extends-f){.pf-ref} is the required extension.
:::
