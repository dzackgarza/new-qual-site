::: {.problem}
Let $V,W$ be finite-dimensional vector spaces, let $X\subseteq W$ be a subspace, and let $T:V\to W$ be linear. Prove that
\[
\dim T^{-1}(X)\ge \dim V-\dim W+\dim X.
\]
:::
