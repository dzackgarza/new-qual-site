::: {.problem}
Given any topological space $Z$ and subset $D\subseteq Z$, let $\operatorname{Cl}_Z(D)$ denote the closure of $D$ in $Z$.
Show that if $X$ and $Y$ are topological spaces and $A\subseteq X$, $B\subseteq Y$, then $\operatorname{Cl}_{X\times Y}(A\times B)=\operatorname{Cl}_X(A)\times\operatorname{Cl}_Y(B)$.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. We have
\[
\operatorname{Cl}_{X\times Y}(A\times B)
\subseteq
\operatorname{Cl}_X(A)\times\operatorname{Cl}_Y(B).
\]
::: {.proof}
Take
\[
(x,y)\in\operatorname{Cl}_{X\times Y}(A\times B).
\]
Let $U\subseteq X$ be any open neighborhood of $x$.
Then
\[
U\times Y
\]
is an open neighborhood of $(x,y)$ in $X\times Y$.
Because $(x,y)$ lies in the closure of $A\times B$,
\[
(U\times Y)\cap(A\times B)\ne\varnothing.
\]
Hence $U\cap A\ne\varnothing$.
Since every open neighborhood $U$ of $x$ meets $A$,
\[
x\in\operatorname{Cl}_X(A).
\]

Similarly, if $V\subseteq Y$ is any open neighborhood of $y$, then
\[
X\times V
\]
is an open neighborhood of $(x,y)$ and therefore meets $A\times B$.
Thus $V\cap B\ne\varnothing$ for every such $V$, so
\[
y\in\operatorname{Cl}_Y(B).
\]
Therefore
\[
(x,y)\in
\operatorname{Cl}_X(A)\times\operatorname{Cl}_Y(B).
\]
:::

<1>2. We have
\[
\operatorname{Cl}_X(A)\times\operatorname{Cl}_Y(B)
\subseteq
\operatorname{Cl}_{X\times Y}(A\times B).
\]
::: {.proof}
Take
\[
(x,y)\in
\operatorname{Cl}_X(A)\times\operatorname{Cl}_Y(B).
\]
Let $W$ be any open neighborhood of $(x,y)$ in $X\times Y$.
By the basis for the product topology, there are open sets $U\subseteq X$ and $V\subseteq Y$ such that
\[
x\in U,
\qquad
y\in V,
\qquad
U\times V\subseteq W.
\]
Since $x\in\operatorname{Cl}_X(A)$,
\[
U\cap A\ne\varnothing;
\]
choose $a\in U\cap A$.
Since $y\in\operatorname{Cl}_Y(B)$,
\[
V\cap B\ne\varnothing;
\]
choose $b\in V\cap B$.
Then
\[
(a,b)\in(U\times V)\cap(A\times B)\subseteq W\cap(A\times B).
\]
Thus every open neighborhood $W$ of $(x,y)$ meets $A\times B$.
Therefore
\[
(x,y)\in\operatorname{Cl}_{X\times Y}(A\times B).
\]
:::

<1>3. Hence
\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Cl}_{X\times Y}(A\times B)
=
\operatorname{Cl}_X(A)\times\operatorname{Cl}_Y(B).
}
\]
::: {.proof}
Combine the two inclusions from <1>1 and <1>2.
:::
