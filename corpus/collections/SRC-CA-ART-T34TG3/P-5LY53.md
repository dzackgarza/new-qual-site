::: problem
Show that if $\xi\in \RR$, then
\[
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \frac{e^{-2 \pi i x \xi}}{\left(1+x^{2}\right)^{2}} d x=\frac{\pi}{2}(1+2 \pi|\xi|) e^{-2 \pi|\xi|}
.\]
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. $f$ holomorphic.
Proof: Cauchy.

<1>2. Q.E.D.
Proof: <1>1.
:::
