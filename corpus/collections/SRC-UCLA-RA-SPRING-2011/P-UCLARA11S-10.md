::: {.problem}
Let $\mathbb D=\{z:|z|<1\}$ and
\[
\Omega=\{z\in\mathbb D:\operatorname{Im}z>0\}.
\]
Evaluate
\[
\sup\left\{\operatorname{Re}f'(i/2): f:\Omega\to\mathbb D\text{ is holomorphic}\right\}.
\]
:::
