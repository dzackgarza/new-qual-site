::: {.problem}
Let $f_n:[0,1]\to[0,\infty)$ be Borel functions satisfying
\[
\sup_n\int_0^1 f_n(x)\log(2+f_n(x))\,dx<\infty.
\]
Suppose $f_n\to f$ Lebesgue almost everywhere.
Show that $f\in L^1([0,1])$ and $f_n\to f$ in $L^1$.
:::
