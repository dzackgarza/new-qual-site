::: {.problem}
For $s\in(1,\infty)$ define
\[
f(s)=\int_0^\infty \frac{x^{s-1}}{e^x-1}\,dx.
\]
Show that $f$ has an analytic continuation to
\[
\{s\in\mathbb C:\operatorname{Re}s>0,\ s\ne1\}
\]
with a simple pole at $s=1$. Compute the residue at $s=1$.
:::
