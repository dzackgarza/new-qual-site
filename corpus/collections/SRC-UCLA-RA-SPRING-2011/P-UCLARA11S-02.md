::: {.problem}
Let $f\in L^3(\mathbb R)$ and
\[
\phi(x)=
\begin{cases}
\sin(\pi x),& |x|\le1,\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\]
Show that
\[
f_n(x):=n\int_{\mathbb R}f(x-y)\phi(ny)\,dy\longrightarrow0
\]
Lebesgue almost everywhere.
:::
