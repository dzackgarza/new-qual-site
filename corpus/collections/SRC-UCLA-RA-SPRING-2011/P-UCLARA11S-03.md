::: {.problem}
Let $\mu$ be a Borel probability measure on $\mathbb R$ and define
\[
f(t)=\int_{\mathbb R}e^{itx}\,d\mu(x).
\]
Suppose that
\[
\lim_{t\to0}\frac{f(0)-f(t)}{t^2}=0.
\]
Show that $\mu$ is supported at $\{0\}$.
:::
