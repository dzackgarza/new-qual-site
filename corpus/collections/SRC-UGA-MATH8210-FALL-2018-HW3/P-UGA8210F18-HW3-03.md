::: {.problem}
Compute the cohomology groups (regular and compactly supported) of the cylinder $S ^ { 1 } \times \RR$ and the open Mobius strip, i.e. the twisted $S ^ { 1 } \times \RR$ . Do not use a Kunneth formula.
¨
:::

::: {.solution}

Write $C=S^1\times\RR$ for the cylinder and let $M$ be the total space of the nontrivial real line bundle over $S^1$, i.e. the open Möbius strip. Cohomology below is de Rham cohomology over $\RR$, and $H_c^*$ denotes compactly supported de Rham cohomology.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

The ordinary cohomology groups of both surfaces are
$$
\boxed{
H^k(C)\cong H^k(M)\cong
\begin{cases}
\RR,&k=0,1,\\
0,&k\ge 2.
\end{cases}}
$$


::: pf-proof

Scalar multiplication in each fiber gives a deformation retraction of both $C$ and $M$ onto their zero sections, each of which is a copy of $S^1$. Homotopy invariance of de Rham cohomology therefore gives
$$
H^k(C)\cong H^k(S^1)\cong H^k(M).
$$
The de Rham cohomology of the circle is $\RR$ in degrees $0$ and $1$ and is zero in all higher degrees, which proves the claim without using a Künneth formula.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

For either line bundle $E\to S^1$, compactly supported Mayer--Vietoris reduces $H_c^*(E)$ to the kernel and cokernel of a map $A_E:\RR^2\to\RR^2$.


::: pf-proof

Choose connected open arcs $U,V\subset S^1$ such that $U\cup V=S^1$ and
$$
U\cap V=W_+\sqcup W_-,
$$
where $W_+$ and $W_-$ are open arcs. Every restriction of $E$ to one of $U,V,W_+,W_-$ is trivial, so each of
$$
E_U, E_V, E_{W_+}, E_{W_-}
$$
is diffeomorphic to $\RR^2$. Hence
$$
H_c^q(E_U)\cong H_c^q(E_V)\cong
\begin{cases}
\RR,&q=2,\\
0,&q\ne2,
\end{cases}
$$
and
$$
H_c^q(E_{U\cap V})\cong
\begin{cases}
\RR^2,&q=2,\\
0,&q\ne2.
\end{cases}
$$
The compactly supported Mayer--Vietoris sequence therefore gives
$$
H_c^0(E)=0,\qquad
H_c^1(E)\cong\ker A_E,\qquad
H_c^2(E)\cong\operatorname{coker}A_E,
$$
where
$$
A_E:H_c^2(E_{W_+})\oplus H_c^2(E_{W_-})
\longrightarrow H_c^2(E_U)\oplus H_c^2(E_V)
$$
is the difference of the two extension-by-zero maps.

Fix an orientation of the base circle and a fiber orientation over $U$. Use it to orient $E_U$ and the two overlap pieces. If the transition function from the $U$-trivialization to the $V$-trivialization has signs $\varepsilon_+,\varepsilon_-\in\{\pm1\}$ on $W_+,W_-$, respectively, then, in the generators determined by integration,
$$
A_E(a,b)=\bigl(a+b,-\varepsilon_+a-\varepsilon_-b\bigr).
$$
Indeed, extension by zero from either overlap component to $E_U$ preserves the chosen orientation, while extension to $E_V$ changes the top-degree generator by the sign of the fiber transition; the Mayer--Vietoris map has the additional minus sign in its second component.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

The compactly supported cohomology groups of the cylinder are
$$
\boxed{
H_c^k(C)\cong
\begin{cases}
\RR,&k=1,2,\\
0,&k\ne1,2.
\end{cases}}
$$


::: pf-proof

For the trivial line bundle $C\to S^1$, choose compatible trivializations over $U$ and $V$. Then $\varepsilon_+=\varepsilon_-=1$, so step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} gives
$$
A_C(a,b)=(a+b,-a-b).
$$
Thus
$$
\ker A_C=\{(a,-a):a\in\RR\}\cong\RR
$$
and the image is the one-dimensional subspace spanned by $(1,-1)$, so
$$
\operatorname{coker}A_C\cong\RR.
$$
The identifications in step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} now give the asserted groups.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

The compactly supported cohomology groups of the open Möbius strip are
$$
\boxed{H_c^k(M)=0\quad\text{for every }k.}
$$


::: pf-proof

For the nontrivial line bundle $M\to S^1$, trivializations over $U$ and $V$ may be chosen so that one overlap transition preserves the fiber orientation and the other reverses it. Thus, after labeling the overlap components suitably,
$$
\varepsilon_+=1,\qquad \varepsilon_-=-1.
$$
By step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref},
$$
A_M(a,b)=(a+b,-a+b),
$$
whose matrix is
$$
\begin{pmatrix}
1&1\\
-1&1
\end{pmatrix}.
$$
Its determinant is $2$, so $A_M$ is an isomorphism over $\RR$. Consequently both its kernel and cokernel vanish. Step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} also gives $H_c^0(M)=0$, proving the claim.

:::
