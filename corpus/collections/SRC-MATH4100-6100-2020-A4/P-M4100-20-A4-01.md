::: {.problem}
Let
\[
E=\left\{\frac{(-1)^n n}{n+1}:n\in\mathbb N\right\}.
\]

(a) Find the limit points of $E$.

(b) Is $E$ closed? Is it open?

(c) Does $E$ contain isolated points?

(d) Find the closure $\overline E$.
:::
