::: {.problem}
For each set below, decide whether it is compact. For every noncompact set, demonstrate failure of both sequential compactness and the open-cover definition by giving

1. a sequence in the set with no subsequence converging to a point of the set, and
2. an open cover with no finite subcover.

The sets are
\[
\mathbb Q,
\qquad
\mathbb Q\cap[0,1],
\qquad
\mathbb R,
\qquad
\mathbb Z\cap[0,10],
\]
\[
\left\{1,\frac12,\frac13,\ldots\right\},
\qquad
\left\{1,\frac12,\frac23,\frac34,\frac45,\ldots\right\}.
\]
:::
