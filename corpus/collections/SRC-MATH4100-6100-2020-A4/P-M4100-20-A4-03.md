::: {.problem}
For each of the following subsets of $\mathbb R$, decide whether it is open, closed, or neither. If it is not open, exhibit a point of the set with no $\varepsilon$-neighborhood contained in the set. If it is not closed, exhibit a limit point not contained in the set.

(a) $\mathbb Q$;

(b) $\mathbb N$;

(c) $(0,\infty)$;

(d) $(0,1]$;

(e)
\[
\left\{1+\frac14+\cdots+\frac1{n^2}:n\in\mathbb N\right\}.
\]
:::
