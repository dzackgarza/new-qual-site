::: problem
(a) Give the $\varepsilon$, $\delta$ definition for continuity of a function $f: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ at a point $x_0 \in \mathbb{R}$.

(b) Assume that $f: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ is continuous and that $\{a_n\}$ is a sequence of real numbers with $\lim_{n \to \infty} a_n = L$.
Prove that $\lim_{n \to \infty} f(a_n) = f(L)$.
:::
