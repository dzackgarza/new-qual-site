::: problem
Let $A$ be a subring of a ring $R$, and let $I$ be an ideal of $R$. Prove that:

1. $A+I=\{a+i:a\in A,\ i\in I\}$ is a subring of $R$.
2. $A\cap I$ is an ideal of $A$.
3. $I$ is an ideal of $A+I$.
4. $(A+I)/I\cong A/(A\cap I)$.
:::
