::: problem
An $F[x]$-module is an $F$-vector space $M$ with a linear map $T_M:M\to M$, where $x\cdot m=T_M(m)$. Describe an $F[x]$-module homomorphism $\varphi:M\to N$ in terms of the linear maps $T_M$ and $T_N$.
:::
