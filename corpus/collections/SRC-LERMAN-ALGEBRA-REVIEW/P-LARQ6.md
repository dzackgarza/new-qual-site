::: problem
Let $F$ be a field, and let $p\in F[x]$ have degree $n\geq1$. Prove that $F[x]/(p)$ is an $n$-dimensional vector space over $F$. Show that
$$
\{1+(p),x+(p),\ldots,x^{n-1}+(p)\}
$$
is a basis.
:::
