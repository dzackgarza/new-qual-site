::: {.problem}
Let $K$ be a field and $R=K[x]/(x^2)$.
For each integer $i\geq0$, compute

(a) $\Ext_R^i(R,R/(x))$

(b) $\Ext_R^i(R/(x),R)$

(c) $\Ext_R^i(R/(x),R/(x))$
:::
