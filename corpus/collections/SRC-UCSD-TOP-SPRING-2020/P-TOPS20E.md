::: problem
Let $M$ be a closed, orientable, connected manifold of dimension $n$.
Suppose there is a continuous map $f : S^n \to M$ with nonzero mapping degree.
Show that $H_k(M; \mathbb{Q}) = H_k(S^n; \mathbb{Q})$ for any $k \geq 0$.
:::
