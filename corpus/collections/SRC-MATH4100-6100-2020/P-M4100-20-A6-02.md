::: {.problem}
Suppose $f:\RR\to\RR$ satisfies
$$
\lim_{h\to0}\bigl(f(x+h)-f(x-h)\bigr)=0
$$
for every $x\in\RR$. Must $f$ be continuous?
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: pf-step

Define
$$
f(x)=
\begin{cases}
1,&x=0,\\
0,&x\neq0.
\end{cases}
$$


::: pf-proof

This defines a function
$$
f:\RR\to\RR.
$$
It will satisfy the given symmetric-difference condition but fail continuity.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

At $x=0$,
$$
f(h)-f(-h)=0
$$
for every $h\neq0$.


::: pf-proof

If $h\neq0$, then both $h$ and $-h$ are nonzero. Hence
$$
f(h)=0=f(-h),
$$
so their difference is zero.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

Therefore
$$
\lim_{h\to0}
\bigl(f(h)-f(-h)\bigr)=0.
$$


::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref}, the expression is identically zero for all sufficiently small
nonzero $h$; indeed it is zero for every nonzero $h$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

Fix $x\neq0$. If
$$
|h|<\frac{|x|}{2},
$$
then both $x+h$ and $x-h$ are nonzero.


::: pf-proof

By the reverse triangle inequality,
$$
|x\pm h|
\geq
|x|-|h|
>
\frac{|x|}{2}
>
0.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-5}

For every fixed $x\neq0$,
$$
\lim_{h\to0}
\bigl(f(x+h)-f(x-h)\bigr)=0.
$$


::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1-4){.pf-ref}, whenever
$$
|h|<\frac{|x|}{2},
$$
one has
$$
f(x+h)=0=f(x-h).
$$
Thus the difference is identically zero in a neighborhood of $h=0$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-6}

The function $f$ satisfies the hypothesis at every $x\in\RR$.


::: pf-proof

Step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} handles $x=0$, and step [](#s1-5){.pf-ref} handles every $x\neq0$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-7}

The function $f$ is not continuous at $0$.


::: pf-proof

Let
$$
x_n=\frac1n.
$$
Then
$$
x_n\longrightarrow0,
$$
but
$$
f(x_n)=0
$$
for every $n$, while
$$
f(0)=1.
$$
Thus
$$
f(x_n)\not\longrightarrow f(0),
$$
so the sequential criterion for continuity fails at $0$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-8}

Therefore the answer is
$$
\boxed{\text{No}.}
$$


::: pf-proof

Steps [](#s1-6){.pf-ref} and [](#s1-7){.pf-ref} give a function satisfying the stated condition everywhere
but failing to be continuous.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-8){.pf-ref} supplies the required counterexample.

:::
