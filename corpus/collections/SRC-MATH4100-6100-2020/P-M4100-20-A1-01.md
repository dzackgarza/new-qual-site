::: {.problem}
Use the triangle inequality to prove that for $x,y\in\RR$,
$$
\bigl||x|-|y|\bigr|\le |x-y|.
$$
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

One has
$$
|x|-|y|\le |x-y|.
$$


::: pf-proof

By the triangle inequality,
$$
|x|
=|(x-y)+y|
\le |x-y|+|y|.
$$
Subtracting $|y|$ from both sides gives
$$
|x|-|y|\le |x-y|.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

One has
$$
|y|-|x|\le |x-y|.
$$


::: pf-proof

Interchanging $x$ and $y$ in step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} gives
$$
|y|-|x|\le |y-x|.
$$
Since $|y-x|=|x-y|$, the desired inequality follows.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

Therefore
$$
\boxed{\bigl||x|-|y|\bigr|\le |x-y|}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Steps [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} and [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} give
$$
-|x-y|
\le
|x|-|y|
\le
|x-y|.
$$
Taking absolute values yields the stated reverse triangle inequality.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} is the required conclusion.

:::
