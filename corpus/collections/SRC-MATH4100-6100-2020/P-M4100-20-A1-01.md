::: {.problem}
Use the triangle inequality to prove that for $x,y\in\RR$,
$$
\abs{\abs{x}-\abs{y}}\le \abs{x-y}.
$$
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

One has
$$
\abs{x}-\abs{y}\le \abs{x-y}.
$$


::: pf-proof

By the triangle inequality,
$$
\abs{x}
=\abs{(x-y)+y}
\le \abs{x-y}+\abs{y}.
$$
Subtracting $\abs{y}$ from both sides gives
$$
\abs{x}-\abs{y}\le \abs{x-y}.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

One has
$$
\abs{y}-\abs{x}\le \abs{x-y}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Interchanging $x$ and $y$ in step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} gives
$$
\abs{y}-\abs{x}\le \abs{y-x}.
$$
Since $\abs{y-x}=\abs{x-y}$, the desired inequality follows.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

Therefore
$$
\boxed{\abs{\abs{x}-\abs{y}}\le \abs{x-y}}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Steps [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} and [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} give
$$
-\abs{x-y}
\le
\abs{x}-\abs{y}
\le
\abs{x-y}.
$$
Taking absolute values yields the stated reverse triangle inequality.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} is the required conclusion.

:::

:::
