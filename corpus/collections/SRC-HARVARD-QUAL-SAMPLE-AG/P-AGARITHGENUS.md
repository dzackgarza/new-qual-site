::: {.problem}
Find the arithmetic genus of $y^3 = x^2 z$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let
\[
C=V(y^3-x^2z)\subseteq\mathbb P^2.
\]

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #plane-cubic}
The curve $C$ is a plane hypersurface of degree $3$.

::: pf-proof
The homogeneous polynomial
\[
F=y^3-x^2z
\]
has degree $3$, so its zero scheme in $\mathbb P^2$ is a plane cubic.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #structure-sheaf-sequence}
The structure sheaf of $C$ fits into
\[
0\longrightarrow
\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^2}(-3)
\xrightarrow{\cdot F}
\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^2}
\longrightarrow
\mathcal O_C
\longrightarrow0.
\]

::: pf-proof
The ideal sheaf of a degree-three hypersurface in $\mathbb P^2$ is the invertible sheaf
\[
\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^2}(-3),
\]
generated by its defining equation $F$.  Quotienting $\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^2}$ by this ideal gives $\mathcal O_C$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #hilbert-polynomial}
The Hilbert polynomial of $C$ is
\[
P_C(n)=3n.
\]

::: pf-proof
Twist the sequence in step [](#structure-sheaf-sequence){.pf-ref} by $\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^2}(n)$ and take Euler characteristics:
\[
\chi(\mathcal O_C(n))
=\chi(\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^2}(n))
-\chi(\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^2}(n-3)).
\]
For $n\gg0$,
\[
\chi(\mathcal O_{\mathbb P^2}(n))
=\binom{n+2}{2},
\]
so
\[
\begin{aligned}
P_C(n)
&=\binom{n+2}{2}-\binom{n-1}{2}\\
&=3n.
\end{aligned}
\]
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #arithmetic-genus}
Therefore
\[
\boxed{p_a(C)=1.}
\]

::: pf-proof
For a projective curve of degree $d$, the Hilbert polynomial has the form
\[
P_C(n)=dn+1-p_a(C).
\]
Here steps [](#plane-cubic){.pf-ref} and [](#hilbert-polynomial){.pf-ref} give
\[
3n=3n+1-p_a(C),
\]
so
\[
p_a(C)=1.
\]
Equivalently, this is the general plane-curve formula
\[
p_a=\frac{(d-1)(d-2)}2
\]
at $d=3$.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
The curve $C$ is rational, so its geometric genus is $0$, while $p_a(C)=1$.

::: pf-proof
The map
\[
\mathbb P^1\longrightarrow C,
\qquad
[s:t]\longmapsto[t^3:t^2s:s^3]
\]
lands in $C$, since $(t^2s)^3=t^6s^3=(t^3)^2s^3$.  It is bijective: the point of $C$ with $z=0$ is $[1:0:0]$, the image of $[0:1]$, and the points with $z=1$ are $(x,y)=(u^3,u^2)$, the images of $[1:u]$.  It is an isomorphism away from the cusp $[0:0:1]$, so it is the normalization of $C$ and the geometric genus is $0$.  The difference $p_a(C)-0=1$ is the $\delta$-invariant of the cusp.
:::

:::

::: pf-qed
Step [](#arithmetic-genus){.pf-ref} gives the requested arithmetic genus.
:::
