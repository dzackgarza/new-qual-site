::: problem
Is $\PP^1 \times \PP^1$ a projective variety? Prove it.

Find the explicit equation cutting out the image of the Segre embedding
\[
\psi(\PP^1\times\PP^1) \subseteq \PP^3 .
\]

Over $\CC$ that image is a $4$-dimensional real manifold; compute its
intersection form.
:::
