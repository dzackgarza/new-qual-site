::: {.problem}
Is $\PP^1 \times \PP^1$ a projective variety?
Prove it.

Find the explicit equation cutting out the image of the Segre embedding
\[
\psi(\PP^1\times\PP^1) \subseteq \PP^3 .
\]

Over $\CC$ that image is a $4$-dimensional real manifold; compute its intersection form.
:::

::: {.solution}
Write homogeneous coordinates on the two factors as
\[
[s_0:s_1]\in\mathbb P^1,
\qquad
[t_0:t_1]\in\mathbb P^1.
\]

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #segre-map-definition}
The Segre map is
\[
\boxed{
\psi:\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1\longrightarrow\mathbb P^3,
\qquad
([s_0:s_1],[t_0:t_1])
\longmapsto
[s_0t_0:s_0t_1:s_1t_0:s_1t_1].
}
\]

::: pf-proof
The four coordinates are bihomogeneous of bidegree $(1,1)$.  Rescaling
\[
(s_0,s_1)\mapsto\lambda(s_0,s_1),
\qquad
(t_0,t_1)\mapsto\mu(t_0,t_1)
\]
rescales all four target coordinates by the common factor $\lambda\mu$, so the formula is well-defined on projective points.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #image-in-quadric}
The image of $\psi$ is contained in the quadric surface
\[
Q=V(z_{00}z_{11}-z_{01}z_{10})\subseteq\mathbb P^3.
\]

::: pf-proof
For a point in the image,
\[
z_{00}=s_0t_0,
\quad
z_{01}=s_0t_1,
\quad
z_{10}=s_1t_0,
\quad
z_{11}=s_1t_1.
\]
Hence
\[
z_{00}z_{11}-z_{01}z_{10}
=s_0s_1t_0t_1-s_0s_1t_1t_0
=0.
\]
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #quadric-in-image}
Every point of $Q$ lies in the image of $\psi$.

::: pf-proof
Associate to
\[
z=[z_{00}:z_{01}:z_{10}:z_{11}]\in Q
\]
the matrix
\[
Z=
\begin{pmatrix}
z_{00}&z_{01}\\
z_{10}&z_{11}
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
The equation of $Q$ says
\[
\det Z=0.
\]
Since $z$ is a projective point, $Z\ne0$, so $Z$ has rank exactly $1$.  Therefore there are nonzero column vectors
\[
s=\begin{pmatrix}s_0\\s_1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
t=\begin{pmatrix}t_0\\t_1\end{pmatrix}
\]
such that
\[
Z=s\,t^{\mathsf T}.
\]
Thus
\[
z_{ij}=s_i t_j,
\]
and $z=\psi([s_0:s_1],[t_0:t_1])$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #segre-isomorphism}
The Segre map is an isomorphism
\[
\boxed{
\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1
\xrightarrow{\sim}
Q=V(z_{00}z_{11}-z_{01}z_{10})\subseteq\mathbb P^3.
}
\]
In particular, $\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1$ is projective.

::: pf-proof
The rank-one factorization in step [](#quadric-in-image){.pf-ref} is unique up to
\[
(s,t)\mapsto(\lambda s,\lambda^{-1}t),
\]
which does not change the two projective points $[s]$ and $[t]$.  Thus $\psi$ is bijective onto $Q$.

On the standard target chart $z_{00}\ne0$, the inverse is explicitly
\[
[z_{00}:z_{01}:z_{10}:z_{11}]
\longmapsto
([z_{00}:z_{10}],[z_{00}:z_{01}]),
\]
and analogous formulas hold on the other three standard charts.  These are regular maps, so $\psi$ is an isomorphism onto $Q$.

Since $Q$ is the zero locus of a homogeneous polynomial in $\mathbb P^3$, it is projective.  Hence so is $\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #h2-generators}
Over $\mathbb C$, let
\[
A=[\mathbb P^1\times\{q\}],
\qquad
B=[\{p\}\times\mathbb P^1]
\]
for any points $p,q\in\mathbb P^1$.  Then
\[
H_2(\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1,\mathbb Z)
\cong
\mathbb ZA\oplus\mathbb ZB.
\]

::: pf-proof
The Künneth theorem gives
\[
H_2(\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1,\mathbb Z)
\cong
H_2(\mathbb P^1,\mathbb Z)\otimes H_0(\mathbb P^1,\mathbb Z)
\oplus
H_0(\mathbb P^1,\mathbb Z)\otimes H_2(\mathbb P^1,\mathbb Z),
\]
which is $\mathbb Z^2$, generated by the two factor classes $A$ and $B$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #intersection-numbers}
The intersection numbers are
\[
A^2=0,
\qquad
B^2=0,
\qquad
A\cdot B=1.
\]

::: pf-proof
Two distinct fibres of the same projection are disjoint.  Thus a representative of $A$ can be moved within its ruling to a disjoint representative, giving
\[
A^2=0.
\]
The same argument gives $B^2=0$.

The curves
\[
\mathbb P^1\times\{q\}
\quad\text{and}\quad
\{p\}\times\mathbb P^1
\]
meet transversely at the single point $(p,q)$.  Their complex orientations give local intersection multiplicity $+1$, so
\[
A\cdot B=1.
\]
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #intersection-form-matrix}
Therefore the intersection form is the hyperbolic plane
\[
\boxed{
\begin{pmatrix}
0&1\\
1&0
\end{pmatrix}
}
\]
in the basis $(A,B)$.

::: pf-proof
The matrix entries are precisely the pairwise intersection numbers from step [](#intersection-numbers){.pf-ref}.

As a consistency check, a hyperplane section of the Segre quadric has class
\[
A+B,
\]
and
\[
(A+B)^2=2,
\]
which equals the degree of the quadric surface in $\mathbb P^3$.
:::
