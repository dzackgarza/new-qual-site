::: problem
For nonnegative measurable $f,g$ on $[0,\infty)$, prove the stated bound
$$
\int_0^\infty\left(\int_0^xf(y)\,dy\right)\frac{g(x)}x\,dx\leq
\left(\int_0^\infty f(y)y^{-1/2}\,dy\right)\|g\|_2.
$$
:::
