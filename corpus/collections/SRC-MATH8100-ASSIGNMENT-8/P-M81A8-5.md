::: problem
Let $(u_n)$ be an orthonormal basis of a Hilbert space.
Characterize convergence of $\sum a_nu_n$ and compute its norm.
Determine whether continuous linear functionals can satisfy $L(u_n)=n^{-1}$ or $L(u_n)=n^{-1/2}$, and compute the norm when one exists.
:::
