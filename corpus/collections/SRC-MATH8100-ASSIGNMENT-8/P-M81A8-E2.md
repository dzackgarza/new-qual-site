::: problem
Let $\|f_k\|_2\leq1$ on $[0,1]$. Analyze almost-everywhere and $L^1$ convergence: prove the limit lies in the closed $L^2$ unit ball, decide whether either hypothesis gives $L^2$ convergence, and prove that almost-everywhere convergence gives $L^1$ convergence.
:::
