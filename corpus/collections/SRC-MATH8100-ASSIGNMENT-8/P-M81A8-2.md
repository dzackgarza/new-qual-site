::: problem
For measurable $X$ of finite measure, prove the strict inclusions $L^\infty(X)\subset L^2(X)\subset L^1(X)$ and the associated norm inequalities. Give counterexamples for infinite-measure spaces and prove that $L^2(X)\subseteq L^1(X)$ forces $m(X)<\infty$. Also prove $L^1\cap L^\infty\subset L^2\subset L^1+L^\infty$ and the interpolation inequality.
:::
