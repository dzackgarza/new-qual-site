::: problem
For $1\leq p\leq\infty$, define $\Lambda_p(f)=\int_0^1x^2f(x)\,dx$ on $L^p([0,1])$.
Prove continuity and compute $\|\Lambda_p\|$ in terms of $p$.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. $|\Lambda_p(f)|\le \int_0^1 x^2|f| \le \|x^2\|_q\|f\|_p$ by Hölder, $1/p+1/q=1$.
::: {.proof}
Hölder.
:::

<1>2. Hence continuous, $\|\Lambda_p\|\le\|x^2\|_q = (2q+1)^{-1/q}$.
::: {.proof}
compute $\|x^2\|_q^q=\int_0^1 x^{2q}dx=1/(2q+1)$.
:::

<1>3. Equality attained: $f=x^{2(q-1)}$ (for $p>1$) gives equality in Hölder.
::: {.proof}
extremal.
:::

<1>4. For $p=1$, $\|\Lambda_1\|=1$; for $p=\infty$, $\|\Lambda_\infty\|=1/3$.
::: {.proof}
$q=\infty,1$ cases.
:::

<1>5. Q.E.D.
::: {.proof}
<1>2.
:::
:::
