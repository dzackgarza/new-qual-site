::: problem
Prove the strict inclusions $\ell^1(\mathbb Z)\subset\ell^2(\mathbb Z)\subset\ell^\infty(\mathbb Z)$ and prove that $\|a\|_\infty\leq\|a\|_2\leq\|a\|_1$.
:::

::: solution
Let $a=(a_j)_{j\in\mathbb Z}$.

<1>1. Prove $\|a\|_\infty\le\|a\|_2$.
::: proof
For every $j$,
\[
|a_j|^2\le\sum_{k\in\mathbb Z}|a_k|^2.
\]
Hence
\[
|a_j|\le\|a\|_2.
\]
Taking the supremum over $j$ gives
\[
\boxed{\|a\|_\infty\le\|a\|_2.}
\]
Thus $\ell^2\subseteq\ell^\infty$.
:::

<1>2. Prove $\|a\|_2\le\|a\|_1$.
::: proof
If $a\in\ell^1$, then
\[
\sum_j|a_j|^2
\le\left(\sum_j|a_j|\right)^2,
\]
because the square on the right is
\[
\sum_j|a_j|^2+2\sum_{j<k}|a_j||a_k|.
\]
Taking square roots yields
\[
\boxed{\|a\|_2\le\|a\|_1.}
\]
Hence $\ell^1\subseteq\ell^2$.
:::

<1>3. Both inclusions are strict.
::: proof
Define a sequence supported on positive integers by
\[
a_j=
\begin{cases}
1/j,&j\ge1,\\
0,&j\le0.
\end{cases}
\]
Then
\[
\sum_j|a_j|^2=\sum_{j=1}^\infty\frac1{j^2}<\infty,
\]
but
\[
\sum_j|a_j|=\sum_{j=1}^\infty\frac1j=\infty.
\]
Thus
\[
a\in\ell^2\setminus\ell^1.
\]

The constant sequence
\[
b_j=1
\qquad(j\in\mathbb Z)
\]
belongs to $\ell^\infty$ but not to $\ell^2$. Therefore
\[
\boxed{\ell^1(\mathbb Z)\subsetneq\ell^2(\mathbb Z)\subsetneq\ell^\infty(\mathbb Z).}
\]
:::
:::
