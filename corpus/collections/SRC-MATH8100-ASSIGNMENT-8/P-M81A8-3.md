::: {.problem}
Prove the strict inclusions $\ell^1(\ZZ)\subset\ell^2(\ZZ)\subset\ell^\infty(\ZZ)$ and prove that $\norm{a}_\infty\leq\norm{a}_2\leq\norm{a}_1$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $a=(a_j)_{j\in\ZZ}$.

::: pf

::: pf-step
Prove $\norm{a}_\infty\le\norm{a}_2$.

::: pf-proof
For every $j$,
$$
\abs{a_j}^2\le\sum_{k\in\ZZ}\abs{a_k}^2.
$$
Hence
$$
\abs{a_j}\le\norm{a}_2.
$$
Taking the supremum over $j$ gives
$$
\boxed{\norm{a}_\infty\le\norm{a}_2.}
$$
Thus $\ell^2\subseteq\ell^\infty$.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
Prove $\norm{a}_2\le\norm{a}_1$.

::: pf-proof
If $a\in\ell^1$, then
$$
\sum_j\abs{a_j}^2
\le\left(\sum_j\abs{a_j}\right)^2,
$$
because the square on the right is
$$
\sum_j\abs{a_j}^2+2\sum_{j<k}\abs{a_j}\abs{a_k}.
$$
Taking square roots yields
$$
\boxed{\norm{a}_2\le\norm{a}_1.}
$$
Hence $\ell^1\subseteq\ell^2$.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
Both inclusions are strict.

::: pf-proof
Define a sequence supported on positive integers by
$$
a_j=
\begin{cases}
1/j,&j\ge1,\\
0,&j\le0.
\end{cases}
$$
Then
$$
\sum_j\abs{a_j}^2=\sum_{j=1}^\infty\frac1{j^2}<\infty,
$$
but
$$
\sum_j\abs{a_j}=\sum_{j=1}^\infty\frac1j=\infty.
$$
Thus
$$
a\in\ell^2\setminus\ell^1.
$$

The constant sequence
$$
b_j=1
\qquad(j\in\ZZ)
$$
belongs to $\ell^\infty$ but not to $\ell^2$. Therefore
$$
\boxed{\ell^1(\ZZ)\subsetneq\ell^2(\ZZ)\subsetneq\ell^\infty(\ZZ).}
$$
:::
