::: {.problem}
Construct a function $f : \RR \to \RR$ that is differentiable at a single point.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: pf-step

Define
$$
f(x)=
\begin{cases}
x^2,&x\in\QQ,\\
0,&x\notin\QQ.
\end{cases}
$$


::: pf-proof

This gives a well-defined real-valued function on all of $\RR$.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

One has
$$
f(0)=0.
$$


::: pf-proof

The number $0$ is rational, so
$$
f(0)=0^2=0.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

For every $h\neq0$,
$$
\left|
\frac{f(h)-f(0)}{h}
\right|
\leq
|h|.
$$


::: pf-proof

If $h\in\QQ$, then
$$
\frac{f(h)-f(0)}{h}
=
\frac{h^2}{h}
=h.
$$
If $h\notin\QQ$, then
$$
f(h)=0,
$$
so the quotient is $0$. In either case its absolute value is at most $|h|$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

The function $f$ is differentiable at $0$, with
$$
f'(0)=0.
$$


::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} and the squeeze theorem,
$$
\lim_{h\to0}
\frac{f(h)-f(0)}{h}
=0.
$$
Thus the derivative exists and equals $0$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-5}

Fix $a\neq0$. There are rational and irrational sequences
$$
q_n\to a,
\qquad
r_n\to a.
$$


::: pf-proof

Both $\QQ$ and $\RR\setminus\QQ$ are dense in $\RR$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-6}

The function $f$ is discontinuous at every $a\neq0$.


::: pf-proof

Using the sequences from step [](#s1-5){.pf-ref},
$$
f(q_n)=q_n^2\longrightarrow a^2,
$$
while
$$
f(r_n)=0\longrightarrow0.
$$
Since $a\neq0$,
$$
a^2\neq0.
$$
Thus the two image sequences have different limits, so the sequential
criterion for continuity fails at $a$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-7}

The function $f$ is not differentiable at any $a\neq0$.


::: pf-proof

Differentiability at a point implies continuity at that point. Step [](#s1-6){.pf-ref} shows
that $f$ is discontinuous at every nonzero point. Therefore it is not
differentiable there.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-8}

Hence $f$ is differentiable at exactly one point:
$$
\boxed{0}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Step [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} proves differentiability at $0$, and step [](#s1-7){.pf-ref} excludes every other
point.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-8){.pf-ref} gives the required example and verifies its differentiability set.

:::
