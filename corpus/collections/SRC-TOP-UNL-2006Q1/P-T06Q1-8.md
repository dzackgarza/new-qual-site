::: {.problem}
Find a $\Delta$-complex structure on the space $X$ obtained by identifying the boundaries of three copies of the unit disk together (using identity maps), and compute the simplicial homology groups of $X$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Write the three disks as $D_1,D_2,D_3$.

<1>1. $X$ is a $\Delta$-complex with vertices $v,w_1,w_2,w_3$, edges $e$ (a loop at $v$) and $r_i\colon v\to w_i$, and $2$-simplices $\sigma_1,\sigma_2,\sigma_3$, where $\sigma_i=[v_0,v_1,v_2]$ has face $[v_0,v_1]$ equal to $e$ and faces $[v_0,v_2]$, $[v_1,v_2]$ both equal to $r_i$.

::: {.proof}
Give the common boundary circle the $\Delta$-complex structure with one vertex $v$ and one loop $e$.
Map $\sigma_i$ to $D_i$ with $v_0,v_1\mapsto v$ and $v_2\mapsto w_i$, the base onto $e$, and both remaining faces onto the radial edge $r_i$.
Identifying the two radial faces of a triangle gives the cone on the circle $e$, a disk, so each $D_i$ receives this structure.
The three structures agree on the boundary circle, which the gluing identifies by the identity, so they form a $\Delta$-complex structure on $X$.
:::

<1>2. $\partial_2\sigma_i=e$, $\partial_1r_i=w_i-v$, and $\partial_1e=0$.

::: {.proof}
$\partial_2[v_0,v_1,v_2]=[v_1,v_2]-[v_0,v_2]+[v_0,v_1]=r_i-r_i+e$.
$r_i$ runs from $v$ to $w_i$, and $e$ is a loop at $v$.
:::

<1>3. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
By step <1>2:

$H_2(X)=\ker\partial_2=\{\sum a_i\sigma_i:a_1+a_2+a_3=0\}$, free on $\sigma_1-\sigma_3$ and $\sigma_2-\sigma_3$, since there are no $3$-simplices.

$\partial_1(ae+\sum b_ir_i)=\sum b_i(w_i-v)$ vanishes exactly when all $b_i=0$, so $\ker\partial_1=\ZZ e=\operatorname{im}\partial_2$ and $H_1(X)=0$.

$\operatorname{im}\partial_1$ is generated by $w_i-v$, so $H_0(X)=C_0/\operatorname{im}\partial_1\cong\ZZ$.

Hence
$$H_n(X)\cong\begin{cases}\ZZ,&n=0,\\0,&n=1,\\\ZZ^2,&n=2,\\0,&n\ge3.\end{cases}$$
:::
