::: {.problem title="?"}
Find a cell structure for, and compute a presentation for, the fundamental group of the space $X$ described below.
$X$ is a quotient space of the disjoint union of two copies $T_1=T_2=S^1\times S^1$ of the 2-torus.
Fix a basepoint $x_0$ in $S^1$.
$X$ is obtained by gluing $T_1$ and $T_2$ together, identifying $(x_0,x)$ in $T_1$ with $(x,x_0)$ in $T_2$, for each $x\in S^1$.
For concreteness, use $(x_0,x_0)$ as your basepoint for $X$.
:::
