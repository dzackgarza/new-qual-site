::: {.problem}
Recall that a topological space $X$ is locally connected if for every point $x\in X$ and every neighbourhood $U$ of $x$ there exists a connected neighbourhood $V$ of $x$ with $V\subseteq U$.

(a) Prove that a topological space $X$ is locally connected if and only if for every open set $U\subseteq X$ the components of $U$ are open.
Now let $p:X\to Y$ be a quotient map.

(b) Prove that if $C$ is a component of an open subset $U\subseteq Y$ then $p^{-1}(C)$ is a union of components of $p^{-1}(U)$.

(c) Deduce that if $X$ is locally connected then so is $Y$.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. If $X$ is locally connected and $U\subseteq X$ is open, every component $C$ of $U$ is open in $X$.

::: {.proof}
Let $x\in C$.
There is a connected neighborhood $V$ of $x$ with $V\subseteq U$.
$V$ is connected, lies in $U$, and meets $C$ at $x$, so $V\subseteq C$ by maximality of $C$.
$V$ contains an open set $W\ni x$, so $x$ is interior to $C$.
:::

<1>2. If every component of every open subset of $X$ is open, then $X$ is locally connected.

::: {.proof}
Let $N$ be a neighborhood of $x$, containing an open set $U\ni x$.
The component $C$ of $U$ containing $x$ is open by hypothesis and connected, and $C\subseteq U\subseteq N$.
:::

<1>3. (b) If $C$ is a component of an open set $U\subseteq Y$, then $p^{-1}(C)$ is a union of components of $p^{-1}(U)$.

::: {.proof}
Let $z\in p^{-1}(C)$ and let $D$ be the component of $p^{-1}(U)$ containing $z$.
$p(D)$ is a connected subset of $U$ containing $p(z)\in C$, so $p(D)\subseteq C$ by maximality of $C$, and $D\subseteq p^{-1}(C)$.
Hence $p^{-1}(C)$ is the union of the components $D$ of its points.
:::

<1>4. If $X$ is locally connected, then every component $C$ of every open set $U\subseteq Y$ is open in $Y$.

::: {.proof}
$p^{-1}(U)$ is open, so by step <1>1 its components are open in $X$.
By step <1>3, $p^{-1}(C)$ is a union of them, hence open, and $C$ is open because $p$ is a quotient map.
:::

<1>5. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Steps <1>1 and <1>2 prove (a), and step <1>3 proves (b).
For (c), step <1>4 and step <1>2 applied to $Y$ show that $Y$ is locally connected.
:::
:::
