::: {.problem}
Use Mayer--Vietoris sequences to compute the singular homology groups of the 2-torus $T=S^1\times S^1$.
(You may use knowledge of the homology groups of the circle $S^1$ in your calculations.)
:::

::: {.solution}
Model $T$ as the square with opposite sides identified, let $x_0\in T$ be the image of an interior point of the square, let $V$ be a small open disk around $x_0$, and let $U=T\setminus\{x_0\}$.
$U$ and $V$ are open and cover $T$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

$U\simeq S^1\vee S^1$, $V\simeq*$, and $U\cap V\simeq S^1$.

::: pf-proof

$V$ is a disk, and $U\cap V=V\setminus\{x_0\}$ is a punctured disk.
The punctured square retracts radially onto its boundary, which becomes the $1$-skeleton $S^1\vee S^1$ of $T$ after the side identifications.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

The map $H_1(U\cap V)\to H_1(U)\oplus H_1(V)$ in the Mayer--Vietoris sequence is zero.

::: pf-proof

Let $a,b$ be the loops of $S^1\vee S^1\simeq U$.
A loop around $x_0$ is homotopic in $U$ to the boundary of the square, which represents $aba^{-1}b^{-1}$ in $\pi_1(U)$; its class in the abelianization $H_1(U)$ is $0$.
Also $H_1(V)=0$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

$H_2(T)\cong\ZZ$ and $H_1(T)\cong\ZZ^2$.

::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1){.pf-ref}, $H_2(U)=H_2(V)=0$, $H_1(U\cap V)\cong\ZZ$, $H_1(U)\oplus H_1(V)\cong\ZZ^2$, and $U$, $V$, $U\cap V$ are path-connected.
The Mayer--Vietoris sequence contains the exact segment
$$0\to H_2(T)\xrightarrow{\delta}H_1(U\cap V)\xrightarrow{0}H_1(U)\oplus H_1(V)\to H_1(T)\xrightarrow{\partial}H_0(U\cap V)\xrightarrow{\phi}H_0(U)\oplus H_0(V),$$
where the zero map is step [](#s2){.pf-ref} and $\phi$ sends the generator to $(1,-1)$, so $\phi$ is injective.
Exactness makes $\delta$ an isomorphism onto $\ZZ$, and makes $\partial=0$, so $H_1(U)\oplus H_1(V)\cong\ZZ^2\to H_1(T)$ is an isomorphism.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

$T$ is path-connected, so $H_0(T)\cong\ZZ$.
For $n\ge3$, $H_n(U)\oplus H_n(V)=0$ and $H_{n-1}(U\cap V)=0$, so $H_n(T)=0$.
With step [](#s3){.pf-ref},
$$H_n(T)\cong\begin{cases}\ZZ,&n=0,\\\ZZ^2,&n=1,\\\ZZ,&n=2,\\0,&n\ge3.\end{cases}$$

:::
