::: {.problem}
Let $X$ be a manifold and let
\[
f:X\to\mathbb R^k
\]
be continuous. Show that for every $\varepsilon>0$ there is a smooth function
\[
g:X\to\mathbb R^k
\]
such that
\[
\lVert g(x)-f(x)\rVert<\varepsilon
\]
for every $x\in X$.
:::
