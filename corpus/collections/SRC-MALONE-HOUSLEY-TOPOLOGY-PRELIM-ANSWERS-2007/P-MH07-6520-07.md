::: {.problem}
Show that $SL_n(\mathbb R)$ is connected and that
\[
\pi_1\bigl(SL_n(\mathbb R)\bigr)
\]
is cyclic for every $n\ge2$. You do not need to prove that any fiber bundles you use are indeed fiber bundles.
:::
