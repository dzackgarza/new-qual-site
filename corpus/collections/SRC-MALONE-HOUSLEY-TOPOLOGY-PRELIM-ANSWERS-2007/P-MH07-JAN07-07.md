::: {.problem}
Consider the vector fields on $\mathbb R^3$
\[
X=x\frac{\partial}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial}{\partial y},
\qquad
Y=\frac{\partial}{\partial x}-\frac{\partial}{\partial z}.
\]
Show that there is no nonempty surface $S\subset\mathbb R^3$ tangent to both vector fields at each of its points.
:::
