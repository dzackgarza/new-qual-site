::: {.problem}
Let $M$ be a closed connected $5$-manifold and assume that
\[
\pi_1(M)\cong\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z,
\qquad
H_2(M;\mathbb Z)=0.
\]
Compute $H_i(M;\mathbb Z)$ for all $i$.
:::
