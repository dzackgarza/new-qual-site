::: {.problem}
Let $X,Y\subset\RR^3$ be one-dimensional submanifolds. Show that there exists $v\in\RR^3$ such that
$$
X\cap(Y+v)=\varnothing,
\qquad
Y+v=\{y+v:y\in Y\}.
$$
:::

::: {.solution}

Define
$$
F:
Y\times\RR^3
\to
\RR^3
$$
by
$$
\boxed{
F(y,v)
\coloneqq
y+v.
}
$$

::: pf

::: pf-step

The map $F$ is a submersion.


::: pf-proof

At
$$
(y,v)\in Y\times\RR^3,
$$
the differential is
$$
dF_{(y,v)}(\eta,w)
=
\eta+w,
$$
where
$$
\eta\in T_yY
$$
and
$$
w\in\RR^3.
$$
Given any
$$
u\in\RR^3,
$$
take
$$
\eta=0,
\qquad
w=u.
$$
Then
$$
dF_{(y,v)}(0,u)=u.
$$
Thus the differential is surjective at every point.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

Therefore
$$
F\pitchfork X.
$$


::: pf-proof

A submersion is transverse to every submanifold of its target.
Indeed, at any point with
$$
F(y,v)\in X,
$$
one has
$$
dF_{(y,v)}
\left(
T_yY\oplus\RR^3
\right)
=
\RR^3,
$$
so the transversality condition is automatic.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

By the parametric transversality theorem, for almost every
$$
v\in\RR^3,
$$
the partial map
$$
F_v:Y\to\RR^3,
\qquad
F_v(y)=y+v,
$$
is transverse to $X$.


::: pf-proof

The parametric transversality theorem states:
if
$$
F:Z\times V\to W
$$
is smooth and transverse to a submanifold
$$
S\subseteq W,
$$
then for almost every
$$
v\in V,
$$
the partial map
$$
F_v:Z\to W
$$
is transverse to $S$.

Apply this with
$$
Z=Y,
\qquad
V=\RR^3,
\qquad
W=\RR^3,
\qquad
S=X,
$$
using step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

If
$$
F_v\pitchfork X,
$$
then
$$
X\cap(Y+v)=\varnothing.
$$


::: pf-proof

Suppose, for contradiction, that
$$
p
\in
X\cap(Y+v).
$$
Then
$$
p=y+v
$$
for some
$$
y\in Y.
$$

Transversality of
$$
F_v
$$
to $X$ at $y$ would require
$$
d(F_v)_y(T_yY)
+
T_pX
=
T_p\RR^3.
$$

But both
$$
d(F_v)_y(T_yY)
$$
and
$$
T_pX
$$
are one-dimensional subspaces of
$$
\RR^3.
$$
Hence their sum has dimension at most $2$, so it cannot equal the $3$-dimensional tangent space
$$
T_p\RR^3.
$$
Contradiction.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-5}

Therefore there exists
$$
v\in\RR^3
$$
such that
$$
\boxed{
X\cap(Y+v)=\varnothing.
}
$$


::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref}, the set of parameters $v$ for which
$$
F_v\pitchfork X
$$
has full measure and is therefore nonempty.
Choose one such $v$.
Then step [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} gives the desired disjointness.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-5){.pf-ref} is the required translation.

:::
