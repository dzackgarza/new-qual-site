::: {.problem}
Prove that there is no map
\[
f:\mathbb{CP}^2\to\mathbb{CP}^2
\]
of negative degree.
:::

Let
$$
u
\in
H^2(\mathbb{CP}^2;\mathbb Z)
$$
be the standard generator.

::: pf

::: pf-step

The integral cohomology ring of $\mathbb{CP}^2$ is
$$
\boxed{
H^*(\mathbb{CP}^2;\mathbb Z)
\cong
\mathbb Z[u]/(u^3),
\qquad
\deg u=2.
}
$$


::: pf-proof

The standard CW structure on $\mathbb{CP}^2$ has one cell in each of dimensions
$$
0,2,4.
$$
Its integral cohomology groups are therefore
$$
H^0\cong\mathbb Z,
\qquad
H^2\cong\mathbb Z,
\qquad
H^4\cong\mathbb Z,
$$
and zero otherwise.
The standard generator
$$
u\in H^2
$$
has
$$
u^2
$$
as a generator of
$$
H^4.
$$
Thus the ring is
$$
\mathbb Z[u]/(u^3).
$$

:::

:::

::: pf-step

For any continuous map
$$
f:\mathbb{CP}^2\to\mathbb{CP}^2,
$$
there is an integer $k$ such that
$$
f^*(u)=ku.
$$


::: pf-proof

The group
$$
H^2(\mathbb{CP}^2;\mathbb Z)
\cong
\mathbb Z
$$
is infinite cyclic generated by $u$.
Any group endomorphism of $\mathbb Z$ is multiplication by some integer.
Hence
$$
f^*(u)=ku
$$
for some
$$
k\in\mathbb Z.
$$

:::

:::

::: pf-step

The induced map on top cohomology satisfies
$$
f^*(u^2)
=
k^2u^2.
$$


::: pf-proof

Pullback preserves cup products.
Therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
f^*(u^2)
&=
f^*(u\smile u)\\
&=
f^*(u)\smile f^*(u)\\
&=
(ku)\smile(ku)\\
&=
k^2u^2.
\end{aligned}
$$

:::

:::

::: pf-step

If $d=\deg f$, then
$$
f^*(u^2)
=
d\,u^2.
$$


::: pf-proof

The manifold
$$
\mathbb{CP}^2
$$
is a closed connected oriented $4$-manifold.
For a self-map of such a manifold, the degree is the integer by which the induced map acts on the top-dimensional orientation class.
Equivalently, identifying
$$
u^2
$$
with a generator of
$$
H^4(\mathbb{CP}^2;\mathbb Z),
$$
one has
$$
f^*(u^2)
=
d\,u^2.
$$

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Hence
$$
d=k^2.
$$


::: pf-proof

Steps <1>3 and <1>4 give
$$
k^2u^2
=
d\,u^2.
$$
Since
$$
u^2
$$
generates an infinite cyclic group,
$$
d=k^2.
$$

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Therefore
$$
\deg f\ge0.
$$


::: pf-proof

By step <1>5,
$$
\deg f=k^2
$$
for an integer $k$.
Every integer square is nonnegative.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Consequently,
$$
\boxed{
\text{there is no self-map }
\mathbb{CP}^2\to\mathbb{CP}^2
\text{ of negative degree}.
}
$$


::: pf-proof

Step <1>6 rules out every negative integer as the degree of such a map.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step <1>7 is the required conclusion.

:::

:::
