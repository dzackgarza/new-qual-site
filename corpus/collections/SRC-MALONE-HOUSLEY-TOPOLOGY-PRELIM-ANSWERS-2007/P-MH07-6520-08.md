::: {.problem}
Prove that there is no map
$$
f:\CP^2\to\CP^2
$$
of negative degree.
:::

::: {.solution}

Let
$$
u
\in
H^2(\CP^2;\ZZ)
$$
be the standard generator.

::: pf

::: pf-step

The integral cohomology ring of $\CP^2$ is
$$
\boxed{
H^*(\CP^2;\ZZ)
\cong
\ZZ[u]/(u^3),
\qquad
\deg u=2.
}
$$


::: pf-proof

The standard CW structure on $\CP^2$ has one cell in each of dimensions
$$
0,2,4.
$$
Its integral cohomology groups are therefore
$$
H^0\cong\ZZ,
\qquad
H^2\cong\ZZ,
\qquad
H^4\cong\ZZ,
$$
and zero otherwise.
The standard generator
$$
u\in H^2
$$
has
$$
u^2
$$
as a generator of
$$
H^4.
$$
Thus the ring is
$$
\ZZ[u]/(u^3).
$$

:::

:::

::: pf-step

For any continuous map
$$
f:\CP^2\to\CP^2,
$$
there is an integer $k$ such that
$$
f^*(u)=ku.
$$


::: pf-proof

The group
$$
H^2(\CP^2;\ZZ)
\cong
\ZZ
$$
is infinite cyclic generated by $u$.
Any group endomorphism of $\ZZ$ is multiplication by some integer.
Hence
$$
f^*(u)=ku
$$
for some
$$
k\in\ZZ.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

The induced map on top cohomology satisfies
$$
f^*(u^2)
=
k^2u^2.
$$


::: pf-proof

Pullback preserves cup products.
Therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
f^*(u^2)
&=
f^*(u\smile u)\\
&=
f^*(u)\smile f^*(u)\\
&=
(ku)\smile(ku)\\
&=
k^2u^2.
\end{aligned}
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

If $d=\deg f$, then
$$
f^*(u^2)
=
d\,u^2.
$$


::: pf-proof

The manifold
$$
\CP^2
$$
is a closed connected oriented $4$-manifold.
For a self-map of such a manifold, the degree is the integer by which the induced map acts on the top-dimensional orientation class.
Equivalently, identifying
$$
u^2
$$
with a generator of
$$
H^4(\CP^2;\ZZ),
$$
one has
$$
f^*(u^2)
=
d\,u^2.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-5}

Hence
$$
d=k^2.
$$


::: pf-proof

Steps [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} and [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} give
$$
k^2u^2
=
d\,u^2.
$$
Since
$$
u^2
$$
generates an infinite cyclic group,
$$
d=k^2.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-6}

Therefore
$$
\deg f\ge0.
$$


::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1-5){.pf-ref},
$$
\deg f=k^2
$$
for an integer $k$.
Every integer square is nonnegative.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-7}

Consequently,
$$
\boxed{
\text{there is no self-map }
\CP^2\to\CP^2
\text{ of negative degree}.
}
$$


::: pf-proof

Step [](#s1-6){.pf-ref} rules out every negative integer as the degree of such a map.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-7){.pf-ref} is the required conclusion.

:::
