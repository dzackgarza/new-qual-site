::: {.problem}
Let $M_{n\times n}$ be the manifold of real $n\times n$ matrices, identified with $\mathbb R^{n^2}$ by choosing an ordering of entries. Let
\[
O(n)=\{A\in M_{n\times n}:AA^T=I\}.
\]
Prove that $O(n)$ is a submanifold of $M_{n\times n}$. What is its dimension?
:::
