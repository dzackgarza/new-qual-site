::: {.problem}
Let $\omega$ be a compactly supported smooth $n$-form on $\mathbb R^n$. Prove that the following are equivalent:

1. There exists a compactly supported smooth $(n-1)$-form $\eta$ such that $\omega=d\eta$.
2. One has
   \[
   \int_{\mathbb R^n}\omega=0.
   \]
:::
