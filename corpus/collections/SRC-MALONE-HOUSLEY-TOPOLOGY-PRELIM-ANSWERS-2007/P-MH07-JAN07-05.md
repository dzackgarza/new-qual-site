::: {.problem}
Let $f:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R$ be smooth. Show that for every $\varepsilon>0$ there is a vector $v\in\mathbb R^n$ with $\lVert v\rVert<\varepsilon$ such that
\[
g_v(x)=f(x)+v\cdot x
\]
is a Morse function.
:::
