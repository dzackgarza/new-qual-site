::: {.problem}
Let $V,W:\mathbb R^4\to\mathbb R^4$ be the vector fields
\[
V(x,y,z,w)=(y,-x,w,-z),
\qquad
W(x,y,z,w)=(w,z,-y,-x).
\]
Is there a nonempty surface $\Sigma\subset\mathbb R^4$ such that
\[
V(p),W(p)\in T_p\Sigma
\]
for every $p\in\Sigma$? Find such a surface or prove that none exists.
:::
