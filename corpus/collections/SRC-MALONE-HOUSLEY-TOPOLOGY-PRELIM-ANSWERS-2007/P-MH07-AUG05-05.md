::: {.problem}
Let
$$
f:\RR^2\to\RR^3,
\qquad
f(x,y)=(x^2-y,\,x+y^2,\,\sin x),
$$
and let $\omega$ be the $2$-form on $\RR^3$ with coordinates $(u,v,w)$ given by
$$
\omega=u\,dv\wedge dw.
$$
Compute the pullback $f^*\omega$.
:::

::: {.solution}

Write the coordinate functions of $f$ as
$$
u\circ f
=
x^2-y,
$$
$$
v\circ f
=
x+y^2,
$$
and
$$
w\circ f
=
\sin x.
$$

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

One has
$$
f^*u
=
x^2-y.
$$


::: pf-proof

The first coordinate function of
$$
f(x,y)
$$
is
$$
x^2-y.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

One has
$$
f^*(dv)
=
d(v\circ f)
=
dx+2y\,dy.
$$


::: pf-proof

Since pullback commutes with exterior differentiation,
$$
f^*(dv)
=
d(f^*v)
=
d(x+y^2)
=
dx+2y\,dy.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

One has
$$
f^*(dw)
=
d(w\circ f)
=
\cos x\,dx.
$$


::: pf-proof

Again,
$$
f^*(dw)
=
d(f^*w)
=
d(\sin x)
=
\cos x\,dx.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

Therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
f^*(dv\wedge dw)
&=
f^*(dv)\wedge f^*(dw)\\
&=
(dx+2y\,dy)
\wedge
(\cos x\,dx).
\end{aligned}
$$


::: pf-proof

Pullback preserves wedge products.
Apply steps [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} through [](#s1-3){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-5}

The wedge product in step [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} simplifies to
$$
-2y\cos x\,dx\wedge dy.
$$


::: pf-proof

Expand:
$$
\begin{aligned}
(dx+2y\,dy)\wedge(\cos x\,dx)
&=
\cos x\,dx\wedge dx
+
2y\cos x\,dy\wedge dx\\
&=
0
+
2y\cos x\,dy\wedge dx\\
&=
-2y\cos x\,dx\wedge dy.
\end{aligned}
$$
Here
$$
dx\wedge dx=0
$$
and
$$
dy\wedge dx
=
-dx\wedge dy.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-6}

Hence
$$
\boxed{
f^*\omega
=
-2y(x^2-y)\cos x\,
dx\wedge dy.
}
$$


::: pf-proof

Since
$$
\omega
=
u\,dv\wedge dw,
$$
one has
$$
f^*\omega
=
(f^*u)
f^*(dv\wedge dw).
$$
Apply step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} and step [](#s1-5){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-6){.pf-ref} is the required pullback.

:::
