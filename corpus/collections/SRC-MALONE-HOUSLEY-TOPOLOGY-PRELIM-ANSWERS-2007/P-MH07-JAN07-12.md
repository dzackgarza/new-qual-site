::: {.problem}
Let $M$ be a closed connected $5$-manifold such that
\[
\pi_1(M)\cong\ZZ/7\ZZ.
\]
Assume
\[
H_2(M;\ZZ)\cong\ZZ.
\]
Compute all the other homology and cohomology groups of $M$ with integral coefficients.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

The manifold $M$ is orientable.


::: pf-proof

For a connected manifold, the orientation character is a homomorphism
$$
w_1:
\pi_1(M)
\to
\ZZ/2.
$$
But
$$
\pi_1(M)
\cong
\ZZ/7
$$
has no nontrivial homomorphism to
$$
\ZZ/2,
$$
because the image of an element of order $7$ must have order dividing both $7$ and $2$.
Hence the orientation character is trivial, so $M$ is orientable.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

Since $M$ is connected,
$$
H_0(M;\ZZ)
\cong
\ZZ.
$$


::: pf-proof

This is the standard zeroth-homology computation for a connected space.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

One has
$$
H_1(M;\ZZ)
\cong
\ZZ/7.
$$


::: pf-proof

For any path-connected space,
$$
H_1(M;\ZZ)
\cong
\pi_1(M)^{\mathrm{ab}}.
$$
The group
$$
\ZZ/7
$$
is already abelian.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

By hypothesis,
$$
H_2(M;\ZZ)
\cong
\ZZ.
$$


::: pf-proof

This is the second assumption.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-5}

The universal coefficient theorem gives
$$
H^0(M;\ZZ)
\cong
\ZZ.
$$


::: pf-proof

Since
$$
H_0(M;\ZZ)
\cong
\ZZ,
$$
one has
$$
H^0(M;\ZZ)
\cong
\operatorname{Hom}(\ZZ,\ZZ)
\cong
\ZZ.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-6}

The universal coefficient theorem gives
$$
H^1(M;\ZZ)=0.
$$


::: pf-proof

The degree-$1$ exact sequence is
$$
0
\to
\operatorname{Ext}
\bigl(
H_0(M),\ZZ
\bigr)
\to
H^1(M;\ZZ)
\to
\operatorname{Hom}
\bigl(
H_1(M),\ZZ
\bigr)
\to
0.
$$
Now
$$
\operatorname{Ext}(\ZZ,\ZZ)=0
$$
and
$$
\operatorname{Hom}(\ZZ/7,\ZZ)=0.
$$
Hence
$$
H^1(M;\ZZ)=0.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-7}

The universal coefficient theorem gives
$$
H^2(M;\ZZ)
\cong
\ZZ\oplus\ZZ/7.
$$


::: pf-proof

The degree-$2$ sequence is
$$
0
\to
\operatorname{Ext}
\bigl(
H_1(M),\ZZ
\bigr)
\to
H^2(M;\ZZ)
\to
\operatorname{Hom}
\bigl(
H_2(M),\ZZ
\bigr)
\to
0.
$$
By steps [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} through [](#s1-4){.pf-ref},
$$
\operatorname{Ext}
\left(
\ZZ/7,\ZZ
\right)
\cong
\ZZ/7
$$
and
$$
\operatorname{Hom}
\left(
\ZZ,\ZZ
\right)
\cong
\ZZ.
$$
Thus
$$
0
\to
\ZZ/7
\to
H^2(M;\ZZ)
\to
\ZZ
\to
0.
$$
Because $\ZZ$ is free, this short exact sequence splits.
Hence
$$
H^2(M;\ZZ)
\cong
\ZZ\oplus\ZZ/7.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-8}

Poincaré duality gives
$$
H_5(M;\ZZ)
\cong
\ZZ.
$$


::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref}, $M$ is a closed orientable $5$-manifold.
Poincaré duality gives
$$
H_i(M;\ZZ)
\cong
H^{5-i}(M;\ZZ).
$$
For
$$
i=5,
$$
use step [](#s1-5){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-9}

Poincaré duality gives
$$
H_4(M;\ZZ)=0.
$$


::: pf-proof

One has
$$
H_4(M;\ZZ)
\cong
H^1(M;\ZZ),
$$
and step [](#s1-6){.pf-ref} gives the latter group as zero.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-10}

Poincaré duality gives
$$
H_3(M;\ZZ)
\cong
\ZZ\oplus\ZZ/7.
$$


::: pf-proof

One has
$$
H_3(M;\ZZ)
\cong
H^2(M;\ZZ).
$$
Apply step [](#s1-7){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-11}

Therefore the integral homology groups are
$$
\boxed{
H_i(M;\ZZ)
\cong
\begin{cases}
\ZZ,&i=0,2,5,\\
\ZZ/7,&i=1,\\
\ZZ\oplus\ZZ/7,&i=3,\\
0,&i=4,\\
0,&i>5.
\end{cases}
}
$$


::: pf-proof

Combine steps [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} through [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} and [](#s1-8){.pf-ref} through [](#s1-10){.pf-ref}.
Homology vanishes above the dimension of $M$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-12}

Poincaré duality gives
$$
H^3(M;\ZZ)
\cong
H_2(M;\ZZ)
\cong
\ZZ.
$$


::: pf-proof

Set
$$
i=2
$$
in
$$
H_i(M;\ZZ)
\cong
H^{5-i}(M;\ZZ).
$$
Then
$$
H^3(M;\ZZ)
\cong
H_2(M;\ZZ)
\cong
\ZZ.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-13}

Poincaré duality gives
$$
H^4(M;\ZZ)
\cong
H_1(M;\ZZ)
\cong
\ZZ/7.
$$


::: pf-proof

Take
$$
i=1
$$
in Poincaré duality and apply step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-14}

Poincaré duality gives
$$
H^5(M;\ZZ)
\cong
H_0(M;\ZZ)
\cong
\ZZ.
$$


::: pf-proof

Take
$$
i=0
$$
and apply step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-15}

Therefore the integral cohomology groups are
$$
\boxed{
H^i(M;\ZZ)
\cong
\begin{cases}
\ZZ,&i=0,3,5,\\
0,&i=1,\\
\ZZ\oplus\ZZ/7,&i=2,\\
\ZZ/7,&i=4,\\
0,&i>5.
\end{cases}
}
$$


::: pf-proof

Use steps [](#s1-5){.pf-ref} through [](#s1-7){.pf-ref} and [](#s1-12){.pf-ref} through [](#s1-14){.pf-ref}.
There is no cohomology above degree $5$ for a closed $5$-manifold.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Steps [](#s1-11){.pf-ref} and [](#s1-15){.pf-ref} give all homology and cohomology groups requested.

:::
