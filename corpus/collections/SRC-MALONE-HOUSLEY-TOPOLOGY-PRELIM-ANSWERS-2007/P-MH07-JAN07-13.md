::: {.problem}
Let $F_n$ be the free group of rank $n$, and let $G\subset F_n$ be a subgroup of index $m$. Prove that $G$ is free and compute its rank.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let
$$
X
\coloneqq
\bigvee_{i=1}^{n}S^1
$$
be the wedge of $n$ circles.

<1>1. One has
$$
\pi_1(X)
\cong
F_n.
$$

::: {.proof}
The fundamental group of a wedge of $n$ circles is the free group on the homotopy classes of the $n$ circle loops.
Thus
$$
\pi_1(X)
\cong
F_n.
$$
:::

<1>2. The subgroup
$$
G
\le
\pi_1(X)
$$
corresponds to a connected covering space
$$
p:Y\to X.
$$

::: {.proof}
The space $X$ is connected, locally path-connected, and semilocally simply connected.
The classification theorem for connected covering spaces therefore assigns to the subgroup
$$
G\le\pi_1(X)
$$
a connected covering
$$
p:Y\to X
$$
such that
$$
p_*\pi_1(Y)
$$
is conjugate to $G$.
After choosing compatible basepoints, identify
$$
\pi_1(Y)
\cong
G.
$$
:::

<1>3. The covering
$$
p:Y\to X
$$
has exactly $m$ sheets.

::: {.proof}
For a connected covering corresponding to a subgroup $G$ of
$$
\pi_1(X),
$$
the number of sheets equals the index
$$
[\pi_1(X):G].
$$
By hypothesis this index is $m$.
:::

<1>4. The covering space $Y$ is a connected graph.

::: {.proof}
The base $X$ is a $1$-dimensional CW complex with one vertex and $n$ edges.
A covering of a CW complex inherits a CW structure in which every open cell lifts homeomorphically to open cells of the same dimension.
Therefore $Y$ is a $1$-dimensional CW complex, hence a graph.
It is connected by construction in step <1>2.
:::

<1>5. The graph $Y$ has exactly $m$ vertices.

::: {.proof}
The base graph $X$ has one vertex
$$
v.
$$
Because the covering has $m$ sheets, the fiber
$$
p^{-1}(v)
$$
has exactly $m$ points.
These are precisely the vertices of the lifted CW structure on $Y$.
Hence
$$
V(Y)=m.
$$
:::

<1>6. The graph $Y$ has exactly $mn$ edges.

::: {.proof}
The graph $X$ has $n$ open $1$-cells.
Each open edge is evenly covered by an $m$-sheeted covering, so it has exactly $m$ lifts to open edges of $Y$.
Thus the total number of edges is
$$
n\cdot m
=
mn.
$$
:::

<1>7. The fundamental group of a connected finite graph with $V$ vertices and $E$ edges is free of rank
$$
E-V+1.
$$

::: {.proof}
Choose a maximal tree
$$
T
$$
in the graph.
Because the graph is connected, a maximal tree contains all $V$ vertices and has exactly
$$
V-1
$$
edges.

Collapse $T$ to a point.
Since $T$ is contractible, this collapse is a homotopy equivalence.
The remaining
$$
E-(V-1)
=
E-V+1
$$
edges become loops attached to one vertex.
Thus the graph is homotopy equivalent to a wedge of
$$
E-V+1
$$
circles.
Its fundamental group is therefore free of that rank.
:::

<1>8. Hence
$$
\pi_1(Y)
$$
is free of rank
$$
mn-m+1.
$$

::: {.proof}
By steps <1>5--<1>6,
$$
V(Y)=m,
\qquad
E(Y)=mn.
$$
Apply step <1>7:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{rank}\pi_1(Y)
&=
E(Y)-V(Y)+1\\
&=
mn-m+1\\
&=
1+m(n-1).
\end{aligned}
$$
:::

<1>9. Therefore
$$
\boxed{
G
\text{ is free of rank }
1+m(n-1).
}
$$

::: {.proof}
By step <1>2,
$$
G
\cong
\pi_1(Y).
$$
Step <1>8 shows that the latter is free of the stated rank.
:::

<1>10. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>9 is the Schreier index formula for finite-index subgroups of free groups.
:::
:::
