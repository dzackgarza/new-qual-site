::: {.problem}
Regard $\mathbb{RP}^2$ as nonzero triples $(x,y,z)\in\mathbb R^3$ modulo nonzero scalar multiplication. Define
\[
f([x:y:z])=
\frac{x^2+2y^2}{x^2+y^2+z^2}.
\]
Compute all critical points of $f$. Show that $f$ is a Morse function and compute the Morse index of each critical point.
:::
