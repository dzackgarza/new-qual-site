::: {.problem}
Let $M_g$ be the closed orientable surface of genus $g$. Show that if $M_g$ retracts onto a graph
\[
X\subset M_g,
\]
then $H_1(X)$ has rank at most $g$.

You may use the algebraic fact that a nonsingular skew-symmetric bilinear form on $\mathbb Q^n$ cannot vanish identically on $V\times V$ for a subspace $V\subseteq\mathbb Q^n$ of dimension greater than $n/2$.
:::
