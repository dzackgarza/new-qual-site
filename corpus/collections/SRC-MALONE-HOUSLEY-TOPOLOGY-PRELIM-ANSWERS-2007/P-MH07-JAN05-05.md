::: {.problem}
Let
\[
X=y\frac{\partial}{\partial x}-(x+1)\frac{\partial}{\partial y},
\qquad
Y=y\frac{\partial}{\partial x}-(x-1)\frac{\partial}{\partial y}
\]
be vector fields on $\mathbb R^2$. Compute $[X,Y]$.
:::

Write
$$
X
=
X^x\frac{\partial}{\partial x}
+
X^y\frac{\partial}{\partial y}
$$
and
$$
Y
=
Y^x\frac{\partial}{\partial x}
+
Y^y\frac{\partial}{\partial y},
$$
where
$$
X^x=y,
\qquad
X^y=-(x+1),
$$
and
$$
Y^x=y,
\qquad
Y^y=-(x-1)=1-x.
$$

::: pf

::: pf-step

The $x$-component of the Lie bracket is
$$
[X,Y]^x
=
X(Y^x)-Y(X^x).
$$


::: pf-proof

For vector fields
$$
X=\sum_iX^i\partial_i,
\qquad
Y=\sum_iY^i\partial_i,
$$
the coordinate formula is
$$
[X,Y]^i
=
X(Y^i)-Y(X^i).
$$
Apply this for the $x$-coordinate.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

One has
$$
X(Y^x)
=
-(x+1).
$$


::: pf-proof

Since
$$
Y^x=y,
$$
$$
X(Y^x)
=
X(y)
=
X^y
=
-(x+1).
$$

:::

:::

::: pf-step

One has
$$
Y(X^x)
=
1-x.
$$


::: pf-proof

Since
$$
X^x=y,
$$
$$
Y(X^x)
=
Y(y)
=
Y^y
=
1-x.
$$

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Therefore
$$
\boxed{
[X,Y]^x=-2.
}
$$


::: pf-proof

Combine steps <1>1--<1>3:
$$
\begin{aligned}
[X,Y]^x
&=
-(x+1)-(1-x)\\
&=
-2.
\end{aligned}
$$

:::

:::

::: pf-step

The $y$-component of the Lie bracket is
$$
[X,Y]^y
=
X(Y^y)-Y(X^y).
$$


::: pf-proof

Apply the coordinate formula from step <1>1 to the $y$-coordinate.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

One has
$$
X(Y^y)=-y.
$$


::: pf-proof

Since
$$
Y^y=1-x,
$$
$$
X(Y^y)
=
X(1-x)
=
-X(x)
=
-y.
$$

:::

:::

::: pf-step

One has
$$
Y(X^y)=-y.
$$


::: pf-proof

Since
$$
X^y=-(x+1),
$$
$$
Y(X^y)
=
Y(-(x+1))
=
-Y(x)
=
-y.
$$

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Therefore
$$
\boxed{
[X,Y]^y=0.
}
$$


::: pf-proof

By steps <1>5--<1>7,
$$
[X,Y]^y
=
-y-(-y)
=
0.
$$

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Hence
$$
\boxed{
[X,Y]
=
-2\frac{\partial}{\partial x}.
}
$$


::: pf-proof

Step <1>4 gives the $x$-component and step <1>8 gives the $y$-component.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step <1>9 is the requested Lie bracket.

:::

:::
