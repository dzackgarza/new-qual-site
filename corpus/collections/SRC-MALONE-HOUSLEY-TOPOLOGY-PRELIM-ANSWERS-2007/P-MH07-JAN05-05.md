::: {.problem}
Let
$$
X=y\frac{\partial}{\partial x}-(x+1)\frac{\partial}{\partial y},
\qquad
Y=y\frac{\partial}{\partial x}-(x-1)\frac{\partial}{\partial y}
$$
be vector fields on $\RR^2$. Compute $[X,Y]$.
:::

::: {.solution}

Write
$$
X
=
X^x\frac{\partial}{\partial x}
+
X^y\frac{\partial}{\partial y}
$$
and
$$
Y
=
Y^x\frac{\partial}{\partial x}
+
Y^y\frac{\partial}{\partial y},
$$
where
$$
X^x=y,
\qquad
X^y=-(x+1),
$$
and
$$
Y^x=y,
\qquad
Y^y=-(x-1)=1-x.
$$

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

The $x$-component of the Lie bracket is
$$
[X,Y]^x
=
X(Y^x)-Y(X^x).
$$


::: pf-proof

For vector fields
$$
X=\sum_iX^i\partial_i,
\qquad
Y=\sum_iY^i\partial_i,
$$
the coordinate formula is
$$
[X,Y]^i
=
X(Y^i)-Y(X^i).
$$
Apply this for the $x$-coordinate.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

One has
$$
X(Y^x)
=
-(x+1).
$$


::: pf-proof

Since
$$
Y^x=y,
$$
$$
X(Y^x)
=
X(y)
=
X^y
=
-(x+1).
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

One has
$$
Y(X^x)
=
1-x.
$$


::: pf-proof

Since
$$
X^x=y,
$$
$$
Y(X^x)
=
Y(y)
=
Y^y
=
1-x.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

Therefore
$$
\boxed{
[X,Y]^x=-2.
}
$$


::: pf-proof

Combine steps [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} through [](#s1-3){.pf-ref}:
$$
\begin{aligned}
[X,Y]^x
&=
-(x+1)-(1-x)\\
&=
-2.
\end{aligned}
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-5}

The $y$-component of the Lie bracket is
$$
[X,Y]^y
=
X(Y^y)-Y(X^y).
$$


::: pf-proof

Apply the coordinate formula from step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} to the $y$-coordinate.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-6}

One has
$$
X(Y^y)=-y.
$$


::: pf-proof

Since
$$
Y^y=1-x,
$$
$$
X(Y^y)
=
X(1-x)
=
-X(x)
=
-y.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-7}

One has
$$
Y(X^y)=-y.
$$


::: pf-proof

Since
$$
X^y=-(x+1),
$$
$$
Y(X^y)
=
Y(-(x+1))
=
-Y(x)
=
-y.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-8}

Therefore
$$
\boxed{
[X,Y]^y=0.
}
$$


::: pf-proof

By steps [](#s1-5){.pf-ref} through [](#s1-7){.pf-ref},
$$
[X,Y]^y
=
-y-(-y)
=
0.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-9}

Hence
$$
\boxed{
[X,Y]
=
-2\frac{\partial}{\partial x}.
}
$$


::: pf-proof

Step [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} gives the $x$-component and step [](#s1-8){.pf-ref} gives the $y$-component.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-9){.pf-ref} is the requested Lie bracket.

:::
