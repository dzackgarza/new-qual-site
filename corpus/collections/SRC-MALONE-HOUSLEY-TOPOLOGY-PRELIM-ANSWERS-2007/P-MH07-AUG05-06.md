::: {.problem}
Prove that for any two disjoint closed subsets $A,B\subset\mathbb R^n$ there is a smooth function
\[
f:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R
\]
such that $f=0$ on $A$ and $f=1$ on $B$.
:::
