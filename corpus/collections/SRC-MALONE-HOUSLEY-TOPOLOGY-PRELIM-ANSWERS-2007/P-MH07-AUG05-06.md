::: {.problem}
Prove that for any two disjoint closed subsets $A,B\subset\RR^n$ there is a smooth function
\[
f:\RR^n\to\RR
\]
such that $f=0$ on $A$ and $f=1$ on $B$.
:::

::: {.solution}

Define
$$
U
\coloneqq
\RR^n\setminus B
$$
and
$$
V
\coloneqq
\RR^n\setminus A.
$$

::: pf

::: pf-step

The sets $U$ and $V$ are open.


::: pf-proof

The sets
$$
A
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
B
$$
are closed by hypothesis.
Therefore their complements are open.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

One has
$$
U\cup V
=
\RR^n.
$$


::: pf-proof

Suppose
$$
x\notin U\cup V.
$$
Then
$$
x\notin U
$$
gives
$$
x\in B,
$$
and
$$
x\notin V
$$
gives
$$
x\in A.
$$
Thus
$$
x\in A\cap B,
$$
contradicting
$$
A\cap B=\varnothing.
$$
Hence
$$
U\cup V=\RR^n.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

There exists a smooth partition of unity
$$
\{\phi_U,\phi_V\}
$$
subordinate to the open cover
$$
\{U,V\}.
$$


::: pf-proof

The smooth manifold
$$
\RR^n
$$
is paracompact.
Therefore every open cover admits a smooth partition of unity subordinate to it.
Hence there exist smooth functions
$$
\phi_U,\phi_V:
\RR^n
\to
[0,1]
$$
such that
$$
\phi_U+\phi_V=1,
$$
$$
\operatorname{supp}\phi_U
\subseteq
U,
$$
and
$$
\operatorname{supp}\phi_V
\subseteq
V.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

Define
$$
\boxed{
f
\coloneqq
\phi_V.
}
$$
Then $f$ is smooth.


::: pf-proof

The function
$$
\phi_V
$$
is smooth by step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-5}

One has
$$
f=0
$$
on $A$.


::: pf-proof

Since
$$
\operatorname{supp}\phi_V
\subseteq
V
=
\RR^n\setminus A,
$$
every point of $A$ lies outside the support of
$$
\phi_V.
$$
Therefore
$$
\phi_V=0
$$
on $A$.
Thus
$$
f=0
$$
on $A$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-6}

One has
$$
f=1
$$
on $B$.


::: pf-proof

Since
$$
\operatorname{supp}\phi_U
\subseteq
U
=
\RR^n\setminus B,
$$
one has
$$
\phi_U=0
$$
on $B$.
Using
$$
\phi_U+\phi_V=1,
$$
it follows that
$$
\phi_V=1
$$
on $B$.
Therefore
$$
f=1
$$
on $B$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-7}

Therefore there exists a smooth function
$$
f:\RR^n\to\RR
$$
with
$$
\boxed{
f|_A=0,
\qquad
f|_B=1.
}
$$


::: pf-proof

The function defined in step [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} has the required values by steps [](#s1-5){.pf-ref} through [](#s1-6){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-7){.pf-ref} is the required conclusion.

:::
