::: {.problem}
Let $\omega$ be a $1$-form on $\RR^n$ and let $X,Y$ be vector fields. Verify
$$
d\omega(X,Y)
=X(\omega(Y))-Y(\omega(X))-\omega([X,Y]).
$$
Use Spivak's normalization convention, in which
$$
(dx\wedge dy)(\partial_x,\partial_y)=1.
$$
:::

::: {.solution}

Write
$$
\omega
=
\sum_{j=1}^{n}
a_j\,dx_j,
$$
$$
X
=
\sum_{i=1}^{n}
X^i\frac{\partial}{\partial x_i},
\qquad
Y
=
\sum_{j=1}^{n}
Y^j\frac{\partial}{\partial x_j}.
$$

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

The exterior derivative of $\omega$ is
$$
\boxed{
d\omega
=
\sum_{1\le i<j\le n}
\left(
\frac{\partial a_j}{\partial x_i}
-
\frac{\partial a_i}{\partial x_j}
\right)
dx_i\wedge dx_j.
}
$$


::: pf-proof

Since
$$
d(dx_j)=0,
$$
one has
$$
d\omega
=
\sum_j
da_j\wedge dx_j.
$$
Also
$$
da_j
=
\sum_i
\frac{\partial a_j}{\partial x_i}
dx_i.
$$
Therefore
$$
d\omega
=
\sum_{i,j}
\frac{\partial a_j}{\partial x_i}
dx_i\wedge dx_j.
$$
Collecting the terms with
$$
i<j
$$
and using
$$
dx_j\wedge dx_i
=
-dx_i\wedge dx_j
$$
gives the displayed formula.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

Evaluating step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} on $(X,Y)$ gives
$$
d\omega(X,Y)
=
\sum_{i,j}
X^iY^j
\left(
\frac{\partial a_j}{\partial x_i}
-
\frac{\partial a_i}{\partial x_j}
\right).
$$


::: pf-proof

Under Spivak's normalization,
$$
(dx_i\wedge dx_j)(X,Y)
=
dx_i(X)dx_j(Y)
-
dx_i(Y)dx_j(X)
$$
for
$$
i<j.
$$
Thus
$$
(dx_i\wedge dx_j)(X,Y)
=
X^iY^j-Y^iX^j.
$$
Substituting this into step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} and combining the two antisymmetric terms gives
$$
d\omega(X,Y)
=
\sum_{i,j}
X^iY^j
\left(
\partial_i a_j-\partial_j a_i
\right).
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

One has
$$
\omega(Y)
=
\sum_j a_jY^j
$$
and therefore
$$
X(\omega(Y))
=
\sum_{i,j}
X^i
\left(
\frac{\partial a_j}{\partial x_i}Y^j
+
a_j\frac{\partial Y^j}{\partial x_i}
\right).
$$


::: pf-proof

The first formula follows from
$$
dx_j(Y)=Y^j.
$$
Apply the vector field
$$
X=\sum_iX^i\partial_i
$$
and use the product rule.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

Similarly,
$$
Y(\omega(X))
=
\sum_{i,j}
Y^i
\left(
\frac{\partial a_j}{\partial x_i}X^j
+
a_j\frac{\partial X^j}{\partial x_i}
\right).
$$


::: pf-proof

The calculation is identical to step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} with $X$ and $Y$ interchanged.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-5}

The Lie bracket is
$$
[X,Y]
=
\sum_j
\left(
\sum_i
X^i\frac{\partial Y^j}{\partial x_i}
-
Y^i\frac{\partial X^j}{\partial x_i}
\right)
\frac{\partial}{\partial x_j}.
$$


::: pf-proof

This is the standard coordinate formula for the Lie bracket.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-6}

Hence
$$
\omega([X,Y])
=
\sum_{i,j}
a_j
\left(
X^i\frac{\partial Y^j}{\partial x_i}
-
Y^i\frac{\partial X^j}{\partial x_i}
\right).
$$


::: pf-proof

Apply the $1$-form
$$
\omega=\sum_j a_jdx_j
$$
to the vector field in step [](#s1-5){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-7}

Subtracting the expressions from steps [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} through [](#s1-6){.pf-ref} yields
$$
\begin{aligned}
&X(\omega(Y))
-Y(\omega(X))
-\omega([X,Y])\\
&\qquad=
\sum_{i,j}
\left(
X^iY^j\frac{\partial a_j}{\partial x_i}
-
Y^iX^j\frac{\partial a_j}{\partial x_i}
\right).
\end{aligned}
$$


::: pf-proof

The terms
$$
\sum_{i,j}
a_jX^i\frac{\partial Y^j}{\partial x_i}
$$
from step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} cancel with the corresponding terms in step [](#s1-6){.pf-ref}.
Likewise, the terms involving
$$
\partial_iX^j
$$
cancel between steps [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} and [](#s1-6){.pf-ref}.
Only the derivatives of the coefficient functions $a_j$ remain.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-8}

Renaming the indices in the second sum of step [](#s1-7){.pf-ref} gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
&X(\omega(Y))
-Y(\omega(X))
-\omega([X,Y])\\
&\qquad=
\sum_{i,j}
X^iY^j
\left(
\frac{\partial a_j}{\partial x_i}
-
\frac{\partial a_i}{\partial x_j}
\right).
\end{aligned}
$$


::: pf-proof

In the second term of step [](#s1-7){.pf-ref},
replace the dummy indices
$$
i\leftrightarrow j.
$$
Then
$$
\sum_{i,j}
Y^iX^j
\frac{\partial a_j}{\partial x_i}
=
\sum_{i,j}
X^iY^j
\frac{\partial a_i}{\partial x_j}.
$$
Substitute this identity.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-9}

Therefore
$$
\boxed{
d\omega(X,Y)
=
X(\omega(Y))
-Y(\omega(X))
-\omega([X,Y]).
}
$$


::: pf-proof

The right-hand side in step [](#s1-8){.pf-ref} is exactly the expression for
$$
d\omega(X,Y)
$$
obtained in step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-9){.pf-ref} is the required formula.
