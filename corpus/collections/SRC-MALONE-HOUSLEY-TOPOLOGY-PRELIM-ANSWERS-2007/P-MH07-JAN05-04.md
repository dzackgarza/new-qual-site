::: {.problem}
Let $M$ be a manifold and let $\xi$ be a smooth $k$-plane field on $M$. Suppose that $X$ and $Y$ are smooth vector fields on $M$ taking values in $\xi$. Show that if
\[
X_p=0
\]
at some $p\in M$, then
\[
[X,Y]_p\in\xi_p.
\]
:::

::: {.solution}

Choose a smooth local frame
$$
E_1,\ldots,E_k
$$
for the plane field $\xi$ on a neighborhood
$$
U
$$
of $p$.

::: pf

::: pf-step

On $U$, write
$$
X
=
\sum_{i=1}^{k}
f_iE_i
$$
and
$$
Y
=
\sum_{j=1}^{k}
g_jE_j
$$
for smooth functions
$$
f_i,g_j:U\to\RR.
$$


::: pf-proof

Because
$$
E_1,\ldots,E_k
$$
form a basis of
$$
\xi_q
$$
for every
$$
q\in U,
$$
and because $X,Y$ take values in $\xi$, they admit unique smooth coefficient functions in this frame.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

The condition
$$
X_p=0
$$
implies
$$
f_i(p)=0
$$
for every
$$
i.
$$


::: pf-proof

At $p$,
$$
0
=
X_p
=
\sum_{i=1}^{k}
f_i(p)E_i(p).
$$
The vectors
$$
E_1(p),\ldots,E_k(p)
$$
are linearly independent.
Hence every coefficient
$$
f_i(p)
$$
must vanish.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

For smooth functions $f,g$ and vector fields $A,B$,
$$
\boxed{
[fA,gB]
=
fg[A,B]
+
fA(g)B
-
gB(f)A.
}
$$


::: pf-proof

Apply both sides to an arbitrary smooth function $h$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
[fA,gB](h)
&=
fA(gB(h))
-
gB(fA(h))\\
&=
fA(g)B(h)
+
fgA(B(h))\\
&\qquad
-
gB(f)A(h)
-
fgB(A(h))\\
&=
\left(
fg[A,B]
+
fA(g)B
-
gB(f)A
\right)(h).
\end{aligned}
$$
Since the two vector fields act identically on every smooth function, they are equal.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

Expanding the bracket gives
$$
\begin{aligned}
[X,Y]
&=
\sum_{i,j}
f_ig_j[E_i,E_j]\\
&\quad
+
\sum_{i,j}
f_iE_i(g_j)E_j\\
&\quad
-
\sum_{i,j}
g_jE_j(f_i)E_i.
\end{aligned}
$$


::: pf-proof

Use bilinearity of the Lie bracket together with step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref}:
$$
[X,Y]
=
\sum_{i,j}
[f_iE_i,g_jE_j].
$$
Substitute the formula from step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} term by term.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-5}

Evaluating at $p$ gives
$$
\boxed{
[X,Y]_p
=
-
\sum_{i,j}
g_j(p)\,
E_j(f_i)(p)\,
E_i(p).
}
$$


::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref},
$$
f_i(p)=0
$$
for every $i$.
Therefore at $p$:

$$
f_i(p)g_j(p)[E_i,E_j]_p
=
0
$$
and
$$
f_i(p)E_i(g_j)(p)E_j(p)
=
0.
$$
Only the third sum from step [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} remains.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-6}

Hence
$$
[X,Y]_p
\in
\xi_p.
$$


::: pf-proof

The right-hand side of step [](#s1-5){.pf-ref} is a linear combination of
$$
E_1(p),\ldots,E_k(p).
$$
These vectors span
$$
\xi_p.
$$
Therefore
$$
\boxed{
[X,Y]_p\in\xi_p.
}
$$

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-6){.pf-ref} is the required conclusion.

:::
