Identify $\mathbb C$ with $\mathbb R^2$ in the usual way. In
\[
\mathbb C^2\setminus\left\{(0,0),\left(0,\tfrac12\right)\right\}
\]
consider the submanifolds
\[
M_1=\{(z,w)\in\mathbb C^2:z^2+w^2=1\},
\]
\[
M_2=\{(z,w)\in\mathbb C^2:z^2-w^2+w=1\},
\]
and
\[
M_3=\{(x_1,y_1,x_2,y_2)\in\mathbb R^4:1-x_1+x_2^2+y_1^2+y_2^2=0\}.
\]
Which pairs $M_i,M_j$ are transverse at the point $p=(1,0)\in\mathbb C^2$?
