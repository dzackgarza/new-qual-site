::: {.problem}
State the Frobenius integrability theorem. Show that the plane field on $\RR^3$ spanned by
$$
X=\frac{\partial}{\partial x}+z\frac{\partial}{\partial y},
\qquad
Y=\frac{\partial}{\partial z}
$$
is not integrable on any nonempty open subset of $\RR^3$.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

Frobenius integrability theorem.


::: pf-proof

Let
$$
\mathcal D\subset TM
$$
be a smooth constant-rank distribution on a smooth manifold $M$.
Then $\mathcal D$ is integrable if and only if it is involutive:
for every pair of smooth vector fields
$$
U,V
$$
with values in $\mathcal D$, one has
$$
[U,V]
\in
\Gamma(\mathcal D).
$$
Equivalently, through every point there are local coordinates in which $\mathcal D$ is spanned by coordinate vector fields.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

The vector fields
$$
X
=
\partial_x+z\partial_y,
\qquad
Y
=
\partial_z
$$
are linearly independent at every point.


::: pf-proof

In coordinates,
$$
X=(1,z,0),
\qquad
Y=(0,0,1).
$$
If
$$
aX+bY=0,
$$
the $x$-coordinate gives
$$
a=0,
$$
and the $z$-coordinate gives
$$
b=0.
$$
Thus they span a smooth rank-$2$ plane field.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

Their Lie bracket is
$$
\boxed{
[X,Y]
=
-\partial_y.
}
$$


::: pf-proof

Using
$$
[fU,V]
=
f[U,V]-V(f)U,
$$
one has
$$
\begin{aligned}
[X,Y]
&=
[\partial_x+z\partial_y,\partial_z]\\
&=
[\partial_x,\partial_z]
+
[z\partial_y,\partial_z]\\
&=
0
+
z[\partial_y,\partial_z]
-
\partial_z(z)\partial_y\\
&=
-\partial_y.
\end{aligned}
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

At no point of $\RR^3$ does
$$
-\partial_y
$$
belong to
$$
\operatorname{span}\{X,Y\}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Suppose at a point there were scalars $a,b$ with
$$
a(1,z,0)+b(0,0,1)
=
(0,-1,0).
$$
The $x$-coordinate gives
$$
a=0.
$$
The $z$-coordinate gives
$$
b=0.
$$
The $y$-coordinate would then give
$$
0=-1,
$$
a contradiction.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-5}

Therefore the distribution is not involutive on any nonempty open subset.


::: pf-proof

Let
$$
U\subseteq\RR^3
$$
be nonempty and open.
The restrictions
$$
X|_U,
\qquad
Y|_U
$$
are smooth sections of the restricted plane field.
By step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref},
$$
[X|_U,Y|_U]
=
-\partial_y|_U,
$$
and step [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} shows that this bracket is nowhere a section of the plane field.
Thus involutivity fails on $U$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-6}

Hence the plane field is not integrable on any nonempty open subset of $\RR^3$.


::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref}, integrability implies involutivity.
Step [](#s1-5){.pf-ref} shows involutivity fails on every nonempty open subset.
Therefore
$$
\boxed{
\operatorname{span}\{\partial_x+z\partial_y,\partial_z\}
\text{ is nowhere integrable on any nonempty open subset}.
}
$$

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-6){.pf-ref} is the required conclusion.

:::
