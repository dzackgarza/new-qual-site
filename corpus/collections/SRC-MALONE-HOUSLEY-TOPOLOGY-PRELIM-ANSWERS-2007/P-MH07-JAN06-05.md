::: {.problem}
State the Frobenius integrability theorem. Show that the plane field on $\mathbb R^3$ spanned by
\[
X=\frac{\partial}{\partial x}+z\frac{\partial}{\partial y},
\qquad
Y=\frac{\partial}{\partial z}
\]
is not integrable on any nonempty open subset of $\mathbb R^3$.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. Frobenius integrability theorem.

::: {.proof}
Let
$$
\mathcal D\subset TM
$$
be a smooth constant-rank distribution on a smooth manifold $M$.
Then $\mathcal D$ is integrable if and only if it is involutive:
for every pair of smooth vector fields
$$
U,V
$$
with values in $\mathcal D$, one has
$$
[U,V]
\in
\Gamma(\mathcal D).
$$
Equivalently, through every point there are local coordinates in which $\mathcal D$ is spanned by coordinate vector fields.
:::

<1>2. The vector fields
$$
X
=
\partial_x+z\partial_y,
\qquad
Y
=
\partial_z
$$
are linearly independent at every point.

::: {.proof}
In coordinates,
$$
X=(1,z,0),
\qquad
Y=(0,0,1).
$$
If
$$
aX+bY=0,
$$
the $x$-coordinate gives
$$
a=0,
$$
and the $z$-coordinate gives
$$
b=0.
$$
Thus they span a smooth rank-$2$ plane field.
:::

<1>3. Their Lie bracket is
$$
\boxed{
[X,Y]
=
-\partial_y.
}
$$

::: {.proof}
Using
$$
[fU,V]
=
f[U,V]-V(f)U,
$$
one has
$$
\begin{aligned}
[X,Y]
&=
[\partial_x+z\partial_y,\partial_z]\\
&=
[\partial_x,\partial_z]
+
[z\partial_y,\partial_z]\\
&=
0
+
z[\partial_y,\partial_z]
-
\partial_z(z)\partial_y\\
&=
-\partial_y.
\end{aligned}
$$
:::

<1>4. At no point of $\mathbb R^3$ does
$$
-\partial_y
$$
belong to
$$
\operatorname{span}\{X,Y\}.
$$

::: {.proof}
Suppose at a point there were scalars $a,b$ with
$$
a(1,z,0)+b(0,0,1)
=
(0,-1,0).
$$
The $x$-coordinate gives
$$
a=0.
$$
The $z$-coordinate gives
$$
b=0.
$$
The $y$-coordinate would then give
$$
0=-1,
$$
a contradiction.
:::

<1>5. Therefore the distribution is not involutive on any nonempty open subset.

::: {.proof}
Let
$$
U\subseteq\mathbb R^3
$$
be nonempty and open.
The restrictions
$$
X|_U,
\qquad
Y|_U
$$
are smooth sections of the restricted plane field.
By step <1>3,
$$
[X|_U,Y|_U]
=
-\partial_y|_U,
$$
and step <1>4 shows that this bracket is nowhere a section of the plane field.
Thus involutivity fails on $U$.
:::

<1>6. Hence the plane field is not integrable on any nonempty open subset of $\mathbb R^3$.

::: {.proof}
By step <1>1, integrability implies involutivity.
Step <1>5 shows involutivity fails on every nonempty open subset.
Therefore
$$
\boxed{
\operatorname{span}\{\partial_x+z\partial_y,\partial_z\}
\text{ is nowhere integrable on any nonempty open subset}.
}
$$
:::

<1>7. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>6 is the required conclusion.
:::
:::
