::: {.problem}
Let
$$
f:M\to N
$$
be a map of degree $1$ between smooth, closed, connected, oriented
$n$-manifolds. Prove that
$$
f_*:\pi_1(M)\to\pi_1(N)
$$
is surjective.
:::

::: {.solution}

Fix
$$
m_0\in M,
\qquad
n_0=f(m_0),
$$
and set
$$
H
\coloneqq
f_*\pi_1(M,m_0)
\le
\pi_1(N,n_0).
$$

::: pf

::: pf-step

Let
$$
p:(\widetilde N_H,\widetilde n_0)
\to
(N,n_0)
$$
be the connected covering corresponding to
$$
H.
$$


::: pf-proof

The manifold
$$
N
$$
is connected, locally path-connected, and semilocally simply connected.
Hence the classification theorem for connected covering spaces gives a
connected covering with
$$
p_*\pi_1(\widetilde N_H,\widetilde n_0)
=
H.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

The map
$$
f
$$
lifts to a map
$$
\widetilde f:
(M,m_0)
\to
(\widetilde N_H,\widetilde n_0)
$$
such that
$$
p\circ\widetilde f=f.
$$


::: pf-proof

The covering-space lifting criterion requires
$$
f_*\pi_1(M,m_0)
\subseteq
p_*\pi_1(\widetilde N_H,\widetilde n_0).
$$
Both groups are
$$
H
$$
by definition, so the lift exists.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

The covering
$$
p
$$
has finitely many sheets.


::: pf-proof

Suppose instead that the index
$$
[\pi_1(N):H]
$$
were infinite.
Then the connected cover
$$
\widetilde N_H
$$
would be a noncompact connected orientable
$$
n
$$
-manifold.
Hence
$$
H_n(\widetilde N_H;\ZZ)=0.
$$

Therefore
$$
\widetilde f_*[M]=0.
$$
Using
$$
f=p\circ\widetilde f,
$$
we obtain
$$
f_*[M]
=
p_*\widetilde f_*[M]
=
0.
$$
But
$$
\deg f=1,
$$
so
$$
f_*[M]
=
[N]
\ne0
$$
in
$$
H_n(N;\ZZ)\cong\ZZ.
$$
This contradiction proves that the index is finite.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Let
$$
d
\coloneqq
[\pi_1(N):H].
$$
Then
$$
p
$$
is a
$$
d
$$
-sheeted covering and
$$
\deg p=\pm d.
$$


::: pf-proof

For a connected covering, the number of sheets equals the index of the
corresponding subgroup.
Since
$$
d<\infty,
$$
the total space is compact because it is a finite-sheeted cover of the
compact manifold
$$
N.
$$
It is orientable by pulling back the orientation of
$$
N
$$
through the local diffeomorphism
$$
p.
$$
A finite
$$
d
$$
-sheeted covering between closed connected oriented
$$
n
$$
-manifolds has degree
$$
\pm d.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-5}

Degree multiplicativity gives
$$
1
=
\deg f
=
(\deg p)(\deg\widetilde f)
=
(\pm d)(\deg\widetilde f).
$$


::: pf-proof

The equality
$$
f=p\circ\widetilde f
$$
from step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} implies
$$
f_*[M]
=
p_*\widetilde f_*[M]
$$
on top-dimensional homology.
Writing each pushforward in terms of degree yields the displayed
multiplicativity formula.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-6}

Hence
$$
d=1.
$$


::: pf-proof

Both
$$
d
$$
and
$$
\deg\widetilde f
$$
are integers, and
$$
d>0.
$$
Step [](#s1-5){.pf-ref} shows that
$$
d
$$
divides
$$
1,
$$
so necessarily
$$
d=1.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-7}

Therefore
$$
\boxed{
f_*:\pi_1(M)\to\pi_1(N)
\text{ is surjective}.}
$$


::: pf-proof

By definition,
$$
d=[\pi_1(N):H].
$$
Step [](#s1-6){.pf-ref} gives
$$
d=1,
$$
so
$$
H=\pi_1(N).
$$
Since
$$
H
=
f_*\pi_1(M),
$$
the induced map is surjective.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-7){.pf-ref} is the required conclusion.

:::
