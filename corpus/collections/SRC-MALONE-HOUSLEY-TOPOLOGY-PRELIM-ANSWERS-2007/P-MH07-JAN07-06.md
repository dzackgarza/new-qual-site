::: {.problem}
View $SL_2(\mathbb R)$, the set of real $2\times2$ matrices of determinant $1$, as a subset of $\mathbb R^4$ by choosing an ordering of the matrix entries.

Show that $SL_2(\mathbb R)$ is a submanifold of $\mathbb R^4$, and compute its tangent space at the identity matrix.
:::

Identify
$$
M_2(\mathbb R)
\cong
\mathbb R^4
$$
by the matrix entries, and define
$$
F:M_2(\mathbb R)\to\mathbb R,
\qquad
F(A)\coloneqq\det A.
$$

::: pf

::: pf-step

One has
$$
SL_2(\mathbb R)=F^{-1}(1).
$$


::: pf-proof

By definition,
$$
SL_2(\mathbb R)
=
\{A\in M_2(\mathbb R):\det A=1\}.
$$

:::

:::

::: pf-step

If
$$
A=
\begin{pmatrix}
a&b\\
c&d
\end{pmatrix}
$$
and
$$
H=
\begin{pmatrix}
\alpha&\beta\\
\gamma&\delta
\end{pmatrix},
$$
then
$$
\boxed{
dF_A(H)
=
d\alpha
-
c\beta
-
b\gamma
+
a\delta.
}
$$


::: pf-proof

Since
$$
F(a,b,c,d)=ad-bc,
$$
the differential is
$$
dF
=
d\,da
-
c\,db
-
b\,dc
+
a\,dd.
$$
Evaluating on
$$
H=(\alpha,\beta,\gamma,\delta)
$$
gives the formula.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

If
$$
A\in SL_2(\mathbb R),
$$
then
$$
dF_A
$$
is nonzero.


::: pf-proof

Suppose
$$
dF_A=0.
$$
By step <1>2, all coefficients of the linear functional must vanish:
$$
d=0,
\qquad
c=0,
\qquad
b=0,
\qquad
a=0.
$$
Thus
$$
A=0,
$$
contradicting
$$
\det A=1.
$$
Therefore
$$
dF_A\ne0.
$$

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Hence
$$
dF_A:M_2(\mathbb R)\to\mathbb R
$$
is surjective for every
$$
A\in SL_2(\mathbb R).
$$


::: pf-proof

A nonzero linear map to the one-dimensional vector space
$$
\mathbb R
$$
is surjective.
Apply step <1>3.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Therefore
$$
1
$$
is a regular value of $F$.


::: pf-proof

By step <1>1, the preimage of $1$ is
$$
SL_2(\mathbb R).
$$
Step <1>4 gives surjectivity of the differential at every point of this preimage.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Hence
$$
\boxed{
SL_2(\mathbb R)
\text{ is a smooth }3\text{-dimensional submanifold of }\mathbb R^4.
}
$$


::: pf-proof

By the regular value theorem,
$$
F^{-1}(1)
$$
is a smooth submanifold of codimension $1$.
Since
$$
\dim M_2(\mathbb R)=4,
$$
its dimension is
$$
4-1=3.
$$

:::

:::

::: pf-step

The tangent space at the identity is
$$
T_I SL_2(\mathbb R)
=
\ker dF_I.
$$


::: pf-proof

For a regular level set
$$
F^{-1}(1),
$$
the regular value theorem identifies the tangent space at a point $A$ with
$$
\ker dF_A.
$$
Apply this at
$$
A=I.
$$

:::

:::

::: pf-step

For
$$
H=
\begin{pmatrix}
\alpha&\beta\\
\gamma&\delta
\end{pmatrix},
$$
one has
$$
dF_I(H)
=
\alpha+\delta
=
\operatorname{tr}H.
$$


::: pf-proof

At
$$
I=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&0\\
0&1
\end{pmatrix},
$$
step <1>2 gives
$$
dF_I(H)
=
1\cdot\alpha
-
0\cdot\beta
-
0\cdot\gamma
+
1\cdot\delta
=
\alpha+\delta.
$$

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Therefore
$$
\boxed{
T_I SL_2(\mathbb R)
=
\left\{
\begin{pmatrix}
a&b\\
c&-a
\end{pmatrix}
:
a,b,c\in\mathbb R
\right\}
=
\{H\in M_2(\mathbb R):\operatorname{tr}H=0\}.
}
$$


::: pf-proof

By steps <1>7--<1>8,
$$
H\in T_I SL_2(\mathbb R)
\iff
\operatorname{tr}H=0.
$$
Writing the trace condition
$$
\alpha+\delta=0
$$
as
$$
\delta=-\alpha
$$
gives the displayed parametrization.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step <1>6 proves the submanifold statement, and step <1>9 computes the tangent space.

:::
