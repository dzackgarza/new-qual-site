::: {.problem}
Let
\[
X=\{(x,y)\in\mathbb R^3\times\mathbb R^3:x\cdot x=y\cdot y=1,\ x\cdot y=0\}.
\]
Show that $X$ is a submanifold of $\mathbb R^3\times\mathbb R^3$.
:::
