::: {.problem}
Let
\[
h:S^3\to\mathbb{CP}^1,
\qquad
h(x,y)=[x:y],
\]
where $S^3=\{(x,y)\in\mathbb C^2:|x|^2+|y|^2=1\}$. Prove that $h$ is a fiber bundle.
:::
