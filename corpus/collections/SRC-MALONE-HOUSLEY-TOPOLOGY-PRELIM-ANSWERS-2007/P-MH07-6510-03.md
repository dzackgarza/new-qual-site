::: {.problem}
Let $M,N\subset\mathbb R^3$ be one-dimensional submanifolds. Show that for every $\varepsilon>0$ there exists $v\in\mathbb R^3$ such that
\[
\|v\|<\varepsilon
\]
and
\[
(M+v)\cap N=\varnothing.
\]
:::
