::: {.problem}
Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional complex vector space, $T:V\to V$ linear, and $F(X)\in\mathbb C[X]$. Show that $F(T)$ is invertible if and only if $F$ and the minimal polynomial of $T$ have no common factor.
:::
