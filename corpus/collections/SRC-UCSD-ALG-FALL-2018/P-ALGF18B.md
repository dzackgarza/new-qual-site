::: problem
Suppose $p$ is a prime which is at most $n$, and $F$ is a field of characteristic $p$.
Suppose $g \in \mathrm{GL}_n(F)$ and $g^{p^m} = I$ for some positive integer $m$.

(a) Prove that $g - I$ is a nilpotent matrix.

(b) Prove that $g^p = I$.
:::
