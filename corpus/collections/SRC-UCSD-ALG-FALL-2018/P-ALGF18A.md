::: problem
Suppose $p < q$ are two odd primes.
Suppose $G$ is a group of order $2pq$.
Prove that $G$ has normal subgroups $N_1$ and $N_2$ such that $|N_1| = pq$, $|N_2| = q$, and $N_2 \subseteq N_1$.
:::
