::: {.problem}
Prove that
\[
|z_1+z_2|^2+|z_1-z_2|^2=2\bigl(|z_1|^2+|z_2|^2\bigr)
\]
for any two complex numbers $z_1,z_2$, and explain the geometric meaning of this identity.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. For every complex number $w$,
\[
|w|^2=w\overline w.
\]

::: {.proof}
If
\[
w=a+ib,
\]
then
\[
w\overline w
=(a+ib)(a-ib)
=a^2+b^2
=|w|^2.
\]
:::

<1>2. One has
\[
|z_1+z_2|^2
=
|z_1|^2+|z_2|^2
+
z_1\overline{z_2}
+
\overline{z_1}z_2.
\]

::: {.proof}
By step <1>1,
\[
\begin{aligned}
|z_1+z_2|^2
&=
(z_1+z_2)
(\overline{z_1}+\overline{z_2})\\
&=
z_1\overline{z_1}
+
z_2\overline{z_2}
+
z_1\overline{z_2}
+
\overline{z_1}z_2,
\end{aligned}
\]
which is the displayed identity.
:::

<1>3. One has
\[
|z_1-z_2|^2
=
|z_1|^2+|z_2|^2
-
z_1\overline{z_2}
-
\overline{z_1}z_2.
\]

::: {.proof}
Again using step <1>1,
\[
\begin{aligned}
|z_1-z_2|^2
&=
(z_1-z_2)
(\overline{z_1}-\overline{z_2})\\
&=
z_1\overline{z_1}
+
z_2\overline{z_2}
-
z_1\overline{z_2}
-
\overline{z_1}z_2.
\end{aligned}
\]
:::

<1>4. Therefore
\[
\boxed{
|z_1+z_2|^2
+
|z_1-z_2|^2
=
2\bigl(
|z_1|^2+|z_2|^2
\bigr).
}
\]

::: {.proof}
Add the formulas in steps <1>2 and <1>3. The mixed terms cancel, leaving
\[
2|z_1|^2+2|z_2|^2.
\]
:::

<1>5. Geometrically, the identity says that in a parallelogram the sum of
the squares of the lengths of the two diagonals equals twice the sum of
the squares of the lengths of two adjacent sides.

::: {.proof}
Identify $\mathbb C$ with the Euclidean plane $\mathbb R^2$. Use
\[
z_1
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
z_2
\]
as the two side vectors of a parallelogram.

The diagonal from the origin to the opposite vertex has vector
\[
z_1+z_2,
\]
while the other diagonal, joining the endpoints of the side vectors, has
vector
\[
z_1-z_2
\]
up to sign. Their lengths are therefore
\[
|z_1+z_2|
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
|z_1-z_2|.
\]
The adjacent side lengths are
\[
|z_1|
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
|z_2|.
\]
Thus step <1>4 is exactly the classical parallelogram law.
:::

<1>6. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>4 proves the algebraic identity and step <1>5 gives its geometric
meaning.
:::
