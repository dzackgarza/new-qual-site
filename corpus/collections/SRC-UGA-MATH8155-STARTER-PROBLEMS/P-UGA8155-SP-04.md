::: {.problem}
Prove that
$$
|z_1+z_2|^2+|z_1-z_2|^2=2\bigl(|z_1|^2+|z_2|^2\bigr)
$$
for any two complex numbers $z_1,z_2$, and explain the geometric meaning of this identity.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

For every complex number $w$,
$$
|w|^2=w\overline w.
$$


::: pf-proof

If
$$
w=a+ib,
$$
then
$$
w\overline w
=(a+ib)(a-ib)
=a^2+b^2
=|w|^2.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

One has
$$
|z_1+z_2|^2
=
|z_1|^2+|z_2|^2
+
z_1\overline{z_2}
+
\overline{z_1}z_2.
$$


::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref},
$$
\begin{aligned}
|z_1+z_2|^2
&=
(z_1+z_2)
(\overline{z_1}+\overline{z_2})\\
&=
z_1\overline{z_1}
+
z_2\overline{z_2}
+
z_1\overline{z_2}
+
\overline{z_1}z_2,
\end{aligned}
$$
which is the displayed identity.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

One has
$$
|z_1-z_2|^2
=
|z_1|^2+|z_2|^2
-
z_1\overline{z_2}
-
\overline{z_1}z_2.
$$


::: pf-proof

Again using step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref},
$$
\begin{aligned}
|z_1-z_2|^2
&=
(z_1-z_2)
(\overline{z_1}-\overline{z_2})\\
&=
z_1\overline{z_1}
+
z_2\overline{z_2}
-
z_1\overline{z_2}
-
\overline{z_1}z_2.
\end{aligned}
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

Therefore
$$
\boxed{
|z_1+z_2|^2
+
|z_1-z_2|^2
=
2\bigl(
|z_1|^2+|z_2|^2
\bigr).
}
$$


::: pf-proof

Add the formulas in steps [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} and [](#s1-3){.pf-ref}. The mixed terms cancel, leaving
$$
2|z_1|^2+2|z_2|^2.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-5}

Geometrically, the identity says that in a parallelogram the sum of
the squares of the lengths of the two diagonals equals twice the sum of
the squares of the lengths of two adjacent sides.


::: pf-proof

Identify $\CC$ with the Euclidean plane $\RR^2$. Use
$$
z_1
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
z_2
$$
as the two side vectors of a parallelogram.

The diagonal from the origin to the opposite vertex has vector
$$
z_1+z_2,
$$
while the other diagonal, joining the endpoints of the side vectors, has
vector
$$
z_1-z_2
$$
up to sign. Their lengths are therefore
$$
|z_1+z_2|
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
|z_1-z_2|.
$$
The adjacent side lengths are
$$
|z_1|
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
|z_2|.
$$
Thus step [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} is exactly the classical parallelogram law.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} proves the algebraic identity and step [](#s1-5){.pf-ref} gives its geometric
meaning.
