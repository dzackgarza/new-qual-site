::: {.problem}
Use the $n$-th roots of unity, i.e. the solutions of $z^n-1=0$, to show that
\[
\cos\frac{2\pi}{n}+\cos\frac{4\pi}{n}+\cos\frac{6\pi}{n}+\cdots+
\cos\frac{2(n-1)\pi}{n}=-1
\]
and
\[
\sin\frac{2\pi}{n}+\sin\frac{4\pi}{n}+\sin\frac{6\pi}{n}+\cdots+
\sin\frac{2(n-1)\pi}{n}=0.
\]

Hint: If
\[
z^n+c_1z^{n-1}+\cdots+c_{n-1}z+c_n=0
\]
has roots $z_1,\ldots,z_n$, then
\[
z_1+\cdots+z_n=-c_1,
\qquad
z_1\cdots z_n=(-1)^n c_n
\]
(the second identity is not used).
:::
