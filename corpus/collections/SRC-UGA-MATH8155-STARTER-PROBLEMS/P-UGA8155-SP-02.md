::: {.problem}
Let $z_k$ ($k=1,\ldots,n$) be complex numbers lying on the same side of a straight line passing through the origin.
Show that
\[
z_1+z_2+\cdots+z_n\ne 0,
\qquad
\frac1{z_1}+\frac1{z_2}+\cdots+\frac1{z_n}\ne 0.
\]

Hint: Consider a special situation first.
:::
