::: {.problem}
Let $z_k$ ($k=1,\ldots,n$) be complex numbers lying on the same side of a straight line passing through the origin.
Show that
$$
z_1+z_2+\cdots+z_n\ne 0,
\qquad
\frac1{z_1}+\frac1{z_2}+\cdots+\frac1{z_n}\ne 0.
$$

Hint: Consider a special situation first.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

There exists a complex number
$$
\lambda,
\qquad
\abs{\lambda}=1,
$$
such that
$$
\operatorname{Re}(\lambda z_k)>0
\qquad
(1\le k\le n).
$$


::: pf-proof

Let $L$ be the line through the origin from the hypothesis. Multiplication
by a unit complex number is a rotation of the plane. Choose $\lambda$ so
that multiplication by $\lambda$ sends $L$ to the imaginary axis and sends
the open half-plane containing all the $z_k$ to the right half-plane.

Then every
$$
w_k\coloneqq\lambda z_k
$$
satisfies
$$
\operatorname{Re}w_k>0.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

One has
$$
\sum_{k=1}^n z_k\ne0.
$$


::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref},
$$
\operatorname{Re}
\left(
\sum_{k=1}^n \lambda z_k
\right)
=
\sum_{k=1}^n
\operatorname{Re}(\lambda z_k)
>
0.
$$
Therefore
$$
\sum_{k=1}^n \lambda z_k\ne0.
$$
Since $\lambda\ne0$,
$$
\sum_{k=1}^n \lambda z_k
=
\lambda
\sum_{k=1}^n z_k,
$$
so the original sum is also nonzero.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

If
$$
\operatorname{Re}w>0,
$$
then
$$
\operatorname{Re}\frac1w>0.
$$


::: pf-proof

Write
$$
w=a+ib,
\qquad
a>0.
$$
Then
$$
\frac1w
=
\frac{a-ib}{a^2+b^2},
$$
and hence
$$
\operatorname{Re}\frac1w
=
\frac{a}{a^2+b^2}
>
0.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

One has
$$
\sum_{k=1}^n\frac1{z_k}\ne0.
$$


::: pf-proof

Let
$$
w_k=\lambda z_k
$$
as in step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref}. Then
$$
\operatorname{Re}w_k>0,
$$
so step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} gives
$$
\operatorname{Re}\frac1{w_k}>0.
$$
Therefore
$$
\operatorname{Re}
\left(
\sum_{k=1}^n\frac1{w_k}
\right)
>
0,
$$
and consequently
$$
\sum_{k=1}^n\frac1{w_k}\ne0.
$$

Since
$$
\frac1{w_k}
=
\frac1{\lambda z_k}
=
\lambda^{-1}\frac1{z_k},
$$
one has
$$
\sum_{k=1}^n\frac1{w_k}
=
\lambda^{-1}
\sum_{k=1}^n\frac1{z_k}.
$$
Because $\lambda^{-1}\ne0$, the reciprocal sum in the problem is nonzero.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Steps [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} and [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} prove the two required inequalities.
