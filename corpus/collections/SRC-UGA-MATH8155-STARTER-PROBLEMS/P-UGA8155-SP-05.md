::: {.problem}
1. Characterize the positive integers $n$ such that
\[
(1+i)^n=(1-i)^n.
\]

2. Let $n$ be a natural number.
   Show that
\[
\left[\frac12(-1+\sqrt3\,i)\right]^n
+
\left[\frac12(-1-\sqrt3\,i)\right]^n
\]
is equal to $2$ if $n$ is a multiple of $3$, and is equal to $-1$ otherwise.
:::
