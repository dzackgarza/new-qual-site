::: {.problem}
Use de Moivre's theorem, i.e.
\[
(e^{i\theta})^n=\cos(n\theta)+i\sin(n\theta),
\qquad
(\cos\theta+i\sin\theta)^n=\cos(n\theta)+i\sin(n\theta),
\]
to find the sum
\[
\sin x+\sin 2x+\cdots+\sin nx.
\]
:::
