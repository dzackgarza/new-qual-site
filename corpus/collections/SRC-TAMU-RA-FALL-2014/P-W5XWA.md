::: {.problem}
Assume that $(x_n)$ is a weakly converging sequence in a Hilbert space $H$.
Show that there is a subsequence $(y_n)$ of $(x_n)$ so that $\frac1n\sum_{j=1}^n y_j$ converges in norm.
:::
