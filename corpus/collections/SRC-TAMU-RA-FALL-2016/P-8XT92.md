::: {.problem}
Suppose that $X$ is a non separable Banach space.
Prove that there is an uncountable subset $A$ of the unit ball of $X$ such that for all $x\ne y$ in $A$, $\|x-y\|>0.9$.
:::

::: {.solution}
We construct a family
\[
(x_\alpha)_{\alpha<\omega_1}
\]
in the unit sphere of $X$ such that
\[
\|x_\alpha-x_\beta\|>0.9
\qquad(\alpha\ne\beta).
\]

<1>1. At every stage $\alpha<\omega_1$, the previously generated closed span is separable and proper.
::: {.proof}
Fix a countable ordinal $\alpha<\omega_1$ and suppose that $x_\beta$ has been chosen for every $\beta<\alpha$.
Set
\[
Y_\alpha
=
\overline{\operatorname{span}}\{x_\beta:\beta<\alpha\}.
\]
Because $\alpha$ is countable, the set
\[
\{x_\beta:\beta<\alpha\}
\]
is countable.
The set of all finite rational linear combinations of these vectors is countable and dense in $Y_\alpha$.
Hence $Y_\alpha$ is separable.

If $Y_\alpha=X$, then $X$ would be separable, contrary to hypothesis.
Therefore
\[
Y_\alpha\subsetneq X.
\]
:::

<1>2. Choose $x_\alpha$ with distance greater than $0.9$ from $Y_\alpha$.
::: {.proof}
The subspace $Y_\alpha$ is closed and proper by <1>1.
Riesz's lemma, applied with parameter $0.9$, gives a vector $x_\alpha\in X$ such that
\[
\|x_\alpha\|=1
\]
and
\[
\operatorname{dist}(x_\alpha,Y_\alpha)>0.9.
\]
This completes the transfinite induction through all $\alpha<\omega_1$.
:::

<1>3. Distinct members of the family are more than $0.9$ apart.
::: {.proof}
Let $\beta<\alpha<\omega_1$.
Then
\[
x_\beta\in Y_\alpha.
\]
Therefore
\[
\|x_\alpha-x_\beta\|
\ge
\operatorname{dist}(x_\alpha,Y_\alpha)
>0.9.
\]
:::

<1>4. The required set is uncountable and lies in the unit ball.
::: {.proof}
Let
\[
A=\{x_\alpha:\alpha<\omega_1\}.
\]
Every $x_\alpha$ has norm $1$, so $A$ lies in the closed unit ball of $X$.
By <1>3, distinct indices give distinct vectors; hence $A$ has cardinality $\omega_1$ and is uncountable.
The same step gives
\[
\|x-y\|>0.9
\qquad(x\ne y,\ x,y\in A).
\]
:::
:::
