::: problem
Let $A$ and $B$ be circles bounding disjoint disks in the plane $z = 0$ in $\RR^3$.
Let $X$ be the subset of the upper half-space of $\RR^3$ that is the union of the plane $z = 0$ and a (topological) cylinder that intersects the plane in $\partial C = A \cup B$.

Compute $H_* (X)$ using the Mayer--Vietoris sequence.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. Use a Mayer--Vietoris decomposition with homotopy data
\[
U\simeq\RR^2,
\qquad
V\simeq C\simeq S^1,
\qquad
U\cap V\simeq A\disjoint B\simeq S^1\disjoint S^1.
\]
::: {.proof}
The plane and the cylinder are closed pieces meeting along the two boundary circles, so replace them by small open collar neighborhoods inside $X$.
The plane-side neighborhood deformation retracts onto the plane, the cylinder-side neighborhood deformation retracts onto $C$, and their intersection is the disjoint union of two annular collars retracting onto $A\disjoint B$.
These replacements do not change the homology groups or the inclusion maps used below, and they satisfy the hypotheses of Mayer--Vietoris.
:::

<1>2. The relevant homology groups are
\[
H_k(U)\cong
\begin{cases}
\ZZ,&k=0,\\
0,&k>0,
\end{cases}
\qquad
H_k(V)\cong
\begin{cases}
\ZZ,&k=0,1,\\
0,&k>1,
\end{cases}
\]
and
\[
H_k(U\cap V)\cong
\begin{cases}
\ZZ^2,&k=0,1,\\
0,&k>1.
\end{cases}
\]
::: {.proof}
The plane is contractible.
The cylinder $S^1\times I$ deformation retracts onto a core circle.
The intersection has two components, each homotopy equivalent to $S^1$.
:::

<1>3. With suitable generators $a,b$ for the two summands of $H_1(U\cap V)$ and a generator $c$ of $H_1(V)$, the Mayer--Vietoris map
\[
\Phi_1:H_1(U\cap V)\longrightarrow H_1(U)\oplus H_1(V)
\]
is
\[
\Phi_1(m a+n b)=\bigl(0,-(m+n)c\bigr).
\]
Hence
\[
\ker\Phi_1\cong\ZZ,
\qquad
\operatorname{coker}\Phi_1=0.
\]
::: {.proof}
Each of $A$ and $B$ bounds a disk in the plane, so both inclusion maps into $U\simeq\RR^2$ induce the zero map on $H_1$.

Choose the orientations of $A$ and $B$ so that, under the two boundary inclusions into the cylinder, both represent the same oriented core generator $c$.
Thus the inclusion into $V$ sends
\[
a\longmapsto c,
\qquad
b\longmapsto c.
\]
With the Mayer--Vietoris convention $(i_*,-j_*)$, this gives the displayed formula.
The map $(m,n)\mapsto m+n$ is surjective and has kernel generated by $(1,-1)$.
:::

<1>4. One has
\[
H_2(X;\ZZ)\cong\ZZ.
\]
::: {.proof}
The degree-$2$ part of the Mayer--Vietoris sequence is
\[
0
\longrightarrow0
\longrightarrow H_2(X)
\longrightarrow\ZZ^2
\xrightarrow{\Phi_1}\ZZ.
\]
Exactness therefore identifies
\[
H_2(X)\cong\ker\Phi_1\cong\ZZ
\]
by <1>3.
:::

<1>5. The degree-zero Mayer--Vietoris map
\[
\Phi_0:H_0(U\cap V)\cong\ZZ^2
\longrightarrow
H_0(U)\oplus H_0(V)\cong\ZZ^2
\]
has the form
\[
\Phi_0(r,s)=(r+s,-r-s).
\]
Consequently
\[
\ker\Phi_0\cong\ZZ,
\qquad
\operatorname{coker}\Phi_0\cong\ZZ.
\]
::: {.proof}
Both components $A$ and $B$ lie in the single connected component of $U$ and also in the single connected component of $V$.
Thus each component generator maps to $(1,-1)$ under $(i_*,-j_*)$.
The image is therefore generated by $(1,-1)$, while the kernel is generated by $(1,-1)$ in the source coordinates.
The quotient
\[
\ZZ^2/\langle(1,-1)\rangle
\]
is infinite cyclic.
:::

<1>6. One has
\[
H_1(X;\ZZ)\cong\ZZ,
\qquad
H_0(X;\ZZ)\cong\ZZ.
\]
::: {.proof}
The relevant exact segment is
\[
\ZZ^2
\xrightarrow{\Phi_1}\ZZ
\longrightarrow H_1(X)
\longrightarrow\ZZ^2
\xrightarrow{\Phi_0}\ZZ^2
\longrightarrow H_0(X)
\longrightarrow0.
\]
By <1>3, $\Phi_1$ is surjective, so exactness gives
\[
H_1(X)\cong\ker\Phi_0\cong\ZZ.
\]
By <1>5,
\[
H_0(X)\cong\operatorname{coker}\Phi_0\cong\ZZ.
\]
:::

<1>7. Therefore
\[
\boxed{
H_k(X;\ZZ)\cong
\begin{cases}
\ZZ,&k=0,1,2,\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}}
\]
::: {.proof}
The groups in degrees $0,1,2$ are given by <1>4 and <1>6. For $k\ge3$, all neighboring Mayer--Vietoris groups from $U$, $V$, and $U\cap V$ vanish by <1>2, so $H_k(X)=0$.
:::
