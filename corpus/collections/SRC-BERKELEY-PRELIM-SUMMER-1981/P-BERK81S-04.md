::: {.problem}
Let $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ be continuous and suppose
\[
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}|f(x)|\,dx<\infty.
\]
Show that there is a sequence $x_n\to\infty$ such that
\[
x_nf(x_n)\to0
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
x_nf(-x_n)\to0.
\]
:::
