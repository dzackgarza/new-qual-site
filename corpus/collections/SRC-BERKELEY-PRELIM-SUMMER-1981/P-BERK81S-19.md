::: {.problem}
Let $n$ be a positive integer and let $\alpha,\beta\in\mathbb R\setminus\{0\}$.
Prove that the number of roots, counted with multiplicity, of
\[
z^{2n}+\alpha^2z^{2n-1}+\beta^2=0
\]
having positive real part is

1. $n$ if $n$ is even;

2. $n-1$ if $n$ is odd.
:::
