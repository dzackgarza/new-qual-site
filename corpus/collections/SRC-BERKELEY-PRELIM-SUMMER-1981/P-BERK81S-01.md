::: {.problem}
Let
\[
y(h)=1-2\sin^2(2\pi h),
\qquad
f(y)=\frac{2}{1+\sqrt{1-y}}.
\]
Justify the asymptotic formula
\[
f(y(h))=2-4\sqrt2\,\pi |h|+O(h^2)
\qquad(h\to0),
\]
with a remainder bounded in absolute value by a constant multiple of $h^2$ near $0$.
:::
