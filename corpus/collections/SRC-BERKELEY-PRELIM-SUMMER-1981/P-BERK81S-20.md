::: {.problem}
Let $y:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ solve
\[
y''=-|y|,
\qquad
y(0)=1,
\qquad
y'(0)=0.
\]

1. Show that $y$ is even.

2. Show that $y$ has exactly one zero on the positive real axis.
:::
