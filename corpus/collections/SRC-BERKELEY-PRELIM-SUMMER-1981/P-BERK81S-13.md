::: {.problem}
Let $G$ be an additive group and let $u,v:G\to G$ be homomorphisms.
Define
\[
f(x)=x-v(u(x)),
\qquad
h(x)=x-u(v(x)).
\]
Show that if $h$ is surjective, then $f$ is surjective.
:::
