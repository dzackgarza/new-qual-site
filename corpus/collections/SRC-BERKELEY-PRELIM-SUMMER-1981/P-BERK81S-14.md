::: {.problem}
Let $f:(0,1)\to\mathbb C$ be $C^1$.
Suppose
\[
f(t)\to0,
\qquad
f'(t)\to C\ne0
\qquad(t\to0^+).
\]
Show that $g(t)=|f(t)|$ is $C^1$ for all sufficiently small $t>0$, and that
\[
\lim_{t\to0^+}g'(t)
\]
exists.
Evaluate this limit.
:::
