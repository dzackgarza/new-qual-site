::: {.problem}
Let $S$ be the vector space of real $n\times n$ skew-symmetric matrices.
For $A\in GL_n(\mathbb R)$, compute the determinant of the linear map
\[
T_A:S\to S,
\qquad
T_A(X)=AXA^T.
\]
:::
