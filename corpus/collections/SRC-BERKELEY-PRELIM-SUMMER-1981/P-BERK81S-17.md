::: {.problem}
Suppose $f$ and $g$ are entire functions such that
\[
|f(z)|\le |g(z)|
\]
for every $z\in\mathbb C$.
Show that
\[
f(z)=c\,g(z)
\]
for some constant $c\in\mathbb C$.
:::
