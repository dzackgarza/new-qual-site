::: {.problem}
(a) Let $t\in\mathbb R$ be not an integer multiple of $\pi$.
For
\[
A=
\begin{pmatrix}
\cos t&\sin t\\
-\sin t&\cos t
\end{pmatrix},
\]
prove that there is no real matrix $B$ for which $BAB^{-1}$ is diagonal.

(b) Prove the same assertion for
\[
A=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&\lambda\\
0&1
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\lambda\in\mathbb R\setminus\{0\}.
\]
:::
