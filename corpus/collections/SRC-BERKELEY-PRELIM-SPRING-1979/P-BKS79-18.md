::: {.problem}
Let $f:[0,1]\to\RR$ be continuous and let $\varepsilon>0$. Show that there exist $n\in\ZZ_{\ge0}$ and rational numbers $C_0,\dots,C_n$ such that
\[
g(x)=\sum_{k=0}^n C_kx^{4k}
\]
satisfies
\[
|g(x)-f(x)|<\varepsilon
\]
for every $x\in[0,1]$.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. The function
$$
h:[0,1]\longrightarrow\RR,
\qquad
h(t)\coloneqq f(t^{1/4}),
$$
is continuous.

::: {.proof}
The map $t\mapsto t^{1/4}$ is continuous from $[0,1]$ to $[0,1]$, and
$f$ is continuous by hypothesis. Their composition is therefore
continuous.
:::

<1>2. There is a real polynomial
$$
p(t)=\sum_{k=0}^n a_kt^k
$$
such that
$$
|p(t)-h(t)|<\frac{\varepsilon}{2}
$$
for every $t\in[0,1]$.

::: {.proof}
Apply the Weierstrass approximation theorem to the continuous function
$h$ from step <1>1, with tolerance $\varepsilon/2$.
:::

<1>3. There are rational numbers
$$
C_0,\ldots,C_n\in\QQ
$$
such that the polynomial
$$
q(t)\coloneqq\sum_{k=0}^n C_kt^k
$$
satisfies
$$
|q(t)-p(t)|<\frac{\varepsilon}{2}
$$
for every $t\in[0,1]$.

::: {.proof}
Since $\QQ$ is dense in $\RR$, for each $k$ choose $C_k\in\QQ$ with
$$
|C_k-a_k|<\frac{\varepsilon}{2(n+1)}.
$$
For $0\le t\le1$,
$$
\begin{aligned}
|q(t)-p(t)|
&\le
\sum_{k=0}^n |C_k-a_k|t^k\\
&\le
\sum_{k=0}^n |C_k-a_k|\\
&<
(n+1)\frac{\varepsilon}{2(n+1)}
=
\frac{\varepsilon}{2}.
\end{aligned}
$$
:::

<1>4. Define
$$
g(x)\coloneqq q(x^4)
=
\sum_{k=0}^n C_kx^{4k}.
$$
Then
$$
|g(x)-f(x)|<\varepsilon
$$
for every $x\in[0,1]$.

::: {.proof}
Fix $x\in[0,1]$ and put $t=x^4$. Then $t\in[0,1]$ and
$$
h(t)=f(t^{1/4})=f(x).
$$
By steps <1>2 and <1>3,
$$
\begin{aligned}
|g(x)-f(x)|
&=
|q(t)-h(t)|\\
&\le
|q(t)-p(t)|+|p(t)-h(t)|\\
&<
\frac{\varepsilon}{2}+\frac{\varepsilon}{2}
=
\varepsilon.
\end{aligned}
$$
:::

<1>5. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>4 gives the required polynomial with rational coefficients and
only powers $x^{4k}$.
:::
:::
