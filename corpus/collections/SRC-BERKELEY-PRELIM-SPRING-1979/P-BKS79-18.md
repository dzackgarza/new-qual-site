::: {.problem}
Let $f:[0,1]\to\mathbb R$ be continuous and let $\varepsilon>0$. Show that there exist $n\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}$ and rational numbers $C_0,\dots,C_n$ such that
\[
g(x)=\sum_{k=0}^n C_kx^{4k}
\]
satisfies
\[
|g(x)-f(x)|<\varepsilon
\]
for every $x\in[0,1]$.
:::
