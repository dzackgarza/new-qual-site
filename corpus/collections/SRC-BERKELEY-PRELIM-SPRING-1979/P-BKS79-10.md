::: {.problem}
Let $f(x)\in\mathbb F_p[x]$ and let
\[
g(x)=x^p-x.
\]
Show that $\gcd(f,g)$ is the product of the distinct linear factors of $f$ in $\mathbb F_p[x]$.
:::
