::: {.problem}
Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional complex vector space with Hermitian inner product $\langle x,y\rangle$.

1. Prove that $E$ has an orthonormal basis.
2. Let $f:E\times E\to\mathbb C$ be linear in the first variable and conjugate-linear in the second. Show that there is a linear map $A:E\to E$ such that
\[
f(x,y)=\langle Ax,y\rangle
\]
for all $x,y\in E$.
:::
