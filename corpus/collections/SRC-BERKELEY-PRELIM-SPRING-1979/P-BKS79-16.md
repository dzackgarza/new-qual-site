::: {.problem}
For which $z\in\CC$ does the series
\[
\sum_{n=0}^\infty\left(\frac{z^n}{n!}+\frac{n^2}{z^n}\right)
\]
converge?
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

The series
$$
\sum_{n=0}^\infty\frac{z^n}{n!}
$$
converges absolutely for every $z\in\CC$.

::: pf-proof

For fixed $z$, the ratio of successive absolute values is
$$
\frac{|z|^{n+1}/(n+1)!}{|z|^n/n!}
=
\frac{|z|}{n+1}
\longrightarrow0.
$$
The ratio test therefore gives absolute convergence.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

If $|z|>1$, then
$$
\sum_{n=1}^\infty\frac{n^2}{z^n}
$$
converges absolutely.

::: pf-proof

For
$$
b_n=\frac{n^2}{z^n},
$$
one has
$$
\frac{|b_{n+1}|}{|b_n|}
=
\left(\frac{n+1}{n}\right)^2\frac1{|z|}
\longrightarrow
\frac1{|z|}<1.
$$
The ratio test gives absolute convergence.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

If $|z|>1$, the series in the problem converges absolutely.

::: pf-proof

It is the termwise sum of the two absolutely convergent series from steps
[](#s1){.pf-ref} and [](#s2){.pf-ref}; the $n=0$ term of the second series is $0$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s4}

If $0<|z|\le1$, the summand in the problem does not tend to $0$.

::: pf-proof

For $n\ge1$,
$$
\left|\frac{n^2}{z^n}\right|
=
\frac{n^2}{|z|^n}
\ge
n^2,
$$
whereas
$$
\frac{z^n}{n!}\longrightarrow0
$$
by step [](#s1){.pf-ref}. Hence
$$
\left|
\frac{z^n}{n!}+\frac{n^2}{z^n}
\right|
\ge
\frac{n^2}{|z|^n}-\frac{|z|^n}{n!}
\longrightarrow\infty.
$$
In particular, the summand does not tend to zero, so the series diverges.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s5}

The series is not defined at $z=0$.

::: pf-proof

For every $n\ge1$, the term $n^2/z^n$ has zero denominator when $z=0$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s6}

Therefore the convergence set is
$$
\boxed{\{z\in\CC:|z|>1\}.}
$$

::: pf-proof

Step [](#s3){.pf-ref} proves convergence throughout this set, while steps [](#s4){.pf-ref} and [](#s5){.pf-ref}
exclude every $z$ outside it.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s6){.pf-ref} is the requested classification.

:::
