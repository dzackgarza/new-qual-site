::: {.problem}
Evaluate
\[
\int_0^\infty\frac{x^2+1}{x^4+1}\,dx.
\]
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

The integrand decomposes as
$$
\frac{x^2+1}{x^4+1}
=
\frac12
\left(
\frac{1}{x^2+\sqrt2x+1}
+
\frac{1}{x^2-\sqrt2x+1}
\right).
$$

::: pf-proof

Factor
$$
x^4+1
=
(x^2+\sqrt2x+1)(x^2-\sqrt2x+1).
$$
Adding the two reciprocal quadratic factors gives
$$
\frac{1}{x^2+\sqrt2x+1}
+
\frac{1}{x^2-\sqrt2x+1}
=
\frac{2(x^2+1)}{x^4+1},
$$
which is the claimed identity.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

An antiderivative of the integrand is
$$
F(x)
=
\frac{1}{\sqrt2}
\left(
\arctan(\sqrt2x+1)
+
\arctan(\sqrt2x-1)
\right).
$$

::: pf-proof

Completing the square gives
$$
x^2\pm\sqrt2x+1
=
\left(x\pm\frac1{\sqrt2}\right)^2+\frac12.
$$
Therefore step [](#s1){.pf-ref} yields
$$
\begin{aligned}
\int\frac{x^2+1}{x^4+1}\,dx
&=
\frac12\int
\frac{dx}{(x+1/\sqrt2)^2+1/2}
+
\frac12\int
\frac{dx}{(x-1/\sqrt2)^2+1/2}\\
&=
\frac{1}{\sqrt2}
\left(
\arctan(\sqrt2x+1)
+
\arctan(\sqrt2x-1)
\right)+C.
\end{aligned}
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

The value of the improper integral is
$$
\boxed{\frac{\pi}{\sqrt2}}.
$$

::: pf-proof

By step [](#s2){.pf-ref},
$$
\begin{aligned}
\int_0^\infty\frac{x^2+1}{x^4+1}\,dx
&=
\lim_{R\to\infty}\bigl(F(R)-F(0)\bigr)\\
&=
\frac1{\sqrt2}
\left(
\frac\pi2+\frac\pi2
-
\arctan(1)
-
\arctan(-1)
\right)\\
&=
\frac\pi{\sqrt2},
\end{aligned}
$$
because $\arctan(1)=\pi/4$ and $\arctan(-1)=-\pi/4$.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s3){.pf-ref} gives the requested value.

:::
