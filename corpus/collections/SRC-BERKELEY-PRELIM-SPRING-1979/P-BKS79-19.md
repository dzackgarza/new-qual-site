::: {.problem}
Let $P$ be an $n\times n$ real matrix such that
\[
x^TPy=-y^TPx
\]
for all column vectors $x,y\in\RR^n$. Prove that $P$ is skew-symmetric.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let
$$
P=(p_{ij})_{1\le i,j\le n}
$$
and let $e_1,\ldots,e_n$ be the standard basis of $\RR^n$.

<1>1. For every $i,j$,
$$
p_{ij}=-p_{ji}.
$$

::: {.proof}
Apply the given identity with
$$
x=e_i,
\qquad
y=e_j.
$$
Then
$$
e_i^TPe_j=-e_j^TPe_i.
$$
The left side is $p_{ij}$ and the right side is $-p_{ji}$, giving the
claim.
:::

<1>2. The matrix $P$ is skew-symmetric:
$$
\boxed{P^T=-P.}
$$

::: {.proof}
The $(i,j)$-entry of $P^T$ is $p_{ji}$. By step <1>1,
$$
p_{ji}=-p_{ij}
$$
for every $i,j$, so $P^T=-P$.
:::

<1>3. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>2 is precisely the required conclusion.
:::
:::
