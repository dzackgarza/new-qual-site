::: {.problem}
Prove that every compact metric space has a countable dense subset.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $(X,d)$ be the given compact metric space, and write
$$
B(x,r)\coloneqq\{y\in X:d(x,y)<r\}.
$$

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

For every integer $n\ge1$, there is a finite subset
$$
F_n\subseteq X
$$
such that
$$
X=\bigcup_{a\in F_n}B\left(a,\frac1n\right).
$$

::: pf-proof

For fixed $n\ge1$, the family
$$
\left\{B\left(x,\frac1n\right):x\in X\right\}
$$
is an open cover of $X$. Compactness gives a finite subcover. Taking
$F_n$ to be the finite set of centers of the balls in that subcover gives
the asserted equality.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

The set
$$
D\coloneqq\bigcup_{n=1}^{\infty}F_n
$$
is countable.

::: pf-proof

Each $F_n$ is finite by step [](#s1){.pf-ref}, and a countable union of finite sets is
countable. Hence $D$ is countable.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

The set $D$ is dense in $X$.

::: pf-proof

Fix $x\in X$ and $\varepsilon>0$. Choose an integer $n\ge1$ such that
$$
\frac1n<\varepsilon.
$$
By step [](#s1){.pf-ref}, some $a\in F_n$ satisfies
$$
x\in B\left(a,\frac1n\right).
$$
Therefore
$$
d(x,a)<\frac1n<\varepsilon.
$$
Since $a\in F_n\subseteq D$, the ball $B(x,\varepsilon)$ meets $D$.
Thus every open ball about every point of $X$ meets $D$, so
$$
\overline D=X.
$$
Hence $D$ is dense in $X$.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s2){.pf-ref} gives a countable subset $D\subseteq X$, and step [](#s3){.pf-ref} proves
that $D$ is dense in $X$.

:::
