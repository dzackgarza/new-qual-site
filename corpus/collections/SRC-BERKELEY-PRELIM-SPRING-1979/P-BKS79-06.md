::: {.problem}
Let $1<\lambda<\infty$ and
\[
f_\lambda(z)=z+\lambda-e^z.
\]
Prove that $f_\lambda$ has exactly one zero in the half-plane $\operatorname{Re}z<0$, and that this zero is real.
:::
