::: {.problem}
Let $P,Q\in\mathbb C[z]$ with
\[
\deg Q\ge\deg P+2.
\]
Prove that there exists $r>0$ such that if $C$ is a closed curve lying outside the circle $|z|=r$, then
\[
\int_C\frac{P(z)}{Q(z)}\,dz=0.
\]
:::
