::: {.problem}
Let $M_{n\times n}$ be the real vector space of $n\times n$ real matrices, with $n\ge2$, and let
\[
\det:M_{n\times n}\to\mathbb R
\]
be the determinant map.

1. Show that $\det$ is $C^\infty$.
2. Show that the derivative of $\det$ at $A$ is the zero linear map if and only if $\operatorname{rank}A\le n-2$.
:::
