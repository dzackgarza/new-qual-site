::: {.problem}
Consider the system
\[
x'=y+tz,
\qquad
y'=z+t^2x,
\qquad
z'=x+e^t y.
\]
Prove that there exists a solution on $[0,1]$ satisfying the source's displayed initial condition
\[
\begin{pmatrix}
1&2&3\\
4&5&6\\
7&8&9
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}
x(0)\\y(0)\\z(0)
\end{pmatrix}
=\text{[right-hand side unrecovered in the retained extraction]},
\]
and also
\[
\int_0^1\bigl(x(t)^2+y(t)^2+z(t)^2\bigr)\,dt=1.
\]
:::
