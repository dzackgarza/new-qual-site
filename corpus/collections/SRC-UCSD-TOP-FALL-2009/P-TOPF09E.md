::: problem
Show that any homotopy equivalence from $\mathbb{CP}^{2n}$ to itself is orientation-preserving, that is has degree $+1$.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. $H^*(\mathbb{CP}^{2n}; \ZZ) = \ZZ[x]/(x^{2n+1})$ with $|x| = 2$, so $H^{2n}(\mathbb{CP}^{2n}) = \ZZ$ generated by $x^{2n}$.
Proof: cohomology of complex projective space.

<1>2. Let $f : \mathbb{CP}^{2n} \to \mathbb{CP}^{2n}$ be a homotopy equivalence; then $f^*(x) = \pm x$ (since $f^*$ is a ring isomorphism, it sends the generator $x$ to $\pm x$).
Proof: $f^*$ is an isomorphism on $H^2 = \ZZ$, so $f^*(x) = \pm x$.

<1>3. The degree of $f$ is the integer $d$ with $f^*(x^{2n}) = d x^{2n}$.
Proof: definition of degree (the induced map on top cohomology).

<1>4. $f^*(x^{2n}) = f^*(x)^{2n} = (\pm x)^{2n} = x^{2n}$ (since $2n$ is even, $(\pm 1)^{2n} = 1$).
Proof: <1>2 and the ring homomorphism property.

<1>5. Hence $d = 1$, so $f$ has degree $+1$ and is orientation-preserving.
Proof: <1>3 and <1>4.

<1>6. Q.E.D.
Proof: <1>5.
:::
