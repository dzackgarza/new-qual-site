::: {.problem}
Let $f$ and $g$ be analytic on a domain $D$ whose closure is compact in $\mathbb C$.
Show that
\[
|f(z)|+|g(z)|
\]
takes its maximum on the boundary $\partial D$.

Hint: consider $f(z)e^{i\alpha}+g(z)e^{i\beta}$ for suitable $\alpha,\beta$.
:::
