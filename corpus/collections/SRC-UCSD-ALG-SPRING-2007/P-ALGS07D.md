::: problem
Let $\mathbb{C}[x]/\langle x^n \rangle$ denote the evident $\mathbb{C}[x]$-module, and let $m, n \in \mathbb{N}$.

(a) Show that there exist $a_1, \ldots, a_k$ such that $$\mathbb{C}[x]/\langle x^n \rangle \otimes_{\mathbb{C}[x]} \mathbb{C}[x]/\langle x^m \rangle \cong \bigoplus_{i=1}^{k} \mathbb{C}[x]/(x^{a_i}).$$

Hint: Figure out the action of $x$ on the obvious $\mathbb{C}$-basis.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. General isomorphism for tensor product of cyclic modules:
<2>1. Let $R$ be any commutative ring with identity, and let $I, J \subseteq R$ be ideals.
Consider the $R$-module tensor product $R/I \otimes_R R/J$.
<2>2. We have a canonical $R$-module isomorphism:
\[
R/I \otimes_R R/J \cong R / (I + J).
\]

<1>2. Application to $R = \mathbb{C}[x]$:
<2>1. Let $R = \mathbb{C}[x]$, $I = \langle x^n \rangle$, and $J = \langle x^m \rangle$.
The sum of principal ideals in the PID $\mathbb{C}[x]$ is generated by the greatest common divisor of their generators:
\[
I + J = \langle x^n \rangle + \langle x^m \rangle = \langle \gcd(x^n, x^m) \rangle = \langle x^{\min(n, m)} \rangle.
\]
<2>2. Applying <1>1:
\[
\mathbb{C}[x]/\langle x^n \rangle \otimes_{\mathbb{C}[x]} \mathbb{C}[x]/\langle x^m \rangle \cong \mathbb{C}[x] / \langle x^{\min(n, m)} \rangle.
\]

<1>3. Conclusion:
The decomposition holds with $k = 1$ and $a_1 = \min(n, m)$:
\[
\mathbb{C}[x]/\langle x^n \rangle \otimes_{\mathbb{C}[x]} \mathbb{C}[x]/\langle x^m \rangle \cong \mathbb{C}[x] / \langle x^{\min(n, m)} \rangle.
\]
Q.E.D.
:::
