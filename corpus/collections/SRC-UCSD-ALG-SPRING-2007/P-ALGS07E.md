::: problem
Recall that a "perfect" field of characteristic $p$ is one for which the Frobenius map $\operatorname{Fr}: x \mapsto x^p$ is onto.

Let $F$ be a perfect field, and $E/F$ an algebraic extension.
Show that $E$ is perfect.
:::
