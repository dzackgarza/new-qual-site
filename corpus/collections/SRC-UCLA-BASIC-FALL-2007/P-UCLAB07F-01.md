::: {.problem}
Let $S\subseteq\mathbb R^n$, equipped with the Euclidean distance restricted to $S$.
Fix $y\in S$ and $r\in\mathbb R$.

(a) Is $E=\{x\in S:d(x,y)\ge r\}$ closed in $S$?

(b) Is $E$ contained in the closure in $S$ of $\{x\in S:d(x,y)>r\}$?

Prove your answers.
:::
