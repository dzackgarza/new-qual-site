::: {.problem}
Suppose $a_n\ge0$ and $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n=\infty$. Does it follow that
\[\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{a_n}{1+a_n}=\infty?\]
Prove your answer.
:::
