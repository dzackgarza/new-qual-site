::: {.problem}
Let $A(x)$ be a differentiable matrix-valued function on $\mathbb R$, with $A(x)$ invertible for every $x$.
Starting from entrywise differentiation, show that $A^{-1}(x)$ is differentiable and \[(A^{-1})'(x)=-A^{-1}(x)A'(x)A^{-1}(x).\]
:::
