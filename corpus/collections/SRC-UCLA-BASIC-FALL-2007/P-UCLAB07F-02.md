::: {.problem}
Let $f:(a,b)\to\mathbb R$ be continuous and differentiable on $(a,b)\setminus\{c\}$. If $\lim_{x\to c}f'(x)=d\in\mathbb R$, show that $f$ is differentiable at $c$ and $f'(c)=d$.
:::
