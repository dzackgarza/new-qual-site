::: {.problem}
Prove that a topological space $X$ is regular if and only if $X$ is $T_1$ and, for every $x\in X$ and every open set $U$ containing $x$, there is an open set $V$ containing $x$ such that
\[
\overline V\subseteq U.
\]
:::
