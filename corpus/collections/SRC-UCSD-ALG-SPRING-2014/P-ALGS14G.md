::: problem
Let $L/K$ be a Galois extension with Galois group $S_3$.
Prove or disprove that $L$ is the splitting field of an irreducible cubic polynomial with coefficients in $K$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Yes.
In fact every Galois extension with group $S_3$ arises this way.

<1>1. Choose a subgroup $H\leq G:=\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$ of order $2$, and put $E=L^H$.
Then
\[
[E:K]=[G:H]=3.
\]
::: {.proof}
This is the degree formula in the Galois correspondence.
:::

<1>2. The extension $E/K$ is finite separable, so there is an element $\alpha\in E$ with $E=K(\alpha)$.
Its minimal polynomial $f\in K[x]$ is irreducible of degree $3$.
::: {.proof}
Every finite subextension of the finite Galois extension $L/K$ is separable, and finite separable extensions are simple.
Since $[E:K]=3$, the minimal polynomial of a primitive element has degree $3$.
:::

<1>3. The normal closure of $E/K$ inside $L$ is $L^{\operatorname{core}_G(H)}$, where
\[
\operatorname{core}_G(H)=\bigcap_{g\in G}gHg^{-1}.
\]
::: {.proof}
The $K$-conjugates of $E=L^H$ inside $L$ are the fields $L^{gHg^{-1}}$.
Their compositum is fixed by the intersection of the conjugate subgroups, hence is $L^{\operatorname{core}_G(H)}$.
:::

<1>4. For a subgroup $H$ of order $2$ in $S_3$, $\operatorname{core}_{S_3}(H)=1$.
::: {.proof}
The three order-$2$ subgroups of $S_3$ are conjugate and their intersection is trivial.
Equivalently, $S_3$ has no nontrivial normal subgroup of order dividing $2$.
:::

<1>5. Therefore the normal closure of $E/K$ is $L$.
Since $E=K(\alpha)$ and $f$ is the minimal polynomial of $\alpha$, this normal closure is exactly the splitting field of $f$.
Hence $L$ is the splitting field of an irreducible cubic polynomial over $K$.
::: {.proof}
By <1>3 and <1>4, the normal closure is $L^1=L$.
The normal closure of a simple separable extension generated by a root of an irreducible polynomial is its splitting field.
:::
:::
