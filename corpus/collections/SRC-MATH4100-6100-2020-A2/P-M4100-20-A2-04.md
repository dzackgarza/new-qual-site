::: {.problem}
(a) Let $(x_n)$ be bounded. Prove that if
\[
\lim_{n\to\infty}|x_n|=0,
\]
then $x_n\to0$.

(b) Prove that every bounded divergent sequence has at least two subsequences converging to different limits.

(c) Find $\liminf a_n$ and $\limsup a_n$ for
\[
a_n=\lfloor\sin n\rfloor.
\]

(d) Find all subsequential limits of each sequence
\[
x_n=4+5(-1)^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor},
\quad
x_n=\cos(n\pi/3),
\]
and
\[
x_n=(-1)^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}+2(-1)^{\lfloor n/3\rfloor}.
\]
:::
