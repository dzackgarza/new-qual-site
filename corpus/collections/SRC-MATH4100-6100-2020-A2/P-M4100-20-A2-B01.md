::: {.problem}
Let $(a_n)$ be a bounded sequence of positive real numbers.

(a) Prove
\[
\liminf_{n\to\infty}\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n}
\le
\liminf_{n\to\infty}\sqrt[n]{a_n}
\le
\limsup_{n\to\infty}\sqrt[n]{a_n}
\le
\limsup_{n\to\infty}\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n}.
\]

(b) Construct a sequence for which all three inequalities are strict.

(c) Use part (a) to prove
\[
\lim_{n\to\infty}\sqrt[n]{n}=1.
\]
:::
