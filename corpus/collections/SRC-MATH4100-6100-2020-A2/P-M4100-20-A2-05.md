(a) Explain why there is no real sequence whose set of subsequential limits is exactly
\[
\left\{\frac1n:n\in\mathbb N\right\}.
\]

(b) Give an example of a sequence whose set of subsequential limits is
\[
\left\{\frac1n:n\in\mathbb N\right\}\cup\{0\}.
\]
