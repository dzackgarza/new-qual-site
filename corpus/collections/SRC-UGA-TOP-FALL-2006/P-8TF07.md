::: problem
a. State van Kampen's theorem.

b. What are the fundamental groups of the torus $T^2$ and the real projective plane $\RP^2$?
(Do not prove your answers.)

c. Use van Kampen's theorem and part (b) to compute the fundamental group of the one-point union $X=T^2\vee\RP^2$.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. A based form of van Kampen's theorem is the following.
::: {.proof}
Let
\[
X=U\cup V,
\]
where $U,V$, and $U\cap V$ are open and path connected, and choose
\[
x_0\in U\cap V.
\]
Let
\[
i_U:U\cap V\hookrightarrow U,
\qquad
i_V:U\cap V\hookrightarrow V
\]
be the inclusions.
Then the homomorphisms induced by the inclusions of $U$ and $V$ into $X$ give a surjection
\[
\pi_1(U,x_0)*\pi_1(V,x_0)
\longrightarrow
\pi_1(X,x_0),
\]
whose kernel is the normal subgroup generated by
\[
(i_U)_*(\gamma)(i_V)_*(\gamma)^{-1}
\qquad
(\gamma\in\pi_1(U\cap V,x_0)).
\]
Equivalently,
\[
\pi_1(X,x_0)
\cong
\frac{\pi_1(U,x_0)*\pi_1(V,x_0)}
{\left\langle\!\left\langle
(i_U)_*(\gamma)(i_V)_*(\gamma)^{-1}:
\gamma\in\pi_1(U\cap V,x_0)
\right\rangle\!\right\rangle}.
\]
:::

<1>2. The groups requested in part (b) are
\[
\pi_1(T^2)\cong\ZZ\oplus\ZZ,
\qquad
\pi_1(\RP^2)\cong\ZZ/2\ZZ.
\]
::: {.proof}
These are the answers requested in part (b); the problem explicitly says not to prove them.
:::

<1>3. The wedge $X=T^2\vee\RP^2$ admits a van Kampen cover $X=U\cup V$ in which
\[
U\simeq T^2,
\qquad
V\simeq\RP^2,
\qquad
U\cap V\simeq\{*\}.
\]
::: {.proof}
Let $p$ be the wedge point.
Choose small open disk neighborhoods $D_T\subset T^2$ and $D_P\subset\RP^2$ of the points identified to form $p$.
In the wedge, take
\[
U=T^2\cup D_P,
\qquad
V=D_T\cup\RP^2.
\]
These are open in the quotient topology.
The disk piece $D_P$ contracts to $p$, so $U$ deformation retracts onto $T^2$; similarly $V$ deformation retracts onto $\RP^2$.
Their intersection is
\[
U\cap V=D_T\cup D_P,
\]
with the two disks meeting only at $p$, hence it contracts to $p$.
Thus the displayed homotopy types hold, and all three sets are path connected.
:::

<1>4. Van Kampen gives
\[
\pi_1(T^2\vee\RP^2)
\cong
\pi_1(T^2)*\pi_1(\RP^2)
\cong
(\ZZ\oplus\ZZ)*(\ZZ/2\ZZ).
\]
::: {.proof}
Apply <1>1 to the cover in <1>3.
Because $U\cap V$ is simply connected,
\[
\pi_1(U\cap V)=1,
\]
so van Kampen imposes no relations between the two free factors.
Using the identifications in <1>2 therefore gives the displayed free product.
:::

<1>5. Equivalently,
\[
\pi_1(X)
\cong
\langle a,b,c\mid [a,b]=1,\ c^2=1\rangle.
\]
::: {.proof}
The torus factor has presentation
\[
\ZZ^2\cong\langle a,b\mid[a,b]=1\rangle,
\]
and the projective-plane factor has presentation
\[
\ZZ/2\ZZ\cong\langle c\mid c^2=1\rangle.
\]
The presentation of a free product is obtained by adjoining the generators and relations of the two factors without adding cross-relations.
:::
