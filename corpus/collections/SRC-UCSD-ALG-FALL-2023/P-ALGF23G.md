::: problem
Suppose $F$ is a field, $f \in F[x]$ is irreducible, and $E$ is a splitting field of $f$ over $F$.
Suppose there exists $\alpha \in E$ such that $f(\alpha) = f(\alpha + 1) = 0$.
Prove that the characteristic of $F$ is positive.
:::
