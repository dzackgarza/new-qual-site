::: {.problem}
Let $f_0,f_1:(I,\partial I)\to(X,x)$ be loops.

1. Say that $f_0$ and $f_1$ are homotopic through loops if they are joined by a homotopy $f_t$ with $f_t(0)=f_t(1)$, but not necessarily equal to $x$. Show that $f_0$ is homotopic to $f_1$ through loops if and only if $[f_0]$ is conjugate to $[f_1]$ in $\pi_1(X,x)$.
2. Show that a homotopy equivalence between path-connected spaces induces an isomorphism on fundamental groups, regardless of the choices of basepoints.
:::
