::: {.problem}
For $k\in\mathbb N$, let
\[
s_k=1+\frac1{2^2}+\cdots+\frac1{k^2}.
\]
Show that
\[
s_k\le 2-\frac1k.
\]
Prove or disprove: $(s_k)$ is a Cauchy sequence.
:::
