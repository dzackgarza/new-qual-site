::: {.problem}
(a) Let $C$ be a path from $(0,1)$ to $(1,2)$. Explain whether
\[
\int_C y e^x\,dx+x e^y\,dy
\]
depends on the choice of $C$.

(b) Give an example of a function defined on the plane whose gradient always points toward the origin.
:::
