::: {.problem}
Let $G$ be a finite elementary abelian $p$-group.
Show that $\Phi(G)=1$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Write $G\cong C_p^d$.
For each coordinate $i$, let
\[
M_i=C_p\times\cdots\times C_p\times 1\times C_p\times\cdots\times C_p.
\]
Then $[G:M_i]=p$, so $M_i$ is maximal.
Their intersection is trivial.
Since the Frattini subgroup is the intersection of all maximal subgroups,
\[
\Phi(G)\le \bigcap_{i=1}^d M_i=1.
\]
Thus $\Phi(G)=1$.
:::
