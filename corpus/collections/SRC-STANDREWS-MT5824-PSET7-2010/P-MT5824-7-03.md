::: {.problem}
Show that
\[
Z(G\times H)=Z(G)\times Z(H).
\]
Show by induction that
\[
Z_i(G\times H)=Z_i(G)\times Z_i(H)
\]
for every $i$.
Deduce that a finite direct product of nilpotent groups is nilpotent.
:::

::: {.solution}
An element $(x,y)$ commutes with every $(g,h)$ exactly when $x$ commutes with every $g\in G$ and $y$ commutes with every $h\in H$.
Hence $Z(G\times H)=Z(G)\times Z(H)$.

Assume $Z_i(G\times H)=Z_i(G)\times Z_i(H)$.
Then
\[
\frac{G\times H}{Z_i(G\times H)}\cong
\frac G{Z_i(G)}\times\frac H{Z_i(H)}.
\]
Taking centers and pulling back under this quotient gives
\[
Z_{i+1}(G\times H)=Z_{i+1}(G)\times Z_{i+1}(H).
\]
The case $i=0$ is immediate, so induction proves the formula.

If $G_1,\dots,G_n$ are nilpotent, choose $c$ at least their nilpotency classes.
Then
\[
Z_c(G_1\times\cdots\times G_n)=Z_c(G_1)\times\cdots\times Z_c(G_n)
=G_1\times\cdots\times G_n,
\]
so the product is nilpotent.
:::
