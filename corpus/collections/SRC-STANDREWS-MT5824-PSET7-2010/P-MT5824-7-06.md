::: {.problem}
Let $G$ be nilpotent with lower central series
\[
G=\gamma_1(G)>\gamma_2(G)>\cdots>\gamma_c(G)>\gamma_{c+1}(G)=1.
\]
Suppose $N\triangleleft G$ is nontrivial.
Choose $i$ maximal such that $N\cap\gamma_i(G)\ne1$.
Show that
\[
[N\cap\gamma_i(G),G]=1,
\]
and deduce that $N\cap Z(G)\ne1$.
:::

::: {.solution}
By maximality of $i$,
\[
N\cap\gamma_{i+1}(G)=1.
\]
Because $N$ is normal,
\[
[N\cap\gamma_i(G),G]\le [N,G]\le N,
\]
while by definition of the lower central series,
\[
[N\cap\gamma_i(G),G]\le[\gamma_i(G),G]=\gamma_{i+1}(G).
\]
Hence
\[
[N\cap\gamma_i(G),G]\le N\cap\gamma_{i+1}(G)=1.
\]
Thus $N\cap\gamma_i(G)\le Z(G)$.
Since this subgroup is nontrivial by the choice of $i$, we obtain $N\cap Z(G)\ne1$.
:::
