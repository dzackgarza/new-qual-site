::: {.problem}
Show that $\gamma_2(G)=G'$.
Deduce that abelian groups are nilpotent.
:::

::: {.solution}
By definition,
\[
\gamma_2(G)=[G,G]=\langle [x,y]:x,y\in G\rangle=G'.
\]
If $G$ is abelian then every commutator is trivial, so $\gamma_2(G)=1$.
Hence $G$ is nilpotent of class at most $1$.
:::
