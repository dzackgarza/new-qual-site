::: {.problem}
Let $f$ be entire and suppose
\[
|f(z)|=|\sin z|
\]
for every $z\in\mathbb C$.
Prove that there is a constant $C$ with $|C|=1$ such that
\[
f(z)=C\sin z.
\]
:::
