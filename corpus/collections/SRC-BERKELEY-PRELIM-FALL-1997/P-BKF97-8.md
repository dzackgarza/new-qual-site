::: {.problem}
Suppose $H_i\trianglelefteq G$ for $1\le i\le k$ and
\[
H_i\cap H_j=\{1\}
\qquad(i\ne j).
\]
Prove that when $k=2$, the group $G$ contains a subgroup isomorphic to
\[
H_1\times H_2,
\]
but that this conclusion need not hold for $k\ge3$.
:::
