::: {.problem}
Let $G$ be generated by two elements $a,b$, each of order $2$.
Prove that $G$ has a cyclic subgroup of index $2$.
:::

::: {.solution}

Define
$$
H\coloneqq\langle ab\rangle.
$$

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #a-normalizes-H}
The element $a$ normalizes $H$.

::: pf-proof
Since
$$
a^2=b^2=1,
$$
one has
$$
a(ab)a
=
ba
=
(ab)^{-1}.
$$
Thus conjugation by $a$ sends the generator $ab$ of $H$ to its inverse,
and therefore sends $H$ to itself.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #G-generated-by-H-and-a}
The group $G$ is generated by $H$ together with $a$.

::: pf-proof
The element
$$
ab
$$
belongs to $H$, and
$$
b
=
a(ab)
$$
because $a^2=1$. Hence both original generators $a$ and $b$ belong to
$\langle H,a\rangle$. Therefore
$$
G=\langle H,a\rangle.
$$
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #every-element-in-H-or-Ha}
Every element of $G$ lies in
$$
H\cup Ha.
$$

::: pf-proof
By step [](#a-normalizes-H){.pf-ref},
$$
aHa^{-1}=H.
$$
Thus
$$
aH=Ha.
$$
Using step [](#G-generated-by-H-and-a){.pf-ref}, every word in elements of $H$ and $a$ can move all
occurrences of $a$ to the right. Since $a^2=1$, the resulting word is
either an element of $H$ or an element of $Ha$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #a-not-in-H}
The element $a$ does not belong to $H$.

::: pf-proof
Suppose instead that
$$
a\in H.
$$
Then step [](#G-generated-by-H-and-a){.pf-ref} gives
$$
G=H,
$$
so $G$ is cyclic. A cyclic group has at most one element of order $2$.
Since both $a$ and $b$ have order $2$, this forces
$$
a=b.
$$
Hence
$$
ab=1,
$$
so
$$
H=\langle ab\rangle=\{1\}.
$$
But $a$ has order $2$, so $a\neq1$, contradicting $a\in H$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #H-index-two}
The subgroup $H$ has index $2$ in $G$.

::: pf-proof
Step [](#every-element-in-H-or-Ha){.pf-ref} shows that there are at most two left cosets of $H$, represented
by $1$ and $a$. Step [](#a-not-in-H){.pf-ref} shows these cosets are distinct. Therefore
$$
[G:H]=2.
$$
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #cyclic-subgroup-index-two}
The group $G$ has a cyclic subgroup of index $2$.

::: pf-proof
By definition,
$$
H=\langle ab\rangle
$$
is cyclic, and step [](#H-index-two){.pf-ref} gives its index.
:::

:::

::: pf-qed
Step [](#cyclic-subgroup-index-two){.pf-ref} is the required conclusion.
:::
