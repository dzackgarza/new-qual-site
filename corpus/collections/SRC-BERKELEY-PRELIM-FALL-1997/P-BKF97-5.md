::: {.problem}
Let
\[
\mathbb D=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<1\}.
\]
Suppose $f:\mathbb D\to\mathbb D$ is analytic and there exist distinct $a,b\in\mathbb D$ with
\[
f(a)=a,
\qquad
f(b)=b.
\]
Prove that $f(z)=z$ for every $z\in\mathbb D$.
:::
