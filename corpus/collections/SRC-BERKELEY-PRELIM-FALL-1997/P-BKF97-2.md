::: {.problem}
Let $f$ be a real-valued function differentiable on an open interval containing $[a,b]$.
Prove that if
\[
f'(a)<0
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
f'(b)>0,
\]
then there is a point $c\in(a,b)$ such that $f'(c)=0$.
:::
