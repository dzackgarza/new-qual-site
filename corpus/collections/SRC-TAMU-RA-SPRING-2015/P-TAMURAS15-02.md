::: {.problem}
Let $\{g_n\}_{n=1}^\infty$ and $g$ be in $L^1(\RR)$ and satisfy
$$
\lim_{n\to\infty}\norm{g_n-g}_1=0.
$$
Prove that there is a subsequence of $\{g_n\}_{n=1}^\infty$ that converges pointwise almost everywhere to $g$.
:::
