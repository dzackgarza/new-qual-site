::: {.problem}
Let $f,g\in C[0,1]$ with $f(x)<g(x)$ for all $x\in[0,1]$.

(i) Prove that there is a polynomial $p(x)$ so that $$f(x)<p(x)<g(x),\qquad x\in[0,1].$$

(ii) Prove that there is an increasing sequence of polynomials $\{p_n(x)\}_{n=1}^\infty$ so that $$f(x)<p_n(x)<g(x),\qquad x\in[0,1],$$ and $p_n\to g$ uniformly on $[0,1]$.
:::
