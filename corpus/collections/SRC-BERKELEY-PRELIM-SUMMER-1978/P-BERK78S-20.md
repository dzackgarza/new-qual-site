:::{.problem}
Let $M_{n\times n}(\mathbb R)$ be the vector space of real $n\times n$ matrices, and define
\[
f:M_{n\times n}(\mathbb R)\to M_{n\times n}(\mathbb R),
\qquad f(X)=X^2.
\]
Find the derivative of $f$.
:::
