:::{.problem}
Let $f:\mathbb C\to\mathbb C$ be entire, and let $a,b>0$.

1. If
   \[
   |f(z)|\le a\sqrt{|z|}+b
   \]
   for all $z$, prove that $f$ is constant.
2. What can one prove about $f$ if
   \[
   |f(z)|\le a|z|^{5/2}+b
   \]
   for all $z$?
:::
