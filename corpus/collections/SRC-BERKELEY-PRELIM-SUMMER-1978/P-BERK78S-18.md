:::{.problem}
Let $N:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R$ be a norm.

1. Prove that $N$ is bounded on the Euclidean unit sphere.
2. Prove that $N$ is continuous.
3. Prove that there are constants $A,B>0$ such that
   \[
   A\|x\|\le N(x)\le B\|x\|
   \]
   for every $x\in\mathbb R^n$.
:::
