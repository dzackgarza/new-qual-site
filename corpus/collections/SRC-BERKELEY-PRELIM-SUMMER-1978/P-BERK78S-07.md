:::{.problem}
1. Solve
   \[
   g'=2g,\qquad g(0)=a,
   \]
   where $a$ is a real constant.
2. Suppose $f:[0,1]\to\mathbb R$ is continuous, $f(0)=0$, and $f$ is differentiable for $0<x<1$ with
   \[
   0\le f'(x)\le2f(x).
   \]
   Prove that $f$ is identically zero.
:::
