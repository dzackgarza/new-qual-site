::: {.problem}
Let $E$ be the set of functions $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ satisfying
\[
f'''+f''-2f=0.
\]

1. Prove that $E$ is a vector space and find its dimension.
2. Let
   \[
   E_0=\{g\in E:\lim_{t\to\infty}g(t)=0\}.
   \]
   Find $g\in E_0$ such that $g(0)=0$ and $g'(0)=2$.
:::
