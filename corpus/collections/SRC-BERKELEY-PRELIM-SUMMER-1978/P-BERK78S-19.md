:::{.problem}
Let $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ be continuous. Suppose $\mathbb R$ contains a countably infinite subset $S$ such that
\[
\int_p^q f(x)\,dx=0
\]
whenever $p,q\notin S$. Prove that $f$ is identically zero.
:::
