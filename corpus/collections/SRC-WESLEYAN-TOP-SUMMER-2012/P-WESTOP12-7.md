::: {.problem}
Prove or disprove each statement:

1. If $A\subset X$ and $X$ is simply connected, then $A$ is simply connected.
2. If $r:X\to A$ is a retraction, then
   \[
   r_*:\pi_1(X)\to\pi_1(A)
   \]
   is surjective.
:::
