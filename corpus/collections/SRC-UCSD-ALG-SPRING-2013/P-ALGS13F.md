::: problem
Let $E$ be the splitting field of $x^p - 2$ over $\mathbb{Q}$, where $p$ is an odd prime.

(a) Prove that $E = \mathbb{Q}[\zeta_p, \sqrt[p]{2}]$ and find $[E:\mathbb{Q}]$.
(Here $\zeta_p$ is a primitive $p$th root of unity.)

(b) Find $\{(\sigma(\zeta_p), \sigma(\sqrt[p]{2})) \mid \sigma \in \mathrm{Gal}(E/\mathbb{Q})\}$.
Justify your answer.
:::
