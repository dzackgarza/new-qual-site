::: {.problem}
(a) Find a polynomial $P(x)$ of degree $2$ such that $P(A)=0$ for
\[
A=\begin{pmatrix}1&3\\4&2\end{pmatrix}.
\]

(b) Prove that such a polynomial $P(x)$ is unique up to multiplication by a constant.
:::
