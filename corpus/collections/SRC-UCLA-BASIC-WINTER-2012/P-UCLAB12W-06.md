::: {.problem}
Let $\gamma$ be a smooth curve from $(1,0)$ to $(1,0)$ in $\mathbb R^2\setminus\{(0,0)\}$ winding once around the origin in the clockwise direction.
Compute
\[
I(\gamma)=\int_\gamma\frac{y\,dx-x\,dy}{x^2+y^2}.
\]
:::
