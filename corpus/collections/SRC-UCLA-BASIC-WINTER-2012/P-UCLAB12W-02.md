::: {.problem}
Let $f_n:[a,b]\to\mathbb R$ be convex functions and suppose
\[
f(x)=\lim_{n\to\infty}f_n(x)
\]
exists for every $x\in[a,b]$ and is continuous on $[a,b]$.
Prove that $f_n\to f$ uniformly.
:::
