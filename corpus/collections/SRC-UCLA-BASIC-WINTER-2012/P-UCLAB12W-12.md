::: {.problem}
Recall that quadratic forms $Q_1(x,y)$ and $Q_2(x',y')$ are equivalent if they are related by a nonsingular change of coordinates $(x,y)\mapsto(x',y')$.

Decide whether
\[
Q_1=xy,
\qquad
Q_2=x^2+y^2
\]
are equivalent over $\mathbb C$ and whether they are equivalent over $\mathbb R$. If not, prove that they are not. If yes, find the matrix of a change of coordinates.
:::
