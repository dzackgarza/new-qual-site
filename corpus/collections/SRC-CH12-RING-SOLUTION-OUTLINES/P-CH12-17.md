::: {.problem}
Show that a ring that is cyclic under addition is commutative.
:::

::: {.solution}
Suppose that R is a ring that is cyclic under addition.
Call its additive generator a. Then any element in R is of the form na for some $n \in \mathbb { Z }$ . Further, $( n a ) ( m a ) = ( n m ) a ^ { 2 } = ( m a ) ( n a )$ by exercise 15. Hence R is commutative.
:::
