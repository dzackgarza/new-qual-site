::: {.problem}
Is $\ZZ_{6}$ a subring of $\ZZ_{12}$?
:::

::: {.solution}
No. The set $\{0,1,\ldots,5\} \subseteq \ZZ _ { 1 2 }$ is not closed under the operations of $\ZZ _ { 1 2 }$: for example $3 + 3 = 6$ in $\ZZ _ { 1 2 }$, whereas $3 + 3 = 0$ in $\ZZ _ { 6 }$.
:::
