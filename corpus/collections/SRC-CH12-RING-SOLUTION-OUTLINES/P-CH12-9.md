::: {.problem}
Prove that the intersection of any collection of subrings of a ring R is a subring of R.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let S be the intersection of a collection of subrings of R.
Each subring contains 0, so $0 \in S$ and S is nonempty.
Let a and b be elements of S. Then a and b are in each subring of the collection.
Thus $a - b$ and ab are in each subring of the collection, and hence in S.
Hence it is a subring.
:::
