::: {.problem}
Let $k$ be the ground field. Find an everywhere regular differential
$n\dash$form on
$$
\AA^n_k.
$$
:::

::: {.solution}
Let
$$
x_1,\ldots,x_n
$$
be the standard coordinate functions on $\AA^n_k$.

<1>1. The cotangent sheaf of affine $n$-space is free:
$$
\Omega^1_{\AA^n_k/k}
\cong
\bigoplus_{i=1}^n
\mco_{\AA^n_k}\,dx_i.
$$

::: {.proof}
The coordinate ring of affine space is
$$
A=k[x_1,\ldots,x_n].
$$
The module of Kähler differentials of a polynomial algebra is free:
$$
\Omega^1_{A/k}
\cong
\bigoplus_{i=1}^n A\,dx_i.
$$
Sheafifying this free module on
$$
\AA^n_k=\Spec A
$$
gives the displayed trivialization of the cotangent sheaf.
:::

<1>2. The top exterior power is a free rank-one sheaf generated by
$$
\omega
\coloneqq
dx_1\wedge\cdots\wedge dx_n.
$$

::: {.proof}
Taking the $n$th exterior power of the free rank-$n$ sheaf in step <1>1
gives
$$
\Omega^n_{\AA^n_k/k}
\coloneqq
\bigwedge^n
\Omega^1_{\AA^n_k/k}
\cong
\mco_{\AA^n_k}.
$$
Under this isomorphism the standard generator is
$$
dx_1\wedge\cdots\wedge dx_n.
$$
Thus $\omega$ is a global section of the top differential sheaf.
:::

<1>3. The form
$$
\boxed{
\omega=dx_1\wedge\cdots\wedge dx_n
}
$$
is everywhere regular and nowhere vanishing.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>2 identifies $\omega$ with the element
$$
1\in\mco_{\AA^n_k}
$$
under a trivialization of the line bundle
$$
\Omega^n_{\AA^n_k/k}.
$$
Hence its germ at every point generates the corresponding stalk. In
particular, it is regular at every point and vanishes nowhere.
:::

<1>4. Equivalently, the canonical bundle of affine space is trivial:
$$
\boxed{
K_{\AA^n_k}
\cong
\mco_{\AA^n_k}.
}
$$

::: {.proof}
The canonical bundle of a smooth $n$-fold $X$ is the determinant of the
cotangent bundle:
$$
K_X=\det T^*(X).
$$
For affine $n$-space this determinant is precisely
$$
\Omega^n_{\AA^n_k/k},
$$
which step <1>2 trivializes by $\omega$.
:::

<1>5. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>3 gives the requested everywhere regular differential $n$-form, and
step <1>4 records its canonical-bundle interpretation.
:::
