::: {.problem}
Define the Weierstrass cubic as $X\da V(y^{2} z-(x^{3}+g_{2} x z^{2}+g_{3} z^{3}) )$ for $g_2, g_3 \in \CC$.
Show that $X$ is nonsingular iff $p(x) \da x^3 + g_2 x + g_3$ has no multiple roots.
:::
