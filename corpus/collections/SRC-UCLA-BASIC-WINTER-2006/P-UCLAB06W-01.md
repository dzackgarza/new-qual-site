::: {.problem}
Show that for each $\varepsilon>0$ there exists a sequence of intervals $(I_n)$ such that
\[
\bigcup_{n=1}^{\infty} I_n\supset\mathbb Q
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}|I_n|<\varepsilon.
\]
:::
