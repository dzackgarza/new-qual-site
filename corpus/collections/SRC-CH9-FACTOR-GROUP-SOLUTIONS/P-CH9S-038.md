::: {.problem}
Let H be a normal subgroup of G and let a belong to G. If the element aH has order 3 in the group $G / H$ and $| H | = 1 0$ , what are the possibilities for the order of $a ?$
:::

Let
$$
m=\operatorname{ord}_G(a).
$$

::: pf

::: pf-step

The order $m$ is finite and divides $30$.


::: pf-proof

Since $aH$ has order $3$,
$$
(aH)^3=H,
$$
so
$$
a^3\in H.
$$
The finite group $H$ has order $10$. By Lagrange's theorem, every
element of $H$ has order dividing $10$, hence
$$
(a^3)^{10}=e.
$$
Therefore
$$
a^{30}=e,
$$
so $m$ is finite and $m\mid30$.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

The integer $3$ divides $m$.


::: pf-proof

The order of the coset $aH$ divides the order of $a$. Indeed, applying
the result of Problem 37 gives
$$
\operatorname{ord}_{G/H}(aH)\mid\operatorname{ord}_G(a).
$$
Since the left-hand side is $3$, one has $3\mid m$.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

The only possible values of $m$ are
$$
\boxed{
\operatorname{ord}(a)\in\{3,6,15,30\}}.
$$


::: pf-proof

By step <1>1, $m$ is a positive divisor of $30$, and by step <1>2 it
is divisible by $3$. The divisors of $30$ with this property are
exactly
$$
3,\ 6,\ 15,\ 30.
$$

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Each of the four values in step <1>3 actually occurs.


::: pf-proof

Let
$$
G=C_{10}\times C_3,
\qquad
H=C_{10}\times\{e\}.
$$
Then $H\trianglelefteq G$, $|H|=10$, and
$$
G/H\cong C_3.
$$
Let $c$ generate $C_3$. For each
$$
d\in\{1,2,5,10\},
$$
choose $h_d\in C_{10}$ of order $d$ and put
$$
a_d=(h_d,c).
$$
The coset $a_dH$ corresponds to $c$ in $G/H$, so it has order $3$.
Since the order of an element in a direct product is the least common
multiple of the coordinate orders,
$$
\operatorname{ord}(a_d)=\operatorname{lcm}(d,3).
$$
For $d=1,2,5,10$, these orders are respectively
$$
3,\ 6,\ 15,\ 30.
$$
Thus every value listed in step <1>3 is attainable.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Steps <1>1--<1>3 show that no other order is possible, and step <1>4
shows that all four listed orders can occur.

:::
