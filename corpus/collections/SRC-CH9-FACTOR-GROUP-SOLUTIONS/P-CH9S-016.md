::: {.problem}
Recall that $Z(D_{6}) = \{e, r^{3}\}$. What is the order of the element $r Z(D_{6})$ in the factor group $D_{6} / Z(D_{6})$?
:::

::: {.solution}

Write
$$
Z=Z(D_6)=\{e,r^3\}.
$$

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

The third power of $rZ$ is the identity coset, while neither its
first nor its second power is the identity coset.


::: pf-proof

Since $r^3\in Z$,
$$
(rZ)^3=r^3Z=Z.
$$
The rotation $r$ has order $6$, so
$$
r\notin\{e,r^3\}=Z
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
r^2\notin\{e,r^3\}=Z.
$$
Hence
$$
rZ\neq Z
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
(rZ)^2=r^2Z\neq Z.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

The requested order is
$$
\boxed{\operatorname{ord}(rZ(D_6))=3}.
$$


::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref}, the least positive exponent $m$ for which
$(rZ)^m=Z$ is $m=3$.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} gives the requested order.

:::
