::: {.problem}
What is the order of the factor group $(\ZZ_{10} \oplus U(10)) / \langle (2, 9) \rangle$?
:::

::: {.solution}

Let
$$
G=\ZZ_{10}\oplus U(10)
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
H=\langle(2,9)\rangle.
$$

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

The group $G$ has order $40$.


::: pf-proof

The unit group modulo $10$ is
$$
U(10)=\{1,3,7,9\},
$$
so $\cardinality{U(10)}=4$. Therefore
$$
\cardinality{G}
=
\cardinality{\ZZ_{10}}\,\cardinality{U(10)}
=
10\cdot4
=
40.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

The element $(2,9)$ has order $10$, and hence $\cardinality{H}=10$.


::: pf-proof

In $\ZZ_{10}$, the additive element $2$ has order
$$
\frac{10}{\gcd(10,2)}=5.
$$
In $U(10)$, the element $9$ has order $2$, since
$$
9^2\equiv1\pmod{10}
$$
and $9\not\equiv1\pmod{10}$. The order of an element in a direct
product is the least common multiple of the orders of its coordinates,
so
$$
\operatorname{ord}(2,9)=\operatorname{lcm}(5,2)=10.
$$
Thus the cyclic subgroup generated by $(2,9)$ has order $10$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

The factor group has order
$$
\boxed{\cardinality{G/H}=4}.
$$


::: pf-proof

The group $G$ is abelian, so $H\trianglelefteq G$. By Lagrange's
theorem and steps [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} through [](#s1-2){.pf-ref},
$$
\cardinality{G/H}
=
\frac{\cardinality{G}}{\cardinality{H}}
=
\frac{40}{10}
=
4.
$$

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} gives the requested order.

:::
