::: {.problem}
Let $G = \ZZ _ { 4 } \oplus U ( 4 ) , \ H = < ( 2 , 3 ) >$ , and $K = < ( 2 , 1 ) >$ . Show that $G / H$ is not isomorphic to $G / K$ . (This shows that $H \approx K$ does not imply that $G / H \approx G / K . )$
:::

::: {.solution}

The operation on
$G=\ZZ_4\oplus U(4)$ is
$$
(a,u)(b,v)=(a+b,uv),
$$
where the first coordinate is taken modulo $4$ and
$U(4)=\{1,3\}$ is multiplicative modulo $4$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

The subgroups $H$ and $K$ are normal subgroups of order $2$.


::: pf-proof

The elements $(2,3)$ and $(2,1)$ both have order $2$, because
$$
(2,3)^2=(0,1)
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
(2,1)^2=(0,1).
$$
Hence
$$
H=\{(0,1),(2,3)\},
\qquad
K=\{(0,1),(2,1)\}.
$$
Since $G$ is abelian, both subgroups are normal.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

The quotient $G/H$ is cyclic of order $4$.


::: pf-proof

Since $\cardinality{G}=4\cdot2=8$ and $\cardinality{H}=2$, Lagrange's theorem gives
$\cardinality{G/H}=4$. Consider the coset
$$
(1,3)H.
$$
Its square is
$$
\bigl((1,3)H\bigr)^2
=(2,1)H.
$$
This is not the identity coset, because $(2,1)\notin H$. On the other
hand,
$$
\bigl((1,3)H\bigr)^4
=(0,1)H
=H.
$$
Thus $(1,3)H$ has order $4$ and generates $G/H$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

Every element of $G/K$ has order dividing $2$.


::: pf-proof

Let $(a,u)\in G$. Since $u\in\{1,3\}$,
$$
u^2=1
\quad\text{in }U(4).
$$
Therefore
$$
(a,u)^2=(2a,1).
$$
Modulo $4$, the element $2a$ is either $0$ or $2$, so
$$
(a,u)^2\in\{(0,1),(2,1)\}=K.
$$
Hence
$$
\bigl((a,u)K\bigr)^2=K
$$
for every coset $(a,u)K$. Thus no element of $G/K$ has order $4$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

The two quotient groups are not isomorphic:
$$
\boxed{G/H\not\cong G/K}.
$$


::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref}, $G/H$ contains an element of order $4$. By step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref},
$G/K$ contains no element of order $4$. Group isomorphisms preserve
element orders, so no isomorphism $G/H\to G/K$ can exist.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} verifies that both quotients are defined, and steps
[](#s1-2){.pf-ref} through [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} distinguish them by element order.

:::
