::: {.problem}
If $N$ is a normal subgroup of $G$ and $\cardinality{G / N} = m$, show that $x^{m} \in N$ for all $x$ in $G$.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

For every $x\in G$,
$$
(xN)^m=N
$$
in the quotient group $G/N$.


::: pf-proof

The quotient $G/N$ is a finite group of order $m$. By Lagrange's
theorem, the order of the element $xN\in G/N$ divides $m$. Therefore
raising $xN$ to the $m$th power gives the identity element $N$ of the
quotient.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

For every $x\in G$, one has $x^m\in N$.


::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref},
$$
N=(xN)^m=x^mN.
$$
The equality $x^mN=N$ is equivalent to $x^m\in N$.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} is the required conclusion for arbitrary $x\in G$.

:::
