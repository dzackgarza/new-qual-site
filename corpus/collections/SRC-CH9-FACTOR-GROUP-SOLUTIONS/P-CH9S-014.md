::: {.problem}
What is the order of the element $1 4 + < 8 >$ in the factor group $\ZZ _ { 2 4 } / < 8 > ?$
:::

::: {.solution}

Let
$$
H=\langle8\rangle
=
\{0,8,16\}
\subseteq\ZZ_{24}.
$$

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

The element $14+H$ has fourth multiple equal to the identity
coset, but its second multiple is nontrivial.


::: pf-proof

In the quotient,
$$
4(14+H)
=
56+H
=
8+H
=
H,
$$
because $8\in H$. On the other hand,
$$
2(14+H)
=
28+H
=
4+H
\neq H,
$$
because $4\notin H$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

The requested order is
$$
\boxed{\operatorname{ord}(14+\langle8\rangle)=4}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} shows that the order divides $4$ but does not divide $2$.
Therefore it is $4$.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} gives the requested order.

:::
