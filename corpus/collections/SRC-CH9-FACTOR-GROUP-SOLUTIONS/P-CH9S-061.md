::: {.problem}
Let H be a normal subgroup of a finite group G and let $x \in G$ . If $g c d ( | x | , | G / H | ) = 1$ , show that $x \in H$ .
:::

::: {.solution}
Let
$$
m=\operatorname{ord}_G(x),
\qquad
n=\operatorname{ord}_{G/H}(xH).
$$

<1>1. The integer $n$ divides $m$.

::: {.proof}
Since $x$ has order $m$,
$$
x^m=e.
$$
Therefore
$$
(xH)^m=x^mH=H.
$$
The order of an element divides every positive exponent that gives the
identity, so $n\mid m$.
:::

<1>2. The integer $n$ divides $|G/H|$.

::: {.proof}
The quotient $G/H$ is finite because $G$ is finite. By Lagrange's
theorem, the order $n$ of the element $xH\in G/H$ divides the order of
$G/H$.
:::

<1>3. One has $n=1$.

::: {.proof}
By steps <1>1 and <1>2, the positive integer $n$ divides both $m$ and
$|G/H|$. The hypothesis
$$
\gcd(m,|G/H|)=1
$$
therefore forces $n=1$.
:::

<1>4. The element $x$ lies in $H$.

::: {.proof}
By step <1>3, the coset $xH$ has order $1$ in $G/H$, so it is the
identity coset:
$$
xH=H.
$$
This is equivalent to $x\in H$.
:::

<1>5. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>4 is the required conclusion.
:::
