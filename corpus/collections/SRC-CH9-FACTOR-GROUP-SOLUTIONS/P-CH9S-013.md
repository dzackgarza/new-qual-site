::: {.problem}
Prove that a factor group of an Abelian group is Abelian.
:::

::: {.solution}

Let $G$ be an abelian group and let $N\trianglelefteq G$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

The factor group $G/N$ is abelian:
$$
\boxed{\text{$G/N$ is abelian}.}
$$


::: pf-proof

Let $aN,bN\in G/N$ be arbitrary. Since $G$ is abelian,
$$
ab=ba.
$$
Therefore
$$
(aN)(bN)
=
(ab)N
=
(ba)N
=
(bN)(aN).
$$
Thus every pair of elements of $G/N$ commutes.

:::

group.
