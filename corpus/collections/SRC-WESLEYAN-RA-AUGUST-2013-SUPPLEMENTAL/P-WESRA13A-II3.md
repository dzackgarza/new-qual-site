::: problem
Let $A\subset\mathbb R$ be Lebesgue measurable and let $f:A\to\mathbb R$ be measurable.
Suppose
\[
\int_K f\,dm=0
\]
for every compact $K\subset A$.
Must $f=0$ almost everywhere?
Prove the conclusion or give a counterexample.
:::
