::: {.problem}
Let $a,b$ be the generators of
$$
\pi_1(S^1\vee S^1).
$$
Draw a picture of the covering space of $S^1\vee S^1$ corresponding to the normal subgroup generated by
$$
a^2,\qquad b^2,\qquad ab.
$$
:::

::: {.solution}

Let
$$
F=\pi_1(S^1\vee S^1)=\langle a,b\rangle
$$
and let
$$
N=\langle\!\langle a^2,b^2,ab\rangle\!\rangle
$$
be their normal closure.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

The quotient group $F/N$ is isomorphic to $\ZZ/2\ZZ$.


::: pf-proof

In the quotient, the relations are
$$
a^2=1,
\qquad
b^2=1,
\qquad
ab=1.
$$
The last relation gives
$$
b=a^{-1}.
$$
Since $a^2=1$, one has $a^{-1}=a$, so $b=a$. Thus the quotient is generated by one element $a$ with the single relation $a^2=1$:
$$
F/N\cong\langle a\mid a^2=1\rangle\cong\ZZ/2\ZZ.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

Equivalently, $N$ is the kernel of the homomorphism
$$
\phi:F\longrightarrow\ZZ/2\ZZ,
\qquad
\phi(a)=\phi(b)=1.
$$


::: pf-proof

The elements $a^2,b^2,ab$ all lie in $\ker\phi$, so
$$
N\subseteq\ker\phi.
$$
The map $\phi$ is surjective, so $F/\ker\phi$ has order $2$. Step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} shows that $F/N$ also has order $2$. Since $N\subseteq\ker\phi$, equality follows.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

The corresponding covering has two vertices, say $v_0$ and $v_1$, indexed by the two cosets of $N$ in $F$.


::: pf-proof

For a subgroup of the fundamental group of a graph, the vertices of the associated Schreier covering graph are the right cosets of the subgroup. By step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref},
$$
[F:N]=2.
$$
Thus there are exactly two vertices.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

Both the $a$-edge and the $b$-edge interchange the two vertices.


::: pf-proof

In the quotient $F/N\cong\ZZ/2\ZZ$, both $a$ and $b$ represent the nonzero element by step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref}. Right multiplication by either generator therefore swaps the two cosets.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-5}

Hence the covering graph is the two-sheeted graph with vertices $v_0,v_1$ and four edges:
$$
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{edge}&\text{initial vertex}&\text{terminal vertex}\\ \hline
a_0&v_0&v_1\\
a_1&v_1&v_0\\
b_0&v_0&v_1\\
b_1&v_1&v_0
\end{array}
$$
where each $a_j$ maps homeomorphically to the $a$-circle and each $b_j$ maps homeomorphically to the $b$-circle.


::: pf-proof

At each vertex of a covering of $S^1\vee S^1$, there must be exactly one outgoing lift of the positively oriented $a$-edge and exactly one outgoing lift of the positively oriented $b$-edge. Step [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} says that each such lift ends at the other sheet. Thus the four listed edges give the entire covering.

Ignoring orientation, this is a graph with two vertices joined by four parallel edges, two labeled $a$ and two labeled $b$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-6}

This graph is a connected regular two-sheeted covering of $S^1\vee S^1$, and its associated subgroup is exactly $N$.


::: pf-proof

The graph is connected because each generator edge joins $v_0$ to $v_1$. The local star at each vertex maps bijectively to the local star at the wedge point, so the map is a covering. Its monodromy action is precisely the quotient action of
$$
F\longrightarrow F/N\cong\ZZ/2\ZZ,
$$
whose stabilizer of the identity coset is $N$. Since $N$ is normal, the covering is regular, with deck group $F/N\cong\ZZ/2\ZZ$.

:::
