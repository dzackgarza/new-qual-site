::: {.remark}
Wesleyan University topology qualifying papers from June 2006 to June 2014, newest first.
The preamble of the August 10, 2011 paper refers to two parts, but the paper has nine numbered problems and no Part II heading.
In Part II Problem 2 of the 2013 paper one boundary circle appears as $S^2\times\{0\}$, which is not a subset of the cylinder $S^1\times I$; the problem is [[P-TOPS11B]], which reads $S^1\times\{0\}$.
:::
