::: {.problem}
Let $A\in M_3(\mathbb R)$ satisfy
\[
\det A=1
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
A^tA=I=AA^t.
\]
Prove that the characteristic polynomial of $A$ has $1$ as a root.
:::
