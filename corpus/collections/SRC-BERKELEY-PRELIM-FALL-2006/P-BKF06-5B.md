::: {.problem}
Let $f$ and $g$ be entire functions such that
\[
\int_{|z|=1}\frac{f(z)}{(\sin z)^m}\,dz
=
\int_{|z|=1}\frac{g(z)}{(\sin z)^m}\,dz
\]
for every positive integer $m$.
Prove that $f=g$.
:::
