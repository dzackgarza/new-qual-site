::: {.problem}
Let $f:\mathbb R\times[0,1]\to\mathbb R$ be continuous.
For $x\in\mathbb R$, define
\[
g(x)=\max\{f(x,y):y\in[0,1]\}.
\]
Show that $g$ is continuous.
:::
