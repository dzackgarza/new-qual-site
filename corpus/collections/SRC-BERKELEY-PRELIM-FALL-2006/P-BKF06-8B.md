::: {.problem}
Let $A$ be an $n\times n$ Hermitian matrix and $B$ an $n\times n$ positive-definite complex matrix.
Prove that there is an invertible complex $n\times n$ matrix $S$ such that
\[
S^*AS
\]
is diagonal and
\[
S^*BS=I.
\]
:::
