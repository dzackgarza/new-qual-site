Wesleyan University topology qualifying papers. The packet holds the August 1, 2005, August 30, 2004 and June 30, 2003 papers; Part II of the August 7, 2013 paper; the June 2006 paper; the last page of the June 13, 2007 paper, from Problem 9(c); and a second copy of the June 30, 2003 paper.
In Part II Problem 2 of the 2013 paper one boundary circle appears as $S^2\times\{0\}$, which is not a subset of the cylinder $S^1\times I$; the problem is [[P-TOPS11B]], which reads $S^1\times\{0\}$.
