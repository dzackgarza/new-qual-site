::: problem
Suppose $A$ is a unital commutative ring.

(a) Suppose $I \trianglelefteq A$ (that means $I$ is an ideal of $A$) and $a \in A$.
Suppose $(I : \langle a \rangle) := \{r \in A \mid ra \in I\}$ and $I + \langle a \rangle$ are finitely generated ideals.
Prove that $I$ is finitely generated.

(b) Let $\Sigma := \{I \trianglelefteq A \mid I \text{ is not finitely generated}\}$.
Prove that, if $\Sigma$ is not empty, then with respect to the inclusion ordering, $\Sigma$ has a maximal element.

(c) Suppose $I$ is a maximal element of $\Sigma$.
Prove that $I$ is a prime ideal.
:::
