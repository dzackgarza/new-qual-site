::: problem
Suppose $p$ and $q$ are two distinct primes and $G$ is a group of order $p^2 q$.
Prove that $G$ is solvable.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. $G$ group.
Proof: Sylow.

<1>2. Q.E.D.
Proof: <1>1.
:::
