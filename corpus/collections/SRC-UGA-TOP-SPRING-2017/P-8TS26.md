::: problem
Let $X\subset\mathbb{R}^2$ be the closed topologist's sine curve: the graph of $y=\sin(1/x)$ for $x\in(0,1]$, together with the vertical limit segment $\{0\}\times[-1,1]$.
Compute $H_0(X)$.
:::
