::: {.problem}
Determine which of the following functions are metrics on $\mathbb R$:
\[
d_1(x,y)=(x-y)^2,
\qquad
d_2(x,y)=\sqrt{|x-y|},
\]
\[
d_3(x,y)=|x^2-y^2|,
\qquad
d_4(x,y)=|x-2y|,
\]
and
\[
d_5(x,y)=\frac{|x-y|}{1+|x-y|}.
\]
:::
