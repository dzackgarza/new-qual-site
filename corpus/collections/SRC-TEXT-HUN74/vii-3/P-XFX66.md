::: {.problem title="Hungerford 7.3.1"}
Let $B$ be an $R$-module.
Show that if $r+r\neq 0$ for all $r\neq 0 \in R$, then an $n$-linear form $B^n\to R$ is alternating $\iff$ it is skew-symmetric.
:::
