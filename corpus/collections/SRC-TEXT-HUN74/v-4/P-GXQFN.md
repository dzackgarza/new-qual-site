::: {.problem title="Hungerford 5.4.12"}
Let $K$ be a subfield of$\mathbb{R}$ and let $f \in K[x]$ be an irreducible quartic.
If $f$ has exactly 2 real roots, the Galois group of $f$ is either $S_{4}$ or $D_{4}$.
:::
