::: {.problem}
Let $F$ be a free group and for a fixed integer $n$, let $H_n$ be the subgroup generated by the set $\{ x^n \mid x \in F \}$.
Show that $H_n \trianglelefteq F$.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. For all $g, x \in F$, $g x^n g^{-1} = (g x g^{-1})^n$.
::: {.proof}
Conjugation by $g$ is a homomorphism $F \to F$, so it sends $x^n$ to $(g x g^{-1})^n$.
:::

<1>2. For all $g, x \in F$, $g x^n g^{-1} \in H_n$.
::: {.proof}
The element $g x g^{-1}$ lies in $F$, so its $n$th power $(g x g^{-1})^n$ is one of the generators of $H_n$. Step <1>1 identifies this power with $g x^n g^{-1}$.
:::

<1>3. For every $g \in F$, $g H_n g^{-1} \subseteq H_n$.
::: {.proof}
Conjugation by $g$ is a homomorphism, so $g H_n g^{-1}$ is the subgroup generated by the conjugates $g x^n g^{-1}$ of the generators of $H_n$. By step <1>2 these conjugates lie in $H_n$, hence so does the subgroup they generate.
:::

<1>4. Q.E.D.
::: {.proof}
Step <1>3 is the definition of $H_n \trianglelefteq F$.
:::
:::
