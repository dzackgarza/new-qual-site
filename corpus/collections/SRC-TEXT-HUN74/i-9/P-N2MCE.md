::: {.problem}
Let $F$ be a free group and for a fixed integer $n$, let $H_n$ be the subgroup generated by the set $\{ x^n \mid x \in F \}$.
Show that $H_n \trianglelefteq F$.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. $H_n$ is generated by $\{x^n : x \in F\}$.
Proof: definition.

<1>2. For any $g \in F$ and any generator $x^n$ of $H_n$, $g x^n g^{-1} = (g x g^{-1})^n$.
Proof: conjugation is a homomorphism, so $(g x g^{-1})^n = g x^n g^{-1}$.

<1>3. $(g x g^{-1})^n \in H_n$ (since $g x g^{-1} \in F$, and $H_n$ contains the $n$-th power of every element of $F$).
Proof: <1>1 and <1>2.

<1>4. Hence $g H_n g^{-1} \subseteq H_n$ for every $g \in F$.
Proof: <1>3 (conjugation sends every generator of $H_n$ into $H_n$, hence sends all of $H_n$ into $H_n$).

<1>5. Therefore $H_n$ is normal in $F$.
Proof: <1>4.

<1>6. Q.E.D.
Proof: <1>5.
:::
