::: {.problem}
If $V$ is a finite dimensional vector space and $$V^m \coloneqq V \oplus V \oplus \cdots \oplus V \quad \text{($m$ summands)},$$ then for each $m\geq 1$, $V^m$ is finite dimensional and $\dim V^m = m(\dim V)$.
:::
