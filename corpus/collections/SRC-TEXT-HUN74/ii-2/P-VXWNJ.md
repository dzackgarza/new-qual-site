::: {.problem title="Hungerford 2.2.1"}
Let $G$ be a finite abelian group that is not cyclic.
Show that $G$ contains a subgroup isomorphic to $\mathbb{Z}_p \oplus \mathbb{Z}_p$ for some prime $p$.
:::
