::: problem
Apply Gram-Schmidt to $1,x,x^2$ in $L^2([-1,1])$ and relate the result to the first three Legendre polynomials. Use orthogonal projection to solve the stated approximation of $x^3$ by quadratics and the corresponding constrained maximization problem.
:::
