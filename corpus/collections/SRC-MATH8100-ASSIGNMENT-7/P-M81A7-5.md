::: problem
Apply Gram-Schmidt to $1,x,x^2$ in $L^2([-1,1])$ and relate the result to the first three Legendre polynomials.
Use orthogonal projection to solve the stated approximation of $x^3$ by quadratics and the corresponding constrained maximization problem.
:::

::: solution
Let
\[
P_0(x)=1,
\qquad
P_1(x)=x,
\qquad
P_2(x)=\frac{3x^2-1}{2}.
\]

<1>1. Gram--Schmidt gives scalar multiples of $P_0,P_1,P_2$.
::: proof
On $[-1,1]$,
\[
\|1\|_2^2=2,
\]
so
\[
e_0=\frac1{\sqrt2}=\frac1{\sqrt2}P_0.
\]
Since $x$ is odd,
\[
\langle x,e_0\rangle=0,
\]
and
\[
\|x\|_2^2=\frac23.
\]
Thus
\[
e_1=\sqrt{\frac32}\,x=\sqrt{\frac32}\,P_1.
\]

For $x^2$, the projection onto $e_1$ vanishes by parity, while
\[
\langle x^2,e_0\rangle e_0
=\frac13.
\]
Hence the orthogonalized quadratic is
\[
x^2-\frac13
=\frac13(3x^2-1)
=\frac23P_2.
\]
Its squared norm is
\[
\int_{-1}^1\left(x^2-\frac13\right)^2dx
=\frac8{45}.
\]
Therefore
\[
e_2
=\sqrt{\frac52}\,P_2.
\]
So the Gram--Schmidt orthonormal system consists of scalar multiples of the first three Legendre polynomials.
:::

<1>2. Find the best quadratic approximation to $x^3$.
::: proof
Let
\[
S=\operatorname{span}\{1,x,x^2\}.
\]
Because $x^3$ is odd, it is orthogonal to $1$ and $x^2$. Its projection onto $x$ is
\[
\frac{\langle x^3,x\rangle}{\|x\|_2^2}x
=\frac{2/5}{2/3}x
=\frac35x.
\]
Hence the closest polynomial of degree at most two is
\[
\frac35x,
\]
corresponding to
\[
a=0,\qquad b=\frac35,\qquad c=0.
\]
The residual is
\[
r(x)=x^3-\frac35x,
\]
and
\[
\begin{aligned}
\|r\|_2^2
&=\int_{-1}^1\left(x^3-\frac35x\right)^2dx\\
&=\frac27-\frac{12}{25}+\frac6{25}\\
&=\frac8{175}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Thus
\[
\boxed{
\min_{a,b,c}\int_{-1}^1|x^3-a-bx-cx^2|^2dx
=\frac8{175}.}
\]
:::

<1>3. Solve the constrained maximization problem.
::: proof
The three moment constraints say exactly that
\[
g\in S^\perp,
\]
while the last condition says $\|g\|_2=1$. Since
\[
x^3=\frac35x+r
\]
with $r\in S^\perp$, every admissible $g$ satisfies
\[
\int_{-1}^1x^3g(x)\,dx
=\langle r,g\rangle.
\]
By Cauchy--Schwarz,
\[
\langle r,g\rangle\le\|r\|_2\|g\|_2
=\sqrt{\frac8{175}}.
\]
Equality is attained for
\[
g=\frac{r}{\|r\|_2}.
\]
Therefore
\[
\boxed{
\max\int_{-1}^1x^3g(x)\,dx
=\sqrt{\frac8{175}}.}
\]
:::
