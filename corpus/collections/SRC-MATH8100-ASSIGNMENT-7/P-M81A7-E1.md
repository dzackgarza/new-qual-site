::: {.problem}
Prove that every nonempty closed convex subset of a Hilbert space has a unique element of minimum norm.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $K$ be a nonempty closed convex subset of a Hilbert space $H$, and set
$$
d=\inf_{x\in K}\norm{x}.
$$

::: pf

::: pf-step
A minimizing sequence is Cauchy.

::: pf-proof
Choose $x_n\in K$ with
$$
\norm{x_n}\longrightarrow d.
$$
Since $K$ is convex,
$$
\frac{x_n+x_m}{2}\in K,
$$
so
$$
\norm{\frac{x_n+x_m}{2}}\ge d.
$$
The parallelogram identity gives
$$
\norm{x_n-x_m}^2
=2\norm{x_n}^2+2\norm{x_m}^2
-4\norm{\frac{x_n+x_m}{2}}^2.
$$
Therefore
$$
\norm{x_n-x_m}^2
\le2\norm{x_n}^2+2\norm{x_m}^2-4d^2.
$$
The right-hand side tends to $0$ as $m,n\to\infty$, so $(x_n)$ is Cauchy.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
The minimum is attained.

::: pf-proof
Because $H$ is complete, $x_n\to x$ for some $x\in H$. Since $K$ is closed,
$$
x\in K.
$$
Norm continuity gives
$$
\norm{x}=\lim_n\norm{x_n}=d.
$$
Thus $x$ has minimum norm in $K$.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
The minimizer is unique.

::: pf-proof
Suppose $x,y\in K$ both satisfy
$$
\norm{x}=\norm{y}=d.
$$
Again $(x+y)/2\in K$, so
$$
\norm{\frac{x+y}{2}}\ge d.
$$
Applying the parallelogram identity,
$$
\norm{x-y}^2
=4d^2-4\norm{\frac{x+y}{2}}^2
\le0.
$$
Hence $x=y$.
:::
