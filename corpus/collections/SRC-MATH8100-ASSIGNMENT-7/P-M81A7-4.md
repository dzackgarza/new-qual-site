::: problem
Verify the orthogonality of the real and complex trigonometric systems in $L^2([0,1])$.
Then prove the Riemann-Lebesgue lemma for $f\in L^1([0,1])$ by writing $f=g+h$ with $g\in L^2$ and $\|h\|_1$ arbitrarily small.
:::
