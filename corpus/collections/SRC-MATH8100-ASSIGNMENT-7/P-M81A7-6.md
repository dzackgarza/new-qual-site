::: problem
Let $\mathcal C=\{f\in L^2([0,1]):\int f=1,\ \int xf=2\}$.
Show that $18x-8\in\mathcal C$ and express $\mathcal C$ as its translate by $\operatorname{span}\{1,x\}^\perp$.
Find the element of $\mathcal C$ with minimum $L^2$ norm.
:::
