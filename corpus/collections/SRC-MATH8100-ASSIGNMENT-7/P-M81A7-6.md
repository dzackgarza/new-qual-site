::: problem
Let $\mathcal C=\{f\in L^2([0,1]):\int f=1,\ \int xf=2\}$.
Show that $g(x)=18x^2-5$ belongs to $\mathcal C$ and express $\mathcal C$ as its translate by $\operatorname{span}\{1,x\}^\perp$.
Find the element of $\mathcal C$ with minimum $L^2$ norm.
:::

::: solution
Let
\[
S=\operatorname{span}\{1,x\}\subset L^2([0,1]).
\]

<1>1. Verify that $g(x)=18x^2-5$ belongs to $\mathcal C$ and identify the affine space.
::: proof
We compute
\[
\int_0^1g(x)\,dx
=18\int_0^1x^2\,dx-5
=6-5
=1,
\]
and
\[
\int_0^1xg(x)\,dx
=18\int_0^1x^3\,dx-5\int_0^1x\,dx
=\frac{18}{4}-\frac52
=2.
\]
Thus $g\in\mathcal C$.

For $f\in L^2([0,1])$,
\[
f\in\mathcal C
\iff
\langle f-g,1\rangle=0
\text{ and }
\langle f-g,x\rangle=0.
\]
The two conditions are exactly
\[
f-g\in S^\perp.
\]
Therefore
\[
\boxed{\mathcal C=g+S^\perp.}
\]
:::

<1>2. Find the minimum-norm element of $\mathcal C$.
::: proof
Write the orthogonal decomposition
\[
g=P_Sg+P_{S^\perp}g.
\]
Then
\[
\mathcal C
=g+S^\perp
=P_Sg+S^\perp.
\]
Hence every $f\in\mathcal C$ has the unique form
\[
f=P_Sg+h,
\qquad h\in S^\perp,
\]
and therefore
\[
\|f\|_2^2
=\|P_Sg\|_2^2+\|h\|_2^2.
\]
The unique minimum is attained at $h=0$, so the minimizer is the unique element of $S$ satisfying the two moment constraints.

Write
\[
f_0(x)=a+bx.
\]
The constraints give
\[
a+\frac b2=1,
\qquad
\frac a2+\frac b3=2.
\]
Solving yields
\[
b=18,
\qquad
a=-8.
\]
Thus
\[
\boxed{f_0(x)=18x-8.}
\]
Its squared norm is
\[
\int_0^1(18x-8)^2\,dx
=28.
\]
:::
:::
