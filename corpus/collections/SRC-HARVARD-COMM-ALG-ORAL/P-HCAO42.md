::: problem
Let $\mathfrak a_1,\ldots,\mathfrak a_r$ be ideals of a commutative ring $A$ such that
$\mathfrak a_i+\mathfrak a_j=(1)$ whenever $i\ne j$.
Show that
\[
\prod_{i=1}^r \mathfrak a_i=\bigcap_{i=1}^r \mathfrak a_i.
\]
:::
