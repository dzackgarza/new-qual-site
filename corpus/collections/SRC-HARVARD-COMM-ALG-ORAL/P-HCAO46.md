::: problem
Let $M$ be a finitely generated $A$-module, and let $x_1,\ldots,x_n\in M$.
Suppose that $x_1,\ldots,x_n$ generate $M_{\mathfrak m}$ for every maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$ of $A$.
Show that they generate $M$.
:::
