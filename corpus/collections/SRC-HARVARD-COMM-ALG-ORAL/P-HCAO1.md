::: problem
Let $A$ be a commutative ring, let $M$ be a finitely generated $A$-module, and let $x_1, \ldots, x_n \in M$.

Suppose that the images of $x_1, \ldots, x_n$ generate $M/\mathfrak mM$ for every maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$ of $A$.
Show that $x_1, \ldots, x_n$ generate $M$.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. Define the submodule $N$ and quotient module $Q$:
<2>1. Let $N = A x_1 + \cdots + A x_n \subseteq M$ be the $A$-submodule of $M$ generated by $x_1, \dots, x_n$.
Proof: definition of submodule.
<2>2. Define the quotient module $Q = M / N$.
Since $M$ is finitely generated as an $A$-module, $Q$ is also a finitely generated $A$-module.
Proof: homomorphic image of a finitely generated module is finitely generated.

<1>2. Show that $Q = \mathfrak{m}Q$ for every maximal ideal $\mathfrak{m} \subset A$:
<2>1. Let $\mathfrak{m} \subset A$ be an arbitrary maximal ideal of $A$.
Proof: setup.
<2>2. By hypothesis, the images of $x_1, \dots, x_n$ generate $M / \mathfrak{m}M$ as an $(A/\mathfrak{m})$-vector space.
Thus $M = N + \mathfrak{m}M$.
Proof: linear combinations modulo $\mathfrak{m}M$.
<2>3. Projecting the equality $M = N + \mathfrak{m}M$ into the quotient $Q = M/N$:
\[
Q = (N + \mathfrak{m}M)/N = \mathfrak{m}(M/N) = \mathfrak{m}Q.
\]
Proof: quotient module arithmetic.

<1>3. Apply Nakayama’s Lemma locally at each maximal ideal:
<2>1. Consider the localization $A_\mathfrak{m}$ and the localized module $Q_\mathfrak{m}$.
Since $Q$ is finitely generated over $A$, $Q_\mathfrak{m}$ is a finitely generated module over the local ring $(A_\mathfrak{m}, \mathfrak{m}A_\mathfrak{m})$.
Proof: localization preserves finite generation.
<2>2. Localizing the identity $Q = \mathfrak{m}Q$ at $\mathfrak{m}$ gives:
\[
Q_\mathfrak{m} = (\mathfrak{m}Q)_\mathfrak{m} = (\mathfrak{m}A_\mathfrak{m}) Q_\mathfrak{m}.
\]
Proof: localization commutes with module products.
<2>3. Since $\mathfrak{m}A_\mathfrak{m}$ is the unique maximal ideal (the Jacobson radical) of $A_\mathfrak{m}$, Nakayama’s Lemma implies:
\[
Q_\mathfrak{m} = 0.
\]
Proof: Nakayama's Lemma for finitely generated modules over local rings.

<1>4. Local-to-global principle:
<2>1. Since $Q_\mathfrak{m} = 0$ for every maximal ideal $\mathfrak{m}$ of $A$, the module $Q = 0$.
Proof: if $Q \neq 0$, the annihilator $\operatorname{Ann}_A(Q)$ is a proper ideal contained in some maximal ideal $\mathfrak{m}$, forcing $Q_\mathfrak{m} \neq 0$.
<2>2. Since $Q = M/N = 0$, we have $N = M$.
Thus $x_1, \dots, x_n$ generate $M$ as an $A$-module.
Proof: $M/N = 0 \iff N = M$.

<1>5. Conclusion:
$x_1, \dots, x_n$ generate $M$. Q.E.D.
Proof: <1>1 through <1>4.
:::
