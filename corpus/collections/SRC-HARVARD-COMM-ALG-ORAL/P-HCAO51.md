::: problem
Let $A\subseteq B$ be rings, with $B$ integral over $A$.
Let $\mathfrak q$ be a prime ideal of $B$, and put $\mathfrak p=\mathfrak q\cap A$.
Show that $\mathfrak q$ is maximal in $B$ if and only if $\mathfrak p$ is maximal in $A$.
:::
