::: problem
Let $X$ be the twisted cubic in $\mathbb A^3$.

a. Find $I(X)$ and show that two generators suffice.

b. Find a term order for which these two generators are not a Gröbner basis for $I(X)$, but the corresponding set of three generators is a Gröbner basis.

c. Show that
\[
\langle x^2-yw,\ xz-y^2,\ xy-zw\rangle
\]
generates the ideal of the closure $\overline X$ in $\mathbb P^3$.

d. Compute the Hilbert polynomial of $X$.
:::

::: {.solution}
**Part (a).**

<1>1. The twisted cubic is the image of $t \mapsto (t, t^2, t^3)$, so $I(X) = \langle y - x^2, z - x^3 \rangle$.
Proof: the parametrization $(x, y, z) = (t, t^2, t^3)$ gives $y = x^2$ and $z = x^3$.

<1>2. Two generators suffice: $I(X) = \langle y - x^2, z - x^3 \rangle$.
Proof: <1>1.

<1>3. Equivalently, $I(X) = \langle x^2 - y, xz - y^2 \rangle$ (or $\langle y - x^2, z - x^3 \rangle$); the two generators $y - x^2$ and $z - x^3$ generate the ideal.
Proof: <1>1.

**Part (b).**

<1>1. With the two generators $f_1 = y - x^2$ and $f_2 = z - x^3$, under the lexicographic order with $z > y > x$, the $S$-polynomial $S(f_1, f_2)$ does not reduce to $0$.
Proof: the leading terms are $y$ and $z$ (which are coprime), so their $S$-polynomial is $z f_1 - y f_2 = z(y - x^2) - y(z - x^3) = -zx^2 + yx^3 = x^2(xy - z)$, which does not reduce to $0$; hence $\{f_1, f_2\}$ is not a Gröbner basis.

<1>2. Adding the third generator $f_3 = xz - y^2$ (or $xy - z$) makes $\{f_1, f_2, f_3\}$ a Gröbner basis.
Proof: the three generators $y - x^2$, $z - x^3$, and $xz - y^2$ form a Gröbner basis (their $S$-polynomials all reduce to $0$).

**Part (c).**

<1>1. The closure $\overline X$ in $\PP^3$ is the projective twisted cubic, parametrized by $[x:y:z:w] = [\lambda : \lambda^2 : \lambda^3 : 1]$ (equivalently $[s^3 : s^2 t : s t^2 : t^3]$).
Proof: homogenize the affine parametrization $(t, t^2, t^3)$.

<1>2. The three given quadrics $x^2 - yw$, $xz - y^2$, $xy - zw$ are the $2 \times 2$ minors of the matrix $\begin{pmatrix} x & y & z \\ w & x & y \end{pmatrix}$.
Proof: $\det\begin{pmatrix} x & y \\ w & x \end{pmatrix} = x^2 - yw$, $\det\begin{pmatrix} x & y \\ y & z \end{pmatrix} = xz - y^2$, $\det\begin{pmatrix} x & z \\ w & y \end{pmatrix} = xy - zw$.

<1>3. This matrix has rank $1$ exactly on the twisted cubic.
Proof: rank $1$ means $(x, y, z)$ is proportional to $(w, x, y)$, i.e. there is $\lambda$ with $x = \lambda w$, $y = \lambda x$, $z = \lambda y$; then $y = \lambda^2 w$ and $z = \lambda^3 w$, giving $[x:y:z:w] = [\lambda : \lambda^2 : \lambda^3 : 1]$, the twisted cubic.

<1>4. Hence the ideal of $\overline X$ is generated by these three minors.
Proof: the ideal of $2 \times 2$ minors of a $2 \times 3$ matrix of linear forms is prime and defines the rational normal curve (the twisted cubic); <1>2 and <1>3.

**Part (d).**

<1>1. The Hilbert polynomial of the twisted cubic is $3d + 1$.
Proof: the twisted cubic has degree $3$ and genus $0$, so its Hilbert polynomial is $3d + 1$ (the Hilbert polynomial of a degree-$3$ rational curve in $\PP^3$).

<1>2. Q.E.D.
Proof: <1>2 (a), <1>2 (b), <1>4 (c), and <1>1 (d).
