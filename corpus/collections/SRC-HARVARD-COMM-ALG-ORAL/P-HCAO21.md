::: problem
Let $R$ be a zero-dimensional Gorenstein $k$-algebra.
What can be said about the $R$-module $\operatorname{Hom}_k(R,k)$?
What is
\[
\operatorname{Hom}_R\!\left(k,\operatorname{Hom}_k(R,k)\right)?
\]
:::
