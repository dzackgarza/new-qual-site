::: problem
Let $F$ be a field, let $E/F$ be a field extension, and let $p(x) \in F[x]$.
If $a \in E$ and $p(a)=0$, show that
\[
p(x)=(x-a)q(x)
\]
for some $q(x) \in E[x]$.
:::
