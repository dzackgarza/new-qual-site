::: problem
Let $M$ be an $A$-module, and let $\mathfrak m$ be a maximal ideal of $A$.
Prove that
\[
M/\mathfrak mM \cong M_{\mathfrak m}/\mathfrak mM_{\mathfrak m}.
\]
:::
