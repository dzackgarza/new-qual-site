::: problem
Give an ideal $I=\langle f_1,\ldots,f_t\rangle$ and a term order such that
\[
\operatorname{in}(I)\ne
\langle \operatorname{in}(f_1),\ldots,\operatorname{in}(f_t)\rangle.
\]
:::
