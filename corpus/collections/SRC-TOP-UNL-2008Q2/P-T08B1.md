::: {.problem}
Let $X$ be the triangular parachute formed from the standard 2-simplex $\Delta^2$ by identifying the three vertices with one another.

(a) Compute a presentation for $\pi_1(X)$.

(b) Show that $\pi_1(X)$ is isomorphic to a free group $F_n$ for some $n$; what is $n$?
:::
