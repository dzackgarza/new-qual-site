::: {.problem}
Let $Y$ be a $\Delta$-complex.

(a) Prove that if $H_4(Y)\ne 0$, then $Y$ must have a simplex of dimension 4.

(b) Prove that if $H_4(Y)=\mathbb Z/7\mathbb Z$, then $Y$ also must have a simplex of dimension 5.
:::


::: {.solution}
Let
\[
\cdots\longrightarrow C_5(Y)
\overset{\partial_5}{\longrightarrow}
C_4(Y)
\overset{\partial_4}{\longrightarrow}
C_3(Y)
\longrightarrow\cdots
\]
be the simplicial chain complex of the $\Delta$-complex $Y$.

<1>1. If $Y$ has no $4$-simplices, then
\[
H_4(Y)=0.
\]
::: {.proof}
The simplicial chain group $C_4(Y)$ is the free abelian group with basis the $4$-simplices of $Y$.
If there are no such simplices, then
\[
C_4(Y)=0.
\]
Therefore
\[
\ker\partial_4=0,
\]
and hence
\[
H_4(Y)
=
\ker\partial_4/\operatorname{im}\partial_5
=0.
\]
:::

<1>2. Thus, if
\[
H_4(Y)\ne0,
\]
then $Y$ has a simplex of dimension $4$.
::: {.proof}
This is the contrapositive of <1>1, proving part (a).
:::

<1>3. Suppose $Y$ has no $5$-simplices.
Then $H_4(Y)$ is a subgroup of the free abelian group $C_4(Y)$ and is therefore torsion-free.
::: {.proof}
If there are no $5$-simplices, then
\[
C_5(Y)=0,
\]
so
\[
\operatorname{im}\partial_5=0.
\]
Consequently
\[
H_4(Y)
=
\ker\partial_4.
\]
The group $C_4(Y)$ is free abelian on the $4$-simplices, and
\[
\ker\partial_4\le C_4(Y).
\]
Every subgroup of a free abelian group is torsion-free: if $0\ne m\in\mathbb Z$ and $mx=0$ in a subgroup, then the same equation holds in the ambient free abelian group, forcing $x=0$.
Hence $H_4(Y)$ is torsion-free.
:::

<1>4. If
\[
H_4(Y)\cong\mathbb Z/7\mathbb Z,
\]
then $Y$ has a simplex of dimension $5$.
::: {.proof}
The group $\mathbb Z/7\mathbb Z$ contains nonzero torsion: every nonzero class $x$ satisfies
\[
7x=0.
\]
This contradicts the torsion-freeness in <1>3 if $Y$ has no $5$-simplices.
Therefore a $5$-simplex must exist, proving part (b).
:::
:::
