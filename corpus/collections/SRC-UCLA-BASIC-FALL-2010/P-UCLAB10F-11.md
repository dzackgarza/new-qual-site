::: {.problem}
Problem 11. Find the function $g$ that minimizes
\[
\int_0^1 |f'(x)|^2\,dx
\]
among smooth functions $f:[0,1]\to\mathbb R$ satisfying $f(0)=0$ and $f(1)=1$.
Is the minimizing function unique?
