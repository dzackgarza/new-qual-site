::: {.problem}
Problem 7. Let $V$ and $W$ be complex inner-product spaces with
\[
\dim V\le\dim W<\infty.
\]
Prove that there is a linear map $T:V\to W$ such that
\[
\langle T(v),T(v')\rangle_W=\langle v,v'\rangle_V
\]
for all $v,v'\in V$.
:::
