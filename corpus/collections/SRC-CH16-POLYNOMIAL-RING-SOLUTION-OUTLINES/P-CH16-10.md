::: {.problem}
Let R be a commutative ring.
Show that $R [ x ]$ has a subring isomorphic to R.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let R be a commutative ring and consider $R [ x ]$ . Define $\phi : R \to R [ x ]$ by $r \mapsto r$ . Clearly φ is one-to-one and a homomorphism.
Now, $\phi ( R )$ is a subring of $R [ x ]$ since it is the image of a homomorphism.
Then $\phi ( R )$ is a subring of $R [ x ]$ isomorphic to R.
:::
