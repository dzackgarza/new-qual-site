::: {.problem}
Are there any nonconstant polynomials in $\ZZ[x]$ that have multiplicative inverses?
Explain your answers.
:::

::: {.solution}
No. Suppose that $f ( x )$ has a multiplicative inverse $g ( x )$ in $\ZZ[x]$. Since $\ZZ$ is an integral domain, the degree rule gives $\deg f + \deg g = \deg ( f g ) = \deg 1 = 0$, so $\deg f = \deg g = 0$. Hence $f ( x )$ is constant, and the units of $\ZZ[x]$ are the constants $\pm 1$.
:::
