::: {.problem}
List all the polynomials of degree 2 in $\ZZ_{2}[x]$. Which of these are equal as functions from $\ZZ_{2}$ to $\ZZ_{2}$?
:::

::: {.solution}

The argument is the card's own: the four polynomials are listed by their coefficients, and their value tables at $0$ and $1$ separate them.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #list}

The polynomials of degree $2$ in $\ZZ_{2}[x]$ are $x^{2}$, $x^{2}+1$, $x^{2}+x$, $x^{2}+x+1$.

::: pf-proof

A polynomial of degree $2$ over $\ZZ_{2}$ is $a_{2}x^{2}+a_{1}x+a_{0}$ with $a_{2}=1$ (the leading coefficient must be nonzero, and $1$ is the only nonzero element of $\ZZ_{2}$) and $a_{1},a_{0}\in\{0,1\}$. The four choices of $(a_{1},a_{0})$ give exactly the four displayed polynomials.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #tables}

The value functions on $\ZZ_{2}=\{0,1\}$ are: $x^{2}\mapsto(0,1)$, $x^{2}+1\mapsto(1,0)$, $x^{2}+x\mapsto(0,0)$, $x^{2}+x+1\mapsto(1,1)$.

::: pf-proof

Substituting $0$ and $1$ and computing in $\ZZ_{2}$: $0^{2}=0$, $1^{2}=1$, and $1+1=0$. So $x^{2}$ gives $(0,1)$; adding $1$ complements both entries, giving $(1,0)$; $x^{2}+x$ gives $(0,1)+(0,1)=(0,0)$; and adding $1$ gives $(1,1)$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #distinct}

No two of the four agree as functions $\ZZ_{2}\to\ZZ_{2}$.

::: pf-proof

Two functions on the two-element set $\ZZ_{2}$ are equal exactly when they agree at $0$ and at $1$. By step [](#tables){.pf-ref} the four value pairs $(0,1),(1,0),(0,0),(1,1)$ are pairwise different, so the four polynomials are pairwise different as functions:
$$
\boxed{x^{2},\ x^{2}+1,\ x^{2}+x,\ x^{2}+x+1\ \text{are pairwise distinct as functions on } \ZZ_{2}.}
$$

:::
