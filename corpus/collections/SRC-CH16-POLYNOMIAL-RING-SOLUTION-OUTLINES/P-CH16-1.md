::: {.problem}
Let $f(x) = 4x^{3} + 2x^{2} + x + 3$ and $g(x) = 3x^{4} + 3x^{3} + 3x^{2} + x + 4$, where $f(x), g(x) \in \ZZ_{5}[x]$. Compute $f(x) + g(x)$ and $f(x) \cdot g(x)$.
:::

::: {.solution}
The collection retains its solution outline as `assets/attachments/Ch16Sltns.pdf`, whose entry "# 1" is this exercise. In the seventh edition the exercise is printed as **Chapter 16 Exercise 1 on p. 300**, where the printed words are the card's statement; the outline and the book number it the same. The card recomputes all eight convolution coefficients rather than only the one the outline displays, and the wording below is its own. Write $f=\sum_{i=0}^{3}a_ix^i$ with $(a_0,a_1,a_2,a_3)=(3,1,2,4)$ and $g=\sum_{j=0}^{4}b_jx^j$ with $(b_0,b_1,b_2,b_3,b_4)=(4,1,3,3,3)$, all coefficients in $\ZZ_5$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #sum}
$f(x)+g(x)=3x^4+2x^3+2x+2$.

::: pf-proof
Addition in a polynomial ring is coefficient-wise, and the coefficients are added in $\ZZ_5$: the coefficient of $x^4$ is $3$; of $x^3$ is $4+3=7\equiv2$; of $x^2$ is $2+3=5\equiv0$; of $x$ is $1+1=2$; and the constant coefficient is $3+4=7\equiv2$. The $x^2$ term vanishes.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #product}
$f(x)g(x)=2x^7+3x^6+x^5+2x^4+3x^2+2x+2$, the coefficient of $x^3$ vanishing.

::: pf-proof
The coefficient of $x^k$ in $fg$ is $\sum_{i+j=k}a_ib_j$ reduced modulo $5$. With the displayed coefficients, and sums before reduction,
$$
\begin{aligned}
c_0&=a_0b_0=3\cdot4=12\equiv2, & c_1&=a_0b_1+a_1b_0=3+4=7\equiv2,\\
c_2&=a_0b_2+a_1b_1+a_2b_0=9+1+8=18\equiv3, & c_3&=a_0b_3+a_1b_2+a_2b_1+a_3b_0=9+3+2+16=30\equiv0,\\
c_4&=a_0b_4+a_1b_3+a_2b_2+a_3b_1=9+3+6+4=22\equiv2, & c_5&=a_1b_4+a_2b_3+a_3b_2=3+6+12=21\equiv1,\\
c_6&=a_2b_4+a_3b_3=6+12=18\equiv3, & c_7&=a_3b_4=4\cdot3=12\equiv2,
\end{aligned}
$$
and $c_k=0$ for $k>7$ because no such $i+j=k$ occurs. Hence the product has the stated coefficients, vanishing in degree $3$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step}
$\boxed{f(x)+g(x)=3x^4+2x^3+2x+2,\qquad f(x)g(x)=2x^7+3x^6+x^5+2x^4+3x^2+2x+2}$ in $\ZZ_5[x]$.

::: pf-proof
The two displays are steps [](#sum){.pf-ref} and [](#product){.pf-ref}. Both computations are additions and multiplications of the representatives $0,\ldots,4$ followed by reduction modulo $5$, which is how sums and products are computed in $\ZZ_5[x]$.
:::
