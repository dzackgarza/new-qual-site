::: problem
Let $f$ be a Riemann integrable function on a closed interval $[a, b]$.
For $x \in [a, b]$, let $g(x) = \int_a^x f(t)\,dt$.
Prove that $g$ is continuous.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. Since $f$ is Riemann integrable on $[a,b]$, it is bounded: there is $M$ with $|f(t)| \le M$ for all $t \in [a,b]$.
Proof: Riemann integrable functions are bounded.

<1>2. For $x, y \in [a,b]$,
$$|g(x) - g(y)| = \left| \int_a^x f - \int_a^y f \right| = \left| \int_y^x f(t)\,dt \right| \le M |x - y|.$$
Proof: <1>1 and the triangle inequality for integrals.

<1>3. Hence $g$ is Lipschitz continuous (with constant $M$).
Proof: <1>2.

<1>4. Therefore $g$ is continuous.
Proof: <1>3 (Lipschitz implies continuous).

<1>5. Q.E.D.
Proof: <1>4.
:::
