::: {.problem}
Let $X=\prod_{n\in\mathbb Z}[0,1]=\{(x_n)_{n\in\mathbb Z}:x_n\in[0,1]\}$, with each $[0,1]$ having the usual Euclidean topology and $X$ having the product topology, and let $$A=\{(x_n)\in X:\text{for some }N\text{ we have }x_n=0\text{ for all }n\ge N\}.$$

(a) Show that $A$ is a dense subset of $X$, and is therefore not a closed subset of $X$.

(b) Show that, as a consequence, $A$ is not a compact subset of $X$.
:::
