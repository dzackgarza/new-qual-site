::: {.problem}
Find the (simplicial) homology groups of the space $X$ obtained from the 3-simplex $\Delta^3=[v_0,v_1,v_2,v_3]$ by gluing the face $[v_0,v_1,v_2]$ to the face $[v_0,v_2,v_3]$ and the face $[v_0,v_1,v_3]$ to the face $[v_1,v_2,v_3]$ (respecting the ordering of vertices as written, i.e., the first gluing map sends $v_1$ to $v_2$, and so on).
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #delta-structure}
$X$ is a $\Delta$-complex with one vertex $v$, one edge $e$, two $2$-simplices $a=[v_0,v_1,v_2]=[v_0,v_2,v_3]$ and $b=[v_0,v_1,v_3]=[v_1,v_2,v_3]$, and one $3$-simplex $\sigma=[v_0,v_1,v_2,v_3]$.

::: pf-proof
The first gluing sends $v_0\mapsto v_0$, $v_1\mapsto v_2$, $v_2\mapsto v_3$, so $v_1\sim v_2\sim v_3$, and on edges $[v_0,v_1]\sim[v_0,v_2]$, $[v_1,v_2]\sim[v_2,v_3]$, $[v_0,v_2]\sim[v_0,v_3]$.
The second sends $v_0\mapsto v_1$, $v_1\mapsto v_2$, $v_3\mapsto v_3$, so $v_0\sim v_1$, and on edges $[v_0,v_1]\sim[v_1,v_2]$, $[v_0,v_3]\sim[v_1,v_3]$, $[v_1,v_3]\sim[v_2,v_3]$.
Chaining these, all four vertices are identified, and $[v_0,v_1]\sim[v_0,v_2]\sim[v_1,v_2]\sim[v_2,v_3]\sim[v_1,v_3]\sim[v_0,v_3]$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #chain-complex}
The simplicial chain complex is $0\to\ZZ\sigma\xrightarrow{d_3}\ZZ a\oplus\ZZ b\xrightarrow{d_2}\ZZ e\xrightarrow{d_1}\ZZ v\to0$ with $d_3(\sigma)=2b-2a$, $d_2(a)=d_2(b)=e$, and $d_1=0$.

::: pf-proof
By step [](#delta-structure){.pf-ref},
$d_3\sigma=[v_1,v_2,v_3]-[v_0,v_2,v_3]+[v_0,v_1,v_3]-[v_0,v_1,v_2]=b-a+b-a$;
$d_2[v_0,v_1,v_2]=[v_1,v_2]-[v_0,v_2]+[v_0,v_1]=e-e+e=e$, and likewise $d_2[v_0,v_1,v_3]=e$;
and $d_1e=v-v=0$.
:::

:::

::: pf-qed
By step [](#chain-complex){.pf-ref}:
$H_0(X)=\ZZ v\cong\ZZ$;
$H_1(X)=\ZZ e/\ZZ e=0$;
$\ker d_2=\{ma+nb:m+n=0\}=\ZZ(a-b)$ and $\operatorname{im}d_3=\ZZ\cdot2(a-b)$, so $H_2(X)\cong\ZZ/2$;
and $d_3$ is injective, so $H_3(X)=0$.
Hence $H_0\cong\ZZ$, $H_1=0$, $H_2\cong\ZZ/2$, $H_3=0$, and $H_k=0$ for $k\ge4$.
:::
