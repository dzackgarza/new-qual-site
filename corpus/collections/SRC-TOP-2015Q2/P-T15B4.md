::: {.problem}
Compute the (reduced) singular homology groups of the space $X=S^1\times(S^1\vee S^1)$, which can be thought of as two copies of $S^1\times S^1$ glued together along their copies of $S^1\times\{x_0\}$.
You may use your knowledge of the homology groups of $T^2=S^1\times S^1$ in your calculations.
:::

::: {.solution}
Write $X=A\cup B$, where $A$ and $B$ are the two copies of $T^2=S^1\times S^1$ and $A\cap B=C=S^1\times\{x_0\}\cong S^1$.
Let $N$ be a small open neighborhood of $x_0$ in $S^1\vee S^1$ that deformation retracts onto $x_0$.
The open sets $A\cup(S^1\times N)$ and $B\cup(S^1\times N)$ deformation retract onto $A$ and $B$, with intersection $S^1\times N$ deformation retracting onto $C$, so the Mayer--Vietoris sequence applies to $A$, $B$, $C$.
Choose bases $(a,b)$ of $H_1(A)$ and $(a',b')$ of $H_1(B)$ with $a$ and $a'$ the classes of $C$, and let $c$ generate $H_1(C)$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #phi-injective}
The Mayer--Vietoris map $\Phi=(i_*,-j_*)\colon H_1(C)\to H_1(A)\oplus H_1(B)\cong\ZZ^4$ is injective, with image the direct summand generated by $(a,0,-a',0)$.

::: pf-proof
$\Phi(c)=(a,0,-a',0)$, which is a primitive vector of $\ZZ^4$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #h2-computation}
$H_2(X)\cong\ZZ^2$.

::: pf-proof
The Mayer--Vietoris sequence contains the exact segment $0=H_2(C)\to H_2(A)\oplus H_2(B)\cong\ZZ^2\to H_2(X)\to H_1(C)\xrightarrow{\Phi}\ZZ^4$.
By step [](#phi-injective){.pf-ref}, $\Phi$ is injective, so $\ZZ^2\to H_2(X)$ is an isomorphism.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #h1-computation}
$H_1(X)\cong\ZZ^3$.

::: pf-proof
The next segment is $H_1(C)\xrightarrow{\Phi}H_1(A)\oplus H_1(B)\to H_1(X)\to H_0(C)\to H_0(A)\oplus H_0(B)$.
$A$, $B$, $C$ are connected, so the last map is $\ZZ\to\ZZ^2$, $1\mapsto(1,-1)$, which is injective.
By exactness and step [](#phi-injective){.pf-ref}, $H_1(X)\cong\ZZ^4/\langle(1,0,-1,0)\rangle\cong\ZZ^3$.
:::

:::

::: pf-qed
$X$ is the union of the connected tori $A$ and $B$ with nonempty intersection, so it is connected and $\widetilde H_0(X)=0$.
For $k\ge3$ the Mayer--Vietoris groups on both sides of $H_k(X)$ vanish, because tori have no homology above degree $2$ and the circle none above degree $1$.
With steps [](#h2-computation){.pf-ref} and [](#h1-computation){.pf-ref},
$$\widetilde H_k(X)\cong\begin{cases}\ZZ^3,&k=1,\\\ZZ^2,&k=2,\\0,&\text{otherwise}.\end{cases}$$
:::
