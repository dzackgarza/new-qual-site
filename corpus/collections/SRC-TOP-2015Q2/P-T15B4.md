::: {.problem}
Compute the (reduced) singular homology groups of the space $X=S^1\times(S^1\vee S^1)$, which can be thought of as two copies of $S^1\times S^1$ glued together along their copies of $S^1\times\{x_0\}$.
You may use your knowledge of the homology groups of $T^2=S^1\times S^1$ in your calculations.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. Write
$$
X=A\cup B,
$$
where $A$ and $B$ are the two copies of $T^2=S^1\times S^1$ and
$$
A\cap B=C=S^1\times\{x_0\}\cong S^1.
$$
::: {.proof}
This is exactly the decomposition given in the problem. Replace the subspaces by small open neighborhoods deformation-retracting onto them if desired, so that Mayer--Vietoris applies without changing homology.
:::

<1>2. The relevant homology groups are
$$
H_2(A)\oplus H_2(B)\cong\mathbb Z^2,
\quad
H_1(C)\cong\mathbb Z,
\quad
H_1(A)\oplus H_1(B)\cong\mathbb Z^4.
$$
::: {.proof}
Each torus has $H_2\cong\mathbb Z$ and $H_1\cong\mathbb Z^2$, while the circle has $H_1\cong\mathbb Z$ and no homology above degree $1$.
:::

<1>3. The Mayer--Vietoris map
$$
H_1(C)\longrightarrow H_1(A)\oplus H_1(B)
$$
is injective.
::: {.proof}
Choose bases $(a,b)$ for $H_1(A)$ and $(a',b')$ for $H_1(B)$ so that $C$ is the first $S^1$ factor in each torus. Under the Mayer--Vietoris map $(i_*,-j_*)$, a generator $c$ of $H_1(C)$ maps to
$$
(a,0,-a',0),
$$
which is nonzero and generates a direct summand of $\mathbb Z^4$.
:::

<1>4. We have
$$
H_2(X)\cong\mathbb Z^2.
$$
::: {.proof}
The Mayer--Vietoris sequence contains
$$
0=H_2(C)\longrightarrow \mathbb Z^2
\longrightarrow H_2(X)
\longrightarrow H_1(C)\longrightarrow\mathbb Z^4.
$$
By <1>3, the last map is injective, so exactness makes $\mathbb Z^2\to H_2(X)$ both injective and surjective.
:::

<1>5. We have
$$
H_1(X)\cong\mathbb Z^3.
$$
::: {.proof}
The next part of Mayer--Vietoris is
$$
H_1(C)\xrightarrow{(i_*,-j_*)}
H_1(A)\oplus H_1(B)
\longrightarrow H_1(X)
\longrightarrow H_0(C)
\xrightarrow{(i_*,-j_*)}
H_0(A)\oplus H_0(B).
$$
All three spaces $A,B,C$ are connected, so the last map is $\mathbb Z\to\mathbb Z^2$, $1\mapsto(1,-1)$, hence injective. Exactness therefore gives
$$
H_1(X)\cong \mathbb Z^4/\langle(1,0,-1,0)\rangle\cong\mathbb Z^3.
$$
:::

<1>6. The space $X$ is connected, so $H_0(X)\cong\mathbb Z$ and $\widetilde H_0(X)=0$.
::: {.proof}
It is a union of the connected tori $A$ and $B$ with nonempty connected intersection $C$.
:::

<1>7. All homology groups in degrees $k\ge3$ vanish.
::: {.proof}
For $k\ge3$, Mayer--Vietoris has zero groups on both sides because the tori have no homology above degree $2$ and the circle has none above degree $1$.
:::

<1>8. Hence
$$
\widetilde H_k(X)\cong
\begin{cases}
\mathbb Z^3,&k=1,\\
\mathbb Z^2,&k=2,\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
$$
::: {.proof}
Combine <1>4--<1>7.
:::
:::
