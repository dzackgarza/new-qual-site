::: {.problem}
Show, using covering space theory, that a finitely presented group $G$ has only finitely many distinct subgroups $H\le G$ of index $4$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Choose a finite presentation $G=\langle g_1,\ldots,g_m\mid r_1,\ldots,r_s\rangle$, and let $X_G$ be its presentation complex: one $0$-cell $v$, one oriented $1$-cell for each $g_i$, and one $2$-cell for each $r_j$, attached along the loop spelling $r_j$.
By the Seifert--van Kampen theorem, $\pi_1(X_G,v)\cong G$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #subgroups-correspond-covers}
Subgroups $H\le G$ of index $4$ correspond bijectively to isomorphism classes of connected $4$-sheeted covering spaces $\widetilde X\to X_G$ with a chosen point $\tilde v$ over $v$.

::: pf-proof
$X_G$ is a connected CW complex, so the classification of covering spaces applies: the based covering $(\widetilde X,\tilde v)$ corresponds to the subgroup $p_*\pi_1(\widetilde X,\tilde v)$, whose index equals the number of sheets.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #cover-determined-by-permutations}
A connected $4$-sheeted covering with the fiber over $v$ labeled $\{1,2,3,4\}$ determines permutations $\rho(g_1),\ldots,\rho(g_m)\in S_4$ satisfying $r_j(\rho(g_1),\ldots,\rho(g_m))=1$ for every $j$, and it is determined by them up to isomorphism.

::: pf-proof
Lifting the loop $g_i$ from each point of the fiber ends at a point of the fiber; this defines the monodromy permutation $\rho(g_i)$.
The attaching loop of each $2$-cell lifts to closed loops, because the $2$-cell lifts, so each relator acts trivially.
Conversely, the permutations determine the covering of the $1$-skeleton, which extends uniquely over the $2$-cells when the relators act trivially.
:::

:::

::: pf-qed
There are at most $24^m$ choices of $(\rho(g_1),\ldots,\rho(g_m))\in S_4^m$, and for each, at most four choices of $\tilde v$ in the fiber.
By steps [](#subgroups-correspond-covers){.pf-ref} and [](#cover-determined-by-permutations){.pf-ref}, $G$ has at most $4\cdot24^m$ subgroups of index $4$.
:::
