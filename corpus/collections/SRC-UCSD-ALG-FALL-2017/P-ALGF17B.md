::: problem
Let $G$ be the following subgroup of $2 \times 2$ matrices over the complex numbers:
\[
G = \left\{
\pm\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix},\;
\pm\begin{pmatrix} i & 0 \\ 0 & -i \end{pmatrix},\;
\pm\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ -1 & 0 \end{pmatrix},\;
\pm\begin{pmatrix} 0 & i \\ i & 0 \end{pmatrix}
\right\}
\]
(You don't have to show this is a group).
Prove that $G$ has the following presentation
\[
\langle a, b \mid a^4 = e,\; a^2 = b^2,\; a^{-1}ba = b^{-1} \rangle.
\]
:::

::: {.solution}
Set
\[
A=\begin{pmatrix}i&0\\0&-i\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
B=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]

<1>1. The matrices $A$ and $B$ satisfy
\[
A^4=I,
\qquad
A^2=B^2,
\qquad
A^{-1}BA=B^{-1}.
\]
::: {.proof}
Direct multiplication gives
\[
A^2=-I,
\qquad
B^2=-I,
\]
and hence $A^4=I$ and $A^2=B^2$.
Also
\[
A^{-1}BA
=
\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}
=B^{-1}.
\]
:::

<1>2. The subgroup generated by $A$ and $B$ is the displayed group $G$.
::: {.proof}
The product
\[
AB=\begin{pmatrix}0&i\\ i&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Thus $\langle A,B\rangle$ contains
\[
\pm I,\qquad \pm A,\qquad \pm B,\qquad \pm AB,
\]
which are exactly the eight matrices displayed in the definition of $G$.
Therefore
\[
\langle A,B\rangle=G.
\]
:::

<1>3. Let
\[
K:=\langle a,b\mid a^4=e,\ a^2=b^2,\ a^{-1}ba=b^{-1}\rangle.
\]
Every element of $K$ has the form
\[
a^r b^\varepsilon,
\qquad
0\le r<4,
\qquad
\varepsilon\in\{0,1\}.
\]
::: {.proof}
From
\[
a^{-1}ba=b^{-1}
\]
we obtain
\[
ba=ab^{-1}.
\]
Hence every word in $a^{\pm1}$ and $b^{\pm1}$ can be rewritten with all powers of $a$ to the left of all powers of $b$.
The relation
\[
b^2=a^2
\]
then reduces the exponent of $b$ modulo $2$, while $a^4=e$ reduces the exponent of $a$ modulo $4$.
Thus every element has one of the eight asserted forms, and therefore
\[
|K|\le8.
\]
:::

<1>4. The assignment
\[
a\longmapsto A,
\qquad
b\longmapsto B
\]
induces an isomorphism $K\cong G$.
::: {.proof}
By <1>1, the matrices $A,B$ satisfy all defining relations of $K$, so the assignment induces a homomorphism
\[
\varphi:K\longrightarrow G.
\]
By <1>2, $A$ and $B$ generate $G$, so $\varphi$ is surjective.
Since $G$ consists of the eight distinct displayed matrices,
\[
|G|=8.
\]
Surjectivity therefore gives $|K|\ge8$, while <1>3 gives $|K|\le8$.
Hence $|K|=8$ and the surjective map $\varphi$ is injective as well.
Thus
\[
G\cong K
=\langle a,b\mid a^4=e,\ a^2=b^2,\ a^{-1}ba=b^{-1}\rangle.
\]
:::
