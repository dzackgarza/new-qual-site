::: problem
Let $G$ be a group of order $231 = (3)(7)(11)$.

(a) Show that $G$ is isomorphic to a semidirect product $\mathbb{Z}_{77} \rtimes \mathbb{Z}_3$.

(b) Show that there are precisely two groups $G$ of order $231$ up to isomorphism.
:::
