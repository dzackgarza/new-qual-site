::: problem
Let $M$ be a $2n+1$ dimensional compact oriented manifold with $\pi_1(M) = \mathbb{Z}/k$, where $k$ is an odd integer.
Show that the degree of any map from $M$ to $\mathbb{RP}^{2n+1}$ is an even integer.
:::
