::: {.problem}
Let $X$ be a CW complex with one $0$-cell, one $1$-cell, two $2$-cells and one $4$-cell.
Assume that the attaching maps of the two $2$-cells to the $1$-cell have degree $2$ and $4$ respectively.

(a) Calculate the Euler characteristic of $X$.

(b) Calculate the integral and mod $2$ homology of $X \times \RP^2$.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

Cellular chain complex and homology of $X$:

::: pf-proof

::: pf-step

The cellular chain groups of $X$ are:
$C_0 = \ZZ\langle e^0 \rangle$, $C_1 = \ZZ\langle e^1 \rangle$, $C_2 = \ZZ\langle e_1^2, e_2^2 \rangle$, $C_3 = 0$, $C_4 = \ZZ\langle e^4 \rangle$.

::: pf-proof

given CW structure of $X$.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

The cellular boundary maps are:
- $d_1 = 0$ (single 0-cell).
- $d_2(e_1^2) = 2e^1$ and $d_2(e_2^2) = 4e^1$, so $\operatorname{im}(d_2) = 2\ZZ \subset \ZZ$.
- $d_3 = 0$ and $d_4 = 0$ (since $C_3 = 0$).

::: pf-proof

degrees of attaching maps.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

The integral homology groups of $X$ are:
- $H_0(X) \cong \ZZ$
- $H_1(X) = \ker(d_1)/\operatorname{im}(d_2) = \ZZ / 2\ZZ \cong \ZZ_2$
- $H_2(X) = \ker(d_2)/\operatorname{im}(d_3) = \{(a, b) \in \ZZ^2 : 2a + 4b = 0\} \cong \ZZ$ (generated by $(-2, 1)$)
- $H_3(X) = 0$
- $H_4(X) \cong \ZZ$, and $H_k(X) = 0$ for $k > 4$.

::: pf-proof

homology = kernel / image.

:::

:::

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

Part (a): Calculate the Euler characteristic $\chi(X)$:

::: pf-proof

::: pf-step

Using the cell counts:
$$
\chi(X) = c_0 - c_1 + c_2 - c_3 + c_4 = 1 - 1 + 2 - 0 + 1 = 3.
$$

::: pf-proof

Euler characteristic formula $\chi(X) = \sum (-1)^n c_n$.

:::

:::

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

Part (b): Integral homology of $X \times \RP^2$:

::: pf-proof

::: pf-step

The homology of $\RP^2$ is $H_0(\RP^2) \cong \ZZ$, $H_1(\RP^2) \cong \ZZ_2$, and $H_k(\RP^2) = 0$ for $k \ge 2$.

::: pf-proof

standard homology of real projective plane.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Apply the Künneth Theorem for integral homology:
$$
0 \to \bigoplus_{i+j=k} H_i(X) \otimes H_j(\RP^2) \to H_k(X \times \RP^2) \to \bigoplus_{i+j=k-1} \operatorname{Tor}_1(H_i(X), H_j(\RP^2)) \to 0.
$$

::: pf-proof

Künneth formula for CW complexes.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Evaluate $H_k(X \times \RP^2; \ZZ)$ degree by degree:
- $k=0$: $\ZZ \otimes \ZZ \cong \ZZ$.
- $k=1$: $(\ZZ_2 \otimes \ZZ) \oplus (\ZZ \otimes \ZZ_2) \cong \ZZ_2 \oplus \ZZ_2$.
- $k=2$: $(\ZZ \otimes \ZZ) \oplus (\ZZ_2 \otimes \ZZ_2) \cong \ZZ \oplus \ZZ_2$.
- $k=3$: $(\ZZ \otimes \ZZ_2) \oplus \operatorname{Tor}_1(\ZZ_2, \ZZ_2) \cong \ZZ_2 \oplus \ZZ_2$.
- $k=4$: $\ZZ \otimes \ZZ \cong \ZZ$.
- $k=5$: $\ZZ \otimes \ZZ_2 \cong \ZZ_2$.
- $k \ge 6$: $0$.

::: pf-proof

tensor product and Tor evaluations.

:::

:::

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s4}

Part (b): Mod 2 homology of $X \times \RP^2$:

::: pf-proof

::: pf-step

Mod 2 boundary maps on $C_*(X; \ZZ_2)$ are all zero because $2 \equiv 4 \equiv 0 \pmod 2$.
Thus $H_0(X; \ZZ_2) \cong \ZZ_2$, $H_1(X; \ZZ_2) \cong \ZZ_2$, $H_2(X; \ZZ_2) \cong \ZZ_2^2$, $H_3(X; \ZZ_2) = 0$, $H_4(X; \ZZ_2) \cong \ZZ_2$.

::: pf-proof

cellular chain complex over $\FF_2$.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

For $\RP^2$, $H_0(\RP^2; \ZZ_2) \cong \ZZ_2$, $H_1(\RP^2; \ZZ_2) \cong \ZZ_2$, $H_2(\RP^2; \ZZ_2) \cong \ZZ_2$.

::: pf-proof

mod 2 homology of $\RP^2$.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

By the Künneth Theorem over the field $\ZZ_2$:
- $H_0(X \times \RP^2; \ZZ_2) \cong \ZZ_2$
- $H_1(X \times \RP^2; \ZZ_2) \cong \ZZ_2^2$
- $H_2(X \times \RP^2; \ZZ_2) \cong \ZZ_2^4$
- $H_3(X \times \RP^2; \ZZ_2) \cong \ZZ_2^3$
- $H_4(X \times \RP^2; \ZZ_2) \cong \ZZ_2^3$
- $H_5(X \times \RP^2; \ZZ_2) \cong \ZZ_2$
- $H_6(X \times \RP^2; \ZZ_2) \cong \ZZ_2$, and $0$ for $k \ge 7$.

::: pf-proof

graded tensor product of $\ZZ_2$-vector spaces.

:::

:::

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Conclusion:
$\chi(X) = 3$, with integral and mod 2 homology as listed above. Q.E.D.
