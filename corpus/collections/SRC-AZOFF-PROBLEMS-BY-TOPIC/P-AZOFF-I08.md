::: {.problem}
[Fall 2002 Problem #8] Suppose $f$ and $g$ are holomorphic mappings of the unit disc $D$ into an open domain $\Omega$, $f$ is one-to-one, and $f(0) = g(0)$. Show that $g(\abs{z} < r) \subset f(\abs{z} < r)$ for each $0 < r < 1$. (The first part of the problem asks for a statement of the Schwarz Lemma.)
