::: problem
Let $F:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R$ be continuously differentiable, with $F(0,0)=0$ and
\[
\|\nabla F(0,0)\|<1.
\]
Prove that there is $r>0$ such that $|F(x,y)|<r$ whenever $\|(x,y)\|<r$.
:::
