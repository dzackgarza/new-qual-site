::: {.problem}
Suppose $(f_n)_{n\in\mathbb N}$ is a sequence of continuous functions $f_n:[0,1]\to\mathbb R$ satisfying
\[
f_n(x)\ge f_{n+1}(x)\ge0
\]
for all $n$ and $x$.
Prove that if $f_n\to0$ pointwise on $[0,1]$, then the convergence is uniform.
:::
