[April 1999 Problem $\# 7 ]$ Let $S : = \{ z \in \mathbb { D } : \operatorname { I m } ( z ) \geq 0 \}$ . Suppose $f : S  \mathbb { C }$ is continuous on S, real on $S \cap \mathbb { R }$ , and holomorphic on the interior of S. Prove that f is the restriction of a holomorphic function on the open unit disk.
