::: problem
Suppose $f : \mathbb { C } \to \mathbb { C }$ is entire and bounded.
Use Cauchy’s formula to prove that $f ^ { \prime }$ is identically zero and hence that f is constant.
This is Liouville’s Theorem.
:::
