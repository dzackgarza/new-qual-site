::: {.problem}
Suppose $f , g : [ 0 , 1 ] \to \mathbb { R }$ with f Riemann integrable and

$$
| g ( x ) - g ( y ) | \leq | f ( x ) - f ( y ) | , \qquad x , y \in [ 0 , 1 ] .
$$

Prove that g is also Riemann integrable.
:::
