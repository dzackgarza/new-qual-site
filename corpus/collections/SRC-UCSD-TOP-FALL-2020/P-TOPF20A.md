::: problem
Show that there does not exist a continuous map $f : S^1 \times S^1 \to S^1$ that satisfies both of the following conditions:

- $f(x, x) = x$ for any $x \in S^1$;

- $f(x, y) = f(y, x)$ for any $x, y \in S^1$.
:::
