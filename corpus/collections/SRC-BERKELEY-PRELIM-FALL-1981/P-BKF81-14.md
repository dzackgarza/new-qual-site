::: problem
Let $V:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R$ be $C^1$ and let $x''_i=-\partial V/\partial x_i$ on a finite interval $(a,b)$.

(a) Show $H(t)=\frac12\langle x'(t),x'(t)\rangle+V(x(t))$ is constant.

(b) If $V\ge M> -\infty$, show $x,x',x''$ are bounded on $(a,b)$ and prove the limits of all three as $t\to b$ exist.
:::
