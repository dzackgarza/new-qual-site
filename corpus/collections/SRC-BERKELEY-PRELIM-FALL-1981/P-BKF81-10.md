::: problem
Let $A=(a_{ij})$ be a differentiable real matrix-valued function with $\det A>0$, and let $B=(b_{ij})=A^{-1}$. Prove
\[
\frac d{dt}\log\det A=\sum_{i,j=1}^n\frac{da_{ij}}{dt}b_{ji}.
\]
:::
