::: problem
Let
\[
A=\begin{pmatrix}a_0&a_1&a_2\\a_2&a_0&a_1\\a_1&a_2&a_0\end{pmatrix}.
\]
(a) Show $A=a_0I_3+a_1E+a_2E^2$ for the cyclic shift matrix $E$.

(b) Find the complex eigenvalues of $A$.

(c) Generalize to $n\times n$ circulant matrices.
:::
