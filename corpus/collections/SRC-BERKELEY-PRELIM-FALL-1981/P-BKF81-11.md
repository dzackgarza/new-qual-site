::: problem
Let
\[
A=\begin{pmatrix}a_0&a_1&a_2\\a_2&a_0&a_1\\a_1&a_2&a_0\end{pmatrix}.
\]
(a) Show $A=a_0I_3+a_1E+a_2E^2$ for the cyclic shift matrix $E$.

(b) Find the complex eigenvalues of $A$.

(c) Generalize to $n\times n$ circulant matrices.
:::

::: solution
Let
$$
E=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&1&0\\
0&0&1\\
1&0&0
\end{pmatrix}.
$$
Then
$$
E^2=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&0&1\\
1&0&0\\
0&1&0
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<1>1. Express $A$ as a polynomial in the cyclic shift.
::: proof
Directly,
$$
a_0I_3+a_1E+a_2E^2
=\begin{pmatrix}
a_0&a_1&a_2\\
a_2&a_0&a_1\\
a_1&a_2&a_0
\end{pmatrix}
=A.
$$
Thus
$$
\boxed{A=a_0I_3+a_1E+a_2E^2.}
$$
:::

<1>2. Diagonalize $E$ over $\mathbb C$ and read off the eigenvalues of $A$.
::: proof
Let
$$
\omega=e^{2\pi i/3}.
$$
For each
$$
\lambda\in\{1,\omega,\omega^2\},
$$
the vector
$$
v_\lambda=
\begin{pmatrix}1\\\lambda\\\lambda^2\end{pmatrix}
$$
satisfies
$$
Ev_\lambda
=\begin{pmatrix}\lambda\\\lambda^2\\1\end{pmatrix}
=\lambda v_\lambda,
$$
because $\lambda^3=1$. The three eigenvalues of $E$ are distinct, so these
vectors form a basis of $\mathbb C^3$.

Since $A=a_0I+a_1E+a_2E^2$,
$$
Av_\lambda
=(a_0+a_1\lambda+a_2\lambda^2)v_\lambda.
$$
Therefore the eigenvalues of $A$ are
$$
\boxed{
a_0+a_1+a_2,
\quad
a_0+a_1\omega+a_2\omega^2,
\quad
a_0+a_1\omega^2+a_2\omega.}
$$
:::

<1>3. Generalize to an $n\times n$ circulant.
::: proof
Let $E_n$ be the cyclic shift matrix
$$
E_ne_j=e_{j-1}
$$
with indices read cyclically in the convention matching the displayed
three-by-three matrix. An $n\times n$ circulant with first row
$$
(a_0,a_1,\ldots,a_{n-1})
$$
has the form
$$
C=\sum_{r=0}^{n-1}a_rE_n^r.
$$

Let
$$
\zeta=e^{2\pi i/n}.
$$
For $k=0,\ldots,n-1$, put
$$
v_k=
\begin{pmatrix}
1\\
\zeta^k\\
\zeta^{2k}\\
\vdots\\
\zeta^{(n-1)k}
\end{pmatrix}.
$$
Then
$$
E_nv_k=\zeta^kv_k.
$$
Hence
$$
Cv_k
=\left(\sum_{r=0}^{n-1}a_r\zeta^{kr}\right)v_k.
$$
The $n$ numbers $\zeta^k$ are distinct, so the corresponding vectors form
a basis. Thus every $n\times n$ circulant is diagonalized by the Fourier
basis, with eigenvalues
$$
\boxed{
\lambda_k=\sum_{r=0}^{n-1}a_r\zeta^{kr},
\qquad k=0,\ldots,n-1.}
$$
:::
