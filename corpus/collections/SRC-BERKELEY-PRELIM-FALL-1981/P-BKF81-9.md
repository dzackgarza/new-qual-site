::: problem
Let $M_{2\times2}(\mathbb R)$ be the space of real $2\times2$ matrices and
\[
A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\-1&3\end{pmatrix},\qquad
B=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\0&4\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Define $L(X)=AXB$. Compute the trace and determinant of $L$.
:::
