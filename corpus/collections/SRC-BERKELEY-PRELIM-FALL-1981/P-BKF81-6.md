::: problem
Let $f,g$ be continuous $1$-periodic functions on $\mathbb R$. Prove that
\[
\lim_{n\to\infty}\int_0^1 f(x)g(nx)\,dx
=\left(\int_0^1f\right)\left(\int_0^1g\right).
\]
:::
