::: {.problem}
Let $L/K$ be a Galois extension with finite Galois group $G$.
Consider the natural action of the group $G$ on $L$.
Let $H$ be a subgroup of $G$.
Prove that there exists an element $\alpha \in L$ whose stabilizer is $H$.
:::


::: {.solution}
Let
\[
E:=L^H=\{x\in L:h(x)=x\text{ for every }h\in H\}
\]
be the fixed field of \(H\).

<1>1. The extension \(E/K\) is finite and separable.
::: {.proof}
Since \(L/K\) is finite Galois, it is finite and separable.
Every intermediate extension of a finite separable extension is again finite and separable.
Thus
\[
K\subseteq E\subseteq L
\]
gives a finite separable extension \(E/K\).
:::

<1>2. There exists \(\alpha\in E\) such that
\[
E=K(\alpha).
\]
::: {.proof}
By <1>1, \(E/K\) is a finite separable extension.
The primitive element theorem therefore provides an element
\[
\alpha\in E
\]
with
\[
E=K(\alpha).
\]
:::

<1>3. Every element of \(H\) fixes \(\alpha\).
::: {.proof}
By construction,
\[
\alpha\in E=L^H.
\]
Thus for every \(h\in H\),
\[
h(\alpha)=\alpha.
\]
Hence
\[
H\subseteq\operatorname{Stab}_G(\alpha).
\]
:::

<1>4. Every element of \(G\) fixing \(\alpha\) belongs to \(H\).
::: {.proof}
Let \(\sigma\in G\) satisfy
\[
\sigma(\alpha)=\alpha.
\]
Every element of \(G=\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)\) fixes \(K\), so \(\sigma\) fixes the field generated by \(K\) and \(\alpha\):
\[
K(\alpha)=E.
\]
Therefore
\[
\sigma\in\operatorname{Gal}(L/E).
\]
By the fundamental theorem of Galois theory, because \(E=L^H\),
\[
\operatorname{Gal}(L/E)=H.
\]
Thus \(\sigma\in H\), proving
\[
\operatorname{Stab}_G(\alpha)\subseteq H.
\]
:::

<1>5. The stabilizer of \(\alpha\) is exactly \(H\).
::: {.proof}
Combine <1>3 and <1>4:
\[
\operatorname{Stab}_G(\alpha)=H.
\]
:::
:::
