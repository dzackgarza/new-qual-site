::: problem
Let $A = \mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{10}]$.

(i) Prove that $2$ is irreducible in $A$.

(ii) Prove that $2$ is NOT prime in $A$.

(iii) Is $A$ a UFD? Justify your answer.
:::
