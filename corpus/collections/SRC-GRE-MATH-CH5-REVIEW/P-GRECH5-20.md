::: {.problem}

\[
A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 2 + i \\ 2 - i & 6 \end{pmatrix}
\]

has which one of the following as an eigenvalue?

(A) $-1$
(B) $3$
(C) $7$
(D) $i$
(E) $1 + i$
