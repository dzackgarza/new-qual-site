::: {.problem}
The eigenvalues of the matrix

\[
A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & b \\ 3 & -1 \end{pmatrix}
\]

are $-4$ and $b - 1$. Find $b$.

(A) $2$
(B) $3$
(C) $4$
(D) $5$
(E) $6$
:::
