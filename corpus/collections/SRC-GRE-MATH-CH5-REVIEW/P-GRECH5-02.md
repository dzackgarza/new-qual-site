::: {.problem}
The solution of the system

\[
\begin{aligned}
ax + ay - z &= 1 \\
x - ay - az &= -1 \\
ax - y + az &= 1
\end{aligned}
\]

is $(x, y, z) = (a, b, a)$. If $a$ is NOT an integer, what is the numerical value of $a + b$?

(A) $-\frac{3}{2}$
(B) $-1$
(C) $0$
(D) $\frac{1}{2}$
(E) $1$
:::
