::: {.problem}
The solution of the system \[ax+ay-z=1,\qquad x-ay-az=-1,\qquad ax-y+az=1\] is $(x,y,z)=(a,b,a)$.
If $a$ is not an integer, what is the numerical value of $a+b$?
:::
