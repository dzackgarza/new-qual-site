::: {.problem}
If
\[\det\begin{pmatrix}a&b&c\\k&l&m\\p&q&r\end{pmatrix}=d,\]
find
\[\det\begin{pmatrix}k&2(a-k)&p+k\\l&2(b-l)&q+l\\m&2(c-m)&r+m\end{pmatrix}.\]
:::
