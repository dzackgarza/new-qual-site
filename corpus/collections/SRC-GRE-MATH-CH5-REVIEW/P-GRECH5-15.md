::: {.problem}
Let $T:\mathbb R^5\to\mathbb R^3$ be linear with a three-dimensional kernel. The set $\{T(\mathbf x):\mathbf x\in\mathbb R^5\}$ is

(A) the trivial subspace
(B) a line through the origin
(C) a plane through the origin
(D) all of $\mathbb R^3$
(E) not determined by the information given.
:::
