::: {.problem}
A square matrix is symmetric if $A=A^\top$. What is the dimension of the subspace $S_{n\times n}(\mathbb R)$ of real symmetric $n\times n$ matrices inside $M_{n\times n}(\mathbb R)$?

(A) $\frac12n$  (B) $\frac12n(n-1)$  (C) $\frac12n^2$  (D) $\frac12n(n+1)$
:::
