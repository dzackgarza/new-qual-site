::: {.problem}
The set of points $(x,y)\in\mathbb R^2$ satisfying
\[\det\begin{pmatrix}x&y&1\\0&y_1&1\\1&y_2&1\end{pmatrix}=0\]
forms which curve?

(A) an ellipse centered at the origin
(B) a line with slope $y_2/y_1$
(C) a circle centered at $(0,y_1)$ with radius $1$
(D) a line with slope $y_2-y_1$
(E) a parabola with vertex $(1,y_2)$
:::
