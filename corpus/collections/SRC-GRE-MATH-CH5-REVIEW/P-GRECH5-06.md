::: {.problem}
The vectors $\mathbf{v}_1 = (-1, 1, 1)$, $\mathbf{v}_2 = (1, 1, 1)$, and $\mathbf{v}_3 = (1, -1, k)$ form a basis for $\RR^3$ for all real values of $k$ EXCEPT $k =$

(A) $-2$
(B) $-1$
(C) $0$
(D) $1$
(E) $2$
:::
