::: {.problem}
If $A$ is invertible and has eigenvalue $3$ with eigenvector $\mathbf x$, which statement must be true?

(A) $A^{-1}$ has eigenvalue $1/3$ with eigenvector $\mathbf x$.
(B) $A^{-1}$ has eigenvalue $1/3$ with the vector of reciprocal entries of $\mathbf x$.
(C) $A^2$ has eigenvalue $3$ with eigenvector $\mathbf x$.
(D) $A^2$ has eigenvalue $6$ with eigenvector $2\mathbf x$.
(E) $A^2$ has eigenvalue $3$ with eigenvector $3\mathbf x$.
:::
