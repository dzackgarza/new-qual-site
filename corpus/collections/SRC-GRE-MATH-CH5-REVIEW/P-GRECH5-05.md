::: {.problem}
If \[\begin{pmatrix}3&-2&-2\\-1&1&1\\3&-1&-2\end{pmatrix}\quad\text{and}\quad\begin{pmatrix}1&a&0\\-1&b&1\\2&c&-1\end{pmatrix}\] are inverses, find $c$.

(A) $-3$  (B) $-2$  (C) $0$  (D) $2$  (E) $3$
:::
