::: {.problem}
If the matrices

\[
\begin{pmatrix} 3 & -2 & -2 \\ -1 & 1 & 1 \\ 3 & -1 & -2 \end{pmatrix} \quad \text{and} \quad \begin{pmatrix} 1 & a & 0 \\ -1 & b & 1 \\ 2 & c & -1 \end{pmatrix}
\]

are inverses of each other, what is the value of $c$?

(A) $-3$
(B) $-2$
(C) $0$
(D) $2$
(E) $3$
:::
