::: {.problem}
For $A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2&3\\4&5&6\\7&8&9\end{pmatrix}$, let $r=\operatorname{rank}(A)$ and $d=\det(A)$.
Find $r-d$.

(A) $-2$  (B) $-1$  (C) $0$  (D) $1$  (E) $2$
:::
