::: {.problem}
For the matrix

\[
A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 \\ 4 & 5 & 6 \\ 7 & 8 & 9 \end{pmatrix}
\]

let $r$ denote its rank and $d$ denote its determinant. What is the value of $r - d$?

(A) $-2$
(B) $-1$
(C) $0$
(D) $1$
(E) $2$
:::
