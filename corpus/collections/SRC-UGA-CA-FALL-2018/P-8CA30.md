::: problem
For each real number $a$, compute
\[
\int_0^\infty\frac{\cos(ax)}{x^4+5x^2+4}\,dx.
\]
:::

::: solution
Factor
\[
x^4+5x^2+4=(x^2+1)(x^2+4)
\]
and decompose
\[
\frac1{(x^2+1)(x^2+4)}
=\frac13\left(\frac1{x^2+1}-\frac1{x^2+4}\right).
\]
For $b>0$ and real $a$, the standard upper-half-plane residue computation gives
\[
\int_0^\infty\frac{\cos(ax)}{x^2+b^2}\,dx
=\frac{\pi}{2b}e^{-b|a|}.
\]
Therefore
\[
\begin{aligned}
\int_0^\infty\frac{\cos(ax)}{x^4+5x^2+4}\,dx
&=\frac13\left(\frac\pi2e^{-|a|}-\frac\pi4e^{-2|a|}\right)\\
&=\boxed{\frac\pi{12}\left(2e^{-|a|}-e^{-2|a|}\right)}.
\end{aligned}
\]
:::
