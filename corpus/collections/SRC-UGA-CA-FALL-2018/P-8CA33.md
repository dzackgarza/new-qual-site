::: problem
Let $p(z)$ be a nonconstant polynomial of even degree with all roots inside the disc $|z|<R$.
Show that there is a holomorphic function $f(z)$ on the annulus $|z|>R$ with $f(z)^2=p(z)$.
:::

::: solution
Write
\[
p(z)=a z^{2m}\prod_{j=1}^N\left(1-\frac{\alpha_j}{z}\right)^{\nu_j},
\]
where $|\alpha_j|<R$ and $\sum_j\nu_j=2m$. On $|z|>R$, every factor
$1-\alpha_j/z$ lies in the disk centered at $1$ of radius $<1$, hence admits
a holomorphic logarithm. Choose a square root $\sqrt a$ of the leading
coefficient and define
\[
f(z)=\sqrt a\,z^m
\exp\!\left(
\frac12\sum_{j=1}^N\nu_j
\Log\left(1-\frac{\alpha_j}{z}\right)
\right).
\]
This is holomorphic for $|z|>R$, and squaring gives exactly $p(z)$.

Equivalently, because the degree is even, the winding number of $p$ around
$0$ along a large circle is even, so the nonvanishing holomorphic function
$p$ on the annulus admits a holomorphic square root.
:::
