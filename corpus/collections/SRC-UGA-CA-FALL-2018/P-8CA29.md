::: problem
Write down a formula that gives a conformal equivalence between the vertical half-strip
\[
\mathcal{S}=\{z\in\mathbb{C}:\ 0<\operatorname{Re}(z)<1,\ \operatorname{Im}(z)>0\}
\]
and the upper half-plane
\[
\mathbb{H}=\{z\in\mathbb{C}:\operatorname{Im}(z)>0\}.
\]
:::

::: solution
The map
\[
\boxed{F(z)=-\cos(\pi z)}
\]
is a conformal equivalence from $\mathcal S$ onto $\mathbb H$.

Indeed, write $z=x+iy$ with $0<x<1$ and $y>0$. Then
\[
\operatorname{Im}F(z)=\sin(\pi x)\sinh(\pi y)>0,
\]
so $F(\mathcal S)\subset\mathbb H$.

To see the geometry and bijectivity, factor $F$ as
\[
z\longmapsto \zeta=e^{\pi i z}
\longmapsto -\frac12\left(\zeta+\frac1\zeta\right).
\]
The exponential maps $\mathcal S$ biholomorphically onto the upper half of the
unit disk: $0<\arg\zeta<\pi$ and $0<|\zeta|<1$. The second map is the standard
Joukowski map carrying that upper half-disk biholomorphically onto
$\mathbb H$. Their composite is $-\cos(\pi z)$.
:::
