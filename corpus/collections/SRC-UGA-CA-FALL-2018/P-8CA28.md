::: problem
Find the Laurent series expansion of
\[
f(z)=\frac{1}{(z-1)(z-2)}
\]
valid in the annulus $1<|z|<2$.
:::

::: solution
Use partial fractions:
\[
\frac1{(z-1)(z-2)}=-\frac1{z-1}+\frac1{z-2}.
\]
For $|z|>1$,
\[
-\frac1{z-1}
=-\frac1z\frac1{1-1/z}
=-\sum_{n=0}^\infty z^{-n-1}.
\]
For $|z|<2$,
\[
\frac1{z-2}
=-\frac12\frac1{1-z/2}
=-\sum_{n=0}^\infty \frac{z^n}{2^{n+1}}.
\]
Hence, on the maximal common annulus $1<|z|<2$,
\[
\boxed{
\frac1{(z-1)(z-2)}
=-\sum_{n=0}^\infty z^{-n-1}
-\sum_{n=0}^\infty\frac{z^n}{2^{n+1}}.}
\]
:::
