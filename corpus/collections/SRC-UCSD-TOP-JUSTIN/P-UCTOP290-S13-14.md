::: {.problem}
Describe submanifolds representing the generators of
$H_k(\RP^n;\ZZ_2)$ and use their intersections to
compute $H^*(\RP^n;\ZZ_2)$ as a ring.
:::

::: {.solution}

The standard CW structure on $\RP^n$ has one cell in
each dimension from $0$ through $n$. Its cellular
boundary maps vanish over $\ZZ_2$, so
$H_k(\RP^n;\ZZ_2)\cong\ZZ_2$ for $0\le k\le n$,
and all other groups vanish. A linear projective
subspace $\RP^k\subset\RP^n$ represents the
nonzero class in degree $k$: a complementary
linear $\RP^{n-k}$ meets it transversely in one point.

Let $a\in H^1(\RP^n;\ZZ_2)$ be Poincaré dual to a
projective hyperplane. Generic projective hyperplanes
meet in a linear $\RP^{n-j}$, so their $j$-fold
intersection represents the dual of $a^j$. The
intersection of that subspace with a complementary
$\RP^j$ is one point modulo two. Hence
$a^j\ne0$ for $0\le j\le n$. Dimensional reasons
give $a^{n+1}=0$, and therefore
$$
H^*(\RP^n;\ZZ_2)\cong\ZZ_2[a]/(a^{n+1}),
\qquad \deg a=1.
$$
:::
