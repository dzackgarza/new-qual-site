::: {.problem}
Extend the table of values of
$\Tor_1^{\ZZ}(A,B)$ and $\Ext^1_{\ZZ}(A,B)$
to the cases where $A$ and $B$ may be $\QQ$ and $\QQ/\ZZ$.
Give the values for $A,B$ among
$\ZZ$, $\ZZ/n$, $\QQ$, and $\QQ/\ZZ$, with positive integers
$m,n$ used for the cyclic entries.
:::

::: {.solution}

Write $\Tor=\Tor_1^{\ZZ}$ and $\Ext=\Ext^1_{\ZZ}$, put $d=\gcd(m,n)$ for
positive integers $m,n$, and let $\widehat\ZZ=\varprojlim_k\ZZ/k$ be the
profinite completion of $\ZZ$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #tor}

The values of $\Tor(A,B)$ for $A,B$ among $\ZZ,\ZZ/n,\QQ,\QQ/\ZZ$ are given by
$$
\begin{array}{c|cccc}
\Tor(A,B)&\ZZ&\ZZ/m&\QQ&\QQ/\ZZ\\\hline
\ZZ&0&0&0&0\\
\ZZ/n&0&\ZZ/d&0&\ZZ/n\\
\QQ&0&0&0&0\\
\QQ/\ZZ&0&\ZZ/m&0&\QQ/\ZZ
\end{array}
$$

::: pf-proof

$\ZZ$ is free and $\QQ$ is torsion-free, hence flat over $\ZZ$, so the rows
and columns of $\ZZ$ and $\QQ$ vanish: $\Tor(\ZZ,B)=\Tor(A,\ZZ)=0$ and
$\Tor(\QQ,B)=0$, the last because tensoring with $\QQ$ is exact, equivalently
because $\QQ$ is a filtered colimit of free groups. The resolution
$0\to\ZZ\xrightarrow{n}\ZZ\to\ZZ/n\to0$ gives
$\Tor(\ZZ/n,B)=\ker(B\xrightarrow{n}B)=B[n]$, the $n$-torsion subgroup: this
is $\ZZ/d$ for $B=\ZZ/m$, $0$ for $B=\ZZ$ or $B=\QQ$ (both torsion-free), and
$\ZZ/n$ for $B=\QQ/\ZZ$, whose $n$-torsion is the cyclic group generated by
$1/n$. The exact sequence $0\to\ZZ\to\QQ\to\QQ/\ZZ\to0$ gives, since
$\Tor(\ZZ,B)=0$ and $\Tor(\QQ,B)=0$, that $\Tor(\QQ/\ZZ,B)=\ker(B\to
B\otimes\QQ)$, the torsion subgroup of $B$; this is $0$ for $B=\ZZ$ and
$B=\QQ$, $\ZZ/m$ for $B=\ZZ/m$, and $\QQ/\ZZ$ for $B=\QQ/\ZZ$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #extbasic}

$\Ext(\ZZ,B)=0$, $\Ext(\ZZ/n,B)=B/nB$, and $\Ext(A,\QQ)=\Ext(A,\QQ/\ZZ)=0$.

::: pf-proof

$\ZZ$ is projective, so its Ext row vanishes, and the resolution
$0\to\ZZ\xrightarrow{n}\ZZ\to\ZZ/n\to0$, applied to $\Hom(-,B)$, gives
$\Ext(\ZZ/n,B)=B/nB$: this is $\ZZ/n$ for $B=\ZZ$, $\ZZ/d$ for $B=\ZZ/m$, and
$0$ for $B=\QQ$ and $B=\QQ/\ZZ$, both of which are divisible and hence
injective over $\ZZ$, so that $\Ext(-,D)=0$ for any divisible $D$ and in
particular for the last two columns.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #hatz}

$\Ext(\QQ/\ZZ,\ZZ)\cong\widehat\ZZ$.

::: pf-proof

Apply $\Hom(\QQ/\ZZ,-)$ to $0\to\ZZ\to\QQ\to\QQ/\ZZ\to0$. Both
$\Hom(\QQ/\ZZ,\ZZ)$ and $\Hom(\QQ/\ZZ,\QQ)$ vanish, since a homomorphism out
of a torsion group lands in the torsion subgroup of the target, which is
trivial for $\ZZ$ and for $\QQ$; and $\Ext(\QQ/\ZZ,\QQ)=0$ because $\QQ$ is
divisible, hence injective. The resulting exact sequence is
$0\to\Hom(\QQ/\ZZ,\QQ/\ZZ)\to\Ext(\QQ/\ZZ,\ZZ)\to0$, so
$\Ext(\QQ/\ZZ,\ZZ)\cong\operatorname{End}(\QQ/\ZZ)$. Decomposing
$\QQ/\ZZ=\bigoplus_p\ZZ[1/p]$ into Prüfer groups, whose endomorphism rings are
the $p$-adic integers $\ZZ_p$, and using that there are no homomorphisms
between Prüfer groups for distinct primes, gives
$\operatorname{End}(\QQ/\ZZ)=\prod_p\ZZ_p=\widehat\ZZ$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #extq}

$\Ext(\QQ,\ZZ)\cong\widehat\ZZ/\ZZ$, $\Ext(\QQ,\ZZ/m)=0$, and
$\Ext(\QQ/\ZZ,\ZZ/m)\cong\ZZ/m$.

::: pf-proof

Apply $\Hom(-,\ZZ)$ to $0\to\ZZ\to\QQ\to\QQ/\ZZ\to0$. The segment
$\Hom(\QQ,\ZZ)\to\Hom(\ZZ,\ZZ)\to\Ext(\QQ/\ZZ,\ZZ)\to\Ext(\QQ,\ZZ)\to\Ext(\ZZ,\ZZ)$
is exact, and $\Hom(\QQ,\ZZ)=0=\Ext(\ZZ,\ZZ)$, so step [](#hatz){.pf-ref}
turns it into the exact sequence
$0\to\ZZ\to\widehat\ZZ\to\Ext(\QQ,\ZZ)\to0$, whence
$\Ext(\QQ,\ZZ)\cong\widehat\ZZ/\ZZ$. Applying $\Hom(\QQ,-)$ to
$0\to\ZZ\xrightarrow{m}\ZZ\to\ZZ/m\to0$ gives, since $\Hom(\QQ,\ZZ)=0$, the
exact sequence
$0\to\Ext(\QQ,\ZZ)\xrightarrow{m}\Ext(\QQ,\ZZ)\to\Ext(\QQ,\ZZ/m)\to0$, so
$\Ext(\QQ,\ZZ/m)\cong(\widehat\ZZ/\ZZ)/m(\widehat\ZZ/\ZZ)$; that group is $0$
because $\widehat\ZZ/\ZZ$ is divisible, every class modulo $m$ in
$\widehat\ZZ$ having an integer representative. Applying $\Hom(\QQ/\ZZ,-)$ to
the same sequence, and using that $\Hom(\QQ/\ZZ,\ZZ)=0$ and that $\ZZ$ is
projective, gives
$\Ext(\QQ/\ZZ,\ZZ/m)\cong\Ext(\QQ/\ZZ,\ZZ)/m\Ext(\QQ/\ZZ,\ZZ)=\widehat\ZZ/m\widehat\ZZ\cong\ZZ/m$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #exttable}

The values of $\Ext(A,B)$ are given by
$$
\begin{array}{c|cccc}
\Ext(A,B)&\ZZ&\ZZ/m&\QQ&\QQ/\ZZ\\\hline
\ZZ&0&0&0&0\\
\ZZ/n&\ZZ/n&\ZZ/d&0&0\\
\QQ&\widehat\ZZ/\ZZ&0&0&0\\
\QQ/\ZZ&\widehat\ZZ&\ZZ/m&0&0
\end{array}
$$

::: pf-proof

The row of $\ZZ$ and the columns of $\QQ$ and $\QQ/\ZZ$ vanish by steps
[](#extbasic){.pf-ref} and [](#hatz){.pf-ref}; the row of $\ZZ/n$ is step
[](#extbasic){.pf-ref}; the entries $\Ext(\QQ,\ZZ)$ and $\Ext(\QQ/\ZZ,\ZZ)$
are steps [](#extq){.pf-ref} and [](#hatz){.pf-ref}, and $\Ext(\QQ,\ZZ/m)$ and
$\Ext(\QQ/\ZZ,\ZZ/m)$ are step [](#extq){.pf-ref}. In particular the two
groups not determined by a finitely generated presentation,
$\Ext(\QQ,\ZZ)=\widehat\ZZ/\ZZ$ and $\Ext(\QQ/\ZZ,\ZZ)=\widehat\ZZ$, are not
finitely generated, and the vanishing of the $\QQ$ column records that $\QQ$
is injective.

:::
