::: problem
Show, by generalising the method of question 1, that the cohomology ring of the $n$-torus $T^n$ is an exterior algebra generated by $H^1$.
In other words, there is a basis $\alpha_1, \ldots, \alpha_n$ for $H^1(T^n;\mathbb{Z})$ such that
\[
H^*(T^n;\mathbb{Z}) = \Lambda^*[\alpha_1, \ldots, \alpha_n].
\]
(Recall: this is the free associative (but non-commutative) algebra in the $\alpha_i$'s, with relations $\alpha_i\alpha_j = -\alpha_j\alpha_i$.
We write $\wedge$ for the product in this algebra.
The dimension of $H^k$ is then $\binom{n}{k}$ with basis $\{\alpha_{i_1} \wedge \alpha_{i_2} \wedge \cdots \wedge \alpha_{i_k} : i_1 < i_2 < \cdots < i_k\}$.)
:::

::: remark
"Question 1" refers to problem 1 of the same source sheet, which computes the cohomology ring of $T = S^1 \times S^1$ from the definition of singular cohomology.
:::
