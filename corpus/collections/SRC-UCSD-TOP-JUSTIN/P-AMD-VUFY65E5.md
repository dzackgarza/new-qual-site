::: {.problem}
Use cellular chain complexes to compute the homology of the non-orientable surface of genus $h$, made by removing $h$ discs from a sphere and gluing Möbius strips in their places.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #sum}

For $h\ge1$, the surface $N_h$ obtained from $S^2$ by removing $h$ open discs and gluing a Möbius strip to each boundary circle is the connected sum $\#^h\RP^2$.

::: pf-proof

A projective plane with an open disc removed is a Möbius strip. Gluing a Möbius strip to a boundary circle of a surface is therefore the connected sum with $\RP^2$, and $S^2$ is the unit for connected sum.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #cw}

$N_h$ is the quotient of a $2h$-gon by the boundary word $a_1a_1a_2a_2\cdots a_ha_h$, so it has a CW structure with one $0$-cell, $h$ $1$-cells and one $2$-cell.

::: pf-proof

$\RP^2$ is the $2$-gon with word $aa$, and the connected sum of polygon surfaces concatenates their words. All corners of the polygon are identified: the edge $a_i$ runs from the corner before its first copy to the corner between its copies, and also from that corner to the corner after its second copy, so these three corners are one point, and consecutive blocks share a corner.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #diff}

The cellular differentials are $d_1=0$ and $d_2(e^2)=2a_1+2a_2+\cdots+2a_h$.

::: pf-proof

Each $1$-cell is a loop at the single $0$-cell. Each letter occurs twice in the attaching word, both times with exponent $+1$.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

By Step [](#diff){.pf-ref}, $d_2$ is injective and its image is generated by $2(a_1+\cdots+a_h)$. The element $a_1+\cdots+a_h$ extends to a basis of $\ZZ^h$, so
$$
\boxed{H_0(N_h)\cong\ZZ,\qquad H_1(N_h)\cong\ZZ^{h-1}\oplus\ZZ/2,\qquad H_2(N_h)=0,}
$$
and $H_k(N_h)=0$ for $k\ge3$.

:::
