::: problem
If $\{C_*^\alpha\}_{\alpha \in I}$ is a directed system of chain complexes, show that "taking homology commutes with direct limits": that is, for any $n$,
\[
\varinjlim H_n(C_*^\alpha) = H_n\left(\varinjlim C_*^\alpha\right).
\]
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. For each $\alpha$, write
$$
Z_n^\alpha=\ker(d_n:C_n^\alpha\to C_{n-1}^\alpha),
\qquad
B_n^\alpha=\operatorname{im}(d_{n+1}:C_{n+1}^\alpha\to C_n^\alpha).
$$
The structure maps of the directed system preserve cycles and boundaries.
::: {.proof}
Every structure map is a chain map, so it commutes with differentials. Hence it sends kernels to kernels and images to images.
:::

<1>2. Directed colimits preserve the exact sequences
$$
0\to Z_n^\alpha\to C_n^\alpha\xrightarrow{d_n}B_{n-1}^\alpha\to0
$$
and
$$
0\to B_n^\alpha\to Z_n^\alpha\to H_n(C_*^\alpha)\to0.
$$
::: {.proof}
Directed colimit is exact by the preceding result on direct limits.
:::

<1>3. In the colimit complex $C_*:=\varinjlim C_*^\alpha$,
$$
Z_n(C_*)\cong\varinjlim Z_n^\alpha,
\qquad
B_n(C_*)\cong\varinjlim B_n^\alpha.
$$
::: {.proof}
From the colimit of the first short exact sequence,
$$
0\to\varinjlim Z_n^\alpha\to\varinjlim C_n^\alpha
\to\varinjlim B_{n-1}^\alpha\to0.
$$
The last module embeds in $\varinjlim C_{n-1}^\alpha$ by exactness applied to $0\to B_{n-1}^\alpha\to C_{n-1}^\alpha$. Therefore the kernel of the induced differential on $C_n$ is exactly $\varinjlim Z_n^\alpha$. The image statement follows similarly from the factorization of the differential through $B_n^\alpha$ and surjectivity onto the colimit of these image modules.
:::

<1>4. Consequently
$$
\boxed{\varinjlim H_n(C_*^\alpha)\cong H_n\!\left(\varinjlim C_*^\alpha\right).}
$$
::: {.proof}
Taking the colimit of
$$
0\to B_n^\alpha\to Z_n^\alpha\to H_n(C_*^\alpha)\to0
$$
gives
$$
0\to\varinjlim B_n^\alpha\to\varinjlim Z_n^\alpha
\to\varinjlim H_n(C_*^\alpha)\to0.
$$
Using <1>3, its cokernel is
$$
Z_n(C_*)/B_n(C_*)=H_n(C_*).
$$
:::
:::
