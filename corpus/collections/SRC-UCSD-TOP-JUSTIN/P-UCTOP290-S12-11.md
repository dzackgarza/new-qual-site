::: problem
If $\{C_*^\alpha\}_{\alpha \in I}$ is a directed system of chain complexes, show that "taking homology commutes with direct limits": that is, for any $n$,
\[
\varinjlim H_n(C_*^\alpha) = H_n\left(\varinjlim C_*^\alpha\right).
\]
:::
