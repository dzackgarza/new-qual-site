::: {.problem}
Compute the homology of:

1. $\RP^2 = M \union_\del D^2$

2. $T^2 = S^1 \cross S^1 = (S^1\cross I)\union_f (S^1\cross I)$ where $(x,0) \sim (x,1) \sim (\bar x, 0) \in \CC$

3. $S^1 \union_{f} B^2$ attached along $\del B^2$ using $z\mapsto z^n$
:::
