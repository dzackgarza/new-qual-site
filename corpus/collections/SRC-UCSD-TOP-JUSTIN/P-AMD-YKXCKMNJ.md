::: {.problem}
$S_1 \vee S_1$ with two discs attached via $(ab)^3$ and $(ab)^6$
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. The fundamental group has presentation
$$
\pi_1(X)=\langle a,b\mid (ab)^3=1,(ab)^6=1\rangle.
$$
::: {.proof}
The $1$-skeleton is $S^1\vee S^1$, with generators $a,b$. Attaching a $2$-cell along a loop imposes the corresponding word as a relation.
:::

<1>2. The second relation is redundant, and
$$
\boxed{\pi_1(X)\cong\mathbb Z*\mathbb Z/3}.
$$
::: {.proof}
The relation $(ab)^3=1$ already implies $(ab)^6=1$. Set $c=ab$, so $b=a^{-1}c$. Then
$$
\pi_1(X)\cong\langle a,c\mid c^3=1\rangle\cong\mathbb Z*\mathbb Z/3.
$$
:::

<1>3. The cellular chain complex in dimensions $2,1,0$ is
$$
0\longrightarrow\mathbb Z^2\xrightarrow{\partial_2}\mathbb Z^2\xrightarrow{0}\mathbb Z\longrightarrow0,
$$
with
$$
\partial_2=
\begin{pmatrix}
3&6\\
3&6
\end{pmatrix}.
$$
::: {.proof}
For a $2$-cell attached by a word in the wedge generators, the cellular boundary records the total exponent sums of the generators. The words $(ab)^3$ and $(ab)^6$ have exponent-sum vectors $(3,3)$ and $(6,6)$ respectively.
:::

<1>4. Hence
$$
\boxed{H_1(X;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z\oplus\mathbb Z/3}
$$
and
$$
\boxed{H_2(X;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z}.
$$
::: {.proof}
The image of $\partial_2$ is generated by $(3,3)$, so
$$
H_1\cong\mathbb Z^2/\langle(3,3)\rangle\cong\mathbb Z\oplus\mathbb Z/3.
$$
The kernel of $\partial_2$ consists of pairs $(x,y)$ satisfying $3x+6y=0$, hence is infinite cyclic generated by $(-2,1)$. Since there are no $3$-cells, this kernel is $H_2$.
:::

<1>5. Also $H_0(X)\cong\mathbb Z$ and $H_k(X)=0$ for $k\ge3$.
::: {.proof}
The complex is connected and $2$-dimensional.
:::
