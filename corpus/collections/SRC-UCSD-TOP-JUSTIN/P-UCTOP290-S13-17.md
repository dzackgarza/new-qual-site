::: {.problem}
For a closed oriented genus-two surface $\Sigma_2$,
compute $H_*(\Sigma_2\times S^1;\ZZ)$ and describe
submanifolds representing generators.
:::

::: {.solution}

Choose oriented simple closed curves
$a_1,b_1,a_2,b_2$ forming a basis of
$H_1(\Sigma_2;\ZZ)$. The homology of the surface is
$\ZZ$ in degrees $0$ and $2$, and $\ZZ^4$ in degree
$1$. Since all groups are free, the Künneth formula
has no torsion terms and gives
$$
H_k(\Sigma_2\times S^1;\ZZ)\cong
\begin{cases}
\ZZ,&k=0,3,\\
\ZZ^5,&k=1,2,\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
$$

A point represents $H_0$. In degree one, the four
curves $a_i\times\{t\},b_i\times\{t\}$ and the
circle $\{x\}\times S^1$ represent a basis. In
degree two, $\Sigma_2\times\{t\}$ and the four
embedded tori $a_i\times S^1,b_i\times S^1$
represent a basis. The whole oriented product
represents the generator of $H_3$.
:::
