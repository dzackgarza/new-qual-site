::: {.problem}
For a closed oriented genus-two surface $\Sigma_2$,
compute $H_*(\Sigma_2\times S^1;\ZZ)$ and describe
submanifolds representing generators.
:::

::: {.solution}

Let $\Sigma=\Sigma_2$ be the closed orientable surface of genus two, with
oriented simple closed curves $a_1,b_1,a_2,b_2$ whose classes form a basis of
$H_1(\Sigma;\ZZ)$, and let $M=\Sigma\times S^1$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #surface}

$H_0(\Sigma)=\ZZ$, $H_1(\Sigma)=\ZZ^4$, $H_2(\Sigma)=\ZZ$, and all higher
groups vanish.

::: pf-proof

$\Sigma$ is a closed connected orientable surface of genus two; it deformation
retracts onto a wedge of four circles, giving $H_1=\ZZ^4$ generated by
$a_1,b_1,a_2,b_2$, and its top class is $\ZZ$ in degree two with the oriented
fundamental class as generator. There is nothing above degree two.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #kunneth}

$H_k(\Sigma\times S^1;\ZZ)$ is $\ZZ$ for $k=0,3$, $\ZZ^5$ for $k=1,2$, and $0$
otherwise.

::: pf-proof

$H_*(S^1;\ZZ)$ is $\ZZ$ in degrees $0,1$ and free, so the Künneth formula has
no Tor terms and gives $H_k(\Sigma\times
S^1)\cong\bigoplus_{i+j=k}H_i(\Sigma)\otimes H_j(S^1)$. For $k=1$:
$H_1(\Sigma)\otimes H_0(S^1)\oplus H_0(\Sigma)\otimes
H_1(S^1)=\ZZ^4\oplus\ZZ$, of rank $5$. For $k=2$: $H_2\otimes H_0\oplus
H_1\otimes H_1=\ZZ\oplus\ZZ^4$, of rank $5$. For $k=3$: $H_2\otimes H_1=\ZZ$.
Degrees $0$ and above $3$ give $\ZZ$ and $0$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #generators}

A point represents $H_0$; the four curves $a_i\times\{t\}$, $b_i\times\{t\}$
together with $\{x\}\times S^1$ represent a basis of $H_1$; the surface
$\Sigma\times\{t\}$ together with the four tori $a_i\times S^1$, $b_i\times
S^1$ represent a basis of $H_2$; and $M$ itself represents $H_3$.

::: pf-proof

These are the images of the Künneth summands: $a_i\times\{t\}$ and
$b_i\times\{t\}$ come from $H_1(\Sigma)\otimes H_0(S^1)$, $\{x\}\times S^1$
from $H_0(\Sigma)\otimes H_1(S^1)$, $\Sigma\times\{t\}$ from
$H_2(\Sigma)\otimes H_0(S^1)$, and $a_i\times S^1$ and $b_i\times S^1$ from
$H_1(\Sigma)\otimes H_1(S^1)$. Each is a closed oriented submanifold of the
stated dimension, and the Künneth isomorphism carries the tensor product of
fundamental classes to the class of the product submanifold, so they are
linearly independent generators of the groups computed in step
[](#kunneth){.pf-ref}.

:::
