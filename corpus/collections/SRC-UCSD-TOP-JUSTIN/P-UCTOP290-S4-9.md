::: {.problem}
Identify the covers of $T^2=\RR^2/\ZZ^2$ corresponding to the
following subgroups of $\pi_1(T^2)=\ZZ^2$, and find their deck
transformation groups. The integers $p,q,r,s$ may be zero.

(a) $\langle(p,0)\rangle$.

(b) $\langle(p,0),(0,q)\rangle$.

(c) $\langle(p,q)\rangle$.

(d) $\langle(p,q),(r,s)\rangle$.
:::

::: {.solution}

Let $T^2=\RR^2/\ZZ^2$, so that $\pi_1(T^2)=\ZZ^2$ acts on the universal cover
$\RR^2$ by translations, and let $H\le\ZZ^2$ be a subgroup.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #principle}

The covering corresponding to $H$ is $\RR^2/H\to T^2$; it is connected, its
deck transformation group is $\ZZ^2/H$, and its total space is a plane if
$H=0$, an open cylinder if $H$ has rank $1$, and a torus if $H$ has rank $2$.

::: pf-proof

$\RR^2$ is simply connected, so the coverings of $T^2$ correspond to the
subgroups of $\pi_1(T^2)=\ZZ^2$ and the cover corresponding to $H$ is
$\RR^2/H$, which is connected because $\RR^2$ is. A deck transformation is a
homeomorphism of $\RR^2/H$ over $T^2$, that is a translation $x\mapsto
x+\lambda$ with $\lambda+\ZZ^2$ fixing $H$, or $\lambda\in N(H)$; since
$\ZZ^2$ is abelian $N(H)=\ZZ^2$ and the deck group is $\ZZ^2/H$. If $H=0$ the
total space is $\RR^2$. If $H=\ZZ v$ has rank one, an automorphism of $\ZZ^2$
carries $v$ to $e_1$, and then $\RR^2/H\cong\RR^2/\ZZ e_1\cong S^1\times\RR$,
an open cylinder. If $H$ has rank two it is a lattice of full rank and
$\RR^2/H$ is a torus. In pictures, the generators of $H$ are the translation
vectors along which the covering plane is glued to itself.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #casea}

For $H=\langle(p,0)\rangle$ with $p\ne0$ the cover is a cylinder with deck
group $\ZZ\oplus\ZZ/\abs{p}$, and for $p=0$ it is the plane with deck group
$\ZZ^2$.

::: pf-proof

$H=p\ZZ\times\{0\}$ has rank one when $p\ne0$, so the total space is a
cylinder by step [](#principle){.pf-ref}. The automorphism $(x,y)\mapsto(x,y)$
followed by reduction identifies $\ZZ^2/H$ with $\ZZ\oplus\ZZ/\abs{p}$,
generated by the class of $(0,1)$ together with the class of $(1,0)$ of order
$\abs p$. For $p=0$ the subgroup is trivial and step [](#principle){.pf-ref}
applies.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #caseb}

For $H=\langle(p,0),(0,q)\rangle$ with $p,q\ne0$ the cover is a torus tiled by
rectangles of side lengths $\abs p$ and $\abs q$, with deck group
$\ZZ/\abs{p}\oplus\ZZ/\abs{q}$; if exactly one of $p,q$ vanishes this is the
cylinder of step [](#casea){.pf-ref} in the corresponding direction, and if
both vanish it is the plane.

::: pf-proof

$H=p\ZZ\oplus q\ZZ$ has rank two when $p,q\ne0$, and
$\ZZ^2/H=(\ZZ/p)\oplus(\ZZ/q)$; the fundamental rectangle of $H$ is the stated
one, and the quotient of $\RR^2$ by its own period lattice is a torus. With
$q=0$ the subgroup is $p\ZZ\oplus0$, the case of step [](#casea){.pf-ref};
with $p=0$ the same argument with the coordinates exchanged; with both zero
the subgroup is trivial.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #casec}

For $H=\langle(p,q)\rangle\ne0$, put $d=\gcd(\abs p,\abs q)$; then $H$ is
generated by $d$ times the primitive vector $(p/d,q/d)$, the cover is a
cylinder, and its deck group is $\ZZ\oplus\ZZ/d$.

::: pf-proof

$(p,q)=d\,(p/d,q/d)$ with $\gcd(\abs p,\abs q)=d$, so $H=d\ZZ\cdot w$ for the
primitive $w=(p/d,q/d)$. An automorphism of $\ZZ^2$ carries $w$ to $(1,0)$, so
$\ZZ^2/H\cong\ZZ^2/\langle(d,0)\rangle\cong\ZZ\oplus\ZZ/d$. The subgroup has
rank one, so the total space is a cylinder by step [](#principle){.pf-ref}.
For $(0,0)$ the subgroup is trivial and the cover is the plane.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #cased}

For $H=\langle(p,q),(r,s)\rangle$ with $\Delta=ps-qr\ne0$, the two vectors
span a lattice of index $\abs\Delta$, so the cover is a torus whose deck group
is $\ZZ/d_1\oplus\ZZ/d_2$ with $d_1=\gcd(\abs p,\abs q,\abs r,\abs s)$ and
$d_2=\abs\Delta/d_1$.

::: pf-proof

The index of the lattice spanned by the two columns is
$\abs{\det}=\abs\Delta$, so $H$ has rank two and the cover is a torus. The
Smith normal form of the $2\times2$ integer matrix with those columns is
$\operatorname{diag}(d_1,d_1d_2)$ where $d_1$ is the gcd of all entries and
$d_1d_2$ is the product of the invariant factors, which is $\abs\Delta$. Hence
$\ZZ^2/H\cong\ZZ/d_1\oplus\ZZ/d_2$, and $d_1$ divides $d_2$. In lowest terms
$\Delta/d_1$ is the gcd of the three $2\times2$ minors.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #casedegen}

If $\Delta=0$ and at least one of the two vectors is nonzero, then $H$ has
rank one, the cover is a cylinder, and with $d=\gcd(\abs p,\abs q,\abs r,\abs
s)$ its deck group is $\ZZ\oplus\ZZ/d$; if both vectors vanish the cover is
the plane with deck group $\ZZ^2$.

::: pf-proof

$\Delta=0$ means the two vectors are parallel, so $H=\ZZ v$ for a primitive
$v$ scaled by $d$, the gcd of the four entries; the computation of step
[](#casec){.pf-ref} then gives $\ZZ^2/H\cong\ZZ\oplus\ZZ/d$ and a cylinder as
total space. With both vectors zero and $\Delta=0$ the subgroup is trivial and
step [](#principle){.pf-ref} applies. The zero case is the only one in which
the number $\abs\Delta/d_1$ of step [](#cased){.pf-ref} is not defined.

:::
