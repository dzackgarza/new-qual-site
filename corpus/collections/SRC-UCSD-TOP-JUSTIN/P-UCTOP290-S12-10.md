::: problem
Show that direct limit is an exact functor: that is, if
\[
\{0 \to A_\alpha \to B_\alpha \to C_\alpha \to 0\}_{\alpha \in I}
\]
is a directed system of short exact sequences of modules, then the corresponding sequence of direct limits is also exact.
Use this to prove the corresponding result for long exact sequences.
:::
