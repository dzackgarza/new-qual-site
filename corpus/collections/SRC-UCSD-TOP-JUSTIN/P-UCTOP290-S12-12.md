::: {.problem}
If
\[
\{0 \to A_*^\alpha \to B_*^\alpha \to C_*^\alpha \to 0\}_{\alpha \in I}
\]
is a directed system of short exact sequences of chain complexes, show that "direct limit commutes with taking the associated long exact sequence".
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. Taking directed colimits degreewise gives a short exact sequence of chain complexes
$$
0\to\varinjlim A_*^\alpha\to\varinjlim B_*^\alpha\to\varinjlim C_*^\alpha\to0.
$$
::: {.proof}
For each degree $n$, the sequence
$$
0\to A_n^\alpha\to B_n^\alpha\to C_n^\alpha\to0
$$
is a directed system of short exact sequences. Exactness of directed colimits gives a short exact sequence in each degree, and naturality of the differentials makes these into chain maps.
:::

<1>2. The sequence in <1>1 has its usual long exact homology sequence
$$
\cdots\to H_n(\varinjlim A_*^\alpha)\to H_n(\varinjlim B_*^\alpha)
\to H_n(\varinjlim C_*^\alpha)\xrightarrow{\partial}\cdots.
$$
::: {.proof}
Every short exact sequence of chain complexes induces a natural long exact sequence in homology.
:::

<1>3. For each of the three systems,
$$
H_n(\varinjlim A_*^\alpha)\cong\varinjlim H_n(A_*^\alpha),
$$
and similarly for $B$ and $C$.
::: {.proof}
Taking homology commutes with directed colimits by the preceding result.
:::

<1>4. Under the identifications in <1>3, the connecting homomorphism $\partial$ in <1>2 is the direct limit of the stagewise connecting homomorphisms $\partial_\alpha$.
::: {.proof}
The connecting map is constructed by choosing a cycle in $C_n$, lifting it to $B_n$, applying the differential, and then viewing the result in $A_{n-1}$. This construction is natural with respect to morphisms of short exact sequences of chain complexes. Hence the structure maps commute with every $\partial_\alpha$, so these maps induce a colimit map, and the representative construction is exactly the connecting map for the colimit sequence.
:::

<1>5. Thus the direct limit of the stagewise long exact sequences is canonically the long exact sequence associated to the direct-limit short exact sequence.
::: {.proof}
Combine <1>2--<1>4. In particular, corresponding terms and all maps, including connecting maps, agree under the canonical colimit/homology isomorphisms.
:::
:::
