::: problem
If
\[
\{0 \to A_*^\alpha \to B_*^\alpha \to C_*^\alpha \to 0\}_{\alpha \in I}
\]
is a directed system of short exact sequences of chain complexes, show that "direct limit commutes with taking the associated long exact sequence".
:::
