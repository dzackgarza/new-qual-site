::: {.problem}
Let $V=S^1\times D^2$ be a solid torus and
$T=\partial V=S^1\times S^1$. Compute and describe
generators for $H_*(V;\ZZ)$, $H_*(V,T;\ZZ)$,
$H^*(V;\ZZ)$, and $H^*(V,T;\ZZ)$. Describe the
intersection pairings
$H_i(V;\ZZ)\times H_{3-i}(V,T;\ZZ)\to\ZZ$.
:::

::: {.solution}

The deformation retraction $V\simeq S^1$ gives
$H_0(V)\cong H_1(V)\cong\ZZ$, generated by a
point and the oriented core
$c=S^1\times\{0\}$; all other absolute homology
vanishes. In $H_1(T)\cong\ZZ^2$, the meridian
$m=\{x\}\times\partial D^2$ maps to zero in
$H_1(V)$, while the longitude maps to $[c]$.
The long exact sequence of $(V,T)$ therefore gives
$$
H_i(V,T;\ZZ)\cong
\begin{cases}
\ZZ,&i=2,3,\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
$$
The relative meridional disk
$d=\{x\}\times D^2$ generates $H_2(V,T)$,
and the orientation class $[V,T]$ generates
$H_3(V,T)$.

All these groups are free. The universal coefficient
theorem gives
$$
H^i(V;\ZZ)\cong
\begin{cases}
\ZZ,&i=0,1,\\
0,&\text{otherwise},
\end{cases}
\qquad
H^i(V,T;\ZZ)\cong
\begin{cases}
\ZZ,&i=2,3,\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
$$
The degree-one absolute generator evaluates to
$1$ on $[c]$. The relative degree-two and
degree-three generators evaluate to $1$ on
$[d]$ and $[V,T]$, respectively.

The only pairings with two nonzero groups occur
at $i=0,1$. An interior point has intersection
number $1$ with $[V,T]$. The core $c$ meets
the meridional disk $d$ transversely at one
point, with sign $+1$ after choosing the
compatible orientations. Thus both pairings
are multiplication $\ZZ\times\ZZ\to\ZZ$.
:::
