::: {.problem}
Using a similar argument in singular (co)homology, calculate the cohomology ring of the closed orientable surface $\Sigma_g$ of genus $g$.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

Choose the standard symplectic basis
$$
a_1,b_1,\ldots,a_g,b_g\in H_1(\Sigma_g;\mathbb Z)
$$
and let
$$
\alpha_1,\beta_1,\ldots,\alpha_g,\beta_g\in H^1(\Sigma_g;\mathbb Z)
$$
be the Poincaré-dual basis. Let $\omega\in H^2(\Sigma_g;\mathbb Z)$ be the orientation class.

::: pf-proof

A closed orientable genus-$g$ surface has
$$
H_0\cong H_2\cong\mathbb Z,\qquad H_1\cong\mathbb Z^{2g},
$$
and the standard handle curves form a symplectic basis for the intersection pairing.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

The only nonzero products of degree-one basis classes are
$$
\alpha_i\smile\beta_j=\delta_{ij}\omega,
\qquad
\beta_i\smile\alpha_j=-\delta_{ij}\omega.
$$
All products $\alpha_i\smile\alpha_j$ and $\beta_i\smile\beta_j$ vanish.

::: pf-proof

Under Poincaré duality, evaluation of the cup product on the fundamental class equals algebraic intersection number:
$$
\langle u\smile v,[\Sigma_g]\rangle=\operatorname{PD}(u)\cdot\operatorname{PD}(v).
$$
The standard curves satisfy $a_i\cdot b_j=\delta_{ij}$, $b_i\cdot a_j=-\delta_{ij}$, and all $a_i\cdot a_j$, $b_i\cdot b_j$ vanish.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Hence $H^*(\Sigma_g;\mathbb Z)$ is the graded ring generated by
$$
1,\alpha_1,\beta_1,\ldots,\alpha_g,\beta_g,\omega
$$
with $|\alpha_i|=|\beta_i|=1$, $|\omega|=2$, relations from step [](#s2){.pf-ref}, and every product involving $\omega$ and a positive-degree class equal to zero.

::: pf-proof

There is no cohomology above degree $2$, so all products of total degree greater than $2$ vanish. The additive groups and all remaining degree-two products are determined by steps [](#s1){.pf-ref} and [](#s2){.pf-ref}.

:::
