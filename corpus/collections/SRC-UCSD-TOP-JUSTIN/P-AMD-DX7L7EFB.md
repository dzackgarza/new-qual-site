::: {.problem}
Identify $\pi_1(M, [(1, \frac{1}{2})])$, and identify the class of $\partial M$.
:::

::: {.solution}
> **AI-Generated Solution** (Gemini 3.7 Flash)

**Goal:** Let $M = ([0, 1] \times [0, 1]) / ((0, y) \sim (1, 1-y))$ be the standard Möbius strip with basepoint $x_0 = [(1, 1/2)] = [(0, 1/2)]$.

1. Compute the fundamental group $\pi_1(M, x_0)$.

2. Identify the homotopy class $[\partial M] \in \pi_1(M, x_0)$ represented by its boundary circle.

<1>1. Compute $\pi_1(M, x_0)$.
<2>1. Define the center circle / core curve $\gamma_0 \colon [0, 1] \to M$ by $\gamma_0(t) = [(t, 1/2)]$.
<2>2. $\gamma_0(0) = [(0, 1/2)] = [(1, 1/2)] = \gamma_0(1) = x_0$, so $\gamma_0$ is a continuous loop based at $x_0$.
<2>3. Let $C = \gamma_0([0, 1]) \subset M$ be the core circle, homeomorphic to $S^1$.
<2>4. Define $H \colon M \times [0, 1] \to M$ by $H([(x, y)], s) = [(x, (1-s)y + s(1/2))]$.
<2>5. $H$ is well-defined: $H([(1, 1-y)], s) = [(1, (1-s)(1-y) + s/2)] = [(1, 1 - ((1-s)y + s/2))] = H([(0, y)], s)$.
<2>6. $H$ is a strong deformation retraction of $M$ onto the core circle $C$.
<2>7. Thus $\pi_1(M, x_0) \cong \pi_1(C, x_0) \cong \mathbb{Z}$, generated by the homotopy class $[\gamma_0]$.
<2>8. Proof: By deformation retraction onto $S^1$.
Q.E.D.

<1>2. Identify the homotopy class of the boundary $\partial M$.
<2>1. The boundary of $M$ comes from the horizontal edges $y = 0$ and $y = 1$ in $[0, 1] \times [0, 1]$.
<2>2. Under the gluing $(0, y) \sim (1, 1-y)$, $(0, 0) \sim (1, 1)$ and $(0, 1) \sim (1, 0)$.
<2>3. A continuous parameterization of the single boundary loop $\partial M$ starting and ending at $p_0 = [(0, 0)] = [(1, 1)]$ is given by: $$\beta(t) = \begin{cases} [(2t, 0)] & \text{for } t \in [0, 1/2], \\ [(2t-1, 1)] & \text{for } t \in [1/2, 1]. \end{cases}$$ <2>4. To compare with basepoint $x_0 = [(0, 1/2)]$, connect $x_0$ to $p_0$ by the straight segment $\eta(s) = [(0, (1-s)/2)]$ for $s \in [0, 1]$.
<2>5. The based boundary loop is $\alpha = \eta \cdot \beta \cdot \eta^{-1}$.
<2>6. Applying the deformation retraction $H$ from <1>1 to $\beta$:

- On $t \in [0, 1/2]$, $H([(2t, 0)], 1) = [(2t, 1/2)] = \gamma_0(2t)$, which traverses the core loop once.

- On $t \in [1/2, 1]$, $H([(2t-1, 1)], 1) = [(2t-1, 1/2)] = \gamma_0(2t-1)$, which traverses the core loop a second time in the same direction.
  <2>7. Thus $H(\alpha, 1) \simeq \gamma_0 \cdot \gamma_0 = \gamma_0^2$.
  <2>8. Therefore, the class of the boundary loop is $[\partial M] = [\gamma_0]^2 = 2 \in \mathbb{Z} \cong \pi_1(M, x_0)$ (i.e. twice the generator).
  <2>9. Proof: By explicit calculation under the deformation retraction.
  Q.E.D.

<1>3. Conclusion.
<2>1. $\pi_1(M, x_0) \cong \mathbb{Z} = \langle a \rangle$, and $[\partial M] = a^2 = 2 \in \mathbb{Z}$.
<2>2. Proof: By <1>1 and <1>2. Q.E.D.
