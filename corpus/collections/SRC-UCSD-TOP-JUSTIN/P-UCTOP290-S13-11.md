::: {.problem}
Let $M^n$ be a connected closed oriented manifold and
let $p:N\to M$ be a connected $d$-sheeted cover.
Show that $N$ is a closed manifold, has a natural
orientation, and that $\deg p=d$.
:::

::: {.solution}

Let $M^n$ be a connected closed oriented manifold and $p\colon N\to M$ a
connected $d$-sheeted covering.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #closed}

$N$ is a closed $n$-manifold.

::: pf-proof

A covering map is a local homeomorphism, so every point of $N$ has a
neighbourhood homeomorphic to an open subset of $\RR^n$, namely a sheet over a
coordinate ball of $M$; so $N$ is an $n$-manifold, and it has no boundary
because $M$ has none. For compactness, take a finite cover of $M$ by evenly
covered open sets $U_1,\dots,U_r$, possible by compactness of $M$, and closed
subsets $V_i\subseteq U_i$ with $V_1\cup\dots\cup V_r=M$; then
$p^{-1}(V_i)\cong V_i\times\{1,\dots,d\}$ is compact, being a finite disjoint
union of closed subsets of the compact $M$ pulled back, and finitely many of
them cover $N$. So $N$ is compact and has no boundary, that is closed.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #oriented}

$N$ carries a natural orientation, and $p$ is orientation-preserving for it.

::: pf-proof

An orientation of $M$ is a continuous choice of generator of
$H_n(M,M\setminus\{x\})$ at each $x$. At a point $y\in N$ the map $p$ is a
homeomorphism of a neighbourhood of $y$ onto a neighbourhood of $p(y)$, so it
induces an isomorphism $H_n(N,N\setminus\{y\})\to H_n(M,M\setminus\{p(y)\})$,
and there is a unique generator of the source mapping to the chosen generator
at $p(y)$; declaring that generator to be the orientation at $y$ gives a
continuous choice, because on a sheet over an evenly covered set it is the
pullback of a continuous choice on that set. Hence $N$ is oriented, and by
construction $p$ carries the orientation at every point to the orientation of
$M$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #degree}

$\deg p=d$, that is $p_*[N]=d[M]$.

::: pf-proof

Take a point $x\in M$ that is a regular value of $p$ — every point is, since
$p$ is a local homeomorphism — and whose fibre meets no boundary; the fibre is
$p^{-1}(x)=\{y_1,\dots,y_d\}$. The degree formula for a map of closed oriented
$n$-manifolds reads $p_*[N]=\big(\sum_{j}\deg(p\text{ at }y_j)\big)[M]$, the
sum of the local degrees over the fibre. By step [](#oriented){.pf-ref} each
$p$ at $y_j$ is orientation-preserving, so each local degree is $+1$, and the
sum is $d$. Hence $\deg p=d$.

:::
