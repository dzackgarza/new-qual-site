::: {.problem}
Let $M^n$ be a connected closed oriented manifold and
let $p:N\to M$ be a connected $d$-sheeted cover.
Show that $N$ is a closed manifold, has a natural
orientation, and that $\deg p=d$.
:::

::: {.solution}

A covering map is a local homeomorphism, so $N$ is an
$n$-manifold without boundary. Each fiber has $d$
points. A finite cover of a compact space is compact:
cover $M$ by finitely many evenly covered open sets
whose smaller closed subsets still cover $M$, and use
their finitely many closed lifts to cover $N$.
Thus $N$ is closed.

Orient each chart of $N$ by pulling back the orientation
of its image in $M$ through $p$. These local orientations
agree on overlaps. For any $x\in M$, the $d$ preimages
of $x$ each have local degree $+1$. Therefore the sum
of local degrees is $d$, and
$$
p_*[N]=d[M].
$$
:::
