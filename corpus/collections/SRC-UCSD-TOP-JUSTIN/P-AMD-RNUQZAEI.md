::: {.problem}
The three-sphere
$$
S^3=\{(z,w)\in\mathbb C^2\mid |z|^2+|w|^2=1\}
$$
is the union of
$$
H_1=\{(z,w)\in S^3\mid |z|^2\leq \tfrac12\}
\quad\text{and}\quad
H_2=\{(z,w)\in S^3\mid |w|^2\leq \tfrac12\}.
$$
Check that each piece is a solid torus, homeomorphic to $B^2\times S^1$, and that their intersection is a torus $T$.

Let $K$ be a torus knot of type $(p,q)$ in $T$, where $p$ and $q$ are coprime positive integers.
Thus, $K$ travels $p$ times longitudinally and $q$ times meridionally.
By partitioning $S^3-K$ with $H_1-K$ and $H_2-K$, calculate a presentation for $\pi_1(S^3-K)$.
:::
