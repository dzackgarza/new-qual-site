::: {.problem}
The three-sphere
$$
S^3=\{(z,w)\in\mathbb C^2\mid |z|^2+|w|^2=1\}
$$
is the union of
$$
H_1=\{(z,w)\in S^3\mid |z|^2\leq \tfrac12\}
\quad\text{and}\quad
H_2=\{(z,w)\in S^3\mid |w|^2\leq \tfrac12\}.
$$
Check that each piece is a solid torus, homeomorphic to $B^2\times S^1$, and that their intersection is a torus $T$.

Let $K$ be a torus knot of type $(p,q)$ in $T$, where $p$ and $q$ are coprime positive integers.
Thus, $K$ travels $p$ times longitudinally and $q$ times meridionally.
By partitioning $S^3-K$ with $H_1-K$ and $H_2-K$, calculate a presentation for $\pi_1(S^3-K)$.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. Each $H_i$ is a solid torus, and $H_1\cap H_2$ is the torus
$$
T=\{(z,w)\in S^3:|z|=|w|=1/\sqrt2\}.
$$
::: {.proof}
For $H_1$, write $z\in D^2_{1/\sqrt2}$ freely and write
$$
w=\sqrt{1-|z|^2}\,u,\qquad u\in S^1.
$$
This gives $H_1\cong D^2\times S^1$. The analogous parametrization gives $H_2\cong S^1\times D^2$. Their intersection is characterized by equality in both inequalities, hence $|z|=|w|=1/\sqrt2$, a product of two circles.
:::

<1>2. Put $U_i=H_i\setminus K$. Each $U_i$ deformation-retracts to the core circle of the solid torus, so
$$
\pi_1(U_1)=\langle x\rangle\cong\mathbb Z,\qquad
\pi_1(U_2)=\langle y\rangle\cong\mathbb Z.
$$
::: {.proof}
The knot $K$ lies on the boundary torus. Push the boundary slightly inward away from $K$ and then use the ordinary radial deformation retraction of a solid torus onto its core circle. This gives a deformation retraction of each $H_i\setminus K$ onto its core.
:::

<1>3. The intersection $U_1\cap U_2=T\setminus K$ is an open annulus, hence has fundamental group $\mathbb Z$, generated by a loop parallel to $K$.
::: {.proof}
Removing an essential simple closed curve from a torus leaves a cylinder. A core circle of this cylinder is isotopic in $T$ to $K$.
:::

<1>4. Under the inclusions into $U_1$ and $U_2$, this generator maps to $x^p$ and $y^q$, respectively.
::: {.proof}
By hypothesis the $(p,q)$ torus knot travels $p$ times longitudinally in the first solid torus and $q$ times longitudinally in the complementary solid torus (equivalently, meridian and longitude are interchanged between the two Heegaard solid tori). The meridional component bounds a disk in the corresponding solid torus and hence contributes trivially to its fundamental group. Thus the class of $K$ becomes $x^p$ in $U_1$ and $y^q$ in $U_2$.
:::

<1>5. Seifert--van Kampen therefore gives
$$
\boxed{\pi_1(S^3\setminus K)\cong\langle x,y\mid x^p=y^q\rangle.}
$$
::: {.proof}
The union $U_1\cup U_2$ is $S^3\setminus K$. Van Kampen identifies the two images of the generator of $\pi_1(U_1\cap U_2)$, imposing the single relation $x^p=y^q$ on the free product $\langle x\rangle*\langle y\rangle$.
:::
