::: {.problem}
Suppose $A,B$ are disjoint, compact subspaces of the Hausdorff topological space $X$.
Prove there are open subsets $U,V$ of $X$ such that $A\subseteq U$, $B\subseteq V$, and $U\cap V=\varnothing$.
:::
