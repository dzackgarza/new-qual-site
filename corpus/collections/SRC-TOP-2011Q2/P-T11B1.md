::: {.problem}
Let $X$ be the space obtained by deleting three distinct points from $\RR^2$.
Compute $\pi_1(X)$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let the deleted points be $p_1,p_2,p_3$, and choose pairwise disjoint closed discs $D_i$ centered at $p_i$.

<1>1. $X$ deformation retracts onto a graph $G$ consisting of the three circles $\partial D_i$ joined to one common basepoint by pairwise disjoint arcs.

::: {.proof}
Choose a closed disc $D$ containing the $D_i$ in its interior.
Radial contraction outside $D$ retracts $X$ onto $D\setminus\{p_1,p_2,p_3\}$.
Pushing each punctured disc $D_i\setminus\{p_i\}$ radially onto $\partial D_i$ gives a deformation retraction onto the compact planar surface $M=D\setminus\bigcup_{i=1}^3\operatorname{int}(D_i)$.
Choose three pairwise disjoint arcs from one point of $\partial D$ to the three inner boundary circles.
Cutting $M$ along these arcs yields a disc whose boundary consists of $\partial D$, two copies of each arc, and the three circles $\partial D_i$.
This disc deformation retracts onto its boundary minus the open outer arc coming from $\partial D$, and regluing gives a deformation retraction of $M$ onto $G$.
:::

<1>2. $G$ is homotopy equivalent to $S^1\vee S^1\vee S^1$.

::: {.proof}
The joining arcs form a tree.
Collapsing that tree to the common basepoint is a homotopy equivalence of graphs and leaves the three circles $\partial D_i$ as three wedge summands.
:::

<1>3. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
By steps <1>1 and <1>2, $X\simeq S^1\vee S^1\vee S^1$.
By the Seifert--van Kampen theorem,
$$\pi_1(X)\cong\pi_1(S^1\vee S^1\vee S^1)\cong\ZZ*\ZZ*\ZZ=\boxed{F_3},$$
the free group on three generators.
:::
:::
