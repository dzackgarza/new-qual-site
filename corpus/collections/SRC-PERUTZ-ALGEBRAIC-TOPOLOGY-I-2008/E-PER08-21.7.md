::: {.problem}
What is the relation between H$\\ast$(X; R) and H$\\ast$(X)?
:::

::: {.solution}
The relation is the cohomological universal coefficient theorem.
For every $n$ there is a natural short exact sequence
\[
0\longrightarrow
\operatorname{Ext}_{\mathbb Z}(H_{n-1}(X;\mathbb Z),R)
\longrightarrow H^n(X;R)
\longrightarrow
\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb Z}(H_n(X;\mathbb Z),R)
\longrightarrow0.
\]
It splits as a sequence of abelian groups, but in general not naturally.

Thus integral homology determines the coefficient-$R$ cohomology groups up to noncanonical isomorphism.
In particular, if $H_{n-1}(X;\mathbb Z)$ is free, the Ext term vanishes and
\[
H^n(X;R)\cong\operatorname{Hom}(H_n(X;\mathbb Z),R).
\]
More generally the torsion in $H_{n-1}$ is precisely what contributes the Ext correction term.
:::
