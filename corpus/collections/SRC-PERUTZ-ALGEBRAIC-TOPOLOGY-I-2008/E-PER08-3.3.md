::: {.problem}
The free product G1 ∗ G2 of groups G1 and G2 is the push-out of the
diagram G1 ← {1} → G2. Define D∞ as the subgroup of the group of affine transformations R → R generated by x ↦ −x and x ↦ x+1. Prove that D∞ ∼= (Z/2)∗(Z/2).
:::
