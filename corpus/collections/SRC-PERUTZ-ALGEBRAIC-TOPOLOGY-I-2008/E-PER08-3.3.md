::: {.problem}
The free product $G_1*G_2$ is the pushout of $G_1\leftarrow\{1\}\to G_2$.

Let $D_\infty$ be the subgroup of the affine transformations of $\mathbb R$ generated by
\[
x\longmapsto -x,
\qquad
x\longmapsto x+1.
\]
Prove that
\[
D_\infty\cong (\mathbb Z/2)*(\mathbb Z/2).
\]
:::
