::: {.problem}
Show that if B ⊂ A ⊂ X, one has a long exact sequence of the triple
· · · → Hi(A, B) → Hi(X, B) → Hi(X, A) → Hi−1(A, B) → . . . ,
and describe the maps in this sequence.
:::
