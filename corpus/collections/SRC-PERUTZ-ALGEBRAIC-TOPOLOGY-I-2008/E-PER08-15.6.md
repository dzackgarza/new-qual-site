::: {.problem}
Show that if a group G acts freely on M = Sn, then the orientation
character π1(M/G) = G → Z/2 of the quotient manifold is given by G ∋ g ↦
deg(g) ∈ {±1} = Z/2. For which n is RP n orientable?
:::
