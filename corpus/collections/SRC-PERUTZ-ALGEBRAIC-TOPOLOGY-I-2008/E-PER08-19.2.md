::: {.problem}
Use cellular homology to compute H∗(Σg), where Σg is a standard closed, orientable surface of genus g (defined, for instance, as a quotient of the 4ggon). Also, compute H∗(Σg#RP 2), the connected sum of Σg and RP 2.
:::
