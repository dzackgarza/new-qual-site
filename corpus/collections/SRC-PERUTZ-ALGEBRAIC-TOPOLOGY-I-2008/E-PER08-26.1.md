::: {.problem}
Let M be a compact, connected, oriented 3-manifold.
Determine the graded ring H$\\ast$(M) when (i) π1(M) is finite; (ii) H1(M) $\\cong$ Z; (iii) H1(M) $\\cong$ Z2. Exhibit two such 3-manifolds with non-isomorphic cohomology rings, both of which have H1 $\\cong$ Z3.
:::
