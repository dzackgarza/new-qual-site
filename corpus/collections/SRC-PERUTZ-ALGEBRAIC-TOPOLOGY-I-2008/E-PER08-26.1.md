::: {.problem}
Let M be a compact, connected, oriented 3-manifold. Determine the
graded ring H∗(M) when (i) π1(M) is finite; (ii) H1(M) ∼= Z; (iii) H1(M) ∼= Z2.
Exhibit two such 3-manifolds with non-isomorphic cohomology rings, both of which
have H1 ∼= Z3.
:::
