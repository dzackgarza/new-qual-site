::: {.problem}
A special case of the Lefschetz fixed point theorem states that if X is a finite CW complex, and g: X → X a map without fixed points, then ∑ p≥0 (−1)p tr Hp(g) = 0. Here Hp(g) is the induced map on H∗(X; Q). What restrictions does this entail for groups acting freely on CP n?
:::
