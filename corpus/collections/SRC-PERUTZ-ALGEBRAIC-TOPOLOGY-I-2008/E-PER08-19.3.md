::: {.problem}
Describe how the product X × Y of finite CW complexes X and Y inherits a structure of CW complex.
(1) Compute the Euler characteristic χ(X × Y ) in terms of χ(X) and χ(Y ). (2) Show that Ccell ∗ (X × Y ) = Ccell ∗ (X) ⊗ Ccell ∗ (Y ) as a graded abelian group.
(You are not asked to compute the differential.)
(3) Compute H∗(Sn × Sn).
(4) Let X = (S3)×n = S3 × · · · × S3. Show that H3p(X) is isomorphic to the exterior power ΛpZn, and that Hq(X) = 0 if q is not a multiple of 3.
:::
