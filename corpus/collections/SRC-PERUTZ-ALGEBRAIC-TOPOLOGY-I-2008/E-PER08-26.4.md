::: {.problem}
Let $n>1$. Show that for every map $f\colon S^{2n}\to\CP^n$, the induced map $\tilde f_*$ on reduced homology is zero.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

The map $f_*$ is zero on $H_{2n}$.

::: pf-proof

Let $u\in H^2(\CP^n;\ZZ)$ be the hyperplane generator, so $u^n$ generates $H^{2n}(\CP^n;\ZZ)$.
Since $n>1$,
\[
H^2(S^{2n};\ZZ)=0,
\]
so $f^*u=0$ and hence
\[
f^*(u^n)=(f^*u)^n=0.
\]
Write
\[
f_*[S^{2n}]=d[\CP^n].
\]
Naturality of evaluation gives
\[
d
=\langle u^n,f_*[S^{2n}]\rangle
=\langle f^*(u^n),[S^{2n}]\rangle
=0.
\]

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

All reduced homology groups of $S^{2n}$ other than $\widetilde H_{2n}$ vanish, so step [](#s1){.pf-ref} gives $\widetilde f_*=0$.

:::
