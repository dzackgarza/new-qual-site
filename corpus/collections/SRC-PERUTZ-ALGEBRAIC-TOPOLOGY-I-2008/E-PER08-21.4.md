::: {.problem}
Working over Z, compute Ext(A, Q/Z) for an arbitrary finitely generated abelian group A.
:::

::: {.solution}
$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Ext}_{\ZZ}(A,\QQ/\ZZ)=0}
$$
for every finitely generated abelian group $A$; in fact the same holds for every abelian group $A$.

::: pf

::: pf-step

$\QQ/\ZZ$ is divisible.

::: pf-proof

For every $n\ge1$, multiplication by $n$ on $\QQ/\ZZ$ is surjective: the class of $q/n$ maps to the class of $q$.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Divisible abelian groups are injective $\ZZ$-modules.

::: pf-proof

By Baer's criterion for $\ZZ$, a module is injective exactly when every homomorphism from an ideal $n\ZZ$ extends to $\ZZ$.
If $f(n)=x$ and the target is divisible, choose $y$ with $ny=x$ and extend by $1\mapsto y$.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Ext vanishes against an injective second argument.

::: pf-proof

Derived functors $\operatorname{Ext}^1(A,-)$ vanish on injective modules.
Hence the displayed Ext group is zero.

For a finitely generated decomposition $A\cong\ZZ^r\oplus\bigoplus_i\ZZ/n_i$, this can also be checked directly: the free summands contribute zero and
$$
\operatorname{Ext}(\ZZ/n_i,\QQ/\ZZ)
\cong(\QQ/\ZZ)/n_i(\QQ/\ZZ)=0.
$$

:::
