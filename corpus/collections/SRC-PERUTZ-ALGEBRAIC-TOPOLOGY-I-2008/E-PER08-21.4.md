::: {.problem}
Working over Z, compute Ext(A, Q/Z) for an arbitrary finitely generated abelian group A.
:::

::: {.solution}
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{Ext}_{\mathbb Z}(A,\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z)=0}
\]
for every finitely generated abelian group $A$; in fact the same holds for every abelian group $A$.

<1>1. $\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z$ is divisible.
::: {.proof}
For every $n\ge1$, multiplication by $n$ on $\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z$ is surjective: the class of $q/n$ maps to the class of $q$.
:::

<1>2. Divisible abelian groups are injective $\mathbb Z$-modules.
::: {.proof}
By Baer's criterion for $\mathbb Z$, a module is injective exactly when every homomorphism from an ideal $n\mathbb Z$ extends to $\mathbb Z$.
If $f(n)=x$ and the target is divisible, choose $y$ with $ny=x$ and extend by $1\mapsto y$.
:::

<1>3. Ext vanishes against an injective second argument.
::: {.proof}
Derived functors $\operatorname{Ext}^1(A,-)$ vanish on injective modules.
Hence the displayed Ext group is zero.

For a finitely generated decomposition $A\cong\mathbb Z^r\oplus\bigoplus_i\mathbb Z/n_i$, this can also be checked directly: the free summands contribute zero and
\[
\operatorname{Ext}(\mathbb Z/n_i,\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z)
\cong(\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z)/n_i(\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z)=0.
\]
:::
:::
