::: {.problem}
The following are covering maps: (1) The quotient map R → R/Z. (2) The map S1 → S1, eit ↦ eint.
(3) The product of covering maps (e.g. Rn → (R/Z)n = Rn/Zn).
(4) The quotient map Sn → RP n.
:::
