::: {.problem}
The following are covering maps: (1) The quotient map R $\\to$ R/Z. (2) The map S1 $\\to$ S1, eit $\\mapsto$ eint.
(3) The product of covering maps (e.g. Rn $\\to$ (R/Z)n = Rn/Zn).
(4) The quotient map Sn $\\to$ RP n.
:::
