::: {.problem}
Use singular cohomology to compute H$\\ast$(S1 $\\times$ S1) as a ring.
[You may find it helpful to think of S1 $\\times$ S1 as a $\\Delta$-complex.]
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $a,b\in H^1(S^1\times S^1;\mathbb Z)$ be the pullbacks of the standard generator of $H^1(S^1)$ under the two projections.
Then
\[
\boxed{H^*(S^1\times S^1;\mathbb Z)\cong\Lambda_{\mathbb Z}(a,b),\qquad |a|=|b|=1.}
\]
Explicitly,
\[
H^0=\mathbb Z,\qquad H^1=\mathbb Za\oplus\mathbb Zb,\qquad H^2=\mathbb Z(a\smile b),
\]
and higher groups vanish, with
\[
a^2=b^2=0,\qquad b\smile a=-a\smile b.
\]

Using the standard two-triangle $\Delta$-complex on the torus, choose cellular/singular $1$-cocycles dual to the horizontal and vertical edge cycles.
The Alexander--Whitney formula evaluates $a\smile b$ as $1$ on an oriented fundamental $2$-cycle, so it generates $H^2$.
Graded commutativity gives $b\smile a=-a\smile b$, and $a^2=b^2=0$ because each factor generator pulls back from $S^1$, where $H^2(S^1)=0$.
:::
