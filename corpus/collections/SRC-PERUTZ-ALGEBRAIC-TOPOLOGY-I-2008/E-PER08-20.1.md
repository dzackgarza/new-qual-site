::: {.problem}
Show that if $E_*$ is a homology theory then $\widetilde E_*$ is a reduced homology theory.
:::

::: {.solution}
Fix a based CW complex $(X,b)$ and define
\[
\widetilde E_n(X)=E_n(X,\{b\}).
\]
Then the Eilenberg--Steenrod axioms for $E_*$ imply the reduced axioms listed in the notes.

<1>1. Dimension.
::: {.proof}
For $S^0=\{b,c\}$, excision and additivity identify
\[
E_n(S^0,\{b\})\cong E_n(\{c\}).
\]
Hence $\widetilde E_n(S^0)=0$ for $n\ne0$ and $\widetilde E_0(S^0)\cong\mathbb Z$.
:::

<1>2. Exactness for $A\subseteq X$.
::: {.proof}
For a based subcomplex $A$ containing $b$, the long exact sequence of the triple can be expressed as
\[
\widetilde E_n(A)\to\widetilde E_n(X)\to E_n(X,A).
\]
By excision, collapsing $A$ to the basepoint gives a natural isomorphism
\[
E_n(X,A)\cong\widetilde E_n(X/A).
\]
Thus
\[
\widetilde E_n(A)\to\widetilde E_n(X)\to\widetilde E_n(X/A)
\]
is exact.
:::

<1>3. Suspension.
::: {.proof}
Let $CX$ be the reduced cone.
It is contractible, so $\widetilde E_*(CX)=0$.
The long exact sequence of the pair $(CX,X)$ gives natural isomorphisms
\[
\widetilde E_n(X)\xrightarrow{\ \cong\ }E_{n+1}(CX,X).
\]
Collapsing $X\subset CX$ identifies $CX/X$ with the reduced suspension $\Sigma X$, and excision gives
\[
E_{n+1}(CX,X)\cong\widetilde E_{n+1}(\Sigma X).
\]
Their composite is the suspension isomorphism, natural in based maps.
:::

<1>4. Additivity.
::: {.proof}
The wedge $\bigvee_iX_i$ is obtained from the disjoint union by identifying all basepoints.
Relative to the common basepoint, additivity for $E_*$ gives
\[
\widetilde E_n\!\left(\bigvee_iX_i\right)
\cong\bigoplus_i E_n(X_i,\{b_i\})
=\bigoplus_i\widetilde E_n(X_i).
\]
:::
:::
