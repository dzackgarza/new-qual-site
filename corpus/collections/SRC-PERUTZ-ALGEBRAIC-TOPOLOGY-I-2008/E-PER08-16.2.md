::: {.problem}
Prove that the splittings of the universal coefficient theorem cannot
be chosen naturally in X by means of the following example.
Take an embedding
i: R2 → RP 2, let D = i({z ∈ R2 : |z| < 1}), and let q be the quotient map
RP 2 → RP 2/(RP 2∖D) ∼= S2. Show that the k = Z/2 universal coefficients sequences
for RP 2 and S2 cannot be split compatibly with q.
:::
