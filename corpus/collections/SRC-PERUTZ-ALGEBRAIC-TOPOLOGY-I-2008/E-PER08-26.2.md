::: {.problem}
Show that the Euler characteristic of a compact, orientable, odd-dimensional manifold is zero.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $M$ be a compact orientable manifold without boundary, of odd dimension $d = 2m + 1$, and let $b_i = \dim_\QQ H_i(M; \QQ)$.
These are finite because $M$ is compact.
The hypothesis that $M$ has no boundary is needed: the closed ball $D^3$ has $\chi = 1$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #betti}
$b_i = b_{d-i}$ for all $i$.

::: pf-proof
Poincaré duality gives $H^i(M; \QQ) \cong H_{d-i}(M; \QQ)$.
Over a field, the universal coefficient theorem gives $H^i(M; \QQ) \cong \Hom(H_i(M; \QQ), \QQ)$, of dimension $b_i$.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
$\chi(M) = 0$.

::: pf-proof
Pair the index $i$ with $d - i$ for $0 \leq i \leq m$:
$$
\chi(M) = \sum_{i=0}^{d} (-1)^i b_i = \sum_{i=0}^{m} \left( (-1)^i b_i + (-1)^{d-i} b_{d-i} \right).
$$
Since $d$ is odd, $(-1)^{d-i} = -(-1)^i$, and $b_{d-i} = b_i$ by step [](#betti){.pf-ref}.
So every summand is $0$.
:::
