::: {.problem}
Show that the Euler characteristic of a compact, orientable, odd-dimensional manifold is zero.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $M$ be a compact orientable manifold of odd dimension $d=2m+1$.
Poincaré duality over $\mathbb Q$ gives
\[
b_i(M)=b_{d-i}(M).
\]
Therefore
\[
\chi(M)=\sum_{i=0}^d(-1)^i b_i
=\sum_{i=0}^{m}\left((-1)^ib_i+(-1)^{d-i}b_{d-i}\right).
\]
Since $d$ is odd,
\[
(-1)^{d-i}=-(-1)^i,
\]
and paired Betti numbers are equal.
Every summand in parentheses is therefore zero, so
\[
\boxed{\chi(M)=0.}
\]
:::
