::: {.problem}
Let \(M\) be a smooth \(n\)-manifold. Prove that \(M\) is orientable if and only if it admits a smooth atlas whose transition maps
\[
\tau_{ij}=\varphi_j\circ\varphi_i^{-1}
\]
have positive Jacobian determinant wherever they are defined:
\[
\det D_x\tau_{ij}>0.
\]
:::

::: {.solution}
For a chart \(\varphi:U\to\mathbb R^n\) and a point \(p\in U\), translation in \(\mathbb R^n\) identifies \(\varphi(p)\) with \(0\), so Exercise 15.2 gives a chart-induced generator, equivalently an isomorphism
\[
\eta_{\varphi,p}:H_n(M,M\setminus\{p\};\mathbb Z)\longrightarrow\mathbb Z.
\]

<1>1. A smooth change of coordinates preserves the chart-induced local orientation exactly when its Jacobian determinant is positive.
::: {.proof}
Let \(\tau\) be a smooth transition map between open subsets of \(\mathbb R^n\), and let \(a\) be a point in its domain.  After translating source and target, regard \(\tau\) as a local diffeomorphism fixing \(0\).

Its induced map on local homology
\[
H_n(\mathbb R^n,\mathbb R^n\setminus\{0\})\cong\mathbb Z
\]
is multiplication by the local degree of \(\tau\) at \(0\).  Since \(\tau\) is a local diffeomorphism, that local degree is
\[
\operatorname{sgn}\det D_0\tau.
\]
Indeed, after restricting to a sufficiently small ball, the standard rescaling homotopy
\[
H_t(v)=
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{\tau(tv)-\tau(0)}{t},&t>0,\\[4pt]
D_0\tau(v),&t=0
\end{cases}
\]
identifies the local map with its invertible derivative through maps having no additional zero near \(0\).  Thus the local degree equals that of the linear map \(D_0\tau\), which is \(+1\) for positive determinant and \(-1\) for negative determinant.

Consequently two overlapping charts induce the same local orientation at \(p\) exactly when their transition Jacobian at \(p\) has positive determinant.
:::

<1>2. An atlas with positive transition Jacobians determines an orientation of \(M\).
::: {.proof}
Suppose \(\{(U_i,\varphi_i)\}\) is a smooth atlas and every transition map has positive Jacobian determinant.  For each \(p\in U_i\), let \(\eta_{i,p}\) be the local orientation induced by \(\varphi_i\).

If \(p\in U_i\cap U_j\), then by <1>1 the positivity of
\(\det D\tau_{ij}\) implies
\[
\eta_{i,p}=\eta_{j,p}.
\]
Hence the chartwise choices glue to a well-defined local orientation \(\eta_p\) at every \(p\in M\).

In the local trivialization of the orientation double cover supplied by any chart, the choice \(p\mapsto\eta_p\) is constant.  Therefore it is a continuous section of the orientation cover.  Thus \(M\) is orientable.
:::

<1>3. If \(M\) is orientable, it has an atlas with positive transition Jacobians.
::: {.proof}
Let \(s\) be a continuous section of the orientation cover of \(M\).  Fix \(p\in M\) and any smooth chart \((U,\varphi)\) around \(p\).  The chart determines one of the two local orientations at \(p\).  If this disagrees with \(s(p)\), compose \(\varphi\) with a fixed linear reflection of \(\mathbb R^n\); this reverses the chart-induced orientation by <1>1.  Thus we may arrange that the chart orientation agrees with \(s\) at \(p\).

Both \(s|_U\) and the chart-induced orientation are continuous sections of a two-sheeted covering.  After replacing \(U\) by the connected component containing \(p\), two such sections that agree at \(p\) agree throughout \(U\).  Doing this at every point produces an atlas \(\{(U_i,\varphi_i)\}\) whose charts all induce the prescribed orientation \(s\).

On an overlap \(U_i\cap U_j\), the two chart-induced local orientations therefore agree at every point.  By <1>1,
\[
\det D_x(\varphi_j\circ\varphi_i^{-1})>0
\]
for every \(x\) where the transition map is defined.  Hence this is the required positively oriented smooth atlas.
:::

The two conditions are therefore equivalent.
:::
