::: {.problem}
For any simply connected space $X$, show that $H^2(X;\ZZ)\cong\operatorname{Hom}(H_2(X;\ZZ),\ZZ)$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Since $X$ is simply connected, $H_1(X;\ZZ)\cong\pi_1(X)^{\mathrm{ab}}=0$ by the degree-one Hurewicz theorem.
The cohomological universal coefficient theorem in degree $2$ gives
$$
0\to\operatorname{Ext}(H_1(X),\ZZ)
\to H^2(X;\ZZ)
\to\operatorname{Hom}(H_2(X),\ZZ)
\to0.
$$
The Ext term is zero, so the right-hand map is a natural isomorphism
$$
\boxed{H^2(X;\ZZ)\cong\operatorname{Hom}_{\ZZ}(H_2(X;\ZZ),\ZZ)}.
$$
:::
