::: {.problem}
For any simply connected space $X$, show that $H^2(X;\ZZ)\cong\operatorname{Hom}(H_2(X;\ZZ),\ZZ)$.
:::

::: {.solution}
::: pf

::: {.pf-step #h1}
$H_1(X; \ZZ) = 0$.

::: pf-proof
By the Hurewicz theorem in degree one, $H_1(X; \ZZ) \cong \pi_1(X)^{\mathrm{ab}}$, and $\pi_1(X) = 1$.
:::

:::

::: pf-step
$H^2(X; \ZZ) \cong \operatorname{Hom}(H_2(X; \ZZ), \ZZ)$.

::: pf-proof
The universal coefficient theorem in degree $2$ gives a natural short exact sequence
$$
0 \to \operatorname{Ext}(H_1(X; \ZZ), \ZZ) \to H^2(X; \ZZ) \to \operatorname{Hom}(H_2(X; \ZZ), \ZZ) \to 0.
$$
By step [](#h1){.pf-ref}, the Ext term is $0$, so the right-hand map is an isomorphism.
:::
