::: {.problem}
Theorem (dual universal coefficients).
If $X$ is a space and $R$ any commutative unital ring, there are natural (in $X$), non-naturally split short exact sequences
\[
0\to\operatorname{Ext}(H_{p-1}(X),R)\to H^p(X;R)\to\operatorname{Hom}_{\ZZ}(H_p(X),R)\to0.
\]
In particular, there are non-canonical isomorphisms $H^n(X)\cong\operatorname{Hom}(H_n(X),\ZZ)\oplus H_{n-1}(X)_{\mathrm{tors}}$, where $A_{\mathrm{tors}}$ denotes the torsion subgroup of $A$.

Verify a case of the theorem by computing $H^*(\RP^2;\ZZ)$ in two ways.
Do the same for $H^*(\RP^2;\ZZ/2)$.
:::

::: {.solution}
The standard CW structure on $\RP^2$ has one cell in each degree $0,1,2$, with cellular chain differential
\[
0\to\ZZ\xrightarrow{\times2}\ZZ\xrightarrow0\ZZ\to0.
\]
Thus
\[
H_0=\ZZ,\qquad H_1=\ZZ/2,\qquad H_2=0.
\]

::: pf

::: pf-step

Integral cohomology by cellular cochains.

::: pf-proof

Dualizing gives
\[
0\leftarrow\ZZ\xleftarrow{\times2}\ZZ\xleftarrow0\ZZ\leftarrow0
\]
with cohomological degrees $2,1,0$.
Hence
\[
H^0(\RP^2;\ZZ)=\ZZ,
\quad H^1=0,
\quad H^2=\ZZ/2.
\]

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Integral cohomology by UCT.

::: pf-proof

UCT gives
\[
0\to\operatorname{Ext}(H_{n-1},\ZZ)\to H^n\to\operatorname{Hom}(H_n,\ZZ)\to0.
\]
For $n=2$, $\operatorname{Ext}(\ZZ/2,\ZZ)=\ZZ/2$; for $n=1$, both terms vanish.
This reproduces the cellular result.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

With $\ZZ/2$ coefficients.

::: pf-proof

Modulo $2$, the cellular differential vanishes, so
\[
H^n(\RP^2;\ZZ/2)\cong\ZZ/2
\qquad(n=0,1,2),
\]
and is zero otherwise.
UCT gives the same answer: in degree $1$ the Hom term $\operatorname{Hom}(\ZZ/2,\ZZ/2)$ contributes, and in degree $2$ the Ext term $\operatorname{Ext}(\ZZ/2,\ZZ/2)$ contributes.

:::
