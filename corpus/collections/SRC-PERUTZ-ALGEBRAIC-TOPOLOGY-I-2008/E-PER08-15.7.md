::: {.problem}
(i) Show that there is no homeomorphism Hn $\\cong$ Rn, where Hn =
{(x1, . . . , xn) $\\in$ Rn : x1 $\\ge$ 0}.
(ii) Let M be a space, and N the subspace of points x $\\in$ M which have a neighbourhood U such that there is a homeomorphism U $\\to$ Hn sending x to 0. Say M is
an n-manifold with boundary if M $\\setminus$ N is an n-manifold. In that case write $\\partial$M for
the boundary N. Check that that $\\partial$M is an (n $-$ 1)-manifold.
:::
