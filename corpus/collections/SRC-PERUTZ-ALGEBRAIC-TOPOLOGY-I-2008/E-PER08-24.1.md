::: {.problem}
Find a cellular approximation to the diagonal Sn $\\to$ Sn $\\times$ Sn.
:::

::: {.solution}
Give $S^n$ the CW structure with one $0$-cell and one $n$-cell.
Then the $n$-skeleton of $S^n\times S^n$ is
\[
S^n\vee S^n.
\]
A cellular approximation to the diagonal is
\[
S^n\xrightarrow{\operatorname{pinch}}S^n\vee S^n
\hookrightarrow S^n\times S^n,
\]
where the pinch map collapses an equatorial $S^{n-1}$ and maps the two resulting hemispheres with degree $1$ onto the two sphere summands.

Indeed, the two coordinate projections of this composite each have degree $1$, exactly as do the two projections of the diagonal $\Delta:S^n\to S^n\times S^n$.
Since
\[
[S^n,S^n\times S^n]_*\cong[S^n,S^n]_*\times[S^n,S^n]_*
\cong\mathbb Z\times\mathbb Z
\]
for $n\ge1$, the two maps are homotopic.
The displayed map lands in the $n$-skeleton, so it is cellular.
:::
