::: {.problem}
Show that the Mayer--Vietoris connecting map $\delta$ can be understood as follows.
Let $z\in S_p(X)$ be a $p$-cycle. By locality, there is a homologous $p$-cycle
\[
z'=x+y,
\]
where $x\in S_p(A)$ and $y\in S_p(B)$. Then
\[
\partial x=-\partial y,
\]
so $\partial x$ is a cycle in $A\cap B$. Show that
\[
\delta[z]=[\partial x].
\]
:::

::: {.solution}
Use the short exact sequence of chain complexes underlying Mayer--Vietoris,
\[
0\longrightarrow S_*(A\cap B)
\xrightarrow{\;j\;}
S_*(A)\oplus S_*(B)
\xrightarrow{\;q\;}
S_*(A+B)
\longrightarrow 0,
\]
where
\[
j(c)=(c,-c),
\qquad
q(a,b)=a+b.
\]
By locality, $S_*(A+B)\hookrightarrow S_*(X)$ induces an isomorphism on homology.

<1>1. The pair $(x,y)$ is a lift of the cycle $z'$.
::: {.proof}
By construction,
\[
q(x,y)=x+y=z'.
\]
Thus $(x,y)\in S_p(A)\oplus S_p(B)$ is a chain lifting $z'\in S_p(A+B)$.
:::

<1>2. Its boundary lies in the image of $j$ and corresponds to $\partial x$.
::: {.proof}
Since $z'$ is a cycle,
\[
0=\partial z'=\partial x+\partial y,
\]
so
\[
\partial y=-\partial x.
\]
Hence
\[
\partial(x,y)
=(\partial x,\partial y)
=(\partial x,-\partial x)
=j(\partial x).
\]
Because $\partial^2x=0$, the chain $\partial x$ is a $(p-1)$-cycle in $A\cap B$.
:::

<1>3. The connecting homomorphism sends $[z']$ to $[\partial x]$.
::: {.proof}
For a short exact sequence of chain complexes, the connecting homomorphism is defined by lifting a cycle, taking the boundary of the lift, and then using the inverse of the injection on that boundary. By <1>1 and <1>2, the chosen lift is $(x,y)$ and
\[
j^{-1}(\partial(x,y))=\partial x.
\]
Therefore
\[
\delta[z']=[\partial x].
\]
:::

<1>4. Therefore $\delta[z]=[\partial x]$ under the locality identification.
::: {.proof}
The cycles $z$ and $z'$ are homologous in $X$, so they represent the same class in $H_p(X)$. Under the locality isomorphism
\[
H_p(S_*(A+B))\xrightarrow{\sim}H_p(X),
\]
the class $[z']$ corresponds to $[z]$. Hence the Mayer--Vietoris connecting map satisfies
\[
\boxed{\delta[z]=[\partial x]}.
\]
:::
:::
