::: {.problem}
Compute degree matrices for the above-mentioned CW structures on $T^2$, $\mathbb{RP}^2$, and the Klein bottle $K^2$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Fix the cell labels used later in the notes.
For $T^2$ and $K^2$, let $v_0$ be the unique $0$-cell, let $a,b$ denote the two oriented $1$-cells, and let $e^2$ be the unique $2$-cell.
For the chosen CW structure on $\mathbb{RP}^2$, let $v_0,v'_0$ be the two $0$-cells, let $a,b$ be the oriented $1$-cells, and let $e^2$ be the unique $2$-cell.

<1>1. For the torus,
\[
D_1=\begin{pmatrix}0&0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
D_2=\begin{pmatrix}0\\0\end{pmatrix}.
\]
::: {.proof}
Both endpoints of each of the loops $a,b$ are the unique vertex $v_0$, so both cellular boundaries in degree $1$ vanish.
Hence $D_1=(0\;0)$.

The attaching word of the $2$-cell is
\[
aba^{-1}b^{-1}.
\]
To compute the $a$-entry of $D_2$, collapse the $b$-circle in the $1$-skeleton.
The attaching map then becomes the loop $aa^{-1}$, whose degree is $0$.
Likewise, after collapsing the $a$-circle, it becomes $bb^{-1}$, again of degree $0$.
Thus both entries of $D_2$ vanish.
:::

<1>2. For the Klein bottle,
\[
D_1=\begin{pmatrix}0&0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
D_2=\begin{pmatrix}0\\2\end{pmatrix}
\]
with rows ordered $(a,b)$.
::: {.proof}
Again $a$ and $b$ are loops at the unique vertex, so $D_1=0$.

The attaching word is
\[
aba^{-1}b.
\]
After collapsing the $b$-circle, this reduces to $aa^{-1}$ and hence has degree $0$ on the $a$-summand.
After collapsing the $a$-circle, it reduces to $bb$, which has degree $2$ on the $b$-summand.
Therefore
\[
D_2=\begin{pmatrix}0\\2\end{pmatrix}.
\]
:::

<1>3. For the chosen two-vertex CW structure on $\mathbb{RP}^2$,
\[
D_1=
\begin{pmatrix}
-1&1\\
1&-1
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
D_2=\begin{pmatrix}2\\2\end{pmatrix},
\]
where the rows of $D_1$ are ordered $(v_0,v'_0)$ and its columns, and the rows of $D_2$, are ordered $(a,b)$.
::: {.proof}
Orient $a$ from $v_0$ to $v'_0$ and $b$ from $v'_0$ to $v_0$.
Then
\[
\partial a=v'_0-v_0,
\qquad
\partial b=v_0-v'_0.
\]
Thus the two columns of $D_1$ are respectively $(-1,1)^T$ and $(1,-1)^T$.

For this CW structure, the attaching word of the $2$-cell is
\[
abab.
\]
Collapsing the $b$-edge gives $aa$, of degree $2$ on the $a$-circle; collapsing the $a$-edge gives $bb$, of degree $2$ on the $b$-circle.
Hence
\[
D_2=\begin{pmatrix}2\\2\end{pmatrix}.
\]
:::

Changing an orientation multiplies the corresponding row or column by $-1$, and permuting cell labels permutes rows or columns; the displayed matrices are therefore the matrices for the source's stated conventions.
:::
