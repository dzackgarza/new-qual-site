::: {.problem}
Let $K$ be the trefoil knot.
We have
\[
\pi_1(S^3\setminus K)=\langle a,b\mid a^2=b^3\rangle.
\]

Explain how to find a word representing a given loop in $S^3\setminus K$.
Find words representing a meridian of $K$ and a longitude of $K$.

Let $Z$ be the kernel of the homomorphism
\[
\pi_1(S^3\setminus K)\longrightarrow \langle a,b\mid a^2,b^3\rangle
\]
that sends $a$ to $a$ and $b$ to $b$.
Show that $Z\cong\mathbb Z$, generated by a longitude, and that $Z$ is contained in the center of $\pi_1(S^3\setminus K)$.
:::
