::: {.problem}
Let K be the trefoil knot.
We’ve seen that π1(S3 ∖K) = ⟨a, b | a2 = b3⟩. How do you find a word representing a given loop in S3 ∖K? Find words representing a meridian for K (i.e., the boundary of a small normal disc) and a longitude (parallel to the knot; not unique!). Let Z be the the kernel of the homomorphism π1(S3 ∖ K) → ⟨a, b | a2, b3⟩ which sends a to a and b to b. Show that Z ∼= Z, generated by a longitude, and that Z is contained in the center of π1(S3 ∖ K). [Interestingly, by an earlier exercise we have ⟨a, b | a2, b3⟩ ∼= PSL2(Z).]
:::
