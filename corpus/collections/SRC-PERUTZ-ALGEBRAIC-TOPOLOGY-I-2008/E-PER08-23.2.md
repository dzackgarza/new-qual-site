::: {.problem}
(a) Suppose that X and Y are compact, connected, orientable nmanifolds.
Describe the cohomology ring of their connected sum X#Y . (b) Prove that CP 2#CP 2 and CP 2#CP 2 are not homotopy-equivalent.
Here CP 2 is the complex projective plane with one orientation, and CP 2 the same space with the opposite orientation.
:::
