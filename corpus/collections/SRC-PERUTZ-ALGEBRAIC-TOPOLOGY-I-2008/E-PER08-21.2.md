::: {.problem}
Show that $H^1(X;R)\cong\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb Z}(\pi_1(X),R)$ for path-connected $X$.
Deduce that $H^1(X;\mathbb Z)$ is a torsion-free abelian group.
:::

::: {.solution}
For a path-connected space $X$,
\[
\boxed{H^1(X;R)\cong\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb Z}(\pi_1(X),R),}
\]
where $R$ is regarded as an additive abelian group.

<1>1. Apply the universal coefficient theorem in degree $1$.
::: {.proof}
Since $X$ is path connected, $H_0(X;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z$, hence
\[
\operatorname{Ext}_{\mathbb Z}(H_0(X),R)=0.
\]
The cohomological UCT therefore gives a natural isomorphism
\[
H^1(X;R)\cong\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb Z}(H_1(X;\mathbb Z),R).
\]
:::

<1>2. Replace $H_1$ by the abelianization of $\pi_1$.
::: {.proof}
The degree-one Hurewicz theorem says
\[
H_1(X;\mathbb Z)\cong\pi_1(X)^{\rm ab}.
\]
Every homomorphism from $\pi_1(X)$ to the abelian group $(R,+)$ factors uniquely through the abelianization, so
\[
\operatorname{Hom}(H_1(X),R)\cong\operatorname{Hom}(\pi_1(X),R).
\]
:::

<1>3. $H^1(X;\mathbb Z)$ is torsion-free.
::: {.proof}
Under the preceding identification,
\[
H^1(X;\mathbb Z)\cong\operatorname{Hom}(H_1(X),\mathbb Z).
\]
If $n\phi=0$ for a nonzero integer $n$, then $n\phi(a)=0$ in the torsion-free group $\mathbb Z$ for every $a$, hence $\phi=0$.
Thus this Hom group is torsion-free.
:::
:::
