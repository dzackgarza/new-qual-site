::: {.problem}
Show that T 2 ∖ {4 points} is a 2-sheeted covering of S2 ∖ {4 points}. Some possible approaches are (a) a direct topological argument; (b) the Weierstrass ℘-function from complex analysis; (c) a pencil of divisors of degree 2 on an elliptic curve.
:::
