::: {.problem}
Prove that the cohomology groups of a compact, simply connected, oriented 4-manifold M are completely determined by the second Betti number b2(M) = dimQ H2(M; Q). Prove that the cohomology ring is determined by the cup-product pairing H2$\\times$H2 $\\to$ Z.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $M$ be a compact simply connected oriented $4$-manifold and let $b_2=\dim_{\mathbb Q}H_2(M;\mathbb Q)$.

<1>1. The integral cohomology groups are determined by $b_2$.
::: {.proof}
Simple connectivity gives $H_1(M;\mathbb Z)=0$.
Poincaré duality gives
\[
H^3(M;\mathbb Z)\cong H_1(M;\mathbb Z)=0,
\qquad
H^1(M;\mathbb Z)\cong H_3(M;\mathbb Z).
\]
Also $H^1=\operatorname{Hom}(H_1,\mathbb Z)=0$, so $H_3=0$.

UCT in degree $3$ gives
\[
0\to\operatorname{Ext}(H_2(M),\mathbb Z)\to H^3(M)\to\operatorname{Hom}(H_3(M),\mathbb Z)\to0.
\]
Both outer target groups force $\operatorname{Ext}(H_2,\mathbb Z)=0$, so $H_2$ is torsion-free.
Compactness gives finite generation, hence
\[
H_2(M)\cong\mathbb Z^{b_2}.
\]
UCT and duality now give
\[
H^0\cong H^4\cong\mathbb Z,\qquad
H^2\cong\mathbb Z^{b_2},\qquad H^1=H^3=0.
\]
:::

<1>2. The ring is determined by the degree-$2$ cup-product pairing.
::: {.proof}
The only product of positive-degree classes that can be nonzero is
\[
H^2(M)\times H^2(M)\xrightarrow{\smile}H^4(M)\cong\mathbb Z.
\]
After choosing the orientation generator $\omega\in H^4$, write
\[
x\smile y=Q_M(x,y)\,\omega.
\]
Poincaré duality says $Q_M$ is a unimodular symmetric bilinear form on $\mathbb Z^{b_2}$.
Since multiplication by the degree-$0$ unit and this pairing account for every possible product, the graded ring is completely determined by $Q_M$.
:::
:::
