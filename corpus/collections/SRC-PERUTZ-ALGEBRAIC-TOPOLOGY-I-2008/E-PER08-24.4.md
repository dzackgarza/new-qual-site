::: {.problem}
Check that the cap product has the properties I claimed two lectures back.
:::

::: {.solution}
At chain level, with the Alexander--Whitney convention, if $c\in S^p(X)$ and $\sigma=[v_0,\dots,v_n]$ is a singular $n$-simplex, the cap product is
\[
c\frown\sigma
=
\langle c,\sigma|[v_0,\dots,v_p]\rangle\,\sigma|[v_p,\dots,v_n]
\]
(up to the global sign convention chosen in the notes).
Direct substitution verifies the claimed identities.

<1>1. Degree, unit, and compatibility with cup product.
::: {.proof}
The formula leaves an $(n-p)$-simplex, hence
\[
H^p(X)\frown H_n(X)\subseteq H_{n-p}(X).
\]
For the degree-$0$ unit $1$, the scalar on the front vertex is $1$, so $1\frown h=h$.
Applying the formula twice to successive front faces gives
\[
(a\smile b)\frown h=a\frown(b\frown h)
\]
with the convention of the notes.
:::

<1>2. Compatibility with evaluation.
::: {.proof}
For $|a|+|b|=|h|$, applying $a$ to the front part left after capping by $b$ is exactly the Alexander--Whitney evaluation defining $a\smile b$.
Hence
\[
\langle a\smile b,h\rangle=\langle a,b\frown h\rangle.
\]
:::

<1>3. Naturality.
::: {.proof}
For $f:X\to Y$, face restriction commutes with composition by $f$.
On a singular simplex $\sigma$ this gives
\[
f_*\bigl(f^*c\frown\sigma\bigr)=c\frown f_*\sigma.
\]
Passing to homology yields
\[
f_*(f^*c\frown h)=c\frown f_*h.
\]
:::

<1>4. The construction descends to (co)homology and respects disjoint unions.
::: {.proof}
The chain-level boundary identity
\[
\partial(c\frown\sigma)=(-1)^p\bigl(c\frown\partial\sigma-(dc)\frown\sigma\bigr)
\]
with the chosen sign convention shows that cocycles cap cycles to cycles and that changing either representative by a boundary changes the result by a boundary.
On a disjoint union every singular simplex lies in one component, so both cup and cap products are computed componentwise.
Thus
\[
H_*(\coprod_iX_i)\cong\bigoplus_iH_*(X_i)
\]
is an isomorphism of modules over
\[
H^*(\coprod_iX_i)\cong\prod_iH^*(X_i).
\]
:::
:::
