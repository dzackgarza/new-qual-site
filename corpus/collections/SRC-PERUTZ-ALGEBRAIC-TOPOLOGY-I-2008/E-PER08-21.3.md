::: {.problem}
Work over Z and fix distinct primes p and q. Compute (i) Ext(Z, Z/pn); (ii) Ext(Z/pn, Z); (iii) Ext(Z/pn, Z/qm); (iv) Ext(Z/pn, Z/pm).
:::
