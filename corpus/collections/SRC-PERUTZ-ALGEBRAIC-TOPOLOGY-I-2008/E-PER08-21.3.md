::: {.problem}
Work over $\mathbb{Z}$ and fix distinct primes $p$ and $q$.
Compute (i) $\operatorname{Ext}(\mathbb{Z},\mathbb{Z}/p^n)$; (ii) $\operatorname{Ext}(\mathbb{Z}/p^n,\mathbb{Z})$; (iii) $\operatorname{Ext}(\mathbb{Z}/p^n,\mathbb{Z}/q^m)$; (iv) $\operatorname{Ext}(\mathbb{Z}/p^n,\mathbb{Z}/p^m)$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Over $\mathbb Z$, write $\operatorname{Ext}$ for $\operatorname{Ext}^1_{\mathbb Z}$.
The basic computation from
\[
0\to\mathbb Z\xrightarrow{\times r}\mathbb Z\to\mathbb Z/r\to0
\]
is
\[
\operatorname{Ext}(\mathbb Z/r,B)\cong B/rB.
\]
Also $\mathbb Z$ is free, so $\operatorname{Ext}(\mathbb Z,B)=0$.

Therefore, for distinct primes $p\ne q$,
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{Ext}(\mathbb Z,\mathbb Z/p^n)=0,}
\]
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{Ext}(\mathbb Z/p^n,\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z/p^n,}
\]
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{Ext}(\mathbb Z/p^n,\mathbb Z/q^m)=0,}
\]
and
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{Ext}(\mathbb Z/p^n,\mathbb Z/p^m)
\cong\mathbb Z/p^{\min(n,m)}.}
\]
Indeed multiplication by $p^n$ is an automorphism of $\mathbb Z/q^m$ when $p\ne q$, while the quotient $(\mathbb Z/p^m)/p^n(\mathbb Z/p^m)$ has order $p^{\min(n,m)}$.
:::
