::: {.problem}
Work over $\ZZ$ and fix distinct primes $p$ and $q$.
Compute (i) $\operatorname{Ext}(\ZZ,\ZZ/p^n)$; (ii) $\operatorname{Ext}(\ZZ/p^n,\ZZ)$; (iii) $\operatorname{Ext}(\ZZ/p^n,\ZZ/q^m)$; (iv) $\operatorname{Ext}(\ZZ/p^n,\ZZ/p^m)$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Over $\ZZ$, write $\operatorname{Ext}$ for $\operatorname{Ext}^1_{\ZZ}$.
The basic computation from
$$
0\to\ZZ\xrightarrow{\times r}\ZZ\to\ZZ/r\to0
$$
is
$$
\operatorname{Ext}(\ZZ/r,B)\cong B/rB.
$$
Also $\ZZ$ is free, so $\operatorname{Ext}(\ZZ,B)=0$.

Therefore, for distinct primes $p\ne q$,
$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Ext}(\ZZ,\ZZ/p^n)=0,}
$$
$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Ext}(\ZZ/p^n,\ZZ)\cong\ZZ/p^n,}
$$
$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Ext}(\ZZ/p^n,\ZZ/q^m)=0,}
$$
and
$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Ext}(\ZZ/p^n,\ZZ/p^m)
\cong\ZZ/p^{\min(n,m)}.}
$$
Indeed multiplication by $p^n$ is an automorphism of $\ZZ/q^m$ when $p\ne q$, while the quotient $(\ZZ/p^m)/p^n(\ZZ/p^m)$ has order $p^{\min(n,m)}$.
:::
