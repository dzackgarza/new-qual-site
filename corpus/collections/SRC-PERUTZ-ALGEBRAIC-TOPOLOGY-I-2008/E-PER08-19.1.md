::: {.problem}
Compute H$\\ast$(RP n; Z/4). Do it in two ways: via the Z/4 cellular chain complex; and via the homology over Z and universal coefficients.
:::

::: {.solution}
Give $\mathbb{RP}^n$ its standard CW structure with one cell in each degree $0,\dots,n$.
Over $\mathbb Z$, the cellular differential is multiplication by $2$ in even positive degrees and $0$ in odd degrees.
Tensoring with $\mathbb Z/4$ gives
\[
d_k=\begin{cases}
2:&k>0\text{ even},\\
0:&k\text{ odd}.
\end{cases}
\]
Therefore
\[
H_k(\mathbb{RP}^n;\mathbb Z/4)\cong
\begin{cases}
\mathbb Z/4,&k=0,\\
\mathbb Z/2,&0<k<n,\\
\mathbb Z/4,&k=n\text{ and }n\text{ odd},\\
\mathbb Z/2,&k=n\text{ and }n\text{ even},\\
0,&k>n.
\end{cases}
\]

<1>1. Cellular calculation.
::: {.proof}
For an interior odd degree $k$, $d_k=0$ and $\operatorname{im}d_{k+1}=2(\mathbb Z/4)$, so
\[
H_k=(\mathbb Z/4)/2(\mathbb Z/4)\cong\mathbb Z/2.
\]
For an interior even degree, $\ker d_k=\{0,2\}\cong\mathbb Z/2$ and $d_{k+1}=0$.
At the top, if $n$ is odd the outgoing differential is zero and there is no incoming differential, giving $\mathbb Z/4$; if $n$ is even the top homology is $\ker(\times2)\cong\mathbb Z/2$.
:::

<1>2. Universal-coefficient calculation.
::: {.proof}
Integral homology is
\[
H_k(\mathbb{RP}^n;\mathbb Z)=
\begin{cases}
\mathbb Z,&k=0,\\
\mathbb Z/2,&0<k<n\text{ odd},\\
0,&0<k<n\text{ even},\\
\mathbb Z,&k=n\text{ odd},\\
0,&k=n\text{ even}.
\end{cases}
\]
Use
\[
0\to H_k(X;\mathbb Z)\otimes\mathbb Z/4
\to H_k(X;\mathbb Z/4)
\to \operatorname{Tor}_1^{\mathbb Z}(H_{k-1}(X;\mathbb Z),\mathbb Z/4)\to0.
\]
Now
\[
(\mathbb Z/2)\otimes\mathbb Z/4\cong\mathbb Z/2,
\qquad
\operatorname{Tor}(\mathbb Z/2,\mathbb Z/4)\cong\mathbb Z/2.
\]
Exactly one of the two flank terms is nonzero in each interior positive degree, and the top degree gives the two parity cases above.
This reproduces the cellular answer.
:::
:::
