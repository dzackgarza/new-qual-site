::: {.problem}
Compute $H_*(\RP^n;\ZZ/4)$.
Do it in two ways: via the $\ZZ/4$ cellular chain complex; and via the homology over $\ZZ$ and universal coefficients.
:::

::: {.solution}
Give $\RP^n$ its standard CW structure with one cell in each degree $0,\dots,n$.
Over $\ZZ$, the cellular differential is multiplication by $2$ in even positive degrees and $0$ in odd degrees.
Tensoring with $\ZZ/4$ gives
\[
d_k=\begin{cases}
2:&k>0\text{ even},\\
0:&k\text{ odd}.
\end{cases}
\]
Therefore
\[
H_k(\RP^n;\ZZ/4)\cong
\begin{cases}
\ZZ/4,&k=0,\\
\ZZ/2,&0<k<n,\\
\ZZ/4,&k=n\text{ and }n\text{ odd},\\
\ZZ/2,&k=n\text{ and }n\text{ even},\\
0,&k>n.
\end{cases}
\]

::: pf

::: pf-step

Cellular calculation.

::: pf-proof

For an interior odd degree $k$, $d_k=0$ and $\operatorname{im}d_{k+1}=2(\ZZ/4)$, so
\[
H_k=(\ZZ/4)/2(\ZZ/4)\cong\ZZ/2.
\]
For an interior even degree, $\ker d_k=\{0,2\}\cong\ZZ/2$ and $d_{k+1}=0$.
At the top, if $n$ is odd the outgoing differential is zero and there is no incoming differential, giving $\ZZ/4$; if $n$ is even the top homology is $\ker(\times2)\cong\ZZ/2$.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Universal-coefficient calculation.

::: pf-proof

Integral homology is
\[
H_k(\RP^n;\ZZ)=
\begin{cases}
\ZZ,&k=0,\\
\ZZ/2,&0<k<n\text{ odd},\\
0,&0<k<n\text{ even},\\
\ZZ,&k=n\text{ odd},\\
0,&k=n\text{ even}.
\end{cases}
\]
Use
\[
0\to H_k(X;\ZZ)\otimes\ZZ/4
\to H_k(X;\ZZ/4)
\to \operatorname{Tor}_1^{\ZZ}(H_{k-1}(X;\ZZ),\ZZ/4)\to0.
\]
Now
\[
(\ZZ/2)\otimes\ZZ/4\cong\ZZ/2,
\qquad
\operatorname{Tor}(\ZZ/2,\ZZ/4)\cong\ZZ/2.
\]
Exactly one of the two flank terms is nonzero in each interior positive degree, and the top degree gives the two parity cases above.
This reproduces the cellular answer.

:::
