::: {.problem}
Show that the Mayer–Vietoris sequence is not merely canonical, but also natural in the following sense.
Given an another excisive triad (X′; A′, B′) and a map f : X $\\to$ X′ such that f(A) $\\subset$ A′ and f(B) $\\subset$ B′, the two long exact sequences and the maps between them induced by f form a commutative diagram.
:::
