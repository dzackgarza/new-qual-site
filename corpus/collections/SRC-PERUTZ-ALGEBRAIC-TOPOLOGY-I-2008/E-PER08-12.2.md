::: {.problem}
Show that the Mayer--Vietoris sequence is not merely canonical, but also natural in the following sense. Given another excisive triad $(X';A',B')$ and a map
\[
f:X\to X'
\]
such that
\[
f(A)\subseteq A',
\qquad
f(B)\subseteq B',
\]
show that the two Mayer--Vietoris long exact sequences and the maps between them induced by $f$ form a commutative diagram.
:::

::: {.solution}
Write $f_\#$ for the singular-chain map induced by $f$.

<1>1. The map $f$ induces a morphism between the two short exact Mayer--Vietoris chain-complex sequences.
::: {.proof}
Because $f(A)\subseteq A'$ and $f(B)\subseteq B'$, one also has
\[
f(A\cap B)\subseteq A'\cap B'.
\]
Hence $f_\#$ restricts to chain maps
\[
S_*(A\cap B)\to S_*(A'\cap B'),
\]
\[
S_*(A)\to S_*(A'),
\qquad
S_*(B)\to S_*(B').
\]
It therefore gives the vertical maps in
\[
\begin{array}{ccccccccc}
0&\to&S_*(A\cap B)&\xrightarrow{j}&S_*(A)\oplus S_*(B)&\xrightarrow{q}&S_*(A+B)&\to&0\\
&&\downarrow f_\#&&\downarrow(f_\#\oplus f_\#)&&\downarrow f_\#&&\\
0&\to&S_*(A'\cap B')&\xrightarrow{j'}&S_*(A')\oplus S_*(B')&\xrightarrow{q'}&S_*(A'+B')&\to&0,
\end{array}
\]
where
\[
j(c)=(c,-c),
\qquad
q(a,b)=a+b,
\]
and similarly for $j',q'$.

The left square commutes because
\[
(f_\#\oplus f_\#)(c,-c)=(f_\#c,-f_\#c)=j'(f_\#c),
\]
and the right square commutes because
\[
q'(f_\#a,f_\#b)=f_\#a+f_\#b=f_\#(a+b)=f_\#q(a,b).
\]
Thus this is a morphism of short exact sequences of chain complexes.
:::

<1>2. Every square involving the ordinary homology maps commutes.
::: {.proof}
Passing to homology preserves composition. Therefore the squares involving
\[
H_p(A\cap B),
\qquad
H_p(A)\oplus H_p(B),
\qquad
H_p(X)
\]
commute by functoriality of singular homology and by the naturality of the locality isomorphisms
\[
H_p(S_*(A+B))\cong H_p(X),
\qquad
H_p(S_*(A'+B'))\cong H_p(X').
\]
:::

<1>3. The square containing the connecting homomorphisms also commutes.
::: {.proof}
Let $[z]\in H_p(X)$ and represent it, using locality, by
\[
z'=x+y
\]
with $x\in S_p(A)$ and $y\in S_p(B)$. By the chain-level description of the Mayer--Vietoris connecting map,
\[
\delta[z]=[\partial x]\in H_{p-1}(A\cap B).
\]
Applying $f_\#$ gives
\[
f_\#z'=f_\#x+f_\#y,
\]
so the same description in $(X';A',B')$ gives
\[
\delta' f_*[z]
=
[\partial(f_\#x)].
\]
Since $f_\#$ is a chain map,
\[
\partial(f_\#x)=f_\#(\partial x).
\]
Therefore
\[
\delta' f_*[z]
=
[f_\#(\partial x)]
=
f_*[\partial x]
=
f_*\delta[z].
\]
Hence the connecting square commutes.
:::

<1>4. Thus the entire Mayer--Vietoris long exact sequence is natural.
::: {.proof}
By <1>2 all squares involving the maps inherited directly from the short exact sequence commute, and by <1>3 the connecting squares commute. Consequently the two Mayer--Vietoris long exact sequences, together with the maps induced by $f$, form a commutative diagram in every degree.
:::
