::: {.problem}
Let $\{g_n\}$ be a sequence of measurable functions on $[0,1]$ such that

(i) $|g_n(x)|\le C$, for a.e. $x\in[0,1]$,

(ii) and $\lim_{n\to\infty}\int_0^a g_n(x)\,dx=0$ for every $a\in[0,1]$.

Prove that for each $f\in L^1([0,1])$, we have $$\lim_{n\to\infty}\int_0^1 f(x)g_n(x)\,dx=0.$$
:::

::: {.solution}
Let
\[
S=\operatorname{span}\{\mathbf 1_{[0,a]}:0\le a\le1\}.
\]
Every interval indicator satisfies
\[
\mathbf 1_{(a,b]}=\mathbf 1_{[0,b]}-\mathbf 1_{[0,a]},
\]
so $S$ contains all step functions. Hence $S$ is dense in $L^1([0,1])$.

Fix $f\in L^1([0,1])$ and $\varepsilon>0$. Choose $h\in S$ such that
\[
\|f-h\|_1<\frac{\varepsilon}{2C}
\]
when $C>0$; if $C=0$, the conclusion is immediate.
Write
\[
h=\sum_{j=1}^m c_j\mathbf 1_{[0,a_j]}.
\]
By hypothesis (ii),
\[
\int_0^1 h(x)g_n(x)\,dx
=\sum_{j=1}^m c_j\int_0^{a_j}g_n(x)\,dx
\longrightarrow0.
\]
Therefore there is $N$ such that for $n\ge N$,
\[
\left|\int_0^1 h g_n\right|<\frac\varepsilon2.
\]

For such $n$, using $|g_n|\le C$ a.e.,
\[
\left|\int_0^1 f g_n\right|
\le
\left|\int_0^1(f-h)g_n\right|
+\left|\int_0^1 h g_n\right|
\le C\|f-h\|_1+\frac\varepsilon2
<\varepsilon.
\]
Hence
\[
\int_0^1 f(x)g_n(x)\,dx\longrightarrow0.
\]
:::
