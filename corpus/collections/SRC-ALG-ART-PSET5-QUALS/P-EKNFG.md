::: {.problem}

Let $E/F$ be a finite Galois extension, and let $K$ be an intermediate field.
Prove that $K/F$ is normal if and only if $\operatorname{Gal}(E/K)$ is a normal subgroup of $\operatorname{Gal}(E/F)$.
:::
