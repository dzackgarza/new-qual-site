::: {.problem}

Let $f(x) = x^3 - 7 \in \QQ[x]$.
Determine the splitting field and Galois group of $f$ over each of $\QQ$, $\RR$, and $\mathbb{F}_{13}$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let \(\alpha=\sqrt[3]{7}\) and let \(\zeta_3\) be a primitive cube root of unity.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

Over \(\QQ\), the splitting field and Galois group are
\[
\boxed{L=\QQ(\alpha,\zeta_3),\qquad \operatorname{Gal}(L/\QQ)\cong S_3}.
\]

::: pf-proof

The polynomial \(x^3-7\) is irreducible over \(\QQ\) by Eisenstein's criterion at \(7\), so \([\QQ(\alpha):\QQ]=3\). Its roots are
\[
\alpha,\qquad \alpha\zeta_3,\qquad \alpha\zeta_3^2,
\]
so the splitting field is \(\QQ(\alpha,\zeta_3)\). The field \(\QQ(\alpha)\) is contained in \(\RR\), while \(\zeta_3\notin\RR\), hence \(\zeta_3\notin\QQ(\alpha)\). Therefore
\[
[L:\QQ]=6.
\]
A separable irreducible cubic has Galois group acting transitively on its three roots, hence as a transitive subgroup of \(S_3\). Since the group has order \(6\), it is all of \(S_3\).

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

Over \(\RR\), the splitting field and Galois group are
\[
\boxed{\CC,\qquad \operatorname{Gal}(\CC/\RR)\cong C_2}.
\]

::: pf-proof

The real root \(\alpha=\sqrt[3]{7}\) already lies in \(\RR\). The other two roots are \(\alpha\zeta_3\) and \(\alpha\zeta_3^2\), which are nonreal. Thus adjoining either one gives \(\CC\), so the splitting field is \(\CC\). Its Galois group over \(\RR\) is generated by complex conjugation and has order \(2\).

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

Over \(\FF_{13}\), the polynomial \(x^3-7\) is irreducible.

::: pf-proof

The nonzero cubes in \(\FF_{13}\) are
\[
1,\ 5,\ 8,\ 12.
\]
Thus \(7\) is not a cube in \(\FF_{13}\), so \(x^3-7\) has no root. A cubic over a field is reducible if and only if it has a root, hence \(x^3-7\) is irreducible.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s4}

The splitting field and Galois group over \(\FF_{13}\) are
\[
\boxed{\FF_{13^3},\qquad \operatorname{Gal}(\FF_{13^3}/\FF_{13})\cong C_3}.
\]

::: pf-proof

Since \(3\mid 12=|\FF_{13}^\times|\), the field \(\FF_{13}\) already contains the three cube roots of unity; explicitly \(1,3,9\) satisfy \(u^3=1\). If \(\beta\) is one root of \(x^3-7\), the other roots are \(3\beta\) and \(9\beta\), so adjoining one root gives the full splitting field.

By step [](#s3){.pf-ref}, \([\FF_{13}(\beta):\FF_{13}]=3\), hence the splitting field is the unique field \(\FF_{13^3}\). Every finite-field extension is Galois with cyclic Galois group generated by Frobenius \(z\mapsto z^{13}\), giving the displayed group.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1){.pf-ref}, step [](#s2){.pf-ref}, and step [](#s4){.pf-ref} give the splitting fields and Galois groups over the three requested base fields.
