::: {.problem}

Let $f(x) = x^3 - 7 \in \mathbb{Q}[x]$.
Determine the splitting field and Galois group of $f$ over each of $\mathbb{Q}$, $\mathbb{R}$, and $\mathbb{F}_{13}$.
