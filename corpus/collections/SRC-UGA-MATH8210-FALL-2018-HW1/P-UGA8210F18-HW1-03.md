::: {.problem}
- Let $M$ be a smooth manifold, let $U \subset M$, and let $\phi : U \to \mathbb{R}^k$ be a chart.
  Let $f : U \to \mathbb{R}^m$ be a continuous function.
  Suppose that $f \circ \phi^{-1}$ is smooth.
  Let $\psi : U \to \mathbb{R}^k$ be another chart with the same domain.
  Show that $f \circ \phi^{-1}$ is smooth.
- A function $f : M \to \mathbb{R}^k$ is smooth if for every $p \in M$ there is a smooth chart $(U, \phi)$ with $p \in U$ so that $f \circ \phi^{-1}$ is smooth on $\phi(U)$.
  Show that if $f$ is smooth, then $f \circ \psi^{-1}$ is smooth for any chart $(V, \psi)$ around $p$.
- Show that the composition of smooth functions between manifolds is smooth.
  Show that the composition of diffeomorphisms is again a diffeomorphism.
- Write down a smooth function $\mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ which is invertible but not a diffeomorphism.
:::

::: {.remark}
In the first item the source's conclusion repeats the hypothesis; the intended conclusion is that $f \circ \psi^{-1}$ is smooth.
