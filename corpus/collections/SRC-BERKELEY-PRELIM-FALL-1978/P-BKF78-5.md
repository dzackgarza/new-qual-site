::: {.problem}
Let
\[
f(z)=a_0+a_1z+\cdots+a_nz^n
\]
be a complex polynomial of degree $n>0$.
Prove that
\[
\frac1{2\pi i}\int_{|z|=R} z^{n-1}|f(z)|^2\,dz
=a_0\overline{a_n}R^{2n}.
\]
:::
