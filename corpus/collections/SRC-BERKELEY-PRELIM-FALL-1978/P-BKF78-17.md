::: {.problem}
Find all automorphisms of the additive group $(\mathbb Q,+)$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $\varphi\colon(\QQ,+)\to(\QQ,+)$ be an additive homomorphism and set
$$
c=\varphi(1).
$$

<1>1. For every $m\in\ZZ$,
$$
\varphi(m)=mc.
$$

::: {.proof}
For $m>0$, additivity gives
$$
\varphi(m)
=
\varphi(\underbrace{1+\cdots+1}_{m\text{ terms}})
=
m\varphi(1)
=
mc.
$$
For $m=0$, additivity gives $\varphi(0)=0$. If $m<0$, then the
positive case gives $\varphi(-m)=(-m)c$, while additivity gives
$\varphi(-m)=-\varphi(m)$. Hence $\varphi(m)=mc$ in every case.
:::

<1>2. For every positive integer $n$,
$$
\varphi(1/n)=c/n.
$$

::: {.proof}
By additivity,
$$
n\varphi(1/n)
=
\varphi(n\cdot1/n)
=
\varphi(1)
=
c.
$$
The unique rational number whose $n$-fold sum is $c$ is $c/n$.
:::

<1>3. For every $x\in\QQ$,
$$
\varphi(x)=cx.
$$

::: {.proof}
Write $x=m/n$ with $m\in\ZZ$ and $n>0$. By additivity and step <1>2,
$$
\varphi(x)
=
\varphi\left(m\frac1n\right)
=
m\varphi(1/n)
=
\frac{mc}{n}
=
cx.
$$
:::

<1>4. The automorphisms of $(\QQ,+)$ are exactly
$$
\boxed{
\Aut(\QQ,+)
=
\left\{
x\longmapsto cx:c\in\QQ^\times
\right\}
}.
$$

::: {.proof}
If $\varphi$ is an automorphism, then $\varphi(1)\neq0$ by injectivity,
so step <1>3 shows that $\varphi$ has the displayed form with
$c\in\QQ^\times$.

Conversely, for each $c\in\QQ^\times$, the map $x\mapsto cx$ is
additive and has inverse $x\mapsto c^{-1}x$. Hence it is an
automorphism.
:::

<1>5. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>4 gives all and only the requested automorphisms.
:::
:::
