::: {.problem}
Let $W\subset\mathbb R^n$ be open and connected, and let $f:W\to\mathbb R$ be a function all of whose partial derivatives vanish.
Prove that $f$ is constant.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. For every $p\in W$, there is an open box $B$ containing $p$
such that
$$
B\subset W.
$$

::: {.proof}
Since $W$ is open, there is $r>0$ such that the Euclidean ball
$B(p,r)$ is contained in $W$. A sufficiently small open box centered
at $p$ is contained in this ball.
:::

<1>2. The function $f$ is constant on every box $B$ as in step
<1>1.

::: {.proof}
Let
$$
x=(x_1,\ldots,x_n),
\qquad
y=(y_1,\ldots,y_n)
$$
be points of $B$. Define
$$
z^{(j)}
=
(y_1,\ldots,y_j,x_{j+1},\ldots,x_n),
\qquad
0\le j\le n,
$$
so that $z^{(0)}=x$ and $z^{(n)}=y$.

Because $B$ is a box, the line segment from $z^{(j-1)}$ to
$z^{(j)}$ lies in $B$ and varies only in the $j$th coordinate.
Restricting $f$ to that segment gives a one-variable differentiable
function whose derivative is
$$
\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_j}=0.
$$
The mean value theorem therefore gives
$$
f(z^{(j)})=f(z^{(j-1)}).
$$
Applying this successively for $j=1,\ldots,n$ yields
$$
f(y)=f(x).
$$
Thus $f$ is constant on $B$.
:::

<1>3. The function $f$ is locally constant on $W$.

::: {.proof}
For each $p\in W$, step <1>1 supplies a neighborhood $B$ of $p$,
and step <1>2 shows that $f$ is constant on that neighborhood.
:::

<1>4. Fix $p_0\in W$ and set
$$
U
=
\{p\in W:f(p)=f(p_0)\}.
$$
Then both $U$ and $W\setminus U$ are open in $W$.

::: {.proof}
If $p\in U$, local constancy from step <1>3 gives a neighborhood of
$p$ on which every point has value $f(p)=f(p_0)$; hence that
neighborhood lies in $U$. Thus $U$ is open.

If $p\in W\setminus U$, local constancy gives a neighborhood of
$p$ on which every point has value $f(p)\neq f(p_0)$; hence that
neighborhood lies in $W\setminus U$. Thus the complement is also
open.
:::

<1>5. One has
$$
U=W.
$$

::: {.proof}
The set $U$ is nonempty because $p_0\in U$. By step <1>4, $U$ is
both open and closed in the connected space $W$. Therefore
$U=W$.
:::

<1>6. Consequently,
$$
\boxed{f\text{ is constant on }W}.
$$

::: {.proof}
By step <1>5, every $p\in W$ satisfies
$$
f(p)=f(p_0).
$$
:::

<1>7. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>6 is the required conclusion.
:::
