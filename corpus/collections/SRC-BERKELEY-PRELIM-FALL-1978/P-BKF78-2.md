::: {.problem}
Let $\{g_n\}$ be a sequence of Riemann-integrable functions from $[0,1]$ to $\mathbb R$ such that
\[
|g_n(x)|\le 1
\]
for every $n$ and $x$.
Define
\[
G_n(x)=\int_0^x g_n(t)\,dt.
\]
Prove that a subsequence of $\{G_n\}$ converges uniformly.
:::
