::: {.problem}
Suppose $h$ is entire,
\[
h(0)=3+4i,
\]
and
\[
|h(z)|\le5
\qquad\text{whenever }|z|<1.
\]
What is $h'(0)$?
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. The value of $h$ at the origin has modulus
$$
\abs{h(0)}=5.
$$

::: {.proof}
Since $h(0)=3+4i$,
$$
\abs{h(0)}
=
\sqrt{3^2+4^2}
=
5.
$$
:::

<1>2. The function $h$ is constant on the unit disk.

::: {.proof}
By hypothesis,
$$
\abs{h(z)}\leq5
$$
whenever $\abs z<1$. Step <1>1 shows that equality holds at the
interior point $z=0$. Thus $h$ attains its maximum modulus at an
interior point of the unit disk. By the maximum modulus principle,
$h$ is constant on the unit disk.
:::

<1>3. The requested derivative is
$$
\boxed{h'(0)=0}.
$$

::: {.proof}
By step <1>2, $h$ is constant on a neighborhood of $0$, so its
derivative at $0$ is zero.
:::

<1>4. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>3 gives the requested value.
:::
:::
