::: {.problem}
Suppose $h$ is entire,
\[
h(0)=3+4i,
\]
and
\[
|h(z)|\le5
\qquad\text{whenever }|z|<1.
\]
What is $h'(0)$?
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #h-modulus-at-zero}
The value of $h$ at the origin has modulus
$$
\abs{h(0)}=5.
$$

::: pf-proof
Since $h(0)=3+4i$,
$$
\abs{h(0)}
=
\sqrt{3^2+4^2}
=
5.
$$
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #h-constant}
The function $h$ is constant on the unit disk.

::: pf-proof
By hypothesis,
$$
\abs{h(z)}\leq5
$$
whenever $\abs z<1$. Step [](#h-modulus-at-zero){.pf-ref} shows that equality holds at the
interior point $z=0$. Thus $h$ attains its maximum modulus at an
interior point of the unit disk. By the maximum modulus principle,
$h$ is constant on the unit disk.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #derivative-value}
The requested derivative is
$$
\boxed{h'(0)=0}.
$$

::: pf-proof
By step [](#h-constant){.pf-ref}, $h$ is constant on a neighborhood of $0$, so its
derivative at $0$ is zero.
:::

:::

::: pf-qed
Step [](#derivative-value){.pf-ref} gives the requested value.
:::
