::: {.problem}
Give examples of conformal maps as follows:

1. from $\{z:|z|<1\}$ onto $\{z:\operatorname{Re}z<0\}$;

2. from $\{z:|z|<1\}$ onto itself, with
\[
f(0)=0,
\qquad
f(1/2)=i/2;
\]

3. from
\[
\left\{z:z\ne0,\ 0<\arg z<\frac{3\pi}{2}\right\}
\]
onto
\[
\left\{z:z\ne0,\ 0<\arg z<\frac{\pi}{2}\right\}.
\]
:::
