::: {.problem}
Give examples of conformal maps as follows:

1. from $\{z:|z|<1\}$ onto $\{z:\operatorname{Re}z<0\}$;

2. from $\{z:|z|<1\}$ onto itself, with
\[
f(0)=0,
\qquad
f(1/2)=i/2;
\]

3. from
\[
\left\{z:z\ne0,\ 0<\arg z<\frac{3\pi}{2}\right\}
\]
onto
\[
\left\{z:z\ne0,\ 0<\arg z<\frac{\pi}{2}\right\}.
\]
:::

::: {.solution}
Write
$$
\DD=\{z\in\CC:\abs z<1\},
\qquad
H_-=\{w\in\CC:\Re w<0\},
$$
and for $0<\alpha<2\pi$ write
$$
S_\alpha
=
\left\{
re^{i\theta}:r>0,\ 0<\theta<\alpha
\right\}.
$$

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #disk-to-halfplane}
A conformal map $\DD\to H_-$ is
$$
\boxed{
f_1(z)=-\frac{1+z}{1-z}
}.
$$

::: pf-proof
For $z\in\DD$,
$$
\Re\left(\frac{1+z}{1-z}\right)
=
\frac{1-\abs z^2}{\abs{1-z}^2}
>0,
$$
so $f_1(z)\in H_-$. Solving $w=f_1(z)$ for $z$ gives
$$
z=\frac{w+1}{w-1}.
$$
If $\Re w<0$, then
$$
1-\abs{\frac{w+1}{w-1}}^2
=
\frac{-4\Re w}{\abs{w-1}^2}
>0,
$$
so this inverse lies in $\DD$. Hence $f_1$ is a bijection
$\DD\to H_-$. Finally,
$$
f_1'(z)=-\frac{2}{(1-z)^2}\neq0
$$
on $\DD$, so $f_1$ is conformal.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #disk-automorphism}
A conformal automorphism of $\DD$ satisfying the prescribed
values is
$$
\boxed{
f_2(z)=iz
}.
$$

::: pf-proof
Multiplication by $i$ is a rotation, hence a conformal bijection of
$\DD$ onto itself. Moreover,
$$
f_2(0)=0,
\qquad
f_2(1/2)=i/2.
$$
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #sector-map}
A conformal map $S_{3\pi/2}\to S_{\pi/2}$ is
$$
\boxed{
f_3(z)=\exp\left(\frac13 L(z)\right)
},
$$
where
$$
L(re^{i\theta})=\log r+i\theta,
\qquad
r>0,\quad0<\theta<\frac{3\pi}{2}.
$$

::: pf-proof
The function $L$ is the holomorphic logarithm on $S_{3\pi/2}$ with
argument in $(0,3\pi/2)$. If
$$
z=re^{i\theta},
\qquad
r>0,
\qquad
0<\theta<\frac{3\pi}{2},
$$
then
$$
f_3(z)=r^{1/3}e^{i\theta/3},
$$
whose argument lies in $(0,\pi/2)$. Thus
$f_3(S_{3\pi/2})\subseteq S_{\pi/2}$.

For $w=\rho e^{i\phi}\in S_{\pi/2}$, we have
$0<3\phi<3\pi/2$, so $z=w^3=\rho^3e^{3i\phi}$ lies in
$S_{3\pi/2}$. The chosen branch gives
$$
L(w^3)=3\log\rho+3i\phi,
$$
and therefore $f_3(w^3)=w$. Conversely, $f_3(z)^3=z$, so
$w\mapsto w^3$ is the inverse of $f_3$. Finally,
$$
f_3'(z)=\frac{f_3(z)}{3z}\neq0
$$
on $S_{3\pi/2}$. Hence $f_3$ is conformal and onto
$S_{\pi/2}$.
:::
