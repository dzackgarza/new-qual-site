::: {.problem}
Let $M_{n\times n}$ be the vector space of real $n\times n$ matrices, identified with $\mathbb R^{n^2}$, and let $X\subset M_{n\times n}$ be compact.
Let $S\subset\mathbb C$ be the set of all numbers that occur as eigenvalues of at least one matrix in $X$.
Prove that $S$ is compact.
:::
