::: {.problem}
1. Suppose $u,v:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R$ are continuously differentiable and
\[
\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}=\frac{\partial v}{\partial x}.
\]
Show that there is a function $f:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R$ such that
\[
u=\frac{\partial f}{\partial x},
\qquad
v=\frac{\partial f}{\partial y}.
\]

2. Prove that there is no function $f:\mathbb R^2\setminus\{0\}\to\mathbb R$ such that
\[
\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}=-\frac{y}{x^2+y^2},
\qquad
\frac{\partial f}{\partial y}=\frac{x}{x^2+y^2}.
\]
:::
