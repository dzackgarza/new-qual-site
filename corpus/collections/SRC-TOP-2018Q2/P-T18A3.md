::: {.problem}
Show that a closed subset $A\subseteq X$ of a normal (i.e., $T_1$ and $T_4$) space $(X,\mathcal T)$, with the subspace topology, is normal.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #a-t1}
$A$ is $T_1$.

::: pf-proof
If $a\in A$, then $\{a\}$ is closed in $X$, so $\{a\}=A\cap\{a\}$ is closed in $A$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #a-t4}
Disjoint closed subsets $C,D$ of $A$ have disjoint open neighborhoods in $A$.

::: pf-proof
Write $C=A\cap F$ and $D=A\cap G$ with $F,G$ closed in $X$; since $A$ is closed in $X$, so are $C$ and $D$.
By normality of $X$ there are disjoint open $U,V\subseteq X$ with $C\subseteq U$ and $D\subseteq V$.
Then $U\cap A$ and $V\cap A$ are open in $A$, contain $C$ and $D$, and are disjoint.
:::

:::

::: pf-qed
Steps [](#a-t1){.pf-ref} and [](#a-t4){.pf-ref} show that $A$ is $T_1$ and $T_4$.
:::
