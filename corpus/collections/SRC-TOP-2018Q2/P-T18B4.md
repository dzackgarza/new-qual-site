::: {.problem}
Let $\Delta_2$ and $\Delta'_2$ be distinct $2$-simplices, and let $X$ be the quotient space obtained by identifying the six vertices of $\Delta_2\sqcup\Delta'_2$ to a single point.
Identify a $\Delta$-complex structure for $X$ and compute the simplicial homology groups $H^\Delta_n(X)$ for all $n$.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step}
$X$ is a $\Delta$-complex with one $0$-simplex $v$, six $1$-simplices $e_0,e_1,e_2,e'_0,e'_1,e'_2$ (the edges of the two triangles, $e_i$ opposite the $i$th vertex), and two $2$-simplices $\sigma,\sigma'$.

::: pf-proof
Only the vertices are identified, so the open edges and open triangles remain distinct, and each triangle's three faces are three distinct loops at $v$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #chain-complex}
The simplicial chain complex is $0\to\ZZ^2\xrightarrow{\partial_2}\ZZ^6\xrightarrow{\partial_1}\ZZ\to0$ with $\partial_1=0$, $\partial_2(\sigma)=e_0-e_1+e_2$, and $\partial_2(\sigma')=e'_0-e'_1+e'_2$.

::: pf-proof
Every edge has both endpoints at $v$, so its boundary is $v-v=0$; the formulas for $\partial_2$ are the alternating face sums.
:::

:::

::: pf-qed
By step [](#chain-complex){.pf-ref}, $H_0^\Delta(X)=\ZZ v\cong\ZZ$.
The vectors $\partial_2\sigma$ and $\partial_2\sigma'$ have disjoint supports, so $\partial_2$ is injective and $H_2^\Delta(X)=0$.
$H_1^\Delta(X)=\ZZ^6/\langle e_0-e_1+e_2,\ e'_0-e'_1+e'_2\rangle\cong\ZZ^3/\langle(1,-1,1)\rangle\oplus\ZZ^3/\langle(1,-1,1)\rangle\cong\ZZ^4$, since $(1,-1,1)$ is primitive.
There are no simplices above dimension $2$, so $H_n^\Delta(X)=0$ for $n\ge3$.
:::
