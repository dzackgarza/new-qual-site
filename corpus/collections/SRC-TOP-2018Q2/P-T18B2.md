::: {.problem}
A topological space $X$ is called *locally Euclidean* if every point $x\in X$ has an open neighborhood $U$ such that $U$ is homeomorphic to an open set in $\RR^n$ for some $n$.
Prove that if $X$ is locally Euclidean and $p:\widetilde X\to X$ is a covering space, then $\widetilde X$ is locally Euclidean.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $\widetilde x\in\widetilde X$ and $x=p(\widetilde x)$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #evenly-covered-euclidean-nbhd}
$x$ has an open neighborhood $U$ that is evenly covered by $p$ and homeomorphic to an open subset of some $\RR^n$.

::: pf-proof
Choose an evenly covered open neighborhood $U_0$ of $x$ and an open neighborhood $V$ of $x$ homeomorphic to an open subset of $\RR^n$.
Put $U=U_0\cap V$.
If $p^{-1}(U_0)=\bigsqcup_\beta W_\beta$ with each $p|_{W_\beta}\colon W_\beta\to U_0$ a homeomorphism, then $p^{-1}(U)=\bigsqcup_\beta(W_\beta\cap p^{-1}(U))$ and each $W_\beta\cap p^{-1}(U)\to U$ is a homeomorphism; so $U$ is evenly covered.
$U$ is open in $V$, so it is homeomorphic to an open subset of $\RR^n$.
:::

:::

::: pf-qed
Let $W$ be the sheet over the neighborhood $U$ of step [](#evenly-covered-euclidean-nbhd){.pf-ref} that contains $\widetilde x$.
$W$ is open in $\widetilde X$, and $p|_W\colon W\to U$ is a homeomorphism, so $W$ is homeomorphic to an open subset of $\RR^n$.
Since $\widetilde x$ was arbitrary, $\widetilde X$ is locally Euclidean.
:::
