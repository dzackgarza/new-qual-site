::: {.problem}
Let $F_n$ denote the free group on $n$ letters.
Use covering space theory to show that for all $n$, there exists a subgroup $H_n$ of $F_2$ such that $H_n\cong F_n$.
For your choices of $H_n$ constructed, determine if each $H_n$ is normal in $F_2$.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. Topological setup for $F_2$:
<2>1. Let $X = S^1 \vee S^1$ be the figure-eight space (wedge sum of two directed circles labeled $a$ and $b$) with a single vertex $v$.
The fundamental group is $\pi_1(X, v) \cong F_2 = \langle a, b \rangle$.
Proof: Seifert–van Kampen Theorem for wedge of circles.

<1>2. Covering space construction for $n \ge 2$:
<2>1. Let $d = n - 1 \ge 1$.
Define the surjective group homomorphism $\phi: F_2 \to \mathbb{Z}_d$ by:
\[
\phi(a) = 1 \pmod d, \qquad \phi(b) = 0 \pmod d.
\]
Let $H_n = \ker(\phi) \le F_2$.
Proof: universal property of free groups.
<2>2. The subgroup $H_n$ has index $[F_2 : H_n] = d = n - 1$.
The corresponding covering space $p: \widetilde{X}_d \to X$ has $d$ vertices $\{v_0, v_1, \ldots, v_{d-1}\}$ with:
- directed $a$-edges $v_i \xrightarrow{a} v_{i+1 \pmod d}$ forming an oriented $d$-cycle,
- directed $b$-loops $v_i \xrightarrow{b} v_i$ at each vertex $v_i$.
Proof: covering space corresponding to the transitive permutation representation of $\mathbb{Z}_d$.
<2>3. $\widetilde{X}_d$ is a connected 1-dimensional CW complex (a graph) with $V = d = n - 1$ vertices and $E = 2d = 2(n - 1)$ edges.
The Euler characteristic of $\widetilde{X}_d$ is:
\[
\chi(\widetilde{X}_d) = V - E = (n - 1) - 2(n - 1) = 1 - n.
\]
Proof: Euler characteristic formula for finite graphs.
<2>4. The fundamental group of any connected finite graph is free with rank $r = 1 - \chi(\widetilde{X}_d)$:
\[
r = 1 - (1 - n) = n.
\]
Thus $H_n \cong \pi_1(\widetilde{X}_d) \cong F_n$.
Proof: fundamental group of a connected graph is free of rank $1 - \chi$.

<1>3. Case $n = 1$:
<2>1. For $n = 1$, choose $H_1 = \langle a \rangle \cong \mathbb{Z} = F_1$.
This corresponds to the infinite cyclic covering of $X$ with respect to the loop $a$.
Proof: cyclic subgroup generated by $a$.

<1>4. Determination of normality:
<2>1. For $n \ge 2$, $H_n = \ker(\phi)$ is the kernel of a group homomorphism from $F_2$, so $H_n$ is a **normal subgroup** of $F_2$ ($H_n \triangleleft F_2$).
Proof: kernels of homomorphisms are normal subgroups.
<2>2. For $n = 1$, $H_1 = \langle a \rangle$ is not normal in $F_2$, because $b a b^{-1} \notin \langle a \rangle$.
Proof: reduced words in free groups.

<1>5. Conclusion:
For every $n \ge 1$, $F_2$ contains a subgroup $H_n \cong F_n$; the constructed subgroup is normal for all $n \ge 2$. Q.E.D.
Proof: <1>2 through <1>4.
:::
