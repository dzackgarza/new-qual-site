::: {.problem}
Show that if $X$ and $Y$ are topological spaces, and $X\times Y$, with the product topology, is contractible, then both $X$ and $Y$ are contractible.
:::

::: {.solution}
A contractible space is homotopy equivalent to a point, hence nonempty; so $X$ and $Y$ are nonempty, and we fix $x_0\in X$ and $y_0\in Y$.
Let $H\colon(X\times Y)\times[0,1]\to X\times Y$ be a homotopy from $\id_{X\times Y}$ to the constant map at a point $(x_*,y_*)$.

<1>1. $H_X(x,t)=\pi_X(H((x,y_0),t))$ is a homotopy from $\id_X$ to the constant map at $x_*$.

::: {.proof}
$H_X$ is a composite of continuous maps, $H_X(x,0)=\pi_X(x,y_0)=x$, and $H_X(x,1)=\pi_X(x_*,y_*)=x_*$.
:::

<1>2. $H_Y(y,t)=\pi_Y(H((x_0,y),t))$ is a homotopy from $\id_Y$ to the constant map at $y_*$.

::: {.proof}
$H_Y(y,0)=\pi_Y(x_0,y)=y$ and $H_Y(y,1)=\pi_Y(x_*,y_*)=y_*$.
:::

<1>3. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Steps <1>1 and <1>2 are contractions of $X$ and $Y$.
:::
:::
