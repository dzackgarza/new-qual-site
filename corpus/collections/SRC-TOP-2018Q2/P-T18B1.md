::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $X$ be a path-connected space containing points $x_0$ and $x_1$, and let $P$ be the set of path-homotopy classes of paths from $x_0$ to $x_1$ in $X$.
In other words, elements of $P$ are equivalence classes of paths from $x_0$ to $x_1$, where two paths $h_0$ and $h_1$ are equivalent if there exists a homotopy $h_t:[0,1]\to X$ from $h_0$ to $h_1$ such that $h_t(0)=x_0$ and $h_t(1)=x_1$ for all $t\in[0,1]$.
Prove that there is a bijection from $P$ to $\pi_1(X,x_0)$.
:::
