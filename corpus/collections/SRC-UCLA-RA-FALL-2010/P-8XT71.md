::: {.problem}
Let $\mathcal{F}$ be the family of functions $f$ holomorphic on $\mathbb{D}$ with $$\iint_{x^2+y^2<1}|f(x+iy)|^2\,dx\,dy<1.$$ Prove that for each compact subset $K\subset\mathbb{D}$ there is a constant $A$ so that $|f(z)|<A$ for all $z\in K$ and all $f\in\mathcal{F}$.
:::

::: {.solution}
If $K=\varnothing$, any $A>0$ works.
Assume henceforth that $K\ne\varnothing$.
Since $K$ is compact and $K\subset\mathbb D$, the distance
\[
\delta=\operatorname{dist}(K,\mathbb C\setminus\mathbb D)
\]
is strictly positive.
Set
\[
r=\frac\delta2.
\]
Then
\[
B(z,r)\subset\mathbb D
\qquad(z\in K).
\]

Fix $f\in\mathcal F$ and $z\in K$.
Since a holomorphic function has the area mean-value property on disks compactly contained in its domain,
\[
f(z)=\frac1{\pi r^2}\iint_{B(z,r)}f(w)\,dA(w).
\]
Hence Cauchy--Schwarz gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
|f(z)|
&\le \frac1{\pi r^2}
\iint_{B(z,r)}|f(w)|\,dA(w)\\
&\le \frac1{\pi r^2}
\left(\iint_{B(z,r)}1\,dA\right)^{1/2}
\left(\iint_{B(z,r)}|f(w)|^2\,dA(w)\right)^{1/2}\\
&\le \frac1{\sqrt\pi\,r}
\left(\iint_{\mathbb D}|f(w)|^2\,dA(w)\right)^{1/2}\\
&<\frac1{\sqrt\pi\,r}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Thus the constant
\[
A=\frac1{\sqrt\pi\,r}
=\frac{2}{\sqrt\pi\,\delta}
\]
depends only on $K$ and satisfies
\[
|f(z)|<A
\qquad(z\in K,\ f\in\mathcal F).
\]
:::
