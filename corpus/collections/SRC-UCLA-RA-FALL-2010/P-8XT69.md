::: {.problem}
Let $$f(z)=\sum_{n=0}^\infty a_n z^n$$ be a holomorphic function in $\mathbb{D}$.
Show that if $$\sum_{n=2}^\infty n|a_n|\le|a_1|$$ with $a_1\ne 0$, then $f$ is injective.
:::
