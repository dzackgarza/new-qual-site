::: {.problem}
Let $$f(z)=\sum_{n=0}^\infty a_n z^n$$ be a holomorphic function in $\mathbb{D}$.
Show that if $$\sum_{n=2}^\infty n|a_n|\le|a_1|$$ with $a_1\ne 0$, then $f$ is injective.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $z,w\in\mathbb D$ with $z\ne w$.
Then
\[
\frac{f(z)-f(w)}{z-w}
=a_1+\sum_{n=2}^\infty a_n\frac{z^n-w^n}{z-w}.
\]
For every $n\ge2$,
\[
\frac{z^n-w^n}{z-w}
=z^{n-1}+z^{n-2}w+\cdots+w^{n-1}.
\]
Since $|z|,|w|<1$, each of the $n$ summands on the right has modulus strictly less than $1$.
Hence
\[
\left|\frac{z^n-w^n}{z-w}\right|<n.
\]

If $a_n=0$ for every $n\ge2$, then $f(z)=a_0+a_1z$ and injectivity follows from $a_1\ne0$.
Otherwise at least one of the preceding inequalities is multiplied by a nonzero $|a_n|$, so
\[
\begin{aligned}
\left|
\sum_{n=2}^\infty a_n\frac{z^n-w^n}{z-w}
\right|
&\le
\sum_{n=2}^\infty |a_n|
\left|\frac{z^n-w^n}{z-w}\right|\\
&<\sum_{n=2}^\infty n|a_n|\\
&\le |a_1|.
\end{aligned}
\]
Therefore
\[
\left|\frac{f(z)-f(w)}{z-w}-a_1\right|<|a_1|,
\]
so the quotient $(f(z)-f(w))/(z-w)$ cannot be zero.
Thus $f(z)\ne f(w)$ whenever $z\ne w$, and $f$ is injective on $\mathbb D$.
:::
