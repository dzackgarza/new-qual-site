::: {.problem}
Let $$f(z)=\sum_{n=0}^\infty a_n z^n$$ be a holomorphic function in $\DD$.
Show that if $$\sum_{n=2}^\infty n\abs{a_n}\le\abs{a_1}$$ with $a_1\ne 0$, then $f$ is injective.
:::

::: {.solution}
The collection's provenance is the Fall 2010 qualifying examination, so no retained solution set covers the problem and the argument is the card's own. Write $f(z)=\sum_{n\ge0}a_nz^n$, holomorphic on $\DD$, with $a_1\ne0$ and $\sum_{n\ge2}n\abs{a_n}\le\abs{a_1}$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #divided-difference}
For $z\ne w$ in $\DD$,
$$
\frac{f(z)-f(w)}{z-w}=a_1+\sum_{n\ge2}a_n\frac{z^n-w^n}{z-w},
$$
and for each $n\ge2$ the factor $\frac{z^n-w^n}{z-w}$ is a sum of $n$ monomials of modulus less than $1$.

::: pf-proof
The power series converges on the disc and converges absolutely on compact subsets, and the difference of the two series can be rearranged term by term, the $n=0$ terms cancelling and the $n=1$ term giving $a_1(z-w)/(z-w)=a_1$. For the factor, $z^n-w^n=(z-w)(z^{n-1}+z^{n-2}w+\cdots+w^{n-1})$, a sum of $n$ monomials $z^{n-1-k}w^k$; each has modulus $\abs{z}^{n-1-k}\abs{w}^k<1$ because $\abs{z},\abs{w}<1$ and the exponents are non-negative and not both zero.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #strict}
For every $n\ge2$, $\abs{\dfrac{z^n-w^n}{z-w}}<n$.

::: pf-proof
The modulus of a sum is at most the sum of the moduli, and step [](#divided-difference){.pf-ref} gives $n$ summands each of modulus less than $1$; the sum is therefore less than $n$, and it is $n$ summands because $n-1-k$ runs from $0$ to $n-1$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #linear-case}
If $a_n=0$ for every $n\ge2$, then $f$ is injective on $\DD$.

::: pf-proof
Then $f(z)=a_0+a_1z$, so $f(z)=f(w)$ gives $a_1(z-w)=0$, and $a_1\ne0$ forces $z=w$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #bound}
If some $a_n\ne0$ with $n\ge2$, then $\abs{\sum_{n\ge2}a_n\frac{z^n-w^n}{z-w}}<\abs{a_1}$ for all $z\ne w$ in $\DD$.

::: pf-proof
At least one $n\ge2$ has $\abs{a_n}>0$, and for that $n$ the inequality of step [](#strict){.pf-ref} is strict, so the weighted sum is strictly smaller than $\sum_{n\ge2}\abs{a_n}n$:
$$
\abs{\sum_{n\ge2}a_n\frac{z^n-w^n}{z-w}}\le\sum_{n\ge2}\abs{a_n}\abs{\frac{z^n-w^n}{z-w}}<\sum_{n\ge2}n\abs{a_n}\le\abs{a_1},
$$
the last inequality being the hypothesis. The middle sum converges because $\sum_{n\ge2}n\abs{a_n}$ converges, the series for $f$ having radius of convergence at least $1$ on $\DD$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step}
$\boxed{f\text{ is injective on }\DD}$.

::: pf-proof
Let $z\ne w$ in $\DD$. In the case of step [](#linear-case){.pf-ref} injectivity is that step; otherwise step [](#bound){.pf-ref} and step [](#divided-difference){.pf-ref} give
$$
\abs{\frac{f(z)-f(w)}{z-w}-a_1}<\abs{a_1},
$$
so the quotient lies in the open disc of radius $\abs{a_1}$ about $a_1$, which does not contain $0$; hence the quotient is nonzero and $f(z)\ne f(w)$.
:::
