::: {.problem}
Prove the following form of Jensen's inequality: If $f:[0,1]\to\mathbb{R}$ is continuous then $$\int_0^1 e^{f(x)}\,dx\ge\exp\left(\int_0^1 f(x)\,dx\right).$$ Moreover, if equality occurs then $f$ is a constant function.
:::
