::: {.problem}
Let $F$ be a finite field of order $p$. Compute the order of
\[
SL_3(F),
\]
the group of $3\times3$ matrices over $F$ with determinant $1$.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. The order of $GL_3(F)$ is
$$
\abs{GL_3(F)}=(p^3-1)(p^3-p)(p^3-p^2).
$$

::: {.proof}
An invertible $3\times3$ matrix is equivalent to an ordered basis of $F^3$, given by its columns.

There are $p^3-1$ choices for the first nonzero column. Once it is chosen, its span has $p$ elements, so there are $p^3-p$ choices for the second column. The first two independent columns span a plane with $p^2$ elements, leaving $p^3-p^2$ choices for the third column.
:::

<1>2. The determinant map
$$
\det:GL_3(F)\longrightarrow F^\times
$$
is surjective and has kernel $SL_3(F)$.

::: {.proof}
The kernel statement is the definition of $SL_3(F)$. For every $a\in F^\times$, the diagonal matrix
$$
\operatorname{diag}(a,1,1)
$$
has determinant $a$, so the determinant map is surjective.
:::

<1>3. One has
$$
\abs{SL_3(F)}
=
\frac{(p^3-1)(p^3-p)(p^3-p^2)}{p-1}.
$$

::: {.proof}
Since $F$ has $p$ elements, its multiplicative group $F^\times$ has order $p-1$. By step <1>2 and the first isomorphism theorem,
$$
GL_3(F)/SL_3(F)\cong F^\times.
$$
Thus
$$
[GL_3(F):SL_3(F)]=p-1,
$$
and step <1>1 gives the displayed formula.
:::

<1>4. Therefore
$$
\boxed{\abs{SL_3(F)}=p^3(p^3-1)(p^2-1)}.
$$

::: {.proof}
Factor
$$
p^3-p=p(p^2-1),
\qquad
p^3-p^2=p^2(p-1)
$$
in step <1>3 and cancel $p-1$.
:::

<1>5. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>4 gives the requested order.
:::
