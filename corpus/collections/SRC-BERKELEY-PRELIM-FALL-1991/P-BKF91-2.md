::: {.problem}
Let $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ be continuous and suppose
\[
|f(x)-f(y)|\ge |x-y|
\]
for all $x,y\in\mathbb R$. Prove that the range of $f$ is all of $\mathbb R$.
:::
