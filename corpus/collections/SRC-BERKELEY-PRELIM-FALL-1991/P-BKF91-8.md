::: {.problem}
Let $a_1,a_2,\ldots$ be positive real numbers.

1. Prove that
\[
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n<\infty
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\sqrt{a_na_{n+1}}<\infty.
\]

2. Prove that the converse is false.
:::
