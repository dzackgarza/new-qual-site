::: {.problem}
Let $f$ be analytic in the unit disk $|z|<1$. Assume that there is a constant $M>0$ such that
\[
\int_0^{2\pi}|f'(re^{i\theta})|\,d\theta\le M
\qquad(0\le r<1).
\]
Prove that
\[
\int_0^1|f(x)|\,dx<\infty.
\]
:::
