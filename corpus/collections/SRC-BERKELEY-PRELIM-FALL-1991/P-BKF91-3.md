::: {.problem}
Let $n$ be a positive integer. Evaluate
\[
I=\frac{1}{2\pi i}\int_C\frac{z^{n-1}}{3z^n-1}\,dz,
\]
where $C$ is the counterclockwise unit circle $|z|=1$.
:::
