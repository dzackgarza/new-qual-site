::: {.problem}
Let $p$ be a nonconstant complex polynomial whose zeros all lie in the half-plane $\operatorname{Im}z>0$.

1. Prove that
\[
\operatorname{Im}\frac{p'(x)}{p(x)}>0
\]
for real $x$.

2. Find a relation between $\deg p$ and
\[
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}
\operatorname{Im}\frac{p'(x)}{p(x)}\,dx.
\]
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $d=\deg p$, and list the zeros of $p$, with multiplicity, as
$$
\alpha_j=a_j+ib_j,
\qquad
b_j>0,
\qquad
1\le j\le d.
$$

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

For every real $x$,
$$
\frac{p'(x)}{p(x)}
=
\sum_{j=1}^d\frac{1}{x-\alpha_j}.
$$

::: pf-proof

Write
$$
p(z)=c\prod_{j=1}^d(z-\alpha_j),
\qquad c\ne0.
$$
Since no zero $\alpha_j$ is real, $p(x)\ne0$ for $x\in\RR$. Taking the logarithmic derivative of the finite product gives the displayed identity.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

For every real $x$ and every $j$,
$$
\operatorname{Im}\frac{1}{x-\alpha_j}
=
\frac{b_j}{(x-a_j)^2+b_j^2}>0.
$$

::: pf-proof

Since
$$
x-\alpha_j=(x-a_j)-ib_j,
$$
one has
$$
\frac{1}{x-\alpha_j}
=
\frac{(x-a_j)+ib_j}{(x-a_j)^2+b_j^2}.
$$
The asserted imaginary part follows, and it is positive because $b_j>0$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

For every real $x$,
$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Im}\frac{p'(x)}{p(x)}>0}.
$$

::: pf-proof

By steps [](#s1){.pf-ref} and [](#s2){.pf-ref},
$$
\operatorname{Im}\frac{p'(x)}{p(x)}
=
\sum_{j=1}^d
\frac{b_j}{(x-a_j)^2+b_j^2},
$$
a finite sum of strictly positive terms.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s4}

For each $j$,
$$
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}
\frac{b_j}{(x-a_j)^2+b_j^2}\,dx
=
\pi.
$$

::: pf-proof

With
$$
u=\frac{x-a_j}{b_j},
$$
the integral becomes
$$
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{du}{1+u^2}
=
\pi.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s5}

Therefore
$$
\boxed{
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}
\operatorname{Im}\frac{p'(x)}{p(x)}\,dx
=
\pi\deg p}.
$$

::: pf-proof

Use the finite sum in step [](#s3){.pf-ref}, integrate term-by-term, and apply step [](#s4){.pf-ref}. There are exactly $d=\deg p$ roots counted with multiplicity.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s3){.pf-ref} proves part 1, and step [](#s5){.pf-ref} gives the requested relation in part 2.

:::
