::: {.problem}
Let $X,Y$ be metric spaces and let $f:X\to Y$ be continuous. Let $K_1,K_2,\ldots$ be nonempty compact subsets of $X$ with
\[
K_{n+1}\subseteq K_n
\]
for all $n$, and put
\[
K=\bigcap_{n=1}^{\infty}K_n.
\]
Prove that
\[
f(K)=\bigcap_{n=1}^{\infty}f(K_n).
\]
:::
