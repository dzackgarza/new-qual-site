::: {.problem}
Let $X,Y$ be metric spaces and let $f:X\to Y$ be continuous. Let $K_1,K_2,\ldots$ be nonempty compact subsets of $X$ with
\[
K_{n+1}\subseteq K_n
\]
for all $n$, and put
\[
K=\bigcap_{n=1}^{\infty}K_n.
\]
Prove that
\[
f(K)=\bigcap_{n=1}^{\infty}f(K_n).
\]
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

One has
$$
f(K)\subseteq\bigcap_{n=1}^{\infty}f(K_n).
$$

::: pf-proof

Since
$$
K\subseteq K_n
$$
for every $n$, one has
$$
f(K)\subseteq f(K_n)
$$
for every $n$. Taking the intersection over $n$ gives the inclusion.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

Let
$$
y\in\bigcap_{n=1}^{\infty}f(K_n).
$$
For each $n$, choose $x_n\in K_n$ such that
$$
f(x_n)=y.
$$

::: pf-proof

The membership $y\in f(K_n)$ gives at least one such $x_n$ for every $n$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

The sequence $(x_n)$ has a subsequence converging to some point $x\in K_1$.

::: pf-proof

Nestedness gives $K_n\subseteq K_1$ for every $n$, so the entire sequence lies in the compact metric space $K_1$. Sequential compactness yields a convergent subsequence
$$
x_{n_j}\longrightarrow x\in K_1.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s4}

The limit point $x$ belongs to every $K_m$.

::: pf-proof

Fix $m$. For all sufficiently large $j$, one has $n_j\ge m$, and therefore
$$
x_{n_j}\in K_{n_j}\subseteq K_m.
$$
The compact set $K_m$ is closed in the metric space $X$, so the limit of this tail also belongs to $K_m$. Thus $x\in K_m$. Since $m$ was arbitrary,
$$
x\in\bigcap_{m=1}^{\infty}K_m=K.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s5}

One has $f(x)=y$.

::: pf-proof

By continuity of $f$ and step [](#s3){.pf-ref},
$$
f(x)
=
\lim_{j\to\infty}f(x_{n_j})
=
\lim_{j\to\infty}y
=y.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s6}

Therefore
$$
\bigcap_{n=1}^{\infty}f(K_n)\subseteq f(K).
$$

::: pf-proof

Starting with an arbitrary $y$ in the left-hand side, steps [](#s2){.pf-ref}, [](#s3){.pf-ref}, [](#s4){.pf-ref} and [](#s5){.pf-ref} produce $x\in K$ with $f(x)=y$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s7}

Hence
$$
\boxed{f(K)=\bigcap_{n=1}^{\infty}f(K_n)}.
$$

::: pf-proof

Combine steps [](#s1){.pf-ref} and [](#s6){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s7){.pf-ref} is the required equality.

:::
