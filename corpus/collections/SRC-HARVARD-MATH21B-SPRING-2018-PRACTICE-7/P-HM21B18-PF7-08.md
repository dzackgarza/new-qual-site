::: {.problem}
Solve
$$
x_{n+1}-x_n=5y_n,
\qquad
y_{n+1}-y_n=5x_n
$$
with initial condition
$$
x_0=5,\qquad y_0=7.
$$
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

The solution is
$$
\boxed{
\binom{x_n}{y_n}
=6\cdot6^n\binom11-(-4)^n\binom1{-1}
}.
$$
Equivalently,
$$
x_n=6^{n+1}-(-4)^n,
\qquad
y_n=6^{n+1}+(-4)^n.
$$


::: pf-proof

::: pf-step

The recurrence has matrix form
$$
v_{n+1}=Mv_n,
\qquad
M=\begin{pmatrix}1&5\\5&1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
v_0=\binom57.
$$


::: pf-proof

The two scalar recurrence equations are equivalent to
$$
x_{n+1}=x_n+5y_n,
\qquad
y_{n+1}=5x_n+y_n,
$$
which is precisely the displayed matrix recurrence.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

The matrix $M$ has eigenpairs
$$
6,\binom11
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
-4,\binom1{-1}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Direct multiplication gives
$$
M\binom11=6\binom11,
\qquad
M\binom1{-1}=-4\binom1{-1}.
$$
The two eigenvectors are linearly independent, so they form a basis of $\RR^2$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

The initial vector decomposes as
$$
v_0=6\binom11-\binom1{-1}.
$$


::: pf-proof

The right-hand side equals
$$
\binom66-\binom1{-1}=\binom57=v_0.
$$

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Applying $M^n$ to the decomposition in step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} and using the eigenpairs in step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} gives
$$
v_n=M^nv_0
=6\cdot6^n\binom11-(-4)^n\binom1{-1},
$$
which is the formula in step [](#s1){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1){.pf-ref} gives the requested closed-form solution satisfying the initial condition.

:::
