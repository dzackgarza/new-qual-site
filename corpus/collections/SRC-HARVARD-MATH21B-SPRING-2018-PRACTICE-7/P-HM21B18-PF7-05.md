::: {.problem}
Let
\[
A=23000I_6+
\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\1\\1\\1\\1\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}1&3&5&7&9&11\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Find all eigenvalues and eigenvectors of $A$ and document the reasoning.
:::

::: {.solution}

Set
\[
u=\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\1\\1\\1\\1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
v=\begin{pmatrix}1\\3\\5\\7\\9\\11\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
B=uv^T.
\]
Then $A=23000I_6+B$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

The complete eigenspace decomposition is
\[
\boxed{
E_{23036}(A)=\operatorname{span}\{u\},
\qquad
E_{23000}(A)=v^\perp
}.
\]
Hence $23036$ has algebraic and geometric multiplicity $1$, while $23000$ has algebraic and geometric multiplicity $5$.


::: pf-proof

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

The rank-one map $B$ satisfies
\[
Bx=(v^Tx)u
\]
for every $x\in\RR^6$.


::: pf-proof

This is the defining action of the outer product $uv^T$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

The vector $u$ is a $36$-eigenvector of $B$.


::: pf-proof

One has
\[
v^Tu=1+3+5+7+9+11=36.
\]
Therefore step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} gives
\[
Bu=(v^Tu)u=36u.
\]

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

The $0$-eigenspace of $B$ is $v^\perp$ and has dimension $5$.


::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref},
\[
Bx=0
\iff
v^Tx=0,
\]
because $u\ne0$. Thus $\ker B=v^\perp$. Since $v\ne0$, this hyperplane has dimension $5$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

The two eigenspaces in steps [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} and [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} form a direct sum equal to $\RR^6$.


::: pf-proof

The vector $u$ does not lie in $v^\perp$ because $v^Tu=36\ne0$. Hence
\[
\operatorname{span}\{u\}\cap v^\perp=\{0\}.
\]
Their dimensions are $1$ and $5$, so their direct sum has dimension $6$ and therefore equals $\RR^6$.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

On $\operatorname{span}\{u\}$, the operator $B$ has eigenvalue $36$, while on $v^\perp$ it has eigenvalue $0$. Adding $23000I_6$ shifts these eigenvalues to $23036$ and $23000$ without changing the eigenspaces. Step [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} shows that these are all eigenvectors and accounts for all multiplicities.

:::

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

One convenient basis for the $23000$-eigenspace is
\[
\begin{pmatrix}3\\-1\\0\\0\\0\\0\end{pmatrix},
\quad
\begin{pmatrix}5\\0\\-1\\0\\0\\0\end{pmatrix},
\quad
\begin{pmatrix}7\\0\\0\\-1\\0\\0\end{pmatrix},
\quad
\begin{pmatrix}9\\0\\0\\0\\-1\\0\end{pmatrix},
\quad
\begin{pmatrix}11\\0\\0\\0\\0\\-1\end{pmatrix}.
\]


::: pf-proof

Each displayed vector has dot product $0$ with $v$, so each lies in $v^\perp=E_{23000}(A)$ by step [](#s1){.pf-ref}. They are linearly independent because their last five coordinates form $-I_5$. Since the eigenspace has dimension $5$, they form a basis.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1){.pf-ref} gives all eigenvalues and eigenspaces, and step [](#s2){.pf-ref} supplies an explicit eigenbasis for the multiplicity-$5$ eigenspace.

:::
