::: {.problem}
(a) Find the Fourier series on $[-\pi,\pi]$ of
\[
f(x)=
\begin{cases}
1,&|x|<\pi/4,\\
0,&|x|\ge\pi/4.
\end{cases}
\]

(b) Given
\[
x^3-\pi^2x
=
\sum_{n=1}^\infty\frac{12(-1)^n}{n^3}\sin(nx),
\]
compute
\[
\sum_{n=1}^\infty\frac1{n^6}.
\]
:::
