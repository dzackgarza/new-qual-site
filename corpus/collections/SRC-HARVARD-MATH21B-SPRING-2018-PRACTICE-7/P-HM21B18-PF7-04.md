::: {.problem}
Find all solutions in variables $x,y,z,u$ of
\[
x-y=4,
\qquad
y-z=5,
\qquad
z+u=7.
\]
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. The complete solution set is
\[
\boxed{
(x,y,z,u)=(16-s,\,12-s,\,7-s,\,s),
\qquad s\in\mathbb R
}.
\]

::: {.proof}
The variable $u$ is free; set $u=s$. The third equation gives
\[
z=7-s.
\]
Then the second equation gives
\[
y=z+5=12-s,
\]
and the first gives
\[
x=y+4=16-s.
\]
Thus every solution has the displayed form. Conversely, direct substitution gives
\[
(16-s)-(12-s)=4,
\]
\[
(12-s)-(7-s)=5,
\]
and
\[
(7-s)+s=7,
\]
so every displayed quadruple is a solution.
:::

<1>2. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>1 proves both necessity and sufficiency for the stated parametrization.
:::
:::
