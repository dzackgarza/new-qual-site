::: {.problem}
Let a symmetric matrix $A$ have QR decomposition $A=QR$, and set $B=RQ$.

(a) Verify that $A$ and $B$ have the same eigenvalues.

(b) Is the map $T:A\mapsto B$ linear?

(c) What happens when $T$ is applied to a diagonal matrix?

(d) Iterating $T$ produces matrices converging to a diagonal matrix; take this convergence for granted. Show that the iteration also produces an orthonormal set of eigenvectors of $A$.
:::
