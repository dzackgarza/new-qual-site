::: {.problem}
Let $I=(7,x-3)$ in $\mathbb Z[x]$.

1. Prove that for every $r\in\mathbb Z[x]$ there is an integer $\alpha$ with $0\le\alpha\le6$ such that
\[
r-\alpha\in I.
\]
2. Find $\alpha$ for
\[
r=x^{250}+15x^{14}+x^2+5.
\]
:::
