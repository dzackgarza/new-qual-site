::: {.problem}
For a function $f:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R$, consider:

1. $f$ is continuous;
2. the graph of $f$ is connected in $\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R$.

Prove or disprove each implication
\[
(1)\Longrightarrow(2),
\qquad
(2)\Longrightarrow(1).
\]
:::
