::: {.problem}
Let $N$ be an operator on an $n$-dimensional vector space, $n>1$, such that
\[
N^n=0,
\qquad
N^{n-1}\ne0.
\]
Prove that there is no operator $X$ with
\[
X^2=N.
\]
:::
