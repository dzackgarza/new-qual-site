::: {.problem}
Let
\[
B=\begin{pmatrix}
a&0&0\\
0&b&0\\
0&0&c
\end{pmatrix},
\]
where $a,b,c\in\mathbb R$ and $|a|,|b|,|c|$ are distinct. Show that there are exactly four symmetric matrices of the form $BQ$, where $Q$ is a real orthogonal matrix with determinant $1$.
:::
