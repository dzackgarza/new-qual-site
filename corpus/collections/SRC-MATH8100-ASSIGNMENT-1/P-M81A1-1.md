::: problem
Let $C$ be the ternary Cantor set.

1. Find a point of $C$ that is not an endpoint of a removed interval.

2. Prove that $C$ is nowhere dense and has measure zero.

3. Prove that $C$ is uncountable by mapping its ternary digits $a_k\in\{0,2\}$ to binary digits $b_k=a_k/2$ and showing that the resulting function maps $C$ onto $[0,1]$.
:::
