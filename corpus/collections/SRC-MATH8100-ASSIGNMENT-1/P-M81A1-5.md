::: problem
Let $C([0,1])$ have the uniform metric $d_\infty(f,g)=\sup_{x\in[0,1]}|f(x)-g(x)|$.

1. Prove that $d_\infty$ is a metric and that $C([0,1])$ is complete.
2. Prove that its closed unit ball is closed and bounded, but not compact.
3. Prove that $C([0,1])$ is not totally bounded.
:::
