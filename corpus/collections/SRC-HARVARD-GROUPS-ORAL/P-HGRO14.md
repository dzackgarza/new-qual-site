::: problem
Let $H$ be the commutator subgroup of a group $G$.
Prove that $G/H$ is abelian.
:::

::: solution
Let $H=[G,G]$, the subgroup generated by all commutators
\[
[x,y]=xyx^{-1}y^{-1}.
\]

<1>1. For all $x,y\in G$, the cosets $xH$ and $yH$ commute in $G/H$.
::: proof
Since $[x,y]\in H$,
\[
xyx^{-1}y^{-1}H=H.
\]
Multiplying cosets gives
\[
xyH=yxH.
\]
Equivalently,
\[
(xH)(yH)=(yH)(xH).
\]
:::

<1>2. Hence $G/H$ is abelian.
::: proof
Every element of $G/H$ is a coset $xH$, and <1>1 shows that every pair of
cosets commute.
:::
:::
