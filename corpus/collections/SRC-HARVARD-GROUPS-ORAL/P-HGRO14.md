::: {.problem}
Let $H$ be the commutator subgroup of a group $G$.
Prove that $G/H$ is abelian.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $H=[G,G]$, the subgroup generated by all commutators
\[
[x,y]=xyx^{-1}y^{-1}.
\]

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

For all $x,y\in G$, the cosets $xH$ and $yH$ commute in $G/H$.

::: pf-proof

Since $[x,y]\in H$,
\[
xyx^{-1}y^{-1}H=H.
\]
Multiplying cosets gives
\[
xyH=yxH.
\]
Equivalently,
\[
(xH)(yH)=(yH)(xH).
\]

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Hence $G/H$ is abelian.

::: pf-proof

Every element of $G/H$ is a coset $xH$, and step [](#s1){.pf-ref} shows that every pair of
cosets commute.

:::
