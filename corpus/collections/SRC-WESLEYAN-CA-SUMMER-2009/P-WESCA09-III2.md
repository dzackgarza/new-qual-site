::: problem
Let $u\in C^2(D)$ be harmonic on a simply connected domain $D\subset\mathbb C$.
Prove that $u$ has a harmonic conjugate $v$, so that $u+iv$ is analytic on $D$.
Hint: consider $u_x-iu_y$.
:::
