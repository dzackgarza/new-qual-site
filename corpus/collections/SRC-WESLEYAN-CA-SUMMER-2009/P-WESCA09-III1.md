::: problem
Let $f$ be analytic on the disk $\Delta(z_0,r)$ and suppose there is $m>0$ such that
\[
|f(z)-f(z_0)|\le m\qquad(z\in\Delta(z_0,r)).
\]
Prove
\[
|f'(z_0)|\le \frac mr.
\]
:::
