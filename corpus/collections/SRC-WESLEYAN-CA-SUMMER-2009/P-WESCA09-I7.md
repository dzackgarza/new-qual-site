::: problem
Let $D$ be the bounded region inside
\[
\{z\in\mathbb C:|\operatorname{Re}z|+|\operatorname{Im}z|=1\},
\]
and orient $\partial D$ positively.
Calculate
\[
\int_{\partial D}\frac{\cos z}{z(z^2+8)}\,dz.
\]
:::
