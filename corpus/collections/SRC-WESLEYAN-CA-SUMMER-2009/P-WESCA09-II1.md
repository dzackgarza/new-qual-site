::: problem
Let $f$ be entire and suppose
\[
|f(z)|\le m e^{\alpha x},\qquad z=x+iy,
\]
where $m,\alpha>0$.
Show that
\[
f(z)=Ae^{\alpha z}
\]
for some $A\in\mathbb C$.
:::
