::: {.problem}
The free group $F(a,b)$ on two generators is the fundamental group of the one-point union $X=S^1\vee S^1$ of two circles, using the common point $*$ as basepoint, with $a$ and $b$ corresponding to each of the loops $S^1$.
Construct the covering space of $X$ corresponding to the subgroup $H=\langle a^2b,aba,ab^2,bab,b^2a\rangle$ of $F(a,b)$, and determine the index of $H$ in $F(a,b)$.
:::
::: {.solution}
<1>1. Let $\phi:F(a,b)\to\mathbb Z/3$ be the homomorphism with $\phi(a)=\phi(b)=1$.
::: {.proof}
Each of the five generators $a^2b,aba,ab^2,bab,b^2a$ has total exponent sum $3$, so each lies in $K=\ker\phi$. Hence $H\le K$. Since $\phi$ is surjective, $[F(a,b):K]=3$.
:::

<1>2. A Schreier transversal $\{1,a,a^2\}$ for $K$ gives the free basis
$$
s_1=ba^{-1},\qquad s_2=aba^{-2},\qquad s_3=a^3,\qquad s_4=a^2b
$$
of $K$.
::: {.proof}
Apply the Reidemeister--Schreier rewriting process to the index-$3$ kernel of $\phi$ with coset representatives $1,a,a^2$. The nontrivial Schreier generators are exactly the four displayed words.
:::

<1>3. All four Schreier generators lie in $H$.
::: {.proof}
Write the five given generators as
$$
\begin{aligned}
u&=a^2b=s_4,\\
v&=aba=s_2s_3,\\
w&=ab^2=s_2s_4,\\
x&=bab=s_1s_4,\\
y&=b^2a=s_1s_2s_3.
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus $s_4=u$, $s_2=wu^{-1}$, $s_1=xu^{-1}$, and $s_3=s_2^{-1}v$ all lie in $H$.
:::

<1>4. Therefore $H=K$ and $[F(a,b):H]=3$.
::: {.proof}
By <1>1, $H\le K$, while <1>2--<1>3 show that the basis of $K$ lies in $H$, so $K\le H$.
:::

<1>5. The corresponding connected covering graph has three vertices $v_0,v_1,v_2$, and for each label $c\in\{a,b\}$ an oriented $c$-edge
$$
v_0\longrightarrow v_1\longrightarrow v_2\longrightarrow v_0.
$$
The basepoint is $v_0$.
::: {.proof}
The vertices correspond to the three cosets of $H=\ker\phi$, identified with $\mathbb Z/3$. Traversing either an $a$-edge or a $b$-edge adds $1$ modulo $3$, because both generators have image $1$ under $\phi$. Thus each vertex has exactly one incoming and one outgoing edge of each label, so the label-preserving graph map to the rose $S^1\vee S^1$ is a $3$-sheeted covering. The loops at $v_0$ are precisely the words with $\phi$-value $0$, namely $H$.
:::
