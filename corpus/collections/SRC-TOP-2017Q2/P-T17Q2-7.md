::: {.problem}
The free group $F(a,b)$ on two generators is the fundamental group of the one-point union $X=S^1\vee S^1$ of two circles, using the common point $*$ as basepoint, with $a$ and $b$ corresponding to each of the loops $S^1$.
Construct the covering space of $X$ corresponding to the subgroup $H=\langle a^2b,aba,ab^2,bab,b^2a\rangle$ of $F(a,b)$, and determine the index of $H$ in $F(a,b)$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $\phi\colon F(a,b)\to\ZZ/3$ be the surjective homomorphism with $\phi(a)=\phi(b)=1$, and $K=\ker\phi$, a subgroup of index $3$.

<1>1. $H\le K$.

::: {.proof}
Each of $a^2b$, $aba$, $ab^2$, $bab$, $b^2a$ has total exponent sum $3$, so $\phi$ vanishes on it.
:::

<1>2. $K$ is free on $s_1=ba^{-1}$, $s_2=aba^{-2}$, $s_3=a^3$, $s_4=a^2b$.

::: {.proof}
Apply the Reidemeister--Schreier process with the Schreier transversal $\{1,a,a^2\}$.
For the generator $a$ the Schreier generators are $1$, $1$, and $a^2\cdot a=a^3$; for $b$ they are $b\,a^{-1}$, $ab\,a^{-2}$, and $a^2b$.
:::

<1>3. $K\le H$.

::: {.proof}
The generators of $H$ are $u=a^2b=s_4$, $v=aba=s_2s_3$, $w=ab^2=s_2s_4$, $x=bab=s_1s_4$, and $y=b^2a=s_1s_2s_3$.
Hence $s_4=u$, $s_2=wu^{-1}$, $s_1=xu^{-1}$, and $s_3=s_2^{-1}v$ lie in $H$, and by step <1>2 so does $K$.
:::

<1>4. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
By steps <1>1 and <1>3, $H=K$, so $[F(a,b):H]=\boxed{3}$.
The covering space is the graph with vertices $v_0,v_1,v_2$ indexed by $\ZZ/3$, basepoint $v_0$, and for each label $c\in\{a,b\}$ oriented $c$-edges $v_0\to v_1\to v_2\to v_0$, mapped to $S^1\vee S^1$ by sending each $c$-edge around the loop $c$.
Each vertex has exactly one incoming and one outgoing edge of each label, so this is a $3$-sheeted covering.
A word in $a,b$ lifts to a loop at $v_0$ exactly when its image under $\phi$ is $0$, so the subgroup of this based covering is $K=H$.
:::
:::
