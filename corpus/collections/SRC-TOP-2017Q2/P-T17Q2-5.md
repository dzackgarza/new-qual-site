::: {.problem}
If $H:X\times[0,1]\to X$ is a homotopy with $H_0=H_1$ equal to the identity map, show that the map $\gamma:I\to X$ given by $\gamma(t)=H(x_0,t)$ is a loop in $X$ representing an element $g=[\gamma]\in\pi_1(X,x_0)$ which lies in the center of $\pi_1(X,x_0)$, i.e., $gh=hg$ for all $h\in\pi_1(X,x_0)$.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. $\gamma$ is a loop based at $x_0$.

::: {.proof}
Since $H_0=H_1=\operatorname{id}_X$, $\gamma(0)=H(x_0,0)=x_0=H(x_0,1)=\gamma(1)$.
:::

<1>2. For every loop $\alpha$ at $x_0$, $[\alpha][\gamma]=[\gamma][\alpha]$ in $\pi_1(X,x_0)$.

::: {.proof}
Define $F\colon I\times I\to X$ by $F(s,t)=H(\alpha(s),t)$, continuous as the composite of $(s,t)\mapsto(\alpha(s),t)$ with $H$.
On the bottom and top edges $F(s,0)=F(s,1)=\alpha(s)$, and on the vertical edges $F(0,t)=F(1,t)=H(x_0,t)=\gamma(t)$ because $\alpha(0)=\alpha(1)=x_0$.
Traversing the boundary of the square counterclockwise from $(0,0)$ gives the loop $\alpha*\gamma*\bar\alpha*\bar\gamma$, which is therefore nullhomotopic because $F$ extends it over the square.
Hence $[\alpha][\gamma][\alpha]^{-1}[\gamma]^{-1}=1$.
:::

<1>3. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
By step <1>1, $g=[\gamma]\in\pi_1(X,x_0)$, and by step <1>2 it commutes with every element of $\pi_1(X,x_0)$.
:::
:::
