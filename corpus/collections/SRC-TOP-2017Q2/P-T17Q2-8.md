::: {.problem}
Find a $\Delta$-complex structure for, and compute the (simplicial) homology groups of, the space $X$ obtained by gluing a $2$-disk $D^2$ to the $2$-sphere $S^2$ by a homeomorphism from $\partial D^2$ to the equator of $S^2$.
That is, $$X=\{(x,y,z)\in\RR^3:x^2+y^2\le1\text{ and either }x^2+y^2+z^2=1\text{ or }z=0\}.$$ [Your description of a $\Delta$-complex structure need only contain enough detail to describe your homology calculations.]
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. $X$ has a $\Delta$-complex structure with $6$ vertices, $12$ edges, and $9$ triangles: triangulate the equator by vertices $v_0,v_1,v_2$ and edges $e_{01},e_{12},e_{20}$, and cone this triangle to one interior vertex in each of the northern hemisphere $N$, the southern hemisphere $S$, and the attached disk $D$.

::: {.proof}
Each of the three closed disks $N$, $S$, $D$ is subdivided into three $2$-simplices, and the three triangulations agree on their common boundary, the equator.
This is a simplicial complex, hence a $\Delta$-complex, with $3+3=6$ vertices, $3+3\cdot3=12$ edges, and $3\cdot3=9$ triangles.
:::

<1>2. $H_2(X)\cong\ZZ^2$.

::: {.proof}
Let $E=e_{01}+e_{12}+e_{20}$, and let $C_N,C_S,C_D$ be the sums of the three triangles of each disk, oriented so that $\partial C_N=E$, $\partial C_S=-E$, $\partial C_D=E$; within each disk the radial edges cancel.
In a $2$-cycle, cancellation along each radial edge forces the three coefficients within each disk to be equal, so every $2$-cycle is $rC_N+sC_S+tC_D$, with boundary $(r-s+t)E$.
Hence $\ker\partial_2\cong\{(r,s,t)\in\ZZ^3:r-s+t=0\}\cong\ZZ^2$, and with no $3$-simplices $H_2(X)=\ker\partial_2$.
:::

<1>3. $H_0(X)\cong\ZZ$ and $H_1(X)=0$.

::: {.proof}
The complex is connected, so $H_0(X)\cong\ZZ$.
$N\cup S=S^2$ is simply connected, and $X$ is obtained from it by attaching the disk $D$ along the equator.
By the Seifert--van Kampen theorem, attaching a $2$-cell quotients $\pi_1$ by the normal closure of the attaching loop, so $\pi_1(X)=0$.
$H_1(X)$ is the abelianization of $\pi_1(X)$, hence zero.
:::

<1>4. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>1 gives the $\Delta$-complex structure, and steps <1>2 and <1>3, with no simplices above dimension $2$, give
$$H_i(X)\cong\begin{cases}\ZZ,&i=0,\\\ZZ^2,&i=2,\\0,&\text{otherwise}.\end{cases}$$
:::
