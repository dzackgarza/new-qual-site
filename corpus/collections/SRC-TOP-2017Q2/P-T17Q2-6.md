::: {.problem}
Suppose that $X=U\cup V$ with $U,V$ both open and $U\cap V$, $U$, and $V$ path-connected, and $x_0\in U\cap V$.
Suppose also that the inclusion $i:U\cap V\to U$ induces a surjection $i_*:\pi_1(U\cap V,x_0)\to\pi_1(U,x_0)$.
Show that the inclusion $j:V\to X$ also induces a surjection $j_*:\pi_1(V,x_0)\to\pi_1(X,x_0)$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let $k\colon U\to X$ and $l\colon U\cap V\to V$ be the inclusions, so that $k\circ i=j\circ l$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #generated-by-both}
$\pi_1(X,x_0)$ is generated by $k_*\pi_1(U,x_0)$ and $j_*\pi_1(V,x_0)$.

::: pf-proof
$U$ and $V$ are open, and $U$, $V$, $U\cap V$ are path-connected and contain $x_0$, so this is part of the Seifert--van Kampen theorem.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #k-image-in-j-image}
$k_*\pi_1(U,x_0)\subseteq j_*\pi_1(V,x_0)$.

::: pf-proof
Let $[\alpha]\in\pi_1(U,x_0)$.
Since $i_*$ is surjective, $[\alpha]=i_*[\beta]$ for some $[\beta]\in\pi_1(U\cap V,x_0)$.
Then $k_*[\alpha]=(k\circ i)_*[\beta]=(j\circ l)_*[\beta]=j_*(l_*[\beta])$.
:::

:::

::: pf-qed
By steps [](#generated-by-both){.pf-ref} and [](#k-image-in-j-image){.pf-ref}, $\pi_1(X,x_0)$ is generated by elements of the subgroup $j_*\pi_1(V,x_0)$, so $j_*$ is surjective.
:::
