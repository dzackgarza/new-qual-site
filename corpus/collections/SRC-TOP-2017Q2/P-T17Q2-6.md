::: {.problem}
Suppose that $X=U\cup V$ with $U,V$ both open and $U\cap V$, $U$, and $V$ path-connected, and $x_0\in U\cap V$.
Suppose also that the inclusion $i:U\cap V\to U$ induces a surjection $i_*:\pi_1(U\cap V,x_0)\to\pi_1(U,x_0)$.
Show that the inclusion $j:V\to X$ also induces a surjection $j_*:\pi_1(V,x_0)\to\pi_1(X,x_0)$.
:::
::: {.solution}
<1>1. By Seifert--van Kampen, $\pi_1(X,x_0)$ is generated by the images of $\pi_1(U,x_0)$ and $\pi_1(V,x_0)$, with the two images of $\pi_1(U\cap V,x_0)$ identified.
::: {.proof}
The hypotheses that $U,V,U\cap V$ are path-connected and that $U,V$ are open are exactly the standard hypotheses for the based Seifert--van Kampen theorem.
:::

<1>2. Every element coming from $\pi_1(U,x_0)$ has the same image in $\pi_1(X,x_0)$ as an element coming from $\pi_1(V,x_0)$.
::: {.proof}
Let $[\alpha]\in\pi_1(U,x_0)$. Since $i_*:\pi_1(U\cap V,x_0)\to\pi_1(U,x_0)$ is surjective, choose $[\beta]\in\pi_1(U\cap V,x_0)$ with $i_*[\beta]=[\alpha]$. In the van Kampen pushout, the image of $i_*[\beta]$ from the $U$ side is identified with the image of the same loop $\beta$ through the inclusion $U\cap V\hookrightarrow V$. Thus the image of $[\alpha]$ in $\pi_1(X,x_0)$ lies in the image of $j_*:\pi_1(V,x_0)\to\pi_1(X,x_0)$.
:::

<1>3. The homomorphism $j_*$ is surjective.
::: {.proof}
By <1>1, $\pi_1(X,x_0)$ is generated by the images of the $U$- and $V$-groups. The $V$-image is contained in $\operatorname{im}j_*$ by definition, and <1>2 shows the $U$-image is also contained in $\operatorname{im}j_*$. Therefore the whole group lies in $\operatorname{im}j_*$.
:::
:::
