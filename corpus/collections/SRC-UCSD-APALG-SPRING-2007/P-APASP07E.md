::: {.problem}
Let $G$ be a finite group, and let $e_\lambda = \sum_g c_g g$ be a minimal idempotent in the simple component of $\mathbb{C}[G]$ labeled by $\lambda$.
Let $d_\lambda$ be the dimension of a simple $G$-module on which $e_\lambda$ acts nonzero.

(a) What is the dimension of the space $\mathbb{C}[G] e_\lambda$?
What is $\chi^{\text{reg}}(e_\lambda)$, where $\chi^{\text{reg}}$ is the character of the left-regular representation?

(b) Calculate the coefficient $c_e$.
:::

::: {.solution}
By Maschke's theorem and Wedderburn decomposition,
\[
\mathbb C[G]\cong \bigoplus_{\mu} M_{d_\mu}(\mathbb C),
\]
where $d_\mu$ is the dimension of the irreducible representation labeled by $\mu$. A minimal idempotent in the $\lambda$-block is conjugate inside $M_{d_\lambda}(\mathbb C)$ to a matrix unit $E_{11}$. Hence the left ideal generated by it is isomorphic to
\[
M_{d_\lambda}(\mathbb C)E_{11},
\]
the space of matrices whose only possibly nonzero column is the first. Therefore
\[
\boxed{\dim_{\mathbb C}\mathbb C[G]e_\lambda=d_\lambda}.
\]

Left multiplication by $e_\lambda$ on the regular module is idempotent, since $e_\lambda^2=e_\lambda$. Its image is precisely $\mathbb C[G]e_\lambda$. Thus left multiplication by $e_\lambda$ is a projection of rank $d_\lambda$, and therefore its trace is
\[
\boxed{\chi^{\mathrm{reg}}(e_\lambda)=d_\lambda}.
\]

On the other hand, extending the regular character linearly to $\mathbb C[G]$, we have
\[
\chi^{\mathrm{reg}}(e_\lambda)
=
\sum_{g\in G} c_g\chi^{\mathrm{reg}}(g).
\]
For the left-regular representation,
\[
\chi^{\mathrm{reg}}(g)
=
\begin{cases}
|G|,&g=e,\\
0,&g\ne e.
\end{cases}
\]
Hence
\[
\chi^{\mathrm{reg}}(e_\lambda)=|G|c_e.
\]
Combining this with the preceding computation gives
\[
|G|c_e=d_\lambda,
\]
so
\[
\boxed{c_e=\frac{d_\lambda}{|G|}}.
\]
:::
