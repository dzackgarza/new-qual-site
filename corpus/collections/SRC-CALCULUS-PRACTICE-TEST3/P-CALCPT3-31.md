::: {.problem}
If
$$f(x)=\begin{cases}\sqrt{1-x^2} & \text{for } 0\le x\le 1,\\ x-1 & \text{for } 1<x\le 2,\end{cases}$$
then $\int_0^2 f(x)\,dx$ is

(A) $\frac{\pi}{2}$
(B) $\frac{\sqrt2}{2}$
(C) $\frac12+\frac{\pi}{4}$
(D) $\frac12+\frac{\pi}{2}$
(E) undefined
:::
