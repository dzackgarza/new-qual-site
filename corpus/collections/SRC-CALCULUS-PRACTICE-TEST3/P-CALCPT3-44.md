::: {.problem}
If $x$ is a real number and $P$ is a polynomial function, then
$$\lim_{h\to0}\frac{P(x+3h)+P(x-3h)-2P(x)}{h^2}=$$

(A) $0$
(B) $6P'(x)$
(C) $3P''(x)$
(D) $9P''(x)$
(E) $\infty$
:::
