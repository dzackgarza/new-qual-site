::: {.problem}
If $A$ and $B$ are events in a probability space such that $0<P(A)=P(B)=P(A\cap B)<1$, which of the following CANNOT be true?

(A) $A$ and $B$ are independent.
(B) $A$ is a proper subset of $B$.
(C) $A\ne B$
(D) $A\cap B=A\cup B$
(E) $P(A)P(B)<P(A\cap B)$
:::
