::: problem
Let
\[
\Omega_1=\{z:|z|<1,\ 0<\arg z<\alpha<2\pi\},
\qquad
\Omega_2=\{x+iy:x,y>0\}.
\]
(a) Find an explicit conformal map $\Omega_1\to\Omega_2$.

(b) How many conformal maps are there between these two domains?
Prove your answer.
:::
