::: {.problem}
Let $f$ be holomorphic with
\[
f(0)=f'(0)=f''(0)=0.
\]
Prove that there is $\varepsilon>0$ such that for every $w\in B_\varepsilon(0)$, the equation
\[
f(z)=w
\]
has at least three zeros counted with multiplicity.
:::
