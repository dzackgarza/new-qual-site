::: {.problem}
Let $C$ be the positively oriented boundary of the upper semicircle of radius $3$, including the diameter from $(-3,0)$ to $(3,0)$. Compute
\[
\oint_C (2x+y)\,dx+y\,dy
\]
both directly and by Green's theorem.
:::
