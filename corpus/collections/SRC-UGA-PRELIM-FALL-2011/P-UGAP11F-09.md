::: {.problem}
Define an equivalence relation on $\mathbb R^3$ by
\[
(x,y,z)\sim(x',y',z')
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
x+y+z=x'+y'+z'.
\]
Find a basis for the vector space
\[
V=\mathbb R^3/\!\sim
\]
and prove that it is a basis.
:::
