(a) Match the transformations A--F to the trace/determinant pairs
\[
(1,0),(0,-1),(0,1),(2,1),(4,4),(-2,1).
\]
The transformations are: rotation by $90^\circ$; projection onto a line; dilation by $2$; reflection in a line; shear; rotation by $180^\circ$.

(b) Match the initial-value problems
\[
f''=4,\ f(0)=4,\ f'(0)=0;
\]
\[
f''+4f=4,\ f(0)=2,\ f'(0)=2;
\]
\[
f''-4f=-4,\ f(0)=3,\ f'(0)=0;
\]
\[
f'-4f=4,\ f(0)=4
\]
to the four displayed candidate solutions.
