::: {.problem}
(a) Match the transformations A--F to the trace/determinant pairs
$$
(1,0),(0,-1),(0,1),(2,1),(4,4),(-2,1).
$$
The transformations are: rotation by $90^\circ$; projection onto a line; dilation by $2$; reflection in a line; shear; rotation by $180^\circ$.

(b) Match the initial-value problems
$$
f''=4,\ f(0)=4,\ f'(0)=0;
$$
$$
f''+4f=4,\ f(0)=2,\ f'(0)=2;
$$
$$
f''-4f=-4,\ f(0)=3,\ f'(0)=0;
$$
$$
f'-4f=4,\ f(0)=4
$$
to the candidate solutions
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm A&:\quad f(t)=e^{2t}+e^{-2t}+1,\\
\mathrm B&:\quad f(t)=1+\cos(2t)+\sin(2t),\\
\mathrm C&:\quad f(t)=5e^{4t}-1,\\
\mathrm D&:\quad f(t)=4+2t^2.
\end{aligned}
$$
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

The trace/determinant matching in the order in which the six pairs are listed is
$$
\boxed{\mathrm{B,D,A,E,C,F}}.
$$


::: pf-proof

A projection onto a line has eigenvalues $1,0$, hence trace $1$ and determinant $0$, giving B for $(1,0)$. A reflection in a line has eigenvalues $1,-1$, hence trace $0$ and determinant $-1$, giving D for $(0,-1)$. A rotation by $90^\circ$ has matrix
$$
\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},
$$
with trace $0$ and determinant $1$, giving A for $(0,1)$. A shear has matrix, in suitable coordinates,
$$
\begin{pmatrix}1&s\\0&1\end{pmatrix},
$$
with trace $2$ and determinant $1$, giving E for $(2,1)$. Dilation by $2$ is $2I_2$, with trace $4$ and determinant $4$, giving C for $(4,4)$. Rotation by $180^\circ$ is $-I_2$, with trace $-2$ and determinant $1$, giving F for $(-2,1)$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

The first initial-value problem matches candidate $\mathrm D$.


::: pf-proof

For $f(t)=4+2t^2$,
$$
f''(t)=4,
\qquad
f(0)=4,
\qquad
f'(0)=0.
$$
Thus candidate D satisfies exactly the first problem.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

The second initial-value problem matches candidate $\mathrm B$.


::: pf-proof

For $f(t)=1+\cos(2t)+\sin(2t)$,
$$
f''(t)=-4\cos(2t)-4\sin(2t),
$$
so $f''+4f=4$. Also $f(0)=2$ and
$$
f'(0)=-2\sin0+2\cos0=2.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

The third initial-value problem matches candidate $\mathrm A$.


::: pf-proof

For $f(t)=e^{2t}+e^{-2t}+1$,
$$
f''(t)=4e^{2t}+4e^{-2t},
$$
and hence $f''-4f=-4$. Moreover $f(0)=3$ and $f'(0)=2-2=0$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-5}

The fourth initial-value problem matches candidate $\mathrm C$.


::: pf-proof

For $f(t)=5e^{4t}-1$,
$$
f'(t)=20e^{4t},
$$
so $f'-4f=4$, and $f(0)=4$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-6}

Therefore the matching in part (b), in the order of the four initial-value problems, is
$$
\boxed{\mathrm{D,B,A,C}}.
$$


::: pf-proof

This is exactly the sequence established in steps [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} through [](#s1-5){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} proves part (a), and step [](#s1-6){.pf-ref} proves part (b).

:::
