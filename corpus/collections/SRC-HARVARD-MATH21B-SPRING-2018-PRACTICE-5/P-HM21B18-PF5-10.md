::: {.problem}
Solve the initial-value problems
$$
f''+f=3\sin(2t)+1,
\qquad
f(0)=3,\quad f'(0)=0,
$$
and
$$
f''-2f'+f=t^2,
\qquad
f(0)=2,\quad f'(0)=-4.
$$
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

The general solution of the first differential equation is
$$
f(t)=c_1\cos t+c_2\sin t-\sin(2t)+1.
$$


::: pf-proof

The homogeneous equation $f''+f=0$ has solution
$$
f_h(t)=c_1\cos t+c_2\sin t.
$$
For a particular solution, take
$$
f_p(t)=A\sin(2t)+B.
$$
Then
$$
f_p''+f_p=(-4A+A)\sin(2t)+B
=-3A\sin(2t)+B.
$$
Matching $3\sin(2t)+1$ gives $A=-1$ and $B=1$, proving the displayed
general solution.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

The solution of the first initial-value problem is
$$
\boxed{f(t)=2\cos t+2\sin t-\sin(2t)+1}.
$$


::: pf-proof

From step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref},
$$
f(0)=c_1+1=3,
$$
so $c_1=2$. Differentiating gives
$$
f'(t)=-c_1\sin t+c_2\cos t-2\cos(2t),
$$
and $f'(0)=c_2-2=0$, so $c_2=2$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

The general solution of the second differential equation is
$$
f(t)=(c_1+c_2t)e^t+t^2+4t+6.
$$


::: pf-proof

The homogeneous operator factors as
$$
D^2-2D+1=(D-1)^2,
$$
so
$$
f_h(t)=(c_1+c_2t)e^t.
$$
For a polynomial particular solution $f_p=At^2+Bt+C$, one has
$$
f_p''-2f_p'+f_p
=At^2+(B-4A)t+(2A-2B+C).
$$
Matching this with $t^2$ gives
$$
A=1,\qquad B=4,\qquad C=6.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

The solution of the second initial-value problem is
$$
\boxed{f(t)=-4e^t-4te^t+t^2+4t+6}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} gives
$$
f(0)=c_1+6=2,
$$
so $c_1=-4$. Also
$$
f'(t)=(c_1+c_2+c_2t)e^t+2t+4,
$$
and therefore
$$
f'(0)=c_1+c_2+4=-4.
$$
With $c_1=-4$, this gives $c_2=-4$.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Steps [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} and [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} are the two requested solutions.

:::
