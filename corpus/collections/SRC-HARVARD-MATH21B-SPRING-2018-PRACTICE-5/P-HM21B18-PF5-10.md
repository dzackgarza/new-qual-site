Solve the initial-value problems
\[
f''+f=3\sin(2t)+1,
\qquad
f(0)=3,\quad f'(0)=0,
\]
and
\[
f''-2f'+f=t^2,
\qquad
f(0)=2,\quad f'(0)=-4.
\]
