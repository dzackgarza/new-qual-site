::: {.problem}
Solve
\[
D^{30}f-f=\sin(2t).
\]
:::

::: {.solution}
For $k=0,\ldots,29$, set
\[
\zeta_k=e^{2\pi i k/30}.
\]

<1>1. The characteristic roots of the homogeneous equation
\[
D^{30}f-f=0
\]
are exactly $\zeta_0,\ldots,\zeta_{29}$, each with multiplicity one.

::: {.proof}
The characteristic polynomial is
\[
p(r)=r^{30}-1.
\]
Its roots are precisely the thirty distinct thirtieth roots of unity
$\zeta_k=e^{2\pi i k/30}$. They are simple because
$p'(r)=30r^{29}$ does not vanish at any root of $p$.
:::

<1>2. The general complex-valued homogeneous solution is
\[
f_h(t)=\sum_{k=0}^{29}C_k e^{\zeta_k t},
\qquad C_k\in\mathbb C.
\]

<2>1. Each function $e^{\zeta_k t}$ solves the homogeneous equation.

::: {.proof}
Since $D^{30}e^{\zeta_k t}=\zeta_k^{30}e^{\zeta_k t}$ and
$\zeta_k^{30}=1$, one has
\[
(D^{30}-1)e^{\zeta_k t}=0.
\]
:::

<2>2. The thirty functions $e^{\zeta_k t}$ are linearly independent.

::: {.proof}
Their Wronskian at $t=0$ is the Vandermonde determinant
\[
\det\!\bigl(\zeta_k^j\bigr)_{0\le j,k\le29}
=\prod_{0\le k<\ell\le29}(\zeta_\ell-\zeta_k),
\]
which is nonzero because the roots in step <1>1 are distinct. Hence these
thirty solutions are linearly independent. The solution space of a homogeneous
linear differential equation of order $30$ has dimension $30$, so they form a
basis.
:::

<2>3. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Steps <2>1 and <2>2 show that the displayed linear combination is exactly the
general homogeneous solution.
:::

<1>3. A particular solution is
\[
f_p(t)=-\frac{\sin(2t)}{2^{30}+1}.
\]

::: {.proof}
For every $n\ge0$,
\[
D^n\sin(2t)=2^n\sin\!\left(2t+\frac{n\pi}{2}\right).
\]
With $n=30$ this gives
\[
D^{30}\sin(2t)
=2^{30}\sin(2t+15\pi)
=-2^{30}\sin(2t).
\]
Therefore
\[
(D^{30}-1)\left(-\frac{\sin(2t)}{2^{30}+1}\right)
=\sin(2t).
\]
:::

<1>4. The general complex-valued solution is
\[
\boxed{
f(t)=\sum_{k=0}^{29}C_k e^{\zeta_k t}
-\frac{\sin(2t)}{2^{30}+1}
}.
\]
For a real-valued solution, take $C_0,C_{15}\in\mathbb R$ and impose
$C_{30-k}=\overline{C_k}$ for $1\le k\le14$.

::: {.proof}
By step <1>2 every homogeneous solution is $f_h$, and by step <1>3 the displayed
$f_p$ is one particular solution. Linearity gives every solution as
$f_h+f_p$. The stated conjugacy conditions pair the roots
$\zeta_{30-k}=\overline{\zeta_k}$ and are exactly the conditions making the
homogeneous sum real-valued.
:::

<1>5. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>4 gives the complete solution of the differential equation.
:::
:::
