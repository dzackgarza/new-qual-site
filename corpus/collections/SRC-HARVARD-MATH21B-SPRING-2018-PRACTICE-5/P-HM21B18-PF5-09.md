::: {.problem}

(a) Find
$$
\det\begin{pmatrix}1&1&1\\1&5&0\\1&0&5\end{pmatrix}.
$$

(b) Find the determinant of
$$
\begin{pmatrix}
1&1&1&1&1&1\\
1&5&0&0&0&0\\
1&0&5&0&0&0\\
1&0&0&5&0&0\\
1&0&0&0&5&0\\
1&0&0&0&0&5
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

(c) Let $A_n$ be the symmetric tridiagonal matrix with zero diagonal and off-diagonal entries $\sqrt1,\sqrt2,\ldots,\sqrt{n-1}$. Compute $\det A_2$, prove the recurrences
$$
\det A_4=(-3)\det A_2,
\quad
\det A_6=(-5)\det A_4,
\quad
\det A_8=(-7)\det A_6,
$$
and compute $\det A_8$.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

The determinant in part (a) is
$$
\boxed{15}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Expanding along the first row gives
$$
\det\begin{pmatrix}
1&1&1\\
1&5&0\\
1&0&5
\end{pmatrix}
=
1\begin{vmatrix}5&0\\0&5\end{vmatrix}
-1\begin{vmatrix}1&0\\1&5\end{vmatrix}
+1\begin{vmatrix}1&5\\1&0\end{vmatrix}.
$$
Thus
$$
\det A=25-5-5=15.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

The determinant in part (b) is
$$
\boxed{0}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Let $v_1,\ldots,v_6$ be the columns of the displayed matrix. Directly,
$$
5v_1-v_2-v_3-v_4-v_5-v_6=0.
$$
This is a nontrivial linear dependence among the columns, so the matrix is
singular and its determinant is zero.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

If $D_n=\det A_n$, then for every $n\ge2$,
$$
D_n=-(n-1)D_{n-2},
$$
where $D_0=1$.


::: pf-proof

The last row of $A_n$ has exactly one nonzero entry, namely
$\sqrt{n-1}$ in column $n-1$. Expanding along that row gives a factor
$-\sqrt{n-1}$. In the resulting $(n-1)\times(n-1)$ minor, the last column has
exactly one nonzero entry, again $\sqrt{n-1}$, now in its last row. Expanding
along that column contributes no additional sign and leaves the leading
$(n-2)\times(n-2)$ principal block, which is $A_{n-2}$. Therefore
$$
D_n=-\sqrt{n-1}\sqrt{n-1}\,D_{n-2}
=-(n-1)D_{n-2}.
$$
The convention $D_0=1$ is the determinant of the empty matrix and makes the
recurrence valid at $n=2$ as well.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

One has
$$
\boxed{\det A_2=-1}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Explicitly,
$$
A_2=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},
$$
so $\det A_2=-1$. Equivalently, step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} gives
$D_2=-D_0=-1$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-5}

The requested determinant recurrences are
$$
\boxed{
\det A_4=-3\det A_2,\qquad
\det A_6=-5\det A_4,\qquad
\det A_8=-7\det A_6}.
$$


::: pf-proof

These are step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} specialized to $n=4,6,8$, respectively.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-6}

The determinant of $A_8$ is
$$
\boxed{105}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Starting from step [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} and applying step [](#s1-5){.pf-ref},
$$
\det A_4=(-3)(-1)=3,
$$
$$
\det A_6=(-5)(3)=-15,
$$
and therefore
$$
\det A_8=(-7)(-15)=105.
$$

:::
