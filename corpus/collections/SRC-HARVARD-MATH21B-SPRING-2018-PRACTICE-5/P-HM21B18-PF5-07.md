::: {.problem}
Assume a $3\times3$ matrix $A$ has eigenbasis
\[
\left\{
\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\1\end{pmatrix},
\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\\0\end{pmatrix},
\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\\1\end{pmatrix}
\right\}
\]
with corresponding eigenvalues $1,2,3$. Find $A$.
:::

Let
\[
S=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&1&1\\
1&0&0\\
1&0&1
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
D=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&0&0\\
0&2&0\\
0&0&3
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

::: pf

::: pf-step

The matrices $A,S,D$ satisfy
\[
AS=SD.
\]


::: pf-proof

The columns of $S$ are exactly the three given eigenvectors. Multiplying $A$ by the first, second, and third columns therefore multiplies those columns by $1,2,3$, respectively. This columnwise statement is precisely $AS=SD$.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

The inverse of $S$ is
\[
S^{-1}=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&1&0\\
1&0&-1\\
0&-1&1
\end{pmatrix}.
\]


::: pf-proof

Direct multiplication gives
\[
\begin{pmatrix}
1&1&1\\
1&0&0\\
1&0&1
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}
0&1&0\\
1&0&-1\\
0&-1&1
\end{pmatrix}
=I_3.
\]

:::

:::

::: pf-step

The required matrix is
\[
\boxed{
A=SDS^{-1}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
2&-2&1\\
0&1&0\\
0&-2&3
\end{pmatrix}}.
\]


::: pf-proof

Since the three eigenvectors form an eigenbasis, $S$ is invertible. Step <1>1 therefore gives
\[
A=SDS^{-1}.
\]
Using step <1>2 and multiplying the three matrices gives the displayed result.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

The displayed matrix has the required eigenpairs.


::: pf-proof

Directly,
\[
A\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\1\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
A\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\\0\end{pmatrix}
=2\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\\0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
A\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\\1\end{pmatrix}
=3\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\\1\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Thus the reconstruction satisfies exactly the prescribed data.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step <1>3 gives the requested matrix, and step <1>4 verifies it.

:::

:::
