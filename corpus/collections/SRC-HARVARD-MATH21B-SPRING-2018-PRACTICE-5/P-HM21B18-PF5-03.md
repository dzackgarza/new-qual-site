::: {.problem}
(a) Suppose a $2\times2$ matrix $A$ has eigenvalues $2,3$ with eigenvectors
\[
v_1=(1,1)^T,
\qquad
v_2=(1,-1)^T.
\]
For each of $B=A^T$, $B=A^2-A$, and $B=A^{-1}$, decide whether $2,3$ are eigenvalues of $B$ and whether $v_1,v_2$ are both eigenvectors of $B$.

(b) For each of five source-displayed matrices, match the continuous system $\dot x=Ax$ to one of phase portraits A--F. The full matrix table and portraits are not recoverable in the retained extraction.
:::
