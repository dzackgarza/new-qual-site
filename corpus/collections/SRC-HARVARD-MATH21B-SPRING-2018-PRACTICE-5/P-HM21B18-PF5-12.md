::: {.problem}
(a) Find the Fourier series on $[-\pi,\pi]$ of
\[
f(x)=
\begin{cases}
\pi,&-1\le x\le1,\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\]

(b) Use Parseval's theorem to evaluate
\[
\sum_{n=1}^\infty\frac{\sin^2 n}{n^2}.
\]

(c) For $0\le a\le\pi$, repeat the computation with
\[
f_a(x)=
\begin{cases}
\pi,&-a\le x\le a,\\
0,&\text{otherwise},
\end{cases}
\]
and find
\[
g(a)=\sum_{n=1}^\infty\frac{\sin^2(na)}{n^2}.
\]
:::
