::: problem
Let $\{f_k(z)\}$, $k=1,2,3,\ldots$, be a sequence of analytic functions in a star-shaped domain $D\subset\mathbb{C}$.
Suppose that the sequence of partial sums
\[
F_n(z):=\sum_{k=1}^n f_k(z),\qquad n=1,2,3,\ldots
\]
is uniformly convergent in $D$ to the limit function $F(z)$.
Prove that $F(z)$ is analytic in $D$.
:::
