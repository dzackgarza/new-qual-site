::: {.problem}
Let $\mathbb{E}\subset\CC$ denote the open unit disk.
Let $f:\mathbb{E}\to\mathbb{E}$ be an analytic function with $f(0)=0$ which is not identically zero in some neighborhood of the origin.
Prove that:

(a) There exists a positive integer $m$ such that
\[
f(0)=f'(0)=\cdots=f^{(m-1)}(0)=0\qquad\text{and}\qquad f^{(m)}(0)\neq 0.
\]

(b) For this number $m$, the following hold for all $z\in\mathbb{E}$:
\[
|f(z)|\le|z|^m\qquad\text{and}\qquad |f^{(m)}(0)|\le m!.
\]
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

There is a positive integer $m$ with
\[
f(0)=f'(0)=\cdots=f^{(m-1)}(0)=0\qquad\text{and}\qquad f^{(m)}(0)\neq 0.
\]

::: pf-proof

If every derivative $f^{(k)}(0)$ vanished, the Taylor series of $f$ at $0$ would be zero, so $f$ would vanish on a disk about $0$, contrary to hypothesis. Let $m$ be the least $k$ with $f^{(k)}(0)\ne0$. Since $f(0)=0$, one has $m\ge1$. This proves part (a).

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

There is a holomorphic $g$ on $\mathbb E$ with
\[
f(z)=z^mg(z),
\qquad
g(0)=\frac{f^{(m)}(0)}{m!}.
\]

::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1){.pf-ref}, the power series of $f$ at $0$ begins with the term $\frac{f^{(m)}(0)}{m!}z^m$. Hence $f(z)/z^m$ has a removable singularity at $0$, and its extension $g$ is holomorphic on $\mathbb E$ with the stated value at $0$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

For every $z\in\mathbb E$,
\[
\abs{g(z)}\le1.
\]

::: pf-proof

Fix $z\in\mathbb E$ and let $\abs z<r<1$. On the circle $\abs\zeta=r$,
\[
\abs{g(\zeta)}=\frac{\abs{f(\zeta)}}{r^m}<\frac1{r^m},
\]
because $f$ maps into $\mathbb E$. The maximum modulus principle on the disk $\abs\zeta\le r$ gives $\abs{g(z)}\le r^{-m}$. Letting $r\uparrow1$ gives $\abs{g(z)}\le1$.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

By steps [](#s2){.pf-ref} and [](#s3){.pf-ref},
\[
\boxed{\abs{f(z)}=\abs z^m\abs{g(z)}\le\abs z^m}
\]
for all $z\in\mathbb E$, and
\[
\boxed{\abs{f^{(m)}(0)}=m!\,\abs{g(0)}\le m!}.
\]
This proves part (b).

:::
