::: problem
Evaluate
\[
\int_0^\infty\frac{\cos 5x}{x^2+1}\,dx.
\]
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. $\int_0^\infty \frac{\cos 5x}{x^2+1}\, dx = \frac{1}{2}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \frac{\cos 5x}{x^2+1}\, dx = \frac{1}{2}\operatorname{Re}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \frac{e^{5ix}}{x^2+1}\, dx$.
Proof: the integrand is even, and $\cos 5x = \operatorname{Re}(e^{5ix})$.

<1>2. $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \frac{e^{5ix}}{x^2+1}\, dx = 2\pi i \operatorname{Res}_{z=i}\left(\frac{e^{5iz}}{z^2+1}\right)$.
Proof: close the contour in the upper half-plane (where $e^{5iz}$ decays since $\Im z > 0$); the only pole in the upper half-plane is $z = i$.

<1>3. $\operatorname{Res}_{z=i}\frac{e^{5iz}}{z^2+1} = \frac{e^{5i\cdot i}}{2i} = \frac{e^{-5}}{2i}$.
Proof: $z^2 + 1 = (z-i)(z+i)$, so the residue at the simple pole $z = i$ is $\frac{e^{5iz}}{z+i}\big|_{z=i} = \frac{e^{-5}}{2i}$.

<1>4. Hence $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \frac{e^{5ix}}{x^2+1}\, dx = 2\pi i \cdot \frac{e^{-5}}{2i} = \pi e^{-5}$.
Proof: <1>2 and <1>3.

<1>5. Therefore $\int_0^\infty \frac{\cos 5x}{x^2+1}\, dx = \frac{1}{2}\operatorname{Re}(\pi e^{-5}) = \frac{\pi}{2} e^{-5}$.
Proof: <1>1 and <1>4.

<1>6. Q.E.D.
Proof: <1>5.
:::
