::: problem
(a) Let $f(z)$ be analytic inside and on a circle $C$ of radius $r$ centered at $a$.
Prove that
\[
f(a)=\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}f\bigl(a+re^{i\theta}\bigr)\,d\theta.
\]

(b) Let $L$ be a simple closed curve.
Suppose that $f(z)$ is analytic inside and on $L$.
Prove that the maximum value of $|f(z)|$ occurs on $L$, unless $f(z)$ is a constant.
:::
