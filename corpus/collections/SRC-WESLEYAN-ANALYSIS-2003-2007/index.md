::: {.remark}
The real-analysis and complex-analysis papers of the Wesleyan University analysis preliminary examination from 2003 to 2007. The packet prints the 2006 real-analysis paper twice.
The 2003 paper has ten short-answer questions and five Part II problems; Part II Problem 3 is the same problem as [[P-WESRA08-II4]].
The Casorati--Weierstrass and open mapping problems of the August 9, 2006 complex-analysis paper repeat problems of the August 2, 2004 paper.
Four Section I problems of the Summer 2007 complex-analysis paper repeat problems of earlier papers.
:::
