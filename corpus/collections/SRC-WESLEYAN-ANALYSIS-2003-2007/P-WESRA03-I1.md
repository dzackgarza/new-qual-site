::: {.problem}
Define the Borel sigma-algebra on $\RR$.
:::

::: {.solution}
The Borel sigma-algebra on $\RR$ is the smallest sigma-algebra containing every open subset of $\RR$:
$$
\boxed{\mathcal B(\RR)=\sigma(\{U\subset\RR:U\text{ open}\}).}
$$
Equivalently, it is the sigma-algebra generated by the open intervals.
:::
