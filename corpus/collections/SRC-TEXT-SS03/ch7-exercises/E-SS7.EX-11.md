::: {.exercise}
Let $\varphi(x)=\sum_{p\leq x}\log p$, where the sum runs over primes. Prove that the following assertions are equivalent as $x\to\infty$:

(i) $\varphi(x)\sim x$;

(ii) $\pi(x)\sim x/\log x$;

(iii) $\psi(x)\sim x$;

(iv) $\psi_1(x)\sim x^2/2$.
:::
