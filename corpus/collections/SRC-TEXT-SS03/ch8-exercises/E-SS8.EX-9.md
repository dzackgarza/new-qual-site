::: exercise
9. Prove that the function u defined by

$$
u (x, y) = \operatorname{Re} \left(\frac {i + z}{i - z}\right) \quad \text { and } \quad u (0, 1) = 0
$$

is harmonic in the unit disc and vanishes on its boundary. Note that u is not bounded in D.
:::
