::: {.exercise}
Show that
$$
\wp(z)=c+\pi^2\sum_{m=-\infty}^{\infty}
\frac{1}{\sin^2(\pi(z+m\tau))}
$$
for an appropriate constant $c$. In fact, by part (b) of the previous problem, $c=-F(\tau)$, where $F$ is the forbidden Eisenstein series.
:::
