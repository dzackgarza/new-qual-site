::: exercise
12. Consider the function defined by

$$
f (x + i y) = \sqrt {| x | | y |}, \quad \text { whenever } x, y \in \mathbb {R}.
$$

Show that f satisfies the Cauchy-Riemann equations at the origin, yet f is not holomorphic at 0.
:::
