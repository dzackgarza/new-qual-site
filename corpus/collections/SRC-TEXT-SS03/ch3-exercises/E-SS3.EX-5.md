::: exercise
5. Use contour integration to show that

$$
\int_ {- \infty} ^ {\infty} \frac {e ^ {- 2 \pi i x \xi}}{(1 + x ^ {2}) ^ {2}} d x = \frac {\pi}{2} (1 + 2 \pi | \xi |) e ^ {- 2 \pi | \xi |}
$$

for all ξ real.
:::
