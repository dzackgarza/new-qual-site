::: exercise
3. Show that

$$
\int_ {- \infty} ^ {\infty} \frac {\cos x}{x ^ {2} + a ^ {2}} d x = \pi \frac {e ^ {- a}}{a}, \quad \mathrm{for} a > 0.
$$
:::
