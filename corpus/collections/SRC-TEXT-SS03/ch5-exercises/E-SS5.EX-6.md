::: exercise
6. Prove Wallis’s product formula

$$
{\frac {\pi}{2}} = {\frac {2 \cdot 2}{1 \cdot 3}} \cdot {\frac {4 \cdot 4}{3 \cdot 5}} \dots {\frac {2 m \cdot 2 m}{(2 m - 1) \cdot (2 m + 1)}} \dots .
$$

[Hint: Use the product formula for sin z at $z = \pi / 2 . ]$
:::
