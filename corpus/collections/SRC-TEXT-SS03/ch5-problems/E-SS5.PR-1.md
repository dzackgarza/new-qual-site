::: exercise
Prove that if $f$ is holomorphic in the unit disc, bounded, and not identically zero, and $z_1,z_2,\ldots$ are its zeros, with $|z_k|<1$, then

$$
\sum_n(1-|z_n|)<\infty.
$$

*Hint.* Use Jensen's formula.
:::
