::: exercise
11. Let $f ( z ) = e ^ { a z } e ^ { - e ^ { z } }$ where $a > 0$ . Observe that in the strip $\{ x + i y : ~ | y | < \pi \}$ the function $f ( x + i y )$ is exponentially decreasing as $| x |$ tends to infinity.
    Prove that

$$
\hat {f} (\xi) = \Gamma (a + i \xi), \quad \text {   for   all   } \xi \in \mathbb {R}.
$$
:::
