::: exercise
6. Show that

$$
1 + \frac {1}{3} + \frac {1}{5} + \dots + \frac {1}{2 n - 1} - \frac {1}{2} \log n \rightarrow \frac {\gamma}{2} + \log 2,
$$

where $\gamma$ is Euler’s constant.
:::
