::: {.problem}
<1>1. Let $K,F\subset\mathbb R^2$, with $K$ closed and bounded and $F$ closed. Prove that if $K\cap F=\varnothing$, then
\[
d(K,F):=\inf\{d(x,y):x\in K,\ y\in F\}>0.
\]

<1>2. Is <1>1 still true if $K$ is only assumed closed? Prove your assertion.
:::
