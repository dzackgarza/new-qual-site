::: {.problem}
Let $T$ be a complex $n\times n$ matrix.
Suppose $\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_r$ are distinct eigenvalues with corresponding nonzero eigenvectors $v_1,\ldots,v_r$.
Prove that $v_1,\ldots,v_r$ are linearly independent.
:::
