::: {.problem}
Give an example of continuous functions $f_n\colon[0,1]\to\mathbb R$ such that
\[
f(t)=\lim_{n\to\infty}f_n(t)
\]
is continuous, but
\[
\int_0^1 f_n(t)\,dt
\]
does not converge to $\int_0^1 f(t)\,dt$.
:::
