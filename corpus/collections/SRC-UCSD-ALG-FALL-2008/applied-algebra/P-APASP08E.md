::: problem
Let $P(\mathcal{T})$ be the Young idempotent corresponding to the row group of the tableau $$\mathcal{T} = \begin{array}{cccccc} 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 & & \\ 5 & 6 & & & & \\ 7 & & & & & \end{array}$$ Let $\chi$ be the character of the representation resulting from the action of $S_7$ on the left ideal $\mathbb{C}[S_7] P(\mathcal{T})$.
Give the expansion of $\chi$ in terms of the character basis $\{\chi^\lambda\}_{\lambda \vdash 7}$.

Hint: Use the Frobenius map.
:::

::: remark
Both available scans of the paper garble the printed tableau; the reading above is best-effort.
The scans do show an entry in the third row, and since the tabloid's entries are a permutation of $1,\ldots,7$ with $1$--$6$ placed in the first two rows, the third row holds $7$.
The row group, and hence $P(\mathcal{T})$ and $\chi$, depends only on the row membership, so this placement is forced rather than guessed.
:::
