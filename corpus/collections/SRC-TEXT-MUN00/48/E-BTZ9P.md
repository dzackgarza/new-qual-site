::: {.exercise}

If $f_n$ is a sequence of continuous functions $f_n: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ such that $f_n(x) \to f(x)$ for each $x \in \mathbb{R}$, show that $f$ is continuous at uncountably many points of $\mathbb{R}$.
:::
