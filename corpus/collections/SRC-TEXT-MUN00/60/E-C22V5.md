::: {.exercise}

Compute the fundamental groups of the "solid torus" $S^1 \times B^2$ and the product space $S^1 \times S^2$.
:::

::: {.solution}
The ball $B^2$ is contractible, so
$$
S^1\times B^2\simeq S^1\times\{0\}\cong S^1.
$$
Hence
$$
\pi_1(S^1\times B^2)\cong\pi_1(S^1)\cong\ZZ.
$$

For the second product, the product formula for fundamental groups gives
$$
\pi_1(S^1\times S^2)\cong\pi_1(S^1)\times\pi_1(S^2).
$$
Since $S^2$ is simply connected,
$$
\pi_1(S^1\times S^2)\cong\ZZ\times0\cong\ZZ.
$$
Thus both spaces have infinite cyclic fundamental group.
:::
