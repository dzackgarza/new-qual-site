::: {.exercise}

Let $p: E \to X$ be the map constructed in the proof of Lemma 60.5. Let $E'$ be the subspace of $E$ that is the union of the $x$-axis and the $y$-axis.
Show that $p \mid E'$ is not a covering map.
:::
