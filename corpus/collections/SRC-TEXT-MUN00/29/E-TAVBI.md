::: {.exercise}

Theorem.
Let $f: X \to Y$.
Then $f$ is continuous if and only if for every convergent net $(x_\alpha)$ in $X$, converging to $x$, say, the net $(f(x_\alpha))$ converges to $f(x)$.
:::
