::: {.exercise}

A subset $K$ of $J$ is said to be cofinal in $J$ if for each $\alpha \in J$, there exists $\beta \in K$ such that $\alpha \preceq \beta$.
Show that if $J$ is a directed set and $K$ is cofinal in $J$, then $K$ is a directed set.
:::
