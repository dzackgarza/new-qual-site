::: {.exercise}

Corollary.
Let $G$ be a topological group; let $A$ and $B$ be subsets of $G$.
If $A$ is closed in $G$ and $B$ is compact, then $A \cdot B$ is closed in $G$.
[Hint: First give a proof using sequences, assuming that $G$ is metrizable.]
