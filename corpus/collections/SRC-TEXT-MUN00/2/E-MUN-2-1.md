::: {.exercise}

Let $f: A \to B$ . Let $A_0 \subset A$ and $B_0 \subset B$ .

(a) Show that $A_0 \subset f^{-1}(f(A_0))$ and that equality holds if $f$ is injective.

(b) Show that $f(f^{-1}(B_0)) \subset B_0$ and that equality holds if $f$ is surjective.
:::
