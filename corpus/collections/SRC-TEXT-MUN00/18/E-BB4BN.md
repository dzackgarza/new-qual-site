::: {.exercise title="Munkres §18.5"}

Show that the subspace $(a, b)$ of $\mathbb{R}$ is homeomorphic with $(0, 1)$ and the subspace $[a, b]$ of $\mathbb{R}$ is homeomorphic with $[0, 1]$.
:::
