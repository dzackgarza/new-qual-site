::: {.exercise}

Let $p: X \to B$ be a covering map (not necessarily regular); let $G$ be its group of covering transformations.

(a) Show that the action of $G$ on $X$ is properly discontinuous.

(b) Let $\pi: X \to X/G$ be the projection map.
Show there is a covering map $k: X/G \to B$ such that $k \circ \pi = p$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Assume $X$ is connected, as in the classification of covering spaces, and let $G$ be the group of covering transformations of $p:X\to B$.

(a) Fix $x\in X$, and choose an evenly covered neighborhood $U$ of $p(x)$. Let $V$ be the sheet over $U$ containing $x$. If $g\in G$ and
$$
g(V)\cap V\ne\varnothing,
$$
then $g(V)$ and $V$ are sheets over the same $U$, hence they are equal. Since $p\restrict_V$ is injective and $pg=p$, this forces $g\restrict_V=1$. A covering transformation that agrees with the identity at one point (indeed on an open set) is the identity on connected $X$. Thus
$$
g(V)\cap V=\varnothing\qquad(g\ne1).
$$
So the action is properly discontinuous.

(b) Define
$$
k:X/G\to B,\qquad k([x])=p(x).
$$
This is well defined since every $g\in G$ satisfies $pg=p$. If $\pi:X\to X/G$ is the orbit projection, then
$$
k\pi=p.
$$
Because $\pi$ is a quotient map and $p$ is continuous, $k$ is continuous.

Let $U\subset B$ be evenly covered by $p$, say
$$
p^{-1}(U)=\coprod_{i\in I}V_i.
$$
The group $G$ permutes the sheets $V_i$. Choose one representative $V_j$ from each $G$-orbit of sheets. By part (a), no nonidentity element stabilizes a sheet. Therefore $\pi\restrict_{V_j}$ is injective, and
$$
k:\pi(V_j)\longrightarrow U
$$
is a homeomorphism with inverse $\pi\circ(p\restrict_{V_j})^{-1}$.

Moreover,
$$
k^{-1}(U)=\coprod_{[j]}\pi(V_j),
$$
where $[j]$ ranges over the $G$-orbits of sheets. These sets are open and pairwise disjoint: their inverse images under the quotient map are unions of sheets. Thus $U$ is evenly covered by $k$. Hence
$$
\boxed{k:X/G\to B\text{ is a covering map and }k\pi=p.}
$$
:::
