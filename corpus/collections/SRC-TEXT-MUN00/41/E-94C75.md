::: {.exercise}

(a) Show that the product of a paracompact space and a compact space is paracompact.
[Hint: Use the tube lemma.]

(b) Conclude that $S_\Omega$ is not paracompact.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: pf-step
Part (a): Product of paracompact $X$ and compact $Y$ is paracompact.

::: pf-proof

::: pf-step
Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an arbitrary open covering of $X \times Y$.
:::

::: pf-step
For each $x \in X$, the slice $\{x\} \times Y$ is compact, so there exists a finite subcollection $\mathcal{A}_x \subset \mathcal{A}$ covering $\{x\} \times Y$.
:::

::: pf-step
Let $U_x = \bigcup_{A \in \mathcal{A}_x} A$, which is an open neighborhood of $\{x\} \times Y$ in $X \times Y$.
:::

::: pf-step
By the Tube Lemma, there exists an open neighborhood $W_x$ of $x$ in $X$ such that $W_x \times Y \subseteq U_x$.
:::

::: pf-step
The family $\{W_x\}_{x \in X}$ forms an open covering of $X$.
:::

::: pf-step
Since $X$ is paracompact, choose a locally finite open refinement $\mathcal{V} = \{V_\beta\}_{\beta \in B}$ of $\{W_x\}_{x \in X}$.
:::

::: pf-step
For each $\beta \in B$, choose $x(\beta) \in X$ such that $V_\beta \subseteq W_{x(\beta)}$.
:::

::: pf-step
Define the collection of open sets in $X \times Y$:
$$\mathcal{C} = \{ (V_\beta \times Y) \cap A \mid \beta \in B, \, A \in \mathcal{A}_{x(\beta)} \}.$$
:::

::: pf-step
Every $(V_\beta \times Y) \cap A$ is open and contained in $A \in \mathcal{A}$. For any $(x, y) \in X \times Y$, there is $\beta$ with $x \in V_\beta \subseteq W_{x(\beta)}$, and since $\mathcal{A}_{x(\beta)}$ covers $W_{x(\beta)} \times Y$, $(x, y) \in A$ for some $A \in \mathcal{A}_{x(\beta)}$, so $(x, y) \in (V_\beta \times Y) \cap A$.
:::

::: pf-step
For any $(x, y) \in X \times Y$, local finiteness of $\mathcal{V}$ yields a neighborhood $N_x$ of $x$ in $X$ intersecting only finitely many $V_{\beta_1}, \dots, V_{\beta_k}$. Then $N_x \times Y$ is an open neighborhood of $(x, y)$ in $X \times Y$ that intersects only elements of $\mathcal{C}$ associated with $\beta_1, \dots, \beta_k$. Since each $\mathcal{A}_{x(\beta_i)}$ is finite, $N_x \times Y$ intersects only finitely many members of $\mathcal{C}$.
:::

::: pf-step
The open cover $\mathcal A$ therefore has a locally finite open refinement. Hence $X\times Y$ is paracompact.
:::

:::

:::

::: pf-step
Part (b): $S_\Omega = [0, \omega_1)$ is not paracompact.

::: pf-proof

::: pf-step
Let $\overline{S}_\Omega = [0, \omega_1]$ be the compact ordinal space.
:::

::: pf-step
If $S_\Omega$ were paracompact, then by Part (a), the product space $S_\Omega \times \overline{S}_\Omega$ would be paracompact.
:::

::: pf-step
Every paracompact Hausdorff space is normal.
:::

::: pf-step
$S_\Omega \times \overline{S}_\Omega$ is not normal: the diagonal $\Delta = \{(x, x) \mid x \in S_\Omega\}$ and the top edge $S_\Omega \times \{\Omega\}$ are disjoint closed sets that cannot be separated by disjoint open sets.
:::

::: pf-step
Therefore, $S_\Omega$ cannot be paracompact. Q.E.D.
:::
