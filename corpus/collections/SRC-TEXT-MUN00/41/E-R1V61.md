::: {.exercise}

Is every locally compact Hausdorff space paracompact?
:::

::: {.solution}
No. A counterexample is
$$
S_\Omega=[0,\Omega),
$$
the space of countable ordinals with the order topology. It is Hausdorff and locally compact: for every $\alpha<\Omega$, a sufficiently small closed order interval about $\alpha$ is a compact ordinal interval. But $S_\Omega$ is not paracompact by [[E-94C75]](b). Hence local compactness plus Hausdorffness does not imply paracompactness.
:::
