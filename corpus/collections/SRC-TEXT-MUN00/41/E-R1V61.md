::: {.exercise}

Is every locally compact Hausdorff space paracompact?
:::

::: {.solution}
No. A counterexample is
$$
S_\Omega=[0,\Omega),
$$
the space of countable ordinals with the order topology. It is Hausdorff, and it is locally compact: for $\alpha<\Omega$ the closed interval $[0,\alpha+1]$ is compact, being a closed interval in the well-ordered set $\overline S_\Omega$, which has the least upper bound property (Theorem 27.1), and it is a compact neighbourhood of $\alpha$ (§29, the definition of local compactness). But $S_\Omega$ is not paracompact by [[E-94C75]](b). Hence local compactness plus Hausdorffness does not imply paracompactness.
:::
