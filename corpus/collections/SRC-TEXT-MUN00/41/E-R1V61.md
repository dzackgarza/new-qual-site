::: {.exercise}

Is every locally compact Hausdorff space paracompact?
:::

::: {.solution}
No. The standard counterexample is
\[
S_\Omega=[0,\Omega),
\]
the space of countable ordinals with the order topology. It is Hausdorff and locally compact: for every \(\alpha<\Omega\), a sufficiently small closed order interval about \(\alpha\) is a compact ordinal interval. But §41, Exercise 3(b), shows that \(S_\Omega\) is not paracompact. Hence local compactness plus Hausdorffness does not imply paracompactness.
:::
