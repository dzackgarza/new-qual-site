::: {.exercise}

(a) If $X$ is normal and $y$ is a point of $\beta(X) - X$, show that $y$ is not the limit of a sequence of points of $X$.

(b) Show that if $X$ is completely regular and noncompact, then $\beta(X)$ is not metrizable.
:::
