::: {.exercise}

Show that if $U$ is an open connected subspace of $\mathbb{R}^2$, then $U$ is path connected.
[Hint: Show that given $x_0 \in U$, the set of points that can be joined to $x_0$ by a path in $U$ is both open and closed in $U$.]
:::
