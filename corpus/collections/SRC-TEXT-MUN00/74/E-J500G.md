::: {.exercise}

Consider the space $X$ obtained from a seven-sided polygonal region by means of the labelling scheme $abaaab^{-1}a^{-1}$.
Show that the fundamental group of $X$ is the free product of two cyclic groups.
[Hint: See Theorem 68.7.]
:::

::: {.solution}
By the polygon presentation theorem, Theorem 74.2, provided all seven vertices of the region are identified: writing $v_0,\dots,v_6$ for the successive vertices, the edges labelled $a$ are the arrows $v_0\to v_1$, $v_2\to v_3$, $v_3\to v_4$, $v_4\to v_5$ and $v_0\to v_6$, so $a$ puts $v_0,v_2,v_3,v_4$ in one class and $v_1,v_3,v_4,v_5,v_6$ in another; these meet in $v_3$, so all seven vertices are one class, as the theorem requires. Hence
$$
\pi_1(X)\cong
\langle a,b\mid ab a^3 b^{-1}a^{-1}=1\rangle.
$$
The relator is equivalent to $a^3=1$: indeed
$$
ab a^3b^{-1}a^{-1}=1
\iff
ba^3b^{-1}=1
\iff
a^3=1.
$$
Thus
$$
\pi_1(X)\cong\langle a,b\mid a^3=1\rangle.
$$
There is no relation involving $b$. Therefore, by the normal-form theorem for free products,
$$
\boxed{\pi_1(X)\cong C_3*\ZZ,}
$$
the free product of the cyclic group of order $3$ and an infinite cyclic group.
:::
