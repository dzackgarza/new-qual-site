::: {.exercise}

Consider the space $X$ obtained from a seven-sided polygonal region by means of the labelling scheme $abaaab^{-1}a^{-1}$.
Show that the fundamental group of $X$ is the free product of two cyclic groups.
[Hint: See Theorem 68.7.]
:::

::: {.solution}

The relator computation below is that of Math StackExchange answer 1104612 (https://math.stackexchange.com/a/1104612) by WWK, posted 2015-01-14 to question 1103462, "Practice Problem Fundamental Group of 7-figured polygon" (https://math.stackexchange.com/questions/1103462), asked by ProbsNot on the same day. WWK begins at the normal subgroup the scheme generates and never checks the hypothesis of Theorem 74.2, that the pasting map identifies all seven vertices of the region, which the book leaves to the reader (printed p. 451); that check is the first paragraph, and it is this card's own.

Write $v_0,\dots,v_6$ for the successive vertices of the region. The edges labelled $a$ are the arrows $v_0\to v_1$, $v_2\to v_3$, $v_3\to v_4$, $v_4\to v_5$ and $v_0\to v_6$, so $a$ puts $v_0,v_2,v_3,v_4$ in one class and $v_1,v_3,v_4,v_5,v_6$ in another; these meet in $v_3$, so all seven vertices are one class, as the theorem requires. Hence Theorem 74.2 gives
$$
\pi_1(X)\cong
\langle a,b\mid ab a^3 b^{-1}a^{-1}=1\rangle .
$$
The relator is a conjugate of $a^3$: since $(ab)^{-1}=b^{-1}a^{-1}$,
$$
(ab)\,a^{3}\,(ab)^{-1}=aba\,a\,a\,b^{-1}a^{-1}=abaaab^{-1}a^{-1},
$$
which is the relator read off the scheme. A normal subgroup contains an element exactly when it contains each of its conjugates, so the least normal subgroup $N$ of $F(a,b)$ containing the relator contains $a^3$ and is contained in the least normal subgroup containing $a^3$; the two coincide.

Thus $\pi_1(X)\cong\langle a,b\mid a^3=1\rangle$, and this is the quotient of $\ZZ*\ZZ$ by the least normal subgroup generated by the element $a^3$ of the first factor. In the notation of Theorem 68.7 (the theorem the printed hint names) take $G_1=\langle a\rangle$, $G_2=\langle b\rangle$, $N_1=3\ZZ$, $N_2=0$; the least normal subgroup of $G_1*G_2$ containing $N_1$ and $N_2$ is the one generated by $a^3$, so
$$
\pi_1(X)\cong \ZZ/3\ZZ * \ZZ,
$$
the free product of the cyclic group of order $3$ and an infinite cyclic group.
