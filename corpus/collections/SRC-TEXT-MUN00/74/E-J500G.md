::: {.exercise}

Consider the space $X$ obtained from a seven-sided polygonal region by means of the labelling scheme $abaaab^{-1}a^{-1}$.
Show that the fundamental group of $X$ is the free product of two cyclic groups.
[Hint: See Theorem 68.7.]
:::

::: {.solution}
By the polygon presentation theorem, all vertices are identified and
\[
\pi_1(X)\cong
\langle a,b\mid ab a^3 b^{-1}a^{-1}=1\rangle.
\]
The relator is equivalent to \(a^3=1\): indeed
\[
ab a^3b^{-1}a^{-1}=1
\iff
ba^3b^{-1}=1
\iff
a^3=1.
\]
Thus
\[
\pi_1(X)\cong\langle a,b\mid a^3=1\rangle.
\]
There is no relation involving \(b\). Therefore, by the normal-form theorem for free products,
\[
\boxed{\pi_1(X)\cong C_3*\mathbb Z,}
\]
the free product of the cyclic group of order \(3\) and an infinite cyclic group.
:::
