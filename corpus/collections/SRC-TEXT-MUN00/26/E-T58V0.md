::: {.exercise}

Let $p: X \to Y$ be a closed continuous surjective map such that $p^{-1}(\theset{y})$ is compact, for each $y \in Y$.
(Such a map is called a perfect map.)
Show that if $Y$ is compact, then $X$ is compact.
[Hint: If $U$ is an open set containing $p^{-1}(\theset{y})$, there is a neighborhood $W$ of $y$ such that $p^{-1}(W)$ is contained in $U$.]
:::

::: {.solution}
The published solution is **"positron0802", *Solutions to Topology, Chapter 3* (1 January 2021), Exercise 26.12** (`tmp/sources/munkres-solutions-positron-ch3.pdf`); the argument follows the book's hint as they use it.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #tube}
If $U$ is an open subset of $X$ containing a fibre $p^{-1}(\{y\})$, then there is a neighbourhood $W$ of $y$ with $p^{-1}(W)\subset U$.

::: pf-proof
$X-U$ is closed in $X$, so its image $p(X-U)$ is closed in $Y$ because $p$ is a closed map, and it does not contain $y$: a point $x$ with $p(x)=y$ lies in $p^{-1}(\{y\})\subset U$. Put $W=Y-p(X-U)$, an open set containing $y$. Since $X-U\subset p^{-1}\big(p(X-U)\big)$, taking complements gives $p^{-1}(W)=X-p^{-1}\big(p(X-U)\big)\subset X-(X-U)=U$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #fibre-covered}
For each $y\in Y$ there is a neighbourhood $W_{y}$ of $y$ whose preimage is covered by finitely many members of a given open cover $\mathcal{A}$ of $X$.

::: pf-proof
Fix $y$. The fibre $p^{-1}(\{y\})$ is compact by hypothesis, and the members of $\mathcal{A}$ are open sets of $X$ covering it, so finitely many of them, say $A_{1},\dots,A_{m}$, cover $p^{-1}(\{y\})$. Their union $U$ is open and contains the fibre, so step [](#tube){.pf-ref} gives a neighbourhood $W_{y}$ of $y$ with $p^{-1}(W_{y})\subset U=A_{1}\cup\dots\cup A_{m}$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #finite-subcover}
Every open cover $\mathcal{A}$ of $X$ has a finite subcollection covering $X$.

::: pf-proof
The sets $W_{y}$ for $y\in Y$ form an open cover of $Y$, which is compact, so $W_{y_{1}},\dots,W_{y_{k}}$ cover $Y$ for finitely many points $y_{1},\dots,y_{k}$. Since $p$ is surjective,
$$
X=p^{-1}(Y)=\bigcup_{j=1}^{k}p^{-1}(W_{y_{j}}),
$$
and each $p^{-1}(W_{y_{j}})$ is covered by the finitely many members of $\mathcal{A}$ produced for $y_{j}$ in step [](#fibre-covered){.pf-ref}. The union of those finitely many finite collections is a finite subcollection of $\mathcal{A}$ covering $X$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step}
$X$ is compact.

::: pf-proof
Step [](#finite-subcover){.pf-ref} shows that every open cover of $X$ has a finite subcover.
:::
