::: {.exercise}

Let $T = S^1 \times S^1$, the torus.
There is an isomorphism of $\pi_1(T, b_0 \times b_0)$ with $\ZZ \times \ZZ$ induced by projections of $T$ onto its two factors.

(a) Find a covering space of $T$ corresponding to the subgroup of $\ZZ \times \ZZ$ generated by the element $m \times 0$, where $m$ is a positive integer.

(b) Find a covering space of $T$ corresponding to the trivial subgroup of $\ZZ \times \ZZ$.

(c) Find a covering space of $T$ corresponding to the subgroup of $\ZZ \times \ZZ$ generated by $m \times 0$ and $0 \times n$, where $m$ and $n$ are positive integers.
:::

::: {.solution}
Write
$$
q:\RR\to S^1,\qquad q(t)=e^{2\pi i t}.
$$
Under the standard identification $\pi_1(T)\cong\ZZ\oplus\ZZ$:

(a) Define
$$
p_m:S^1\times\RR\to S^1\times S^1,\qquad
p_m(z,t)=(z^m,q(t)).
$$
This is a covering map. Since $\pi_1(S^1\times\RR)\cong\ZZ$, the generator represented by the first circle maps to $(m,0)$. Thus the corresponding subgroup is
$$
\langle(m,0)\rangle.
$$

(b) The universal covering
$$
p:\RR^2\to T,\qquad p(s,t)=(q(s),q(t))
$$
has simply connected total space, so its associated subgroup is trivial.

(c) Define
$$
p_{m,n}:T\to T,\qquad p_{m,n}(z,w)=(z^m,w^n).
$$
Then
$$
(p_{m,n})_*:\ZZ^2\to\ZZ^2,\qquad (r,s)\mapsto(mr,ns),
$$
so its image is precisely
$$
\langle(m,0),(0,n)\rangle.
$$
Hence these three maps realize the required covering spaces.

The three answers are stated, for the subgroups $2\ZZ\oplus3\ZZ$ and the trivial group, by Paul Frost, answer 4590100 (https://math.stackexchange.com/a/4590100), posted 2022-12-02 to Math Stack Exchange question 4590058, "About covering spaces of the torus" (https://math.stackexchange.com/questions/4590058, asked by Lam18373 on the same day): the maps $e_n:S^1\to S^1$, $e_n(z)=z^n$, correspond to the subgroups $n\ZZ$, the map $e_\infty:\RR\to S^1$ corresponds to the trivial subgroup, and therefore $e_2\times e_3$ corresponds to $2\ZZ\oplus3\ZZ$ and $e_\infty\times e_\infty$ to the trivial subgroup. Each of those correspondences is asserted there, not computed, and part (a)'s covering $S^1\times\RR$ is not given in the thread; the computations of the three images in $\pi_1(T)$, and the covering of part (a), are the card's.
:::
