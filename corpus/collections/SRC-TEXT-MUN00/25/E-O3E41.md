::: {.exercise}

A space $X$ is said to be weakly locally connected at $x$ if for every neighborhood $U$ of $x$, there is a connected subspace of $X$ contained in $U$ that contains a neighborhood of $x$.
Show that if $X$ is weakly locally connected at each of its points, then $X$ is locally connected.
[Hint: Show that components of open sets are open.]
:::

::: {.solution}
The retained published solution is **positron0802, *Solutions to Topology, Chapter 3* (1 January 2021), Exercise 25.6, PDF p. 9** (`tmp/sources/munkres-solutions-positron-ch3.pdf`); the argument follows the book's hint as that set uses it. One slip in that set's printed conclusion is corrected: the last sentence says that $X$ is locally connected, which is the exercise.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #connected-inside}
Let $U$ be open in $X$ and $C$ a component of $U$. For $x\in C$ there is a connected subspace $C_x\subseteq C$ containing a neighbourhood of $x$ in $X$.

::: pf-proof
Since $U$ is a neighbourhood of $x$, weak local connectedness at $x$ supplies a connected subspace $C_x\subseteq U$ and a neighbourhood $V_x$ of $x$ with $V_x\subseteq C_x$. The set $C_x$ is connected and meets $C$, because $x\in C_x\cap C$, so a connected subset of $U$ meeting the component $C$ of $U$ lies in $C$; hence $C_x\subseteq C$, and $V_x\subseteq C$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #components-open}
Every component of every open subset of $X$ is open in $X$.

::: pf-proof
With $U$ open and $C$ a component of $U$, step [](#connected-inside){.pf-ref} gives for each $x\in C$ a neighbourhood $V_x$ of $x$ with $V_x\subseteq C$. Hence $C=\bigcup_{x\in C}V_x$ is a union of open sets, so it is open. As $U$ was an arbitrary open set, every component of every open set is open.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step}
$X$ is locally connected.

::: pf-proof
A space is locally connected if and only if every component of every open set is open, which is Theorem 25.3 of §25; step [](#components-open){.pf-ref} establishes exactly that condition. So the hypothesis that $X$ is weakly locally connected at each of its points implies that $X$ is locally connected.
:::
