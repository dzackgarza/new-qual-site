::: {.exercise}

A space $X$ is said to be weakly locally connected at $x$ if for every neighborhood $U$ of $x$, there is a connected subspace of $X$ contained in $U$ that contains a neighborhood of $x$.
Show that if $X$ is weakly locally connected at each of its points, then $X$ is locally connected.
[Hint: Show that components of open sets are open.]
:::

::: {.solution}
It suffices to show that every component of every open set is open.

Let $U\subseteq X$ be open, let $C$ be a component of $U$, and let $x\in C$. By weak local connectedness at $x$, applied to the neighborhood $U$, there is a connected subspace $A_x\subseteq U$ that contains a neighborhood $V_x$ of $x$. Since $A_x$ is connected and meets the component $C$ at $x$, maximality of components gives
\[
A_x\subseteq C.
\]
Hence
\[
x\in V_x\subseteq C.
\]
So every point of $C$ is an interior point of $C$, and $C$ is open.

Now, given an open neighborhood $U$ of a point $x$, the component of $U$ containing $x$ is a connected open neighborhood of $x$ contained in $U$. Therefore $X$ is locally connected.
:::
