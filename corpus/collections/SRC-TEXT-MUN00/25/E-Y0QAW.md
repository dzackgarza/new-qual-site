::: {.exercise}

Let $p: X \to Y$ be a quotient map.
Show that if $X$ is locally connected, then $Y$ is locally connected.
[Hint: If $C$ is a component of the open set $U$ of $Y$, show that $p^{-1}(C)$ is a union of components of $p^{-1}(U)$.]
:::
