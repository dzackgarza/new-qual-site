::: {.exercise title="Munkres §42.2"}

(a) Show that for each $x \in S_\Omega$, the section of $S_\Omega$ by $x$ has a countable basis and hence is metrizable.

(b) Conclude that $S_\Omega$ is not paracompact.
:::
