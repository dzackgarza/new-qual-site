::: {.exercise}

Let$G = G_1 * G_2$.
Given$c \in G$, let$cG_1c^{-1}$denote the set of all elements of the form$cxc^{-1}$, for$x \in G_1$.
It is a subgroup of$G$; show that its intersection with$G_2$consists of the identity alone.
:::
