::: {.exercise}

Let $A$ be a subspace of $\mathbb{R}^n$; let $h: (A, a_0) \to (Y, y_0)$.
Show that if $h$ is extendable to a continuous map of $\mathbb{R}^n$ into $Y$, then $h_*$ is the trivial homomorphism (the homomorphism that maps everything to the identity element).
:::

::: solution
**Goal:** Prove that if a pointed continuous map $h: (A, a_0) \to (Y, y_0)$ from a subspace $A \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$ extends to a continuous map $H: \mathbb{R}^n \to Y$, then the induced homomorphism $h_*: \pi_1(A, a_0) \to \pi_1(Y, y_0)$ is trivial.

<1>1. Factorization through the ambient space $\mathbb{R}^n$:
    *Proof:*
    <2>1. Let $i: (A, a_0) \hookrightarrow (\mathbb{R}^n, a_0)$ denote the canonical inclusion map.
    <2>2. Since $H: (\mathbb{R}^n, a_0) \to (Y, y_0)$ is a continuous extension of $h$, the composition satisfies:
        $$h = H \circ i: (A, a_0) \to (Y, y_0).$$
    <2>3. By functoriality of the fundamental group operator $\pi_1$:
        $$h_* = (H \circ i)_* = H_* \circ i_*,$$
        where $i_*: \pi_1(A, a_0) \to \pi_1(\mathbb{R}^n, a_0)$ and $H_*: \pi_1(\mathbb{R}^n, a_0) \to \pi_1(Y, y_0)$.

<1>2. Simple connectivity of $\mathbb{R}^n$:
    *Proof:*
    <2>1. The Euclidean space $\mathbb{R}^n$ is convex, and the straight-line homotopy $K(x, t) = (1 - t)x + t a_0$ exhibits $\mathbb{R}^n$ as contractible to $a_0$.
    <2>2. In particular, $\mathbb{R}^n$ is simply connected, so its fundamental group is trivial:
        $$\pi_1(\mathbb{R}^n, a_0) = \{1\}.$$

<1>3. Triviality of the homomorphism $h_*$:
    *Proof:*
    <2>1. Let $[\gamma] \in \pi_1(A, a_0)$ be an arbitrary element.
    <2>2. Since $\pi_1(\mathbb{R}^n, a_0) = \{1\}$, the image $i_*([\gamma])$ must be the identity element $1 \in \pi_1(\mathbb{R}^n, a_0)$.
    <2>3. Because group homomorphisms preserve the identity element:
        $$h_*([\gamma]) = (H_* \circ i_*)([\gamma]) = H_*(1) = 1 \in \pi_1(Y, y_0).$$
    <2>4. Thus $h_*([\gamma]) = 1$ for all $[\gamma] \in \pi_1(A, a_0)$.

<1>4. Conclusion:
    The induced homomorphism $h_*$ is trivial. Q.E.D.
:::
