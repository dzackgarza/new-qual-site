::: {.exercise}

Let $\alpha$ be a path in $X$ from $x_0$ to $x_1$; let $\beta$ be a path in $X$ from $x_1$ to $x_2$.
Show that if $\gamma = \alpha * \beta$, then $\hat{\gamma} = \hat{\beta} \circ \hat{\alpha}$.
:::
