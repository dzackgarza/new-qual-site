::: {.exercise}

Let $x_0$ and $x_1$ be points of the path-connected space $X$.
Show that $\pi_1(X, x_0)$ is abelian if and only if for every pair $\alpha$ and $\beta$ of paths from $x_0$ to $x_1$, we have $\hat{\alpha} = \hat{\beta}$.
:::
