::: {.exercise}

Find a point-finite open covering $\mathcal{A}$ of $\mathbb{R}$ that is not locally finite.
(The collection $\mathcal{A}$ is point-finite if each point of $\mathbb{R}$ lies in only finitely many elements of $\mathcal{A}$.)
:::
