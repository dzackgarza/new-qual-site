::: {.exercise}

Consider the covering map $p: \mathbb{R} \times \mathbb{R}_+ \to \mathbb{R}^2 - 0$ of Example 6 of §53. Find liftings of the paths

$$
f(t) = (2 - t, 0),
$$

$$
g(t) = ((1 + t) \cos 2\pi t, (1 + t) \sin 2\pi t),
$$

$$
h(t) = f * g.
$$

Sketch these paths and their liftings.
:::
