::: {.exercise}

Consider the following conditions on a locally $m$-euclidean space $X$:

(i) $X$ is compact Hausdorff.

(ii) $X$ is an $m$-manifold.

(iii) $X$ is metrizable.

(iv) $X$ is normal.

(v) $X$ is Hausdorff.

Show that (i) $\Rightarrow$ (ii) $\Rightarrow$ (iii) $\Rightarrow$ (iv) $\Rightarrow$ (v).
:::
