::: {.exercise title="Munkres §13.3"}

Show that the collection $\mathcal{T}_c$ given in Example 4 of §12 is a topology on the set $X$.
Is the collection

$$
\mathcal{T}_\infty = \ts{U \mid X - U \text{ is infinite or empty or all of } X}
$$

a topology on $X$?
