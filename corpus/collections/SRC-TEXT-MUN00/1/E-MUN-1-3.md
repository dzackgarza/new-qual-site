::: {.exercise}

(a) Write the contrapositive and converse of the following statement: "If $x < 0$, then $x^2 - x > 0$," and determine which (if any) of the three statements are true.

(b) Do the same for the statement "If $x > 0$, then $x^2 - x > 0$ ."
:::
