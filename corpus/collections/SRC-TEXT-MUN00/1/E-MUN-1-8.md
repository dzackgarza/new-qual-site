::: {.exercise}

If a set $A$ has two elements, show that $\mathcal{P}(A)$ has four elements.
How many elements does $\mathcal{P}(A)$ have if $A$ has one element?
Three elements?
No elements?
Why is $\mathcal{P}(A)$ called the power set of $A$ ?
:::
