::: {.exercise}

Show that the set $\mathcal{B}(\mathbb{R}, \mathbb{R})$ of bounded functions $f: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ is closed in $\mathbb{R}^{\mathbb{R}}$ in the uniform topology, but not in the topology of compact convergence.
:::
