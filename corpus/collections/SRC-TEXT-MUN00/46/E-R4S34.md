::: {.exercise}

Show that the sets $B_C(f, \epsilon)$ form a basis for a topology on $Y^X$.
:::

::: {.solution}
The argument is the card's own; no retained solution set is cited on the card. It checks the two basis axioms for the sets $B_{C}(f,\varepsilon)$ of Munkres §46, printed pp. 286--287.

Throughout, $(Y,d)$ is a metric space, $X$ a space, and for compact $C\subset X$, $f\in Y^{X}$ and $\varepsilon>0$,
$$
B_{C}(f,\varepsilon)=\Big\{g\in Y^{X}\ \mid\ \sup_{x\in C}d\big(f(x),g(x)\big)<\varepsilon\Big\},
$$
with the supremum over $C=\varnothing$ read as $0$; write $d_{C}(u,v)=\sup_{x\in C}d(u(x),v(x))$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #nonempty}
Every element of $Y^{X}$ lies in some member of the collection.

::: pf-proof
If $X\ne\varnothing$ choose $x_{0}\in X$ and take $C=\{x_{0}\}$, compact; then $d_{C}(f,f)=0<\varepsilon$, so $f\in B_{C}(f,\varepsilon)$. If $X=\varnothing$ then $B_{\varnothing}(f,\varepsilon)=Y^{X}$ contains $f$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #intersection}
If $h\in B_{C}(f,\varepsilon)\cap B_{D}(g,\delta)$, then $h$ lies in a member of the collection contained in that intersection.

::: pf-proof
The set $C\cup D$ is compact, being a union of two compact sets, and
$$
r=\min\{\varepsilon-d_{C}(f,h),\ \delta-d_{D}(g,h)\}>0
$$
because both numbers are positive. Let $k\in B_{C\cup D}(h,r)$ and $x\in C$; then
$$
d\big(f(x),k(x)\big)\le d\big(f(x),h(x)\big)+d\big(h(x),k(x)\big)\le d_{C}(f,h)+r\le\varepsilon,
$$
and in fact the last inequality is strict because $r\le\varepsilon-d_{C}(f,h)$ and $d(h(x),k(x))<r$. So $k\in B_{C}(f,\varepsilon)$, and the same computation with $D$, $g$, $\delta$ gives $k\in B_{D}(g,\delta)$. Hence $B_{C\cup D}(h,r)\subset B_{C}(f,\varepsilon)\cap B_{D}(g,\delta)$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step}
The sets $B_{C}(f,\varepsilon)$ form a basis for a topology on $Y^{X}$.

::: pf-proof
Steps [](#nonempty){.pf-ref} and [](#intersection){.pf-ref} are the two conditions a basis must satisfy (Lemma 13.2 and the discussion preceding it); the topology they generate is the topology of compact convergence.
:::
