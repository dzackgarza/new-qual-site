::: {.exercise}

In the finite complement topology on $\mathbb{R}$, to what point or points does the sequence $x_n = 1/n$ converge?
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #converges-to-every-point}
The sequence $x_n=1/n$ converges to $\boxed{\text{every point of }\RR}$.

::: pf-proof
Let $x\in\RR$ and let $U$ be an open neighborhood of $x$.
In the finite complement topology, $U=\RR-F$ for a finite set $F$.
The terms $1/n$ are distinct, so at most $\abs{F}$ indices $n$ satisfy $1/n\in F$, and $x_n\in U$ for all other $n$.
Hence every neighborhood of $x$ contains $x_n$ for all but finitely many $n$, which is convergence $x_n\to x$.
:::
