::: {.exercise}

Let $A$, $B$, and $A_\alpha$ denote subsets of a space $X$.
Determine whether the following equations hold; if an equality fails, determine whether one of the inclusions $\supset$ or $\subset$ holds.

(a) $\overline{A \cap B} = \overline{A} \cap \overline{B}$.

(b) $\overline{\bigcap A_\alpha} = \bigcap \overline{A}_\alpha$.

(c) $\overline{A - B} = \overline{A} - \overline{B}$.
:::
