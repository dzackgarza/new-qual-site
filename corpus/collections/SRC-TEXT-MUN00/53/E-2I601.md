::: {.exercise}

Let $p: E \to B$ be a covering map; let $B$ be connected.
Show that if $p^{-1}(b_0)$ has $k$ elements for some $b_0 \in B$, then $p^{-1}(b)$ has $k$ elements for every $b \in B$.
In such a case, $E$ is called a $k$-fold covering of $B$.
:::

::: solution
**Goal:** Prove that the fiber cardinality function $b \mapsto |p^{-1}(b)|$ of a covering map $p: E \to B$ is constant across every connected component of the base space $B$.

<1>1. Local constancy of fiber cardinality:
    For every $b \in B$, there exists an open neighborhood $U$ of $b$ such that $|p^{-1}(y)| = |p^{-1}(b)|$ for all $y \in U$.
    *Proof:*
    <2>1. Since $p: E \to B$ is a covering map, $b$ has an evenly covered open neighborhood $U \subseteq B$.
    <2>2. By definition of evenly covered, $p^{-1}(U) = \bigsqcup_{\alpha \in J} V_\alpha$ is a disjoint union of open sets $V_\alpha \subseteq E$, where each restriction $p|_{V_\alpha}: V_\alpha \to U$ is a homeomorphism.
    <2>3. For any $y \in U$ and each $\alpha \in J$, the restriction $p|_{V_\alpha}$ is a bijection onto $U$, so the fiber $p^{-1}(y)$ contains exactly one element in each slice $V_\alpha$:
        $$|p^{-1}(y) \cap V_\alpha| = 1 \quad \text{for all } \alpha \in J.$$
    <2>4. Since the slices $\{V_\alpha\}_{\alpha \in J}$ are pairwise disjoint and cover $p^{-1}(U)$:
        $$|p^{-1}(y)| = \sum_{\alpha \in J} |p^{-1}(y) \cap V_\alpha| = |J|.$$
    <2>5. Setting $y = b$ gives $|p^{-1}(b)| = |J|$.
    <2>6. Thus $|p^{-1}(y)| = |p^{-1}(b)| = |J|$ for all $y \in U$.

<1>2. Connectedness propagation:
    *Proof:*
    <2>1. Let $k = |p^{-1}(b_0)|$, and define the level set:
        $$S_k = \ts{b \in B \mid |p^{-1}(b)| = k}.$$
    <2>2. $S_k$ is non-empty because $b_0 \in S_k$.
    <2>3. $S_k$ is open in $B$: for any $b \in S_k$, <1>1 provides an evenly covered open neighborhood $U$ of $b$ with $|p^{-1}(y)| = |p^{-1}(b)| = k$ for all $y \in U$, so $U \subseteq S_k$.
    <2>4. The complement $B \setminus S_k = \bigcup_{m \neq k} S_m$ is a union of open sets, hence open in $B$. Thus $S_k$ is closed.
    <2>5. Since $B$ is connected and $S_k$ is a non-empty clopen subset of $B$, we must have $S_k = B$.

<1>3. Conclusion:
    $|p^{-1}(b)| = k$ for every $b \in B$. Q.E.D.
:::
