::: {.exercise}

Let $X$ be the wedge of two circles; let $p: E \to X$ be a covering map.
The fundamental group of $E$ maps isomorphically under $p_*$ onto a subgroup $H$ of the fundamental group of $X$; the latter is free on two generators $\alpha$ and $\beta$.

(a) For each of the four covering spaces $E$ given in [[E-79XXG]], determine the cardinality of a system of free generators for the fundamental group of $E$.

(b) For each of these covering spaces, find, in terms of $\alpha$ and $\beta$, a system of free generators for the subgroup $H$ of the fundamental group of $X$.
:::

::: solution
**Goal:** Determine the rank (cardinality of a free generating set) and explicit generators in terms of $\alpha, \beta \in \pi_1(X) \cong F_2$ for the subgroups corresponding to the four covering graphs of $X = S^1 \vee S^1$ in Figures 81.4–81.7.

<1>1. General rank formula for $d$-fold coverings of $S^1 \vee S^1$:
    *Proof:*
    <2>1. $X$ is a 1-dimensional CW complex with 1 vertex and 2 edges, so $\chi(X) = 1 - 2 = -1$.
    <2>2. A connected $d$-sheeted covering space $E$ is a graph with $V = d$ vertices and $E = 2d$ edges.
    <2>3. The Euler characteristic of $E$ is $\chi(E) = V - E = d - 2d = -d$.
    <2>4. Since $E$ is a connected 1-dimensional CW complex, its fundamental group $\pi_1(E)$ is a free group of rank:
        $$\operatorname{rank}(\pi_1(E)) = 1 - \chi(E) = 1 - (-d) = 1 + d.$$
    <2>5. By the Nielsen-Schreier Theorem, the corresponding subgroup $H = p_*(\pi_1(E, e_0)) \le F_2$ has index $[F_2 : H] = d$ and free rank $1 + d$.

<1>2. Part (a): Cardinality of free generating systems.
    - **Fig 81.4 (2-fold covering):** Degree $d = 2 \implies \operatorname{rank} = 1 + 2 = 3$.
    - **Fig 81.5 (3-fold covering):** Degree $d = 3 \implies \operatorname{rank} = 1 + 3 = 4$.
    - **Fig 81.6 (3-fold covering):** Degree $d = 3 \implies \operatorname{rank} = 1 + 3 = 4$.
    - **Fig 81.7 (4-fold covering):** Degree $d = 4 \implies \operatorname{rank} = 1 + 4 = 5$.

<1>3. Part (b): Free generators for $H \le F_2 = \langle \alpha, \beta \rangle$.
    *Proof:*
    <2>1. **Figure 81.4 ($d = 2$, $\alpha$ swaps sheets $0 \leftrightarrow 1$, $\beta$ loops at each sheet):**
        - Choose maximal tree $T$ containing the edge $\tilde{\alpha}_1$ from $0$ to $1$.
        - The non-tree edges yield the 3 free generators:
          $$H = \big\langle \beta, \; \alpha^2, \; \alpha \beta \alpha^{-1} \big\rangle.$$
    <2>2. **Figure 81.5 ($d = 3$, $\alpha$ is 3-cycle $(0\,1\,2)$, $\beta$ fixes $0$ and swaps $1 \leftrightarrow 2$):**
        - Choose maximal tree $T = \{0 \xrightarrow{\alpha} 1, \; 1 \xrightarrow{\alpha} 2\}$.
        - Non-tree edges give the 4 free generators:
          $$H = \big\langle \beta, \; \alpha^3, \; \alpha \beta \alpha^{-2}, \; \alpha^2 \beta \alpha^{-1} \big\rangle.$$
    <2>3. **Figure 81.6 ($d = 3$, $\alpha = (0\,1\,2)$ and $\beta = (0\,2\,1)$):**
        - Choose maximal tree $T = \{0 \xrightarrow{\alpha} 1, \; 1 \xrightarrow{\alpha} 2\}$.
        - Tracing loops for non-tree edges gives the 4 free generators:
          $$H = \big\langle \alpha^3, \; \beta^3, \; \alpha \beta, \; \alpha^2 \beta \alpha \big\rangle.$$
    <2>4. **Figure 81.7 ($d = 4$, $\mathbb{Z}_2 \times \mathbb{Z}_2$ covering, $\alpha = (0\,1)(2\,3)$ and $\beta = (0\,2)(1\,3)$):**
        - Choose maximal tree $T = \{0 \xrightarrow{\alpha} 1, \; 0 \xrightarrow{\beta} 2, \; 1 \xrightarrow{\beta} 3\}$.
        - The remaining 5 non-tree edges give the 5 free generators:
          $$H = \big\langle \alpha^2, \; \beta^2, \; \alpha \beta \alpha^{-1} \beta^{-1}, \; \alpha \beta^2 \alpha^{-1}, \; \beta \alpha^2 \beta^{-1} \big\rangle.$$
    Q.E.D.
