::: {.exercise}

Let $X$ be the wedge of two circles; let $p: E \to X$ be a covering map.
The fundamental group of $E$ maps isomorphically under $p_*$ onto a subgroup $H$ of the fundamental group of $X$; the latter is free on two generators $\alpha$ and $\beta$.

(a) For each of the four covering spaces $E$ given in [[E-79XXG]], determine the cardinality of a system of free generators for the fundamental group of $E$.

(b) For each of these covering spaces, find, in terms of $\alpha$ and $\beta$, a system of free generators for the subgroup $H$ of the fundamental group of $X$.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: pf-step

Rank formula for finite $d$-fold coverings of $S^1 \vee S^1$:
    *Proof:*

::: pf-proof

::: pf-step

$X$ is a 1-dimensional CW complex with 1 vertex and 2 edges, so $\chi(X) = 1 - 2 = -1$.

:::

::: pf-step

A connected $d$-sheeted covering space $E$ is a graph with $V = d$ vertices and $E = 2d$ edges.

:::

::: pf-step

The Euler characteristic of $E$ is $\chi(E) = V - E = d - 2d = -d$.

:::

::: pf-step

Since $E$ is a connected 1-dimensional CW complex, its fundamental group $\pi_1(E)$ is a free group of rank:
        $$\operatorname{rank}(\pi_1(E)) = 1 - \chi(E) = 1 - (-d) = 1 + d.$$

:::

::: pf-step

By the Nielsen-Schreier Theorem, the corresponding subgroup $H = p_*(\pi_1(E, e_0)) \le F_2$ has index $[F_2 : H] = d$ and free rank $1 + d$.

:::

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Part (a): Cardinality of free generating systems.
    - **Fig 81.4 (2-fold covering):** Degree $d = 2 \implies \operatorname{rank} = 1 + 2 = 3$.
    - **Fig 81.5 (3-fold covering):** Degree $d = 3 \implies \operatorname{rank} = 1 + 3 = 4$.
    - **Fig 81.6 (4-fold covering):** Degree $d = 4 \implies \operatorname{rank} = 1 + 4 = 5$.
    - **Fig 81.7 (infinite covering):** The bi-infinite chain of $A$-edges is a maximal tree, and each vertex has one $B$-loop. Thus the free rank is countably infinite.

:::

::: pf-step

Part (b): Free generators for $H \le F_2 = \langle \alpha, \beta \rangle$.
    *Proof:*

::: pf-proof

::: pf-step

**Figure 81.4 ($d = 2$, $\alpha$ swaps sheets $0 \leftrightarrow 1$, $\beta$ loops at each sheet):**
        - Choose maximal tree $T$ containing the edge $\tilde{\alpha}_1$ from $0$ to $1$.
        - The non-tree edges yield the 3 free generators:
          $$H = \big\langle \beta, \; \alpha^2, \; \alpha \beta \alpha^{-1} \big\rangle.$$

:::

::: pf-step

**Figure 81.5 ($d = 3$):**
        - As in [[E-79XXG]], $\alpha$ swaps the base vertex $0$ with $u$ and fixes $w$, while $\beta$ swaps $0$ with $w$ and fixes $u$.
        - Choose the $\alpha$-edge $0\to u$ and the $\beta$-edge $0\to w$ as a maximal tree. The other two connecting edges and the loops at $u,w$ give:
          $$H = \big\langle \alpha^2,\;\beta^2,\;\alpha\beta\alpha^{-1},\;\beta\alpha\beta^{-1}\big\rangle.$$

:::

::: pf-step

**Figure 81.6 ($d = 4$):**
        - Use the vertices $0,u,m,r$ of [[E-79XXG]]. The maximal tree has edges $0\xrightarrow{\alpha}u$, $0\xrightarrow{\beta}m$, and $m\xrightarrow{\alpha}r$.
        - The other five edges give:
          $$H=\big\langle
          \alpha^2,\;\beta^2,\;\alpha\beta\alpha^{-1},\;
          \beta\alpha^2\beta^{-1},\;
          \beta\alpha\beta\alpha^{-1}\beta^{-1}
          \big\rangle.$$

:::

::: pf-step

**Figure 81.7 (infinite covering):**
        - The $A$-edges form a bi-infinite maximal tree with vertices indexed by $j\in\ZZ$, where the tree path from the base vertex to $j$ projects to $\alpha^j$. Each vertex has a $B$-loop. These loops give the free basis
          $$H=\big\langle\alpha^j\beta\alpha^{-j}:j\in\ZZ\big\rangle.$$
    Q.E.D.
:::
