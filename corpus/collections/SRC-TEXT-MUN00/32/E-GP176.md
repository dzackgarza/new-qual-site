::: {.exercise}

Is $\RR^\omega$ normal in the product topology?
In the uniform topology?

It is not known whether $\RR^\omega$ is normal in the box topology.
Mary-Ellen Rudin has shown that the answer is affirmative if one assumes the continuum hypothesis [RM]. In fact, she shows it satisfies a stronger condition called paracompactness.
:::

::: {.solution}
In the product topology, $\RR^\omega$ is metrizable, for example by
$$
D(x,y)=\sup_{n\ge1}\frac{\min\{\abs{x_n-y_n},1\}}{n}.
$$
Every metric space is normal, so $\RR^\omega$ is normal in the product topology.

The uniform topology is induced by the uniform metric
$$
\bar\rho(x,y)=\sup_n\min\{\abs{x_n-y_n},1\}.
$$
Hence it too is metrizable and therefore normal.
:::
