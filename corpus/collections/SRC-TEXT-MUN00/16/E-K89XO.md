::: {.exercise}

A map $f: X \to Y$ is said to be an open map if for every open set $U$ of $X$, the set $f(U)$ is open in $Y$.
Show that $\pi_1: X \times Y \to X$ and $\pi_2: X \times Y \to Y$ are open maps.
:::
