::: {.exercise title="Munkres §45.3"}

Prove the following.

Theorem (Arzela's theorem).
Let $X$ be compact; let $f_n \in \mathcal{C}(X, \mathbb{R}^k)$.
If the collection $\ts{f_n}$ is pointwise bounded and equicontinuous, then the sequence $f_n$ has a uniformly convergent subsequence.
:::
