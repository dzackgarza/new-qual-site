::: {.exercise}

If $K$ is the Klein bottle, calculate $H_1(K)$ directly.
:::

::: {.solution}

The computation above is the card's own, from Theorem 74.2 and Corollary 75.2; a second computation in a different model, by Paul VanKoughnett, is cited at the foot of this solution rather than transcribed, because it computes singular homology of a $\Delta$-complex, which the book does not develop.

Let $K$ be the Klein bottle, presented as the square with edges labelled $a,b,a^{-1},b$ and the usual identifications.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #scheme}
The labelling scheme $aba^{-1}b$ has one vertex class, so $\pi_{1}(K)\cong\langle a,b\mid aba^{-1}b=1\rangle$.

::: pf-proof
The two $a$-edges join $v_{0}$ to $v_{1}$ and $v_{2}$ to $v_{3}$, and the two $b$-edges join $v_{1}$ to $v_{2}$ and $v_{3}$ to $v_{0}$; so the pasting map identifies all four vertices in one class. A polygon whose edge labels form one vertex class with the labels $w_{1},\ldots,w_{n}$ has fundamental group $\langle\alpha_{1},\ldots,\alpha_{n}\mid w_{1}\cdots w_{n}=1\rangle$ (Theorem 74.2), which for $aba^{-1}b$ is $\langle a,b\mid aba^{-1}b=1\rangle$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #abelianization}
$H_{1}(K)\cong\big(\ZZ[a]\oplus\ZZ[b]\big)/\langle 2[b]\rangle$.

::: pf-proof
By Corollary 75.2 , for a presentation $G=\langle x_{1},\ldots,x_{n}\mid r_{1},\ldots,r_{m}\rangle$ the group $G/[G,G]$ is the quotient of the free abelian group on the classes $[x_{i}]$ by the subgroup generated by the images of the relators. Here the single relator is $x=aba^{-1}b$, and in the abelianization the class of a product is the sum of the classes of its factors, with $[a^{-1}]=-[a]$; so the image of $x$ is
$$
[a]+[b]-[a]+[b]=2[b].
$$
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #answer}
$H_{1}(K)\cong\ZZ\oplus\ZZ/2$.

::: pf-proof
By step [](#abelianization){.pf-ref}, $H_{1}(K)$ is the quotient of $\ZZ\oplus\ZZ$ by the subgroup generated by $(0,2)$. The first summand $\ZZ[a]$ maps isomorphically onto the first coordinate and meets the subgroup trivially, and the second coordinate is $\ZZ/2\ZZ$; so the quotient is $\ZZ\oplus\ZZ/2$.
:::

:::

:::
A second computation of the same group, in a different model, is that of Paul VanKoughnett, answer 7339 (https://math.stackexchange.com/a/7339), posted 2010-10-20 to Math Stack Exchange question 7334, "Homology of the Klein bottle" (https://math.stackexchange.com/questions/7334, asked by NebulousReveal on 2010-10-20). Take a $\Delta$-complex model with one vertex, edges $a$, $b$, $c$ and triangles $U$, $L$; then the $1$-cycles are free abelian on $a,b,c$, and $\partial U=a+b-c$, $\partial L=c+a-b$, so
$$
H_{1}=\langle a,b,c\rangle/\langle a+b-c,\ a-b+c\rangle
=\langle b,c\rangle/\langle 2b-2c\rangle\cong\ZZ\oplus\ZZ/2 .
$$
That answer computes the singular homology of a $\Delta$-complex, an invariant the book does not develop, and its agreement with the $H_{1}$ of §75 is the Hurewicz theorem, which the book states without proof (printed p. 455); the computation on this card uses only Theorem 74.2 and Corollary 75.2.

:::
