::: {.exercise}

Find a circle about the origin containing all the roots of the polynomial equation $x^7 + x^2 + 1 = 0$.
:::

::: {.solution}
Let \(z\) be a root of
\[
p(z)=z^7+z^2+1.
\]
If \(|z|\ge2\), then
\[
|z|^7=|z^2+1|\le |z|^2+1.
\]
But for \(r\ge2\),
\[
r^7\ge 2^5r^2=32r^2>r^2+1,
\]
which is impossible. Therefore every root satisfies \(|z|<2\).

Thus the circle
\[
\boxed{|z|=2}
\]
contains all roots in its interior. (A smaller optimal radius can be obtained from the positive solution of \(r^7=r^2+1\), but no optimality is requested.)
:::
