::: {.exercise}

Let $\ts{A_\alpha}$ be a collection of connected subspaces of $X$; let $A$ be a connected subspace of $X$.
Show that if $A \cap A_\alpha \neq \varnothing$ for all $\alpha$, then $A \cup (\bigcup A_\alpha)$ is connected.
:::
