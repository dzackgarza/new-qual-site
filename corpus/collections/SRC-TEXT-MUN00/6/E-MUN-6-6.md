::: {.exercise}

(a) Let $A = \{1, \ldots, n\}$ . Show there is a bijection of $\mathcal{P}(A)$ with the cartesian product $X^n$, where $X$ is the two-element set $X = \{0, 1\}$ .

(b) Show that if $A$ is finite, then $\mathcal{P}(A)$ is finite.
:::
