::: {.exercise}

A space $X$ is said to be perfectly normal if $X$ is normal and if every closed set in $X$ is a $G_\delta$ set in $X$.

(a) Show that every metrizable space is perfectly normal.

(b) Show that a perfectly normal space is completely normal.
For this reason the condition of perfect normality is sometimes called the "$T_6$ axiom."
[Hint: Let $A$ and $B$ be separated sets in $X$. Choose continuous functions $f, g: X \to [0, 1]$ that vanish precisely on $\overline{A}$ and $\overline{B}$, respectively.
Consider the function $f - g$.]

(c) There is a familiar space that is completely normal but not perfectly normal.
What is it?
:::
