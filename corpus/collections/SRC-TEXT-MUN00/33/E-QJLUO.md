::: {.exercise}

Let $X$ be completely regular; let $A$ and $B$ be disjoint closed subsets of $X$.
Show that if $A$ is compact, there is a continuous function $f: X \to [0, 1]$ such that $f(A) = \ts{0}$ and $f(B) = \ts{1}$.
:::
