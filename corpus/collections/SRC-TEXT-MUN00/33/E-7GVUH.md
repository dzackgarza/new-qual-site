::: {.exercise}

Examine the proof of the Urysohn lemma, and show that for given $r$,

$$
f^{-1}(r) = \bigcap_{p > r} U_p - \bigcup_{q < r} U_q,
$$

where $p, q$ range over the rationals.
:::
