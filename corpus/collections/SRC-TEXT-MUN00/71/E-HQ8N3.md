::: {.exercise}

What can you say about the fundamental group of $X \vee Y$ if $X$ is homeomorphic to $S^1$ and $Y$ is homeomorphic to $S^2$?
:::

::: {.solution}

Write $X\vee Y$ for the union of $X$ and $Y$ with $X\cap Y=\theset{q}$ a single point.

::: pf

::: pf-step

The hypotheses of Exercise 1 of this section hold.

::: pf-proof

$X$ and $Y$ are closed subspaces of $X\vee Y$ meeting only in $q$; each is homeomorphic to a compact space, hence the union is the wedge in the sense of the exercise. The point $q$ is a deformation retract of an open arc $W_X\subseteq X$ containing $q$ (an open interval in $S^1$ retracts onto any of its points), and of the open disk $W_Y\subseteq Y$ of radius $1$ about $q$ (a convex set retracts onto any of its points, and $S^2$ is a metric space with its usual topology).

:::

:::

::: pf-step

$\pi_1(X\vee Y,q)\cong\pi_1(X,q)*\pi_1(Y,q)$.

::: pf-proof

Exercise 1 of this section, printed p. 438.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

$\pi_1(X,q)\cong\ZZ$ and $\pi_1(Y,q)$ is trivial.

::: pf-proof

$\pi_1(S^1)\cong\ZZ$ (Theorem 54.5, printed p. 345). $S^2$ is simply connected by Theorem 59.3 (printed p. 369), since $2\ge2$, so $\pi_1(S^2)$ is the trivial group.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

$\pi_1(X\vee Y,q)\cong\ZZ$.

::: pf-proof

By steps 2 and 3, $\pi_1(X\vee Y,q)\cong\ZZ*\{1\}$, the free product of $\ZZ$ with the trivial group. A reduced word in this free product is a sequence of nontrivial elements of $\ZZ$ with the letters from the trivial factor omitted, and the multiplication is that of $\ZZ$; so $\ZZ*\{1\}\cong\ZZ$, also by the extension condition of Lemma 68.3 (printed p. 418) applied twice.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

$\pi_1(X\vee Y,q)\cong\ZZ$, generated by the class of a loop going once around the circle.

:::
