::: {.exercise title="Munkres §57.2"}

Show that if $g: S^2 \to S^2$ is continuous and $g(x) \neq g(-x)$ for all $x$, then $g$ is surjective.
[Hint: If $p \in S^2$, then $S^2 - \ts{p}$ is homeomorphic to $\mathbb{R}^2$.]
:::
