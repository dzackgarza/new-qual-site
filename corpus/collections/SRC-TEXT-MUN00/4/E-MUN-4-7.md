::: {.exercise}

Let $a \in \mathbb{R}$ and $a \neq 0$ . Define $a^0 = 1$, and for $n \in \mathbb{Z}_+$, $a^{-n} = 1 / a^n$ . Show that the laws of exponents hold for $a, b \neq 0$ and $n, m \in \mathbb{Z}$ .
:::
