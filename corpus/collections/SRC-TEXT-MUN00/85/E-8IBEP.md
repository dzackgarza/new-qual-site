::: {.exercise}

Let $p: \mathbb{R} \to S^1$ be the standard covering map; consider the covering map $p \times p: \mathbb{R} \times \mathbb{R} \to S^1 \times S^1$.
Let $b_0 = (1, 0) \in S^1$; set $X = (b_0 \times S^1) \cup (S^1 \times b_0)$; let $E = (p \times p)^{-1}(X)$; and let $q: E \to X$ be the covering map obtained by restricting $p \times p$.
The fundamental group of $X$ has free generators $\alpha$ and $\beta$, where $\alpha$ is represented by a loop in $b_0 \times S^1$ and $\beta$ by a loop in $S^1 \times b_0$.
Find a system of free generators for the subgroup $q_*(\pi_1(E, e_0))$, where $e_0$ is the origin in $\mathbb{R}^2$.
:::

::: solution
**Goal:** Find a system of free generators for the subgroup $H = q_*(\pi_1(E, e_0)) \le \pi_1(X, x_0) \cong F(\alpha, \beta)$ corresponding to the grid covering of the figure-eight space.

<1>1. Identification of the total space and covering map:
    *Proof:*
    <2>1. The base space $X = (b_0 \times S^1) \cup (S^1 \times b_0) \cong S^1 \vee S^1$ has fundamental group $\pi_1(X, x_0) = F(\alpha, \beta)$, where $\alpha$ corresponds to traveling along $b_0 \times S^1$ (vertical coordinate) and $\beta$ corresponds to $S^1 \times b_0$ (horizontal coordinate).
    <2>2. The total space $E = (p \times p)^{-1}(X) = (\mathbb{R} \times \mathbb{Z}) \cup (\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{R}) \subset \mathbb{R}^2$ is the infinite grid graph with vertices $V = \mathbb{Z}^2$.
    <2>3. A path in $E$ starting at $(0, 0)$ corresponding to a word $w \in F(\alpha, \beta)$ terminates at $(\operatorname{exp}_\beta(w), \operatorname{exp}_\alpha(w)) \in \mathbb{Z}^2$.
    <2>4. Thus a loop lifts to a closed path in $E$ if and only if the total exponent of both $\alpha$ and $\beta$ is zero.
    <2>5. Hence $H = q_*(\pi_1(E, e_0)) = [F(\alpha, \beta), F(\alpha, \beta)]$ is the commutator subgroup of the free group on two generators.

<1>2. Maximal spanning tree construction in the grid graph $E$:
    *Proof:*
    <2>1. Let $T \subset E$ be the tree consisting of the entire horizontal axis $\mathbb{R} \times \{0\}$ together with all vertical lines $\{m\} \times \mathbb{R}$ for all $m \in \mathbb{Z}$:
        $$T = (\mathbb{R} \times \{0\}) \cup (\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{R}).$$
    <2>2. $T$ contains all vertices $\mathbb{Z}^2$ and contains no cycles, so $T$ is a maximal spanning tree for $E$.
    <2>3. The edges of $E$ not in $T$ are precisely the horizontal unit segments at non-zero integer heights:
        $$e_{m, n} = [m, m+1] \times \{n\}, \quad \text{for } m \in \mathbb{Z}, \, n \in \mathbb{Z} \setminus \{0\}.$$

<1>3. Construction of the free basis:
    *Proof:*
    <2>1. By the standard maximal-tree theorem for fundamental groups of 1-complexes (Theorem 84.1), the fundamental group $\pi_1(E, e_0)$ is freely generated by the simple cycles formed by each edge $e_{m, n} \notin T$ completed by paths in $T$ to the basepoint $(0, 0)$.
    <2>2. The unique simple path in $T$ from $(0, 0)$ to $(m, n)$ is:
        $$(0, 0) \xrightarrow{\text{along } \mathbb{R} \times \{0\}} (m, 0) \xrightarrow{\text{along } \{m\} \times \mathbb{R}} (m, n),$$
        which projects under $q$ to the word $\beta^m \alpha^n$.
    <2>3. Traversing the horizontal edge $e_{m, n}$ from $(m, n)$ to $(m+1, n)$ projects to $\beta$.
    <2>4. The return path in $T$ from $(m+1, n)$ to $(0, 0)$ traverses $\{m+1\} \times \mathbb{R}$ down to $(m+1, 0)$ and then $\mathbb{R} \times \{0\}$ back to $(0, 0)$, projecting to $\alpha^{-n} \beta^{-(m+1)}$.
    <2>5. Concatenating these segments yields the loop:
        $$g_{m, n} = \beta^m \alpha^n \beta \alpha^{-n} \beta^{-(m+1)}.$$

<1>4. Conclusion:
    A system of free generators for $q_*(\pi_1(E, e_0))$ is:
    $$\{ \beta^m \alpha^n \beta \alpha^{-n} \beta^{-(m+1)} \mid m \in \mathbb{Z}, \, n \in \mathbb{Z} \setminus \{0\} \}.$$
    Q.E.D.
:::
