::: {.exercise}

Let $F$ be a free group on two free generators $\alpha$ and $\beta$.
Let $H$ be the subgroup generated by $\alpha$.
Show that $H$ has infinite index in $F$.

:::

::: {.solution}

The index of a subgroup $H$ of $G$ is the cardinality of the set $G/H$ of right cosets when that set is finite (Definition, printed p. 514); the index is infinite exactly when there are infinitely many right cosets.

Every element of the free group $F=F(\alpha,\beta)$ has a unique expression as a reduced word in the letters $\alpha^{\pm1},\beta^{\pm1}$, and the elements of $H=\langle\alpha\rangle$ are exactly the words $\alpha^k$, $k\in\ZZ$ (printed pp. 413 and 418).

Consider the right cosets
$$
H\beta^n,\qquad n\in\ZZ .
$$
If
$$
H\beta^m=H\beta^n,
$$
then
$$
\beta^{m-n}\in H=\langle\alpha\rangle,
$$
so for some $k\in\ZZ$,
$$
\beta^{m-n}=\alpha^k .
$$
A nonzero power of $\beta$ is a reduced word involving the letter $\beta$ and not $\alpha$, and a nonzero power of $\alpha$ is a reduced word involving $\alpha$ and not $\beta$; by uniqueness of reduced words the two words are equal only when both are empty, that is, when
$$
m-n=0\quad\text{and}\quad k=0 .
$$
Thus the cosets $H\beta^n$ are pairwise distinct as $n$ varies, and there are infinitely many of them. Therefore
$$
\boxed{[F:H]=\infty.}
$$

:::
