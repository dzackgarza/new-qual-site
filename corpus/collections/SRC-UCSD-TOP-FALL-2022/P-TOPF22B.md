::: {.problem}
Let $X$ be the space obtained from a torus by attaching discs along the two curves shown.
Find the fundamental group of $X$, and identify the homology group $H_2(\widetilde{X})$ of the universal cover of $X$ as a module over the group ring $\mathbb{Z}[\pi_1(X)]$.
:::

::: {.solution}
<1>1. The two curves in the source diagram are essential simple closed curves on the torus meeting transversely in one point. Hence, after a self-homeomorphism of the torus, they may be taken to be the standard generators $a,b$ of
$$
\pi_1(T^2)=\langle a,b\mid [a,b]\rangle.
$$
::: {.proof}
The algebraic intersection of the two oriented curves is $\pm1$, so their homology classes form a basis of $H_1(T^2;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^2$. A basis change in $SL_2(\mathbb Z)$ is realized by a torus homeomorphism.
:::

<1>2. Attaching the two discs adds the relations $a=1$ and $b=1$, so
$$
\boxed{\pi_1(X)=1.}
$$
::: {.proof}
Van Kampen gives
$$
\pi_1(X)\cong\langle a,b\mid [a,b],a,b\rangle=1.
$$
:::

<1>3. Use the standard CW structure on the torus and the two new $2$-cells. Then
$$
0\to C_2(X)\cong\mathbb Z^3\xrightarrow{\partial_2}
C_1(X)\cong\mathbb Z^2\xrightarrow{0}\mathbb Z\to0,
$$
and in suitable bases
$$
\partial_2=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&1&0\\
0&0&1
\end{pmatrix}.
$$
::: {.proof}
The original torus $2$-cell has attaching word $[a,b]$, whose cellular boundary is zero after abelianization. The two added discs attach along $a$ and $b$, contributing the other two columns.
:::

<1>4. Therefore
$$
H_2(X;\mathbb Z)=\ker\partial_2\cong\mathbb Z.
$$
::: {.proof}
The displayed matrix has kernel generated by the original torus $2$-cell.
:::

<1>5. Since $\pi_1(X)=1$, the universal cover is $\widetilde X=X$, and the group ring is $\mathbb Z[\pi_1(X)]=\mathbb Z$. Hence
$$
\boxed{H_2(\widetilde X)\cong\mathbb Z}
$$
as a $\mathbb Z[\pi_1(X)]$-module.
::: {.proof}
Combine <1>2 and <1>4.
:::
:::
