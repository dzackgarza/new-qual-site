::: {.problem}
Let D be a subset of R, and let $f \colon D \to \mathbb { R }$ be a function. The graph of f is the subset

$$
G : = \left\{ ( x , y ) : x \in D , \ y = f ( x ) \right\}
$$

of $\mathbb { R } ^ { 2 }$ . Prove that if G is compact, then $f$ is continuous.
:::
