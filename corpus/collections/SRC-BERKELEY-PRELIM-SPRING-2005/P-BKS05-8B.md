Let $f : \mathbb { R } \to \mathbb { R }$ be differentiable on R. Suppose that $f ( 0 ) = 0$ , and that $| f ^ { \prime } ( x ) | \leq | f ( x ) |$ for all $x \in \mathbb { R }$ . Prove that $f ( x ) = 0$ for all $x \in \mathbb { R }$
