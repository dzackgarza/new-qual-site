::: problem
Let $f_n:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ be differentiable with $|f_n'(x)|\le1$ for all $n,x$. Assume $f_n(x)\to g(x)$ for every $x$. Prove that $g:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ is continuous.
:::
