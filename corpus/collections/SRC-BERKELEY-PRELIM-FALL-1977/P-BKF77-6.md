::: problem
Let $u:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R$ be defined by
\[
u(x,y)=x^3-3xy^2.
\]
Show that $u$ is harmonic and find $v:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R$ such that
\[
f(x+iy)=u(x,y)+iv(x,y)
\]
is analytic.
:::
