::: problem
Let $f:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R$ have continuous partial derivatives satisfying
\[
\left|\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_j}(x)\right|\le K
\]
for every $x$ and $j$. Prove that
\[
|f(x)-f(y)|\le\sqrt n\,K\|x-y\|.
\]
:::
