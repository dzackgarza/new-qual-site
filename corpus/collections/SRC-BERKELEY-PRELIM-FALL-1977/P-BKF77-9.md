::: problem
Let $f:[0,1]\to\mathbb R$ be continuously differentiable with $f(0)=0$. Prove that
\[
\sup_{0\le x\le1}|f(x)|\le\sqrt{\int_0^1(f'(x))^2\,dx}.
\]
:::
