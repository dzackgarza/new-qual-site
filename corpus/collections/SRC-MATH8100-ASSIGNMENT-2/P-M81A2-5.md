::: problem
Let $N\subseteq[0,1]$ be a Vitali nonmeasurable set.

1. Prove that each measurable subset of $N$ has measure zero.

2. Prove that $m^*([0,1]\setminus N)=1$.

3. Deduce that Lebesgue outer measure is not additive on all pairs of disjoint sets.
:::
