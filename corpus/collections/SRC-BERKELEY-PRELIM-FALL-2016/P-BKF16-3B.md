::: {.problem}
(a) Suppose that $I$ is a closed interval and $f:I\to I$ is smooth with $|f'|$ bounded by some number $r<1$ on $I$. Let $a_0\in I$ and put $a_{n+1}=f(a_n)$. Prove that the sequence $(a_n)$ tends to the unique root of $f(x)=x$ in $I$.

(b) Show that if $a_0$ is real and $a_{n+1}=\cos(a_n)$, then $a_n$ tends to a root of $\cos(x)=x$.
:::
