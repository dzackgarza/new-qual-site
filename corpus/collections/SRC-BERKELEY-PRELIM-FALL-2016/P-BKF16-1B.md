::: {.problem}
Let
\[
C=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-x^2}\,dx
\]
and let $S_n$ be the $(n-1)$-dimensional “surface area” of the unit sphere in $\mathbb R^n$ (so $S_2=2\pi$, $S_3=4\pi/3$).

(a) Prove that $C^n=S_n\Gamma(n/2)/2$, where
\[
\Gamma(s)=\int_0^\infty e^{-t}t^{s-1}\,dt.
\]
(Evaluate the integral of $e^{-(x_1^2+\cdots+x_n^2)}$ over $\mathbb R^n$ in rectangular and polar coordinates.)

(b) Show that $s\Gamma(s)=\Gamma(s+1)$ and $\Gamma(1)=1$.

(c) Evaluate $C$.
(Hint: $S_2=2\pi$.)

(d) Evaluate $S_4$.
:::
