::: {.problem}
Let $R\subseteq S$ be commutative domains with the same identity, and assume that $S$ is an integral extension of $R$.
Let $I$ be a nonzero ideal of $S$.
Prove that $I\cap R$ is a nonzero ideal of $R$.
:::
