::: {.problem}
Let D be a principal ideal domain.

(a) For $a , b \in D$ , define a least common multiple of a and b.

(b) Show that $d \in D$ is a least common multiple of a and b if and only if $( a ) \cap ( b ) = ( d )$
:::
