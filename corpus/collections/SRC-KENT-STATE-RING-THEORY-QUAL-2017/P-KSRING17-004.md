::: {.problem}
Denote the set of invertible elements of the ring $\ZZ_{n}$ by $U_{n}$.

(a) List all the elements of $U_{18}$.

(b) Is $U_{18}$ a cyclic group under multiplication? Justify your answer.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

The unit group is
$$
\boxed{
U_{18}
=
\{
\overline1,
\overline5,
\overline7,
\overline{11},
\overline{13},
\overline{17}
\}.
}
$$


::: pf-proof

A residue class
$$
\overline a\in\ZZ/18\ZZ
$$
is a unit if and only if
$$
\gcd(a,18)=1.
$$
Among the representatives
$$
0,1,\ldots,17,
$$
those relatively prime to
$$
18=2\cdot3^2
$$
are exactly
$$
1,5,7,11,13,17.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

The element
$$
\overline5\in U_{18}
$$
has order $6$.


::: pf-proof

Compute successive powers modulo $18$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
5^1&\equiv5,\\
5^2&\equiv25\equiv7,\\
5^3&\equiv35\equiv17,\\
5^4&\equiv85\equiv13,\\
5^5&\equiv65\equiv11,\\
5^6&\equiv55\equiv1
\pmod{18}.
\end{aligned}
$$
The first six powers are distinct, so the order of $\overline5$ is exactly $6$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

Therefore
$$
\boxed{
U_{18}
\text{ is cyclic, generated by }\overline5.
}
$$


::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref},
$$
\abs{U_{18}}=6.
$$
By step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref}, $\overline5$ has order $6$, so its cyclic subgroup has all six elements of $U_{18}$. Hence
$$
U_{18}
=
\langle\overline5\rangle.
$$

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} answers part (a), and step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} answers part (b).
