::: {.problem}
Let $R$ be a commutative ring with identity and define
$$
U_{2}(R) = \left\{ \begin{bmatrix} a & b \\ 0 & c \end{bmatrix} \st a, b, c \in R \right\}.
$$
Prove that every $R$-automorphism of $U_{2}(R)$ is inner.
:::

::: {.solution}

Let
$$
A
\coloneqq
U_2(R),
\qquad
e
\coloneqq
E_{11},
\qquad
n
\coloneqq
E_{12}.
$$
Every element of $A$ has a unique expression
$$
cI+(a-c)e+bn
$$
with $a,b,c\in R$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

The additive subgroup generated by commutators in $A$ is exactly
$$
Rn.
$$


::: pf-proof

Let
$$
X=
\begin{pmatrix}
a&b\\
0&c
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
Y=
\begin{pmatrix}
d&e\\
0&f
\end{pmatrix}.
$$
Because $R$ is commutative, the diagonal entries of
$$
[X,Y]
\coloneqq
XY-YX
$$
are zero. Hence every commutator lies in
$$
Rn.
$$

Conversely, for every $r\in R$,
$$
[e,rn]
=
e(rn)-(rn)e
=
rn-0
=
rn.
$$
Thus every element of $Rn$ is itself a commutator, proving the equality.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

If
$$
\varphi:A\to A
$$
is an $R$-algebra automorphism, then
$$
\varphi(Rn)=Rn.
$$


::: pf-proof

Ring automorphisms preserve commutators:
$$
\varphi([X,Y])
=
[\varphi(X),\varphi(Y)].
$$
Therefore they preserve the additive subgroup generated by commutators. By step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref}, that subgroup is exactly $Rn$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

There exists a unit
$$
u\in R^\times
$$
such that
$$
\varphi(n)=un.
$$


::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref},
$$
\varphi(n)\in Rn,
$$
so
$$
\varphi(n)=un
$$
for some $u\in R$.

Because $\varphi$ is an $R$-algebra automorphism, its restriction
$$
\varphi\restrict_{Rn}:Rn\to Rn
$$
is an $R$-module automorphism. Under the free rank-one identification
$$
Rn\cong R,
\qquad
rn\longleftrightarrow r,
$$
this restriction is multiplication by $u$. Such a map is bijective if and only if
$$
u\in R^\times.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

There exists
$$
b\in R
$$
such that
$$
\varphi(e)=e+bn.
$$


::: pf-proof

Write
$$
\varphi(e)
=
\begin{pmatrix}
\alpha&b\\
0&\gamma
\end{pmatrix}.
$$
The relations
$$
en=n,
\qquad
ne=0
$$
hold in $A$. Applying $\varphi$ and using step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} gives
$$
\varphi(e)\,un
=
un,
$$
and
$$
un\,\varphi(e)
=
0.
$$

The first equality is
$$
\alpha u\,n
=
u\,n.
$$
Since $u$ is a unit,
$$
\alpha=1.
$$
The second equality is
$$
u\gamma\,n
=
0,
$$
so again because $u$ is a unit,
$$
\gamma=0.
$$
Thus
$$
\varphi(e)
=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&b\\
0&0
\end{pmatrix}
=
e+bn.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-5}

Define
$$
g
\coloneqq
\begin{pmatrix}
u&-b\\
0&1
\end{pmatrix}.
$$
Then $g$ is a unit of $A$.


::: pf-proof

Since
$$
u\in R^\times,
$$
the upper triangular matrix $g$ has invertible diagonal entries. Explicitly,
$$
g^{-1}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
u^{-1}&u^{-1}b\\
0&1
\end{pmatrix}.
$$
Direct multiplication verifies
$$
gg^{-1}
=
g^{-1}g
=
I.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-6}

Conjugation by $g$ sends
$$
e\longmapsto e+bn
$$
and
$$
n\longmapsto un.
$$


::: pf-proof

Using the inverse from step [](#s1-5){.pf-ref},
$$
\begin{aligned}
geg^{-1}
&=
\begin{pmatrix}
u&-b\\
0&1
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}
1&0\\
0&0
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}
u^{-1}&u^{-1}b\\
0&1
\end{pmatrix}\\
&=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&b\\
0&0
\end{pmatrix}\\
&=
e+bn.
\end{aligned}
$$
Likewise,
$$
\begin{aligned}
gng^{-1}
&=
\begin{pmatrix}
u&-b\\
0&1
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}
0&1\\
0&0
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}
u^{-1}&u^{-1}b\\
0&1
\end{pmatrix}\\
&=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&u\\
0&0
\end{pmatrix}\\
&=
un.
\end{aligned}
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-7}

The automorphisms
$$
\varphi
$$
and
$$
\operatorname{Ad}_g:
X\longmapsto gXg^{-1}
$$
agree on all of $A$.


::: pf-proof

Because $\varphi$ is an $R$-algebra automorphism, it fixes every scalar matrix:
$$
\varphi(rI)=rI
$$
for all $r\in R$.
Conjugation by $g$ also fixes scalar matrices because they are central.

By steps [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} and [](#s1-6){.pf-ref}, the two maps agree on $e$, and by steps [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} and [](#s1-6){.pf-ref} they agree on $n$.

Every element of $A$ has the form
$$
cI+(a-c)e+bn.
$$
Since both maps are $R$-linear, agreement on
$$
I,\ e,\ n
$$
implies agreement on every element of $A$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-8}

Therefore every $R$-automorphism of $U_2(R)$ is inner:
$$
\boxed{
\varphi(X)
=
gXg^{-1}
\quad
\text{for some }g\in U_2(R)^\times.
}
$$


::: pf-proof

Step [](#s1-7){.pf-ref} proves
$$
\varphi=\operatorname{Ad}_g
$$
for the unit $g$ constructed in step [](#s1-5){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-8){.pf-ref} is the required conclusion.

:::
