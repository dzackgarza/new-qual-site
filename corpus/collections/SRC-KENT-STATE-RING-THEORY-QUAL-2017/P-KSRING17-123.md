::: {.problem}
Let D be an integral domain and F its field of fractions.
Let P be a prime ideal in D and $D _ { P } = \{ a b ^ { - 1 } \mid a , b \in D , \ b \notin P \} \subseteq F$ . Show that $D _ { P }$ has a unique maximal ideal.
:::
