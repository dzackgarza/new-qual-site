::: {.problem}
Let $V$ be the complex vector space of polynomials of degree at most $3$.

(a) Show that
\[
\langle f,g\rangle=\int_{-1}^1 f(t)\overline{g(t)}\,dt
\]
defines an inner product on $V$.

(b) Find an orthonormal basis of $V$.
:::
