::: {.problem}
Solve
\[
x+2y+3z+3u+2v+w=3,
\]
\[
y+2z+2u+v=2,
\]
\[
z+u=1.
\]

(a) Write the system as $A\vec x=\vec b$.

(b) Row reduce $[A\mid b]$.

(c) Write the general solution.
:::

::: {.solution}

Let
\[
\vec x=(x,y,z,u,v,w)^T.
\]

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

The system has matrix form $A\vec x=\vec b$ with
\[
\boxed{
A=\begin{pmatrix}
1&2&3&3&2&1\\
0&1&2&2&1&0\\
0&0&1&1&0&0
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\vec b=\begin{pmatrix}3\\2\\1\end{pmatrix}}.
\]


::: pf-proof

The coefficients in the three rows of $A$ are exactly the coefficients of $x,y,z,u,v,w$ in the three displayed equations, and the right-hand sides are $3,2,1$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

The reduced row-echelon form of the augmented matrix is
\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{rref}[A\mid\vec b]
=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&0&0&0&0&1&\mid&0\\
0&1&0&0&1&0&\mid&0\\
0&0&1&1&0&0&\mid&1
\end{pmatrix}}.
\]


::: pf-proof

Starting from
\[
\begin{pmatrix}
1&2&3&3&2&1&\mid&3\\
0&1&2&2&1&0&\mid&2\\
0&0&1&1&0&0&\mid&1
\end{pmatrix},
\]
apply
\[
R_2\leftarrow R_2-2R_3,
\qquad
R_1\leftarrow R_1-3R_3,
\qquad
R_1\leftarrow R_1-2R_2.
\]
The resulting matrix is the displayed reduced row-echelon form.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

With free parameters $a,b,c\in\RR$ assigned to $u,v,w$, respectively, the general solution is
\[
\boxed{
\begin{pmatrix}x\\y\\z\\u\\v\\w\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}0\\0\\1\\0\\0\\0\end{pmatrix}
+a\begin{pmatrix}0\\0\\-1\\1\\0\\0\end{pmatrix}
+b\begin{pmatrix}0\\-1\\0\\0\\1\\0\end{pmatrix}
+c\begin{pmatrix}-1\\0\\0\\0\\0\\1\end{pmatrix}}.
\]


::: pf-proof

Step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} gives
\[
x+w=0,
\qquad
y+v=0,
\qquad
z+u=1.
\]
Setting $u=a$, $v=b$, and $w=c$ yields $x=-c$, $y=-b$, and $z=1-a$, which is exactly the displayed parametrization.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Steps [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} and [](#s1-2){.pf-ref}, and [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} prove parts (a), (b), and (c), respectively.

:::
