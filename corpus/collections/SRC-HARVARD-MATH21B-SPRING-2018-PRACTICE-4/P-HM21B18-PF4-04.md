::: {.problem}
Solve
\[
x+2y+3z+3u+2v+w=3,
\]
\[
y+2z+2u+v=2,
\]
\[
z+u=1.
\]

(a) Write the system as $A\vec x=\vec b$.

(b) Row reduce $[A\mid b]$.

(c) Write the general solution.
:::
