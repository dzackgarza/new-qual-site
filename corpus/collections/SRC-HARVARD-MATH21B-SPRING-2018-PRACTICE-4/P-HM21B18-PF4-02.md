::: {.problem}
(a) Among the five matrices
\[
A=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&-1\end{pmatrix},
B=\begin{pmatrix}-1&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix},
C=\begin{pmatrix}2&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix},
D=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix},
E=\begin{pmatrix}-1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix},
\]
connect pairs that are similar and have the same eigenvalues.

(b) Match the four initial-value problems
\[
f''+f=2\sin t,
\quad
f''+f=6\sin(2t),
\quad
f''-f=2\sin t,
\quad
f''-f=6\sin(2t),
\]
all with $f(0)=1$, $f'(0)=0$, to the four candidate solutions displayed in the source.
