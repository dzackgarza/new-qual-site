::: {.problem}
(a) Find all eigenvalues and eigenvectors of
\[
A=\begin{pmatrix}
3&9&9&9\\
9&3&9&9\\
9&9&3&9\\
9&9&9&3
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

(b) Find all eigenvalues and eigenvectors of
\[
B=\begin{pmatrix}
6&1&2&0&0\\
0&6&1&2&0\\
0&0&6&1&2\\
2&0&0&6&1\\
1&2&0&0&6
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Hint: write $B=6I_5+C+2C^2$ for the cyclic shift matrix $C$.
:::
