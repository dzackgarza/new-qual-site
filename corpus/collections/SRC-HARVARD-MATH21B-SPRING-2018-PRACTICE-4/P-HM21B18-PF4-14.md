::: {.problem}
Let $Q$ be the $40\times40$ cyclic-shift permutation matrix and set
$$
L=Q+Q^T+3I_{40}.
$$
Find $\det L$.
:::

::: {.solution}

Let
$$
\zeta=e^{2\pi i/40},
\qquad
v_k=(1,\zeta^k,\zeta^{2k},\ldots,\zeta^{39k})^T
\quad(0\le k\le39).
$$

::: pf

::: pf-step

The eigenvalues of $L$ are
$$
\mu_k=3+\zeta^k+\zeta^{-k}
=3+2\cos\frac{2\pi k}{40},
\qquad 0\le k\le39.
$$


::: pf-proof

For the cyclic shift $Q$, one has $Qv_k=\zeta^kv_k$. Since $Q^T=Q^{-1}$,
$$
Q^Tv_k=\zeta^{-k}v_k.
$$
Therefore
$$
Lv_k=(3+\zeta^k+\zeta^{-k})v_k.
$$
The $40$ vectors $v_k$ form the Fourier basis of $\CC^{40}$, so these are all eigenvalues of $L$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

If $r,s$ are the roots of
$$
z^2+3z+1=0,
$$
then
$$
\det L=r^{40}+s^{40}-2.
$$


::: pf-proof

For every $40$th root of unity $\omega$,
$$
3+\omega+\omega^{-1}
=\omega^{-1}(\omega^2+3\omega+1)
=\omega^{-1}(\omega-r)(\omega-s).
$$
Taking the product over all $40$th roots of unity gives
$$
\det L
=\left(\prod_{\omega^{40}=1}\omega^{-1}\right)
\left(\prod_{\omega^{40}=1}(\omega-r)\right)
\left(\prod_{\omega^{40}=1}(\omega-s)\right).
$$
The product of the roots of $z^{40}-1$ is $-1$, hence the first factor is $-1$. Since $40$ is even,
$$
\prod_{\omega^{40}=1}(\omega-r)=r^{40}-1,
\qquad
\prod_{\omega^{40}=1}(\omega-s)=s^{40}-1.
$$
Also $rs=1$. Consequently
$$
\det L
=-(r^{40}-1)(s^{40}-1)
=r^{40}+s^{40}-2.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

Let $S_n=r^n+s^n$. Then
$$
S_0=2,
\qquad
S_1=-3,
\qquad
S_n=-3S_{n-1}-S_{n-2}.
$$
Moreover,
$$
S_{2n}=S_n^2-2.
$$


::: pf-proof

Because $r+s=-3$ and $rs=1$,
$$
r^n+s^n=(r+s)(r^{n-1}+s^{n-1})-rs(r^{n-2}+s^{n-2}),
$$
which gives the recurrence. Also
$$
S_n^2=r^{2n}+s^{2n}+2(rs)^n=S_{2n}+2,
$$
which gives the doubling identity.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

One has
$$
S_5=-123,
\qquad
S_{10}=15127,
\qquad
S_{20}=228826127,
\qquad
S_{40}=52361396397820127.
$$


::: pf-proof

The recurrence in step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} gives
$$
S_2=7,
\qquad S_3=-18,
\qquad S_4=47,
\qquad S_5=-123.
$$
Applying the doubling identity three times gives
$$
S_{10}=(-123)^2-2=15127,
$$
$$
S_{20}=15127^2-2=228826127,
$$
and
$$
S_{40}=228826127^2-2=52361396397820127.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-5}

Therefore
$$
\boxed{\det L=52361396397820125}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Substitute the value of $S_{40}$ from step [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} into the identity $\det L=S_{40}-2$ from step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref}.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-5){.pf-ref} is the requested determinant.

:::
