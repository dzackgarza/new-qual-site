::: {.problem}
(a) Find the Fourier series on $[-\pi,\pi]$ of
$$
f(x)=|x|-\frac\pi2.
$$

(b) Use Parseval's theorem, without evaluating the integral directly, to compute
$$
\sqrt{\frac1\pi\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}
\left(3\sin(5x)+4\cos(12x)+3\sin x+4\cos(7x)+5\cos x+5\sin(2x)\right)^2dx}.
$$
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

The constant Fourier coefficient and all sine coefficients of $f$ vanish.


::: pf-proof

The function $f(x)=|x|-\pi/2$ is even, so every sine coefficient is zero. Also
$$
\frac1\pi\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}f(x)\,dx
=\frac2\pi\int_0^\pi\left(x-\frac\pi2\right)dx=0,
$$
so the constant coefficient vanishes as well.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

For $n\ge1$, the cosine coefficient is
$$
a_n=\frac{2}{\pi n^2}\bigl((-1)^n-1\bigr)
=
\begin{cases}
-\dfrac4{\pi n^2},&n\text{ odd},\\[4pt]
0,&n\text{ even}.
\end{cases}
$$


::: pf-proof

By evenness,
$$
a_n=\frac2\pi\int_0^\pi\left(x-\frac\pi2\right)\cos(nx)\,dx.
$$
The constant term contributes zero because $\int_0^\pi\cos(nx)\,dx=0$. Integration by parts gives
$$
\int_0^\pi x\cos(nx)\,dx
=\left[\frac{x\sin(nx)}n+\frac{\cos(nx)}{n^2}\right]_0^\pi
=\frac{(-1)^n-1}{n^2},
$$
which yields the stated coefficient.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

The Fourier series in part (a) is
$$
\boxed{
|x|-\frac\pi2
=-\frac4\pi\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}
\frac{\cos((2k+1)x)}{(2k+1)^2}}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Substitute the coefficients from steps [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} through [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} into the Fourier expansion. The periodic extension of $f$ is continuous, so the Fourier series converges to $f$ at every $x\in[-\pi,\pi]$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

The value in part (b) is
$$
\boxed{10}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Under the inner product
$$
\langle g,h\rangle=\frac1\pi\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}g(x)h(x)\,dx,
$$
the functions $\sin(nx)$ and $\cos(nx)$ for positive integers $n$ form an orthonormal family. The six modes appearing in the given trigonometric polynomial are distinct. Parseval therefore gives its squared norm as
$$
3^2+4^2+3^2+4^2+5^2+5^2=100.
$$
Taking the nonnegative square root gives $10$.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} proves part (a), and step [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} proves part (b).
