::: {.problem}
Let
$$
\binom{p(t+1)}{a(t+1)}
=
\begin{pmatrix}0.1&0.8\\0.9&0.2\end{pmatrix}
\binom{p(t)}{a(t)},
\qquad
\binom{p(0)}{a(0)}=\binom{100}{0}.
$$

(a) Find the eigenvalues and eigenvectors.

(b) Find a closed-form solution.

(c) Normalize an eigenvector for the largest eigenvalue so its entries sum to $100$ and interpret the limiting distribution.
:::

::: {.solution}

Let
$$
M=\begin{pmatrix}0.1&0.8\\0.9&0.2\end{pmatrix}.
$$

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1-1}

The complete eigenstructure of $M$ is
$$
\boxed{
E_1=\operatorname{span}\left\{\binom89\right\},
\qquad
E_{-0.7}=\operatorname{span}\left\{\binom1{-1}\right\}}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Since the columns of $M$ each sum to $1$, $1$ is an eigenvalue. The trace is $0.3$, so the other eigenvalue is $0.3-1=-0.7$. Direct substitution gives
$$
M\binom89=\binom89,
\qquad
M\binom1{-1}=-0.7\binom1{-1}.
$$
The two eigenvalues are distinct, so these one-dimensional spaces are the complete eigenspaces.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-2}

The initial vector decomposes as
$$
\binom{100}{0}
=\frac{100}{17}\binom89
+\frac{900}{17}\binom1{-1}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Writing
$$
\binom{100}{0}=\alpha\binom89+\beta\binom1{-1}
$$
gives $8\alpha+\beta=100$ and $9\alpha-\beta=0$. Adding yields $17\alpha=100$, hence $\alpha=100/17$ and $\beta=900/17$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-3}

The closed-form solution is
$$
\boxed{
\binom{p(t)}{a(t)}
=\frac{100}{17}\binom89
+\frac{900}{17}(-0.7)^t\binom1{-1}}.
$$


::: pf-proof

By step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref}, the two summands in step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} evolve by factors $1^t$ and $(-0.7)^t$, respectively. Applying $M^t$ to the decomposition in step [](#s1-2){.pf-ref} gives the displayed formula.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s1-4}

The normalized equilibrium distribution is
$$
\boxed{\binom{800/17}{900/17}},
$$
so asymptotically about $47.06\%$ of the bacteria are phosphorus bacteria and $52.94\%$ are arsenic bacteria.


::: pf-proof

The eigenvector $(8,9)^T$ for eigenvalue $1$ has coordinate sum $17$, so multiplying it by $100/17$ gives the displayed vector with total $100$. Also $|-0.7|<1$, so the second term in step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} tends to zero as $t\to\infty$. Thus the solution converges to this normalized eigenvector.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step [](#s1-1){.pf-ref} proves part (a), step [](#s1-3){.pf-ref} proves part (b), and step [](#s1-4){.pf-ref} proves part (c).

:::
