::: {.problem}
Let $L$ be a finite Galois extension of $F$.
Let $H_1$ and $H_2$ be subgroups of $G = \operatorname{Gal}(L/F)$ and consider their fixed fields $E_1 = L^{H_1}$ and $E_2 = L^{H_2}$ respectively.
Show that $\operatorname{Gal}(L/E_1 \cap E_2)$ is the subgroup of $G$ generated by $H_1$ and $H_2$.
:::

Let
$$
H=\langle H_1,H_2\rangle
$$
be the subgroup of $G$ generated by $H_1$ and $H_2$.

::: pf

::: pf-step

One has
$$
L^H\subseteq E_1\cap E_2.
$$


::: pf-proof

Since
$$
H_1\subseteq H
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
H_2\subseteq H,
$$
every element fixed by all of $H$ is fixed by all of $H_1$ and all of $H_2$. Hence
$$
L^H\subseteq L^{H_1}\cap L^{H_2}=E_1\cap E_2.
$$

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Conversely,
$$
E_1\cap E_2\subseteq L^H.
$$


::: pf-proof

Let
$$
x\in E_1\cap E_2.
$$
Then every element of $H_1$ fixes $x$, and every element of $H_2$ fixes $x$. Every element of the generated subgroup
$$
H=\langle H_1,H_2\rangle
$$
is a finite product of elements from $H_1\cup H_2$ and their inverses. Since each such factor fixes $x$, every element of $H$ fixes $x$. Thus $x\in L^H$.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Therefore
$$
\boxed{L^H=E_1\cap E_2}.
$$


::: pf-proof

Combine steps <1>1 and <1>2.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

By the fundamental theorem of Galois theory,
$$
\operatorname{Gal}(L/L^H)=H.
$$


::: pf-proof

For the finite Galois extension $L/F$, the subgroup fixing the fixed field $L^H$ pointwise is exactly $H$.

:::

:::

::: pf-step

Hence
$$
\boxed{
\operatorname{Gal}(L/E_1\cap E_2)
=\langle H_1,H_2\rangle.
}
$$


::: pf-proof

Substitute the fixed-field identity from step <1>3 into step <1>4.

:::

:::

::: pf-qed

Step <1>5 is the required equality.

:::

:::
