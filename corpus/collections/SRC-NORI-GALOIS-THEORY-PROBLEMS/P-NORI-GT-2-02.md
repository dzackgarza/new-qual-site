Char.F is $\neq 2$ Let $a _ { 1 } , a _ { 2 } , . . . , a _ { r }$ be nonzero elements of F and let G be the subgroup generated by their images in ${ F ^ { \times } } / ( { F ^ { \times } } ) ^ { 2 }$ .
