::: problem
Show that a closed, compact, simply-connected $3$-manifold $M^3$ is homotopy equivalent to $S^3$.
:::

::: solution
**Theorem.**  
Every closed, compact, simply-connected $3$-manifold is homotopy equivalent to $S^3$.

*Proof.*

1. The statement is the Poincaré conjecture in dimension three.
2. Perelman proved that a closed, compact, simply-connected 3-manifold is homeomorphic to $S^3$.
3. Any homeomorphism induces a homotopy equivalence.

Therefore $M^3$ is homotopy equivalent to $S^3$.
:::
