::: {.problem}
Suppose $f$ is defined on $[-1,1]$ and $f'''$ is continuous. Show that
\[
\sum_{n=1}^\infty
\left(
n\left(f\left(\frac1n\right)-f\left(-\frac1n\right)\right)-2f'(0)
\right)
\]
converges.
:::
