::: {.problem}
Let $V$ be the vector space of polynomials of degree at most $10$, and let $D:V\to V$ be differentiation, $Dp=p'$.

1. Show that $\operatorname{tr}D=0$.
2. Find all eigenvectors of $D$ and of $e^D$.
:::
