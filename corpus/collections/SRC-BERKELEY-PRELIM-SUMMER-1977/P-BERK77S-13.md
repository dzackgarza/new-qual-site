:::{.problem}
Let $f$ be analytic and suppose
\[
f(z)=1+2z+3z^2+\cdots
\qquad(|z|<1).
\]
Define real numbers $a_0,a_1,a_2,\dots$ by
\[
f(z)=\sum_{n=0}^\infty a_n(z+2)^n.
\]
What is the radius of convergence of
\[
\sum_{n=0}^\infty a_nz^n?
\]
:::
