::: {.problem}
Let $A$ be an $r\times r$ real matrix. Prove that
\[
e^A=I+A+\frac{A^2}{2!}+\cdots+\frac{A^n}{n!}+\cdots
\]
converges entrywise, and hence defines a well-defined matrix $e^A$.
:::
