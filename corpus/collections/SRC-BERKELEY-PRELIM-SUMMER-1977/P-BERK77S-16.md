::: {.problem}
Use the residue theorem to evaluate
\[
I(a)=\int_0^{2\pi}\frac{d\theta}{a+\cos\theta},
\]
where $a$ is real and $a>1$.

Why is the resulting formula for $I(a)$ also valid for certain nonreal complex values of $a$?
