::: {.problem}
Show that every rotation of $\mathbb R^3$ has an axis. More precisely, let $A$ be a real $3\times3$ matrix such that
\[
A^T=A^{-1},
\qquad \det A>0.
\]
Prove that there is a nonzero vector $v$ such that $Av=v$.
:::
