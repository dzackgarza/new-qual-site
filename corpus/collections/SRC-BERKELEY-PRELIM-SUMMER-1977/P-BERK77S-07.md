::: {.problem}
Let $A:\mathbb R^6\to\mathbb R^6$ be linear and suppose
\[
A^{26}=I.
\]
Show that
\[
\mathbb R^6=V_1\oplus V_2\oplus V_3,
\]
where $V_1,V_2,V_3$ are two-dimensional $A$-invariant subspaces.
:::
