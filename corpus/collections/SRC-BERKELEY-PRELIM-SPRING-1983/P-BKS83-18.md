::: {.problem}
Let $F=\mathbb F_7$. How many $3\times3$ matrices over $F$ have determinant $2$? How many have determinant $3$?
:::

::: {.solution}
::: pf

::: {.pf-step #gl3-order}
The number of invertible $3\times3$ matrices over $\FF_7$ is
$$
\abs{\operatorname{GL}_3(\FF_7)}
=
(7^3-1)(7^3-7)(7^3-7^2)
=
33\,784\,128.
$$

::: pf-proof
An invertible matrix is determined by an ordered basis of $\FF_7^3$.
There are
$$
7^3-1
$$
choices for the first column. After it is chosen, its span has $7$
elements, leaving
$$
7^3-7
$$
choices for the second column. The first two columns then span a
$2$-dimensional subspace with $7^2$ elements, leaving
$$
7^3-7^2
$$
choices for the third column. Multiplying gives the formula above.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #fiber-size}
For each $a\in\FF_7^\times$, the number of matrices with determinant
$a$ is
$$
\frac{\abs{\operatorname{GL}_3(\FF_7)}}6.
$$

::: pf-proof
The determinant is a surjective group homomorphism
$$
\det:\operatorname{GL}_3(\FF_7)\longrightarrow\FF_7^\times.
$$
It is surjective because
$$
\det\!\begin{pmatrix}
a&0&0\\
0&1&0\\
0&0&1
\end{pmatrix}
=a
$$
for every $a\in\FF_7^\times$. Its kernel is
$\operatorname{SL}_3(\FF_7)$, so every fiber is a coset of the kernel and
has the same cardinality. Since
$$
\abs{\FF_7^\times}=6,
$$
each fiber has one sixth of the elements of
$\operatorname{GL}_3(\FF_7)$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #count-boxed}
The two requested numbers are both
$$
\boxed{5\,630\,688}.
$$

::: pf-proof
Both $2$ and $3$ are nonzero in $\FF_7$, so step [](#fiber-size){.pf-ref} applies to each.
Using step [](#gl3-order){.pf-ref},
$$
\frac{33\,784\,128}{6}
=
5\,630\,688.
$$
Matrices with determinant $2$ and matrices with determinant $3$ therefore
have the same displayed cardinality.
:::

:::

::: pf-qed
Step [](#count-boxed){.pf-ref} gives both requested counts.
:::
