::: {.problem}
A fractional linear transformation maps the annulus
\[
r<|z|<1,
\qquad r>0,
\]
onto the domain bounded by the circles
\[
\left|z-\frac14\right|=\frac14
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
|z|=1.
\]
Find $r$.
:::
