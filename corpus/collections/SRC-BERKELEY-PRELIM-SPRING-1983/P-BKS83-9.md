:::{.problem}
Let $F:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^n$ be continuous and suppose there is $\lambda>0$ such that
\[
\|F(x)-F(y)\|\ge\lambda\|x-y\|
\]
for all $x,y\in\mathbb R^n$. Prove that $F$ is one-to-one, onto, and has a continuous inverse.
:::
