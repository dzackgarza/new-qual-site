::: {.problem}
Let $V$ be a real vector space of dimension $n$, and let
\[
S:V\times V\to\mathbb R
\]
be a nondegenerate bilinear form. Suppose $W\subset V$ is a subspace such that
\[
S|_{W\times W}=0.
\]
Prove that
\[
\dim W\le\frac n2.
\]
:::
