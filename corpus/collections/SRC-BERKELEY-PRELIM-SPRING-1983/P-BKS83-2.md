::: {.problem}
Let $A=(a_{ij})$ be a real $n\times n$ matrix satisfying
\[
a_{ii}>0,
\qquad
a_{ij}\le0\quad(i\ne j),
\]
and
\[
\sum_{i=1}^n a_{ij}>0
\qquad(1\le j\le n).
\]
Prove that
\[
\det A>0.
\]
:::
