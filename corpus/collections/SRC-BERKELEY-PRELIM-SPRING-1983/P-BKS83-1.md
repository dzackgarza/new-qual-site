:::{.problem}
Let $f:(0,\infty)\to(0,\infty)$ be monotone decreasing and suppose
\[
\int_0^\infty f(x)\,dx<\infty.
\]
Prove that
\[
\lim_{x\to\infty}x f(x)=0.
\]
:::
