::: {.problem}
Let $G$ be an abelian group generated by at most $n$ elements. Prove that every subgroup of $G$ is generated by at most $n$ elements.
:::

::: {.solution}
::: pf

::: {.pf-step #subgroups-of-zn}
Every subgroup $K\le\ZZ^n$ is generated by at most $n$ elements.

::: pf-proof
The proof is by induction on $n$. For $n=0$ the assertion is immediate.
Assume it for $n-1$, and let
$$
\pi:\ZZ^n\longrightarrow\ZZ
$$
be projection onto the first coordinate.

If $\pi(K)=0$, then
$$
K\le\ker\pi\cong\ZZ^{n-1},
$$
so the induction hypothesis gives at most $n-1$ generators.

Suppose instead that $\pi(K)\ne0$. Every nonzero subgroup of $\ZZ$ has the
form $d\ZZ$ for a unique integer $d>0$. Choose $v\in K$ with
$$
\pi(v)=d.
$$
The subgroup
$$
K_0\coloneqq K\cap\ker\pi
$$
lies in $\ker\pi\cong\ZZ^{n-1}$, so by induction it is generated by at most
$n-1$ elements, say $w_1,\ldots,w_r$.

For any $x\in K$, one has $\pi(x)\in d\ZZ$, so
$$
\pi(x)=md
$$
for some $m\in\ZZ$. Hence
$$
\pi(x-mv)=0,
$$
and therefore $x-mv\in K_0$. Thus
$$
x\in\langle v,w_1,\ldots,w_r\rangle.
$$
So $K$ is generated by at most $1+(n-1)=n$ elements.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #lift-to-zn}
Let $H\le G$. There is a subgroup $K\le\ZZ^n$ and a surjective
homomorphism
$$
K\twoheadrightarrow H.
$$

::: pf-proof
Choose generators $g_1,\ldots,g_n$ for $G$, padding a shorter generating
set with zero elements if necessary. Define
$$
\varphi:\ZZ^n\longrightarrow G,
\qquad
\varphi(a_1,\ldots,a_n)
=
a_1g_1+\cdots+a_ng_n.
$$
This map is surjective. Set
$$
K\coloneqq\varphi^{-1}(H).
$$
Then $K$ is a subgroup of $\ZZ^n$, and the restriction
$$
\varphi|_K:K\longrightarrow H
$$
is surjective because every $h\in H$ has some preimage under $\varphi$,
and every such preimage belongs to $K$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #h-is-n-generated}
The subgroup $H$ is generated by at most $n$ elements.

::: pf-proof
By step [](#subgroups-of-zn){.pf-ref}, the subgroup $K$ from step [](#lift-to-zn){.pf-ref} has generators
$$
k_1,\ldots,k_r
\qquad
(r\le n).
$$
Since $\varphi|_K$ is surjective, every element of $H$ is the image of an
integer linear combination of the $k_i$. Therefore
$$
H
=
\langle\varphi(k_1),\ldots,\varphi(k_r)\rangle,
$$
so $H$ has at most $r\le n$ generators.
:::

:::

::: pf-qed
Step [](#h-is-n-generated){.pf-ref} applies to every subgroup $H\le G$.
:::
