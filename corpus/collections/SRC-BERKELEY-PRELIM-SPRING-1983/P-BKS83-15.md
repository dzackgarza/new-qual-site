::: {.problem}
Let $f,g$ be complex polynomials and suppose
\[
\deg g\ge\deg f+2.
\]
Show that there is $r>0$ such that
\[
\int_C\frac{f(z)}{g(z)}\,dz=0
\]
for every simple closed curve $C$ disjoint from the closed disk $|z|\le r$.
:::
