:::{.problem}
Let $M$ be an invertible real $n\times n$ matrix. Show that there is a decomposition
\[
M=UT,
\]
where $U$ is orthogonal and $T$ is upper triangular with positive diagonal entries. Is this decomposition unique?
:::
