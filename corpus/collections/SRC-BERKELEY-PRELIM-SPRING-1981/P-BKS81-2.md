:::{.problem}
Let $T:V\to V$ be linear. Suppose $x\in V$ satisfies
\[
T^m x=0,
\qquad
T^{m-1}x\ne0
\]
for some positive integer $m$.
Show that
\[
x,Tx,\ldots,T^{m-1}x
\]
are linearly independent.
:::
