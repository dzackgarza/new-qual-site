:::{.problem}
Let $G$ be a finite group. For $a\in G$, let
\[
C(a)=\{bab^{-1}:b\in G\}
\]
be its conjugacy class.

1. Prove that $|C(a)|$ divides $|G|$.
2. Must all conjugacy classes have the same number of elements?
3. If $G$ has only two conjugacy classes, prove that $|G|=2$.
:::
