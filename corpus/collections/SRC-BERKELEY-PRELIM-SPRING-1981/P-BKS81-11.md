::: {.problem}
Evaluate
\[
\int_C\frac{e^z-1}{z^2(z-1)}\,dz,
\]
where $C$ is the closed curve shown in the source exam.

The retained extraction does not contain the contour figure.
:::

::: {.solution}
The retained source PDF itself does not contain the curve $C$: in the place
where the figure should occur, it prints
`../Fig/Pr/Sp81-11.ps not found`. Thus the numerical value cannot be
determined from the retained source. The dependence on the missing curve can
nevertheless be computed exactly.

<1>1. For
$$
F(z)\coloneqq\frac{e^z-1}{z^2(z-1)},
$$
the only singularities are simple poles at $0$ and $1$, with
$$
\operatorname{Res}_{z=0}F=-1
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\operatorname{Res}_{z=1}F=e-1.
$$

::: {.proof}
At $z=0$,
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Res}_{z=0}F
&=
\lim_{z\to0}
\frac{e^z-1}{z(z-1)}\\
&=
\left(\lim_{z\to0}\frac{e^z-1}{z}\right)
\left(\lim_{z\to0}\frac1{z-1}\right)\\
&=
1\cdot(-1)
=
-1.
\end{aligned}
$$
At $z=1$,
$$
\operatorname{Res}_{z=1}F
=
\lim_{z\to1}\frac{e^z-1}{z^2}
=
e-1.
$$
:::

<1>2. For any closed piecewise smooth curve $C$ avoiding $0$ and $1$,
$$
\boxed{
\int_C\frac{e^z-1}{z^2(z-1)}\,dz
=
2\pi i
\left(
-\operatorname{Ind}_C(0)
+(e-1)\operatorname{Ind}_C(1)
\right)
}.
$$

::: {.proof}
This is the residue theorem in winding-number form, using the two residues
from step <1>1.
:::

<1>3. No unique numerical evaluation is determined by the retained source.

::: {.proof}
The formula in step <1>2 depends on
$$
\operatorname{Ind}_C(0)
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\operatorname{Ind}_C(1).
$$
Those integers are exactly the geometric data supplied by the missing
contour figure. The retained PDF supplies neither, so distinct possible
curves give distinct integral values.
:::

<1>4. Q.E.D.

::: {.proof}
Step <1>2 gives the complete evaluation from the available data, and step
<1>3 identifies why it cannot be specialized to a number without the
missing figure.
:::
:::
