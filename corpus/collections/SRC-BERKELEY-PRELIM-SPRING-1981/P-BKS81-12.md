:::{.problem}
For $x\in\mathbb R$, let
\[
A_x=\begin{pmatrix}
x&1&1&1\\
1&x&1&1\\
1&1&x&1\\
1&1&1&x
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

1. Prove that
   \[
   \det A_x=(x-1)^3(x+3).
   \]
2. Prove that if $x\ne1,-3$, then
   \[
   A_x^{-1}=-\frac{1}{(x-1)(x+3)}A_{-x-2}.
   \]
:::
