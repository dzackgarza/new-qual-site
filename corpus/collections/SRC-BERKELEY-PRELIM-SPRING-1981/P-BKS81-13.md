:::{.problem}
Determine which of the following series converge:

1. \[
   \sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{(2n)!(3n)!}{n!(4n)!}.
   \]
2. \[
   \sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{n^{1+1/n}}.
   \]
:::
