::: {.problem}
Let $M$ be one of
\[
\mathbb R,\qquad \mathbb C,\qquad \mathbb Q,\qquad \mathbb F_9,
\]
and let $I\subset M[x]$ be the ideal generated by
\[
x^4+2x-2.
\]
For which choices of $M$ is the quotient ring $M[x]/I$ a field?
:::

::: {.solution}
Put
$$
p(x)\coloneqq x^4+2x-2.
$$
For every field $M$, the quotient
$$
M[x]/(p)
$$
is a field exactly when $p$ is irreducible in $M[x]$.

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #reducible-over-r}
Over $\RR$, the polynomial $p$ is reducible.

::: pf-proof
One has
$$
p(0)=-2<0
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
p(1)=1>0.
$$
By the intermediate value theorem, $p$ has a real root in $(0,1)$.
Therefore it has a linear factor over $\RR$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #reducible-over-c}
Over $\CC$, the polynomial $p$ is reducible.

::: pf-proof
By the fundamental theorem of algebra, every complex polynomial of degree
greater than $1$ has a root in $\CC$, hence a nontrivial linear factor.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #irreducible-over-q}
Over $\QQ$, the polynomial $p$ is irreducible.

::: pf-proof
Eisenstein's criterion applies at the prime $2$: the coefficients of
$x^3,x^2,x$, and the constant term are all divisible by $2$, the leading
coefficient is not divisible by $2$, and the constant term $-2$ is not
divisible by $4$. Hence $p$ is irreducible in $\QQ[x]$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #reducible-over-f9}
Over $\mathbb F_9$, the polynomial $p$ is reducible.

::: pf-proof
The prime field $\mathbb F_3$ is contained in $\mathbb F_9$. In
characteristic $3$,
$$
p(-1)
=
(-1)^4+2(-1)-2
=
-3
=
0.
$$
Thus $-1\in\mathbb F_3\subset\mathbb F_9$ is a root, so $p$ has a linear
factor over $\mathbb F_9$.
:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #field-exactly-for-q}
Therefore
$$
\boxed{M[x]/I\text{ is a field exactly for }M=\QQ.}
$$

::: pf-proof
By steps [](#reducible-over-r){.pf-ref}, [](#reducible-over-c){.pf-ref}, [](#irreducible-over-q){.pf-ref}, and [](#reducible-over-f9){.pf-ref}, among the four listed fields the polynomial $p$ is
irreducible only over $\QQ$. The quotient criterion stated at the start of
the solution gives the conclusion.
:::
