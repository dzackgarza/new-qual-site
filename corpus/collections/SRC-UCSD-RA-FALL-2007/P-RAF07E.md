::: problem
For $a \in \mathbb{R}$, let $f_a$ be the function on $\mathbb{R}$ defined by $f_a(x) := e^{iax}$.

(a) Show that $f_a$ is a tempered distribution on $\mathbb{R}$.

(b) Find the Fourier transform of $f_a$.
:::
