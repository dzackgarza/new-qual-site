::: {.problem}
Let $(X,\tau)$ be compact Hausdorff, and let $\tau'$ be another topology on $X$ such that
\[
\tau'\subsetneq\tau.
\]
Show that $(X,\tau')$ is compact but not Hausdorff.
:::
