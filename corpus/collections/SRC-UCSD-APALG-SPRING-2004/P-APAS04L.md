::: problem
Let $I$ and $J$ be ideals in $k[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$ where $k$ is a field.

(a) Show that $\sqrt{\sqrt{I}}=\sqrt{I}$.

(b) Show that $\sqrt{I\cap J}=\sqrt{IJ}=\sqrt{I}\cap\sqrt{J}$.

(c) Is $x^2-y^2\in\sqrt{\langle x^2+x,\ x^2-y\rangle}$?

(d) Is $x^2+y^2\in\sqrt{\langle x+y,\ x^2-y\rangle}$?
:::
