::: {.problem}
Let $\beta\in\mathbb C$.

(a) Show that for every $n\ge0$,
\[
\left(\frac{\beta^n}{n!}\right)^2
=\frac1{2\pi i}\int_{\partial D(0,1)}
\frac{\beta^n e^{\beta z}}{n!z^{n+1}}\,dz.
\]

(b) Show that
\[
\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left(\frac{\beta^n}{n!}\right)^2
=\frac1{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}e^{2\beta\cos\theta}\,d\theta.
\]
:::

::: {.solution}
For (a), Cauchy's differentiation formula gives
\[
\frac1{2\pi i}\int_{\partial D(0,1)}\frac{e^{\beta z}}{z^{n+1}}\,dz
=\frac1{n!}\left.\frac{d^n}{dz^n}e^{\beta z}\right|_{z=0}
=\frac{\beta^n}{n!}.
\]
Multiplying by $\beta^n/n!$ proves the identity.

For (b), on $|z|=1$ expand
\[
e^{\beta/z}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{\beta^n}{n!z^n}.
\]
Hence
\[
\frac1z e^{\beta(z+1/z)}
=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{\beta^n e^{\beta z}}{n!z^{n+1}}.
\]
Integrating termwise and applying (a) yields the series on the left. Parametrizing $z=e^{i\theta}$ gives $z+z^{-1}=2\cos\theta$ and therefore the stated integral.
:::
