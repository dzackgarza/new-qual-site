::: {.problem}
Let $f:\mathbb C\to\mathbb C$ be entire and let $a\in\mathbb R$.

(a) If $\operatorname{Re}f(z)\le a$ for all $z$, show that $f$ is constant.

(b) If $\operatorname{Re}f(z)\ge a$ for all $z$, show that $f$ is constant.

(c) If $[\operatorname{Re}f(z)]^2\le[\operatorname{Im}f(z)]^2$ for all $z$, show that $f$ is constant.

(d) If $[\operatorname{Re}f(z)]^2\ge[\operatorname{Im}f(z)]^2$ for all $z$, show that $f$ is constant.

(e) If $h$ is entire and there is $a>0$ with
\[
\operatorname{Re}f(z)\le a\operatorname{Re}h(z)
\]
for all $z$, show that $f(z)=\alpha h(z)+\beta$ for some $\alpha,\beta\in\mathbb C$.
:::

::: {.solution}
For (a), $e^f$ is entire and
\[
|e^{f(z)}|=e^{\operatorname{Re}f(z)}\le e^a,
\]
so Liouville makes $e^f$ constant. Differentiating gives $f'e^f=0$, hence $f'=0$.

Part (b) follows by applying (a) to $-f$.

For (c),
\[
|e^{f(z)^2}|=e^{(\operatorname{Re}f)^2-(\operatorname{Im}f)^2}\le1,
\]
so $e^{f^2}$ is constant, hence $f^2$ and therefore $f$ are constant. Part (d) follows by applying (c) to $if$.

For (e), the entire function $f-ah$ has nonpositive real part, so by (a) it is constant. Thus $f=ah+\beta$, which has the required form.
:::
