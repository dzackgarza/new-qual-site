::: {.problem title="?"}
Let $f$ be a non-constant meromorphic function on the complex plane $\mathbb{C}$ that obeys $$f(z)=f(z+\sqrt{2})=f(z+i\sqrt{2}).$$ (In particular, the poles of these three functions coincide.)
Assume $f$ has at most one pole in the closed unit disc $\overline{D}=\{z:|z|\le 1\}$.

(a) Prove that $f$ has exactly one pole in $\overline{D}$.

(b) Prove that this is not a simple pole.
:::
