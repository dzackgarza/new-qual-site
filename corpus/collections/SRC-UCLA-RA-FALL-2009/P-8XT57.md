::: {.problem}
Let $f$ be a non-constant meromorphic function in the complex plane.
Assume that if $f$ has a pole at the point $z\in\mathbb{C}$, then $z$ is of the form $n\pi$ with an integer $n\in\mathbb{Z}$.
Assume that for all non-real $z$ we have the estimate $$|f(z)|\le\bigl(1+|\operatorname{Im}(z)|^{-1}\bigr)e^{-|\operatorname{Im}(z)|}.$$ Prove that for every integer $n\in\mathbb{Z}$, $f$ has a pole at the point $\pi n$.
:::
