::: {.problem}
Consider the unit sphere in $\mathbb{R}^3$ with poles removed: $$S=\bigl\{(\cos\theta\sin\phi,\,\sin\theta\sin\phi,\,\cos\phi):0<\phi<\pi\text{ and }\theta\in\mathbb{R}\bigr\}.$$ Give an explicit formula for a conformal map from the complex plane onto $S$ so that horizontal lines are mapped to circles of constant $\phi$ and vertical lines are mapped to arcs of constant $\theta$.
:::
