::: {.problem}
Let $f$ be an analytic function in the open unit disc $D=\{z:\abs{z}<1\}$ that obeys $\abs{f(z)}\le 1$ for all $z\in D$.
Let $g$ be the restriction of $f$ to the real interval $(0,1)$ and assume $$\lim_{r\to 1}g(r)=1\qquad\text{and}\qquad\lim_{r\to 1}g'(r)=0.$$ Prove that $f$ is constant.
:::

::: {.solution}

::: pf

::: {.pf-step #s1}

As $r\to1^-$,
$$
1-g(r)=o(1-r).
$$

::: pf-proof

Let $\varepsilon>0$.
Since $g'(r)\to0$, there is $r_0<1$ such that
$$
\abs{g'(t)}<\varepsilon
\qquad(r_0<t<1).
$$
If $r_0<r<s<1$, then
$$
\abs{g(s)-g(r)}
=\abs{\int_r^s g'(t)\,dt}
\le \varepsilon(s-r).
$$
Letting $s\to1^-$ and using $g(s)\to1$ gives
$$
\abs{1-g(r)}\le\varepsilon(1-r).
$$
Since $\varepsilon$ is arbitrary,
$$
\frac{\abs{1-g(r)}}{1-r}\longrightarrow0.
$$

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s2}

If $f$ is nonconstant and $a=f(0)$, then $\abs{a}<1$ and
$$
h(z)=\frac{f(z)-a}{1-\overline a\,f(z)}
$$
is a holomorphic self-map of $D$ satisfying $h(0)=0$.

::: pf-proof

If $\abs{f(z_0)}=1$ at some interior point, the maximum modulus principle would make $f$ constant.
Thus nonconstancy implies
$$
\abs{f(z)}<1
\qquad(z\in D),
$$
in particular $\abs{a}<1$.

The Möbius map
$$
\varphi_a(w)=\frac{w-a}{1-\overline a\,w}
$$
is an automorphism of $D$ carrying $a$ to $0$.
Therefore $h=\varphi_a\circ f$ is a holomorphic self-map of $D$ and $h(0)=0$.

:::

:::

::: {.pf-step #s3}

Under the nonconstancy assumption,
$$
1-\abs{h(r)}=o(1-r)
\qquad(r\to1^-).
$$

::: pf-proof

Set
$$
\xi=\varphi_a(1)=\frac{1-a}{1-\overline a}.
$$
Since
$$
\abs{1-a}=\abs{1-\overline a},
$$
one has $\abs{\xi}=1$.

For any $w$ for which the denominators are nonzero,
$$
\varphi_a(w)-\varphi_a(1)
=
\frac{(1-\abs{a}^2)(w-1)}
{(1-\overline a\,w)(1-\overline a)}.
$$
As $r\to1^-$, one has $f(r)=g(r)\to1$, so
$$
1-\overline a\,f(r)\longrightarrow1-\overline a\ne0.
$$
Hence the displayed identity and step [](#s1){.pf-ref} imply
$$
h(r)-\xi=o(1-r).
$$
Because $\abs{h(r)}\le1$ and $\abs{\xi}=1$,
$$
0\le1-\abs{h(r)}
\le \abs{\xi-h(r)}
=o(1-r).
$$

:::

:::

::: pf-step

The function $f$ is constant.

::: pf-proof

Suppose otherwise.
By step [](#s2){.pf-ref}, the normalized map $h$ satisfies $h(0)=0$ and maps $D$ into itself.
Schwarz's lemma therefore gives
$$
\abs{h(r)}\le r
\qquad(0<r<1),
$$
so
$$
1-\abs{h(r)}\ge1-r.
$$
This contradicts step [](#s3){.pf-ref}, which says
$$
\frac{1-\abs{h(r)}}{1-r}\longrightarrow0.
$$
Thus $f$ is constant.
Since $g(r)\to1$, the constant is in fact $1$.

:::
