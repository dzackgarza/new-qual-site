Show that if $p_1: \tilde{X}_1 \to X_1$ and $p_2: \tilde{X}_2 \to X_2$ are covering spaces, so is their product $p_1 \times p_2: \tilde{X}_1 \times \tilde{X}_2 \to X_1 \times X_2$.
