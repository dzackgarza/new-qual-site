Show that an H-space or topological group structure on a path-connected, locally path-connected space can be lifted to such a structure on its universal cover. [For the group $SO(n)$ considered in the next section, the universal cover for $n > 2$ is a 2-sheeted cover, a group called $\operatorname{Spin}(n)$.]
