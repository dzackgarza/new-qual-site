::: {.definition title="Ideal"}
An **ideal** $I\normal R$ is a subset where $(I, +) \leq (R, +) \in \Grp$ is a subgroup and for $x\in R, i\in I$, $xi \in I$.
Equivalently,

- $RI \subseteq I$

- $I + I \subseteq I$

Note that $0$ is in every ideal.
:::
