::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 8.2.11"}
Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional inner product space, and let $\{\alpha_1, \ldots, \alpha_n\}$ be an orthonormal basis for $V$.
Show that for any vectors $\alpha, \beta$ in $V$
\[
(\alpha \mid \beta) = \sum_{k=1}^{n} (\alpha \mid \alpha_k)\overline{(\beta \mid \alpha_k)}.
\]
:::
