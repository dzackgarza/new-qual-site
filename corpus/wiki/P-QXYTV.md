Suppose that $f:[a,b]\to\mathbb{R}$ is continuous, $f\geq 0$ on $[a,b]$, and put $M=\sup\{f(x):x\in[a,b]\}$.
Prove that $$\lim_{p\to\infty}\left(\int_a^b f(x)^p\,dx\right)^{1/p}=M.$$
