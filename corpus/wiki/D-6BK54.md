::: {.definition title="Monodromy"}
For $p: \tilde X \to X$ a covering space and $x_0 \in X$, unique path lifting gives a right action of $\pi_1(X, x_0)$ on the fiber $p\inv(x_0)$:
\[
\tilde x \cdot [\gamma] \da \tilde\gamma(1)
,\]
where $\tilde\gamma$ is the lift of $\gamma$ starting at $\tilde x$.
This is the **monodromy action**.
It is transitive iff $\tilde X$ is path-connected, and the stabilizer of $\tilde x$ is $p_*\pi_1(\tilde X, \tilde x)$, so the fiber is identified with the coset space $p_*\pi_1(\tilde X)\backslash \pi_1(X)$.
:::

::: {.concept}
See Hatcher, §1.3, p. 69.
:::
