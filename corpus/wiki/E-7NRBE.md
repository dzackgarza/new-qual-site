Show that $\beta(\mathbb{Z}_+)$ has cardinality at least as great as $I^I$, where $I = [0, 1]$. [Hint: The space $I^I$ has a countable dense subset.]

::: {.remark}
Munkres, *Topology*, §38 Exercise 8.
:::
