Let $X$ equal the countable union $\bigcup B_n$. Show that if $X$ is a nonempty Baire space, at least one of the sets $\overline{B}_n$ has a nonempty interior.

::: {.remark}
Munkres, *Topology*, §48 Exercise 1.
:::
