Using cup products, show that every map $S^{k+\ell} \to S^k \times S^\ell$ induces the trivial homomorphism $H_{k+\ell}(S^{k+\ell}) \to H_{k+\ell}(S^k \times S^\ell)$, assuming $k > 0$ and $\ell > 0$.
