::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 3.2.6"}
Let $T$ be a linear transformation from $\RR^3$ into $\RR^2$, and let $U$ be a linear transformation from $\RR^2$ into $\RR^3$.
Prove that the transformation $UT$ is not invertible.
Generalize the theorem.
:::
