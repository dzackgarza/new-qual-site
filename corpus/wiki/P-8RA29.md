::: problem
Let $0<p<\infty$ and $\{x_n\}_{n=1}^\infty$ be a bounded sequence of complex numbers.
Prove the equivalence of the following two statements:

(i) $\displaystyle\lim_{N\to\infty}\frac1N\sum_{n=1}^N|x_n|^p=0$;

(ii) for every $\varepsilon>0$,
\[
\lim_{N\to\infty}\frac1N\bigl|\{1\le n\le N:|x_n|\ge\varepsilon\}\bigr|=0.
\]
:::
