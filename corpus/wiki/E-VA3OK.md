:::{.exercise title="Computing $\zeta(2)$ by integration"}
By computing
\[
{1\over 2\pi i}\oint {\cot(\pi z)\over z^2}\dz
,\]
say using a large rectangle, show that
\[
\zeta(2) = \sum_{k\geq 0} {1\over k^2} = {\pi^2\over 6}
.\]

:::
