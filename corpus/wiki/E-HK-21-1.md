::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 2.1.1"}
If $F$ is a field, verify that $F^n$ is a vector space over $F$, where $F^n$ is the set of $n$-tuples of elements of $F$ with componentwise addition and scalar multiplication.
:::
