Suppose that $f,g:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$ are
differentiable, that $f(x)\leq g(x)$ for all $x\in\mathbb{R}$, and
that $f(x_0)=g(x_0)$ for some $x_0$. Prove that $f'(x_0)=g'(x_0)$.
