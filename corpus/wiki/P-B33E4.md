:::{.problem title="Hungerford 5.5.2"}
Show that if $p\in\mathbb Z$ is prime, then $a^p = a$ for all $a\in\mathbb Z_p$, or equivalently
$c^p \equiv c \mod p$ for all $c\in\mathbb Z$.
:::
