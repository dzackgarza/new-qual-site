::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 7.2.11"}
Prove that if $A$ and $B$ are $3 \times 3$ matrices over the field $F$, a necessary and sufficient condition that $A$ and $B$ be similar over $F$ is that they have the same characteristic polynomial and the same minimal polynomial. Give an example which shows that this is false for $4 \times 4$ matrices.
:::
