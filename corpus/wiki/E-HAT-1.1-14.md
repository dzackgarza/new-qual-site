Show that the isomorphism $\pi_1(X \times Y) \approx \pi_1(X) \times \pi_1(Y)$ in Proposition 1.12 is given by $[f] \mapsto (p_{1*}([f]), p_{2*}([f]))$ where $p_1$ and $p_2$ are the projections of $X \times Y$ onto its two factors.
