Let $A \subset X$ and $B \subset Y$.
Show that in the space $X \times Y$,

$$
\overline{A \times B} = \overline{A} \times \overline{B}.
$$

::: {.remark}
Munkres, *Topology*, §17 Exercise 9.
:::
