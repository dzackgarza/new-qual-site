::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 10.3.4"}
Let $f$ be a symmetric bilinear form on $C^n$ and $g$ a skew-symmetric bilinear form on $C^n$. Suppose $f + g = 0$. Show that $f = g = 0$.
:::
