::: {.definition title="Cycle"}
An $n\dash$chain $\alpha \in C_n(X)$ with $\del_n \alpha = 0$, i.e. an element of $Z_n(X)\da \ker \del_n$.
Homology is cycles modulo boundaries, $H_n(X) = Z_n(X)/B_n(X)$ with $B_n(X)\da \im \del_{n+1}$.
:::

::: {.concept}
See Hatcher, p. 106.
:::
