::: {.theorem}
$$
f \in L^{1} \Longrightarrow \lim _{h \rightarrow 0} \int|f(x+h)-f(x)|=0
.$$

Proof idea: $\varepsilon/3$ argument, approximate by compactly supported functions.
:::
