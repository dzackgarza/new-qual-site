::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 3.6.2"}
Use Theorem 20 to prove the following. If $W$ is a subspace of a finite-dimensional vector space $V$ and if $\{g_1, \ldots, g_r\}$ is any basis for $W^0$, then
\[
W = \bigcap_{i=1}^{r} N_{g_i}.
\]
:::
