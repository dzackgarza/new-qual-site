:::{.definition title="Limsup/Liminf"}
\[  
\limsup_n a_n = \lim_{n\to \infty} \sup_{j\geq n} a_j &= \inf_{n\geq 0} \sup_{j\geq n} a_j \\ 
\liminf_n a_n = \lim_{n\to \infty} \inf_{j\geq n} a_j &= \sup_{n\geq 0} \inf_{j\geq n} a_j
.\]
:::
