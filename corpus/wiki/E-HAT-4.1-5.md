For a pair $(X, A)$ of path-connected spaces, show that $\pi_1(X, A, x_0)$ can be identified in a natural way with the set of cosets $\alpha H$ of the subgroup $H \subset \pi_1(X, x_0)$ represented by loops in $A$ at $x_0$.
