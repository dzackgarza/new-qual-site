::: problem
(a) Define the span of vectors $v_1, \dots, v_k$ in $\mathbb{R}^n$.

(b) Prove that if $w$ lies in the span of $v_1, \dots, v_k$, then the span of $v_1, \dots, v_k, w$ is equal to the span of $v_1, \dots, v_k$.
:::
