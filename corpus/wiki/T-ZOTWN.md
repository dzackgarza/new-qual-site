:::{.theorem title="Artin-Wedderburn?"}
If $R$ is a nonzero, unital, *semisimple* ring then 
\[
R \cong \bigoplus_{i=1}^m \mathrm{Mat}(n_i, D_i)
,\]
a finite sum of $n_i\times n_i$ matrix rings over division rings $D_i$.
:::
