::: problem
- Show that if $H, K \leq G$ are subgroups and $H \leq N_G(K)$, then $HK$ is a subgroup.

  - Find a counterexample where $H\leq G$, $K$ is only a subset and not a subgroup, and $HK$ fails to be a subgroup?
:::
