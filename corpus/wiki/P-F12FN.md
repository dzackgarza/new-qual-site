::: problem
Let $f_n(x) = \frac{nx}{n+x}$ for $x \in [0, \infty)$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$.

(a) Find a function $f$ such that $\{f_n\}$ converges to $f$ pointwise on $[0, \infty)$.

(b) Is the convergence uniform on $[0, \infty)$? Justify your answer.
:::
