::: {.theorem ref="SchwarzReflection"}
If $f$ is continuous and holomorphic on $\HH^+$ and real-valued on $\RR$, then the extension defined by $F^-(z) = \bar{f(\bar{z})}$ for $z\in \HH^-$ is a well-defined holomorphic function on $\CC$.
:::
