:::{.proposition title="Integrals distribute over disjoint sets"}
\[
X \subseteq A \disjoint B \implies \int_X f &\leq \int_A f + \int_B f\\
X = A \disjoint B \implies \int_X f &= \int_A f + \int_B f
.\]
:::
