::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 9.5.7"}
Let $V$ be the space of complex $n \times n$ matrices equipped with the inner product
\[
(A \mid B) = \operatorname{tr}(AB^*).
\]
If $B$ is an element of $V$, let $L_B$, $R_B$, and $T_B$ denote the linear operators on $V$ defined by

(a) $L_B(A) = BA$.

(b) $R_B(A) = AB$.

(c) $T_B(A) = BA - AB$.

Consider the three families of operators obtained by letting $B$ vary over all diagonal matrices. Show that each of these families is a commutative self-adjoint algebra and find their spectral resolutions.
:::
