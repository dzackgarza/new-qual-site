::: problem
1. $\displaystyle \int \frac {\sin (x)}{\cos ^2 (x)} ~dx = \color {blue} {\sec (x)}$

- **Solution:** $\frac {\sin (x)}{\cos ^2 (x)} = \tan (x) \sec (x)​$

$\displaystyle \int \csc (ax) \cot (ax) ~dx = - \frac {1}{a} \csc (ax)$
:::
