   1. $\displaystyle \int \frac {\sin^3(x)}{\cos(x) - \cos^3(x)} ~dx = \color {blue} {- \ln (\cos(x))}$

   - **Solution:** $\frac {\sin^3(x)}{\cos(x) - \cos^3(x)} = \frac {\sin^3(x)}{\cos(x) \sin ^2 (x)} = \tan (x)$
