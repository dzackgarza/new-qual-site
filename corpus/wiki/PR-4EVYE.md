:::{.proposition}
For $\ts{a_k}$ is a non-increasing sequence in $\RR$ then
\[
\sum_{k\geq 1} a_k < \infty \iff \sum_{k\geq 1} 2^k a_{2^k}<\infty
.\]
:::
