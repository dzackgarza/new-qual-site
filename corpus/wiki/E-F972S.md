::: {.exercise title="Munkres §18.3"}


Let $X$ and $X'$ denote a single set in the two topologies $\mathcal{T}$ and $\mathcal{T}'$, respectively. Let $i: X' \to X$ be the identity function.

(a) Show that $i$ is continuous if and only if $\mathcal{T}'$ is finer than $\mathcal{T}$.

(b) Show that $i$ is a homeomorphism if and only if $\mathcal{T}' = \mathcal{T}$.
:::
