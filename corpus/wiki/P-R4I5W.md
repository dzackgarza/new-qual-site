::: problem
6. $\displaystyle \int \sqrt {1 + \cos (2ax)} ~dx = \color {blue} {\frac {\sqrt {2}}{a} \sin (ax)}$

- **Solution:** $\sqrt {1 + \cos (2ax)} = \sqrt {2 \cos ^2 (ax)} = \sqrt {2} \cos (ax)$

- **Used 2019**
:::
