:::{.proposition title="Plancherel"}
\[
\norm{f}_{L^2}^2 &= \norm{\hat{f}}_{L^2} \\
\int_{\RR^d} \abs{f}^2 &= \int_{\RR^d} \abs{\hat f}^2
.\]
