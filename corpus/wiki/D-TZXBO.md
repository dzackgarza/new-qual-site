::: {.definition}
A ring $R$ is **Noetherian** if the ACC holds: every ascending chain of ideals $I_1 \leq I_2 \cdots$ stabilizes in the sense that there exists some $N$ such that $I_N = I_{N+1} = \cdots$.
:::
