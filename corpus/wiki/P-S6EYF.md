Compute the integral homology groups of the space $X = Y \cup Z$ which is the union of the sphere 
$$
Y = \theset{x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 1}
$$ 
and the ellipsoid 
$$
Z =  \theset{x^2 + y^2 + {z^2 \over 4} = 1}
.$$
