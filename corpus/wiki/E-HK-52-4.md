::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 5.2.4"}
Let $A$ be a $2 \times 2$ matrix over a field $F$. Show that $A$ is invertible if and only if $\det A \neq 0$. When $A$ is invertible, give a formula for $A^{-1}$.
:::
