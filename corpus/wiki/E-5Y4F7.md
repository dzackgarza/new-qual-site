::: {.exercise title="Munkres §72.3"}


Let $G$ be a group; let $x$ be an element of $G$; let $N$ be the least normal subgroup of $G$ containing $x$. Show that if there is a normal, path-connected space whose fundamental group is isomorphic to $G$, then there is a normal, path-connected space whose fundamental group is isomorphic to $G/N$.
:::
