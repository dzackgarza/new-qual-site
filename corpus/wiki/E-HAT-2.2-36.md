Show that $H_i(X \times S^n) \approx H_i(X) \oplus H_{i-n}(X)$ for all $i$ and $n$, where $H_i = 0$ for $i < 0$ by definition. Namely, show $H_i(X \times S^n) \approx H_i(X) \oplus H_i(X \times S^n, X \times \{x_0\})$ and $H_i(X \times S^n, X \times \{x_0\}) \approx H_{i-1}(X \times S^{n-1}, X \times \{x_0\})$. [For the latter isomorphism the relative Mayer–Vietoris sequence yields an easy proof.]
