::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 5.2.9"}
Let $K$ be a commutative ring with identity and $D$ an alternating $n$-linear function on $n \times n$ matrices over $K$. Show that

(a) $D(A) = 0$, if one of the rows of $A$ is 0.

(b) $D(B) = D(A)$, if $B$ is obtained from $A$ by adding a scalar multiple of one row of $A$ to another.
:::
