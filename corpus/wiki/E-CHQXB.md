Theorem. Let $A \subset X$. Then $x \in \overline{A}$ if and only if there is a net of points of $A$ converging to $x$.

[Hint: To prove the implication $\Rightarrow$, take as index set the collection of all neighborhoods of $x$, partially ordered by reverse inclusion.]

::: {.remark}
Munkres, *Topology*, §29 Supplementary Exercises (Nets) Exercise 6.
:::
