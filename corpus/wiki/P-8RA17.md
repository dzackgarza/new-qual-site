::: problem
Let $\{f_k\}$ be a sequence of functions in $L^1(\mathbb{R})$ which converges, in the $L^1$ sense, to some function $f\in L^1(\mathbb{R})$.
Prove that there is a subsequence of $\{f_k\}$ which converges pointwise almost everywhere to $f$.
:::
