::: {.exercise title="?"}
\envlist

- Let $G$ be a group of order $p$ with $v$ and $e$ positive integers, $p$ prime, $p > v$, and $v$ is not a multiple of $p$.
  Show that $G$ has a normal Sylow p-subgroup.
:::
