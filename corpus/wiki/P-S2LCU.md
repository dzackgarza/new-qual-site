- Show that if $L/K$ is separable, then $L$ is normal $\iff$ there exists a polynomial $p(x) = \prod_{i=1}^n x- \alpha_i\in K[x]$ such that $L = K(\alpha_1, \cdots, \alpha_n)$ (so $L$ is the splitting field of $p$).
