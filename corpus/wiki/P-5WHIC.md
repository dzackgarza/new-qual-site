::: {.problem title="Hungerford 4.6.3"}
Let $A$ be a cyclic $R-$module of order $r \in R$.

1. Show that if $s$ is relatively prime to $r$, then $sA = A$ and $A[s] = 0$.

2. If $s$ divides $r$, so $sk = r$, then $sA \cong R/(k)$ and $A[s] \cong R/(s)$.
:::
