::: {.theorem}
The suspension map
\[
\pi_i(S^n) \to \pi_{i+1}(S^{n+1})
\]
is an isomorphism for $i < 2n-1$ and a surjection for $i = 2n-1$.
More generally $\pi_i(X)\to\pi_{i+1}(\Sigma X)$ has the same behaviour whenever $X$ is an $(n-1)\dash$connected CW complex.
This is what makes the stable homotopy groups of spheres well defined.
:::

::: {.concept}
See Hatcher, §4.2, Corollary 4.24, p. 360; it follows from the homotopy excision Theorem 4.23.
:::
