![](../../assets/Complex_Analysis/900 Unsorted/figures/2021-12-19_20-00-12.png)

There is a product expansion
\[
\zeta(s) = \prod_{p\in \spec \ZZ} {1\over 1-p^{-s}}
,\]
where $\spec \ZZ$ are the primes of $\ZZ$.
:::
