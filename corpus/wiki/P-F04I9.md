::: problem
Suppose $f: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}^2$ and $g: \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}$ are differentiable. Prove that the composite function $f \circ g$ cannot have a differentiable inverse.
:::
