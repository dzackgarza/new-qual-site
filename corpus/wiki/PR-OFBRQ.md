:::{.proposition title="Classification of quadratic extensions"}
If $\FF$ is a field with $\ch(\FF)\neq 2$ and $E_{/\FF}$ is a degree 2 extension, then $E$ is Galois and $E = F(\sqrt{a})$ for some squarefree $a\in \FF$.
:::
