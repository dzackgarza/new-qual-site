::: problem
2. Suppose that for a field $F / K$ that $a \in F$ is algebraic and has odd degree over $K$.
   Prove that $a^{2}$ is also algebraic and has odd degree over $K$, and furthermore that $K(a)=K\left(a^{2}\right)$
:::
