::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 3.4.3"}
Let $T$ be a linear operator on $F^n$, let $A$ be the matrix of $T$ in the standard ordered basis for $F^n$, and let $W$ be the subspace of $F^n$ spanned by the column vectors of $A$.
What does $W$ have to do with $T$?
:::
