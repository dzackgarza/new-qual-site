::: {.problem title="Hungerford 2.4.9)."}
Let $Z(G)$ be the center of $G$.
Show that if $G/Z(G)$ is cyclic, then $G$ is abelian.

> Note that Hungerford uses the notation $C(G)$ for the center.
:::
