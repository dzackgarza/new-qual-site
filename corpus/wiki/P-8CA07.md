::: problem
Let $L$ be a positive constant.
Let $\{a_n\}_{n=0}^\infty$ be a strictly decreasing sequence of positive numbers, where $a_n\ge L$ for all $n$.
Let $\mathbb{E}\subset\mathbb{C}$ denote the open unit disk.

(a) Prove that $f(z):=\sum_{n=0}^\infty a_n z^n$ defines an analytic function in $\mathbb{E}$.

(b) What is the radius of convergence of the power series $f(z)$?

(c) Show that $f$ has no zeros in $\mathbb{E}$.
:::
