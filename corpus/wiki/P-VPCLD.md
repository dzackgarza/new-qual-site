1. Apply the spectral theorem: $D$ will have the eigenvalues on the diagonal, and $A$ will be the column of eigenvectors.
   Thus
$$
A = \left[ \begin{array} { l l } { 2 } & { 1 } \\ { 1 } & { 1 } \end{array} \right], \quad D = \left[\begin{array}{rr}
1 & 0 \\
0 & -2
\end{array}\right].
$$
