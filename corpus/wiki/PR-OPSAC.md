:::{.proposition title="Bernoulli's Inequality"}
\[
(1 + x)^n \geq 1 +nx \quad x\geq -1, \text{ or } n\in 2\ZZ \text{ and } \forall x
.\]

As a consequence,
\[
1-x \leq e^{-x}
.\]

:::
