::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 8.5.4"}
Let $V$ be $C^2$, with the standard inner product.
Let $T$ be the linear operator on $V$ which is represented in the standard ordered basis by the matrix
\[
A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & i \\ i & 1 \end{bmatrix}.
\]
Show that $T$ is normal, and find an orthonormal basis for $V$, consisting of characteristic vectors for $T$.
:::
