::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 8.2.15"}
Suppose $V = W_1 \oplus W_2$ and that $f_1$ and $f_2$ are inner products on $W_1$ and $W_2$, respectively. Show that there is a unique inner product $f$ on $V$ such that

(a) $W_2 = W_1^{\perp}$;

(b) $f(\alpha, \beta) = f_k(\alpha, \beta)$, when $\alpha, \beta$ are in $W_k$, $k = 1, 2$.
:::
