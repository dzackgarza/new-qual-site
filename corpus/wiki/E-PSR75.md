::: {.exercise title="Munkres §24.3"}


Let $f: X \to X$ be continuous. Show that if $X = [0, 1]$, there is a point $x$ such that $f(x) = x$. The point $x$ is called a fixed point of $f$. What happens if $X$ equals $[0, 1)$ or $(0, 1)$?
:::
