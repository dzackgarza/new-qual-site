::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 3.1.11"}
Let $V$ be the space of $n \times 1$ matrices over $F$ and let $W$ be the space of $m \times 1$ matrices over $F$.
Let $A$ be a fixed $m \times n$ matrix over $F$ and let $T$ be the linear transformation from $V$ into $W$ defined by $T(X) = AX$.
Prove that $T$ is the zero transformation if and only if $A$ is the zero matrix.
:::
