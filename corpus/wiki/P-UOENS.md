- **Used 2019**

5. $\displaystyle \int \sin (x) \cos (x) \cot (x) \tan (x) ~dx = \color {blue} {\frac {1}{2} \sin^2 (x)}$

- **Solution:** $\sin (x) \cos (x) \cot (x) \tan (x) = \sin (x) \cos (x)$
:::
