::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 3.5.10"}
Let $F$ be a subfield of the complex numbers. We define $n$ linear functionals on $F^n$ ($n \geq 2$) by
\[
f_k(x_1, \ldots, x_n) = \sum_{j=1}^{n} (k - j)x_j, \quad 1 \leq k \leq n.
\]
What is the dimension of the subspace annihilated by $f_1, \ldots, f_n$?
:::
