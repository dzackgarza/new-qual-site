::: {.definition title="Derived Series"}
Set $G^{(0)} = G$ and $G^{(i+1)} = [G^{(i)}, G^{(i)}]$, then $G^{(0)} \geq G^{(1)} \geq \cdots$ is the *derived series* of $G$.
:::
