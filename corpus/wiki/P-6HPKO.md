::: problem
- (**Important**) Prove that if $G/Z(G)$ is cyclic then $G$ is abelian.

  > Write $Z = Z(G)$, fix $x,y\in G$.
  > Since $G/Z = \gens{gZ}$, $xZ = (gZ)^m = g^mZ$ and $yz = (gZ)^n = g^nz$ $g^{-m}x, g^{-n}y \in Z \implies x = g^m z_1, y = g^n z_2$ $xy = g^m z_1 g^n z_2$, everything commutes.
:::
