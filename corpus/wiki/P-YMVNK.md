::: problem
- Suppose $X$ is a $G\dash$set, so there is a permutation action of $G$ on $X$.
  Let $x_1, x_2\in X$, and show that the stabilizer subgroups $\Stab_G(x_1), \Stab_G(x_2)\leq G$ are conjugate in $G$.
:::
