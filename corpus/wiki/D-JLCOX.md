:::{.definition}
For an ideal $I\normal R$, the **radical** 
\[
\rad{I} \definedas \theset{r\in R\suchthat r^n\in I\text{ for some } n\geq 0}
,\] 
so $x^n \in I \implies x\in \rad{I}$.

An ideal is **radical** iff $\rad{I} = I$. 
:::
