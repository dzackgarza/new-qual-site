- Show that any cyclic group is abelian.
- Show that every subgroup of a cyclic group is cyclic.
- Show that $$\phi(n) = n \prod_{p\mid n}\qty{1 - {1\over p}}.$$
- Compute $\aut(\ZZ/n\ZZ)$ for $n$ composite.
- Compute $\aut(\qty{\ZZ/p\ZZ}^n)$.
