::: {.exercise title="Munkres §74.2"}

Consider the space $X$ obtained from a seven-sided polygonal region by means of the labelling scheme $abaaab^{-1}a^{-1}$.
Show that the fundamental group of $X$ is the free product of two cyclic groups.
[Hint: See Theorem 68.7.]
:::
