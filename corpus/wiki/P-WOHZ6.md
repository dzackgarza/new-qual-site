:::{.problem title="?"}
Prove that the following series converges uniformly on the set $\theset{z \suchthat \Im(z) < \ln 2}$:
\[
\sum_{n=1}^\infty {\sin(nz) \over 2^n}
.\]
Suppose $0 < r < \abs{z_0}$ and show that the series converges uniformly on $\abs{z} \leq r$.


:::
