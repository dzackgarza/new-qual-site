:::{.theorem ref="CauchyTheorem"}
If $f$ is holomorphic on a region $\Omega$ with $\pi_1 \Omega = 1$, then for any closed path $\gamma \subseteq \Omega$,
\[ 
\int_{\gamma} f(z) \dz = 0
.\]
:::
