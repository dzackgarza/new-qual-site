:::{.proposition title="?"}
If $\dim_k V, \dim_k W < \infty$ then there is an isomorphism
\[
V\dual \tensor_k W &\mapsvia{\sim} \Hom_{\kmod}(V, W) \\
\tilde v \tensor w &\mapsto \tilde v(\wait) w
.\]
:::
