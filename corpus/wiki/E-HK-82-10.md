::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 8.2.10"}
Let $V$ be the vector space of all $n \times n$ matrices over $C$, with the inner product $(A \mid B) = \operatorname{tr}(AB^*)$.
Find the orthogonal complement of the subspace of diagonal matrices.
:::
