::: {.definition}
A polynomial $p(x)$ over a field is **irreducible** iff $\deg p \geq 1$ and $p(x)= q(x)r(x) \implies q(x)$ or $r(x)$ is a nonzero constant.

The bound is $\geq 1$, not $>1$: every linear polynomial is irreducible.
:::
