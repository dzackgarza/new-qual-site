::: {.definition title="Normal Closure"}
If $K/k$ is algebraic, then there is an extension $N_k/K$ such that $N_k/k$ is normal and $N_k/K/k$ is a tower.
$N_k$ is referred to as the **normal closure** of $K/k$.
:::
