Let $A_1, A_2, A_3$ be compact sets in $\mathbb{R}^3$.
Use the Borsuk–Ulam theorem to show that there is one plane $P \subset \mathbb{R}^3$ that simultaneously divides each $A_i$ into two pieces of equal measure.
