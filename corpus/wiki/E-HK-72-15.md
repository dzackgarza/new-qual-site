::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 7.2.15"}
Let $F$ be a subfield of the field of complex numbers, and let $A$ be an $n \times n$ matrix over $F$.
Let $p$ be the minimal polynomial for $A$.
If we regard $A$ as a matrix over $C$, then $A$ has a minimal polynomial $f$ as an $n \times n$ matrix over $C$.
Use a theorem on linear equations to prove $p = f$.
Can you also see how this follows from the cyclic decomposition theorem?
:::
