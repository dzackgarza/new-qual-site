::: problem
Let $X$ be a space obtained by attaching two $2$-cells to the torus $S^1\times S^1$, one along a simple closed curve $1\times S^1$ and the other along $S^1\times 1$.
Calculate the fundamental group and homology groups of $X$.
:::
