::: problem
Let $\DD$ be a closed disk embedded in the torus $T = S^1 \times S^1$ and let $X$ be the result of removing the interior of $\DD$ from $T$ . Let $B$ be the boundary of $X$, i.e. the circle boundary of the original closed disk $\DD$.

Compute $H_i (T, B)$ for all $i$.
:::
