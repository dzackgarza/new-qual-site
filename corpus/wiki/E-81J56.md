::: {.exercise title="Munkres §62.5"}

Theorem.
Let $U$ be a simply connected open set in $\mathbb{R}^2$.
If $C$ is a simple closed curve lying in $U$, then each bounded component of $\mathbb{R}^2 - C$ also lies in $U$.

(This condition actually characterizes the simply connected open sets of $\mathbb{R}^2$.
See [RW]. The space $\mathbb{R}^2 - C$ has, of course, only one bounded component, as we shall prove in the next section.)
:::
