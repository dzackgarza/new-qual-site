::: {.definition title="R-orientability"}
The same notion with $\ZZ$ replaced by a commutative ring $R$ with identity: an **$R\dash$orientation** of an $n\dash$manifold $M$ is a consistent choice of generators $\mu_x$ of $H_n(M, M\sm\ts{x}; R)\cong R$, consistent in the sense that each point has a ball neighborhood $B$ and a class in $H_n(M, M\sm B; R)$ restricting to $\mu_y$ throughout $B$.
Every manifold is $\ZZ/2\dash$orientable, and for $R$ with $2 \neq 0$ in $R$, $R\dash$orientability is equivalent to $\ZZ\dash$orientability.
:::

::: {.concept}
See Hatcher, §3.3, p. 235.
:::
