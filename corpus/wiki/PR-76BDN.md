::: {.proposition title="Showing ideals are maximal/prime with quotients"}
\envlist

- $R/\mfm$ is a field $\iff \mfm \in \mspec(R)$ is maximal.

- $R/\mfp$ is an integral domain $\iff \mfp \in \spec(R)$ is prime.

- $R/J$ is reduced $\iff J$ is radical.
:::
