:::{.proposition title="Convergence in $L^1$ implies convergence of norms"}
If $f\in L^1$, then
\[
\norm{f_n - f}_{L^1} \convergesto{n\to\infty}0 \iff \norm{f_n}_{L^1} \convergesto{n\to\infty} \norm{f}_{L^1}
.\]
:::
