Show that
\[
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \frac{d x}{\left(1+x^{2}\right)^{n+1}}=\frac{1 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 \cdots(2 n-1)}{2 \cdot 4 \cdot 6 \cdots(2 n)} \cdot \pi
.\]
