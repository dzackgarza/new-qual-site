::: problem
Suppose $\{f_{n}:n \geq 1\}$ is a sequence of continuous functions on $\mathbb{R}^{m}$ with the property that for every $x \in \mathbb{R}^{m}$,
\[
\sum_{n = 1}^{\infty}\left| f_{n}(x) - f_{n - 1}(x) \right| \leq \frac{1}{1 + |x|^{2}}.
\]
Prove the sequence $\{f_{n}:n \geq 1\}$ converges to a continuous function.
:::
