- Prove Fatou's lemma using the Monotone Convergence Theorem.

- Show that if $\theset{f_n}$ is in $L^1$ and $\sum \int \abs{f_n} < \infty$ then $\sum f_n$ converges to an $L^1$ function and $$\int \sum f_n = \sum \int f_n.$$
