::: {.exercise title="Munkres §67.2"}

Show that if $G_1$ is a subgroup of $G$, there may be no subgroup $G_2$ of $G$ such that $G = G_1 \oplus G_2$.
[Hint: Set $G = \mathbb{Z}$ and $G_1 = 2\mathbb{Z}$.]
:::
