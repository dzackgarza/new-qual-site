::: {.exercise title="Munkres §82 Supplementary"}

Let $p: E \to X$ be a covering map such that $\pi_1(X, x_0)$ is countable.
Show that if $X$ is regular with a countable basis, so is $E$.
[Hint: Let $\mathcal{B}$ be a countable basis for $X$ consisting of path-connected sets. Let $\mathcal{C}$ be the collection of path components of $p^{-1}(B)$, for $B \in \mathcal{B}$. Compare Exercise 6 of §53.]
:::
