Suppose $0 < a < b \leq \infty$, and find examples of functions $f \in L^p((0, \infty ))$ if and only if:

- $a < p < b$

- $a \leq p \leq b$

- $p = a$

*Hint: consider functions of the following form:*
\[
f(x) \da x^{- \alpha} \abs{ \log(x) }^{ \beta}
.\]
