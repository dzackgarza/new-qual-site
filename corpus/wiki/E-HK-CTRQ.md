::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 1.6.3"}
For each of the two matrices

$$
\left[ \begin{array}{c c c} 2 & 5 & - 1 \\ 4 & - 1 & 2 \\ 6 & 4 & 1 \end{array} \right], \qquad \left[ \begin{array}{c c c} 1 & - 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 2 & 4 \\ 0 & 1 & - 2 \end{array} \right]
$$

use elementary row operations to discover whether it is invertible, and to find the inverse in case it is.
:::
