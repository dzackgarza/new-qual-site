(a) Show that if $X$ has the discrete topology, then $X$ is paracompact.

(b) Show that if $f: X \to Y$ is continuous and $X$ is paracompact, the subspace $f(X)$ of $Y$ need not be paracompact.

::: {.remark}
Munkres, *Topology*, §41 Exercise 4.
:::
