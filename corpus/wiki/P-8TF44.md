::: problem
Recall that a covering space $\pi:\widetilde{X}\to X$ between path-connected spaces is called normal (or regular) provided that, for one and hence any $x_0\in X$, the deck transformation group of $\pi$ acts transitively on $\pi^{-1}(x_0)$.
Give examples both of a $3$-sheeted normal covering space of $S^1\vee S^1$, and of a path-connected $3$-sheeted non-normal covering space of $S^1\vee S^1$.
:::
