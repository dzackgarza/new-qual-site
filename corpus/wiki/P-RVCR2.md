::: {.problem}
Let $R$ be a commutative ring containing a field $k$, and suppose that $\dim_k R < \infty$.
Let $\alpha \in R$.

1. Show that there exist $n\in \mathbb N$ and $\{ c_0, c_1, \cdots c_{n-1}\} \subseteq k$ such that $$a^n + c_{n-1}a^{n-1} + \cdots + c_1 a + c_0 = 0.$$

2. Suppose that (a) holds and show that if $c_0 \neq 0$ then $a$ is a unit in $R$.

3. Suppose that (a) holds and show that if $a$ is not a zero divisor in $R$, then $a$ is invertible.
:::
