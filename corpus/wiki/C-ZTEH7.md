:::{.corollary}
If additionally $f=g/h$ where $h(z_0) = 0$ and $h'(z_0)\neq 0$, 
\[
\Res_{z=z_0} {g(z) \over h(z)} = {g(z_0) \over h'(z_0)}
.\]

Note that if $f(z) = 1/h(z)$ and $z_0$ is a simple pole, this reduces to
\[
\Res_{z=z_0}{1\over h(z)} = {1\over h'(z_0)}
.\]

:::
