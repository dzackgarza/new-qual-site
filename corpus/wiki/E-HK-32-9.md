::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 3.2.9"}
Let $T$ be a linear operator on the finite-dimensional space $V$.
Suppose there is a linear operator $U$ on $V$ such that $TU = I$.
Prove that $T$ is invertible and $U = T^{-1}$.
Give an example which shows that this is false when $V$ is not finite-dimensional.
(Hint: Let $T = D$, the differentiation operator on the space of polynomial functions.)
:::
