Show that under the map $\langle X, Y \rangle \to \operatorname{Hom}(\pi_n(X, x_0), \pi_n(Y, y_0))$, $[f] \mapsto f_*$, the action of $\pi_1(Y, y_0)$ on $\langle X, Y \rangle$ corresponds to composing with the action on $\pi_n(Y, y_0)$, that is, $(\gamma f)_* = \beta_\gamma f_*$. Deduce a bijection of $[X, K(\pi, 1)]$ with the set of orbits of $\operatorname{Hom}(\pi_1(X), \pi)$ under composition with inner automorphisms of $\pi$.
