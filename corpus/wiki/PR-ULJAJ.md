Some useful properties:

- $\psi_a \in \Aut(\DD)$

- $\psi_a(S^1) = S^1$

- $\psi_a(0) = a$ and $\psi(a) = 0$

- With this choice of sign, $\psi_{a} \inv = \psi_{a}$, so $\psi_a^2 = \id$.

- $\psi_a'(z) = {\abs{a}^2 - 1 \over \qty{1-\bar{a} z}^2 }$

- $\psi_a(\lambda z) = \lambda\psi_{\bar\lambda a}(z)$
:::
