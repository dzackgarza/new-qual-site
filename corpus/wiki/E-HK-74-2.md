::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 7.4.2"}
Let $T$ be a linear operator on a finite-dimensional vector space and let $A$ be the matrix of $T$ in some ordered basis.
Then $T$ has a cyclic vector if and only if the determinants of the $(n-1) \times (n-1)$ submatrices of $xI - A$ are relatively prime.
:::
