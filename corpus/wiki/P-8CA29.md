::: problem
Write down a formula that gives a conformal equivalence between the vertical half-strip
\[
\mathcal{S}=\{z\in\mathbb{C}:\ 0<\operatorname{Re}(z)<1,\ \operatorname{Im}(z)>0\}
\]
and the upper half-plane
\[
\mathbb{H}=\{z\in\mathbb{C}:\operatorname{Im}(z)>0\}.
\]
:::
