::: problem
(a) Let $\{f_n\}$ be a sequence of analytic functions on a region $\Omega$ in $\mathbb{C}$ and assume that for some function $g$ on $\Omega$, $f_n\to g$ uniformly on compact subsets of $\Omega$.
Show that $f_n'\to g'$ uniformly on compact subsets of $\Omega$ and that $g$ is analytic on $\Omega$.

(b) Explain what part (a) has to do with differentiation of power series.
:::
