::: problem
Prove the following portion of van Kampen's theorem. 
If $X = A\cup B$ and $A$, $B$, and $A \cap B$ are nonempty and path connected with $\pt \in A \cap B$, then there is a surjection 
$$
\pi_1 (A, \pt) \ast \pi_1 (B, \pt) \to \pi_1 (X, \pt)
.$$
:::
