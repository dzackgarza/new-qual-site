::: problem
- Prove the *fundamental theorem of cosets*: for $xH, yH\in G/H$,
$$
xH = yH \iff x\inv y\in H \iff y\inv x \in H
$$

> Use that $xH = yH\iff x\sim y$ is an equivalence relation (reflexive/symmetric/transitive)
:::
