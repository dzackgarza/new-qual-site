(a) Let $p: X \to Y$ be a continuous map. Show that if there is a continuous map $f: Y \to X$ such that $p \circ f$ equals the identity map of $Y$, then $p$ is a quotient map.

(b) If $A \subset X$, a retraction of $X$ onto $A$ is a continuous map $r: X \to A$ such that $r(a) = a$ for each $a \in A$. Show that a retraction is a quotient map.

::: {.remark}
Munkres, *Topology*, §22 Exercise 2.
:::
