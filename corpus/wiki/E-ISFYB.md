:::{.exercise title="Uniformly bounded derivatives implies equicontinuous"}
Show that if $\ts{f_k}$ is a differentiable sequence with $\abs{f_k'(x)} \leq M$ uniformly bounded, then $\ts{f_k}$ is equicontinuous.

> Hint: MVT.

:::
