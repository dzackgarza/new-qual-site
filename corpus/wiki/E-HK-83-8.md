::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 8.3.8"}
Let $V$ be the vector space of the polynomials over $\RR$ of degree less than or equal to 3, with the inner product
\[
(f \mid g) = \int_0^1 f(t)g(t)\,dt.
\]
If $t$ is a real number, find the polynomial $g_t$ in $V$ such that $(f \mid g_t) = f(t)$ for all $f$ in $V$.
:::
