:::{.definition title="$L^\infty$"}
\[
L^\infty(X)
\definedas \theset{f: X\to \CC \suchthat f \text{ is essentially bounded }}
\definedas \theset{f: X\to \CC \suchthat \pnorm{f}\infty < \infty}
.\]

:::
