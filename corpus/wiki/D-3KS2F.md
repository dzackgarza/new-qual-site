::: {.definition title="Boundary"}
The **boundary** of a subset $A\subseteq X$ is defined as $\bd A \da \cl_X(A) \sm \interior{A}$.
Equivalently, every point $p\in A$ intersects both $A$ and $X\sm A$.
:::
