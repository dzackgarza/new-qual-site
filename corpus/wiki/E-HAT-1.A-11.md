Apply the two preceding problems to show that if $F$ is a finitely generated free group and $x \in F$ is not the identity element, then there is a normal subgroup $H \subset F$ of finite index such that $x \notin H$. Hence $x$ has nontrivial image in a finite quotient group of $F$. In this situation one says $F$ is residually finite.
