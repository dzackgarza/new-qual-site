::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 6.8.11"}
What's wrong with the following proof of Theorem 13? Suppose that the minimal polynomial for $T$ is a product of linear factors. Then, by Theorem 5, $T$ is triangulable. Let $\mathfrak{B}$ be an ordered basis such that $A = [T]_{\mathfrak{B}}$ is upper-triangular. Let $D$ be the diagonal matrix with diagonal entries $a_{11}, \ldots, a_{nn}$. Then $A = D + N$, where $N$ is strictly upper-triangular. Evidently $N$ is nilpotent.
:::
