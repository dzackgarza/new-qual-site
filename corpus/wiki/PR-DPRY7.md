:::{.proposition title="Eigenfunction of the Fourier transform"}
\[
g(x) \da e^{-\pi \abs{t}^2} \implies \hat g(\xi) = g(\xi) \qtext{and}
\hat g_{t}(x) = g(tx) = e^{-\pi t^2 \abs{x}^2}
.\]
:::
