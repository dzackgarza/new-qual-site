Show that $(\alpha \frown \varphi) \frown \psi = \alpha \frown (\varphi \smile \psi)$ for all $\alpha \in C_k(X; R)$, $\varphi \in C^\ell(X; R)$, and $\psi \in C^m(X; R)$. Deduce that cap product makes $H_*(X; R)$ a right $H^*(X; R)$ module.
