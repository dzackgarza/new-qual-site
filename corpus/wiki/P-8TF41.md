::: problem
Let $\{Y_n\}_{n=1}^\infty$ be a sequence of connected subsets of a topological space $X$.
Suppose that $Y_n\cap Y_{n+1}$ is nonempty for all $n$.
Show that $\bigcup_n Y_n$ is connected.
:::
