:::{.proposition title="Integration by parts, special case"}
\[
F(x):=\int_{0}^{x} f(y) d y \quad \text { and } \quad G(x):=\int_{0}^{x} g(y) d y \\
\implies
\int_{0}^{1} F(x) g(x) d x=F(1) G(1)-\int_{0}^{1} f(x) G(x) d x
.\]

:::
