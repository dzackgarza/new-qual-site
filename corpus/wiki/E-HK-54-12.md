::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 5.4.12"}
If $V$ is the vector space of $n \times n$ matrices over $F$ and $B$ is a fixed $n \times n$ matrix over $F$, let $L_B$ and $R_B$ be the linear operators on $V$ defined by $L_B(A) = BA$ and $R_B(A) = AB$.
Show that

(a) $\det L_B = (\det B)^n$;

(b) $\det R_B = (\det B)^n$.
:::
