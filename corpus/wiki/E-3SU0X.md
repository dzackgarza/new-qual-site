Check the details of Example 3 of §22: for the map $p: \mathbb{R} \to A = \ts{a, b, c}$ defined by

$$
p(x) =
\begin{cases}
a & \text{if } x > 0, \\
b & \text{if } x < 0, \\
c & \text{if } x = 0,
\end{cases}
$$

determine the quotient topology on $A$ induced by $p$.

::: {.remark}
Munkres, *Topology*, §22 Exercise 1.
:::
