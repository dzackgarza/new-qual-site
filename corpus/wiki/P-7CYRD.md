:::{.problem title="?"}
Suppose $(X, d)$ is a metric space. State criteria for continuity of a function $f : X \to X$ in terms of:

i. open sets;

ii. $\eps$'s and $\delta$'s; and

iii. convergent sequences.

Then prove that (iii) implies (i).
