::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 6.4.10"}
Let $A$ be a $3 \times 3$ matrix with real entries.
Prove that, if $A$ is not similar over $R$ to a triangular matrix, then $A$ is similar over $C$ to a diagonal matrix.
:::
