::: problem
Let $f: X \to Y$ be a (not necessarily invertible) function, and $A \subseteq X$.

(a) Prove that $A \subseteq f^{-1}(f(A))$.

(b) Prove that if $f$ is injective (one-to-one) then $A = f^{-1}(f(A))$.

(c) Give an example for which $A \neq f^{-1}(f(A))$.
:::
