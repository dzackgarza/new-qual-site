By Exercise 35 in §4.2 there is a bundle $S^2 \to \mathbb{CP}^3 \to S^4$. Let $S^2 \to E_k \to S^4$ be the pullback of this bundle via a degree $k$ map $S^4 \to S^4$, $k > 1$. Use the Leray-Hirsch theorem to show that $H^*(E_k; \mathbb{Z})$ is additively isomorphic to $H^*(\mathbb{CP}^3; \mathbb{Z})$ but has a different cup product structure in which the square of a generator of $H^2(E_k; \mathbb{Z})$ is $k$ times a generator of $H^4(E_k; \mathbb{Z})$.
