::: problem
Let $1\le p<q<\infty$.

(a) Which of the following statements (i)--(iv) are true, and which are false?
Justify all the negative answers by a counterexample and all positive answers with a proof.

(i) $L^p(\mathbb{R})\subseteq L^q(\mathbb{R})$

(ii) $L^q(\mathbb{R})\subseteq L^p(\mathbb{R})$

(iii) $L^p([0,1])\subseteq L^q([0,1])$

(iv) $L^q([0,1])\subseteq L^p([0,1])$

(b) For which values of $r\ge 1$ is it true that $L^p(\mathbb{R})\cap L^q(\mathbb{R})\subseteq L^r(\mathbb{R})$?
:::
