Prove that $\CP^2\#\CP^2$ and $S^2\times S^2$ are not homotopy-equivalent. (Recall that the connect-sum ($\#$) of two closed oriented connected $n$-manifolds is defined by removing an open $n$-ball from each and gluing the resulting manifolds using a homeomorphism between their boundary $(n-1)$-spheres, in such a way that the orientations match to make a new closed oriented connected $n$-manifold.)
