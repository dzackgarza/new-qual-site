For $X$ a connected CW complex with $\pi_i(X) = 0$ for $1 < i < n$ for some $n \geq 2$, show that $H_n(X) / h\bigl(\pi_n(X)\bigr) \approx H_n\bigl(K(\pi_1(X), 1)\bigr)$, where $h$ is the Hurewicz map.
