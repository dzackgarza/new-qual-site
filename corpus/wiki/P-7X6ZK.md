1. $\displaystyle \int_{0}^{\pi} \sqrt {1 + \cos (2x)} ~dx= \color {blue} {2\sqrt {2}}$

- **Solution:** $\sqrt {1 + \cos (2x)} = \sqrt {2 \cos ^2 (x)} = \sqrt {2} \cos (x)$
