The elementary divisors are given by:

\begin{align*}
(x-1)^3 && (x^2+1)^4 && (x+2) \\
(x-1) && (x^2 + 1)^2 && \\
&& (x^2+1)^2 && 
.\end{align*}

The invariant factors are:

\begin{align*}
d_3 &= (x-1)^3(x^2+1)^4(x+2) \\
d_2 &= (x-1)(x^2+1)^2 \\
d_1 &= (x^2+1)^2
.\end{align*}
