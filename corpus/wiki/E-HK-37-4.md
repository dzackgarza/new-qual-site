::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 3.7.4"}
Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional vector space over the field $F$ and let $T$ be a linear operator on $V$. Let $c$ be a scalar and suppose there is a non-zero vector $\alpha$ in $V$ such that $T\alpha = c\alpha$. Prove that there is a non-zero linear functional $f$ on $V$ such that $T^t f = cf$.
:::
