::: {.definition}
The equivalence class $[f]$ of a map $f: X\to Y$ under homotopy.
The set of all such classes is written
\[
[X, Y] \da \Hom_{\Top}(X,Y) / \homotopic
,\]
and homotopy classes are what induced maps on homology and homotopy groups actually depend on: $f\homotopic g \implies f_* = g_*$.
For based spaces, $[S^n, Y]_* = \pi_n(Y)$.
:::

::: {.concept}
See Hatcher, p. 3.
:::
