:::{.theorem}
If $S \leq G$ and $N \normal G$, then
\[
\frac{SN}{N} \cong \frac{S}{S\intersect N} \quad \text{ and }\quad \abs{SN} = \frac{\abs S \abs N}{\abs{S\intersect N}}
.\]
