::: {.definition title="Universal Cover"}
A covering space $p: \tilde X \to X$ with $\tilde X$ simply connected.
It exists iff $X$ is path-connected, locally path-connected, and semilocally simply connected, and is then unique up to isomorphism of covering spaces.
It covers every other connected covering space of $X$, which is the reason for the name, and its deck transformation group is $\pi_1(X)$.
:::

::: {.concept}
See Hatcher, §1.3, pp. 59 and 68; existence is Theorem 1.38, p. 67.
:::
