::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 10.4.16"}
Let $n$ be a positive integer, $I$ the $n \times n$ identity matrix over $C$, and $J$ the $2n \times 2n$ matrix given by
\[
J = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & I \\ -I & 0 \end{bmatrix}.
\]
Let $M$ be a $2n \times 2n$ matrix over $C$ of the form
\[
M = \begin{bmatrix} A & B \\ C & D \end{bmatrix}
\]
where $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$ are $n \times n$ matrices over $C$.
Find necessary and sufficient conditions on $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$ in order that $M^tJM = J$.
:::
