::: problem
Let $f$ be a function mapping $\mathbb{R}$ to $\mathbb{R}$. Give the definitions of each of the following concepts in words, without using logical symbols or the word “not”.

a. $f$ is surjective

b. $f$ is not injective

c. $f$ is uniformly continuous

d. $f$ is not uniformly continuous
:::

::: solution
a. Every real number is the value of $f$ at some real number.

b. There are two distinct real numbers at which $f$ takes the same value.

c. For every positive $\varepsilon$ there is a positive $\delta$ such that whenever two real numbers differ by at most $\delta$, their images under $f$ differ by at most $\varepsilon$.

d. There is a positive $\varepsilon$ such that for every positive $\delta$ there are two real numbers differing by at most $\delta$ whose images under $f$ differ by more than $\varepsilon$.
:::
