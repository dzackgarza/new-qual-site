::: {.exercise title="Munkres §74.7"}

If $m > 1$, show the fundamental group of the $m$-fold projective plane is not abelian.
[Hint: There is a homomorphism mapping this group onto the group $\mathbb{Z}/2 * \mathbb{Z}/2$.]
:::
