:::{.exercise title="Sum formulas: $1/n^2+a^2$"}
Show that
\[
\sum_{k\in \ZZ} {1\over k^2 + a^2} = {\pi \coth(\pi a) \over a} \qquad\text{for } a>0
.\]

:::
