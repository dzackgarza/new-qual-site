If $A,B$ are (skew)-symmetric, then $A^t = \pm A$ and $B^t = \pm B$ respectively.
But then
$$
(A+B)^t = A^t + B^t = \pm A + \pm B = \pm(A + B),
$$

which shows that $A+B$ is (skew)-symmetric.
