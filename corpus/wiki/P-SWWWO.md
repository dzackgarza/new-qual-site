::: problem
Let $F:\RR^2\to \RR$ be continuously differentiable with $F(0, 0) = 0$ and $\norm{\nabla F(0, 0)} < 1$.

Prove that there is some real number $r> 0$ such that $\abs{F(x, y)} < r$ whenever $\norm{(x, y)} < r$.
:::
