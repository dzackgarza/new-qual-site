::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 5.2.11"}
Let $K$ be a commutative ring with identity, and let $D$ be an alternating 2-linear function on $2 \times 2$ matrices over $K$. Show that $D(A) = (\det A)D(I)$ for all $A$. Now use this result (no computations with the entries allowed) to show that $\det(AB) = (\det A)(\det B)$ for any $2 \times 2$ matrices $A$ and $B$ over $K$.
:::
