::: {.fact}
If $ p: V\to {\mathbf{R}} $ is a sublinear function and $ \phi: U\to {\mathbf{R}} $ a linear functional on $ U\leq V $ with $ \phi \leq p $, then there exists a $ p{\hbox{-}} $sublinear extension $ \tilde \phi: V\to {\mathbf{R}} $
:::
