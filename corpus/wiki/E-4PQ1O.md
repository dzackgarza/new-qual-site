::: {.exercise title="Munkres §29 Supplementary"}

Suppose that

$$
(x_\alpha)_{\alpha \in J} \to x \text{ in } X \quad \text{and} \quad (y_\alpha)_{\alpha \in J} \to y \text{ in } Y.
$$

Show that $(x_\alpha \times y_\alpha) \to x \times y$ in $X \times Y$.
:::
