::: {.problem title="?"}
Prove that a metric space $X$ is **normal**, i.e. if $A, B \subset X$ are closed and disjoint then there exist open sets $A \subset U \subset X, ~B \subset V \subset X$ such that $U \cap V = \emptyset$.
:::
