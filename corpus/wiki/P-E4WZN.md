::: problem
If $R_1 \neq R_2$, prove that the radius of convergence, $R$, of the power series $\sum_{n=0}^\infty (a_n+b_n)x^n$ is $\min\{R_1, R_2\}$.
What can be said about $R$ when $R_1 = R_2$?
:::
