::: {.definition title="Excision"}
The property of a homology theory that homology is unchanged by cutting out a subspace well inside the subspace of a pair: for $Z\subseteq A \subseteq X$ with $\cl_X(Z) \subseteq A\interior$, the inclusion $(X\sm Z, A\sm Z)\injects (X, A)$ induces isomorphisms on all relative groups.
This is one of the Eilenberg-Steenrod axioms, and it is the axiom that fails for homotopy groups.
:::

::: {.concept}
See Hatcher, p. 119; the axioms are on p. 160.
:::
