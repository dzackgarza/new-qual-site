::: {.theorem title="Closed graph"}
If $X, Y\in \Banach$ and $T \in L(X, Y)$ is a closed linear operator, i.e. the graph $\Gamma(T) \subseteq X\times Y$ is closed, then $T$ is bounded.
:::
