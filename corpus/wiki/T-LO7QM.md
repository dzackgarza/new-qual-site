::: {.theorem title="Stokes Theorem"}
Suppose $\omega \da f(z)\dz$ is a differential 1-form on an orientable manifold $\Omega$, then $$\int_{\bd\Omega}\omega = \int_\Omega d\omega \qquad \text{i.e.} \qquad \int_{\bd\Omega}f(z)\dz = \int_\Omega d(f(z)\dz)$$
:::
