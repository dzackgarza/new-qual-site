::: problem
Using mathematical induction, show that for each positive integer $n$,
$$1^2 - 2^2 + 3^2 - \dots + (-1)^{n-1}n^2 = (-1)^{n-1}n(n+1)/2.$$
(Hint: in the induction step, consider separately the cases where $n$ is even or odd.)
:::
