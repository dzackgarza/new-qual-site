::: problem
Let $f(x) = x^7 - 3\in \QQ[x]$ and $E/\QQ$ be a splitting field of $f$ with $\alpha \in E$ a root of $f$.

a. Show that $E$ contains a primitive 7th root of unity.

b. Show that $E\neq \QQ(\alpha)$.
:::
