Let $X$ be the adjunction space formed from the disjoint union of the normal, path-connected space $A$ and the unit ball $B^2$ by means of a continuous map $f: S^1 \to A$. (See [[E-HYC1G]].) Show that $X$ satisfies the hypotheses of Theorem 72.1. Where do you use the fact that $A$ is normal?

::: {.remark}
Munkres, *Topology*, §72 Exercise 2.
:::
