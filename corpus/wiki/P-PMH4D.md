Show that
\[
\int_{0}^{\infty} \frac{x^{a-1}}{1+x^{n}} d x=\frac{\pi}{n \sin \frac{a \pi}{n}}
\]
using complex analysis, $0<a<n$.
Here $n$ is a positive integer.
