:::{.exercise title="?"}
Give an example of a non-equicontinuous family.

:::

:::{.solution}
Take $f_k(z) \da z^k$ on $[0, 1]$ -- fix any $z_0\in [0, 1)$, then $\abs{f_k(1) - f_k(x_0)} \convergesto{k\to\infty} 1$.
:::
