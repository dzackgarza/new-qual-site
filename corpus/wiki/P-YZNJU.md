::: problem
Use de Moivre's theorem (i.e. $\left(e^{i \theta}\right)^{n}==\cos n \theta+i \sin n \theta$, or $\left.(\cos \theta+i \sin \theta)^{n}=\cos n \theta+i \sin n \theta\right)$ to find the sum

$$
\sin x+\sin 2 x+\cdots+\sin n x
$$
:::
