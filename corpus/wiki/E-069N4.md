::: {.exercise title="Munkres §26.8"}

Theorem.
Let $f: X \to Y$; let $Y$ be compact Hausdorff.
Then $f$ is continuous if and only if the graph of $f$,

$$
G_f = \ts{x \times f(x) \mid x \in X},
$$

is closed in $X \times Y$.
[Hint: If $G_f$ is closed and $V$ is a neighborhood of $f(x_0)$, then the intersection of $G_f$ and $X \times (Y - V)$ is closed. Apply Exercise 7.]
:::
