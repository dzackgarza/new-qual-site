::: problem
Let $F = \FF_2$ and let $\bar F$ denote its algebraic closure.

a. Show that $\bar F$ is not a finite extension of $F$.

b. Suppose that $\alpha \in \bar F$ satisfies $\alpha^{17} = 1$ and $\alpha\neq 1$.
Show that $F(\alpha)/F$ has degree 8.
:::
