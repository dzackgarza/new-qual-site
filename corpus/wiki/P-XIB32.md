::: {.problem}
Suppose that $\alpha$ is a root in $\mathbb C$ of $P(x) = x^{17} - 2$.
How many field homomorphisms are there from $\mathbb Q (\alpha)$ to:

1. $\mathbb C$,

2. $\mathbb R$,

3. $\overline{\mathbb Q}$, the algebraic closure of $\mathbb Q$?
:::
