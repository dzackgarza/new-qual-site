:::{.proposition title="One-step module morphism test"}
A map $\phi: M\to N$ is a morphism in $\rmod$ iff
\[
\phi(r.x + y) = r.\phi(x) + \phi(y) \in N && \forall r\in R, x,y\in M
.\]
:::
