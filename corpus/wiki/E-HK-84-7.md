::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 8.4.7"}
Let $V$ be a complex inner product space and $T$ a self-adjoint linear operator on $V$. Show that

(a) $||\alpha + iT\alpha|| = ||\alpha - iT\alpha||$ for every $\alpha$ in $V$.

(b) $\alpha + iT\alpha = \beta + iT\beta$ if and only if $\alpha = \beta$.

(c) $I + iT$ is non-singular.

(d) $I - iT$ is non-singular.

(e) Now suppose $V$ is finite-dimensional, and prove that
\[
U = (I - iT)(I + iT)^{-1}
\]
is a unitary operator; $U$ is called the Cayley transform of $T$. In a certain sense, $U = f(T)$, where $f(x) = (1 - ix)/(1 + ix)$.
:::
