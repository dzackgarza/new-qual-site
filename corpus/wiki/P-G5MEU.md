Let $K = \QQ[\sqrt 2 + \sqrt 5]$.

a. Find $[K: \QQ]$.

b. Show that $K/\QQ$ is Galois, and find the Galois group $G$ of $K/\QQ$.

c. Exhibit explicitly the correspondence between subgroups of $G$ and intermediate fields between $\QQ$ and $K$.
