::: problem
Let $\lambda$ be a fixed complex number with $\operatorname{Re}(\lambda)>1$.
How many solutions does the equation
\[
e^z=z+\lambda
\]
have in the left half-plane $\{z\in\mathbb{C}:\operatorname{Re}(z)<0\}$?
:::
