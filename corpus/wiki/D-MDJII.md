::: {.definition}
A subset $E\subseteq \RR^n$ is **Lebesgue measurable** iff for every $\eps> 0$ there exists an open set $O \supseteq E$ such that $m_*(O\setminus E) < \eps$.
In this case, we define $m(E) \da m_*(E)$.
:::
