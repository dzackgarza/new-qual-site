::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 5.3.7"}
An $n \times n$ matrix $A$ is called triangular if $A_{ij} = 0$ whenever $i > j$ or if $A_{ij} = 0$ whenever $i < j$.
Prove that the determinant of a triangular matrix is the product $A_{11}A_{22} \cdots A_{nn}$ of its diagonal entries.
:::
