::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 9.3.5"}
Let
\[
A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & \frac{1}{2} \\ \frac{1}{2} & \frac{1}{3} \end{bmatrix}.
\]

(a) Show that $A$ is positive.

(b) Let $V$ be the space of $2 \times 1$ real matrices, with the inner product $(X \mid Y) = Y^tAX$.
Find an orthonormal basis for $V$, by applying the Gram-Schmidt process to the basis $\{X_1, X_2\}$ defined by
\[
X_1 = \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix}, \quad X_2 = \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}.
\]

(c) Find an invertible $2 \times 2$ real matrix $P$ such that $A = P^tP$.
:::
