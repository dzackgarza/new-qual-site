:::{.exercise title="?"}
Consider the Vandermonde matrix:
\[
A \da 
\left(\begin{array}{ccc}
1 & \cdots & 1 \\
\lambda_{1} & \cdots & \lambda_{k} \\
\vdots & & \vdots \\
\lambda_{1}^{k-1} & \cdots & \lambda_{k}^{k-1}
\end{array}\right)
.\]

Show that
\[
\det A = \prod_{i < j} (\lambda_i - \lambda_j)
.\]
:::
