::: {.definition}
A map $f: X\to Y$ inducing isomorphisms
\[
f_*: \pi_n(X, x_0) \mapsvia{\sim} \pi_n(Y, f(x_0))
\]
for every $n\geq 0$ and every basepoint.
Such an $f$ induces isomorphisms on all homology and cohomology groups.
By Whitehead's theorem, a weak homotopy equivalence between CW complexes is an honest homotopy equivalence.
:::

::: {.concept}
See Hatcher, §4.1, p. 352; Whitehead is Theorem 4.5, p. 346.
:::
