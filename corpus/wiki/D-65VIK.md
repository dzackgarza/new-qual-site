:::{.definition title="Zeros"}
If $f$ is analytic and not identically zero on $\Omega$ with $f(z_0) = 0$, then there exists a nonvanishing holomorphic function $g$ such that
\[
f(z) = (z-z_0)^n g(z)
.\]
We refer to $z_0$ as a **zero of order $n$**.
:::
