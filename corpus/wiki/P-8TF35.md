::: problem
Given two points $a$ and $b$ of a space $S$, a collection of sets $A_1,\ldots,A_n$ in $S$ is called a simple chain from $a$ to $b$ if $a\in A_1$, $b\in A_n$, and $A_i\cap A_j\neq\varnothing$ if and only if $|i-j|\leq 1$.

Prove that if $\{U_\alpha\}$ is a collection of open sets covering $S$ and $S$ is a connected space, then there is a simple chain of elements in $\{U_\alpha\}$ joining $a$ and $b$ for any $a$ and $b$.
Hint: Consider the set $C_a$ of all $b$ so that there is a simple chain of elements joining $a$ and $b$.
Prove that $C_a$ is open and closed.
:::
