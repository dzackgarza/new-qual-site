::: {.exercise title="Munkres §46.6"}

Show that in the compact-open topology, $\mathcal{C}(X, Y)$ is Hausdorff if $Y$ is Hausdorff, and regular if $Y$ is regular.
[Hint: If $\overline{U} \subset V$, then $\overline{S(C, U)} \subset S(C, V)$.]
:::
