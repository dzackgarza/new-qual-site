::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 8.5.6"}
Let $T$ be a normal operator on a finite-dimensional complex inner product space.
Prove that $T$ is self-adjoint, positive, or unitary according as every characteristic value of $T$ is real, positive, or of absolute value 1. (Use Theorem 22 to reduce to a similar question about diagonal matrices.)
:::
