::: exercise
Suppose $f\colon[0,1]\to\mathbb{R}$ is continuous and $\int_0^1 f(t)t^n\,dt=0$ for all $n=0,1,2,\ldots$.
Show that $f(t)=0$ for all $t\in[0,1]$.
