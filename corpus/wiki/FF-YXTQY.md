::: {.fact}
Counterexample: $ \left\{{1 \over n}\right\}, {\mathbf{Z}} $ are nowhere dense, $ {\mathbf{Q}}, {\mathbf{Z}}\cup\qty{(a, b)\cap{\mathbf{Q}}} $ is *not* nowhere dense
:::
