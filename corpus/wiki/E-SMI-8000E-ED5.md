::: {.exercise title="Smith 8000e Euclidean domains 5"}
Assume $R$ is a Euclidean domain. Prove every $m \times n$ matrix over $R$ can be diagonalized by invertible row and column operations.

[Hint: use induction on the size of the upper left entry of the matrix instead of on the number of prime factors.]
:::
