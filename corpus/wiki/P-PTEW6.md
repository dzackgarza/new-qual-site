::: {.problem title="Hungerford 5.1.14"}
\envlist

1. If $F = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt 2, \sqrt 3)$, compute $[F: \mathbb{Q}]$ and find a basis of $F/\mathbb{Q}$.

2. Do the same for $\mathbb{Q}(i, \sqrt 3, \zeta_3)$ where $\zeta_3$ is a complex third root of 1.
:::
