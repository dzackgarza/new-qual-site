::: {.proposition}
The space $X = C([0, 1])$, continuous functions $f: [0, 1] \to \RR$, equipped with the norm
\[
\norm{f}_\infty \da \sup_{x\in [0, 1]} \abs{f(x)}
\]
is a **complete** metric space.
:::
