::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 10.4.13"}
Let $g$ be a non-singular bilinear form on a finite-dimensional vector space $V$. Suppose $T$ is an invertible linear operator on $V$ and that $f$ is the bilinear form on $V$ given by $f(\alpha, \beta) = g(\alpha, T\beta)$. If $U$ is a linear operator on $V$, find necessary and sufficient conditions for $U$ to preserve $f$.
:::
