::: problem
Let $(X,d)$ be a compact metric space.
Suppose that $f_n \colon X \to [0,\infty)$ is a sequence of continuous functions with $f_n(x) \geq f_{n+1}(x)$ for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $x \in X$, and such that $f_n \to 0$ pointwise on $X$.
Prove that $\{f_n\}_{n=1}^\infty$ converges uniformly on $X$.
:::
