::: problem
Let $(X,d)$ be a compact metric space.
Suppose that $f_n \colon X \to [0,\infty)$ is a sequence of continuous functions with $f_n(x) \geq f_{n+1}(x)$ for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $x \in X$, and such that $f_n \to 0$ pointwise on $X$.
Prove that $\{f_n\}_{n=1}^\infty$ converges uniformly on $X$.
:::
::: {.solution}
> **AI-Generated Solution**

<1>1. Reduce to Dini's theorem.
Proof: this is Dini's theorem: a monotone pointwise-convergent sequence of continuous functions on a compact space converges uniformly.
We prove it directly for this statement ($f_n \searrow 0$). <1>2. For $\eps > 0$ and each $n$, the set $U_n \da \{x : f_n(x) < \eps\}$ is open.
Proof: $f_n$ is continuous, and $U_n = f_n^{-1}(-\infty, \eps)$ is the preimage of an open set.
<1>3. The sets $U_n$ increase and cover $X$.
Proof: $f_n \ge f_{n+1}$ gives $U_n \subseteq U_{n+1}$.
For each $x$, $f_n(x) \to 0$, so $f_n(x) < \eps$ eventually, i.e. $x \in U_n$ for all large $n$; hence $X = \cup_n U_n$.
<1>4. Some $U_N$ already equals $X$.
Proof: $\{U_n\}$ is an open cover of the compact space $X$ (<1>2, <1>3), so it has a finite subcover $U_{n_1} \subseteq \cdots \subseteq U_{n_k}$; since the $U_n$ are nested, $X = U_{n_k}$, and taking $N = n_k$ works.
<1>5. Uniform convergence: $f_n \to 0$ uniformly on $X$.
Proof: by <1>4, for $\eps > 0$ there is $N$ with $f_N(x) < \eps$ for all $x$; monotonicity gives $0 \le f_n(x) \le f_N(x) < \eps$ for all $n \ge N$ and all $x$.
Thus $\sup_X f_n \le \eps$ for $n \ge N$.
<1>6. Q.E.D.
:::
