::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 4.3.1"}
Use the Lagrange interpolation formula to find a polynomial $f$ with real coefficients such that $f$ has degree $\leq 3$ and $f(-1) = -6$, $f(0) = 2$, $f(1) = -2$, $f(2) = 6$.
:::
