::: {.definition title="Projective Resolution"}
An exact sequence
\[
\cdots \to P_2 \to P_1 \to P_0 \to M \to 0
\]
with every $P_i$ projective; taking each $P_i$ free gives a **free resolution**, and every module admits one.
Any two projective resolutions of $M$ are chain homotopy equivalent over $\id_M$, which is what makes $\Ext$ and $\Tor$ independent of the choice.
:::

::: {.concept}
See Hatcher, §3.1, pp. 193-195.
:::
