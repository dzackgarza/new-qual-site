::: {.theorem}
$$\begin{align*}
m(X) = \infty \implies
&\text{No inclusions (use $p\dash$test)} \\
m(X) < \infty \implies
&L^\infty(X) \subset L^2(X) \subset L^1(X) \\
&\ell^1(\ZZ) \subset \ell^2(\ZZ) \subset \ell^\infty(\ZZ)
\end{align*}$$
:::
