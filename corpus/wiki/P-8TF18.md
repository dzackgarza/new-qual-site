::: problem
Fix an integer $n$.
Let $X$ be the surface obtained from the cylinder $S^1\times[0,1]$ by identifying the point $(z,0)$ with the point $(z^n,1)$ for all $z\in S^1$.
Find the fundamental group of $X$.
:::
