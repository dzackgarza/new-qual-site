:::{.proposition title="?"}
If either of $\dim_k V, \dim_k W$ is finite, then
\[
V\dual \tensor_k W\dual \mapsvia{\sim} (V\tensor W)\dual \\
v\tensor w &\mapsto (x \tensor y \mapsto v(x) w(y))
.\]

:::
