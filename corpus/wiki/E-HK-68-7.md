::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 6.8.7"}
Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional vector space over $F$, and let $T$ be a linear operator on $V$. Suppose that $T$ commutes with every diagonalizable linear operator on $V$. Prove that $T$ is a scalar multiple of the identity operator.
:::
