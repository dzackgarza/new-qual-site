::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 8.5.3"}
For
\[
A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 \\ 2 & 3 & 4 \\ 3 & 4 & 5 \end{bmatrix}
\]
there is a real orthogonal matrix $P$ such that $P^tAP = D$ is diagonal. Find such a diagonal matrix $D$.
:::
