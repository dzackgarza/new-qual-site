::: {.proposition title="Ratio Test"}
Consider $\sum c_k z^k$, set $R = \lim \abs{c_{k+1} \over c_k}$, and recall the **ratio test**:

- $R\in (0, 1) \implies$ convergence.

- $R\in (1, \infty] \implies$ divergence.

- $R=1$ yields no information.
:::
