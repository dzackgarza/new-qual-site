::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 3.6.3"}
Let $S$ be a set, $F$ a field, and $V(S; F)$ the space of all functions from $S$ into $F$:
\[
(f + g)(x) = f(x) + g(x), \qquad (cf)(x) = cf(x).
\]
Let $W$ be any $n$-dimensional subspace of $V(S; F)$. Show that there exist points $x_1, \ldots, x_n$ in $S$ and functions $f_1, \ldots, f_n$ in $W$ such that $f_i(x_j) = \delta_{ij}$.
:::
