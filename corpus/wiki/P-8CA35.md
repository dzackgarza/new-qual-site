::: problem
Let $g$ be analytic for $|z|<1$ and $|g(z)|<1$ for $|z|<1$.
Prove that if $g$ has more than one fixed point in $|z|<1$, then $g$ must be the identity function.

(For partial credit, you may assume that $g$ is actually analytic on $|z|\le 1$ with $|g(z)|<1$ for $|z|=1$.)
:::
