Let $X$ be a space that is locally $m$-euclidean. Show that $X$ is metrizable if and only if $X$ is paracompact Hausdorff.

::: {.remark}
Munkres, *Topology*, §50 Supplementary Exercises (Locally Euclidean Spaces) Exercise 8.
:::
