::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 5.4.10"}
Let $A$ be an $n \times n$ matrix over the field $F$.
Prove that there are at most $n$ distinct scalars $c$ in $F$ such that $\det(cI - A) = 0$.
:::
