::: {.theorem title="$S_n$ is Solvable Exactly Below Degree Five"}
$S_n$ is solvable if and only if $n\leq 4$.

For $n\geq 5$ the derived series stalls: $[A_n, A_n] = A_n$ because $A_n$ is simple and nonabelian, so $S_n \geq A_n \geq A_n \geq \cdots$ never reaches the identity.
:::
