::: {.theorem}
If $f, g$ are holomorphic on $\bar{D}(z_0)$ and 
$$
\abs{f - g} < \abs{f} + \abs{g} \quad\text{on}\quad \bd D
,$$ 
then $f,g\neq 0$ on $\bd D$ and have the same number of zeros.
:::
