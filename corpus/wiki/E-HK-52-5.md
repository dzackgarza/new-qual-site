::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 5.2.5"}
Let $A$ be a $2 \times 2$ matrix over a field $F$, and suppose that $A^2 = 0$. Show for each scalar $c$ that $\det(cI - A) = c^2$.
:::
