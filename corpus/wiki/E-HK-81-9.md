::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 8.1.9"}
Let $V$ be a real or complex vector space with an inner product. Show that the quadratic form determined by the inner product satisfies the parallelogram law
\[
||\alpha + \beta||^2 + ||\alpha - \beta||^2 = 2||\alpha||^2 + 2||\beta||^2.
\]
:::
