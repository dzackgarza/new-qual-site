::: {.definition title="Ring Morphism"}
A morphism $f\in \Ring(X, Y)$ satisfies:

- $f(1_X) = 1_Y$

- $f(a+b) = f(a) + f(b)$

- $f(ab) = f(a)f(b)$
:::
