:::{.definition title="The symmetric group"}
The transposition presentation:
\[
S_n \da \gens{ \sigma_1, \cdots, \sigma_{n-1} \st \sigma_i^2, [\sigma_i, \sigma_j]\, (j\neq i+1), \sigma_i \sigma_{i+1} \sigma_i = \sigma_{i+1} \sigma_i \sigma_{i+1} } 
.\]

:::
