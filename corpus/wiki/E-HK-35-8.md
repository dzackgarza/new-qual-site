::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 3.5.8"}
Let $W$ be the subspace of $\RR^5$ which is spanned by the vectors
\[
\alpha_1 = \epsilon_1 + 2\epsilon_2 + \epsilon_3, \quad \alpha_2 = \epsilon_2 + 3\epsilon_3 + 3\epsilon_4 + \epsilon_5,
\]
\[
\alpha_3 = \epsilon_1 + 4\epsilon_2 + 6\epsilon_3 + 4\epsilon_4 + \epsilon_5.
\]
Find a basis for $W^0$.
:::
