Compute the Pontryagin product structure in $H_*(L; \mathbb{Z}_p)$ where $L$ is an infinite-dimensional lens space $S^\infty / \mathbb{Z}_p$, for $p$ an odd prime, using the coproduct in $H^*(L; \mathbb{Z}_p)$.
