:::{.lemma title="Congruences for fixed points"}
If $G\actson X$ for $G$ a $p\dash$group, then letting $\Fix(G) \da \ts{x\in X \st gx=x}$, one has
\[
\size X \equiv \size \Fix(G) \mod p
.\]
:::
