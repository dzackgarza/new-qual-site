::: problem
Let $M<\infty$ and $\mathcal{F} \subseteq C[a,b]$.
Assume that each $f \in \mathcal{F}$ is differentiable on $(a,b)$ and satisfies $|f(a)| \leq M$ and $|f'(x)| \leq M$ for all $x \in (a,b)$.
Prove that $\mathcal{F}$ is equicontinuous on $[a,b]$.
:::
