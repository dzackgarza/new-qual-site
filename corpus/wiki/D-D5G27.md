:::{.definition title="Distance to a subspace"}
For $(X, d)$ a metric space, $S \subset X$, and $f\in X$, the **distance from $f$ to $S$** is
\[
\dist(f, S) \da \inf_{s\in S} d(f, s)
.\]
:::
