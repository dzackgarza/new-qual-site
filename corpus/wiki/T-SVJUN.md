::: {.theorem title="Recognizing Direct Products"}
We have $G \cong H \times K$ when

1. $H, K \normal G$

2. $G = HK$.

3. $H\intersect K = \theset{e} \subset G$

> Note: can relax to $[h,k] = 1$ for all $h, k$.
:::
