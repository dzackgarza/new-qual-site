::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 5.4.7"}
Let $A$ be an $n \times n$ matrix over $K$, a commutative ring with identity. Suppose $A$ has the block form
\[
A = \begin{bmatrix}
A_1 & 0 & \cdots & 0 \\
0 & A_2 & \cdots & 0 \\
\vdots & \vdots & & \vdots \\
0 & 0 & \cdots & A_k
\end{bmatrix}
\]
where $A_j$ is an $r_j \times r_j$ matrix. Prove
\[
\det A = (\det A_1)(\det A_2) \cdots (\det A_k).
\]
:::
