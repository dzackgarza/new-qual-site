::: {.exercise title="Sum formulas: $1/n^2$"}
Show
\[
\sum_{n \geq 1} \frac{1}{n^{2}}=\frac{\pi^{2}}{6}
\]
by integrating $\pi \cot(\pi z)z^{-2}$.
:::
