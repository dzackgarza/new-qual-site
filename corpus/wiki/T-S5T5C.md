::: {.theorem title="Sylow 3"}
\envlist

1. $n_p \divides m~$, and in particular, $n_p \leq m$.

2. $n_p \equiv 1 \mod p$.

3. $n_p = [G : N_G(S_p)]$ where $N_G$ is the normalizer.
:::
