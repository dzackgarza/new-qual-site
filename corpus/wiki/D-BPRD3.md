:::{.definition title="Normal Closure of a Subgroup"}
The smallest normal subgroup of $G$ containing $H$:
\[
H^G \definedas \{gHg^{-1}: g\in G\} = \bigcap \theset{N: H \leq N \normal G }
.\]
:::
