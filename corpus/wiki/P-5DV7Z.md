12. $\displaystyle \int \frac {x}{x^2+9} ~dx = \color{blue} {\frac 1 2 \ln(x^2 + 9)}$

- **Solution:** $u = x^2 + 9$, $du = 2x ~dx$

- **Solution:** $\frac {x}{x^2+9} ~dx = \frac {1}{2} \cdot \frac {1}{u} ~du$
