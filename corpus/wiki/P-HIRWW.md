::: {.problem title="Hungerford 2.4.1"}
Let $G$ be a group and $A \trianglelefteq G$ be a normal abelian subgroup.
Show that $G/A$ acts on $A$ by conjugation and construct a homomorphism $\varphi: G/A \to \mathrm{Aut}(A)$.
:::
