Show that if $\tilde{H}^n(X; \mathbb{Q})$ and $\tilde{H}^n(X; \mathbb{Z}_p)$ are zero for all $n$ and all primes $p$, then $\tilde{H}_n(X; \mathbb{Z}) = 0$ for all $n$, and hence $\tilde{H}^n(X; G) = 0$ for all $G$ and $n$.
