::: problem
Given two spaces $X$ and $Y$, let $\pi_X:X\times Y\to X$ and $\pi_Y:X\times Y\to Y$ be the two standard projection maps.

(a) Show that, for any other space $Z$, a function $f:Z\to X\times Y$ is continuous if and only if $\pi_X\circ f$ and $\pi_Y\circ f$ are continuous.

(b) Use this to show that the cartesian product of two path-connected topological spaces is path-connected.
:::
