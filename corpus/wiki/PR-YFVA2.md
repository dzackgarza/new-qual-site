::: {.proposition title="Functional analytic properties of $L^1$ and $L^2$"}
For any measure space $(X, \mcm, \mu)$,

- $L^1(X)$ is Banach space.

- $L^2(X)$ is a (possibly non-separable) Hilbert space.
:::
