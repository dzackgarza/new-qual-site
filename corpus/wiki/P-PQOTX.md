::: problem
Show that
\[
\int_{0}^{2 \pi} \frac{d \theta}{(a+\cos \theta)^{2}}=\frac{2 \pi a}{\left(a^{2}-1\right)^{3 / 2}}, \quad \text { whenever } a>1
.\]
:::
