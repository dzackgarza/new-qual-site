::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 8.4.13"}
Let $V$ and $W$ be finite-dimensional inner product spaces having the same dimension.
Let $U$ be an isomorphism of $V$ onto $W$.
Show that:

(a) The mapping $T \to UTU^{-1}$ is an isomorphism of the vector space $L(V, V)$ onto the vector space $L(W, W)$.

(b) $\operatorname{trace}(UTU^{-1}) = \operatorname{trace}(T)$ for each $T$ in $L(V, V)$.

(c) $UT_{\alpha,\beta}U^{-1} = T_{U\alpha, U\beta}$ ($T_{\alpha,\beta}$ defined in Exercise 10).

(d) $(UTU^{-1})^* = UT^*U^{-1}$.
:::
