:::{.theorem title="Characterization of conformal maps"}
Conformal maps $\DD\to\DD$ have the form
\[
g(z) = \lambda {a-z \over 1 - \bar a z}, \quad \abs{a} < 1, \quad \abs{\lambda} = 1
.\]

:::{.remark}
Stein and Shakarchi, *Complex Analysis*, Ch. 8 Theorem 2.2.
:::
:::
