:::{.theorem title="Schur's Lemma"}
If $M \in \modsleft{G}$ is an irreducible representation of $G$ with $\dim_k M < \infty$ and $k=\bar{k}$, then there is an isomorphism
\[
M & \mapsvia{\sim} \Aut_G(M, M)
.\]
:::
