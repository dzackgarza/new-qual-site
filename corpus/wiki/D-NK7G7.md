:::{.definition title="Center"}
The **center** of $G$ is defined as
\[
Z(G) = \ts{ g\in G \st [g, h] = e \, \forall h\in H}
= \ts{ g\in G \st Z(g) = G } 
,\]
the subgroup of *central* elements:
those $g\in G$ that commute with every element of $G$.
:::
