::: {.theorem title="Extreme Value Theorem"}
For $f:X \to Y$ continuous with $X$ compact and $Y$ ordered in the order topology, there exist points $c, d\in X$ such that $f(x) \in [f(c), f(d)]$ for every $x$.
:::
