Let $n$ be a natural number. Show that

$$
[1 / 2(-1+\sqrt{3} i)]^{n}+[1 / 2(-1-\sqrt{3} i)]^{n}
$$

is equal to 2 if $n$ is a multiple of 3 , and it is equal to $-1$ otherwise.
