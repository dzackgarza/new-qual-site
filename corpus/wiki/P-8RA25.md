::: problem
Let $f(x)$ be Lebesgue measurable on $\mathbb{R}$.
Suppose that $f(x)$ and $xf(x)$ are both integrable over the whole of $\mathbb{R}$.
Define
\[
F(t):=\int_{\mathbb{R}}f(x)\sin(tx)\,dx.
\]
Prove that $F(t)$ is differentiable at every $t$, and find a formula for $F'(t)$.
:::
