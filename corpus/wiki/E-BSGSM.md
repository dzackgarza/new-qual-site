::: {.exercise title="?"}
Show that normal groups absorb conjugacy classes: if $N\normal G$ and $[g_i]$ is a conjugacy class in $g$, either $[g_i] \subseteq N$ or $[g_i] \intersect N = \emptyset$.
:::
