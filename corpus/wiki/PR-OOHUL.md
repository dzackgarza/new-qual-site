:::{.proposition title="Polar Cauchy-Riemann equations"}
\[
\frac{\partial u}{\partial r}=\frac{1}{r} \frac{\partial v}{\partial \theta} \quad \text { and } \quad \frac{1}{r} \frac{\partial u}{\partial \theta}=-\frac{\partial v}{\partial r}
.\]
:::
