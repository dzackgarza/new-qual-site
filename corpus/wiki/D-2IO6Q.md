:::{.definition title="Jacobson Radical"}
The **Jacobson radical** $\jacobsonrad{R}$ is the intersection of all maximal ideals, i.e.
\[  
\jacobsonrad{R} = \Intersect_{\mfm \in \mspec R} \mfm
.\]
:::
