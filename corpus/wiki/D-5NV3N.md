::: {.definition title="Central Series"}
A **central series** for a group $G$ is a terminating normal series $G \to G^1 \to \cdots \to \theset{e}$ such that each quotient is **central**, i.e. $[G, G^i] \leq G^{i-1}$ for all $i$.
:::
