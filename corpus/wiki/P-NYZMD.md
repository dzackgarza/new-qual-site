Consider $L^2([0, 1])$ and define
\[
e_0(x) &= 1 \\
e_1(x) &= \sqrt{3}(2x-1)
.\]

a. Show that $\ts{e_0, e_1}$ is an orthonormal system.

b. Show that the polynomial $p(x)$ where $\deg(p) = 1$ which is closest to $f(x) = x^2$ in $L^2([0, 1])$ is given by
\[
h(x) = x - {1\over 6}
.\]
