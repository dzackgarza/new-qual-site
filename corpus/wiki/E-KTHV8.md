::: {.exercise title="Munkres §13.1"}

Let $X$ be a topological space; let $A$ be a subset of $X$.
Suppose that for each $x \in A$ there is an open set $U$ containing $x$ such that $U \subset A$.
Show that $A$ is open in $X$.
:::
