::: problem
Suppose $V$ and $W$ are vector spaces and $T: V \to W$ is a linear transformation. Suppose $v_1, \dots, v_k \in V$. Prove that if $T(v_1), \dots, T(v_k)$ form a linearly independent set in $W$, then $v_1, \dots, v_k$ form a linearly independent set in $V$.
:::
