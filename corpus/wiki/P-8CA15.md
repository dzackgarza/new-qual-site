::: problem
Let $\Omega$ be a star-shaped domain of $\mathbb{C}$.
Let $\{f_n\}_{n=1}^\infty$ be a sequence of injective analytic functions on $\Omega$ that converges uniformly on every compact subset of $\Omega$ to a function $f$.
Prove that:

(a) $f$ is analytic on $\Omega$, and

(b) $f$ is either injective or a constant.
:::
