How many path-connected 2-fold covering spaces does $S^1 \vee \RP 2$ have?
What are the total spaces?

::: {.solution}
First note that $\pi_1(X) = \pi_1(S^1) \ast_{\pt} \pi_1(\RP 2)$ by Van-Kampen, and this is equal to $\ZZ \ast \ZZ_2$.
:::
