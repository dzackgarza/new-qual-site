:::{.proposition title="Homology of 3-manifolds"}
Let $M^3$ be a 3-manifold, then its homology is given by the following (by cases):

- Orientable: $H_* = (\ZZ, \ZZ^r, \ZZ^r, \ZZ)$

- Nonorientable: $H_* = (\ZZ, \ZZ^r, \ZZ^{r-1} \oplus \ZZ_2, \ZZ)$

:::
