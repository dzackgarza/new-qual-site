Consider the metric space $(\mathbb{Q},d)$
where $\mathbb{Q}$ denotes the rational numbers and $d(x,y)=|x-y|$.
Let $E=\{x\in\mathbb{Q}:x>0,\,2<x^2<3\}$. Is $E$ closed and bounded
in $\mathbb{Q}$? Is $E$ compact in $\mathbb{Q}$?
