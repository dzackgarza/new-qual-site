:::{.proposition}
If $H\leq K \leq G$, then 
\[
\size  G = [G:1] \geq [G:H] \geq [G:K] \geq [G:G] = 1
.\]
In particular, If $H, K\leq G$ are just arbitrary, since $H \intersect K \leq H, K$ we have $[H: H \intersect K] \geq [G:H] \text{ and } [G:K]$.
:::
