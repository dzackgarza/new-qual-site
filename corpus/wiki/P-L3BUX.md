:::{.problem title="?"}
Let $(X, d)$ be a compact metric space, and let $f : X \to X$ be an isometry: 
\[
\forall~ x, y \in X, \qquad d(f (x), f (y)) = d(x, y)
.\]
Prove that $f$ is a bijection.

:::
