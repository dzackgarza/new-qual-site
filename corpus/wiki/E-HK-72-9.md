::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 7.2.9"}
Let $A$ be the real matrix
\[
A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 3 & 3 \\ 3 & 1 & 3 \\ -3 & -3 & -5 \end{bmatrix}.
\]
Find an invertible $3 \times 3$ real matrix $P$ such that $P^{-1}AP$ is in rational form.
:::
