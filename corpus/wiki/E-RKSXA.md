::: {.exercise title="Munkres §78.2"}


Let $H^2$ be the subspace of $\mathbb{R}^2$ consisting of all points $(x_1, x_2)$ with $x_2 \geq 0$. A 2-manifold with boundary (or surface with boundary) is a Hausdorff space $X$ with a countable basis such that each point $x$ of $X$ has a neighborhood homeomorphic with an open set of $\mathbb{R}^2$ or $H^2$. The boundary of $X$ (denoted $\partial X$) consists of those points $x$ such that $x$ has no neighborhood homeomorphic with an open set of $\mathbb{R}^2$.

(a) Show that no point of $H^2$ of the form $(x_1, 0)$ has a neighborhood (in $H^2$) that is homeomorphic to an open set of $\mathbb{R}^2$.

(b) Show that $x \in \partial X$ if and only if there is a homeomorphism $h$ mapping a neighborhood of $x$ onto an open set of $H^2$ such that $h(x) \in \mathbb{R} \times 0$.

(c) Show that $\partial X$ is a 1-manifold.
:::
