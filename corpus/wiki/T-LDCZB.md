:::{.theorem title="Riesz Representation for Hilbert Spaces"}
If $\Lambda$ is a continuous linear functional on a Hilbert space $H$, then there exists a unique $y \in H$ such that
\[
\forall x\in H,\quad \Lambda(x) = \inner{x}{y}
.\]
:::
