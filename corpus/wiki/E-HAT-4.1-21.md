For this problem it is convenient to use the notations $X^n$ for the $n$th stage in a Postnikov tower for $X$ and $X_m$ for an $(m-1)$-connected covering of $X$, where $X$ is a connected CW complex. Show that $(X^n)_m \simeq (X_m)^n$, so the notation $X_m^n$ is unambiguous. Thus $\pi_i(X_m^n) \approx \pi_i(X)$ for $m \leq i \leq n$ and all other homotopy groups of $X_m^n$ are zero.
