::: problem
Prove that if $f \in \mathcal{R}$ on $[a,b]$ and $\alpha \in C^1[a,b]$, then the Riemann integral $\int_a^b f(x)\alpha'(x)dx$ exists and $$\int_a^b f(x) d\alpha(x)= \int_a^b f(x)\alpha'(x)dx.$$
:::
