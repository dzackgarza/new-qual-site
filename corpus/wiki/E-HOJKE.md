::: {.exercise title="?"}
Show that TFAE:

- $A\in \Field$

- $A$ is a simple ring, so $\Id(A) = \ts{ 0, A }$.

- If $B\in \Field$ is nonzero then every ring morphism $A\to B$ is injective.
