:::{.theorem title="Mean Value Theorem"}
If $f: [a, b] \to \RR$ is continuous on a closed interval and differentiable on $(a, b)$, then there exists $\xi \in [a, b]$ such that
\[
f(b) - f(a) = f'(\xi)(b-a)
.\]


:::
