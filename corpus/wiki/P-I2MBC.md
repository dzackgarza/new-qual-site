::: problem
Now we'll take the tensor product of two abelian groups, that is, $\ZZ\dash$modules.
Take $\ZZ/p\ZZ$ and $\ZZ/q\ZZ$, where $p$ and $q$ are distinct primes.
What is their tensor product?
:::
