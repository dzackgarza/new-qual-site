::: {.definition}
A family of functions $\mcf \da \ts{f_j}_{j\in J}$ is **normal** iff every sequence $\ts{f_k}$ has a subsequence that converges locally uniformly, i.e. $\ts{f_{k_i}}$ converges uniformly on every compact subset.
:::
