:::{.proposition title="Composite Extensions"}
If $F/k$ is finite and Galois and $L/k$ is arbitrary, then $FL/L$ is Galois and
\[
\Gal(FL/L) = \Gal(F / F\intersect L) \subset \Gal(F/k)
.\]
:::
