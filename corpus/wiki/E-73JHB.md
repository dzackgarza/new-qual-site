Show that the one-point compactification of $\mathbb{Z}_+$ is homeomorphic with the subspace $\ts{0} \cup \ts{1/n \mid n \in \mathbb{Z}_+}$ of $\mathbb{R}$.

::: {.remark}
Munkres, *Topology*, §29 Exercise 8.
:::
