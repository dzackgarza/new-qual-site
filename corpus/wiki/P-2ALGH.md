3. $\displaystyle \int \ln ^2 (x) ~dx = \color{blue} {2 x - 2 x \ln (x) + x \ln ^2 (x)}​$

- **Solution:** $u = \ln ^2 (x)​$, $v = x​$, $du = \frac {2 \ln (x)}{x} ~dx​$, $dv = dx​$
- **Solution:** $x \ln ^2 (x) - \int x \cdot \frac {2 \ln (x)}{x} ~dx = x \ln ^2 (x) - 2(x \ln (x) - x)$
