::: {.proposition title="Recognizing direct sums"}
If $M_1, M_2 \leq M$ are submodules, then $M = M_1 \oplus M_2$ if the following conditions hold:

- $M_1 + M_2 = M$

- $M_1 \intersect M_2 = 0$
:::
