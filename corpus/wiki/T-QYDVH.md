:::{.theorem title="Orbit-Stabilizer"}

\[\size  {Gx} = [G: G_x] = \size  {G} / \size {G_x} \quad \text{if $G$ is finite}
.\]

> Mnemonic: $G/G_x \cong Gx$.

:::
