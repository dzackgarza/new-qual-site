::: problem
a) Find the Taylor expansion of $f(x) = \ln(2 + x)$ about the origin.

b) Find the radius of convergence $R$ of that series.

c) Use Taylor's theorem to show that the series converges to $f(x)$ on $[0, R/2]$.
:::
