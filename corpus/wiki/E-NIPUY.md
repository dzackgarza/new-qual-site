:::{.problem title="?"}
Show $f(x) = x^{-n}$ for $n\in \ZZ_{\geq 0}$ is uniformly continuous on $[0, \infty)$.

:::

:::{.solution}
\[
x^{1\over n} - y^{1\over n} \leq (x-y)^{1\over n} \convergesto{x\to y}0
,\]
using $(a+b)^m \geq a^m + b^m$
:::
