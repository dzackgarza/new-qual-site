::: {.exercise title="Munkres §56.1"}


Given a polynomial equation

$$
x^n + a_{n-1}x^{n-1} + \dots + a_1 x + a_0 = 0
$$

with real or complex coefficients. Show that if $\abs{a_{n-1}} + \cdots + \abs{a_1} + \abs{a_0} < 1$, then all the roots of the equation lie interior to the unit ball $B^2$. [Hint: Let $g(x) = 1 + a_{n-1}x + \cdots + a_1 x^{n-1} + a_0 x^n$, and show that $g(x) \neq 0$ for $x \in B^2$.]
:::
