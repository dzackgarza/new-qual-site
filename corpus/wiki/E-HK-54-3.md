::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 5.4.3"}
An $n \times n$ matrix $A$ over a field $F$ is skew-symmetric if $A^t = -A$. If $A$ is a skew-symmetric $n \times n$ matrix with complex entries and $n$ is odd, prove that $\det A = 0$.
:::
