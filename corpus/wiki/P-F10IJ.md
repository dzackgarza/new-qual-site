::: problem
Suppose $f: A \to B$ and $g: B \to C$ are functions. Prove or give a counterexample:

a. If $f$ and $g$ are injective (one-to-one), then $g \circ f$ is injective.

b. If $g \circ f$ is injective, then $f$ and $g$ are injective.
:::
