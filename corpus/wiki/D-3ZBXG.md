:::{.definition title="Injection"}
A map $\iota:A\to B$ is **injective** if it admits a *left* inverse $p:B\to A$ satisfying $p\circ \iota = \id_A$.
Equivalently,
\[
\iota(x) = \iota(y) \in B \implies x = y \in A
.\]
:::
