:::{.definition title="Derived Functor"}
For a functor $T$ and an $R\dash$module $A$, a *left derived functor* $(L_{nT})$ is defined as $h_{n}(TP_{A})$, where $P_{A}$ is a projective resolution of $A$.
:::
