::: problem
Let $G = <a, b>$ and $H \leq G$ where $H = <aba^{-1}b^{-1},~ a^2ba^{-2}b^{-1},~ a^{-1}bab^{-1},~ aba^{-2}b^{-1}a>$.
To what well-known group is $H$ isomorphic?
:::
