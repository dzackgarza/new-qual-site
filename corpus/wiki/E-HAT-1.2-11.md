The mapping torus $T_f$ of a map $f: X \to X$ is the quotient of $X \times I$ obtained by identifying each point $(x, 0)$ with $(f(x), 1)$. In the case $X = S^1 \lor S^1$ with $f$ basepoint-preserving, compute a presentation for $\pi_1(T_f)$ in terms of the induced map $f_*: \pi_1(X) \to \pi_1(X)$. Do the same when $X = S^1 \lor S^1 \lor S^1$. [One way to do this is to regard $T_f$ as built from $X \lor S^1$ by attaching cells.]
