::: {.theorem title="Characterizations of Diagonalizability"}
$M$ is diagonalizable over $\FF \iff \min_M(x, \FF)$ splits into distinct linear factors over $\FF$, or equivalently iff all of the roots of $\min_M$ lie in $\FF$.
:::
