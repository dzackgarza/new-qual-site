::: {.lemma}
If $\size G = pq$ with $p<q$ distinct primes, then $G$ has a normal subgroup of size $q$.

This is immediate from Sylow theory: $[n_q]_q = 1, n_q \divides p, p<q$ forces $n_q = 1$.
:::
