::: {.exercise}
Prove that the size of a conjugacy class of $g_i$ is the index of its centralizer, $[G: Z(g_i)] \da [G: C_G(g_i)]$.
:::

::: solution
**Goal:** Prove that for any element $x \in G$ in a group $G$, the cardinality of its conjugacy class $\operatorname{Cl}(x)$ equals the index of its centralizer $C_G(x)$ in $G$: $|\operatorname{Cl}(x)| = [G : C_G(x)]$.

<1>1. Group action setting:
    *Proof:*
    <2>1. Let $G$ act on itself by conjugation: $(g, y) \mapsto g y g^{-1}$ for $g, y \in G$.
    <2>2. The orbit of $x \in G$ under this conjugation action is the conjugacy class:
        $$\mathcal{O}_x = \{g x g^{-1} \mid g \in G\} = \operatorname{Cl}(x).$$
    <2>3. The stabilizer (isotropy group) of $x$ under this action is:
        $$\operatorname{Stab}_G(x) = \{g \in G \mid g x g^{-1} = x\} = \{g \in G \mid g x = x g\} = C_G(x).$$

<1>2. Bijection between left cosets and conjugates:
    The map $\Phi: G / C_G(x) \to \operatorname{Cl}(x)$ defined by $\Phi(g C_G(x)) = g x g^{-1}$ is a well-defined bijection.
    *Proof:*
    <2>1. **Well-defined and Injective:** For any $g, h \in G$:
        $$g x g^{-1} = h x h^{-1} \iff h^{-1} g x g^{-1} h = x \iff (h^{-1} g) x (h^{-1} g)^{-1} = x \iff h^{-1} g \in C_G(x) \iff g C_G(x) = h C_G(x).$$
        The left-to-right implication proves injectivity, while the right-to-left implication proves well-definedness.
    <2>2. **Surjective:** Any element of $\operatorname{Cl}(x)$ is of the form $g x g^{-1} = \Phi(g C_G(x))$ for some $g \in G$.

<1>3. Conclusion:
    The bijection $\Phi$ establishes that:
    $$|\operatorname{Cl}(x)| = |G / C_G(x)| = [G : C_G(x)].$$
    For a finite group $G$, this gives $|\operatorname{Cl}(x)| = \frac{|G|}{|C_G(x)|}$. Q.E.D.
:::
