::: problem
Find Laurent series about the indicated singularity for each of the following functions.
Classify the type of singularity in each case and describe the (maximal) domain of convergence of each series:

(a) $\dfrac{z-\sin z}{z^3}$, $z=0$;

(b) $\dfrac{z}{(z+1)(z+2)}$, $z=-2$.
:::
