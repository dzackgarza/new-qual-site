:::{.definition title="Complex log"}
Define
\[
\log(z) \da \ln\qty{\abs{z}} + i\Arg(z)
,\]
where $\Arg(z)$ is the principal argument in $(-\pi, \pi]$.
Note that this is sometimes written
\[
\log(z) \da \ln\qty{\abs{r}} + i\theta
.\]
:::
