::: {.definition title="Local Ring"}
A ring $R$ is **local** iff it contains a unique maximal ideal $\mfm$, so $\mspec R = \ts{ \mfm }$.
As a consequence, there is a uniquely associated **residue field** $\kappa \da R/\mfm$.
:::
