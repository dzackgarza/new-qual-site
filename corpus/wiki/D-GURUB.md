::: {.definition title="Ring"}
A **ring** is a triple $(R, +, \cdot) \in \CRing$ such that

- $(R, +)\in \Ab\Grp$,

- $(R, \cdot) \in \Monoid$

- Distributivity: $a(b+c) = ab + ac$.
:::
