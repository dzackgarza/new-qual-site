::: {.problem}
Let $E/F$ be a Galois field extension, and let $K/F$ be an intermediate field of $E/F$.
Show that $K$ is normal over $F$ iff $\mathrm{Gal}(E/K) \trianglelefteq \mathrm{Gal}(E/F)$.
:::
