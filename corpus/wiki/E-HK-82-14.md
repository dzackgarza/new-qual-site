::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 8.2.14"}
Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional inner product space, and let $\mathfrak{B} = \{\alpha_1, \ldots, \alpha_n\}$ be an orthonormal basis for $V$.
Let $T$ be a linear operator on $V$ and $A$ the matrix of $T$ in the ordered basis $\mathfrak{B}$.
Prove that
\[
A_{ij} = (T\alpha_j \mid \alpha_i).
\]
:::
