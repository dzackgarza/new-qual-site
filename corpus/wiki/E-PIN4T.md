::: problem
9. For a field $K$ take $f \in K[x]$ and let $n=\operatorname{deg} f$.
   Prove that for a splitting field $F$ of $f$ over $K$ that $[F: K] \leq n !$.
   Furthermore prove that $[F: K]$ divides $n !$.
:::
