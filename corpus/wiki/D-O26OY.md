:::{.definition title="Characteristic"}
Using that every ring has a $\mods{\ZZ}$ structure, the **characteristic** of a ring $R$ is the smallest $n$ such that $n\actson 1_R = 0_R$, i.e. $\sum_{i=1}^n 1_R = 0_R$.

:::
