:::{.definition title="Uniform Continuity"}
For $f: (X, d_{x}) \to (Y, d_{Y})$ metric spaces,
\[
\forall \eps > 0, ~\exists \delta > 0 \text{ such that } \quad d_{X}(x_{1}, x_{2}) < \delta \implies d_{Y}(f(x_{1}), f(x_{2})) < \eps
.\]
:::
