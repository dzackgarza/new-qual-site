::: {.proposition title="Properties of the norm"}
Let $K$ be a number field and $N:K\to \ZZ$ be its norm function.

- $N(ab) = N(a)N(b)$

- $a\divides b \in K \implies N(a)\divides N(b)\in \ZZ$.

- $a\in K\units \iff N(a) = \pm 1$.
:::
