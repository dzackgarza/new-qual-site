::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 1.4.8"}
Let

$$
A = \left[ \begin{array}{c c c} 3 & - 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & - 3 & 0 \end{array} \right].
$$

For which triples $(y_{1}, y_{2}, y_{3})$ does the system $AX = Y$ have a solution?
:::
