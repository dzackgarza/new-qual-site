::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 8.1.11"}
Show that the formula
\[
\left(\sum_j a_j x^j \,\middle|\, \sum_k b_k x^k\right) = \sum_{j,k} \frac{a_j b_k}{j + k + 1}
\]
defines an inner product on the space $R[x]$ of polynomials over the field $\RR$.
Let $W$ be the subspace of polynomials of degree less than or equal to $n$.
Restrict the above inner product to $W$, and find the matrix of this inner product on $W$, relative to the ordered basis $\{1, x, x^2, \ldots, x^n\}$.
(Hint: To show that the formula defines an inner product, observe that
\[
(f \mid g) = \int_0^1 f(t)g(t)\,dt
\]
and work with the integral.)
:::
