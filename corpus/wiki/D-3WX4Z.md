::: {.definition title="Splitting an exact sequence"}
The sequences *splits* when a morphism $f_2^{-1}: C \into B$ exists.
In $\textbf{Ab}$, this means $B \cong A \oplus C$, in $\mathbf{Grp}$ it's $B \cong A \semidirect_\phi C$.
:::
