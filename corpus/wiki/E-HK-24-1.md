::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 2.4.1"}
Show that the vectors
\[
\alpha_1 = (1, 1, 0, 0), \quad \alpha_2 = (0, 0, 1, 1),
\]
\[
\alpha_3 = (1, 0, 0, 4), \quad \alpha_4 = (0, 0, 0, 2)
\]
form a basis for $\RR^4$. Find the coordinates of each of the standard basis vectors in the ordered basis $\{\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3, \alpha_4\}$.
:::
