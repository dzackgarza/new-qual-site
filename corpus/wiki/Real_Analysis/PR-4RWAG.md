:::{.proposition}
Uniformly Cauchy iff uniformly convergent, i.e.
\[
\norm{f_n - f_m} \convergesto{m, n\to \infty} 0 \iff \exists f,\, \norm{f_n - f} \convergesto{n\to\infty} 0
.\]

:::
