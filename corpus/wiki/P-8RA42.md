::: problem
Let $f$ be continuously differentiable on $\mathbb{R}$, and suppose that $f$ and $f'$ are both integrable. Prove
\[
\lim_{k \rightarrow \infty}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\sin(kx)f(x)\, dx = 0.
\]
:::
