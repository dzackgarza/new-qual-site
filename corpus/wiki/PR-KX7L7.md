:::{.proposition title="?"}
If $T:V \injects W$ is injective, then $T\tensor \one_X: V\tensor X \injects W\tensor X$ is also injective for any $X$.
Thus $F(\wait) = (\wait \tensor X)$ is right-exact for any $X$.
:::
