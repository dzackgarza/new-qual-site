::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 5.3.6"}
Let $A$ be a $2 \times 2$ matrix over a field. Prove that $\det(I + A) = 1 + \det A$ if and only if $\operatorname{trace}(A) = 0$.
:::
