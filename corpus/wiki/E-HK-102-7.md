::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 10.2.7"}
Let $q$ be the quadratic form on $\RR^3$ given by
\[
q(x_1, x_2, x_3) = x_1x_2 + 2x_1x_3 + x_3^2.
\]
Find an invertible linear operator $U$ on $\RR^3$ such that
\[
(U^{\dagger}q)(x_1, x_2, x_3) = x_1^2 - x_2^2 + x_3^2.
\]
(Hint: Express $U$ as a product of operators similar to those used in Exercises 5 and 6.)
:::
