:::{.proposition title="Poincaré Duality for manifolds"}
For $M^n$ a closed orientable manifold without boundary and $\FF$ a field,
\[
H_k(M^n; \FF) \cong H^{n-k}(M^n; \FF)
\iff
M^n \text{ is closed and orientable}
.\]

:::
