::: problem
2. $\displaystyle \int \frac {x^3}{\sqrt {25-x^2}} ~dx = \color {blue} {\frac {1}{3} (\sqrt {25-x^2})^3 - 25 \sqrt {25-x^2}}$

- **Solution:** $\sin (u) = \frac {1}{5} x$, $\cos (u) ~du = \frac {1}{5} ~dx$

- **Solution:** $\frac {x^3}{\sqrt {25-x^2}} ~dx = \frac {\sin ^3 (u)}{125 \cos (u)} \cos (u) ~du = \frac {1}{125} \cdot \sin ^3 (u) ~du$

- **Another Solution:** $u = \sqrt {25 - x^2}$, $u ~du = - x ~dx$

- **Another Solution:** $\frac {x^2}{\sqrt {25-x^2}} \cdot x ~dx = \frac {25 - u^2}{u} \cdot u ~du = (25 - u^2) ~du$
:::

Proof: <2>1. Make the algebraic substitution $u = \sqrt{25 - x^2}$.
Then $u^2 = 25 - x^2 \implies x^2 = 25 - u^2$, and $2u \, du = -2x \, dx \implies x \, dx = -u \, du$.
<2>2. Rewrite the integrand in terms of $u$: $$\int \frac{x^3}{\sqrt{25-x^2}} \, dx = \int \frac{x^2 \cdot x \, dx}{\sqrt{25-x^2}} = \int \frac{25 - u^2}{u} (-u \, du) = \int (u^2 - 25) \, du.$$ <2>3. Integrate polynomials in $u$: $$\int (u^2 - 25) \, du = \frac{1}{3} u^3 - 25u + C.$$ <2>4. Substitute back $u = \sqrt{25-x^2}$: $$\frac{1}{3} (\sqrt{25-x^2})^3 - 25\sqrt{25-x^2} + C = \frac{1}{3}(25-x^2)^{3/2} - 25(25-x^2)^{1/2} + C.$$

<1>2. Alternative derivation via trigonometric substitution: Proof: <2>1. Substitute $x = 5\sin(\theta)$ with $\theta \in (-\pi/2, \pi/2)$.
Then $dx = 5\cos(\theta)\, d\theta$ and $\sqrt{25-x^2} = 5\cos(\theta)$.
<2>2. The integral transforms into: $$\int \frac{125\sin^3(\theta)}{5\cos(\theta)} \cdot 5\cos(\theta) \, d\theta = 125 \int \sin^3(\theta) \, d\theta = 125 \int (1 - \cos^2(\theta)) \sin(\theta) \, d\theta.$$ <2>3. Substituting $w = \cos(\theta) \implies dw = -\sin(\theta)\, d\theta$: $$125 \int (w^2 - 1) \, dw = 125 \left(\frac{w^3}{3} - w\right) + C.$$ <2>4. Since $w = \cos(\theta) = \frac{\sqrt{25-x^2}}{5}$: $$125 \left(\frac{(\sqrt{25-x^2})^3}{3 \cdot 125} - \frac{\sqrt{25-x^2}}{5}\right) + C = \frac{1}{3}(\sqrt{25-x^2})^3 - 25\sqrt{25-x^2} + C.$$ Q.E.D.
