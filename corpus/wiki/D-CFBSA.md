:::{.definition title="Laplacian and Harmonic Functions"}
A real function of two variables $u(x, y)$ is **harmonic** iff it is in the kernel of the Laplacian operator:
\[  
\Delta u \definedas \qty{\dd{^2}{x^2} + \dd{^2}{y^2}}u = 0
.\]
:::
