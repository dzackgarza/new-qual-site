::: problem
a) Define what is meant for an infinite series $\sum_{n=1}^\infty b_n$ of real numbers $b_n$ to converge.

b) Let $a_1, a_2, \dots$ be a sequence of positive real numbers such that $a_1 > a_2 > \cdots > a_n > a_{n+1} > \cdots$ and $\lim_{n \to \infty} a_n = 0$. Prove that the infinite series $\sum_{n=1}^\infty (-1)^n a_n$ converges.
:::
