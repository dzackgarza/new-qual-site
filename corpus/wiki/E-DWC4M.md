Let $f, g: X \to Y$ be continuous; assume that $Y$ is Hausdorff. Show that $\ts{x \mid f(x) = g(x)}$ is closed in $X$.

::: {.remark}
Munkres, *Topology*, §31 Exercise 5.
:::
