:::{.problem title="Hungerford 5.1.1"}
Let $F/K$ be a field extension. 
Show that

1.  $[F: K] = 1$ iff $F = K$.

2.  If $[F: K]$ is prime, then there are no intermediate fields between
    $F$ and $K$.

3.  If $u\in F$ has degree $n$ over $K$, then $n$ divides $[F: K]$.

:::
