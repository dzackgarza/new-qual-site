Let $G$ be a locally compact, connected topological group. Show that $G$ is paracompact. [Hint: Let $U_1$ be a neighborhood of $e$ having compact closure. In general, define $U_{n+1} = \overline{U}_n \cdot U_1$. Show the union of the sets $\overline{U}_n$ is both open and closed in $G$.]

This result holds without assuming $G$ is connected, but the proof requires more effort.

::: {.remark}
Munkres, *Topology*, §41 Exercise 9.
:::
