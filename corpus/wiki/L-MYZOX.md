::: {.lemma title="Abel's Test"}
If $f(z) \da \sum c_k z^k$ is a power series with $c_k \in \RR^{\geq 0}$ and $c_k\decreasesto 0$, then $f$ converges on $S^1$ except possibly at $z=1$.
:::
