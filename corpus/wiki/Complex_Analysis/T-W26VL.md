:::{.theorem}
Every map $g\in \Aut(\DD)$ is of the form 

\[
\Aut(\DD) = \ts{ \lambda \psi_a(z) \st a\in \DD, \lambda \in S^1 }
,\]
i.e. they are all Blaschke factors and rotations.
:::
