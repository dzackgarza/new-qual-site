:::{.theorem}
What are the automorphisms of $\DD, \CC, \CP^1$?
For the disc, it's known:
\[
\Aut_\CC(\DD) = \ts{ z\mapsto e^{i\theta} \qty{\alpha - z \over 1 - \bar{\alpha} z} }
.\]
:::
