Show that the set of all sequences $(x_1, x_2, \ldots)$ such that $\sum x_i^2$ converges is complete in the $\ell^2$-metric. (See [[E-39RRX]].)

::: {.remark}
Munkres, *Topology*, §43 Exercise 7.
