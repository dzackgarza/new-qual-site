:::{.theorem}
If $\sum_{k=1}^\infty c_k z^j$ converges on $\abs{z} < 1$ then
\[
\lim_{z\to 1^-} \sum_{k\in \NN} c_k z^k = \sum_{k\in \NN} c_k
.\]
:::
