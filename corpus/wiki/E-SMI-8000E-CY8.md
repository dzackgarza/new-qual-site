::: {.exercise title="Smith 8000e cycles 8"}
Prove that if $H$ is a transitive subgroup of $S(n)$, any two of the equivalence classes defined in [[E-SMI-8000E-CY7]] have the same number of elements. Hence a transitive subgroup of $S(p)$, where $p$ is prime, either contains no 2-cycles or all 2-cycles. Thus a transitive subgroup of $S(p)$ containing one 2-cycle — e.g. a subgroup containing a 2-cycle and a $p$-cycle — is all of $S(p)$. [Hint: use [[E-SMI-8000E-CY6]] and [[E-SMI-8000E-CY7]].]
:::
