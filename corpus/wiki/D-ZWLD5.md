::: {.definition title="Singular Chain"}

\[x \in C_{n}(x) \implies X = \sum_{i} n_{i} \sigma_{i} = \sum_{i} n_{i} (\Delta^n \mapsvia{\sigma_{i}} X) .\]
:::
