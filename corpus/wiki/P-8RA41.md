::: problem
If $\mu$ is Lebesgue measure on $\mathbb{R}$ and $f \in L(\mathbb{R},d\mu)$, prove that $F$ is absolutely continuous where
\[
F(x) = \int_{(-\infty,x]} f\, d\mu.
\]
:::
