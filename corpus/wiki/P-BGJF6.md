A splitting field of $f$ over $F$ is an extension $L \geq F$ that contains every root of $f$, so that $f$ can be decomposed as a product of linear factors i.e. $f(x) = \prod_{i=1}^{\deg f} (x-\alpha_i)^{m_i}$ in $L[x]$.
