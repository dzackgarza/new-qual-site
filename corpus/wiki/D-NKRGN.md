::: {.definition}
An integral domain $R$ is **Euclidean** if $R$ admits a degree function $d:R\to \ZZ_{\geq 0}$ such that for all $x,y\in R$ there exist $q,r\in R$ with $x = qy + r$ and either $f(r) < f(y)$ or $r=0$.
:::
