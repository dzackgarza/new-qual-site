Let $X$ be the subspace of $\mathbb{R}^2$ that is the union of the circles $C_n$ of radius $n$ and center $(n, 0)$ for $n = 1, 2, \cdots$. Show that $\pi_1(X)$ is the free group $*_n \pi_1(C_n)$, the same as for the infinite wedge sum $\bigvee_\infty S^1$. Show that $X$ and $\bigvee_\infty S^1$ are in fact homotopy equivalent, but not homeomorphic.
