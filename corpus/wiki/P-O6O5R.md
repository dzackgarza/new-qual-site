::: {.problem title="Hungerford 4.2.9"}
If $F_1, F_2$ are free modules of a ring with the invariant dimension property, then $$\mathrm{rank}(F_1 \oplus F_2) = \mathrm{rank} F_1 + \mathrm{rank} F_2.$$
:::
