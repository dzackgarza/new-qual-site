::: problem
Let $f = u+iv$ be complex-differentiable with continuous partial derivatives at a point $z = re^{i\theta}$ with $r\neq 0$.
Show that
\[  
\dd{u}{r} = {1\over r}\dd{v}{\theta} \qquad \dd{v}{r} = -{1\over r}\dd{u}{\theta}
.\]
:::
