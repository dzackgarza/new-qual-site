::: problem
6. General construction: construct a tree $T$ by picking a basepoint in $G$ and adding a vertex for every non-backtracking walk in $G$.

In this case, it's the infinite 3-valent graph (also called the infinite $k-$regular tree)

This is the universal cover, because $T$ is connected and acyclic (i.e. a tree).
This means that $\pi_1(T) = 0$, so $T$ is simply connected.
Since universal covers are simply connected and unique up to isomorphism, this is it.
:::
