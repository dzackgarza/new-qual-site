::: problem
22. For $n>2$ let $\zeta_{n}$ denote a primitive $n$ th root of unity over $Q$.
    Prove that
\[
\left[\boldsymbol{Q}\left(\zeta_{n}+\zeta_{n}^{-1}: \boldsymbol{Q}\right)\right]=\frac{1}{2} \varphi(n)
\]
where $\varphi$ is Euler's totient function.
:::
