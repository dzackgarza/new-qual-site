::: problem
Define a linear map $\mathbb{R}^2\to\mathbb{R}^2$ by the matrix
\[
A=\begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 \\ 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix}.
\]
Let $T^2=\mathbb{R}^2/\mathbb{Z}^2$ be endowed with the quotient topology.

(a) Prove that $A$ induces a well-defined, continuous map $f:T^2\to T^2$.

(b) Suppose $g:T^2\to T^2$ is a continuous map that is homotopic to $f$.
Prove or disprove: $g$ has a fixed point.
:::
