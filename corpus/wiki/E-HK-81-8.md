::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 8.1.8"}
Let $A$ be a $2 \times 2$ matrix with real entries.
For $X$, $Y$ in $\RR^{2 \times 1}$ let
\[
f_A(X, Y) = Y^t A X.
\]
Show that $f_A$ is an inner product on $\RR^{2 \times 1}$ if and only if $A = A^t$, $A_{11} > 0$, $A_{22} > 0$, and $\det A > 0$.
:::
