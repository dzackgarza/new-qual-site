::: problem
Let $\mathbb{D}$ denote the open unit disk in the complex plane.
Does there exist an analytic function $f:\mathbb{D}\to\mathbb{D}$ such that

(i) $f(1/2)=3/4$,

(ii) $f'(1/2)=2/3$?
:::
