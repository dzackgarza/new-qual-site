::: problem
a. State the classification theorem for compact connected surfaces (with or without boundary).

b. Show that any compact connected surface with a nonempty boundary is homotopy equivalent to a wedge of circles.
(Hint: you may assume that any compact connected surface without boundary is given by identifying edges of a polygon in pairs.)

c. For each surface in your classification, say how many circles are needed in the wedge from part (b). (Hint: you should be able to do this even if you have not done part (b).)
:::
