::: problem
Find a bijective conformal map from $G$ to $\HH$, where
\[  
G \definedas \theset{z\in \CC \suchthat \abs{z-1} < \sqrt 2,\, \abs{z+1} < \sqrt 2} \setminus [0, i)
.\]
:::
