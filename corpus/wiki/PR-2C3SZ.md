:::{.proposition title="Young's Convolution Inequality"}
\[
{1\over r} \da {1\over p} + {1\over q} - 1 \implies \pnorm{f \ast g}{r} \leq \pnorm{f}{p} \norm{g}{q}
.\]

:::
