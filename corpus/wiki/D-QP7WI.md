::: {.definition title="Poincaré Duality"}
For a closed, orientable $n\dash$manifold, following map $[M] \frown \wait$ is an isomorphism: $$ D: H^k(M; R) \to H_{n-k}(M; R) \\ D(\alpha) = [M] \frown \alpha$$
:::
