::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 8.1.6"}
Let $(\mid)$ be the standard inner product on $\RR^2$, and let $T$ be the linear operator $T(x_1, x_2) = (-x_2, x_1)$. Now $T$ is 'rotation through $90^\circ$' and has the property that $(\alpha \mid T\alpha) = 0$ for all $\alpha$ in $\RR^2$. Find all inner products $[\mid]$ on $\RR^2$ such that $[\alpha \mid T\alpha] = 0$ for each $\alpha$.
:::
