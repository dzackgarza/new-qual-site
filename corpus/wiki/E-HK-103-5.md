::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 10.3.5"}
Let $V$ be an $n$-dimensional vector space over a subfield $F$ of $C$.
Prove the following.

(a) The equation $(Pf)(\alpha, \beta) = \frac{1}{2}f(\alpha, \beta) - \frac{1}{2}f(\beta, \alpha)$ defines a linear operator $P$ on $L(V, V, F)$.

(b) $P^2 = P$, i.e., $P$ is a projection.

(c) $\operatorname{rank} P = \frac{n(n-1)}{2}$; $\operatorname{nullity} P = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}$.

(d) If $U$ is a linear operator on $V$, the equation $(U^{\dagger}f)(\alpha, \beta) = f(U\alpha, U\beta)$ defines a linear operator $U^{\dagger}$ on $L(V, V, F)$.

(e) For every linear operator $U$, the projection $P$ commutes with $U^{\dagger}$.
:::
