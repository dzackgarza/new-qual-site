::: problem
Let $f_n(x) = \frac{x}{1+nx^2}$ for $n \in \mathbb{N}$.
Let $\mathcal{F} := \{f_n \, \colon \, n = 1, 2, 3, \ldots\}$ and $[a,b]$ be any compact subset of $\mathbb{R}$.
Is $\mathcal{F}$ equicontinuous?
Justify your answer.
:::
