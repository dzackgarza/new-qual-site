::: {.definition title="Hausdorff"}
A topological space $X$ is **Hausdorff** or $T_2$ iff points can be separated by disjoint neighborhoods: for every $p\neq q \in X$ there exist disjoint open sets $U\ni p$ and $V\ni q$.
:::
