::: problem
Let $Y$ be the annulus given as the region between two nested rectangles, with edges identified in pairs as follows.
On the outer rectangle: the top edge $A$ oriented rightward, the right edge $B$ oriented downward, the bottom edge $C$ oriented leftward, and the left edge $C$ oriented downward.
On the inner rectangle: the top edge $B$ oriented rightward, the bottom edge $A$ oriented rightward, and both vertical edges $D$ oriented upward.

(a) Explain why $Y$ is a surface.

(b) Is $Y$ orientable?

(c) What surface is $Y$?
:::
