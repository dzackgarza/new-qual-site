::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 3.2.1"}
Let $T$ and $U$ be the linear operators on $\RR^2$ defined by
\[
T(x_1, x_2) = (x_2, x_1) \quad \text{and} \quad U(x_1, x_2) = (x_1, 0).
\]

(a) How would you describe $T$ and $U$ geometrically?

(b) Give rules like the ones defining $T$ and $U$ for each of the transformations $(U + T)$, $UT$, $TU$, $T^2$, $U^2$.
:::
