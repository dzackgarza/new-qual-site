(a) Show that $H_0(X, A) = 0$ iff $A$ meets each path-component of $X$.

(b) Show that $H_1(X, A) = 0$ iff $H_1(A) \to H_1(X)$ is surjective and each path-component of $X$ contains at most one path-component of $A$.
