:::{.definition title="Simple Function"}
A **simple function** $s: \CC\to X$ is a finite linear combination of indicator functions of measurable sets, i.e. 
\[
s(x) = \sum_{j=1}^n c_j \chi_{E_j}(x)
.\]
:::
