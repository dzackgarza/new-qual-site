::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 10.2.1"}
The following expressions define quadratic forms $q$ on $\RR^2$. Find the symmetric bilinear form $f$ corresponding to each $q$.

(a) $ax_1^2$

(b) $bx_1x_2$

(c) $cx_2^2$

(d) $2x_1^2 - \frac{1}{3}x_1x_2$

(e) $x_1^2 + 9x_2^2$

(f) $3x_1x_2 - x_2^2$

(g) $4x_1^2 + 6x_1x_2 - 3x_2^2$
:::
