Define $f_n: [0,1] \to \mathbb{R}$ by the equation $f_n(x) = x^n$. Show that the sequence $(f_n(x))$ converges for each $x \in [0,1]$, but that the sequence $(f_n)$ does not converge uniformly.

::: {.remark}
Munkres, *Topology*, §21 Exercise 6.
:::
