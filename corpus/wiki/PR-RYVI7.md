:::{.proposition title="Bessel's Inequality"}
For $x\in H$ a Hilbert space and $\theset{e_k}$ an orthonormal sequence,
\[  
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\| \inner{x}{e_{k} } \|^{2} \leq \|x\|^{2}
.\]

> Note that this does not need to be a basis.

:::
