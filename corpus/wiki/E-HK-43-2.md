::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 4.3.2"}
Let $\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \delta$ be real numbers. We ask when it is possible to find a polynomial $f$ over $\RR$, of degree not more than 2, such that $f(-1) = \alpha$, $f(1) = \beta$, $f(3) = \gamma$ and $f(0) = \delta$. Prove that this is possible if and only if
\[
3\alpha + 6\beta - \gamma - 8\delta = 0.
\]
:::
