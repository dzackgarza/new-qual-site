::: {.problem}
Let $R$ be a PID. Let $n > 0$ and $A \in M_n(R)$ be a square $n\times n$ matrix with coefficients in $R$.

Consider the $R$-module $M \coloneqq R^n / \mathrm{im}(A)$.

1. Give a necessary and sufficient condition for $M$ to be a torsion module (i.e. every nonzero element is torsion).
   Justify your answer.

2. Let $F$ be a field and now let $R \coloneqq F[x]$.
   Give an example of an integer $n>0$ and an $n \times n$ square matrix $A \in M_n(R)$ such that $M \coloneqq R^n/\mathrm{im}(A)$ is isomorphic as an $R-$module to $R \times F$.
:::
