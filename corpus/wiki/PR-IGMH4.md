:::{.proposition title="Riemann-Lebesgue: Fourier transforms have small tails."}
\[
f\in L^1 \implies
\hat{f}(\xi) \rightarrow 0 \text { as }|\xi| \rightarrow \infty
,\]

if $f \in L^1$, then $\hat f$ is continuous and bounded.
:::
