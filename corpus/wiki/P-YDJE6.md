Let $n\in \ZZ^{\geq 1}$ and $0<\theta<\pi$ and show that
\[
{1\over 2\pi i} \int_{\abs z = 2} {z^n \over 1 -3z\cos(\theta) + z^2} \,dz = {\sin(n\theta) \over \sin(\theta)}
.\]
:::
