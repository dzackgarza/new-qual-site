::: {.exercise title="Munkres §34.4"}


Let $X$ be a locally compact Hausdorff space. Is it true that if $X$ has a countable basis, then $X$ is metrizable? Is it true that if $X$ is metrizable, then $X$ has a countable basis?
:::
