::: {.definition title="Kronecker Pairing"}
A map pairing a chain with a cochain, given by
\[
H^n(X; R) \cross H_{n}(X; R) \to R \\ ([\psi, \alpha]) \mapsto \psi(\alpha)
\]
which is a nondegenerate bilinear form.
:::
