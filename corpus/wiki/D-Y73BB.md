::: {.definition title="Kronecker Pairing"}
Evaluation of cochains on chains descends to the **Kronecker pairing**
\[
H^n(X;R)\times H_n(X;R)\longrightarrow R,
\qquad ([\psi],[\alpha])\longmapsto\psi(\alpha).
\]
Its adjoint is the natural map
\[
H^n(X;R)\longrightarrow\operatorname{Hom}_R(H_n(X;R),R)
\]
in the universal coefficient theorem. The pairing need not be nondegenerate over an arbitrary coefficient ring.
:::
