:::{.proposition title="Splitting Fields of Cyclotomic Polynomials"}
The splitting field of $x^m-1$ is $\QQ(\zeta_m)$ for $\zeta_m$ any primitive root of unity, and 
\[
\Gal(\QQ(\zeta_m)_{/\QQ}) \cong (\ZZ/m\ZZ)\units
.\]
:::
