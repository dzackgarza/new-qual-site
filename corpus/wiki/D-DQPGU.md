:::{.definition title="Symplectic Group"}
\[
\mathrm{Sp}_{2n}(\CC) \da \ts{ A \in \GL_{2n}(\CC) \st A^tJA = J } &&
J \da
\begin{bmatrix}
0 & 1_n
\\
1_n & 0
\end{bmatrix}
.\]

:::
