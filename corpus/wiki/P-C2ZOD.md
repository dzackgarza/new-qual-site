:::{.problem title="Hungerford 5.3.8"}
Show that no finite field is algebraically closed.

*Hint: if $K = \{a_i\}_{i=0}^n$, consider*
\[ 
f(x) = a_1 + \prod_{i=0}^n (x - a_i) \in K[x]
\] 
*where $a_1 \neq 0$.*

:::
