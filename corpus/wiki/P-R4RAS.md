Is the following function continuous, differentiable, continuously differentiable?
\[  
f: \RR^2 &\to \RR  \\
f(x, y) &= 
\begin{cases}
{xy \over \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}} & (x, y) \neq (0, 0) \\
0 & \text{else}.
\end{cases}
\]
