::: problem
Expand the following functions into Laurent series in the indicated regions:

(a) $\displaystyle f(z) = \frac{z^2 - 1}{ (z+2)(z+3)}, \; \; 2 < |z| < 3$, $3 < |z| < + \infty$.

(b) $\displaystyle f(z) = \sin \frac{z}{1-z}, \; \; 0 < |z-1| < + \infty$
:::
