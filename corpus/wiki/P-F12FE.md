::: problem
Suppose $f: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ satisfies $f(xy) = xf(y) + yf(x)$ for all $x, y \in \mathbb{R}$. Prove that $f(1) = 0$ and that $f(u^n) = n u^{n-1} f(u)$ for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $u \in \mathbb{R}$.
:::
