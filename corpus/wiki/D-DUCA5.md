::: {.definition title="Generating Set"}
$S = \theset{s_{i}}$ is a generating set for an $R\dash$ module $M$ iff $$x\in M \implies x = \sum r_{i} s_{i}$$ for some coefficients $r_{i} \in R$ (where this sum may be infinite).
:::
