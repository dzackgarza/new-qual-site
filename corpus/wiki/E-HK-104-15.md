::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 10.4.15"}
Let $f$ be the bilinear form on $C^2$ defined by
\[
f((x_1, x_2), (y_1, y_2)) = x_1y_2 - x_2y_1.
\]
Show that

(a) if $T$ is a linear operator on $C^2$, then $f(T\alpha, T\beta) = (\det T)f(\alpha, \beta)$ for all $\alpha, \beta$ in $C^2$.

(b) $T$ preserves $f$ if and only if $\det T = +1$.

(c) What does (b) say about the group of $2 \times 2$ matrices $M$ such that $M^tAM = A$ where
\[
A = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ -1 & 0 \end{bmatrix}?
\]
:::
