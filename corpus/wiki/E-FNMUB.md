::: {.exercise title="Munkres §18.13"}

Let $A \subset X$; let $f: A \to Y$ be continuous; let $Y$ be Hausdorff.
Show that if $f$ may be extended to a continuous function $g: \overline{A} \to Y$, then $g$ is uniquely determined by $f$.
:::
