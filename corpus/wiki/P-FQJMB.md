::: problem
Define

$$
E(z)=e^{x}(\cos y+i \sin y) .
$$

- Show that $E(z)$ is the unique function analytic on $\mathbb{C}$ that satisfies

$$
E^{\prime}(z)=E(z), \quad E(0)=1 .
$$

- Conclude from the first part that 
\[
E(z)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{z^{n}}{n !}
.\]
:::
