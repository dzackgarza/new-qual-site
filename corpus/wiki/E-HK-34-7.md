::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 3.4.7"}
Let $T$ be the linear operator on $\RR^3$ defined by
\[
T(x_1, x_2, x_3) = (3x_1 + x_3,\; -2x_1 + x_2,\; -x_1 + 2x_2 + 4x_3).
\]

(a) What is the matrix of $T$ in the standard ordered basis for $\RR^3$?

(b) What is the matrix of $T$ in the ordered basis
\[
\{\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3\}
\]
where $\alpha_1 = (1, 0, 1)$, $\alpha_2 = (-1, 2, 1)$, and $\alpha_3 = (2, 1, 1)$?

(c) Prove that $T$ is invertible and give a rule for $T^{-1}$ like the one which defines $T$.
:::
