Let $\tilde{X}$ and $\tilde{Y}$ be simply-connected covering spaces of the path-connected, locally path-connected spaces $X$ and $Y$.
Show that if $X \simeq Y$ then $\tilde{X} \simeq \tilde{Y}$.
