::: problem
Let $f, g\in L^1([0, 1])$, define $F(x) = \int_0^x f(y)\dy$ and $G(x) = \int_0^x g(y)\dy$, and show
\[
\int_0^1 F(x)g(x) \,dx = F(1)G(1) - \int_0^1 f(x) G(x) \, dx
.\]
:::
