::: problem
a) Let $f$ be a function defined on an interval $(a,b)$ and let $c \in (a,b)$. Define what it means to say that $f$ is continuous at $c$.

b) Use this definition to show that $f(x) = 1/x$ is continuous at $x = 1$.
:::
