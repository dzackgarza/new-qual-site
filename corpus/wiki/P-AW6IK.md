Show by example that there exists a function $f(z)$ that is holomorphic on $\theset{z\in \CC \suchthat 0 < \abs{z} < 1}$ and for all $r<1$,
\[
\int_{\abs z = r} f(z) \, dz = 0
,\]
but $f$ is not holomorphic at $z=0$.
