:::{.definition}
An element $r\in R$ is a **zero-divisor** iff there exists an $a\in R\smz$ such that $ar = ra = 0$, i.e. $r\divides 0$.
Equivalently, the map
\[  
r\cdot: R &\to R \\
x &\mapsto rx
\]
fails to be injective.
:::
