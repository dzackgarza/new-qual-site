::: problem
Suppose that $f$ is holomorphic in an open set containing $\DD$ except for a pole $z_0 \in \bd \DD$.
Let $\sum_{n=0}^\infty a_n z^n$ be the power series expansion of $f$ in $\DD$, and show that $\lim \frac{a_n}{a_{n+1}} = z_0$.
