Suppose that
\[
1\leq p_j \leq \infty, && \sum_{j=1}^n {1\over p_j} = {1\over r} \leq 1
.\]

Show that if $f_j \in L^{p_j}$ for each $1\leq j \leq n$, then
\[
\prod f_j \in L^r, && \norm{ \prod f_j }_r \leq \prod \norm{f_j}_{p_j}
.\]
