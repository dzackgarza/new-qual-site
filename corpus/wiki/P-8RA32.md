::: problem
Let $f_n(x)=\dfrac{x}{1+nx^2}$, $x\in\mathbb{R}$.
Show that

(a) $f_n$ converges uniformly to a function $f$, and

(b) $f'(x)=\lim_{n\to\infty}f_n'(x)$ is true for $x\neq 0$, and false for $x=0$.
:::
