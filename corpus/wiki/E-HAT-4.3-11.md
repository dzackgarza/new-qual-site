For a space $B$, let $\mathcal{F}(B)$ be the set of fiber homotopy equivalence classes of fibrations $E \to B$. Show that a map $f: B_1 \to B_2$ induces $f^*: \mathcal{F}(B_2) \to \mathcal{F}(B_1)$ depending only on the homotopy class of $f$, with $f^*$ a bijection if $f$ is a homotopy equivalence.
