::: {.exercise title="Munkres §7.8"}


Let $X$ denote the two-element set $\{0,1\}$ ; let $\mathcal{B}$ be the set of countable subsets of $X^{\omega}$ . Show that $X^{\omega}$ and $\mathcal{B}$ have the same cardinality.
:::
