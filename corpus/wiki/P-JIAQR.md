::: problem
13. $\displaystyle \int \frac {x}{1+x^4} ~dx = \color{blue} {\frac {1}{2} \tan ^{-1} (x^2)}$

- **Solution:** $u =x^2$, $du = 2x ~dx$

- **Solution:** $\frac {1}{2} \cdot \frac {2x ~dx}{1+x^4} = \frac {1}{2} \frac {du}{1 + u^2}$
:::
