Let $p: E \to B$ be a covering map; let $B$ be connected. Show that if $p^{-1}(b_0)$ has $k$ elements for some $b_0 \in B$, then $p^{-1}(b)$ has $k$ elements for every $b \in B$. In such a case, $E$ is called a $k$-fold covering of $B$.

::: {.remark}
Munkres, *Topology*, §53 Exercise 3.
:::
