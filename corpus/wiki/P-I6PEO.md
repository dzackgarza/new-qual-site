Let $f$ be a function of bounded variation on $[a,b]$.
Furthermore, assume that for some $c>0$, $|f(x)| \geq c$ on $[a,b]$.
Show that $g(x) = 1/f(x)$ is of bounded variation on $[a,b]$.
