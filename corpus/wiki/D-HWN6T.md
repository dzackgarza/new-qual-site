::: {.definition title="Quasi-isomorphism"}
A chain map $f: C_* \to D_*$ such that the induced maps
\[
H_n(f): H_n(C_*) \mapsvia{\sim} H_n(D_*)
\]
are isomorphisms for every $n$.
A chain homotopy equivalence is a quasi-isomorphism, but not conversely; formally inverting the quasi-isomorphisms produces the derived category.
:::

::: {.concept}
See Weibel, *An Introduction to Homological Algebra*, §1.1.
:::
