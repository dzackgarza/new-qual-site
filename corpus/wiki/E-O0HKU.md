Show that if $f: X \to Y$ is continuous, where $X$ is compact and $Y$ is Hausdorff, then $f$ is a closed map (that is, $f$ carries closed sets to closed sets).

::: {.remark}
Munkres, *Topology*, §26 Exercise 6.
:::
