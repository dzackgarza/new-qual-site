::: {.definition title="Relative boundaries"}
For a pair $(X,A)$, a **relative boundary** is a chain $\alpha\in C_n(X)$ of the form $\alpha = \del\beta + \gamma$ with $\beta \in C_{n+1}(X)$ and $\gamma \in C_n(A)$; equivalently a boundary in $C_n(X,A) = C_n(X)/C_n(A)$.
Relative homology is relative cycles modulo relative boundaries.
:::

::: {.concept}
See Hatcher, p. 115.
:::
