::: problem
For a function $f: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$, give the definition of continuity of $f$ at a point $a$. For functions $f$ and $g$ from $\mathbb{R}$ to $\mathbb{R}$, prove that if $f$ is continuous at $a$ and $g$ is continuous at $b = f(a)$, then $g \circ f$ is continuous at $a$. [The composition $g \circ f$ is defined by $(g \circ f)(x) = g(f(x))$ for $x \in \mathbb{R}$.]
:::
