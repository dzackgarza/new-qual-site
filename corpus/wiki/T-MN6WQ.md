:::{.theorem title="Commuting Sums with Integrals (integrable)"}
If $\theset{f_n}$ integrable with either $\sum \int \abs{f_n} < \infty$ or $\int\sum \abs{f_n} < \infty$, then
\[
\int\sum f_n = \sum \int f_n
.\]
:::
