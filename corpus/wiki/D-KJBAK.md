::: {.definition title="Dense"}
A subspace $Q\subset X$ is **dense** iff every neighborhood of every point in $x$ intersects $Q$.
Equivalently, $\cl_X(Q) = X$.

::: {.remark}
Munkres, *Topology*, 2nd ed., §17, p. 95.
:::
:::
