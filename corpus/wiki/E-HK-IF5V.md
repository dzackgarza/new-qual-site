::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 1.3.1"}
Find all solutions to the system of equations

$$
\begin{array}{l} (1 - i) x _ {1} - i x _ {2} = 0 \\ 2 x _ {1} + (1 - i) x _ {2} = 0. \end{array}
$$
:::
