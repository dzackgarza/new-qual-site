::: {.definition title="Suspension"}
Compact represented as $\Sigma X = CX \coprod_{\id_{X}} CX$, two cones on $X$ glued along $X$.
Explicitly given by

\[\Sigma X = \frac{X\cross I}{(X\cross \theset{0}) \union (X\cross \theset{1}) \union (\theset{x_{0}} \cross I)} .\]
:::
