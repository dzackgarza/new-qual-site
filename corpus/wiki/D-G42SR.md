::: {.definition}
Say a sequence $f_k\to f$ **locally uniformly** on $\Omega$ if $f_k\to f$ uniformly on every compact subset.
Equivalently, $f_k \to f$ uniformly on any bounded subset of strictly positive distance to $\bd \Omega$.
:::
