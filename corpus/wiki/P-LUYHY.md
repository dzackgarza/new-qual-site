::: problem
Suppose $1\leq p,q,r \leq \infty$ with
\[
{1\over p } + {1 \over q} = 1 + {1 \over r}
.\]

Prove that
\[
f \in L^p, g\in L^q \implies f \convolve g \in L^r \text{ and } \norm{f \convolve g}_r \leq \norm{f}_p \norm{g}_q
.\]
:::
