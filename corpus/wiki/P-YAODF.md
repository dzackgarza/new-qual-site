::: {.problem title="Hungerford 5.6.1"}
Let $F/K$ be a field extension.
Let $\mathrm{char} K = p \neq 0$ and let $n\geq 1$ be an integer such that $(p, n) = 1$.
If $v\in F$ and $nv \in K$, then $v\in K$.
:::
