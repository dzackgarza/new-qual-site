Compute the following integrals:

- $\displaystyle \int_0^\infty \frac{x^{a-1}}{1 + x^n} \, dx$, $0< a < n$
- $\displaystyle \int_0^\infty \frac{\log x}{(1 + x^2)^2}\, dx$
