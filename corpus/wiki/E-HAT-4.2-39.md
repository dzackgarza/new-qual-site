Show that the indeterminacy of a Toda bracket $\langle f, g, h \rangle$ with $f \in \pi_i^s$, $g \in \pi_j^s$, $h \in \pi_k^s$ is the subgroup $f \cdot \pi_{j+k+1}^s + h \cdot \pi_{i+j+1}^s$ of $\pi_{i+j+k+1}^s$.
