::: problem
14. $\displaystyle \int \frac {x^2}{1+x^6} ~dx = \color{blue} {\frac {1}{3} \tan ^{-1} (x^3)}$

- **Solution:** $u =x^3$, $du = 3 x^2 ~dx$

- **Solution:** $\frac {1}{3} \cdot \frac {3 x^2 ~dx}{1+x^6} = \frac {1}{3} \frac {du}{1 + u^2}$

:::
