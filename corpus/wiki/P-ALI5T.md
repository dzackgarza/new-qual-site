::: problem
Assume $f(z)$ is analytic in region $D$ and $\Gamma$ is a rectifiable curve in $D$ with interior in $D$.
Prove that if $f(z)$ is real for all $z \in \Gamma$, then $f(z)$ is a constant.
:::
