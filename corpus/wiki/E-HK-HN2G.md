::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 1.6.8"}
Let

$$
A = \left[ \begin{array}{c c} a & b \\ c & d \end{array} \right].
$$

Prove, using elementary row operations, that $A$ is invertible if and only if $(ad - bc) \neq 0$ .
:::
