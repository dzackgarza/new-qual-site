::: {.definition title="Linear Independence"}
A generating $S$ for a module $M$ is linearly independent if $\sum r_{i} s_{i} = 0_M \implies \forall i,~r_{i} = 0$ where $s_{i}\in S, r_{i} \in R$.
:::
