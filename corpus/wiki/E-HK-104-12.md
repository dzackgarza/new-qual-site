::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 10.4.12"}
Let $F$ be a subfield of $C$, $V$ the space of $n \times 1$ matrices over $F$, $A$ an invertible $n \times n$ matrix over $F$, and $f$ the bilinear form on $V$ given by $f(X, Y) = X^tAY$.
If $M$ is an $n \times n$ matrix over $F$, show that $M$ preserves $f$ if and only if $A^{-1}M^tA = M^{-1}$.
:::
