::: problem
Consider the following matrix:
\[
B \da 
\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 3 & 3
\\
2 & 2 & 3
\\
-1 & -2 & -2
\end{bmatrix}
.\]

a. Find the minimal polynomial of $B$.

b. Find a $3\times 3$ matrix $J$ in Jordan canonical form such that $B = JPJ\inv$ where $P$ is an invertible matrix.
:::
