::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 10.3.11"}
Let $f$ be any skew-symmetric bilinear form on $\RR^3$.
Prove that there are linear functionals $L_1, L_2$ such that
\[
f(\alpha, \beta) = L_1(\alpha)L_2(\beta) - L_1(\beta)L_2(\alpha).
\]
:::
