::: problem
Compute
\[
\int_{\abs z = 1} \qty{z + {1\over z}}^{2n} {dz \over z}
\]
and use it to show that
\[
\int_0^{2\pi} \cos^{2n}(\theta) \, d\theta = 2\pi \qty{1\cdot 3 \cdot 5 \cdots (2n-1) \over 2 \cdot 4 \cdot 6 \cdots (2n)}
.\]
:::
