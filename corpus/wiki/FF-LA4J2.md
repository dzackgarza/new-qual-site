::: {.fact}
$ E\subseteq {\mathbf{R}}^n $ is measurable $ \iff $ for all $ A\subset {\mathbf{R}}^n $,

$$m_*(A) = m_*(E\cap A) + m_*(E\cap A^c)$$
:::
