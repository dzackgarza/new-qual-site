Let $E$ be a subspace of $\mathbb{R}^2$ obtained by deleting a subspace of $\{0\} \times \mathbb{R}$.
For which such spaces $E$ is the projection $E \to \mathbb{R}$, $(x, y) \mapsto x$, a fiber bundle?
