::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 6.3.7"}
Let $n$ be a positive integer, and let $V$ be the space of polynomials over $\RR$ which have degree at most $n$ (throw in the 0-polynomial).
Let $D$ be the differentiation operator on $V$.
What is the minimal polynomial for $D$?
:::
