Describe each set in the $z$-plane in (a) and (b) below, where $\alpha$ is a complex number and $k$ is a positive number such that $2|\alpha|<k$.

(a) $|z-\alpha|+|z+\alpha|=k$;

(b) $|z-\alpha|+|z+\alpha| \leq k$.
