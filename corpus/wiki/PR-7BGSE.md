:::{.proposition}
\[  
\frac 1 p + \frac 1 q = 1 \implies \pnorm{f g}{1} \leq \pnorm{f}{p} \pnorm{g}{q}
.\]

With integrals:
\[
\int_X \abs{fg} \leq \qty{\int_X \abs{f}^p}^{1\over p} \qty{\int_X \abs{f}^q}^{1\over q}
.\]


:::
