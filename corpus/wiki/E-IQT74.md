::: {.exercise title="Munkres §71.1"}


Let $X$ be a space that is the union of subspaces $S_1, \ldots, S_n$, each of which is homeomorphic to the unit circle. Assume there is a point $p$ of $X$ such that $S_i \cap S_j = \ts{p}$ for $i \neq j$.

(a) Show that $X$ is Hausdorff if and only if each space $S_i$ is closed in $X$.

(b) Show that $X$ is Hausdorff if and only if the topology of $X$ is coherent with the subspaces $S_i$.

(c) Give an example to show that $X$ need not be Hausdorff. [Hint: See [[E-29WP0]].]
:::
