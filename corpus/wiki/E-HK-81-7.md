::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 8.1.7"}
Let $( \mid )$ be the standard inner product on $C^2$. Prove that there is no non-zero linear operator on $C^2$ such that $(\alpha \mid T\alpha) = 0$ for every $\alpha$ in $C^2$. Generalize.
:::
