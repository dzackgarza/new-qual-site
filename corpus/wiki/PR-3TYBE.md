:::{.proposition title="?"}
Let $L/K/k$ with $L/k$ Galois. Then
\[
K/k \text{ is Galois } \iff \Gal(L/K)\normal \Gal(L/k)
,\]
and moreover $\Gal(K/k) = G$.
:::
