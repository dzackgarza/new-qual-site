:::{.proposition title="An intermediate extension is Galois iff its group is normal"}
Let $L/K/k$ with $L/k$ Galois. Then
\[
K/k \text{ is Galois } \iff \Gal(L/K)\normal \Gal(L/k)
,\]
and in that case
\[
\Gal(K/k) \cong \Gal(L/k) / \Gal(L/K)
.\]
:::
