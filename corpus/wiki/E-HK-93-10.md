::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 9.3.10"}
Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional inner product space.
If $T$ and $U$ are linear operators on $V$, we write $T < U$ if $U - T$ is a positive operator.
Prove the following:

(a) $T < U$ and $U < T$ is impossible.

(b) If $T < U$ and $U < S$, then $T < S$.

(c) If $T < U$ and $0 < S$, it need not be that $ST < SU$.
:::
