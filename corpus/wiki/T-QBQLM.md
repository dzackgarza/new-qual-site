::: {.theorem}
$\GF(p^n)\cong \frac{\FF_p[x]}{(f)}$ where $f \in \FF_p[x]$ is any irreducible of degree $n$, and $\GF(p^n) \cong \FF_p[\alpha] \cong \spanof_{\FF_p}\theset{1, \alpha, \cdots, \alpha^{n-1}}$ for any root $\alpha$ of $f$.
:::
