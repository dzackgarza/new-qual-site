::: {.theorem title="The Spectral Theorem"}
\envlist

1. Hermitian (self-adjoint) matrices (i.e. $A^{\dagger} = A$) are diagonalizable over $\CC$.

2. Symmetric matrices (i.e. $A^t = A$) are diagonalizable over $\RR$.
:::
