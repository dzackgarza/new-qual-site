::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 5.2.7"}
Let $K$ be a commutative ring with identity. Show that the determinant function on $2 \times 2$ matrices $A$ over $K$ is alternating and 2-linear as a function of the columns of $A$.
:::
