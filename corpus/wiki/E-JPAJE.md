:::{.exercise title="Mobius transformations as cross-ratios"}
Write a Mobius transformation $f(z) = {az+b\over cz + d}$ as a cross-ratio.

:::

:::{.solution}
\[
T(z) = \qty{ z; {d-b\over a-c}, -{b\over a}, -{d\over c}}
.\]
:::
