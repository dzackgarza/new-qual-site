Show that a map $p: E \to B$ is a fibration if the map $\pi: E^I \to E_p$, $\pi(\gamma) = (\gamma(0), p\gamma)$ has a section, that is, a map $s: E_p \to E^I$ such that $\pi s = \mathbb{1}$.
