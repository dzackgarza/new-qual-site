:::{.proposition title="Mean Value Property"}
If $u$ is harmonic on $\Omega$ then
\[
u(z_0) = u(x_0 + i y_0) = {1\over 2\pi r} \oint_{\bd \DD_r(z_0)} u \ds = {1\over \pi r^2} \iint_{\DD_r(z_0)} u(x, y) \dx \dy
.\]
:::
