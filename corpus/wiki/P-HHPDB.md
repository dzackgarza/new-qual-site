Let $\phi\in L^1(\RR^n)$ such that $\int \phi = 1$ and define $\phi_t(x) = t^{-n}\phi(t\inv x)$.
Show that if $f$ is bounded and uniformly continuous then $f\ast \phi_t \converges{t\to 0}\to f$ uniformly.
