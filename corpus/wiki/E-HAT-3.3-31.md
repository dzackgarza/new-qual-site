Show that if $M$ is a compact $R$-orientable $n$-manifold, then the boundary map $H_n(M, \partial M; R) \to H_{n-1}(\partial M; R)$ sends a fundamental class for $(M, \partial M)$ to a fundamental class for $\partial M$.
