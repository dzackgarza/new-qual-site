::: {.exercise title="?"}
Show that a real-valued holomorphic function is constant.
:::

::: {.solution}
Use that $\RR$ is not open in $\CC$ and apply the open mapping theorem to conclude: $f(\CC)$ must be open in $\CC$ if $f$ is holomorphic and nonconstant.
:::
