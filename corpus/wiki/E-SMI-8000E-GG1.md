::: {.exercise title="Smith 8000e galois 1"}
(i) Prove that the polynomial $f(X) = X^2 + X + 1$ is irreducible over $k = \ZZ/2$, and hence that the quotient $k[X]/(f)$ defines a field of 4 elements.

(ii) Prove that the splitting field of $X^2 + X + 1$ over $\ZZ/2$ has dimension 2, hence the Galois group is $\ZZ/2$, a solvable group — but prove that the polynomial is not "solvable by radicals", i.e. the root field is not a radical extension.
:::
