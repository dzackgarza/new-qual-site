Show that 
\[
\displaystyle \int_0^\infty \frac{x^{a-1}} {1+x^n} \dx=\frac{\pi}{n\sin \frac{a\pi}{n}}
.\]
using complex analysis, $0< a < n$. Here $n$ is a positive integer.
