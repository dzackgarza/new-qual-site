10. $\displaystyle \int \sqrt {4-x} ~dx = \color{blue} {- \frac {2}{3}(4-x)^{\frac {3}{2}}}$

- **Solution:** $u = 4 - x$, $du = - ~dx$

- **Solution:** $\sqrt {4-x} ~dx = - u^{\frac {1}{2}} ~du$
