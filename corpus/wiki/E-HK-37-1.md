::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 3.7.1"}
Let $F$ be a field and let $f$ be the linear functional on $F^2$ defined by $f(x_1, x_2) = ax_1 + bx_2$. For each of the following linear operators $T$, let $g = T^t f$, and find $g(x_1, x_2)$.

(a) $T(x_1, x_2) = (x_1, 0)$;

(b) $T(x_1, x_2) = (-x_2, x_1)$;

(c) $T(x_1, x_2) = (x_1 - x_2,\; x_1 + x_2)$.
:::
