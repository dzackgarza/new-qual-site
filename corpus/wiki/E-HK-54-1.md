::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 5.4.1"}
Use the classical adjoint formula to compute the inverses of each of the following $3 \times 3$ real matrices.
\[
\begin{bmatrix} -2 & 3 & 2 \\ 6 & 0 & 3 \\ 4 & 1 & -1 \end{bmatrix}, \qquad \begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta & 0 & -\sin\theta \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ \sin\theta & 0 & \cos\theta \end{bmatrix}
\]
:::
