Criticize the following "proof" that $S^2$ is simply connected. Let $f$ be a loop in $S^2$ based at $x_0$. Choose a point $p$ of $S^2$ not lying in the image of $f$. Since $S^2 - \ts{p}$ is homeomorphic with $\mathbb{R}^2$, and $\mathbb{R}^2$ is simply connected, the loop $f$ is path homotopic to the constant loop.

::: {.remark}
Munkres, *Topology*, §59 Exercise 2.
:::
