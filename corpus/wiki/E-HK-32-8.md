::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 3.2.8"}
Let $V$ be a vector space over the field $F$ and $T$ a linear operator on $V$.
If $T^2 = 0$, what can you say about the relation of the range of $T$ to the null space of $T$?
Give an example of a linear operator $T$ on $\RR^2$ such that $T^2 = 0$ but $T \neq 0$.
:::
