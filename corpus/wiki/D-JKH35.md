::: {.definition title="Product topology"}
For $(X, \tau_X)$ and $(Y, \tau_Y)$ topological spaces, defining
\[
\tau_{X \cross Y} \da \ts{U \cross V \st U\in \tau_X,\, V\in \tau_Y}
\]
yields the **product topology** on $X \cross Y$.
:::
