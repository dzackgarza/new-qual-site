::: {.proposition title="Separability test: $\gcd$ with derivative"}
$f$ is separable iff $\gcd(f, f')=1$, so $f, f'$ share no common roots.
Moreover, the multiple roots of $f$ are precisely the roots of $\gcd(f, f')$.
:::
