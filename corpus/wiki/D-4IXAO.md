::: {.definition title="Orthonormal sequence "}
A countable collection of elements \( \ts{ u_i } \)  is **orthonormal** if and only if

1. \( \inner{u_i}{u_j} = 0 \) for all \( j \neq k \) and

2. \( \norm{u_j}^2 \da \inner{u_j}{u_j} = 1 \) for all \( j \).
:::
