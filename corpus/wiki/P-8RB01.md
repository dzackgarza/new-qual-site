::: problem
(a) Use the definition of the derivative to prove that if $f$ and $g$ are differentiable at $x$, then $fg$ is differentiable at $x$.

(b) Use the definition of the Riemann integral to prove that if $f$ is bounded on $[a,b]$ and is continuous everywhere except for finitely many points in $(a,b)$, then $f\in\mathscr{R}$ on $[a,b]$.
:::
