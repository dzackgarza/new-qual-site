:::{.exercise title="?"}
Show that if $f$ is constant on any closed disk $r\bar\DD$ for $r<1$, then $f$ is constant on $\DD$.

:::

:::{.solution}
By MMP applied to $r\bar \DD$, since $f$ achieves its maximum on the interior $r\DD$, $f$ is necessarily constant.

:::
