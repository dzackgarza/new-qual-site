:::{.proposition title="Cauchy-Riemann implies holomorphic"}
If $f = u+iv$ with $u, v\in C^1(\RR)$ satisfying the Cauchy-Riemann equations on $\Omega$, then $f$ is holomorphic on $\Omega$ and
\[
f'(z) = \del f = {1\over 2}\qty{u_x + iv_x}
.\]
:::
