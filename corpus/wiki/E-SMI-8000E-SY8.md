::: {.exercise title="Smith 8000e sylow 8"}
(i) If $P$ is a Sylow subgroup of $S(2p)$, prove $P$ is abelian.

(ii) Prove it for $p > 3$, and $P$ a Sylow $p$-subgroup of $S(3p)$.

(iii) Prove it for $p > n$, with $P$ a Sylow $p$-subgroup of $S(np)$.
:::
