::: {.proposition title="Top homology for manifolds"}
If $M^n$ is a closed connected manifold, then $H_n = \ZZ$ and $\tor(H_{n-1}) = 0$.
More generally,
\[
\begin{cases}
\ZZ & M^n \text{ is orientable } 
\\
0 & \text{else}.
\end{cases}
\]
:::
