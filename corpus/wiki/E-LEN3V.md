Let $\ts{A_n}$ be a sequence of connected subspaces of $X$, such that $A_n \cap A_{n+1} \neq \varnothing$ for all $n$. Show that $\bigcup A_n$ is connected.

::: {.remark}
Munkres, *Topology*, §23 Exercise 2.
:::
