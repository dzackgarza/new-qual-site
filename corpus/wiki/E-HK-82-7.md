::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 8.2.7"}
Let $V$ be the inner product space consisting of $\RR^2$ and the inner product whose quadratic form is defined by
\[
||(x_1, x_2)||^2 = (x_1 - x_2)^2 + 3x_2^2.
\]
Let $E$ be the orthogonal projection of $V$ onto the subspace $W$ spanned by the vector $(3, 4)$. Now answer the four questions of Exercise 6.
:::
