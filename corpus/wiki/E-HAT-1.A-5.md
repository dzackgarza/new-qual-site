Construct a connected graph $X$ and maps $f, g: X \to X$ such that $fg = \mathbb{1}$ but $f$ and $g$ do not induce isomorphisms on $\pi_1$. [Note that $f_*g_* = \mathbb{1}$ implies that $f_*$ is surjective and $g_*$ is injective.]
