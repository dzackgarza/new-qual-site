::: problem
1. Any covering map $p: S^1\cross S^1 \into \RP^2$ would induce an injection on fundamental groups, but $\pi_1(T) = \ZZ^2$ and $\pi_1(\ZZ_2)$ - but there are no homomorphisms between these groups.
   Why?
   One of them has an element of order 2, the other does not.
:::
