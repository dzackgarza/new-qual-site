::: problem
Let $\{f_k\}$ be any sequence of functions in $L^2([0,1])$ satisfying $\|f_k\|_2\le M$ for all $k\in\mathbb{N}$.
Prove that if $f_k\to f$ almost everywhere, then
\[
\lim_{k\to\infty}\int_0^1 f_k(x)\,dx=\int_0^1 f(x)\,dx.
\]
:::
