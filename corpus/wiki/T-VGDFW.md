:::{.theorem title="Automorphisms of the half-plane"}
\[
\Aut_\CC(\HH) = \ts{ z \mapsto {az+b \over cz+d} \st a,b,c,d\in \RR, ad-bc=1 } \cong\PSL_2(\RR)
.\]

:::{.remark}
Stein and Shakarchi, *Complex Analysis*, Ch. 8 Theorem 2.4.
:::
:::
