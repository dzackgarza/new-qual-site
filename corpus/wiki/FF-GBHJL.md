::: {.fact}
There is a commutative monoid $\Sigma = \gens{P, K, T, S \suchthat S=0, 3P = PT, PK = PT}$ where $P = \RP^2, K$ is the Klein bottle, and $T$ is a 2-torus of genus 1. Thus every surface is homeomorphic to either $\Sigma_{g}$ for $g\geq 0$, i.e. $S^2, \Sigma_1 = T^2, \Sigma_2 = T^2\# T^2, \cdots$ **or** if it is nonorientable, a surface $\bar \Sigma_1 = \RP^2, \bar \Sigma_2 = \RP^2 \# \RP^2, \cdots$.
:::
