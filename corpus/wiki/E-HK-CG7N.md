::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 1.3.2"}
If

$$
A = \left[ \begin{array}{c c c} 3 & - 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & - 3 & 0 \end{array} \right]
$$

find all solutions of AX = 0 by row-reducing A.
:::
