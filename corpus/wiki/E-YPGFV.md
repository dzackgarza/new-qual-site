:::{.exercise title="Integral computation"}
Compute
\[
\int_{\bd\DD} {e^z\over z^2}\dz
.\]

#complex/exercise/completed

:::

:::{.solution}
By Cauchy's formula,
\[
\int {f(z) \over (z-0)^2}\dz = 2\pi i f^{(1)}(0) = 2\pi i
.\]
:::
