::: problem
For each positive integer $n$, let $f_n(x) = \frac{x}{x+n}$ for $x \in [0, \infty)$.
Show that the sequence of functions $\{f_n\}$ converges pointwise on $[0, \infty)$ to the $0$-function but does not converge uniformly.
:::
