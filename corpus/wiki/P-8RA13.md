::: problem
For $n\in\mathbb{N}$, let $u_n(x)=\dfrac{\ln(1+x^n)}{n}$.
For which $x\in\mathbb{R}$ does $f(x)=\sum_{n=1}^\infty u_n(x)$ converge?
For which $x\in\mathbb{R}$ is $f$ differentiable?
:::
