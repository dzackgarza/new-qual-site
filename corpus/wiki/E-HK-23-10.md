::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 2.3.10"}
Let $V$ be a vector space over the field $F$. Suppose there are a finite number of vectors $\alpha_1, \ldots, \alpha_r$ in $V$ which span $V$. Prove that $V$ is finite-dimensional.
:::
