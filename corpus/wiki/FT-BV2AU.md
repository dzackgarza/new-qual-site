::: {.theorem}
Let $f\in L^1$ and $\varepsilon> 0$.

1. Small Tails: there exists an $N$ such that $\int_{B_N^c} f < \varepsilon$.

2. Absolute Continuity: there exists a $\delta$ such that $m(E) < \delta \implies \int_E \abs{f} < \varepsilon$.
:::
