:::{.proposition title="$\pi_1$ of a product, Hatcher 1.12"}
If $X, Y$ are path-connected, then 
\[
\pi_1 (X \cross Y) = \pi_1(X) \cross \pi_1(Y)
.\]

:::{.remark}
Hatcher, *Algebraic Topology*, Proposition 1.12.
:::
:::
