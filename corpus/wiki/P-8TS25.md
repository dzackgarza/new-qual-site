::: problem
Let $X$ be the quotient space
\[
X=\frac{S^1\times[0,1]}{(e^{i\theta},1)\sim(e^{3i\theta},0)\text{ for all }e^{i\theta}\in S^1}.
\]
Show how to give $X$ the structure of a cell complex, and use this to compute the homology (in all degrees) of $X$.
:::
