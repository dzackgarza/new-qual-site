::: {.theorem title="Sylow 2"}
All Sylow$\dash p$ subgroups $S_p$ are conjugate, i.e.
\[
S_p^i, S_p^j \in \mathrm{Syl}_p(G) \implies \exists g \text{ such that } g S_p^i g\inv = S_p^j
\]
:::
