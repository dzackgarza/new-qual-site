::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 7.2.6"}
Let $T$, $V$, and $F$ be as in Exercise 5. Suppose $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are vectors in $V$ which have the same $T$-annihilator.
Prove that, for any polynomial $f$, the vectors $f\alpha$ and $f\beta$ have the same $T$-annihilator.
:::
