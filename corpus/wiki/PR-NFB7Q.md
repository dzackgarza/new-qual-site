::: {.proposition}
The following are dense subspaces of $L^2([0, 1])$ and $L^1(\RR)$:

- Bounded measurable functions with bounded support

- Simple functions

- Step functions

- $C_0([0, 1])$

- Smoothly differentiable functions $C_0^\infty([0, 1])$

- Smooth compactly supported functions $C_c^\infty$
:::
