:::{.definition title="Exact Sequences"}
A sequence of \(R\dash\)module morphisms 
\[
0 \mapsvia{d_1} A \mapsvia{d_2} B \mapsvia{d_3} C \to 0
\]
is *exact* iff $\im d_i = \ker d_{i+1}$.
:::
