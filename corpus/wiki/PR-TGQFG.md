:::{.proposition title="Volumes of Rectangles"}
If $\ts{R_j} \covers R$ is a covering of $R$ by rectangles,
\[
R = \overset{\circ}{\Disjoint_{j}} R_j &\implies \abs R = \sum \abs R_j \\
R \subseteq \Union_j R_j &\implies \abs R \leq \sum \abs R_j
.\]

:::
