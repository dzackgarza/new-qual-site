:::{.proposition title="Uniform Convergence of Series"}
A series of functions $\sum_{n=1}^\infty f_n(x)$ converges uniformly iff 
\[  
\lim_{n\to \infty} \norm{ \sum_{k\geq n} f_k }_\infty = 0
.\]
:::
