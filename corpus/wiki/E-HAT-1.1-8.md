Does the Borsuk–Ulam theorem hold for the torus? In other words, for every map $f: S^1 \times S^1 \longrightarrow \mathbb{R}^2$ must there exist $(x, y) \in S^1 \times S^1$ such that $f(x, y) = f(-x, -y)$?
