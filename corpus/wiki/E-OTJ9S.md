::: {.exercise title="Munkres §18.4"}

Given $x_0 \in X$ and $y_0 \in Y$, show that the maps $f: X \to X \times Y$ and $g: Y \to X \times Y$ defined by

$$
f(x) = x \times y_0 \quad \text{and} \quad g(y) = x_0 \times y
$$

are imbeddings.
:::
