::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 3.5.6"}
Let $m$ and $n$ be positive integers and $F$ a field.
Let $f_1, \ldots, f_m$ be linear functionals on $F^n$.
For $\alpha$ in $F^n$ define
\[
T\alpha = (f_1(\alpha), \ldots, f_m(\alpha)).
\]
Show that $T$ is a linear transformation from $F^n$ into $F^m$.
Then show that every linear transformation from $F^n$ into $F^m$ is of the above form, for some $f_1, \ldots, f_m$.
:::
