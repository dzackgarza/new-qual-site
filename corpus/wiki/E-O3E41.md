A space $X$ is said to be weakly locally connected at $x$ if for every neighborhood $U$ of $x$, there is a connected subspace of $X$ contained in $U$ that contains a neighborhood of $x$. Show that if $X$ is weakly locally connected at each of its points, then $X$ is locally connected. [Hint: Show that components of open sets are open.]

::: {.remark}
Munkres, *Topology*, §25 Exercise 6.
:::
