::: problem
Let $f:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$ be a nonnegative integrable function.

(a) Show that $\sin\circ f$ is integrable.

(b) Use Fubini's theorem to show that
\[
\int_{[0,\infty)} m(\{x:f(x)\ge y\})\cos y\,dy=\int_{\mathbb{R}}\sin\bigl(f(x)\bigr)\,dx.
\]
:::
