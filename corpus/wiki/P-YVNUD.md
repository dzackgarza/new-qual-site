::: problem
1.
Let $f(z) \in H({\mathbb D})$, $\text{Re}(f(z)) >0$ and $f(0)= a>0$. 
Show that 
$$
\abs{ \frac{f(z)-a}{f(z)+a}} \leq |z|, \; \; \; |f'(0)| \leq 2a
.$$
:::
