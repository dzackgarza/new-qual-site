::: {.lemma title="?"}
Let $V$ be a vector space over $k$ an algebraically closed and $A \in \mathrm{End}(V)$.
Then if $W \subseteq V$ is an invariant subspace, so $A(W) \subseteq W$, the $A$ has an eigenvector in $W$.
:::
