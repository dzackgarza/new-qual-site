::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 7.3.2"}
Use the result of Exercise 1 and the Jordan form to prove the following.
Let $A$ and $B$ be $n \times n$ matrices over the field $F$ which have the same characteristic polynomial
\[
f = (x - c_1)^{d_1} \cdots (x - c_k)^{d_k}
\]
and the same minimal polynomial.
If no $d_i$ is greater than 3, then $A$ and $B$ are similar.
:::
