::: {.exercise title="Hoffman and Kunze 7.2.5"}
Let $T$ be a linear operator on the vector space $V$ over the field $F$. If $f$ is a polynomial over $F$ and $\alpha$ is in $V$, let $f\alpha = f(T)\alpha$. If $V_1, \ldots, V_k$ are $T$-invariant subspaces and $V = V_1 \oplus \cdots \oplus V_k$, show that
\[
fV = fV_1 \oplus \cdots \oplus fV_k.
\]
:::
