::: problem
Compute the homology groups $H_i(\RP^n/\RP^m;\ZZ)$ of the quotient space $\RP^n/\RP^m$, for $m<n$.
Use cellular homology, using the standard CW structure on $\RP^n$ with $\RP^m$ as its $m$-skeleton.
:::
