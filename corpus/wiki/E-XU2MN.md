Let $X$ be a space that is locally $m$-euclidean. Show that $X$ is Hausdorff if and only if $X$ is completely regular.

::: {.remark}
Munkres, *Topology*, §50 Supplementary Exercises (Locally Euclidean Spaces) Exercise 7.
:::
