::: problem
(a) What does it mean to say that $p:Y\to X$ is a covering map?

(b) Let $p:Y\to X$ be a covering map.
Prove that, for any $y\in Y$, the homomorphism
\[
p_*:\pi_1(Y,y)\to\pi_1(X,p(y))
\]
is injective.

(c) Let $p:Y\to X$ be a covering map with $Y$ and $X$ path-connected.
Suppose that $p_*$ is an isomorphism.
Prove that $p$ is a homeomorphism.
:::
