::: problem
Let $k$ be a field of characteristic zero and $A, B \in M_n(k)$ be two square $n\times n$ matrices over $k$ such that $AB - BA = A$.
Prove that $\det A = 0$.

Moreover, when the characteristic of $k$ is 2, find a counterexample to this statement.
:::
