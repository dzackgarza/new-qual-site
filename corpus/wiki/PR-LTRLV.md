::: {.proposition title="Lipschitz $\iff$ differentiable with bounded derivative."}
A function $f: (a, b) \to \RR$ is Lipschitz $\iff f$ is differentiable and $f'$ is bounded.
In this case, $\abs{f'(x)} \leq C$, the Lipschitz constant.
:::
