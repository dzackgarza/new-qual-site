6) Let $D = \{ z \in \mathbf { C } : | z | < 1 \}$ and P and Q be distinct points in D. Prove the following statement: If f and g are conformal (or equivalently biholomorphic) self-maps of D, $f ( P ) = g ( P )$ and $f ( Q ) = g ( Q )$ , then $f \equiv g$
