::: problem
Let $k$ be an even integer, and let $n$ be any positive integer.
Show that there is a map $f : S^{2n+1} \to \mathbb{RP}^{2n+1}$ of degree $k$.
Show that there is no map from $S^{2n+1}$ to $\mathbb{RP}^{2n+1}$ of odd degree.
:::
