::: problem
Let $A$ be a PID and let $M$ be a torsion finitely generated $A$-module. Suppose that $p \in A \setminus \{0\}$ is a prime element such that $p^k$ is one of the elementary divisors of $M$ for some $k \geq 1$. Show that $M$ has a submodule which is isomorphic to $A/(p)$ as an $A$-module.
:::
