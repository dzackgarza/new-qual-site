::: problem
Let $G$ be a finite group of order $p^nm$ where $p$ is a prime and $m$ is not divisible by $p$.
Prove that if $H$ is a subgroup of $G$ of order $p^k$ for some $k<n$, then the normalizer of $H$ in $G$ properly contains $H$.
:::
