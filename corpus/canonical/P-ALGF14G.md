::: problem
Let $L/K$ be a Galois extension with finite Galois group $G$. Consider the natural action of the group $G$ on $L$. Let $H$ be a subgroup of $G$. Prove that there exists an element $\alpha \in L$ whose stabilizer is $H$.
:::
