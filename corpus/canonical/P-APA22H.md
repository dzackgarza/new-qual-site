::: problem
Let $\{v_1, v_2, v_3, v_4\}$ be a linearly independent set in a $\mathbb{C}$-vector space $V$. Prove that the tensor $\omega = v_1 \wedge v_2 + v_3 \wedge v_4$ cannot be represented as a single-term exterior product.
:::
