(a) Describe the Galois group of $\mathbb{F}_{p^n}$ over $\mathbb{F}_p$, where $p$ is a prime and $n \geq 1$.

(b) Find fields $K$ and $F$ such that $K$ is a splitting field over $F$, but $K$ is NOT separable over $F$.
