::: problem
Suppose that $D$ is an integral domain and $M$ is a $D$-module.

(1) Prove that if $M$ is flat, then it is torsion-free.

(2) Prove that if $D$ is a PID, and $M$ is finitely generated and torsion-free, then $M$ is flat.
:::
