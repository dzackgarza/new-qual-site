::: problem
Let $A,B\in\mathbb{R}^{n\times n}$ be two real symmetric positive definite matrices. If $A-B$ is positive definite, is $B^{-1}-A^{-1}$ also positive definite? If yes, give a proof; if not, give a counterexample.
:::
