::: problem
(a) Prove that for $n \geq 5$, the alternating group $A_n$ has no subgroup $H$ such that $|A_n : H| = n/2$.
Conclude that $A_n$ has no subgroup isomorphic to $S_{n-1}$.

(b) Is the result of part (a) true when $n = 4$?
:::
