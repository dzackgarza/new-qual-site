Let $f \in L^1(\mathbb{R})$ and define $E_\alpha = \{x : |f(x)| > \alpha\}$. Prove that

$$\int_{\mathbb{R}} |f(x)| \, dx = \int_0^\infty m(E_\alpha) \, d\alpha.$$
