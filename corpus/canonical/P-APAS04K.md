::: problem
Consider the equations
\begin{align}
x^2+2y^2&=2\\
x^2-xy+y^2&=1.
\end{align}

(a) Let $I$ be the ideal of $\mathbb{C}[x,y]$ generated by these equations.
Find the Gröbner basis for $I$ relative to lexicographic order where $y>x$.

(b) Find a Gröbner basis for $\mathbb{C}[x]\cap I$.

(c) Find all solutions to these equations that lie in $\mathbb{C}^2$.

(d) Find a vector space basis for $\mathbb{C}[x,y]/I$.
:::
