::: problem
Let $f(z)$ be analytic in the disk $B(0, 2)$.
Show that there is a function $g(z)$, analytic in the unit disk $\mathbb{D}$, such that the series $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} f^{(n)}(z)$ converges to $g$ in $\mathcal{O}(\mathbb{D})$.
:::
