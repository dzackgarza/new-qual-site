::: problem
Let $M$ be a closed connected simply-connected $4$-manifold.
Show that $H_1(M; \mathbb{Z}) = H_3(M; \mathbb{Z}) = 0$ and that $H_2(M; \mathbb{Z})$ is a free abelian group.
:::
