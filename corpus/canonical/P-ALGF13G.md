::: problem
Let $L/K$ be an algebraic extension. Let
\[
K^{\mathrm{ab}} = \{\alpha \in L \mid K[\alpha]/K \text{ is Galois and } \mathrm{Gal}(K[\alpha]/K) \text{ is abelian}\}.
\]

(a) Prove that if $K \subseteq E \subseteq L$, $E/K$ is Galois and $\mathrm{Gal}(E/K)$ is abelian, then $E \subseteq K^{\mathrm{ab}}$.

(b) Prove that $K^{\mathrm{ab}}$ is a subfield of $L$.
:::
