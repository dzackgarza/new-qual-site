::: problem
Let $X$ be the CW complex formed by attaching $k$ two-cells $e^2_1, \ldots, e^2_k$ to the circle $S^1\ (= e^0 \cup e^1)$ via attaching maps with degrees $n_1, n_2, \ldots, n_k$. Compute $\pi_2(X)$ in terms of $n_1, \ldots, n_k$.
:::
