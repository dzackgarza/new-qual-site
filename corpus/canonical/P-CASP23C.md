::: problem
Let $f(z)$ be analytic on an open set containing $\overline{\mathbb{D}}$.
Suppose that $|f(z)| > m$ for $|z| = 1$ and $|f(0)| < m$ for a positive number $m$.
Prove that $f(z)$ has a zero in $\mathbb{D}$.
:::
