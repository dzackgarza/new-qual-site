::: problem
Let $f$ and $g$ be meromorphic functions in $\mathbb{C}$. Assume that $|f(z) + g(z)| \leq |g(z)|$ for every $z \in \mathbb{C}$ which is not a pole of either $f$ or $g$. Show that there is a constant $c$ with $|c + 1| \leq 1$ such that $f(z) = cg(z)$.
:::
