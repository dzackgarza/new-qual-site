::: problem
Let $L/\mathbb{Q}$ be a splitting field for the polynomial $x^{11} - 1$. Find all intermediate fields $L/M/\mathbb{Q}$. For each intermediate field $M$ find an element $\alpha \in M$ such that $M = \mathbb{Q}(\alpha)$.
:::
