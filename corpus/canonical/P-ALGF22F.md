::: problem
Let $A$ be a subring of an integral domain $B$ and let $C$ be the integral closure of $A$ inside $B$. Let $f$ and $g$ be monic polynomials with coefficients in $B$ such that all of the coefficients of $fg$ lie in $C$. Prove that the coefficients of $f$ and $g$ belong to $C$.
:::
