::: problem
Let $G$ be a simple group of order $168 = (2^3)(3)(7)$.
For each $n$ below, calculate the number of subgroups of order $n$ inside $G$.
(For some $n$ the answer could be 0.)

(a) $n = 7$

(b) $n = 21$

(c) $n = 42$
:::
