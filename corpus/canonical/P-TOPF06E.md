::: problem
Let $M$ be a $2n$ dimensional compact manifold without boundary. Show that
$$
\sum_i \dim H^i(M; \mathbb{Z}/2) = \chi(M) \pmod{2}
$$
where $\chi(M)$ denotes the Euler characteristic of $M$.
:::
