::: problem
Let $n \geq 1$ be an integer.
Let $u: \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{R}$ be a harmonic function such that $|u(x)| \leq C(1 + |x|^n)$ for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$.
Show that $u$ is a polynomial.
:::
