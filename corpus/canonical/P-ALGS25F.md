::: problem
Suppose $p$ is an odd prime. Let $E$ be a splitting field of $x^p - 2x - 1$ over $\mathbb{F}_p$. Prove that
\[
\operatorname{Gal}(E/\mathbb{F}_p) \simeq \langle 2 + p\mathbb{Z}\rangle \subseteq (\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z})^\times.
\]
:::
