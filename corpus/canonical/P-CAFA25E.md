::: problem
Let $f : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}$ be an entire function, and define $f_n(z) = f(nz)$.
Suppose that $\mathcal{F} = \{f_n : n \geq 1\}$ is a normal family on the annulus $\{1 < |z| < 2\}$.
Show that $f$ is constant.
:::
