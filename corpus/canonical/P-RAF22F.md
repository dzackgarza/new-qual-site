::: problem
Suppose that $\mu$ is a Radon measure on $X$ (a locally compact Hausdorff space).
Assume $\varphi \in L^1(\mu)$ and $\varphi \geq 0$.
Prove that $\nu(E) = \int_E \varphi \, d\mu$ is a Radon measure.
:::
