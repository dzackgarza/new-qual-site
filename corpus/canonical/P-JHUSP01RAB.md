2.) Suppose that $f \in L ^ { 1 } ( \mathbb { R } ^ { n } )$ . Prove that for every $\varepsilon > 0$ there is a $\delta > 0$ such that if A is measurable with measure $< \delta$ then

$$
| \int _ { A } f d x | < \varepsilon .
$$
