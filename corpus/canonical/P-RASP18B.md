::: problem
Let $\{f_n\}_{n \geq 1}$ be a sequence of functions in $L^2([0,1], m)$ such that $\|f_n\|_2 \leq 1$ for all $n \geq 1$. Assume that $\lim_{n \to \infty} f_n(x) = 0$ for almost every $x \in [0,1]$.

1. Show that $\lim_{n \to \infty} \|f_n\|_1 = 0$.

2. Give an example showing that we do not necessarily have $\lim_{n \to \infty} \|f_n\|_2 = 0$.
:::
