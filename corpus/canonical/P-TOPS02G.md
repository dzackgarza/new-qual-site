::: problem
If all $n$-fold cup products vanish on $H^*(Y)$ and $f : X \to Y$ is a continuous map, prove all $n+1$ fold cup products vanish in $H^*(C_f)$ where $C_f$ is the mapping cone of $f$.
:::
