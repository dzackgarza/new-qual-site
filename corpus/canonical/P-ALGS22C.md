::: problem
Let $R$ be a PID and let $M$ be a finitely generated $R$-module.
Let $K$ be the field of fractions of $R$.

Suppose that you do not know $M$ but are given the value of $\dim_K(M \otimes_R K)$ as well as $\dim_{R/\mathfrak{m}}(M \otimes_R (R/\mathfrak{m}))$ for each maximal ideal $\mathfrak{m}$ of $R$.
Which of the following can you determine precisely from this information?
If the data can be determined, explain how, and if not, give 2 examples of $M$ to show that the calculation is ambiguous.

(a) The rank of $M$.

(b) The number of elementary divisors of $M$ that are powers of a given prime $p$.

(c) The exact list of elementary divisors of $M$.
:::
