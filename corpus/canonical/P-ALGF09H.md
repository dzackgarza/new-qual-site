::: problem
Let $R$ be a commutative ring with identity. Suppose that $I$ and $J$ are ideals of $R$ such that $R/I$ and $R/J$ are Noetherian rings. Prove that $R/(I \cap J)$ is also a Noetherian ring.
:::
