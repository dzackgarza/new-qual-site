::: problem
Let $X$ be a path-connected space with $\pi_{\geq 2}(X) = 0$ and whose fundamental group is a free group on a set $S$.
Show that there is a homotopy equivalence between a bouquet of circles, indexed by $S$, and $X$.
:::
