Let $X_n$ be the bouquet of $n$ circles, whose fundamental group (based at the vertex of the bouquet) is the free group $F_n$ on $n$ generators.

(a) Draw a covering of $X_3$ by $X_5$. Find the subgroup of $F_3 = \langle a, b, c \rangle$ to which your cover corresponds under the correspondence between subgroups of $F_3$ and based connected covers of $X_3$.

(b) Show that $X_4$ cannot cover $X_3$.
