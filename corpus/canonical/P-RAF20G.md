::: problem
Let $X$ be a locally compact Hausdorff topological vector space. Let $f \in C_0(X)$ and $f_k \in C_0(X)$ ($k = 1, 2, \ldots$). Prove that $f_k \to f$ weakly in $C_0(X)$ if and only if $\sup_{k \geq 1} \|f_k\|_u < \infty$ and $f_k \to f$ pointwise on $X$.
:::
