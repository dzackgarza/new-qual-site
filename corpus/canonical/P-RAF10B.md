::: problem
Let $f \in L^1(\mathbb{R})$.
Prove that the function
$$
g(y) = \int_{\mathbb{R}} \sin(y^2 x)\,f(x)\,dx
$$
is continuous and bounded on $\mathbb{R}$.
:::
