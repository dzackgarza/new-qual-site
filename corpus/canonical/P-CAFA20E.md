::: problem
Let $\mathcal{F}$ be a non-empty family of analytic functions on the unit disc $\mathbb{D}$. Assume for every $f \in \mathcal{F}$ it holds that
$$\int_{\mathbb{D}} |f(z)|(1 - |z|)^5 \, dm < 10.$$
Prove $\mathcal{F}$ is a normal family.

Here $dm$ denotes the standard measure in $\mathbb{R}^2$ (i.e.\ $dm = dx\,dy$ for $z = x + iy$).
:::
