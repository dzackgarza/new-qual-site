::: problem
(a) Let $I\subseteq\mathbb{C}[x,y]$ be an ideal such that $\mathrm{V}(I)=\{(0,0),(1,1)\}\subset\mathbb{C}^2$. Prove that the quotient ring $\mathbb{C}[x,y]/I$ is a finite-dimensional $\mathbb{C}$-vector space.

(b) Is the conclusion of (a) still true if we replace $\mathbb{C}$ by $\mathbb{R}$? Justify your answer.
:::
