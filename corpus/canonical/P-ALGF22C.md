::: problem
Let $K = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2}, \sqrt{3}, i)$ as a subfield of $\mathbb{C}$, where $i = \sqrt{-1}$.

(a) Find, with proof, $\operatorname{Gal}(K/\mathbb{Q})$.

(b) Find an element $\beta \in K$ such that $K = \mathbb{Q}(\beta)$.
:::
