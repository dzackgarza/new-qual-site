::: problem
Let $G = \langle a, b \rangle$ denote the free group on two letters, and let $H \leq G$ be the subgroup generated by the elements $\{x_i\}_{i \in \mathbb{Z}}$, where $x_i = a^i b^{-1} a^{1-i}$.
Show that $H$ is free.
:::
