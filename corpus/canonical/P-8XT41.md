Assume that $f : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}$ is an entire function, not identically equal to 0 and let $\mathcal{Z} = \{z \in \mathbb{C} : f(z) = 0\}$.
Prove that if $\mathcal{Z}$ is unbounded, then $f$ has an essential singularity at $\infty$.
