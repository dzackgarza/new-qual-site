::: problem
Let $h \in C([-1, 1], \mathbb{R})$ be a one-to-one function and let $Z$ denote the space of functions of the form $p(h) := \sum_{k=0}^n a_k h^k$ for some $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $a_k \in \mathbb{C}$. Show $Z$ is a dense subspace of $L^1([-1, 1], \mathcal{B}_{[-1,1]}, m)$.
:::
