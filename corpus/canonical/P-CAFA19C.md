::: problem
Let $A_1 = \{z \in \mathbb{C} : 0 < |z| < 1\}$ and $A_2 = \{z \in \mathbb{C} : 1 < |z| < 2\}$.
Prove $A_1$ and $A_2$ are not conformally equivalent.
