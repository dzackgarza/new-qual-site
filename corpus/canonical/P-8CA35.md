Let $f : D \to D$ be a holomorphic function where $D = \{z \in \mathbb{C} : |z| < 1\}$ is the unit disk. Prove that if $f$ has at least 2 fixed points then $f$ is the identity map. (Note: A point $a$ is said to be a fixed point of $f$ if $f(a) = a$.)
