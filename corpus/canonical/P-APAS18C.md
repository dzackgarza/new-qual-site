::: problem
Let $P\in\mathbb{C}^{n\times n}$ be a positive definite Hermitian matrix.
Show that
\[
|x^*y|^2\leq(x^*Px)(y^*P^{-1}y)
\]
for all $x,y\in\mathbb{C}^n$.
:::
