4. Suppose that $u : \mathbb { C } \to \mathbb { R }$ is a harmonic function such that

$$
u ( z ) \leq 1 0 \log ( | z | + 2 ) ,
$$

for all $z \in \mathbb { C }$ . Prove that u is constant.
