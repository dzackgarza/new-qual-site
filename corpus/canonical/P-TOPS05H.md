::: problem
Show there is no map $f : \mathbb{HP}^n \to \mathbb{CP}^{2n}$ such that the induced map $H^{4n}(f) : H^{4n}(\mathbb{HP}^n; \mathbb{Z}) \to H^{4n}(\mathbb{CP}^{2n}; \mathbb{Z})$ maps the generator of $\mathbb{HP}^n$ to $k(\alpha^{2n})$ for $k \neq 0$.
:::
