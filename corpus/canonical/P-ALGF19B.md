::: problem
For any finite group $H$ and prime $\ell$, we denote the set of Sylow $\ell$-subgroups of $H$ by $\mathrm{Syl}_\ell(H)$. Suppose $p < q$ are odd primes, $G$ is a finite group, and $|\mathrm{Syl}_p(G)| = p + 1$ and $|\mathrm{Syl}_q(G)| = q + 1$.

(a) Suppose $P \in \mathrm{Syl}_p(G)$. Prove that $G$ has a Sylow $q$-subgroup $Q$ such that $Q \subseteq N_G(P)$.

(b) In the setting of part (a), let $H = PQ$. Argue why $H$ is a subgroup and prove that either $Q \trianglelefteq H$ or $|\mathrm{Syl}_q(H)| = q + 1$.

(c) In the setting of part (b), prove that $H \simeq P \times Q$.
:::
