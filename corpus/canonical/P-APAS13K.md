::: problem
Let $S$ be the parametric surface defined by
\begin{align*}
x&=u-2v,\\
y&=uv,\\
z&=v.
\end{align*}

(a) Compute a reduced Gröbner basis for the ideal generated by this set of equations relative to the lexicographic order where $u>v>x>y>z$.

(b) Find the equation of the smallest variety $V$ that contains $S$.

(c) Show that $S=V$.
:::
