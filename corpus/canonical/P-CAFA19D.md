::: problem
Let $b \in \mathbb{D}$ and set $f(z) = z^7 - 2z^5 + b$.

(a) How many roots (counting multiplicity) does $f$ have in $\mathbb{D}$? How many simple roots does $f$ have in $\mathbb{D}$?

(b) How many simple roots does $f$ have in $\{1 \leq |z| < 2\}$?
:::
