::: problem
Let $A\in\mathbb{R}^{n\times n}$ be a matrix such that $\operatorname{rank} A^2\leq\operatorname{rank} A^3$. Show that
\[
\operatorname{rank} A^k\leq\operatorname{rank} A^{k+1}
\]
for all integers $k\geq 3$.
:::
