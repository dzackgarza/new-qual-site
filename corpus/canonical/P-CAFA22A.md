::: problem
Let $G \subset \mathbb{C}$ be a bounded, simply connected region and let $f$ be an analytic self-map of $G$ (i.e. $f(G) \subset G$). Assume that $f$ has two fixed points.
Show that $f(z) = z$.
:::
