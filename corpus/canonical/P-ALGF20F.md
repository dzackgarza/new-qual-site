::: problem
Let $A$ be a commutative unital ring.
Let $\{a_1, \ldots, a_n\} \subset A$ be such that the ideal generated by $\{a_1, \ldots, a_n\}$ equals $A$.
For every $1 \leq i \leq n$, put $S_i = \{1, a_i, a_i^2, \ldots\}$.
Let $M$ be an $A$-module with submodule $N$, and assume that for all $1 \leq i \leq n$ we have $S_i^{-1}M = S_i^{-1}N$.
Prove that $N = M$.
(Hint: for $x \in M$ consider $\{ a \in A \mid ax \in N \}$.)
:::
