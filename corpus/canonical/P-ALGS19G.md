::: problem
(a) Suppose $F/\mathbb{F}_{p^n}$ is a finite field extension where $p$ is prime and $n \in \mathbb{Z}^+$. Prove that $\operatorname{Gal}(F/\mathbb{F}_{p^n}) = \langle \sigma^n \rangle$, where $\sigma(a) := a^p$ is the Frobenius automorphism of $F$. (You may use without proof the fact that $F/\mathbb{F}_p$ is a Galois extension and $\operatorname{Gal}(F/\mathbb{F}_p) = \langle \sigma \rangle$.)

(b) Suppose $g(x)$ is an irreducible factor of $x^{p^n} - x + 1$ in $\mathbb{F}_{p^n}[x]$ where $p$ is prime and $n$ is a positive integer. Prove that $\deg g = p$.

Hint: Suppose $\alpha$ is a zero of $g(x)$ and notice that $\sigma^n(\alpha) = \alpha - 1$.
:::
