::: problem
Let $\alpha_n := 1 - \frac{1}{n^2}$. Construct a function $f \in H(\mathbb{D})$ whose sequence of zeros (counting multiplicity) is precisely $\{\alpha_n\}$ and for which $|f(z)| \leq 1$ in $\mathbb{D}$.
:::
