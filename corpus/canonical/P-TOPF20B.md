::: problem
Let $X$ be a path connected CW complex whose fundamental group is finite.
Show that any continuous map $f : X \to T^n$ is null homotopic.
(Here $T^n = S^1 \times \cdots \times S^1$ is the $n$-dimensional torus.)
:::
