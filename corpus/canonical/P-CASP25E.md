::: problem
Let $f_n : \mathbb{D} \to \mathbb{C}$ be a bounded sequence of holomorphic functions, and let $\{z_m\}_{m \geq 1}$ be a sequence in $\mathbb{D}$ that converges in $\mathbb{D}$. Assume that $\lim_{n \to \infty} f_n(z_m)$ exists for all $m \geq 1$. Show that the sequence $\{f_n\}$ converges uniformly on compact subsets of $\mathbb{D}$.
:::
