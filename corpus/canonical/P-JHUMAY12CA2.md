Suppose $f$ is holomorphic on the open unit disc $D(0,1)$ and continuous on $\overline{D(0,1)}$.
Assume $|f(\xi)| < 1$ for $\xi \in \partial D(0,1)$.
Show that there exists a unique point $a \in D(0,1)$ such that $f(a) = a$.
