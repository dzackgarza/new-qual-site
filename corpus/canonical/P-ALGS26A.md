::: problem
Suppose $G$ is a group of order $5 \cdot 7 \cdot 11$.

(a) Prove that $G \cong (\mathbb{Z}/11\mathbb{Z}) \rtimes (\mathbb{Z}/35\mathbb{Z})$.

(b) Prove that there are exactly two such groups up to isomorphism.
:::
