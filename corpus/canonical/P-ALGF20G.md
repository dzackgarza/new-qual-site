::: problem
Let $K$ be the splitting field over $\mathbb{Q}$ of $f(x) = x^4 - 4x^2 + 1$.
Find $\mathrm{Gal}(K/\mathbb{Q})$, and find all fields $E$ such that $\mathbb{Q} \subsetneq E \subsetneq K$.
:::
