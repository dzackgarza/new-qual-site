::: problem
Let $X$ be the space obtained from a solid ball $B^3$ by identifying pairs of antipodal points on its boundary sphere $S^2$.
Decompose $X$ as a CW-complex and compute its homology $H_*(X; \mathbb{Z})$.
:::
