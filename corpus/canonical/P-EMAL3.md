::: problem
Let $V$ be a vector space over a field $F$. The evaluation map $e : V \to (V^\vee)^\vee$ is defined by $e(v)(f) := f(v)$ for $v \in V$ and $f \in V^\vee$.

(a) Prove that $e$ is an injection.

(b) Prove that $e$ is an isomorphism if and only if $V$ is finite dimensional.
:::
