::: problem
Let $A$ be an integral domain and let $F$ be its field of fractions. For any nonzero element $f$ of $A$, let $A[f^{-1}]$ be the subring of $F$ generated by $A$ and $f^{-1}$. Suppose that the ideal generated by $f_1, \ldots, f_k \in A \setminus \{0\}$ is the entire ring $A$, i.e. $(f_1, \ldots, f_k) = A$.

(a) Prove that for any positive integers $n_1, \ldots, n_k$ we have that
\[
(f_1^{n_1}, \ldots, f_k^{n_k}) = A.
\]

(b) Prove that $A = A[f_1^{-1}] \cap A[f_2^{-1}] \cap \cdots \cap A[f_k^{-1}]$. (Hint: for $x$ in the right hand side consider $\{a \in A \mid ax \in A\}$).
:::
