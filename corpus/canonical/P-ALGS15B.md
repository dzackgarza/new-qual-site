::: problem
(a) Let $K/F$ be a field extension and let $f \in F[x]$. If $A = F[x]/(f)$, show that $K \otimes_F A \cong K[x]/(f)$ as $F$-algebras.

(b) Again let $K/F$ be a field extension, assume that $\mathrm{char}\, F = 0$, and let $\alpha, \beta \in K$ be algebraic over $F$. Let $F_1 = F(\alpha)$ and $F_2 = F(\beta)$. Consider the $F$-algebra $R = F_1 \otimes_F F_2$. Show that $R$ is isomorphic as a ring to a direct product of finitely many fields.
:::
