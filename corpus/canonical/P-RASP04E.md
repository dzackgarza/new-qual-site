::: problem
Suppose that $\{f_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty} \subset L^3(\mu)$ such that $\lim_{n \to \infty} \int_X f_n \varphi\,d\mu$ exists in $\mathbb{C}$ for all $\varphi \in L^{3/2}(\mu)$. Show $M := \sup_n \|f_n\|_{L^3(\mu)} < \infty$.
:::
