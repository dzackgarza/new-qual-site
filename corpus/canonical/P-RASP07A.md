::: problem
Prove Chebyshev's Inequality: If $f \in L^p(X, \mu)$, with $0 < p < \infty$, then for any positive number $r$,
$$
\mu(\{x : |f(x)| > r\}) \leq \left[\frac{\|f\|_p}{r}\right]^p.
$$
:::
