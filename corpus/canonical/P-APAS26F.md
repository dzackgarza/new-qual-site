::: problem
Let $\mathcal{B}$ be a commutative subalgebra of an algebra $\mathcal{A}$. Prove that $\mathcal{B}$ is a maximal commutative subalgebra of $\mathcal{A}$ if and only if it is equal to its own centralizer. Using this result, or otherwise, classify the maximal commutative subalgebras of $\operatorname{End}(V)$, where $V$ is a Hilbert space.

Note: On this exam, an algebra is a finite-dimensional complex vector space equipped with an associative, bilinear, unital multiplication and an antilinear, antimultiplicative, involutive conjugation; a Hilbert space is a finite-dimensional complex vector space equipped with a scalar product.
:::
