Let $\Sigma_g$ be a closed orientable surface of genus $g$. A map $\pi : \Sigma_g \to S^2$ is a double branched cover if there is a set $Q = \{p_1, p_2, \ldots, p_n\} \subseteq S^2$ of branch points, so that $\pi$ restricted to $\Sigma_g - \pi^{-1}(Q)$ is a double cover of $S^2 - Q$, but the points $p_i$ have only one preimage each. Use Euler characteristic to find a formula relating $g$ and $n$.
