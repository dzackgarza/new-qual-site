Let $\{f_n\}$ be a sequence of real valued functions in $L^{4/3}(0,1)$ such that $f_n \to 0$ in measure as $n \to \infty$ and $\int_0^1 |f_n(x)|^{4/3} \, dx \leq 1$.
Show that $\int_0^1 |f_n(x)| \, dx \to 0$ as $n \to \infty$.
