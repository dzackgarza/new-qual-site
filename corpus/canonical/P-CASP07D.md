::: problem
Let $n$ and $m$ be positive integers.
Fix $w$ with $|w| \leq 1$ and consider the equation $$z^n + \frac{1}{z^m} = w.$$ How many roots (counting multiplicities) are there in $B(0, 2)$?
:::
