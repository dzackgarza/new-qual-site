::: problem
Let $R$ be a commutative ring, and let $S$ be a multiplicative system in $R$. Let $S^{-1}R$ be the localization of $R$ at $S$ and let $\phi \colon R \to S^{-1}R$ be the canonical homomorphism sending $r \mapsto \frac{r}{1}$. For an ideal $I$ of $R$ we write $I^e$ for the ideal of $S^{-1}R$ generated by $\phi(I)$, and for an ideal $J$ of $S^{-1}R$ we write $J^c$ for the ideal of $R$ given by $\phi^{-1}(J)$.

(a) Prove that for any ideal $J$ of $S^{-1}R$, one has $(J^c)^e = J$, while for any ideal $I$ of $R$, one has $(I^e)^c = \{x \in R \mid sx \in I \text{ for some } s \in S\}$.

(b) Show that if $R$ is noetherian, then $S^{-1}R$ is noetherian. Give an example which shows that the converse need not hold.
:::
