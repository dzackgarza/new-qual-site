::: problem
Let $A$ be an $m \times n$ matrix whose columns are one-hot binary vectors: each column contains exactly one entry equal to $1$ and all other entries are equal to $0$. Let $r_i$ denote the sum of the entries in row $i$ of $A$. Determine the nonzero singular values of $A$, and give explicit orthonormal bases for each of the corresponding left and right singular spaces.
:::
