::: problem
Let $t < 1$.
Show that $f(z) = e^{tz} + z + 1$ has exactly one zero (counting multiplicities) in the left half-plane $\{z : \operatorname{Re} z < 0\}$.

Hint: Consider first $|z| < \varepsilon$ for $\varepsilon > 0$.
:::
