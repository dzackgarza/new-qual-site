::: problem
Let $X$ and $Y$ be two Banach spaces and denote by $L(X, Y)$ the space of all continuous linear operators from $X$ to $Y$. Let $A_n \in L(X, Y)$ ($n = 1, 2, \ldots$). Assume that $\lim_{n \to \infty} A_n(x)$ exists for each $x \in X$. Define $A(x) = \lim_{n \to \infty} A_n(x)$. Prove that $A \in L(X, Y)$.
:::
