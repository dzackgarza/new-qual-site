::: problem
Let $X$ be a compact Hausdorff topological space.
Let $\{K_j\}$ ($j = 1, 2, \ldots$) be a sequence of decreasing, nonempty compact subsets of $X$.
Prove that $\bigcap_{j=1}^\infty K_j \neq \emptyset$.
:::
