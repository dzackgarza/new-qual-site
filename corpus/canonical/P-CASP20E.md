::: problem
Let $A_1 = \mathbb{D} \setminus \left\{0, \frac{1}{2}\right\}$ and $A_2 = \mathbb{D} \setminus \left\{0, -\frac{1}{2}\right\}$.
Find all bijective analytic maps from $A_1$ to $A_2$.
:::
