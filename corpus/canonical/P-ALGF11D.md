::: problem
Assume that $A$ is a commutative Noetherian ring, and let $a_n \in A$ for $n \geq 0$.
Prove that the power series
\[
f = \sum_{n=0}^\infty a_n x^n
\]
is nilpotent if and only if each $a_n \in A$ is nilpotent.
:::
