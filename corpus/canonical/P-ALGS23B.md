::: problem
Suppose $G$ is a finite group of order $p^k m$ where $p$ is prime and $p \nmid m$.
Let $\operatorname{Syl}_p(G)$ be the set of all the Sylow $p$-subgroups of $G$.
Suppose $P_1 \cap P_2 = \{1\}$ for every two distinct Sylow $p$-subgroups $P_1$ and $P_2$.

(a) Prove that $N_G(P_1) \cap P_2 = \{1\}$, where $P_1, P_2 \in \operatorname{Syl}_p(G)$ are distinct.

(b) Suppose $P_0 \in \operatorname{Syl}_p(G)$ and consider its action on $\operatorname{Syl}_p(G)$ by conjugation.
For $P \in \operatorname{Syl}_p(G)$, let $\mathcal{O}_P$ be the $P_0$-orbit of $P$.
Prove that if $P \neq P_0$, then $|\mathcal{O}_P| = p^k$.

(c) Prove that $|G| \equiv 1 \pmod{p^k}$.
:::
