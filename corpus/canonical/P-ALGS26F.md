::: problem
Let $f(x) = x^4 + x + 1 \in \mathbb{F}_2[x]$. Let $E = \mathbb{F}_2[x]/\langle f \rangle$.

(a) Show that $E$ is a field.

(b) Let $\alpha = (x^2 + x) + \langle f \rangle \in E$. Find the number of elements in $\mathbb{F}_2(\alpha)$.
:::
