::: problem
Consider the equations
\begin{align*}
x^2-xy-2x&=0,\\
y^2-2xy-y&=0.
\end{align*}

(a) Let $I$ be the ideal of $\mathbb{C}[x,y]$ generated by these equations. Find the reduced Gröbner basis for $I$ relative to lexicographic order where $y>x$.

(b) Find a reduced Gröbner basis for $\mathbb{C}[x]\cap I$.

(c) Find all solutions to these equations that lie in $\mathbb{C}^2$.

(d) Find a vector space basis for $\mathbb{C}[x,y]/I$.
:::
