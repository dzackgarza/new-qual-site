::: problem
Let $A$ be a finite-dimensional algebra over $\mathbb{C}$ with center $Z(A)$, and let $(V,\rho)$ be an irreducible representation of $A$. Show that $\rho(z)=\chi(z)I_V$ for each $z\in Z(A)$, where $\chi(z)$ is a scalar. Show that the map $\chi\colon Z(A)\to\mathbb{C}$ defined by $z\mapsto\chi(z)$ is an algebra homomorphism.
:::
