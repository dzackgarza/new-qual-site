::: problem
Let $X$ be a $\sigma$-compact locally compact Hausdorff space. Let $\mu$ be a Radon measure on $X$. Let $f \geq 0$ be a measurable function. Prove that if for any open subset $U$, $\mu(U) = \int_U f\,d\mu$, then $f = 1$ $\mu$-a.e.
:::
