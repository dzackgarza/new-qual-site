::: problem
Suppose $E/F$ is a separable extension and $[E:F] = n$. Suppose $\overline{E}$ is an algebraic closure of $E$. Prove that
$$E \otimes_F \overline{E} \simeq \underbrace{\overline{E} \oplus \cdots \oplus \overline{E}}_{n \text{ times}}$$
as rings.
:::
