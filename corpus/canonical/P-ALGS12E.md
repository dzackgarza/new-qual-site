::: problem
Consider a polynomial $f \in K[x]$, where $K$ is an algebraically closed field. Suppose that $f$ has the property that for all matrices $A \in M_n(K)$ of any size $n$, if $f(A) = 0$, then $A$ is a diagonalizable matrix; then we say that the polynomial $f$ forces diagonalizability.

(a) Characterize by a simple rule exactly which polynomials in $K[x]$ force diagonalizability. Prove your answer.

(b) Fix $m \geq 1$. Is every square matrix $A$ with entries in $K$ satisfying $A^m = I$ diagonalizable? (The answer depends on $K$.)
:::
