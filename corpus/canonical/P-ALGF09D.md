::: problem
Let $G$ be any finite group and $F$ any field. Show that there exist fields $L$ and $E$ with $F \subseteq L \subseteq E$, such that $E$ is Galois over $L$ with the Galois group of $E/L$ being isomorphic to $G$.
:::
