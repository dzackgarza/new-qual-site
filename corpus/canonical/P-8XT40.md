Let $f : [0,1] \to [0,1]$ be a continuous function and $\phi : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be a $C^1$ function with $\phi(0) = 0$.
Prove

$$\int_0^1 \phi \circ f \, dx = \int m(\{x \in [0,1] : f(x) > t\}) \, \phi'(t) \, dt$$
