::: problem
Let $(X, \mathcal{M}, \mu)$ be a measure space and $f \in L^1(\mu)$.
Prove that
$$
\lim_{t \to \infty} t \, \mu\{x \in X : |f(x)| \geq t\} = 0.
$$
:::
