::: problem
Consider the ideal $I$ generated by the polynomials $x^3 - y^2 + 1$, $x^3 + y^2 + z^2 - 1$, and $y^2 + y + 1$.

(a) Calculate a Gröbner basis for $I$ with respect to the lexicographic order $x > y > z$.

(b) Calculate all the solutions given by the common zeros of the generating polynomials, i.e.\ calculate the variety $V(I)$ of $I$.
:::
