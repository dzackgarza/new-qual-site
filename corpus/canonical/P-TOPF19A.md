::: problem
Let $f : \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}^n$ be a continuous map.
Suppose that there exists a uniform constant $C$ such that $\|f(\vec{x}) - \vec{x}\| \leq C$ for any $\vec{x} \in \mathbb{R}^n$.
Prove that $f$ is surjective.
:::
