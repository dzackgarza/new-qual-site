::: problem
The cone $CX$ of a space $X$ is $X \times I$ with $X \times \{0\}$ crushed to a point. The mapping cone of a map $f \colon X \to Y$ is the space $C_f$ obtained by gluing $CX$ to $Y$ using the map $f \colon X \times \{1\} \to Y$. Show that there is a long exact sequence
\[
\cdots \to H_i X \to H_i Y \to \widetilde{H}_i C_f \to H_{i-1} X \to \cdots.
\]
:::
