::: problem
Let $R$ be a commutative ring with unit, $M$ be an $R$-module and $I$ an ideal of $R$.
Suppose that $M_{\mathfrak{m}} = 0$ for all maximal ideals $\mathfrak{m}$ of $R$ that contain $I$.
Prove that $M = IM$.
:::
