::: problem
Let $X$ be a space whose homology is given by
\[
H_k(X; \mathbb{Z}) =
\begin{cases}
\mathbb{Z}_4 & \text{if } k = 2, \\
\mathbb{Z} & \text{if } k = 0, \\
0 & \text{otherwise.}
\end{cases}
\]
Compute $H_*(\mathbb{RP}^2 \times X; \mathbb{Z})$ and $H_*(\mathbb{RP}^2 \times X; \mathbb{Z}_2)$.
:::
