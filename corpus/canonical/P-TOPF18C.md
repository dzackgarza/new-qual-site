::: problem
Let $Y$ be a space obtained by attaching a $4$-ball $B^4$ to a $3$-sphere via a degree $6$ map $\varphi : \partial B^4 \to S^3$.
Calculate the integral homology $H_*(Y \times \mathbb{RP}^2; \mathbb{Z})$.
:::
