::: problem
Suppose $A$ is a unital commutative ring and $\langle a_1, \ldots, a_n\rangle = A$. Suppose $M$ is an $A$-module and $S_{a_i} := \{1, a_i, a_i^2, \ldots\}$. Suppose that $S_{a_i}^{-1}M = 0$ for all $i$. Prove that $M = 0$.
(Hint. For $x \in M$, consider the annihilator $\operatorname{ann}(x)$ of $x$.)
:::
