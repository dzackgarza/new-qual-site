::: problem
(a) Show the following: Let $G$ be a finite group, and let $g \mapsto A_g \in \operatorname{GL}(\mathbb{C}^n)$ and $g \mapsto B_g \in \operatorname{GL}(\mathbb{C}^n)$ be two equivalent representations of $G$. Show that the rings of invariants $\mathbb{C}[x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n]^G$ defined by these two actions have the same Hilbert series.

(b) Give two examples of an action of a group $G$ on a two-dimensional vector space which lead to two different Hilbert series of the corresponding rings $\mathbb{C}[x,y]^G$.
:::
