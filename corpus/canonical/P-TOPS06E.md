::: problem
Let $X(n, k) = \mathbb{RP}^n / \mathbb{RP}^k$ denote the quotient space of $\mathbb{RP}^n$ obtained by identifying $\mathbb{RP}^k$ to a point for $0 < k < n$. Calculate the mod $2$ cohomology ring of $X(2k+2, k)$.
:::
