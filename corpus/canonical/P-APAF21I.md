::: problem
(a) Let $C(d)$ be the group generated by the cyclic permutation $\gamma=(1\ 2\ \dots\ d)$ in the symmetric group $S(d)$. Explicitly describe the dual group of $C(d)$.

(b) State the definition of the Cayley graph of $C(d)$, and find its eigenvalues and eigenvectors.
:::
