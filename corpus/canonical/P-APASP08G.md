::: problem
Show that
$$e^{x_1 + x_2 + \cdots} = \sum_{\lambda} s_\lambda \cdot \frac{1}{h_\lambda}$$
where $h_\lambda$ denotes the product of the hook lengths of $\lambda$.
:::
