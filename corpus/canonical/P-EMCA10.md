(a) State Rouche's theorem.

(b) Let $f$ be analytic in a neighborhood of $0$, and satisfying $f'(0) \neq 0$. Use Rouche's theorem to show that there exists a neighborhood $U$ of $0$ such that $f$ is a bijection in $U$.
