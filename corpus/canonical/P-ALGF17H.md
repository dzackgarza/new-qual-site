::: problem
Let
\[
R = \mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{-10}] = \{ a + b\sqrt{-10} \mid a,b \in \mathbb{Z} \}.
\]
Let $I = \langle 2, \sqrt{-10} \rangle$ be the ideal of $R$ generated by $2$ and $\sqrt{-10}$.

(a) Show that $I$ is not a free $R$-module.

(b) Show that $I$ is a projective $R$-module.
:::
