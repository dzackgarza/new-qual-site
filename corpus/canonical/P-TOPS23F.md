::: problem
Let $M^n$ be a path-connected closed orientable manifold such that there exists a map $f : S^n \to M$ of degree $\pm 1$. Show that $H^*(M; \mathbb{F}) = H^*(S^n; \mathbb{F})$ whenever $\mathbb{F}$ is a field, and hence that $H^*(M; \mathbb{Z}) \cong H^*(S^n; \mathbb{Z})$.
:::
