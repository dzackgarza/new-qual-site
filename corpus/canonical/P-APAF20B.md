::: problem
Suppose $\mathbb{C}^3$ is given its usual inner product, and $\psi\colon\mathbb{C}^3\to\mathbb{C}^3$ is a linear map. Further suppose that the singular values of $\psi$ are $\sigma_1=3$, $\sigma_2=2$ and $\sigma_3=1$.

(a) Determine, with justification, the norms $\|\psi\|_{\mathrm{op}}$ and $\|\psi\|_{\mathrm{Frob}}$.

(b) Let $\lambda\in\mathbb{C}$ be an eigenvalue of $\psi$. Prove that $|\lambda|\in[1,3]$.

(c) Suppose now that $\psi$ is self-adjoint. Prove that $\lambda\in\{-3,-2,-1,1,2,3\}$.
:::
