::: problem
Let $F$ be a finite field of order $q$ and let $E/F$ be a field extension. Suppose that an element $a \in E$ is algebraic over $F$. Prove that $[F(a) : F]$ is the smallest positive integer $n$ such that $a^{q^n} = a$ and that it divides every other such positive integer.
:::
