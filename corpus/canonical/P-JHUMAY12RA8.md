Let $f \in L^1(\mathbb{R})$. Define its Fourier transform $\hat{f}(\xi) = \int f(x) e^{-2\pi i x \cdot \xi} \, dx$. Show that $\hat{f}(\xi) \in C_0(\mathbb{R})$, that is the Fourier transform is continuous and vanishes at infinity. You may not quote the Riemann-Lebesgue lemma without sketching a proof.
