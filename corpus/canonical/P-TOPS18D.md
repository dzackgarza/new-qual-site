::: problem
Let $Y$ be a space obtained by attaching a $4$-ball, via a degree $6$ map of its boundary, to a $3$-sphere.
Calculate the integral homology $H_*(Y \times \mathbb{RP}^2; \mathbb{Z})$.
:::
