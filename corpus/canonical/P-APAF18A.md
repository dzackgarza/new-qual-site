::: problem
For the following matrix
\[
A=\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 2 & 3 & 4 \\
0 & 1 & 2 & 3 \\
0 & 0 & 1 & 2 \\
0 & 0 & 0 & 1
\end{bmatrix},
\]
determine its Jordan canonical form (JCF) and find a nonsingular matrix $P$ such that $P^{-1}AP$ gives the JCF.
:::
