Let $N$ be a knotted solid torus in $S^3$, let $T$ be its boundary torus, and let $X$ be its exterior (that is, the closure of $S^3 - N$). Use Mayer-Vietoris to compute the homology $H_*(X; \mathbb{Z})$.
