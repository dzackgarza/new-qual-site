::: problem
Let $f = x^6 - 5$.
Let $K$ be the splitting field of $f$ over $F = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{5})$.
Find the Galois group $\operatorname{Gal}(K/F)$ and show it is isomorphic to a familiar group.
:::
