::: problem
Let $A$ be a set and $1 < p < \infty$. Prove that a sequence $f_n \in \ell^p(A)$ converges weakly to $f \in \ell^p(A)$ if and only if $(f_n)$ converges to $f$ pointwise and $\sup_n \|f_n\|_p < \infty$.
:::
