::: problem
(a) Let $X \subset \mathbb{R}^3$ be the union of a $2$-dimensional sphere and two disjoint chords intersecting the sphere only at their endpoints. Compute the fundamental group of $X$ by finding a more standard space $Y$ which is homotopy equivalent to $X$. Give a short explanation for why $X$ and $Y$ are homotopy equivalent.

(b) Now let $X$ be obtained similarly as the union of a $2$-dimensional sphere and two chords intersecting the sphere only at their endpoints, but such that the chords intersect at precisely one point lying in the interior of each chord. What is the fundamental group of $X$?
:::
