::: problem
Let $G$ be a finite group, let $V$ be a finite-dimensional complex representation of $G$, and let $\chi\colon G\to\mathbb{C}$ be the character of $V$. Let $g\in G$ be a group element such that $g$ is conjugate to $g^{-1}$. Prove that $\chi(g)$ is a real number.
:::
