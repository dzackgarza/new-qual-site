::: problem
Let $f$ be an analytic function in $B(0, 2)$.
Prove the sequence $g_N(z) := \sum_{n=1}^{N} \frac{f^{(n)}(z)}{n!}$, $N \geq 1$, converges in the space $H(\mathbb{D})$ of analytic functions on $\mathbb{D}$.
:::
