::: problem
Suppose $A$ is a commutative unital ring, and $M$ is an $A$-module.

(a) Prove that, if $M_{\mathfrak{m}} = 0$ for any maximal ideal $\mathfrak{m}$ of $A$, then $M = 0$.

(b) Prove that if $M_{\mathfrak{m}}$ is a flat $A_{\mathfrak{m}}$-module for any maximal ideal $\mathfrak{m}$ of $A$, then $M$ is a flat module. (You do not need to prove that localization is an exact functor.)
:::
