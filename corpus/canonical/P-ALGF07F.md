::: problem
Let $F \geq K$ be an extension field of degree 2.

(a) If $K$ has characteristic not 2, show $F$ is Galois over $K$.

(b) Give an example where $F$ is Galois over $K$ even though $\operatorname{char} K = 2$.
:::
