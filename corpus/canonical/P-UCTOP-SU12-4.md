Let $X$ be a path-connected space whose homology groups in positive dimensions are $H_k(X; \mathbb{Z}) = \mathbb{Z}/k\mathbb{Z}$.
Compute the integer homology $H_*(\mathbb{RP}^2 \times X; \mathbb{Z})$.
