::: problem
Let $X$ be a $3$-dimensional CW complex obtained by attaching a single $3$-dimensional cell to $S^2$ via an attaching map of degree $3$.
Compute the homology group $H_k(X \times X; \mathbb{Z})$ for all $k \geq 0$.
:::
