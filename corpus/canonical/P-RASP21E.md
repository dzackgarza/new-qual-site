::: problem
Find all $f \in L^2([-1, 1])$ such that
$$
\int_{-1}^1 \left| f(x) - \sqrt{2}\, x \right|^2 dx \leq \frac{1}{4}
$$
and
$$
\int_{-1}^1 \left| f(x) - \sqrt{\frac{2}{5}}\, x^2 \right|^2 dx \leq \frac{9}{4}.
$$
:::
