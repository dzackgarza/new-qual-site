::: problem
Find all $f \in L^2([-1,1])$ such that
$$
\int_{-1}^{1} |f(x) - \sqrt{3}\,x|^2\,dx \leq \frac{1}{4}
$$
and
$$
\int_{-1}^{1} |f(x) - \sqrt{5}\,x^2|^2\,dx \leq \frac{9}{4}.
$$
:::
