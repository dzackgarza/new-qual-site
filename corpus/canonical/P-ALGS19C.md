::: problem
Suppose $A$ is a unital commutative ring with no non-zero nilpotent elements.
Suppose $N \in M_n(A)$ is nilpotent.
Prove that $N^n = 0$.

Hint: Prove that $N^n \equiv 0 \pmod{\mathfrak{p}}$ for any $\mathfrak{p} \in \operatorname{Spec}(A)$.
:::
