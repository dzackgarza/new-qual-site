::: problem
Let $R$ be an integral domain. Recall that for any $R$-modules $M, N$, $\mathrm{Hom}_R(M,N)$ is also an $R$-module. Let $M$ and $N$ be torsion $R$-modules.

(a) Suppose that either $M$ or $N$ is finitely generated as an $R$-module. Prove that $\mathrm{Hom}_R(M,N)$ is also a torsion $R$-module.

(b) Give a counterexample showing that $\mathrm{Hom}_R(M,N)$ need not be torsion as an $R$-module if $M$ and $N$ are both infinitely generated. Justify your answer.
:::
