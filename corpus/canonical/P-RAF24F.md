::: problem
Show that for any $p > 1$, there is a constant $C_p < \infty$ such that
\[
\sum_{k=-\infty}^{\infty} |\hat{f}(k)| \le \|f\|_{L^1} + C_p \|f'\|_{L^p}
\quad\text{for each } f \in C^1(\mathbb{T}).
\]
:::
