::: problem
Let $h$ be a bounded harmonic function on the unit disc $\mathbb{D} = \{z : |z| < 1\}$.
Assume that
$$
\limsup_{z \to a} h(z) \leq 0
$$
for all $a \in \partial \mathbb{D} \setminus \{1\}$.
Show that $h \leq 0$ in $\mathbb{D}$.
:::
