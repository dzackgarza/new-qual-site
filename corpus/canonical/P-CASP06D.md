::: problem
Assume that $f$ and $g$ are entire functions of finite order $\lambda$.
Assume that for a sequence $a_n$, $f(a_n) = g(a_n)$ and $\sum |a_n|^{-(\lambda + \epsilon)} = \infty$ for some $\epsilon > 0$.
Show that $f = g$.
:::
