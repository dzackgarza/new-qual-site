::: problem
Let $q : S^3 \to \mathbb{RP}^3$ be the usual quotient map which identifies antipodal points.
It can be used to attach a $4$-ball to $\mathbb{RP}^3$, forming a space $X = \mathbb{RP}^3 \cup_q B^4$.
Compute $\pi_4(X)$.
:::
