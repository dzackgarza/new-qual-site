::: problem
Let $f \in L^1(\mathbb{R}^n)$ and $f > 0$ in $\mathbb{R}^n$.
Prove that the strict inequality $|\hat{f}(\xi)| < \hat{f}(0)$ holds for any $\xi \in \mathbb{R}^n$ with $\xi \neq 0$.
:::
