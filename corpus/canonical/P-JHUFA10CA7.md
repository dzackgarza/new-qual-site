Determine all entire functions $f$ (i.e., $f(z)$ is holomorphic and is defined for all $z \in \mathbb{C}$) that satisfy the inequality:

$$|f(z)| \leq |\operatorname{Re} z|^2 + |z|^{3/2} \quad \text{whenever } |z| > 1.$$
