::: problem
Show that
$$
\frac{\cos \pi z}{\pi^2 \sin^2 \pi z} = \sum_{n \in \mathbb{Z}} \frac{(-1)^n}{(z - n)^2}.
$$
(Hint: Compare singular parts and use periodicity.)
:::
