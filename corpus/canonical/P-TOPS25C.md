::: problem
Let $T$ be the standard torus, and let $e$ and $f$ be generators of $H_1(T; \mathbb{Z}) \cong \mathbb{Z}^2$. Now let $X$ be the space obtained by gluing two discs onto $T$ along their boundary circles: the first attaches along a curve with homology class $e + 4f$, and the second along a curve with homology class $4e + f$. Calculate the integral homology groups $H_*(X; \mathbb{Z})$.
:::
