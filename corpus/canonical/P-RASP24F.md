::: problem
Let $f \in L^2(\mathbb{R}^n)$ be such that $f(x) = 0$ for a.e. $x \in \mathbb{R}^n \setminus A$, where $m(A) < \infty$.
Show that, for any measurable $E \subset \mathbb{R}^n$,
$$
\int_E |\hat{f}(\xi)|^2\,d\xi \leq m(A)\,m(E)\,\|f\|_{L^2}^2.
$$
:::
