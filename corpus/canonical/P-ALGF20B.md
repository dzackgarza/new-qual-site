::: problem
Suppose that $G$ is a group with $|G| = 2^k m$, where $m$ is odd and $k \geq 0$. Assume that $G$ has a cyclic Sylow $2$-subgroup. Prove that $G$ has a characteristic subgroup $H$ which has order $m$.
:::
