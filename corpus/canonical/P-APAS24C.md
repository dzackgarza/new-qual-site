::: problem
Let $A\in M_n(\mathbb{C})=\mathbb{C}^{n\times n}$. Prove that $A$ is Hermitian if and only if $x^HAx\in\mathbb{R}$ for all $x\in\mathbb{C}^n$.

Note: $x^H=\overline{x^T}$.
:::
