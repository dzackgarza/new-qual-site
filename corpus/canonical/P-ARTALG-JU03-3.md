(a) Let $R$ be the ring of all polynomials in $\mathbb{Q}[x]$ having no $x$-term.
Show that $x^5$ and $x^6$ have no GCD in $R$.

(b) If $S$ is a Euclidean domain and $T$ is a subring of $S$, is it true that $T$ is a Euclidean domain?
Justify your answer.
