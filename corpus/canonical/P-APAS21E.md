::: problem
Let $G$ be a group (possibly infinite) and let $V$ be a finite-dimensional $G$-module over $\mathbb{C}$. Assume that $V$ admits a $G$-invariant inner product $\langle -, - \rangle$. Prove that $V$ is completely reducible.
:::
