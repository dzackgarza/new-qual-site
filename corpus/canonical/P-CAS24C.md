::: problem
Let $f : \mathbb{D} \to \mathbb{C}$ be holomorphic. Assume $\operatorname{Re} f(z) > 0$ for all $z \in \mathbb{D}$. Show that
\[
|f'(0)| \le 2\operatorname{Re} f(0).
\]
:::
