::: problem
Suppose $f \in \operatorname{Hol}(\mathbb{C}_+)$ has the property that, for any sequence $z_n$ in $\mathbb{C}_+$ that converges to a point in $(0, 1)$, $f(z_n) \to 0$.
Prove that $f \equiv 0$ on $\mathbb{C}_+$.
:::
