::: problem
Let $R = \mathbb{Z}[x]/(2x-1)$.
Let $S = \{1, 2, 4, 8, \ldots\} \subseteq \mathbb{Z}$.

(a) Show that $R$ is isomorphic as a ring to the localization $S^{-1}\mathbb{Z}$.

(b) Is $R$ a free $\mathbb{Z}$-module?
:::
