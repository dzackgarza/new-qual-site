::: exercise
7. Prove that

$$

\int_ {0} ^ {2 \pi} \frac {d \theta}{(a + \cos \theta) ^ {2}} = \frac {2 \pi a}{(a ^ {2} - 1) ^ {3 / 2}}, \quad \text { whenever } a > 1.

$$
:::
