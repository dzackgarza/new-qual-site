::: problem
The classification of closed connected surfaces says that every such surface is homeomorphic to either a connect-sum of $g \geq 0$ tori (denoted $\Sigma_g$) or a connect-sum of $h \geq 1$ projective planes (denoted $N_h$). Commensurability is the equivalence relation on spaces generated by saying that $X \sim Y$ if $X$ is a finite cover of $Y$, or vice versa.
What are the commensurability classes of closed connected surfaces?
:::
