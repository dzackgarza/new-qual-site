Let $X$ be the CW complex formed by attaching $k$ two-cells $e_1^2, \ldots, e_k^2$ to the circle $S^1$ via attaching maps with degrees $n_1, n_2, \ldots, n_k$.
Compute $\pi_2(X)$ in terms of $n_1, \ldots, n_k$.
