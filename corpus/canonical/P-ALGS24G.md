::: problem
Let $K$ be a field with $729$ elements. Let $\mathbb{F}_3$ be the prime field of $K$ and set $G = \operatorname{Gal}(K/\mathbb{F}_3)$. Consider the action of $G$ on $K$ given by $\sigma \cdot u = \sigma(u)$. Describe the orbits of this action, calculate their sizes, and calculate how many orbits there are of each size.
:::
