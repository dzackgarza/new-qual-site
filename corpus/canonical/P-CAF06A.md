::: problem
If $f(z)$ is analytic in $|z| < 1$, continuous on $|z| \leq 1$ and satisfies $|f| = 1$ on $|z| = 1$, show that $f(z)$ is rational.

Hint: First consider the case that $f(z)$ has no zero.
:::
