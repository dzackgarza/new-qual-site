::: problem
(a) Suppose $D$ is an integral domain and $M$ is a flat $D$-module.
Prove that $M$ is torsion-free.

(b) Suppose $D$ is a PID and $M$ is a finitely generated flat $D$-module.
Prove that $M$ is a free $D$-module.
:::
