::: problem
Suppose that $S^3 = M \cup_\Sigma N$ is a decomposition of the $3$-sphere into two compact $3$-manifolds-with-boundary, glued along their common boundary surface $\Sigma$. Prove that $H_1(N; \mathbb{Z}) \cong H_1(M; \mathbb{Z})$. Let $X$ be the compact $3$-manifold-with-boundary obtained by removing the interior of a small ball from $\mathbb{RP}^3$. Conclude that $X$ cannot be embedded in $S^3$.
:::
