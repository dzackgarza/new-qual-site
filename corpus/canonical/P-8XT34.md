Let $\varphi : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be a non-negative, $C^\infty$ function with compact support such that

$$\int_{\mathbb{R}} \varphi(x) \, dx = 1.$$

Define

$$\varphi_\sigma(x) = \sigma^{-1} \varphi\left(\frac{x}{\sigma}\right) \quad \text{and} \quad u_\sigma(x) = \int \varphi_\sigma(x - y) u(y) \, dy.$$

For $u \in L^2(\mathbb{R})$, prove

$$\int_{\mathbb{R}} |u_\sigma(x)|^2 \, dx \leq \int_{\mathbb{R}} |u(x)|^2 \, dx.$$
