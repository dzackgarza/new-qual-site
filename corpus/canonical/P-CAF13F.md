::: problem
Let $\varphi: \mathbb{D} \setminus [0, 1) \to \mathbb{D}$ be a conformal bijection. Prove that $\varphi$ does not extend to a homeomorphism $\overline{\mathbb{D}} \to \overline{\mathbb{D}}$.
:::
