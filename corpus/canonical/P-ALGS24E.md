::: problem
Suppose $A$ is a Noetherian local ring and its only maximal ideal is $\mathfrak{m}$.

(a) Carefully state Nakayama's lemma for $A$-modules.

(b) For every finitely generated $A$-module $M$, prove that
\[
d(M) = \dim_{A/\mathfrak{m}}(M/\mathfrak{m}M),
\]
where $d(M)$ is the minimum number of elements needed to generate $M$.

(c) Prove that every finitely generated projective $A$-module $P$ is free.
:::
