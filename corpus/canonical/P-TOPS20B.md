::: problem
Let $n$ be a positive even number.
Show that there does not exist a continuous map $f : S^n \to S^n$ such that for any $\vec{x} \in S^n$, we have $f(\vec{x}) \neq \vec{x}$ and $f(\vec{x}) \neq -\vec{x}$.
:::
