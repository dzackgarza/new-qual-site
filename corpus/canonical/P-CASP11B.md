::: problem
Let $G$ be a bounded region and
$$\mathcal{F} = \{f \in C(\overline{G}, \mathbb{C}) : f \text{ is analytic in } G\},$$
and put $\|f\| = \sup_{z \in \partial G} |f(z)|$. Show that $\mathcal{F}$ is a complete metric space with $d(f, g) = \|f - g\|$.
:::
