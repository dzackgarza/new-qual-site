::: problem
Consider the circle in the form of the abelian group $T = \mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Z}$. Show that there is a long exact sequence relating homology with coefficients in $\mathbb{Z}$, $\mathbb{R}$ and $T$ and use it to compute $H_*(\mathbb{RP}^\infty; T)$.
:::
