::: problem
Let $f : \mathbb{D} \to \mathbb{C}$ be holomorphic.
Assume that

(i) $|f(z)| < 2$ for all $z \in \mathbb{D}$,

(ii) $f\!\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right) = 1$.

Show that $f$ has no zeros in the disc $|z| < \dfrac{1}{7}$.
:::
