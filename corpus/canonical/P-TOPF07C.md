::: problem
Let $X$ be a space whose homology groups are $\mathbb{Z}$, $0$, $\mathbb{Z}_6$ in dimensions $0$, $1$, $2$ and zero otherwise. Compute the integral homology $H^*(X \times \mathbb{RP}^3; \mathbb{Z})$.
:::
