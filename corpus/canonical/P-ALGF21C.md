::: problem
Let $\zeta \in \mathbb{C}$ be a primitive $n$th root of $1$ for some $n \geq 2$.
Show that $\sqrt[3]{2} \notin \mathbb{Q}(\zeta)$.
(Hint: use the fundamental theorem of Galois theory).
:::
