::: problem
Let $G = \{z \in \mathbb{C} : |z| > 1\}$.
Let $u : G \to \mathbb{R}$ be a continuous bounded function, such that $u$ is harmonic in $G$.
Assume $u(z) \leq 0$ for all $|z| = 1$.
Show that $u(z) \leq 0$ for all $z \in G$.
:::
