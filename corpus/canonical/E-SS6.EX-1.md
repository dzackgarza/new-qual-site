::: exercise
1. Prove that

$$

\Gamma (s) = \lim _ {n \to \infty} \frac {n ^ {s} n !}{s (s + 1) \cdots (s + n)}

$$

whenever $s \neq 0 , - 1 , - 2 , . . .$

[Hint: Use the product formula for $1 / \Gamma$ , and the definition of the Euler constant γ.]
:::
