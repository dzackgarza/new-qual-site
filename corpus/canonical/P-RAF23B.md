::: problem
For $a > 0$, let $(S_a f)(x) = f(x/a)$ for Lebesgue measurable functions $f$ on $\mathbb{R}$. Then for any $f \in L^1(\mathbb{R}, m)$, $S_a f \to f$ in $L^1$ as $a \to 1$.
:::
