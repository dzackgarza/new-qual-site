::: problem
Prove or disprove the following statement.

Let $U = \{z \in \mathbb{C} : |z| > 3\}$.
There exists a holomorphic function $f$ in $U$ such that
$$
f'(z) = \frac{z^2 + 2}{z(z-1)(z-2)}.
$$
:::
