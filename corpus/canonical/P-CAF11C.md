::: problem
Let $f \in H(\mathbb{D})$ be nonconstant such that $f(\mathbb{D}) \subset \{z \in \mathbb{C} : \operatorname{Re} z < 0\}$ and $f(0) = -1$. Prove that for any $z \in \mathbb{D}$,
$$\frac{1 - |z|}{1 + |z|} \leq |f(z)| \leq \frac{1 + |z|}{1 - |z|}.$$
:::
