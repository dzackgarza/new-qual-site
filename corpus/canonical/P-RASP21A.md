::: problem
Let $A, B \subseteq \mathbb{R}$ be Lebesgue measurable with $\{a + b \mid a \in A, b \in B\} \subseteq \mathbb{R} \setminus \mathbb{Q}$. Prove that either $m(A) = 0$ or $m(B) = 0$.
:::
