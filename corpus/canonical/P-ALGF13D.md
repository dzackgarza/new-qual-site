::: problem
Let $A$ be a commutative ring. Let $P$ and $Q$ be $A$-modules.

(a) Prove that $P \oplus Q$ is an injective $A$-module if and only if both $P$ and $Q$ are injective $A$-modules.

(b) Let $A \subseteq B$, where $B$ is another commutative ring and $A$ is a unital subring of $B$. Prove that if $P$ is a projective $A$-module, then $P \otimes_A B$ is a projective $B$-module.
:::
