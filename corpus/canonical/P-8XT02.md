Prove the following statement without using Egorov's Theorem: Suppose $\{f_k\}_{k=1}^{\infty}$ is a sequence of measurable functions defined on a measurable set $E$, $f_k \to f$ a.e. on $E$ and there exists $g \in L^1(E)$ such that $|f_k| \le g$ for all $k$. Given $\epsilon > 0$, there exists a closed set $A_\epsilon$ such that $m(E \setminus A_\epsilon) < \epsilon$ and $f_k \to f$ uniformly on $A_\epsilon$.
