::: problem
Let $K$ be the space $\{[x, y, z, w] \in \mathbb{CP}^3 : x^4 + y^4 + z^4 + w^4 = 0\}$.
It is known that $K$ is a simply-connected closed manifold with Euler characteristic $24$.
Compute $H_*(K; \mathbb{Z})$.
:::
