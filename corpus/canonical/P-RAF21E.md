::: problem
Let $f_n, f \in L^2(\mathbb{R})$ satisfy $f_n \to f$ weakly and $\|f_n\|_2 \to \|f\|_2$ as $n \to \infty$.
Show that $f_n \to f$ in $L^2(\mathbb{R})$.
:::
