::: problem
Let $X$ be the suspension of $\mathbb{RP}^2$, i.e.
$$
X = \mathbb{RP}^2 \times [0, 1] / (\mathbb{RP}^2 \times \{0\}), (\mathbb{RP}^2 \times \{1\}).
$$
Prove that $X$ is not homotopy equivalent to a compact manifold.
:::
