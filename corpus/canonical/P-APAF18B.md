::: problem
Let $A\in\mathbb{C}^{n\times n}$ be a matrix such that $|\lambda_i|<1$ for all its eigenvalues $\lambda_i$. Let $B_k=A^k$. Do we necessarily have
\[
\lim_{k\to\infty}\Biggl(\sum_{i,j=1}^{n}\bigl|(B_k)_{ij}\bigr|^3\Biggr)=0?
\]
If yes, give a proof; if no, give a counterexample.
:::
