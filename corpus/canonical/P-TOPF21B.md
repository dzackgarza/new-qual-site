::: problem
Let $\mathbb{CP}^2$ be the complex projective plane.

(a) Compute $\pi_1(\mathbb{CP}^2)$.

(b) Could $\mathbb{CP}^2$ be an $n$-sheeted covering space ($n > 1$) of another space $D$? If so, compute $H_*(D; \mathbb{Z})$. If not, explain your reason.

(c) Compute $H_*(\mathbb{CP}^2 \times \mathbb{RP}^2; \mathbb{Z})$.

(d) Compute $H_*(\mathbb{CP}^2 \times \mathbb{RP}^2; \mathbb{Z}/2)$.
:::
