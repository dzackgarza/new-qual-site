::: problem
Let $\gamma$ be the closed curve given by the circle $|z - i/2| = 1$ traversed once in the positive (counterclockwise) direction.
Compute $$\int_\gamma \frac{\sin(i\pi z/2)}{z^2 + 1}\,dz.$$
:::
