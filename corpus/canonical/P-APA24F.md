::: problem
Let $(V, \varphi)$ be a finite-dimensional unitary representation of a finite group $G$. A vector $v \in V$ is said to be cyclic if $\{\varphi(g)v : g \in G\}$ spans $V$. Prove that $(V, \varphi)$ is irreducible if and only if every nonzero vector in $V$ is cyclic.
:::
