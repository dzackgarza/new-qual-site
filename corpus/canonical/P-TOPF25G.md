::: problem
Show that $\mathbb{CP}^2$ is not homotopy equivalent to $S^2 \vee S^4$.
Now, by considering the attaching map of the 4-cell in the standard cell decomposition of $\mathbb{CP}^2$, show that $\pi_3(S^2)$ is not trivial.
:::
