::: problem
Let $Y$ be a closed subspace of $L^2([0,1])$ each of whose elements may be represented by a continuous function on $[0,1]$.
Prove that there exists $C > 0$ so that
$$
\|f\|_{L^\infty([0,1])} \leq C \|f\|_{L^2([0,1])}
$$
for all $f \in Y$.
:::
