::: problem
Let $A\in\mathbb{C}^{n\times n}$ be a matrix with eigenvalues $\lambda_1,\dots,\lambda_n\in\mathbb{C}$.
If
\[
\|A\|_F^2\leq|\lambda_1|^2+\cdots+|\lambda_n|^2,
\]
show that $A$ is normal, i.e., $AA^*=A^*A$.
:::
