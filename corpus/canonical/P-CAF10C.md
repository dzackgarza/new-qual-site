::: problem
Let $G$ be a domain and $\{f_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}$ a sequence of analytic functions in $G$ that converge to $f$ in $H(G)$.
Assume that each $f_n$ is 1-to-1. Show that $f$ is either constant or 1-to-1.
:::
