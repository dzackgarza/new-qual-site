::: problem
Suppose that $G$ is a simple group of order $|G| = 168 = 2^3 \cdot 3 \cdot 7$.

(a) Show that $G$ does not have any subgroup $H$ with index $[G:H] \leq 6$.

(b) Show that $G$ does have a subgroup $H$ with index $[G:H] = 8$.
:::
