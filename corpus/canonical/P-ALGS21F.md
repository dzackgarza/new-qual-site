::: problem
$A$ is a matrix in $M_{12}(\mathbb{C})$. The characteristic polynomial of $A$ is $(x+2)^3(x-1)^3(x+1)^6$; the minimal polynomial of $A$ is $(x+2)^2(x-1)(x+1)^4$; $A$ has exactly 3 invariant factors; and $A$ has exactly 7 elementary divisors.

Show that there is exactly one similarity class of such matrices, and find an explicit matrix $A$ in this similarity class.
:::
