::: problem
Let $n$ be a positive integer and $\lambda > 1$ a real number. Consider the equation
$$(**)\quad z^n - e^{z - \lambda} = 0.$$

(a) Find all the roots of (**) with $|z| = 1$.

(b) Show that there are exactly $n$ distinct simple roots of (**) with $|z| < 1$.
:::
