::: problem
Let $(X, \mathcal{M}, \mu)$ be a measure space. Assume that $f : X \to \mathbb{R}$ and $f_n : X \to \mathbb{R}$ ($n = 1, 2, \ldots$) are all $\mu$-measurable and that $f_n \to f$ in measure. Let $p$ be a real number such that $1 \leq p < \infty$. Assume there exists $g \in L^p(\mu)$ such that $|f_n| \leq g$ on $X$ for all $n \geq 1$. Prove that all $f$ and $f_n$ ($n = 1, 2, \ldots$) are in $L^p(\mu)$ and that $f_n \to f$ in the $L^p(\mu)$-norm.
:::
