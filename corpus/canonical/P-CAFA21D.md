::: problem
Let $a_n \in \Delta$ be a sequence such that $a_n \to 1$.
Let $f_n : \Delta \to \Delta$ be a sequence of holomorphic functions such that $f_n(0) = a_n$.
Show that $f_n \to 1$ uniformly on compact subsets of $\Delta$.
:::
