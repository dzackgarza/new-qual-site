::: problem
Let $M$ be a closed (that is, compact and without boundary) path-connected orientable $3$-manifold. Suppose that $M$ contains a $2$-dimensional orientable submanifold $\Sigma$ which is non-separating, meaning that $M \setminus \Sigma$ is still path-connected. Show that $H_1(M; \mathbb{Z})$ contains a subgroup isomorphic to $\mathbb{Z}$.
:::
