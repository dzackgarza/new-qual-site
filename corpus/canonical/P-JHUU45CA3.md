Let $f$ and $g$ be functions holomorphic defined on a domain $U \subseteq \mathbb{C}$.
Set $\varphi(z) = |f(z)| + |g(z)|$ for $z \in U$.
If $\varphi$ assumes a maximum value on $U$, show that both $f$ and $g$ are constants on $U$.
