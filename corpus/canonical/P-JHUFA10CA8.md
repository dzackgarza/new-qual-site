Let $D$ denote the open unit disc.
Let $g : D \to D$ be a surjective holomorphic mapping for which $g(0) = 0$.
Suppose that $z = g(w)$ gives a two-sheeted branched covering of the image with exactly one branch point at $w = 0$.

(a) Express the given conditions explicitly in terms of $g$ and its derivatives.

(b) Show that $|g(w)| \leq |w|^2$ for all $|w| < 1$.

(c) Suppose that $g(1/2) = i/4$.
What is the strongest statement about $g(w)$ that follows from the assertion in (b)? Explain.
