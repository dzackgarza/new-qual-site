::: problem
(a) Show that $\mathbb{Z}[i]$ is a PID.

(b) Let $A$ be a proper subring of $\mathbb{Z}[i]$ which contains $\mathbb{Z}$.
Suppose the field of fractions of $A$ is $\mathbb{Q}(i)$.
Show that $A$ is NOT a unique factorization domain (UFD).
:::
