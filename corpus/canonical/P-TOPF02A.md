::: problem
Let $E = \mathbb{R} \times \mathbb{Z} \cup \mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{R}$ be the subset of the plane whose points have at least one of the coordinates an integer. Let $S^1 \vee S^1 \subset \mathbb{R}^2 \times \mathbb{R}^2$ be the one-point union of circles. Define $p : E \to S^1 \vee S^1$.

(a) Verify that $p$ is a covering space map.

(b) Let $\alpha : (I, 0) \to (E, (0, 0))$ be the loop which traverses the unit square $I = \{0, 1\} \cup \{0, 1\} \times I$ once counterclockwise. Prove that $p \alpha$ is the commutator of the two loops of the figure-eight.

(c) Prove that $p_* : \pi_1(E, (0, 0)) \to \pi_1(S^1 \vee S^1, (1, 1))$ is a monomorphism. Show that this implies that $\pi_1(S^1 \vee S^1, (1, 1))$ is not abelian.
:::
