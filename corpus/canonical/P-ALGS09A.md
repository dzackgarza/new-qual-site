::: problem
Let $f, g: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ be the functions defined by $f(x) = -x$ and $g(x) = x + 1$.
Let $G$ be the subgroup generated by $f$ and $g$ of the group of all permutations of the set $\mathbb{R}$.
Prove carefully that $$G \cong \langle a, b \mid a^2 = e,\; ab = b^{-1}a \rangle.$$
:::
