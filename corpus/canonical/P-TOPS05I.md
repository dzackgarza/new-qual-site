::: problem
Let $p$ be an odd prime.
Recall the lens space $L(p, q)$ is a $3$-dimensional compact manifold with
$$
H_*(L(p, q); \mathbb{Z}) = \begin{cases} \mathbb{Z}_p & i = 0, 3 \\ \mathbb{Z} & i = 1 \\ 0 & i = 2. \end{cases}
$$
Construct a space $X$ with $H^*(X; \mathbb{Z}) = H^*(L(p, q); \mathbb{Z})$ but $X$ is not homotopy equivalent to $L(p, q)$.
Verify that $X$ is not homotopy equivalent to $L(p, q)$.
:::
