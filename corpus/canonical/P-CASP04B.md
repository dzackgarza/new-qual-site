::: problem
Let $f \in \mathcal{O}(\mathbb{D})$ and assume that $|f(z)| \leq 1$ in $\mathbb{D}$.
Show that $$\frac{|f(0)| - |z|}{1 + |f(0)||z|} \leq |f(z)| \leq \frac{|f(0)| + |z|}{1 - |f(0)||z|}.$$
:::
