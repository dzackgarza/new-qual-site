::: problem
Let $F$ be a field of characteristic $p$ and let $f \in F[x]$ be a polynomial, $f(x) = \sum_i f_i x^i$. Give necessary and sufficient conditions on the $\{f_i\}$ for $f(x^p)$ itself to be a $p$-th power, i.e.\ $f(x^p) = g(x)^p$ for some $g \in F[x]$. In particular, prove that your condition is necessary.
:::
