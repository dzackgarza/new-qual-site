::: problem
Let $f : [0, 1] \to \mathbb{R}$ be a continuous function.
Define the signed Borel measure $\mu$ on $[0, 1]$ by $d\mu = f \, dm$.
Assume
$$
\int_{[0,1]} x^n \, d\mu = 0, \quad n = 0, 1, 2, \ldots
$$
Prove that $\mu = 0$.
:::
