::: problem
Suppose $n$ is a positive integer, $p$ is a prime number, and $a \in \mathbb{F}_p^\times$. Suppose $n \mid p - 1$. Suppose $E$ is a splitting field of $x^n - a$ over $\mathbb{F}_p$.

(a) Prove that $E/\mathbb{F}_p$ is a Galois extension and $\operatorname{Gal}(E/\mathbb{F}_p)$ can be embedded into $\mathbb{F}_p^\times$.

(b) Prove that $\operatorname{Gal}(E/\mathbb{F}_p) \cong \langle a^{\frac{p-1}{n}} \rangle \subseteq \mathbb{F}_p^\times$.
:::
