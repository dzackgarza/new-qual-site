::: problem
Let $g \in L^1(\mathbb{R}, m)$ be chosen so that $\int_\mathbb{R} g(x) \, dx = 3$. Find, with justification, the following limit:
$$
\lim_{n \to \infty} \int_\mathbb{R} g(x) \sin^2(nx) \, dx.
$$
:::
