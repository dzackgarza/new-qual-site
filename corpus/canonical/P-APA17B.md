::: problem
Let $A, B \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$ be two real symmetric matrices. If $AB = BA$, show that there exists an orthogonal matrix $Q \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$ such that $Q^T AQ$ and $Q^T BQ$ are both diagonal.
:::
