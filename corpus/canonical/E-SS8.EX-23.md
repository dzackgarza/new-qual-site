::: exercise
23. If

$$

F (z) = \int_ {1} ^ {z} \frac {d \zeta}{(1 - \zeta^ {n}) ^ {2 / n}},

$$

then $F$ maps the unit disc conformally onto the interior of a regular polygon with n sides and perimeter

$$

2 ^ {\frac {n - 2}{n}} \int_ {0} ^ {\pi} (\sin \theta) ^ {- 2 / n} d \theta .

$$
:::
