::: problem
Assume that $X$ is a path-connected, locally simply-connected space with fundamental group isomorphic to $\mathbb{Z}_2 \times \mathbb{Z}_2$. How many path-connected covering spaces of $X$ are there, up to equivalence (isomorphism)?
:::
