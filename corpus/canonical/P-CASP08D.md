::: problem
(a) Let $m$ be a positive integer.
Describe the set of all entire functions $f$ for which there exist positive constants $C_1$ and $C_2$ such that $|f(z)| \leq C_1|z|^m$ for all $|z| \geq C_2$.

(b) Describe the set of all functions $R$, meromorphic in $\mathbb{C}$, analytic except at $z = \pm 1$, and satisfying all of the following:

(i) $R$ has a pole of order 1 at $z = 1$.

(ii) $R$ has a pole of order 2 at $z = -1$.

(iii) $|R(z)| \leq C|z|$ for all $|z| \geq 2$.
:::
