::: problem
Let $\alpha = \sqrt{2 + \sqrt{2}}$ in $\mathbb{C}$ and let $f$ be the minimal polynomial of $\alpha$ over $\mathbb{Q}$.
Let $E$ be the splitting field for $f$ over $\mathbb{Q}$.
Determine the Galois group $\operatorname{Gal}(E/\mathbb{Q})$.
:::
