::: problem
Let $E/F$ be a field extension. Let $f(x) \in F[x]$ be a polynomial of degree $n$ with $n$ distinct roots $\alpha_1, \ldots, \alpha_n$ in $E$. Suppose $[F[\alpha_1, \alpha_2] : F] = n(n-1)$.

(i) Prove that $f(x)$ is irreducible over $F$ and $f(x)/(x-\alpha_1)$ is irreducible over $F[\alpha_1]$.

(ii) Suppose $g(x)$ is the minimal polynomial of $\alpha_1+\alpha_2$ over $F$. Prove that $g(\alpha_i+\alpha_j) = 0$ for any $1 \leq i < j \leq n$. (Hint: first show that $g(\alpha_1+\alpha_j) = 0$ for any $2 \leq j \leq n$; remember you can use the first part!)
:::
