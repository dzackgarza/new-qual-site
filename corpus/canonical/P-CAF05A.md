::: problem
Find all functions $f \in H(B(0; 2))$ such that:

(a) $|f(z)| = 1$ if $|z| = 1$.

(b) $f$ has a zero of multiplicity 2 at $z = 1/2$ and no other zeroes.

Hint: Consider first the case where $f$ satisfies (a), but has no zeroes.
:::
