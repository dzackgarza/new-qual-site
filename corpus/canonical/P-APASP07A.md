::: problem
(a) Let $H$ be generated by $a$ and $b$ with the relations $a^3 = b^3 = 1$ and $ab = ba$ (i.e.\ $H \cong \mathbb{Z}/3 \times \mathbb{Z}/3$). Write down all irreducible representations of $H$.

Let now $G$ be the group generated by $a$, $b$, $c$ with the relations as in (a) as well as $c^3 = 1$ and $c a^i b^j c^{-1} = a^i b^{i+j}$.
You can assume that $G$ has order 27.

(b) Calculate the matrices of the generators $a$, $b$, and $c$ in the representation $W$ induced by a given irreducible representation $V$ of $H$.
Determine for which $V$ all elements of $H$ act via multiples of the identity matrix on $W$.
Show directly that in this case the induced representation is reducible.

(c) Determine all irreducible representations of $G$.
:::
