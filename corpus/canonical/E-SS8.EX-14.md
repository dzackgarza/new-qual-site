::: exercise
14. Prove that all conformal mappings from the upper half-plane H to the unit disc D take the form

$$

e ^ {i \theta} \frac {z - \beta}{z - \overline {{\beta}}}, \qquad \theta \in \mathbb {R} \text {   and   } \beta \in \mathbb {H}.

$$
:::
