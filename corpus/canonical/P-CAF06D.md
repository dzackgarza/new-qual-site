::: problem
Assume that $f$ is an entire function.
Let $M(r) = \sup_{|z|=r} |f(z)|$.
Assume that the discrete sequence $\{a_n\}$ are zeros of $f$ and $f(0) = 1$.
Show that $$k\log 2 \leq \log(M(2|a_k|)).$$
:::
