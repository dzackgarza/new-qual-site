::: problem
Assume that $(\lambda, x)$ is an eigenpair of $A \in M_n$ such that $\operatorname{am}(\lambda) = \operatorname{gm}(\lambda) = 1$. Prove that there exists a nonsingular matrix $(x \quad X)$ with inverse $(y \quad Y)^*$ such that
\[
\begin{pmatrix} y^* \\ Y^* \end{pmatrix} A (x \quad X) = \begin{pmatrix} \lambda & 0 \\ 0 & M \end{pmatrix}.
\]
:::
