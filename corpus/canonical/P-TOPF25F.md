::: problem
Let $F_2 = \langle a, b \rangle$ be the free group of rank 2, let $\theta$ be the homomorphism $F_2 \to \mathbb{Z}_4$ given by $a \mapsto 1$, $b \mapsto 2$, and let $K = \ker \theta$. Find a minimal set of generators for $K$ as a subgroup of $F_2$.
:::
