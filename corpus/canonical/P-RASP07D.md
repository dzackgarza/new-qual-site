::: problem
Prove that if $1 < p < \infty$ and $f_n$ converges to $f$ weakly in $\ell^p(\mathbb{N})$, then $\sup_n \|f_n\|_p < \infty$ and $f_n \to f$ pointwise.

(The converse is also true, but you are not asked to prove it here.)
:::
