::: problem
(a) State the Dominated Convergence Theorem for Lebesgue integrals.

(b) Let $\{f_n\}$ be a sequence of measurable functions on a Lebesgue measurable set $E$ which converges in measure to a function $f$ on $E$. Suppose that for every $n$, $|f_n| \leq g$ with $g$ integrable on $E$. Using the above theorem show that
$$
\int_E |f_n - f| \to 0.
$$
:::
