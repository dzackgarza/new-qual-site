::: problem
(a) Let $F \subseteq E$ be a field extension, and $f(x) \in F[x]$. Prove that
\[
E \otimes_F F[x]/\langle f(x) \rangle \simeq E[x]/\langle f(x) \rangle
\]
as $E$-algebras. (Notice that $\langle f(x) \rangle$ at the left hand side is $F[x]f(x)$ and in the right hand side is $E[x]f(x)$). (Hint: $\{x^i \mid 0 \leq i \leq \deg f - 1\}$ is an $F$-basis of $F[x]/\langle f(x) \rangle$.)

(b) Let $E/F$ be a finite Galois extension. Prove that
\[
E \otimes_F E \simeq E \oplus \cdots \oplus E
\]
($[E:F]$ times) as $E$-algebras.
:::
