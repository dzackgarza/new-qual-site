::: problem
Determine the Laurent series expansions of the function $f: \mathbb{C} \setminus \{0, \pm 2\} \to \mathbb{C}$ given by $$f(z) = \frac{1}{z^3 - 4z}$$ in the annuli $\mathbb{A}(0; 0, 2)$ and $\mathbb{A}(0; 2, \infty)$.
:::
