::: problem
Show that there exist polynomials $p_n$ such that

(i) $p_n(0) = 1$, $p_n'(0) = 0$,

(ii) $p_n(z) \to 0$ as $n \to \infty$ for all fixed $z \in \mathbb{C} \setminus \{0\}$.
:::
