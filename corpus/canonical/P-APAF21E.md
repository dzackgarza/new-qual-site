::: problem
Let $G$ be a finite group and let $V$ be an irreducible complex representation of $G$.
If $g\in G$ lies in the center of $G$, show that there exists $c\in\mathbb{C}$ with
\[
g\cdot v=cv
\]
for all $v\in V$.
:::
