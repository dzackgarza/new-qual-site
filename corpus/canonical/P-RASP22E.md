::: problem
Let $X \subseteq \mathbb{R}$ be a Borel set, let $\mu$ be the restriction of Lebesgue measure to $X$, and let $1 \leq p < q < \infty$. Assume that $L^q(X, \mu) \subseteq L^p(X, \mu)$ and let $T : L^q(X, \mu) \to L^p(X, \mu)$ be the inclusion map. Prove that $T$ is bounded and that $\mu(X) < \infty$.
:::
