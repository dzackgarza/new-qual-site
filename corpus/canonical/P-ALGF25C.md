::: problem
A subgroup $L$ of $\mathbb{Q}^n$ is called a lattice if it is finitely generated and its $\mathbb{Q}$-span is $\mathbb{Q}^n$.

(a) Prove that for every lattice $L$ of $\mathbb{Q}^n$, there exists $x \in \mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{Q})$ such that $L = x\mathbb{Z}^n$.

(b) Suppose $L_1, \ldots, L_m$ are lattices in $\mathbb{Q}^n$. Prove that $L_1 + \cdots + L_m$ is also a lattice in $\mathbb{Q}^n$.

(c) Prove that for every finite subgroup $G$ of $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{Q})$, there exists $x \in \mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{Q})$ such that $xGx^{-1} \subseteq \mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{Z})$.
(Hint. Think about a lattice which is $G$-invariant.)
:::
