::: problem
Let $p(z) = a_n z^n + a_{n-1} z^{n-1} + \cdots + a_0$ (where $a_n \neq 0$, and $n \geq 1$) be a complex polynomial.
Prove by a topological argument that $p$ must have a root in the complex plane.
:::
