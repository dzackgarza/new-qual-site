::: problem
Let $f : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}$ be an entire function such that $|f(z)| = 1$ for $|z| = 1$.
Show that there exists $a \in \mathbb{C}$ and $n \geq 0$ such that $f(z) = az^n$.
:::
