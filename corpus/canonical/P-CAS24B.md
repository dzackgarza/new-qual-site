::: problem
Let $f : \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}$ be an entire nowhere zero function.
Define $U = \{z : |f(z)| < 1\}$.
If $U \neq \emptyset$, show that the connected components of $U$ are unbounded.
:::
