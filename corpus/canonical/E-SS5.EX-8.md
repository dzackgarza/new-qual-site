::: exercise
8. Prove that for every z the product below converges, and

$$

\cos (z / 2) \cos (z / 4) \cos (z / 8) \dots = \prod_ {k = 1} ^ {\infty} \cos (z / 2 ^ {k}) = \frac {\sin z}{z}.

$$

[Hint: Use the fact that sin $2 z = 2 \sin z \cos z . ]$
:::
