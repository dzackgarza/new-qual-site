::: problem
Let $f$ be analytic in $B(0, 1 + \varepsilon)$ for some small $\varepsilon > 0$.
Assume $|f(z)| < 1$ for all $|z| = 1$.
Prove there is a unique $z_0$ with $|z_0| < 1$ such that $f(z_0) = z_0$.
:::
