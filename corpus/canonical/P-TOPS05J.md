::: problem
Let $X \vee Y$ be the one point union of $X$ and $Y$.
Prove for each $\ell > 0$ there is a split short exact sequence
$$
0 \to H_\ell(X \vee Y) \to H_\ell(X \times Y) \to H_\ell(X \times Y, X \vee Y) \to 0.
$$
:::
