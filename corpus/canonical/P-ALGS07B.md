::: problem
Let $I$, $J$ be two ideals in a commutative ring $R$ (with unit).

(a) Define $K = \{x : x \in I \text{ and } x \in J\}$. Show that $K$ is an ideal.

(b) If $R$ is a principal ideal domain, so $I = (i)$, $J = (j)$, give a formula for a generator $k$ of $K$.
:::
