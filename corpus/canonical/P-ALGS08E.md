::: problem
Let $p(x)$ be a polynomial over $\mathbb{Q}$ with Galois group $\mathbb{Z}_2 \times \mathbb{Z}_2$. What can be said about the solvability of $p(x)$ by radicals? What if the Galois group is $S_3$?
:::
