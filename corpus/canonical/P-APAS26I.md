::: problem
Let $n \ge 2$. Let $S_n = \operatorname{Aut}\{1, \ldots, n\}$ be the symmetric group, and consider the unitary representation $(V, \varphi)$ of $S_n$ in which
\[
V = \bigoplus_{i=1}^{n} \mathbb{C}|i\rangle, \qquad \varphi(\pi)|i\rangle = |\pi(i)\rangle.
\]
Decompose $(V, \varphi)$ into irreducible unitary representations of $S_n$, with proof. Write out the character table of $S_3$.
:::
