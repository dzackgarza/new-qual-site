::: problem
Let $\{f_n\}$ be a sequence in $H(G)$ such that $f_n(z)$ converges for every $z \in G$ to a complex value $f(z)$, i.e. $\lim_{n \to \infty} f_n(z) = f(z) \in \mathbb{C}$. Assume that $|f_n'(z)| \leq h(z)$ for some continuous real-valued function $h$ in $G$. Show that $f(z)$ is analytic in $G$ and $f_n \to f$ in $H(G)$.
:::
