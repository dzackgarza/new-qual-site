::: problem
(a) Let $B(n)$ be the permutation group generated by the transpositions $\tau_i = (2i-1\ 2i)$, $1 \le i \le n$. Show that every character $\chi$ of $B(n)$ takes values in $\{-1, 1\}$.

(b) Explicitly describe the dual group of $B(n)$.
:::
