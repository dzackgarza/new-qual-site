::: problem
Let $R = \mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{-6}] = \{a + b\sqrt{-6} \mid a,b \in \mathbb{Z}\}$. Let $I = (2, \sqrt{-6})$ be the ideal of $R$ generated by $2$ and $\sqrt{-6}$.

(a) Show that $I$ is not a free $R$-module.

(b) Show that $I$ is a flat $R$-module.
:::
