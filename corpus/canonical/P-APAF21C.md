::: problem
(a) Define $A^{1/2}$ for a positive semidefinite $A\in M_n$.

(b) Define $|A|$ for any $A\in M_{m,n}$.

(c) Prove that the eigenvalues of $|A|$ are the singular values of $A$.

(d) Prove that $A$ is positive semidefinite if and only if $|A|=A$.

(e) Prove that $|A|$ and $|A^H|$ are similar.
:::
