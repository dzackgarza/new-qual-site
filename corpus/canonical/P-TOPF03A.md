::: problem
Commensurability is the equivalence relation on spaces generated by saying that $X \sim Y$ if $X$ is a finite cover of $Y$ (or vice versa).
What are the commensurability classes of closed (not necessarily orientable) $2$-dimensional surfaces?
:::
