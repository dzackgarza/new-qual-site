::: problem
Let $f$ be holomorphic on a neighborhood of $\mathbb{D}$.
Suppose $\sup_{z \in \partial\mathbb{D}} |f(z)| \leq 1$, and that $f$ has no fixed points on $\partial\mathbb{D}$.
Prove that $f$ has exactly one fixed point in $\mathbb{D}$.
:::
