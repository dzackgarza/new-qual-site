Let $f \in L^1(\mathbb{R})$ and set $h(x) = \int_{[x, x+1]} f(t) \, dt$.

a. Show that $h(x)$ is absolutely continuous.

b. Show that $\lim_{x \to \infty} h(x) = 0$.
