::: problem
Let $G$ be the group of order $16$ which is generated by $2$ elements $a, b$ that satisfy the relations ($e$ is the identity)
\[
a^8 = e,\quad b^2 = e,\quad b a b^{-1} = a^5.
\]
(Any other relation is a consequence of these relations, i.e., we have described a presentation of $G$.)

(a) Describe all of the $1$-dimensional irreducible complex representations of $G$.

(b) How many irreducible complex representations does $G$ have and what are their dimensions?
:::
