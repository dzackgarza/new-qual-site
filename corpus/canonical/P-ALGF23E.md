::: problem
Suppose $p$ is a prime number and the minimal polynomial of $a \in M_p(F)$ is $t^p - 1$.

(a) Find the Jordan form of $a$ if $F = \mathbb{C}$.
Justify your answer.

(b) Find the Jordan form of $a$ if $F = \mathbb{F}_p$.
Justify your answer.
:::
