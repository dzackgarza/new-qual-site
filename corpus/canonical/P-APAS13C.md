::: problem
(a) Define the $p$-norm $\|A\|_p$ and Frobenius norm $\|A\|_F$ of a matrix $A\in M_{m,n}$.

(b) Suppose that $D\in M_n$ with $D=\operatorname{diag}(d_1,d_2,\ldots,d_n)$. Prove that for all $1\le p\le\infty$ the $p$-norm of $D$ is given by $\|D\|_p=\max_{1\le i\le n}|d_i|$.

(c) Given $b\in\mathbb{C}^{n-1}$, find $\|B\|_2$ for the matrices
\[
B=\begin{pmatrix} 0 & b^H \\ b & 0 \end{pmatrix}
\quad\text{and}\quad
B=bb^H.
\]
(Show your work. Simply writing down the answer will not be sufficient.)
:::
