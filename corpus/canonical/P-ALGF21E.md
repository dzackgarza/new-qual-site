::: problem
Suppose that $A$ is a complex $7 \times 7$ matrix such that $A^5 = 2A^4 + A^3$. Suppose that $\mathrm{rk}\, A = 5$ and $\mathrm{tr}\, A = 4$, where $\mathrm{rk}$ indicates the rank and $\mathrm{tr}$ indicates the trace of a matrix. Find the Jordan canonical form of $A$.
:::
