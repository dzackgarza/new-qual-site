::: problem
Construct a basepointed covering space of $X = S^1 \vee S^1$ corresponding to the subgroup of the free group $\langle a, b \rangle = \pi_1(X)$ generated by the elements $aba^{-1}b^{-1}$, $ab^{-1}a^{-1}b$, $a^{-1}bab^{-1}$, and $a^{-1}b^{-1}ab$.
:::
