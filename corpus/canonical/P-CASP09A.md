::: problem
Suppose $f(z) = \sum_{n=0}^{N} a_n z^n$ where $a_n \in \mathbb{C}$ and $N > 0$.
Let $M$ be the maximum of $|f(z)|$ on the unit circle about the origin.

(a) Show that $|a_0| \leq M$.

(b) Show that $|a_N| \leq M$.
:::
