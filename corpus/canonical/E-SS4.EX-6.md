::: exercise
6. Prove that

$$

\frac {1}{\pi} \sum_ {n = - \infty} ^ {\infty} \frac {a}{a ^ {2} + n ^ {2}} = \sum_ {n = - \infty} ^ {\infty} e ^ {- 2 \pi a | n |}

$$

whenever $a > 0$ . Hence show that the sum equals coth $\pi a$
:::
