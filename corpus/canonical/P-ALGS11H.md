::: problem
Assume $A$ is a commutative ring with unity, and let $f_1, \ldots, f_n \in A$ generate the unit ideal $(1)$. Assume that the rings of fractions $A_{f_1}, \ldots, A_{f_n}$ are Noetherian. Prove that $A$ is Noetherian.
:::
