::: problem
Consider the set $L$ of $3$-manifolds which can be formed by gluing together the boundaries of two solid tori $S^1 \times B^2$ using a homeomorphism. Consider the function $d : L \to \mathbb{N}$ given by the total dimension of the mod-$2$ homology: $d(M) = \sum_i \dim H_i(M; \mathbb{Z}_2)$. What is the maximal value of $d$ on $L$?
:::
