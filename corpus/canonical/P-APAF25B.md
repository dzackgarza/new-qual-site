::: problem
Given $n\geq 1$, let $A\in M_n(\mathbb{C})=\mathbb{C}^{n\times n}$. Prove if $x^H Ax=0$, for all $x\in\mathbb{C}^n$, then $A=0$.

(Notationally: for all $m,n\geq 1$, $z^H=\overline{z}^T$ for all $z\in M_{m,n}(\mathbb{C})=\mathbb{C}^{m\times n}$.)
:::
