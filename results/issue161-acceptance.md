**Item 1: exactness at every term, proved.** The card states the triple sequence, then proves it exact at $\pi_n(X,B,x_0)$, $\pi_n(X,A,x_0)$ and $\pi_n(A,B,x_0)$ for every $n\ge1$, and at the extension term $\pi_1(X,A,x_0)$ with $\pi_0(A,B,x_0)=\pi_0(A,x_0)/i_*\pi_0(B,x_0)$; the example fixing that the sequence stops there is on the card. Degrees $n\ge2$ are Hatcher's three paragraphs, printed pp. 344--345, transcribed with the compression criterion's two directions (printed pp. 343--344) used in each; degrees $n=1$ are the three arguments transcribed at $n=1$, where the point is that every homotopy written down is a homotopy of maps of triples --- the start point inside $A$, and inside $B$ at $\pi_1(X,B,x_0)$ and $\pi_1(A,B,x_0)$. The five-lemma is proved at all four set-valued windows of the truncated ladder under the exercise's "for all choices of $x_0$", with the clause stated, shown to be used at other basepoints, and shown to be necessary by an explicit example whose single-basepoint form fails. The degenerate case $B=\{x_0\}$ is checked as well: there part (b) is part (a), its four windows being four of the six part (a) settles, and the windows part (a) has beyond those exist only because the pair sequence continues two steps further.
