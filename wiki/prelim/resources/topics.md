# Topics

## UCLA Basic Examination

[[attachments/UCLA_Basic_Exam_Topics.pdf|UCLA Basic Examination syllabus]] (January 2019)

The syllabus examines three areas: the fundamentals of analysis, namely one-variable calculus, metric-space topology in $\RR^n$, and multivariable calculus; linear algebra; and the numerical linear algebra of its applied-mathematics list.
Its suggested references, in the order it gives them: for analysis and multivariable calculus, Tao's *Analysis I* and *II*, Gamelin and Greene's *Introduction to Topology* Chapter 1, Edwards' *Advanced Calculus of Several Variables* Chapters I--III, [@Apo74], Rosenlicht's *Introduction to Analysis*, and [@Rud64]; for linear algebra, Petersen's *Linear Algebra and Lecture Notes*, Lang's *Linear Algebra*, [@HK71], Marcus and Minc's *Introduction to Linear Algebra*, and *Schaum's Outline of 3000 Solved Problems of Linear Algebra*; and, for readers with an interest in applied mathematics, D. Serre's *Matrices: Theory and Applications*, Ralston and Rabinowitz's *A First Course in Numerical Analysis*, 2nd edition, Chapters 9 and 10, and Atkinson's *An Introduction to Numerical Analysis*, 2nd edition, Chapters 7, 8 and 9.

## Linear algebra

Finite-dimensional vector spaces over $\RR$ and linear maps between them: subspaces, quotient spaces, dimension, bases, matrix representations.
Positive definite inner products, orthonormal bases, extensions of orthonormal subsets to orthonormal bases.
Eigenvalues and eigenvectors of automorphisms.
Characteristic polynomials.

References: [@Art91, chs. 1, 3, 4; @HK71, chs. 1--6].

## Abstract algebra

Definitions of groups, rings, fields, and modules over a ring.
Homomorphisms of these objects.
Subgroups, normal subgroups, quotient groups.
Cyclic groups, and the structure theorem for finite abelian groups.
Ideals, prime and maximal ideals, and their quotients, with the examples $\ZZ$, $k[X]$, and rings of algebraic integers.
Field extensions, splitting fields of polynomials, normal extensions.

References: [@Art91, chs. 2, 10--14; @Her64].

## Point-set topology

Open and closed sets, continuous functions.
Connectedness, compactness, the Hausdorff property, normality.
Metric spaces, $\RR^n$, the Heine--Borel theorem.

Reference: [@Mun00, pt. I].

## Calculus

The differential of a smooth map between open subsets of Euclidean spaces.
The matrix of partial derivatives.
The inverse and implicit function theorems.
Multivariable Riemann integration.

References: [@Rud64; @Bro96].

## Complex analysis

Holomorphic functions, the Cauchy integral formula, power series representations of holomorphic functions, radius of convergence, meromorphic functions, residues.

Reference: [@Ahl73, chs. 1--5].

## Real analysis

Advanced calculus at the level of [@Rud64] and [@Bro96].

## Additional topics

Pointwise and uniform convergence of functions, equicontinuity, $\ell^2$, $L^2(S^1)$, Hilbert spaces, orthonormal bases.
