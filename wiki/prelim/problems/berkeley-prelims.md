# Berkeley prelims

The Berkeley preliminary examination is a written examination in calculus,
classical analysis, abstract algebra, linear algebra, and complex analysis.
The [syllabus](attachments/syllabus.pdf) states the examination's format and
purpose, the syllabus of each subject, suggested texts, and the grading and
review policy.

Each sitting held here is one collection, with that sitting's problems in exam
order; the sittings run from Spring 1977 to Spring 2019.
The collections are listed by institution, subject, and date on
[[archives/card-archives|the Sources listing]], under Berkeley.
