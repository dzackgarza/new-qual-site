# UCLA prelims

The UCLA Basic Examination is the department's written qualifying examination
in one-variable and multivariable analysis, metric-space topology, and linear
algebra; [[prelim/resources/topics|the topic list]] links its syllabus
(January 2019) and the preparation texts it names.

Each sitting held here is one collection, with that sitting's problems in exam
order; the sittings run from Fall 2001 to Fall 2018.
The collections are listed with the other examination collections by
institution, subject, and date on [[archives/card-archives|the Sources listing]], under UCLA.
