## Coverage of the sets held in this repository

**Math 538 homework solutions**, `tmp/sources/hatcher-math538-homework4-solutions.pdf` (19 pages, scanned
handwriting; retrieved from `tkmd.org/wp/wp-content/uploads/YazOkulu1/AlgebraicTopologyWeek2-part2-3.pdf`).
The sheets name only the course (Math 538) and the homework number, so no author is attributed here.
Cite both locators: the PDF page and the sheet's own handwritten page. Hatcher §3.3 is covered as

| exercise | locator |
| --- | --- |
| 3 | HMW 3 handwritten p.1 = PDF p.13 |
| 4 | HMW 3 p.2 = PDF p.14 |
| 6 | HMW 3 pp.2–4 = PDF pp.14–16 |
| 7 | HMW 4 p.1 = PDF p.17 |
| 8 | HMW 4 pp.1–2 = PDF pp.17–18 |
| 9 (printed there as #8) | HMW 4 p.2 = PDF p.18 |
| 17 | HMW 4 p.3 = PDF p.19 |

Two defects found on checking it against Hatcher and recorded on the cards: the §3.3 Ex.9 sheet concludes
$d=\pm p$ from local signs that are individually $\pm1$ without proving they agree, and the §3.3 Ex.6
sheet reads the pair sequence only for $n\ge3$, where $H_{n-2}(S^{n-1})=0$.

**Kenneth DeMason, *M382C Full Credit HW Problems* (4 April 2021)**,
`tmp/sources/demason-m382c-hatcher-20210404.pdf` ([resources](https://math.mcmaster.ca/~demasonk/resources.html),
[PDF](https://math.mcmaster.ca/~demasonk/files/exercises/DeMason%2C%20Kenneth%20M382C%20HW%20Full%20Credit%2020210404.pdf)).
The table of contents locates §2.2 Ex. 10 pp. 76–77, Ex. 33 pp. 79–80 and Ex. 42 p. 80; further
chapter-two items are indexed there. The Ex. 42 section explicitly says the proof is not fully fleshed
out, leaves faithfulness as an intuition, and gives only a rank-$1$ mod-2 example, so it is **not**
accepted as a complete published proof of that exercise.

**Morten Poulsen, *Allen Hatcher: Algebraic Topology* (3 November 2006)**,
`tmp/sources/poulsen-at-ex.pdf` (33 pages, `441762` bytes). Numbering is Hatcher's own. The set stops inside
chapter two, so it holds no exercise from §2.3, §2.B except exercise 10, or any chapter-three or chapter-four
section. Its chapter-two coverage is §2.1 exercises 1, 4, 9, 11, 16, 18, 29, 30; §2.2 exercises 2, 4, 7, 9,
13, 17, 20, 21, 22, 23, 32, 36; §2.B.10; §2.C exercises 2, 8 and 9. An appendix proves that a covering map is
open and that a covering space of a CW complex is a CW complex with the induced skeleton filtration. Two
slips found on checking exercise 2.C.8 (printed p. 30): the homotopy inverse is given the wrong codomain, and
the countable bound's factor, the set of vertex functions, is left unexplained. A second file formerly
listed here under the same author, `hatcher-solutions-morten-poulsen.pdf`, was a corrupt copy of this one and
has been deleted.

**Stanford Math 215C, Spring 2009, solution sets**, linked from
[Ralph Cohen's Math 215C page](https://math.stanford.edu/~ralph/math215c/). Cite the individual set and printed page, not an
inferred author:

- [`solution3.pdf`](https://math.stanford.edu/~ralph/math215c/solution3.pdf): Hatcher §3.3 Ex. 9 and 10, p. 1; Ex. 11, pp. 1–2.
- [`solution4.pdf`](https://math.stanford.edu/~ralph/math215c/solution4.pdf): Hatcher §3.3 Ex. 21, p. 1 (its proposed
  $\ZZ\times\RR$ counterexample is **not** accepted: it gives an injection on Hom groups, not an uncountability argument);
  Ex. 22, pp. 1–2 (relative Mayer–Vietoris); Ex. 24, p. 2.
- [`solution2.pdf`](https://math.stanford.edu/~ralph/math215c/solution2.pdf): Hatcher §3.B Ex. 1, pp. 3–4 (whose integral and mod-2 homology
  computations are used here, but which explicitly skips cohomology); Ex. 3, p. 5 (the cellular Tor example and its non-natural splitting).

Other coverage and checked corrections are recorded on the cards rather than inferred from these selections.

**Guanyu Li, [`Hatcher.pdf`](https://guanyulee.github.io/Hatcher.pdf)**, local file
`tmp/sources/hatcher-solutions-guanyu-li.pdf`. Numbering is internal, so cite the parenthetical Hatcher
exercise number as well as the internal one. Its §3.3 material (printed pp. 56–59) has solutions to
Hatcher §3.3 Ex. 3 (pp. 56), 4 (p. 57), 8 (p. 59), 10 (p. 57), 11 (pp. 57–58), 17 (p. 58),
18 (pp. 58–59) and 25 (p. 59). Hatcher §3.3 Ex. 1 and 2 are blank there; §3.3 Ex. 22 and 31 are not
covered in this section. Checked defects recorded on the cards: the Ex. 10 argument omits infinite-sheeted
covers, Ex. 18 leaves the subgroup-realization step terse, and Ex. 25 writes `Ext` where the universal
coefficient sequence has `Hom`. Li's Ex. 11 contains an explicit quotient-cell construction at the top
of p. 58; this is not a blanket endorsement of the other arguments or of complete chapter coverage.

**Steven V. Sam, *Solutions to Algebraic Topology* (2008)**, `tmp/sources/hatcher-solutions-sam-2008.pdf`
(30 pages; obtained from the UC Riverside Math 205B page, where the host warns that some solutions in it
are poor — check every argument against the book). Its printed page numbers run one behind the PDF page
index because of a cover sheet; cite printed pages. It treats the following sections only:

| section | exercises solved |
| --- | --- |
| §1.1, §1.2, §1.3, §1.A | a selection |
| §2.1 | a selection |
| §2.2 | 2, 8, 17, 20, 21, 22 |
| §3.2 | 1, 4, 11 |
| §3.3 | 8 (printed p.14–15), 17 (pp.15–16), 32 (p.16) |
| §3.C | 5 (p.16) |
| §4.1, §4.2, §4.3 | a selection |

It has **no §3.A, §3.B or §3.E at all**, and within §3.3 and §3.C only the exercises listed. So for
Hatcher's chapter-3 sections other than the few above there is no published solution in the checkout,
and a source still to be acquired. Coverage of the Riemannian Hunger set listed above stops at
§2.1, so it does not help for chapter 3 either.


For the algebraic correction on §3.3 Ex. 21, use **Martin Frankland, *Cohomology of the Hawaiian
Earring* (2017)**, `tmp/sources/frankland-hawaiian-earring-2017.pdf` ([PDF](https://uregina.ca/~franklam/Frankland_HawaiianEarring_20170222.pdf)).
Theorem 1.2 (p. 2) is Specker's theorem: $\operatorname{Hom}(\prod_{\NN}\ZZ,\ZZ)$ is the countable
free group on the coordinate projections. Theorem 1.3 (p. 2) gives $H^1(E;\ZZ)\cong\oplus_{\NN}\ZZ$,
and Theorem 1.4 (p. 3) the induced dual isomorphism. The cardinality argument cannot answer the
bracketed one-point-compactification question either way.

## Solutions to Munkres's *Topology*

Four published sets were located for the exercises of Munkres, *Topology*, 2nd ed. (2000). Cite the set, the
exercise and the set's **own** printed page, not Munkres's: the manual's page numbers are the ones a reader
follows in the PDF.

**Dan Whitman, *Topology, Second Edition by James Munkres: Solutions Manual* (30 November 2019)**,
`tmp/sources/munkres-solutions-whitman.pdf` (277 pages, 1,454,860 bytes;
[PDF](https://math-study.net/wp-content/TopologySolutions.pdf)). Every page carries its own "Page $n$" in the
footer, three less than the PDF page index, so cite the footer. This is the complete set for Chapter 1 and the
one the cards of §1, §2, §4 and §5 are transcribed from:

| section | printed pages | exercises |
| --- | --- | --- |
| §1 Fundamental Concepts | 1–10 | 1.1–1.10, all |
| §2 Functions | 11–19 | 2.1–2.6, all |
| §3 Relations | 20–37 | 3.1–3.15, all |
| §4 The Integers and the Real Numbers | 38–66 | 4.1–4.11, all |
| §5 Cartesian Products | 67–74 | 5.1–5.5, all |
| §6 Finite Sets | 75–80 | 6.1–6.7, all |
| §7 Countable and Uncountable Sets | 81–93 | 7.1–7.9, all |
| §8 The Principle of Recursive Definition | 94–100 | 8.1–8.8, all |
| §9 Infinite Sets and the Axiom of Choice | 101–107 | 9.1–9.8, all |
| §10 Well-Ordered Sets | 108–118 | 10.1–10.11, all |
| §11 The Maximum Principle | 119–127, 128 | 11.1–11.8 and the well-ordering supplement |
| §13, §16–§22 | 141–258 | every exercise of those sections, and the topological-groups supplement |

It has **no §12, §14 or §15**: its chapter two begins at §13 on printed p. 141. Its proofs are numbered by
lemmas of its own (Lemma 4.5.1, 4.9.2, …) and cite Munkres's own earlier exercises; those lemma numbers are
Whitman's, not the book's. Its §5 Exercise 4(f) carries a NOTE that older printings of Munkres read
"$m:X^A\to X^B$" and the retained printing reads $m:(A^\omega)^n\to B^\omega$; the card follows the
retained printing and records the variant.

**"shenapse", *Exercise-Munkres***, `tmp/sources/munkres-solutions-shenapse.pdf` (93 pages; the GitHub
repository [shenapse/Exercise-Munkres](https://github.com/shenapse/Exercise-Munkres) holds the TeX and this
PDF; solutions written around 2010, and its README states outright that they "have not undergone reliable
review"). Its footer runs four behind the PDF page index. It covers **Chapter 1, §1–§11** and was used as an
independent check on §1, §4 and §5. The author's name is not in the file: the handle is the only attribution,
so cite it as such.

**"9beach", *A solutions manual for Topology by James Munkres***,
`tmp/sources/munkres-solutions-9beach.pdf` ([release PDF](https://github.com/9beach/munkres-topology-solutions/releases/download/v0.1.1/munkres-topology-solutions.pdf),
14 pages; [HTML](https://9beach.github.io/math/munkres-topology-solutions/)). Its sources are `chap-01` to
`chap-04`, so it covers **§1–§4 only**, and not every exercise of those; it was used as a third check on §1
and §2. Same attribution problem as shenapse: a handle and no name.

**"positron0802", *Solutions to Topology, Chapter 2* and *Chapter 3*** (1 January 2021),
`tmp/sources/munkres-solutions-positron-ch2.pdf` and `munkres-solutions-positron-ch3.pdf` (31 and 26 pages;
[Chapter 2](https://positron0802.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/topology-2-munkres.pdf),
[Chapter 3](https://positron0802.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/topology-3-munkres.pdf);
the author's name is not in the files, so the handle is the attribution). Numbering is Munkres's own,
"Exercise 12.1" through "Exercise 29.x", and the exercises are numbered consecutively within each PDF, so
cite the book's exercise number and the set's PDF page. It contains **every exercise of Chapter 2 from §13 on
and of Chapter 3**, which is what the corpus still needs after Whitman:

| section | in the set | Munkres prints |
| --- | --- | --- |
| §12 Topological Spaces | — | **no exercises** (printed pp. 75–78) |
| §13, §16–§22 | all exercises | pp. 83, 91–144 |
| §23–§29 | all exercises | pp. 148–186 |

So the sections Whitman lacks are not a gap in any set: **§12, §14 and §15 print no exercises at all**, which
the set states in the text ("No Exercises.") and which the retained book confirms — the `Exercises` headings
of Chapter 2 fall on printed pp. 83 (§13), 91 (§16) and onward, never inside §12, §14 or §15. The same is true
of §65, whose exercises do not exist in the printing.

**Ana Emilia de Orellana, *Solutions — Topology Munkres***, `tmp/sources/munkres-solutions-anadeore.pdf`
(36 pages; [Solutions.pdf](https://github.com/AnadeOre/munkres-topology-solutions/blob/master/Solutions.pdf)).
A **selection**, not a complete set: §13, §16–§21, §23–§28, §30–§34 and §37, with each section's own page
range in its contents. It is the only located set with printed pagination that reaches Chapter 4 (§30–§34)
and §37 at all, and it solves a subset of the exercises in each. The author warns that the solutions may
contain errors.

**"kyp44", *A solutions manual for Topology by James Munkres (2nd Edition)***, sources at
[kyp44/Topology](https://github.com/kyp44/Topology) (`solutions.tex` and `sections/sec_01.tex` …
`sec_22_TG.tex`). Chapters 1 and 2 only, stopping at §22 and the topological-groups supplement; it too has no
§12, §14 or §15 file. Work in progress, no chapter 3 or beyond.

Nothing located covers Chapters 4–14 complete: §30–§34 and §37 have only Orellana's selection, and §35, §36,
§38–§64, §66–§85 have no located set with printed pagination. The `dbFin` pages, the Riemannian Hunger blog
and the commercial manuals are HTML or paywalled, print no pagination, and are not cited here.

No set was found for Munkres §12, §14, §15 or §65, and none in this repository before this search; the
`dbFin` pages and the commercial solution manuals are HTML or paywalled and print no pagination, so neither is
cited here.
