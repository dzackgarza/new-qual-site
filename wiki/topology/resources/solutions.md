# Solution sources

Solution sets for qualifying-exam problems in topology and for the exercises of Munkres, *Topology*, 2nd ed. (2000) and Hatcher, *Algebraic Topology* (2002). Each entry names the work, its author or handle, where it is published, and the sections and exercises it treats. A set's own printed page is cited where it has one; a web page has no printed page, so the date it was read is given instead.

## Hatcher, *Algebraic Topology*

**[Riemannian Hunger, solutions to Hatcher's *Algebraic Topology*](https://riemannianhunger.wordpress.com/solutions-to-algebraic-topology-by-allen-hatcher/).** One post per exercise, for Chapter 0 Exercises 1--29 and §2.1 Exercises 1--31.

**Math 538 homework solutions, homework sheets 3 and 4** ([scan](https://tkmd.org/wp/wp-content/uploads/YazOkulu1/AlgebraicTopologyWeek2-part2-3.pdf)), 19 pages of handwriting. The sheets name the course and the homework number only. They treat §3.3 Exercises 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9 and 17; their Exercise 9 is printed as #8. The handwritten pages locate them: Exercise 3 on HMW 3 p. 1, Exercise 4 on HMW 3 p. 2, Exercise 6 on HMW 3 pp. 2--4, Exercise 7 on HMW 4 p. 1, Exercise 8 on HMW 4 pp. 1--2, Exercise 9 on HMW 4 p. 2, and Exercise 17 on HMW 4 p. 3. On §3.3 Exercise 9 the sheet concludes $d=\pm p$ from local signs that are individually $\pm1$ without proving they agree; on Exercise 6 it reads the pair sequence only for $n\geq3$, where $H_{n-2}(S^{n-1})=0$.

**Kenneth DeMason, *M382C Full Credit HW Problems* (4 April 2021)**
([PDF](https://math.mcmaster.ca/~demasonk/files/exercises/DeMason%2C%20Kenneth%20M382C%20HW%20Full%20Credit%2020210404.pdf)).
Its table of contents locates §2.2 Exercise 10 on pp. 76--77, Exercise 33 on pp. 79--80 and Exercise 42 on p. 80, and indexes further chapter-two items. The Exercise 42 section states that its proof is not complete, leaves faithfulness as an intuition, and works one rank-$1$ example over $\FF_2$.

**Morten Poulsen, *Allen Hatcher: Algebraic Topology* (3 November 2006)**, 33 pages, numbering Hatcher's own. It treats §2.1 Exercises 1, 4, 9, 11, 16, 18, 29 and 30; §2.2 Exercises 2, 4, 7, 9, 13, 17, 20, 21, 22, 23, 32 and 36; §2.B Exercise 10; and §2.C Exercises 2, 8 and 9. An appendix proves that a covering map is open and that a covering space of a CW complex is a CW complex with the induced skeleton filtration. On §2.C Exercise 8 (printed p. 30) the homotopy inverse is given the wrong codomain, and the factor in the countable bound, the set of vertex functions, is left unexplained.

**Stanford Math 215C, Spring 2009, solution sets**, from [Ralph Cohen's course page](https://math.stanford.edu/~ralph/math215c/). Cite the individual set and its printed page.

- [`solution3.pdf`](https://math.stanford.edu/~ralph/math215c/solution3.pdf): §3.3 Exercises 9 and 10 on p. 1, Exercise 11 on pp. 1--2.

- [`solution4.pdf`](https://math.stanford.edu/~ralph/math215c/solution4.pdf): §3.3 Exercise 21 on p. 1, whose proposed $\ZZ\times\RR$ counterexample gives an injection on Hom groups rather than an uncountability argument; Exercise 22 on pp. 1--2 by relative Mayer--Vietoris; Exercise 24 on p. 2.

- [`solution2.pdf`](https://math.stanford.edu/~ralph/math215c/solution2.pdf): §3.B Exercise 1 on pp. 3--4, which computes the integral and mod-2 homology and omits cohomology; Exercise 3 on p. 5, the cellular Tor example and its non-natural splitting.

**Guanyu Li, [`Hatcher.pdf`](https://guanyulee.github.io/Hatcher.pdf)**, with internal numbering in addition to Hatcher's. Its §3.3 section, printed pp. 56--59, treats Exercise 3 (p. 56), 4 (p. 57), 8 (p. 59), 10 (p. 57), 11 (pp. 57--58), 17 (p. 58), 18 (pp. 58--59) and 25 (p. 59); Exercises 1 and 2 are blank, and 22 and 31 are absent. On Exercise 10 the argument omits infinite-sheeted covers, Exercise 18 leaves the subgroup-realization step terse, and Exercise 25 writes $\Ext$ where the universal coefficient sequence has $\Hom$. Exercise 11 contains an explicit quotient-cell construction at the top of p. 58.

**Steven V. Sam, *Solutions to Algebraic Topology* (2008)**, 30 pages, from the UC Riverside Math 205B page. Its printed page numbers run one behind the PDF index because of a cover sheet. It treats a selection of §1.1, §1.2, §1.3, §1.A and §2.1; §2.2 Exercises 2, 8, 17, 20, 21 and 22; §3.2 Exercises 1, 4 and 11; §3.3 Exercises 8 (printed pp. 14--15), 17 (pp. 15--16) and 32 (p. 16); §3.C Exercise 5 (p. 16); and a selection of §4.1, §4.2 and §4.3.

**Other sets.** [Nicholas Proudfoot, Math 634: Algebraic Topology I, University of Oregon, Fall 2015, homework solutions](https://pages.uoregon.edu/njp/hw1solutions.pdf) (`hw1solutions.pdf` through `hw7solutions.pdf`; there is no `hw8`): Chapter 0 Exercises 2, 3, 9, 10; §1.1 Exercises 2, 3, 6, 12, 16, 20; §1.2 Exercises 4, 7, 8, 9, 14, 15, 21; §1.3 Exercises 4, 9, 10, 12, 14, 15, 18, 20, 23, 26; §4.1 Exercises 4, 11, 12, 14, 15; §4.2 Exercises 1, 2, 30, 31, 32, 39. [`hat-sols.com`](https://hat-sols.com) treats Chapter 0 Exercises 1 and 2 and §1.1 Exercises 1, 2 and 12; [`coycatrett/hatcher-sols`](https://github.com/coycatrett/hatcher-sols) treats §2.1.

**Homological algebra.** S. Weibel, *An Introduction to Homological Algebra*, Cambridge Studies in Advanced Mathematics 38 (1994): Corollaries 2.6.16 and 2.6.17 with Theorem 2.7.2, printed p. 58; Proposition 3.1.3 with the remark after Proposition 3.1.4 and Corollary 3.1.5, printed pp. 66--67. The Stacks Project proves the two colimit statements as Tag 00DD and Tag 00M7.

**Martin Frankland, *Cohomology of the Hawaiian Earring* (2017)**
([PDF](https://uregina.ca/~franklam/Frankland_HawaiianEarring_20170222.pdf)).
Theorem 1.2 (p. 2) is Specker's theorem: $\Hom(\prod_{\NN}\ZZ,\ZZ)$ is the countable free group on the coordinate projections. Theorem 1.3 (p. 2) gives $H^1(E;\ZZ)\cong\bigoplus_{\NN}\ZZ$, and Theorem 1.4 (p. 3) the induced dual isomorphism.

**dbFin, *Munkres (2000) Topology with Solutions*, Chapter 4** (read 2026-11-10): `https://dbfin.com/topology/munkres/chapter-4/<section>/problem-<n>-solution/`. A page for every numbered exercise of §30--§36, no page for any of the Supplementary Exercises "Review of the Basics", and no solution for Exercise 30.18. Depth varies from a complete argument (§30.1, §32.7, §32.9, §33.3, §34.6, §35.2, §35.9, §36.5) to one sentence (§32.4) or a restatement of the exercise (§30.18).

**"Solutions to exercises for Mathematics 205A", University of California, Riverside, Part 3 (Fall 2014)**, PDF pp. 42--50 of the course's collected sets, each set keeping its own printed page numbers. Section III.2 answers **§43 Exercises 1(a), 1(b), 3(b), 6(a), 6(c) on printed pp. 4--5**, with its Additional exercise 2 (printed p. 5) asking verbatim for the completeness of $\ell^2$, which is §43 Exercise 7; section III.3 answers **§48 Exercises 1, 2 and 4 on printed pp. 6--7**. Its answer to §48 Exercise 2 takes the cosets of a $\QQ$-subspace to have nonempty interior and then argues that they do not; what the argument shows is that the cosets are dense with empty interior, hence not closed.

**"Tze Heng", *Munkres' Topology: Self-study Notes and Exercises Attempts*, Chapter 7** (read 2026-11-11): `https://blog.ttheng.com/docs/mathematics/munkres-topology-self-study/chapt-7/exercises/`. It states every exercise of §43--§47 and answers §43 Exercises 1, 2 and 3. Its Exercise 1(b) offers $\QQ$ as a space in which every point has a ball with compact closure, which is false, a closed interval of $\QQ$ not being compact; its Exercise 3(b) does not record that the two completions give the same limit.

**"Topology I and II Notes"**, `https://locallyringed.space/Topology%20Notes.pdf`, compiled 16 November 2017, with no author named. Lecture notes organized by Munkres section, with numbered results of their own ("Lemma 1.43.3", "Theorem 1.44.1", …). They cover §43--§46 and prove the sections' theorems, not their exercises.

**MAT 327, University of Toronto, 2023, problem sets 1--6 with solutions**, from `https://www.math.toronto.edu/laithy/3272023/`. The sheets are the course's own problems, so a Munkres exercise is matched by statement. The first three are by Jiawei Chen and the last by Arthur Lei Qiu. Its Question 1, Problem 1(b) of `327ps6sol.pdf`, PDF p. 1, proves the uniform boundedness principle for a complete metric space and for a locally compact Hausdorff space, which is §48 Exercise 10.

**Aleksey Zinger, *MAT 530: Topology & Geometry, I*, Fall 2005, problem-set solutions** (`ps2sol.pdf` … `ps9sol.pdf` from [the course page](https://www.math.stonybrook.edu/~azinger/mat530/)). Twelve numbered problem sets, three to six pages each, with no printed page numbers; the sheet's heading is of the form "Solution to Problem p235, #1", the book's page and exercise number. Its chapter-4 items are §37 Exercise 1 (sheet 4, pp. 1--2, with the counterexample showing that the printed $T_1$ hypothesis of part (c) does not suffice) and §37 Exercise 5 (sheet 4, pp. 2--3); it also treats §41 Exercises 2 and 7 (sheet 6, pp. 1 and 2--3) and §1 Exercise 7 (sheet 4, p. 67 transfinite induction).

**"Solutions to exercises for Mathematics 205A", University of California, Riverside, Fall 2014**, `solutions5a.pdf`, 21 pages, also collected in the course's 107-page set. Section VI.4 answers **§38 Exercises 2 and 3 on printed p. 9**: it prints Exercise 3 with the annotation "Just give a necessary condition on the topology of the space" and states the sufficiency in one sentence, and its imbedding for Exercise 2 goes into $[-1,1]^3$ where the book prints $[0,1]^3$.

**"Feryll", *Praying For Stockholm***, four posts on Munkres, 25--31 December 2014, each cited by URL and date read.

## Munkres, Chapters 10 and 11 (§61--§73)

**dbFin, *Munkres (2000) Topology with Solutions*, Chapter 11** (read 2026-11-11): `https://dbfin.com/topology/munkres/chapter-11/section-67-direct-sums-of-abelian-groups/`. Six pages, one per exercise of §67, each a short argument. Its §67 Problem 3 observes that Munkres prints $\{2x+3y, x-y\}$, whose coordinate matrix has determinant $-5$, proves that the pair generates a proper subgroup, and offers $x+y$ as the repair.

**"Solutions to exercises for Mathematics 205A", University of California, Riverside, Part 9 (Fall 2014)**, 11 pages, each set keeping its own printed page numbers.

| section | printed pages | exercises |

| §68 Free Products of Groups | 2--3 | 2 and 3 |
| §69 Free Groups | 4 | 1, 3 and 4 |
| §70 The Seifert--van Kampen Theorem | 6--7 | 1 and 3 |

Its §68 Exercise 2 records that the printed odd-length clause is indispensable at length one, and its §70 Exercise 3 handles the finite-presentation claim through the pushout form.

**Takumi Murayama, *Selected Solutions to Munkres's Topology, 2nd Ed.* (MAT365, Princeton, Fall 2012)**, TeX source at `https://github.com/awasthi/math-solutions/blob/master/Munkres/Munkres%20Solutions.tex`. A selection, not a manual: there is no Chapter 10, and of Chapter 11 it treats §67 Exercises 2 and 4, §68 Exercise 2, §71 Exercises 2, 4 and 5, and §73 Exercise 1. On §71 Exercise 4 it replaces a countable base at the wedge point by one assumed strictly nested, which a countable base need not admit, and its closing comparison produces no point outside the diagonal neighbourhood; on §71 Exercise 5 it concludes from the boundedness of a loop's image that the loop meets only finitely many circles, which is false because every circle of the family passes through the base point; on §71 Exercise 2 it leaves the openness of $X_1\cup W_2$ unchecked; and its §68 Exercise 2 amends the printed statement to odd length at least three without saying so.

**Harvard Math 131 (2000--01)**, solution sets posted beside the reading schedule at `https://people.math.harvard.edu/~ctm/home/text/class/harvard/131/01/html/home/solns/soln<n>.pdf`: `soln9`--`soln11` cover the Chapter 7 and Chapter 9 homework, and no Chapter 11 set was posted. They are typeset in a Type 3 font whose text does not extract, so they are read as page images.

**Robert Gardner, ETSU MATH 4357/5357 notes**, prove the theorems of §61--§73 and give no solutions of their exercises.
