# Degree of a map

The degree of a map $S^n\to S^n$ is the integer by which the map multiplies the top cohomology group.
It is a homotopy invariant of the map, and it decides when a map is homotopic to the antipodal map.

[[D-XC53X]]

## Properties of the degree

The degree is multiplicative, a reflection reverses its sign, and the antipodal map has degree $(-1)^{n+1}$ [@Hat02, §2.2]; conversely, maps of equal degree are homotopic [@Hat02, Corollary 4.25].
