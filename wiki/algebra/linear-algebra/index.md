- [[algebra/linear-algebra/rational-canonical-form|Rational canonical form]] -- invariant factors and companion matrices, over any field.

- [[algebra/linear-algebra/the-spectral-theorem|The spectral theorem]] -- unitary and orthogonal diagonalization, and simultaneous diagonalization.
