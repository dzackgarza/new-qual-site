# Undergraduate review

The undergraduate facts a qualifying exam assumes without stating them, each with the card that states it and the page that uses it.

## Dimension and rank

A basis gives every vector a unique coordinate expression, a linear map is determined by its values on a basis, and the rank-nullity theorem is the statement that a map loses exactly the dimension of its kernel.

## Matrices of a map

Similar matrices are the matrices of one operator in two bases, and equivalent matrices are the matrices of one map between two pairs of bases, so similarity classifies operators and equivalence classifies maps.

## Eigenvalues

The characteristic polynomial has the eigenvalues as roots with algebraic multiplicity, the minimal polynomial is the monic polynomial of least degree that annihilates the operator, and the operator is diagonalizable exactly when the minimal polynomial has distinct roots.

## The determinant

The permutation expansion, cofactor expansion along a row, the adjugate inverse, and the identification of the trace and the determinant with the elementary symmetric functions of the eigenvalues are on [[algebra/linear-algebra/determinants-and-eigenvalues|Determinants and eigenvalues]], and factoring a cubic by the rational root theorem is on [[algebra/linear-algebra/minimal-and-characteristic-polynomials|Minimal and characteristic polynomials]].

## Inner products and forms

A bilinear form is read by its Gram matrix, nondegeneracy is invertibility of that matrix, and the orthogonal complement of a subspace has complementary dimension when the form is nondegenerate.
