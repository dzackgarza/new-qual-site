# Undergraduate review

The undergraduate facts a qualifying exam assumes are stated where they are used:

- the permutation expansion of the determinant, the $3\times 3$ formula, cofactor expansion, and the adjugate inverse; block multiplication; trace and determinant as the elementary symmetric functions of the eigenvalues; similarity invariance of the trace; and powers of an upper-triangular matrix are on [[algebra/linear-algebra/determinants-and-eigenvalues|Determinants and eigenvalues]];
- factoring a cubic by the rational root theorem and polynomial long division is on [[algebra/linear-algebra/minimal-and-characteristic-polynomials|Minimal and characteristic polynomials]].
