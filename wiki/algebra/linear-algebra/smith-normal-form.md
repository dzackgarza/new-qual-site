# Smith normal form

[[T-SMTHN]]

The form is the matrix statement of the structure theorem: the diagonal entries of the Smith normal form of $A$ are the invariant factors of the module $R^n/\im(A)$, so a classification of modules and a classification of matrices up to equivalence are the same computation.

For $R = \ZZ$ the form classifies finitely generated abelian groups, and for $R=k[x]$ applied to $xI-B$ it gives the invariant factors of the rational canonical form of a square matrix $B$ over a field $k$; the computation itself is on [[algebra/modules/classify-this-module|Classify this module]].
