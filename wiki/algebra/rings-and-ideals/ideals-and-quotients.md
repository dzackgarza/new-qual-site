## Units and simplicity

[[PR-DDDXH]]

::: {.proof}
$\implies$: If $0\neq x\in I\normal R$ and every nonzero element is a unit, then $x$ is a unit, so $xx\inv = 1 \in I$ and $I = R$.

$\impliedby$: For $x\in R\nonzero$, $Rx = R$ gives $1 = rx$ for some $r$, so $x = r\inv$.
:::
