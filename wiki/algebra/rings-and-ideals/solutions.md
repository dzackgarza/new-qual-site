# Solutions (rings)

Joseph A. Gallian, *Contemporary Abstract Algebra*, seventh edition: solution outlines for a selection of the exercises of chapters 12--17.

- [[SRC-CH12-RING-SOLUTION-OUTLINES|Chapter 12, introduction to rings]] -- pp. 237--248: the definition and examples of rings, properties of rings, subrings.

- [[SRC-CH13-RING-SOLUTION-OUTLINES|Chapter 13, integral domains]] -- pp. 249--261: integral domains, fields, the characteristic of a ring.

- [[SRC-CH14-IDEAL-SOLUTION-OUTLINES|Chapter 14, ideals and factor rings]] -- pp. 262--275: ideals, factor rings, prime and maximal ideals.

- [[SRC-CH15-RING-HOMOMORPHISM-SOLUTION-OUTLINES|Chapter 15, ring homomorphisms]] -- pp. 280--292: homomorphisms and their properties, the field of quotients.

- [[SRC-CH16-POLYNOMIAL-RING-SOLUTION-OUTLINES|Chapter 16, polynomial rings]] -- pp. 293--304: notation and terminology, the division algorithm and its consequences.

- [[SRC-CH17-POLYNOMIAL-SOLUTION-OUTLINES|Chapter 17, factorization of polynomials]] -- pp. 305--321: reducibility tests, irreducibility tests, unique factorization in $\ZZ[x]$.
