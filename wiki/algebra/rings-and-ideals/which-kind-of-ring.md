# Which kind of ring is this?

## The chain of inclusions

The classes of commutative rings are totally ordered by inclusion, and the examples that separate consecutive classes carry the same proof that each inclusion is strict.

## Quotients

[[PR-76BDN]]

[[P-R9XZL]]

For a field $k$ and a nonconstant $f\in k[x]$, the ideal $\generators f$ is maximal exactly when it is generated by an irreducible, by [[P-ODNWF|the description of an ideal generated by an irreducible]] applied in the PID $k[x]$, and exactly when $k[x]/\generators f$ is a field, by [[PR-76BDN|the quotient-ring criteria]].

## Transporting properties

A property of $R$ that is defined by its ideals passes to quotients and to localizations; the two implications used most often are that a quotient by a maximal ideal is a field and that a finite integral domain is a field.

::: {.fact}
If $\mfm\subseteq R$ is a maximal ideal and $x\in R\sm\mfm$, then $\mfm + Rx = R$.
:::
