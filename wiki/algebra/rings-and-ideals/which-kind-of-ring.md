::: {.fact title="Maximal ideals of $k[x]$"}
For a field $k$ and a nonconstant $f\in k[x]$, the ideal $\generators f$ is maximal exactly when $f$ is irreducible, by [[P-ODNWF|the description of an ideal generated by an irreducible]] applied in the PID $k[x]$, and exactly when $k[x]/\generators f$ is a field, by [[PR-76BDN|the quotient-ring criteria]].
:::

## Transporting properties

::: {.fact}
If $\mfm\subseteq R$ is a maximal ideal and $x\in R\sm\mfm$, then $\mfm + Rx = R$.
:::
