# Number theory

Throughout, $n\geq1$ is an integer and $a$ is an integer.

## Units modulo $n$

::: {.fact title="The unit group and Euler's function"}
The group $(\ZZ/n\ZZ)^\times$ is what makes Euler's theorem and Fermat's little theorem instances of one theorem: its order is $\phi(n)$, and $\ZZ/n\ZZ$ splits over the prime powers dividing $n$, so the unit group and $\phi$ are computed prime by prime.
:::
