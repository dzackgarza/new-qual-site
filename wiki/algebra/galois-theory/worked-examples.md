# Worked examples

The computations by degree, done in full.
The method is [[algebra/galois-theory/compute-this-galois-group|Compute this Galois group]]; this page is what it looks like carried out.

## Quadratics

Every quadratic is Galois with group $C_2$, and $\QQ(\sqrt d)$ for squarefree $d$ are pairwise distinct.
The discriminant test is the entire content: $\sqrt\Delta \in \QQ$ exactly when $f$ splits.

## Cubics

$G$ is $S_3$ or $A_3 \cong C_3$, and the discriminant decides:
\[
\Delta \text{ a square in } \QQ \implies G = A_3, \qquad \text{otherwise } G = S_3
.\]
For a depressed cubic $t^3+pt+q$, $\Delta = -4p^3-27q^2$.

## Quartics

The candidates are $S_4, A_4, D_4, C_4, C_2^2$, and the resolvent cubic separates them.

[[D-4DWC5]]

[[PR-E4T3S]]

:::{.remark title="The procedure"}
Compute the resolvent cubic and factor it over $\QQ$:

- irreducible with $\Delta$ not a square: $G = S_4$;
- irreducible with $\Delta$ a square: $G = A_4$;
- splits completely: $G = C_2^2$;
- exactly one rational root: $G$ is $D_4$ or $C_4$, separated by whether $f$ stays irreducible over $\QQ(\sqrt\Delta)$.

:::

## Cyclotomic fields

Treated on [[algebra/galois-theory/cyclotomic-and-radical-extensions|Cyclotomic and radical extensions]] -- the group is $(\ZZ/n)^\times$ and no computation is needed.

## Finite fields

The group is cyclic, generated by Frobenius, so the only question is the degree.
