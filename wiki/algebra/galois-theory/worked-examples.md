# Worked examples

Galois groups of irreducible polynomials over $\QQ$ of small degree, using the methods on [[algebra/galois-theory/compute-this-galois-group|Compute this Galois group]].

## Quadratics

An irreducible quadratic $f\in\QQ[x]$ with discriminant $\Delta$ has splitting field $\QQ(\sqrt\Delta)$ and Galois group $C_2$.
The fields $\QQ(\sqrt d)$ for squarefree integers $d\neq1$ are pairwise distinct, and a quadratic $f$ splits over $\QQ$ if and only if $\sqrt\Delta \in \QQ$.

## Cubics

For a cubic $t^3+pt+q$, $\Delta = -4p^3-27q^2$.

[[PR-XPDHE]]

## Quartics

For an irreducible quartic $f\in\QQ[x]$, the Galois group is one of $S_4$, $A_4$, $D_4$, $C_4$, $C_2^2$, and the [[D-4DWC5|resolvent cubic]] together with the discriminant $\Delta$ determines which.

## Cyclotomic fields

$\Gal(\QQ(\zeta_n)/\QQ)\cong(\ZZ/n)^\times$; see [[algebra/galois-theory/cyclotomic-and-radical-extensions|Cyclotomic and radical extensions]].

## Finite fields

$\Gal(\FF_{p^n}/\FF_p)$ is cyclic of order $n$, generated by the Frobenius automorphism $x\mapsto x^p$; see [[algebra/fields/finite-fields|Finite fields]].
