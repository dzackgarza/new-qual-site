# Basics

## Definitions

For a finite group $G$ and $g\in G$, the [[D-ZTPHF|order]] of $g$ divides $\size G$, by Lagrange's theorem applied to the cyclic subgroup $\langle g\rangle$.

## Subgroups

A nonempty subset $H\subseteq G$ is a [[D-IQ4OX|subgroup]] if and only if $xy^{-1}\in H$ for all $x,y\in H$.
The [[D-D36IY|subgroup generated by a subset]] $S$ is the intersection of all subgroups containing $S$, so a property that holds for every subgroup containing $S$ holds for $\langle S\rangle$.
The [[D-BQ4BQ|commutator]] $[x,y]$ is trivial if and only if $x$ and $y$ commute, and the commutators of $G$ generate the commutator subgroup $[G,G]$, the first term of the [[D-W2QAA|derived series]].

## Conjugacy

Conjugation is an action of $G$ on itself and on the set of its subgroups.
For the action on elements, the orbit of $x$ is its [[D-HLDEY|conjugacy class]] and its stabilizer is the [[D-PX64W|centralizer]] $C_G(x)$, so $\size{\operatorname{Cl}(x)}=[G:C_G(x)]$ for finite $G$.
For the action on subgroups, the orbit of $H$ is the set of [[D-ES7MP|conjugate subgroups]] of $H$ and its stabilizer is the [[D-OZ2RR|normalizer]] $N_G(H)$, so $H$ has $[G:N_G(H)]$ conjugates.

## Normal subgroups

A subgroup $N\le G$ is a [[D-EKE4Q|normal subgroup]] if and only if $gNg^{-1}=N$ for all $g\in G$, equivalently if and only if $N$ is a union of conjugacy classes.
For $N$ normal, multiplication of cosets $(aN)(bN)=abN$ is well defined, and $G/N$ is a group.
A nontrivial normal subgroup of a finite [[D-FIB7S|$p$-group]] $G$ meets the [[D-NK7G7|center]] $Z(G)$ nontrivially.
If $M,N\normal G$ and $M\intersect N=1$, then $mn=nm$ for all $m\in M$ and $n\in N$.

## Centralizers and centers

For a subset $S\subseteq G$, the [[D-PX64W|centralizer]] $C_G(S)$ consists of the elements fixing every element of $S$ under conjugation, and the [[D-OZ2RR|normalizer]] $N_G(S)$ of the elements with $gSg^{-1}=S$; thus $C_G(S)\le N_G(S)$.
The [[D-NK7G7|center]] $Z(G)=C_G(G)$ is normal in $G$, and if $G/Z(G)$ is cyclic then $G$ is abelian.

## Cosets

Two left cosets of a subgroup $H\le G$ are equal or disjoint, so the left cosets partition $G$.
Their number is the [[D-VJGH5|index]] $[G:H]$, and for finite $G$ the partition gives $\size G=[G:H]\,\size H$, which is Lagrange's theorem.
For subgroups $K\le H\le G$ of finite index, $[G:K]=[G:H]\,[H:K]$.
