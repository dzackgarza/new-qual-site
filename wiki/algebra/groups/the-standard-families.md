# The standard families

## Nonabelian models and $p$-groups

Induction on $\size{G}$ through the centre is the standard argument for a finite $p$-group, and the fixed-point congruence is its counting form.

## Cyclic groups

A cyclic group of order $n$ is $\ZZ/n\ZZ$, and its subgroups, quotients and automorphisms are read off the divisors of $n$.
