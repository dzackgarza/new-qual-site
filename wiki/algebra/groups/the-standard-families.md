# The standard families

## Nonabelian models and $p$-groups

$D_4$ and $Q_8$ are the two nonabelian groups of order $8$, and the number of elements of order $2$ separates them.
A finite $p$-group has a nontrivial center, so induction on $\size{G}$ through the center is the standard argument for the class, and the fixed-point congruence is its counting form.

## Cyclic groups

A cyclic group of order $n$ is $\ZZ/n\ZZ$, and it is the group of that order with exactly one subgroup of each order dividing $n$; its subgroups and quotients are cyclic, and its automorphisms are the units of $\ZZ/n\ZZ$.

## Symmetric and alternating groups

$S_n$ is generated by transpositions and $A_n$ by $3$-cycles, and conjugation in $S_n$ preserves the cycle type, so the conjugacy classes of $S_n$ are indexed by the partitions of $n$.
