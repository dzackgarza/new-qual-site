# Representations

Here a **representation** of a finite group $G$ over a field $k$ means a
finite-dimensional $k$-vector space $V$ with a homomorphism
$\rho:G\to\GL_k(V)$; equivalently, a finite-dimensional left $k[G]$-module.
The [[D-FHUV5|group ring]] supplies the algebra, and [[D-CYAJI|semisimple
modules]] give the decomposition language used by Maschke.
