# Finite fields

For each prime power $q=p^n$ there is a field $\FF_q$ with $q$ elements, unique up to isomorphism, and $\FF_{p^n}/\FF_p$ is Galois with cyclic Galois group generated by the Frobenius automorphism $x\mapsto x^p$.

A finite field of characteristic $p$ has prime subfield $\FF_p$.
If $f\in\FF_p[x]$ is irreducible of degree $n$, then $\FF_p[x]/\gens f$ is a field with $p^n$ elements, and every field with $p^n$ elements is a splitting field of $x^{p^n}-x$ over $\FF_p$.

The subfields of $\FF_{p^n}$ are the fields $\FF_{p^m}$ with $m\divides n$, one for each such $m$, and $x^{p^n}-x$ is the product of the monic irreducible polynomials over $\FF_p$ of degree dividing $n$.
Hence a monic irreducible $f\in\FF_p[x]$ of degree $d$ divides $x^{p^n}-x$ if and only if $d\divides n$, if and only if $f$ splits over $\FF_{p^n}$.

::: {.remark title="Structure of finite fields"}
\envlist

- $\FF_{p^n}$ exists and is unique up to isomorphism, as the splitting field of $x^{p^n} - x$ over $\FF_p$.

- $\FF_{p^m}$ embeds in $\FF_{p^n}$ if and only if $m \divides n$, so the lattice of subfields of $\FF_{p^n}$ is the lattice of divisors of $n$.

- $\FF_{p^n}/\FF_p$ is Galois with cyclic Galois group of order $n$, generated by $x\mapsto x^p$.

- The multiplicative group $\FF_{q}^\times$ is cyclic of order $q-1$.

Hence every extension $\FF_{p^n}/\FF_{p^m}$ is Galois with cyclic Galois group of order $n/m$, generated by $x\mapsto x^{p^m}$.
:::
