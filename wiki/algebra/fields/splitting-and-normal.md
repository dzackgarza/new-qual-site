# Splitting fields and normal extensions

Normal extensions are the ones whose definition can be read off a single polynomial, and the price of that reading is that normality is not transitive through a tower.

## The normal closure

[[P-0T149]]

[[D-99IUP]]

::: {.example title="The normal closure of $\QQ(2^{1/4})/\QQ$"}
The normal closure of $\QQ(2^{1/4})$ in $\CC$ is $\QQ(2^{1/4}, i)$, the splitting field of $x^4-2$: the other roots are $\pm i\,2^{1/4}$, and $\QQ(2^{1/4},i)$ is generated by the real root and $i$.
:::

## Normality is not transitive

::: {.example title="Normality is not transitive"}
In the tower $\QQ\subseteq\QQ(\sqrt 2)\subseteq\QQ(2^{1/4})$ both steps have degree $2$ and are therefore normal, and $\QQ(2^{1/4})/\QQ$ is not: $x^4-2$ is irreducible over $\QQ$ by Eisenstein at $2$, has the root $2^{1/4}$ in $\QQ(2^{1/4})\subseteq\RR$, and its other roots $\pm i\,2^{1/4}$ are not real.
:::
