## Degrees and simple extensions

The degree is a dimension, the tower law makes it multiplicative, and $K[\alpha]=K(\alpha)$ exactly when $\alpha$ is algebraic over $K$, with degree the degree of the minimal polynomial.

## Splitting fields and normality

A finite extension is normal exactly when it is a splitting field, and the normal closure of a simple extension is the splitting field of its minimal polynomial.

## Separability

Separability is decided on the derivative, holds automatically over a perfect field, and fails for $x^p-t$ over $\FF_p(t)$.

## Finite fields

The field of $p^n$ elements exists and is unique, its subfields are the $\FF_{p^d}$ with $d\divides n$, and its Galois group is cyclic and generated by Frobenius.
