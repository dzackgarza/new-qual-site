# Modules over a PID

## Simple, indecomposable, and cyclic

::: {.fact title="Simple implies indecomposable, and not conversely"}
A simple module is generated by each of its nonzero elements, hence cyclic, and it has no proper nonzero direct summand, hence is indecomposable.
The two notions differ over $\ZZ$: the group $\ZZ$ is indecomposable and not simple.
:::
