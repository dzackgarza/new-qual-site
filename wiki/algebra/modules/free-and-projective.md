# Free and projective modules

## Free modules, rank, and torsion

A free module is one with a basis, its rank is the cardinality of that basis and is well defined over a commutative ring, and a submodule of a free module over an integral domain can be torsion-free without being free — the nonprincipal ideals are the standard examples.

## Projective modules

A projective module is a direct summand of a free one, which is the criterion that every short exact sequence ending at it splits.
