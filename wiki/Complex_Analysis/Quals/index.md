# Quals

Past complex-analysis qualifying exams, mostly by topic.
[[Complex_Analysis/Quals/Preface|Preface]] records thanks and the partial-derivative notation used with Tie’s problems.

The 8155-style sheets run through the syllabus: real variables and continuity, Cauchy and Liouville, Laurent and singularities, residues, maps of the disc, Schwarz, Rouché, Riemann mapping.
Later numbers are further exams on the same topics (computing integrals, maximum modulus, polynomials, Morera, conformal maps).
[[Questions from Tie|Extra Questions from Jingzhi Tie]] and [[Unsorted|Unsorted]] are overflow.
[[Complex_Analysis/Quals/2021_Fall|Fall 2021]] is that exam kept together.
