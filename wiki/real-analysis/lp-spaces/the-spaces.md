## Density and duality arguments

For $1\leq p<\infty$, with $\mu$ $\sigma$-finite when $p=1$, every continuous linear functional on $L^p$ is $g\mapsto\int fg$ for one $g\in L^q$ ([[T-5BFVS]]), so two continuous maps on $L^p$ that agree on a dense subset agree on $L^p$, and a function $f\in L^p$ with $\int fg=0$ on a dense subset of $L^q$ has $f=0$ almost everywhere.
