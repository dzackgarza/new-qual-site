# Real analysis topics

- Foundations: metric spaces, compactness, and completeness; see [[real-analysis/undergraduate/sets-and-compactness|Sets and compactness]].

- Measure: $\sigma$-algebras, outer measure, Lebesgue measure, measurable functions, and convergence in measure; see [[real-analysis/measure/index|Measure]].

- Integration: the convergence theorems, $L^1$, and differentiation; see [[real-analysis/integration/index|Integration]].

- Product measures: the Fubini and Tonelli theorems, and iterated integration; see [[real-analysis/fubini-tonelli/index|Fubini and Tonelli]].

- $L^p$ spaces: Hölder's and Minkowski's inequalities, duality, and interpolation; see [[real-analysis/lp-spaces/index|$L^p$ spaces]].

- Fourier analysis: Fourier series and the Fourier transform on $\RR^n$; see [[real-analysis/fourier/index|Fourier]].

- Functional analysis: Banach and Hilbert spaces, the Hahn--Banach and open mapping theorems, and compact operators; see [[real-analysis/functional-analysis/index|Functional analysis]].
