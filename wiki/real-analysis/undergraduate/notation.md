# Notation

| --- | --- |

| $f(x) \converges{\abs x \to \infty}\to 0$ | $f$ vanishes at infinity |
| $\displaystyle\int_{\abs{x} \geq N} \abs f \convergesto{N\to \infty} 0$ | $f$ has small tails |
| $\mathcal{H}$ | A Hilbert space |
| $X$ | A topological or metric space |
| $\del_x f = \dd{}{x}f = \dd{f}{x}$ | The partial derivative of $f$ with respect to $x$ |
