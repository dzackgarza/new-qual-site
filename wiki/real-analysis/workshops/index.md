# Workshops

The [[SRC-RA-WORKSHOP|UNL real analysis workshop]] collects its problems by day, and Days 1, 9, and 10 are qualifying exam collections.
The two week-long sessions review the material before the measure-theory and integration problems.

- [[real-analysis/workshops/real-week-1-preliminaries|Week 1: preliminaries]] -- continuity and uniform continuity, series and uniform convergence, compactness, and the Baire category theorem.

- [[real-analysis/workshops/real-week-2-measure-theory|Week 2: measure theory and Fubini--Tonelli]] -- measures, outer measure, measurable functions, and the Fubini--Tonelli theorems.
