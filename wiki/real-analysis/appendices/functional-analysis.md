# Appendix: Functional analysis

The [[T-KQTPR|open mapping]], [[T-FO27T|bounded inverse]], and [[T-TTLXS|closed graph]] theorems concern a single linear operator between Banach spaces, and each is proved from the [[FT-6WPJI|Baire category theorem]].
The [[FT-JRRRW|uniform boundedness principle]] concerns a family of operators and is also proved from the [[FT-6WPJI|Baire category theorem]].
The [[T-OW2QG|Hahn--Banach theorem]] concerns extension of linear functionals and has no completeness hypothesis.
