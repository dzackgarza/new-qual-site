# Inequalities

Let $(X,\mathcal M,\mu)$ be a measure space, and let $p,q\in[1,\infty]$ be conjugate exponents, $\frac1p+\frac1q=1$, unless stated otherwise.
Each entry names the quantity its inequality bounds and links its statement; all but the Borel--Cantelli lemma are stated on [[real-analysis/appendices/appendix-inequalities|Common inequalities]].

## Between integrals

- An integral of a product, by the $L^p$ and $L^q$ norms: [[PR-7BGSE|Hölder's inequality]].

- The $L^p$ norm of a sum, by the two norms: [[PR-XUVZY|Minkowski's inequality]].

- A convex function of an average, under a probability measure: [[PR-W4ICW|Jensen's inequality]].

- The $L^r$ norm of a convolution, by the $L^p$ and $L^q$ norms: [[PR-2C3SZ|Young's convolution inequality]].

## Between a norm and a measure

- The measure of a level set, by the $L^p$ norm: [[PR-YO7MV|the Markov and Chebyshev inequalities]].

- The measure of the set where infinitely many events occur: [[T-OTR5M|the Borel--Cantelli lemma]].

## Elementary inequalities

- A product of two nonnegative numbers, by a sum of $p$- and $q$-powers: [[PR-BPOH2|Young's product inequality]].

- The $p$th power of a sum, by the two $p$th powers: [[PR-7KTA6|the convexity bound $(a+b)^p\leq2^{p-1}(a^p+b^p)$]].

- A difference of norms, by the norm of the difference: [[PR-IJOPU|the reverse triangle inequality]].

- A geometric mean, by an arithmetic mean: [[PR-IQYTA|the AM--GM inequality]].

- An inner product, by the product of the norms: [[PR-X5D4Z|the Cauchy--Schwarz inequality]].

- $(1+x)^n$, by $1+nx$: [[FF-Z3E3C|Bernoulli's inequality]].

- $1+x$, by $e^x$: [[PR-CHY3F|the exponential inequality]].
