# Fibre products and base change

For closed subschemes $Y,Z\subseteq X$, $Y\times_XZ$ is the [[D-SCHFIBRES|scheme-theoretic intersection]] ([[PR-SCHINT]]).

[[D-SCHFPR]]

## Fibres

[[PR-SCHFIB]]

::: {.example title="The fibre of $\Spec\ZZ[i]\to\Spec\ZZ$ over $(3)$"}
For $\Spec\ZZ[i]\to\Spec\ZZ$, the fibre over $(3)$ is $\Spec\FF_9$: the fibre ring is $\FF_3[x]/(x^2+1)$, and $x^2+1$ is irreducible over $\FF_3$, so the fibre is one point whose residue field has degree $2$ over $\FF_3$ ([[PR-SCHFIB]]).
:::

## Base change

[[D-SCHBC]]

A scheme over the fraction field $K$ of a ring $R$ spreads out over $R$ when it is the base change of an $R$-scheme along $\Spec K\to\Spec R$ ([[D-SCHBC]]).
::: {.example title="Integrality is not stable under base change"}
$\Spec\CC$ is an integral $\RR$-scheme, and its base change $\Spec(\CC\otimes_\RR\CC)\cong\Spec\CC\sqcup\Spec\CC$ is not integral ([[D-SCHBC]]).
:::
