# Subschemes and points

A subscheme of $X$ is a locally closed subscheme, a closed subscheme of an open subscheme: an open subscheme is an open subset with the restricted structure sheaf, and a closed subscheme is a closed subset together with a sheaf of ideals ([[D-SCHSUB]]).
Two closed subschemes can share a closed set and differ in their functions: the reduced structure on a closed subset, and the multiple of it that carries nilpotents, are different schemes with the same underlying space ([[D-SCHSUB]], [[D-SCHRED]]).

[[D-SCHSUB]]

[[D-SCHRED]]

A closed subscheme $Y\subseteq X$ is determined by its ideal sheaf $\mci_Y\subseteq\OO_X$, and every quasicoherent ideal sheaf on $X$ arises this way ([[D-SCHSUB]]).

## Fibres and images

[[D-SCHIMG]]

[[PR-SCHINT]]

The scheme-theoretic image of a morphism $f\colon X\to Y$ is cut out by the kernel of $\OO_Y\to f_*\OO_X$ when that kernel is quasicoherent, for instance when $f$ is quasicompact and quasiseparated, so it records the equations that vanish on the image rather than the set of image points; when $X$ is reduced it is the closure of the set-theoretic image with its reduced induced structure ([[D-SCHIMG]]).
