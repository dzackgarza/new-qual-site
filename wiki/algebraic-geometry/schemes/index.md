# Schemes

$\Spec$ and $\Proj$, gluing, fibre products, and the properties — reduced, irreducible, integral, Noetherian — read off the ring.

A scheme $X$ is affine exactly when the canonical morphism $X\to\Spec\OO_X(X)$ is an isomorphism.

## Syllabus

At the level of [@Har10a, Sections II.2–II.3].

- [[algebraic-geometry/schemes/ringed-spaces|Ringed spaces]], ringed and locally ringed spaces, their morphisms, and why a morphism of schemes is a morphism of locally ringed spaces.

- [[algebraic-geometry/schemes/what-is-a-scheme|What is a scheme]], $\Spec A$ and its structure sheaf, the definition with the reason for each clause, and the criterion $X\cong\Spec\OO_X(X)$ for affineness.

- [[algebraic-geometry/schemes/properties-from-the-ring|Properties from the ring]], reduced, irreducible, integral, and noetherian schemes in terms of rings.

- [[algebraic-geometry/schemes/dimension|Dimension]], the dimension of a scheme, Krull dimension of its local rings, and codimension.

- [[algebraic-geometry/schemes/fibre-products-and-base-change|Fibre products and base change]], existence of fibre products, intersections, fibres, base change to field extensions, and the functor of points.
