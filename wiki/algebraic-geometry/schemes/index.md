# Schemes

$\Spec$ and $\Proj$, gluing, fibre products, and the properties — reduced, irreducible, integral, Noetherian — read off the ring.

The definition of a scheme is the starting point for everything on this page: the next questions are how you recognise an affine one, whether the Noetherian hypothesis in Serre's criterion can be weakened, and what goes wrong without quasicompactness.

- [[algebraic-geometry/schemes/what-is-a-scheme|What is a scheme]], the definition with the reason for each clause, and the test for affineness that needs no cohomology.

- [[algebraic-geometry/schemes/properties-from-the-ring|Properties from the ring]], the dictionary of reduced, irreducible, integral and Noetherian, and what generic points are for.

- [[algebraic-geometry/schemes/proj-and-gluing|Proj and gluing]], how non-affine schemes are built, and the graded ring that builds projective space in one step.

- [[algebraic-geometry/schemes/fibre-products-and-base-change|Fibre products and base change]], the construction behind intersections, fibres and field extensions, and the functor of points that explains it.

- [[algebraic-geometry/schemes/subschemes-and-points|Subschemes and points]], the many structures on one closed set, and what a function on a scheme evaluates to.
