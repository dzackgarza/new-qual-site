# Schemes

$\Spec$ and $\Proj$, gluing, fibre products, and the properties — reduced, irreducible, integral, Noetherian — read off the ring.

A scheme $X$ is affine exactly when the canonical morphism $X\to\Spec\OO_X(X)$ is an isomorphism.

- [[algebraic-geometry/schemes/what-is-a-scheme|What is a scheme]], the definition with the reason for each clause, and the criterion $X\cong\Spec\OO_X(X)$ for affineness.

- [[algebraic-geometry/schemes/properties-from-the-ring|Properties from the ring]], reduced, irreducible, integral, and noetherian schemes in terms of rings, and generic points.

- [[algebraic-geometry/schemes/proj-and-gluing|Proj and gluing]], gluing affine schemes along open subschemes, and $\Proj$ of a graded ring.

- [[algebraic-geometry/schemes/fibre-products-and-base-change|Fibre products and base change]], intersections, fibres, and base change to field extensions as fibre products, and the functor of points.

- [[algebraic-geometry/schemes/subschemes-and-points|Subschemes and points]], the closed subscheme structures on one closed set, and values of functions in residue fields.
