## Support

[[D-UDIVH]]

The support of a sheaf need not be closed: for an open immersion $j\colon U\hookrightarrow X$, extension by zero gives $\supp(j_!\ZZ_U)=U$.
For a coherent sheaf on a noetherian scheme, the support is closed.
