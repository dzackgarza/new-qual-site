# Weil and Cartier

[[D-5PQ5W]]

[[PR-DIVZEROPOLE]]

A Weil divisor is a formal integer combination of prime divisors; a Cartier divisor is given by local equations $f_i\in K^\times$ on an open cover $\{U_i\}$ with $f_i/f_j\in\OO^\times(U_i\cap U_j)$.
If every local ring is a unique factorization domain, every prime divisor has a local equation, and the two groups agree.
On the affine quadric cone $xy=z^2$, the line $x=z=0$ is a prime divisor with no local equation at the vertex; there $\Cl\cong\ZZ/2$, generated by that line, while every Cartier divisor is principal.
On a smooth curve every local ring is a discrete valuation ring, so codimension-one subvarieties and local equations are related by the valuation at each point.

## Computing a Picard group

[[FE-ADOKK]]

For an integral projective curve $X$ over an algebraically closed field with normalization $\tilde X$, the comparison with $\tilde X$ is the exact sequence
\[
0\to\bigoplus_{p\in X}\tilde\OO_p^\times/\OO_{X,p}^\times\to\Pic(X)\to\Pic(\tilde X)\to0 ,
\]
where $\tilde\OO_p$ is the integral closure of $\OO_{X,p}$.
For an open subset $U = X \sm Z$ of a smooth variety, the comparison is the excision sequence
\[
\ZZ^{\theset{\text{components of } Z \text{ of codimension } 1}} \to \Cl(X) \to \Cl(U) \to 0 .
\]
It gives $\Cl(\AA^n)=0$ from $\Cl(\PP^n)=\ZZ$, and $\Cl(\PP^n\setminus V(f))\cong\ZZ/d$ for an irreducible form $f$ of degree $d$.
