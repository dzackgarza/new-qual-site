# The class group and the Picard group

For an integral scheme $X$ with rational-function sheaf $\mck$, the Cartier divisors modulo principal divisors are the invertible sheaves modulo isomorphism:
$$
\CaCl(X)\iso\Pic(X),\qquad [D]\longmapsto[\OO_X(D)]
$$
[@Har77, Proposition II.6.15].
Let $X$ be noetherian, integral and separated. If $X$ is regular in codimension one, the Weil divisors modulo principal divisors form the group $\Cl(X)$; if $X$ is in addition normal, a Cartier divisor is a locally principal Weil divisor, so $\CaCl(X)\into\Cl(X)$ [@Har77, Remark II.6.11.2]; the inclusion is an isomorphism, hence $\Cl(X)\cong\Pic(X)$, when every local ring $\OO_{X,x}$ is a unique factorization domain [@Har77, Proposition II.6.11 and Corollary II.6.16], and in particular for a regular $X$ [@Har77, Remark II.6.11.1A].
On a scheme that is not locally factorial the inclusion is strict, and [[D-5PQ5W]] records both groups on the affine quadric cone.

## The Picard group

[[D-MODPIC]]

$\Pic$ is a contravariant functor to abelian groups: a morphism $f\colon X\to Y$ of ringed spaces pulls an invertible $\OO_Y$-module back to an invertible $\OO_X$-module ([[D-MODPULL]]).

## Cartier divisors and invertible sheaves

[[PR-DIVLB]]

## The divisor of a rational section

Let $D$ be a Cartier divisor on a noetherian integral separated scheme $X$ that is regular in codimension one, with rational-function sheaf $\mck$. The invertible sheaf $\OO_X(D)\subseteq\mck$ has global sections
$$
\Gamma(X,\OO_X(D))=\theset{f\in\mck(X)^\times:\operatorname{div}(f)+D\ge0}\cup\theset{0}
$$
([[D-DIVOD]], [@Har77, §II.6]).
For a nonsingular projective variety $X$ over an algebraically closed field, the nonzero global sections of $\OO_X(D)$, modulo multiplication by $k^\times$, are the effective divisors linearly equivalent to $D$, so the complete linear system $\linearsystem{D}$ is the projective space of $\Gamma(X,\OO_X(D))$ [@Har77, Proposition II.7.7].
An effective divisor is thus a nonzero section of an invertible sheaf, and a linear system is a linear subspace of sections ([[algebraic-geometry/divisors/line-bundles-and-linear-systems|line bundles and linear systems]]).

## Projective space and open complements

[[FE-DIVPN]]

[[D-CB9XS]]

For a hypersurface $D=V(f)$ of degree $d$ in $\PP^n_k$, the invertible sheaf $\OO_{\PP^n}(D)$ is $\OO_{\PP^n}(d)$, and $\OO_{\PP^n}(1)$ generates $\Pic(\PP^n_k)\cong\ZZ$ [@Har77, Corollary II.6.17].

Let $X$ be a noetherian integral separated scheme regular in codimension one and let $Z\subsetneq X$ be closed.
Restriction of divisors to $U=X\sm Z$ is surjective, with kernel generated by the prime divisors in $Z$; it is an isomorphism when $\operatorname{codim}(Z,X)\ge2$, and when $Z$ is irreducible of codimension one there is an exact sequence $\ZZ\to\Cl(X)\to\Cl(U)\to0$ carrying $1$ to the class of $Z$ [@Har77, Proposition II.6.5].

## Singular curves

[[FE-ADOKK]]

$\Pic^0$ of the nodal cubic is $\GG_m$ and $\Pic^0$ of the cuspidal cubic is $\GG_a$, so neither is an abelian variety; for a smooth projective curve $C$, $\Pic^0(C)$ is the Jacobian of $C$ ([[FE-ADOKK]]).

## Line bundles as torsors

[[D-TORSOR]]

[[PR-ET5PQ]]

A line bundle is locally free of rank one, so its isomorphism class is a class in $H^1(X,\OO_X^\times)$ ([[D-MODPIC]]), and for an integral $X$ the map $[D]\mapsto[\OO_X(D)]$ identifies $\CaCl(X)$ with $\Pic(X)$ ([[PR-DIVLB]], [@Har77, Proposition II.6.15]).
The exponential sequence
$$
0\to\ZZ\to\OO_X\to\OO_X^\times\to0
$$
of a complex manifold then computes $\Pic(X)$ from $H^1(X,\ZZ)$ and $H^1(X,\OO_X)$ ([[PR-ET5PQ]]).
