# Divisors

Weil divisors against Cartier divisors, class groups, and the Picard group as
something you compute rather than define.

The standard exchange: describe both kinds of divisor on a curve, say when they
agree, then produce the Weil divisor attached to $f \in K^*$ under the canonical
isomorphism. After that comes a computation — $\Pic$ of a nodal or cuspidal
curve, or whether a variety can have $\Pic = \ZZ/3$.
