# Divisors

Weil divisors against Cartier divisors, class groups, and the Picard group as something you compute rather than define.

The standard exchange: describe both kinds of divisor on a curve, say when they agree, then produce the Weil divisor attached to $f \in K^*$ under the canonical isomorphism.
After that comes a computation — $\Pic$ of a nodal or cuspidal curve, or whether a variety can have $\Pic = \ZZ/3$.

- [[algebraic-geometry/divisors/weil-and-cartier|Weil and Cartier]], the two definitions, the degree map, and the two comparison sequences that compute a class group.
- [[algebraic-geometry/divisors/line-bundles-and-linear-systems|Line Bundles and Linear Systems]], the sheaf $\OO(D)$, complete linear systems, base loci, and the map to projective space.
- [[algebraic-geometry/divisors/positivity|Positivity]], ample against very ample, Serre's criterion, and the three equivalence relations.
