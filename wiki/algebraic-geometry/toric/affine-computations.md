# Affine toric computations

For a cone $\sigma\subseteq N_\RR$, $U_\sigma=\Spec k[S_\sigma]$ with $S_\sigma=\sigma^\vee\cap M$.
A Hilbert basis $m_1,\ldots,m_r$ of $S_\sigma$ gives a closed embedding $U_\sigma\hookrightarrow\AA^r$, and the ideal of the image is generated by the binomials $x^a-x^b$ for the relations $\sum a_im_i=\sum b_im_i$.
