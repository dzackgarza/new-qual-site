# Topology

The torus-orbits of $X_\Sigma$ are indexed by the cones of $\Sigma$ ([[PR-O8V3I]]), and the fundamental group, the Euler characteristic, and the Betti numbers of $X_\Sigma$ are computed from the fan ([[PR-TORMOR]], [[T-FULBETTI]]).

## The orbit decomposition

[[PR-O8V3I]]

The orbit of a cone $\sigma$ is the torus of dimension $n-\dim\sigma$ obtained by forgetting the coordinates in $\sigma$, and its closure is the toric variety of the star of $\sigma$ ([[D-FULSTAR]]).
So $X_\Sigma$ is the disjoint union of its orbits, and the closure of an orbit is the union of the orbits of all cones containing it.

## The fundamental group

[[PR-TORMOR]]

Over $\CC$, with the classical topology, let $i\colon T_N\hookrightarrow X_\Sigma$ be the inclusion of the torus, and take the basepoint $1\in T_N$ together with its image $i(1)\in X_\Sigma(\CC)$.
The induced map on fundamental groups
$$
i_*\colon \pi_1(T_N,1)\longrightarrow\pi_1\bigl(X_\Sigma(\CC),1\bigr)
$$
is surjective with kernel $N_\Sigma$ (Cox--Little--Schenck, *Toric Varieties*, Theorem 12.1.5 for the surjectivity and Propositions 12.1.8 and 12.1.9 for the kernel), so
$$
\pi_1\bigl(X_\Sigma(\CC),1\bigr)\cong N/N_\Sigma ,
$$
where $N$ is the cocharacter lattice, identified with $\pi_1(T_N,1)$ by $u\mapsto[t\mapsto\lambda^u(e^{2\pi it})]$ through the one-parameter subgroup $\lambda^u$ of $u\in N$, and $N_\Sigma$ is the sublattice generated by $\abs{\Sigma}\cap N$ (Cox--Little--Schenck, *Toric Varieties*, Theorem 12.1.10).
The direction is that of $i_*$, from the torus to $X_\Sigma$: the fan is arbitrary here, and $X_\Sigma(\CC)$ is simply connected exactly when $N_\Sigma=N$ ([[PR-TORMOR]]).
For a complete fan the support is $N_\RR$, so $N_\Sigma=N$ and $X_\Sigma(\CC)$ is simply connected; in particular $\PP^n$ is, while the torus $T$ itself is not.
The rays alone do not decide this: the affine quadric cone $\CC^2/\{\pm1\}$ has two rays generating an index-two sublattice of $N$, and its fundamental group is trivial because the lattice points of its cone generate $N$.
Completeness is the other lattice condition on the fan, with the valuative reading that every one-parameter subgroup has a limit at $0$ exactly when the fan covers $N_\RR$ ([[PR-TORMOR]]).

## Euler characteristic

For an $n$-dimensional toric variety over $\CC$ the topological Euler characteristic is the number of $n$-dimensional cones, because an orbit of positive dimension is a torus and contributes zero, while a fixed point contributes one ([[T-FULBETTI]], [[PR-TORMOR]]).
For $\PP^n$ that is $n+1$, for $\PP^1\times\PP^1$ it is $4$, and for the Hirzebruch surface $\FF_a$ it is $4$ for every $a$, so the Euler characteristic does not separate the surfaces $\FF_a$ ([[FE-TORHIRZ]]).

## Betti numbers

[[T-FULBETTI]]

The odd Betti numbers of a smooth complete toric variety vanish and the even ones are determined by the numbers of cones of each dimension, so the Poincaré polynomial is a fan measurement ([[T-FULBETTI]]).

[[T-TORCOHOM]]

For a smooth complete fan the cohomology ring is the Stanley--Reisner ring of the fan: the polynomial ring on one generator per ray, modulo the Stanley--Reisner ideal generated by the monomials of rays that span no cone, and modulo the linear relations of the rays ([[T-TORCOHOM]]).
The Betti number formula is the Hilbert series of that presentation, and in the smooth case the Chow ring and the cohomology ring coincide ([[T-TORCOHOM]]).

## The moment map

[[D-TORMOMENT]]

Over $\CC$ the projective toric variety $X_P$ of a lattice polytope $P$ carries the action of the compact torus $T_c\cong(S^1)^n$, and the moment map $\mu\colon X_P\to P$ has image $P$ ([[D-TORMOMENT]]).
The map $\mu$ is not a torus bundle over $P$: the preimage of the relative interior of a face $F$ of dimension $k$ is the orbit attached to $F$, which has complex dimension $k$ ([[D-TORMOMENT]]), because the cone $\sigma_F$ of the normal fan has dimension $n-k$ ([[D-TORPOLY]]) and an orbit of dimension $n-\dim\sigma$ corresponds to a cone $\sigma$ ([[PR-O8V3I]]); the fibre over a point of that interior is the compact torus $(S^1)^k$ of real dimension $k$ ([[D-TORMOMENT]], [@Ful93, §4.2]). So $X_P$ is a union of torus bundles over the relative interiors of the faces, and the orbit stratification of $X_P$ is the face stratification of $P$.

The Euler characteristic and the Betti numbers are blind to the lattice embedding except through the cone counts, so $\FF_0$ and $\FF_1$ have the same Betti numbers and are not isomorphic; the finer invariant is the intersection form, which sees $a$ ([[FE-TORHIRZ]]).
