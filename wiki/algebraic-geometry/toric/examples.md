# Examples

A fan $\Sigma$ gives a normal toric variety $X_\Sigma$ ([[D-Q7Q2N]]), and the dictionary is read off the fans of a few examples.

## The torus and affine space

[[D-Q7Q2N]]

The fan $\theset{0}$ is the torus $T=(\CC^\times)^n$, and a fan with a single maximal cone $\sigma$ is the affine variety $\Spec\CC[\dualof{\sigma}\cap M]$.
For $\sigma=\Cone(e_1,\ldots,e_n)$ the dual cone is the positive orthant, so the semigroup is generated by the coordinate functions and $X_\sigma=\CC^n$; the cones spanned by all but one of $e_1,\ldots,e_n,-e_1-\cdots-e_n$ give $\PP^n$.

## Projective space

[[FE-TORP2]]

The rays $e_1,e_2,-e_1-e_2$ form a complete fan, and the duals of the three maximal cones give the charts with coordinates $(x,y)$, $(x^{-1},x^{-1}y)$ and $(y^{-1},xy^{-1})$.
Each consecutive pair has determinant $\pm1$ and the support is $N_\RR$: the two conditions are the smoothness and completeness criteria for a fan ([[PR-FULCRIT]]).

## Hirzebruch surfaces

[[FE-TORHIRZ]]

For $a\ge0$ the rays $(1,0)$, $(0,1)$, $(-1,a)$, $(0,-1)$ give the Hirzebruch surface $\FF_a$, which is smooth and complete for every $a$ by the same two criteria ([[PR-FULCRIT]]).
The relations among the ray generators give $D_2^2=-a$ and $D_4^2=a$, so $a=0$ gives $\PP^1\times\PP^1$ and $a=1$ gives $\PP^2$ blown up at a point ([[FE-TORBLOW]]).
Every $a$ has four rays and four maximal cones, so the combinatorics of the fan does not determine the surface: the lattice embedding does, and $\FF_a\cong\FF_b$ exactly when $a=b$ ([[FE-TORHIRZ]], [[FE-FULHIRZBUN]]).

## A cone of determinant seven

[[FE-TORDUAL]]

The cone $\sigma=\Cone((2,1),(1,4))$ has determinant $2\cdot4-1\cdot1=7$, so $X_\sigma$ is singular ([[PR-FULCRIT]]); its minimal resolution is a star subdivision by rays at lattice distance one ([[FE-TORMINRES]]).

## The cuspidal cubic

The cuspidal cubic $V(x^3-y^2)$ is the spectrum of the semigroup algebra of $\generators{2,3}\subseteq\NN$, and that semigroup is not saturated: its saturation is the cone $\Cone(1)$, and the normalization is the inclusion $\AA^1\to V(x^3-y^2)$ of semigroup algebras ([[D-FULSAT]]).
It is therefore not of the form $X_\Sigma$, whose coordinate rings are spectra of saturated semigroup algebras.

## Weighted projective space

[[FE-TORWPS]]

Weighted projective space $\PP(q_0,\ldots,q_n)$ is the quotient lattice construction: $N=\ZZ^{n+1}/\ZZ(q_0,\ldots,q_n)$, and the rays are the images of the standard basis vectors, so the weighted scaling relation is part of the lattice rather than part of the grading.
Its singularities are cyclic quotient singularities at the points where a ray is not primitive ([[FE-FULQUOT]]), and $\PP(1,\ldots,1)$ is $\PP^n$.

## Products and blowups

[[FE-TORBLOW]]

A product of toric varieties has the product fan in $N_1\oplus N_2$, which is why $\PP^1\times\PP^1$ is the fan with four rays; a blowup at a torus-fixed point is a star subdivision of the fan at that cone's ray, and the exceptional divisor is the orbit closure of the new ray ([[FE-TORBLOW]]).
Every smooth complete toric surface is $\PP^2$, a Hirzebruch surface $\FF_a$, or a blowup of one of these at torus-fixed points ([[T-TORSURF]]).
Toric morphisms are maps of fans ([[PR-TORMOR]], [[algebraic-geometry/toric/surfaces-and-morphisms|toric surfaces and toric morphisms]]).
