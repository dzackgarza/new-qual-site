# Toric varieties

Fans, the orbit-cone correspondence, and toric resolution of singularities.

## Syllabus

At the level of [@Ful93, Chapters 1–4].

- [[algebraic-geometry/toric/the-dictionary|The fan dictionary]], the lattices $M$ and $N$, strongly convex rational polyhedral cones and their duals, fans, the affine toric variety $\Spec\CC[\sigma^\vee\cap M]$ of a semigroup algebra, gluing along faces, the orbit-cone correspondence, and orbit closures.

- [[algebraic-geometry/toric/examples|Examples]], the torus $(\CC^\times)^n$, $\CC^n$, $\PP^n$, the Hirzebruch surfaces $\mathbb{F}_n$, the quadric cone, the cuspidal cubic, weighted projective spaces, and products and blowups of these.

- [[algebraic-geometry/toric/affine-computations|Affine toric computations]], dualising a cone by hand, the equations of $X_\sigma$, the cone over the rational normal curve, and minimal resolution by convex hull.

- [[algebraic-geometry/toric/criteria|Criteria and numerical invariants]], the fan conditions for smooth, simplicial (orbifold), complete, projective, and Fano, the Betti numbers from the cone counts, and the degree as a normalised volume.

- [[algebraic-geometry/toric/polytopes-and-divisors|Polytopes and divisors]], polytopes and their polar duals, the normal fan, $T$-Weil divisors from the rays, Cartier divisors from support functions, $\Cl$ and $\Pic$, the canonical divisor, the polytope $P_D$ and its lattice points as global sections, the ampleness and very ampleness criteria, and the reflexive polytopes behind the toric Fanos.

- [[algebraic-geometry/toric/surfaces-and-morphisms|Toric surfaces and toric morphisms]], toric morphisms from maps of fans, blowups as star subdivisions, resolution of singularities, the classification of smooth complete toric surfaces, intersection numbers, and weighted projective space.

- [[algebraic-geometry/toric/toric-del-pezzo-surfaces|Toric del Pezzo surfaces]], the five smooth toric del Pezzo surfaces as the smooth reflexive polygons, and their blowup relations.

- [[algebraic-geometry/toric/topology|Topology]], the fundamental group as $N$ modulo the sublattice spanned by the fan, the Euler characteristic as the number of maximal cones, and the moment map.
