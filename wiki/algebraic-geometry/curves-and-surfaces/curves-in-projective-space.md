# Curves in projective space

A curve in $\PP^n$ is studied through three numbers and one embedding: its degree, its genus, and the dimension of the linear span, together with the projection that lowers $n$ without changing the curve.

[[PR-CRVDEGBD]]

## Rational normal curves

The $d$-uple embedding of $\PP^1$ is a curve of degree $d$ in $\PP^d$, the rational normal curve of degree $d$, and it is the curve of degree $d$ and genus $0$ that spans $\PP^d$ [@Har77, Exercise IV.3.4] ([[FE-VARVERONESE]]).
Every irreducible curve of degree $d$ spanning $\PP^d$ is rational normal, so degree $d$ and span $\PP^d$ determine the curve up to projective equivalence.

## Curves on a quadric

[[FE-CRVQUAD]]

A divisor of type $(a,b)$ on the quadric $Q\cong\PP^1\times\PP^1\subseteq\PP^3$ has arithmetic genus $ab-a-b+1$, so the curves of low degree on $Q$ are the two rulings $(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$ and the conics $(1,1)$ [@Har77, Exercise III.5.6(c)].
