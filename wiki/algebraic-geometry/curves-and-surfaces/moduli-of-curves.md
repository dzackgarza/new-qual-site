# Moduli of curves

[[D-CRVMOD]]

For genus $1$, [[algebraic-geometry/curves-and-surfaces/elliptic-curves|elliptic curves]] gives the coarse moduli space $\AA^1$ with coordinate $j$, and the isotrivial family $ty^2=x^3-x$ over $\Spec k[t,t^{-1}]$ described there has every geometric fibre isomorphic to $y^2=x^3-x$ without being the constant family ([[D-CRVMOD]]).
