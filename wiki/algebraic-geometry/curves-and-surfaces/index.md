# Curves and surfaces

Genus in its several senses, Riemann--Hurwitz, embeddings, and the
classification of curves of small genus.

More oral questions land here than anywhere else, because a curve is small
enough to compute with and rich enough to test everything above it. Genus is
the pivot: arithmetic against geometric, how they differ for a singular curve,
whether either depends on the embedding, and how Riemann--Hurwitz computes one
from a map.
