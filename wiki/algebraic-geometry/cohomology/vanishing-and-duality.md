# Vanishing and duality

Serre's criterion reads affineness from the vanishing of coherent cohomology, and on a smooth projective variety the dualizing sheaf is the canonical sheaf, so Serre duality pairs $H^i(X,\mcl)$ with $H^{n-i}(X,\omega_X\otimes\dualof{\mcl})$ ([[T-5IOUR]], [[T-COHSD]]).

## Serre's criterion

[[T-5IOUR]]

A noetherian scheme $X$ is affine exactly when $H^1(X,\mci)=0$ for every coherent ideal sheaf $\mci\subseteq\OO_X$, and exactly when $H^p(X,\mcf)=0$ for every quasicoherent $\mcf$ and every $p>0$ ([[T-5IOUR]]).
For a quasicompact quasiseparated scheme the quasicoherent form of the criterion holds with quasicoherent ideals in place of coherent ones, and quasicompactness cannot be omitted ([[T-5IOUR]]).

## Serre duality

[[T-COHSD]]

Let $X$ be smooth and proper of dimension $n$ over a field $k$, with canonical sheaf $\omega_X=\Omega^n_{X/k}$, and let $\mcl$ be locally free of finite rank.
Then cup product followed by the trace $t_X\colon H^n(X,\omega_X)\to k$ is a perfect pairing
$H^i(X,\mcl)\otimes H^{n-i}(X,\omega_X\otimes\dualof{\mcl})\to k$ ([[T-COHSD]]).
On $X=\PP^n$ the dualizing sheaf is $\omega=\OO(-n-1)$, which is the computation the general theorem is modelled on ([[T-COHSD]]).

## Riemann--Roch for curves and surfaces

[[T-MWDVL]]

[[T-COHRRS]]

Riemann--Roch for a smooth projective curve, $\ell(D)-\ell(K-D)=\deg D+1-g$, is the Euler characteristic $\chi(\OO_X(D))=\deg D+1-g$ together with the Serre duality $H^1(X,\OO_X(D))\cong\dualof{H^0(X,\OO_X(K-D))}$ ([[T-MWDVL]]); taking $D=K$ gives $\deg K=2g-2$ ([[T-MWDVL]]).
On a smooth projective surface the Riemann--Roch formula
$\chi(\OO_X(D))=\chi(\OO_X)+\tfrac12D\cdot(D-K_X)$, together with the vanishing $h^2(\OO_X(D))=h^0(\OO_X(K_X-D))$, gives the effectivity criterion of [[T-COHRRS]].

## Finiteness and vanishing for ample twists

[[T-CARTSERRE]]

[[T-KODVAN]]

For a projective scheme over a noetherian ring and a coherent sheaf $\mcf$, each $H^i(X,\mcf)$ is a finitely generated module, and for $m\gg0$ the twist $\mcf(m)$ is generated by global sections and has no higher cohomology ([[T-CARTSERRE]]).
Kodaira vanishing: for $X$ smooth projective over a field of characteristic zero and $L$ ample, $H^i(X,\omega_X\otimes L)=0$ for $i>0$, equivalently $H^i(X,L^{-1})=0$ for $i<n$ ([[T-KODVAN]]).
The characteristic-zero hypothesis is necessary ([[T-KODVAN]]).

## GAGA and the topology of hyperplane sections

[[T-GAGA]]

[[T-LEFHYP]]

[[T-HARDLEF]]

For a projective scheme $X$ over $\CC$ with associated analytic space $X^{an}$, the natural maps $H^i(X,\mcf)\to H^i(X^{an},\mcf^{an})$ are isomorphisms for every coherent $\mcf$, and $\mcf\mapsto\mcf^{an}$ is an equivalence of categories ([[T-GAGA]]).
The Lefschetz hyperplane theorem and hard Lefschetz relate the cohomology of a smooth projective variety to that of a hyperplane section ([[T-LEFHYP]], [[T-HARDLEF]]).
