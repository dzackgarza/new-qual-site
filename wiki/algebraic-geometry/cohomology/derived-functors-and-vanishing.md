## Affine and Grothendieck vanishing

[[T-COHAFF]]

[[T-COHGROTH]]

Affine vanishing fails for sheaves that are not quasicoherent: on $X=\AA^1_k$ with distinct closed points $P,Q$, $U=X\setminus\{P,Q\}$, and $j\colon U\hookrightarrow X$, the sequence $0\to j_!\ZZ_U\to\ZZ_X\to\ZZ_P\oplus\ZZ_Q\to0$, with skyscraper sheaves $\ZZ_P,\ZZ_Q$, gives $H^1(X,j_!\ZZ_U)\cong\ZZ^2/\ZZ\neq0$, because $\ZZ_X$ is flasque on the irreducible space $X$ [@Har77, Exercise III.2.1].
