# Cohomology

$H^1(X,\OO_X^\times)\cong\Pic X$, and for $\OO_X$-modules $\mcf,\mcg$ with $\mcg$ locally free, $\Ext^1(\mcg,\mcf)\cong H^1(X,\mcf\otimes\mcg^\vee)$ classifies extensions of $\mcg$ by $\mcf$.
By [[algebraic-geometry/cohomology/vanishing-and-duality|Serre's criterion]], a noetherian scheme $X$ is affine if and only if $H^1(X,\mci)=0$ for every coherent ideal sheaf $\mci\subseteq\OO_X$.

## Syllabus

At the level of [@Har10a, Sections III.1–III.8, III.12].

- [[algebraic-geometry/cohomology/homological-algebra|Homological algebra]], abelian categories, chain complexes and their cohomology, injective resolutions, right derived functors, and universal $\delta$-functors.

- [[algebraic-geometry/cohomology/derived-functors-and-vanishing|Derived functors and vanishing]], enough injectives in the category of $\OO_X$-modules, right derived functors of global sections, flasque and fine resolutions, affine vanishing, and Grothendieck vanishing.

- [[algebraic-geometry/cohomology/computing-cohomology|Computing cohomology]], Čech cohomology and its agreement with sheaf cohomology on a noetherian separated scheme, the cohomology of $\OO_{\PP^n}(d)$, and $H^1$ as the obstruction to lifting sections and as $\Pic X$.

- [[algebraic-geometry/cohomology/projective-schemes|Cohomology of projective schemes]], finiteness, Serre vanishing for large twists, and the Euler characteristic and Hilbert polynomial.

- [[algebraic-geometry/cohomology/vanishing-and-duality|Vanishing and duality]], Serre's characterization of affine schemes by vanishing of quasicoherent cohomology, the dualizing sheaf and its agreement with the canonical sheaf on a nonsingular projective variety, and Serre duality.

- [[algebraic-geometry/cohomology/families|Cohomology in families]], higher direct images, formal functions, semicontinuity, and when cohomology commutes with base change.
