# Computing cohomology

Derived functors define the theory; Čech computes it; one calculation on projective space supplies every number.

[[D-PTIW0]]

[[T-IJW1K]]

Memorising the three lines is worth it, but the shape is what to hold: $H^0$ is the polynomials, $H^n$ is dual to the polynomials with a shift by $-n-1$, and everything between vanishes.
The shift is the canonical sheaf $\OO(-n-1)$ of [[../sheaves-of-modules/differentials|differentials]], and the duality between the two ends is Serre duality in the case it is proved first.

## What $H^1$ means

[[PR-ET5PQ]]

Two answers to the same question, and an examiner usually wants both: $H^1$ obstructs lifting sections, and $H^1$ of the units *is* the Picard group.
The cocycle in one description is the difference of local lifts, and in the other the transition functions of a bundle.
