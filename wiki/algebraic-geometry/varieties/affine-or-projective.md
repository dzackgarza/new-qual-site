# Affine or projective

[[D-CP2MH]]

## Is it projective?

A variety is projective when it is isomorphic to an irreducible closed subset of some $\PP^N$ ([[D-CP2MH]], [[D-VARQAP]]).

[[PR-VA4S3]]

## Is it affine?

[[PR-EFW6B]]

A closed subvariety of an affine variety is affine, and a complete connected affine variety is a point, so a variety containing a complete curve is not affine ([[D-VARCOMP]]).

## Examples

[[FE-VARSEGREPROD]]

Serre's criterion characterizes affineness cohomologically: a noetherian scheme $X$ is affine exactly when $H^p(X,\mcf)=0$ for every quasicoherent $\mcf$ and every $p>0$, equivalently when $H^1(X,\mci)=0$ for every coherent sheaf of ideals $\mci$ ([[T-5IOUR]]).
