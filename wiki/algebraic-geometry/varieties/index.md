# Varieties

The classical dictionary: radical ideals against closed subsets, and the
correspondence that makes geometry into algebra.

Examiners use this topic to check that the scheme language sitting on top of it
has something underneath. Questions here are concrete — is $\PP^1\times\PP^1$
projective, is the complement of a hypersurface in $\PP^2$ affine, do two plane
curves meeting in infinitely many points share a factor — and they are asked
because the answer has to come from a picture, not a citation.

Dimension, degree, and the Hilbert polynomial live here: what the leading term
of $P_X(r)$ means, what its constant term means.
