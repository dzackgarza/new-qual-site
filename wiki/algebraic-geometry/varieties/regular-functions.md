# Regular functions

On a projective variety over an algebraically closed field $k$, the ring of global regular functions is $k$ [@Har77, Theorem I.3.4]; the sheaf $\OO_X$ records the regular functions on every open subset.

[[D-VARREG]]

For an affine variety $X$ the ring $A(X)$ of functions regular on all of $X$ is $\OO_X(X)$; on any variety the local ring at $p$ is $\OO_{X,p}$ and the field of rational functions is $k(X)$ ([[D-VARREG]]).

## Affine, quasi-affine, projective, and quasi-projective varieties

[[D-VARQAP]]

$\AA^2 \sm \theset{0}$ is quasi-affine and not affine: a function regular off a point of $\AA^2$ extends to $\AA^2$, so its ring of global regular functions is $k[x,y]$, and the inclusion $\AA^2\sm\theset{0}\to\AA^2=\Spec k[x,y]$ is not an isomorphism [@Har77, Exercise I.3.6].

Two regular functions that agree on a dense open set agree, so a rational function is determined by its restriction to any nonempty open set on which it is regular.
