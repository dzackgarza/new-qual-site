# Blowups

The one birational modification that is asked about by name.
Everything an examiner wants follows from two formulas, and both are worth being able to derive rather than quote.

[[D-VARBLOW]]

[[PR-VAREM1]]

## What it is used for

Resolving curve singularities is the first use, and in dimension one it is not even needed: the normalization already does it, because normal plus dimension one is regular.

[[D-QJ5M9]]

In dimension two blowups are the mechanism of the whole classification.
Castelnuovo says the $(-1)$-curves are exactly the ones that can be contracted, so running the contractions until none remain produces a minimal model, and the del Pezzo surfaces are what one gets by blowing $\PP^2$ up rather than down.

[[D-VARFANO]]

The canonical formula $K_{\Bl_p X} = \pi^* K_X + (n-1)E$ is what makes that classification numerical: each blowup of a surface drops $K^2$ by one and leaves the genus alone, so $K^2$ counts how far a surface is from its minimal model.
