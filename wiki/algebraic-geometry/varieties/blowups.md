# Blowups

[[D-VARBLOW]]

[[PR-VAREM1]]

## Resolution and minimal models

[[D-QJ5M9]]

By Castelnuovo's theorem ([[T-SRFCAST]]), an integral curve on a smooth projective surface is the exceptional curve of the blowup of a smooth surface at a point exactly when it is a $(-1)$-curve ([[PR-VAREM1]]); contracting $(-1)$-curves until none remain gives a minimal surface.

[[D-VARFANO]]

A point blowup leaves $p_g$ unchanged, because $p_g$ is a birational invariant in every characteristic ([[T-BIRINV]]), and it leaves $q$ unchanged by Hartshorne's computation of the higher direct images: $R^i\pi_*\OO_{\widetilde X}=0$ for $i>0$, so $H^1(\OO_{\widetilde X})\cong H^1(\OO_X)$ [@Har77, Proposition V.3.4].
Hence a surface obtained from a surface $X_0$ by $r$ point blowups has the $p_g$ and $q$ of $X_0$ and $K^2=K_{X_0}^2-r$.
