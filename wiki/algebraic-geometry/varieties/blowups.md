# Blowups

[[D-VARBLOW]]

[[PR-VAREM1]]

## Resolution and minimal models

For curves, normalization resolves singularities because a normal one-dimensional variety is regular.

[[D-QJ5M9]]

By Castelnuovo's theorem, an integral curve on a smooth projective surface is the exceptional curve of the blowup of a smooth surface at a point exactly when it is a $(-1)$-curve; contracting $(-1)$-curves until none remain gives a minimal surface.
Over an algebraically closed field, the del Pezzo surfaces are $\PP^1\times\PP^1$ and the blowups of $\PP^2$ at $r\le8$ points in general position.

[[D-VARFANO]]

For a surface, $K_{\Bl_p X} = \pi^* K_X + E$ gives $K_{\Bl_pX}^2=K_X^2-1$, while $p_g$ and $q$ are unchanged; a surface obtained from a surface $X_0$ by $r$ point blowups has $K^2=K_{X_0}^2-r$.
