# Differentials

[[D-4GCH6]]

## The two sequences

Neither sequence in [[D-4GCH6]] is left exact in general; injectivity of the leftmost map requires additional hypotheses.
The relative sequence is also exact on the left when $X\to Y$ is smooth.
For a finite separable morphism $f\colon X\to Y$ of smooth curves, $f^*\Omega_Y \to \Omega_X$ is injective and its cokernel $\Omega_{X/Y}$ is a torsion sheaf whose length at $p$ is the coefficient of $p$ in the ramification divisor; Riemann--Hurwitz is the degree count of this sequence.
The conormal sequence is exact on the left when $X$ and $Z$ are smooth over a field, and taking determinants gives adjunction, the computation behind the genus formulas in [[algebraic-geometry/curves-and-surfaces/index|curves and surfaces]].

## The canonical sheaf

Since $\omega_{\PP^1}\cong\OO(-2)$, $H^0(\PP^1,\Omega^1)=0$; and $\omega_{\PP^n}^\vee=\OO(n+1)$ is ample, so $\PP^n$ is Fano.
