# Differentials

[[D-4GCH6]]

## The two sequences

Both sequences of [[D-4GCH6]] are exact on the right, and the leftmost map is injective only under additional hypotheses: for the conormal sequence this holds when $X$ is smooth over $k$ and $Z$ is nonsingular in $X$ [@Har77, Theorem II.8.17].
The relative sequence is exact on the left when $X\to Y$ is smooth [@Har77, §II.8].
For a finite separable morphism $f\colon X\to Y$ of smooth curves, $f^*\Omega_Y \to \Omega_X$ is injective and its cokernel $\Omega_{X/Y}$ is a torsion sheaf whose length at $p$ is the coefficient of $p$ in the ramification divisor; Riemann--Hurwitz is the degree count of this sequence [@Har77, Chapter IV, §2].
The conormal sequence is exact on the left when $Z$ is nonsingular in a nonsingular $X$, and taking determinants gives adjunction, the computation behind the genus formulas in [[algebraic-geometry/curves-and-surfaces/index|curves and surfaces]].

## The canonical sheaf

Since $\omega_{\PP^1}\cong\OO(-2)$ has no global sections, $H^0(\PP^1,\Omega^1)=0$; and $\omega_{\PP^n}^\vee=\OO(n+1)$ is ample, so $\PP^n$ is Fano [@Har77, Example II.8.20.1].
