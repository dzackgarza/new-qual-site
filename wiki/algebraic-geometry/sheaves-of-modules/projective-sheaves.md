# Sheaves on projective space

On $\Spec A$, $M\mapsto\widetilde M$ is an equivalence from $A$-modules to quasicoherent sheaves.
On $\Proj S$ it is not: graded $S$-modules that agree in all degrees $\ge n_0$ give isomorphic sheaves, and a graded module annihilated by a power of the irrelevant ideal $S_+$ gives $0$.

[[D-MODGRMOD]]

If $S$ is generated by finitely many elements of $S_1$ over $S_0$, then every quasicoherent $\mcf$ on $\Proj S$ satisfies $\mcf\cong\widetilde{\Gamma_*(\mcf)}$, where $\Gamma_*(\mcf)=\bigoplus_{n\in\ZZ}\Gamma(\Proj S,\mcf(n))$.

## Generation and ampleness

[[D-MODGG]]

Global sections $s_0,\ldots,s_n$ generating an invertible sheaf give a morphism to $\PP^n$.
Every quasicoherent sheaf on an affine scheme is globally generated.

[[D-MODAMPLE]]

A globally generated invertible sheaf gives a morphism to projective space, a very ample one gives an immersion, and $\mcl$ is ample exactly when $\mcl^{\otimes m}$ is ample for some $m>0$.

## Serre's theorems

[[T-MODSERRE]]

On an affine scheme every quasicoherent sheaf is globally generated and has vanishing higher cohomology; Serre's theorems give both properties for $\mcf(n)$ with $n\ge N_0(\mcf)$ on a projective scheme.

[[T-MODRES]]

For an additive function $\lambda$ on coherent sheaves on $\PP^n_k$, such as $\chi$, the resolution gives $\lambda(\mcf)=\sum_j(-1)^j\lambda(\mce_j)$, a combination of the values $\lambda(\OO(-d))$; for $\chi$ these come from $H^*(\PP^n,\OO(d))$ in [[algebraic-geometry/cohomology/computing-cohomology|computing cohomology]].
