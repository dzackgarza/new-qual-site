# Sheaves on projective space

On $\Spec A$, $M\mapsto\widetilde M$ is an equivalence from $A$-modules to quasicoherent sheaves [@Har77, Corollary II.5.5].
On $\Proj S$ it is not: graded $S$-modules that agree in all degrees $\ge n_0$ give isomorphic sheaves, and a graded module annihilated by a power of the irrelevant ideal $S_+$ gives $0$ [@Har77, Proposition II.5.11 and Exercise II.5.9].

[[D-MODGRMOD]]

When $S$ is finitely generated by $S_1$ as an $S_0$-algebra, every quasicoherent sheaf $\mcf$ on $\Proj S$ satisfies $\widetilde{\Gamma_*(\mcf)}\cong\mcf$, where $\Gamma_*(\mcf)=\bigoplus_{n\in\ZZ}H^0(\Proj S,\mcf(n))$ [@Har77, Proposition II.5.15].

## Generation and ampleness

[[D-MODGG]]

Global generation is what turns sections into a morphism to $\PP^n$.
Every quasicoherent sheaf on an affine scheme is globally generated, so global generation becomes restrictive on nonaffine schemes such as projective schemes.

[[D-MODAMPLE]]

The three conditions form a ladder: globally generated gives a morphism, very ample gives an immersion (a closed immersion when $X$ is proper), and ample means that some positive power is very ample.
Ampleness is preserved by positive tensor powers [@Har77, Exercise II.7.5].
