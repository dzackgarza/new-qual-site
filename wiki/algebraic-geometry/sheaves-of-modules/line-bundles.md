# Locally free sheaves and the Picard group

[[T-MODVB]]

Locally free sheaves do not form an abelian category: the cokernel of $\OO_{\AA^1}\xrightarrow{x}\OO_{\AA^1}$ is the skyscraper sheaf $\OO_{\AA^1}/(x)$, which is not locally free, so kernels and cokernels are taken among all $\OO_X$-modules.

## Rank one

[[D-MODPIC]]

For the comparison with divisor classes, see [[algebraic-geometry/divisors/index|divisors]].
For $X$ projective over an algebraically closed field, a line bundle $\mcl$ generated by sections $s_0,\ldots,s_n\in H^0(X,\mcl)$ defines a morphism $X\to\PP^n$, and that morphism is a closed embedding exactly when the subspace $V$ they span separates points and tangent vectors [@Har77, Proposition II.7.3]; generating $\mcl$ alone gives only that the morphism is defined, since $s_0=x^2$ and $s_1=y^2$ on $X=\PP^1$ with $\mcl=\OO(2)$ have no common zero and give the degree-two map $[x:y]\mapsto[x^2:y^2]$ ([[T-DIVMAPPN]], [[D-MODAMPLE]]).

The twisting sheaves $\OO(d)$ are constructed in [[algebraic-geometry/sheaves-of-modules/quasicoherence|quasicoherence and twisting]].
