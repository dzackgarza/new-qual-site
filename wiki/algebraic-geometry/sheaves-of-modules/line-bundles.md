# Locally free sheaves and the Picard group

[[T-MODVB]]

The correspondence identifies locally free sheaves with vector bundles through sheaves of sections and transition functions.
Locally free sheaves do not form an abelian category: the cokernel of $\OO_{\AA^1}\xrightarrow{x}\OO_{\AA^1}$ is the skyscraper sheaf $\OO_{\AA^1}/(x)$, which is not locally free, so kernels and cokernels are taken among all $\OO_X$-modules.

## Rank one

[[D-MODPIC]]

On a noetherian integral separated locally factorial scheme, $\Pic(X)\cong\Cl(X)$ ([[../divisors/index|divisors]]).
Global sections $s_0,\ldots,s_n$ generating an invertible sheaf $\mcl$ give a morphism $\varphi\colon X\to\PP^n$ with $\varphi^*\OO(1)\cong\mcl$ and $\varphi^*x_i=s_i$; $\mcl$ is very ample over $k$ when some such $\varphi$ is an immersion.

$\Pic(\PP^n_k)\cong\ZZ$, generated by $\OO(1)$: [[algebraic-geometry/sheaves-of-modules/quasicoherence|quasicoherence and twisting]].
