# Sheaves of modules

Quasicoherence, coherence, twisting sheaves, and line bundles.

The recurring question is whether some natural sheaf is quasicoherent — the sheaf of differentials, for instance — and the recurring computation is the cohomology of $\OO(n)$ on projective space, because everything else is eventually reduced to it.

- [[algebraic-geometry/sheaves-of-modules/quasicoherence|Quasicoherence and twisting]], the local test, and why the twists generate everything.

- [[algebraic-geometry/sheaves-of-modules/differentials|Differentials]], the two exact sequences, and the canonical sheaf that the genus formulas come out of.
