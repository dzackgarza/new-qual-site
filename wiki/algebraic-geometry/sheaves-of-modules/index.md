# Sheaves of modules

Quasicoherence, coherence, twisting sheaves, and line bundles.

For $X$ projective over a noetherian ring and $\mcf$ coherent on $X$, there is a surjection $\bigoplus_{i=1}^r\OO_X(-d_i)\to\mcf$, so long exact sequences reduce statements about $H^j(X,\mcf)$ to the cohomology of the sheaves $\OO(d)$ on projective space.

- [[algebraic-geometry/sheaves-of-modules/quasicoherence|Quasicoherence and twisting]], the local test, and coherent sheaves as quotients of finite sums of twists.

- [[algebraic-geometry/sheaves-of-modules/differentials|Differentials]], the relative and conormal sequences, the canonical sheaf, and adjunction.

- [[algebraic-geometry/sheaves-of-modules/operations|Operations and functoriality]], the module operations, the two image functors and their adjunction, and ideal sheaves.

- [[algebraic-geometry/sheaves-of-modules/line-bundles|Locally free sheaves and the Picard group]], the vector bundle dictionary, and the group $\Pic X$ of invertible sheaves under tensor product.

- [[algebraic-geometry/sheaves-of-modules/projective-sheaves|Sheaves on projective space]], graded modules, global generation and ampleness, and Serre's theorems.
