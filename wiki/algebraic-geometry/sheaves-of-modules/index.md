# Sheaves of modules

This chapter covers quasicoherent and coherent sheaves, twisting sheaves, and line bundles.

Natural sheaves such as the sheaf of differentials are tested for quasicoherence on affine charts, while resolutions by twists reduce many coherent-sheaf cohomology computations on projective space to $H^*(\PP^n,\OO(d))$.

## Syllabus

At the level of [@Har10a, Sections II.5, II.8].

- [[algebraic-geometry/sheaves-of-modules/quasicoherence|Quasicoherent and coherent sheaves]], $\OO_X$-modules, the sheaf $\widetilde M$ associated to a module, the local test for quasicoherence, and coherent sheaves.

- [[algebraic-geometry/sheaves-of-modules/operations|Operations and functoriality]], the module operations, direct and inverse image and their adjunction, and ideal sheaves of closed subschemes.

- [[algebraic-geometry/sheaves-of-modules/differentials|Differentials]], derivations, the sheaf of relative differentials, the relative and conormal sequences, and the Euler sequence.
