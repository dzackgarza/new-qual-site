# Sheaves of modules

Quasicoherence, coherence, twisting sheaves, and line bundles.

The recurring question is whether some natural sheaf is quasicoherent — the sheaf of differentials, for instance — and the recurring computation is the cohomology of $\OO(n)$ on projective space, because everything else is eventually reduced to it.
