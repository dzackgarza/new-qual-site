# Quasicoherence and twisting

A quasicoherent sheaf is locally the sheaf associated to a module on each affine chart ([[D-QNTZY]]); the sheaf of differentials ([[D-4GCH6]]) and the twisting sheaves $\OO(n)$ ([[D-CB9XS]]) are quasicoherent.

## The twists

[[D-CB9XS]]

[[FE-SHFPONE]]

For $S=k[x_0,\ldots,x_n]$, $H^0(\PP^n_k,\OO(d))=S_d$ for $d\ge0$ ([[T-IJW1K]]).
For a closed subscheme $X=V(I)\subseteq\PP^n_k$, the Hilbert polynomial of $S/I$ from [[algebraic-geometry/varieties/dimension-and-degree|dimension and degree]] equals $h^0(X,\OO_X(d))$ for $d\gg0$ ([[D-COHEULER]]).

Every coherent sheaf on projective space is a quotient of a sum of twists, and admits a finite resolution by twists ([[T-MODRES]]).
Finite resolutions by twists reduce cohomological statements about coherent sheaves to the groups $H^*(\PP^n,\OO(d))$ ([[T-MODRES]]).
