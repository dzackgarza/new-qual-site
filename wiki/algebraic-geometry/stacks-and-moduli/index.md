# Stacks and moduli

A moduli problem is a functor on schemes, and the questions are whether it is a sheaf for a topology finer than the Zariski topology, whether it is represented by a scheme, and, when it is not, by what kind of geometric object it is represented.

- [[algebraic-geometry/stacks-and-moduli/functors-and-topologies|Functors and topologies]] — the functors of points of $\AA^n$ and $\PP^n$, representability by gluing open subfunctors, sites and the étale and fppf topologies, faithfully flat descent, and torsors.

- [[algebraic-geometry/stacks-and-moduli/spaces-and-stacks|Hilbert schemes, algebraic spaces and stacks]] — the Hilbert scheme as a representable moduli functor, quotients by étale equivalence relations, and algebraic and Deligne--Mumford stacks.
