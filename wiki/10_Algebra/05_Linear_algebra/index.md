# Linear algebra

Canonical forms, the spectral theorem in finite dimension, and the matrix groups $\mathrm{GL}_n$, $\mathrm{SL}_n$, $\mathrm{O}_n$, $\mathrm{U}_n$.

[[000_Definitions|Definitions]] and [[020_Undergrad|Undergrad Review]] are the undergraduate base. [[50_Linear Algebra|Linear Algebra]] covers adjoints, inner products, and the SNF algorithm. [[050_Polynomials|Polynomials in Linear Algebra]] is the $F[t]$-module view. Then the three forms: [[100_JCF|Jordan Canonical Form]], [[120_RCF|Rational Canonical Form]], [[140_SNF|Smith Normal Form]], and [[200_Diagonalization|Diagonalizability]]. [[510_Matrix_Groups|Matrix Groups]] and [[520_Counterexamples|Matrix Counterexamples]] are the $\mathrm{GL}_n$ layer. [[500_Enumerating|Enumerating]] and [[900_Exercises|Exercises]] are drill.
