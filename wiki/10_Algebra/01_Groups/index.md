Start at [[01_Groups_Basics|Basics]] and [[00_Notation_Algebra|Notation]]. Then [[12_Sylow_Theorems|Sylow Theorems]], [[13_Groups_Classification|Classification]], and [[14_Groups_Series|Series]] (composition series, solvability).
