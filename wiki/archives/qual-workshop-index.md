# Workshop index

Weekly qualifying-exam workshop pages, grouped by subject and in the order the
weeks were held; each entry names what that week's page covers.

## Complex analysis

- [[complex-analysis/workshops/index|Complex analysis workshops]] -- the subject's
  workshop pages.

- [[complex-analysis/workshops/complex-week-1-preliminaries|Week 1: preliminaries]] --
  complex arithmetic and the geometry of the plane, uniform convergence and the
  Heine--Cantor theorem, the inverse and implicit function theorems, Green's and
  Stokes' theorems, and the Riemann sphere.

- [[complex-analysis/workshops/complex-week-2-cauchy|Week 2: Cauchy's integral formula and residues]] --
  Blaschke factors, Cauchy's integral formula and Cauchy's inequalities, and the
  residue formulas with the $ML$ estimate and Jordan's lemma, with references to
  [@SS03, secs. 1.3, 3.6].

## Real analysis

- [[real-analysis/workshops/index|Real analysis workshops]] -- the subject's
  workshop pages.

- [[real-analysis/workshops/real-week-1-preliminaries|Week 1: preliminaries]] --
  continuity and uniform continuity, series and uniform convergence, the
  Heine--Borel theorem and compactness, normed spaces, bounded variation, the
  Baire category theorem, the mean value and Taylor theorems, Riemann
  integrability, and the Weierstrass approximation theorem.

- [[real-analysis/workshops/real-week-2-measure-theory|Week 2: measure theory and Fubini--Tonelli]] --
  metric spaces and convergence, then measurable sets and functions, the
  convergence theorems, and the Fubini and Tonelli theorems; the page's
  references are [@Fol99, chap. 1; @SS05, chaps. 1--2].

## Algebra

- [[algebra/workshops/index|Algebra workshops]] -- the subject's workshop pages.

- [[algebra/workshops/algebra-week-1-groups-warmup|Week 1: groups warmup]] --
  subgroups, Cauchy's and Lagrange's theorems, the four isomorphism theorems, the
  special families of groups, the finite groups of order at most $20$, and the
  implications among the classes of groups.

- [[algebra/workshops/algebra-week-2-finite-group-theory|Week 2: finite group theory]] --
  direct and semidirect products, the product formula for $\size{HK}$, group
  actions and transitive subgroups, the structure theorem for finitely generated
  abelian groups, simple groups, automorphisms, and series of groups.

- [[algebra/workshops/algebra-week-3-sylow-theory|Week 3: Sylow theory]] --
  the three Sylow theorems, the counting arguments that precede classification,
  criteria for a group to be abelian, the groups of specified orders, and direct
  and semidirect products.

- [[algebra/workshops/algebra-week-4-rings|Week 4: rings]] --
  ideals and quotients, elements and factorization, radicals and the two spectra,
  the classes of rings, existence by Zorn's lemma, and localization.

- [[algebra/workshops/algebra-week-n-rep-theory|Representation theory]] --
  representations and the group ring, simple, semisimple, and indecomposable
  modules, the theorems of Maschke and Schur, and characters with the character
  table.

- [[algebra/workshops/algebra-week-n-1-linear-algebra|Linear algebra]] --
  the Jordan canonical form with the minimal and characteristic polynomials, and
  a practice section.

## Topology

- [[topology/workshops/index|Topology workshops]] -- the subject's workshop pages.

- [[topology/workshops/topology-week-1-preliminaries|Week 1: point-set]] --
  topologies with open, closed, and clopen sets and bases, continuity and
  homeomorphisms, subspaces, products and quotients, closures and retracts,
  metric spaces, completeness and boundedness, and compactness.
