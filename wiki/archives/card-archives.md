# Source archives

The Sources listing is the catalogue of the collection pages.
A collection is one source, and its page lists that source's problems in source
order, with the locator of each problem in the printed or scanned original; each
entry links the problem card, which holds the statement and, where the corpus
has one, its solution.

The collections are of four kinds, and the listing sorts and filters on subject
area, institution, and date.

- **University examinations**, one collection per sitting, filed under the
  institution, the subject area, and the term and year.

- **Compilations**, several sittings or several subjects of one institution
  bound into one document; a compiled collection holds that packet's problems in
  the packet's order rather than splitting the sittings.

- **Homework sets**, one collection per assignment of a course.

- **Textbooks**, one collection per book, whose sections follow the book's
  chapters and exercises.

Material that is not held as a collection — an examination archive at another
institution, a solution manual, a practice packet with no transcribed problems —
is annotated on [[prelim/resources/problems|the prelim problem banks]],
[[prelim/resources/solutions|the prelim solutions]], and
[[archives/solution-compendia|the solution collections]].
