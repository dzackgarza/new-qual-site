# Cambridge Part III examination papers

Part III of the Cambridge Mathematical Tripos is a one-year taught course at
master's level, the fourth year of the tripos for Cambridge undergraduates and
the MASt for entrants from other universities, and it is examined by one written
paper for each lecture course of the year.

The papers are numbered with the courses. The 2021 set runs from 101 Commutative
Algebra and 113 Algebraic Geometry, through 136 Local Fields and 160
Representation Theory of Symmetric Groups, to 201 Advanced Probability, 309
General Relativity, and 324 Quantum Computation.

- [Part III of the Mathematical Tripos examination papers, 2021](https://www.maths.cam.ac.uk/postgrad/part-iii/current/tripos-examination-papers/2021) --
  the papers of that year, one PDF for each course, listed by course number.
