# Resources

## Examinations

One collection per sitting, Fall 2004 to Spring 2026, each listing that
sitting's problems in exam order.
The collections are listed by institution, subject, and date on
[[archives/card-archives|the Sources listing]], under UCSD applied algebra.

## Subject pages

[[applied-algebra/matrix-analysis/index|Matrix analysis]] treats Hermitian,
positive definite, unitary, and normal matrices, the spectral decomposition and
the matrix factorizations, eigenvalue location, and matrix norms.

[[applied-algebra/representation-theory/index|Representation theory]] treats
characters of finite groups, induced representations, the representations of
the symmetric group, and the Frobenius--Schur indicator.

[[applied-algebra/symmetric-functions/index|Symmetric functions]] treats the
five bases, the change-of-basis identities, the Littlewood--Richardson rule, and
the Frobenius characteristic map.

[[applied-algebra/grobner-bases/index|Gröbner bases and varieties]] treats
Buchberger's algorithm, the elimination and membership tests, and the
Nullstellensatz.

[[applied-algebra/invariant-theory/index|Invariant theory]] treats the
finiteness theorems of Noether and Hilbert, Molien's formula, and the Reynolds
operator.

## Texts

- [@HK71] — linear algebra over a field: eigenvalues, the characteristic
  polynomial, inner product spaces, and the diagonalization of Hermitian and
  normal operators.

- [@DF04] and [@Art91] — abstract algebra: group actions, rings, and the
  polynomial ring over a field.

- [@Ser77] and [@FH91] — the representation theory of finite groups: characters,
  induced representations, and the representations of the symmetric group.

- [@AM18] and [@Eis95] — commutative algebra: ideals, monomial orders, and the
  Nullstellensatz.
