# $\SL_2(\RR)$ and modular forms

Recall that a [[D-7PTGC|modular form]] of weight $k$, for an even $k$, is a holomorphic $f\colon\HH\to\CC$ with $f\big((az+b)/(cz+d)\big)=(cz+d)^kf(z)$ for every $\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}\in\SL_2(\ZZ)$ and a $q$-expansion with no negative powers; the quotient $\SL_2(\ZZ)\backslash\HH$ is the moduli space of lattices up to scale (C. McMullen, *Advanced Complex Analysis*, Theorem 5.26).

[[T-VGDFW]]

The matrix group acts by [[D-FRVBV|Möbius transformations]] ([[T-VGDFW]]); the matrices with integer entries and determinant $1$ form the discrete subgroup $\SL_2(\ZZ)$, which is generated by $z\mapsto z+1$ and $z\mapsto-1/z$ ([[E-SS10.PR-2]], and [@SS03, ch. 9, §2] where the group is defined).

## Modular forms

[[D-7PTGC]]

::: {.example title="Eisenstein series"}
For an even integer $k\geq4$ the series
$$
G_k(z)\coloneqq\sum_{(m,n)\neq(0,0)}\frac{1}{(mz+n)^k}
$$
converges absolutely and uniformly on compact subsets of $\HH$, and satisfies $G_k\qty{(az+b)/(cz+d)} = (cz+d)^kG_k(z)$ for every $\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}\in\SL_2(\ZZ)$ [@SS03, ch. 9, §2.1, Theorem 2.1(i), (iii)].
Stein--Shakarchi write this same series as $E_k(\tau)=\sum_{(n,m)\neq(0,0)}(n+m\tau)^{-k}$, and the modular character of that theorem reads $E_k(\tau+1) = E_k(\tau)$ and $E_k(\tau) = \tau^{-k}E_k(-1/\tau)$.
$G_k$'s $q$-expansion begins with a constant term and no negative powers [@SS03, ch. 9, §2.2, Theorem 2.5], so $G_k$ is a modular form of weight $k$.
:::
