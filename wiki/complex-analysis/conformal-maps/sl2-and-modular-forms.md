# SL2(R) and modular forms

The group $\SL_2(\RR)$ acts on the upper half plane by Möbius transformations, and the functions that transform under the subgroup $\SL_2(\ZZ)$ by the factor $\SL_2$ prescribes are the modular forms; the quotient $\SL_2(\ZZ)\backslash\HH$ is the moduli space of lattices up to scale.

[[T-VGDFW]]

A matrix $\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}$ with real entries and determinant $1$ acts by $z\mapsto(az+b)/(cz+d)$, and the whole group acts by biholomorphisms of $\HH$; the ones with integer entries and determinant $1$ form the discrete subgroup $\SL_2(\ZZ)$, generated by $z\mapsto z+1$ and $z\mapsto-1/z$.

## Modular forms

[[D-7PTGC]]

::: {.example title="Eisenstein series"}
For an even integer $k\geq4$ the series
$$
G_k(z)\coloneqq\sum_{(m,n)\neq(0,0)}\frac{1}{(mz+n)^k}
$$
converges absolutely and uniformly on compact subsets of $\HH$, and satisfies $G_k\qty{(az+b)/(cz+d)} = (cz+d)^kG_k(z)$ for every $\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}\in\SL_2(\ZZ)$, because the substitution permutes the lattice $\ZZ z+\ZZ$.
Its $q$-expansion begins with a constant term and no negative powers, so $G_k$ is a modular form of weight $k$ [@Ser73, ch. 7].
:::

The fundamental domain of $\SL_2(\ZZ)$ is $\theset{z\in\HH : \abs z\geq1,\ \abs{\Re z}\leq\frac12}$, with the two sides identified by $z\mapsto z+1$ and the arc $\abs z = 1$ by $z\mapsto -1/z$; a modular form is determined by its values there, and the growth condition at $\infty$ is a condition at the cusp.
