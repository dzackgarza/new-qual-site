# The Schwarz lemma

A holomorphic map $f\colon\DD\to\DD$ with $f(0)=0$ satisfies $\abs{f(z)}\leq\abs z$ and $\abs{f'(0)}\leq 1$, and equality in either bound, at a single $z\neq 0$ in the first, holds only for a rotation.
