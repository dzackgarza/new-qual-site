## Review

::: {.fact title="What to be able to do without hesitating"}
\envlist

- Write $z = x+iy = re^{i\theta}$, multiply and divide in polar form, and take $n$-th roots as the vertices of a regular $n$-gon ([[complex-analysis/basics/complex-arithmetic|Complex arithmetic]]).

- Decide whether a continuous function on a compact set is uniformly continuous, and whether a sequence of functions converges uniformly ([[complex-analysis/basics/uniform-convergence|Uniform convergence]]).

- Compute a contour integral from a parameterization, and recognise when the integrand has a [[D-6RZQN|primitive]] so that the value depends only on the endpoints ([[complex-analysis/cauchy-theory/contours-and-primitives|Contours and primitives]]).

- Pass between the plane and the sphere: which sequences converge to $\infty$, and what a Möbius transformation does to a generalized circle ([[complex-analysis/basics/the-riemann-sphere|The Riemann sphere]]).
:::
