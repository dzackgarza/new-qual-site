# Counting zeros

The [[T-JXDQT|argument principle]] and [[T-CJCKL|Rouché's theorem]] count the zeros minus the poles of a meromorphic function in a region, and [[T-FZWEC|Hurwitz's theorem]] carries the zeros of a locally uniform limit to the limit.

- [[complex-analysis/counting-zeros/how-many-zeros-in-this-region|How many zeros in this region?]], the hypotheses under which each theorem applies.

- [[complex-analysis/counting-zeros/the-argument-principle|The argument principle]], zeros minus poles as an integral of the [[D-IA5FS|logarithmic derivative]].

- [[complex-analysis/counting-zeros/rouches-theorem|Rouché's theorem]], equal counts for functions related by a strict inequality on the boundary.

- [[complex-analysis/counting-zeros/hurwitz|Hurwitz's theorem]], zeros of a locally uniform limit.
