# Counting zeros

The argument principle, Rouché's theorem, and Hurwitz's theorem count the zeros of a holomorphic or meromorphic function in a region.

- [[complex-analysis/counting-zeros/how-many-zeros-in-this-region|How many zeros in this region?]], the hypotheses under which each theorem applies.

- [[complex-analysis/counting-zeros/the-argument-principle|The argument principle]], zeros minus poles as an integral of the logarithmic derivative.

- [[complex-analysis/counting-zeros/rouches-theorem|Rouché's theorem]], equal counts for functions related by a strict inequality on the boundary.

- [[complex-analysis/counting-zeros/hurwitz|Hurwitz's theorem]], zeros of a locally uniform limit.
