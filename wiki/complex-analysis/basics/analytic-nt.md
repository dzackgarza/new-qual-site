# Summation by parts

[[PR-2MTPE]]

The identity writes a finite sum as a boundary term plus a sum of differences, so a series converges when the partial sums of one factor are bounded, the other factor tends to $0$, and its successive differences are absolutely summable.
Both [[FT-3ZL25|Dirichlet's test]] and [[L-MYZOX|Abel's test]] are consequences of it, applied with one factor having bounded partial sums: that factor is $a_k$, with partial sums $A_k$, in [[PR-2MTPE]], and $b_k$ in [[FT-3ZL25|Dirichlet's test]].
