# Quals

Past topology qualifying exams, split by program.

[[40_Topology/08_Quals/UCSD/index|UCSD]] is three exams (Summer 2003, Fall 2014, Fall 2017) plus a solutions packet; some problems from those exams are written out under Quals/Old. [[40_Topology/08_Quals/UGA/index|UGA]] is the UGA topology qual, filed by topic: general topology, $\pi_1$, covers, cells, homology and degree, surfaces, fixed points.
