# Written UCSD statements

Problem statements for three UCSD topology sittings, with the cards underneath:

- [[Fall 2014]]: tetrahedron skeleton, linked solid tori, a homology-$[\mathbb{Z},\mathbb{Z}_3,0,\mathbb{Z}]$ factor, a rank-one $H_2$ four-manifold, $T^2$ with a 2-cell, $\mathbb{CP}^2\#\mathbb{CP}^2$ vs $S^2\times S^2$, nonzero-degree cohomology injectivity, higher homotopy of $\Sigma_g$.
- [[Summer 2003]]: covering maps among $S^2$, $T^2$, and $K$; free-product subgroups; a prescribed homology; a knotted solid torus in $S^3$; and the rest of that paper.
- [[Final Fall 2017]]: six problems stored as cards only.

The card-only lists for the same years live one folder up, under UCSD, and contain different problems from those sittings.
